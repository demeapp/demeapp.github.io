The official record

What the item proposed

Sky Bridges, Chief Operating Officer, Aboriginal Peoples Television Network (APTN), will give a presentation on Indigenous Day Live. Indigenous Day Live, APTN's premier event to celebrate National Aboriginal Day is coming to Toronto on June 23, 2018 at the Fort York National Historical Site. APTN is a registered, not-for-profit charity. Indigenous Day Live has grown to be the largest celebration of Aboriginal culture in Canada. The celebration is a day-long event with activities for all that leads up to a high-quality musical broadcast show.

What was decided

The Aboriginal Affairs Committee received the item for information.
