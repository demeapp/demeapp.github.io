What the item proposed

Melanie Ribau, Senior Services Specialist, Indigenous Community Connections and Partnerships, Toronto Public Library (TPL), will give a presentation on Indigenous initiatives at the TPL. On April 18, 2017, the TPL Board endorsed 42 strategies for Indigenous initiatives as part of TPL's official response to the Truth and Reconciliation Commission's (TRC) Calls to Action. These strategies have been developed for consultation with Indigenous communities and service providers in Toronto, and have been informed by the TRC's Calls to Action, the Indigenous Matters Committee of the Canadian Federation of Library Associations, informal conversations with community members, an external scan of Indigenous initiatives at other public and academic libraries in Canada, and a TPL staff survey of Indigenous initiatives.
