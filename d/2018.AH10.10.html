The official record

What the item proposed

On April 27, 2018, the Province of Ontario confirmed Toronto's funding allocation through the Ontario Development Charges Rebate Program of $60 million over five years, beginning in 2018/2019. This represents almost half of the province-wide program funding of $125 million. The purpose of the program is to stimulate the creation of new non-luxury rental housing in communities, such as Toronto, where suitable rental homes have become increasingly hard for individuals and families to find and afford. The results of the 2018 Open Door Program Call for Affordable Rental Housing Applications will be outlined in a companion report at the July 2018 meeting of Council. It will recommend 422 mid-range rental homes in three mixed-income developments as eligible to participate in the first year of Toronto's delivery of the Ontario Development Charges Rebate Program.
