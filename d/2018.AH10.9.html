The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Community Housing Corporation (TCHC) is undertaking a multi-phase plan to redevelop Regent Park, the country's largest social housing community. TCHC is halfway through the revitalization that will ultimately replace 2,083 Rent-Geared-to-Income (RGI) units and develop approximately 5,417 market units within a 69-acre site. In January 2004, Council approved a report on the financing plan for Regent Park that recommended property tax exemptions of 15 years for all new RGI homes in the Regent Park Revitalization Plan Area (shown in Attachment 1) effective from when the homes are developed and appropriate agreements signed. The business model for the revitalization was premised on developing market housing alongside social housing to help fund the development, as well as operating and capital maintenance savings, and City contributions including property tax exemptions.
