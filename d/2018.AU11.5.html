City Council was asked to award RFP No. 9171-17-7291 to Welch LLP.

Official title: Award of Request for Proposal 9171-17-7291 to Welch LLP for External Audit Services for City Arenas, Community Centres and Miscellaneous Entities

AdoptedMar 26, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to advise on the results of Request for Proposal (RFP) 9171-17-7291 for external audits of the annual financial statement audits for the years 2018 to 2022 inclusive for City arenas, community centres and miscellaneous entities (listed in Attachment 1), and to request authority to enter into an agreement with the recommended proponent, Welch LLP.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the Auditor General to negotiate and enter into an agreement in the amount of $747,000 excluding all applicable taxes and charges, $844,110 including HST and all applicable charges, $760,147, net of HST recoveries, with Welch LLP, being the proponent that met all the requirements of the Request for Proposal and providing the best overall value, for external audit services to perform the annual financial statement audits for the years ending December 31, 2018 to 2022 inclusive for City arenas, community centres and miscellaneous entities listed in Attachment 1 to the report (January 24, 2018) from the Auditor General and the Chief Purchasing Officer, Purchasing and Materials Management, on the terms and conditions set out in Request for Proposal 9171-17-7291 and in a form satisfactory to the City…
