What the item proposed

The Auditor General initiated the audit of the City's Information Technology (IT) infrastructure and asset management in 2017. The results of the Phase 1 of this review were presented to the Audit Committee on February 7, 2018. This current report provides results of our Phase 2 audit of the City’s IT infrastructure and asset management. In this Phase 2 report, we have identified the following opportunities for improvement related to various IT projects and initiatives: - Improving the accuracy of inventory information: The Information Technology Asset Management (ITAM) System was not reliable due to incomplete and inaccurate information. Improvements are needed here. - Better planning: Computers valuing $1.9 million were purchased in bulk but not deployed for a significant amount of time.
