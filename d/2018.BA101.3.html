Award of Tender Call No. 33-2018 to Capital Sewer Services Inc. for the Full Length Trenchless Rehabilitation of Existing Gravity Sewers and Related Works at Various Locations in the City of Toronto Service District A

AdoptedOct 10, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Chief Purchasing Officer recommends that the Bid Award Panel award Tender Call No. 33-2018 to Capital Sewer Services Inc for the Full Length Trenchless Rehabilitation of Existing Gravity Sewers and Related Works at Various Locations in the City of Toronto Service District A. The panel is asked to authorize the contract award.

The official summary names a figure of $12,676,737.76.
