What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (April 11, 2018) from the Chief Purchasing Officer: Call Number: Request for Quotation 3919-18-0090 Description: For the supply, delivery, labour, equipment, unloading and placing of Unshrinkable Fill, including but not limited to, in underground service and utility trenches, and around structures on a 24 hours, 7 days a week basis at various locations within the City of Toronto for the period effective from date of Award to April 15, 2019 with the option to renew the Contract for four (4) additional separate one (1) year periods. Should the option years be exercised, the General Manager or Director of each Division will request the Chief Purchasing Officer to process the renewals under the same terms and conditions.…
