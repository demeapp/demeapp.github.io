The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. The Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the following contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (May 10, 2018) from the Chief Purchasing Officer: Call Number: Request For Quotation No. 0505-18-0061 Description: For the non-exclusive supply and delivery Various Medical Supplies to various Purchasing and Materials Management (Stores) locations from the date of award to April 30, 2019, with the option to renew the Contract for additional four (4) additional one (1) year periods at the sole discretion of the City and subject to budget approval(s). Prior to the renewal of the contract, PMMD will perform a market analysis to verify the current market conditions as compared to the proposed increase by the vendor and the Stores Division will monitor vendor performance during the first term of the contract.
