What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. The Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the following contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (May 10, 2018) from the Chief Purchasing Officer: Call Number: Tender Call No. 36-2018 Description: The supply of all materials, equipment, labour and supervision required to complete the park improvement works at Smithfield Park including but not limited to a new sport courts, and an accessible asphalt walkway system, on behalf of the Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division of the City of Toronto. Recommended Bidder: Mopal Construction Ltd. Contract Award Value: $ 613,800 net of all applicable taxes and charges $ 693,594 including HST and all applicable charges $ 624,603 net of HST recoveries Contract is expected to start following the award and end on July 31, 2018.
