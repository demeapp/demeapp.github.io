What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: Call Number: Request for Proposal (RFP) No. 9101-18-7064 Description: For security guard services at Toronto St. Lawrence Market Complex to protect persons, property, and the public, as required by the Corporate Security Division of the City of Toronto for a period of two (2) years from July 1, 2018 to June 30, 2020 with the City having the option to extend the Contract for three (3) additional separate one (1) year periods. Should the option years be exercised, the Director of Corporate Security or designate, will request the Director of Purchasing and Materials Management to process the renewals under the same terms and conditions, with the exception of any price adjustment.
