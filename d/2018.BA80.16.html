What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: Call Number: Tender Call No. 61-2018, Contract No. 18EY-118TR Description: The work in this contract includes milling and paving of patch areas on arterials, collectors and local roads, limited repairs to sidewalks, curbs, and adjustments (catch basins, maintenance holes, etc.) in the Etobicoke York District, Wards 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 11, 12, 13 and 17 and any additional work as directed by the Contract Administration. Recommended Bidder: Gazzola Paving Limited Contract Award Value: $1,454,162 net of all applicable taxes and charges $1,643,203 including HST and all applicable charges $1,479,755 net of HST recoveries Contract is expected to start on date of award and end on October 12, 2018.
