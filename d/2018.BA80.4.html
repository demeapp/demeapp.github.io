The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: Call Number: Request for Quotation No. 6617-17-7049 Description: For the non-exclusive supply and delivery of Water Soluble Polyelectrolyte (dry) for Sludge Dewatering (DEW) (Part B) and Water Soluble Polyelectrolyte (Emulsion) for Sludge Dewatering (Part C) for the City of Toronto's Highland Creek Wastewater Treatment from date of award to March 31, 2019 with the option to renew the Contract for two (2) additional separate one (1) year periods at the sole discretion of the City. Should the option(s) to renew be exercised, then the General Manager, Toronto Water Division will request the Chief Purchasing Officer of Purchasing and Materials Management to process the necessary contract renewals under the same terms and conditions.
