What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: Call Number: Request for Quotation No. 9115-18-7042 Description: For the non-exclusive supply of predictive maintenance services including all labour, materials, tools, transportation and mileage (including sample collection), equipment and any incidentals necessary to perform Vibration Analysis, Infrared Thermography, Oil Analysis, Motor Current Analysis, Ultrasound Analysis, or any other predictive technology from date of award to March 31, 2019, with the option to renew the Contract for four (4) additional one (1) year periods. Should the option(s) to renew be exercised, then the General Manager, Toronto Water Division will request the Chief Purchasing Officer of Purchasing and Materials Management to process the necessary contract renewals under the same terms and conditions.
