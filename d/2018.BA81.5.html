What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: Call Number: Tender Call No. 53-2018 Description: Major Road Resurfacing on Keele Street from Rogers Road to Avon Drive; Civic Improvements on Keele Street from Donald Avenue to Ewart Avenue; Local Road Reconstruction and Substandard Water Service Upgrades on Ewart Avenue from Scott Road to Keele Street (Contract No. 18ECS-TI-09MR). Recommended Bidder: Sanscon Construction Ltd. Contract Award Value: $1,302,806 net of all applicable taxes and charges $1,472,170 including HST and all applicable charges $1,325,735 net of HST recoveries The contract is expected to start on the date of award and end 42 calendar days from the actual construction commencement date. The engineering estimate for this project is $1,464,290 net of all taxes and charges.
