What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (June 21, 2018) from the Chief Purchasing Officer: Call Number: Request for Proposal No. 9101-18-7030 Description: For city-wide security guard services as required by the Corporate Security Division of the City of Toronto for a period of three (3) years from July 1, 2018 to June 30, 2021 with the City having the option to extend the Contract for an additional one (1) year period. Should the option years be exercised, the Director of Corporate Security or designate, will request the Chief Purchasing Officer of Purchasing and Materials Management to process the renewals under the same terms and conditions, with the exception of any price adjustment.
