The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (June 26, 2018) from the Chief Purchasing Officer: Call Number: Request for Tender (RFT) No. 19-2018; Contract No. 18ECS-MI-02HU Description: The Construction Services for the Humber Treatment Plant Engineering and Construction Services Field Office.
