What was decided

The Bid Award Panel Adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (July 4, 2018) from the Director, Purchasing and Materials Management: Call Number: RFQ No. 6016-18-0099 Description: For the Supply and Delivery of Eighteen (18) Latest Model, Trailer Mounted Air Jacketed Asphalt Career with a Capacity of 2 Ton and with Diesel Heated System. The Request for Quotation was structured to purchase only seventeen (17) units. Following the evaluation Fleet services has determined the need for one (1) additional unit to be purchased which is in accordance with the terms and conditions of the Request for Quotation. Lowest Bidders total bid price was increased to reflect eighteen (18) units. Recommended Bidder: Amaco Construction Equipment Inc. Contract Award Value: $743,150 net of all applicable taxes…
