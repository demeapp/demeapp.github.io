What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (July 13, 2018) from the Chief Purchasing Officer: Call Number: Request for Proposal No. 9117-18-7074 Description: The purpose of this Request for Proposal is to better understand impact of transients in the water distribution network. Transients are very high pressure spikes generated in the water network due to sudden operational changes such as power failure, pump stoppage and valve closure. The study will be useful to establish correlation between transients and watermain breakages.
