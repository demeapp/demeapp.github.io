What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (July 9, 2018) from the Chief Purchasing Officer: Call Number: Tender Call No. 103-2018 Description: The supply of all materials, equipment, labour and supervision required to complete the park improvement works at Cloverdale Park including but not limited to a new playground, gathering space and an accessible asphalt walkway system, on behalf of the Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division of the City of Toronto. Recommended Bidder: Mopal Construction Ltd. Contract Award Value: $ 778,200 net of all applicable taxes and charges $ 879,366 including HST and all applicable charges $ 791,897 net of HST recoveries Contract is expected to start following the award and end on August 31, 2018.
