Award of Request for Quotation No. 3907-18-7040 to Various Vendors, for the Non-Exclusive Rehabilitations and Building Repairs for the City of Toronto's Water Division

AdoptedAug 23, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Bid Award Panel was asked to award Request for Quotation No. 3907-18-7040 to Various Vendors, for the Non-Exclusive Rehabilitations and Building Repairs for the City of Toronto's Water Division.

What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (August 8, 2018) from the Chief Purchasing Officer: Call Number: Request for Quotation No. 3907-18-7040 Description: For the non-exclusive supply, delivery, installation, and warranty of labour, equipment, materials and supervision necessary for the completion of the Work on Additions, Rehabilitations and Building Repairs for the City of Toronto's Water Division for a period of eighteen (18) months from date of award, with the option to renew for one (1) additional one (1) year period. Should the option(s) to renew be exercised, then the General Manager, Toronto Water Division will request the Director of Purchasing and Materials Management to process the necessary contract renewals under the same terms and conditions.…
