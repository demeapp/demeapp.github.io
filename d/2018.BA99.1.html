Award of Request for Proposal No. 9117-18-5058 to Doug Dixon & Associates Inc. for Engineering Design and Services during Construction for F.G. Gardiner Expressway Substructure Rehabilitation of Twelve (12) Bents and Five (5) Piers

AdoptedOct 3, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Bid Award Panel was asked to award Request for Proposal No. 9117-18-5058 to Doug Dixon & Associates Inc for Engineering Design and Services during Construction for F.G. Gardiner Expressway Substructure Rehabilitation of Twelve (12) Bents and Five (5) Piers.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Call Issued: July 18, 2018 Call Closed: August 28, 2018 Number of Addenda Issued: Eight (8) Number of Bids: Three (3) Proponent Name Doug Dixon & Associates Inc. Parsons Inc. WSP Canada All proposals met the 75 percent technical threshold score and had their cost of services opened and evaluated. Range of Scores: 82.34 to 90.39.

What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (September 27, 2018) from the Chief Purchasing Officer: Call Number: Request for Proposal (RFP) No. 9117-18-5058 Description: Engineering Design Services and services during construction for F.G. Gardiner Expressway substructure rehabilitation of twelve (12) bents 120 to 131, west of Spadina Ave and five (5) piers for eastbound on ramp from 164 to 168 west of Lower Simcoe Street. Recommended Proponent: Doug Dixon & Associates Inc. Contract Award Value: $920,212 net of all applicable taxes and charges $1,039,840 including HST and all applicable charges $936,408 net of HST recoveries Contract is expected to start on date of award and end on December 31, 2021.
