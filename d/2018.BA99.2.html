What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (September 24, 2018) from the Chief Purchasing Officer: Call Number: RFP 9118-18-5016 Description: For professional technical services for provision of all architectural, technical and administrative services for the design and construction contract administration to construct the Western North York Community Centre (Proposed Net Zero Energy Building). Recommended Proponent: MacLennan Jaunkalns Miller Architects Ltd. Contract Award Value: $2,688,750 net of all applicable taxes and charges $3,038,288 including HST and all applicable charges $2,736,072 net of HST recoveries Contract is expected to start on date of award and end on completion of the construction contract administration phase (Dec. 2023).
