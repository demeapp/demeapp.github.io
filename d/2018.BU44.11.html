Danforth: An Even Greater Avenue

Referred back for more workMay 28, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Paula Fletcher recommends that Include funding in Transportation Budget for a Major Corridor Study that includes sufficient funds for a complete streets review.

Council sent it back for more work.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Currently there are a number of planning studies of the Danforth underway- one from Coxwell to Victoria Park as well as one transit related study for main and Danforth. In 2019 the section form Coxwell to Broadview is to be considered for a study by City Planning. There is also the Master Planning for the new police station at Coxwell and Danforth in 2019 as well as a proposed corridor study for the entire avenue which is to begin in 2019. Two of the 5 Business Improvement Area's also have a streetscape study underway. Economic Development has a city wide retail study underway which will include the 5 Business Improvement Areas on the over 7 kilometre stretch of the Danforth The many studies underway could be brought together within an overarching concept - that of making Danforth an even greater street. What was decided

The Budget Committee referred the item to the Interim Chief Financial Officer, the Deputy City Manager, Cluster A and the Deputy City Manager, Cluster B for consideration as part of the 2019 Budget process.
