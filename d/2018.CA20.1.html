Appointment of Member to the French Language Advisory Committee

AdoptedMar 26, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to appoint the recommended candidate(s) to the French Language Advisory Committee for a term ending November 30, 2018. Board appointments are made at the pleasure of Council, until a successor is appointed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend the appointment of a member of the public to the French Language Advisory Committee (FLAC).

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council appoint the following candidate as a public member on the French Language Advisory Committee, at pleasure of Council, for a term of office ending on November 30, 2018: - Barbara Ceccarelli
