The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend the appointment of a Member of Council to the Yonge and St. Clair Business Improvement Area board.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council appoint Councillor Josh Matlow, Ward 22, St. Paul's, to the Yonge and St. Clair Business Improvement Area Board of Management for a term of office starting when the Board of Management is established and ending on November 30, 2018 and until a successor is appointed.
