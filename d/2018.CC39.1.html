The official record

What the item proposed

Councillor Shelley Carroll has resigned City Council effective April 5, 2018. City Council needs to officially declare her seat vacant and approve a process for the appointment of a replacement.

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council declare a vacancy in the office of Councillor, Ward 33 – Don Valley East. 2. City Council reschedule its next regular meeting to start on Wednesday, May 23, 2018 and hold a special meeting on Tuesday, May 22, 2018 at 9:30 a.m. in the Council Chamber to fill the Ward 33 vacancy by appointment. 3. City Council fill the vacancy in accordance with the following: a. the City Clerk advertises the vacancy inviting any interested and qualified applicants to seek appointment to City Council; b. interested persons complete a Consent of Nominee and Acknowledgement of Qualifications and provide personal identification showing their name and qualifying address within the City of Toronto; c. the deadline for submitting the required forms to the City Clerk is Monday, May 14, 2018 at 4:30 p.m.; d. the City Clerk…
