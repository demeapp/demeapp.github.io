What the item proposed

The City Solicitor has been made aware of a request from a member of Council for indemnification of legal expenses arising from the member's commencement of a defamation action. A report will be submitted to City Council from the City Solicitor in relation to this request. A similar request for indemnification was made in 2009. In that request, the City Solicitor retained an independent external lawyer to assess whether in the circumstances it was appropriate for the City to reimburse the Councillor's legal expenses. The City Solicitor will undertake a similar process for the current request.
