The official record

What the item proposed

The applicant appealed its Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment applications and referred its Site Plan Control application to the Ontario Municipal Board ("the Board") on May 29, 2017. The two appeals and referral were consolidated by the Board. The first prehearing conference was held before the Board on January 16, 2018. A second Prehearing conference is scheduled for June 5, 2018. Notwithstanding recent amendments to the Planning Act and their proclamation on April 3, 2018, the newly formed Local Planning Appeal Tribunal will maintain jurisdiction over the appeals according to the rules of the former Ontario Municipal Board. Following settlement discussions with the appellant during the first quarter of 2018, the appellant provided a final settlement offer on April 12, 2018.
