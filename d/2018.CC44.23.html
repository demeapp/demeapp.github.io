What the item proposed

Marlin Spring Investments Ltd. (the "Applicant") is the owner of lands municipally known as 30 and 44 Zorra Street (the "Subject Site"). On October 28, 2015, the former owners of the Subject Site, 1127792 Ontario Limited (the "Former Owners"), submitted a Zoning By-law Amendment application to the City proposing to amend the Etobicoke Zoning Code to permit the development of a 36-storey residential building containing 379 units on the Subject Site (the "Application"). The proposed building has a total gross floor area of 28,170 square metres and an overall density of 5.4 times the area of the land. The building includes 401 vehicular parking spaces to be located in 5 levels of underground garage. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (July 17, 2018) from the City Solicitor remain confidential at the discretion of the City Solicitor as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
