What the item proposed

At its meeting on May 5, 2015, City Council adopted a Principles Report (Item TE5.8) for a City-initiated land use study of the area known as the "Bathurst - Bloor Four Corners" at the intersection of Bathurst Street and Bloor Street. The Report recommended that the Director of Community Planning, Toronto and East York District to schedule community consultations, provide recommendations for the preservation of community resources, and report back with an official plan amendment and zoning by-law amendment related to retail uses in the area. At its meeting on December 13, 2016, City Council adopted the Final Report regarding the Bathurst - Bloor Four Corners Study (Item TE20.5), and the resulting Official Plan Amendment No. 349 and Zoning By-law Amendment 1283-2016.
