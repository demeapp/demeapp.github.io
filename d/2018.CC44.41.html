What the item proposed

On December 21, 2016, a Zoning By-law Amendment application (the "Original Application") was submitted for the site at 150-158 Pearl Street and 15 Duncan Street. The Original Application proposed to develop a 59-storey (188.26 metres, including mechanical penthouse) mixed use building. The proposal incorporated the existing heritage properties at 15 Duncan Street and 158 Pearl Street. The proposed development would include commercial uses at grade and residential above at 150-158 Pearl Street and commercial on all levels of the four storey heritage building at 15 Duncan Street. The proposed gross floor area was 42,811 square metres, including 40,676 square metres of residential gross floor area and 2,135 square metres of non-residential gross floor area.
