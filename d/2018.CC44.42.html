The official record

What the item proposed

RioCan Management Inc. ("RioCan") is the owner of lands municipally known as 3140-3170 Dufferin Street and 60-68 Apex Road (the "Subject Site"). The Subject Site is located at the northwest corner of Dufferin Street and Apex Road. On November 15, 2015, the Applicant submitted an Official Plan Amendment application to the City, proposing to develop two mixed-use buildings of 28 and 22 storeys separated by a new public road oriented in a north to south configuration (the "Application"). The Application proposed a gross floor area of approximately 59,400 square metres and a Floor Space Index (FSI) of 3.86. There would be 578 residential units, 878 parking spaces and 1135 bicycle parking spaces. A new 1900 square metre public park was also proposed.
