What the item proposed

In response to direction from the Community Development and Recreation Committee, a cross-divisional working group was formed to assess the City of Toronto’s existing risk management plans for medical coverage for electronic dance music (EDM) and other entertainment events at venues that attract large crowds. Where appropriate, the working group will propose new measures to ensure greater public safety at these events. This report provides an update on the status of this work, and recommends next steps.
