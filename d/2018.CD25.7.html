Healthcare Supports for the New Hope Leslieville

AdoptedJan 17, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Deputy City Manager was asked to confirm these six new positions in a report for the Community Development and Recreation Committee meeting on February 28, 2018.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Please find attached a letter from Lynne Raskin, the CEO of South Riverdale Community Health Centre (SRCHC) regarding health services for the new Hope Shelter on Leslie St. As you will recall, one of the requirements for the Hope Shelter was to have a healthcare professional attached to the facility, and City Council endorsed the SRCHC as the provider within the neighbourhood. I understand that commitments have been made by the province for health service providers at six shelters through the LHIN. I am requesting that the Deputy City Manager confirm these six new positions in a report to the CDRC on February 28, 2018 and to report on: - Which six shelters will receive health service providers - The criteria for initiating these services at these locations - The start date - How the services will be delivered - What commitments have been made, if any, for future shelters

What was decided

The Community Development and Recreation Committee: 1. Requested the Deputy City Manager, Cluster A to confirm the six new healthcare professional positions at six shelters through the Local Health Integration Network (LHIN) and report to the February 28, 2018 Community Development and Recreation Committee meeting on: - which six shelters will receive health service providers; - the criteria for initiating these services at these locations; - the start date; - how the services will be delivered; and - what commitments have been made, if any, for future shelters.
