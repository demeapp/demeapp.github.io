Amendment to Contract No. 47020183 for the Provision of Powered Stretcher Solution, All Components and Accessories

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Contract No. 47020183 issued to Rowland Emergency Vehicle Products Inc., as a result of RFP No. 0505-15-0125 for the provision of powered stretcher solutions, components and accessories by an additional amount of $2,774,848 excluding all taxes and charges or $2,823,685, net of HST recoveries. The amendment is required to purchase an additional 32 powered stretchers with mechanical loading devices, 20 stretchers and corresponding accessories to address an increase in the number of ambulances from an increased complement of staff and new technology to reuse ambulance patient care compartments.

What was decided

The Community Development and Recreation Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1.C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Financial Control Bylaw), granted authority to amend Contract No. 47020183 for the provision of powered stretcher solution, components and accessories, by an additional amount of $2,774,848 excluding all taxes and charges, increasing the current contract value from $7,986,338 to $10,761,186 excluding all taxes and charges (or net of HST recoveries from $8,126,898 to $10,950,583).
