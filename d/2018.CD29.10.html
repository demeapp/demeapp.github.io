Councillor Joe Mihevc recommends that Staff to research directly with the 16 Out of the Cold shelter operators on the issues raised in the attached report, with a view to improving city supports.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Interfaith Coalition to Fight Homelessness has produced a report on the Out of the Cold Shelters. The report makes a number of suggestions to improve the services OOTC delivers and to strengthen the relationship with the city. The work of faith communities is integral to the health of our city, especially as it pertains to our homeless community. We need to support their efforts. As such, I request that Community Development and Recreation Committee approve the recommendation.

What was decided

The Community Development and Recreation Committee: 1. Directed the General Manager, Shelter, Support and Housing Administration, in consultation with other appropriate staff, to consult directly with the 16 Out of the Cold shelter operators on the issues raised in the report from the Interfaith Coalition to Fight Homelessness attached to the letter (June 13, 2018) from Councillor Joe Mihevc, with a view to improving city supports, and report to the July 11, 2018 Community Development and Recreation Committee meeting on the 2018-2019 support strategy for the Out of the Cold shelter program. 2.
