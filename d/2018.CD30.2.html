The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Contract No. 47018546, issued to Roy Foss Chevrolet Buick GMC for the non-exclusive supply and delivery of latest model 4WD Chevrolet Tahoes with Special Service Package for Toronto Paramedic Services (TPS) and Toronto Fire Services, in the amount of $684,865, excluding all taxes and charges (or $696,919 net of HST recoveries), increasing the current contract from $819,629 to $1,504,494 excluding all taxes and charges. This amendment is required for TPS to purchase in total an additional sixteen (16) latest model 4WD Chevrolet Tahoes, 6 units in 2018 and 10 units in 2019, to support the Division's Community Paramedicine Wellness Clinics and Home Visit Program, and to meet TPS' growth in operational demand as a result of a growing and aging population.
