The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to present preliminary information on the suitability of the property identified in Confidential Attachment 1, (the "Property"), for shelter or other City uses. This report was requested by City Council at its meeting of January 31 and February 1, 2018 and initial costs estimates and suitability analysis are outlined herein and in Confidential Attachment 1. Given the preliminary suitability analysis completed and estimated costs, staff do not recommend the acquisition of the Property.

What was decided

The Community Development and Recreation Committee: 1. Deferred consideration of the item indefinitely.
