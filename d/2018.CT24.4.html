The Board of Directors was asked to direct Civic Theatres Toronto management to initiate a revised process to find a new operating name for Civic Theatres Toronto.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of January 17, 2018, the Policies and Priorities Committee considered Item RX5.1.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Civic Theatres Toronto: 1. Directed Civic Theatres Toronto management to report to the Board at its meeting on February 15, 2018 with a revised process to find a new operating name for Civic Theatres Toronto. 2. Directed Civic Theatres Toronto management to complete the strategic planning process and bring a draft strategic plan to the Board for discussion no later than June 2018. 3. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (November 24, 2017) from the President and Chief Executive Officer remain confidential as they pertain to the security of the property of the local board.
