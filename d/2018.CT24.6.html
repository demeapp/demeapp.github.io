The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of December 20, 2017, the Board of Directors of the St. Lawrence Centre for the Arts directed Civic Theatres Toronto management to move forward with Phase 2 of the capital and operations update. The purpose of this report is to recommend that Phase 2 actions be deferred and that alternative options be explored for the St. Lawrence Centre for the Arts.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Civic Theatres Toronto: 1. Directed Civic Theatres Toronto Management to defer any further action on Phase 2 of the proposal as presented in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (December 15, 2017) from the President and Chief Executive Officer, Civic Theatres Toronto to the Board of Directors of the St. Lawrence Centre for the Arts (SL15.1). 2. Directed Civic Theatres Toronto Management to explore alternative options to meeting the future goals for the St. Lawrence Centre for the Arts.
