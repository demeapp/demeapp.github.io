Toronto Centre for the Arts Capital Reserve Fund - 2018 Budget

AdoptedJun 20, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Board of Directors of Civic Theatres Toronto was asked to approve the Capital budget in the amount of $515,900, as noted in the report and in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (May 18, 2018) from the Vice President of Finance and Administration.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of June 4, 2018, the Finance, Audit and Risk Management Committee considered the report (May 18, 2018) from the Vice President of Finance and Administration on Toronto Centre for the Arts Capital Reserve Fund - 2018 Budget

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Civic Theatres Toronto: 1. Approved the Capital budget in the amount of $515,900, as noted in the report and in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (May 18, 2018) from the Vice President of Finance and Administration, Civic Theatres Board. 2. Directed Civic Theatres Toronto staff to work with City staff to obtain the approval required from City Council. 3. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (May 18, 2018) from the Vice President of Finance and Administration, Civic Theatres Board remain confidential as it pertains to the security of the property of the municipality or local board.
