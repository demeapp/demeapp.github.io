Authority to Reopen and Issue a Debenture

AdoptedMar 28, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Debenture Committee was asked to approve the provision of long-term financing of the capital projects listed in Attachment A through the issuance of a 10 year sinking fund debenture.

The official summary names a figure of $300 million.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report requests that the necessary Bill be introduced and adopted by the Debenture Committee to give effect to the issuance of a 10 year sinking fund debenture in the amount of $300 million that was syndicated and sold in the domestic and global capital markets on March 20, 2018 and will be delivered to investors on April 4, 2018. This financing is a reopening of a $400 million debenture that will mature on June 7, 2027, increasing the outstanding amount to $700 million. This report also provides details of the projects that are being debt-financed from the proceeds of this transaction contained in Attachment A.

What was decided

The Debenture Committee adopted the following: 1. The Debenture Committee approve the provision of long-term financing of the capital projects listed in Attachment A to the report (March 22, 2018) from the Interim Chief Financial Officerthrough the issuance of a 10 year sinking fund debenture in the amount of $300 million in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the purchase letter provided by the lead manager, CIBC Capital Markets, Inc. and entered into by the Mayor and the Interim Chief Financial Officer, dated March 20, 2018 (the "Purchase Letter"), and as further described in the report (March 22, 2018) from the Interim Chief Financial Officer. 2. The Debenture Committee authorize the introduction of the necessary Bills to the Debenture Committee to authorize the issuance of debentures. 3.
