What the item proposed

This report provides the Debenture Committee with information on the City's new Green Debenture Program. The Green Debenture Program responds to changes in global investor demand and aligns with the City's environment and energy sustainability goals. In addition, under this program, the City may be able to achieve slightly lower borrowing costs. The planned green debenture issuance in 2018 is within the Council approved multi-year debenture and temporary borrowing limit for 2015-2018. The estimated total green debenture issuance in 2018 is up to $300 million. City of Toronto's green debentures share the same financial and legal characteristics as other City's general obligation debentures issued in the past which are backed by the credit and taxing power of the City .
