What the item proposed

Aretha Phillip, Manager, Public Appointments, City Clerk's Office will give a presentation on City of Toronto Public Appointments.

What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee recommends to the Executive Committee that: 1. The City Clerk, in consultation with the City Manager, be requested to review, as early as possible, the candidate qualifications for Agencies, Boards, Commissions and Corporations contained in the Public Appointments Policy, with the goal of reducing barriers and increasing participation of persons with disabilities in the qualified pool for consideration in the civic appointment process. 2. The City Clerk be requested to organize a special information session targeted to the widest possible disability community, as a means of outreach and recruitment to increase representation of persons with disabilities, as early as possible in the 2018-2022 City of Toronto appointments process.
