The official record

What the item proposed

This report brings forward Business Improvement Area (BIA) annual operating budgets for approval by City Council as required by the City of Toronto Act, 2006. City Council approval is required to permit the City to collect funds through the tax levy on behalf of the BIAs. Complete budgets and supporting documentation received by February 14, 2018 have been reviewed and included in this report. Based on the 83 established BIAs: 21 BIA budgets are submitted for approval in this report, 9 BIA budgets were adopted by Council on December 5, 2017 (ED25.7), 52 BIA budgets were adopted by Council on January 31, 2018 (ED26.5) and 1 BIA, Historic Queen East, is inactive. The recommendations in this report reflect 2018 Operating Budgets approved by the respective BIA Boards of Management and have been reviewed by City staff to ensure BIA budgets reflect Council’s approved policies and practices.
