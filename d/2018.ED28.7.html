City Council was asked to approve the deaccessioning of the City of Toronto's Lancaster bomber FM104 from the Historical Collection.

Official title: Deaccessioning and Transfer of the City of Toronto's Lancaster Bomber

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend that the City of Toronto's Lancaster bomber be deacessioned from the Historical Collection and transferred, with conditions, to the British Columbia Aviation Museum in Sidney, British Columbia. Council’s approval is necessary because the value of the artifact exceeds the delegated signing authority of the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture.

What was decided

The Economic Development Committee deferred consideration of the item until the July 9, 2018 Economic Development Committee meeting to allow the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture to explore new opportunities for funding.
