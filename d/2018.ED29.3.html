What the item proposed

Between February 17 and 23, 2018, Councillor Michael Thompson led an advance trade mission, with one city staff, in preparation for a future Mayor-led trade mission to Argentina and Mexico. Both countries have been identified as priority markets by the World Trade Centre (WTC). The trade mission visited the cities of Buenos Aires (Argentina) and Mexico City (Mexico). The purpose of the advance mission was to explore opportunities for trade and export development in Argentina and Mexico. Meetings included: building relations with local trade organizations, Global Affairs Canada, local government officials and various channel partners who can assist in expanding trade opportunities for Toronto based companies.
