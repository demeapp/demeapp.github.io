The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on April 23, 2018, the Toronto Film, Television and Digital Media Board considered Item FB17.2 on Options for Film Studio Infrastructure.

What was decided

The Economic Development Committee: 1. Requested the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture: a. to report to the Toronto Film Television, and Digital Media Board at its June 4, 2018 meeting on the Film Studio requirements for the Pinewood site and the status of the current master plan for the site; and b. to undertake a study to determine how much more studio space would be acquired over the next five to ten years to accommodate a healthy growth rate for the Film, Television, and Digital media industry, including future sites in the east end of the Port Lands.
