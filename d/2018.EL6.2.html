The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides a summary and analysis of grievance and arbitration activity for the six month period of July 1, 2017 to December 31, 2017. The number of open grievances at the City has been steadily declining since 2009, with a further 9.5 percent decrease in the number of open grievances from year-end 2016 (1,743 open grievances) to December 31, 2017 (1,577 open grievances). The number of grievances being resolved or withdrawn continues to exceed the number of new grievances being filed, with 467 open grievances resolved or withdrawn during the second half of 2017 and 294 new grievances filed during the same time period.

What was decided

The Employee and Labour Relations Committee received the report (April 9, 2018) from the Executive Director, Human Resources for information.
