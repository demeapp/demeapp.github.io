The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information on the actions that have been taken related to the Exhibition Place's request to the City on September 4, 2015, for an Official Plan Amendment with respect to that part of Exhibition Place falling under the Open Space / Parks designation. Under the heading "Decision History" there is a list of all meetings that have dealt with this matter since 2015.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place deferred the item to a future meeting of the Board at the earliest opportunity and before the end of the second quarter 2018, when the Acting Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning is available to attend the meeting, with the request that the Chief Executive Officer: 1. Convey the Board's request to the Acting Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, to determine the meeting date; and 2. Meet with staff from CreateTO, City Legal Division, Purchasing and Materials Management Division, and any other City staff required, to review the renewal obligations within the lease for the Liberty Grand Entertainment Complex, in light of the concerns raised by the deputant related to the impact of the proposed Official Plan Amendment on the lease, and report back to the Board.
