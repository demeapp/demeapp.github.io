What the item proposed

At the July 27, 2017 Board meeting the Chief Executive Officer provided a report (EP 5.11) on information for Exhibition Place related to the 2016 Current Value Assessment ("2016 CVA") returned by the Municipal Property Assessment Corporation (MPAC) and resulting 2017 property taxes levied of $3.305M by the City of Toronto for commercial tenanted properties. For comparison, the 2016 taxes were $1.191M. The Board had directed Exhibition Place staff to work with City as well as MPAC regarding this significant increase in property tax for 2017. Since then MPAC has provided a Post Roll Amended Notice ("PRAN") that adjusts the 2016 CVA apportioned to commercial tenants by reducing its value by $71.74M, to $68.9M from $140.6M for 2017 forward.
