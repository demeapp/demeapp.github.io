Toronto City Council · Decision

Multi-year Agreement with a Provincial Agency Client and Exhibition Place

AdoptedMar 22, 2018 · Toronto City Council

This report recommends a multi-year agreement between a Provincial Agency client and Exhibition Place within Beanfield Centre. The Board is asked to approve two license agreements with client option to extend for one additional year with the Ontario Agency for Beanfield Centre for a 3-year term commencing in 2019 and expiring in 2021 on the terms and conditions of the Event Agreement and such other terms.

A citywide decision.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends a multi-year agreement between a Provincial Agency client and Exhibition Place within Beanfield Centre.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved two license agreements with client option to extend for one additional year with the Ontario Agency for Beanfield Centre for a three-year term commencing in 2019 and expiring in 2021 on the terms and conditions of the Event Agreement and such other terms and conditions satisfactory to the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place and the City Solicitor. 2. Directed that the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 8, 2018) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place remain confidential in its entirety and not be released publicly as it pertains to the security of the property of the Board.
