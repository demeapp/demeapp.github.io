The official record

What the item proposed

The Board of Governance of Exhibition Place is responsible for the oversight of the general management of the affairs, activities and assets of Exhibition Place. The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place By-law No. 1-2017 (to govern the proceedings of the Board of Governors of Exhibition Place) includes the authority to establish Board Committees and to assign duties to Board Committees (s.G.21). The By-law also sets out the Principles that are to be used in interpreting the By-law including the following principles: 2. Principles of the procedures by-law (1) The following Members' rights are the principles upon which the procedures by-law is based: (a) The majority of Members have the right to decide; (c) All Members have the right to information to help make decisions, unless otherwise prevented by law; On Friday January 20, 2017, the Board of Governors made the following decision:…

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place referred the item to the Chief Executive Officer, to be considered in her report to the Board on the future of committees.
