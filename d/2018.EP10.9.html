CEE Elevator Services Limited - Contract Extension

AdoptedMar 22, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Board was asked to approve the extension of the purchase order to CEE Elevator Services Limited for a six-month term commencing February 1, 2018.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approving of a contract extension with CEE Elevator Services Limited ("CEE") for the non-exclusive supply of all labour, equipment and materials necessary for maintenance and repair services to elevators and escalators in various buildings at Exhibition Place. A Request for Quotation for these services was issued on December 6, 2017, through the City of Toronto Purchasing and Material Management Division and the two quotations received exceed the budget available for these services. Working with City staff and the Exhibition Place Operations Department, the recommendation is to work in revising the specification and scope of work for Exhibition Place and to include the Board requirements as part of shared services in the City of Toronto-Wide Request for Quotation to be issued over the next few months.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the extension of the purchase order to CEE Elevator Services Limited for a six-month term commencing February 1, 2018 and expiring on July 31, 2018 in the amount of $65,000.00, excluding HST for the provision of these services at the same prices as per previous Request for Quotation 6718-13-3134 issued in 2013.
