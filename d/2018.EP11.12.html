Toronto City Council · Decision

Heritage Consulting Services for McGillivray Fountain Restoration and Upgrade

AdoptedMay 31, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place was asked to approve the award of a purchase order to Stevens Burgess Architects Ltd. for the provision of Heritage Consulting Services for the Restoration and Upgrade of the McGillivray Fountain at Centennial Square.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that the Board enter into an agreement with Stevens Burgess Architects Ltd. ("SBA") for the provision of Heritage Consulting Services for the Restoration and Upgrade of the McGillivray Fountain at Centennial Square. The Request for Proposal for these services was issued on January 26, 2018 through the City of Toronto Purchasing and Materials Management Division and the recommendation is to award to the highest scoring proponent.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the award of a purchase order to Stevens Burgess Architects Ltd. for the provision of Heritage Consulting Services for the Restoration and Upgrade of the McGillivray Fountain at Centennial Square, on the terms and conditions outlined in the report (May 16, 2018) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place, and other such terms and conditions satisfactory to the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place and the City Solicitor.
