Multi-Year Agreement with Royal Agricultural Winter Fair and Use of Office Space

AdoptedMay 31, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place was asked to approve a five-year rental agreement with Royal Agricultural Winter Fair for the 2018-2022 Royal Fair under the terms and conditions of the standard licence agreement for the Enercare Centre.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As reported in 2017, commencing in 2016, the Board Chair and the Chair of the Royal Agricultural Winter Fair ("RAWF") Board of Directors had several meetings with Exhibition Place staff and the General Manager, Toronto Economic Development and Culture Division to discuss and to negotiate the development of a long-term plan for the Royal Agricultural Fair on the Exhibition Place grounds. The RAWF is a 96-year old cultural event in Toronto and the objective of the multi-year agreement for both event and office space at Exhibition Place is to provide the RAWF with a known and sustainable occupancy cost for the next 5 years from 2018 to 2022, based on the RAWF's operating results, while reducing the existing level of subsidization now provided by Exhibition Place and the City through a grant from Toronto Economic Development and Culture Division.
