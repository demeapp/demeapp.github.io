The official record

What the item proposed

This report is seeking approval of a four (4) year licence agreement with Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment Partnership (MLSEP)The Licence Agreement provides MLSEP the right to use the parking lot (approximately as shown on Schedule A to Appendix A (the "Lot") on BMO Field game days throughout the Toronto FC and Toronto Argonauts Football seasons. The use of this Lot will be solely for parking for team officials, players and staff as well as storage of equipment related to the conversion of BMO Field. The terms and conditions of this proposed agreement are set out in Appendix A.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved a four-year licence agreement with Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment Partnership (MLSEP) for the Gravel Lot (shown on Schedule A of Appendix A to the report (July 5, 2018) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place) on the condition that the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place takes all necessary actions to ensure compliance with the requirements of the Municipal Code Chapter 63, Exhibition Place; and on the terms and conditions set-out in Appendix A to the report (July 5, 2018) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place and such other terms and conditions as approved by the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place and the City Solicitor.
