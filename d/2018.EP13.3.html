The official record

What the item proposed

The Interim City Manager has provided the 2019 Budget Process, Directions and Guidelines to all City Divisions and Agencies. The primary direction is for all City Divisions and Agencies to submit a zero change budget over 2018. A two phase submission process is included in this direction: 1. Base budget submission, including changes to reach the zero target due June 26, 2018; and 2. New request submissions due August 27th, if any. Based on these direction, Exhibition Place staff have developed a 2019 Operating Budget that has a budget surplus of $160,000 an increase of $10,000 (7 percent) over the 2018 Operating Budget. Following the conclusion of the 2018 election, the new City Council will likely be providing additional directions and guidelines that Exhibition Place will address through the 2019 Budget process which will continue until approximately March 2019.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the 2019 Operating Budget. 2. Requested that City Council transfer any surplus above budget for 2019 to the “Exhibition Place Conference Centre Reserve Fund”. 3. Directed the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place to submit the 2019 Budget to the City Financial Planning Division as part of the 2019 Budget Process.
