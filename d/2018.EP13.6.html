Toronto City Council · Decision

Committee Structure Review Status Update

AdoptedJul 19, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place was asked to amend the format of the Community Liaison Consultation Meeting to be co-hosted by one Councillor Member and one Citizen Member of the Board.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides a status on actions taken related to the committee structure for the Board that was put in place in 2017 and recommendations for the future.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Amended the format of the Community Liaison Consultation Meeting to be co-hosted by one Councillor Member and one Citizen Member of the Board. 2. Amended the format of the Tenant Liaison Consultation Meeting to be co-hosted by one Councillor Member and one Citizen Member of the Board. 3. Directed the Corporate Secretary to seek interest from Board members in being co-hosts of the Tenant Liaison Consultation Meeting and the Community Liaison Consultation Meeting. 4. Directed the Chief Executive Officer to ensure that full information on any Consultation Meetings are shared on a timely basis with all Board members. 5. Dissolved the Business Development Committee. 6. Dissolved the Finance and Audit Committee.
