What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of an additional bridge contract extension with CEE Elevator Services Limited ("CEE") for the non-exclusive supply of all labour, equipment and materials necessary for maintenance and repair services to elevators and escalators in various buildings at Exhibition Place. A Request for Quotation for these services was issued on December 6, 2017, through the City of Toronto Purchasing and Material Management Division but the two quotations received exceeded the budget available. The decision was made to revise the specifications and scope of work for Exhibition Place, however, rather than reissue a stand-alone RFQ, to include the Exhibition Place requirements as part of shared services in the City of Toronto-wide Request for Quotation. To date the City of Toronto-wide Request for Quotations has not been issued therefore, this additional extension is being…

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the bridge contract extension of the purchase order to CEE Elevator Services Ltd. for a one-year term commencing August 1, 2018 and expiring on July 31, 2019 in the amount of $200,000.00, excluding HST for the provision of services on the terms and conditions stated in the report (July 5, 2018) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place.
