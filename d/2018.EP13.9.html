Official title: Tender Award for Elevators Retrofit at Enercare Centre

AdoptedJul 19, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends the award of a contract to CEE Elevator Service Ltd. for retrofit of existing elevators No. 3 and No. 4 at the Enercare Centre. The tender call for this contract was issued on June 07, 2018, through the City of Toronto Purchasing and Material Management Division (PMMD) and the recommendation is to award the contract to the lowest compliant bidder within the available budget.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Awarded Contract No. 18-076-87613 to CEE Elevator Service Ltd. for the retrofit of elevators No. 3 and No. 4 at Enercare Centre in the amount of $598,505.33, which includes a cash and contingency allowance of $80,000.00, exclusive of HST; this being the lowest compliant tender received following the public tendering of the project through the City of Toronto's Purchasing and Material Management Division.
