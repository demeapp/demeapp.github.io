What the item proposed

This report recommends approving a contract with Black & McDonald Ltd. for the installation of traffic signal lights at the "T" intersection of Princes' Boulevard and Newfoundland Drive. The tender call for this contract was issued on July 6, 2018, through the City of Toronto Purchasing and Material Management Division and the recommendation is to award the contract to the lowest acceptable bidder meeting specifications within the available budget.
