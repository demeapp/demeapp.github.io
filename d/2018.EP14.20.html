What the item proposed

This report recommends an amendment of the Consultant Agreement with Mr. Robert Reynolds to act as an advisor to Exhibition Place with respect to the negotiations of the Collective Agreement with International Alliance of Theatrical Stage Employees (IATSE Local 58). The IATSE Local 58 Collective Agreement expired on December 31, 2017; notice to bargain was given by IATSE on November 23, 2017; the first meeting was held in January 2018; and negotiations continue.
