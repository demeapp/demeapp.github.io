What the item proposed

This report summarizes the discussions at the Tenant Liaison Consultation meeting held on June 19, 2018. Leading the meeting was the Co-host Councillor Karygiannis. Exhibition Place staff in attendance included Gilles Bouchard, Director of Event Management Services; Ron Kostick, Tenant Coordinator; Tony Porter, Director, Parking and Security Services; and Jennifer Foster, Administrative Assistant to the General Manager of Sales and Event Management. The specific purpose of this consultation meeting was to tour the grounds to understand the digital and City pedestrian signage in place; understand the concerns of tenants on wayfinding; and to develop an action plan to address outstanding issues.
