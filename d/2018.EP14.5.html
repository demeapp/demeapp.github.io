The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information on the status of the Exhibition Place health and safety system, specifically on activities, priorities and performance during the second quarter of 2018. There were 0 lost time injuries and a 40 percent decrease in the number of medical aid injuries in the second quarter of 2018 relative to 2017. Workplace Safety and Insurance Board (WSIB) invoiced costs for second quarter 2018 were $6,325.91 lower than in second quarter 2017. This cost benefit was due to lower incident rates and lower costs on Long Term Disability claims that are still on the file.
