What the item proposed

This report outlines the revised accessibility program at Exhibition Place and recommends the approval of revised Accessibility Policy attached as Appendix A and the new Multi-Year (2019 - 2025) Accessibility Plan attached as Appendix B. Exhibition Place is required pursuant to the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005 (AODA) to have accessibility policies governing how the organization achieves accessibility and meets legislated requirements. Exhibition Place first adopted its Accessibility Policy in 2010. Recently, City of Toronto Council approved a corporate policy and Exhibition Place has reviewed its existing policy in light of the City benchmark and are proposing revisions in line with the City's.
