The official record

What the item proposed

The City of Toronto Internal Audit was engaged by Exhibition Place in 2018 for the review of the Payroll and Benefits Process Controls. This operational review is the third of a three (3) year cycle in respect to overall risk assessment of operations. This review covers the 12 months period from June 1, 2017 to May 31, 2018. The Internal Audit Report is attached as Appendix B to this report along with the Highlights of the review that summarizes issues of the engagement, the suggestions and recommendations (Appendix A). Management has provided responses to the audit findings which will help to improve our overall accounting and internal control procedures.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Received the report (November 1, 2018) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place for information.
