What the item proposed

This report provides notice as required of proposed amendments to the Board's, Financial By-law 2-16 and Borrowing and Money By-law 3-85.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Requested the Chief Executive Officer to submit the By-law amendments to Financial By-law 2-16 as set out in Appendix A to the report (November 1, 2018) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place and the By-law amendments to Borrowing and Money By-law 3-85 set out in Appendix B to the first meeting in 2019 of the Board for approval.
