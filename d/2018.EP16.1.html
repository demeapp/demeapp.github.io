The official record

What the item proposed

This report outlines the terms of a Memorandum of Settlement negotiated with IATSE Local 58. These terms which are set out in detail in the confidential attachment have yet to be ratified by the IATSE Local 58 membership however pending ratification by IATSE Local 58 scheduled for November 15, 2018, this report is recommending approval by the Board.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Ratified and approved the Memorandum of Settlement, attached as Schedule A to Confidential Attachment 1 dated November 14, 2018, to the report (November 14, 2018) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place, between the Board of Governors of Exhibition Place and the International Alliance of Theatrical Stage Employees and Moving Picture Technicians, Artists and Allied Crafts of the United States, its Territories and Canada, Local 58, Toronto. 2. Authorized staff to make the necessary amendments to rates of pay, benefits and other issues identified as agreed changes to the collective agreement in the Memorandum of Settlement. 3.
