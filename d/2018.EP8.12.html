The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends the continuation of the Consultant Agreement with Mr. Robert Reynolds to act as an advisor to Exhibition Place with respect to the negotiations of the Collective Agreement with International Alliance of Theatrical Stage Employees (IATSE Local 58). The IATSE Local 58 Collective Agreement expired December 31, 2017 and IATSE Local 58 gave notice to bargain in a letter dated November 23, 2017 with the first meeting to be held in January 2018.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved entering into a consultant agreement with Mr. Robert Reynolds to act as advisor to Exhibition Place on labour negotiations for the IATSE Local 58 Collective Agreement on an as-needed basis commencing February 1, 2018 and expiring on the conclusion of these negotiations, generally on the same terms and conditions as the existing agreement set out in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (January 16, 2018) from the Chief Executive Officer. 2. The Board direct that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (January 16, 2018) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place remain confidential in its entirety and not be released publicly as it pertains to the security of the property belonging to the Board.
