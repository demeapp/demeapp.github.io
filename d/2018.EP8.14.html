No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends a revised organization structure involving some divisions of the Finance Department which existing structure is as set out in Appendix A to this report.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Adopted the recommendations contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (January 16, 2018) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place. 2. Directed that the recommendations in Confidential Attachment 1, and Confidential Appendix B to the report (January 16, 2018) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place be released after all affected employees have been informed of the Board's decision at the discretion of the Chief Executive Officer, and that the remaining portion of Confidential Attachment 1 remain confidential as it involves personal matters about identifiable individuals employed by the Board. February 23, 2018 – Confidential Appendix B to the report (January 16, 2018) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place is now public in its entirety.
