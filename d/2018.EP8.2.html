Proposed Improvements to the Exhibition GO Station

Adopted with changesJan 30, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Board was asked to request that the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place work with Metrolinx to organize a Community Liaison Consultation meeting for presentation of the Metrolinx proposals.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

With the increase in activity at Exhibition Place and ridership from the surrounding neighbourhoods, the Exhibition Place GO Station is not meeting the needs of its customers. Over the past few months, Metrolinx has been consulting with the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC), City of Toronto Planning staff and Exhibition Place staff on possible short and long term proposals for improvement to the GO Station. As a first step to a public consultation on their proposals, Metrolinx will attend the Board meeting and will provide a presentation on the proposed improvements/changes including background, needs assessment and the preliminary functional site plan options to present the proposals and seek feedback from the Board.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Requested the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place to work with Metrolinx to organize a Community Liaison Consultation meeting and a Tenant Liaison Consultation meeting for presentation of the Metrolinx proposals and to report back to the Board on matters raised by the local community and tenants at those meetings.
