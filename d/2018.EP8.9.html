The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends a one-year agreement with the Festival Management Committee (FMC) for the Peeks Toronto Caribbean Carnival 2018 at Exhibition Place based on the same terms and conditions as approved by the Board in 2017. The Toronto Caribbean Carnival is a major cultural event that has been held at Exhibition Place since the mid-1990s. The Festival Management Committee was established and took over as the main body and event organizer commencing in 2006. Given the importance of the Toronto Caribbean Carnival to the community-at-large and the fact that the parade is free for the citizens of Toronto, the Board and staff have been working with Festival Management Committee for several years to provide financial support by sharing some of the revenues generated from this event. The Peeks Toronto Caribbean Carnival is scheduled to be held at Exhibition Place, Saturday, August 4, 2018.…

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the execution of a one-year agreement with Festival Management Committee for the Peeks Toronto Caribbean Carnival 2018, on the terms and conditions of the standard license agreement for Exhibition Place outlined in the report (January 16, 2018) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place and such other terms satisfactory to the Chief Executive Officer and City Solicitor.
