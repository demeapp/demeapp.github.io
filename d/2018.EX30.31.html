The official record

What the item proposed

The City of Toronto website has been updated recently. The Communications Working Group, Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee would like staff to provide an update to the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee on the accessibility features of the City of Toronto external website.

What was decided

The Executive Committee: 1. Requested the Chief Information Officer, Information and Technology to make a presentation to the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee by the end of the second quarter in 2018 on the accessibility features of the updated City of Toronto external website, including the internal policies, procedures, and/or processes in place to ensure that content being posted to the external website is accessible and meets Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 standards.
