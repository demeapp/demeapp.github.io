What the item proposed

This report recommends funding of $2,000,000 for two new non-profit housing developments through the City's Home Ownership Assistance Program (HOAP). The program provides up-front funding to support the development of the homes, which is then converted into down payment assistance loans to help qualifying low to moderate income families and individuals purchase the homes at more affordable prices. Funding for the Home Ownership Assistance Program is from the Development Charges Reserve Fund for Subsidized Housing (XR2116). The two non-profit housing developments recommended for funding are the Habitat for Humanity townhomes at 136, 138 and 140 Pinery Trail in Ward 42 Scarborough-Rouge River, and the Options for Homes/Home Ownership Alternatives condominium apartment building at 10 Wilby Crescent in Ward 11 York South-Weston.
