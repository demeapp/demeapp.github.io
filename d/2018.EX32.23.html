What the item proposed

In 2016, Toronto Parks, Forestry and Recreation completed a new soccer field at McNicoll Park. A synthetic turf was installed as an upgrade to the previous sod surface. The new field has since been very well used by many organized soccer clubs in and around Ward 24. It is a destination facility and the only synthetic turf field in the area. In addition to the soccer field and outdoor play areas, the park amenities include a basketball court, water spray pad and playground. Minimal on-street and lay-by parking is available which creates a very difficult situation for users of the park and the soccer field. Hillmount Public school is immediately adjacent to McNicoll Park. There is a very small school parking lot at Hillmount where some users attempt to park, but it is not adequate and is often full of school employees and visitors.
