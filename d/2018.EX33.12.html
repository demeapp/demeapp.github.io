Annual Report on the City's Loan and Loan Guarantee Portfolios

AdoptedApr 24, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to approve the renewal of the line of credit guarantee by the City issued on behalf of the Toronto Symphony Orchestra (TSO) to its lender.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an annual update on the City's loan and loan guarantee portfolios. The City currently guarantees three operating lines of credit and eight capital loan guarantees under Council approved policies for line of credit and loan guarantees for cultural and community-based organizations. In addition to the guarantees, the City currently has outstanding a total of seven direct loans: six to City agencies and corporations, plus one to an external organization. All loans and guarantees are in good standing with one under a repayment settlement plan. This noted loan is being monitored and will be the subject of a future report once the settlement term concludes. This report recommends that a line of credit renewal for a not-for profit entity set to expire in 2018 be extended for an additional three year period.

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve the renewal of the line of credit guarantee by the City issued on behalf of the Toronto Symphony Orchestra to its lender in the amount of $5,000,000 (inclusive of all interest payable by Toronto Symphony Orchestra), for an additional three year period commencing on November 1, 2018 and ending October 31, 2021.
