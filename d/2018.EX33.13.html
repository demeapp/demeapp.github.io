Acreage and Tonnage Taxation Systems for Railway Rights-of-Way

AdoptedApr 24, 2018 · Toronto City Council

This is an information item: This report provides information related to the property taxation of railway rights-of-way using the acreage and tonnage systems and the financial implications for the City of Toronto if Ontario were to switch to a taxation system based on tonnage. No decision is attached.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information related to the property taxation of railway rights-of-way using the acreage and tonnage systems and the financial implications for the City of Toronto if Ontario were to switch to a taxation system based on tonnage.

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Treasurer to continue to work with the Ministry of Finance and other municipalities to explore the implementation of a taxation formula that incorporates both acreage and tonnage based rates resulting in a taxation rate that is greater than the current annual taxes. 2. City Council request the Treasurer to enter into discussions with the Railway Association of Canada and Railway Companies to explore the implementation of an acreage and tonnage based taxation formula and to report back to the Executive Committee in the first quarter of 2019.
