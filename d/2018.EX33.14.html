The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek City Council's approval for additional carry-forward funding adjustments of $841.233 million to the 2018 Approved Capital Budget. The recommended carry forward funding adjustments are in compliance with the City's Carry Forward Policy and have no impact on the City's approved debt for 2018.

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve additional carry forward funding of $841.233 million from unspent capital projects funding as detailed in Appendix 1 to the report (March 14, 2018) from the Interim Chief Financial Officer, in order to continue work on previously approved capital projects, and that the 2018 Approved Capital Budget for respective City Programs and Agencies be adjusted accordingly with no incremental impact on debt.
