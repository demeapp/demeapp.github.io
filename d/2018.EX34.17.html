The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks approval from City Council to accept an in-kind donation from Spin Master LTD. for 6250 units of toys with a value of $102,699.49. The toys will help support programs and reduce the cost of replacing existing toys. Policy on Donations to the City for Community Benefits. Since the estimated value of this offer exceeds the $50,000 threshold for donation acceptance established in the policy, the Toronto Office of Partnerships requires City Council consent before proceeding with the initiative.

What was decided

City Council on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council accept an in-kind donation from Spin Master LTD., valued at $102,699.49 for Shelter, Support and Housing Administration and Toronto Community Housing Corporation.
