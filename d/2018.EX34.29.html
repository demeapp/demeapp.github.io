The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on April 19, 2018, the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee considered a letter from Monica Winkler, on behalf of the Transportation Working Group, Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee on Accessibility During Construction - Eglinton Light Rail Transit (LRT).

What was decided

The Executive Committee: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services to: a. invite representatives from Metrolinx to provide an update to the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee on the construction of the Eglinton Crosstown Light Rail Transit; b. request representatives from the Toronto Transit Commission to provide an update to the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee on the construction of the Eglinton Crosstown Light Rail Transit.
