2018 Levy on Railway Roadways and Rights-of-Way and on Power Utility Transmission and Distribution Corridors

AdoptedJun 26, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to authorize the levy and collection of taxes for the 2018 taxation year on railway roadways and rights-of-way and on land used as transmission or distribution corridors owned by power utilities.

The official summary names a figure of $7.1 million.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks Council authority for the introduction of the by-law necessary to levy and collect taxes for the 2018 taxation year on railway roadways and rights-of-way and on land used as transmission or distribution corridors owned by power utilities, totalling approximately $7.1 million in taxation revenue, of which the municipal share is $6.6 million and the provincial education share is $0.5 million.

What was decided

City Council on June 26, 27, 28 and 29, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the levy and collection of taxes for the 2018 taxation year on railway roadways and rights-of-way and on land used as transmission or distribution corridors owned by power utilities, in accordance with subsection 280 (1) of the City of Toronto Act, 2006, and subsection 257.7 (1) of the Education Act. 2. City Council request the Chief Financial Officer to include information in future annual reports on the potential additional income the City of Toronto could receive if the levy was based on a rate per tonnage as opposed to a rate per acreage.
