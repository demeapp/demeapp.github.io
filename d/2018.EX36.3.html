What the item proposed

At its meeting of January 31, 2018, Toronto City Council ("Council") approved the staff preferred site located at 1627 Danforth Ave (the "Site") for the consolidation of the current Toronto Police Service (the "TPS") 54 and 55 Divisions. As part of this decision, Council also directed the Deputy City Manager, Internal Corporate Services and the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning to report to the Executive Committee with a progress report on the master planning process. This report represents the progress report and provides additional information on the work done to date. The Site is currently used by the Toronto Transit Commission (the "TTC") to house the Toronto Transit Commission's Danforth Division Offices and since 2002 has been used for various other Toronto Transit Commission functions such as warehousing and storage.
