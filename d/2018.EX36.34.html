What the item proposed

Toronto Community Housing Corporation (TCHC) is undertaking a multi-phase plan to redevelop Regent Park, the country's largest social housing community. Toronto Community Housing Corporation is halfway through the revitalization that will ultimately replace 2,083 Rent-Geared-to-Income (RGI) units and develop approximately 5,417 market units within a 69-acre site. In January 2004, Council approved a report on the financing plan for Regent Park that recommended property tax exemptions of 15 years for all new Rent-Geared-to-Income homes in the Regent Park Revitalization Plan Area (shown in Attachment 1) effective from when the homes are developed and appropriate agreements signed.
