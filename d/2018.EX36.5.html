The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to Council direction from its meeting of April 24, 2018 to report to Executive Committee on establishing an Anti-Racism Advisory Committee on Racial Justice. With the final meeting of this term of City Council scheduled for July, City staff do not recommend the establishment of a new advisory committee at this time. Staff will continue to research and consult as required to develop recommendations on the appropriate advisory body to advise Council on equity related matters during the next term of Council. In the first quarter of 2019, staff will report to Council with proposed terms of reference for an equity advisory committee structure in addition to the existing Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee and the Aboriginal Affairs Committee.

What was decided

The Executive Committee received the report (July 3, 2018) from the Interim City Manager for information.
