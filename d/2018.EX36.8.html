The official record

What the item proposed

This report requests an amendment to Schedule 'A' of the City's Financial Control By-law Chapter 71 (Schedule 'A') such that Section 21, Toronto Water Research Initiatives or Projects, be extended to Solid Waste Management Services. The amended section will delegate authority to the General Manager, Solid Waste Management Services, to negotiate, partner and enter into agreements with post-secondary institutions, other municipalities, governmental agencies and non-for-profit professional organizations, for work performed including: - undertaking specialized research initiatives and projects; - advancing policy research and development; - pilot testing innovative; and - alternative service delivery approaches. This report also establishes criteria, which are in line with those existing in Section 21 of Schedule 'A', to be used by the General Manager, Solid Waste Management Services, for…

What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the General Manager, Solid Waste Management Services to negotiate and enter into any and all agreements and amending agreements necessary, subject to available funding, to participate in research initiatives or projects with post-secondary educational institutions, other municipalities or governmental agencies and non-profit organizations, on terms and conditions satisfactory to the General Manager, Solid Waste Management Services, and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor. 2.
