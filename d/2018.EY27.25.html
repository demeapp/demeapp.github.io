Staff recommend denying a front yard parking request at 22 Norval Street.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has assessed an appeal from the owner of 22 Norval Street for front yard parking. Front yard parking at this location is not recommended because it does not meet the tree clearance requirement of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 918 and the Code does not permit the licensing of the parking space within the reciprocal right-of-way. The owner will be given an opportunity to make a deputation before Community Council.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council approved the request for front yard parking at 22 Norval Street, with the following conditions: 1. The parking area be a minimum of 2.2 metres in width and not exceed 2.6 metres and a minimum of 5.3 metres and not exceed 5.9 metres in length. 2. The applicant maintain the existing asphalt parking area , as indicated in Attachment 'A', attached to the report dated December 13, 2017, from the Manager, Right of Way Management, Transportation Services. 3. The applicant disconnect any downspouts, if feasible, or seek an exemption from Toronto Water in accordance with the requirements in Chapter 681-11(S). 4.
