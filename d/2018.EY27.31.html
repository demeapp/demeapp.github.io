Right-Turn and Left-Turn Prohibition Amendment - Weston Road at Little Avenue

AdoptedJan 31, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to amend the existing right turn prohibition in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 pm., Monday to Friday.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to amend the existing southbound "No Right Turn, 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday" regulation for southbound traffic and the existing northbound "No Left Turn, 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday" for northbound traffic on Weston Road at Little Avenue, to exclude bicycles. As Weston Road is a transit route, this matter requires the approval of City Council. Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) staff has been advised of the issue and have not objected to the recommendation.

What was decided

City Council on January 31 and February 1, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend the existing right turn prohibition in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, for southbound traffic on Weston Road at Little Avenue, to exclude bicycles. 2. City Council amend the existing left turn prohibition in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, for northbound traffic on Weston Road at Little Avenue, to exclude bicycles.
