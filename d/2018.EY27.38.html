Traffic Calming Poll Results - Summitcrest Drive and La Rose Avenue, between Royal York Road and Islington Avenue

AdoptedJan 16, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend against speed humps on Summitcrest Drive and La Rose Avenue, between Royal York Road and Islington Avenue after a resident poll.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. At its meeting of June 13, 2017, Etobicoke York Community Council considered Item EY23.44, recommending that a survey of property owners/occupants be conducted on Summitcrest Drive, between Royal York Road and Islington Avenue, and on La Rose Avenue, between Royal York Road and Islington Avenue, to determine community support for a proposed traffic calming plan. The Traffic Calming warrant criteria was achieved on Summitcrest Drive. Although the Traffic Calming Warrant Criteria was not satisfied on La Rose Avenue, we anticipated traffic would divert to La Rose Avenue if traffic calming was installed on Summitcrest Drive. Therefore, both streets were recommended to be polled. This report summarizes the result of the traffic calming poll for owners and occupants…

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Did NOT approve the installation of traffic calming on Summitcrest Drive, between Royal York Road and Islington Avenue. 2. Did NOT approve the installation of traffic calming on La Rose Avenue, between Royal York Road and Islington Avenue.
