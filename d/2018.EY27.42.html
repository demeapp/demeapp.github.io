Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to approve a school bus loading zone at Daisy Avenue.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. To address safety concerns and congestion in the area of Vincent Massey Day Care and Nursery School (68 Daisy Avenue), it is recommended that a school bus loading zone be created to accommodate school buses loading and discharging students. This report also recommends amendments to the existing parking regulations on Daisy Avenue to provide a “Maximum 10 Minute Limit” parking regulation on the north side of the street along a portion of the frontage of Vincent Massey School. This regulation will apply between 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Monday to Friday to provide parents wishing to accompany their children into the school a legal short duration parking area. What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing No Parking, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., on the north side of Daisy Avenue between Twenty Ninth Street and a point 125 metres east. 2. Prohibited parking at all times on the north side of Daisy Avenue, between Twenty Ninth Street and a point 40 metres east. 3. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 10 minutes from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Daisy Avenue, between a point 40 metres east of Twenty Ninth Street and a point 23 metres further east and a point 85 metres east of Twenty Ninth Street and a point 33 metres further east. 4. Authorized a school bus loading zone in operation from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on the north side of Daisy Avenue from a point 63 metres east of Twenty Ninth Street and a point 22 metres further east.
