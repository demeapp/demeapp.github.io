Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to change parking rules on Parkview Gardens.

Official title: Parking Regulation Amendments - Parkview Gardens

AdoptedJan 16, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to obtain approval to implement a "One Hour Parking, 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday" regulation on the east side of Parkview Gardens between Bloor Street West and a point 45.7 metres north and to implement a "No Parking Anytime" regulation on the WEST side of Parkview Gardens between Bloor Street West and a point 45.7 metres north. These regulations are what existed on Parkview Gardens prior to the No Stopping/No Standing regulations that where implemented during the construction of the condominium on the northwest corner of Bloor Street West and Parkview Gardens. Construction has been completed and it is appropriate to reinstate the parking regulations that were in place on Parkview Gardens prior to construction.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the “No Stopping Anytime" regulation on both sides of Parkview Gardens between Bloor Street West and a point 34.5 metres north. 2. Rescinded the “No Standing Anytime” regulation on Parkview Gardens between a point 34.5 metres north of Bloor Street West and a point 16.5 metres further north. 3. Authorized parking for a maximum of one hour from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday" on the east side of Parkview Gardens between Bloor Street West and a point 45.7 metres north. 4. Prohibited parking at all times on the west side of Parkview Gardens between Bloor Street West and a point 45.7 metres north.
