The official record

What the item proposed

The St. Clair Gardens Business Improvement Area and local residents have requested proactive measures be taken by the City's enforcement agencies, in order to avert problem activities at 1708 and 1776 St. Clair Avenue West, and to establish a safer environment for the community. The local Councillor has brought this matter forward in order to address various community safety issues which have had an impact on the quality of life for the local residents.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council deferred consideration of this Item to its meeting on February 21, 2018.
