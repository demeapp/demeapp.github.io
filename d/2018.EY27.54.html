Endorsement of Events for Liquor Licensing Purposes

Adopted with changesJan 16, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to endorse various events for liquor licensing purposes. Endorsement supports the event organizers' liquor licence applications.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

Seeking endorsement of various events for liquor licensing purposes.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Declared, for liquor licensing purposes, the following events to be of municipal significance, and directed that the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario be advised that the Community Council has no objection to their taking place: a. The Society of Beer Drinking Ladies event, taking place at the Junction Brewery, 150 Symes Road, on January 26, 2018, from 8:00 p.m. to 1:00 a.m. b. The 2nd Annual Witchstock event, taking place at the Bellwoods Brewery, 20 Hafis Road, on September 29 and 30, 2018, from 11:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. c. The Mladi Glas Planika Slovenian Folklore Group annual banquet - Zimski Noc/Winter Night, taking place at Our Lady of the Miraculous Medal Parish Hall, 739 Browns Line, on February 3, 2018 from 6:00 p.m. to 1:00 a.m. d.
