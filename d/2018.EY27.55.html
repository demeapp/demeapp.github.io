Installation of Traffic Signal at Matthews Gate and Walsh Avenue

AdoptedJan 16, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to request the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke York District, to undertake a traffic study on Walsh Avenue, between Weston Road and Highway 400.

The official summary names a figure of $200,000.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As you may know, the City recently entered into a settlement agreement with Marmora Freezing Corporation with regard to the Ontario Municipal Board case PL161293. The Board delivered its decision on December 20, 2017, stating that the requested variances represent good planning, and meet the necessary tests under the Planning Act ("Act"). As a condition of the settlement agreement, the applicant (Marmora Freezing Corporation) will enter into an agreement with the City under the Act such that it will pay the City $200,000 towards the installation of traffic signals near the facility at 50 – 60 Marmora Street, at Matthews Gate and Walsh Avenue. The moneys shall be paid to the City in trust upon the issuance of a building permit for the development permitted by the minor variances. What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke York District, to undertake a traffic study on Walsh Avenue, between Weston Road and Highway 400, to determine if a traffic light is warranted, and that study be brought forward to Etobicoke York Community Council at its next meeting.
