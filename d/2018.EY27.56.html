Staff recommend granting a fence exemption for 40 Rakely Court.

Official title: Request to Re-open - 40 Rakely Court - Fence Exemption

AdoptedJan 16, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on November 14, 2017, the Etobicoke York Community Council adopted Item EY26.17, headed "Application for Fence Exemption – 40 Rakely Court". In so doing, Community Council granted the application for exemption submitted by the owner of 40 Rakely Court, with the conditions that a gate be installed in accordance with the submitted plans, and that any future site plan proposal include a clear description of any fence to be maintained or altered, thereby allowing the construction of the front and side yard fences to be installed as requested. The owner has contacted me because he has noticed people are cutting around the fence he installed and he would like to make a slight adjustment shown in the attached sketch. In response to the concerns of the owner I would like to request re-opening of Item EY26.17 and approve the revised sketch of the fence.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item EY26.17, headed "Application for Fence Exemption – 40 Rakely Court, adopted by Etobicoke York Community Council on November 14, 2017. 2. Approved an additional extension to the existing 6 foot chain-link fence towards the south end of the property and terminating abutting 35 Rakely Court, as shown on the sketch attached (dated January 12, 2018).
