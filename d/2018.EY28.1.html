The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an update on the status of negotiations with Hydro One for the licensing of a portion of hydro right-of-way (ROW) for park use within the Emery Village Neighbourhood. Emery Village BIA has produced detailed design drawings of a proposed park on the hydro ROW that would pay tribute to the First Nations history in the area. The plans have been preliminarily reviewed by staff of Parks, Forestry and Recreation and Hydro One, as well as the local Councillor. Additional discussions with Transportation Services and City Planning are required to assess any conflict with a planned road identified in the Emery Village Transportation Master Plan as well as the Emery Village Secondary Plan, both of which are Council approved. If Council determines that a park is preferred for this site, then the Emery Village BIA will need to submit a formal application for the City to review…

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, and the General Manager, Transportation Services, to continue to negotiate with Toronto Hydro officials with respect to a First Nations Park in Emery Village, and report to the Etobicoke York Community Council for its meeting on April 4, 2018. 2. Directed that the Emery Village Business Improvement Area be advised of the Community Council's support for the BIA to move forward with its study of tractor trailer usage of this corridor.
