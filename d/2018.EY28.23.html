The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to obtain approval for the installation of a traffic control signal at the intersection of Walsh Avenue and Mathews Gate. The installation of traffic control signals is recommended based on the pedestrian crossing activity in the vicinity of Walsh Avenue and Mathews Gate, as well as the long spacing to adjacent pedestrian crossing protection. Traffic controls signals will enhance safety for pedestrians and motorists at the intersection, however, the installation may increase delays for eastbound and westbound motorists, as these movements will no longer operate with free flow. As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates bus service on Walsh Avenue, City Council approval of this report is required. TTC has been advised on the matter.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve the installation of traffic control signals at the intersection of Walsh Avenue and Mathews Gate.
