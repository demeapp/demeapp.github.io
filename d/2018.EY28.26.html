What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. To address safety concerns and congestion in the area of Valleyfield Junior School (35 Saskatoon Drive), it is recommended that the current school bus loading zone on Saskatoon Drive be extended further west and a new additional school bus loading zone be created on Benbow Road. In addition, the current "No Parking, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday" regulation on the south side of Saskatoon Drive and on east side of Benbow Road (along the frontage and flank of the school), be amended to create a "Maximum 10 Minute Limit, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday" parking regulation to address the need for additional drop-off and pick-up space for parents of attending school children. What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on both sides of Saskatoon Drive between Benbow Road and a point 88.5 metres east. 2. Approved a parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Saskatoon Drive between Benbow Road (west intersection) and a point 100 metres east. 3. Amended the existing school bus loading zone in operation from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on the south side of Saskatoon Drive from a point 100 metres east of Benbow Road (west intersection) and a point 30 metres further east, to be in effect from Benbow Road (west intersection) and a point 100 metres east. 4.
