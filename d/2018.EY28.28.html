The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. To address safety concerns and congestion in the area of St. Simon School (24 Strathburn Boulevard), it is recommended that a 15 minute parking limit be introduced fronting St. Simon School to facilitate the loading/unloading of students to/from the school. It is also recommended that stopping be prohibited on the far side of Strathburn Boulevard. This recommendation is the result of consultation with the school Principal and area Councillor.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing school bus loading zone in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., daily, on the west side of Strathburn Boulevard, between a point 91 metres south of Wallasey Avenue and a point 31 metres further south. 2. Rescinded the existing stopping prohibition in effect from 8:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Strathburn Boulevard, between a point 56 metres south of Wallasey Avenue and a point 95 metres further south. 3. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 15 minutes from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., and 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Strathburn Boulevard, between a point 23 metres south of Wallasey Avenue and a point 127 metres further south. 4.
