The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend that the ceremonial name of "Vimy Ridge Way" be assigned to Glenhurst Avenue. A ceremonial street dedication does not change the official street name or address information. It does result in the installation of special signage along the selected street. Transportation Services was consulted during the preparation of this report.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Assigned the ceremonial name "Vimy Ridge Way" to Glenhurst Avenue. 2. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke York District, to erect signage marking "Vimy Ridge Way".
