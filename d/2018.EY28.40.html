Request for Transportation Study for Traffic Management on Morrison Street

AdoptedFeb 21, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend: Transportation Services undertake a traffic study on Morrison Street between Second Street and Thirteenth Street to determine if any stop signs or other traffic management devices.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Morrison Street is two-lane local road with a sidewalk on the north side and a speed limit of 50 km/hr. It meets other local roads at thirteen intersections, between Second and Thirteenth Streets, alternating between two-way and four-way stop controls. There are residences and school bus stops on both Morrison Street and its cross-streets. My office has requested traffic studies for four-way stop controls and almost every aforementioned intersection. Anecdotal reports from area residents suggests there is significant confusion and ambiguity for both vehicles and pedestrians traveling through the area with regards to traffic management controls along Morrison. I hereby request a traffic study on Morrison Street, in the area between Second Street and Thirteenth Street, to determine if any stop signs, or other traffic management methods are warranted for improved clarity and safety.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke York District, to undertake a traffic study on Morrison Street, between Second Street and Thirteenth Street, to determine if any stop signs or other traffic management devices are warranted to improve traffic flow and safety and for both vehicles and pedestrians.
