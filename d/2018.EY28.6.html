The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes to amend the former City of Etobicoke Zoning Code with respect to the required non-residential parking space standards (parking space ratio and parking space width) for the existing 12-storey office building containing a gross floor area of 10,323 m² at 1243 Islington Avenue. The existing 128 parking spaces (12 surface spaces and 116 parking spaces within the three level underground parking garage) would be maintained. No exterior or interior alterations to the existing building are proposed. This report provides preliminary information on the above-noted application and seeks Community Council's direction on further processing of the application and on the community consultation process. What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Directed staff to schedule a community consultation meeting for the lands at 1243 Islington Avenue together with the Ward Councillor. 2. Directed that Notice for the community consultation meeting be given to landowners and residents within 250 metres of the site, and expanded in consultation with the Ward Councillor, with the additional cost to be borne by the applicant. 3. Directed that Notice for the public meeting under the Planning Act be given according to the regulations of the Planning Act.
