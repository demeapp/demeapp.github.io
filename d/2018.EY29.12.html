The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is in regards to a matter for which the Etobicoke York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to consider an application submitted by the owner of 17 Finchley Road to maintain an existing swimming pool enclosure fence/gate. The existing fence/gate measures approximately 1.06m. The fence/gate does not meet all of the requirements specified in City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447, Fences.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Granted the application for a fence exemption submitted by the owner of 17 Finchley Road, without conditions, thereby allowing the swimming pool enclosure fence/gate to be maintained, as requested. Directed and require that the fence/gate be maintained in good repair without alteration. Directed and require that at such time as replacement of the swimming pool enclosure fence/gate is required, the installations will comply fully with Municipal Code Chapter 447, or its successor by-law.
