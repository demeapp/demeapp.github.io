The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has assessed an appeal from the owner of 118 Robina Avenue for front yard parking. Front yard parking at this location is not recommended because it the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 918 Code does not permit the licensing of a front yard parking pad where the minimum landscape open space cannot be provided. The owner will be given an opportunity to make a deputation before Community Council.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council approved the request for front yard parking at 118 Robina Avenue, with the following conditions: 1. The parking area be a minimum of 2.2 metres in width and not exceed 2.6 metres and be 5.3 metres in length. 2. The applicant remove the existing concrete and repave the parking area with semi-permeable paving materials, as indicated in Attachment 'A', attached to the report dated March 9, 2018, from the Manager, Right of Way Management, Transportation Services. 3. The applicant disconnect any downspouts, if feasible, or seek an exemption from Toronto Water in accordance with the requirements in Chapter 681-11(S). 4.
