Application for Noise Exemption - 4650 Eglinton Avenue West

Adopted with changesApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to refuse to grant the application for a noise exemption, by the agent of the property owner of 4650 Eglinton Avenue West, for the proposed construction activity.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is in regards to a matter for which the Etobicoke York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to consider an application submitted by the agent for the owner of 4650 Eglinton Avenue West to perform construction activity that will generate construction noise beyond the permitted hours, contrary to the requirements specified in City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 591, Noise.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Refused to grant the application for a noise exemption, submitted by the agent of the property owner of 4650 Eglinton Avenue West, for the proposed construction activity that will generate construction noise beyond the permitted hours, contrary to the requirements specified in City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 591, Noise. 2. Requested the City Clerk to forward a copy of the Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 591, Noise, to the applicant to support a clear understanding of noise restrictions and permissions.
