U-Turn Prohibition - Trethewey Drive, West of Black Creek Drive

AdoptedApr 24, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to prohibit U-turn movements at all times for westbound traffic on Trethewey Drive, between Black Creek Drive and a point 90 metres west of Todd Baylis Boulevard.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to propose the installation of a U-turn prohibition for westbound traffic on Trethewey Drive. This proposed U-Turn prohibition will address safety concerns and reduce vehicle conflicts on Trethewey Drive, west of Black Creek Drive. It is recommended that westbound U-turns be prohibited anytime on Trethewey Drive, between Black Creek Drive and a point 90 metres west of Todd Baylis Boulevard. As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates transit service on Trethewey Drive, City Council approval of this report is required.

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit U-turn movements at all times for westbound traffic on Trethewey Drive, between Black Creek Drive and a point 90 metres west of Todd Baylis Boulevard.
