School Bus Loading Zone, Parking Regulation Amendment - Chartwell Road

AdoptedApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to authorize a school bus loading zone in operation from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on the west side of Chartwell Road from a point 30 metres.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. To address safety concerns and congestion in the area of Castlebar Public School (70 Chartwell Road), it is recommended that a school bus loading zone be created to accommodate school buses loading and discharging students. This amendment will improve traffic operations, address concerns regarding traffic congestion and safety and provide additional facilities for school drop-off and pick-up of attending school children. This recommendation is the result of consultation with the area Councillor and the school's principal.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Authorized a school bus loading zone in operation from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on the west side of Chartwell Road from a point 30 metres north of Ambleside Avenue and a point 30 metres further north.
