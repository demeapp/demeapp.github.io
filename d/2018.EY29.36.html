School Bus Loading Zone, Parking Regulation Amendment - Mount Olive Drive

AdoptedApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing stopping prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday on the east side of Mount Olive Drive between Silverstone.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. To address safety concerns and congestion in the area of John D. Parker Junior School (202 Mount Olive Drive), it is recommended that the current school bus loading zone be shortened from 30 metres to 15 metres to accommodate one school bus that services John D. Parker Junior School. In addition, the current "No Stopping, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday" regulation on the east side of Mount Olive Drive, between Silverstone Drive (northwest intersection) and Gihon Spring Drive, needs to be adjusted to address the need for additional drop-off and pick-up space for parents of attending school children. What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing stopping prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday on the east side of Mount Olive Drive between Silverstone Drive (northwest intersection) and Gihon Spring Drive. 2. Prohibited stopping from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday on the east side of Mount Olive Drive between Gihon Spring Drive and a point opposite the northwest corner of Overskate Court. 3. Amended the existing school bus loading zone in operation from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on the east side of Mount Olive Drive between a point 110 metres north of Silverstone Drive and a point 30 metres further north, to be in effect from a point opposite the northwest corner of Overskate Court to a point 15 metres south thereof.
