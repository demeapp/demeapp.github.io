The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to obtain approval for the amendment of the parking regulations on Jillson Avenue between Windermere Avenue and Willard Avenue. The local councillor has requested, on behalf of the residents of Jillson Avenue that submitted a signed petition, the removal of the current alternate north and south side parking to a fixed south side permit parking only regulation to maximize the amount of available parking spots.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from the 16th day of each month to the last day of each month, April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the south side of Jillson Avenue, between Windermere Avenue and Willard Avenue. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from December 1 of one year to March 31 of the next following year, inclusive, on the south side of Jillson Avenue, between Windermere Avenue and Willard Avenue. 3. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from the 1st day of each month to the 15th day of each month, April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the south side of Jillson Avenue between a point 60.5 metres east of Willard Avenue and a point 5.5 metres east thereof. 4.
