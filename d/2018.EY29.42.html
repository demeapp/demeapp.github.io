The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to replace the existing "No Parking, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday" regulation on the east side of Westroyal Road opposite St Eugene School with a "No Stopping, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday" regulation, to improve motorist safety and reduce congestion in the area of the school. The proposed parking amendment is the result of consultation with the Local Councillor and principal of the school.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Westroyal Road between a point 118.5 metres south of Lethbridge Avenue and a point 173.5 metres further south. 2. Prohibited stopping from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Westroyal Road between a point 118.5 metres south of Lethbridge Avenue and a point 173.5 metres further south.
