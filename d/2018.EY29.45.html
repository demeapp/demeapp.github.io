The official record

What the item proposed

With the adoption of the Vision Zero Road Safety Plan, there have been increased requests to my office for speed limit reductions on local roads in Ward 11. -Wall Avenue­- Wall Avenue, which runs between Langside Avenue and Portage Avenue, is a local road with two-way operation; sidewalks are absent on both sides of the road. The legal speed limit is 40 km/hr. In response to a petition submitted to my office for traffic calming, Transportation Services conducted speed and volume counts on the street and determined that the technical warrants for traffic calming were not met; a reduction in the speed limit to 30 km/hr is being sought instead. ­-Henrietta Street­­- Henrietta Street, which runs from Castleton Avenue to the east limit of the road, is a local road with two-way operation and sidewalks on both sides of the road. What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Directed Transportation Services to reduce the speed limit on the following roads in Ward 11 to 30 km/hr: a. Wall Avenue b. Henrietta Street c. Rockcliffe Boulevard, between Alliance Avenue and Terry Drive/Woolner Avenue d. Guestville Avenue e. Dennis Avenue f. Ralph Street g. Hillborn Avenue.
