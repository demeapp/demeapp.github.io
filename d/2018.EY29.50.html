Official title: Report Request - Feasibility of a Passenger Drop-off Area on Judson Street at Royal York Road

The official record

What the item proposed

The intersection of Judson Street and Royal York Road is a three-way intersection with no traffic controls. Cars often pull over either in the live lane of traffic or the bike lane on Royal York Road to allow passengers out at the Mimico Go station. The long term plan for the area includes a pedestrian bridge over Royal York Road, which will allow pedestrians to access the Mimico GO station from the west side of Royal York without having to cross the road. Creating a passenger drop off area on Judson Street near Royal York Road would allow for a safe and legal way for vehicles to stop and allow passengers out to walk to the Mimico GO station. I am requesting a preliminary feasibility study to ensure there is no underground infrastructure or other impediments that would preclude the creation of a pedestrian drop off area on Judson Street.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke-York District, to report to the May 2, 2018 meeting of Etobicoke York Community Council of the feasibility of installing a pedestrian drop off area on Judson Street at Royal York Road, as well as preliminary costs for design and construction.
