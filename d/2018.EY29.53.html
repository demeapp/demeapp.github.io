What the item proposed

The foundation of the planning process under the Planning Act is transparency and full disclosure to ensure all parties are treated fairly. The application to Council to register the residential component of the building as a condominium gives Community Council an opportunity to make a recommendation to Council on conditions for approval of the condominium registration. The actual retail ground floor area and density approved by the OMB on July 22, 2015 is obscure, puzzling and definitely not transparent.
