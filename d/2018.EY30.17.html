Lane Designation - The West Mall at Sherway Drive

AdoptedMay 22, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to designate the easterly northbound lane on The West Mall, from Sherway Drive to a point a point 30.5 metres south, for northbound right turns only, buses.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to obtain approval to designate the easterly northbound lane on The West Mall at Sherway Drive for right turns only, buses excepted. It is appropriate to designate an exclusive northbound right turn lane based on the existing lane configuration and pavement markings at this intersection. As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates transit service on The West Mall, City Council approval of this report is required.

What was decided

City Council on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council designate the easterly northbound lane on The West Mall, from Sherway Drive to a point a point 30.5 metres south, for northbound right turns only, buses excepted.
