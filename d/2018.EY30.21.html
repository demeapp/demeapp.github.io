Parking Regulation Amendment - Newcastle Street

AdoptedMay 22, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to prohibit parking at all times on the south side of Newcastle Street, between Royal York Road and Audley Street.

A citywide decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to install a "No Parking Anytime" regulation on the south side of Newcastle Street, between Royal York Road and Audley Street. The proposed parking amendment is required as a condition of Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) implementing bus service on Newcastle Street. The proposed parking prohibition is the result of consultation with TTC and the local Councillor.

What was decided

City Council on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke York District, in consultation with the Toronto Transit Commission, to create a communication plan to notify those who normally park in the 50 spaces being eliminated on Newcastle Street. 2. City Council request the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke York District, in consultation with the Toronto Transit Commission and the Toronto Parking Authority, to work with Metrolinx to identify any potential locations for new parking spaces, to replace those lost on Newcastle Street, in the vicinity of the Mimico GO Station.
