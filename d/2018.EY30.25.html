The official record

What the item proposed

With the adoption of the Vision Zero Road Safety Plan, which aims to improve safety across our city using a data-driven and targeted approach, there has been an increase in requests to reduce the speed limit around school zones in Ward 13. Local residents, school administration, trustees and parent councils have all expressed support for the adoption of a 30km/hour speed limit in school zones in attempt to reduce traffic-related deaths and injuries to ZERO. St Cecilia Catholic Elementary School, Annette Street Junior and Senior Public School, High Park Alternative Junior School Clendenan Avenue Clendenan Avenue, which runs from Bloor Street West to Dundas Street West, is a local road with two‐way operation and sidewalks on both sides of the road. What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Directed Transportation Services to reduce the speed limit on the following roads in Ward 13 to 30 km/hr: a. Clendenan Avenue from Annette Street to Glendowynne Road b. Jennings Avenue c. Evelyn Avenue from Annette Street to Woodside Avenue d. Weatherell Street, between Jane Street and Armadale Avenue e. Glenlake Avenue from Oakmount Road to Keele Street f. Althea Road g. Doran Avenue
