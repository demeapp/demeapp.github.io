The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide results of the feasibility study on the options for decommissioning the existing natural pond in Jean Augustine Park and replacing it with an ornamental fountain as per the direction from City Council at its meeting of November 7, 8, and 9, 2017. This report summarizes the results of the technical assessment and feasibility study conducted by Cosburn Nauboris Ltd., which explored different approaches to addressing ongoing maintenance issues and complaints about the pond water quality and aesthetics. Potential options for the pond include: reconstruction of the pond, decommissioning of the pond and replacement with an ornamental water feature, and simply removing the feature.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council received for information: 1. The report (April 18, 2018) from the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, headed "Maintenance of Jean Augustine Park Pond". 2. The communication (May 1, 2018) from the President, Humber Bay Shores Condominium Association.
