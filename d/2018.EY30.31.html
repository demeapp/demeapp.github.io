Temporary Signage Permit for Annual Etobicoke Rotary and Toronto Ribfest 2018 Community Event - June 29-July 2, 2018

AdoptedMay 2, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to approve a special permit to the Rotary Club for their 2018 Canada Day event, and allow the placement of signage in accordance with the following conditions.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Annual Rotary Club Canada Day Ribfest is an annual event that is held in Centennial Park and has become a strong tradition in the community. The annual event is from June 29 through July 2, 2018 at Centennial Park from 11:00am – 11:00pm daily. I fully endorse their plans to hold this event again this year as we have done in previous years. This community event has proven to be an annual success and the temporary promotional signage erected has generated no complaints, that I am aware of, to the offices of the Councillors of Etobicoke. The Municipal Code Chapter 693, Section 693-25, which outlines the requirements for signs being used to advertise charitable, non-profit and community events, does not permit any variance or appeal to the criteria which regulate temporary signage.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council approved a special permit to the Rotary Club for their 2018 Canada Day event, and allowed the placement of signage in accordance with the following conditions: 1. Signs are to be mounted on poles (utility, traffic and hydro poles) no higher than 1 metre above the ground to prevent site line obstructions. 2. Signs are not to be placed on sidewalks or centre median poles which could impede pedestrians. 3. Ribfest organizers must arrange for the removal of the Ribfest signs no later than 24 hours after the event. 4. If a complaint is made about a sign, Ribfest organizers must go out the same day to fix the problem and move the sign to another location. 5. Ribfest organizers must provide a list of all sign locations for approval from Transportation Services. 6. Any other requirements from the Supervisor of Right-of-Way, Transportation Services.
