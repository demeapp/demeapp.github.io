What the item proposed

Seeking endorsement of various events for liquor licensing purposes.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: A. Under its delegated authority, declared for liquor licensing purposes, the following events to be of municipal significance, and directed that the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario be advised that the Community Council has no objection to their taking place: 1. Bellwoods Motley Day 2018, taking place at the Bellwoods Brewery, 20 Hafis Road on June 8, 9 and 10, 2018. 2. The Festool Road Show, taking place at Atlas Tools & Machinery, 871 Islington Avenue, from 11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. on May 19, 2018 3. The customer appreciation event at Scout Consignment, 6 Brentwood Road South, from 11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. on May 6, 2018. 4. The annual Grace International Jerk Food Festival, JerkFest, taking place at Centennial Park, 256 Centennial Park Road, August 10-12, 2018. B.
