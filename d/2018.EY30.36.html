The official record

What the item proposed

As a matter of normal practice, Council promotes good planning that avoids problems by respecting Section 37 of the Planning Act and relevant Official Plan policies. Council may use Section 37 of the Planning Act to incentivize and achieve responsible and orderly growth that conforms to Section 24(2) of the Planning Act only if the relevant Official Plan amendment is not appealed. In that spirit and in the interest of fairness, Council approved a motion at the Council meeting of April 25, 26, 27, 2018 to ask the OMB to use the five day hearing on June 19, 2018 as a pre-hearing and mediation session. At the Council meeting of March 26, 27, 2018, Council decided that further consultation was warranted regarding the subject development in accordance with Sec 34(17) of the Planning Act. There are a few issues that remain unresolved on this site and will hopefully be settled at the mediation…

What was decided

This Item was ruled out of order at the Etobicoke York Community Council meeting on May 2, 2018, and was withdrawn from the agenda.
