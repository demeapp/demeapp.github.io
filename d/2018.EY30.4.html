The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has assessed an appeal from the owner of 55 Brookside Avenue for front yard parking. Front yard parking at this location is not recommended because it does not meet the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 918 in that the code does not permit the licensing of a front yard parking pad where landscaping requirements cannot be provided. The owner will be given an opportunity to make a deputation before Community Council.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council approved the request for front yard parking at 55 Brookside Avenue, with the following conditions: 1. The parking area be 2.2 metres in width and a be 5.3 metres in length. 2. The applicant disconnect any downspouts, if feasible, or seek an exemption from Toronto Water in accordance with the requirements in Chapter 681-11(S). 3. The applicant pay the enforcement fee of $738.94 (HST included) since the parking pad was constructed prior the submission of the application and without authorization from the City. 4. The applicant provide the landscape features substantially in accordance with the plan as indicated in Attachment 'A', attached to the report dated April 9, 2018, from the Manager, Right of Way Management, Transportation Services, to the satisfaction of the General Manager of Transportation Services. 5.
