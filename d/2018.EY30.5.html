Etobicoke York Community Council hears the owner's appeal of a front yard parking refusal at 9 Durie Street.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has assessed an appeal from the owner of 9 Durie Street for front yard parking. Front yard parking at this location is not recommended because it does not meet the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 918 in that the Code does not permit the licensing of a front yard parking pad where on-street permit parking is authorized on same side of the street and the installation of a ramp will result in loss of an on-street permit parking space. The owner will be given an opportunity to make a deputation before Community Council.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council approved the request for front yard parking at 9 Durie Street, with the following conditions: 1. The parking area be a minimum of 2.2 metres in width and not exceed 2.6 metres and be 5.3 metres in length. 2. The applicant pay for the installation of the permanent ramp to service the parking space. 3. The applicant contact Urban Forestry in order to comply with any and all requirements in accordance with the City of Toronto Tree Protection Policy and Specification for Construction Near Trees as construction is proposed near or within the tree protection zone. 4. The applicant disconnect any downspouts, if feasible, or seek an exemption from Toronto Water in accordance with the requirements in Chapter 681-11(S). 5. The applicant provide the landscape features substantially in accordance with the plan as indicated in Attachment 'A', attached to the report…
