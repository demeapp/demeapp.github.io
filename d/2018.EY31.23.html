All-Way Stop Control - Morrison Street and Fourth Street; and Morrison Street and Ninth Street

Adopted with changesJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to not approve an all-way stop control at the intersection of Morrison Street and Fourth Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report presents the results of staff's investigation on a traffic study conducted on Morrison Street between the limits of Second Street and Thirteenth Street, to determine the feasibility for additional all-way stop controls. The investigation is in response to Community Council's directive at its meeting of February 21, 2018, item EY28.40. Staff conducted all-way stop studies at the intersections of Morrison Street and Fourth Street; and, Morrison Street and Ninth Street. Based on the study results, all-way stop control is not recommended at the intersections of Morrison Street and Fourth Street; and, Morrison Street and Ninth Street as the City-wide warrant for the installation of all-way stop controls is not met.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Approved an all-way stop control at the intersection of Morrison Street and Fourth Street. 2. Approved an all-way stop control at the intersection of Morrison Street and Ninth Street.
