Parking Regulation Amendment - Quebec Avenue

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to amend the parking for a maximum 10 minutes regulation in effect from 7:30 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to amend the existing "Maximum 10 Minute" parking regulation in effect from 7:30 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Quebec Avenue between a point 41 metres north of Bloor Street West and a point 20 metres further north, to be in effect between the hours of 7:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday. As a result of this amendment the existing no parking regulation that is in effect at all other times will also be amended to be in effect from 6:00 p.m. on one day to 7:00 p.m. the following day; Anytime Saturday and Sunday. The proposed parking amendments are the result of consultation with the local Councillor.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Amended the parking for a maximum 10 minutes regulation in effect from 7:30 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Quebec Avenue between a point 41 metres north of Bloor Street West and a point 20 metres further north, to be in effect between the hours of 7:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday. 2. Amended the parking prohibition in effect from 6:00 p.m. of one day to 7:30 a.m. the following day; 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday; Anytime Saturday and Sunday, on the east side of Quebec Avenue, between a point 41 metres north of Bloor Street West and a point 20 metres further north, to be in effect from 6:00 p.m. of one day to 7:00 a.m. the following day; Monday to Friday; Anytime Saturday and Sunday.
