Parking Regulation Amendment - Westown Subdivision

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to rescind the "No Parking, 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m., from December 1 of one year to March 31 of the following year" on Lou Pomanti Street, between Mary Chapman Boulevard and Isaac Devins Boulevard.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of the report is to obtain approval to allow long-term overnight parking and short-term (3 hour) daytime parking on Lou Pomanti Street (south side) and Mary Chapman Boulevard (west side). In addition a "No Parking Anytime" regulation will be implemented on the north side of Lou Pomanti Street and on the east side of Mary Chapman Boulevard.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the "No Parking, 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m., from December 1 of one year to March 31 of the following year" on Lou Pomanti Street, between Mary Chapman Boulevard and Isaac Devins Boulevard. 2. Enacted a 12 hour permitted parking from 8:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m., on the south side of Lou Pomanti Street, between Mary Chapman Boulevard and Isaac Devins Boulevard. 3. Prohibited parking at all times on the north side of Lou Pomanti Street, between Mary Chapman Boulevard and Isaac Devins Boulevard. 4. Enacted a 12 hour permitted parking from 8:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m., on the west side of Mary Chapman Boulevard, between a point 20 metres south of Lou Pomanti Street and Isaac Devins Boulevard (south intersection). 5.
