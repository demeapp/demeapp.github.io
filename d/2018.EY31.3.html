What the item proposed

This application proposes a Zoning By-law Amendment to maintain a place of worship use at 1736 Weston Road. A Zoning By-law Amendment to permit the place of worship use was approved by City Council in 2003, subject to a number of conditions that were never fulfilled. Between 2003 and 2008, the applicant requested through Etobicoke York Community Council and City Council that a condition related to off-site parking be amended to be less onerous. The request was deferred by City Council and Etobicoke York Community Council several times and ultimately considered by City Council in 2008. In 2008, City Council approved a Temporary Use By-law, with no requirement for off-site parking, for a period of three years.
