School Bus Loading Zone, Parking Regulation Amendments - Pine Street, MacDonald Avenue

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing school bus loading zone in operation from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on the north side of MacDonald Avenue, between a point 15 metres west of Pine Street and a point 54 metres further west.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. To address safety concerns and congestion in the area of Weston Collegiate Institute (100 Pine Street), it is recommended that a school bus loading zone be relocated to accommodate school buses loading and discharging students. In addition, the existing parking regulations will be adjusted to reflect the relocation of the School Bus Loading Zone. These amendments will improve traffic operations, address concerns regarding traffic congestion and safety and provide additional facilities for school drop-off and pick-up of attending school children. These recommendations are the result of consultation with the area Councillor, the school's principal, and a public meeting.
