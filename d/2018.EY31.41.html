Toronto City Council · Decision

Request Approval of Temporary Signage Permits for Community Events at Franklin Horner Community Centre

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to approve the issuance of a special permit to the Franklin Horner Extravaganza, Hometown Fair, and Breakfast with Santa events to allow the placement of temporary promotional signage at various locations.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Franklin Horner Community Centre, 432 Horner Ave., hosts events throughout the year which encourage local residents to come out and meet their neighbours, participate in family oriented events, enjoy musical performances and other activities. The following events are organized by volunteers and board members at Franklin Horner Community Centre. Extravaganza XIII, Saturday September 8 Hometown Fair, Saturday November 17 Breakfast with Santa, Saturday December 15 On behalf of the Franklin Horner Community Centre organizers and volunteers, I request permission for temporary signage to promote the events.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Approved the issuance of a special permit to the Franklin Horner Extravaganza, Hometown Fair, and Breakfast with Santa events to allow the placement of temporary promotional signage at various locations at and around the vicinity of 432 Horner Avenue. 2. Directed that the Franklin Horner Community Centre provide a list of locations to City staff allowing temporary placement of temporary signage in the above-mentioned areas. 3. Directed that the Franklin Horner Community Centre will remove signage within twenty four (24) hours of the event's closure.
