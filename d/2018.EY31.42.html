Request Approval of Temporary Signage Permits for Community Event - 15th Annual Lakeshore Mardi Gras Festival

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to approve issuance of a special permit to the Lakeshore Mardi Gras event to allow the placement of temporary promotional signage at various locations near Colonel Smith Park.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The annual Lakeshore Mardi Gras Festival is an outdoor music festival by the lake event with fun, family-oriented entertainment, rides, vendors, food and other activities. The Festival is organized by the Lakeshore Mardi Gras Committee. On behalf of the Committee, organizers and participants, the Lakeshore Mardi Gras Festival seeks, permission for temporary signage to promote the events.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Approved issuance of a special permit to the Lakeshore Mardi Gras event to allow the placement of temporary promotional signage at various locations near Colonel Smith Park, in and around the Lake Shore Boulevard West and Kipling area. 2. Directed that the Lakeshore Mardi Gras Committee provide a list of locations to City staff allowing temporary placement of temporary signage in the above-mentioned areas. 3. Directed that the Lakeshore Mardi Gras Committee will remove signage within twelve (12) hours of the event's closure.
