Toronto City Council · Decision

Safety Improvements at Queens Avenue and Stanley Avenue

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to request the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke York District to report to the Community Council on the feasibility of installing a roundabout, intersection realignment.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The intersection of Queens Avenue and Stanley Avenue in the Mimico community of Ward 6, is a large, irregular shape. Stanley Avenue is a one-way street, with a contraflow bike lane, and two-way stop signs on Queens Ave. Because of its size and configuration, the intersection is confusing for all road users and I have witnessed many near miss accidents. I have requested the intersection being studied several times for traffic calming. I am requesting a study on the feasibility of a roundabout, intersection realignment, or any other changes to improve safety at this intersection.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke York District to report to the Community Council on the feasibility of installing a roundabout, intersection realignment, or any other changes to improve safety at the intersection of Queens Avenue and Stanley Avenue in Ward 6.
