Report Request - Traffic Calming on Valermo Drive

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to request the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke York District, to undertake a study of traffic calming measures on Valermo Drive, and report back to the Etobicoke York Community Council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Valermo Drive is an east-west collector road, that runs east off Browns Line and ends at the entrance to Connorvale Park in the Alderwood neighbourhood of Ward 6. The street intersects with thirteen other residential streets along its length, and has a speed limit of 50km/hr. Valermo Drive is populated with single family homes, and a former school which is currently being redeveloped with residential dwellings. Due to its length and central location between Browns Line, Horner Avenue, and Evans Avenue, and Connorvale Park, Valermo Drive is used as a high-speed cut through. I am requesting a traffic study to identify any potential traffic calming measures along the street.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke York District, to undertake a study of traffic calming measures on Valermo Drive, and report to the Etobicoke York Community Council.
