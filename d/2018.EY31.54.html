Temporary Traffic Control Signal - Kirknewton Road and Hunter Avenue

Adopted with changesJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Etobicoke York Community Council recommends that The Director of Transportation Service, Etobicoke York District, approve the installation of a temporary traffic control signals at Kirknewton Road and Hunter Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The construction of the Eglinton Crosstown LRT is a complex multi-year project with work being completed in phases. The duration of construction activities is currently planned to continue until 2021. Construction activities will temporarily reduce the number of traffic lanes through intersections (often to only one lane in each direction) and thus reduce sidewalk space for pedestrians and road space for vehicles. The long-term construction has resulted in extensive gridlock for motorists travelling along the Dufferin Street and Eglinton Avenue West corridor. As a result, drivers have been utilizing local roads to maneuver around gridlock, which has resulted in excessive speeding taking place on Kirknewton Road, which is a one-way southbound from Eglinton Avenue West to Hunter Avenue. What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Transportation Service, Etobicoke York District, to investigate the installation of a temporary traffic control signal at Kirknewton Road and Hunter Avenue, to be in effect until December 31, 2021, or until the completion of the Eglinton Crosstown LRT, and report to the meeting of the Etobicoke York Community Council on July 4, 2018.
