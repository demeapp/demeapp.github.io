Request to Re-open Item EY31.23 - All-Way Stop Control - Morrison Street and Fourth Street; and Morrison Street and Ninth Street

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to re-open item EY31.23, headed "All-Way Stop Control - Morrison Street and Fourth Street; and Morrison Street and Ninth Street", adopted, as amended by Etobicoke York Community Council on June 6, 2018.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on June 6, 2018, Etobicoke York Community Council approved an all-way stop control at the intersection of Morrison Street and Ninth Street. Following feedback from area residents, it has become evident that a stop sign is required at the intersection of Morrison and Eighth Street, rather than Morrison and Ninth Street.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item EY31.23, headed "All-Way Stop Control - Morrison Street and Fourth Street; and Morrison Street and Ninth Street", adopted, as amended by Etobicoke York Community Council on June 6, 2018. 2. Amended Part 2 of that decision to approve an all-way stop control at the intersection of Morrison Street and Eighth Street.
