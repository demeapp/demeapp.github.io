The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on June 6, 2018, Etobicoke York Community Council adopted Item EY31.26, headed "Traffic Calming - Rosemount Avenue, between Dufferin Street and Greenlaw Avenue". Community Council authorized Transportation staff to request a poll to determine support of property owners for the installation of traffic calming on this portion of Rosemount Avenue. City Council, on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018, adopted, as amended, PW29.6, headed “Next Steps on Traffic Safety Measures”, which delegated to community councils the authority to waive petition and polling requirements for traffic calming measures. Accordingly, I am recommending that Etobicoke York Community Council waive the poll in this instance.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item EY31.26, headed "Traffic Calming - Rosemount Avenue, between Dufferin Street and Greenlaw Avenue", adopted by Etobicoke York Community Council on June 6, 2018. 2. Waived the polling requirements for traffic calming measures in this instance, and requested the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway on Rosemount Avenue for traffic calming enhancement, generally as shown on Drawing EY18-047, dated May 2018.
