Traffic Concerns - Riverview Heights

AdoptedJul 23, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to approve the removal of the "Do Not Enter, Buses Excepted" signs for traffic on St Phillips Road accessing Riverview Heights.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to share the findings of the pilot "Do Not Enter, Buses Excepted" prohibition for vehicles travelling on St Phillips Road to Riverview Heights. Based on the results of our studies, feedback from the community and consultation with the local councillor, we are recommending that the "Do Not Enter, Buses Excepted" signs be removed. As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates transit service on St Phillips Road, City Council approval of this report is required.

What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve the removal of the "Do Not Enter, Buses Excepted" signs for traffic on St Phillips Road accessing Riverview Heights.
