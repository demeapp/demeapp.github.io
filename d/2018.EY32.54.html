Toronto City Council · Decision

Pay and Display - Weston Road, between King Street and Fern Avenue

AdoptedJul 23, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The purpose of this report is to obtain approval to rescind the existing "No Parking Anytime" prohibition, on the east side of Weston Road, between a point 77 metres north of King Street and a point 50 metres further north and extend the Pay and Display area in that section from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday; 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on Saturday for $1.00 an hour for a maximum period of 3 hours. City Council is asked to rescind the existing parking prohibition at all times on the east side of Weston Road, between a point 77 metres north of King Street and a point 50 metres north.

The official summary names a figure of $1.00.
