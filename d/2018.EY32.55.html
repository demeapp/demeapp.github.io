Toronto City Council · Decision

Accessible Loading Zone - Victoria Avenue West

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to rescind the maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect from 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., on the south side of Victoria Avenue West, between the west end of Victoria Avenue West and a point 15 metres east.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Etobicoke York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting approval to install an accessible loading zone on the south side of Victoria Avenue West, between the west limit of Victoria Avenue West and a point 15 metres further east. The loading zone will operate at all times to provide curb side access for Wheel-Trans vehicles.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect from 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., on the south side of Victoria Avenue West, between the west end of Victoria Avenue West and a point 15 metres east. 2. Designated an accessible loading zone to operate at all times on the south side of Victoria Avenue West, between the west end of Victoria Avenue West and a point 15 metres east.
