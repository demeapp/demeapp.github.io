Parking Regulation Amendments - Norby Crescent and Sun Row Drive

Received for informationJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Norby Crescent between Sun Row Drive and a point 70 metres east.

Received for information — Council took no action on it.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to replace a portion of the existing "No Parking, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday" regulation on the north side of Norby Crescent, along the frontage of Father Serra School with a "Maximum 10 Minute, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday" regulation. In addition, to implement a "Maximum 10 Minute, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday" regulation on the east side of Sun Row Drive from a point 122 metres north of Norby Crescent and a point 32 metres further north. These amendments will address the need for additional drop-off and pick-up space for parents of attending school children. The proposed parking amendment is the result of consultation with the Local Councillor and principal of the school.
