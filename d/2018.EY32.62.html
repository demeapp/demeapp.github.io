Parking Regulation Amendments - Willard Avenue and Windermere Avenue

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing 10 Minute parking limit, in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:30 a.m., 11:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Willard Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to address concerns raised in the area of James Culnan School (605 Willard Avenue). It is recommended that the existing school bus loading zone located on the east side of Willard Avenue be extended an additional 30 metres to the south in order to accommodate the buses that service the school. In addition, it is recommended that the hours of the existing "Maximum 10 Minute" parking on the west side of Windermere Avenue in effect from 8:30 a.m. to 9:30 a.m. and 11:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, be amended to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and 2:30 p.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, to coincide with the current school entry and dismissal times.
