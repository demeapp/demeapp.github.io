Reducing the Speed Limit on Certain Streets in Ward 12 to 30 km/hr

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to direct Transportation Services to reduce the speed limit on the following roads in Ward 12 to 30 km/hr.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

With the adoption of the Vision Zero Road Safety Plan, there have been increased requests to my office for speed limit reductions on local roads in Ward 12. -Lester Avenue- Lester Avenue runs from Keele Street to Glenhaven Street. The road has a two-way operation and a sidewalk on both sides of the road. The legal speed limit is 50 km/hr. Immaculate Conception Catholic Church is located on the south side of Lester Avenue. I am requesting that the speed limit on the full length of Lester Avenue from Keele Street to Glenhaven Street be reduced to 30 km/hr. -Bicknell Avenue- Bicknell Avenue runs from Rogers Road to Eglinton Avenue West. The road has a two-way operation with a sidewalk on both sides of the road. The legal speed limit is 40 km/hr. There are two schools located on Bicknell Avenue.
