Polling for Speed Humps - Uxbridge Avenue, between Pelham Avenue and Davenport Road

Adopted with changesJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to authorize the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke York District, to request the City Clerk to survey eligible householders in English, Italian and Portuguese, on Uxbridge Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Uxbridge Avenue extends from Pelham Avenue to Davenport Road and is often used as a thoroughfare to bypass heavy traffic on surrounding arterial roads. With increased development and growth in the area along with increased vehicular traffic, pedestrian safety has become a community concern. In addition, we have all experienced traffic complications from the underpass on St. Clair Ave West, between Old Weston Road and Keele Street, as this junction has become a serious bottleneck that has crippled traffic on St. Clair Avenue West and negatively affected our local roads. Given the proximity to Charles-Sauriol Elementary School and Pelham Avenue Parkette, it is necessary to poll the community as they have witnessed excessive speeding and a blatant disregard for the posted traffic regulations. To avoid unnecessary delay, it is important that authority be given now for a poll to be carried…

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and approve the installation of traffic calming on Uxbridge Avenue, between Pelham Avenue and Davenport Road. 2. Requested the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway on Uxbridge Avenue, from Pelham Avenue to Davenport Road, for traffic calming purposes, generally as shown on Drawing EY18-062.DGN, dated May 2018. 3. Directed Transportation Services take to the necessary actions to reduce the speed limit to 30 km/h on Uxbridge Avenue, from Pelham Avenue to Davenport Road, when the speed humps are installed.
