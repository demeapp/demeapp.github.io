Installation of Speed Humps on Pelham Avenue, between Uxbridge Avenue and Laughton Avenue

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to waive the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and approve the installation of traffic calming on Pelham Avenue, from Uxbridge Avenue and Laughton Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Pelham Avenue is 7.5 metres wide and classified as a two lane local road, which runs in an east-west direction from Osler Street in the west to Laughton Avenue in the east. The current speed limit on this street is 30 km/h. I have received requests from residents on Pelham Avenue to install speed humps on Pelham Avenue, between Uxbridge Avenue and Laughton Avenue, in an effort to enhance pedestrian and school zone safety, as a result of the proximity to Charles-Sauriol Elementary School (55 Pelham Avenue) and the Pelham Avenue Parkette, located on the north side of Pelham Avenue, between Uxbridge and Laughton Avenue. Furthermore, residents are concerned with vehicle speeds and traffic infiltration on their street. What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and approve the installation of traffic calming on Pelham Avenue, from Uxbridge Avenue and Laughton Avenue. 2. Requested the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway on Pelham Avenue, from Uxbridge Avenue to Laughton Avenue, for traffic calming purposes, generally as shown on Drawing EY18-061.DGN, dated May 2018.
