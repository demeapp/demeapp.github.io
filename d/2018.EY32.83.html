Toronto City Council · Decision

Speed Limit Amendment - Florence Crescent

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to reduce the speed limit from 40 km/h to 30 km/h on Florence Crescent, between Jane Street and St. Clair Avenue West.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Florence Crescent is a 7.3 metres wide roadway classified as a "local" road. This street has a one-way westbound to southbound operation, starting from Jane Street and terminating at St. Clair Avenue West. There are sidewalks on both sides of this street, which has a posted speed limit of 40 km/h. In response to a petition submitted to my office for traffic calming, Transportation Services conducted speed and volume counts on the street and determined that the technical warrants for traffic calming were not met and so a reduction in the speed limit to 30 km/hr is being sought instead.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 40 km/h to 30 km/h on Florence Crescent, between Jane Street and St. Clair Avenue West.
