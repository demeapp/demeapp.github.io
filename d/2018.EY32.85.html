Request for Comprehensive Traffic Study in Mimico

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to request the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke York District to undertake a comprehensive study of traffic in the Mimico neighbourhood of Ward 6.

The official record

What the item proposed

As stated in the introduction to the Traffic Calming Guide for Toronto, “Traffic calming is intended to improve the quality of life for residents on traffic calmed streets, achieve slower speeds for motor vehicles, and increase the safety for non-motorized users of the street.” Similarly to the phenomenon currently being experienced city-wide, over the last few years the Mimico community in Ward 6 has experienced significant changes to traffic patterns through local streets. Changes include increased traffic volumes, incidences of speeding, disregard for traffic signs and signals, vehicles travelling the wrong way on one-way streets, and using residential streets as shortcuts. There have been several serious accidents involving vehicles, bicycles, and pedestrians in recent years. What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke York District to undertake a comprehensive study of traffic in the Mimico neighbourhood of Ward 6, roughly bounded by Royal York Road to the west, Cavell Avenue to the north, Louise Street to the east, and Lake Shore Boulevard West to the south, and report back on any appropriate traffic calming measures and any other traffic measures deemed suitable to address vehicle volume, speed, and pedestrian and cyclist safety.
