Toronto City Council · Decision

Dayton Avenue Parking

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Dayton Avenue has an existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the east side of Dayton Avenue between the first laneway south of The Queensway and Sinclair Street. Staff recommends that That the Etobicoke York Community Council rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the east side of Dayton Avenue between the first laneway south of The Queensway.
