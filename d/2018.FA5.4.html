Presentation on the City of Toronto’s Policy on Language of Service for People Wanting to Pay and/or Dispute Parking Violations

Received for informationJan 29, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend: The Government Management Committee allow staff to do a presentation during the next French Language Advisory Committee meeting regarding the language of service for people who want.

A citywide decision. Received for information — Council took no action on it.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In recent weeks, a media outlet reported the fact that henceforth, Francophones will no longer be guaranteed access to services in French when they want to dispute parking violations with the City of Toronto. This service is no longer provided by the Province, but has been delegated to the City and, therefore, is no longer subject to the French Language Services Act, 2006. When questioned by the media, Susan Pape was identified as a spokesperson for the City. Ms. Pape referred to a “new municipal process” and suggested that individuals could use interpreters, at their own expense. The French Language Services Commissioner confirms that an investigation has been opened into this file, pursuant to a citizen complaint. It is recommended that the Committee hear a presentation on language of service for people wanting to pay and/or dispute parking violations or other infractions.

What was decided

The French Language Advisory Committee received the item for information.
