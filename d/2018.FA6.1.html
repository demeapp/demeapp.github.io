The official record

What the item proposed

Brendan Nolan, Corporate Management Policy Consultant, City Manager's Office, will provide a verbal update on the establishment of a French-language university in Toronto.

What was decided

The French Language Advisory Committee recommends to the Government Management Committee that: 1. City Council affirm its support for the establishment of a French-language university in Toronto. 2. City Council request CreateTO to engage French-language university staff to determine what assistance, if any, the City of Toronto may be able to provide in the establishment of a French-language university in Toronto. 3. City Council request the City Manager to include Government of Ontario and French-language university staff in future post-secondary institution sectoral collaboration and coordination initiatives undertaken by the City of Toronto.
