The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks Council approval for the acquisition of three water lots along Queens Quay West for nominal consideration.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the Director, Real Estate Services to submit an Offer to Purchase (the “Agreement”) for nominal consideration, to Her Majesty the Queen in Right of Canada as represented by the Minister of Public Works (the "Federal Government"), or the registered owner of the lands, as the case may be, for the purchase of fee simple interests, of some or all of the properties known as Portland Slip, Spadina Slip and Spadina Quay Marina, shown in Appendix B (the "Properties") to the report (February 6, 2018) from the Director Real Estate Services, substantially on the terms and conditions outlined in Appendix A to the report (February 6, 2018) from the Director Real Estate Services and on such other or amended terms and conditions as may be acceptable to the Deputy City Manager, Internal Corporate Services and in a form…
