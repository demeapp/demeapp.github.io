The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Blanket Contract No. 47020391 awarded to TBM Service Group Inc. ("TBM") as a result of the Request for Proposal (RFP) 0203-16-0091 for the supply of all labour, materials and equipment necessary to provide required custodial services at various Facilities Management Division and client locations. The amendment would apply to the period from February 1, 2017 to January 31, 2018 and the first option period from February 1, 2018 to January 31, 2019. Further work is being undertaken by Facilities Management (FM) to ensure effective contract management and administration. The amendment is mainly driven by Facilities Management's efforts to standardize custodial service throughout all divisions and clients within the City of Toronto and to consolidate disparate custodial contracts to ensure more consistent, effective and transparent…

What was decided

The Government Management Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Financial Control By-law) granted authority to amend Contract No. 47020391 with TBM Service Group Inc., for the supply of all labour, materials and equipment necessary to provide custodial services required for various Facilities Management Division locations, by an additional amount of $2,998,982 net of all taxes and charges ($3,051,764, net of HST recoveries), adjusting the current contract value from $9,190,493 net of all taxes and charges ($9,352,245 net of HST recoveries) to $12,189,475 net of all taxes and charges ($12,404,010 net of HST recoveries).
