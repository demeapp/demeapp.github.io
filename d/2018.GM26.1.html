The official record

What the item proposed

This report summarizes changes in Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission (CRTC) policies that resulted in the discontinuance of the Toronto Rogers Community Television Channel broadcast of City Council meetings and explores options for City support to local community broadcasters. The City has communicated with the CRTC and Rogers Communications, on direction of City Council, to request that resources for community programming be reviewed and reinstated. This report outlines options the City could consider to support public access to City content including working with new and existing broadcasters and supporting the application of local broadcasters committed to community content in their programming.

What was decided

The Government Management Committee referred the item to the City Manager for a report on: 1. The following options, together with associated costs: a. Offering City digital content to local broadcasters and exploring partnerships with external broadcasters to encourage them to air City Council meetings. As MM35.1 suggests, broadcasters such as TVO, Rogers or CPAC could broadcast this content. b. Requesting the Federal Government establish a new broadcast channel by directing the Canadian Radio-Television and Telecommunications Commission (CRTC) to require broadcasters to reserve a channel for City Council meetings. The process would require the Minister to consult with the CRTC, and submit an order before Parliament. c. Supporting applications by external broadcasters to the CRTC, including by community groups such as the Canadian Association for Community Television Users and Stations…
