The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Purchase Order No. 6045598 issued to Semple Gooder Roofing Corporation, originally awarded under Tender No. 151-2017 for Roof Replacement, St. Lawrence Centre for the Arts. The Purchase Order amendment being requested is an increase of $837,504 net of all taxes and charges ($852,244 net of HST recoveries). The amendment is required to complete remaining roof work, contemplated in the initial scope of work in the form of alternative pricing, but not awarded under the original Tender. Funds have been identified in the 2018 Capital Budget approved by City Council on February 12, 2018.

What was decided

The Government Management Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Financial Control By-law) granted authority to amend Purchase Order No. 6045598, to complete the roof replacements required on the St. Lawrence Center in accordance with the requirements of Tender 151-2017, by an additional amount of $837,504 net of all taxes and charges ($852,244 net of HST recoveries), adjusting the current contract value from $1,476,006 net of all taxes and charges ($1,501,984 net of HST recoveries) to $2,313,510 net of all taxes and charges ($ 2,354,228 net of HST recoveries).
