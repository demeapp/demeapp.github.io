What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Contract No. 47019200 issued to Bond Paving & Construction Inc., originally awarded under Tender 14-2015, for the non-exclusive supply of all labour, materials, equipment and supervision necessary for the asphalt and concrete reconstruction of pavements and curbs throughout the City of Toronto, for the Facilities Management locations, on an as and when required basis with an option to renew for three (3) additional (1) one year periods. The total contract amendment being requested is for an additional amount of $239,752 net of all taxes and charges ($243,972 net of HST recoveries). The recommended amendment includes $114,752 net of all taxes and charges ($116,772 net of HST recoveries) required to bring the overspent contract back to compliance and an incremental $125,000 net of all taxes and charges ($127,200 net of HST…

What was decided

The Government Management Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1. C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code (Financial Control By-law), granted authority to amend the current authority for Contract No. 47019200 issued to Bond Paving & Construction Inc., by the additional amount of $239,752 net of all taxes and charges ($243,972 net of HST recoveries), from $1,103,200 ($1,122,616 net of HST recoveries) to $1,342,952 net of all taxes and charges ($1,366,588 net of HST recoveries).
