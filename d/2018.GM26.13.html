The official record

What the item proposed

The City was approached by Toronto Council Fire (TCF) to partner in the installation of a large structure at Nathan Phillips Square (NPS) to recognize the experience of Indian residential school survivors in relation to a recommendation from the Truth and Reconciliation Commission (TRC) of Canada. Council has committed to support the TRC's recommendations, including Call to Action 82 which relates to a monument in each provincial or territorial capital to recognize Indian residential school survivors, and directed City staff to work in partnership with the Province of Ontario and local Indigenous communities to pursue the project. What was decided

The Government Management Committee: 1. Directed the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture and the Deputy City Manager, Internal Corporate Services to report prior to the end of the current Council term with an update on the Indian Residential School Survivors Legacy Structure project.
