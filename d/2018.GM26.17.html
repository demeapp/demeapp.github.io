The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends terms of transfer for the lands municipally known as 9 Madison Avenue (the "Property") to CreateTO's corporate entity, Build Toronto Inc.

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the entering into of an agreement to transfer portions of the property municipally known as 9 Madison Avenue as described in Appendix 2 to the report (March 21, 2018) from the Director, Real Estate Services (the "Property"), including any related agreements, to CreateTO's corporate entity, Build Toronto Inc., substantially on the terms and conditions as set out in Appendices 1 and 2 to the report (March 21, 2018) from the Director, Real Estate Services, and on such other and amended terms and conditions as may be approved by the Deputy City Manager, Internal Corporate Services, and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor. 2.
