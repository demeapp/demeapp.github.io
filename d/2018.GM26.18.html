What the item proposed

The French Language Advisory Committee, at its meeting on January 29, 2018, considered Item FA5.3 - Establishment of a French-Language University in Toronto - Update.

What was decided

The Government Management Committee: 1. Requested that the City Manager's Office invite representatives from the Ministry of Advanced Education and Skills Development and/or the Ministry of Francophone Affairs to make a presentation on the status of the establishment of a French-language university in Toronto to the French Language Advisory Committee at its meeting on May 15, 2018.
