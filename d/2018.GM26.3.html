The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks Council's authority for the adoption of the necessary by-law to designate the premises owned by the City of Toronto and occupied by Eva's Initiatives for Homeless Youth at 60 Brant Street as a municipal capital facility, and to provide a property tax exemption for municipal and education purposes. The municipal capital facility agreement will provide an exemption for approximately 16,146 square feet (1,500 square metres) of interior space, which is the entire property at 60 Brant Street. Eva's Initiatives for Homeless Youth provides shelter/transitional housing for homeless youths and a wide array of youth support services.
