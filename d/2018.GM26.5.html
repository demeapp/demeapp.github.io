The official record

What the item proposed

This report relates to the accumulation of arrears for water and sewer services at a property municipally known as 4700 Keele Street, public utility account 166500, which is the main campus of York University. The confidential attachment contains legal advice and legal recommendations about the overdue amount.

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in the confidential letter (April 3, 2018) from the Government Management Committee. 2. City Council authorize the City Treasurer, in consultation with the City Solicitor and the General Manager, Toronto Water, to re-initiate discussions with officials of York University to exchange further information that may bear on the recorded water consumption readings and utility billings, in an effort to reach a mutually satisfactory resolution and report back to the Government Management Committee on the outcome. 3. City Council direct that the confidential instructions to staff in the confidential letter (April 3, 2018) from the Government Management Committee and Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 16, 2018) from the City Solicitor and the Treasurer…
