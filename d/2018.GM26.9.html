The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Purchase Order No. 6045107 issued to 373044 Ontario Limited o/a Trans Canada Construction to carry out the necessary remedial and restoration work at the Giovanni Caboto Artificial Ice Rink (AIR) and Outdoor Pool (ODP) facilities due to unforeseen site conditions encountered during construction and mechanical changes required by the Technical Standards and Safety Authority (TSSA). The Contract for the construction of the State of Good Repairs Facilities Upgrades and Improvements was originally awarded in accordance with the call process for Tender No. 78-2017. The Purchase Order Amendment being requested for the additional work is $395,583 excluding taxes and charges ($402,546 net of HST recoveries). Funds have been identified in the Parks, Forestry and Recreation 2018 Capital Budget approved by City Council on February 12,…

What was decided

The Government Management Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Financial Control By-law) granted authority to amend Purchase Order No. 6045107, to carry out the necessary remedial and restoration work at the Giovanni Caboto Artificial Ice Rink (AIR) and Outdoor Pool (ODP) facilities, awarded in accordance with the requirements of Tender 78-2017, by an additional amount of $395,583 excluding taxes and charges ($402,546 net of HST recoveries), adjusting the current contract value from $2,711,509 net of all taxes and charges ($2,759,232 net of HST recoveries) to $3,107,092 net of all taxes and charges ($3,161,777 net of HST recoveries).
