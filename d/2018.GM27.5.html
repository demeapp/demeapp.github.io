What the item proposed

This report informs the Government Management Committee of the non-competitive purchasing activity valued at $3,000 and above for City Divisions in 2017 including non-competitive purchases approved by Council, non-competitive purchases processed by the Purchasing and Materials Management Division (PMMD), and non-competitive purchases processed by Divisions through Divisional Purchase Orders (DPOs). Non-competitive purchasing activity for the Offices of the Auditor General, Integrity Commissioner, Lobbyist Registrar and Ombudsman are not included as part of this report. These offices are audited separately by external auditors and reported on separately. Overall, the dollar value of non-competitive activity in 2017 compared to 2016, increased by 42 percent, however as compared to total City purchases, non-competitive procurements only represented 3.2 percent of total City purchases.…

What was decided

1. The Government Management Committee received the report (April 13, 2018) from the Treasurer for information.
