Request to Amend Purchase Order No. 6041950 with Calian Ltd for the Time Attendance and Scheduling System (TASS) Project

AdoptedJun 5, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Government Management Committee was asked to grant authority to enter into an amending agreement and amend Purchasing Order No.6041950 with Calian Ltd., in accordance with Section 71-11.1C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Financial Control).

The official summary names a figure of $1,970,582.78.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek authority to amend and increase Purchase Order No. 6041950, with Calian Ltd. by the amount of $1,970,582.78 net of HST ($2,005,265.04 net of HST recoveries), increasing the total contract amount from $13,077,578 to $15,082,843 net of HST recoveries, as it relates to the work on Time Attendance and Scheduling System (TASS) project. Funds for the Purchase Order amendment are included in the Parks, Forestry and Recreation 2018 Council Approved Capital Budget and Future Year Commitments for the work outlined under "Deferred Scope" below. What was decided

The Government Management Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Financial Control), granted authority to enter into an amending agreement and amend Purchasing Order No.6041950 with Calian Ltd. to increase the contract amount by $2,005,265.04 net of HST recoveries, revising the current total contract value from $13,077,578 to $15,082,843 net of HST recoveries.
