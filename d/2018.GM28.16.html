The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Purchase Order No. 6043220 issued to Integrated Building and Design Corp., originally awarded under Tender 346-2015 to carry out the necessary remedial and restoration work at Riverdale Park East. The Purchase Order Amendment being requested is an increase of $350,000 net of all taxes and charges ($356,160 net of HST recoveries). The amendment is required due to unforeseen site conditions encountered, specifically the complete replacement of the aged storm sewer system, and additional storm water management upgrades that directly impacted the timing and installation of a new refrigeration system for the artificial outdoor ice rink, as well as additional state of good repair work required for tennis court fencing, and site accessibility improvements. Funds have been identified in the 2018 Capital Budget approved by City Council…

What was decided

The Government Management Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Financial Control By-law) granted authority to amend Purchase Order No. 6043220, to carry out the necessary remedial and restoration work at Riverdale Park East in accordance with the requirements of Tender 346-2015, by an additional amount of $350,000 net of all taxes and charges ($356,160 net of HST recoveries), adjusting the current contract value from $5,381,000 net of all taxes and charges ($5,475,706 net of HST recoveries) to $5,731,000 net of all taxes and charges ($5,831,866 net of HST recoveries).
