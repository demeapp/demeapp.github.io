Official title: Opportunities to Incorporate Social Procurement Provisions Into a Contract for Electrical Services

The official record

What the item proposed

At its January 23, 2017 meeting, Government Management Committee adopted GM17.8 "Additional Reporting on Contracts Awarded during the 2016 Summer Recess Period" and requested that staff report back regarding opportunities to incorporate provisions of the Social Procurement Policy into a contract with Kudlak-Baird (1982) Ltd. for electrical services. Social procurement provisions cannot be incorporated into the City's existing blanket contract with Kudlak-Baird without the mutual consent of both parties. City staff engaged Kudlak-Baird and presented a range of actions that the company could take to implement components of the Social Procurement Program. Following this meeting, City staff determined that this contract does not align with the Social Procurement Program's objective to create meaningful employment opportunities that meet the program's workforce development criteria.…

What was decided

The Government Management Committee received the report (May 17, 2018) from the General Manager, Facilities Management and the Chief Purchasing Officer for information.
