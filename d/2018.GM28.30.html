The official record

What the item proposed

The French Language Advisory Committee at its meeting on May 15, 2018 considered FA6.1 -Establishment of a French-Language University in Toronto - Update

What was decided

City Council on June 26, 27, 28 and 29, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council affirm its support for the establishment, in Toronto, of the Université de l’Ontario français as established pursuant to the Université de l'Ontario français Act, 2017, SO 2017, c 34, Sch 43, as amended. 2. City Council request CreateTO to engage staff of the Université de l’Ontario français to determine any non-financial assistance, the City of Toronto may be able to provide in its establishment in Toronto. 3. City Council request the City Manager to include Government of Ontario staff and staff of the Université de l’Ontario français in future post-secondary institution sectoral collaboration and coordination initiatives undertaken by the City of Toronto.
