French Language Needs for Refugee Newcomers to Toronto

AdoptedJun 5, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The French Language Advisory Committee recommends that The Executive Director, Social Development, Finance and Administration, engage the Centre francophone de Toronto, La Passerelle-I.D.É., and other Francophone organizations, as appropriate.

The official record

What the item proposed

The French Language Advisory Committee at its meeting on May 15, 2018 considered FA6.3 - French Language Needs for Refugee Newcomers to Toronto.

What was decided

The Government Management Committee: 1. Requested the Executive Director, Social Development, Finance and Administration to engage the Centre francophone de Toronto, La Passerelle-I.D.É., and other Francophone organizations, as appropriate, to ensure that the linguistic needs of refugee newcomers from Montréal to Toronto are taken into account.
