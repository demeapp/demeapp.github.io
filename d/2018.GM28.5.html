What the item proposed

This report submits, for the Committee's information, a Funding Valuation Report prepared by Conduent HR Consulting as at December 31, 2017 on the Toronto Fire Department Superannuation and Benefit Fund (the Fund), which finances the pension plan (the Plan) and provides information on the automatic cost-of-living increase of 1.56 percent in pensioner benefits effective January 1, 2018, called for under By-law 1428-2017 governing the Plan and the Fund. By-law 1428-2017 contains a consolidation of former governing By-law 3349-96 and all its subsequent amending by-laws, and harmonizes the Plan text with a number of established administrative practices. On May 1, 2018, new provincial funding rules for defined-benefit pension plans came into effect.
