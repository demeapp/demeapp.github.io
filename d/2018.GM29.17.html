The official record

What the item proposed

Teranet Inc. (Teranet) is currently the City's authorized agent for the collection of the Municipal Land Transfer Tax (MLTT) using the Ontario Electronic Land Registration System under a service agreement that expires January 31, 2023. This report recommends that the agreement be extended for a 20-year term effective February 1, 2019 with a City option to renew it for another ten (10) years, subject to a modest service fee adjustment as described in the Confidential Attachment 2. Extending the agreement now, well in advance of the termination date, is recommended to provide long term cost predictability at rates that have declined significantly as a share of tax proceeds since introduction of the tax in 2008.

What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the Chief Financial Officer, in consultation with the City Solicitor, to negotiate and execute an extension of the agreement with Teranet Inc. for the collection of Municipal Land Transfer Tax for a term of 20 years with City option to renew for a further ten (10) years, effective February 1, 2019 on financial terms substantially as outlined in Confidential Attachment 2 to the report (June 18, 2018) from the Interim Chief Financial Officer and upon such other terms as may be acceptable to the Chief Financial Officer. 2.
