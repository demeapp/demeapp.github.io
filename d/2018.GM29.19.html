What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Purchase Order No. 6045978 issued following award of tender No. 234-2017 to Phoenix Restoration for the restoration of the perimeter wall at Casa Loma, 1 Austin Terrace. The recommended amendment is to include restoration of the perimeter wall at the Stables, across the street from the main castle. The total Purchase Order Amendment being requested is for an additional amount of $930,200 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($946,572 net of HST recoveries). The requested amendment is required to enable Economic Development and Culture to add an alternative price item originally included in Tender 234-2017. Competitive pricing was received on the alternative price item, however, this item was originally excluded due to budgetary limitations at the time of the award. What was decided

The Government Management Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Finance Control By-Law) granted authority to amend Purchase Order 6045978 issued to Phoenix Restoration, for the restoration of the perimeter wall at Casa Loma, by an additional amount of $930,200 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($946,572 net of HST recoveries) revising the current contract value from $1,730,700 to $2,660,900 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($2,707,732 net of HST recoveries).
