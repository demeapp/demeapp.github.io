What the item proposed

This report responds to City Council direction from July 04, 2017 report CC31.24, for the Deputy City Manager to report back to the Government Management Committee on the overall financial implications of the following transaction, prior to finalizing any purchase or lease agreements for either property: the City acquisition of Remicorp Industries Inc. ("ML Ready Mix") owned 29 Judson Street and the Toronto Port Lands Company ("TPLC") lease out of a portion of 545 Commissioners Street to ML Ready Mix. ML Ready Mix currently owns and operates a legal nonconforming concrete batching facility in South Etobicoke located at 29 Judson Street. These operations are adjacent to multiple residential and other sensitive uses which have resulted in inherent land use conflicts and ongoing community concerns.
