Toronto City Council · Decision

Exploring Active Transportation Options in City Facilities and Maintenance

AdoptedJul 3, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Government Management Committee was asked to request the Director of the Environment and Energy Division and General Manager of Fleet Services, in consultation with relevant City Divisions to explore the use of cargo bikes for use in City operations.

The official record

What the item proposed

As identified in TransformTO, transportation accounts for about one-third of the greenhouse gas emissions in Toronto and is also the primary local source of emissions that contribute to air pollution. Thus, a commitment to active transportation and other low emissions sources is key to achieving our emissions reduction targets as a city and is reflective of Toronto's TransformTO climate action strategy approved unanimously by Council in July 2017. The City of Toronto, as a corporation, is demonstrating shown strong leadership by making significant progress in reducing emissions from its operations through actions that reduce the release of methane into the atmosphere, implementing energy efficiency retrofits in City buildings, installing renewable energy systems and implementation of the City's Green Fleet Plan. The City encourages employees to use active and low emission transportation…

What was decided

The Government Management Committee: 1. Requested the Director, Environment and Energy and the General Manager, Fleet Services, in consultation with relevant City Divisions, to explore the use of cargo bikes for use in City operations and maintenance, including the possibility of pilot programs using local cargo bike manufacturers, and report to the Government Management Committee in the first quarter of 2019.
