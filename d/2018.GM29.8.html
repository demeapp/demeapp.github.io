Amendment to Agreement with Osler, Hoskin & Harcourt LLP for Legal Services Related to Pension and Benefits Issues

AdoptedJul 3, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Treasurer, the City Solicitor and the Chief Purchasing Officer recommends that The Government Management Committee, in accordance with Section 71-11.1C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Finance Control By-Law) authorize the City Solicitor to amend the retainer.

The official record

What the item proposed

As part of the City of Toronto's ongoing efforts to merge the city-sponsored pension plans into the Ontario Municipal Employees Retirement System (OMERS), the law firm of Osler, Hoskin & Harcourt LLP (Osler) was retained in 2012, with an expected completion of services date of April 1, 2017, in the amount of $150,000. As the required regulations to facilitate these mergers were not released by the provincial government until November 2015, Osler's services were further required and Council authority was obtained in May 2017, extending that date to April 1, 2020 and a $300,000 amendment to the contract was also made, for a total of $450,000. At present, the membership of four (4) of the city-sponsored plans have either formally consented to a merger with OMERS, or have given their approval to the allocation of surplus, should it exist, after a proposed merger. What was decided

The Government Management Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Finance Control By-Law) authorized the City Solicitor to amend the retainer with Osler, Hoskin & Harcourt LLP, by increasing the value from $450,000 to $900,000, net of all applicable taxes and charges, and increase the value on Purchaser Purchase Order 6035311 by the same amount.
