The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides the Board of Health (BOH) with an overview of its current formal and informal committees. The BOH is asked to appoint new members on the BOH Budget and the Performance Appraisal of the Medical Officer of Health Committees and approve the 2018 recommended appointments to the Toronto Urban Health Fund Review Panel and the Toronto Food Policy Council. In addition to the committee appointments, the BOH is also requested to appoint a public member of the BOH to the Association of Local Public Health Agencies (alPHa) Board of Directors and Board of Health Section, and to encourage participation of all BOH members at alPHa meetings and conferences held in Toronto in 2018.

What was decided

The Board of Health: 1. Appointed the Board of Health members below to the following Committees for a term of office ending on November 30, 2018, and until successors are appointed: a. Board of Health Budget Committee: - Councillor Joe Cressy - Councillor Sarah Doucette - Councillor Joe Mihevc - Councillor Neethan Shan - Angela Jonsson - Paul Nagpal b. Board of Health Performance Appraisal of the Medical Officer of Health Committee: - Councillor Sarah Doucette - Councillor Joe Mihevc - Councillor Neethan Shan - Elisa Levi 2. Appointed Stacey Berry to the Association of Local Public Health Agencies' (alPHa) Board of Directors and Board of Health (BOH) Section, and agreed to pay expenses incurred by the BOH-appointed representative when participating in the 2018 meetings and when attending alPHa's semi-annual meetings/conferences in 2018, within the approved 2018 budget. 3.
