Re-affirming City of Toronto as a Nuclear Weapons-Free Zone

AdoptedApr 24, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council, on November 7, 8, and 9, 2017, adopted Item MM34.35, and in so doing, has requested the Board of Health to hold public hearings on the dangers of nuclear weapons and radiation fallout. No new decision is attached.
