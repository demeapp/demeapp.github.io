The official record

What the item proposed

The Board of Health - Performance Appraisal of the Medical Officer of Health Committee met on March 5, 2018 to consider the 2017 Performance Planner and the 2018 Work Plan for the Medical Officer of Health. The above recommendations are forwarded to the Board of Health for its consideration.

What was decided

The Board of Health: 1. Approved Confidential Recommendations 1 and 2 in Confidential Attachment 1, appended to the letter (March 5, 2018) from the Board of Health - Performance Appraisal of the Medical Officer of Health Committee; and 2. Directed that Confidential Recommendation 2 from the Board of Health - Performance Appraisal of the Medical Officer of Health Committee be made public once adopted, and that Confidential Recommendation 1, the 2017 Performance Planner, and the 2018 Work Plan, as amended, for the Medical Officer of Health remain confidential, as they contain personal information about an identifiable individual, including municipal or local board employees.
