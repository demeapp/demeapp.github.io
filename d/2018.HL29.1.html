The official record

What the item proposed

Chronic diseases, such as cancer and heart disease, are the leading cause of death in Toronto. Designing the built environment in ways that promote health can reduce risk factors for these diseases. This includes design that increases opportunities for physical activity, healthy eating, and social cohesion. To demonstrate how changes to the built environment can promote health and well-being, Toronto Public Health (TPH) was awarded $2.3 million in funding over five years to support the Healthy by Design: Active Apartment Neighbourhoods Project from the Public Health Agency of Canada (PHAC). A requirement of the funding was to develop, through community engagement and partnership with a number of academic, private, and non-profit organizations, new site designs for apartment towers that included features such as a sports court, playground, and vegetable gardens.
