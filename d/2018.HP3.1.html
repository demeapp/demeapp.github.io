What the item proposed

Committee members will review the Medical Officer of Health's 2017 Performance Planner and 2018 Work Plan.

What was decided

The Board of Health - Performance Appraisal of the Medical Officer of Health Committee recommends that: 1. The Board of Health approve Confidential Recommendations 1 and 2 of the Board of Health - Performance Appraisal of the Medical Officer of Health Committee. 2. The Board of Health direct that Confidential Recommendation 2 of the Board of Health - Performance Appraisal of the Medical Officer of Health Committee be made public once adopted, and that Confidential Recommendation 1, the 2017 Performance Planner, and the 2018 Work Plan, as amended, for the Medical Officer of Health remain confidential, as they contain personal information about an identifiable individual, including municipal or local board employees.
