What the item proposed

Councillor Janet Davis has submitted the following Administrative Inquiry: "I am submitting this Administrative Inquiry (under Municipal Code S27-61) to seek information regarding the status the negotiations on our shared-use agreements with the Toronto District School Board, many of which expired in 2002. At its July 2006 meeting, City Council authorized the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation to execute a Master Shared Use Lease Agreement with the Toronto District School Board for the exclusive use of space within Toronto District School Board facilities. What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, referred Administrative Inquiry IA38.1 from Councillor Janet Davis, Ward 31, Beaches-East York regarding the Status of the Negotiations on Shared-use Agreements with the Toronto District School Board and Answer IA38.1a from the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, to the Community Development and Recreation Committee for consideration.
