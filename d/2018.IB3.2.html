What the item proposed

This report provides the Toronto Investment Board ("Board") with information on the operation of the City of Toronto's Sinking Funds which are used for repayment of the City's debentures (debt). The City's Investment Policy requires a staff report to the Board at the beginning of the year to inform and recommend the course of action with regards to cash outflows (to retire maturing debt) and inflows (contributions, investment coupons and maturities). The report informs the Board that cash and short-term investments (less than 12 months) in the 4 percent sub-fund be made available, as required by the Investment Policy, to make payments to the Canadian Depository for Securities (CDS) for two maturing City of Toronto debentures totaling $425 million in 2018 and expenses related to making these transfers. What was decided

The Toronto Investment Board directed staff, in their capacity as interim managers for the Board, to take the following actions: 1. Transfer the cash (including sinking fund contributions, maturing investments and coupon interest) accumulations in the 2 percent, 2.5 percent, and 3.5 percent sub-funds to the respective Short-Term Sinking Funds for investment on an interim basis until the Board is in a position to direct long term investment of the funds. 2. Invest the cash accumulations (including sinking fund contributions, maturing investments and coupon interest) in the 5 percent Sinking Fund and invest in longer-term fixed income securities matched to the one remaining payment obligation maturing in July 2021 in accordance with the Council-approved Investment Policy.
