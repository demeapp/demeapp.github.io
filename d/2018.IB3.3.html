Establishment of a Personal Trading Policy

Referred back for more workFeb 5, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend: The Investment Board develop and implement appropriate policies, such as a personal trading policy, to enhance the protection, and proper use.

Council sent it back for more work.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council requested the Integrity Commissioner, to review the Code of Conduct for Members of Local Boards (Restricted Definition) to recommend if amendments were necessary to reflect the special duties of the Investment Board. The Integrity Commissioner recommends that the Toronto Investment Board establish a personal trading policy for its board members and staff supporting the Board to ensure that the Board is meeting best practices of boards with similar mandates. The Integrity Commissioner makes this recommendation in conjunction with recommendations to City Council to amend the Code of Conduct for Members of Local Boards (Restricted Definition) (EX30.17 - January 10, 2018), which also applies to members of the Investment Board. The Integrity Commissioner will not be recommending that City Council establish separate, unique codes of conduct for all of its several local boards.…

What was decided

The Toronto Investment Board: 1. Referred the item to Corporate Finance staff with the request that staff consult with the Integrity Commissioner and submit a draft policy for the Board's review.
