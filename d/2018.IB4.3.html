What the item proposed

I write to follow-up on my presentation to the Toronto Investment Board of Management on November 6, 2017. It was a pleasure to meet with the Board last year to discuss how the Office of the Integrity Commissioner can be a resource to the Board and its members. As part of my ongoing mandate to provide education to local boards, I am enclosing a copy of the Office's Annual Report for 2017. It includes select sample advice to members of Council and the City's local boards as well select summaries of complaints that I believe will help members understand their Code of Conduct obligations. I would appreciate it if you could include this letter and a copy of the report on an upcoming agenda of your Board so that it can be received for information. In addition, all of the Office's reports to Council and anonymized reports to local boards are available on the Integrity Commissioner's website…

What was decided

The Toronto Investment Board: 1. Received the 2017 Annual Report of the Office of the Integrity Commissioner for information.
