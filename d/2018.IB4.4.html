The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides the Toronto Investment Board ("Board") with information on the budgeted earned investment income required by the City of Toronto's Long Term Fund. This earned investment income is used to fund the City's Operating Budget as well as allocations to the Reserve Funds. The City Council-approved Investment Policy (Section 6.1) requires a staff report to the Toronto Investment Board following the approval of the budget, to inform the Toronto Investment Board of the amount required and the timing with regards to cash outflows to the Short Term Fund.

What was decided

The Toronto Investment Board: 1. Received the report (March 20, 2018) from the Acting Executive Director, Corporate Finance for information.
