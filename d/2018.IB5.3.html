The official record

What the item proposed

The Infrastructure Specialist, Aon will provide a presentation that explains Infrastructure as an asset class. Various characteristics of the sectors, revenue models, diversification considerations, and various ways to invest (portfolio build-out) will be included in this presentation.

What was decided

The Toronto Investment Board referred the item to the Acting Executive Director, Corporate Finance to work with Aon and report back to the next meeting of the Board with specific examples on investing in the infrastructure sector including timelines.
