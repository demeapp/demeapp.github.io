The official record

What the item proposed

The Real Estate Specialist from Aon will provide a presentation that explains real estate as an asset class. The presentation will cover the strategic role of real estate, diversification benefits, institutional overview and access, along with two examples of real estate investments for institutions.

What was decided

The Toronto Investment Board: 1. Directed staff to provide an analysis of the Real Asset allocation in the Council-approved Investment Policy once the relevant information is received.
