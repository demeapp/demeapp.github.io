Asset Mix - Real Assets

The official record

What the item proposed

The Council-approved Investment Policy allows for a targeted 10 percent allocation to Real Assets category with breakdown between the sub-categories of Real Estate, and Infrastructure. The purpose of this report is to consider the information on Real Assets provided by the investment consultant (Aon), and for the Toronto Investment Board (the Board) to decide on next steps.

What was decided

The Toronto Investment Board: 1. Directed staff to invest the 10 percent of the overall portfolio allocated to Real Assets in other liquid asset categories in order to maintain future flexibility to invest in Real Assets, provided the guidelines of the Council-approved Investment Policy are maintained.
