The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an update on changes to the Payday Loans Act and the City of Toronto Act (COTA) as they relate to payday loan establishments. It recommends using the new authorities to create a licence for payday loan establishments, and to cap the number of stores to those that currently operate in Toronto and are licensed by the Province of Ontario, as of May 1, 2018. This report further recommends Municipal Licensing and Standards (MLS) conduct broad consultation and research on payday lenders and report back to the Licensing and Standards Committee in one year with final recommendations on regulations for the industry and any adjustments to the cap on City licences. In 2013, the Province of Ontario began reviewing its regulation of payday lending. As a result of this review, the provincial government gave municipalities the authority to regulate payday loan establishments.…
