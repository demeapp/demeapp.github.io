Licensing and Standards Committee considers a request for changes to Firework Regulations.

Official title: Changes to Firework Regulations

Adopted with changesApr 10, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Please accept this letter to add a new item of business to the April 2018 Licensing and Standards Committee Agenda which asks staff to report on the feasibility of modifying the firework permit regulations to add Labour Day and New Year's Eve celebrations to the list of events requiring a temporary vendor's permit to purchase fireworks and exempt firework permits for Diwali, Labour Day and New Year's Eve celebrations.

What was decided

The Licensing and Standards Committee: 1. Requested the Fire Chief and General Manager, Toronto Fire Services, and the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards, to report back to the Licensing and Standards Committee at its meeting on June 14, 2018 regarding: a. Exempting residents from obtaining a firework permit to use fireworks on their own property to celebrate the following celebrations: the Diwali holidays, Labour Day, New Year's Eve, Eastern Orthodox Easter, Noruz Iranian New Year, and other appropriate cultural or religious celebrations; and b. Expanding the days that temporary fireworks vendors can sell fireworks to include seven days before Labour Day and New Year's Eve.
