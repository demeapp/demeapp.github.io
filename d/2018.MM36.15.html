What the item proposed

The applicant, Queen Developments Inc., is proposing a City block redevelopment bound by Shuter Street to the north and Queen Street East to the south between Mutual and Dalhousie Street. In 2005 Council enacted both Official Plan and Zoning amendments (By-laws 179-2005 and 180-2005) relating to the development. The overall development is proceeding in two phases with Phase I corresponding to the Site Plan Application for 30 and 50 Mutual Street (15 270190 STE 27 SA). The applicant is seeking Official Plan and Zoning amendments relating to what will be Phase II of the development, which correspond to Zoning Application (16 165239 STE 27 OZ) and Official Plan Amendment (17 257006 STE 27 OZ) for 88 Queen Street East.
