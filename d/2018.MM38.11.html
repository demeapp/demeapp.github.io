The official record

What the item proposed

On December 10, 2015, City Council appointed Kevin Garland to the Board of Civic Theatres Toronto for a term of office ending November 30, 2018. Ms. Garland has recently accepted the position of Executive Director of Soulpepper Theatre Company on an interim basis until a permanent Executive Director is put in place following the sudden departure of its Artistic Director and Executive Director. In establishing Civic Theatres Toronto as a local board, City Council approved the following additional eligibility criteria: "Citizens are ineligible for appointment, and may not continue to serve as a member of the Board, if any of the following conditions apply to them or their immediate family members: - the person is a supplier of goods or services to the City's theatre facilities; - the person has an employment relationship with another performing arts theatre; - the person has an…

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council temporarily rescind the appointment of Kevin Garland to the Board of Directors of Civic Theatres Toronto while she is employed with Soulpepper Theatre and that City Council re-appoint Kevin Garland to complete her current term, effective when Ms. Garland gives notice of that termination of employment in writing to the City Clerk.
