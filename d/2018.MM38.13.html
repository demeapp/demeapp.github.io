The official record

What the item proposed

Last year, residents raised concerns that some City communications, while translated into multiple languages, were not translated into French. In a City as diverse as Toronto, I strongly support City communications being translated into many languages, to ensure our residents have access to important services. French, as an Official Language, should also be one of those languages into which City materials are translated. Requires Re-opening Item EX25.3 - May 24, 25 and 26, 2017 City Council meeting, only as it pertains to French language services.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the City Manager to review the existing Multilingual Information Provisions Policy to determine whether the inclusion of French in City services is adequately addressed, and if not, to make recommendations through the Executive Committee, on changes that may be required. 2. City Council request the City Manager to consult with the City's Council Advisory Bodies in any future review of the City of Toronto's Multilingual Information Provisions Policy.
