The official record

What the item proposed

Recently we have seen strengthened economic, business and cultural relationships between China and Canada at all levels of government. 2018 has been deemed the year of Canada-China tourism. Both Premier Kathleen Wynne and Mayor John Tory have led trade missions to China in recent years resulting in significant economic investment to Ontario and Toronto, as well as job growth. Tianjin is one of the four national central cities of the People's Republic of China with a population of over 15 million people and is the world's sixth most populous city proper. Like Toronto, Tianjin City is a waterfront centre of trade and finance and as such, is often characterized as a leader in the area of politics, economics and culture. Friendship agreements between Toronto and other international cities have been successfully implemented and leveraged for mutually beneficial economic and cultural gains.…

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture to undertake the necessary work to determine the feasibility and economic advantage for Toronto to enter into a Friendship City agreement with Tianjin, China and to report back to the Economic Development Committee in the first quarter of 2019. 2. City Council request the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture to include in the report back in the first quarter of 2019, an analysis of other cities in the region that have, or wish to have, a Sister City, Friendship City, or Memorandum of Understanding with Toronto, such report to also include an evaluation of each city, and identify and prioritize those cities that are best placed to contribute to our international Economic Development Strategy.
