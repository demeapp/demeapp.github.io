The official record

What the item proposed

On Thursday, March 8, 2018, the Etobicoke York Panel of the Committee of Adjustment refused two applications for minor variances for the property municipally known as 1982 Islington Avenue West. This site is located in the Natural Heritage System, is subject to the regulations of Toronto and Regional Conservation Authority, the Ravine By-law and the Private Tree By-law and is occupied by a listed heritage building and requested variances to the Zoning By-law to allow for the proposed office use, residential use, building height and parking. In the March 1, 2018, reports prepared for each of applications from the Director, Community Planning, Etobicoke York District, Community Planning staff recommended refusal of the applications as the applications did not maintain the general intent and purpose of the Official Plan or Zoning By-law and the requested variances were not minor or…

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the City Solicitor, along with appropriate City Staff, to attend the Toronto Local Appeal Body Hearing to uphold the decision of the Committee of Adjustment with respect to applications A0994/17EYK (Part1) and A0993/17EYK (Part 2) regarding 1982 Islington Avenue. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to retain outside consultants, as necessary.
