The official record

What the item proposed

City Council must address upcoming vacancies in the positions of City Manager and Deputy City Manager. The attached reports have been prepared to address these vacancies. This Motion is urgent as City Council must deal with these vacancies before the end of March 2018.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council appoint Giuliana Carbone, effective April 4, 2018, as the Interim City Manager for the City of Toronto, reporting to Council, with all the powers and duties imposed upon the City Manager by Municipal Code Chapter 169, Officials, and as chief administrative officer under Section 140 of the City of Toronto Act, 2006. 2. City Council repeal By-law 463-2015, "To Appoint Peter Wallace as City Manager"effective April 4, 2018. 3. City Council request the Interim City Manager to ensure that an Acting Deputy City Manager for Cluster A is in place effective April 4, 2018 and until City Council appoints a permanent City Manager. 4.
