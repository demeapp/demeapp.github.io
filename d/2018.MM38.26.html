The official record

What the item proposed

The Annex is a neighbourhood located in the heart of downtown Toronto, and is home to families, youth, and residents of all ages. The community also continues to face growing development pressure, and increased need for amenity space to serve all residents in the community. In 2015, the City of Toronto began consultations to revitalize Huron Street Playground, a well-used and large greenspace in the community. Although officially designated as a playground, the space measures roughly 2,048 square metres, is well used by the community for play uses, passive recreation, and as an unofficial dogs-off-leash area. Roughly 370 square metres is currently used as the playground itself. After a robust community consultation process that included visiting students at the local Public School, community meetings, and more, a newly revitalized space was unveiled in 2017. What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation to work in consultation with the local Councillor on the construction of an enclosed dogs-off-leash area in the Huron Street Playground, and to hold a community consultation meeting in the second quarter of 2018 to present designs in keeping with the design of the Phase 1 park enhancement's long-term vision for a dogs-off-leash area.
