Council considers designating 68 Daisy Avenue as a heritage property.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of October 2, 3, and 4, 2017, Toronto City Council adopted Item EY24.5 stating its intention to designate the property at 68 Daisy Avenue, originally known as the Daisy Avenue Public School and now known as the Vincent Massey Childcare Centre, under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act for its cultural heritage value. At its meeting of July 4, 5, 6 and 7, 2017 City Council adopted item CC31.22, which authorized the City Solicitor, together with other appropriate staff, to attend at the Ontario Municipal Board in support of the Zoning By-law Amendment application for 68 Daisy Avenue as revised and described in the report (June 29, 2017) from the City Solicitor and as set out in Confidential Appendices 1 and 2 to the report (June 29, 2017) from the City Solicitor, subject to the applicant agreeing to accept the Settlement Terms as set out in Confidential Appendix…

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend its decision on Item EY24.5 adopted October 2, 3, and 4, 2017, to revise the heritage attributes in the Statement of Significance (East Addition - 1956) attached as Attachment 3 to the report (February 28, 2017) from the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, by removing reference to "the projecting end extension at the east with its windows on the east and north elevation with their four vertical sections of glazing."
