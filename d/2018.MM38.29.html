What the item proposed

On November 4, 2015, a Zoning By-law Amendment application was submitted for the site at 71 to 73 Redpath Avenue, 174 to 200 Soudan Avenue and 18 Brownlow Avenue. The application proposed two residential towers of 25 and 20 storeys linked by a 4-storey base building including 463 new rental dwelling units and 269 new residential parking spaces in an underground garage. On February 28, 2017, a revised proposal was submitted revising the tower heights to 24-storeys each. The revised proposal reduced the number of new residential units to 369 and new parking spaces to 274. The Applicant has since submitted a further revised Zoning By-law Amendment application to the City changing the boundaries of the application and reducing the site size.
