The official record

What the item proposed

Our downtown is made up of vibrant neighbourhoods, home to hundreds of thousands of residents. 240,000 people currently live downtown, a number that is expected to reach 500,000 in the next 25 years. The City has established the downtown core as a mixed-use neighbourhood where people live, work and play. Every day, we work hard to build a more liveable downtown – to provide the services, facilities and infrastructure people need to call their neighbourhoods home. At the same time, the weekday population of downtown grows to more than 900,000 people. With one-third of all the jobs in the City located downtown, alongside a number of universities and colleges, it is vital for the overall economy that people can move in and out of the downtown core easily. What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council re-affirm that non-emergency utility work should not take place overnight in downtown. 2. City Council direct that in circumstances where overnight work is necessary, appropriate consultation with the Ward Councillor and notification to local residents and stakeholders in advance will be required, to the satisfaction of the General Manager, Transportation Services. 3. City Council re-affirm that the new policy for utility work in the roadway in the area bounded by Jarvis Street, Bathurst Street, Dundas Street and Lake Shore Boulevard/Harbour Street applies only to major and minor arterial roadways and not to non-arterial roadways.
