2183 Lake Shore Boulevard West - Site Plan Conditions - by Councillor Justin J. Di Ciano, seconded by Councillor Michael Thompson

Councillor Justin J. Di Ciano, seconded by Councillor Michael Thompson, asks City Council to council delete clause 9(b) of the Post Approval Conditions in Schedule C of the site plan agreement for 2183 Lake Shore Boulevard West.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of December 16, 2013, City Council directed staff to attend at the Ontario Municipal Board in support of applications for an Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment filed by the owner, Empire Communities (2183 Lake Shore Blvd.) Ltd., for the property known municipally as 2183 Lake Shore Boulevard West. http://app.toronto.ca/tmmis/viewAgendaItemHistory.do?item=2013.EY29.5 The Official Plan Amendment and the Zoning By-law Amendment have been approved and a s. 37 agreement has been entered into between the City and the owner and registered on title. The only outstanding matter is the finalization of the site plan agreement. There is one condition in the site plan agreement which should be deleted. That is, the requirement for free visitor parking. Lack of visitor parking in that area is a real problem. If visitor parking is free, it could lead to abuses by people…

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. Council delete clause 9(b) of the Post Approval Conditions in Schedule C of the site plan agreement for 2183 Lake Shore Boulevard West and replace it with the following: “9(b) The Owner agrees that it will not designate any visitor parking stalls for the exclusive use of commercial owner(s)/tenant(s).”
