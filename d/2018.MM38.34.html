The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of January 31 and February 1, 2018, City Council directed the City Manager and the Acting Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning to report to the March 26, 27 and 28, 2018 meeting of City Council on the measures that are in place to ensure Toronto Hydro will fulfill its commitment to comply with the North York Centre Secondary Plan with particular regard for the maximum density provisions. This Motion is urgent as it responds to the above-noted City Council direction and advises of discussions held between City staff and Toronto Hydro staff for its property at 5800 Yonge Street. This report was prepared by City Planning and Legal Services, in consultation with the City Manager's Office.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff as set out in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 23, 2018) from the City Solicitor and the Acting Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning. 2. City Council direct that the confidential instructions to staff and the confidential information in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 23, 2018) from the City Solicitor and the Acting Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning remain confidential in their entirety, at the discretion of the City Solicitor, as they contain advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
