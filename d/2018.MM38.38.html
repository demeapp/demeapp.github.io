No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Several senior positions in the Toronto public service are vacant and are being filled on a temporary basis. These vacancies include the City Manager, the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, Chief Financial Officer, Deputy City Manager, Chief Building Official and Executive Director, CEO of TTC and President of the Toronto Parking Authority and many others in other important positions. While we know that others have assumed these positions on an acting basis, Councillors are concerned about filling these positions on a permanent basis. None of us have received information about the status of the recruitment and selection processes underway. In the absence of the City Manager, it is unclear who is involved and leading these processes.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the City Manager to report to the April 17, 2018 Executive Committee meeting, on the status of processes to fill the vacancies of senior staff at the City and its agencies.
