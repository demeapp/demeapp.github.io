Staff recommend: City Council increase the Approved 2018 Capital Budget for Shelter, Support.

The official record

What the item proposed

The City is working with Fife House Foundation Inc. (Fife House), a non-profit organization providing safe housing for individuals living with HIV/AIDS, to acquire and renovate the property at 9 Huntley Street for the purpose of creating new transitional housing. The City will purchase the property and enter into a 20 year lease agreement with Fife House. Funding for the acquisition and renovation is composed of a combination of Federal, Provincial and City funding, along with fundraising efforts by Fife House. City Council at its meeting on December 5, 6, 7 and 8, 2017 (EX29.13 Creating Transitional Housing at 9 Huntley Street) approved $500,000 of Section 37 funding to be advanced to Fife House for renovation costs. An additional $113,135 has become available and is recommended to be provided to Fife House to supplement fundraising efforts for the 9 Huntley Street renovation.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council increase the Approved 2018 Capital Budget for Shelter, Support and Housing Administration, account CHS045-01, by $113,135 gross, $0 net, fully funded by Section 37 (Planning Act Reserve Funds) community benefits from 43, 49, 51 Gerrard Street West and 695 Bay Street (Source Account XR3026-3700691) for the purpose of providing funds to Fife House Foundation Inc. for capital improvements to 9 Huntley Street. 2. City Council authorize the Director, Affordable Housing Office to provide $113,135 from the Section 37 (Planning Act Reserve Funds), referred to in Part 1 above, to Fife House Foundation Inc., subject to Fife House Foundation Inc. signing an assignment of the municipal capital facility agreement, governing the use of the funds and the financial reporting requirements.
