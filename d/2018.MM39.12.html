The official record

What the item proposed

On August 21 and 22, 2018 the future of 1,200 acres of cemetery trust parklands known as 'Mount Pleasant Group of Cemeteries' are at stake. Mount Pleasant Group of Cemeteries currently claims to be a privately-owned cemetery, but there is strong evidence suggesting it is a historic public trust created by statute of the legislature of Upper Canada in 1826, which continues to be governed by that legislation. Although the legislation governing the public trust is Provincial, the loss of this green space would have a significant impact on Toronto residents. A group of concerned citizens has formed a corporation called 'Friends of Toronto Public Cemeteries'.
