What the item proposed

A variance application to allow for the alteration of an existing fifteen-storey residential building by converting indoor amenity space into two dwelling units for a total of 236 residential apartment dwelling units was before the Committee of Adjustment, Toronto and East York Panel (the "Committee"), on March 28, 2018. This application included variances for the proposed number of dwelling units and parking spaces as well as the amount and configuration of amenity space. City Planning staff had written a report dated March 26, 2018 (the "Planning Staff Report") to the Committee recommending that certain conditions with respect to the proposed amenity space be imposed by the Committee on any approval granted. What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council confirm the City Solicitor's appeal of the Committee of Adjustment's decision to approve the minor variances related to file A1132/17TEY in order to enable certain conditions to be imposed on any approval. 2.
