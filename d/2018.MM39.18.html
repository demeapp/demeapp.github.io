What the item proposed

A variance application to allow for the alteration of an existing two-storey residential building with five dwelling units was before the Committee of Adjustment, Toronto and East York Panel (the "Committee"), on March 21, 2018. The proposal included the addition of two new dwelling units, a complete third storey addition, a new front entrance and terrace and a below grade terrace with exterior stairs on the east side; with the existing rear parking area to be retained and used for seven vehicular parking spaces, eight bicycle parking spaces and a new ancillary building for garbage and recycling storage.
