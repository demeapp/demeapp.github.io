What the item proposed

On February 28, 2018, the Committee of Adjustment refused a variance application brought by the owner of 1984 Queen Street East to alter the existing one-storey commercial building on site by constructing rear and west side ground floor additions (Application No. A1017/17TEY). Variances from the applicable Zoning By-law were sought for bicycle parking spaces, parking spaces, rear yard setback, minimum required setback from the curb and maximum ground floor height. The owner appealed the refusal to the Toronto Local Appeal Body. In a report from the Acting Director, Community Planning, Toronto and East York District (February 22, 2018) Community Planning Staff opposed the variance for rear yard setback, which Staff believe does not conform with the general intent of the applicable Zoning By-law and the Official Plan as amended.
