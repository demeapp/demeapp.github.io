The official record

What the item proposed

As part of the Lawrence-Allen Revitalization Plan, the Toronto Community Housing Corporation has begun their development to construct 233 units of affordable housing at municipal addresses listed in this Motion that meets Council's exemption policies for non-profit housing and is exempt from planning application fees, building fees, municipal development charges and parkland dedication requirements that would otherwise apply. In accordance with City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks, Use Of, Toronto Community Housing Corporation is not exempt from applicable Transportation Services' application and permit fees during construction (including construction access, tower crane, hoarding and covered walkway etc.). The approximate fees for 6 months is $140,000.00.
