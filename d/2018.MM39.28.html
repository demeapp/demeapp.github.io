The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this Motion is to request the application of permanent underspending from the Capital Budget for Economic Development and Culture to an urgent new project at the Toronto Centre for the Arts. The Toronto Centre for the Arts, built in 1993, requires a Building Condition Assessment to aid in future capital planning. To date, a Building Condition Assessment has not been done at this facility. The reallocation of this funding at this time will fulfil this need on a time line that will allow consideration of the resulting report as part of the 2019 Capital Budget process. Funding is available for this project through savings on roofing work at the St. Lawrence Centre for the Arts. All the planned 2018 roofing work at St. Lawrence Centre for the Arts will be completed by October 1, 2018.
