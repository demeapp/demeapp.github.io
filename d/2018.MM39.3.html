The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting held on March 26 and 27, 2018, City Council adopted Item MM38.33, that included Part 1, which authorized the City Solicitor to attend and oppose the appeal for 74 Royal York Road at the Toronto Local Appeal Body Hearing. The Application is not subject to site plan control and therefore revisions to the proposal are required to determine if the conditions detailed in the report from Community Planning and memorandum from Transportation Services, and any other such conditions as required, can be appropriately implemented through the proposed development. The purpose of this Motion is to amend and replace Part 1 of City Council's decision to give full force to City Council’s intent in Item MM38.33 as adopted. Requires Re-opening Motion MM38.33 - City Council March 26 and 27, 2018 only as it pertains to Part 1.

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council delete Part 1 of its decision on Item MM38.33. Part to be deleted: 1. City Council direct the City Solicitor and appropriate City staff to attend the Toronto Local Appeal Body in order to oppose this appeal unless the Toronto Local Appeal Body imposes the conditions of approval detailed in the report (January 8, 2018) from the Director, Community Planning, Etobicoke York District and the memorandum (January 3, 2018) from the Manager, Traffic Planning/Right of Way Management, Transportation Services, and any other such conditions that are deemed necessary or appropriate to the satisfaction of the City Solicitor, in consultation with the Acting Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, or designates, and the General Manager, Transportation Services, or designates, and the local Ward…
