The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of March 9, 2017, City Council adopted without amendments and without debate Item EY20.1. On July 7, 2017, City Council enacted Site Specific By-law 733-2017 (amending the former City of Etobicoke Zoning Code) permitting a five-storey apartment building containing 67 residential units for seniors. Accordingly, City Council is requested to approve a technical amendment to By-law 733-2017, by deleting the reference to "22 percent" in Section 8 and replacing it with "40 percent". This amendment will not result in a change to the proposed development, but is required to accurately reflect the proposal approved by City Council. All required notices were given pursuant to the Planning Act prior to enactment of By-law 733-2017.

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend Site Specific By-law 733-2017 by deleting the reference to "22 percent" in Section 8 and replacing it with "40 percent", substantially in accordance with Attachment 1: Draft Technical By-law Amendment to Site Specific By-law 733-2017 to the report (April 10, 2018) from the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning. 2. City Council determine that no further notice is required as the amendment in Part 1 above is technical in nature.
