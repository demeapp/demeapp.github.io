The official record

What the item proposed

The Principal of Greenwood College School approached our office regarding concerns with speeding and aggressive driving on Mount Pleasant Road (east roadway), between Balliol Street and Davisville Avenue, and indicated that the school would like traffic calming measures installed. Greenwood College School is the only property abutting this section of Mount Pleasant Road (east roadway). In order for traffic calming measures to be installed, the City of Toronto Traffic Calming Policy, as adopted by City Council, requires that a poll be conducted along the subject street, a minimum of 50 percent plus one of the affected households (with frontage or flankage) respond to that poll and at least 60 percent of the responding households vote in favour of the proposal. What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council waive the requirement under the City of Toronto Traffic Calming Policy that the City Clerk poll eligible households on Mount Pleasant Road (east roadway), between Balliol Street and Davisville Avenue, to determine whether residents support the installation of speed humps until such time as Toronto and East York Community Council has considered a request to install traffic calming on Mount Pleasant Road (east roadway) between Balliol Street and Davisville Avenue.
