What the item proposed

Since 2005, Canadian Tire Jumpstart Charities (Jumpstart) has broken down financial barriers to help get more Canadian kids into sport and play. Built on the premise that sports equip kids for life, Jumpstart has helped over 1.6 million kids between the ages of 4 to 18 get into the game. On September 19, 2017, Canadian Tire Corporation committed to help fundraise an incremental $50 million to help Jumpstart expand its mandate to address physical barriers and to become a champion of inclusive play. Through investments in destination playgrounds, infrastructure grants and funding for parasports, Jumpstart is on a mission to normalize sport and play to create a truly inclusive environment regardless of a child’s ability. What was decided

City Council on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation to accept an in-kind donation of a playground valued at $1.2 million from the Canadian Tire Jumpstart Charities, in compliance with the City's Policy on Donations for Community Benefits. 2. City Council authorize the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation to negotiate and sign donation and construction agreements, on terms and conditions satisfactory to the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor.
