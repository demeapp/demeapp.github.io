Extending Alcohol Sales and Services Hours during the FIFA World Cup Championship (World Cup 2018) - by Councillor Cesar Palacio, seconded by Councillor Frank Di Giorgio

AdoptedMay 22, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Cesar Palacio, seconded by Councillor Frank Di Giorgio, asks City Council to authorize the extension of alcohol sales and service hours in the City of Toronto to begin at 9:00 a.m. between June 14 and July.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The 2018 FIFA World Cup takes place in Russia, between June 14 and July 15, 2018. These events bring together Torontonians as a community. We are fortunate in Toronto to have such a great selection of restaurants, cafés and bars to come together and watch the games. An extension of alcohol sales and hours at Toronto establishments will provide a venue for Torontonians and an outlet for businesses to show their support as well. The time difference between Toronto and Russia has resulted in early morning match times, with games airing at 9:00 a.m. As a result, residents may wish to visit local cafés and restaurants earlier during the FIFA World Cup Championship 2018.

What was decided

City Council on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the extension of alcohol sales and service hours in the City of Toronto to begin at 9:00 a.m. between June 14 and July 15, 2018 to allow the public and local cafes and restaurants to enjoy and benefit from the FIFA World Cup Championship (World Cup 2018). 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to introduce a bill substantially as set out in Appendix 1 of Motion MM41.14.
