What the item proposed

This Motion seeks Council authority to release $410,042.57 in Section 37 funds to the Transportation Services Division for the purpose of funding streetscape and laneway improvements in the area near Trent Avenue, Kelvin Avenue and Luttrell Avenue in the Danforth East neighbourhood. At its meeting on December 5, 6, 7 and 8, 2017, City Council adopted Motion MM35.18 to, among other things, amend the Section 37 provisions of By-law 123-2016 to authorize that a cash payment of $400,000 (plus indexing and any accrued interest) be allocated to Transportation Services, Public Realm Section and to the Laneway Project for improvements to the public realm and laneways in the area near Trent Avenue, Kelvin Avenue and Luttrell Avenue.
