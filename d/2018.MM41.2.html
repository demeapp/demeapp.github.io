The official record

What the item proposed

On May 2, 2018, the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation published an article based on an in-camera report on Item CC39.10, Actions Taken to Date Arising from Council's Decision Respecting the Auditor General's Investigation into a Certain Land Transaction, which it claims they received through a source at City Hall. On May 2 and 3, 2018 Jennifer Pagliaro from the Toronto Star also published articles based on the same report which it claims it had obtained. During the public debate at City Council on April 26, 2018, the Mayor and myself had argued that CC39.10 should be made public. The City takes information security very seriously. It also goes to extreme measures to ensure confidentiality by printing individual Councillors' names across every page that is distributed as part of official staff reports. What was decided

City Council on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Integrity Commissioner, in consultation with the City Manager, to investigate the circumstances of a breach of confidentiality in regards to Item CC39.10 and to report back directly to City Council at the July 23, 24 and 25, 2018 meeting with their findings including the name or names of the Council and/or staff members responsible.
