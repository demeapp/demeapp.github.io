What the item proposed

Much thought, effort and consultation among stakeholders has gone into the field improvement project for Islington Junior Middle School as this is the only green space that is accessible to the entire community 7 days/week. The objective of the project is first and foremost to remedy the existing issues but also to make this a space that is visually appealing to the surrounding high rises and accessible to all community members. As such, a number of features have been incorporated into the design that honour both the school’s and the community’s needs (for example, outdoor classroom space, mindfulness labyrinth, quiet play areas, community garden, cricket pitch, basketball courts, asphalt games and additional green space in the form of tree islands added to our colas area.)
