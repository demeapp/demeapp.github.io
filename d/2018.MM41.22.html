What the item proposed

Bruce Junior Public School is located in the Leslieville and serves a diverse and growing community. It has been part of the neighbourhood since 1923 and offers instruction from Junior Kindergarten to Grade 8. They have a vibrant Early Years partnership with WoodGreen Community Services, providing seamless childcare and learning opportunities for families and children of all ages and backgrounds. In the last couple of years, the School Yard Revitalization Committee has been working with the Toronto District School Board to redesign the school's outdoor space in a manner that fosters creative learning, movement and connection to nature. Natural environments provide health benefits for all ages. Contact with nature can positively influence physical and mental health and well-being, and improve quality of life.
