The official record

What the item proposed

Section 45(9) funds associated with the development at 30 Old Mill Road have been secured and received by the City. This Motion is to release $51,944.64 of these funds and allocate them to the public heritage monument to be located at an appropriate site in a Ward 5 park. This motion is urgent to ensure the commissioning process can begin in 2018. The monument will be dedicated to Joshua Glover, a fugitive slave whose legal case advanced the abolition of slavery in the US. He made the journey to Canada in 1864 via the Underground Railroad, resided in Etobicoke, and was employed by the Montgomery family for whom Montgomery's Inn is named. He remained in Canada until his death in 1888. What was decided

City Council on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council increase the 2018 Approved Capital Budget for Economic Development and Culture by $51,944.64 gross, $0 net (CAC116-10), fully funded by Section 45 funds obtained in the development at 30 Old Mill Road (XR3028-4500092) for the purpose of completing the selection, fabrication and installation of a new project, CAC116-10 Joshua Glover Memorial, a public heritage monument to be commissioned by the City of Toronto in consultation with the Etobicoke Historical Society, and pending all necessary approvals, be placed in a City park in Ward 5.
