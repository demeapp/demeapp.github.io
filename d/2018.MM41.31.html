What the item proposed

A Zoning By-law Amendment application was previously considered by City Council at its meeting on July 12, 13, 14 and 15, 2016 (Item TE17.13). The application proposed a 47-storey mixed-use building containing 408 residential dwelling units and 20 rental replacement units and 349 square metres of retail space at 68 and 70 Charles Street East and 628, 634, 636 and 638 Church Street. The City Council decision contained conditions relating to the introduction of the bills and implemented a holding symbol ('H'). The proposed holding provision in the Draft Zoning By-law provided that the 'H' be lifted at such time Toronto Water has identified improvements to the water system for implementation. Toronto Water and Engineering and Construction Services staff have confirmed that this condition has been fulfilled.
