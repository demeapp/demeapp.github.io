The official record

What the item proposed

A local establishment with support from the Toronto Entertainment District Business Improvement Area has proposed to operate a parklet café along the westerly curb lane of Duncan Street just south of Richmond Street West. These recommendations enable City staff to approve the proposed parklet café subject to standard conditions. City Council has recently approved similar installations on Elm Street, Temperance Street, and King Street. Duncan Street is a wide public street in the Entertainment District with one lane for travel in each direction. It terminates at Queen Street West in the north and King Street West in the south so it is not a major route for vehicular travel. The parklet café would not displace any lanes used for vehicular travel or paid parking.
