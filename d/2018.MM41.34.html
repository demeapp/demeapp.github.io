The official record

What the item proposed

For the lands known municipally as 1603 Eglinton Avenue West, approved in 2014 for redevelopment, the City secured $384,345.00 in Section 37 funds in order to provide a monthly transit pass at no cost to the tenant for each residential tenancy for a period of 12 months. The funds were secured by a Section 37 Agreement registered on title, between the City and Empire Communities Inc. A payment of $384,345.00 has been received and remains unspent. It is have been determined that due to delays in the construction of the project, and the introduction of PRESTO transit payment cards, and the completion of the Oakwood LRT Station by 2021, the Toronto Transit Commission monthly pass is no longer necessary to encourage transit ridership. What was decided

City Council on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Director, Community Planning, North York District, to bring forward a zoning by-law amendment to the North York Community Council pertaining to 1603 Eglinton Avenue West, to amend the Section 37 provisions of By-law 998-2011 (Ontario Municipal Board) in order to reallocate the funds previously secured but no longer needed for the original purposes. 2.
