What the item proposed

At its meeting of March 10, 2016, City Council adopted Item TE14.3 which, among other matters, approved the Rental Housing Demolition application (14 153097 STE 22 RH) to demolish two (2) existing residential rental units at 86 and 88 Soudan Avenue. Part 2.d of Item TE14.3 required the owner, as a precondition for the issuance of the Preliminary Approval for the Section 111 demolition permit for the property, to obtain issuance of Notice of Approval Conditions for site plan approval from the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, pursuant to Section 114 of the City of Toronto Act, 2006. Part 3 of Item TE14.3 required the owner, as a precondition for the issuance of the Preliminary Approval for the Section 111 demolition permit for the property, to obtain the issuance of the first building permit.
