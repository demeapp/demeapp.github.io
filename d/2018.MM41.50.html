Toronto City Council · Decision

Remuneration for Public Directors of CreateTO, Build Toronto and the Toronto Port Lands Company - by Councillor David Shiner, seconded by Councillor Jim Hart

AdoptedMay 22, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Councillor David Shiner, seconded by Councillor Jim Hart, asks City Council to direct that the public members of CreateTO, Build Toronto and Toronto Port Lands Company be compensated for attendance at meetings of all three Boards.

The official summary names a figure of $50,000.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council at its meeting on May 24, 25 and 26, 2017, adopted Item EX25.9, established the Toronto Realty Agency Board, now the CreateTO Board. City Council approved remuneration for public members which includes an annual retainer and a per diem for meetings. The Chair receives an annual retainer of $50,000 and $500 per meeting, to a maximum of $70,000 per year. Other public members receive an annual retainer of $10,000 and $500 per meeting, to a maximum of $20,000 per year. At its meeting on December 5, 6, 7 and 8, 2017, City Council adopted Item EX29.5 and among other things, amended the Shareholder Directions for Build Toronto and the Toronto Port Lands Company to provide that: - the Directors of the CreateTO Board are also members of the Build Toronto and Toronto Port Lands Company Corporation Boards by virtue of position; and - the public Directors receive no additional…

What was decided

City Council on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct that the public members of CreateTO, Build Toronto and Toronto Port Lands Company be compensated for attendance at meetings of all three Boards up to the approved maximum retainer established by City Council, retroactive to January 1, 2018, and that the Shareholder Directions for Build Toronto and the Toronto Port Lands Company be amended accordingly.
