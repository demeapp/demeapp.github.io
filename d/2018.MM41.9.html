What the item proposed

83 Alexander Street, the Church Street Public School, is a significant green space and children's education facility. The Toronto District School is investing in ongoing capital investments on the site and additional Section 37 funds were allocated to enhance this work with a decorative fence feature for the south-west edge of the property in 2017. As work at the site has continued, additional opportunities to improve site safety, security, and the state of good repair have been identified. In particular, there is an opportunity to enhance the public realm and improve the school grounds by investing in a higher standard of fencing around the remainder of the property.
