The official record

What the item proposed

The 2018 Council Approved Parks, Forestry and Recreation Capital Budget includes $0.070 million for the Magwood Park Fitness Stations and $0.418 million for the Ravina Gardens Wading Pool Conversion sub-projects. Through the investigation phase, it was determined that Magwood Park is an archaeologically sensitive area and authority is required from City Council to cancel installing fitness stations in this park, reducing the 2018 cash flow, and releasing commitments on development related funding sources to the appropriate accounts. Improvements to Ravina Gardens have been underway since 2016, with the first phase of playground upgrades completed in 2017.
