What the item proposed

This Motion seeks to release $7,500 of Section 37 funds to Heritage Toronto for a heritage plaque commemorating Greenwood Avenue's History of Brickmaking. Greenwood Avenue, south of Danforth Avenue, was Toronto's brickmaking capital in the 19th and 20th centuries. From the 1860's to the 1920's, there were at least 10 brickyards located along Greenwood Avenue that produced hundreds of thousands of bricks annually and employed hundreds of people. They included John Price Brick Maker, Morley and Ashbridge Brick Manufacturers, Taylor Brickyard, Logan Brickyards, John Price Brickyards, Wagstaff Brick Yards, the Isaac Price Brickyards, and more.
