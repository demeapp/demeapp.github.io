What the item proposed

As a requirement of its Section 37 Agreement for the redevelopment of the lands at 587 Yonge Street located on the east side of Yonge Street between Gloucester Street and Dundonald Street, 'The Clover on Yonge Street' (Cresford Developments Inc.) is designing and constructing local park improvements in James Canning Gardens. These improvements are part of the Master Plan for the Yonge Street Linear Park System formed by George Hislop Park, Norman Jewison Park and James Canning Gardens that provides a continuous corridor from Charles Street to the north to Dundonald Street to the south. This area of the City is densely populated and, in addition to local residents, attracts a large number of commuters and visitors on a regular basis.
