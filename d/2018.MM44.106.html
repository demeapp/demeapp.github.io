What the item proposed

The Creative Co-location Facilities property tax sub-class was created through Council's adoption of Item EX31.1: 2018 Property Tax Rates and Related Matters on February 12, 2018, as amended, and through the enactment of By-law 166-2018 (The Tax Levy By-law for 2018) and By-law 168-2018 (To Amend City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 767, Taxation, Property Tax, by adding eligibility criteria for a Creative Co-location Facilities subclass for the Commercial Residual and Industrial property tax classes), which by-law was further amended by By-law 640-2018. The City Council decision and by-laws provide that the Creative Co-location Facilities subclass applies within the Residual Commercial and Industrial property tax classes.
