The official record

What the item proposed

Additional funds are required to complete the build out of a new public park at 50 St. Joseph Street, to be known as Clover Hill Park. The need for additional funds is due to the increase in construction costs over time and a dispute with the developer of the subject property over the allocation of certain costs under the Section 37 Agreement which secured the construction of this park. Staff have estimated that up to an additional $250,000 will be required to complete the build out of the park. Once costs are finalized, any remaining monies that are not needed to complete the park will be returned to the appropriate Section 37 accounts. This Motion is urgent because staff and the community want the park construction to begin this year, and construction will not commence until additional funds are available.
