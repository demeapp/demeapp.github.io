The official record

What the item proposed

The City is currently finalizing the Zoning By-law and Section 37 Agreement for 8 to 30 Widmer Street as required for the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal to issue its Order. To permit the development to proceed during the Council recess and provide direction for the Section 37 Agreement to secure the Section 42 parkland requirement for this site, City staff from Parks, Forestry and Recreation, Real Estate Services, City Planning in consultation with the Toronto Parking Authority and CreateTO, are proposing a recommendation that would direct the parkland dedication requirement from this site to support the creation of a new public park at the south-east corner of Spadina Avenue and Adelaide Street West while expanding the supply of public parking downtown.

What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the full cash-in-lieu payment of parkland dedication collected from the development application at 8 to 30 Widmer Street to the Parkland Acquisition South District Reserve Account (XR2208); the funds will be used to advance the development of a park with a minimum of 500m2 of unencumbered park space at the City-owned lands located at 105 Spadina Avenue and 363 Adelaide Street West.
