What the item proposed

This Motion seeks Council authority to release an additional $1,000,000.00 in Section 37 monies to secure 203.9 square metres of space located at 200 Dundas Street East for the Indigenous Centre for Innovation and Entrepreneurship, and to fund capital improvements to the future home of the Indigenous Centre for Innovation and Entrepreneurship. As part of the development located at 59-71 Mutual Street, a Section 37 benefit agreement was signed to secure 203.9 square metres of space located at 200 Dundas Street East with the developer agreeing to provide a maximum of $1.2 million towards the purchase of that space. The City was to be responsible for providing the balance of the funding and it has been determined the City needs to provide $1.0 million in order to complete the purchase this year.
