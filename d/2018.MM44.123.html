What the item proposed

On January 31 and February 1, 2018 City Council adopted Item EX30.2 which gave Shareholder direction to Toronto Community Housing Corporation to transfer the Rooming House and Agency House groupings of Toronto Community Housing Corporation's scattered portfolio to non-profit agencies. City staff began working with Toronto Community Housing Corporation staff in February 2018 to determine how these transfers will take place. Part 1.e. of EX30.2 was intended to provide a framework for equitably allocating the outstanding value of the mortgages on the Agency Houses amongst the Agencies who would be offered the opportunity to purchase them. What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. Council direct the Board of Directors, Toronto Community Housing Corporation to instruct its Chief Executive Officer to transfer ownership of the Toronto Community Housing Corporation Agency House portfolio to non-profit corporations as approved by City Council on January 31 and February 1, 2018 in Item EX30.2 in each case at a below-market transfer price determined by the City Manager.
