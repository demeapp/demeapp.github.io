The official record

What the item proposed

Authority is being sought to release Section 37 funds to Real Estate Services for a design for exterior lighting for the Young People's Theatre, located at the City-owned property at 165 Front Street East. In 2011, the Heritage Lighting Master Plan for Old Town Toronto was completed, which included a lighting vision and opportunities for Front Street facades and opportunities to use night time lighting design to highlight architectural details. Real Estate Services will be awarding a contract for the preparation of a design and tender ready documents for exterior architectural lighting at the Young People's Theatre. The design will recognize the historic importance of the building, and will enhance the façade and the historical character of the site. What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend the 2018 - 2027 Council Approved Capital Budget and Plan for Facilities, Real Estate, and Environment and Energy, to add a new project called "Young People's Theatre Lighting" to be used for the architectural lighting design for the Young People's Theatre, with total project cost and cash flow funding of $60,971.00 in 2018, and to be fully funded from Section 37 community benefits, obtained from the development at 20 to 26 Lombard Street and 25 Richmond Street East (Source Account: XR3026-3700825).
