What the item proposed

The 12 Alexander Street Theatre Project, also known as Buddies in Bad Times Theatre is a City of Toronto owned facility. The Management of the 12 Alexander Street Theatre Project have identified a need for capital improvements within their building in order to install a new accessibility ramp, complete structural and roof repairs, make washrooms more accessible, and expand capacity in the community event space and ground floor. The cost of these improvements is $335,500.00. Of the original $4,750,000.00 in Section 37 funds secured and received for various purposes in the development approved in 836-850 Yonge Street, $4,511,687.87 remains. The Section 37 agreement specifies that one of the purposes of these funds is improvements to community and cultural facilities in the Ward.
