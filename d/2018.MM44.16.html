What the item proposed

This Motion seeks authority to create a new capital sub-project called Earlscourt Park Improvements, in the Park Development project, for the purpose of converting the existing wading pool to a splash pad, to be determined through the design process and consultation with the local City Councillor and the community. The total project cost of $0.235 million will be added in 2018 with cash flow of $0.020 million in 2019 for design and $0.215 million in 2020 for construction. Funding for the project is from various Section 42 Alternate Rate Cash-in-lieu. Through multiple community meetings for the Earlscourt Park Playground enhancements, which is scheduled for completion in 2018, the community strongly expressed their desire for a new splash pad.
