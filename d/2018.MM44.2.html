The official record

What the item proposed

The applicant applied to the Committee of Adjustment for consent to sever the property at 1129 Huntingwood Drive into two lots. The existing three-storey townhouse will be maintained on Part 2, and a new three-storey detached dwelling will be constructed on Part 1. The minor variance applications request a number of proposed variances to the City of Toronto Zoning By-law 569-2013 and Agincourt North Community 12797 Zoning By-law. At its hearing on June 21, 2018, the Committee of Adjustment refused the applications. The applications are of concern because the proposed severance fails the criteria of Section 51(24) of the Planning Act, specifically, subsections c) and f), the proposed variance applications do not conform to the policies in the Official Plan and do not maintain the general intent of the Zoning By-law, are not desirable for the appropriate development of the land and are…

What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the City Solicitor and City Planning staff to attend the Toronto Local Appeal Body hearing to support the Committee of Adjustment's decision to refuse the consent and minor variances requested for 1129 Huntingwood Drive.
