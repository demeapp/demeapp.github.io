What the item proposed

This Motion seeks authority to increase funding for the Dane Park Construction sub-project in the 2018 Parks, Forestry and Recreation Capital Budget by $79,000. The Park was transferred to Parks, Forestry and Recreation from the Developer in base condition with unacceptable fill. As per the Registered Plan of Subdivision, the Developer is responsible for remediating the park lands prior to transferring lands to the City. The Contractor who was retained by Parks, Forestry and Recreation to complete above base park improvements discovered the unacceptable fill and has been remediating the lands as instructed. Under the Planning Act, the Developer is required to convey the lands in environmentally acceptable standards. As such, the Developer is obligated to reimburse the City for any remediated work. What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend the 2018 Approved Parks, Forestry and Recreation Capital Budget for the Dane Park Construction sub-project in the Park Development project, by increasing the total project cost by $79,000 from $858,000 to $937,000 and that the 2018 cash flow be increased from $835,265 to $914,265 with funding from a Developer contribution.
