The official record

What the item proposed

The section of Wilmington Avenue, between the traffic control signals at Overbrook Avenue and Sheppard Avenue West, is approximately 1.2 kilometres in length. In this section of street, there are no controlled pedestrian crossings. Residents have reported that the long spacing, as well as the speed and volume of traffic on Wilmington Avenue, makes it difficult to cross the street. Therefore, a pedestrian crossover is proposed on Wilmington Avenue, immediately south of Searle Avenue. This crossing will be located mid-way between the adjacent controlled crossings and will serve the transit stops located at this intersection. The proposed pedestrian crossover will benefit all residents in this neighbourhood, which includes parks and a synagogue, as well as William Lyon Mackenzie Collegiate Institute and the Anne and Max Tanenbaum Community Hebrew Academy of Toronto.
