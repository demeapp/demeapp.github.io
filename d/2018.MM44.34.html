The official record

What the item proposed

The applicant applied to the Committee of Adjustment for consent to sever the property at 7 Aragon Avenue into two lots. The proposed lots would each have a frontage of 11.57 metres and a lot area of 750 square metres. The applicant is proposing to demolish the existing house and build two new detached houses requiring variances to the City of Toronto Zoning By-law 569-2013 and the Scarborough Tam O'Shanter Community Zoning By-law 12360. At its hearing on May 31, 2018, the Committee of Adjustment refused the application. The application is of concern because the proposed severance and variances do not conform to the policies in the Official Plan and do not maintain the general intent of the Zoning By-law. Copies of the Decision Notices are attached. The application was appealed to the Toronto Local Appeal Body by the applicant. The hearing date has not yet been scheduled. This motion…

What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the City Solicitor and City Planning staff to attend the Toronto Local Appeal Body to support the Committee of Adjustment's decision to refuse the consent and minor variances requested for 7 Aragon Avenue.
