The official record

What the item proposed

The organization, Canadian Centre for Bio-Ethical Reform, is an example of the groups which are occupying public right-of-way space for the display of extremely graphic and/or disturbing images which appear to be intended to shock, alarm, or cause dismay, including outside schools, community centres, and other places which serve young children and other vulnerable members of the public. In addition, these displays occupy the public right-of-way in a manner that obstructs and/or interferes with the use of the public right-of-way by members of the public and impede the safe flow of pedestrian movement in and around the vicinity of the displays. What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. Pending the final report on these matters in the first quarter of 2019, City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services to review current by-laws designed to protect members of the public from harm, including provisions under City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks, Use of, and other by-laws intended to prevent the obstruction or interference with the public right-of-way by any group that seeks to use the public right-of-way for the display of graphic images and consider enforcement options available.
