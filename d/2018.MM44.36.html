The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on November 7, 8 and 9, 2017, in considering Official Plan, Zoning By-law and Municipal Shelter By-law, City Council directed the General Manager, Transportation Services to examine opportunities to prepare a public realm plan to provide an enhanced streetscape on George Street between its intersection with Gerrard Street East and Dundas Street East. This public realm plan would take into account redevelopment in the area while recognizing the historic Indigenous presence and the relationship to some of the most vulnerable members of our society and to report to the June 6, 2018 meeting of the Toronto and East York Community Council on boulevard, roadway and other public realm improvements to improve safety, security and mobility and create more pedestrian-friendly, liveable streets.
