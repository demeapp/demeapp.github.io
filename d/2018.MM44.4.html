What the item proposed

The area that encompasses Blessed Pope Paul VI Catholic School (270 Laughton Avenue) has a growing population and an increasing number of children and young families. The school and community require a safe playground with enhanced recreational amenities that meet the needs of the neighbourhood. This initiative will help to foster a stronger school community by improving the existing playground that ensures our youth remain healthy and active. Functional recreational facilities for families, students and children to enjoy will create a "win/win" situation for everyone. We need a place for children to play, congregate, and most importantly, get to know one another.
