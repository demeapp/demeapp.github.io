Councillor Paula Fletcher asks City Council to add Dundas Street East between Pape Avenue and Greenwood Avenue as a Community Safety Zone.

Official title: A Community Safety Zone on Dundas: Pape to Greenwood - by Councillor Paula Fletcher, seconded by Councillor Sarah Doucette

AdoptedJul 23, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At the June 26 to 229, 2018 City Council meeting Council approved dozens of Community Safety Zones around schools to help create a safer walk to school. I asked residents of Ward 30 about their routes to school and added several new Community Safety Zones based on what I heard. I have recently heard that residents and active transportation advocates would like to add Dundas between Pape and Greenwood as a Community Safety Zone. This stretch of Dundas is heavily used by students going to Leslieville Public School and is a busy arterial road where safety is always a concern. Adding this as a Community Safety Zone will make it easier to implement traffic calming and pedestrian protection measures. REQUIRES RE-OPENING: Item PW30.5 - City Council June 26, 27, 28 and 29, 2017 to add a community safety zone.

What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council add Dundas Street East between Pape Avenue and Greenwood Avenue as a Community Safety Zone.
