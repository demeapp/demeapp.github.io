What the item proposed

Ashbridges Bay Skateboard Park is the largest skatepark in the Ontario. The park acts a regional park for the City, and is used heavily by local skateboarders and those from across Toronto. The park is located on public lands at the corner of Coxwell Avenue and Lake Shore Boulevard East, and is a great distance from all residential housing. This park was planned, designed and built in two phases. Phase one included ramps, rails and other elements, and the Phase two included a bowl. The second phase was to be funded through a dedicated reserve fund, however, these funds were re-allocated to another park project. Parks, Forestry and Recreation staff included funding in a subsequent capital budget to complete the second phase. Unfortunately lighting was not included in the project. What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation to include funding in the 2019 divisional Capital Plan for the installation of lights at Ashbridges Bay Skateboard Park in 2019, for consideration in the 2019 Budget Process. 2. City Council direct the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation to initiate the design process in 2018 to support construction in 2019 using existing allocations within the 2018 budget.
