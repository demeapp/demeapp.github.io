What the item proposed

The Motion seeks authorization to amend Transportation Services' 2018 – 2027 Capital Plan to include additional Section 37 Planning Act Reserve Funds of $12,000,000.00 to fund costs related to the extension of the North York Service Roads. As part of the North York Centre Secondary Plan, an Environmental Assessment was completed in 1993 for the North York Service Road Project to support the approved levels of development in the North York Centre. The North York Service Road Project includes an extension of Doris Avenue and Beecroft Road generally between Sheppard Avenue and Finch Avenue, as parallel routes to Yonge Street. The “Strategy for the Implementation of the North York Centre Plan Service Road-North York Centre”, North York Community Council Report No. 8, Clause No. 20 was approved by City Council at its meeting of July 29, 30 and 31, 1998.
