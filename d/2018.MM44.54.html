What the item proposed

In 2015, City Council adopted the Oakwood Vaughan Strategy and Actions, and forwarded the Actions to the Budget Committee for consideration in 2016. The Actions made significant contributions to the Oakwood-Vaughan neighbourhood by focusing on social engagement, economic development, and community safety. The Actions proposed to introduce long-overdue City services and general improvements to the community and neighbourhood, including a daily Youth Hub at the Maria A. Shcuhuka library, expansion of business education programs, and the establishment of the Oakwood-Vaughan Neighbourhood Action Partnership, a community network composed of residents, community organizations, and City staff. However out of the 26 improvements and services outlined, only 11 have been fulfilled to this date, and the rest remain only partially fulfilled or not achieved. What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Executive Director, Social Development, Finance and Administration to report on fulfilled and outstanding actions of the Oakwood Vaughan Strategy and Actions Plan, as indicated in Attachment 1 of Item CD7.7, by the first quarter of 2019. 2. City Council request that any outstanding commitments be listed in the Oakwood Vaughan Strategy and Actions within budget lines to the Budget Committee for consideration as part of the 2019 Budget process.
