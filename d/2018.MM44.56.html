The official record

What the item proposed

This Motion seeks authority to create a new capital sub-project called Ward 24 Park Improvements, in the Park Development project, for the purpose of improvements to various parks in Ward 24 to be determined through the design process. The total project cost of $1,514,564 will be added in 2018 with cash flow of $50,000 in 2018 and $1,464,564 in 2019. Funding for the project is from various Section 37 and Section 45 community benefits. This Motion is urgent as the additional funding is required to initiate the design process for various parks beginning in 2018.
