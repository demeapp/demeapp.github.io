What the item proposed

Ramsden Park Road serves as a gateway to Ramsden Park, a neighbourhood green space that is currently undergoing a massive renovation. The local community has long identified that the road has fallen into disrepair; with cracked and uneven pavement, poor lighting, and struggling vegetation. Moving forward with the necessary improvements has been a multi-year process. During the 2014 community consultations for Ramsden Park improvements, I requested that Ramsden Park Road streetscape improvements be included in the scope of work. At the time, I was informed that the two projects could not be combined and accomplished concurrently. Instead, Transportation Services would address the streetscape through a separate process, after the park renovations concluded.
