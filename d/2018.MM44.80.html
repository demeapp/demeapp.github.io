What the item proposed

Eglinton Avenue is in the midst of a transformation, with the construction of the Eglinton Crosstown well underway. This transit mega project will transform the heart of midtown Toronto for generations to come, connecting this important avenue to the rest of the city. This report recommends the release of a total of $ 822,345.00 in funds secured by the City through agreements made under Section 37 of the Planning Act. The funding will be applied to capital improvements and/or capital facilities for a range a projects in Ward 15, as identified below. Oakwood Village Toronto Public Library Branch Auditorium Revitalization – The Oakwood Village library is an important hub for the local community, and has seen a steady growth in demand for services and space.
