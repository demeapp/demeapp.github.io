The official record

What the item proposed

The applicant has applied to the Committee of Adjustment, North York District (Application B0082/17NY) to obtain consent to sever the property into two undersized residential lots. At its hearing on June 13, 2018 the Committee of Adjustment refused the application. A copy of the Notice of Decision is attached. The applicant has appealed the Committee of Adjustment decision to the Toronto Local Appeal Body (TLAB). A hearing has been scheduled for November 2, 2018. This Motion would give the City Solicitor the authority to attend the TLAB hearing in opposition to the application. It would also give the City Solicitor authority to retain outside consultants as necessary in order to oppose the application. Finally, this Motion would give the City Solicitor authority to settle the matter on behalf of the City in his/her discretion after consultation with the Chief Planner and Executive…

What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the City Solicitor and appropriate staff to attend the Toronto Local Appeal Body regarding Committee of Adjustment decision B0082/17NY and uphold the Committee's decision to refuse the lot severance for 273B Ranee Avenue. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to retain outside consultants as necessary to oppose the proposed variances requested in Committee of Adjustment decision B0082/17NY for 273B Ranee Avenue. 3. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to settle the matter on behalf of the City in the City Solicitor's discretion after consultation with the Ward Councillor.
