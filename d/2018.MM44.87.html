What the item proposed

This Motion seeks authority to create a new capital sub-project called James Canning Gardens Redevelopment, in the Park Development project, in the 2018 Parks, Forestry and Recreation Capital Budget and 2019-2027 Capital Plan. As per the Section 37 Agreement for 587 to 599 Yonge Street, 2 to 4 Dundonald Street, 7 to 9 Gloucester Street, the Developer, The Clover on Yonge Inc., are required to design and construct park improvements to James Canning Gardens. The proposed park improvements were tendered by the Developer and the value exceeds the Developer's Section 37 requirement and additional funds in the amount of $381,000 are required for park to be completed as designed. The total project cost of $381,000 will be added in 2018 with cash flow in 2019. Funding for the project is from two Section 37 community benefits.
