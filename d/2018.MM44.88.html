What the item proposed

The 2018 Council Approved Parks, Forestry and Recreation Capital Budget includes $165,000 in 2018 for the Woodbine Park Playground under the Ward 32 Park Improvements sub-project and $300,000 over 2019 and 2020 for the Fairmount Park Playground Upgrades sub-project. Project scope for the Woodbine Park Playground sub-project in the Park Development project includes design and construction of playground area improvements as determined through the community consultation process. This motion seeks authority from City Council to increase funding by $69,000 from various Section 42 Alternate Rate Cash-in-lieu of Parkland Dedication funding sources. The additional funding is required to award the tender which exceeds the available Council-approved funding for the project, so that improvements can be completed by the end of this year.
