Legacy Fund Allocations Committee - Selection of Candidates for Interview

Adopted with changesMar 1, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend: The Facilities and Finance Nominating Panel select six of the candidates listed in Confidential Attachment 1 for interview at its next meeting for appointment to the Legacy Fund.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Facilities and Finance Nominating Panel must select candidates for interview for appointment to the Legacy Fund Allocations Committee to fill one vacancy. The Nominating Panel will conduct the interviews at its next meeting and make recommendations to City Council.

What was decided

The Facilities and Finance Nominating Panel: 1. Selected the following three candidates for interview at its next meeting for appointment to the Legacy Fund Allocations Committee: Candidate #36 Candidate #44 Candidate #46 2. Directed that the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachment 1to the report (February 8, 2018) remain confidential in its entirety as it relates to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Legacy Fund Allocations Committee.
