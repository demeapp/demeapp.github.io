The official record

What the item proposed

The Tribunals Nominating Panel must select six candidates to be interviewed for appointment to the Toronto Licensing Tribunal to fill two recent vacancies for public member positions. The Nominating Panel will meet on May 7, 2018 to conduct interviews and recommend candidates to City Council.

What was decided

The Tribunals Nominating Panel: 1. Selected the following candidates for interview for appointment to the Toronto Licensing Tribunal: Candidate # 134 Candidate # 141 Candidate # 149 Candidate # 151 Candidate # 175 Candidate # 181 Candidate # 183 2. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1, 2 and 3 to the report (April 16, 2018) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Licensing Tribunal.
