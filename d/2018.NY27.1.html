The official record

What the item proposed

These applications for Zoning By-law Amendments and Draft Plan of Subdivision propose to amend the former North York Zoning By-law and City of Toronto Zoning By-law and subdivide the lands at 36 and 37 Jane Osler Boulevard and 42 and 44 Cartwright Avenue to permit 8 detached dwellings on 8 residential lots on a public street terminating in a cul-de-sac. This report reviews and recommends approval of the application to amend the Zoning By-laws. This report also recommends that City Council designate the proposed lots as a Class 4 Area under relevant provincial noise guidelines administered by the Ministry of Environment and Climate Change (MOECC). A Class 4 noise area classification allows for higher daytime and night-time sound level limits than would otherwise be permitted in relation to a noise sensitive land use such as residential dwellings and associated outdoor living areas.
