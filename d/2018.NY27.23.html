The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting authority to amend the existing parking regulations on the south side of Willowbank Boulevard, between Avenue Road and Castle Knock Road. The proposed parking amendments will address the concerns raised by local residents of street obstructions caused by vehicles stopped on the south side of this street. This report also identifies and rectifies conflicting parking by-laws on Willowbank Boulevard by introducing "house-keeping" by-law amendments.
