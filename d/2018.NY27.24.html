North York Community Council was asked to decide on a speed limit change on Glencairn Avenue.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to reduce the speed limit to 30 km/h on Glencairn Avenue, between Bathurst Street and Mona Drive. The implementation of the 30 km/h speed limit will promote a reduced operating speed on this section of roadway and address the residents' concerns with regards to vehicle speeds.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on Glencairn Avenue, between Bathurst Street and a point 310.5 metres west of Mona Drive. 2. Amended the existing 40 km/h speed limit on Glencairn Avenue, between Yonge Street and a point 310.5 metres west of Mona Drive, to operate between Yonge Street and Mona Drive. 3. Reduced the speed limit from 40 km/h to 30 km/h on Glencairn Avenue, between Mona Drive and a point 310.5 metres west.
