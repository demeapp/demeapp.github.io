Toronto City Council · Decision

Endorsement of Events for Liquor Licensing Purposes

AdoptedJan 31, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to endorse various events for liquor licensing purposes. Endorsement supports the event organizers' liquor licence applications.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Seeking endorsement of events for Municipal Significance for liquor licensing purposes.

What was decided

City Council on January 31 and February 1, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council advise the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario that it has no objection, for liquor licensing purposes, to the following request noting that applicants are required to comply with other applicable by-laws and to obtain the necessary permits for any patio areas extending into the City's right of way: a. Liquor Licence extension, from Hamaz Tashjian, Manager, Armenian Community Centre, for the ACC (Armenian Community Centre) Summerfest 2018, to be held on Friday, July 13, 2018 from 5:00 p.m. to 2:00 a.m.; Saturday, July 14, 2018 from 4:00 p.m. to 2:00 a.m.; and Sunday, July 15, 2018 from 5:00 p.m. to 12:00 a.m. at the Armenian Community Centre, 50 Hallcrown Place.
