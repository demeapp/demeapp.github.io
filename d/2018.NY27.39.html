Toronto City Council · Decision

Parking Regulation - Killdeer Crescent

AdoptedJan 16, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend: Rescind the parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of the northerly east-west section of Killdeer Crescent.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Upon receiving petition from Killdeer Crescent residents, I conducted a survey with the affected households to allow parking on both sides of the street on the north end of Killdeer Crescent. Currently, parking is allowed only one side but residents have expressed concerns with lack of on-street parking. The poll resulted in favour of allowing parking on both sides of the street. The statutory three-hour parking limit will still be in effect.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of the northerly east-west section of Killdeer Crescent, between Brentcliffe Road (north intersection) and a point 90 metres east.
