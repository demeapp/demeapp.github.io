Update on Investigation into Tree Removal - 7 Waxwing Place

AdoptedJan 16, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend: Urban Forestry prepare a report detailing, step-by-step, the investigation and subsequent fines issued to the violating parties.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In April 2016 the owners of 7 Waxwing Place applied to the Committee of Adjustment process to replace the existing structure with a new two storey home. Forestry staff reviewed the application. They said they had no objections to the Application. Urban Forestry's RNFP group issued a permit authorizing the removal of two trees and the injuries of ten others on the property to allow for the approved variances. In July 2017 two large trees in the front yard were removed. These trees were protected under the private tree bylaw and not included in the permit issued by the RNFP group. The removal of these trees has greatly upset the neighbourhood. I would ask Urban Forestry to prepare a report on the investigation into the illegal removal of these trees and report to the April meeting of North York Community Council.

What was decided

North York Community Council directed that: 1. Urban Forestry staff prepare a report for the April 4, 2018 meeting of the North York Community Council: a. detailing, step-by-step, the investigation and subsequent fines issued to the violating parties; and b. comparing the process used and fines issued in the 7 Waxwing Place case to the guidelines set out in the City of Toronto Private Tree Bylaw.
