Official title: Re-opening of North York Community Council Item NY25.10, headed "Parking Amendments - Downsview Neighbourhood"

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting held on October 17, 2017, North York Community Council adopted without amendment Item NY25.10 and, in so doing, authorized numerous parking regulation changes on streets within the Downsview neighbourhood. As the parking regulation changes on Downsview Avenue, west of a point 88 metres west of Ridge Road, and Victory Drive, west of Keele Street, do not reflect the requirements of the residents on these streets, it is recommended that the Item be re-opened and amended to address these concerns.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Deleted Parts 1 to 11 of the decision on Item NY25.10, and adopted instead, the following: "1. Rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Victory Drive, between Agate Road and Ianhall Road. 2. Rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the east side of Agate Road, between Victory Drive and Wilson Avenue. 3. Prohibit parking from 6:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Victory Drive, between Agate Road and Julian Road. 4. Prohibit stopping at all times on the east side of Agate Road, between Victory Drive and Wilson Avenue. 5. Prohibit stopping at all times on the west side of Agate Road, from Victory Drive to a point 15 metres south. 6.
