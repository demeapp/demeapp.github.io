North York Community Council was asked to approve a road alteration on Malcolm Road.

Official title: Road Alterations and Parking Amendments - Malcolm Road

AdoptedJan 16, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

During the construction staging for 25 Malcolm Road development, the road was temporarily altered to one-way northbound traffic operation from Millwood Road to north of 25 Malcolm Road site and a two-way traffic operation from that point to Randolph Road. Residents have requested that this traffic operation be maintained. Further, there are parking bylaw amendments required for housekeeping purposes.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Approved the alteration of Malcolm Road, generally as shown on attached drawing No. NYCD-0001. 2. Rescinded the one-way northwestbound operation of Malcolm Road, between Millwood Road and a point 66 metres west. 3. Designated one-way northwestbound operation of Malcolm Road, between Millwood Road and a point 40 metres west. 4. Prohibited stopping on the northeast side of Malcolm Road, between Millwood Road and a point 55.4 metres west. 5. Prohibited stopping on the southwest side Malcolm Road, between Millwood Road and a point 22 metres west, and between a point 36 metres west of Millwood Road and a point 7.3 metres further west. 6. Prohibited standing at all times on the southwest side of Malcolm Road, between a point 22 metres west of Millwood Road and a point 14 metres further west. 7.
