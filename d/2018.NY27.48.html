Official title: Re-opening of North York Community Council Item NY21.54, headed, "Re-opening of North York Community Council Item NY19.17 - Parking Prohibition - Ianhall Road"

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This item is being reopened due to a discrepancy in the time that the local school children are being let out, which is 3:30 p.m.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Deleted Parts 1 and 2 of the decision on Item NY21.54 and adopted instead, the following: "1. Authorize parking for a maximum period of 15 minutes from 8:00 a.m. to 8:30 a.m. and 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north and east sides of Ianhall Road, between Victory Drive and a point 76 metres north. 2. Prohibit parking from 8:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. and 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north and east sides of Ianhall Road, between Victory Drive and a point 76 metres north."
