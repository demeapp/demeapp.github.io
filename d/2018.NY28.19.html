The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend that North York Community Council appoint two directors to and remove two directors from the Yonge Lawrence Village Business Improvement Area (BIA) Board of Management.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. In accordance with the City's Public Appointments Policy, appointed the following nominees to the Yonge Lawrence Village Business Improvement Area (BIA) Board of Management at the pleasure of North York Community Council, and for a term expiring at the end of the term of Council or as soon thereafter as successors are appointed: Loewenberg, Madeleine Misch, Chris 2. Removed the following directors from the Yonge Lawrence Village Business Improvement Area (BIA) Board of Management: Waugh, David Waxman, Clifford
