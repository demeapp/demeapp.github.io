What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision provided it is in keeping with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting authority to enter into an encroachment agreement with the owner of 1 York Valley Crescent to maintain a 1.9 metre high wooden fence, setback 1.7 metres from the city curb and approximately 2.9 metres from the adjacent driveway; fence footings for the fence located within the right of way area at a depth of 1.22 metres; 1.9 metre high cedar hedges with a setback of 2.0 metres from the curb; 1.8 metre width stone interlocking walkway; soft landscaping fronting and flanking the property; and a sprinkler system within the boulevard area.
