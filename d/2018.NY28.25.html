Parking Prohibitions - Farmview Crescent, Ringwood Crescent, George Henry Boulevard and Parfield Drive

AdoptedFeb 21, 2018 · Toronto City Council

North York Community Council was asked to change parking rules on Farmview Crescent, Ringwood Crescent, George Henry Boulevard and Parfield Drive.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the construction in the Parkway Forest community across the street has begun along Don Mills, the number of cars parking on several streets in the Henry Farm Community directly west of the development has increased significantly, resulting in severe congestion. Consequently, I am submitting the attached petition requesting that parking prohibitions be implemented on Farmview Crescent, between Parfield Drive and George Henry Boulevard; on both sides of Ringwood Crescent, between the west and east intersections with George Henry Boulevard; on the north side of George Henry Boulevard, between Silkwood Crescent and Don Mills Road; on both sides of Parfield Drive, between Meadowglade Crescent and George Henry Boulevard on weekday mornings. The effected have been petitioned, 92 signatures are included. Transportation Services staff have been consulted regarding the above requested parking…

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. on both sides of Farmview Crescent, between Parfield Drive and George Henry Boulevard; on both sides of Ringwood Crescent, between the west and east intersections with George Henry Boulevard; on the north side of George Henry Boulevard, between Silkwood Crescent and Don Mills Road; on both sides of Parfield Drive, between Meadowglade Crescent and George Henry Boulevard.
