The official record

What the item proposed

The Jewish Community Centre at 4588-4600 Bathurst Street are being utilized by the United Jewish Appeal Federation (UJA) for the construction of the Sherman Campus, a non-profit community centre for the benefit of Toronto's vibrant Jewish community. As a non-profit organization, pursuant to the Former City of North York's By-laws and Official Plan policies the lands were formerly not subject to any conveyance of parkland requirements as set out in Section 42 of the Planning Act. Phase 1 of the Sherman Campus was reconstructed on that basis. This motion ensures that future phases of reconstruction will not be subject to Article III of Chapter 416 of the City of Toronto Municipal Code, consistent with the former City of North York's parkland conveyance bylaw, which was in place at the time the lands were rezoned for the Sherman Campus. What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 415, Development of Land, Article III, Schedule B to include the lands known in the year 2018 as 4588-4600 Bathurst Street.
