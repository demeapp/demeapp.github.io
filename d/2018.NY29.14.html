The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates bus service on Antibes Drive, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting approval to allow weekend parking on one side of the street in two separate sections of Antibes Drive. Currently, parking is prohibited at all times on both sides of this street. The parking amendments are feasible and will provide on-street parking opportunities for the Antibes Community Centre, as their parking demand can exceed the capacity of their on-site parking lot during the weekend periods. This report also identifies and rectifies missing parking by-laws and a conflicting heavy vehicle prohibition by-law on Antibes Drive by introducing "house-keeping" by-law amendments.

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the inner perimeter of Antibes Drive, between Torresdale Avenue (south intersection) and Torresdale Avenue (north intersection). 2. City Council prohibit parking at all times on the inner perimeter of Antibes Drive, between Torresdale Avenue (south intersection) and a point 60.4 metres south of Antibes Drive (eastbound one-way segment). 3. City Council prohibit parking from Monday to Friday on the inner perimeter of Antibes Drive, between a point 60.4 metres south of Antibes Drive (eastbound one-way segment) and a point 45 metres further north. 4.
