Reopening of North York Community Council Item NY28.25, headed "Parking Prohibitions - Farmview Crescent, Ringwood Crescent, George Henry Boulevard and Parfield Drive"

AdoptedApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

North York Community Council was asked to reopen Item NY28.25 entitled "Parking Prohibitions – Farmview Crescent, Ringwood Crescent, George Henry Boulevard and Parfield Drive.".

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting held on February 21, 2018 North York Community Council adopted without amendment Item NY28.25 entitled "Parking Prohibitions - Farmview Crescent, Ringwood Crescent, George Henry Boulevard and Parfield Drive" and, in so doing, approved parking prohibitions to be in effect from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. on all or portions of Farmview Crescent, Ringwood Crescent, George Henry Boulevard and Parfield Drive. However, it was subsequently noticed that these parking prohibitions were intended to be in effect from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., Monday to Friday. In order to correct this oversight, Item NY28.25 must be reopened and Recommendation 1 amended to reflect the correct times and days of these parking prohibitions. Transportation Services staff have been consulted regarding the above and have no concerns with the requested amendment. I respectfully request that this matter be added…

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Amended Part 1 of the decision on Item NY28.25, by inserting the words, "Monday to "Friday" immediately after the reference to the times "6:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., so that Part 1 of the Decision is now the following: "1. Prohibit parking from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, on both sides of Farmview Crescent, between Parfield Drive and George Henry Boulevard; on both sides of Ringwood Crescent, between the west and east intersections with George Henry Boulevard; on the north side of George Henry Boulevard, between Silkwood Crescent and Don Mills Road; on both sides of Parfield Drive, between Meadowglade Crescent and George Henry Boulevard."
