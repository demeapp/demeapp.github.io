The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks approval to amend the Fire Route By-law to designate certain locations as fire routes within the meaning of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 880, as amended. It was previously considered by City Council at its meeting on January 31 and February 1, 2018. The implementing Bill for this item has not yet been enacted. On March 2, 2018, City Council's delegation of fire route designations to Community Councils came into effect. City Council no longer has jurisdiction to deal with this matter, including the enactment of Bills. Community Councils now have final decision making authority for fire route designations. As a result, this report is resubmitted to Community Council for further consideration and final decision under the delegated authority in Subsection 27-152 F(1) of Chapter 27, Council Procedures.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Designated part or those parts of the private road or roads shown on the site plan filed with the Fire Chief in respect of the municipal addresses set out below, as a fire route pursuant to Municipal Code Chapter 880 - Fire Routes: a. 75 Billy Bishop Way; b. 65 Overlea Boulevard; c. 2350 Bayview Avenue; and d. 99 The Donway West. 2. Authorized the Fire Chief and City Solicitor to take the appropriate action to make a designated Fire Route.
