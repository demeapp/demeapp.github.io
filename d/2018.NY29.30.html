Assumption of Services, Plan 66M-2458 - 1100 Sheppard Avenue West

PostponedApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to assume the services installed within De Boers Drive and that the City formally assume the roads within the Registered Plan of Subdivision 66M-2458.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report requests Council's authority for the City to assume the municipal roads and services within De Boers Drive, in accordance with the terms of the Subdivision Agreement for Plan 66M-2458, registered on July 20, 2007 between 1609830 Ontario Inc. and the City of Toronto. Two Site Plan Control approvals were issued on December 18, 2008 and February 3, 2010.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Deferred consideration of the item until its meeting on July 4, 2018.
