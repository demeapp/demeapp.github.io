Application for Fence Exemption - 97 Regent Road

Adopted with changesApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

North York Community Council was asked to refuse the application for an exemption submitted by the owner of 97 Regent Road, to maintain an 2.7 metre high existing vertical board on board fence.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is in regards to a matter for which the North York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to consider an application submitted by the owner of 97 Regent Road to maintain an existing vertical board on board fence in the side and rear yard. The existing vertical board on board fence does not meet the requirements specified in City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447, Fences.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Granted the exemption to the owners of 97 Regent Road the 2.7 metre high existing vertical board on board fence in the side and rear yard to be preserved in the same condition without alteration. 2. Directed and requires that, at such time as replacement of the fence is required, that the replacement fence will comply with Municipal Code Chapter 447, or its successor by-law.
