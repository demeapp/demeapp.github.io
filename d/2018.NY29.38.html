Application for Fence Exemption - 211 Rumsey Road

PostponedApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

North York Community Council was asked to refuse to grant the application for an exemption permit, by the property owner of 211 Rumsey Road for the existing fence in lieu of complying.

Council put off deciding this item.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report concerns a matter for which the North York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to advise Community Council concerning an application by the property owner for a site-specific Fence Exemption, pursuant to Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447- Fences. The property owner is seeking Community Council's permission to keep the existing rod iron fence which exceeds the permitted height.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Deferred consideration of the item until its meeting on June 6, 2018.
