The official record

What the item proposed

The residential development at 871-873 Wilson Avenue was recently constructed. This development provides a private driveway that travels north-south between the south side of Wilson Avenue and the north side of Winston Park Avenue. The site plan approval for this development stated that the property's driveway would provide inbound and outbound access at Wilson Avenue and outbound access only at Winston Park Boulevard. Complaints have been received that motorists are entering the driveway from Winston Park Avenue as a cut-through route to access Wilson Avenue, resulting in safety concerns for residents. A site meeting at the property's Winston Park Boulevard driveway with Transportation Services on March 15, 2018 confirmed this activity. What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Prohibited eastbound left turns at all times, from Winston Park Boulevard to the driveway to No's. 871-873 Wilson Avenue, located on the north side of Winston Park Boulevard, at a point approximately 124 metres west of Lady York Avenue. 2. Prohibited westbound right turns at all times, from Winston Park Boulevard to the driveway to No's. 871-873 Wilson Avenue, located on the north side of Winston Park Boulevard, at a point approximately 124 metres west of Lady York Avenue.
