North York Community Council was asked to reduce speed limit from 50km/hr to 30km/hr on Deloraine Avenue from Avenue Road to Ledbury Street.

Official title: Request for Speed Limit Reduction Around Old Orchard Playground - Deloraine Avenue from Avenue Road to Ledbury Street and Old Orchard Grove from Avenue Road to Ledbury Street

AdoptedApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Local residents have requested that the speed limit be reduced on Deloraine Avenue and Old Orchard Grove as both streets abut the Old Orchard Grove Playground which was recently revitalized and is one of the most used playgrounds in Ward 16. We want to ensure that vehicles slow down in front of the park and in this residential neighbourhood where most local children come to play. I am requesting a speed limit reduction to 30km/hr on Deloraine Avenue between Avenue Road to Bathurst Street and on Old Orchard Grove from Avenue Road to Ledbury Street.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Reduced speed limit from 50km/hr to 30km/hr on Deloraine Avenue from Avenue Road to Ledbury Street. 2. Reduced speed limit from 50km/hr to 30km/hr on Old Orchard Grove from Avenue Road to Ledbury Street.
