Speed Limit Reduction - Brookfield Road, Plymbridge Crescent, Plymbridge Road, and Donino Avenue

AdoptedApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

North York Community Council was asked to reduce the speed limit on Brookfield Road, between Old Yonge Street/Donino Avenue and Plymbridge Crescent, from 40 km/h to 30km/h.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

A number of residents in the Hogg's Hollow neighbourhood have contacted me with concerns about cars travelling at excessive speeds on residential streets. For this reason, the local residents' association, the York Mills Valley Association (YMVA), has requested on behalf of the neighbourhood that the speed limit be reduced to 30 km/h on four streets: Brookfield Road, Plymbridge Crescent, Plymbridge Road, and Donino Avenue.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit on Brookfield Road, between Old Yonge Street/Donino Avenue and Plymbridge Crescent, from 40 km/h to 30km/h. 2. Reduced the speed limit on Plymbridge Crescent, between Brookfield Road and Plymbridge Road, from 40km/h to 30km/h. 3. Reduced the speed limit on Plymbridge Road, between Donwoods Drive and May Tree Road, from 40 km/h to 30km/h. 4. Reduced the speed limit on Donino Avenue, between Mill Street/Brookfield Road and the south end of Donino Avenue, from 40 km/h to 30km/h.
