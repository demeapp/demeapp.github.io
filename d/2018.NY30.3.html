What the item proposed

The applicant has appealed the applications to amend the Official Plan and former North York Zoning By-law and for Site Plan Control for the site at 4800 Yonge Street due to Council's failure to make a decision within the prescribed time period under the Planning Act. A prehearing conference was held on October 26, 2017 and a 9 day hearing has been scheduled to begin November 20, 2018.
