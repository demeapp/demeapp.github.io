Short-Term Parking - Wilson Heights Boulevard

PostponedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing stopping prohibition in effect at all times on the east side of Wilson Heights Boulevard, between a point 154 metres north of Reiner Road and the north end of Wilson Heights.

Council put off deciding this item.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to designate a short-term parking area at the north end of Wilson Heights Boulevard. The proposed short-term parking area will assist pick-up/drop-off activity for a daycare centre located at 777 Wilson Heights Boulevard. This designation will not adversely impact traffic or parking operations on Wilson Heights Boulevard.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Deferred consideration of the item until its meeting on July 4, 2018.
