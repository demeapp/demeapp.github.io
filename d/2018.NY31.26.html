Permanent Public Highway Closures for the Lawrence Heights Revitalization Project - Northeast District - Stage 1 - Bredonhill Court and Bagot Court; Stage 2 - Portion of Varna Drive and Polaris Lane

AdoptedJun 26, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to authorize the permanent closure of the public highways named Bredonhill Court and Bagot Court, shown as Parts 1 and 2 respectively on Sketch No. PS-2017-100 (the "Stage 1 Highways").

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services recommends that Bredonhill Court, Bagot Court, a portion of Varna Drive and Polaris Lane be permanently closed for incorporation into the Lawrence Heights Revitalization Project - Northeast District. A new public road network will be constructed to replace the above-noted closed public highways as part of the revitalization project.

What was decided

City Council on June 26, 27, 28 and 29, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the permanent closure of the public highways named Bredonhill Court and Bagot Court, shown as Parts 1 and 2 respectively on Sketch PS-2017-100 (the "Stage 1 Highways") and, following the dedication by plan of subdivision of a new east-west replacement public highway south of Varna Drive, the permanent closure of part of the Varna Drive public highway and the Polaris Lane public highway, shown as Parts 1 and 2 respectively on Sketch PS-2017-104 (the "Stage 2 Highways"), attached to the report (May 11, 2018) from the Acting Director, Transportation Services, North York District. 2. City Council enact the by-laws substantially in the form of the draft by-laws attached to the report (May 11, 2018) from the Acting Director, Transportation Services, North York District.
