What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision provided it is in keeping with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting authority to enter into an encroachment agreement with the owners of 55 De Vere Gardens for a 2.0 metres high wooden fence with a variable setback 1.8 metres to 2.1 metres from the roadway curb; a 3.5 metres high wooden pool shed with a length and width of 2.8 metres located inside the fenced area, setback 2.4 metres from the roadway curb on the Harley Avenue flankage of the subject premises. The fence on the public right of way does not meet the requirements of the City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks, Use of, and there is no provision for an encroaching shed on the public right of way. What was decided

North York Community Council: 1.
