Speed Limit Reduction - Beaverhall Drive and Banbury Road

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to reduce the speed limit from 40 km/h to 30 km/h on Beaverhall Drive, between Banbury Road and Bobwhite Crescent/Mellowood Drive.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Beaverhall Drive and Banbury Road are frequently used by schoolchildren and families walking to Windfields Junior High School. For this reason, Transportation Services staff have advised that they will not object to reducing the speed limit to 30km/h on Beaverhall Drive, between Banbury Road and Bannatyne Drive, and on Banbury Road between Pinnacle Road and Beaverhall Drive. This speed limit reduction is warranted due to the close proximity to a school, as outlined in the 30km/h Speed Limit Policy adopted by City Council in 2015, and falls within the overall strategic objectives of Toronto's Road Safety Plan.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 40 km/h to 30 km/h on Beaverhall Drive, between Banbury Road and Bobwhite Crescent/Mellowood Drive. 2. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on Beaverhall Drive, between Bobwhite Crescent/Mellowood Drive and Bannatyne Drive. 3. Reduced the speed limit from 40 km/h to 30 km/h on Banbury Road, between Beaverhall Drive and Portsmith Road. 4. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on Banbury Road, between Pinnacle Road and Portsmith Road.
