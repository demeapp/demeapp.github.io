The official record

What the item proposed

In response to community concerns regarding pedestrian safety and vehicle speeding, North York Community Council approved speed reductions and increased signage on Roselawn Avenue. Despite these changes vehicles continue to speed on Roselawn between Dufferin Street and Marlee Avenue. This stretch of Roselawn does not have stop signs or traffic lights, which further compromises the safety of all road users. In addition, there is a high volume of pedestrian crossings because of local park access and a busy school bus pick-up / drop-off zone. These conditions make this stretch of roadway incredibly unsafe for pedestrians. A review of possible pedestrian safety measures is required to address local safety concerns and meet the City's Vision Zero objectives

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Directed Transportation Services to conduct a traffic study, and report back once traffic study is completed, on Roselawn Avenue between Dufferin Street and Marlee Avenue to improve pedestrian safety and meet Vision Zero objectives, and this review should consider all possible safety measures, including but not limited to: a. installing an all-way stop at a one of the cross streets between Dufferin Street and Marlee Avenue; b. converting the existing crosswalk at Times Road to a signalized crossing; c. modifying the road where Roselawn Avenue turns into Castlefield Avenue at Salinas Court with the goal of enhancing pedestrian safety; d. adding photo radar and/or other measures used in school safety zones; and e. improving access to the south end of J.T. Watson Park.
