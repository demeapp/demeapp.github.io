Request for Parking Poll for Residents on Glen Long Avenue between Haven Road and Lansdowne Avenue

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to direct City Clerk's staff to conduct a poll for residents on Glen Long Avenue between Haven Road and Lansdowne Avenue in order to gauge support for changing the current parking regime on the north side.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Local residents have expressed concern regarding the existing parking regulations on Glen Long Avenue and have proposed making changes so that the condition on the north side of the street would match those in place on the south. This would see the regulations change such that the three-hour parking limit on the north side would be revoked and that parking from 8:00am to 4:00pm (Monday to Friday) would be prohibited.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Directed City Clerk's staff to conduct a poll for residents on Glen Long Avenue between Haven Road and Lansdowne Avenue in order to gauge support for changing the current parking regime on the north side of the street to match regulations in place on the south.
