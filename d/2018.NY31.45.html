Toronto City Council · Decision

Parking Regulations - Kenrae Road

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to authorize parking for a maximum period of one hour, from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Kenrae Road, between Laird Drive and a point 42 metres west.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I have received a request to allow one hour parking, Monday to Friday from 8:00am to 6:00pm, on north side of Kenrae Road so that adjacent businesses have short term parking for customers. I have consulted with affected properties and Transportation Services.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized parking for a maximum period of one hour, from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Kenrae Road, between Laird Drive and a point 42 metres west. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., except Sunday, on both sides of Kenrae Road, between Laird Drive and Randolph Road. 3. Prohibited parking from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Kenrae Road, between a point 42 metres west of Laird Drive and Randolph Road. 4. Prohibited parking from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Kenrae Road, between Laird Drive and Randolph Road.
