Toronto City Council · Decision

Corner Parking Prohibition - Highview Avenue

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to authorize the prohibition of parking at all times on the west side of Highview Avenue, between Downsview Avenue and a point 29 metres north.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents on Highview Avenue in the Downsview neighbourhood have reported concerns with motorists parking on the west side of Highview Avenue, in close proximity to the all-way stop sign controlled intersection with Downsview Avenue. These parked motorists impede turning vehicles at this intersection, which may include school buses. In order to deter this activity, it is recommended that parking be prohibited at all times on the west side of Highview Avenue, between Downsview Avenue and the first driveway on the west side of the street. The proposed changes will improve the flow of traffic and will result in the loss of approximately two on-street parking spaces. Transportation Services has been working with my office regarding the changes to the parking regulations on Highview Avenue and is in support of the recommendations.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized the prohibition of parking at all times on the west side of Highview Avenue, between Downsview Avenue and a point 29 metres north.
