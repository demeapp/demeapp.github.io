Preliminary Report - Zoning By-law Amendment Application - 37 Wilket Road

The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes to amend the Zoning By-law to permit five three-storey townhouse units in one block fronting onto Bayview Avenue at 37 Wilket Road. A concurrent application for consent has also been submitted to the Committee of Adjustment (B0020/18NY), in order to sever the lot (37 Wilket Road) into two parcels. The parcel to the north would contain the proposed townhouses and the parcel to the south would contain a new detached dwelling fronting onto Wilket Road. The existing detached dwelling at 37 Wilket Road would be demolished. This report provides preliminary information on the above-noted application and seeks Community Council's directions on further processing of the application and on the community consultation process. What was decided

North York Community Council directed that: 1. City Planning staff schedule a community consultation meeting for the lands at 37 Wilket Road together with the Ward Councillor. 2. City Planning staff give notice for the community consultation meeting to landowners and residents within 120 metres of the site, and that the notice area of the community consultation meeting be extended to include, in addition to the 120 metre radius notice area, all landowners and residents in the area bounded by Glenridge Avenue and Valley Ridge Place in the west, Bayview Ridge in the south, Windfields Park in the east, and York Mills Road in the north, and that the applicant pay the City for the costs associated with extending the notice area. 3. City Clerk's staff give notice for the public meeting under the Planning Act according to the regulations of the Planning Act.
