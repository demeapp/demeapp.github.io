Short-Term Parking - Blessed Margherita of Cittá di Castello Catholic School

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing parking prohibition for a maximum period of 15 minutes at all times regulation on the north side of Spenvalley Drive, between Yatescastle Drive and a point 120 metres east.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting North York Community Council to authorize the extension of the length of the short-term parking area on the north side of Spenvalley Drive, east of Yatescastle Drive, to provide additional on-street parking for Blessed Margherita of Cittá di Castello Catholic School. The proposed parking amendments will provide an additional eight on-street parking spaces for student pick-up/drop-offs and will not negatively impact the two-way flow of traffic on Spenvalley Drive.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition for a maximum period of 15 minutes at all times regulation on the north side of Spenvalley Drive, between Yatescastle Drive and a point 120 metres east. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition at all times regulation on the north side of Spenvalley Drive, between a point 120 metres east of Yatescastle Drive and Watney Crescent (west intersection). 3. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 15 minutes at all times on the north side of Spenvalley Drive, between Yatescastle Drive and the west side of Markay Street. 4. Prohibited parking at all times on the north side of Spenvalley Drive, between the west side of Markay Street and Watney Crescent (west intersection).
