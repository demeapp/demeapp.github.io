The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides preliminary information and raises issues for follow-up with regard to the applications. It indicates the expected timing for a Final Report to City Council. The application has been circulated to all appropriate agencies and City divisions for comment and a staff review of the application is underway. The application proposes a 29-storey residential building comprising 339 units (65% one-bedroom units, 24% two-bedroom units and 11% three-bedroom units) and the retention of the southern block of six townhouses at 15 Holmes Avenue. The proposal also includes the demolition of the existing two detached dwellings (19 and 21 Holmes Avenue) and two blocks of townhouses on the east and west border of the site to accommodate the new development. North York Community Council: 1. Directed City Clerk's staff to give notice for the public meeting under the Planning Act according to the regulations of the Planning Act. 2. Given the proximity to existing buildings, requested the applicant to comply fully with the height limit in the Secondary Plan. 3. Requested the applicant to make better use of its entire site in order to potentially reduce heights and/or minimize impacts on adjacent buildings. 4. Directed that when evaluating requests for exemptions to the Official Plan for density incentives, staff only positively consider those requests which provide demonstrated benefit to the public and which could be shown to be desirable on similar sites within the Secondary Plan area. 5.
