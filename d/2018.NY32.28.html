Speed Limit Reductions - York University and Downsview Neighbourhoods

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to reduce the speed limit from 50 km/h to 40 km/h on Shoreham Drive, between Murray Ross Parkway and a point 150 metres west.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting North York Community Council authorize a speed limit reduction from 50 km/h to 40 km/h on the following streets in the York University and Downsview neighbourhoods: - Shoreham Drive, between Murray Ross Parkway and a point 150 metres west; - Sentinel Road, between Stilecroft Drive (north intersection) and Sheppard Avenue West; - Dovehouse Avenue, between Sentinel Road and a point 150 metres west of Sharpecroft Boulevard; and - the entire length of Evelyn Wiggins Drive. What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 40 km/h on Shoreham Drive, between Murray Ross Parkway and a point 150 metres west. 2. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 40 km/h on Sentinel Road, between Stilecroft Drive (north intersection) and Sheppard Avenue West. 3. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 40 km/h on Dovehouse Avenue, between Sentinel Road and a point 150 metres west of Sharpecroft Boulevard. 4. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 40 km/h on Evelyn Wiggins Drive, between Assiniboine Road and Murray Ross Parkway.
