Speed Limit Reduction - Wilmington Avenue

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to reduce the speed limit from 50 km/h to 40 km/h on Wilmington Avenue, between Acton Avenue and a Sheppard Avenue West.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting North York Community Council authorize a speed limit reduction from 50 km/h to 40 km/h on Wilmington Avenue, between Acton Avenue and Sheppard Avenue West. The implementation of a 40 km/h speed limit will be consistent with the adjacent segments on this street and adjacent streets. The reduced speed limit will promote reduced travel speeds and provide a safer environment for pedestrians and cyclists, which includes students destined to/from nearby schools. The reduced speed limit will complement the upcoming approval of bicycle facilities on Wilmington Avenue and Faywood Boulevard, scheduled for installation within the next several years.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 40 km/h on Wilmington Avenue, between Acton Avenue and Sheppard Avenue West.
