Short-Term Parking - Ridley Boulevard

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to authorize parking for a maximum period of 10 minutes, 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Ridley Boulevard, between Old Orchard Grove and a point 85 metres north.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting North York Community Council to authorize the installation of a short-term parking area on the west side of Ridley Boulevard, north of Old Orchard Grove, to provide on-street parking for the day nursery schools that operate out of St. Timothy Church. The proposed parking amendments will provide approximately 12 on-street parking spaces for pick-up/drop-offs during the morning arrival time and will not negatively impact the two-way flow of traffic on Ridley Boulevard.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 10 minutes, 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Ridley Boulevard, between Old Orchard Grove and a point 85 metres north.
