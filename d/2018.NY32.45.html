Traffic Calming - Briar Hill Avenue

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to direct City Clerks to poll eligible householders on Briar Hill Avenue, between Avenue Road and Chaplin Crescent, to determine whether residents support the installation of traffic calming.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services staff have reviewed the need for traffic calming on Briar Hill Avenue, between Avenue Road and Chaplin Crescent, to address residents' concerns regarding vehicle speeds. Our assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the traffic calming policy has been satisfied. Therefore, traffic calming should be installed on the subject section of Briar Hill Avenue, subject to positive results of the traffic calming poll.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Directed City Clerks to poll eligible householders on Briar Hill Avenue, between Avenue Road and Chaplin Crescent, to determine whether residents support the installation of traffic calming, in accordance with the City of Toronto Traffic Calming Policy. 2. Subject to favourable results of the poll, directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway on Briar Hill Avenue, between Avenue Road and Chaplin Crescent, for traffic calming purposes, generally as shown in the speed hump plan that Transportation Services circulates to residents during the polling process.
