Toronto City Council · Decision

Parking Prohibition - Hillcrest Avenue

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to prohibit parking at all times on the south side of Hillcrest Avenue, between Doris Avenue and a point 24 metres west.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting North York Community Council approve the prohibition of parking on the south side of Hillcrest Avenue, between Doris Avenue and a point 24 metres west. The proposed parking prohibition will enhance the existing 15 metre long corner parking prohibition and will improve east west traffic flow at the intersection of Hillcrest Avenue and Doris Avenue. This change will result in the loss of two pay-and-display parking spaces on the south side of Hillcrest Avenue.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking at all times on the south side of Hillcrest Avenue, between Doris Avenue and a point 24 metres west. 2. Rescinded the existing parking machine regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday and from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday, for a maximum period of three hours at the rate of $4.00 per hour, on the south side of Hillcrest Avenue, between a point 89 metres east of Yonge Street and a point 15 metres west of Doris Avenue. 3. Authorized a parking machine regulation to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday and from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday, for a maximum period of three hours at the rate of $4.00 per hour, on the south side of Hillcrest Avenue, between a point 89 metres east of Yonge Street and a point 24 metres west of Doris Avenue.
