Parking Amendments - Cherokee Public School

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing school bus loading zone in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., daily, on the south side of Cherokee Boulevard.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting North York Community Council to approve amendments to the parking regulations on the south side of Cherokee Boulevard, in the vicinity of Cherokee Public School, to extend the existing school bus loading zone by 40 metres. The proposed parking amendments are feasible and will accommodate the increase in school buses servicing Cherokee Public School. The school bus loading zone will be extended east into the area where parking is currently allowed for up to 10 minutes, from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday. What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing school bus loading zone in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., daily, on the south side of Cherokee Boulevard, between a point opposite the east limit of Apache Trail and a point 38 metres east. 2. Rescinded the existing 10 minute maximum parking limit, in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Cherokee Boulevard, between Shawnee Circle (northwest intersection) and a point 215 metres east. 3. Authorized a school bus loading zone to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., daily, on the south side of Cherokee Boulevard, between Apache Trail and a point 78 metres east. 4.
