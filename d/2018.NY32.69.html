The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has assessed an appeal from the owner of 633 Lauder Avenue for front yard parking. Front yard parking at this location is not recommended because it does not meet the criteria of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 918 in that the Code does not permit the licensing of a front yard parking pad where on-street permit parking is authorized on the opposite side of the street and is less than 90% allocated. The owner will be given an opportunity to make a deputation before Community Council.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Granted the appeal and approved front yard parking at 633 Lauder Avenue, with the following conditions: a. that the parking area be 2.2 metres in width and a minimum of 5.3 metres and not exceed 5.9 metres in length; b. that the applicant disconnect any downspouts, if feasible, or seek an exemption from Toronto Water in accordance with the requirements in Chapter 681-11(S); c. that the applicant provide the landscape features, including the planting of a tree on site, substantially in accordance with the plan as indicated in Attachment 'A', attached to the report (June, 8, 2018) from the Manager, Right of Way Management, Transportation Services, to the satisfaction of the General Manager of Transportation Services; and d. that the applicant pay all applicable fees and comply with all other criteria set out in the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter…
