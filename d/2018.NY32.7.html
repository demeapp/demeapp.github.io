The official record

What the item proposed

This Zoning By-law Amendment application proposes to partially lift the Holding (H) Symbol on the site in order to increase the maximum gross floor area of the entire site from 259,798 square metres to 267,919 square metres to accommodate a new wing addition for a brain sciences facility at 2075 Bayview Avenue, Sunnybrook Hospital. The proposed addition would be approximately 8,121 square metres and connected to the existing main building (the E wing). The proposed development is consistent with the Provincial Policy Statement (2014) and conforms and does not conflict with the Growth Plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe (2017). The proposed addition would be integrated with the existing Sunnybrook health facility and would support the objectives of the PPS (2014) and the Growth Plan (2017) by improving an existing healthcare facility that will help improve the lives of patients.
