Toronto City Council · Decision

Request for Implementation of All Way Stop at Glen Rush Boulevard and Frontenac Avenue

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to authorize all-way compulsory stop at the intersection of Glen Rush Boulevard and Frontenac Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This intersection is located at a primary entrance to Yeshiva Yesodei Hatorah. The need for a stop sign at this intersection was just brought to my attention by the school community as construction of the new school is close to being finalized. In September, over 600 students will walk to this school, many of them through this intersection. Unfortunately, with the Council break there will not be another opportunity to consider the all-way stop until 2019. In order to ensure the safety of the students and control the flow of traffic in front of the school, I am requesting that this all-way stop be implemented.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized all-way compulsory stop at the intersection of Glen Rush Boulevard and Frontenac Avenue.
