Toronto City Council · Decision

Request For Implementation Of All Way Stop At Old Orchard Grove And Grey Road

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to authorize all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Old Orchard Grove and Grey Road.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Old Orchard Playground is located adjacent to the intersection and has seen a major increase in use since we implemented the new playground late last year. I have requested an All Way Stop study for this location but due to recent water main construction on the street, we haven’t been able to conduct this study in advance of this July 2018 meeting of North York Community Council. With the Council break until 2019, I am asking that we put this additional stop sign in place to help direct the flow of traffic and protect the hundreds of residents that use this intersection to access the park.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Old Orchard Grove and Grey Road.
