The official record

What the item proposed

I am requesting that City Council approve the naming of the outdoor space at 75 The Donway West as "West Park", in recognition of the civic engagement and community contributions of Terry West, the former President of the Don Mills Residents Inc. (DMRI). The Property Naming Policy was approved by City Council in July 2015 and came into effect in October 2015. It establishes the criteria, process, and community consultation requirements to name or rename a City property, including naming a City property after an individual or organization. Terry West has been an integral part of the DMRI and the Don Mills community for many years. Terry was the President of the DMRI for over 15 years and is the DMRI representative on umbrella groups such as Federation of North Toronto Resident Associations (FoNTRA). Terry continues to represent the Don Mills community in all matters related to planning…

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Requested staff to report back to the North York Community Council at its next meeting on a request to name the outdoor space at 75 The Donway West as "West Park".
