Blythwood Junior Public School Road Safety Measures

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing 10 minute maximum parking limit, in effect from 8:30 a.m. to 9:30 a.m., 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m., 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Dear Chair and Colleagues, North York District Traffic Operations staff conducted a detailed traffic study at Blythwood Junior Public School in response to several requests from the Traffic & Safety Committee. The recommendations listed were proposed by City staff to improve safety for students and families attending Blythwood Junior Public School. The Traffic & Safety Committee conducted extensive consultation with parents of Blythwood Public School students. Unfortunately, this consultation process delayed Transportation Services from submitting a formal report for consideration at the July 4, 2018 meeting of North York Community Council.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing 10 minute maximum parking limit, in effect from 8:30 a.m. to 9:30 a.m., 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m., 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Strathgowan Crescent, between a point 54 metres north of Blythwood Road and Strathgowan Avenue. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition, in effect from 9:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m., 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Strathgowan Crescent, between a point 54 metres north of Blythwood Road and Strathgowan Avenue. 3. Rescinded the existing standing prohibition, in effect from 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Strathgowan Crescent, between Blythwood Road and a point 54 metres north. 4.
