The official record

What the item proposed

I attended the Lawrence Park Ratepayers Association (LPRA) board meeting on May 29, 2018. The LPRA board debated whether it was in the best interests of LP residents and everyone who uses LP roads to reduce the posted speed limit to 30km/hr on local, residential roads. They took into account: - the results of a recent neighbourhood road safety survey; - recently completed petitions for nine (9) local streets plus three more petitions in-progress; and, - that three local roads and one other road segment already had a posted 30km/hr speed limit. The LPRA Board concluded that there was sufficient need and support for the reduction of the posted speed limit to 30km/hr and subsequently adopted a motion to reflect that conclusion.
