The official record

What the item proposed

The York Beltline Trail is a critical recreational asset in our community. After many years of hard work, the trail will be extended this summer from Walter Saunders Park to Marlee Ave. Despite significant progress, many sections of the trail remain in need of upgrades and repairs. This motion identifies deficiencies and actions for improvement along the existing portion of the trail.

What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1.
