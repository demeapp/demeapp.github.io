Toronto City Council · Decision

Relocation of Traffic Control Signals from Diana Drive to Maryport Avenue

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The North York Community Council was asked to request that Transportation Services investigate the feasibility of relocating the traffic signals on Keele Street at Diana Drive to Keele Street at Maryport Avenue and report back to North York Community.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Due to the approval of a new, sizeable condominium development at 3100 Keele Street, at the corner of Maryport Avenue, and with a pedestrian/cycling connection to Downsview Park on the east side of Keele Street, it would be beneficial to investigate the possibility of relocating the traffic control signals from Diana Drive to Maryport Avenue.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Requested the Acting Director, Transportation Services, North York District, to investigate the feasibility of relocating the traffic signals on Keele Street at Diana Drive to Keele Street at Maryport Avenue and report back to North York Community Council in February 2019.
