Naming of Unassumed Street Between Rean Drive and Barberry Place as "Karen's Way"

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Dear colleagues of North York Community Council. There is a new unassumed street being constructed one block south of Sheppard Avenue East, between Rean Drive and Barberry Place, that needs a street name. Councillor Shiner recommends that That North York Community Council approve the naming of the unassumed street between Rean Drive and Barberry Place as "Karen's Way".

The official record

What the item proposed

Dear colleagues of North York Community Council. There is a new unassumed street being constructed one block south of Sheppard Avenue East, between Rean Drive and Barberry Place, that needs a street name. The existing unassumed roadway runs east to west between Rean Drive and Barberry Place, south of Sheppard Ave. East and east of Bayview Ave. There is a plan in place to widen this road for full public access and eventually to extend it one block west to Kenaston Gardens. Staff in Engineering and Construction Services have previously identified the need for a street name. Your support of this request would be greatly appreciated.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Approved the naming of the unassumed street between Rean Drive and Barberry Place as "Karen's Way". 2. Directed that the recommended name be referred to Engineering and Constructions Services to confirm that Police, Fire Services, and EMS have no objections to the proposed name. 3. Directed that public notice be given and the necessary By-law be prepared for this name to be considered at the first meeting of the North York Community Council in the new term of Council.
