What the item proposed

Portions of a new residential development within the Stanley Greene neighbourhood in the Downsview Lands have recently been constructed. This development is located on the east side of Keele Street, between Wilson Avenue and Sheppard Avenue West, and includes a number of new streets in an internal road network. The development is comprised mainly of townhouses, as well as some single detached homes, located on the west side of Stanley Greene Boulevard. These homes were constructed with substandard garages that can only accommodate one small vehicle. Accordingly, the majority of residents living in this neighbourhood are required to park their vehicles on the street. Currently, residents are parking on both sides of the street, potentially impeding the two-way flow of traffic, as well as emergency vehicles.
