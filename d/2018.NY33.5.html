Reopening of North York Community Council Item NY26.22, headed, "Traffic Calming - King High Avenue and Allingham Gardens"

AdoptedJul 23, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The staff recommendation is that North Community Council re-open Item NY26.22, entitled "Traffic Calming - King High Avenue and Allingham Gardens".

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting held on November 14, 2017, North York Community Council adopted without amendment Item NY26.22 and, in so doing, denied the installation of speed humps on King High Avenue and Allingham Gardens. As the staff recommendations do not reflect the requirements of the residents on these streets, it is recommended that the Item be re-opened and amended to address these concerns.

What was decided

North Community Council: 1. Deleted Parts 1 and 2 of the decision on Item NY26.22 and adopted instead the following: "1. The Acting Director of Transportation Services, North York District request the City Clerk to poll eligible householders on King High Avenue, between Invermay Avenue and Allingham Gardens, and on Allingham Gardens, between King High Avenue and Wilson Avenue, to determine whether residents support the installation of traffic calming, in accordance with the City of Toronto Traffic Calming Policy. 2.
