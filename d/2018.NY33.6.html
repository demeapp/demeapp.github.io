Loading Zone on Stayner Avenue at Marlee Avenue

The official record

What the item proposed

Earlier this year I submitted a request to Transportation Services to review the feasibility of establishing a commercial loading zone on the south side of Stayner Avenue west of Marlee Avenue in order to address the frequent issuance of parking tickets to vehicles making daily deliveries to Zito's Marketplace. There is currently no on-site loading space large enough for daily deliveries to the Marketplace as the curb lane on Marlee Avenue is occupied by a painted bike lane. A designated loading zone will help address these concerns. I have consulted with Toronto Police Services 13 Division's Parking Enforcement team who support this effort to establish a commercial loading zone on Stayner Avenue west of Marlee Avenue.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Designated a Commercial Loading Zone to operate at all times on the south side of Stayner Avenue, between Marlee Avenue and a point 27 metres west.
