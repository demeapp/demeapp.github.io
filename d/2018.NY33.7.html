The official record

What the item proposed

The principal of Cummer Valley Middle School has expressed concerns with the lack of on-street parking for parents dropping-off and picking-up students, as well as parents parking illegally on both sides of Maxome Avenue and U-turning in front of the school where students cross. Also, the principal has advised that the school bus loading zone in front of the school is no longer required as buses use the circular driveway in front of the school. Rescinding the existing parking prohibition on Maxome Avenue in front of the school and implementing a 10 minute parking limit during school arrival and dismissal times would address the parking concerns. Prohibiting U-turns at all times on Maxome Avenue, between Gustav Crescent and Laredo Court, would address potential conflicts between U-turning motorists and students crossing Maxome Avenue. What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday on the west side of Maxome Avenue, between a point 61 metres south of Donna Court and a point 244 metres south of Donna Court. 2. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 10 minutes, from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and from 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Maxome Avenue, between Gustav Crescent and a point 166 metres further south. 3. Rescinded the School Bus loading zone in effect from 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m., on the west side of Maxome Avenue, between a point opposite the south limit of Gustav Crescent and a point 60 metres further south. 4. Prohibited northbound and southbound U-turns at all times on Maxome Avenue, between Gustav Crescent and Laredo Court.
