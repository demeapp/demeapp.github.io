The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks the approval of the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority ("TPA") to further amend and extend a lease agreement (the "Lease") with The Beaton Group Inc. (the "Tenant") related to a City owned Toronto Parking Authority property located at 21 Connell Court (the "Property"). As part of Toronto Parking Authority's acquisition of the Property in June 2017, a lease-back provision was negotiated with the Tenant (formerly the owner of the Property). The Tenant was requested an initial lease extension expiring March 3, 2018, and has requested a further extension of 2 months and 28 days expiring May 31, 2018. The City's Real Estate Services Division ("RES") has reviewed and are supportive of the proposed extension. City Legal has been engaged to prepare and review the necessary Lease amending and extension document.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized staff to enter into a further Lease Amending and Extension Agreement with The Beaton Group Inc. with respect to 21 Connell Court, in accordance with the terms and conditions as generally outlined in Appendix A: Lease Amending and Extension Agreement of the report (March 20, 2018) from the Acting President, and on such other or amended terms and conditions as may be acceptable to the Acting President and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor. 2.
