Lease Agreement Extension - Infoware Canada Inc. - 50 Richmond Street East, Unit 500

AdoptedApr 16, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto Parking Authority was asked to the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority authorize staff to enter into a Lease Extending Agreement ("Lease Extension") with Infoware Canada Inc. (the "Tenant").

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks the approval of the Toronto Parking Authority ("TPA") Board to extend an existing lease (the "Lease") with Infoware Canada Inc. (the "Tenant") at 50 Richmond Street East, Unit 500 (the "Property"). The proposed term of the Lease extension is one (1) year with an option to extend for one (1) further year. City Real Estate Services and City Legal have been engaged to provide oversight on the process and document preparation.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized staff to enter into a Lease Extending Agreement ("Lease Extension") with Infoware Canada Inc. (the "Tenant") at 50 Richmond Street East, Unit 500 (the "Property"), in accordance with the terms and conditions of the original lease and the amended terms as set out in Table 1.0 - Summary of Terms and Conditions, of the report (March 27, 2018), from the Acting President, and on such other or amended terms and conditions as may be acceptable to the Acting President and in a form acceptable to the City Solicitor.
