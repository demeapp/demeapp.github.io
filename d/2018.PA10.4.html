Parking Management Renewal Agreement - CreateTO (on behalf of TEDCo/TPLC) -115 Unwin Avenue (Municipal Carpark 243)

AdoptedApr 16, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto Parking Authority was asked to the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority authorize staff to enter into a parking management renewal agreement with CreateTO to continue Toronto Parking Authority.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks the approval of the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority ("TPA") to enter into a parking management renewal agreement (the "Agreement") with CreateTO (on behalf of the City of Toronto Economic Development Corporation (TEDCo) o/a Toronto Port Lands Company (TPLC)) (the "Owner") in order to continue Toronto Parking Authority management of the 123-space surface parking lot located at 115 Unwin Avenue (the "Property"), known as Municipal Carpark 243, for a further two (2) year term, in accordance with the terms and conditions outlined in Table 1.0 - Summary of Parking Management Renewal Agreement, and Appendix C - Toronto Parking Authority and Owner Obligations, of this report. City Legal has been engaged to review the necessary documents.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorize staff to enter into a parking management renewal agreement with CreateTO to continue Toronto Parking Authority management of the 123-space surface parking lot located at 115 Unwin Avenue, known as Municipal Carpark 243, in accordance with the terms and conditions as generally outlined in Table 1.0 - Summary of Parking Management Renewal Agreement, and Appendix C - Toronto Parking Authority and Owner Obligations, of the report (March 28, 2018) from the Acting President, and on such other or amended terms and conditions as may be acceptable to the Acting President and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor.
