Toronto Parking Authority was asked to the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority grant authority to award the Contract for the construction of Municipal Carpark No. 279, located at 287.

The official record

What the item proposed

Design drawings for the project were prepared by Marton & Smith Landscape Architects on behalf of Toronto Parking Authority. Tenders were prepared by Toronto Parking Authority to include all technical requirements, material and labour for the construction of Carpark No. 279. Toronto Parking Authority tendered the work to six (6) pre-qualified general contractors on March 7, 2018. The call closed on March 27, 2018 and one (1) bid was received and considered as listed below in the ascending order without the HST. No Bid Bevcon construction & Paving Ltd. No Bid M+S Architectural Ltd. No Bid Alto Restoration Ltd. No Bid

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. granted authority to staff to award the Contract for the construction of Municipal Carpark No. 279, located at 287 Rushton Road, in the amount of $423,718.50 plus $26,281.50 as a contingency allowance, being the sum total amount of $450,000.00 net of all taxes and charges, to Melrose Paving Co. Ltd., having submitted the lowest compliant bid and meeting all specifications in conformance with the tender requirements.
