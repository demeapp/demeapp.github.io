The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto Parking Authority ("TPA") provides comprehensive operations and maintenance services to Toronto Transit Commission ("TTC") for its commuter parking facilities pursuant to a parking services agreement ("Original Agreement"). This arrangement has been continuous since 2002. The most recent ten (10)-year contract expired June 30, 2017 and was further extended to June 30, 2018, in order for Toronto Transit Commission and Toronto Parking Authority to work toward formalizing the terms and conditions of a new parking services agreement. Since commencing negotiations in October 2016, Toronto Transit Commission and Toronto Parking Authority have now settled the major terms and conditions of a parking services agreement renewal ("Renewal Agreement") whereby Toronto Parking Authority would continue to provide comprehensive operations and maintenance services to Toronto Transit…

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized Toronto Parking Authority staff to enter a Parking Facility Management Agreement ("Agreement") or such other form of agreement as may be acceptable to the City Solicitor, with the Toronto Transit Commission ("TTC") for operation and maintenance of the Commission's commuter parking facilities for a term of ten (10) years, generally in accordance with the parameters set out in the section, "Terms and Conditions", including in Table 1.0 - "Summary of Major Terms and Conditions" and as further detailed in Appendix 'A' - "Toronto Transit Commission and Toronto Parking Authority Responsibilities and Obligations", of the report (April 30, 2018) from the Acting President and any such other amended terms and conditions as may be acceptable to the Acting President and the City Solicitor.
