The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks the approval of the Toronto Parking Authority ("TPA") Board to enter into a parking management agreement ("Agreement") with 2294690 Ontario Ltd. and 2289369 Ontario Ltd. ("Owner") to manage a 24-space surface parking facility located at 5203 to 5215 Yonge Street (the "Subject Property"), in accordance with the terms and conditions outlined in Table 1.0 - Proposed Terms and Conditions, and Appendix B - Toronto Parking Authority and Owner Obligations, of this report. The property is directly adjacent to a 50-space Toronto Parking Authority municipal surface parking lot at 10 Kingsdale Avenue (Carpark 400). The proposed arrangement would facilitate integration of the operation of the two facilities, thereby increasing the total number of public parking spaces to 74. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized staff to enter into a parking management agreement ("Agreement") with 2294690 Ontario Ltd. and 2289369 Ontario Ltd. ("Owner") to manage the 24-space surface parking facility located at 5203 to 5215 Yonge Street, ("Subject Property") for municipal parking purposes in accordance with the terms and conditions as outlined in Table 1.0 - Proposed Terms and Conditions, and Appendix B - Toronto Parking Authority and Owner Obligations contained in the report (April 26, 2018) from the Acting President, and any such other or amended terms and conditions as may be acceptable to the Acting President and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor.
