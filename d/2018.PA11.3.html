The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto Parking Authority, like any organization, has established a range of Board- enacted Policies which form the guiding principles and procedures by which it operates. The Policies are comprehensively reviewed on a biannual basis, but in some instances, other factors lead to a need for particular attention. This report outlines proposed adjustments to three specific Toronto Parking Authority policies under the "Parking Charges" category, namely: - Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 2-6: Cost Recovery - Street Occupancy Permits at On-Street Meter Locations - Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 2-12: Cost Recovery - Permanent Removal of On-Street Meter Locations; and - Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 2-1: Parking Rates - Off-Street Facilities. The proposed changes are "housekeeping" in nature and do not fundamentally alter the intent, or introduce new…

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Approved amendments to Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolutions as follows: a) Policy Resolution 2-1 - Parking Rates - Off-Street Facilities, shown in Appendix A of the report (April 30, 2018) from the Acting President; b) Policy Resolution 2-6 - Cost Recovery - Street Occupancy Permits at On-Street Meter Locations, shown in Appendix B of the report (April 30, 2018) from Acting President; and c) Policy Resolution 2-12 Cost Recovery - Permanent Removal of On-Street Meter Locations, shown in Appendix C of the report (April 30, 2018) from the Acting President. 2. Directed staff to enact the amendments as approved in Part 1 above.
