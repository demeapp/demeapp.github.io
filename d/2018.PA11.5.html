Toronto Parking Authority - 2018 Results of Follow-up of Previous Audit Recommendations

AdoptedMay 15, 2018 · Toronto City Council

This is an information item: On an annual basis, the Auditor General reviews the implementation status of outstanding audit recommendations and reports the review results to the City Council through the Audit Committee. This report provides the recommendation follow-up results for the following two Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) reports: - Toronto Parking Authority – Phase 1: Audit of Real Estate Activities, 2015 - Toronto Parking Authority Phase 2: Audit of the Revenue Operations of Off-Street Controlled Facilities, 2016. No decision is attached.
