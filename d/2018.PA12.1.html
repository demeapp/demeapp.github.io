The official record

What the item proposed

This report presents the audited financial statements for the year ended December 31, 2017 to the Toronto Parking Authority Board for approval and provides highlights of the 2017 financial results. PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP ("PWC") has substantially completed their audit of the Toronto Parking Authority's financial statements for the year ended December 31, 2017. A draft of these financial statements along with a copy of PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP's year-end report to the Board of Directors is attached. Our audit partner Terri McKinnon will be attending the June 25, 2018 Board meeting to provide an update on the status of their work as well as a summary on significant accounting and financial reporting matters dealt with during the audit process.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Approved the audited financial statements of the Toronto Parking Authority for the year ended December 31, 2017.
