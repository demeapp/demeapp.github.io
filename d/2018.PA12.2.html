Fire Alarm / Sprinkler System Upgrades - Contract Award - Carparks 42 (91 Via Italia) and 111 (74 Clinton Street)

AdoptedJun 25, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto Parking Authority was asked to grant authority to the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, to award the Contract for the Fire Alarm and Sprinkler System Upgrades for Carparks 42 and 111.

The official summary names a figure of $599,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

A tender package including all technical requirements, material and labour for the Fire Alarm and Sprinkler Upgrades was prepared by Morrison Hershfield Limited, as previously approved by the Toronto Parking Authority Board (Minute No. 17-076, May 29, 2017). Morrison Hershfield Limited tendered the work on behalf of Toronto Parking Authority to five (5) pre-qualified general contractors on April, 2018. The call closed on May 17, 2018 and four (4) bids were received and considered as listed below in the ascending order (amounts shown not including HST). Table 1 - Summary of Bids Received (net of applicable taxes) General Contractors Amount Percentage General Sprinklers Inc. $599,000.00 100 Classic Fire Protection Inc. $620,000.00 104 Paul and Douglas Automatic Sprinklers Ltd. $649,000.00 108 Superior Sprinkler Co. Ltd. $659,000.00 110 Forest City Fire Protection No Bid

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Granted authority to the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, to award the Contract for the Fire Alarm and Sprinkler System Upgrades for Carparks 42 and 111, located at 91 Via Italia and 74 Clinton Street respectively, in the amount of $599,000.00 plus $26,000.00 as a contingency allowance, being the sum total amount of $625,000.00 net of all taxes and charges, to General Sprinklers Inc., having submitted the lowest compliant bid and meeting all specifications in conformance with the tender requirements.
