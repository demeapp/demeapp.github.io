Rate Changes - Off-Street Municipal Parking Facilities - Municipal Carpark 424

AdoptedJun 25, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto Parking Authority was asked to approve the parking rate adjustment for Carpark 424 (2180 Bayview Avenue) as identified in Table 1 - Carpark 424 - Proposed Rate Adjustments, of the report (May 25, 2018) from the Acting President.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) reviews the parking rates at all of its off-street municipal "Green P" parking facilities annually, and at specific facilities experiencing atypical conditions on a more frequent basis as may be necessary. An increase in demand at Carpark 424 is affecting operations at this facility. Staff recommend that the Toronto Parking Authority Board authorize an adjustment in the parking rates at this parking facility, in order to address the emerging imbalances in supply and demand.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Approved the parking rate adjustment for Carpark 424 (2180 Bayview Avenue) as identified in Table 1 - Carpark 424 - Proposed Rate Adjustments, of the report (May 25, 2018) from the Acting President; and 2. Directed Toronto Parking Authority staff to implement the rate changes by July 31, 2018.
