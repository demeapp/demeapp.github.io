The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that the Toronto Parking Authority ("TPA") Board of Directors approve entering into a Master Agreement Adoption Agreement with Rogers Communications Canada Inc. ("Rogers") and the Province of Ontario, using Ontario’s Ministry of Government and Consumer Services Vendor of Record ("VOR") Program, for the delivery of wireless, mobile and data services and associated hardware, from the date of approval to January 31, 2020, with two 2-year extension options. The Minister of Government Services entered into a Master Agreement with Rogers as Vendor of Record OSS-00415819 effective February 1, 2014 as a result of being the highest scoring proponent from the Province's competitive call process. The Toronto Parking Authority wishes to enter into a separate agreement from the date of approval to January 31, 2020 in the amount of $200,275 inclusive of operating and one-time…

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, to enter into a Master Agreement ("Agreement") Adoption Agreement with Rogers and the Province of Ontario for use of the Vendor of Record Agreement No. OSS-00415819; and 2. Authorized the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, to grant a contract award to Rogers Communications Canada Inc. as Vendor Of Record, for the delivery of mobile and data services from the date of approval to January 31, 2020, with two 2-year extension options, on the same terms and conditions as the Province of Ontario's Agreement, and in a form satisfactory to the Acting President and City Solicitor.
