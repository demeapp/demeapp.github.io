What the item proposed

The Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) operates its off-street parking facilities under two basic formats - controlled or gated facilities, and uncontrolled facilities relying on proof-of-payment arrangements. The majority of facilities fall under the latter operation, comprising in the order of 200 sites and approximately 20,000 spaces. More specifically, customers have two choices for payment of the parking fees at these uncontrolled sites; namely, the purchase of a receipt from a pay-and-display machine, which then must be physically placed on the vehicle dash; or through a virtual payment using the Green P app on their mobile device. Enforcement is a key element of the program in encouraging payment compliance at these facilities. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized Toronto Parking Authority staff to enter into a purchase agreement with A.C.C.E.O. Solutions Inc. for the "gtechna" enforcement system, hardware including handheld ticketing devices and printers, associated custom application development, system implementation and training in an amount of $438,435.90, plus HST, and a contingency amount of ten percent (10%), plus HST. 2. Authorized Toronto Parking Authority staff to enter into a four (4) year (first year is under warranty) support agreement at an annual cost of $94,228.52, plus HST, annually for the four (4) years following Year One of the contract execution.
