The official record

What the item proposed

This report outlines proposed adjustments to two (2) specific Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) policies under the "Parking Charges" category, namely: - Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 2-6: Cost Recovery - Street Occupancy Permits at On-Street Meter Locations; and - Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 2-12: Cost Recovery - Permanent Removal of On-Street Meter Locations. The proposed changes are "housekeeping" in nature and do not fundamentally alter the intent, or introduce new policy direction. These items are related to cost recovery details.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Amended Policy Resolution 2-12 Cost Recovery - Permanent Removal of On-Street Meter Locations, shown in Appendix B to the report (August 8, 2018) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority by deleting the following: "If an on-street occupancy permit to permanently remove an on-street parking stall, where there is currently no on-street paid meter, the cost recover amount will still be charged. The closest hourly rate, which the cost recover is based on, will apply to the meter location in question." 2. Approved amendments to Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolutions, as amended by Part 1 above, as follows: a.
