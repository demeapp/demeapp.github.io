The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto Parking Authority ("TPA") will be revising its existing Letter of Understanding ("LOU") with the City of Toronto's Economic Development and Culture Division ("EDC") to: 1. Authorize Toronto Parking Authority to commence operating a newly constructed 49-space surface parking lot at the Fort York Visitors' Centre, 250 Fort York Boulevard ("FYVC"); and 2. Authorize Toronto Parking Authority to continue managing parking operations on the 132-space surface parking lot at 800 Fleet Street (Municipal Carpark 271). Operational management of the property was recently transferred from the City of Toronto's Real Estate Services Division ("RES") to Economic Development and Culture. Since 2012 Toronto Parking Authority has managed parking at this location under a Toronto Parking Authority/Real Estate Services Master Umbrella Parking Agreement ("MUPA"). What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Approved the new parking rate for the Fort York Visitors' Centre parking facility located at 250 Fort York Boulevard as identified in Table 1: Fort York Visitors' Centre Proposed Parking Rate to the report (August 9, 2018) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, for implementation as soon as the new parking facility commences operation. 2. Approved the parking rate adjustment for Carpark 271 (800 Fleet Street) as identified in Table 2: Carpark 271 - Proposed Rate Adjustments to the report (August 9, 2018) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, to be implemented by September 28, 2018.
