What the item proposed

The Toronto Parking Authority, like any organization, has established a range of Board- enacted Policies which form the guiding principles and procedures by which it operates. The Policies are comprehensively reviewed on a biannual basis, but in some instances, other factors lead to a need for particular attention in the interim. This report outlines proposed technical adjustments to Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolutions 5-8, Investment of Reserve Funds; and 5-9, Authorized Signatories Resolution. Board-approved Policy Resolution 5-8 contains redundant language that conflicts with another Board policy, namely Resolution 5-7, Procurement Policy, in that Board approval would be required to retain an investment manager, regardless of expenditure level. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Approved amendments to Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 5-8, Investment of Reserve Funds, to delete the requirement that retention of an investment manager, regardless of expenditure level, as shown in Appendix A of the report (October 5, 2018) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, and directed staff to enact the amendments as approved. 2. Approved amendments to Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 5-9, Authorized Signatories Resolution, to authorize a combination of authorized signatories to approve all cheque payments and any authorized signatory to approve electronic payments, as shown in Appendix B of the report (October 5, 2018) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, and directed staff to enact the amendments as approved.
