The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority Finance and Operations groups developed the scope of work and documents for the Request For Proposal call. The documents were prepared to include all technical requirements, material and labour to provide carbon monoxide maintenance and calibration work at the various Toronto Parking Authority sites three (3) times per year. The Request For Proposal documents were posted publicly on MERX public tendering network on April 19, 2018. Three (3) subsequent Addenda were issued and the call closed on July 3, 2018. Two (2) bids were received and considered. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Granted authority to award the Contract for the Carbon Monoxide Systems Preventative Maintenance Program (2018-2023) for a term of three (3) years plus optional two (2) year extension, in the total amount (5-years) of $307,160.00 plus $30,000 as a contingency allowance, being the sum total amount of $337,160.00, net of all taxes and charges, to Arjay Engineering Limited, having submitted the lowest price compliant submission and meeting all specifications in conformance with the Request for Proposals (RFP) requirements. For the first year of the contract, 2018-19, the contract amount will be $61,432.00 plus $6,000 as a contingency allowance, being the sum total of $67,432.00, net of all taxes and charges.
