What the item proposed

The Toronto Parking Authority has established a range of Board-enacted Policies which form the guiding principles and procedures by which it operates. The Policies are comprehensively reviewed on a biannual basis, but in some instances, other factors lead to a need for particular attention in the interim. On October 17, 2018, federal and provincial legislation relating to cannabis came into effect. Recreational use of cannabis was legalized by the Federal government and Bill 36 was passed in the provincial legislature, amending various statutes to adjust for the legalization of recreational cannabis. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Approved amendments to Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 4-11, Smoking, as shown in Appendix A to the report (November 9, 2018) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority and directed staff to enact the amended policy as approved. 2. Approved amendments to Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 4-13, Substance Abuse, as shown in Appendix B to the report (November 9, 2018) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority and directed staff to enact the amended policy as approved.
