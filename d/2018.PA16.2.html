The official record

What the item proposed

This report addresses the Toronto Association of Business Improvement Areas' ("TABIA") 2018 request for the Toronto Parking Authority's ("TPA") continued annual support of Toronto Association of Business Improvement Areas' citywide campaigns. For the past fourteen (14) years, the Toronto Parking Authority has provided annual funding for this Campaign, this year in the amount of $50,000, which plays an important part in allowing Toronto Association of Business Improvement Areas to offer affordable media to Toronto's Business Improvement Areas ("BIAs") and also aligns with Toronto Parking Authority's own marketing efforts. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Supported the Toronto Association of Business Improvement Areas' request for continued annual funding support, and approved a $50,000 contribution from the Toronto Parking Authority towards the 2018 Toronto Association of Business Improvement Areas Citywide Program.
