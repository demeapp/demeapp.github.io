The official record

What the item proposed

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place ("EP"), in February 2016 adopted recommendations of a Parking Consultant Report ("Report") prepared by WSP Canada Inc. including a recommendation for a substantial upgrade of the parking technology at Exhibition Place. EP staff have engaged the Toronto Parking Authority ("TPA"), in their detailed assessments in light of TPA's expertise, to address the parking technology deficiencies indicated in the Report. This report seeks TPA Board approval for a suitable form of agreement with EP, for TPA to provide consultancy, purchasing, installation and support services relating to the upgrade of parking technology at the Enercare Centre parking garage ("Enercare Garage"), and sixteen (16) parking booths throughout the grounds, such agreement to include the general terms and conditions set out in Appendix A, 'Terms and Conditions' to this report. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized staff to enter a Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU"), or such other form of agreement as may be acceptable to the City Solicitor, with The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place ("EP") for Toronto Parking Authority ("TPA") to provide consultancy, purchasing, installation, and support services relating to the parking technology at the Enercare Centre parking garage ("Enercare Garage"), and sixteen (16) parking booths throughout the grounds, the general terms and conditions of which are set out in Appendix A, 'Terms and Conditions' to the report (January 22, 2018) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, and any such other amended terms and conditions as may be acceptable to the Acting President and the City Solicitor.
