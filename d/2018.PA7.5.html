Official title: Update regarding the Investigation regarding the Auditor General's Observations of a Land Acquisition at Finch Avenue West and Arrow Road

The official record

What the item proposed

On July 10, 2017, the Board directed the Interim President to retain, on behalf of the Board, an independent third party, selected by the City Manager, to conduct an investigation into the conduct of Board Employees related to the transaction in the Auditor General's report (June 22, 2017) to the Board. On November 21, 2017, representatives of the Outside Investigator retained for that purpose, attended and provided the Board with a verbal update regarding the investigation. In order to facilitate the timely completion of the investigation, the City Solicitor seeks further direction on this matter at the Board's meeting on February 5, 2018.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Adopted the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 of the report (January 19, 2018) from the City Solicitor. 2. Directed that the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachment 1 of the report (January 19, 2018) from the City Solicitor remain confidential in its entirety, as it contains advice which is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
