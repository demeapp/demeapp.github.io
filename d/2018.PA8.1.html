The official record

What the item proposed

The City of Toronto, Transportation Services Division ("TSD"), is constructing a secure, sheltered bicycle parking facility with a variety of amenities within the municipal parking garage at City Hall. Construction is nearing completion and the facility is scheduled to open in April 2018. This report seeks the approval of the Toronto Parking Authority ("TPA") Board to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") with Transportation Services regarding the ongoing operation and maintenance of the Nathan Philips Square Bike Station ("Station") in accordance with the terms and conditions outlined in Appendix B - TPA and City Obligations.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized staff to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") with the Transportation Services Division of the City of Toronto ("TSD") to accommodate the ongoing operation and maintenance of the bicycle parking facility referred to as the Nathan Philips Square Bike Station ("Station") within the underground parking garage at 110 Queen Street West (Municipal Carpark 36) ("Carpark") for municipal purposes in accordance with the terms and conditions outlined in Appendix B - TPA and City Obligations, of the report (February 14, 2018) from the Acting President, and any such other or amended terms and conditions as may be acceptable to the Acting President and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor.
