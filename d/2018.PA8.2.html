Bike Share Toronto - Purchase of Equipment 2018, Public Transit Infrastructure Fund (PTIF) Program and Metrolinx

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority (“TPA”) was asked to authorize the purchase of new equipment to support the 2018 planned phased expansion of the Bike Share Toronto system.

What the item proposed

Bike Share Toronto has been allocated capital funding from two sources; the federal-provincial-municipal Public Transit Infrastructure Fund (PTIF) program, supported 50 percent by the federal government and matched 50 percent by the municipality, in each of 2017 and 2018 as part of the larger overall program for Toronto; and Metrolinx bike share funds pursuant to a 2015 agreement, "Bike Share Funding Agreement TPA and Metrolinx". These opportunities enable the ongoing phased expansion plans for the Bike Share system (envisaged to comprise a network in the order of 6,000 bikes over a five year period). This report seeks approval from the Toronto Parking Authority Board for the 2018 purchase of new equipment, including supply, delivery and installation, of approximately 1,000 new bicycles, 90 new stations, 1,570 docks, and ancillary elements, from PBSC Urban Solutions Inc.
