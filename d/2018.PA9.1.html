Update regarding the Investigation regarding the Auditor General's Observations of a Land Acquisition at Finch Avenue West and Arrow Road

Adopted with changesMar 27, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto Parking Authority was asked to board receive a further update, from its Outside Investigator, as to the progress of the Investigation into the conduct of Board Employees related to the transaction.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On July 14, 2017 the Office of the City Solicitor sent an invitation on behalf of the City Manager to provide investigative and legal services to law firms with experience in complex workplace and forensic investigations, particularly in relation to complicated development and real estate transactions and financial management issues, as well as experience in working with municipalities and knowledge of municipal governance issues. On November 21, 2017 the Investigators from Torys attended at the Toronto Parking Authority Board meeting and provided a progress update of the Investigation. A further update on the progress of the Investigation is being provided at this time.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Confirmed its actions in closed session in issuing instructions to staff. 2. Directed that the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 26, 2018) from the City Solicitor, remain confidential in its entirety, as it contains advice which is subject to solicitor-client privilege and relates to personal matters about identifiable individuals, including municipal or local board employees. 3. Agreed to report to City Council on the actions the Board has taken so far on this matter.
