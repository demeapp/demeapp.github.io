The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek instruction for proposed alterations to designated heritage properties and related litigation on the above-noted matter. No hearings have been scheduled.

What was decided

The Toronto Preservation Board recommends that: 1. City Council adopt the recommendations contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 21, 2018) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council direct the recommendations contained in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Appendix A and Confidential Appendix B to the report (March 21, 2018) from the City Solicitor be made public at such time as City Council adopts the recommendations with the remainder of Confidential Attachment 1 to remain confidential. (Appendix B will be on file with the City Clerk for the purpose of the March 26, 27 and 28, 2018 City Council Meeting.)
