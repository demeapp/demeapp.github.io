The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request City Council’s direction with respect to the Historic Yonge Street Heritage Conservation District (HCD) Plan as it relates to the North Downtown Yonge Site and Area Specific Policy No. 382 (Official Plan Amendment (CPA) 183). City Council designated the Historic Yonge Street HCD on March 10, 2016 and adopted the Historic Yonge Street HCD Plan under by-law 235-2016 in order to manage change within the district and conserve the area’s cultural heritage value. Council directed the City Solicitor to appear before the Ontario Municipal Board, now the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal (LPAT), to defend the by-law in the case of any appeals. The designation and Plan were subsequently appealed to the 0MB.
