The Toronto Preservation Board considers proposed alterations to the heritage property.

Official title: Alterations to Designated Heritage Properties, Intention to Designate 120 Peter Street under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act and Authority to Enter into a Heritage Easement Agreement - 120 Peter Street, 122 - 124 Peter Street and 357 - 359 Richmond Street West

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek further instruction in relation to a development application for a site that contains several heritage resources, and a related ongoing appeal at the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal. A Local Planning Appeal Tribunal teleconference has been scheduled for August 10 and a prehearing conference has been scheduled for September 28, 2018.

What was decided

The Toronto Preservation Board recommends that: 1. City Council adopt the confidential recommendations contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to this report. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the instructions in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Appendices "A", "B", "C", and "D" if adopted by City Council, with the balance of the Confidential Attachment 1 to remain confidential as it contains advice which is subject to solicitor-client privilege, save and except Confidential Appendices "E" and "F" both set forth in Confidential Attachment 1 are authorized for public release in any event.
