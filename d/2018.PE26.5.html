The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to advise the Parks and Environment Committee of the intent to issue a new competitive call and associated criteria for the provision of fuel and biodiesel for City of Toronto vehicles and equipment. The option term for the current fuel and biodiesel contract expires on December 31, 2018, with the option to extend for one additional year, at the City's discretion. The Fleet Services Division will not exercise this option, but rather, issue a new competitive call with the intent to award for a start day of January 1, 2019.

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. Consistent with TransformTO's goal to reduce the City's carbon footprint by 80 percent by 2050, City Council direct the General Manager, Fleet Services to conduct a pilot with approximately 100 City of Toronto fleet vehicles; fuel will be provided under the current fuel supply contract with B10 being used in the four coldest months of the year and B20 for the balance of the year with the goal of the pilot expanding biofuel usage once the results have been evaluated; and these results will be used to inform the subsequent fuel supply tender to be issued in the second quarter of 2019. 2.
