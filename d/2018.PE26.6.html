Establishing Ownership of the Eastern Gap Seawall and Adjacent Lands on the Toronto Islands

Referred back for more workApr 9, 2018 · Toronto City Council

What the item proposed

This report is provided in response to City Council's adoption of Item MM35.25 at its meeting on December 5, 2017, requesting information about the ownership of a portion of the seawall on the west side of the Eastern Gap and identify the legal owner of the lands on Ward's Island immediately adjacent to the seawall. The ownership was determined by Engineering and Construction Services in consultation with Legal Services, as follows: - The northern portion of the seawall on the west side of the Eastern Gap is federally-owned by the Toronto Port Authority, which is reflected in the parcel register for PIN 21418-0263 (LT). - The southern portion of the seawall on the west side of the Eastern Gap is federally-owned pursuant to the provisions of the Act to Approve an Agreement between the Government of Ontario Respecting Public Harbours, An, 1962 S.O 455 (1962-1963). - The lands immediately…

What was decided

The Parks and Environment Committee: 1. Referred the item to the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, and the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, to undertake additional consultations with City divisions (including Engineering and Construction Services, Real Estate Services, Legal Services, and the City Manager's Office), the Federal Government, PortsToronto, the Provincial Government, the Toronto Islands Residential Community Trust Corporation, the Toronto Island Community Association, and other appropriate stakeholders, and report back to the Parks and Environment Committee in the first quarter of 2019 with a summary of consultation outcomes and a status report on the planned repairs by the respective owners.
