What the item proposed

This report responds to a request from the Parks and Environment Committee to report on the feasibility of installing bike tune-up stations in City parks. Parks, Forestry and Recreation's multi-use paths in parks and other green spaces help form part of the City's bikeway network. Although bike repair stations have been increasing in popularity over the last few years and contribute to safe cycling infrastructure, there are a number of challenges typically encountered by municipalities, including Transportation Services, the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC), and private enterprises that frequently arise. Regardless of model type and features, challenges generally include vandalism and high incidences of theft of component parts, air pump failures, and units unable to hold up to the extreme weather conditions experienced in the City of Toronto. What was decided

The Parks and Environment Committee: 1. Referred the item to the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, and the General Manager, Transportation Services, with a request to consider opportunities to partner with cycling enterprises to provide cycling services, including bike rentals and repairs, to overlap with the existing and planned cycling networks in the City of Toronto.
