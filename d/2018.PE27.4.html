What the item proposed

Since its launch in 2014, the Residential Energy Retrofit Program has provided financing to support property owners in undertaking energy efficiency and water conservation improvements. The Program operates as two streams: the Home Energy Loan Program (HELP) for eligible homes and the High-rise Retrofit Improvement Support Program (Hi-RIS) for multi-unit residential buildings. This report provides an update on the status of HELP and Hi-RIS, including the impacts of refinements adopted by City Council in 2017 to enhance the Programs and drive greater Program uptake. It also seeks City Council authorization to extend HELP and Hi-RIS until December 2019 to further explore program enhancements aligned with the implementation of TransformTO.
