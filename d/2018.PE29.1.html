The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek approval to accept a donation and enter into a construction agreement with North Toronto Tennis Club for the construction of two new clay tennis courts at Lytton Park through the repurposing of an underutilized lawn bowling green. The report also seeks direction aimed at mitigating the impact of this donation on current users of the lawn bowling facility.

What was decided

The Parks and Environment Committee: 1. Deferred consideration of the item until January 2019 to enable the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, to consult with members of the community, interested parties and organizations, and the local Councillor.
