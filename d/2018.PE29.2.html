What the item proposed

This report presents a draft Biodiversity Strategy for Toronto (the draft Strategy) for the purpose of public consultation. The draft Strategy recognizes that biodiversity is a key component of a sustainable city and supports the vision of Toronto being home to biodiverse communities that contribute to resilient urban ecosystems. The United Nations Environment Programme defines biodiversity as a combination of "bio" (life) and "diversity," generally referring to the variety and variability of life on Earth. Biodiversity can be visualized as an interconnected web of life – the variability among living organisms from all sources, including terrestrial and aquatic ecosystems. The draft Strategy aims to increase the quality and quantity of natural habitat within the City in order to support healthier, more robust biodiversity and increase access to, and awareness of, nature.
