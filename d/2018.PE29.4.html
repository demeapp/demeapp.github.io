The official record

What the item proposed

The City of Toronto Annual Energy Consumption and Greenhouse Gas Emissions report was published in June 2018 to provide information on 2017 energy consumption and greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions from a select group of City of Toronto operations. It contains information provided to the Ontario Ministry of Energy based on the requirements of the Ontario Green Energy Act, Regulation 397/11; the regulation requires that the annual report be posted to the City website. The data in the 2017 report serves as the base year for the TransformTO strategy to reduce energy consumption in corporate buildings by an average of 40 percent by 2040, a key initiative to reduce GHG emissions across the City. This report is being brought to the Committee for their information.

What was decided

The Parks and Environment Committee received the report (June 26, 2018) from the Director, Environment and Energy, for information.
