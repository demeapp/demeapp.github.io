What the item proposed

Toronto City Council, in December 2017, considered Item ED25.4, Advancing Broadband Infrastructure and Internet Connectivity, and adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the Chief Information Officer and the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, in consultation with other City Divisions, Agencies and Corporations, to establish an interdivisional Internet Connectivity governance structure, comprised of a Working Group and Steering Committee; this governance structure will coordinate efforts, identify options and seek City Council direction as required to improve access to affordable high-speed internet for all Toronto residents and businesses, and to ensure that City infrastructure and regulation evolves and aligns with improving technology standards. 2. What was decided

The Parks and Environment Committee recommended that: 1. The Chief Information Officer and the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, as part of their review on improvements to access to affordable high-speed internet for all Toronto residents and businesses, give consideration to: a. increasing cellular phone access in major Toronto parks, including Toronto Island, to ensure that there is consistent coverage between Ward's Island and Hanlan's Point Park; and b. determining the viability of installing WiFi hotspots in large parks, like Toronto Island Park, and the steps necessary to achieve this, if there is no existing policy or procedure to do so.
