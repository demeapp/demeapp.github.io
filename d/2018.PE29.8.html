The official record

What the item proposed

As part of the Bloor West Village Avenue Study, a great deal of community input was received expressing concerns over the effects of both local and city wide development on High Park. As part of the recommendations, City Council directed Parks, Forestry and Recreation staff to review the current High Park Woodland and Savannah Management Plan to identify the scope of work and associated timing of an update to that Plan. This review will be integral piece to help protect the future preservation of the park.

What was decided

The Parks and Environment Committee: 1. Requested the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, to complete a review of the High Park Woodland and Savannah Management Plan and report back with recommendations, including funding options, by the fourth quarter of 2019.
