What the item proposed

This report by the Chief Building Official and Executive Director, Toronto Building, (the "CBO") reviews and comments on an application by Juxta Inc. to amend the Sign By-law to legalize and allow for two existing illuminated third party wall signs, each with static copy, at 146 Bloor Street West. The applicant is also seeking to implement a site-specific restriction on the content to be displayed, as well as the prohibition of any new third party signs on the property going forward. In October 2010, a similar application was considered by the Sign Variance Committee for one third party wall sign on the west elevation of the building. The Sign Variance Committee adopted staff recommendations and refused the requested variances.
