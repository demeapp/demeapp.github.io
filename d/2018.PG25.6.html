What the item proposed

This report and attached bulletin summarize the results of the 2016 Living in the City Survey. In August 2016, survey questionnaires were mailed to 50,000 households occupying units built between 2006 and 2015 in the Downtown, the Secondary Plan areas for North York, Etobicoke, Scarborough, and Yonge-Eglinton Centres and other Secondary Plan areas that have experienced residential growth during the 2006-2015 period. A total of 5,476 questionnaires were returned, representing an 11 percent response rate, which is accurate to ±1.3 percent at a confidence interval of 95 percent. The survey captures the choices and experiences of people living in these growth areas. The results provide information about residents' housing needs at the neighbourhood, building and unit scales, as well as the types of households and individuals that have chosen to occupy dwelling units in these areas.
