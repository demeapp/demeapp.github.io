The official record

What the item proposed

In 2017 the first set of Official Plan Indicators was reported on to Planning and Growth Management Committee. This report provides Committee with an update to the majority of the Official Plan Indicators. The Official Plan Indicators presented to Committee in 2017, contains 19 indicators relating to "Directing Growth, Economy & Employment, Transportation, Housing, the Quality of the Built Environment and Climate Change". This report provides updates to 15 of the 19 original indicators. Partnering with other Divisions will enable new Indicators to be developed enabling City Planning to present a broader set of Indicators in order to track the progress of the Official Plan policies and the policies implementation by City Divisions, agencies and boards.
