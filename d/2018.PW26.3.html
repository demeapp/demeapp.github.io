Contract Award for Tender Call 235-2017, Contract No. 17ECS-MI-03AB, for Effluent Disinfection System at the Ashbridges Bay Treatment Plant

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to advise of the results of Tender Call 235-2017, Effluent Disinfection System at the Ashbridges Bay Treatment Plant, and request the authority to award a contract to Graham Construction and Engineering LP in the amount of $209,363,500 net of all taxes ($213,048,298 net of HST recoveries). This is the first of two major construction contracts resulting from the Ashbridges Bay Treatment Plant Environmental Assessment initiated in 1990. Works under this contract will replace the existing chlorine based effluent disinfection system with a state of the art ultraviolet (UV) disinfection facility. The UV disinfection will also allow the City to comply with the Federal Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations, which require removal of chlorine from treated effluent discharged from wastewater treatment plants. What was decided

The Public Works and Infrastructure Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 195-8.4 of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 195 (Purchasing By-Law), granted authority to award Contract 17ECS-MI-03 AB, Tender Call 235-2017, for the Effluent Disinfection System at the Ashbridges Bay Treatment Plant to Graham Construction and Engineering LP, in the amount of $ 209,363,500 net of all taxes ($ 213,048,298 net of HST recoveries) having submitted the lowest compliant bid and meeting the specifications in conformance with the Tender requirements.
