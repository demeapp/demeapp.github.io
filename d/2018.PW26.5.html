The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek approval to extend previously-approved long-term road closures for the construction of the Metrolinx Eglinton Crosstown LRT. Crosslinx Transit Solutions (CTS) has experienced delays in receiving utility and Ministry of Environment (MOE) permits, and plan approvals by project stakeholders. Consequently, they have revised their construction staging schedules, impacting the anticipated timelines to close specific road and sidewalk segments. Specifically, this report recommends the continuation of: - The ongoing closure of the Eglinton Avenue westbound lanes approaching Allen Road, to support the construction of Cedarvale Station.
