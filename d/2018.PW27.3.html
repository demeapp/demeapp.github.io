The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to advise on the results of Request for Proposals (RFP) No. 9117-17-7338 for the Provision of Professional Engineering and Program Management Services for the delivery of a portion of the Coordinated Toronto Water and Transportation Services Capital Works Program for capital construction in years 2019 to 2022, and to request authority to enter into agreements with R.V. Anderson Associates Limited in the amount of $23,147,682 net of all taxes ($23,555,081 net of HST recoveries). There is an urgent need for the award of this assignment as any delay will jeopardize the City's ability to construct the infrastructure renewals and upgrades planned for delivery through this contract in 2019.

What was decided

The Public Works and Infrastructure Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 195-8.4A of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 195 (Purchasing By-Law), granted authority to negotiate and enter into agreements with R.V. Anderson Associates Ltd., being the highest overall scoring proponent meeting the requirements of Request for Proposal (RFP) No. 9117-17-7338 to provide Program Management, Engineering Design and Construction Administration Services for the coordinated Toronto Water and Transportation Services 2019 to 2022 Capital Works Program in the amount of $23,147,682 net of HST, including disbursements, provisional allowances and contingencies as follows: a.
