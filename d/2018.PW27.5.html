The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend an extension to the existing school bus loading zone located on the east side of Winona Drive, between Benson Avenue and Tyrrel Avenue. The extension will provide additional curbside space for school buses picking up and dropping off students at the Winona Drive Senior Public School. This report also corrects a number of discrepancies between the parking regulations in Chapter 950 of the Toronto Municipal Code and the regulations posted on Winona Drive, between Benson Avenue and Tyrrel Avenue. This report is submitted to Public Works and Infrastructure Committee as Winona Drive, between Benson Avenue and Tyrrel Avenue, forms a shared boundary between the Toronto and East York Community Council and the Etobicoke York Community Council.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the east side of Winona Drive, between Davenport Road and a point 95 metres south of Benson Avenue, to be in effect between Davenport Road and a point 112 metres south of Benson Avenue. 2.
