What the item proposed

The Toronto-York Spadina Subway Extension (TYSSE), completed in December 2017, provides an 8.6 km extension of the University Branch of the Line 1 - Yonge-University Subway - an important connection in the northwest of the City, linking the Vaughan Metropolitan Centre and York University to the existing subway network. In conjunction with the opening of the Toronto-York Spadina Subway Extension (TYSSE), the operation of various TTC bus routes in the area were changed to promote new connections and improve subway access for customers. Changes to TTC bus routes included rerouting, modifications to the frequency of service, and the replacement of some routes by the subway.
