Toronto City Council · Decision

Through Prohibition - Farm Greenway at Victoria Park Avenue

AdoptedApr 24, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to prohibit eastbound through movements from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., and from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, from Farm Greenway to Terraview Boulevard.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report recommends an eastbound through prohibition (with a bicycle exemption) from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday eastbound from Farm Greenway across Victoria Park Avenue to Terraview Boulevard. Currently, this through movement is allowed at all times. This matter is being tabled at the Public Works and Infrastructure Committee as it impacts both the North York Community Council and the Scarborough Community Council areas.

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit eastbound through movements from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., and from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, from Farm Greenway to Terraview Boulevard at Victoria Park Avenue, bicycles excepted.
