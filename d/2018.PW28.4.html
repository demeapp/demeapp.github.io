What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council consent to the assignment of the agreement dated March 14, 2017 between the City of Toronto (the "City") and Terratec Environmental Ltd. ("Terratec") for the haulage and beneficial use of biosolids generated at the Ashbridges Bay Treatment Plant (the "ABTP") (the "Biosolids Agreement"), from Terratec to Revolution Environmental Solutions LP ("ES LP"), an Ontario limited partnership within the corporate structure of Terrapure Environmental("Terrapure"). Terrapure has acquired ownership of Terratec and the proposed assignment of the Biosolids Agreement is part of a broader restructuring of Terrapure, a Canadian provider of environmental and recycling services.
