What the item proposed

The City of Toronto (the City) and Regional Municipality of York (York Region) have a mutual interest to negotiate the renewal of the Water Supply Agreement, wherein the City supplies water to York Region. The current Water Supply Agreement was executed in 2005 and expires on May 31, 2025 and York Region has requested that the Water Supply Agreement be renewed to 2040 to secure a long-term water supply for York Region. Since 2005, several economic and environmental factors (e.g., continued aging of water infrastructure) that impact the water pricing model in Schedule "D" of the Water Supply Agreement have changed with respect to meeting the original business objectives. The City is interested in renegotiating and amending the water pricing model in Schedule "D" of the Water Supply Agreement in order to address these changes.
