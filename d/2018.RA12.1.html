The official record

What the item proposed

As part of the implementation of the City-wide Real Estate Model in Fall 2017, an interim leadership team was put in place for the launch and start-up of CreateTO, including a Transition Lead as the administrative head of the new agency to the end-of-June 2018. Odgers Berndtson was retained to engage the market in the recruitment of a permanent Chief Executive Officer. With appropriate engagement of the CreateTO Board, it is anticipated the recruitment process will continue into the Summer and Fall of 2018 with the appointment of a Chief Executive Officer targeted for Q2 2019 with the required Council ratification. The interim leadership put in place was made up of a multi-disciplinary team, specifically employees from Build Toronto, the Toronto Port Lands Company, and the City of Toronto.
