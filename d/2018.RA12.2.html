The official record

What the item proposed

In October 2017, the Board of Directors of Build Toronto approved the sale of 253 Markham Avenue and 12, 20 and 30 Dunelm Street to Options for Homes and authorized Build Toronto Management to enter into a purchase and sale agreement which reflected the terms and conditions contained in the Confidential Property Sale Submission. In December 2017 the Board of Directors approved an amendment to the purchase and sale agreement. Subsequent to this time the City introduced its Development Charge by law which increased development charges by approximately 90% and the Province of Ontario eliminated its Investment in Affordable Housing program. Due to the substantial financial impact of these two measures, management is recommending a further amendment to the purchase and sale agreement as outlined in the confidential attachments to this report.
