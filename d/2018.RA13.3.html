The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Waterfront Studios Development Inc. (TWSDI) requested to takedown approximately 5 acres of land for a 99 year lease at 101 Commissioners Street, Toronto. This site is located within the Port Lands film media district. Toronto Port Lands Company has approved a Concept Plan by letter dated October 9, 2018 (Attachment 1). The proposal presents 224,864 square feet of studio space, new communication, broadcasting and office space. This expansion enhances Toronto Pinewood Studios state-of-the art sound stages that anchor the Port Lands Media, Innovation and Creativity Cluster.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO received the report (October 10, 2018) from the Executive Advisor, Asset and Portfolio Management and Major Projects, CreateTO for information.
