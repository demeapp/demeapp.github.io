The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of the report is to present the marketing and procurement process and negotiation of a purchase and sale agreement with the selected purchaser for the sale of 260 Eighth Street, a 83,368 square metres (20.6 acre) property located west of Islington Avenue, north of Birmingham Avenue, in Ward 6, Etobicoke Lakeshore. The property was marketed to over 4,000 potential purchasers during July and August 2017 and after two rounds of bids, a purchaser was selected and negotiations commenced which were delayed for a number of reasons until a Purchase and Sale Agreement was signed on March 6, 2018.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Approved the sale of 260 Eighth Street to the purchaser and substantially on the terms and conditions set out in the confidential attachment to the report (March 8, 2018) from the Executive Advisor, Asset and Portfolio Management and Major Projects, CreateTO the Head, Development, CreateTO and the Director, Development, CreateTO and any other terms and conditions as may be acceptable to the Transition Lead, CreateTO. 2.
