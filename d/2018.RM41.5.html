Various presentations, introductions and announcements were made to City Council on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018. May 22, 2018 Councillor Karygiannis, during the morning session of the meeting, advised Council that hateful graffiti was found on a school in Councillor De Baeremaeker's Ward the previous day. Councillor Karygiannis thanked Transportation Services and Solid Waste Management Services for their efforts to remove the graffiti quickly. Councillor De Baeremaeker also addressed Council to express his disappointment about the hateful graffiti found in his Ward and to thank staff for their efforts to remove it. May 23, 2018 Councillor McMahon, during the morning session of the meeting, advised Council that one of the Condolence Motions was for Douglas Crosbie, who died in Leslieville while riding his bicycle to work on May 16, 2018.
