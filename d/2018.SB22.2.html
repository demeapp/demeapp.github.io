The official record

What the item proposed

Kahara Media (the "Applicant") has made an application for three variances from regulations contained in the Sign By-law, for the erection and display of one non-illuminated third party wall sign displaying static copy, containing one rectangular sign face, measuring 6.53 metres horizontally by 23.16 metres vertically (the "Proposed Sign"). The Proposed Sign would be located on the southerly facing building wall, between the thirteenth and nineteenth storeys, of an existing nineteen storey office building located on the property municipally known as 415 Yonge Street (the "Premises"). Variances from the regulations contained in §§ 694-22D, 694-25B(1)(a) and 694-25B(1)(b) of the Sign By-law (the "Requested Variances") are required for the property owner, 415 Artis Yonge Street Limited, to make a successful application for a sign permit for the erection and display of the Proposed Sign.…

What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Adjourned the hearing on this matter until its meeting on March 20, 2018 at the request of the Applicant.
