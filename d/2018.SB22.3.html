The official record

What the item proposed

Burlington Signs National (the "Applicant") made a variance application concerning one first party illuminated wall sign displaying static copy (the "Proposed Sign"), to be located on portions of the ninth and tenth storeys of the southerly elevation of a hotel building located at 970 Dixon Road (the "Premises"). After conducting a written hearing in accordance with the requirements of the Sign By-law, a decision was made to refuse to grant the necessary variances. Ruth's Chris Steak House (the "Appellant") subsequently filed an appeal of the decision. Staff have determined that there was not enough information provided in the application to establish that the Proposed Sign meets all nine of the established criteria as contained in §694-30A of the Sign By-law.
