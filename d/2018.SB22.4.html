What the item proposed

910458 Ontario Ltd. C/O Jay Harris (the "Applicant") has appealed a decision respecting a request for variance. The request sought to obtain relief from the Sign By-law provision pertaining to the minimum required setback from the front property line in order for the Applicant to obtain a permit to allow for one non-illuminated ground sign, containing two sign faces, each displaying static copy (the "Proposed Sign"), along the southerly property frontage, perpendicular to Sheppard Avenue West, of the property municipally known as 100 Sheppard Avenue West (the "Premises"). Staff have determined that there was insufficient information provided to establish that the Proposed Sign meets all nine of the established criteria in §694-30A of the Sign By-law.
