The official record

What the item proposed

This report addresses an appeal concerning two variances sought respecting a proposal for one first party illuminated projecting sign displaying static copy (the "Proposed Sign"). It contains three sign faces, identifying the "Amsterdam Barrel House", and is to be located on the westerly elevation of the two storey restaurant building at the property municipally known as 85 Laird Drive (the "Premises"). The variances were originally applied for by Lisa Dey of Roberts Signs and Awnings (the "Applicant"). After conducting a review in accordance with the requirements of the Sign By-law, a decision was made to refuse to grant the requested variances. Subsequently, the Applicant filed an appeal of the decision. It has been determined that there was not enough information provided to establish that the Proposed Sign meets all nine established criteria as contained in §694-30A of the Sign…

What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Granted the requested variances for the Proposed Sign at the premises municipally known as 85 Laird Drive, as described below: a. The requirement contained at §694-21I, which states that an E-Employment sign district may contain an overhanging structure sign, a ground sign, a wall sign, an electronic wall sign, or an electronic ground sign, be varied to allow for the Proposed Sign, which is a projecting sign. b. The requirement contained at §694-14H, which states that there shall be a minimum vertical clearance of 2.5 metres from the ground, sidewalk or walkway to the lowest point of a projecting sign located above, be varied to allow for the Proposed Sign which as has a vertical clearance of 2.44 metres. Sign Description: One first party projecting sign, which may be illuminated, and shall be located on the first and second storey of the west elevation…
