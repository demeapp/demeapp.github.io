Application by 1640156 Ontario Inc. for One Third Party Wall Sign at 146 Bloor Street West

AdoptedApr 18, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Sign Variance Committee was asked to refuse to grant the three variances requested to §§ 694-22D, 694-25B(1)(a), and 694-25B(1)(b) required to allow the issuance of a permit for the erection and display.

The official record

What the item proposed

1640156 Ontario Inc. (the "Applicant") has made an application seeking three variances from the Sign By-law for one non-illuminated third party wall sign to be located at the premises municipally known as 146 Bloor Street West (the "Proposed Sign"). The Proposed Sign would contain one sign face, measuring 3.52 metres horizontally by 10.8 metres vertically, displaying static copy. The Proposed Sign would be located on the easterly elevation of the existing eight-storey glass and stone office building, covering portions of the fourth, fifth and sixth storeys, at an overall height of 23.53 metres. In addition, the Applicant requests that a condition be imposed limiting the sign copy on the Proposed Sign to only display: Copy that promotes live theatre or movie productions; or Copy that is non-profit advertising.
