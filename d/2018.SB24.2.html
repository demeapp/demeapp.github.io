The official record

What the item proposed

Pattison Outdoor (the "Applicant"), on behalf of the property owner, has applied to seek one variance from the Sign By-law, required to allow for the erection and display of one third party ground sign (the "Proposed Sign"). The Proposed Sign is an illuminated ground sign, to be located on the westerly frontage, adjacent to Keele Street, at the premises municipally known as 3985-3993 Keele Street. The Proposed Sign will have two sign faces, each displaying static copy and each measuring 6.10 metres horizontally by 3.05 metres vertically configured in a v-shaped formation so that each of the sign faces can be visible to north and southbound vehicular traffic travelling along Keele Street. The highest point of the Proposed Sign measured from grade would be 8.23 metres. The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Granted the variance requested to § 694-25C(1)(c) required to allow the issuance of a permit for the erection and display of one third party ground sign at the property municipally known as 3985-3993 Keele Street, as described in Attachment 1 to the report dated March 29, 2018 from the Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building.
