The official record

What the item proposed

Pattison Outdoor (the "Applicant"), on behalf of the property owner, has applied to seek a variance to the Sign By-law, required to allow for the erection and display of one third party electronic ground sign (the "Proposed Sign"). The Proposed Sign is an illuminated electronic ground sign, to be located at the south-westerly corner of the premises municipally known as 115 City View Drive. The Proposed Sign is to be built at a height of 9.14 metres and contain two sign faces, in a back to back configuration, each measuring 6.10 metres horizontally by 3.05 metres vertically. The southerly facing sign face is to display electronic static copy. The northerly facing sign face is to display static copy. What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Refused the variance requested to §694-24A(10) required to allow the issuance of a permit for the erection and display of the third party electronic ground sign, located on the premises municipally known as 115 City View Drive, as described in Attachment 1 to the report (June 29, 2018) from the Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building, on condition that the third party ground sign currently located on the premises municipally known as 10 Marmac Drive be removed prior to the issuance of the permit.
