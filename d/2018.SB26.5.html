What the item proposed

This report reviews and makes recommendations from a request by Kramer Design Associates (the "Applicant"), on behalf of the property owner, Bentall Kennedy, in respect of a third party sign variance application seeking to replace and consolidate three existing projecting signs, known as "B", "C" and "D", with a single larger electronic projecting sign; effectively, removing signs "B“ and “D”, currently displaying mechanical (tri‐vision) copy and static copy, respectively; and, increasing sign "C" to dimensions of 24.69 metres vertically by 31.55 metres horizontally, currently displaying electronic moving copy (the "Proposed Sign").
