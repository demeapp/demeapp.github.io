The Sign Variance Committee decides on Permit World's application for One Third Party Electronic Ground Sign at 3 Bestobell Road.

The official record

What the item proposed

Permit World Inc. (the "Applicant), has applied as agent for Fisnik Pristine (the owner of the subject premises, 3 Bestobell Road), seeking six variances from the provisions of the Sign By-law, required for the erection and display of one third party electronic ground sign displaying electronic static copy (the "Proposed Sign"), at a height of 15.24 metres, containing a single rectangular sign face, facing easterly. The sign face on the Proposed Sign displays electronic static copy and measures 14.63 metres horizontally by 4.27 metres vertically (individual sign face area of 62.47 m²). The Proposed Sign is proposed to be displayed on the premises at 3 Bestobell Road, an E-Employment Sign District immediately adjacent to F.G. Gardiner Expressway, with the sign face oriented perpendicular to and to be primarily directed to west-bound traffic on the Expressway. The Proposed Sign requires…

What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Refused to grant the variances from §§ 694-22E, 694-24A(1), 694-25C(2)(a), 694-25C(2)(b), 694-25C(2)(d) and 694-25C(2)(f) required to allow the issuance of a permit for the erection and display of one third party electronic ground sign to be located at the premises municipally known as 3 Bestobell Road as described in Attachment 1 to the report dated June 29, 2018 from the Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building.
