The official record

What the item proposed

This report reviews and makes recommendations regarding a proposal by Pattison Outdoor Advertising (the "Applicant") for one double-sided electronic ground sign, displaying electronic static copy (the "Proposed Sign"), as described in detail at Attachment 1 to this report. The Proposed Sign would be located along the easterly frontage of 4560 Dufferin Street, approximately 17 metres from the intersection of Dufferin Street and Finch Avenue West, on the premises of an existing used car lot. The attributes of the Proposed Sign include a sign face area of 18.6 square metres on each side, which is in line with the Sign By-law requirements for electronic ground signs in an Employment (E) Sign District. What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Granted the requested variances to §§ 649-25(2)(d),(e),(f) described as required variances 3, 4 and 5 in Attachment 1 of the report (September 4, 2018) from the Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building, to allow the issuance of a permit for the erection and display of the proposed sign at 4560 Dufferin Street, as described in Attachment 1 of this report, on the condition that the double sided ground sign, located at the premises municipally known as 4590 Dufferin Street, displaying mechanical (tri-vision) copy, be removed.
