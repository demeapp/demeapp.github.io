The official record

What the item proposed

This report addresses an appeal concerning variances respecting two non-illuminated first party wall signs displaying static copy (the "Proposed Signs"). The Proposed Sign #1 is to be located on portions of the third and fourth storey of the easterly elevation, and Proposed Sign #2 is to be located on portions of the sixth and seventh storey of the southerly elevation of the seven-storey office building at the property municipally known as 180 John Street ("180 John"). The variances were originally applied for by Allied Properties REIT, represented by their agent, Zip Signs. After conducting a review in accordance with the requirements of the Sign By-law, a decision to refuse to grant the requested variances was issued. What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Granted variance #2 as requested for Proposed Sign #2 at the premises municipally known as 180 John Street, as described in Attachment 1 of the report (August 29, 2018) from the Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building. 2. Refused requested variance #1 at the premises municipally known as 180 John Street, as described in Attachment 1 of the report (August 29, 2018) from the Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building.
