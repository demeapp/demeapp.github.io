The official record

What the item proposed

This report addresses an appeal concerning one variance sought respecting a proposal for one first party wall sign displaying static copy (the "Proposed Sign"). The Proposed Sign is to be located on a portion of the mechanical penthouse above the fifth storey of the southwest elevation of the five storey office building at the property municipally known as 90 Wynford Drive (the "Premises"). The sign is in the form of a logo or corporate symbol for "Scotiabank". The Bank of Nova Scotia, through their agent Zip Signs, was the original applicant for this variance. After conducting a review in accordance with the requirements of the Sign By-law, a decision was made to refuse to grant the requested variance. What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Granted the requested variance for the Proposed Sign at the premises municipally known as 90 Wynford Drive, as described in Attachment 1 of the report (August 29, 2018) from the Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building, subject to replacing the word "overrun" with "penthouse" in Attachment 1 of this report.
