The official record

What the item proposed

This report addresses an appeal concerning five variances sought respecting a proposal for two first party electronic window signs displaying electronic static copy (the "Proposed Signs"). The Proposed Signs are to be located on the first storey of the east and south elevations of the multi-storey mixed-use commercial building at the property municipally known as 48 Yonge Street (the "Premises"). The purpose of the signs is to advertise, promote and direct attention to goods available at the "Rexall" drugstore on the ground floor. The variance was originally applied for by Christina Wiggins of Jones Neon Displays Ltd. (the "Applicant"). After conducting a review in accordance with the requirements of the Sign By-law, a decision was made to refuse to grant the requested variances. Subsequently, the Applicant filed an appeal of that decision. Based on the rationale and materials submitted…

What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Refused to grant the requested variances for the Proposed Signs at the premises municipally known as 48 Yonge Street, as described in Attachment 1 of the report (August 29, 2018) from the Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building.
