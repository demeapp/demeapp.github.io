The official record

What the item proposed

This report reviews an application for three sign variances by Pattison Outdoor (the "Applicant"), on behalf of the property owner, to allow for one third party electronic ground sign displaying electronic static copy (the "Proposed Sign") at the property municipally known as 1129 Leslie Street (the "Premises"), described in detail in Attachment 1. The Proposed Sign would be located along the west frontage of the Premises, which contains a surface-level parking lot and is surrounded by trees. This parking lot is located immediately west of three separate one-storey buildings set back from Leslie Street with office and employment uses. The Proposed Sign is intended to capture the attention of traffic travelling along Leslie Street. What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Granted the five requested variances to sections 694-18E(5), 694-25C(2)(b), 694-25C(2)(k), 694-25C(2)(e) and 694-25C(2)(f), subject to the specified conditions, required to allow the Applicant to obtain a permit for the erection and display of one third party electronic ground sign at 1129 Leslie Street, as described in Attachment 1 to the supplementary report (October 24, 2018) from the Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building.
