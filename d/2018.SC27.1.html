The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report concerns a matter for which the Scarborough Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to advise Council concerning an application by the property owners of 80 Maywood Park for a site-specific Fence Exemption, pursuant to Section 447-5.C of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447- Fences. The property owner(s) is seeking Council's permission to be exempt concerning an existing wood privacy fence located in the front yard along the west lot line, which does not comply with restrictions contrary to Section 447-2 of the by-law as detailed below in the following chart: The subject property, 80 Maywood Park, is a single story detached residential dwelling located in Ward 35, in a Single Family Residential Zoned Area, west of Kennedy Road Avenue and south of Eglinton Avenue. Municipal Licensing Standards…

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Refused to grant the application for an exemption permit, by the property owner of 80 Maywood Park, for a proposed fence that fails to comply with the provisions of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447, Fences.
