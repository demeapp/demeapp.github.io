The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends the adoption of a by-law to designate the southbound right-turn lane on Birchmount Road at Rolark Drive for right turns only, buses excepted. Currently, there is an undesignated southbound bus bay at this intersection.

What was decided

City Council on January 31 and February 1, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council designate the westerly southbound lane on Birchmount Road, between Rolark Drive and a point 100 metres north, for southbound right turns only, buses excepted.
