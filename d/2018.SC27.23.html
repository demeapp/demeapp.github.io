The official record

What the item proposed

In an effort to address high vehicle traffic volumes at Highcastle Public School turn prohibitions are required on Highcastle Road and Military Trail. Motorists have for some time accessed Ellesmere Road through Military Trail and Highcastle Road driving through the local community avoiding the main arterial road designated for this purpose. This "cut through" has negatively affected the community specifically children walking to Highcastle Public School. The population at Highcastle Public School grew significantly when students who attended the neighbouring local school, Brooks Road Park School where transferred due to its closure in 2013. What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Prohibited westbound left turns from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., Monday to Friday at Military Trail and Highcastle Road. 2. Prohibited southbound right turns from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., Monday to Friday at Highcastle Road and Military Trail. 3. Requested Transportation Services to install signage reflecting the prohibitions on Nielson Road and Military Trail and at Neilson Road and Highcastle Road.
