Scarborough Community Council was asked to change parking rules on Dolly Varden Boulevard, Bluefin Crescent.

Official title: Parking Prohibitions - Dolly Varden Boulevard, Bluefin Crescent

AdoptedFeb 21, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report recommends the prohibition of parking from 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m. on both sides of Dolly Varden Boulevard and on both sides of Bluefin Crescent. Currently, parking is permitted for up to three hours on Bluefin Crescent, and most of Dolly Varden Boulevard.

What was decided

The Director, Transportation Services, Scarborough District recommends that: 1. Scarborough Community Council prohibit parking from 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m., on the east and south sides of Dolly Varden Boulevard, between Brimorton Drive and Daventry Road. 2. Scarborough Community Council prohibit parking from 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m., on the west and north sides of Dolly Varden Boulevard, between Bluefin Crescent (north intersection) and Daventry Road. 3. Scarborough Community Council prohibit parking from 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m., on both sides of Bluefin Crescent, between Dolly Varden Boulevard (northeast intersection) and Dolly Varden Boulevard (southwest intersection).
