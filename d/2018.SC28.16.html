The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report recommends the installation of permissive parking regulations on the south side of Dean Park Road in front of the public school and restrictive parking regulations on the north side of Dean Park Road. The proposed parking regulations will accommodate an accessible pick-up and drop-off area for students and reduce parking related congestion in front of the school.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Rescinded the stopping prohibition from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, in effect on the north side of Dean Park Road between Red River Crescent (west intersection) and a point 75 metres west. 2. Rescinded the stopping prohibition from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, in effect on the south side of Dean Park Road between a point 33 metres east of Red River Crescent (east intersection) and a point 160 metres further west. 3. Prohibited stopping from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Dean Park Road between Braymore Boulevard and a point 75 metres west of Red River Crescent (west intersection). 4. Prohibited stopping at all times on the south side of Dean Park Road, between a point 30 metres east of Sudbury Hall Drive (east intersection) and a point 67 metres further east. 5.
