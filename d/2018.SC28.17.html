The official record

What the item proposed

The Agincourt Community Zoning By-law No. 10076 of the former City of Scarborough contained performance standards which set out minimum lot frontage and lot area requirements based on the size of the existing lot on the registered plan of subdivision. "Performance Standard 1" set a maximum of one single family dwelling per lot as shown on a registered plan. As such, lot frontage and area maximums were required to be in conformity with what existed on the registered plan of subdivision and any deviation from those standards required a variance. When the City Wide Zoning By-law 569-2013 was brought forward to City Council, specific provisions related to minimum lot frontage and lot area for the Agincourt Community were not included. What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Directed the Director, Community Planning, Scarborough District in consultation with the Director, Zoning and Committee of Adjustment, to bring forward to a statutory public meeting of Scarborough Community Council on April 4, 2018, a staff report and amending zoning by-law to Zoning By-law 569-2013, to restore the minimum lot frontages and areas for the Agincourt Community (as set out in the map attached to this motion).
