Staff recommend: The Director, Transportation Services, Scarborough District, be requested to report to the July 4.

Official title: Request for a Report Regarding the Feasibility of Installing Traffic Calming Measures on Benleigh Drive

AdoptedFeb 21, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

Please accept this letter as my request for your support to add a new item of business which asks for a staff report regarding the feasibility of installing traffic calming measures on Benleigh Drive. As you may know, Benleigh Drive is a main feeder street between McCowan Road and Bellamy Road –which has a posted speed limit of 50 km/h. Lately, I have been receiving several complaints from residents, who live in the local community, saying that too often motorists speed through Benleigh Drive to cut through the subdivision. While this practice of cutting through the subdivision to avoid traffic lights can be expected, Bendale Junior Public School is situated nearby and has about 400 students that attend the school. These speeding motorists pose a major safety concern to both the children attending this school and the parents who are dropping their kids off to school.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Scarborough District, be requested to report to the July 4, 2018 Scarborough Community Council meeting on the use of traffic calming measures to help reduce vehicle speeds on Benleigh Drive including curb extensions (mid-block) and raised concrete islands as mentioned in Chapter 8: Street Design for Roadways (p. 126 and p.139) of the "Toronto Complete Streets Guidelines".
