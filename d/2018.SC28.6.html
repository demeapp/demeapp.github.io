The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is submitted for information in response to a request by Scarborough Community Council, at its meeting on September 7, 2016, to report on the percentage of sidewalks that need to be repaired in the Scarborough District that is the result of damage caused by the growth of tree roots. As part of the Transportation Services Division's 2016 sidewalk inspection program in the Scarborough District, approximately 8,000 "discontinuities" in the sidewalk surface were identified. "Discontinuities" are defined as a vertical change in the elevation of the sidewalk surface of 20mm or greater which can create tripping hazards. Of this total, 2,900-3,500 (35-45%) were located in the immediate vicinity of trees.
