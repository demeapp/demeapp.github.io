Request for Fence Exemption - 119 Amberjack Boulevard - Fence in Front Yard

Adopted with changesApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council was asked to refuse to grant the application for an exemption permit, by the owner(s) of 119 Amberjack Boulevard, for an existing hedge fence located in the front yard.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report concerns a matter for which the Scarborough Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to advise Community Council concerning an application by the owner(s) of 119 Amberjack Blvd for a site-specific Fence Exemption, pursuant to Section 447-2 of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447- Fences. The property owner(s) is seeking Council's permission to be exempt concerning existing hedges located in the front yard along the north side lot line which does not comply with restrictions contrary to Section 447-2 of the bylaw as detailed below in the following chart: The subject property 119 Amberjack Blvd is located in Ward 38, in a single family residential area. A Notice of Violation was issued on August 22, 2017 concerning Chapter 447-Fences to the owner. GENERAL LOCATION SPECIFIC LOCATION EXISTING…

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Granted the application by the owner(s) of 119 Amberjack Boulevard for the existing hedge fence, without conditions; directs and requires that the installation be maintained in good repair without alteration; directs and requires that at such time as replacement of the fence is required that such installation will comply with Municipal Code Chapter 447, or its successor by-law.
