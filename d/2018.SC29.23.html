Toronto City Council · Decision

Request for Report - School Zone Review

AdoptedApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

In accordance with the City of Toronto's School Safety Zone initiative, I am requesting Scarborough Community Council support my motion requesting Transportation Services, to study the feasibility of a speed limit reduction on Centennial Road - from Lawson Road to Lawrence Avenue East in Ward 44 (Reduction from current 50 km/h to 40 km/h) and to report back to Scarborough Community Council for the June 6, 2018 community council session. No new decision is attached.
