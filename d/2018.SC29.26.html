The official record

What the item proposed

With an increasing number of tragic traffic incidences occurring at our local community schools an urgent need to implement measures is required. Located on Dearham Wood in southeast Scarborough, Poplar Road Jr. Public School is experiencing high volumes of traffic and is in need of a Signalized Pedestrian Crossover (PXO). An increasing number of seniors, students and young children with their caregivers cross Dearham Wood to attend Poplar Road Jr. Public School and Dearham Wood Daycare. The painted lines currently painted on the road are not serving as a deterrent for motorist, leaving residents at a disadvantage in a safety situation as they cross the road. A Signalized Pedestrian Crossover would provide a clear notification to vehicles to stop to allow pedestrians to cross. What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Scarborough District to consult with the Principal of Poplar Road Jr. Public School and to report back to the June 6, 2018 meeting of the Scarborough Community Council on the installation of a Signalized Pedestrian Crossover (PXO) on Dearham Wood.
