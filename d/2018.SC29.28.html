The official record

What the item proposed

Please accept this letter as my request to add a new item of business which asks for staff to report on the feasibility of installing a traffic light at Brimorton Drive and Dolly Varden Boulevard. As you may know, Brimorton Drive is a main feeder street that links a number of subdivisions in my ward. Likewise, Dolly Varden Boulevard is also a very busy street that many young athletes, staff, students and seniors use to access the Centennial Recreation Centre on a daily basis. Public programming at the recreation centre has significantly expanded in recent years and have increased traffic in this area. Lately, I have been receiving several complaints from members of the community regarding speeding in the area that presents a major safety concern to the regular patrons of this recreation centre. What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Scarborough District to report to the June 6, 2018 meeting of Scarborough Community Council on the feasibility of installing a traffic light at Brimorton Drive and Dolly Varden Boulevard.
