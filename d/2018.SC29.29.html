Scarborough Community Council considers a request to install an All-Way Stop Control at Barrymore Road at Kilbride Road.

The official record

What the item proposed

Please accept this letter as my request to add a new item of business which asks for staff to report on the feasibility of installing an All-Way Stop Control at Barrymore Road at Kilbride Road. As you may know, Barrymore Road is a main feeder street between Lawrence Avenue East and Danforth Road – which has a posted speed limit of 40 km/h. I have received numerous complaints from local residents who have concerns of motorists speeding through Barrymore Road to cut through the subdivision. Therefore, I would like to move the motion below that would create a safer environment for pedestrians and drivers alike that: Staff report to the June, 2018 Scarborough Community Council with a report on the feasibility of installing an All-Way Stop Control at Barrymore Road and Kilbride Road. If you have any questions regarding this matter, please feel free to contact me at 416- 392-0204.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Scarborough District to report to the June 6, 2018 meeting of Scarborough Community Council on the feasibility of installing an All-way Stop Control at Barrymore Road and Kilbride Road.
