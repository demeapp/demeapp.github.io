The official record

What the item proposed

Please accept this letter as my request to add a new item of business which requests a staff report regarding the feasibility of installing a pedestrian crosswalk at Greencedar Circuit and Daphne Road. Greencedar Circuit and Daphne Road is a dangerous intersection. There have been two fatalities reported at this location in recent history. Most recently, a senior citizen was hit by a car, and subsequently died, while crossing Greencedar Circuit at Daphne Road around 7:00 p.m. on March 2, 2018. A second fatality was reported, on July 18, 2010 around 1:00 a.m., where a 16 year old teen, who was involved in a single vehicle crash, was thrown from his car and was fatally injured. This location is very well known to my local residents that motorists often speed along Greencedar Circuit and make dangerous high speed turns onto Daphne Road. What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Scarborough District to report to the July 4, 2018 meeting of Scarborough Community Council on the feasibility of installing a Pedestrian Crosswalk at Greencedar Circuit and Daphne Road.
