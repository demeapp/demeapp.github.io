What the item proposed

Requesting that Scarborough Community Council re-open Delegated Item SC25.18.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Re-opened delegated Item SC25.18 headed Overnight Parking Regulation Pilot Review - Silver Springs Boulevard Area adopted on October 17, 2017. 2. Amended Recommendation 1 to delete the words "Finch Avenue West" and replace them with the words "Finch Avenue East", so that the Recommendation reads as follows: "1. Prohibited parking from 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m. on both sides of all streets within the Scarborough Community Council's delegated authority under Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 27, in the area bounded by Finch Avenue East, Victoria Park Avenue, Steeles Avenue East, and the GO Railway Stouffville Subdivision."
