The official record

What the item proposed

Please accept this letter as my request to add a new item of business to the April 2018 Scarborough. Community Council agenda, entitled: Stopping illegal large trucks from infiltrating the Greenbrae community. I am asking for your support for the following motion: That staff report back to the May 2018 Scarborough Community Council meeting regarding possible permanent measures to enforce compliance with the site plan agreement at 678 Markham Road that were put in place to restrict large trucks from infiltrating the Greenbrae community. If you have any questions regarding this matter, please feel free to contact me at 416-392-0204.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Scarborough District to report back to the May 2, 2018 meeting of Scarborough Community Council regarding possible permanent measures to enforce compliance with the site plan agreement at 678 Markham Road that were put in place to restrict large trucks from infiltrating the Greenbrae community.
