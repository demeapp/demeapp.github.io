The official record

What the item proposed

This City-initiated zoning by-law amendment is subject to the new provisions of the Planning Act and the City of Toronto Act, 2006. On February 21, 2018, Scarborough Community Council directed the Director, Community Planning, Scarborough District, in consultation with the Director, Zoning and Committee of Adjustment, to bring forward to a statutory public meeting of Scarborough Community Council on April 4, 2018, a staff report and amendment to Zoning By-law No. 569-2013, to restore the minimum lot frontages and lot areas for the residential properties shown in the “Affected Area” on Attachment 1 to this report. This report recommends a City-initiated technical amendment to Chapter 900 Site Specific Exceptions of Zoning By-law No. 569-2013 to amend the regulations contained in Exception RD 267 to restore the lot frontage and lot area requirements that existed prior to the date of the…

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council enact a Zoning By-law amendment to Zoning By-law 569-2013, substantially in accordance with Attachment 2 to the report (March 7, 2018) from the Director, Community Planning, Scarborough District. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to make such stylistic and technical changes to the draft Zoning By-law Amendment as may be required.
