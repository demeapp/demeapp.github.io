City Council considers endorsing community events for liquor licensing purposes.

Official title: Endorsement of Events for Liquor Licensing Purposes

AdoptedMay 22, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report requests that Community Council consider requests from various event holders for Community Council to declare events municipally significant, and for Council to endorse temporary extensions to licensed hours or areas, for liquor licensing purposes.

What was decided

City Council on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council endorse the requests for extended licensed hours or areas listed below: Event Name Contact Location(s) Event Date(s) Event Hours Extension to Hours or Area The Guild Alive With Culture Arts Festival Councillor Paul Ainslie On the grounds of the Guild Park and Gardens at 201 Guildwood Parkway July 28 and July 29, 2018 11:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Extension of licensed area (patio) Canada Day Event Royal Canadian Legion Branch 13 Councillor Gary Crawford 1577 Kingston Road July 1, 2018 11:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Extension of licenced area (patio)
