Parking Review - Kingston Road between Midland and Highview Avenue

AdoptedMay 22, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to rescind the existing maximum one hour parking regulation in effect at all times, on the northwest side of Kingston Road between Highview Avenue and a point.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report recommends extending the duration of the permitted parking from one hour to a maximum of three hours along the northwest side of Kingston Road between Claremore Avenue/Cliffside Drive and Midland Avenue. The local merchants have requested that the one hour maximum permissive parking be extended to three hours along this section of roadway. Councillor Crawford has indicated that the majority of store owners support the permissive parking for a maximum three hour period. The extended permissive parking duration will allow sufficient time for shop and restaurant patrons to shop and consume meals without being constrained to short term permissive parking.

What was decided

City Council on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018, adopted the following: 1, City Council rescind the existing maximum one hour parking regulation in effect at all times, on the northwest side of Kingston Road between Highview Avenue and a point 105 metres south of Sandown Avenue. 2. City Council rescind the existing maximum one hour parking regulation in effect at all times, on the northwest side of Kingston Road, between a point 80 metres south of Sandown Avenue and a point 18 metres further south. 3. City Council rescind the existing maximum one hour parking regulation in effect at all times on the northwest side of Kingston Road, between a point 40 metres south of Sandown Avenue and Midland Avenue.
