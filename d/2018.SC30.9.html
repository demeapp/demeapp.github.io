Proposed Amendments to Parking Prohibitions - Town Centre Court

AdoptedMay 2, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council was asked to amend the existing parking prohibition in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 8:30 a.m., on the north side of Town Centre Court, between a point 58 metres.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This staff report recommends a technical amendment to parking regulations on Town Centre Court west of its northeast intersection with Borough Drive. The parking regulation signs are in place, but the parking regulation currently references a previous ending time that has since been updated.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 8:30 a.m., on the north side of Town Centre Court, between a point 58 metres west of Borough Drive and a point 71 metres west of Borough Drive, to be in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. 2. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 8:30 a.m., on the north side of Town Centre Court, between a point 29 metres west of Borough Drive and a point 42 metres west of Borough Drive, to be in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. 3. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 8:30 a.m., on the south side of Town Centre Court, between a point 60 metres west of Borough Drive and a point 107 metres west of Borough Drive, to be in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m.
