All-Way Stop Control Review - Brimorton Drive and Dolly Varden Boulevard

PostponedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Scarborough Community Council was asked to authorize all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Brimorton Drive and Dolly Varden Boulevard.

Council put off deciding this item.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report recommends that an All-Way Stop Control be installed at the intersection of Brimorton Drive and Dolly Varden Boulevard as the existing conditions meet the warrant for the installation of such a traffic control device. This location currently has a stop control facing the northbound and southbound approaches on Dolly Varden Boulevard at this four-way intersection.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council deferred consideration of the item until the July 4, 2018 meeting.
