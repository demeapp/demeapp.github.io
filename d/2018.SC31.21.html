Traffic Calming Review - Fundy Bay Boulevard

Adopted with changesJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Scarborough Community Council was asked to reduce the speed limit from 40 km/h to 30 km/h on Fundy Bay Boulevard, between Bamburgh Circle and Birchmount Road.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that the Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. As a result of a Scarborough Community Council request, Transportation staff have reviewed the need for traffic calming on Fundy Bay Boulevard between Birchmount Road and Bamburgh Circle. The assessment indicates traffic calming is not warranted on this street under the City Council's Traffic Calming Policy. Staff have also reviewed feasibility of reducing the existing speed limit from 40 km/h to 30km/h. The assessment indicates that a 30 km/h speed limit is warranted under the City Council's 30km/h Policy.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 40 km/h to 30 km/h on Fundy Bay Boulevard, between Bamburgh Circle and Birchmount Road. 2. Waived the poll and directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadways of Fundy Bay Boulevard, between Bamburgh Circle South to Birchmount Road, for traffic calming purposes, generally as shown on the location Plan, attached to the report entitled "Traffic Calming Review - Fundy Bay Boulevard".
