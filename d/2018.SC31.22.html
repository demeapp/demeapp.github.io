All-Way Stop Control Review - Canongate Trail and Ockwell Manor Drive (South Intersection)

Adopted with changesJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Scarborough Community Council was asked to refuse to authorize all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Canongate Trail and Ockwell Manor Drive (south intersection).

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report recommends that an All-Way Stop Control not be installed at the intersection of Canongate Trail and Ockwell Manor Drive (south intersection) as the existing conditions do not meet the warrant for the installation of such a traffic control device. This location currently has a stop control facing the westbound on Ockwell Manor Drive approach of this T-type intersection.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Canongate Trail and Ockwell Manor Drive (south intersection). 2.
