The official record

What the item proposed

City Council, at its meeting of April 24 – 27, 2018 adopted Member Motion 39.31 entitled "Request for Report from City Planning – Milliken Employment District. This report responds to that request. In consultation with Metrolinx, City Planning staff reviewed the existing permitted uses in the zoning by-laws for the specific area identified on the Map attached to the Member Motion (Attachment 1), to determine if any of these uses will have a negative impact on future transit plans and operations along the Stouffville rail corridor (Regional Express Rail and Smart Track). Staff are of the opinion that by utilizing all of the current Provincial and municipal planning tools and imposing appropriate conditions of development on new development, that impacts from industry can be mitigated to acceptable levels.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council deferred consideration of the item until the July 4, 2018 meeting.
