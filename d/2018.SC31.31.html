Traffic Control Signals at Progress Avenue at the Entrance for Toyota Canada Inc. (830 Progress Avenue) and Bell (831 Progress Avenue) and sidewalk extension

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Scarborough Community Council was asked to have Economic Development staff and Transportation Department staff report back to the July 2018, Scarborough Community Council with a report on the feasibility of installing Traffic Control Signals.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Please accept this letter as my request to add a new item of business to the June 2018, Scarborough Community Council agenda which asks Economic Development staff and Transportation staff to report on the feasibility of installing Traffic Control Signals on Progress Avenue at the Entrance for Toyota Canada Inc. (830 Progress Avenue) and Bell (831 Progress Avenue) and extending the existing sidewalk to the entrance of Toyota Canada Inc. head office.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Requested Economic Development staff and Transportation Services staff to report to the July 4, 2018 meeting on the feasibility of installing Traffic Control Signals on Progress Avenue at the Entrance for Toyota Canada Inc. (830 Progress Avenue) and Bell (831 Progress Avenue) and extending the existing sidewalk to the entrance of Toyota Canada Inc. head office.
