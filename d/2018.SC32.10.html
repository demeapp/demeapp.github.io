The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides preliminary information and an early opportunity to raise issues for follow-up with regard to the applications. It seeks Community Council's direction on the community consultation process and indicates the expected timing for a Final Report to City Council. The application has been circulated to all appropriate agencies and City divisions for comment and a staff review of the application is underway. The proposed development is for five new rental apartment buildings on a new private street. Four of the buildings are 4-storey stacked townhouses, with a total of 66 units, while the fifth building is a 27-storey residential apartment building containing 320 units. A combined 2 level underground parking garage is proposed to accommodate parking for 388 vehicles. A total of 438 parking spaces are being provided which includes 30 spaces that are tandem parking spaces…

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council deferred consideration of the item until the first quarter of 2019.
