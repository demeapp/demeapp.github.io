The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report advises on the feasibility of reducing the speed limit from 50 km/h to 40 km/h on East Avenue between Island Road and East Willow Gate/Mastwood Crescent.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 40 km/h on East Avenue, between Island Road and East Willow Gate/Mastwood Crescent.
