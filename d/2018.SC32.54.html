The Scarborough Community Council was asked to authorize an all-way stop control at the intersection of Edge Park Avenue and Karnwood Drive.

Official title: Request for All-way Compulsory Stop Controls on Edge Park Avenue and Karnwood Drive

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Please accept this letter as my request for your support seeking the installation of All Way Stop Controls (stop signs) on the streets and at the intersections noted below. In all instances, these requests are the result of serious concerns expressed by parents of children attending nearby school (Clairlea Public School). Motorists using the street along which this school is situated are driving in such a manner as to pose safety concerns for the children. It is clear that the presence of an all-way stop control at this intersection would contribute significantly to the safety of the students crossing these residential streets. It should be noted that other actions are also being undertaken including periodic speed enforcement by Toronto Police. However, an all-way stop control is required.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized an all-way stop control at the intersection of Edge Park Avenue and Karnwood Drive.
