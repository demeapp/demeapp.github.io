Council Member Nominations to the Hockey Hall of Fame Board of Directors

AdoptedJan 31, 2018 · Toronto City Council

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to nominate Council Members for appointment to the Hockey Hall of Fame Board of Directors.

What was decided

City Council on January 31 and February 1, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council the nominate the following Members for appointment to the Hockey Hall of Fame Board of Directors for a term of office beginning at the Annual Meeting in Spring 2018 to the Annual Meeting in Spring 2019: Councillor Mark Grimes Councillor Stephen Holyday Councillor Frances Nunziata
