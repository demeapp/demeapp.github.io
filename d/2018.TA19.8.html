The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an update on the activities of the Executive Compensation and Performance Review Committee since January 2017. It recommends annual Chief Executive Officer performance reviews, and a timeline for the completion of a compensation benchmarking study. During 2017, the Committee also conducted a Chief Executive Officer Performance Review and this report provides a recommendation respecting Chief Executive Officer compensation.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Approved Recommendations 1 and 2 contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (January 26, 2018) from the Chair, Executive Compensation and Performance Review Committee with respect to Chief Executive Officer compensation. 2. Authorized the Executive Compensation and Performance Review Committee to undertake a compensation benchmarking study to be completed by the end of the third quarter of 2018. 3. Directed that the Executive Compensation and Performance Review Committee conduct an Annual Performance Review of the Chief Executive Officer and submit its recommendations as a result of the Annual Performance Review to the Board for final approval. 4.
