The official record

What the item proposed

The Federation of Canadian Municipalities' (FCM) Green Municipal Fund (GMF) has offered Toronto Atmospheric Fund a $5 million loan, complemented by a $750 thousand grant, for the next phase of Toronto Atmospheric Fund's TowerWise program which aims to demonstrate and de-risk the technical and economic feasibility of deep retrofits. This project aligns with TransformTO which indicates that to achieve our 80 percent greenhouse gas reduction target by 2050, average greenhouse gas reductions of 40 percent are required across all buildings. Funds would be dedicated to implementation and ongoing monitoring of multi-measure retrofits of four multi-residential buildings, and developing a scale-up strategy informed by lessons learned. Authorized borrowing from the Federation of Canadian Municipalities and pledging Toronto Atmospheric Fund assets as security in compliance with Toronto Atmospheric Fund's By-Law Number 1, Section 12. 2.
