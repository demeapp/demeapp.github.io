What the item proposed

At its June 25, 2018 meeting, the Toronto Atmospheric Fund Grants and Programs Committee recommended that the Toronto Atmospheric Fund Board of Directors approve four grants with a combined value of $127,125.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Approved the following grant requests: a. $42,200 over nine months to the Pembina Institute to analyze the environmental and economic value of implementing delivery microhubs and light commercial electric vehicles for last-mile deliveries in Toronto and adjacent municipalities. b. $24,750 over 1.5 months to the MaRS Discovery District to design a program structure to help Class B and C building owners evaluate and incorporate relevant technologies to improve existing building energy performance, and to accelerate the adoption of these technologies for further commercialization. c. $39,675 over 18 months to the School for Social Entrepreneurs Ontario to engage racialized and newcomer Torontonians to understand climate issues as they pertain to co-benefits and community concerns, and to develop the necessary skills to create local…
