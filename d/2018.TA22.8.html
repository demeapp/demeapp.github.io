The official record

What the item proposed

The Investment Committee has adopted a Target Portfolio – a target asset mix for investment of Toronto Atmospheric Fund's endowments - that has a good probability of generating the revenues Toronto Atmospheric Fund requires with an acceptable risk/return profile and the required liquidity. It recognizes the importance of reducing volatility and the benefit of direct investments in this regard, the current challenges with traditional fixed income, and opportunities in private funds to increase return while reducing risk. Recommendations are also directed at reducing manager concentration and increasing diversification of mandates. Progress can be made to reach the target asset mix but minor amendments of the Statement of Investment Objectives and Principles (SIOP) will eventually be required.
