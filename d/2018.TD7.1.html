The Tenant Issues Committee recommends to Executive Committee that: 1. City Council request the Fire Chief to report to Community Development and Recreation Committee and Tenant Issues Committee on any recommended proposed regulatory and legislative changes with respect to the retroactive installation of automatic sprinklers in multi-unit residential buildings.
