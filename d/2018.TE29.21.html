The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with the city-wide residential demolition control under Article II, Demolition Control, of Municipal Code Ch. 363, as amended by By-law No. 1009-2006, enacted by City Council on September 27, 2006 under the authority of Section 33 of the Planning Act, I refer the application at 130 Sears St. (Application No. 17 239068 DEM 00 DM) to Toronto and East York Community Council for consideration and to decide whether to grant or refuse the application, including any conditions, if any, to be attached to the permit. This staff report is regarding a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the application to demolish the detached dwelling at 130 Sears Street with the following conditions: a. that all debris and rubble be removed immediately after demolition; b. that any holes on the properties resulting from the demolition of the buildings or resulting from the proposed investigation of soil conditions and environmental site remediation are backfilled with clean fill; and c. should the owner fail to complete the new building within five (5) years from the day demolition of the buildings is commenced, the City Clerk shall be entitled to enter on the collector's roll, to be collected in a like manner as municipal taxes, the sum of twenty thousand dollars ($20,000.00) for each dwelling unit for which a demolition permit is issues, and that each sum shall, until payment, be a lien or charge upon the land for which the…
