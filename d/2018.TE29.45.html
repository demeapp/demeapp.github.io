The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Liberty Residences Inc. is constructing a 28-storey residential condominium (ZEN King West Condos) at 19 Western Battery Road located in the north-east quadrant of East Liberty Street and Western Battery Road. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the east sidewalk on Western Battery Road fronting the site for a period of 35 months. Pedestrians will be directed to use the west sidewalk on Western Battery Road.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the closure of the east sidewalk on Western Battery Road, between East Liberty Street and a point 68.1 metres north, from January 24, 2018 to December 31, 2020. 2. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation on the east side of Western Battery Road, between East Liberty Street and a point 68.1 metres north. 3. Rescinded the existing standing prohibition in effect at all times on the east and north sides of Western Battery Road, between a point 65 metres north of East Liberty Street and a point 68.1 metres north of East Liberty Street. 4. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west, south and east sides of Western Battery Road, between East Liberty Street and a point 68.1 metres to the north. 5.
