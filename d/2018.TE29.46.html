The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Deltera Incorporated and EllisDon Corporation are developing the lands bounded by Wellington Street West to the north, Spadina Avenue to the east, Front Street West to the south and Draper Street to the west. The development will consist of seven high-rise buildings of various heights containing office, retail and residential uses. Deltera Inc. will develop six out of seven buildings and EllisDon Corp. will develop the remaining one building to be located in the north-west corner of Front Street West and Spadina Avenue. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the sidewalk and a portion of the westbound curb lane on the north side of Front Street West for construction staging purposes.…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the closure of the north sidewalk and a 3.3 metres wide portion of the westbound curb lane on Front Street West, between Spadina Avenue and a point 61.5 metres west and provision of the temporary walkway within the closed lane from March 1, 2018 to December 31, 2020. 2. Approved the closure of the north sidewalk and a 3.3 metres wide portion of the westbound curb lane on Front Street West, between a point 61.5 metres west of Spadina Avenue and Draper Street and provision of the temporary walkway within the closed lane from March 1, 2018 to June 30, 2022. 3.
