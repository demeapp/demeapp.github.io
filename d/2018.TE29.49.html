The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Tridel is constructing a mixed-use development consisting of 38-storey and 46-storey high condominium buildings at the site municipally known as 575 and 585 Bloor Street East and 100 Howard Street. The site is bounded by Bloor Street East to the north, Parliament Street to the east, Howard Street to the south and Edgedale Road to the west. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the north sidewalk and a portion of the westbound lane on Howard Street abutting the site for a period of 35 months to set up a construction staging area for the development. Pedestrians will be directed to use the south sidewalk on Howard Street.
