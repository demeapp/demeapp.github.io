The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Clark Construction Management is constructing a 9-storey condominium building at 128 Hazelton Avenue. The site is located in the south-west corner of Davenport Road and Hazelton Avenue. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the sidewalk and a portion of the northbound lane on the west side of Hazelton Avenue for a period of 21 months to accommodate construction staging operations. Pedestrian operations on Hazelton Avenue will be maintained in a covered and protected walkway within the closed portion of the northbound lane. The south sidewalk and a portion of the eastbound lane on Davenport Road also needs to be closed to accommodate the construction staging area for the development.…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the closure of the west sidewalk and a 2.6 metre wide portion of the northbound lane on Hazelton Avenue, between Davenport Road and a point 32 metres south and provision of a temporary pedestrian walkway within the closed portion of the northbound lane, from February 7, 2018 to October 31, 2019. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Hazelton Avenue, between Davenport Road and a point 32 metres south. 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the west side of Hazelton Avenue, between Davenport Road and a point 32 metres south. 4. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the east side of Hazelton Avenue, between Davenport Road and a point 23 metres south. 5.
