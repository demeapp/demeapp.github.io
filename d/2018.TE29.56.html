The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Fieldgate Urban is constructing a 6-storey mixed-use development at 1884 Queen Street East. The site is bounded by a public laneway (Ln N Queen E Woodbine) to the north, existing TTC electric substation and Toronto Fire Station 227 to the east, Queen Street East to the south and Woodbine Avenue to the west. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the west portion of the public laneway abutting the site for a period of 15 months to accommodate construction staging operations for the development.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the closure of the west portion of the public laneway (Ln N Queen St E Woodbine), between Woodbine Avenue and a point 30.4 metres east, from January 24, 2018 to March 31, 2019. 2. Directed the applicant to post a 24-hour monitored construction hotline number on the hoarding board, which must be prominently placed and legible from 20 metres and on all elevations from the construction site. 3. Directed the applicant work to with the STEPS Initiative to provide and install public art, including mural artwork, onto every elevation of the hoarding board with adequate spotlighting for night-time illumination, at their sole cost, to the satisfaction of the Ward Councillor. 4.
