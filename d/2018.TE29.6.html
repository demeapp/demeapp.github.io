What the item proposed

An application for Rental Housing Demolition and Conversion under Section 111 of the City of Toronto Act (Chapter 667 of the Municipal Code) has been filed for a residential rental property at 85-91 Broadway Avenue and 198 Redpath Avenue (16 262583 STE 22 RH).
