Staff recommend a new turn prohibition.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to implement a left turn prohibition at all times for vehicles exiting westbound from the driveway at No. 2915 St. Clair Avenue East, Esso Gas Station at the southeast corner of St. Clair Avenue East and Squires Avenue. The proposed turn prohibition will address concerns with regards to cut through traffic south of St. Clair Avenue East.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited westbound left turns, from driveway of 2915 St. Clair Avenue East at all times, for the traffic on Squires Avenue at a point 38 metres south of St. Clair Avenue East.
