Staff recommend against speed humps on Ridge Hill Drive after a resident poll.

Official title: Speed Hump Poll Results - Ridge Hill Drive

Adopted with changesJan 16, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is reporting on the results of the traffic calming poll undertaken on Ridge Hill Drive, between Old Park Road and Hilltop Road. The minimum response rate was achieved, however, the minimum 60 percent "in favour" required by the traffic calming policy in order to install speed humps on the subject section of Ridge Hill Drive was not achieved. Accordingly, continuing the process to install speed humps on Ridge Hill Drive, between Old Park Road and Hilltop Road, is not recommended.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council directed: 1. The City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway to install four speed humps on Ridge Hill Drive, between Old Park Road and Hilltop Road, generally as shown on Drawing No. 421G-2625, dated August 2017, attached to the report (November 24, 2017) from the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District.
