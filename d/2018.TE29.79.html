Staff recommend against speed humps on St. Joseph Street after a resident poll.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services staff have reviewed the need for traffic calming, specifically speed humps, on St. Joseph Street, between Queen's Park Crescent East and Bay Street, to address residents' concerns regarding vehicle speeds. Our assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the traffic calming policy has not been satisfied. Therefore, traffic calming should not be installed on St. Joseph Street, between Queen's Park Crescent East and Bay Street.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council directed: 1. The Acting Director of Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District to request the City Clerk to poll eligible householders on St. Joseph Street, between Queen's Park Crescent East and Bay Street, to determine whether residents support the installation of traffic calming, in accordance with the City of Toronto Traffic Calming Policy. 2. Subject to favourable results of the poll: a. the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway on St. Joseph Street, between Queen's Park Crescent East and Bay Street, for traffic calming purposes, generally as shown on the copy of Drawing No. 421G - 2800, dated December 2017, attached to the report (November 29, 2017) from the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District; and b. to approve the reduction of the speed limit from 40km/h to…
