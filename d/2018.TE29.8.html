No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes a Zoning By-law Amendment for all of the properties located in the Olde East York Village retail area on Coxwell Avenue to increase the existing front yard setback permission.

What was decided

City Council on January 31 and February 1, 2018, adopted the following: 1.
