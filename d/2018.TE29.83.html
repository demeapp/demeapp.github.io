Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to designate a bus loading zone to operate at all times on the south side of Mill Street.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to designate a Bus Loading Zone on the south side of Mill Street, east of Cherry Street, to provide a safe pick-up/drop-off area for tour buses near the Distillery District. Buses are currently using Mill Street, west of Cherry Street, to load and unload passengers impeding traffic flow, causing congestion and impacting the safety of all road users.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Designated a bus loading zone to operate at all times on the south side of Mill Street, between a point 33 metres east of Cherry Street East and a point 31 metres further east. 2. Authorized the removal of parking machines on the south side of Mill Street, between a point 33 metres east of Cherry Street East and a point 31 metres further east, operating from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. Monday to Saturday and from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. on Sunday, for a maximum of three hours at a rate of $3.00 per hour.
