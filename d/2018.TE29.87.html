The official record

What the item proposed

The local resident associations has expressed a keen desire to remedy the dangers created by the volume of traffic that moves through its community to access Rosedale Valley Road from Park Road. Residents report that congestion, and the frustration it causes drivers, is resulting in blocked intersections, unsafe traffic maneuvers, and cars queueing as far as Bloor Street. With the dozens of new high rise residential buildings contemplated for the area, a fulsome review of current conditions and a full range of solutions is necessary to ensure a continued livable and dynamic neighborhood and to achieve Toronto's objectives, as detailed in Vision Zero.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed Transportation Services to initiate a study to review and to report back to Toronto and East York Community Council on the safety and effectiveness of traffic operations on Park Road from Church Street to Rosedale Valley Road during rush-hour, including an assessment of the effectiveness of existing traffic lights, signs, and any changes that could be made to reduce congestion in intersections and enhance pedestrian safety. 2.
