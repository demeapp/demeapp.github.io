Endorsement of Events for Liquor Licensing Purposes

AdoptedJan 31, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to endorse various events for liquor licensing purposes. Endorsement supports the event organizers' liquor licence applications.

A citywide decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report requests that Community Council consider requests from various event holders for Community Council to declare events municipally significant, and for Council to endorse temporary extensions to licensed hours or areas, for liquor licensing purposes.
