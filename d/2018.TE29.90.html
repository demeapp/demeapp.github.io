Appointment to the Applegrove Community Complex Board of Management

AdoptedJan 16, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to appoint the recommended candidate(s) to the Applegrove Community Complex Board for a term ending March 31, 2018. Board appointments are made at the pleasure of Council, until a successor is appointed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Appointments to the Applegrove Community Complex Board of Management are a matter for which the Community Council has been delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision, provided that the recommendations do not vary from the City of Toronto's Public Appointments Policy. At its meeting on October 23, 2017, the Board of Management for Applegrove Community Complex accepted the resignation of Mercedes Lee who had been appointed for a term ending on March 31, 2018. At its meeting November 20, 2017, and in accordance with Applegrove’s Constitution, the Board of Management selected a qualified individual, Stacey Young, to serve the remainder of the term expiring March 31, 2018.
