Appointments to the Board of Management of Cecil Community Centre

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to appoint the recommended candidate(s) to the Board of Management of Cecil Community Centre.

The official record

What the item proposed

At the Cecil Community Centre Annual General Meeting held on October 26, 2017, the membership nominated the individuals listed above for appointment by the Toronto and East York Community Council to the Board of Management of Cecil Community Centre. The Board of Management struck a Nominations Committee earlier this year to fill upcoming vacancies on the Board and confirmed that there would be six vacancies, as listed above. The Nominations Committee, among other duties, reviewed the role, responsibilities. and required skills of a Board member and identified additional desirable skills. those being: finance, fundraising and resource development and public relations. The six nominees were screened and interviewed by the Nominations Committee and these candidates met the criteria and fit best with the immediate priorities facing the organization.
