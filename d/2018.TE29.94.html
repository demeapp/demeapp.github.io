Foxbar Road Parking Amendment - Rescind 10 Minute Daycare Pick up Parking

AdoptedJan 16, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing maximum 10-minute parking limit in effect from 4:00 p.m. and 6:00 pm., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Foxbar Road.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The enclosed recommendation is to rescind the existing maximum 10-minute parking limit in effect from 4:00 pm to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday on Foxbar Road. This regulation is no longer required as the daycare at Deer Park United Church has been relocated, as the building has been incorporated within the redevelopment project at 129 St. Clair Avenue West.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing maximum 10-minute parking limit in effect from 4:00 p.m. and 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Foxbar Road, between a point 15 metres south of St. Clair Avenue West and a point 53.6 metres further south.
