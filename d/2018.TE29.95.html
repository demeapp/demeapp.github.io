What the item proposed

It has been brought to my attention by a family member of the current owner at 1 Edmund Gate that this is an architecturally and culturally significant property, but it currently has no protection under the Ontario Heritage Act. The main house and guest-house, built for Hume Blake in 1928-1929 and titled, 'Residence on the Hill', together represent a stately example in the neighbourhood of the Georgian Revival architectural style characterized by Classical details, symmetry and order. Also, the property's designer, Donald Mackenzie Waters (1894-1968), was an important Ontario-born architect who enjoyed a prolific career in residential and commercial architecture in Toronto and beyond. I understand that several other properties designed by Waters are already included on the City's heritage register. What was decided

Toronto and East York Community Council recommends that: 1. Direct the Manager, Heritage Preservation Services, to report back to Toronto and East York Community Council on the possibility of designating the building at 1 Edmund Gate under Part IV of the Ontario Heritage Act.
