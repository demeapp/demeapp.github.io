Installation / Removal of On-Street Parking Restrictions - Brant Street in Ward 20

Staff recommend: Toronto and East York Community Council (TEYCC) re-open Item TE27.56 entitled "Installation/removal of On-Street Parking Restrictions – Various Streets in Wards 20 and 28 ".

The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto and East York Community Council, at its meeting of October 17, 2017 adopted Item TE27.56 entitled, "Installation/Removal of On-Street Parking Restrictions – Various Streets in Wards 20 and 28" which requested authorization to implement taxi stands on Portland Street, Brant Street, Wellington Street, and Widmer Street. This was to accommodate additional taxi stand spaces in the vicinity of the King Street Transit Pilot. Based on field observations and in consultation with the Toronto Police Service and the Councillor's Office, Transportation Services recommends that the taxi stand that was recently introduced on Brant Street be removed, which requires a re-opening of TE27.56. The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item TE27.56 entitled "Installation/removal of On-Street Parking Restrictions – Various Streets in Wards 20 and 28 ", with respect to Attachment 1 – Amendments to Traffic and Parking Regulations Revised. 2. Deleted the following entry under Attachment 1 of Item TE27.56: Stands for Taxicabs Highway Side Location No. of taxicabs Times and/or Days Brant Street East Between a point 15 metres north of King Street West and a point 24 metres further north 4 Anytime No Standing Highway Side Between Prohibited Times and/or Days Brant Street East A point 15 metres north of King Street West and a point 24 metres further north Anytime
