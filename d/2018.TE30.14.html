The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council refuse an application to demolish a heritage structure on the property at 170 Merton Street, which is subject to Intention to Designate under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act. Under the Act, if City Council fails to make a decision on the application within 90 days of issuing a Notice of Receipt of the application, the application is deemed to be permitted under Section 34 of the Ontario Heritage Act. Based on issuance of the Notice of Receipt, the applicant must be notified of Council's decision by April 10, 2018.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council refuse the issuance of a demolition permit for the heritage property at 170 Merton Street, in accordance with Section 34 of the Ontario Heritage Act. 2. If the owner appeals City Council’s decision to refuse the issuance of a demolition permit under Section 34 of the Ontario Heritage Act for the heritage property at 170 Merton Street, City Council authorize the City Solicitor and the necessary City staff to attend the Ontario Municipal Board hearing in opposition to the appeal.
