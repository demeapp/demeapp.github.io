The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council designate the property at 462 Eastern Avenue under Part IV of the Ontario Heritage Act, endorse the conservation strategy generally described for the property and give authority to enter into a Heritage Easement Agreement with the owner of the property. The property at 462 Eastern Avenue is listed on the City of Toronto's Heritage Register. The applicant is proposing to alter the existing 4-storey building with 2-storey addition and to construct a new mid-rise building behind. The related Zoning By-law Amendment, Official Plan Amendment and Site Plan applications have been appealed to the Ontario Municipal Board.
