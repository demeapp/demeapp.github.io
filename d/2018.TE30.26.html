Request for an Extension of Operating Hours of the Boulevard Café Permit Located at 162 Ossington Avenue, Foxley Street Flankage

Staff recommend: The request for an extension of operating hours of the boulevard café permit located at 162 Ossington Avenue, Foxley Street flankage be considered as the former City of Toronto.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Toronto and East York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. To report on the request received on October 18, 2017 from Organico Inc., operating as I Deal Coffee, for an extension of operating hours of the boulevard café permit located at 162 Ossington Avenue, Foxley Street flankage.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the extension of operating hours of the boulevard café located at 162 Ossington Avenue, Foxley Street flankage under the former City of Toronto, Municipal Code, Chapter 313, Section F(2)(a). The owner and occupant shall ensure the boulevard café is closed and cleared by 11:00 p.m. 2. Directed Municipal Licensing and Standards staff to review the boulevard café permit in 2019, at the request of the Ward Councillor.
