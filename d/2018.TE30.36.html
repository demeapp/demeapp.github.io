Toronto City Council · Decision

Appointments to Business Improvement Area Boards of Management

Adopted with changesFeb 21, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to appoint the recommended candidate(s) to the Business Improvement Area Boards of Management.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend that Toronto and East York Community Council appoint directors to the Hillcrest Village, Parkdale Village and Queen Street West Business Improvement Area Boards of Management and remove directors from the Church-Wellesley Village, CityPlace and Fort York, Mount Pleasant, Upper Village and Waterfront Business Improvement Area Boards of Management.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. In accordance with the City's Public Appointments Policy, appointed the following nominees to the Business Improvement Area Boards of Management set out below at the pleasure of Toronto and East York Community Council, and for a term expiring at the end of the term of Council or as soon thereafter as successors are appointed: Hillcrest Village: Hannah, Heather Parkdale Village: Crowe, Nicole Deans, Richard James Queen Street West: Gauthier, Philippe Upper Village: Khalaf, Ismail Lee, Suki Rose, Amanda Spring, Martin 2. Removed the following directors from the Business Improvement Area Boards of Management set out below: Church-Wellesley Village: Gaudreault, Francis Lapointe, Lysa CityPlace and Fort York: Ostojic, Mario Mount Pleasant: Cowan, Paige Upper Village: Goldstein, Lionel Stojkovic, Nabojsa The Waterfront: Pitfield, Alison
