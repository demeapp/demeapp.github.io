Toronto City Council · Decision

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to re-open item TE28.62 entitled "Road Alteration - Leslie Street".

Official title: Re-opening TE28.62 - Leslie Street Traffic Calming

AdoptedApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Back in November you supported residents of Leslie Street who were calling for their street to be made safer by adding two traffic islands to narrow the road, slow traffic and keep people safe. You also supported my recommendation to look at additional traffic calming. Transportation Services has reported back that while extra speed humps are not possible, additional traffic islands placed between the existing speed humps will improve safety on Leslie.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened item TE28.62 entitled "Road Alteration - Leslie Street". 2. Amended Item TE28.62 by adding the following two new Recommendations 5 and 6: "5. Toronto and East York Community Council authorize the installation of three traffic islands on the west side of Leslie Street, between Queen Street East and Dundas Street East, generally as shown on the attached Drawing 421G-2930, dated March 2018" "6. Toronto and East York Community Council amend the existing one-hour maximum parking regulation in effect from 8:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday on the east side of Leslie Street, between the first public lane north of Queen Street East and a point 100.5 metres south of Dundas Street East, to be in effect between a point 44 metres north of Queen Street East and a point 100.5 metres south of Dundas Street East."
