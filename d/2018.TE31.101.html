Black Lab Brewery Sales by the Glass Permit application to the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario

AdoptedApr 24, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to support the Black Lab Brewery application to the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario for a Manufacturer's Limited Liquor Sales Licence for sales by the glass.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I received a request from the owner of Black Lab Brewery that they require a resolution from the local Municipal Council in order to obtain a Manufacturer's Limited Liquor Sale licence, which would allow for a "sales by the glass" permit for their brewery at 818 Eastern Avenue.

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council support the Black Lab Brewery application to the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario for a Manufacturer's Limited Liquor Sales Licence for sales by the glass at its manufacturing site located at 818 Eastern Avenue.
