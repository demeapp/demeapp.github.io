The official record

What the item proposed

The property is located in the Annex, directly north of the East Annex Heritage Conservation District, and adjacent to the Madison Avenue Heritage Conservation District and the forthcoming West Annex Heritage Conservation District. The Annex is an area with distinct heritage considerations and districts and a rich architectural history. As a part of its history, the Annex has been at the forefront of balancing downtown intensification and the protection of historical neighbourhoods. Its unique location has created a mixture of apartment buildings and low-rise residential. However, despite this mix, there are few low-rise multi-unit apartment buildings, outside of sub-divided single family dwellings. 241 Bedford Road is one of the few examples of this type of residential built form in the neighbourhood. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the Senior Manager, Heritage Preservation Services, to evaluate the Registered Property at 241 Bedford Road for designation under Part IV of the Ontario Heritage Act and to report to the Toronto Preservation Board, Toronto East York Community Council and City Council.
