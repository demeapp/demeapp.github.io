324 Richmond Street West - Request for City Solicitor to Attend at the Toronto Local Appeal Body

City Council was asked to authorize the City Solicitor to appeal the Committee of Adjustment's Decision regarding Application No. A1081/17TEY respecting 324 Richmond Street West.

The official record

What the item proposed

Petaluma Building Corporation (the "Applicant") has applied to the Committee of Adjustment (Application No. A1081/17TEY) for variances to alter the redevelopment plan approved under site specific by-law 1041-2014 (the "By-law") to permit a 27-storey mixed-use building with reduced parking at 324 Richmond Street West (the "Application"). Previously in 2014, City Council approved a site specific zoning by-law amendment for the subject property permitting the construction of a 25-storey mixed-use building in accordance with the performance standards set out in the By-law. In the current Application, the same Applicant is now seeking two additional storeys and related increases to the height, an increase to the gross floor area, and a reduction to the minimum number of parking spaces required. City Planning submitted a staff report, dated March 23, 2018, to the Committee of Adjustment…

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to appeal the Committee of Adjustment's Decision regarding Application A1081/17TEY respecting 324 Richmond Street West. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to negotiate a settlement of the appeal of Application A1081/17TEY respecting 324 Richmond Street West and authorize the City Solicitor to settle the matter on behalf of the City, at the discretion of the City Solicitor after consultation with the Ward Councillor. 3. In the event that a settlement is not reached, City Council authorize the City Solicitor, along with appropriate City Staff, to attend the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal in order to oppose the variances requested in Application A1081/17TEY respecting 324 Richmond Street West and to retain outside consultants as necessary.
