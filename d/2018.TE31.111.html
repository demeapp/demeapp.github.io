Re-opening of Item TE 30.99 - Parking Amendments on Emerson Avenue, between Bloor Street West and Paton Road - Ward 18

AdoptedApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to re-open item TE30.99 entitled "Parking Amendments – Emerson Avenue, between Bloor Street West and Paton Road", with respect to Recommendations 5.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto and East York Community Council, at its meeting on February 21, 2018 adopted recommendations to amend the parking regulations on Emerson Avenue, between Bloor Street West and Paton Road. Once adopted, and the necessary bills passed, parking would be placed on the west side of Emerson Avenue (Bloor Street West to Paton Road), while alternating to the east side of the street, between April and November. Soon after this item was adopted, it was brought to my attention there had been an error in the recommendations. Therefore, the purpose of re-opening this item is to make the necessary corrections.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item TE30.99 entitled "Parking Amendments – Emerson Avenue, between Bloor Street West and Paton Road", with respect to Recommendations 5 and 6. 2. Deleted Recommendation 5 of Item TE30.99 and replace it with the following new Recommendation 5: "5. Toronto and East York Community Council prohibit parking from the 16th day to the last day of each month, from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the east side of Emerson Avenue, between a point 68 metres north of Bloor Street West and Paton Road." 3. Deleted Recommendation 6 of Item TE30.99 and replace it with the following new Recommendation 6: "6. Toronto and East York Community Council authorize alternate side permit parking on Emerson Avenue, between Bloor Street West and Paton Road, from 12:01 a.m. 10:00 a.m. daily.
