Residential Demolition Application - 1144 Queen Street West

Adopted with changesApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to refuse the application to demolish the subject mixed use building that contains 2 residential units at 1144 Queen Street West because there is no permit.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with city-wide residential demolition control under Article II, Demolition Control, of Municipal Code Ch. 363, as amended by By-law No. 1009-2006, enacted by City Council on September 27, 2006 under the authority of Section 33 of the Planning Act, I refer the demolition application for 1144 Queen Street West (Application No. 18-113596 DEM) to Toronto and East York Community Council for consideration and to decide whether to grant or refuse the application, including any conditions, if any, to be attached to the permit application. This staff report is regarding a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the application to demolish the subject mixed use building that contains 2 residential units at 1144 Queen Street West without conditions.
