Residential Demolition Application - 47 Saulter Street

Adopted with changesApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to refuse the application to demolish the subject residential building that contains one residential unit at 47 Saulter Street because there is no permit for a replacement.

The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with city-wide residential demolition control under Article II, Demolition Control, of Municipal Code Ch. 363, as amended by By-law No. 1009-2006, enacted by City Council on September 27, 2006 under the authority of Section 33 of the Planning Act, I refer the demolition application for 47 Saulter Street (Application No. 18-119220 DEM) to Toronto and East York Community Council for consideration and to decide whether to grant or refuse the application, including any conditions, to be attached to the permit application. This staff report is regarding a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Refused the application to demolish the subject residential building that contains one residential unit at 47 Saulter Street because there is no permit for a replacement building issued on this site. 2. Requested the Senior Manager, Heritage Preservation Services to evaluate the Registered Property at 47 Saulter Street for designation under Part IV of the Ontario Heritage Act and to report to the Toronto Preservation Board, Toronto East York Community Council and City Council.
