Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to officially name the future park located at 190 Manor Road East "Manor Community Green".

Official title: Proposed Official Naming of Two Future Parks at 190 and 250 Manor Road East, Ward 22

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division is recommending that the future park located at 190 Manor Road East be officially named "Manor Community Green". The Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division is recommending that the future park located at 250 Manor Road East be officially named "Cudmore Creek Park" and that the playground within this park be officially named "Mona Piper Playground". The Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division has reviewed the policy criteria in the Council-approved City of Toronto Property Naming Policy and verifies that the proposed park name meets the criteria.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Officially named the future park located at 190 Manor Road East "Manor Community Green". 2. Officially named the future park located at 250 Manor Road East "Cudmore Creek Park". 3. Officially named the new playground within the future park located at 250 Manor Road East "Mona Piper Playground".
