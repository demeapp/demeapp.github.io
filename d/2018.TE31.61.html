What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. PCL Constructors Canada Incorporated is constructing a 32-storey office building at 16 York Street. The site is located in the south-west corner of Bremner Boulevard and York Street. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the sidewalk and a portion of the eastbound lane on the south side of Bremner Boulevard for a period of approximately 21 months in order to enable construction of the development. Pedestrian operations will be maintained in a covered and protected walkway maintained within the closed portion of the eastbound lane.

What was decided

Rescinded the existing parking prohibition during all times on the south side of Bremner Boulevard, between York Street and Lower Simcoe Street. 3.
