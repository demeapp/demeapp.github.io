The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Menkes Developments Ltd., is constructing a 29-storey mixed-use development at 60 Shuter Street and 187-189 Church Street. The site is located in the north-east corner of Church Street and Shuter Street. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the sidewalk and a portion of the southbound lane on the west side of Dalhousie Street for a period of 27 months. Pedestrians will be directed to a 1.5 metre wide covered and protected walkway within the closed portion of the southbound lane on Dalhousie Street. In addition, approval is requested to temporarily convert a section of Dalhousie Street abutting the site from a two-way traffic operation to a one-way northbound traffic operation.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the closure of the west sidewalk and a 3.3 metre wide portion of the southbound lane on Dalhousie Street, between a point 5 metres north of Shuter Street and a point 40.5 metres further north, from April 11, 2018 to June 30, 2020. 2. Approved the implementation of one-way northbound traffic operation on Dalhousie Street, between Shuter Street and a point 52 metres north. 3. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Dalhousie Street, between Shuter Street and a point 52 metres north. 4. Rescinded the existing parking machine regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday and 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday on the east side of Dalhousie Street, between Shuter Street and a point 86.5 metres north. 5.
