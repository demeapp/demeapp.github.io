The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. PCL Constructors Canada Inc. is redeveloping the existing mixed-use development at 700 Bay Street, located in the north-west corner of Walton Street and Bay Street. The redevelopment is currently in progress. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the sidewalk and a portion of the westbound lane on the north side of Walton Street abutting the site to facilitate construction and redevelopment of the eastern portion of the site. The proposed construction staging area is being requested for a period of 23 months.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the closure of the sidewalk and 2.6 metre wide portion of the westbound lane on the north side of Walton Street, between a point 38 metres east of Elizabeth Street and point 32 metres further east, from April 11, 2018 to February 28, 2020. 2. Rescinded the existing parking machine regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday; and 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. Sunday on the north side of Elizabeth Street, between a point 70 metres east of Walton Street and a point 26.5 metres west of Bay Street. 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the north side of Walton Street, between a point 70 metres east of Elizabeth Street and a point 26.5 metres west of Bay Street. 4.
