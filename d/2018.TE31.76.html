The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to rescind the existing Thursday alternate side parking arrangement in effect from April 1 through November 30, on Walmer Road, between Austin Terrace and Russell Hill Drive, on Castle View Avenue and on Russell Hill Drive. The proposed change will provide parking on one side of the street only as requested by Councillor Joe Mihevc on behalf of area residents.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the parking regulations outlined in Appendix A attached to the report (February 23, 2018) entitled, from the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District. 2. Enacted the parking regulations outlined in Appendix B attached to the report (February 23, 2018) entitled, from the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District, with Appendix B amended to include the section below under "Permit Parking": Street Side Location Period Time Russell Hill Drive Odd From Spadina Road to a point 55 metres west All times 12:01 a.m. to 10:00 a.m.
