Parking Amendments - Spruce Street

AdoptedApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Spruce Street, between a point 30 metres east of Sumach Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to amend the existing parking prohibitions on Spruce Street, between Sumach Street and River Street, in order to generate additional on-street spaces.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Spruce Street, between a point 30 metres east of Sumach Street and a point 15.7 metres further east. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Spruce Street, between River Street and a point 45.7 metres west. 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the north side of Spruce Street, between Dyer Lane and a point 5.5 metres east. 4. Prohibited parking at all times on the north side of Spruce Street, between a point 17 metres east of Dyer Lane and River Street. 5.
