Parking Amendments - Bain Avenue

Adopted with changesApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing 10-minute maximum time limit parking in effect from 8:30 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., 11:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. and 3:30 p.m. to 4:50.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. In order to better co-ordinate with current pick-up/drop-off times at Withrow Avenue Public School, Transportation Services is requesting approval to amend the current hours of operation of the 10-minute student pick-up/drop-off zone located on Bain Avenue, between Broadview Avenue and Ingham Avenue. To enhance safety during pick-up/drop-off times, and deter students from crossing the roadway, Transportation Services is also requesting approval to relocate this 10-minute parking zone from the north side of Bain Avenue to the south side of the street, fronting the school. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing 10-minute maximum time limit parking in effect from 8:30 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., 11:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. and 3:30 p.m. to 4:50 p.m., Monday to Friday on the north side of Bain Avenue between a point 96 metres east of Broadview Avenue and a point 76 metres further east. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 9:00 a.m. to 11:30 a.m., and 1:00 p.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday on the north side of Bain Avenue between a point 96 metres east of Broadview Avenue and a point 76 metres further east. 3.
