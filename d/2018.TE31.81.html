Parking Amendments - Barrington Avenue

Adopted with changesApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to designate a taxicab stand to operate at all times on the west side of Barrington Avenue, between a point 14 metres north of Danforth Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to designate a taxicab stand for three taxis on the west side of Barrington Avenue, from a point 14 metres north of Danforth Avenue to a point 17 metres further north. The proposed changes will facilitate safe and efficient pick-up/drop-off activity for passengers travelling to and from Main Street Transit Station.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Designated a taxicab stand to operate at all times on the west side of Barrington Avenue, between a point 14 metres north of Danforth Avenue and a point 17 metres further north. 2. Prohibited parking at all times on the west side of Barrington Avenue, between a point 31 metres north of Danforth Avenue and a point 22 metres further north. 3. Amended the maximum existing one-hour parking limit in effect, from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Barrington Avenue between Danforth Avenue and Balfour Avenue, to be in effect from a point 53 metres north of Danforth Avenue and Balfour Avenue. 4. Prohibited parking permit holders from parking in the taxicab stand located on the west side of Barrington Avenue, between a point 14 metres north of Danforth Avenue and a point 17 metres further north.
