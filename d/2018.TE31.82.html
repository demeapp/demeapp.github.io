The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Main Street, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting approval to rescind the taxicab stand by-law near Main Street Transit Station and to amend conflicting traffic by-laws on Main Street between Danforth Avenue and Hamstead Avenue. The above recommendation will ensure that the existing posted signs are consistent with the traffic by-laws.

What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council rescind the existing taxicab stand in effect at all times on the east side of Main Street, between a point 23 metres north of Danforth Avenue and a point 11 metres further north. 2. City Council amend the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, May, July, September and November on the east side of Main Street, between Doncaster Avenue and Hamstead Avenue, to be in effect from a point 30.5 metres north of Doncaster Avenue and Hamstead Avenue. 3. City Council rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, on the east side of Main Street, between Danforth Avenue and a point 111.3 metres north of Danforth Avenue. 4.
