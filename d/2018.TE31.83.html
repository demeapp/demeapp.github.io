Traffic and Parking Amendments - Hayden Street

AdoptedApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve the widening of the pavement width of Hayden Street from 6.7 metres to 8.5 metres, between Yonge Street and a point 57 metres east.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to widen the pavement width and amend the current traffic and parking regulations on Hayden Street, east of Yonge Street, as it relates to the development of No. 1 Bloor Street East. The proposed road alteration, traffic and parking amendments will address requirements of the site plan agreement, particularly with regards to safe turn movements of large vehicles and accommodating two-way traffic from the site.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council recommends that: 1. Approved the widening of the pavement width of Hayden Street from 6.7 metres to 8.5 metres, between Yonge Street and a point 57 metres east. 2. Amended the existing one-way eastbound traffic operation on Hayden Street, between Yonge Street and Church Street, to operate between a point 57 metres east of Yonge Street and Church Street. 3. Authorized a stop control for westbound traffic on Hayden Street at Yonge Street. 4. Prohibited westbound left turns from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, at the intersection of Hayden Street and Yonge Street. 5. Prohibited southbound left turns from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on Hayden Street at a point 55 metres east of Yonge Street, the driveway access to 1 Bloor Street East. 6.
