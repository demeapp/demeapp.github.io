Traffic Calming - Speed Humps - Milverton Boulevard

Adopted with changesApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to deny the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Milverton Boulevard, between Coxwell Avenue and Monarch Park Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services staff have reviewed the need for traffic calming, specifically speed humps on Milverton Boulevard, between Coxwell Avenue and Monarch Park Avenue, to address residents' concerns regarding vehicle speeds. Our assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the traffic calming policy has not been satisfied. Therefore, traffic calming should not be installed on the subject section of Milverton Boulevard.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council directed: 1. The Acting Director of Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District to request the City Clerk to poll eligible householders on Milverton Boulevard, between Coxwell Avenue and Monarch Park Avenue, to determine whether residents support the installation of traffic calming, in accordance with the City of Toronto Traffic Calming Policy. 2. Subject to favourable results of the poll, the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway on Milverton Boulevard, between Coxwell Avenue and Monarch Park Avenue, for traffic calming purposes, generally as shown on the copy of Drawing No. 421G-2920, dated March 2018, attached to the report (March 2, 2018) from the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District.
