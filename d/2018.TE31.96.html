Parking Amendments on Sheridan Avenue, between Dundas Street West and College Street

AdoptedApr 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to amend the existing parking on the west side of Sheridan Avenue, between a point 42 metres north of Dundas Street West and College Street, in effect.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents have contacted my office regarding an unsafe situation on Sheridan Avenue where cars often park on both sides of the street, either illegally, or legally during alternate side parking switchover days. This practice is making motorist and cyclist travel hazardous due to the double sided parking and reduced road width. I have raised this concern with City staff, who have advised that this ongoing can be resolved if parking was permanently placed on one side of the street. After further discussion with City staff, it was determined that there are two additional parking spaces on the west side of Sheridan Avenue, between Dundas Street West and College Street. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking on the west side of Sheridan Avenue, between a point 42 metres north of Dundas Street West and College Street, in effect anytime from the 1st day to the 15th day of each month, April 1 to November 30, inclusive, and in effect anytime from December 1 of one year to March 31 of the next following year, inclusive, to be in effect at all times. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition, on the east side of Sheridan Avenue, between a point 42 metres north of Dundas Street West and College Street, in effect anytime from the 16th day to the last day of each month, from April 1 to November 30, inclusive. 3.
