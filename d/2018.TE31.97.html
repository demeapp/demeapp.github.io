The official record

What the item proposed

Over the past several years, many area residents in the Junction Triangle neighbourhood have contacted my office expressing concerns regarding the business located at 1540 Dupont Street. Area residents allege that the following issues have occurred: patrons being overserved alcohol; public drunkenness; alcohol being served to minors; fights breaking out inside the establishment and spilling to surrounding residential streets; drug dealing/consumption and foul language being used by patrons on the street. Area residents further allege that this behavior has been ongoing for many years and has impacted their quality of life. On behalf of area residents, I am requesting that proactive and coordinated measures continue to be taken by Municipal Licensing and Standards, Toronto Police Services (11 Division), the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario and Toronto Fire Services with respect…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1.Requested the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards, in conjunction with Toronto Fire Service, Toronto Police Services (11 Division) and the Alcohol and Gaming Commission on Ontario, to patrol, monitor and coordinate routine inspections at 1540 Dupont Street due to the elevated level of complaints, and to report back to the June 6, 2018 meeting of the Toronto and East York Community Council.
