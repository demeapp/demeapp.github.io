All-way Stop Control and Road Modifications - Fort York Boulevard and Queens Wharf Road

AdoptedApr 24, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to authorize an all-way stop control at the intersection of Fort York Boulevard and Queens Wharf Road subject to all related costs being borne by the developer.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The developer of the CityPlace neighbourhood, Concord Adex, has proposed to reconstruct the intersection of Fort York Boulevard and Queens Wharf Court to permit full vehicular turn movements and legal pedestrian crossings. Currently, the raised central boulevard of Fort York Boulevard runs east-to-west through the intersection, dividing Queens Wharf Road into two separate segments. Vehicles are physically blocked from turning left and while many pedestrians choose to mount the boulevard and cross at this location, it is not safe or barrier-free. This intersection is an important connection in the CityPlace neighbourhood. It is the most direct pedestrian route to the local library branch for four condo towers that are occupied or under construction, as well an additional two towers that have been approved. What was decided

City Council on April 24, 25, 26 and 27, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize an all-way stop control at the intersection of Fort York Boulevard and Queens Wharf Road subject to all related costs being borne by the developer Concord Adex Inc. 2. City Council approve the alteration of Fort York Boulevard at Queens Wharf Road by removing a section of the median in order to accommodate the all-way stop control subject to all related costs being borne by the developer Concord Adex Inc.
