What the item proposed

This application proposes a six storey mixed use building at 1001 Queen Street East. The building proposed is 20.0 metres in height and results in a density of 3.9 times the area of the lot. Two retail units are proposed to occupy the ground floor, fronting onto Queen Street East, 16 residential units are to be located on floors two through six, and 17 vehicular parking spaces are to be accessed via Carl Stryg Lane to the rear of the site. A total of 22 bicycle parking spaces are proposed. This report reviews and recommends approval of the application to amend the Zoning By-law. The proposed building represents a moderate and incremental intensification along Queen Street East.
