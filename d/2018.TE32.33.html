Refusal of a Boulevard Café Permit Application Located at 796 College Street, Roxton Road Flankage

Adopted with changesMay 2, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to the application for the proposed boulevard café permit located at 796 College Street, Roxton Road flankage be denied.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Toronto and East York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This is a report on the refusal to issue a permit by Municipal Licensing and Standards in the matter of an application for a boulevard café permit located at 796 College Street, Roxton Road flankage.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the operating conditions proposed by the Ward Councillor for the proposed boulevard café permit located at 796 College Street, Roxton Road flankage, with the following conditions: a. The Licensee shall not provide or permit music on the outdoor patio. b. The Licensee shall install a visual/noise barrier to minimize the emission of the noise from the patio. c. The Licensee shall close the patio at 10:00 pm daily. d. The Licensee shall ensure the maximum number of seating on the patio is 16. e. The Licensee shall undergo a review of the permit and conditions in Two (2) year's time by the local councillor.
