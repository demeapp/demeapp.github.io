The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. EllisDon Corporation is constructing a mixed-use development at 11 and 25 Ordnance Street. The subject site is located in the south-east quadrant of Ordnance Street and Strachan Avenue. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the south and west sidewalk on Ordnance Street fronting the site for a period of 26 months. Pedestrians will be directed to use the north and east sidewalk on Ordnance Street.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved closure of the south and west sidewalk on Ordnance Street, between Strachan Avenue and the south end of Ordnance Street, from May 9, 2018 to June 30, 2020. 2. Prohibited stopping at all times on the south and west sides of Ordnance Street, between Strachan Avenue and the south end of Ordnance Street. 3. Directed the applicant to post a 24-hour monitored construction hotline number on the hoarding board, which must be prominently placed and legible from 20 metres and on all elevations from the construction site. 4. Directed the applicant to provide and install public art, including mural artwork, onto every elevation of the hoarding board with adequate spotlighting for night-time illumination, at their sole cost, to the satisfaction of the Ward Councillor. 5.
