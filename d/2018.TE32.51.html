What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Jarvis Street East, City Council approval of this report is required. Brookfield Multiplex Construction Canada is constructing a 50-storey condominium building at 175 – 191 Dundas Street East. The sidewalk and northbound curb lane on the east side of Jarvis Street abutting the site are closed to accommodate construction staging operations for the development. City Council, at its meeting on November 8, 2016, approved the subject construction staging area from November 1, 2016 to May 31, 2017. Transportation Services is requesting approval to extend the subject closure of the sidewalk and northbound curb lane on the east side of Jarvis Street for a period of approximately 6 months (i.e. from May 29, 2018 to November 30, 2018). City Council approve the continuation of the closure of the sidewalk and northbound curb lane on the east side of Jarvis Street, between a point 4.2 metres south of Dundas Street East and a point 54.0 metres further south and provision of a protected pedestrian walkway within the closed portion of the northbound curb lane, from May 29, 2018 to November 30, 2018. 2. City Council direct the applicant to continue posting a 24-hour monitored construction hotline number on the hoarding board, which must be prominently placed and legible from 20 metres and on all elevations from the construction site. 3.
