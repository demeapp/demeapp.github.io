What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Cresford Developments is constructing a 38-storey residential building with retail uses and a 5-level underground parking garage at the lands municipally known as 480-494 Yonge Street. The site is bounded by Grosvenor Street to the north, Yonge Street to the east, existing commercial uses (478 Yonge Street) to the south and St. Luke Lane to the west. Construction of the development will be undertaken in two phases. Phase 1 of the construction includes excavation, piling and shoring operations, installation of heritage façade retention system along the eastern façade of the existing heritage building and restoration of the heritage clock tower.
