Parking Amendments - Cooper Street, Freeland Street

Adopted with changesMay 2, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing 1 hour maximum time limit parking in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, on both sides of Cooper Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. As part of on-going construction on Cooper Street, between Queens Quay East and Lake Shore Boulevard East and future construction on Freeland Avenue, between Queens Quay East and Lake Shore Boulevard East, permit holders in area 5G (Toronto Island) have been impacted by the loss of curbside parking opportunity on these roadways. This report will amend the current parking regulations and permit parking operating hours on both Cooper Street and Freeland Street in order to help: - deter on-street parking by non-permit holders - generate additional on-street parking for permit holders in area 5G (Toronto Island).

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing 1 hour maximum time limit parking in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, on both sides of Cooper Street, between Queens Quay East and Lake Shore Boulevard East. 2. Rescinded the existing 1 hour maximum time limit parking in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on the east side of Freeland Street, between a point 106.7 metres north of Queens Quay East and Lake Shore Boulevard East. 3. Rescinded the existing 1 hour maximum time limit parking in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on the west side of Freeland Street, between a point 161.5 metres north of Queens Quay East and Lake Shore Boulevard East. 4.
