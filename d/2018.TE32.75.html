Westover Hill Road Parking Regulations

AdoptedMay 2, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to prohibit parking from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Westover Hill Road, between Eglinton Avenue West and Dov Altman.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Due to the added congestion resulting from the Eglinton Crosstown construction on Westover Hill Road between Dewbourne Avenue and Eglinton Avenue West, Metrolinx, Transportation Services, and the Toronto Parking Authority approved removing the morning rush hour parking referenced in Recommendation 1 and 2, to create clear passage for residents exiting eastbound off of Westover Hill Road. Upon investigation, Traffic Operations staff discovered anomalies in the Municipal Code. Recommendation 3 and 4 are housekeeping items to amend the Municipal Code where conflicts occur with existing signage on the street.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community council: 1. Prohibited parking from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Westover Hill Road, between Eglinton Avenue West and Dov Altman Lane. 2. Amended the existing pay-and-display parking in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, for a maximum period of three hours at a rate of $2.00 per hour, on the east side of Westover Hill Road, between Eglinton Avenue West and a point 41 metres south, to be in effect from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, and from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Saturday, between Eglinton Avenue West and Dov Altman Lane. 3. Rescinded the existing maximum two-hour parking limit operating from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, except public holidays, on the east side of Westover Hill Road, between a point 30.5 metres south of Eglinton Avenue West and Eglinton Avenue West. 4.…
