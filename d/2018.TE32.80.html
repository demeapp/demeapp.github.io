Toronto City Council · Decision

Parking Amendment for Brock Avenue

AdoptedMay 2, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to prohibit stopping at all times on the west side of Brock Avenue, between Delaney Crescent and a point 9 metres south.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents in the Brockton Triangle neighbourhood brought to my attention a serious safety concern at the intersection of Brock Avenue and Delaney Crescent. In essence, when motorists, cyclists and pedestrians attempt to exit Delaney Crescent, at Brock Avenue, they cannot see northbound traffic due parking being permitted on the west side of Brock Avenue, immediately south of Delaney Crescent. Residents inform me of close calls and have asked if a 'no stopping' rule could be introduced, similar to the existing one north of Delaney Crescent, City staff did not object. The recommendations in the motion will not only shift the permitted parking area further south on Brock Avenue, but it will also discourage parking close to the intersection, which will allow for clear sightlines for traffic exiting Delaney Crescent.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited stopping at all times on the west side of Brock Avenue, between Delaney Crescent and a point 9 metres south. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Brock Avenue, between Delaney Crescent and a point opposite the south limit of Middleton Avenue.
