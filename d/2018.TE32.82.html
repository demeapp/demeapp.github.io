Accessible Loading Zone Request on the North Side of College Street, between a Point of 77 Metres West of Dufferin Street and a Point 11 Metres Further West

AdoptedMay 22, 2018 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to designate an accessible loading zone to operate from 8:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, and from 6:00 p.m. to 11:59 p.m., Saturday,.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on College Street, City Council approval for this item is required. My office received a request for an accessible loading zone from Casa dos Açores, which is located at 1136 College Street. Casa dos Açores, established in 1985, is a community cultural hub, whose mission is to promote the heritage of Portugal's Azores region in Ontario. While the membership of this social club is diverse in terms of age, a sizeable portion of members are older adults, who are not able bodied. These members regularly attend functions/programming at Casa dos Açores and would benefit greatly from the presence of an accessible loading zone in the immediate vicinity.

What was decided

City Council on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council designate an accessible loading zone to operate from 8:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, and from 6:00 p.m. to 11:59 p.m., Saturday, on the north side of College Street, between a point 77 metres west of Dufferin Street and a point 11 metres further west. 2. City Council rescind the existing parking machine regulations that operate from 8:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, and from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Saturday, on the north side of College Street, between a point 77 metres west of Dufferin Street and a point 11 metres further west.
