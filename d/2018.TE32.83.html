Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect each Thursday, April 1 to November.

Official title: Parking Amendment for Osler Street - Dupont Street to Cariboo Avenue

AdoptedMay 2, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents have contacted my office regarding on-street parking shortages on Osler Street from Dupont Street to Cariboo Avenue. Residents have also brought to my attention that there are more parking spaces on the east side of Osler Street, versus the west side. In response this concern, I asked City staff to undertake an assessment of on-street parking potential on both sides of Osler Street. City staff confirmed that there are forty spaces on the east side of Osler Street and twenty-seven spaces on the west side of the street. I have consulted with Osler Street residents on the possibility of permanently placing parking on the east side of the street and there have been no objections. I am by way of this motion requesting that parking be placed on the east side of Osler Street, between Dupont Street and Cariboo Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect each Thursday, April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the east side of Osler Street, between Cariboo Avenue and Dupont Street. 2. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times except each Thursday, April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the west side of Osler Street, between Cariboo Avenue and Dupont Street, to be in effect at all times. 3. Rescinded the permit parking regulations in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. each Thursday from April 1 to November 30 on the even (west) side of Osler Street, between Cariboo Avenue and Dupont Street and from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily, except Thursdays from April 1 to November 30, on the odd (east) side of Osler Street, between Cariboo Avenue and Dupont Street. 4.
