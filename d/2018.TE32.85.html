Toronto City Council · Decision

Parking Amendment for Sudbury Street

AdoptedMay 2, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to prohibit stopping at all times on the north side of Sudbury Street, between a point 40 metres west of Dovercourt Road and 13 metres further west.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents in the West Queen West Triangle brought to my attention a serious safety concern at the intersection of Sudbury Street and the public lane bounded by Sudbury Street, Queen Street West, Lisgar Street and Dovercourt Road. The issue is that motorists, cyclists and pedestrians have difficulty seeing eastbound and westbound traffic as they exit the public lane onto Sudbury Street. Residents inform me of close calls and have asked that a 'no stopping' rule be introduced. City staff reviewed this matter and agreed to the requested change. The recommendation in this motion will therefore improve sightlines for traffic exiting the public lane in question onto Sudbury Street.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited stopping at all times on the north side of Sudbury Street, between a point 40 metres west of Dovercourt Road and 13 metres further west.
