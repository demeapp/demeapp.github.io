Reducing the Speed Limit on Dovercourt Road - Dundas Street West to Queen Street West

AdoptedMay 2, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to direct the General Manager, Transportation Services to reduce the speed limit on Dovercourt Road, between Dundas Street West and Queen Street West, from 40 km/h.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The current speed limit on Dovercourt Road, between Dundas Street West and Queen Street West is 40 km/h. This section of Dovercourt Road is heavily used by motorists and pedestrians to travel to multiple local schools and a community centre. Residents in both of our respective Wards have raised safety concerns over excessive traffic volumes and speeding on Dovercourt Road. As traffic incidents are dramatically reduced when vehicles operate at 30 km/h, the purpose of this motion seeks to lower the current speed limit on Dovercourt Road, between Dundas and Queen Street West, from 40 km/h to 30 km/h.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services to reduce the speed limit on Dovercourt Road, between Dundas Street West and Queen Street West, from 40 km/h to 30 km/h.
