The official record

What the item proposed

At its April 2018 meeting, Toronto and East York Community Council (TE31.62) extended the existing permit and conditions for the development at 19 Duncan Street, on Duncan Street south of Adelaide Street West, for approximately one month. Community members had raised some concerns about the safety of this area with the construction staging area, so the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District, was directed to review these concerns in consultation with the ward councillor. Staff are continuing to work with the ward councillor and the developer to review options, and anticipate finalizing the issue in time for the June 2018 meeting of Community Council. As a result, I am requesting an additional extension of the existing permit and conditions for one more month.
