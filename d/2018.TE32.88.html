Downtown Yonge Business Improvement Area 2018 Parklets Program

AdoptedMay 2, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve an exemption from Municipal Code Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks to permit within the Downtown Yonge Business Improvement Area, parklets in the eastbound, south curb.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In May 2018, the Downtown Yonge Business Improvement Area (DYBIA) is proposing a parklet and greening project within the limits of existing street parking spaces along the south side of Elm Street between Yonge Street and Bay. Parklets are sidewalk extensions that provide space and amenities for residents and visitors to stop, sit, and rest while taking in the activities of the street. This particular installation was piloted last year and enhanced the general pedestrian and retail experience on Elm Street between April and October 2017. The Downtown Yonge BIA worked with faculty and students at Ryerson University to develop this project and it successfully repurposed existing street parking spots as vibrant, usable park space. All costs and insurance requirements associated with the parklets are to be borne by the DYBIA and its agents.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved an exemption from Municipal Code Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks to permit within the Downtown Yonge Business Improvement Area, parklets in the eastbound, south curb lane of Elm Street, within existing street parking spaces in front of the 15 and 17 Elm Street, between the dates of May 15, 2018 and October 31, 2018 including set-up and take-down. 2. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services to issue a street occupation permit from May 15, 2018 to October 31, 2018 in the eastbound south curb lane of Elm Street in front of 15 and 17 Elm Street to facilitate the implementation of parklets and a complimentary greening strategy subject to terms and conditions to the satisfaction of the General Manager, Transportation Services.
