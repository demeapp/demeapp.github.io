The official record

What the item proposed

Starting several years ago members of the school community and local residents worked to narrow the intersection of Pape and Riverdale to make it safer to walk to school. This has meant that some of the old parking regulations, implemented when it was a two-lane road at Pape and Riverdale, no longer make sense. Riverdale Ave, between Pape Avenue and Carlaw Avenue is presently not licensed for permit parking. Currently, parking is allowed, outside the hours of a 4pm-6pm restriction, where cars are still subject to the three-hour maximum parking duration. Adding this block of Riverdale Avenue to the permit parking program would add much needed parking spaces into the pool of parking spaces for the use of 8B permit holders. What was decided

City Council on May 22, 23 and 24, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council exempt the implementation of permit parking on the south side of Riverdale Avenue, between Pape Avenue and Carlaw Avenue, from the requirements of subsection 925-4B of City of Toronto, Municipal Code, Chapter 925, Permit Parking, until such time as the Toronto and East York Community Council considers a request for implementation of permit parking at that location.
