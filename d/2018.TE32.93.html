Toronto City Council · Decision

Potential Heritage Properties at 490, 492, 494, 496 and 500 College Street and 307, 309 and 311 Palmerston Boulevard

AdoptedMay 2, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to request the Senior Manager, Heritage Preservation Services research and evaluate the properties at 490, 492, 494, 496 and 500 College Street and 307, 309 and 311.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

It has been brought to my attention that properties along College Street and Palmerston Boulevard are being consolidated and considered for redevelopment purposes. The homes on these streets contribute significantly to the character of the neighbourhood and as such must be evaluated to ensure that we are not going to lose any buildings of historical value.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the Senior Manager, Heritage Preservation Services to research and evaluate the properties at 490, 492, 494, 496 and 500 College Street and 307, 309 and 311 Palmerston Boulevard for inclusion on the City's Heritage Register and to report back to the Toronto and East York Community Council with the results of this evaluation by July 2018.
