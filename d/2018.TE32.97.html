Toronto City Council · Decision

Guiderail at the Northwest Corner of the Curve at O'Connor Drive and Broadview Avenue

AdoptedMay 2, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to request the General Manager, Transportation Services to re-install the guiderail at the northwest corner of the curve at O'Connor Drive and Broadview Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

During the O’Connor Drive Road Resurfacing in the summer of 2015, a guiderail at the northwest corner of the curve at O'Connor Drive and Broadview Avenue was removed and it was not re-installed. Local residents have since raised safety concerns about this corner and would like the guiderail re-installed. This matter has been brought to the attention of the General Manager, Transportation Services and no report has been provided as to why this guiderail should not be installed. If there are any reasons this guiderail should not be re-installed, any concerns about the use of guiderails in such a manner or AODA concerns then those concerns should be reported to Toronto and East York Community Council.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services to re-install the guiderail at the northwest corner of the curve at O'Connor Drive and Broadview Avenue. 2. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services report back to Toronto and East York Community Council if there are safety concerns that prevent this installation.
