The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services recommends that the public lane known as Rodega Lane, abutting the south limit of 65 King Street East and east of Leader Lane be permanently closed. Rodega Lane will be incorporated into the development of an 18 storey office building at 65, 71-75, 79-81 83-85 King Street West and 43 Colborne Street and will allow the developer to create a through-block connection that will function as a private lane.

What was decided

City Council on June 26, 27, 28 and 29, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the permanent closure of the public lane known as Rodega Lane, abutting the south limit of 65 King Street East and east of Leader Lane, shown as Part 1 on Sketch PS-2017-071 (the "Lane"), attached to the report (May 14, 2018) from the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District. 2. City Council enact a by-law substantially in the form of the draft By-law Appendix A to the report (May 14, 2018) from the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District.
