Traffic Signal Investigation on Eglinton Avenue West near Hilltop Road

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to direct the General Manager, Transportation Services to investigate installing a signalized intersection on Eglinton Avenue West at Hilltop Road, or, as an alternative.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Over the years residents have repeatedly requested a crosswalk be placed on Eglinton Avenue West near Hilltop Road since the local amenities at the location entice a high number of people to cross Eglinton. Although a signalized intersection exists to the east at Bathurst Street and to the west at Rostrevor Road, the Hilltop Road vicinity is a preferred crossing location in day to day life. An added consideration to the need for a crossing in this vicinity is now presented by the Eglinton Crosstown Light Rail Train project (LRT). The Forest Hill Station will have its secondary entrance located east of Hilltop Road. It is anticipated that this LRT entrance will significantly increase the number of people crossing here. It is prudent that staff assess an optimal location for pedestrians crossing Eglinton Avenue West in the vicinity of Hilltop Road.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services to investigate installing a signalized intersection on Eglinton Avenue West at Hilltop Road, or, as an alternative, a mid-block pedestrian activated signalized crossing between Rostrevor Road and Hilltop Road, and, upon completion of the investigation, to hold a community consultation meeting, with the Ward Councillor and Transportation Services staff, to present options for consideration to the residents.
