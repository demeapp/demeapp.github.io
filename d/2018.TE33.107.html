Toronto City Council · Decision

Realignment of Permit Parking Area "3G" to exclude 1025 Dupont Street

WithdrawnJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The exclusion of 1025 Dupont Street from the "3G" permit parking area is a means to ensure that current permit holders have sufficient parking space. The issuance of parking permits to this address would negatively impact on the already limited supply of parking spaces in the neighbourhood. The Toronto and East York Community Council recommends that That the General Manager, Transportation Services examine and submit a report back to the Toronto and East York Community Council by July 4.

The item was withdrawn before a final decision.
