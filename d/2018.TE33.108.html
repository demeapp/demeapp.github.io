Re-opening of Item TE27.87 - Junction Triangle Traffic Management Committee - Traffic Management Plan, Phase 2

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to re-open item TE27.87 entitled "Junction Triangle Traffic Management Committee - Traffic Management Plan, Phase 2", with respect to Recommendation 3.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto and East York Community Council, at its meeting on October 17, 2017 adopted recommendations to amend the parking regulations on Edwin Avenue, north of Dupont Street. Unfortunately, since the 'no stopping' regulation was introduced in late fall 2017, little changed on this section of Edwin Avenue. At 15 metres, the current 'no stopping' rule does very little to a) prevent parking too close to the Edwin Avenue/Dupont Street intersection and b) lessen congestion. The intent of this motion is to extend the length of this 'no stopping' rule in order to reduce congestion.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened item TE27.87 entitled "Junction Triangle Traffic Management Committee - Traffic Management Plan, Phase 2", with respect to Recommendation 3. 2. Deleted Recommendation 3 of Item TE27.87 and replace it with the following new Recommendation 3: "3. Toronto and East York Community Council prohibit stopping at all times on both sides of Edwin Avenue, between Dupont Street and a point 28 metres further north."
