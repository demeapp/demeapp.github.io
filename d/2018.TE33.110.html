The official record

What the item proposed

The stretch of College Street West, between Bathurst Street and Lansdowne Avenue, is unique in the City of Toronto as much of the original built form still remains. The purpose of this City Planning review is to focus on the unique character of the Little Italy neighbourhood and the surrounding neighbourhood in order to effectively plan for new development that will complement the existing built form and heritage attributes. The request for this review comes at a time when development pressure is making its way to College Street West. There is some urgency to this request because two parcels of land on the north side of College have recently been assembled for development. The eastern parcel is on the north-east corner of College and Palmerston, including properties on both College and Palmerston. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the Senior Manager, Heritage Preservation Services to research and evaluate the properties at 548 – 556 College, 373 – 379 Manning, 400 Euclid and the large brick barn behind these properties for inclusion on the City's Heritage Register and to report back to the Toronto and East York Community Council with the results of this evaluation. 2. Requested the Director, Community Planning, Toronto and East District to conduct a review of development on College Street West between Bathurst Street and Lansdowne Ave, including an inventory of current applications and current development pressure in the area, and to work with the Director, Transportation Services to review potential streetscape improvements on College Street West in the context of current and future development proposals. 3.
