Implementation of Permit Parking on Riverdale Avenue, between Pape Avenue and Carlaw Avenue

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to amend Schedule A of City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 925, Permit Parking, to incorporate Riverdale Avenue, between Pape Avenue and Carlaw Avenue, on an area name basis.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of May 22, 2018, City Council approved an exemption for the implementation of permit parking on Riverdale Avenue, Pape Avenue and Carlaw Avenue from the requirements of subsection 925-4B of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 925, Permit Parking. Riverdale Ave, between Pape Avenue and Carlaw Avenue is presently not licensed for permit parking. Currently, parking is allowed, outside the hours of 4pm-6pm restriction, where cars are still subject to the three-hour maximum parking duration. Given that most of the residents on Riverdale Ave, between Pape Avenue and Carlaw Avenue have valid 8B permits, including this stretch of roadway would allow for more flexibility and parking availability.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended Schedule A of City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 925, Permit Parking, to incorporate Riverdale Avenue, between Pape Avenue and Carlaw Avenue, on an area name basis, within permit parking area 8B, under the operating hours of 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., 7 days a week.
