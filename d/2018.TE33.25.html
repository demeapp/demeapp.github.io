The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Toronto and East York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. To report on the request received on April 27, 2018 from 10673021 Canada Inc., operating as Blackjack BBQ, for an extension of operating hours of the boulevard café permit located on the commercial frontage of 1044 Gerrard Street East and on the Galt Avenue flankage.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Denied the request for an extension of operating hours of the boulevard café permit located on the commercial frontage of 1044 Gerrard Street East and on the Galt Avenue flankage.
