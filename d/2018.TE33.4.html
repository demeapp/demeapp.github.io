The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes seven 4-storey (12.2 metres) townhouses for the property at 1025 Dupont Street. Each townhouse will include a parking space in an integral garage, which is accessed through a rear private driveway. Planning staff are of the opinion that the proposal is appropriate for the site. The proposed townhouses have been designed to be consistent with the pattern of development along Dupont Street and fit with the existing character of the neighbourhood to the south. This report reviews and recommends approval of the application to amend the Zoning By-laws.
