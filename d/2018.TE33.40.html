Proposed Official Naming of Future Park at Bathurst Street and Fort York Boulevard

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to officially name the future park located at Bathurst Street and Fort York Boulevard "Lower Garrison Creek Park".

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended to be inconsistent with City policy or by-laws. The Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division is recommending that the future park located at Bathurst Street and Fort York Boulevard, informally called Mouth of the Creek Park be officially named "Lower Garrison Creek Park". The Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division has reviewed the policy criteria in the Council-approved "City of Toronto Property Naming Policy" and verifies that the proposed park name meets the criteria.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Officially named the future park located at Bathurst Street and Fort York Boulevard "Lower Garrison Creek Park".
