What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Minto Group is constructing a 25-storey mixed-use building at 94 Cumberland Street. The site is bounded by commercial uses (25 Bellair Street) to the north, residential uses (80 Cumberland Street) to the east, Cumberland Street to the south and Bellair Street to the west. Construction staging areas for the development have been set up on the north side of Cumberland Street, east side of Bellair Street and on the west side of Genoa Street abutting the site. Toronto and East York Community Council, at its meeting on April 5, 2016, approved the construction staging area on Cumberland Street, from April 1, 2016 to July 31, 2018. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the continuation of the closure of the sidewalk and curb lane on the north side of Cumberland Street, between Bellair Street and a point 37.2 metres east of Bellair Street, from August 1, 2018 to December 31, 2019. 2. Approved the continuation of the closure of a 1.6 metres wide portion of the roadway on the west side of Genoa Street, between a point 27.3 metres south of Yorkville Avenue and a point 31.2 metres further south, from August 1, 2018 to June 30, 2019. 3. Directed the applicant to continue posting a 24-hour monitored construction hotline number on the hoarding board, which must be prominently placed and legible from 20 metres and on all elevations from the construction site. 4.
