What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services seeks approval to amend the existing parking regulations on Leslie Street, south of Commissioners Street. These proposed parking amendments are required to support road alterations related to the planned relocation of an existing railway spur line in the area. City Council has approved the sale of the property known as 675 Commissioners Street by Toronto Port Lands Company to Canada Post. Canada Post is redeveloping this property, which is located at the southwest corner of Leslie Street and Commissioners Street.
