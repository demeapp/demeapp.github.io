Commercial Loading Zone - Victoria Street

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to enact a commercial loading zone to operate at all times on the west side of Victoria Street, between a point 9.0 metres south of King Street East and a point 15.1 metres further south.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services is requesting approval for the Toronto and East York Community Council to implement a commercial loading zone on the west side of Victoria Street on the eastern side of the Metropole Condos located at 7 King Street East. This staff report is a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. The proposed commercial loading zone will provide a dedicated space for deliveries and passenger pick-up or drop-off, serving the condominium building and adjacent businesses. These activities cannot be accommodated at the frontage of 7 King Street East because of an on-street accessible loading zone and a public space installation that is part of the King Street Transit Pilot.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Enacted a commercial loading zone to operate at all times on the west side of Victoria Street, between a point 9.0 metres south of King Street East and a point 15.1 metres further south. 2. Rescinded the existing parking machine regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. Monday to Saturday and from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. Sunday, on the west side of Victoria Street, between King Street East and a point 24.1 metres south.
