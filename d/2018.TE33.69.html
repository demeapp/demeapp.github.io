Parking Amendments - Charles Street East

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to prohibit stopping at all times on the north side of Charles Street East, between a point 88 metres east of Church Street and a point 39 metres further east.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to implement a stopping prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Charles Street East, fronting Gerstein Centre at No.100 Charles Street East. The proposed stopping prohibition is recommended in order to discourage motorists from parking and blocking access to the Gerstein Centre's driveways. The proposed change will not affect the existing pay and display and overnight permit parking spaces.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited stopping at all times on the north side of Charles Street East, between a point 88 metres east of Church Street and a point 39 metres further east. 2. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Charles Street East, between Church Street and a point 45.7 metres further east, to be in effect from Church Street to a point 54 metres east. 3. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect from 7:30 a.m. to 9:30 a.m., Monday to Friday, except public holidays, on the north side of Charles Street East, between a point 94.8 metres west of Church Street and Ted Rogers Way/Jarvis Street, to be in effect from Church Street to a point 94.8 metres further west. 4.
