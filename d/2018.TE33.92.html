Toronto City Council · Decision

Traffic Calming - Speed Humps - Simpson Avenue

Adopted with changesJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to deny the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Simpson Avenue, between Carlaw Avenue and Logan Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services staff have reviewed the need for traffic calming, specifically speed humps, on Simpson Avenue, between Carlaw Avenue and Logan Avenue, to address residents' concerns regarding vehicle speeds. Our assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the traffic calming policy has not been satisfied. Therefore, traffic calming should not be installed on the subject section of Simpson Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Deleted the polling requirement and approve the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Simpson Avenue, between Carlaw Avenue and Logan Avenue. 1. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway on Simpson Avenue, between Carlaw Avenue and Logan Avenue, for traffic calming purposes, generally as shown on the copy of Drawing No. 421G-3005, dated May 2018, attached to the report (May 8, 2018) from the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District.
