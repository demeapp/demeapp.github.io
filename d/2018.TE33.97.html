What the item proposed

At the Cecil Community Centre Board of Management meeting on April 26, 2018, the Board members unanimously voted in favour of the appointment of Stanley Ho by the Toronto and East York Community Council to the Board of Management of Cecil Community Centre. Upon learning of Ms. Sham’s resignation, the Governance Committee of the Board of Management commenced a search for a suitable candidate to fill this vacancy. The Governance Committee, among other duties, reviewed the role, responsibilities and required skills of a Board member and identified additional desirable skills, those being: finance, fundraising and resource development and public relations. Mr. Ho was screened and interviewed by the Governance Committee his skills and background met the criteria and fit best with the immediate priorities facing the organization.
