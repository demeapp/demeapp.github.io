The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to appoint, at pleasure of Council, the following public members to the Applegrove Community Complex Board of Management for terms of office ending on March 31.

The official record

What the item proposed

Appointments to the Applegrove Community Complex Board of Management are a matter for which the Community Council has been delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision, provided that the recommendations do not vary from the City of Toronto's Public Appointments Policy. At the Annual Meeting on March 26, 2018, the membership of Applegrove Community Complex accepted the resignations of Pierre Bois who had been appointed for a term ending on March 31, 2018, and Jessica Dolmer who had been appointed for a term ending on March 31, 2019. The March 26, 2018, Annual Meeting nominated the following qualified individuals to serve 2 year terms expiring on March 31, 2020.
