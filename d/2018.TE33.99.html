Re-opening Item TE26.120 - Dewbourne Avenue - Request to Extend No Parking By-law

AdoptedJun 6, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to consent to reopening Item TE26.120 - Dewbourne Avenue, Request to Extend No Parking By-law, considered at its September 6, 2017 meeting.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The south side of Dewbourne Avenue east of Bathurst Street had the stretch of curb with No Parking at all times extended eastward in September 2017. This was passed due to safety concerns for residents trying to exit their drives when Bathurst Street vehicles arriving onto Dewbourne Avenue, were pushed into the incorrect lane to avoid parked vehicles. Since then, vehicles with handicap permits have parked regularly in the location. This stretch of No Parking needs to become No Stopping to better address the original safety concern.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Reopened Item TE26.120 - Dewbourne Avenue, Request to Extend No Parking By-law. 2. Deleted Recommendation 2 and replaced it with the following new Recommendation: "2. Prohibit stopping at all times on the south side of Dewbourne Avenue, between Bathurst Street and a point 24.8 metres east.”
