What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. A 13-storey residential building is currently being constructed by Alterra Group at 49-51 Camden Street. The site is bounded by Camden Street to the north, existing commercial development to the east, existing residential development to the south and Brant Street to the west. Construction staging areas for the development have been set up within the south sidewalk and a portion of the westbound lane on the south side of Camden Street abutting the site. The existing construction staging areas was approved by the Toronto and East York Community Council from September 8, 2016 to June 30, 2018.
