What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Diamond Corp. and Toronto Community Housing Corporation (TCHC) will jointly redevelop lands located at 250 Davenport Road and 181 Bedford Road. The site is bounded by Pears Avenue to the north, existing residential uses to the east, Davenport Road to the south and Bedford Road to the west. The redevelopment of the site will include a new 27-storey residential condominium building and a total of 52 townhouses. Construction staging area for the site will be set up on the north side of Davenport Road, east side of Bedford Road and south side of Pears Avenue.
