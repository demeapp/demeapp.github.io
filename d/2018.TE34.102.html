Construction Staging Area Time Extension - Blue Jays Way and Mercer Street - 355 King Street West

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve the continuation of the closure of the sidewalk and curb lane on the east side of Blue Jays Way, between King Street West and a point 20 metres south of Mercer Street, from September 1, 2018.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. A mixed-use development consisting of 44-storey and 48-storey residential condominium buildings atop an 8-storey hotel is currently being constructed by EllisDon at the lands municipally known as 355 King Street West. The site is bounded by King Street West to the north, existing commercial development and a public laneway to the east, Mercer Street to the south and Blue Jays Way to the west. Construction staging areas for the development have been set up within the east sidewalk and a portion of the northbound curb lane on Blue Jays Way and the north sidewalk and a portion of the westbound lane on Mercer Street. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the continuation of the closure of the sidewalk and curb lane on the east side of Blue Jays Way,between King Street West and a point 20 metres south of Mercer Street, from September 1, 2018 to, June 30, 2019. 2. Approved the continuation of the closure of the north sidewalk and curb lane on Mercer Street, between Blue Jays Way and a point 75.5 metres east, from September 1, 2018 to June 30, 2019. 3. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on the east side of Blue Jays Way, between King Street West and a point 20 metres south of Mercer Street, from July 4, 2018 to June 30, 2019. 4. Enacted stopping prohibition in effect at all times on the east side of Blue Jays Way, between King Street West and a point 20 metres south of Mercer Street, from July 4, 2018 to June 30, 2019. 5.
