Accessible Loading Zone - Church Street

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to designate an accessible loading zone on the east side of Church Street, between a point 30.5 metres south of Adelaide Street East and a point 11 metres further south.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to implement an accessible loading zone on the east side of Church Street, south of Adelaide Street East, fronting St. James Cathedral Centre. The establishment of an accessible loading zone will provide unimpeded curbside access for Wheel-Trans and will not result in the loss of any pay-and-display parking spaces.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York D Community Council: 1. Designated an accessible loading zone on the east side of Church Street, between a point 30.5 metres south of Adelaide Street East and a point 11 metres further south, to operate from 9:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. and from 6:30 p.m. of one day to 7:30 a.m. of the next following day, Monday to Friday, and anytime on Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays. 2. Amended the existing parking machine regulation in effect on both sides of Church Street, between Adelaide Street East and King Street East, from 6:30 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, to operate on the west side of Church Street, between Adelaide Street East and King Street East. 3.
