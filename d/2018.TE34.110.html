The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to implement an accessible loading zone fronting 85 Henry Lane Terrace (Cathedral Court Co-op) located on the south side of Henry Lane Terrace, at Albert Franck Place. The establishment of an accessible loading zone will provide unimpeded curbside access for Wheel-Trans and will not result in the loss of any permit parking spaces.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Designated an accessible loading zone to operate at all times on the south side of Henry Lane Terrace, between a point 2.5 metres west of the projected west curb line of Albert Franck Place and a point 11 metres further east. 2. Amended the existing 1-hour maximum parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, on the south and west sides of Henry Lane Terrace, from Frederick Street to George Street South (north intersection), to be in effect from George Street South (north intersection) to a point 2.5 metres west of the projected west curb line of Albert Franck Place. 3.
