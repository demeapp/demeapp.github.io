Road Alteration - Merton Street

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to reduce the speed limit on Merton Street, between Yonge Street and Mount Pleasant Road, from 40 km/h to 30 km/h.

What the item proposed

This staff report is a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to reduce the speed limit from 40 km/h to 30 km/h on Merton Street, between Yonge Street and Mount Pleasant Road. A community consultation meeting was held on June 7, 2018 at the Geneva Centre of Autism located at 112 Merton Street. Traffic Operations, Road Safety Unit and Cycling Infrastructure and Programs staff presented four options with regards to addressing speed and pedestrian safety concerns on Merton Street between Yonge Street and Mount Pleasant Road. Attendees provided comments on the proposed plan and decided in favour of narrowing the roadway through the use of pavement markings and flexible posts. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit on Merton Street, between Yonge Street and Mount Pleasant Road, from 40 km/h to 30 km/h.
