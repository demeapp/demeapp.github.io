Student Pick-up/Drop-off Area - Madison Avenue

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to designate a student pick-up/drop-off area on the east side of Madison Avenue, approximately 60 metres north of Bloor Street West, for Howlett Academy located at No. 15 Madison Avenue. The pick-up/drop-off area will provide parking for up to 10 minutes during school arrival and dismissal times. This will have a negligible impact on traffic operations.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing maximum one-hour parking limit in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, on the east side of Madison Avenue, between Bloor Street West and Lowther Avenue. 2. Amended the existing parking machine regulation on the east side of Madison Avenue, between Bloor Street West and Lowther Avenue, operating from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, and 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. on Sunday, for a maximum period of three hours at a rate of $2.00 per hour, to operate between Bloor Street West and a point 96.5 metres north of Bloor Street West, and between a point 125 metres north of Bloor Street West and Lowther Avenue. 3.
