Parking Amendments - Old Park Road

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to amend the existing fifteen-minute maximum parking limit in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:15 a.m., from 11:15 a.m. to 1:15 p.m. and from 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to reduce the length of the existing student pick-up/drop-off zone and amend the existing parking prohibition on the west side of Old Park Road within the "T" of the intersection at Shallmar Boulevard to prohibit stopping from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday. The proposed changes will enhance traffic flow and provide a greater turning radius for school buses at the intersection of Old Park Road and Shallmar Boulevard.
