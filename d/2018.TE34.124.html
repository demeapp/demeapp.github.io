Parking Amendments - Hazelton Avenue

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Hazelton Avenue, between Scollard Street and a point 32 metres south of Davenport Road.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to prohibit stopping at all times on the west side of Hazelton Avenue, between Scollard Street and Davenport Road, and on the east side of Hazelton Avenue, between Scollard Street and a point 9 metres north, in order to enhance traffic flow and to provide more space for vehicles to turn safely at the intersection.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Hazelton Avenue, between Scollard Street and a point 32 metres south of Davenport Road. 2. Amended the existing stopping prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Hazelton Avenue, between Yorkville Avenue and Scollard Street, to be in effect from Yorkville Avenue to a point 32 metres south of Davenport Road. 3. Authorized the existing stopping prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Hazelton Avenue, between Davenport Road and a point 32 metres south, to remain after the completion of the construction project at No. 128 Hazelton Avenue (Item TE29.52 at the January 16, 2018 Toronto and East York Community Council meeting). 4.
