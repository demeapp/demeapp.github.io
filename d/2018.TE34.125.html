City Council was asked to approve parking regulation changes for Jarvis Street.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Jarvis Street

AdoptedJul 23, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Jarvis Street, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting approval to extend an existing signed no standing at all times prohibition on the west side of Jarvis Street, in front of No. 178 Jarvis Street, in order to accommodate a proposed curb depression for accessible passengers.

What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit standing at all times on the west side of Jarvis Street, between a point 56.5 metres north of Shuter Street and a point 18 metres further north. 2. City Council prohibit parking at all times on the west side of Jarvis Street, between Shuter Street and a point 44.5 metres north. 3. City Council amend the existing parking machines in effect from 9:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. on Saturday, and from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. on Sunday, for a maximum period of three hours at a rate of $4.00 per hour, on the west side of Jarvis Street, between Queen Street East and a point 56.5 metres north of Shuter Street, to be in effect from Queen Street East to Shuter Street, and from a point 44.5 metres north of Shuter Street to a point 12 metres further north. 4.…
