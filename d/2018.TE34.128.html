Parking Amendments - Austin Avenue

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect from the 16th day to the last day of each month from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the north side of Austin Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to rescind the alternate-side parking regulation on Austin Avenue, between Pape Avenue and Marjory Avenue, and provide parking on the south side of the street year round. The proposed parking amendment will increase on-street parking availability for permit holders, will not impact traffic operations, but will have a negative impact on mechanical street cleaning.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from the 16th day to the last day of each month from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the north side of Austin Avenue, between Pape Avenue and Marjory Avenue. 2. Prohibited parking at all times on the north side of Austin Avenue, between Pape Avenue and Marjory Avenue. 3. Amended the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., at all times, except no parking first day to the 15th day of each month from April 1 to November 30, inclusive; and no parking anytime from December 1 of one year to March 31 of the next following year, inclusive; and no parking anytime from a point 76 metres east of Pape Avenue to a point 5.9 metres further east on the odd/south side of Austin Avenue, between Pape Avenue and a point 36.6 metres west of Marjory Avenue, to be in…
