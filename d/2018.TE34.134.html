Traffic Safety - Soudan Avenue

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to adopt the traffic regulations outlined in Appendix "C" attached to the report entitled, "Traffic Safety - Soudan Avenue", from the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District was directed to conduct a comprehensive review of traffic and pedestrian safety on Soudan Avenue, between Yonge Street and Bayview Avenue. The proposed recommendations for safety improvements include: a plan to coordinate construction staging activity for all developments; a plan to improve pedestrian crossing locations through the use of curb depressions and pavement markings; a plan to install advance 'Playground Ahead' and additional '30 km/h' speed limit signs; a review of all traffic regulations; and a plan to work with the Toronto Police Services on enhancing enforcement of excessive driver speeds, running stop signs and travelling the wrong way…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Adopted the traffic regulations outlined in Appendix C attached to the report (May 16, 2018) from the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District.
