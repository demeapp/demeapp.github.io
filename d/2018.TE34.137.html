What the item proposed

This Report is related to a matter that has been delegated by City Council to Community Council for final decision. The Transportation Services Division (in collaboration with the Toronto Transit Commission, the "TTC") have negotiated a Tunnel Encroachment Agreement with the developer at 587-599 Yonge Street (The Clover On Yonge Inc.) for a proposed pedestrian tunnel connection between the 44 storey condominium building and Wellesley Subway Station. The publicly accessible tunnel will be located under Dundonald Street and will connect to the existing Wellesley Subway Station through an existing knock-out panel built by the TTC in 2017. The top of the existing knock-out panel is less than the minimum required right-of-way depth of cover (1.2 meters) required by the City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 743, "Use of Streets and Sidewalks" (“Chapter 743”).
