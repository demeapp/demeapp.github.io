The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Since Don Mount Court was redeveloped into Rivertowne, local residents have been working with their local Councillor and staff in Transportation Services, City Planning, Toronto Community Housing Corporation (TCHC), as well as Toronto Police Services and the Ralph Thornton Centre to address traffic safety issues. Recommendations developed by the Community Traffic Working Group were reviewed by staff and discussed at a community meeting. This report provides an update on the implementation of road safety improvements for which residents have indicated support.
