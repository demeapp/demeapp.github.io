The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report concerns a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The temporary parking amendments recommended are required to enhance traffic operations and pedestrian safety during this year's Canadian National Exhibition (CNE), which takes place from August 17 to September 3, 2018, inclusive.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the temporary parking regulations on streets in the vicinity of Exhibition Place that are impacted during the Canadian National Exhibition, identified in Appendix A attached to the report (June 8, 2018) from the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District.
