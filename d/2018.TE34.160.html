Toronto City Council · Decision

Conduct a Poll for Calming Measures on Montrose Avenue between College Street and Dundas Street West

Adopted with changesJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to direct Transportation Services to conduct a poll of Montrose Ave between College and Dundas St W on the installation of calming measures to address resident safety concerns regarding speeding and report.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the City of Toronto experiences unprecedented growth, it is also experiencing significant road safety concerns. In Ward 19, many residents are seeking to curb volume, speed and irreverence of traffic signage on their streets by requesting a traffic review from Transportation Services for calming measures. On behalf of the Montrose Ave residents who reside between College and Dundas St W and who are concerned about the safety of the most vulnerable on their street, I kindly request Transportation Services to review and make this segment of Montrose Ave safer for all users.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed Transportation Services to conduct a traffic study followed by a poll of Montrose Ave between College and Dundas St W on the installation of calming measures to address resident safety concerns regarding speeding and report back to Toronto and East York Community Council
