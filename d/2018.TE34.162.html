Toronto City Council · Decision

Expanding Shoppers Drug Mart Female Hygiene Pilot Project

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to request Transportation and Right of Way staff exempt 465 Yonge Street, and 524 Queen Street West from Municipal Code Chapter 743 to allow for the temporary installation of free female hygiene.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Shoppers Drug Mart has piloted a free female hygiene product dispenser at 351 Queen Street East to great effect. For those who are economically marginalized, access to these products can come at great expense or sacrifice. The Shoppers Drug Mart project is just one way that our communities are finding ways to help provide those in need with dignity. In order to expand this program and provide wider access to these products, Shoppers Drug Mart would like to expand the pilot to two store locations at 465 Yonge Street and 524 Queen Street West. The dispensers, largely identical to the dimensions and function of newspaper boxes, would need to be located in the public realm in the same space.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested Transportation and Right of Way staff exempt 465 Yonge Street, and 524 Queen Street West from Municipal Code Chapter 743 to allow for the temporary installation of free female hygiene product dispensers by Shoppers Drug Mart locations through September 2019.
