The official record

What the item proposed

At its June 13, 2018 AGM, the Ralph Thornton Community Centre (RTCC) held elections to fill seven positions on its Board of Management. Seven individuals were nominated by the members to fill those vacancies. The following members retired from the Board upon the expiry of their two-year terms on June 13, 2018: - Graham Chernoff - Jeff Garkowski - Michele Harding The following members retired from the Board prior to the expiry of their appointments on June 2019: - Elizabeth Doyle - Glenn Ewald The following members were nominated by the members at the June 13, 2018 AGM to fill two-year terms effective June 2018: - Bernnitta Hawkins - Megan Lorius - Daniella Moss - Janet Routliffe - William Somerville - Ejay Tupe The above-noted terms will expire in 2020. The following member was nominated by the members at the June 13, 2018 AGM to fill a one-year term effective June 2018: - Sharon Ho The…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council appointed, at pleasure of Council, the following nominees to the Board of Management: 1. for two-year terms effective June 2018, for a term ending June 2020, or until successors are appointed: - Bernnitta Hawkins - Megan Lorius - Daniella Moss - Janet Routliffe - William Somerville - Ejay Tupe 2. for a one-year term effective June 2018, for a term ending June 2019, or until a successor is appointed: - Sharon Ho
