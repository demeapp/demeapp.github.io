Toronto City Council · Decision

Parking Amendments - Sunnyside Avenue

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to amend the existing parking prohibition in effect from 9:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m., 1:30 p.m.to 3:00 p.m., and from 4:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Sunnyside Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The pick-up and drop-off times for the Howard Junior Public School and daycare operate for 15 minutes during the weekday morning, mid-day and afternoon periods. This zone is located on the east side of Sunnyside Avenue just south of Howard Park Avenue. The afternoon times are from 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday. However, parents using the school and daycare have advised that they are sometimes picking up their children between the hours of 4:00 p.m. and 5:00 p.m. and they have asked that the afternoon pick-up and drop-off times be extended to 5:00 p.m. The school and daycare concur with this request which will not have any undue effects on this street.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect from 9:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m., 1:30 p.m.to 3:00 p.m., and from 4:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Sunnyside Avenue, between a point 40.9 metres north of Marmaduke Street (south intersection) and a point 62.2 metres further north, to operate from 9:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m., and from 1:30 p.m.to 3:00 p.m., Monday to Friday. 2. Amended the existing maximum 15 minutes parking regulation in effect from 8:30 a.m. to 9:30 a.m., 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m., and from 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Sunnyside Avenue, between a point 40.9 metres north of Marmaduke Street (south intersection) and a point 62.2 metres further north, to operate from 8:30 a.m. to 9:30 a.m., 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m., and from 3:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday.
