Toronto City Council · Decision

City Council was asked to approve parking regulation changes for Liberty Street.

Official title: Parking Amendment - Liberty Street

AdoptedJul 23, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Parking is prohibited at all times on the north side of Liberty Street, between Atlantic Avenue and Hanna Avenue and is allowed with payment restrictions on the south side from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday; and from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday for a maximum period of 3 hours. This section of Liberty Street is in the heart of Liberty Village and experiences congestion due to heavy traffic volumes and construction activities in this area. Removing the pay and display parking and prohibiting standing at all times on the south side of Liberty Street will help to alleviate this bottleneck and will improve traffic flow and safety for all road users on Liberty Street. This change would result in the loss of six pay and display parking spaces on this street.

What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council rescind the existing parking machines in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday and 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday for a maximum period of three hours at a rate of $3.00 per hour, on the south side of Liberty Street, between Atlantic Avenue and Hanna Avenue. 2. City Council prohibit standing at all times on the south side of Liberty Street, between Atlantic Avenue and Hanna Avenue.
