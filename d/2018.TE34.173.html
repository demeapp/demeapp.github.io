All-way stop control at the intersection of Richmond Street West and Walnut Avenue

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to authorize an all-way stop control at the intersection of Richmond Street West and Walnut Avenue (west leg).

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Walnut Avenue intersects the north side of Richmond Street West in a stop controlled intersection. Based on the Transportation Services review, the installation of the all-way stop control is technically warranted at this intersection. There are planned developments near this intersection and residents are concerned about the safety for road users. The installation of all-way stop control at this intersection will improve safety for all road users at this intersection.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized an all-way stop control at the intersection of Richmond Street West and Walnut Avenue (west leg).
