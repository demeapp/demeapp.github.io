Toronto City Council · Decision

School Bus Loading Zone on the South Side of Richmond Street West, West of Bathurst Street

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to designate a school bus loading zone to operate from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Richmond Street West.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The school bus loading zone for St. Mary's Catholic School is located on the north side of Portugal Square at the front of the school. As many as 11 school buses arrive at the same time at the end of the school day and the queue of buses extend well beyond the school property line. Providing an additional school bus loading zone at the rear entrance of the school on Richmond Street West will accommodate the additional school bus demand. This will enhance safety for students and will improve bus loading operations during peak dismissal time at the school. This proposal will not result in the loss of any overnight permit parking spaces on Richmond Street West, as the school bus loading zone will operate from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Designated a school bus loading zone to operate from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Richmond Street West, between a point 72 metres west of Bathurst Street and a point 50 metres further west. 2. Prohibited stopping from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Richmond Street West, between a point 72 metres west of Bathurst Street and a point 50 metres further west.
