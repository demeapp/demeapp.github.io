Barton Ave - No Entry Restriction

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to prohibit eastbound through movements from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, Bicycles Excepted, at the intersection of Barton Street and Christie Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Seaton Village is a thriving neighbourhood in Ward 20. Characterized by a traffic 'maze' with many one-way streets to minimize vehicular traffic in the area, many residents walk or cycle to local destinations. The grid of one-way streets in the area serves as a way to minimize car traffic, and prioritize the safety of the many residents who walk their kids to school, to the nearest subway station, exit the neighbourhood by bike, or access one of the many Bike Share stations in the area. Historically however, there have existed concerns with Barton Avenue, the only street in the neighbourhood that runs east-west. Unfortunately, many cars and trucks use this street to pass through our neighbourhoods to avoid major arterials during peak periods, oftentimes at high speeds and in erratic ways. This creates a dangerous environment for pedestrians, students on their way to school, and cyclists…

What was decided

Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited eastbound through movements from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, Bicycles Excepted, at the intersection of Barton Street and Christie Street.
