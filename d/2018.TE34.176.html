Traffic and Parking Amendments - Brant Street

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:00 midnight to 7:00 a.m. daily on the east side (odd side) of Brant Street, from Adelaide Street West to Richmond Street West.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In July 2016, City Council approved (2016.TE17.6) re-zoning for a new hotel at 49-51 Camden Street, on the south-east corner of the intersection of Camden Street and Brant Street. It is currently under construction. Hotel uses tend to be associated with relatively high passenger pick-up and drop-off activity including taxi traffic. While the Official Plan requires that large developments of this nature accommodate these activities on site, City staff determined that it would not be feasible in this case due to the small size of the redevelopment site. To accommodate standing taxis and the pick-up and drop-off activity associated with the hotel, the developer proposed to construct a layby on Brant Street. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:00 midnight to 7:00 a.m. daily on the east side (odd side) of Brant Street, from Adelaide Street West to Richmond Street West. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. daily on the east side of Brant Street, between a point 26 metres north of Camden Street and Richmond Street West. 3. Rescinded the existing accessible loading zone in effect at all times on the east side of Brant, between a point 15 metres north of Camden Street and a point 11 metres further north. 4. Rescinded the existing standing prohibition in effect at all times on the east side of Brant, between a point 15 metres north of Camden Street and a point 11 metres further north. 5.
