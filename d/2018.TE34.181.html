Toronto City Council · Decision

Traffic Regulations and Speed Bumps - Sussex Mews

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing speed limit of 40 km/h on Sussex Mews, between Harbord Street and Bloor Street West.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Building streets that are safe for all road users – pedestrians, cyclists, and motorists – is the responsibility of all of us. My office has heard a number of concerns from area residents regarding Sussex Mews, a laneway in Harbord Village. Transportation Services have reviewed the concerns, and are recommending that the speed limit be lowered on the laneway, but that speed bumps also be installed. My office and the local neighbourhood are both in agreement with the recommendations.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing speed limit of 40 km/h on Sussex Mews, between Harbord Street and Bloor Street West. 2. Authorized the installation of speed bumps on Sussex Mews, between Bloor Street West and Sussex Avenue, as shown on the attached Drawing No. 421G–3128 dated July 2018 attached to the Letter (July 3, 2018) from Councillor Joe Cressy.
