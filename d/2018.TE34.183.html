The official record

What the item proposed

On June 27, 2018, the City of Toronto has reaffirmed its commitment to reduce traffic deaths in the City by approving the additional funding of $22 million to accelerate its Vision Zero road-safety plan. While the recent financial commitment feels long-overdue, especially after the recent spike in pedestrian and cyclist deaths in the City, it is a welcome sign of progress for a city consistently plagued with road safety issues. Given this much-needed boost in funding for Vision Zero, it should be the City's utmost priority to utilize the new funds to investigate how to improve road safety in some of the most vulnerable intersections for pedestrians and cyclists.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed Transportation Staff working on Vision Zero to consider the following intersections and roads for priority treatment: a. Bathurst Street and Davenport Road b. Bathurst Street and Nina Street c. Bathurst Street and Melgund Road d. Eglinton Avenue West and Hilltop Road / Chiltern Hill Road
