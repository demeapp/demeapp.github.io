Toronto City Council · Decision

Installing Speed Humps on Glebeholme Boulevard, between Greenwood Avenue and Coxwell Avenue

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to requests the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District, to investigate the feasibility of traffic calming measures on Glebeholme Boulevard.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Area residents have presented a petition requesting the installation of speed humps on Glebeholme Boulevard between Greenwood and Coxwell Avenues. Residents representing about 70% of the households on the street signed the petition to address concerns regarding vehicle speeds and road safety.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District, to investigate the feasibility of traffic calming measures on Glebeholme Boulevard, between Greenwood Avenue and Coxwell Avenue, in the form of speed humps.
