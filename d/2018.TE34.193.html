Toronto City Council · Decision

Heritage Listing for 28-30 Langley Ave

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Paula Fletcher recommends that The Senior Manager of Heritage Preservation Services be requested to list 28-30 Langley Ave on the City's Heritage Register and report to the Toronto Preservation Board and Toronto and East York.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The building located at 28-30 Langley Avenue has a rich history in the Riverdale neighbourhood. The building started as the nurses' residence to the Cholera Hospital which predated the Riverdale Hospital. The house has a very grand look from the outside, with stained glass and beautiful masonry work. It was also a boarding house for girls and women – a safe place for young women to live who came to the city to work. It is currently used as a licenced rooming house.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council requested: 1. The Senior Manager of Heritage Preservation Services to list 28-30 Langley Avenue on the City's Heritage Register and to report to the Toronto Preservation Board and the Toronto and East York Community Council.
