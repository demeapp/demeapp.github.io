City Council considers the report on Cherry Street Parking.

Official title: Cherry Street Parking

AdoptedJul 23, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Local businesses have contacted Transportation Services indicating that "No Parking 9pm-4am" signs have been removed resulting in long term parking. Upon following up with Signs and Markings Unit City Staff found that the signs were removed in 2017 due to the fact that they were not bylawed. These signs have been in place for at least 10 years (as far back as the google street view goes). The same parking prohibition exists on the opposite side, west side of Cherry Street, as well as the east side between Villiers Street and Commissioners Street (just north of the subject location), therefore, to be consistent I would ask you to support the following.

What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit parking from 9:00 p.m. of one day to 4:00 a.m. of the next following day, on the east side of Cherry Street, between Commissioners Street and a point 457.7 metres further south.
