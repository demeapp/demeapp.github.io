Toronto City Council · Decision

Empire Avenue: Technical Amendment

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing permit parking in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily except no parking from the first day to the 15th day of each month, Apr. 1 to Nov. 30, inclusive.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I have been informed by Transportation Services that the by-lawed parking regulations do not match the signs. A motion was passed to make the changes but a Bill was never submitted. This motion solves a technical issue and will not result in any change to the on-street parking configuration.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing permit parking in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily except no parking from the first day to the 15th day of each month, Apr. 1 to Nov. 30, inclusive, and no parking anytime from Dec. 1 of one year to Mar. 31 of the next following year, inclusive, and no parking anytime from Queen Street East to a point 48.8 metres south of it on the even (west) side of Empire Avenue from Eastern Avenue to Queen Street East 2. Rescinded the existing permit parking in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily except no parking from the 16th day to the last day of each month, Apr. 1 to Nov. 30, inclusive, and no parking anytime from Queen Street East to a point 48.8 metres south of it on the odd (east) side of Empire Avenue from Eastern Avenue to Queen Street East 3.
