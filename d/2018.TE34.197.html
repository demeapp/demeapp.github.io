Pedestrian Safety around Hideaway Park

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to authorize the installation of speed bumps in the following laneways as shown in Drawing Nos. 421G-3107 and 421G-3108, attached: · First north-south laneway west of Brooklyn Avenue south of Dundas Street East.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Recently I was contacted by residents, parents and park users around Hideaway Park concerned about pedestrian safety in the alleys that are used by kids and indeed all road users to access the park. Of particular concern was the blind corner at the south-east corner of the park. I was happy to attend a site visit with City Transportation staff, several local parents and other local residents to see this issue for myself. Staff have drafted the language and completed the attached drawings for submission. I am asking that you support my motion to add speed bumps in the surrounding laneways and to add a stop sign to the particularly dangerous south-eastern corner.
