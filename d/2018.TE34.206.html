Toronto City Council · Decision

Amendments to pick-up and drop-off area for the Alexandra Park Daycare

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.; 6:00 p.m. of one day to 7:00 a.m. of the next following day, Monday to Friday.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Alexandra Park daycare provides critical service for our downtown neighbourhoods. Currently, they are located to ongoing construction activities associated with the Alexandra Park Revitalization. In order to continue to facilitate the safe operation of pick-up and drop-off activities for the daycare, additional time is needed to permit these activities to take place on August Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council : 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.; 6:00 p.m. of one day to 7:00 a.m. of the next following day, Monday to Friday; and anytime Saturday and Sunday, on the east side of Augusta Avenue, between a point 134 metres north of Wolseley Street and a point 18 metres further north. 2. Rescinded the existing maximum 10 minute parking limit in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Augusta Avenue, between a point 134 metres north of Wolseley Street and a point 18 metres further north. 3. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:00 midnight to 7:00 a.m. daily, except no parking anytime from a point 134 metres north of Wolseley street to a point 18 metres further north, on the east side (odd side) of Augusta Avenue,…
