Toronto City Council · Decision

Addressing Road Safety Concerns near Wilkinson Junior Public School

AdoptedJul 23, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The staff recommendation is that Toronto City Council prohibit U-turns on Donlands Avenue, between Strathmore Boulevard and Glebeholme Boulevard.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The staff and parents of students attending Wilkinson Junior Public School have raised road safety concerns about Donlands Avenue. In consultation with them and Transportation Services staff, recommendations have been developed to address some road safety challenges specific to this school.

What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit U-turns on Donlands Avenue, between Strathmore Boulevard and Glebeholme Boulevard. 2.
