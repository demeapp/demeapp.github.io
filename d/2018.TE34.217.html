The official record

What the item proposed

In June, 2018, I requested Transportation Services, on behalf of the area residents, to investigate traffic flow concerns on Dalhousie Street as a result of on-going construction at the site of 60 Shuter Street and 187-189 Church Street. It has been determined that simultaneous movements of large construction vehicles and private vehicles create congested traffic conditions on Dalhousie Street due to available narrow road width. Therefore, staff is recommending that the existing on-street pay and display parking spaces on the east side of Dalhousie Street be removed to increase the available road width and facilitate smoother two-way traffic flow. This will result in the loss of nine on-street parking spaces, but will improve safety for both motorists and pedestrians while construction occurs.
