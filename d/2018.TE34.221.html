School Bus Loading Zone - Dan Leckie Way

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to amend the existing parking machine regulation on the east side of Dan Leckie Way, between Fort York Boulevard and Iceboat Terrace, operating from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As our downtown communities grow at a rapid pace, it's vital that we constantly get out into the community to see how our city streets are being used and how they work in practice for local residents. To facilitate this, earlier this year I organized a series of community Safety Walks across Ward 20 in partnership with staff, the TPS and local residents. We came together to identify several safety issues where our streets can be improved to make our communities more livable. One such safety improvement that was identified was to create a school bus loading zone on Dan Leckie Way for CityPlace families to send their children to school. This solution and the associated parking amendments to clear the loading zone will make it safer for parents to continue raising their families in our downtown communities.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking machine regulation on the east side of Dan Leckie Way, between Fort York Boulevard and Iceboat Terrace, operating from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, and 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday for a maximum period of three hours at a rate of $3.00 per hour, to operate between Fort York Boulevard and a point 20 metres south of Iceboat Terrace. 2. Designated a school bus loading zone to operate from 6:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday on the east side of Dan Leckie Way, between Iceboat Terrace and 20 metres south.
