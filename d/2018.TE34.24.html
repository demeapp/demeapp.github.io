What the item proposed

The Zoning By-law application proposes a revised 8-storey (32 metres to the top of the mechanical penthouse) building containing 101 units, ground floor non-residential space and two levels of below-grade parking on the lands located at 6 Noble Street in the City's Parkdale neighbourhood. The original submission proposed a 14-storey (51 metres to the top of the mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building comprised of commercial space located on portions of the ground floor and second floor, 174 residential units and two levels of below-grade parking. City Planning staff had expressed significant concerns regarding the proposed building's height, massing and contextual fit within its predominantly low-scaled surroundings; the relationship with Noble Street's narrow right-of-way width; and the impact due to lack of setbacks and insufficient stepbacks on neighbouring properties.
