149 - 157 Bathurst Street - Zoning Amendment and Rental Housing Demolition Applications - Request for Direction Report

Adopted with changesJul 23, 2018 · Toronto City Council

This is an interim report: staff are not yet in a position to give City Council a final recommendation on the rental housing demolition application application for 149 - 157 Bathurst Street. City Council is asked to direct staff to keep reviewing the application and resolve the outstanding issues.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes to amend the Zoning By-law to permit the redevelopment of the site at 149-157 Bathurst Street containing five rowhouse buildings, with a 16-storey mixed-use building containing retail uses at grade, 88 residential units above and 18 parking spaces accommodated in a parking stacker. The building will have a height of 49.5 metres (52 metres including the mechanical penthouse). As proposed, this application would require the demolition of 7 existing dwelling units (3 rental dwelling units and 4 owner occupied dwelling units) and one formerly licensed rooming house containing 6 dwelling rooms. On March 16, 2017, the applicant appealed the Zoning By-law Amendment application to the former Ontario Municipal Board, now Local Planning Appeal Tribunal, citing Council's failure to make a decision within the time prescribed by the Planning Act.
