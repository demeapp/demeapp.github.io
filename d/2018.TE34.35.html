The official record

What the item proposed

The application proposes a 59-storey mixed-use building including retail, office and residential uses at 90 and 100 Simcoe Street, 130 Pearl Street, and 203, 207 and 211 Adelaide Street West. A total of 524 dwelling units are proposed as well as 17,171 square metres of office floor area and 785 metres of retail floor area. The total gross floor area of the proposed development is 53,600 square metres, representing a density of 18.29 times the area of the site. The existing building at 100 Simcoe Street is identified as a contributing property in the King-Spadina Heritage Conservation District Plan, and is a listed building on the City's Heritage Register. This building is proposed to be demolished.
