The official record

What the item proposed

On November 27, 2017 the applicant appealed of the Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application to the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal (LPAT), citing Council's failure to make a decision on the application within the prescribed timelines of the Planning Act. A pre-hearing for these matters was held on June 14, 2018 and no hearing dates were set. This application proposes to redevelop the site at 5 Scrivener Square, 4-10 & 10R Price Street, and 1095-1107 Yonge Street with two mixed-use buildings of 8-storeys (35 metres, including mechanical penthouse) and 26-storeys (97.3 metres, including mechanical penthouse). The two new buildings would contain a total of 182 rental dwelling units, 3,085 square metres of non-residential floor area, 164 parking spaces within a 4-level underground parking garage, and 203 bicycle parking spaces.
