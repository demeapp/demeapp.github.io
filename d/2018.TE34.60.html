The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with city-wide residential demolition control under Article II, Demolition Control, of Municipal Code Ch. 363, as amended by By-law No. 1009-2006, enacted by City Council on September 27, 2006 under the authority of Section 33 of the Planning Act, I refer the demolition applications for 422 and 424 Roncesvalles Avenue (Application Nos. 18-112232 DEM and 18-112242 DEM) to Toronto and East York Community Council for consideration and to decide whether to grant or refuse the applications, including any conditions, if any, to be attached to the permit applications. This staff report is regarding a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approve the application to demolish the subject building, not withstanding that there is no building permit for a replacement building on the site, subject to the satisfaction of the following conditions: a. written confirmation from the City Solicitor in consultation with the Acting Director, Community Planning, Toronto and East York District that the final form of the Zoning By-laws are acceptable to the City and have been sent to the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal (former Ontario Municipal Board), as per condition 1 of partial order, Case No. PL160368, issued on March 29, 2017; b. that all debris and rubble be removed from the site immediately after demolition; and c. that any holes on the properties are backfilled with clean fill. 2.
