The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with the city-wide residential demolition control under Article II, Demolition Control, of Municipal Code Ch. 363, as amended by By-law No. 1009-2006, enacted by City Council on September 27, 2006 under the authority of Section 33 of the Planning Act, I refer the application at 903 Palmerston Ave (Application No. 18 166444 DEM 00 DM) to Toronto and East York Community Council for consideration and to decide whether to grant or refuse the application, including any conditions, if any, to be attached to the permit. This staff report is regarding a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council deferred consideration of the item indefinitely.
