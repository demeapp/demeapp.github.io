Toronto City Council · Decision

Naming of 4 Lanes south of Queen Street East, extending between Leuty Avenue and Glen Manor Drive

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve the name "Sandycove Way" for the public lane south of Queen Street East, extending between Leuty Avenue and Wineva Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends names for 4 public lanes south of Queen Street East, extending between Leuty Avenue and Glen Manor Drive. Community Councils have delegated authority to decide street naming matters which comply with the City of Toronto Street Naming Policy.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the name "Sandycove Way" for a public lane south of Queen Street East, extending between Leuty Avenue and Wineva Avenue. 2. Approved the name "Stephen's Green Way" for a public lane south of Queen Street East extending between Wineva Avenue and Hammersmith Avenue. 3. Approved the name "Liffey Way" for a public lane south of Queen Street East, extending between Hammersmith Avenue and Scarboro Beach Boulevard. 4. Approved the name "Bloomsday Way" for a public lane south of Queen Street East, extending between Scarboro Beach Boulevard and Glen Manor Drive.
