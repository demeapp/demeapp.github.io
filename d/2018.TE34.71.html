Impose Operating Conditions to a Boulevard Café Permit Located at 1224 Danforth Avenue

Adopted with changesJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Director of Policy and Strategic Support, Municipal Licensing and Standards recommends that The application for the proposed boulevard café permit located at 1224 Danforth Avenue be approved.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Toronto and East York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The Ward Councillor has requested operating conditions be imposed on the boulevard café permit located at 1224 Danforth Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the application for the proposed boulevard café permit located at 1224 Danforth Avenue, with the following conditions: a. There shall be no amplified music allowed on the patio; b. The patio will be closed and cleared by 11:00 p.m., Seven (7) days a week; and c. Municipal Licensing and Standards staff will review and report back to the Toronto and East York Community Council in April 2019.
