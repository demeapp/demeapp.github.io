The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has assessed an appeal from the owner of 104 Highland Avenue for front yard parking. This property is located in a ward where an application for front yard parking cannot be accepted, but it does meet the physical requirements set out in the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 918. However, front yard parking is not recommended because the Code does not permit the licensing of a front yard parking pad where the result of a neighbourhood poll was negative. The owner will be given an opportunity to make a deputation before Community Council.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Granted the appeal for front yard parking at 104 Highland Avenue, with the following conditions: a. the parking area be a minimum of 2.2 metres in width and not exceed 2.6 metres and a minimum of 5.3 metres and not exceed 5.9 metres in length; b. the applicant pay for the installation of the permanent ramp to service the parking space; c. the applicant disconnect any downspouts, if feasible, or seek an exemption from Toronto Water in accordance with the requirements in Chapter 681-11(S); d. the applicant provide the landscape features substantially in accordance with the plan as indicated in Attachment 'A', attached to the report dated June 8, 2018, from the Manager, Right of Way Management, Transportation Services, to the satisfaction of the General Manager of Transportation Services; and e. the applicant pay all applicable fees and…
