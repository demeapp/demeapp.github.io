Proposed Official Naming of Future Park at 20 Adelaide Place, Ward 20

AdoptedJul 4, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to officially name the future park located at 20 Adelaide Place "Adelaide Place Parkette".

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division is recommending that the future parkland located at 20 Adelaide Place be officially named "Adelaide Place Parkette". The Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division has reviewed the policy criteria in the Council-approved City of Toronto Property Naming Policy and verifies that the proposed park name meets the criteria.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Officially named the future park located at 20 Adelaide Place "Adelaide Place Parkette".
