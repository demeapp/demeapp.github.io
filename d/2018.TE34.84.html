The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is consistent with the Property Naming Policy. On April 4, 2018, an application was received to name the staircase located at the end of Heath Street East, to honour Jeffrey E. Mann. Parks, Forestry, and Recreation has reviewed the proposal, and acknowledges Jeffrey Mann's contributions made to his students and neighbourhood, however, this proposal does not meet a requirement in the Council-approved Property Naming Policy, specifically section 3.8.7,"Criteria to Name or Rename a City Property" which indicates: "Names of recent events or recently deceased individual may be considered after two years". Mr. Mann passed away September 2, 2016, therefore this request cannot be recommended by the Division. Parks, Forestry and Recreation recommends that this report be…

What was decided

City Council on July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, adopted the following: 1. Despite the provisions of the Property Naming Policy, City Council name the staircase and greenspace located at the end of Heath Street East "Jeffrey E. Mann Landing" and direct staff to install the appropriate plaque on or before September 15, 2018.
