The official record

What the item proposed

This presentation, by Elaine Baxter-Trahair, Acting Deputy City Manager, Cluster A, will provide an overview of capital funding programs and plans for child care in Toronto schools. This item was previously requested by the City-School boards Advisory Committee. School board staff will also be available to answer questions.

What was decided

The City-School Boards Advisory Committee recommends to the Executive Committee that: 1. City Council request the Acting General Manager, Children's Services, to consult with School Boards, and to report to the Community Development and Recreation Committee at its June 13, 2018 meeting on the current policies and practices related to supervision, space and nutrition programs for lunch room programs in Toronto schools.
