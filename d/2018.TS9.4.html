The official record

What the item proposed

This is the final progress report on the Interagency Staff workplan for the City-School Boards Advisory Committee. As reported in October 2017, the majority of activities on the Workplan are completed or are in full progress. The major item on the workplan that was not initiated was exploration of alternate tools for school capital development. By consensus, school boards determined not to pursue this activity with the City at this time.

What was decided

The City-School Boards Advisory Committee recommends to the Executive Committee that: 1. City Council forward the report (March 26, 2018) from the Executive Director, Social Development, Finance and Administration to the four School Boards operating in Toronto and to the Minister of Education.
