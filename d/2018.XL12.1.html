McCormick Playground Arena Board - Selection of Candidates for Interview

Staff recommend: The McCormick & Bill Bolton Arena Nominating Panel interview 3 of the candidates listed in Confidential Attachment 1 at its March 22.

The official record

What the item proposed

The McCormick & Bill Bolton Arena Nominating Panel must select candidates for interview to fill one vacancy on the McCormick Playground Arena Board. The Nominating Panel will conduct the interviews at its March 22, 2018 meeting and make recommendations to Toronto and East York Community Council.

What was decided

The McCormick & Bill Bolton Arena Nominating Panel: 1. Selected the following candidates for interview at its March 22, 2018 meeting for appointment to the McCormick Playground Arena Board: Candidate # 30 Candidate # 32 Candidate # 44 2. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (February 20, 2018) from the City Clerk remain confidential in its entirety as it relates to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the McCormick Playground Arena Board.
