Toronto City Council · Decision

Appointment of Public Members to the McCormick Playground Arena Board

Adopted with changesMar 22, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The McCormick and Bill Bolton Arena Nominating Panel was asked to appoint the recommended candidate(s) to the McCormick Playground Arena Board for a term ending February 22, 2021. Board appointments are made at the pleasure of Council, until a successor is appointed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The McCormick and Bill Bolton Arena Nominating Panel will interview and recommend one candidate to Toronto and East York Community Council for appointment to the McCormick Playground Arena Board.

What was decided

The McCormick and Bill Bolton Arena Nominating Panel recommends that: 1. Toronto and East York Community Council appoint the following candidate to the McCormick Playground Arena Board, at pleasure of Council, for a term of office ending on February 22, 2021, and until a successor is appointed: - Patrick Rettig 2. Toronto and East York Community Council direct that Confidential Attachments 1, 2 and 3 to the report (March 14, 2018) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the McCormick Playground Arena Board.
