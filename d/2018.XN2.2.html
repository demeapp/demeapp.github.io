Toronto City Council · Decision

Appointment of Public Members to the Toronto Public Library

Adopted with changesFeb 20, 2018 · Toronto City Council

The Interview Subcommittee for Toronto Public Library Board was asked to appoint the recommended candidate(s) to the Toronto Public Library for a term ending November 30, 2018. Board appointments are made at the pleasure of Council, until a successor is appointed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Interview Subcommittee for Toronto Public Library will conduct interviews and recommend two candidates to the Civic Appointments Committee for appointment to the Toronto Public Library.

What was decided

The Interview Subcommittee for Toronto Public Library Board recommends to the Civic Appointments Committee that: 1. City Council appoint the following candidates to the Toronto Public Library Board, at pleasure of Council, for a term of office ending on November 30, 2018 and until successors are appointed: Candidate #492 Candidate #524 2. City Council direct that Confidential Attachments 1, 2 and 3 to the report (February 9, 2018) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Public Library Board.
