The Interview Subcommittee for Toronto Port Authority was asked to appoint the recommended candidate(s) to the PortsToronto Board.

Official title: Appointment of Member to PortsToronto Board of Directors

Adopted with changesJan 17, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Interview Subcommittee for Toronto Ports Authority will conduct interviews during its meeting on January 17, 2018 and recommend one candidate to the Civic Appointments Committee for appointment to the PortsToronto Board of Directors.

What was decided

The Interview Subcommittee for Toronto Port Authority recommends to the Civic Appointments Committee that: 1. City Council appoint the following candidate to the PortsToronto Board of Directors, at pleasure of Council, for a term of office commencing April 1, 2018 and ending March 31, 2021, and until a successor is appointed: Candidate #58 2. City Council direct that the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachments 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 to the report (January 10, 2018) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to PortsToronto.
