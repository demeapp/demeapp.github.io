Staff recommend: Expenditures to reimburse Grey Canada for strategic planning, creative development.

Official title: Advertising Expenditures Report

AdoptedFeb 22, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report outlines the proposed media plan and expenditures related to the 2018 advertising campaign as provided for in the 2018 Operating Budget. The paid advertising will complement the public relations, events, group events and social media strategy to ensure we are reaching our target audiences in the Greater Toronto Area and beyond.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Approved expenditures to reimburse Grey Canada for strategic planning, creative development, and any outsourced production costs related to advertising programs as required for the Toronto Zoo brand, not to exceed $295.0 thousand, plus HST. 2. Approved expenditures with Zenith Optimedia Canada Inc. for media purchases related to the 2018 advertising campaign, not to exceed $956.0 thousand, plus HST.
