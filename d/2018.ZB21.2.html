The official record

What the item proposed

This report updates the Board on recent developments related to the biogas facility and presents details on the revised dates and timeline requested by ZooShare for the anticipated completion and commissioning of the facility, for approval by the Board.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Approved the revised milestone dates requested by ZooShare as proposed in Schedule I in the report (May 17, 2018) from the Interim Director, Administrative Services, Toronto Zoo, for the biogas initiative, and any necessary amendments to the Operating and Ground Lease Agreements related to the revised dates, to enable completion of the Phase 1: Electrical Components portion of the ZooShare biogas facility to meet the Independent Electricity System Operator (IESO) Feed-in-Tariff (FIT) deadline of 2018-07-18 related to commissioning of the Combined Heat and Power Unit (CHP) and to avoid potential for cancellation of the FIT Agreement by IESO. 2.
