Official title: Chief Executive Officer Search Update - Toronto Zoo

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

A confidential update on the status of the search for a new Chief Executive Officer will be provided at the upcoming special meeting of the Board.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Approved the appointment of Mr. Dolf C. DeJong as Chief Executive Officer effective September 6, 2018. 2. Directed the Interim Chief Executive Officer to execute an employment agreement based on mutually satisfactory terms and in accordance with policies in place for management staff at the Toronto Zoo and execute related documents as necessary. 3. Directed the release of the name and summary profile, in the confidential attachment, of the new Chief Executive Officer, by Public Relations at the discretion of the Board Chair after the successful candidate has been advised of the approval of the Board.
