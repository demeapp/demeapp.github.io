Toronto City Council · Decision

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo considers the report on Board Composition.

Official title: Board Composition

Adopted with changesNov 29, 2018 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council has recently been reduced in size to 25 Councillors. This change will add to the work demands on Councillors who in addition to sitting on Standing Committees, City Council, and handling constituent matters, have to sit on the boards of several City agencies, including the Toronto Zoo Board of Management. Key City Officials are currently considering if and how the workload of City Councillors can be well managed. A part of this consideration is whether reduced City Councillor representation on the Toronto Zoo Board of Management can be achieved to reduce City Councillor workload.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Advised City Council that their preference as to the composition of the Board, should they wish to recommend changes, is to: a. Eliminate one City Councillor position from the Board; b. Reduce the Board composition from 12 to 11 members; c. Include the Chair of the Toronto Zoo Wildlife Conservancy as a Board member; and d. That the Mayor’s Designate be a citizen member. 2. Requested City Council to consider appointment of qualified citizen members from outside the City of Toronto boundary.
