The official record

What the item proposed

The Program Manager, Customer Service and Issues Management, Engineering and Construction Services, Infrastructure and Development Services, will give a presentation on Six Points Interchange Street Naming. The City of Toronto invited the public to submit names for the new streets in November 2018. This presentation will highlight the process and share the short-list of street names being considered, including three Indigenous street names.

What was decided

The Aboriginal Affairs Advisory Committee received the item for information.
