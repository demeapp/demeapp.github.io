The official record

What the item proposed

Randy J. Pitt, Reaching Home Manager, Aboriginal Labour Force Development Circle, will give a presentation on Indigenous Set-Aside of Affordable Housing Units. The presentation will outline a request to the City of Toronto to establish a set-aside for affordable housing units specifically geared to the Indigenous homeless population in the Greater Toronto Area.

What was decided

The Aboriginal Affairs Advisory Committee recommends to the Planning and Housing Committee that: 1. City Council endorse an Indigenous set-aside for new affordable housing units, including more deeply affordable housing units. 2. City Council request City staff to consider the presentation from Randy J. Pitt, Reaching Home Manager, Aboriginal Labour Force Development Circle, as part of the HousingTO 2020-2030 Action Plan. 3. City Council request the Executive Director, Housing Secretariat, to report to the Aboriginal Affairs Advisory Committee with recommendations on Recommendation 1 in no later than six months.
