What the item proposed

This report provides information regarding the implementation status of publicly reported recommendations made by the Forensic Unit of the Auditor General’s Office. These recommendations were made in the context of the annual reports on the Fraud and Waste Hotline and other special reviews conducted. Confidential recommendations may also be made to management as a result of investigations. The implementation status of confidential recommendations is communicated directly to the City Manager. Following up on outstanding recommendations from previous reports is important and ensures that management has taken appropriate action to implement recommendations. From January 1, 2007 to December 31, 2016, a total of 30 recommendations made by the Forensic Unit were reported publicly. Twenty-nine of the recommendations were implemented before 2017.
