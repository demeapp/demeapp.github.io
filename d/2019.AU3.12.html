What the item proposed

This report responds to Audit Committee's request to report on the Information and Technology Division's outstanding audit recommendation wherein the Chief Information Officer was requested to develop a Cyber Security Program that supported ongoing vulnerability assessment and penetration testing using industry standards applied by subject matter experts. The City already has a foundation of cyber security measures in place to protect the City's information technology systems. The Auditor General's recommendations will enhance existing cyber security practices and assist with the detection, prevention and responses to future cyber threats. The City launched its formal Cyber Security Program in 2017 to enhance security capabilities given the increasing complexity in cyber security.
