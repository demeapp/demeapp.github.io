What the item proposed

The Auditor General's Annual Work Plan includes a series of ongoing and upcoming audits along the housing continuum. The focus of this audit is the administration of the City's centralized waiting list for rent-geared-to-income (RGI) assistance. For many people, this list is the gateway to stable housing and access to financial assistance for housing in Toronto. This audit is timely as the City develops its new housing and homelessness action plan for the next 10 years. The findings and recommendations can assist the City in setting its priority actions to more expeditiously open up and fill social housing spaces. Although we recognized the City requires more social housing units to meet the demand for subsidized housing, there is an opportunity to make better use of the existing units.
