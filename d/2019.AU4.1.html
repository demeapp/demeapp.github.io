What the item proposed

Over the past decade, the City of Toronto, like other large organizations, is increasingly conducting business and key operations online and in a networked environment. This makes operations more efficient and citizens are served better. The City stores a vast amount of confidential and sensitive data, such as information about employees and citizens' personal records. It also maintains a large number of systems that are critical to the City's functioning, such as water, fire services, transportation, and emergency response systems.
