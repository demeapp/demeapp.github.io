Award of Request for Quotation No. 6114-18-0302 to Metro Freightliner Hamilton and Tallman Truck Centre Limited for the Supply and Delivery of Twenty-Four (24) Latest Model Various Types Truck Chassis and Cab, 60,000 LBS (27,216 KG) GVWR, Tandem Axle Complete with Various Bodies

AdoptedDec 5, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend awarding Request for Quotation No. 6114-18-0302 to Metro Freightliner Hamilton and Tallman Truck Centre Limited for the Supply and Delivery of Twenty-Four (24) Latest Model Various Types Truck Chassis and Cab, 60,000 LBS (27,216 KG) GVWR, Tandem Axle Complete with Various Bodies. The Bid Award Panel is asked to approve the award.

The official summary names a figure of $4,174,888.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
