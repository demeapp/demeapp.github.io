Award of Request for Quotation No. 6125-18-0282 to Kal Tire for the Non-exclusive Provision of 24/7 Mobile Roadside and On-site Tire Services

AdoptedDec 5, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend awarding Request for Quotation No. 6125-18-0282 to Kal Tire for the Non-exclusive Provision of 24/7 Mobile Roadside and On-site Tire Services. The Bid Award Panel is asked to approve the award.
