Award of Request for Quotation (RFQ) No. 1004-18-0242 to Workplace Resource for the Non-exclusive Supply, Delivery, and Installation of New Herman-Miller Action Office (AO) Series Workstations

AdoptedJan 30, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend awarding Request for Quotation (RFQ) No. 1004-18-0242 to Workplace Resource for the Non-exclusive Supply, Delivery, and Installation of New Herman-Miller Action Office (AO) Series Workstations. The Bid Award Panel is asked to approve the award.

The official summary names a figure of $1,377,243.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
