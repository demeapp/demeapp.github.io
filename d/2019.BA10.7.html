Award of Request for Quotation No. 6106-18-0097 to Enterprise Rent A Car Company for the Non-exclusive Short-term Rental Services, Including Third Party Automotive Insurance Coverage, of Various Vehicle Types

AdoptedJan 30, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend awarding Request for Quotation No. 6106-18-0097 to Enterprise Rent A Car Company for the Non-exclusive Short-term Rental Services, Including Third Party Automotive Insurance Coverage, of Various Vehicle Types. The Bid Award Panel is asked to approve the award.
