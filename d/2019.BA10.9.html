Award of Tender Call No. 243-2018 to W.S. Nicholls Construction Incorporated for the Humber Treatment Plant Waste Gas Burner Upgrades

Staff recommend awarding Tender Call No. 243-2018 to W.S. Nicholls Construction Incorporated for the Humber Treatment Plant Waste Gas Burner Upgrades. The Bid Award Panel is asked to approve the award.
