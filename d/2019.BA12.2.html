Award of Tender Call No. 206-2018 to Trisan Construction for the Construction of Sewers and Watermains Associated with the Basement Flooding Protection Program

AdoptedFeb 13, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend awarding Tender Call No. 206-2018 to Trisan Construction for the Construction of Sewers and Watermains Associated with the Basement Flooding Protection Program. The Bid Award Panel is asked to approve the award.

The official summary names a figure of $15,987,255.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
