The Bid Award Panel was asked to award the Major Road Resurfacing on Scarlett Road from Humber River to Bernice Crescent; and St. Clair Avenue West from 100m East of Scarlett Road to Jane Street; Watermain Abandoning from Edinborough Court to East Drive contract to Gazzola Paving Limited.
