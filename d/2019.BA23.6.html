AdoptedMay 1, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The Bid Award Panel was asked to award the Local Road Reconstruction and Replacement of Substandard Water Services on Riverside Drive, Riverside Trail and Morningside Ave and Road Resurfacing on Riverside Crescent and Sidewalk Construction on Riverside Drive-Phase 2 contract to Trisan Construction.
