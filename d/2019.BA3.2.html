Award of Tender Call No. 32-2018 to Insituform Technologies Limited for Full Length Trenchless Rehabilitation of Existing Gravity Sewers and Related Works at Various Locations in the City of Toronto Service Districts B and D

AdoptedDec 19, 2018 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend awarding Tender Call No. 32-2018 to Insituform Technologies Limited for Full Length Trenchless Rehabilitation of Existing Gravity Sewers and Related Works at Various Locations in the City of Toronto Service Districts B and D. The Bid Award Panel is asked to approve the award.
