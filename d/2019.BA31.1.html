Award of Request for Proposal 9117-19-5002 to Morrison Hershfield Limited for Project Condition Assessment, Design for Interim Repair, Feasibility Report and Preliminary Design for Replacement of Gardiner Expressway East and Lake Shore Boulevard East Reconfiguration

AdoptedJun 26, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The Chief Purchasing Officer asks the Bid Award Panel to award Request for Proposal 9117-19-5002 to Morrison Hershfield Limited for project Condition Assessment, Design for Interim Repair, Feasibility Report and Preliminary Design for Replacement of Gardiner Expressway East and Lake Shore Boulevard East Reconfiguration. 4 bids were received.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
