Award of Request for Quotation 9150-19-0126 to Renewi Canada Ltd. for the Provision of Processing Services of Source Separated Organics With or Without Haulage Services

AdoptedJun 26, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The Chief Purchasing Officer asks the Bid Award Panel to award Request for Quotation 9150-19-0126 to Renewi Canada Ltd for the Provision of Processing Services of Source Separated Organics With or Without Haulage Services, with a bid price of $4,650,000 excluding HST. 2 bids were received.

The official summary names a figure of $4,650,000.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
