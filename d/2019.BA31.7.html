Award of Request for Tender 133-2019 to Bevcon Construction, for the Repair of Distressed Asphalt at Various Locations within the Toronto and East York District

AdoptedJun 26, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The Chief Purchasing Officer asks the Bid Award Panel to award to Bevcon Construction for the Repair of Distressed Asphalt at Various Locations within the Toronto and East York District, with a bid price of $633,704 excluding HST. 4 bids were received.
