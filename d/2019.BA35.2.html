Award of Request for Quotation 3407-19-0087 to Konica Minolta for the Non-Exclusive Rental and Delivery of Five New High-speed Digital Black and White Reproduction Systems with One In-line Booklet Maker, and One New High-speed Digital Colour Reproduction System

AdoptedJul 24, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The Chief Purchasing Officer asks the Bid Award Panel to award Request for Quotation 3407-19-0087 to Konica Minolta for the Non-Exclusive Rental and Delivery of Five New High-speed Digital Black and White Reproduction Systems with One In-line Booklet Maker, and One New High-speed Digital Colour Reproduction System, with a bid price of $3,187,685 excluding HST. 3 bids were received.

The official summary names a figure of $3,187,685.
