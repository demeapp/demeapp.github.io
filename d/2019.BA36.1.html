What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (July 22, 2019) from the Chief Purchasing Officer: Call Number: Tender Call 158-2019, Contract 19ECS-TI-16BE Description: Donino Avenue Bridge over the West Don River Rehabilitation and Superstructure Replacement. This includes the removal and reconstruction of the bridge superstructure, modification of the abutments to semi-integral abutments, relocation of the existing watermain onto a new support structure adjacent to the bridge and buried at the bridge approaches.
