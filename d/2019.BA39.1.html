Award of Tender Call Number 178-2019 to Maple-Crete Inc. for Supply, Replace and Adjust Frames, Grates and Covers for Catch Basins and Maintenance Holes in Toronto East York and Scarborough Districts

AdoptedAug 14, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The Chief Purchasing Officer asks the Bid Award Panel to award Tender Call 178-2019 to Maple-Crete Inc for supply, Replace and Adjust Frames, Grates and Covers for Catch Basins and Maintenance Holes in Toronto East York and Scarborough Districts, with a bid price of $1,625,089.73 excluding HST. 3 bids were received.
