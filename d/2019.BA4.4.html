What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (December 20, 2018) from the Chief Purchasing Officer: Call Number: Request for Quotation Number 3907-18-5049 Description: For the supply and delivery of all labour and materials equipment, tools and supervision necessary to perform Minor Building Repairs and Alterations at various Child Care Centres and District Office Locations throughout the City of Toronto on an as and when required basis for a one (1) year period from the date of award, with the option to renew for four (4) additional one year periods. Should the option(s) be exercised, then the General Manager, Children's Services will request the Chief Purchasing Officer to process the renewals under the same terms and conditions.
