Staff recommend rescinding the award of Request for Quotation No. 4201-18-5067 to Gorbern Mechanical Contractors Ltd.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to rescind the Bid Award Panel award of Request for Quotation (RFQ) 4201-18-5067 as adopted by the Bid Award Panel on November 28, 2018, BA110.4 ( http://app.toronto.ca/tmmis/viewAgendaItemHistory.do?item=2018.BA110.4 ). BA110.4 was submitted in error and Request for Quotation 4201-18-5067 was previously awarded by the Bid Award Panel on October 31, 2018, BA104.3 ( http://app.toronto.ca/tmmis/viewAgendaItemHistory.do?item=2018.BA104.3 ).

What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Rescinded its November 28, 2018 decision to award Request for Quotation No. 4201-18-5067 as per Item BA110.4, as the report (November 28, 2018) from the Chief Purchasing Officer was submitted in error.
