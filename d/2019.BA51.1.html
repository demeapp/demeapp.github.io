What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (October 11, 2019) from the Chief Purchasing Officer. Call Number: Request for Proposal 9119-19-7187 Description: The contract being awarded is for the provision of Construction Management Services at the East Wing of Union Station (Part 1) and select floors at City Hall (Part 2) utilizing a Canadian Construction Documents Committee 5B agreement and supplemental conditions. The projects will run concurrent, and the Construction Management Services Firm will act as a Prime Agent, on behalf of the City of Toronto, and provide complete turnkey projects including the procurement and delivery of all construction and necessary services and materials including the installation of furniture, fixtures, and equipment.
