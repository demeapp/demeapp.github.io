What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (December 11, 2019) from the Chief Purchasing Officer: Call Number: RFP 9119-19-7136 Description: For the provision of Professional Planning and Engineering Services to undertake the Mount Dennis Planning Framework Study. The purpose of this comprehensive Study is to support the development of a renewed neighbourhood vision for the Mount Dennis area and to recommend tools to implement the vision. When complete, the Study deliverables will inform amendments to the City's Official Plan and Zoning By-law, and potentially recommend updates to other City strategies, guidelines and capital plans.
