Award of Request for Proposal No. 9117-18-7186 to WSP Canada Group Limited for the Provision of Detailed Design and Construction Administration Engineering Services for Assignments under the Basement Flooding Protection Program Phase 4

AdoptedJan 9, 2019 · Toronto City Council
