Award of Request for Quotation No. 3701-18-0327 to Various Vendors for the Non-exclusive Supply of all Labour, Equipment and Material Necessary to Perform Arboricultural Services at Various Locations for the Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division

AdoptedJan 28, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend awarding Request for Quotation No. 3701-18-0327 to Various Vendors for the Non-exclusive Supply of all Labour, Equipment and Material Necessary to Perform Arboricultural Services at Various Locations for the Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division. The Bid Award Panel is asked to approve the award.
