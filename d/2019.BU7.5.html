What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide City Council with the City of Toronto capital spending for the four month period ended April 30, 2019, as well as projected actual expenditures to December 31, 2019. Furthermore, this report seeks Council's approval for in-year budget adjustments to the 2019 Approved Capital Budget and additional carry-forward from 2018 that have no impact on approved debt. As illustrated in Table 1 below, 2019 the City's capital expenditure was $748.3 million or 13.1 percent of the 2019 capital budget of $5,793 billion and is projecting to expend $4,589 billion or 79.0 percent by December 31, 2019.
