The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide City Council with the City of Toronto capital spending for the six month period ended June 30, 2019, as well as projected actual expenditures to December 31, 2019. Furthermore, this report seeks Council's approval for in-year budget adjustments to the 2019 Approved Capital Budget that have no impact on 2019 approved debt. As illustrated in Table 1 below, City's 2019 capital expenditure was $1.326 billion or 22.9 percent of the 2019 capital budget of $5.788 billion for the period ended June 30, 2019 and is projecting to expend $4.220 billion or 72.9 percent by December 31, 2019. What was decided

The Budget Committee recommends to the Executive Committee that: 1.
