The official record

What the item proposed

The Civic Appointments Committee must select candidates to interview for appointment to the Board of Health. Following the interviews, the Civic Appointments Committee will recommend six public members and one education representative for appointment to City Council.

What was decided

The Civic Appointments Committee: 1. Selected the following candidates for interview for appointment to the Board of Health: Candidate #1-145 Candidate #1-170 Candidate #1-369 Candidate #1-389 Candidate #2-12 Candidate #2-19 Candidate #2-27 Candidate #2-29 Candidate #2-47 Candidate #2-92 Candidate #2-135 Candidate #2-140 2. Directed that the nominees from the Toronto District School Board and the Toronto Catholic District School Board be interviewed at its next meeting for appointment to the Board of Health. 3. Directed that the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachments 1, 2 and 3 to the report (January 3, 2019) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Board of Health.
