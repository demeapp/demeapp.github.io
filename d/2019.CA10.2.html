The official record

What the item proposed

A public member of the Toronto and Region Conservation Authority (TRCA) Board has recently resigned. The Civic Appointments Committee must select candidates to be interviewed for appointment to the TRCA. The Civic Appointments Committee will conduct interviews at its November 13, 2019 meeting and recommend one candidate to City Council.

What was decided

The Civic Appointments Committee: 1. Selected the following candidates to be interviewed at the November 13, 2019 meeting of the Committee for appointment to the Toronto and Region Conservation Authority: - TRC2-1 - TRC2-11 - TRC2-25 - TRC2-26 - TRC2-27 2. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (October 4, 2019) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto and Region Conservation Authority.
