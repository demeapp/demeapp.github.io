The official record

What the item proposed

The Civic Appointments Committee must select candidates to interview for appointment to the Toronto Transit Commission. Following the interviews, the Civic Appointments Committee will recommend two public members for appointment to City Council.

What was decided

The Civic Appointments Committee: 1. Selected the following candidates for interview for appointment to the Toronto Transit Commission: Candidate #2-16 Candidate #2-37 Candidate #2-47 Candidate #2-66 Candidate #2-89 Candidate #2-102 2. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1, 2 and 3 to the report (February 4, 2019) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Transit Commission.
