The official record

What the item proposed

The Civic Appointments Committee must select candidates to be interviewed for appointment to the Toronto Parking Authority Board. The Civic Appointments Committee will conduct interviews at its May 28, 2019 meeting and recommend five candidates to City Council.

What was decided

The Civic Appointments Committee: 1. Selected the following candidates to be interviewed at the May 28, 2019 meeting of the Committee for appointment to the Toronto Parking Authority Board: Candidate #TPA1-130 Candidate #TPA2-8 Candidate #TPA2-15 Candidate #TPA2-25 Candidate #TPA2-32 Candidate #TPA2-46 Candidate #TPA2-50 Candidate #TPA2-62 Candidate #TPA2-64 Candidate #TPA2-65 2. Direct that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 25, 2019) from the City Clerk remain confidential in its entirety as it relates to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Parking Authority Board.
