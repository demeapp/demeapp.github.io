The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to have City Council appoint one (1) member of the public to the Toronto Music Advisory Committee to fill a current vacancy due to a recent resignation.

What was decided

City Council on October 2 and 3, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council appoint Kaya Pino to the Toronto Music Advisory Committee, at pleasure of Council, for a term of office ending on November 14, 2022.
