The official record

What the item proposed

There are currently two vacancies on the Toronto Public Library Board due to recent resignations. The Civic Appointments Committee must select candidates to interview for appointment to the Toronto Public Library Board. Following the interviews, the Civic Appointments Committee will recommend two candidates for appointment to City Council.

What was decided

The Civic Appointments Committee: 1. Selected the following candidates to be interviewed at its next meeting for appointment to the Toronto Public Library Board: Candidate TPL1-595 Candidate TPL1-686 Candidate TPL2-84 Candidate TPL2-101 Candidate TPL2-112 Candidate TPL2-129 Candidate TPL2-153 Candidate TPL2-200 Candidate TPL2-234 Candidate TPL2-256 2. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (September 6, 2019) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Public Library Board.
