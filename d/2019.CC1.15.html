The official record

What the item proposed

Lifetime Wellington Street West Inc. (the "Applicant") is the owner of the property municipally known as 485-489 Wellington Street West (the "Subject Property"). The Applicant applied for a zoning by-law amendment in order to facilitate a proposed redevelopment of the Subject Property. The application proposes a 16-storey building with 431 square metres of commercial space on the first floor and 136 residential units above (the "Application"). The Application is proposed to include 34 parking spaces in two levels of underground parking. The Applicant appealed the Application to the Ontario Municipal Board (the "OMB") (now known as the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal) (the "LPAT") due to City Council's failure to make a decision.
