The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek further instruction in relation to a development application for a site that contains heritage resources and potential heritage resources, and a related ongoing appeal at the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal ("LPAT"). There was a proposal to amend the Zoning By-law to permit the construction of a 64-storey mixed-use building on the development site. Further direction is required in the context of a scheduled LPAT hearing scheduled to commence on January 13, 2020.
