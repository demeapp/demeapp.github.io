What the item proposed

Latch Developments Ltd. (the "Applicant") is the owner of the property municipally known as 859 The Queensway (the "Subject Property"). The Applicant applied for a zoning by-law amendment, with accompanying plan of subdivision application, in order to facilitate a proposed mixed-use redevelopment of the Subject Property (the "Application"). The Application proposes a 14-storey mixed use building. On January 24, 2018, the Applicant appealed the zoning by-law amendment application to the Ontario Municipal Board (the "OMB") (now known as the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal) (the "LPAT") due to City Council's failure to make a decision. The first Prehearing Conference for this matter was held on October 4, 2018. A second Prehearing Conference is scheduled for March 21, 2019.
