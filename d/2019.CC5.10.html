What the item proposed

Antorisa Investments Ltd. ("Antorisa", or the "Owner") is the owner of the lands known as 280-290 Jarvis Street, 102-110 Gerrard Street East and 189-193 Mutual Street. On December 29, 2016, Antorisa made applications for an Official Plan Amendment, Zoning By-law Amendment and Rental Housing Demolition with respect to the subject lands in order to permit a 25 storey-building and adjoining 10-storey building with a 3-storey podium. The heritage buildings on site were proposed to be re-located on site and incorporated into the design of the new development. The applications were refused by City Council and the Official Plan Amendment and the Zoning By-law Amendment matters were appealed by the Owner to the LPAT on May 24, 2017.
