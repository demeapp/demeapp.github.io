The official record

What the item proposed

Samper Developments Limited (the "Applicant") is the owner of lands municipally known as 45 LaRose Avenue (the "Subject Site"). The Subject Site is located on the south side of LaRose Avenue, west of Scarlett Road. The Applicant submitted a Zoning By-law Amendment application (the "Application") to the City in December, 2016. The Application was deemed complete on August 1, 2017. The Applicant proposed to develop an infill apartment building, consisting of a seven-storey rental apartment building on the western portion of the Subject Site. This area of the Subject Site is currently occupied by surface parking serving the existing apartment building on the east side of the Subject Site. The proposed building has an area of 15,587 square metres, with a total of 187 units. What was decided

City Council on April 16 and 17, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the City Solicitor to attend the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal hearing to oppose the proposed development at 45 LaRose Avenue on the grounds there was a density transfer to 1407 Royal York Road from 45 LaRose Avenue. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to retain external consultants as necessary. 3.
