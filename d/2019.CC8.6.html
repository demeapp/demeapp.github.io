The official record

What the item proposed

City Council adopted Official Plan Amendment No. 231 (OPA 231) regarding Employment Areas in December 2013. OPA 231 is currently under appeal before the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal (LPAT or Tribunal). The Local Planning Appeal Tribunal has scheduled September 16 to October 4, 2019 to hear the remaining appeals regarding OPA 231 employment lands forecasting and policies pertaining to the conversion of employment lands. This report requests direction from City Council for the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal hearing respecting conversion and forecasting issues.
