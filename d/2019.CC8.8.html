What the item proposed

Official Plan Amendment 387 was adopted by City Council on February 1, 2018 by By-law 87-2018 ("OPA 387"). OPA 387 amends the City of Toronto Official Plan (the "Official Plan") by altering the right-of-way widths for certain existing roadways shown on Map 3; by identifying the Broadview Avenue extension on Map 5 as a surface transit priority route; and by identifying the extensions of Broadview Avenue and Bouchette Street as planned but unbuilt roads on Schedule 2, along with other new streets in the South of Eastern area. OPA 387 was appealed by the Toronto Port Authority ("Ports Toronto") to the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal ("LPAT") on March 1, 2018 (the "Appeal").
