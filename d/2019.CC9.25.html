The official record

What the item proposed

City Council adopted the St. Lawrence Neighbourhood Heritage Conservation District and St. Lawrence Neighbourhood Heritage Conservation District Plan (the "St. Lawrence HCD") at its meeting of December 2015. The St. Lawrence HCD was subsequently appealed to the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal (the "LPAT" or "Tribunal"). The LPAT has scheduled a 14 day hearing on the merits of the St. Lawrence HCD beginning November 12, 2019. This report requests direction from City Council for the LPAT hearing respecting conversion and forecasting issues.
