Toronto City Council · Decision

Amendments to the Procedures By-law for the Board of Directors of TO Live to Allow Remote Participation

Adopted with changesMay 9, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The Board of Directors of TO Live was asked to amend its Procedures to permit Board Members to participate electronically (remotely) in Board meetings in accordance with the provisions of the City of Toronto Act.

How the vote went

Amend Item — Carried, 10-0

Vote taken at: Board of Directors of TO Live

The City's published voting record gives the result above, but its name-by-name rows for this vote are incomplete — so no breakdown is shown, rather than a wrong one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on March 1, 2019, the Chair, Board of Directors of TO Live gave notice in accordance with Section 45(2) of the Board's Procedures By-law that he would be recommending amendments to the By-law at this meeting. The proposed amendments would permit Board Members to participate electronically (remotely) in Board meetings in accordance with the provisions of the City of Toronto Act.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of TO Live recommends that: 1. City Council approve the Board of Directors of TO Live Procedures By-law in Attachment 1 to the report (May 7, 2019) from the City Clerk.
