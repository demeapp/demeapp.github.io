Exhibition for 2019/20 Programming Season

Adopted with changesJun 13, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The Board of Directors of TO Live was asked to approve the additional new program/exhibition, as outlined in Confidential Attachments 1, 2, 3 and 4 to the report (May 17, 2019) from the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report presents details around an additional proposed program/exhibition for the fall of 2019 (not included in the current approved 2019 budget) at the Toronto Centre for the Arts.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of TO Live: 1. Approved the additional new program/exhibition, as outlined in Confidential Attachments 1, 2, 3 and 4 to the report (May 17, 2019) from the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live. 2. Directed staff to proceed with the necessary preparations for the program/exhibition prior to City Council consideration of approval of a TO Live Programming Reserve Fund. 3. Requested the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live to report to the next Board meeting with a proposal for the establishment of an Advisory Body on the Exhibition for the 2019-2020 Programming Season and with proposed terms of reference for the Advisory Body. 4. Changed the composition of the Risk Committee to five Members, changed the name to Programming Risk Committee, and appointed Paul Bernards to the Risk Committee. 3.
