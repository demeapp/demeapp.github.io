Hospitality Organization to Occupy Space within the Sony Centre

PostponedJun 13, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Board of Directors of TO Live was asked to approve the proposal for a hospitality organization to occupy space within the Sony Centre for the purposes of operating a restaurant/barroom.

Council put off deciding this item.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides additional background and decision histories related to item 3.1a "Chief Executive Officer's General Status Update - Supplementary Report on Hospitality Organization to Occupy Space within the Sony Centre" considered at the May 9, 2019 Board meeting of the TO Live Board of Directors. The Audit Committee of the TO Live Board will consider this item at its June 10, 2019 meeting.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of TO Live deferred consideration of the item until the next meeting of the Board of Directors of TO Live.
