Toronto City Council · Decision

Transfer of Reserve Funds to Toronto Live Foundation

AdoptedJun 13, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Board of Directors of TO Live was asked to approve the transfer of $1.657 million from the Toronto Centre for the Arts Stabilization Reserve Fund (XQ1060) to the new Toronto Live Foundation.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report is to request funds to be transferred from TO Live's reserve funds (Toronto Centre for the Arts Stabilization Reserve Fund and Sony Centre Stabilization Reserve) to the newly incorporated "Toronto Live Foundation" as seed funding.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of TO Live recommends that: 1. City Council approve the transfer of $1.657 million from the Toronto Centre for the Arts Stabilization Reserve Fund (XQ1060) and $0.666 million from the Sony Centre Stabilization Reserve (XQ2031) to the new Toronto Live Foundation. 2. City Council approve an in-year budget adjustment to increase the 2019 Council Approved Operating Budget for TO Live by $2.323 million gross and $0 net, fully funded from the Toronto Centre for the Arts Stabilization Reserve Fund ($1.657 million) and the Sony Centre Stabilization Reserve ($0.666 million) to reflect the transfer of funds to the Toronto Live Foundation.
