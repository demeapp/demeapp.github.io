On June 20th, 2018 the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee received for information a presentation by the Policy Development Officer, Poverty Reduction Strategy, Social Policy, Analysis and Research, Social Development, Finance and Administration on the Toronto Poverty Reduction Strategy - Update on 2018 Community Engagement Initiatives. http://app.toronto.ca/tmmis/viewAgendaItemHistory.do?item=2018.DI20.5 In an effort to ensure accessibility considerations are embedding in the implementation of the Poverty Reductions Strategy I request that a representative from the Poverty Reduction Strategy make a presentation to provide an update on poverty reduction activities and seek feedback from the committee at a future 2019 Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee Meeting.
