What the item proposed

Annette Synowiec, Interim Director, Policy, Planning and Support, Solid Waste Management Services will give a presentation on single-use and takeaway items reduction. The presentation is generally for information on activity that has taken place to date, the feedback received related to accessibility, and to provide an overview of activities in advance of the next round of consultation in the fall. Annette will speak to the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee regarding stage 2 consultation activities and seek member input on this.

What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee received the item for information.
