The official record

What the item proposed

In 2018, the City of Toronto received funding from Transport Canada to conduct a trial with an automated transit shuttle in the City of Toronto and examine the potential for this technology to fill an unmet need in the transit system. Lindsay Wiginton, Project Manager, Transportation Services will provide an update on the project, including service design, and seek the Committee's feedback on accessibility requirements which will be considered as staff develop a Request for Proposals to seek a qualified vendor to provide the service.

What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee received the item for information.
