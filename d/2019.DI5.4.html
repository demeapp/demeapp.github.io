What the item proposed

Annette Synowiec, Interim Director Policy, Planning and Outreach and Charlotte Ueta, Manager Solid Waste Policy and Planning, will give a presentation to provide details of the second phase of public consultations on how to reduce single-use and takeaway items. Based on the input received in Phase 1 last year, this second phase of consultation will seek feedback on specific items and proposed approaches for reduction and associated timelines.

What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee received the item for information.
