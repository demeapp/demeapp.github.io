The committee was asked to plan public Wi-Fi for community recreation facilities.

Official title: Public Wi-Fi in Community Recreation Facilities

AdoptedMar 6, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Access to digital information and services is vital to public access to services in the City of Toronto. Residents particularly in Neighbourhood Improvement Areas face barriers to access as a result of a lack of technology and internet services. In addition, ensuring the city's public facilities remain competitive is essential to optimizing opportunities for community and business use of these facilities. Parks Forestry and Recreation experiences on-going requests for Public Wi-Fi to enable residents to search for programs and services, for community sport organizations to stream games at indoor arenas and for community space users to host meetings using digital platforms. The City of Toronto's community centres and indoor arena facilities should be prioritized as part of the city's broader strategy to address the digital divide, improve geographic broadband and integrating Wi-Fi access…

What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee: 1. Directed the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation and the Chief Information Officer to include in any connectivity plans and strategies for improvements to affordable, high-speed internet that are currently underway, the feasibility of implementing Public Wi-Fi in Community Recreation facilities including community centres and arenas.
