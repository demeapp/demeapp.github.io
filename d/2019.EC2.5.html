What the item proposed

This report recommends the following 2019 allocations for Community Events in the Community Investment Funding (CIF) program. Twenty-five (25) proposals are recommended for funding by a grant review panel for a funding total of $370,596 and a total of $54,404 be held for Appeals. The balance of the 2019 funding for Community Projects ($1,000,000) is being allocated to the seven (7) organizations funded in 2018 in the Rapid Response Youth Gun Violence Prevention and Intervention Grant as well as three (3) additional grantees who were recommended for funding in the grant call but did not receive an allocation due to insufficient funding. Additionally, a one-time capacity building grant is recommended to support Community Service Partnership (CSP) funded organizations to fully meet CSP eligibility requirements and be able to apply for funding later this year in the next funding cycle…

What was decided

City Council on March 27 and 28, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve allocations for the following Community Investment Funding program, Community Events as recommended in Appendix A to the report (February 15, 2019) from the Executive Director, Social Development, Finance and Administration, from the 2019 Approved Operating Budget for Social Development, Finance and Administration: a. total funding of $370,596 to 25 community organizations for the Community Events grant stream; and b. funding up to a total of $54,404 be held for Appeals for Community Events grant stream. 2.
