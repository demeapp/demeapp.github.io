The official record

What the item proposed

As you know, I have committed to a number of initiatives to ensure Toronto remains a safe, clean, and prosperous city. One of those initiatives involves my continued support and encouragement of our businesses and commitment to strengthening our economy. I want our city to grow in an ambitious, thoughtful and inspiring way so that we can draw admiration and investment from around the world, and maintain a quality of life that is good for residents and obvious to visitors and potential newcomers. That will help us to continue to attract tourists to our great city, even in the winter months. We attracted 44 million tourists in 2017, a record year. But I don’t believe we should limit the fun you can have in Toronto to just the summer months. And I want to see us create more records under my leadership in years to come because tourists mean jobs, investment and good advertising for our…

What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee: 1. Directed City staff to: a. Work with partners like Tourism Toronto to significantly step up the pace of applications to get more winter-focussed events and festivals in Toronto, more all-season patios and more winter recreation activities in Toronto's public parks; and b. Report back to the Economic and Community Development Committee with a plan to foster and enhance Toronto's night economy.
