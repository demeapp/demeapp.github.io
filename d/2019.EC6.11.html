What the item proposed

This report is to obtain City Council's authority to award Request for Proposal No. 6815-18-7217 for the provision of Short Term Accommodations for shelter clients through the use of hotel/motel services for operated shelters for the period of one (1) year from date of award with the option to renew the contract for an additional four (4) separate one (1) year periods for a total award of $541.5 million net of all applicable taxes, $611.9 million including HST and all applicable charges and $551.0 million net of HST recoveries. Should the option(s) be exercised, then the General Manager of Shelter, Support and Housing Administration will request the Chief Purchasing Officer to process the renewals under the same terms and conditions.
