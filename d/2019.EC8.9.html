The official record

What the item proposed

Campbell House Museum is a City-owned building, situated on land leased from Canada Life Assurance Company at the north-west corner of Queen and University Avenue. It is operated by the Sir William Campbell Foundation with oversight by the City-appointed Campbell House Board of Management. Since 1972, the Museum's largest source of operating and capital funds has been The Advocates' Society (TAS). TAS began reducing its support to the Museum in 2018 and will likely provide its final grant in 2020. In response, the Foundation (whose board of directors is composed of TAS members) has proposed winding up its affairs and transferring responsibility for the Museum and the House to the City of Toronto under the Economic Development and Culture Division. What was decided

City Council on October 29 and 30, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council extend its appreciation to The Advocates' Society for its past and present support to Campbell House Museum. 2. City Council request The Advocates' Society to maintain its financial support for Campbell House Museum beyond 2020. 3. City Council request the Sir William Campbell Foundation to maintain its charitable status, seek broader community representation on its Board of Directors, and continue to manage Campbell House Museum. 4. City Council approve the additional $32,000 grant request made in 2019 by the Sir William Campbell Foundation to operate Campbell House Museum, to be funded from the 2019 approved operating budget for Economic Development and Culture. 5.
