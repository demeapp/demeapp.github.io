The official record

What the item proposed

The Collective Agreement between the Board and Labourers' International Union of North America, Local 506 ("LiUNA Local 506") expired on March 31, 2018. Notice to Bargain was received from LiUNA on February 22, 2018. Prior to commencing bargaining, the parties agreed to try and resolve issues that were at arbitration. These efforts were unsuccessful. On September 26, 2018 the union filed for conciliation with the Ministry of Labour. Following the appointment of a conciliator, bargaining meetings were held on April 8, 2019, April 16, 2019, May 2, 2019, June 5, 2019, July 3, 2019 and July 30, 2019 (into July 31, 2019) with LiUNA Local 506, City Labour Relations staff, Exhibition Place staff and the Ministry of Labour Conciliator.
