What the item proposed

At its meeting of June 13, 2019 the Board approved without amendment the 2020 Operating Budget surplus of $175,000 an increase of $15,000 or 9 percent over 2019. At that time the City was undergoing a budget modernisation process and the City Manager had not provided the 2020 Budget Process, Directions and Guidelines yet to City Divisions and Agencies for its 2020 submissions. In November 2019 the City Manager issued the budget process and guidelines to all City Divisions and Agencies. The City Manager request to Exhibition Place for 2020 was to prepare an operating budget that better align with our actual historical performance. Previously, as directed by the City the Exhibition Place operating budget was prepared on a "budget to budget" basis. What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Directed the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place to submit the revised 2020 Budget to the City Financial Planning Division as part of the 2020 Budget Process.
