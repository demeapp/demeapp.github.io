Toronto City Council · Decision

Changing the Legal Name of Civic Theatres Toronto to TO Live

AdoptedJan 30, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Gary Crawford recommends that City Council legally change the name of Civic Theatres Toronto to TO Live, following the new branding already announced publicly, and amend Municipal Code Chapter 23 accordingly.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On the January 18th meeting of Civic Theatres Board, Item CT1.7 - Presentation on New Branding for Civic Theatres Toronto ( http://app.toronto.ca/tmmis/viewAgendaItemHistory.do?item=2019.CT1.7 ) was considered. I am requesting that this item be put on the agenda of the Executive Committee as a new business Item, as the new name of Civic Theatres has already been announced publically.

What was decided

City Council on January 30 and 31, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council legally change the name of Civic Theatres Toronto to the name TO Live and amend Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 23, Civic Theatres accordingly.
