The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of November 1, 2019, the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee considered Item DI6.2 and recommended that the Executive Committee request the Director, Strategic Recruitment to provide an update to the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee in the second quarter of 2020 that includes information on the specific recruitment tactics that will be used with the different disability communities. Barbara Shulman, Director of Strategic Recruitment, and Hali Farah, Senior HR Consultant, People and Equity gave a presentation to the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee on the City's strategy for increasing recruitment of people with disabilities.

What was decided

The Executive Committee: 1. Requested the Director, Strategic Recruitment to provide an update to the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee in the second quarter of 2020 that includes information on the specific recruitment tactics that will be used with the different disability communities.
