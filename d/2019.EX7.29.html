What the item proposed

At its meeting of June 4, 2019, the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee considered a letter (May 21, 2019) from Michael Miceli, Member, Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee on Outstanding 2014-2018 Agenda Items. In an effort for the new Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee members to gain an understanding of the work undertaken in the last term of the committee and the outcomes of committee decisions, I am requesting the Accessibility Consultant, Equity, Diversity and Human Rights to provide a review of decisions and report on outstanding agenda items for the 2014-2018 term at a future 2019 Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee meeting.
