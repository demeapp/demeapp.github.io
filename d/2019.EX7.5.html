The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide a recommended approach for planning and building new recreation facilities that will best serve the existing and future communities located along Don Mills Road, specifically the communities located at: Don Mills Road and Lawrence Avenue East, Wynford Drive, and Don Mills Road and Eglinton Avenue East, and Flemingdon Park (Don Mills Corridor). The Don Mills Corridor is anticipated to grow by 25,000 residents, with approximately three quarters of this growth concentrated at the intersection of Eglinton Avenue East and Don Mills Road. This transformation is mainly attributed to the construction of the Eglinton Crosstown Light Rail Transit line as well as the approval of a new mixed-use community on the former Celestica Lands.
