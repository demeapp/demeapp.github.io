Etobicoke York Community Council decides: Parking Regulation Amendment - King Street, between Weston Road and George Street.

Official title: Parking Regulation Amendment - King Street, between Weston Road and George Street

AdoptedNov 5, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to obtain approval to amend the existing stopping prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m. Monday to Friday on the south side of King Street, between Weston Road and George Street to allow the Weston Area Emergency Food Bank, located at 1 King Street, to receive deliveries from 9:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. Monday to Friday.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking from 9:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m., Monday to Friday on the south side of King Street, between a point 32.5 metres east of Weston Road and a point 14 metres further east. 2. Amended the existing stopping prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday on the south side of King Street, between a point 32.5 metres east of Weston Road and a point 14 metres further east, to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:30 a.m. and from 11:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday.
