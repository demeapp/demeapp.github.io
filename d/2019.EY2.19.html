The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Etobicoke York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report outlines our investigation to reduce the speed limits on Homeview Avenue, White Avenue and Pritchard Avenue from 50 km/h to 30 km/h, between Jane Street and Florence Crescent. The results of the analysis indicate that the criteria to reduce the speed limits down to 30 km/h were not satisfied.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on Homeview Avenue, between Jane Street and Florence Crescent. 2. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on White Avenue, between Jane Street and Florence Crescent. 3. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on Pritchard Avenue, between Jane Street and Florence Crescent.
