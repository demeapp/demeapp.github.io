The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes to amend the former City of Etobicoke Zoning Code and Site Specific By-law No. 514-2003 to permit an 8-storey (29 m in height, including the mechanical equipment) mixed-use building containing 651 m2 of at-grade non-residential gross floor area and 100 residential units above at 880, 884, 886, 888 and 890 The Queensway. A total of 106 vehicular parking spaces are proposed both at the rear of the site and within a two-level underground garage. On March 26, 2018, the applicant filed an appeal of the Zoning By-law Amendment application to the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal ("LPAT"), citing City Council's failure to make a decision on the application within the prescribed timelines of the Planning Act.
