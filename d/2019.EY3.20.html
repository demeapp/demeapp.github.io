Staff recommend parking regulation changes on Monkton Avenue.

Official title: Parking Regulation Amendment - Monkton Avenue

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to propose an amendment to the current "No Parking, 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday" regulation on the west side of Monkton Avenue, between Springbrook Gardens and the south limit of the road. The residents raised concerns regarding the lack of on-street parking during the late afternoon period, Monday to Friday, and during the day on Saturdays. Based on a petition received from the affected residents, staff recommends a "No Parking, 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday" regulation on the west side of Monkton Avenue, between Springbrook Gardens and the south limit of Monkton Avenue. The proposed parking amendments are supported by the ward Councillor.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing 'No Parking' on the west side of Monkton Avenue, between Van Dusen Boulevard and the south end of Monkton Avenue from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday. 2. Prohibited parking on the west side of Monkton Avenue, between Van Dusen Boulevard and Springbrook Gardens, from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday. 3. Prohibited parking on the west side of Monkton Avenue, between Springbrook Gardens and the south end of Monkton Avenue, from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday.
