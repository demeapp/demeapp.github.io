City Council was asked to extend permit parking to part of Castleton Avenue.

Official title: Implementation of Permit Parking on Castleton Avenue, between Henrietta Street and Corbett Avenue

The official record

What the item proposed

Castleton Avenue, between Henrietta Street and Corbett Avenue is signed “No Parking” on both sides of the street. Several residents on this portion of the street have no on-site parking and therefore require access to the residential on-street permit parking program, which is already in place on the remaining sections of Castleton Avenue. I believe it's reasonable to incorporate the block of Castleton Avenue, between Henrietta Street and Corbett Avenue into the permit parking program in order to accommodate resident's long term parking needs. This motion seeks an exemption from subsection 925-4B of City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 925, Permit Parking, provides that: Where permit parking is not currently available and where a petition requesting permit parking signed by the residents of a minimum of 25% of the total number of residential premises having a residential address on…

What was decided

City Council on February 26, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council exempt the implementation of permit parking on Castleton Avenue, between Henrietta Street and Corbett Avenue, from the requirements of subsection 925‐4B of City of Toronto, Municipal Code, Chapter 925, Permit Parking, until such time as Etobicoke York Community Council considers a request for implementation of permit parking at that location.
