The official record

What the item proposed

Requesting an expanded Notice area for the planning applications for 2370 Finch Avenue West.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Deleted Part 2 of the decision on Item EY28.5, headed "Preliminary Report - 2370 Finch Avenue West - Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment Application", adopted by the Community Council on February 21, 2018, and replaced it with the following: 2. Notice for the community consultation meeting be given to all landowners and residents within the boundaries of Steeles Avenue to the north, Highway 401 to the south, Highway 400 to the east and the Humber River to the west, with the addition cost to be paid for by the applicant.
