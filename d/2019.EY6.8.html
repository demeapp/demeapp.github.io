The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend that the ceremonial name of "Alain Sutton Way" be assigned to City View Drive between Belfield Road and Dixon Road. A ceremonial street dedication does not change the official street name or address information. It does result in the installation of special signage along the selected street. Transportation Services was consulted during the preparation of this report.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Assigned the ceremonial name "Alain Sutton Way" to City View Drive between Belfield Road and Dixon Road. 2. Directed Transportation Services to erect signage marking "Alain Sutton Way."
