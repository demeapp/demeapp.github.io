City Council was asked to approve parking regulation changes on Isaac Devins Boulevard.

Official title: Parking Regulation Amendment - Isaac Devins Boulevard

AdoptedJun 25, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to obtain approval to allow long-term overnight parking and short-term (3 hour) daytime parking on the south side of Isaac Devins Boulevard (south leg). In addition, a "No Parking Anytime" regulation will be implemented on the north side of Isaac Devins Boulevard (south leg).

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the "No Parking, 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m., from December 1 of one year to March 31 of the following year" on Isaac Devins Boulevard from Isaac Devins Boulevard (east leg) to Bob Yuill Drive. 2. Authorized a 12 hour permitted parking from 8:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m., on the south side of Isaac Devins Boulevard (south leg) from Isaac Devins Boulevard (east leg) to Bob Yuill Drive. 3. Prohibited parking at all times on the north side of Isaac Devins Boulevard (south leg) from Isaac Devins Boulevard (east leg) to Bob Yuill Drive.
