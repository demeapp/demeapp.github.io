What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to propose an amendment to the current "No Parking, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday" regulation on the east side of Humbervale Boulevard between Glenroy Avenue and a point 102 metres south, to be in effect from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday to Friday. In addition, staff propose the introduction of a “Maximum 15 Minute Limit, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday” parking regulation on the west side of Grenview Boulevard South between Glenroy Avenue and a point 80.0 metres south of, along the east flank of Sunnylea Junior School. What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Humbervale Boulevard between Glenroy Avenue and a point 102 metres south, to be in effect from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday to Friday. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Grenview Boulevard South between Meadowvale Drive and Sunnylea Avenue West. 3. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 15 minutes from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Grenview Boulevard South between Glenroy Avenue and a point 80 metres south.
