City Council was asked to approve parking regulation changes on West Deane Park Drive, near Josyf Cardinal Slipyj School.

Official title: Parking Regulation Amendment - West Deane Park Drive, near Josyf Cardinal Slipyj School

AdoptedJun 25, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to propose the introduction of a “No Parking, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday” parking regulation on the south side of West Deane Park Drive, along a portion of the frontage of Josyf Cardinal Slipyj School, 35 West Deane Park Drive. The proposed “No Parking, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday” parking restriction is required to accommodate operational needs which includes a student drop off program that assists students entering and exiting from vehicles to Josyf Cardinal Slipyj School.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of West Deane Park Drive, between a point opposite Amberwood Road and a point 44 metres east of Beaver Bend Crescent. 2. Rescinded the existing maximum ten-minute parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday on the south side of West Deane Park Drive, between a point 25 metres east of Amberwood Road and a point 44 metres east of Beaver Bend Crescent.
