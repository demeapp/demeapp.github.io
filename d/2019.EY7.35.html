The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks to inform Etobicoke York Community Council that Transportation Services is in the process of preparing a final report, which will provide recommendations regarding the encroachment of an existing fence, shed and electrical outlet within the Harlton Crescent public road allowance. The subject encroachments are associated with the property at 70 Haverson Boulevard and do not meet the requirements of the City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks, Use of, for delegated approval by the General Manager of Transportation Services. As a result, the encroachments must be approved by Community Council, which has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision in this regard.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Approved the request to maintain an approximate 2.19 metre high fence, shed, and electrical outlet on the flankage of 70 Haverson Boulevard, within the Harlton Crescent public right of way, with the following conditions: a. The property owner shall, at their own expense, enter into an encroachment agreement with the City of Toronto on terms and conditions satisfactory to the General Manager, Transportation Services, including the following: i. The property owner shall assume all liability and damages related to the encroachments and release and indemnify the City with respect to any claims in relation thereto. ii. The property owner must retain valid property insurance for the duration of the encroachment installation on the right of way in an amount and with coverages satisfactory to the General Manager, Transportation Services. iii.
