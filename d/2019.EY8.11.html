What the item proposed

This staff report is in regards to a matter for which the Etobicoke York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to consider an application submitted by the owner of 84 Waniska Avenue to maintain a front yard horizontal board on board fence with a height of 1.2 to 1.5 metres that fails to provide open construction, which does not meet all of the requirements specified in City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447, Fences.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council deferred consideration of this Item to its meeting on October 10, 2019.
