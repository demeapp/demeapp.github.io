U-Turn Prohibition - Elmbrook Crescent

AdoptedSep 16, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to prohibit westbound U-turn movements at all times on Elmbrook Crescent, between Renforth Drive (south intersection) and Millsborough Crescent.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to prohibit westbound U-turns at all times on Elmbrook Crescent, between Renforth Drive (south intersection) and Millsborough Crescent. This proposed prohibition, requested by an area resident, will address traffic safety concerns and congestion in the area.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Prohibited westbound U-turn movements at all times on Elmbrook Crescent, between Renforth Drive (south intersection) and Millsborough Crescent.
