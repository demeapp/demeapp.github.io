Speed Limit Reduction - Erie Street

Adopted with changesSep 16, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to not reduce the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on Erie Street, between Falstaff Avenue and Maple Leaf Drive.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report outlines the results of an investigation to reduce the speed limit on Erie Street from 50 km/h to 30 km/h, between Falstaff Avenue and Maple Leaf Drive. The analysis indicates that the criteria to reduce the speed limit to 30 km/h were not satisfied.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on Erie Street, between Falstaff Avenue and Maple Leaf Drive.
