Traffic Calming Poll Results - Strathnairn Avenue, between Lonborough Avenue and Keele Street

Adopted with changesSep 16, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to not authorize installing traffic calming (speed humps) on Strathnairn Avenue, between Lonborough Avenue and Keele Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report summarizes the result of the traffic calming poll for owners and occupants of Strathnairn Avenue, between Lonborough Avenue and Keele Street. Polling ballots were mailed to the affected residents on May 16, 2019 and 36% of the residents on Strathnairn Avenue, between Lonborough Avenue and Keele Street, responded to the polling. As a result, the minimum response rate of 50% plus 1 votes needed to proceed with the installation was not achieved.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit to 30 km/h on Strathnairn Avenue, between Lonborough Avenue and Keele Street. 2. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Strathnairn Avenue, between Lonborough Avenue and Keele Street 3. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway on Strathnairn Avenue for traffic calming enhancement, generally as shown on Drawing EY19-033A and EY19-033B dated March 2019.
