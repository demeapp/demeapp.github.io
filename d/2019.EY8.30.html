Etobicoke York Community Council decides: Parking Regulation Amendment - Millwick Drive.

Official title: Parking Regulation Amendment - Millwick Drive

AdoptedSep 16, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to prohibit stopping at all times on Millwick Drive, from Milvan Drive (southeast intersection) to a point 378 metres further northwest on the north and east side of the street, and to a point 338 metres further northwest on the west and south side of the street. The proposed amendment to the existing parking prohibition will address the concerns of the business owners about heavy vehicles parked on both sides of Millwick Drive and will improve traffic operations on this section of the road.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Millwick Drive, from a point 162 metres southeast of Milvan Drive (northwest intersection) to Milvan Drive (southeast intersection). 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Millwick Drive, from a point 159 metres southeast of Milvan Drive (northwest intersection) to Milvan Drive (southeast intersection). 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the north and east side of Millwick Drive, from Milvan Drive (southeast intersection) to a point 378 metres north and west. 4. Prohibited stopping at all times on the south and west side of Millwick Drive, from Milvan Drive (southeast intersection) to a point 338 metres north and west. 5.
