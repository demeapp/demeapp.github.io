What the item proposed

This report advises City Council, pursuant to Chapter 195 of the Toronto Municipal Code (Purchasing By-law, Section 195-7.4), of the non-competitive contract with Steelcore Construction Limited to procure emergency construction services for the completion of shelter infrastructure. Facilities Management is managing a project to convert a former motel at 3306 Kingston Road into a fully functional shelter for Shelter Support and Housing Administration program requirements. For this purpose, award of Tender Call Number 276-2017 was made to Steelcore Construction Limited on January 17, 2018. During renovations, it was determined that the site required extensive remedial work due to site conditions that could not have been foreseen prior to purchase. City Council on January 30 and 31, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council receive the report (December 27, 2018) from the Interim General Manager, Facilities Management, and the Chief Purchasing Officer for information.
