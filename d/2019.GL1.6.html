Staff recommend exempting a Union Station tourism lease from late-payment fees.

Official title: Grant of Exemption from Late Payment Fees - Tourism Space at Union Station

AdoptedJan 30, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Infrastructure Ontario and Lands Corporation, on behalf of Her Majesty the Queen in Right of Ontario as represented by the Minister of Infrastructure, has been negotiating a lease agreement with the City for space at Union Station to be used for the promotion of tourism in Ontario and it has advised that it cannot agree to pay interest on late payments or returned cheque fees, as it does not have Treasury Board approval to do so, as required under the Financial Administration Act. Accordingly, this report seeks an exemption from the requirement to charge late payment fees and returned cheque fees under the lease.

What was decided

City Council on January 30 and 31, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council grant an exemption from the requirement to charge late payment fees of 15 percent per year for outstanding receivable balances and returned cheque fees (currently $40 per returned cheque) under the lease to Her Majesty the Queen in Right of Ontario, as represented by the Minister of Infrastructure (the "Tenant"), for space at Union Station for the promotion and marketing of tourism in Ontario.
