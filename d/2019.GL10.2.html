Toronto City Council · Decision

Automated Income Verification for Property Tax, Water, and Solid Waste Relief Programs

PostponedDec 2, 2019 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to authorize the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding with the Canada Revenue Agency to indirectly collect information from the Canada Revenue Agency to verify income and related information of applicants to City programs.

Council put off deciding this item.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks City Council's authority to allow the City to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding with the Canada Revenue Agency to obtain information from the Canada Revenue Agency to verify income and related information for the purposes of determining eligibility of applicants to various City programs, beginning with the City's Property Tax, Water, and Solid Waste Relief Programs. This report also seeks City Council's authority to make the necessary amendments to the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapters 767, 844, and 849 that will allow for the indirect collection and use of personal information, including Social Insurance Numbers, and any other such information that may be required by the Canada Revenue Agency, pursuant to the Memorandum of Understanding between the City and the Canada Revenue Agency, for the purposes of determining eligibility under the City's programs.

What was decided

The General Government and Licensing Committee: 1. Deferred consideration of the item until its meeting on January 6, 2020, with a request that the Controller consult with stakeholders on the recommendations.
