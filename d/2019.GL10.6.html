City Hall Council Chamber Modernization Options

Referred back for more workDec 17, 2019 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to direct the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, and the City Clerk to undertake a feasibility study for the modernization of the City Hall Council Chamber.

Council sent it back for more work.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide information about modernization options for the City Hall Council Chamber requested by the General Government and Licensing Committee at its October meeting. There is a unique opportunity to address a number of issues with the Council Chamber, including achieving compliance with the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act prior to the January 1, 2025 deadline, enhance security and life safety requirements, update of Chamber technologies, and meeting the needs of the Members of Council. A Council Chamber project can be rolled out in phases to minimize disturbance to activities in the Chamber. During the construction phase, City Council would need to meet in an alternate location, as the Chamber would be closed. A contingency plan for off-site meetings is maintained by the City Clerk.

What was decided

City Council on December 17 and 18, 2019, referred Item GL10.6 to the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management and the City Clerk, and City Council directed the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, and the City Clerk to explore options to integrate the modernization of the Council Chamber into existing capital initiatives, including accessibility, security, technology and state-of-good-repair projects or programs.
