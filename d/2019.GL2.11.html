What the item proposed

This report advises on the results of Request for Proposal (RFP) Number 9138-18-7006 for the provision of collection agency services to support the collection of defaulted fines under the Provincial Offences Act (POA) and seeks approval of the collection rates under the City of Toronto Act, 2006. Existing contracts expire on March 31st, 2019 and new contracts are required in order to continue service. Staff are requesting authority to negotiate and enter into agreements with eleven recommended Proponents in accordance with the terms in the RFP, as listed in Attachment 1. This report also proposes that City Council approve an average commission rate, plus non-recoverable tax, for First Assignment, Second Assignment, and Third Assignment Accounts.
