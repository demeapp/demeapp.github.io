What the item proposed

I am requesting the members of the General Government and Licensing Committee consider the following initiative to advance technological use in the City of Toronto municipal government by the creation of a Startup in Residence (STIR) pilot project dealing with procurement and e-procurement. The purpose of the STIR program is to act as a lead for procurement reform, to introduce City agencies, departments, commissions, and boards to new innovation and technologies, and to serve as an incubator to introduce the necessary procurement reforms needed to allow increased innovation to continue for the long term. These products will enhance government productivity, efficiencies, and help improve the resident experience with municipal government.
