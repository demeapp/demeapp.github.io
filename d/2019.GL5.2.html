What the item proposed

This report provides an update on the research and consultations completed to date that inform the ongoing review of the Body Rub Parlour (BRP) and Holistic Centre By-laws. It seeks City Council's endorsement on an approach to regulating these industries based on the principles of ensuring the health, safety, and well-being of persons; consumer protection; and ensuring the general well-being of the City of Toronto, including mitigating community impacts. The City regulates adult entertainment parlours, including BRPs, to ensure the health and safety of workers and the public and to mitigate for community nuisance. In 1998, the City began regulating holistic services to ensure public health and safety and consumer protection.
