What the item proposed

This report provides an update on the status of the merger of four pre-Ontario Municipal Employees Retirement System (OMERS) pension funds with OMERS. The merger process is a technical and regulatory process, with approvals of pensioners and the Financial Services Commission of Ontario, as well as detailed work by City and OMERS staff. The York Fund has successfully merged with OMERS as of January 8, 2019 and it is anticipated that the three other funds will merge in either late 2019 or early 2020. In addition to pensioners receiving their existing pensions plus OMERS inflationary indexing, members of funds which have surpluses will also receive a share of the surplus, upon the wind-up of their fund.
