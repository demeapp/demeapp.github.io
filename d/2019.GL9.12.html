The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks authority to accept future parkland located at 55 Lake Shore Boulevard East subject to an Easement in favour of Enwave Energy Corporation and to enter into an Easement Agreement with respect to the same.

What was decided

City Council on November 26 and 27, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the acceptance of the future parkland located at 55 Lake Shore Boulevard East encumbered by a permanent Easement ("Easement") in favour of Enwave Energy Corporation ("Enwave") to accommodate below-grade district energy infrastructure, in accordance with Chapter 3.2.3 (Parks and Open Spaces), Policy 8.a) of the City of Toronto's Official Plan. 2.
