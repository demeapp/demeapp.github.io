The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with Part H, Section 22 of the Board of Health Procedures By-law, the Board of Health elects a Vice Chair at its first meeting in each calendar year for a term of office starting January 21, 2019 and ending December 31, 2019, and until a successor is appointed. All members are eligible for election.
