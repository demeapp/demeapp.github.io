What the item proposed

The ChemTRAC program aims to increase public awareness of chemicals in the environment and reduce pollution from industrial and commercial sources by annually reporting to the public on the manufacture, use, or release of chemicals by local industries. It is administered by Toronto Public Health (TPH) and is guided by the Environmental Reporting and Disclosure By-law, which requires certain facilities to report each year if they use or release any of 25 priority substances above identified thresholds. The information reported to ChemTRAC is then publically released. The information for 2018 is currently available online through the program's interactive map and in the 2019 annual report. ChemTRAC, with its lower reporting thresholds and focus on local industries, complements the National Pollutant Release Inventory which captures information from facilities with large emissions.…
