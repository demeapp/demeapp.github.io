What the item proposed

This report outlines the Toronto Public Health (TPH) 2020 Operating Budget Submission for consideration by the Board of Health. Toronto Public Health's 2020 Operating Budget Submission totals $269,629.5 thousand gross, $69,821.3 thousand net, and 1,919.2 positions. This submission is $12,900.2 thousand gross, $5,944.6 thousand net above the 2019 Operating Budget and reflects a reduction of 13 positions. The gross increase of $12,900.2 thousand from the 2019 Operating Budget is predominately related to the Ontario Seniors Dental Care Program.
