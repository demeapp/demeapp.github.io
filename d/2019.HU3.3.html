The official record

What the item proposed

On April 11, 2019, the Government of Ontario announced a $90 million investment for the Ontario Seniors Dental Care Program (OSDCP), a new publicly-funded dental program for low-income Ontario seniors. On June 7, 2019, Toronto Public Health (TPH) received a letter (see Appendix A) indicating that the City of Toronto will receive $15,543.3 thousand in additional base funding for 2019-2020 from the Ministry of Health (formerly the Ministry of Health and Long-Term Care) (the Ministry) to implement the OSDCP. On August 20, 2019, the Ministry provided the 2019 Public Health Funding and Accountability Agreement stating that the above funding would be prorated at $11,657.4 thousand for the period of April 1 to December 31, 2019. What was decided

The Board of Health Budget Committee recommends to the Board of Health that: 1. City Council increase the 2019 Operating Budget for Toronto Public Health by $2,111.0 thousand gross, $0 net, and 52 permanent positions to be used for pre-implementation and implementation costs associated with delivering the Ontario Seniors Dental Care Program.
