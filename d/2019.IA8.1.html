What the item proposed

Councillor Paula Fletcher has submitted the following Administrative Inquiry: "The Union Station Rail Corridor and its signal system is a critical piece of infrastructure serving this city, with, for example, some 210,000 GO passenger trips on it every weekday. A May 18, 2006, City Staff Report on the Union Station District Plan noted that 'Commencing this year, GO Transit will be undertaking a significant repair and improvement program to the rail corridor including track improvements, signals.' There is a July 31, 2006 media release from the Ontario Ministry of Transportation and GO Transit announcing that work had started. However, a November 3, 2014 media release from the Ontario Minister of Transportation and Metrolinx says the work will start in 2015.
