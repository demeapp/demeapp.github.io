The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with Section 25 of the Toronto Investment Board Procedures By-law, regular meetings of the Board are determined by the Board and a schedule of those meetings shall be publicly available by the first Board meeting in each calendar year. Traditionally, the Toronto Investment Board's meetings are held in the afternoon in the committee rooms on the second floor of Toronto City Hall (100 Queen Street West). On December 4, 5, and 13, 2018, City Council approved the 2019 annual schedule of meetings for City Council and its Committees. Please note that the meeting schedule is available on the City of Toronto's website at www.toronto.ca/council .

What was decided

The Toronto Investment Board: 1. Approved the following meeting dates for 2019: - Thursday April 11 - Wednesday June 12 - Wednesday September 25 - Friday December 13
