What the item proposed

In an effort to protect the City's tree canopy against invasive pests, it is necessary for the City to implement a Gypsy Moth control program in 2019, including an aerial spray in certain areas of the City. The report requests that City Council grant authority to enter into a non-competitive contract with Zimmer Air Service Inc. for control of European Gypsy Moth outbreak for 2019, in the amount of $1,204,616 net of HST ($1,225,818 net of HST recoveries). European Gypsy Moth (Lymantria dispar) is an invasive alien pest that was first found in Ontario in 1969. This pest cannot be eradicated and if left unchecked, populations can grow to outbreak levels, which can lead to tree defoliation and ultimately affect the health of the urban forest canopy.
