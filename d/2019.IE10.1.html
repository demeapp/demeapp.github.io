What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to advise of the results of the Request for Proposal (RFP) No. 9117-19-7037 for Program Management Assignment PM6A2 to provide professional engineering services for preliminary and detailed design, during construction and post-construction, to support the delivery of Toronto Water's coordinated 2020 to 2023 Standalone Watermain Replacement Program and to request the authority to enter into an agreement with R.V. Anderson Associated Limited (RVA) in the amount of $20,367,396, net of all taxes, $23,015,158 including HST and all applicable charges ($20,725,862 net of HST recoveries).
