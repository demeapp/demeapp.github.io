What the item proposed

In Spring 2018, United Jewish Appeal (UJA) received the Water Service Infrastructure Agreement (WSIA) from City staff for execution. UJA completed and submitted the Agreement to Engineering Services along with the required securities on April 5, 2018. Work on the community centre project commenced in fall 2017 and was proceeding under a Conditional Permit. The site servicing permit was required to keep on schedule. On May 3, 2018 UJA was advised that a technical drafting error had been made by City Staff in the definition. Minor amendments were to be provided and a subsequent draft WSIA was in discussion. On May 15, 2018 the Site Servicing Permit was issued and servicing work commenced. In June 2018, Engineering staff noticed an update note on the plan provided by UJA regarding a connection at manhole 99 highlighting the internal drop structure in manhole 99.
