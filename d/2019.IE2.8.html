The official record

What the item proposed

In November 2018, a planned Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) track-removal project - affecting Victoria Street and Shuter Street – along with planned City of Toronto streetscape improvement work, was deferred indefinitely, due to concerns regarding aging, below-grade Toronto Hydro Corporation (THC) infrastructure. Engineering and Construction Services staff identified that upgrades to the THC infrastructure would be necessary, before the TTC and streetscape work could proceed. The City of Toronto streetscape project, which proposed the installation of a bump-out on the south side of Victoria Street, fronting Massey Hall, as well as installation of new traffic signals at the intersection of Shuter Street and Victoria Street, had been coordinated in conjunction with the TTC track removal project, in order to minimize disruption to area stakeholders. What was decided

City Council on March 27 and 28, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the Deputy City Manager, Infrastructure and Environment, to consult with the Toronto Hydro Corporation, the Toronto Transit Commission, Massey Hall, St. Michael's Hospital, the Elgin and Winter Garden Theatre, and the Downtown Yonge Business Improvement Area and report to the Infrastructure and Environment Committee on May 23, 2019 with a plan coordinating the Toronto Hydro rebuilds, the Toronto Transit Commission track removal and the City of Toronto streetscape improvements on Victoria Street and Shuter Street such that the work be completed by August 2020.
