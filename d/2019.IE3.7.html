What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to receive Council authority to enter into and execute an additional amending agreement to the Street Furniture Agreement to make changes to the specific allocations of street furniture elements, maintenance and inspection of elements, timeframe for non-powered elements to be re-installed and to implement an improved customer service procedure. The Coordinated Street Furniture Program is a public-private partnership with Astral Media to install, operate, and maintain approximately 24,500 pieces of street furniture over a 20-year period (currently in Year 12). The Agreement with Astral began in 2007, and follows a fiscal year of September 1 to August 31.
