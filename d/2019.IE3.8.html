The official record

What the item proposed

As you know, Toronto is a beautiful city with a beautiful waterfront. Lake Ontario is the 13th largest body of fresh water in the world and we have to do all we can to ensure that it, and our entire waterfront, are protected. Right now, when a major storm hits our city, our wastewater system goes into overdrive to prevent major flooding. As the water rushes in, our current system pushes wastewater overflow, basically raw sewage, into the lake when it reaches a certain threshold – preventing it from rushing back up sewers and into basements. Poor water quality especially in the Don River, Inner Harbour and Taylor-Massey Creek, are as a result of combined sewer overflow (CSO) and stormwater runoff. What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. Directed the General Manager, Toronto Water to report in the second quarter of 2019 on a plan to accelerate the City’s Wet Weather Flow Master Plan to eliminate wastewater from Toronto’s clean water ways, including Lake Ontario. 2. Directed the General Manager, Toronto Water in the same report to outline the range of federal and provincial infrastructure programs which would upon application, assist Toronto with the funding of the accelerated Wet Weather Flow Master Plan.
