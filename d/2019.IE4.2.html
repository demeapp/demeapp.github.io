Construction Staging Area - Broadway Avenue (85-91 Broadway Avenue and 198 Redpath Avenue)

AdoptedMay 14, 2019 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to approve the closure of the south sidewalk and a 2.0 metre wide portion of the eastbound curb lane on Broadway Avenue, between Redpath Avenue and a point 41 metres west, from March 26, 2019 to August 31, 2021.

The official record

What the item proposed

Fitzrovia Real Estate Inc. is constructing a 38-storey rental building at 85-91 Broadway Avenue and 198 Redpath Avenue. The site is bounded by Broadway Avenue to the north, Redpath Avenue the east, existing residential uses (178-192 Broadway Avenue) to the south and existing residential uses (75 Broadway Avenue) to the west. St. Monica Catholic School, St. Monica Child Care Centre and North Toronto Collegiate Institute, have all been notified of the construction staging plan and the developer has committed to holding information sessions with the schools. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the south sidewalk and a portion of the eastbound lane on Broadway Avenue abutting the south side of the site for a period of 30 months to accommodate the construction staging and operations. What was decided

City Council on May 14 and 15, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve the closure of the south sidewalk and a 2.0 metre wide portion of the eastbound curb lane on Broadway Avenue, between Redpath Avenue and a point 41 metres west, from March 26, 2019 to August 31, 2021. 2. City Council rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Broadway Avenue, between Redpath Avenue and 74.5 metres west. 3. City Council rescind the existing maximum one-hour parking limit in effect from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Broadway Avenue, between Redpath Avenue and 74.5 metres west. 4. City Council rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Broadway Avenue, between Redpath Avenue and 42.5 metres west. 5.
