The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek Council approval for temporary daily short-term curb lane closures on a section of Highway 27 in the vicinity of Finch Avenue West and Humber College Boulevard. The proposed lane closures will be implemented intermittently during the off-peak period and to undertake early works for the Finch West Light Rail Transit (LRT) construction. The temporary lane closures are being requested from May 2019 to October 2019. The report further seeks Council authority to close a portion of the boulevard space within the northwest, northeast, southeast and southwest quadrants of the intersection of Highway 27 and Finch Avenue West. These areas of land are required for construction material and equipment laydown purposes during construction of the LRT portal and guideway.
