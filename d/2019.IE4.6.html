What the item proposed

The waste management landscape in Toronto and across Ontario is imminently set to significantly change. With a desire to move to an Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) model, municipalities will soon no longer be responsible for managing and processing their recyclable material streams, nor responsible for any of the associated costs. This new Provincial framework brings with it a climate of uncertainty, where stability in processing services needs to be taken seriously and is the prudent direction to take. Some changes to consider are: 1. Future contracts for processing will be removed from municipal authority and made the responsibility of product producers, or others with a commercial connection as designated by provincial regulation, likely facilitated through engagement with a Producer Responsibility Organization (PRO). 2.
