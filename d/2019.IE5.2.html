Amendment to Purchase Order Number 6025318 for Headworks and Odour Control Upgrades at the Highland Creek Treatment Plant with AECOM Canada Ltd.

AdoptedMay 23, 2019 · Toronto City Council

This report requests authority to amend Purchase Order Number 6025318 with AECOM Canada Ltd. (AECOM) for additional professional services associated with the Headworks and Odour Control Upgrades Project at the Highland Creek Treatment Plant (HCTP) in the amount of $489,000 net of all taxes and charges ($497,607 net of HST recoveries).

The official summary names a figure of $489,000.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Purchase Order Number 6025318 with AECOM Canada Ltd. (AECOM) for additional professional services associated with the Headworks and Odour Control Upgrades Project at the Highland Creek Treatment Plant (HCTP) in the amount of $489,000 net of all taxes and charges ($497,607 net of HST recoveries).

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1.C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Financial Control By-Law), granted authority to amend Purchase Order Number 6025318 with AECOM Canada Ltd., for the provision of professional services for contract administration and site inspection services during construction of Headworks and Odour Control Upgrades at the Highland Creek Wastewater Treatment Plant by an additional amount of $489,000 net of all taxes and charges ($497,607 net of HST recoveries), revising the current contract value from $5,542,878 net of all taxes and charges ($5,640,432 net of HST recoveries) to $6,031,878 net of all taxes and charges ($6,138,039 net of HST recoveries).
