Contract Award for Purchase of Equipment for the Biosolids Master Plan Implementation at the Highland Creek Wastewater Treatment Plant, Request for Proposal Number 9117-18-7202

AdoptedMay 23, 2019 · Toronto City Council

This report recommends acceptance of Request for Proposal (RFP) Number 9117-18-7202 for the purchase of equipment for two new Fluidized Bed Incinerators and associated peripherals including emissions monitoring and control equipment and to request authority to enter into an agreement with SUEZ Water Technologies and Solutions Canada in the amount of $40,932,000 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($41,652,403 net of HST recoveries), being the highest overall scoring vendor proponent meeting the proposal requirements.
