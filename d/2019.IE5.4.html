Toronto City Council · Decision

Contract Award for Tender Call Number 54-2019 for the Rehabilitation of Four Bridges over the Don Valley Parkway

AdoptedMay 23, 2019 · Toronto City Council

This report advises of the results of Tender Call Number 54-2019, Contract Number 19ECS-TI-07BE, for the rehabilitation of four (4) bridges over the Don Valley Parkway, namely, the Don Mills Road Bridge, Spanbridge Road Bridge, Wynford Drive Bridge and Lawrence Avenue Bridge and requests authority to award the contract to Grascan Construction Ltd./Torbridge Construction Ltd., in the amount of $26,520,000 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($26,986,752 net of HST recoveries).

The official summary names a figure of $26,520,000.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to advise of the results of Tender Call Number 54-2019, Contract Number 19ECS-TI-07BE, for the rehabilitation of four (4) bridges over the Don Valley Parkway, namely, the Don Mills Road Bridge, Spanbridge Road Bridge, Wynford Drive Bridge and Lawrence Avenue Bridge and request authority to award the contract to Grascan Construction Ltd./Torbridge Construction Ltd., in the amount of $26,520,000 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($26,986,752 net of HST recoveries).

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 195-8.4A of the Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 195 (Purchasing By-Law), granted authority to award Contract Number 19ECS-TI-07BE, Tender Call Number 54-2019, for the Rehabilitation of Don Mills Road Bridge over Don Valley Parkway, Spanbridge Road Bridge over Don Valley Parkway, Wynford Drive Bridge over Don Valley Parkway and Lawrence Avenue Bridge over Don Valley Parkway, in the amount of $26,520,000 net of all taxes ($26,986,752 net of HST recoveries) to Grascan Construction Ltd. / Torbridge Construction Ltd., having submitted the lowest/sole compliant bid and meeting the specifications in conformance with the Tender requirements.
