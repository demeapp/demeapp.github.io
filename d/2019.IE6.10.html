What the item proposed

On May 30, 2017, the Province of Ontario passed the Safer School Zones Act, 2017 to facilitate the municipal adoption of Automated Speed Enforcement technology on roads with speed limits under 80 km/h in school and community safety zones. However, this legislation has not yet been proclaimed as in force, and the associated regulations proposed to be enacted are not yet drafted. The City cannot implement an automated speed enforcement program until the Province proclaims that this legislation is in force. In January 2018, City Council authorized the General Manager, Transportation Services, to proceed with the development of Automated Speed Enforcement in conjunction with the Province and other partnering municipalities.
