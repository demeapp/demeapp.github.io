The official record

What the item proposed

The Ten Year Cycling Network Plan, adopted by Council in June 2016 seeks to build on the existing network of cycling routes to connect gaps in the current network, grow the network into new parts of the city, and renew existing parts of the network to improve safety. Following the completion of feasibility assessments, design and consultation with area residents, this report seeks Council authority to install the following cycling infrastructure totalling approximately 11.6 lane kilometres proposed in the Cycling Network Plan: - Scarlett Road (cycle track: Wards 2 and 5, 3.1 lane km) - Blue Jays Way (bicycle lane: Ward 10, 1.1 lane km) - Vaughan Road (bicycle lane: Ward 12, 550 m) & 550 m of shared lanes - Willowdale Avenue (cycle track: Ward 18, 2.8 lane km) - Lawrence Avenue East (bicycle lane: Ward 25, 3.5 lane km) This report also seeks Council authority to make improvements…

Lawrence Avenue East (from Port Union Road to Rouge Hills Drive). 2. City Council authorize the installation of cycle tracks on the following sections of roadway, as described in Attachment 3 to the report (June 14, 2019) from the General Manager, Transportation Services: a.
