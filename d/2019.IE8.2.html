The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Contract No. 47020532 and 47020352 issued to Lystek International Inc. (Lystek) and Third High Farms Limited (Third High Farms), respectively, associated with the award of Request for Proposal (RFP) No. 9117-16-7164 for Biosolids Haulage and Beneficial Use Services. The values of the amendments are as follows: - Increase Contract No. 47020532, issued to Lystek by $728,000 net of all taxes and charges ($740,813 net of HST recoveries), revising the current contract value from $8,476,115 to $9,204,115 net of taxes and charges ($9,366,107 net of HST recoveries). - Increase Contract No. 47020352, issued to Third High Farms by $1,247,000 net of all taxes and charges ($1,268,947 net of HST recoveries), revising the current contract value from $11,119,607 to $12,366,607 net of taxes and charges ($12,584,259 net of HST recoveries).…

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code (Financial Control By-law), granted authority to amend the following Contracts with Lystek International Inc. and Third High Farms Limited to provide additional professional services for Biosolids Haulage and Beneficial Use as follows: a. Increase Contract No. 47020532, issued to Lystek by $728,000 net of all taxes and charges ($740,813 net of HST recoveries), revising the current contract value from $8,476,115 to $9,204,115 net of taxes and charges ($9,366,107 net of HST recoveries). b. Increase Contract No. 47020352, issued to Third High Farms by $1,247,000 net of all taxes and charges ($1,268,947 net of HST recoveries), revising the current contract value from $11,119,607 to $12,366,607 net of taxes and charges ($12,584,259 net of HST recoveries).
