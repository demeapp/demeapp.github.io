The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting held on October 2 and 3, 2019, City Council adopted Item TE8.11, with amendments, which recommended amendments to Zoning By-laws 438-86 and 569-2013 to permit redevelopment of 56 Yonge Street, 21 Melinda Street, 18 to 30 Wellington Street West, 187 to 199 Bay Street and 25 King Street West for a 65-storey office building and a 3-storey glass pavilion. The Bills enacting the amending Zoning By-laws are prepared for this meeting of Council. The applicant is requesting the maximum office gross floor area identified in the draft amending Zoning By-laws be increased from 315,000 square metres to 335,000 square metres based on recent survey of the existing office space below grade on the subject site.
