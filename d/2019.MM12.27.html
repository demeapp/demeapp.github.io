What the item proposed

At its meeting on October 29 and 30, 2019, City Council adopted Item EC8.5, headed "Resourcing the Regent Park Social Development Plan". The item included a supplementary report from the Acting Executive Director, Social Development Finance and Administration titled Resourcing the Regent Park Social Development Plan: Update on Investments, Employment and Training. The report was requested by the Committee and went straight to City Council. As a result, it was not fully considered as part of the Economic and Community Development Committee's deliberations on Item EC8.5. The supplementary report provides valuable insights into the City and Toronto Community Housing Corporation's efforts to foster job creation as part of the Regent Park revitalization.
