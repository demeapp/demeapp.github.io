Coordinating Construction and Reducing Impacts in Downtown Yonge - by Councillor Kristyn Wong-Tam, seconded by Councillor Mike Layton

City Council was asked to request the General Manager, Transportation Services and the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning to immediately form a monthly working group with membership to include Engineering and Construction Services, Transportation Services. The Downtown Yonge area has seen, and will continue to experience, an unprecedented amount of growth over the next five years, along with necessary investments to replace aging infrastructure. This has made it challenging to coordinate projects with public and private parties that often need to occupy the same right-of-way and use the same arterial roadways for the delivery of their construction materials.

What the item proposed

The Downtown Yonge area has seen, and will continue to experience, an unprecedented amount of growth over the next five years, along with necessary investments to replace aging infrastructure. This has made it challenging to coordinate projects with public and private parties that often need to occupy the same right-of-way and use the same arterial roadways for the delivery of their construction materials. Toronto is committed to achieving pedestrian and road safety through initiatives like Vision Zero. There are 26 projects already underway or commencing within the next year, which we are aware of, within the small geographic area between Bay Street, Mutual Street, College Street/Carlton Street and Queen Street.
