The official record

What the item proposed

City Council, at its meeting on December 4, 5 and 13, 2018, approved the appointment of an Interim Controller, pending the completion of a recruitment process to appoint a permanent Controller. Having completed the recruitment process, this report recommends the appointment of the individual named in a Confidential Attachment, to the position of Controller, and to the statutory office of Deputy Treasurer for the City of Toronto. This matter is urgent to conclude the recruitment process and enable the successful candidate to commence employment in a timely manner. The appointment of the Controller is a statutory appointment that requires Council's approval as per the City of Toronto Act, 2006.
