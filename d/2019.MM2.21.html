The official record

What the item proposed

The Richard L. Hearn Generation Station (the "Hearn Generating Station"), with its late Art Deco façade and 213 metre high chimney stack, is a defining feature of the Port Lands and one of the largest structures in Canada. The Hearn Generating Station was recognized as Provincial heritage property and has been listed in the City's Heritage Register as a rare example of a mid-century pre-nuclear generating station, playing a major role in defining the industrial character of the Port Lands. The Senior Manager, Heritage Preservation Services will be reporting on the evaluation of the Hearn Generating Station property under Ontario Regulation 9/06, the criteria prescribed for municipal designation under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act, at future meetings of the Toronto Preservation Board and City Council. What was decided

City Council on January 30 and 31, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the Deputy City Manager, Corporate Services, in consultation with the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO, to initiate negotiations on an expedited basis with the appropriate parties for the acquisition of the Hearn Generating Station site at market value, taking into consideration the environmental condition of the property and any other relevant factors, including recent transaction values, and otherwise on terms satisfactory to the Deputy City Manager, Corporate Services. 2. City Council direct the Deputy City Manager, Corporate Services to report to the Executive Committee at its meeting of April 9, 2019 regarding the status of such negotiations, and for the approval of the agreed upon business terms, if applicable.
