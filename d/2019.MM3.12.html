What the item proposed

RioCan Holdings (Queensway) Inc. (the "Applicant") is the owner of lands municipally known as 1001, 1007, 1001 and 1037 The Queensway in the City of Toronto (the "Subject Site"). The Applicant submitted a Zoning By-law Amendment application to the City in December of 2015, proposing to redevelop the Subject Site. The Zoning By-law Amendment application proposes to construct four buildings (three 12 storey buildings and one 20 storey tower), featuring a mix of residential, commercial/retail uses. (the "Application"). On November 23, 2017, the Applicant appealed the Zoning By-law Amendment application to the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal due to City Council's failure to make a decision within the time allotted by the Planning Act.
