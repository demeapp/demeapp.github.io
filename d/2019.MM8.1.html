The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto 360 "TO360" Wayfinding project is a multi-modal wayfinding system that includes pedestrian, vehicular, cycling and transit wayfinding information delivered by the Transportation Services Division. Following the successful completion of a pilot project in the downtown core, TO360 is being rolled out City-wide. Approximately 225 TO360 on-street signs and 500 Bikeshare station wayfinding maps will be completed by the end of 2019. As part of City-wide rollout, Exhibition Place has requested that Transportation Services supply and install nine (9) on-street wayfinding signs on Exhibition Place property in 2019. This installation represents the second and final phase of TO360 wayfinding sign installations at Exhibition Place, and will help meet the objectives of Exhibition Place's wayfinding masterplan. What was decided

City Council on June 18 and 19, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend the Approved 2019-2028 Capital Budget and Plan for Transportation Services by creating a new TO360 Exhibition Place Wayfinding project with total 2019 project costs of $128,722.30 gross and $0 debt, cash flowed in 2019, fully funded from the TO360 Reserve Fund (XR1414) which includes funding provided by Exhibition Place for the supply and installation of Toronto 360 (TO360) Wayfinding on-street signs on Exhibition Place property, and the replacement of a map on an existing TO360 on-street sign, in accordance with an Agreement executed between the City of Toronto and Exhibition Place.
