What the item proposed

This Motion seeks to release $2,200 in Section 37 funds to Heritage Toronto for a heritage plaque commemorating the life of Sam Richardson. The plaque is proposed to be installed at Central Technical School. Born in Toronto in 1919, Sam Richardson was a gifted track-and-field athlete. His great grandparents had been enslaved in the United States and fled to Canada through the Underground Railroad. In the 1930s, he attended Central Technical School on Harbord Street where he excelled in track-and field and played with the football and rugby teams. In 1934, he represented Canada at the British Empire Games (now the Commonwealth Games) in London, England where he won both gold and silver medals.
