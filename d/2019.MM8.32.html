What the item proposed

Former City Councillor Michael Feldman held office for 18 years in York Centre. Before being elected to City Council in 1992, Mr. Feldman was a successful businessman who founded Teela Data Management, a company specializing in real estate information. During his time as City Councillor, Mr. Feldman served as Deputy Mayor. He was a champion for local and City-wide issues. He was also a chair of the Metro Housing Development Corporation, a public housing agency serving the municipality of Metropolitan Toronto, whose constituent municipalities would form the merged City of Toronto after 1997. In York Centre, Mr. Feldman is known for championing and raising funds for the colourful mural on the wall of the 401 at the southeast corner of Bathurst Street and Wilson Avenue. What was decided

City Council on June 18 and 19, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation to consider the feasibility of renaming the Wilson Heights Parkette to Michael Feldman Park as well as installing the appropriate signage and report back to the September 16, 2019 meeting of the North York Community Council.
