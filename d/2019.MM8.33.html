What the item proposed

Born in 1928, Eliezer Wiesel is widely known as a vibrant representative of the Jewish community that worked most of his life on behalf of oppressed people around the world. As an author, storyteller and teacher, Elie Wiesel conveyed his personal experience of the Holocaust, defending human rights and spreading peace around the world. During Second World War, his family was imprisoned at Auschwitz when Elie was just 15 years old. There, Elie Wiesel endured the horrors of Nazism and saw his parents and younger sister perish at the concentration camps. The scaring experiences led Elie Wiesel to write the million copy selling book La Nuit (Night). Translated into 30 different languages, the book became a powerful narrative of the horrors committed by the Nazis in Europe against the Jewish community. What was decided

City Council on June 18 and 19, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation to consider the feasibility of renaming Clanton Park to Elie Wiesel Park as well as install the appropriate signage and report back to the September 16, 2019 meeting of the North York Community Council.
