The official record

What the item proposed

The film, television and digital media industry is a major economic driver for Toronto, and contributes to the vitality, prosperity and creativity of our City. In 2018, the industry achieved a production investment of $2 billion, supporting 30,000 jobs. City staff have been approached by a major production with a request to film at the R.C. Harris Water Treatment Plant in 2019. The production is the forthcoming feature film by Oscar-winning director Guillermo del Toro who has a long and impactful history producing in Toronto and will again generate significant production investment and positive media coverage for the City. In 2003, City Council adopted a policy for Security Measures at Water and Wastewater Facilities for Filming, which prohibited filming in the interior of water treatment plants. What was decided

City Council on June 18 and 19, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize a one-time exemption to the Security Measures at Water and Wastewater Facilities for Filming Policy to allow for interior filming at the R.C. Harris Water Treatment Plant, with specific filming locations, a schedule for filming, and security requirements subject to the approval of the General Manager, Toronto Water or designate; all other provisions of the Application for Filming at Water and Wastewater Facilities will remain applicable.
