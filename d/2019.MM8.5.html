The official record

What the item proposed

Milton Berger was a dedicated public servant to the people of North York and Toronto as an Alderman and Councillor between 1977 and 2000. He died on May 19, 2019 at the age of 94.

What was decided

City Council on June 18 and 19, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the City Manager to investigate the feasibility of naming a significant City park, facility or roadway in honour of the late former Councillor Milton Berger, including consultation with the Berger family, and bring a recommendation to City Council for approval at or by its November 26 and 27, 2019 meeting. 2. City Council acknowledge the municipal significance of the late former Councillor Milton Berger, as outlined in the Property Naming Policy.
