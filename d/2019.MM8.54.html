What the item proposed

In June 2015, the Truth and Reconciliation Commission of Canada released its Calls to Action report. The Truth and Reconciliation Commission mandate is to inform all Canadians about what happened in the Indian Residential Schools and to guide and inspire reconciliation and renewed relationships between Aboriginal peoples and Canadians. The Calls to Action are the Commission's recommendations to address the residential schools legacy and to advance the reconciliation process in Canada. We all have a responsibility to work towards reconciliation, and it is critical to support events taking place in our communities focused on building trust and reconciliation. The Mississaugas of the Credit First Nation Toronto Island Friendship Committee annually organizes a series of events and tours connecting the Mississaugas of Credit First Nation to the Toronto Islands and its residents. What was decided

City Council on June 18 and 19, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation to waive ferry passenger fees required by Chapter 441, Fees and Charges to those registered for two events organized by the Mississauga's of the Credit First Nation to be held on Toronto Island Park on Sunday, July 7, 2019 and Sunday, September 29, 2019.
