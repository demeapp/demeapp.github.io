Appointment of Public Members as Rooming House Licensing Commissioner and Deputy Commissioner

Adopted with changesJun 10, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The Nominating Panel – Rooming House Licensing Commissioner and Deputy Commissioner conducted interviews and recommend two candidates to City Council for appointment as the Rooming House Licensing Commissioner and Deputy Commissioner.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Nominating Panel – Rooming House Licensing Commissioner and Deputy Commissioner will conduct interviews and recommend two candidates to City Council for appointment as the Rooming House Licensing Commissioner and Deputy Commissioner.

What was decided

The Nominating Panel – Rooming House Licensing Commissioner and Deputy Commissioner recommends that: 1. City Council appoint the following candidate as the Rooming House Licensing Commissioner for a term of office ending on November 14, 2022, and until a successor is appointed: Alison Griggs 2. City Council appoint the following candidate as the Rooming House Licensing Deputy Commissioner for a term of office ending on November 14, 2022, and until a successor is appointed: Keith Cooper 3. City Council direct that Confidential Attachments 1, 2, and 3 to the report (May 31, 2019) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment as the Rooming House Licensing Commissioner and Deputy Commissioner.
