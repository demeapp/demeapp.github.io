The official record

What the item proposed

Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendments and Plan of Subdivision applications have been submitted to the City, proposing the redevelopment of the existing 3.56 hectare (8.8 acre) site at the southeast corner of Eglinton Avenue East and Laird Drive at 815-845 Eglinton Avenue East. Proposed are a mix of uses comprised of residential, commercial, and office with various heights of 2, 4, 10, 12, 18, 21, 36 and 38-storeys, a total of 1,673 residential units, a floor space index of 3.84, gross floor area of 138,830 square metres, a 2,100 square metre (1-storey) community space, and a 3,770 square metre public park. Also proposed are a private internal street and a new public street extension of Don Avon Drive.
