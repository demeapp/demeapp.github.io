The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting North York Community Council to approve the installation of additional parking machines on the west and north sides of Settlers Road, south of Sheppard Avenue East. A review by Transportation Services and Toronto Parking Authority staff concluded that the west and north sides of Settlers Road, extending beyond the existing pay-and-display machines, can accommodate a further 20 paid parking spaces.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west and north sides of Settlers Road, between a point 235 metres south and west of Sheppard Avenue East and a point 405 metres south and west of Sheppard Avenue East. 2. Amended the existing parking machine regulation in effect on the west side of Settlers Road, from a point 39.4 metres south of Sheppard Avenue East to a point 199 metres south of Sheppard Avenue East, from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday at a rate of $2.00 per hour and for a maximum period of 3 hours to be in effect from a point 40 metres south of Sheppard Avenue East to a point 199 metres south of Sheppard Avenue East. 3.
