The official record

What the item proposed

Bethesda Court is a small cul-de-sac located in close proximity to Rippleton Public School. During pick up and drop off hours, the residents of Bethesda Court have indicated that their street is often occupied by 14-16 vehicles at a time, which results in a significant amount of congestion. These vehicles often block driveways, hinder garbage pick-up, and prevent snow removal on Bethesda Court. All six of the households on Bethesda Court have signed a petition supporting stopping prohibitions on their street. Transportation staff have no objections to the proposed stopping prohibitions.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Prohibited stopping from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on both sides of Bethesda Court, between Rippleton Road and the west end of Bethesda Court.
