What the item proposed

This application proposes to develop a 24-unit, 3-storey (11.15 metre high) infill stacked rental townhouse building on an existing surface parking area fronting Brookdale Avenue in the southeast corner of the site at 3311 Bathurst Street. The existing 9-storey rental apartment building on Bathurst Street would be retained and the existing underground parking garage would be expanded to accommodate additional parking for both the existing apartment building and the proposed stacked townhouse development. The proposed residential infill development is compatible with its surrounding context. The proposal introduces an appropriate built form to the site with development massed to address Brookdale Avenue.
