Final Report - Zoning By-Law Amendment Application - 2535-2537 Bayview Avenue

AdoptedDec 17, 2019 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to amend City of Toronto Zoning By-law 569-2013 for the lands at 2535-2537 Bayview Avenue substantially in accordance with the draft Zoning By-law Amendment attached as Attachment No. 6 to this report.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes to amend the Zoning By-law to permit seven townhouse units fronting Bayview Avenue and two detached houses to the rear of the site fronting onto a shared private north-south driveway. The townhouses are separated into two blocks of three units on the north side and four units on the south side of the site. The townhouses are proposed to be three storeys and the detached houses would be two storeys in height. Vehicular access to the townhouse rear integral garages and the front integral garages of the detached houses is proposed from a shared private east-west driveway off Bayview Avenue. The proposed floor space index is 0.80 times the area of the lot and the proposed lot coverage is 45%.
