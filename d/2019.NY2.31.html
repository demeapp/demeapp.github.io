Toronto City Council · Decision

Councillor Pasternak recommends restoring a daytime parking ban on Bainbridge Avenue.

Official title: Parking Restriction on Bainbridge Avenue

AdoptedJan 15, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting held on July 4, 2018, North York Community Council rescinded the parking prohibition in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Bainbridge Avenue, from Bathurst Street to a point 133 metres west (NY32.52). Local residents thought the parking prohibition was too strict and requested that parking be permitted for the statutory maximum period of three hours on that portion of the street. However, since the changes were signed on-street, residents have requested that the 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, parking prohibition be reinstated on the north side of Bainbridge Avenue, between a point 113 metres west of Bathurst Street and a point 133 metres west of Bathurst Street.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Bainbridge Avenue, between a point 113 metres west of Bathurst Street and a point 133 metres west of Bathurst Street.
