Final Report - Zoning Amendment Application - 1202-1204 Avenue Road

PostponedApr 24, 2019 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to amend Zoning By-law 7652, for the lands at 1202 and 1204 Avenue Road substantially in accordance with the draft Zoning By-law Amendment attached as Attachment No. 6 to this report.

Ward 8 is named in the record. Council put off deciding this item.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes to amend the former City of North York Zoning By-law 7625 and the new City-wide 569-2013 Zoning By-law at 1202 and 1204 Avenue Road to permit a seven unit, three-storey townhouse development at the north-west corner of Avenue Road and Hillhurst Boulevard. Access to seven rear parking spaces is proposed via a driveway entering and exiting from Hillhurst Boulevard. The proposed development is consistent with the Provincial Policy Statement (2014) and conforms with the Growth Plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe (2017). This report reviews and recommends approval of the applications to amend the Official Plan and the Zoning By-law.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Adjourned its public meeting under the Planning Act until May 22, 2019, at 9:45 a.m.
