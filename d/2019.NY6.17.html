Parking Regulation Amendments and Left-turn Prohibition - Snowcrest Avenue

AdoptedMay 22, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Transportation Services asks North York Community Council to prohibit stopping on the west side of Snowcrest Avenue between Cummer Avenue and a point 90 metres further south.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting North York Community Council to prohibit stopping on the west side of Snowcrest Avenue between Cummer Avenue and a point 90 metres further south. In addition, we are recommending to prohibit eastbound left turns out of the driveway access to the Lester B. Pearson Elementary School parking lot on Snowcrest Avenue during school arrival and dismissal times.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Prohibited stopping, 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday (buses excepted), on the west side of Snowcrest Avenue between Cummer Avenue and a point 90 metres further south. 2. Prohibited eastbound left turns, from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and from 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 pm., Monday to Friday, out of the driveway access serving 7 Snowcrest Avenue, located on the east side of Snowcrest Avenue, at a point 70 metres south of Cummer Avenue.
