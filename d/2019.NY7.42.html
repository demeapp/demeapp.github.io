What the item proposed

This staff report is in regards to a matter for which the North York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to consider an application submitted by the homeowners of 2 Bond Avenue to erect a 2.5 meter high wooden fence with stucco finish in the front yard. The wood fence does not meet the requirements specified in City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447, Fences. The following chart indicates the violations and relevant sections of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447, Fences: The subject property 2 Bond Avenue is located in Ward 16, in a residential zone.
