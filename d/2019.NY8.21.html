City Council was asked to approve parking regulation changes on Botham Road.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Botham Road

AdoptedSep 16, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to amend the parking regulations on the west side of Botham Road, between Franklin Avenue and Stuart Avenue, to provide a short-term parking area. The proposed short-term parking area will assist pick-up/drop-off activity for St. Edward Catholic School located at No. 1 Botham Road. This designation will not adversely impact traffic operation on Botham Road.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 10 minutes, from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and from 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Botham Road, between Franklin Avenue and Stuart Avenue. 2. Prohibited parking from 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Botham Road, between Franklin Avenue and Stuart Avenue. 3. Rescinded the parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Botham Road, between Franklin Avenue and Stuart Avenue.
