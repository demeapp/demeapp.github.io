The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision provided it is in keeping with City policy or by-laws. The property at 2781 Yonge Street includes the property addresses from 2779 to 2781 Yonge Street and 15 to 21 Strathgowan Avenue. It will be a mixed use, 9-storey building with 90 residential dwelling units, 28 rental units, and 800 square metres of commercial floor space. Transportation Services is requesting authority to enter into an encroachment agreement with the owner of 2781 Yonge Street to install and maintain canopies and door swings on Yonge Street and Strathgowan Avenue, as well as a planter on the boulevard area of Strathgowan Avenue.
