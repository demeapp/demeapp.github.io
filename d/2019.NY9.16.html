The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about the naming of a new park at 1 Queen Magdalene Place, a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended to be inconsistent with City policy. The Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division is recommending that the new park be named "Clara Will Park". The Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division has reviewed the policy criteria in the Council-approved "City of Toronto Property Naming Policy" and verifies that the proposed park name meets the criteria.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Officially named the new park located at 1 Queen Magdalene Place, "Clara Will Park".
