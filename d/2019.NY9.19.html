What the item proposed

This staff report is in regards to a matter for which the North York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to consider an application submitted by the owner(s) of 31 Rykert Crescent for a site-specific Fence Exemption, pursuant to the Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447- Fences. The property owner(s) is seeking Council's permission to retain an existing solid wood fence and gates which are within 2.4 metres of a driveway, and be exempt from Chapter 447-1.2.C – Open fence construction required. The subject property 31 Rykert Crescent is located in Ward 15, on a residentially zoned property. GENERAL LOCATION SPECIFIC LOCATION EXISTING CONSTRUCTION & DEFICIENCY BY-LAW SECTION & REQUIREMENT Rear Yard Fence and gates along north and west side of property.
