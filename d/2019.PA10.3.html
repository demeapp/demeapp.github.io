The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to obtain instructions regarding claims submitted by Harbridge + Cross Ltd. to the Toronto Parking Authority relating to the construction project involving the addition of two levels to Municipal Car Park 1 located at 20 Charles Street East.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Adopted the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (November 8, 2019) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority. 2. Authorized the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (November 8, 2019) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, at the discretion of the President, Toronto Parking Authority, in consultation with the City Solicitor. 3. Directed that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Attachment 2 to the report (November 8, 2019) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, remain confidential in their entirely, as they pertain to potential litigation that affects the Toronto Parking Authority.
