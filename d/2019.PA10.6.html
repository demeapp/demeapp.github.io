The official record

What the item proposed

As we have discussed previously, the Toronto Parking Authority ("TPA") is being led by an Acting President and one of the key short-term strategic goals of the Board is to select a long-term President. It is proposed that the recruitment process for a long-term President be initiated, under delegated authority to the Chair. The selection panel is intended to be comprised of Brian Johnston (CEO of CreateTO), Allison Mendes (TPA Board member), and Arlene Yam Fritz (TPA VP Human Resources and Transformation). The search firm will be retained in accordance with TPA policies, resulting from a Request for Quotation already issued. A hiring recommendation will be made to the TPA Board.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Delegated to the Chair, Toronto Parking Authority Board, the recruitment process for the President of the Toronto Parking Authority, including: a. establishing a selection panel to consider candidates; b. working with the search firm retained by the Toronto Parking Authority through its Request For Quotation process; c. consulting with the City of Toronto's Director of Strategic Recruitment, Compensation and Employment Services during the selection and recruitment process; and d. bringing forward the final recommended candidate for the Board's consideration and decision. 2. Requested the Chair, Toronto Parking Authority Board, to update the Board on the recruitment process as needed.
