What the item proposed

As directed by the Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) Board, staff have conducted a review of parking passes issued to former and current Board members, City Councillors, and TPA retirees under the long-standing TPA policy. This review included an assessment of pass holder utilization and the rationale of issuing passes to these groups. A survey was also conducted across a spectrum of public sector transportation-focussed agencies to understand generally the practice of issuing passes to retirees. Based on staff's review, the following is noted: - The intent of providing a lifetime pass to retirees is to honour and reward their long-time commitment to the TPA. TPA policy provides lifetime passes upon retirement to those with a minimum of 20 years of service.
