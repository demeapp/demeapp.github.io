The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto Parking Authority (TPA or Licensor) Board of Directors, at its meeting of December 19, 2018, authorized staff to enter into a new license agreement (the License) with 1413652 Ontario Inc. operating as KA Car Care (the Licensee or KA Car Care) at Municipal Carpark 58, located at 9 Bedford Road (the Property or CP58), which is managed by TPA, generally in accordance with the terms and conditions as set out in Item PA1.3, as approved. This report seeks the acknowledgement and approval of the TPA Board of Directors to a change in the ownership entity of the Licensee to 2676417 Ontario Inc. In addition, the City of Toronto (the City) is the registered owner of the Property and, as such, will be a party to the License.
