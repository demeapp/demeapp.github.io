The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) is undertaking the construction of three new elevators at its Lansdowne Subway Station as part of their Easier Access Phase III Program, providing an additional exit access for customers. The TTC has approached the Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) and requested the TPA's permission to enter, occupy, and use portions of TPA land located at 695 Lansdowne Avenue (Municipal Car Park 81) ("Construction Premises"), as shown in Appendix 1, both temporarily during construction and permanently following the completion of construction. The project will result in the permanent loss of two parking stalls to accommodate the new infrastructure. In addition, the TTC will occupy different sections of the Subject Property on a temporary basis over the approximate three-year construction period. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized staff to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding, or other form of agreement as may be acceptable to the City Solicitor, with the Toronto Transit Commission to accommodate the construction of the elevators at the Lansdowne Subway Station located at 695 Lansdowne Avenue (Municipal Car Park 81), in accordance with the parameters outlined in the "Terms and Conditions" section of the report (March 8, 2019) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, and on such other or amended terms and conditions as may be acceptable to the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, and the City Solicitor.
