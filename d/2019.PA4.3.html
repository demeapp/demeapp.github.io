The Parking Authority board conveys condolences on the passing of former president Reginald W. Lewis.

Official title: Reginald W. Lewis, Major General (Retired), 1930-2019 - General Manager and President, Parking Authority of Toronto, from 1974-1990

AdoptedMar 22, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Current and past Toronto Parking Authority employees were deeply saddened to learn of the recent passing of one of our great leaders and a true gentleman, Mr. Reginald W. Lewis. Mr. Lewis served the organization, then known as the Parking Authority of Toronto, as General Manager and President from 1974 to 1990 with unparalleled distinction. He was a guiding force and continues to this day to be remembered as a role model exemplifying the highest standards in every way to which the organization aspires.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Conveyed, on behalf of all current and past employees of the Toronto Parking Authority, its deepest condolences on the passing of Reginald W. Lewis, General Manager and President, Parking Authority of Toronto, from 1974-1990, to Sheila, his wife of 59 years and Mr. Lewis' family. 2. Requested the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, to develop a fitting memorial tribute to permanently commemorate Reginald W. Lewis' stature in the history of the Toronto Parking Authority and the reverence in which he is held to this day by staff who worked under his leadership and guidance and those that knew of him.
