What the item proposed

Call Number: Request for Quotation Number RFQ2019-01 Description: The non-exclusive supply and delivery of pavement marking services for TPA's outdoor surface parking lots from the date of award for a term of two years, plus one optional two-year extension as required by the TPA. Should the option be exercised, then the Director of Operations will request the Vice President of Operations to process the renewals under the same terms and conditions. TPA's Finance and Operations groups developed the scope of work and documents for the RFQ call to include all technical requirements, material, and labour to provide pavement marking, including lines and painted symbols for all TPA and TTC outdoor lots. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Granted authority to award the contract for pavement marking on outdoor surface parking lots (2019-2022) to Diamond Line Painting, having submitted the lowest bid and meeting all specifications in conformance with the Request for Quotation requirements, for a term of two years, plus one optional two-year extension at the sole option of the Toronto Parking Authority, in the estimated amount (four years) of $338,253.50 plus $51,000 as a contingency and repair allowance, being the sum total amount of $389,253.50, plus annual inflation adjustment for the option years based on the Consumer Price Index in accordance with the contract provisions and net of all taxes and charges. 2.
