What the item proposed

Call Number: Request for Quotation Number RFQ2019-03 Description: The non-exclusive provision of landscaping maintenance services for TPA's outdoor surface parking lots and parking garage structures within the east end locations, from the date of award for a term of three years, plus two optional one-year extensions as required by the TPA. Should the option(s) be exercised, then the Director of Operations will request the Vice President of Operations to process the renewals under the same terms and conditions. TPA's Finance and Operations groups developed the scope of work and documents for the RFQ call to include all technical requirements, material, and labour to provide the landscaping maintenance and other landscaping services for TPA and TTC outdoor parking lots and garage sites in the east end of Toronto. The call documents were posted publicly on the MERX public tendering network…

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Granted authority to award the contract for the landscaping maintenance services for the east end (2019-2023) to Hank Deenen Landscaping Ltd., being the lowest bid and meeting all specifications in conformance with the Request for Quotation requirements, for a term of three years, plus two optional one-year extensions at the sole option of the Toronto Parking Authority, in the estimated amount (five years) of $547,792.56 plus $55,000 as a contingency allowance, being the sum total amount of $602,792.56, subject to annual inflation adjustment as applicable based on the Consumer Price Index in accordance with the contract provisions and net of all taxes and charges. 2.
