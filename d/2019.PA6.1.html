What the item proposed

On an annual basis, the Auditor General reviews the implementation status of outstanding audit recommendations and reports the review results to City Council through the City's Audit Committee. This report provides the implementation status of recommendations contained in the following four audit reports issued to the Toronto Parking Authority (TPA): - Toronto Parking Authority Phase 1: Audit of Real Estate Activities, 2015; - Toronto Parking Authority Phase 2: Audit of the Revenue Operations of Off-Street Controlled Facilities, 2016; - Auditor General's Observations of a Land Acquisition at Finch Avenue West and Arrow Road by the Toronto Parking Authority, 2016; and - Auditor General's Observations of a Land Acquisition at Finch Avenue West and Arrow Road by the Toronto Parking Authority - Part 2, 2017.
