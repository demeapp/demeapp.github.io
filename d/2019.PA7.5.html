What the item proposed

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) considered Item PA15.6, Toronto Parking Authority - Status Update of Security Services Assessment, at its October 24, 2018 meeting. The Item set out the preliminary results of an assessment of the TPA's security strategies, plans, and deployment conducted by the City of Toronto's Director of Corporate Security. The Board adopted a comprehensive framework comprising a range of recommendations and initial actions to further the goals of augmenting, enhancing, and updating the TPA's security program. Among other things, the Board directed that steps be initiated leading to the City's Corporate Security Division assuming direct management of the TPA's security program.
