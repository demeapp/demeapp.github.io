What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) has a need to acquire parking revenue control equipment, including software licensing and hardware to meet EuroPay/MasterCard/Visa (EMV) Interac and Payment Card Industry (PCI) compliance requirements as well as to acquire equipment needed for upcoming operational changes. TPA has been proceeding with the phased implementation of a multi-year program of upgrading the revenue control equipment in its controlled access (gated) parking facilities. The Board of Directors approved the initial plan in July of 2014, as revised in July of 2015. Overall, approximately two-thirds of the necessary upgrades are in place, including point-of-sale fee computers and lobby pay stations. What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized the expenditure of $1,473,587 plus contingency of $73,679 totalling $1,547,266 plus Harmonized Sales Tax to HUB Technologies for the acquisition of parking revenue control equipment, including associated software and support agreements to support modifications to operations and to meet Payment Card Industry compliance standards at 16 Municipal Car Parks, as outlined in Attachment 1 to the report (July 15, 2019) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority.
