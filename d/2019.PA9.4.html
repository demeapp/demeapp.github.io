The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to City Council's request to the Toronto Parking Authority Board to request the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to report to the Budget Committee on the following for consideration prior to the 2020 budget process: a. ridership changes at Bike Share stations directly adjacent to the Richmond/Adelaide and Bloor Street West bike lanes, from before implementation to implementation; and b. the feasibility and financial impact of extending Bike Share travel times from 30 minutes to 45 minutes for annual members.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Referred the report (September 6, 2019) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority, to the Budget Committee for consideration during the 2020 budget process.
