What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council state its intention to designate the heritage properties at 445, 447, 449, 451 and 453 Adelaide Street West under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act. City Council listed the properties on the City of Toronto's Heritage Register in 2017. The property at 445 Adelaide Street West contains a detached house form building dating to 1880, while the adjoining group of four-part row houses at 447-453 Adelaide Street West were completed in 1904. The properties at 445, 447, 449, 451 and 453 Adelaide Street West are identified as a contributing heritage properties in the King-Spadina Heritage Conservation District (HCD), which was adopted by City Council in October, 2017 and is currently under appeal.
