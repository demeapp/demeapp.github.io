Council was asked to give direction on the Harris-White House designation objection.

Official title: 33 Murray Avenue - Harris-White House - Request for Direction

AdoptedJan 21, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek further instruction in relation to an objection by the Estate of Vivien R. White to City Council's October 2, 2017 decision to state its intention to designate the property at 33 Murray Avenue under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act, which is currently before the Conservation Review Board (CRB).

What was decided

The Toronto Preservation Board recommends that: 1. City Council adopt the confidential recommendations contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (January 17, 2019) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the instructions in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Appendices A through C, if adopted by City Council, with the balance of the Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (January 17, 2019) from the City Solicitor to remain confidential as it contains advice which is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
