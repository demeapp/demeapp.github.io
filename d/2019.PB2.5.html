The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that Toronto City Council approve the proposed alterations to heritage properties located within the proposed development site at 363 Yonge Street, that Council include the properties at 367 Yonge Street and 381 Yonge Street on the City of Toronto's Heritage Register, that Council state its Intention to Designate the four properties included in the proposed development site (363-365 Yonge Street, 367 Yonge Street, 381 Yonge Street and 385-391 Yonge Street) under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act, and that Council grant authority to enter into a Heritage Easement Agreement for the subject properties. The Richard S. Williams Block at 363-365 Yonge was amongst some of the earliest properties recognized on the City of Toronto's Heritage Register (March 15, 1974).

What was decided
