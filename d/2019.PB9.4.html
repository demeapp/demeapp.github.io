Request for Directions regarding 10-16 Wellesley Street West, 5-7 St. Nicholas Street, and 586 Yonge Street

Adopted with changesSep 23, 2019 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to give the City Solicitor confidential direction on a development matter at Request for Directions regarding 10-16 Wellesley Street West, 5-7 St. Nicholas Street, and 586 Yonge Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report will request City Council's Direction with respect to the above-noted properties in a report forthcoming from the City Solicitor to the Toronto Preservation Board meeting of September 23, 2019. The properties are subject to a development application that is under appeal at the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal ("LPAT"). This report will be made available prior to the Toronto Preservation Board Meeting scheduled for September 23, 2019.

What was decided

The Toronto Preservation Board recommends that: 1. City Council adopt the confidential recommendations contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (September 20, 2019) from the City Solicitor, as amended by the confidential transmittal from the Toronto Preservation Board. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the instructions in Confidential Attachment 1, as amended and Confidential Appendices A through F, attached to the report (September 20, 2019) from the City Solicitor, if adopted by City Council, with the balance of the Confidential Attachment 1 to remain confidential as it contains advice which is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
