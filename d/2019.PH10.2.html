The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of a City-initiated amendment to Zoning By-law 569-2013, as amended, for a portion of the City-owned property at 777 Victoria Park Avenue, as part of the Housing Now Initiative. The recommended Zoning By-law Amendment (Attachment 4) will facilitate the development of approximately 508 new residential units on the site, including at least 254 affordable rental units. The proposed amendment will permit over 41,000 square metres of development, comprising a tall building at the north end of the site (up to 23 storeys) and a mid-rise building at the south end of the site (up to 11 storeys). The proposed development will include retail and community space at grade, including a child care facility, in addition to residential uses.
