What the item proposed

This report presents proposed inclusionary zoning policy directions for the purposes of public and stakeholder consultation and feedback. Inclusionary zoning (IZ) is a land-use planning tool that enables the City to require affordable housing units be included in new residential developments. On April 11, 2018 the Province enacted Ontario Regulation 232/18 under the Planning Act, 1990 to give municipalities the authority to implement inclusionary zoning. The Act and regulation require that municipalities have an assessment report prepared that will be considered as part of the development and prior to the adoption of Official Plan policies. The assessment report is required to include information related to housing need and demand and the financial impacts on the housing market from inclusionary zoning.
