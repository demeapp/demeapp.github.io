What the item proposed

This report responds to City Council's request, originating from a Member's Motion, that the General Manager, Shelter Support and Housing Administration report on potential measures and funding options to expand the housing allowance program, including the creation of a new upper tier of the Municipal Land Transfer Tax (MLTT). This report provides background on the City's housing allowance program, reviews the impact of expanding the program, and addresses funding options. Housing allowances are an important tool to help address homelessness. By increasing access to affordable housing and assisting people to move out of emergency shelters more quickly, housing allowances represent a proven pathway to stable housing.
