The official record

What the item proposed

In February 2019, City Council directed Toronto Community Housing Corporation (TCHC) to review the state of repairs and maintenance needed of the major electrical services in multi-residential, high-rise portfolio. This report provides an overview of the electrical safety measures undertaken by TCHC to ensure that electrical systems are well maintained and are operating as intended. To enhance the health and safety of tenants, programs and processes to maintain electrical systems and to mitigate fire and life safety hazards are a key feature of TCHC’s core work. These include preventative maintenance programs and proactive inspections and audits, all of which support capital planning and our ability to respond to emerging issues and tenant needs.
