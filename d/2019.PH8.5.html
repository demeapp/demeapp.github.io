What the item proposed

This report responds to Planning and Housing Committee's direction through Item PH5.7 to investigate the feasibility of creating a support centre to replace the functionality of the Province's Local Planning Appeal Support Centre (LPASC) for Committee of Adjustment and Toronto Local Appeal Body (TLAB) matters. Staff conducted this feasibility study by completing a jurisdictional scan, reviewing the Province's LPASC and the City's Mediation Pilot Program, and considering the role that City of Toronto officers and employees currently play in connection with TLAB and Local Planning Appeal Tribunal (LPAT) proceedings. It is important to note that the Province's LPASC was not mandated to provide advice or professional services related to Committee of Adjustment and TLAB matters.
