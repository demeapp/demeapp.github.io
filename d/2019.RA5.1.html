The official record

What the item proposed

On January 31, 2019, City Council approved a signature affordable housing initiative, "Housing Now," to leverage City-owned land for the purpose of creating new mixed-use, mixed-income, transit-oriented communities. Through the initiative, 11 properties were identified for development, which are expected to result in a total of approximately 10,000 new residential units. City Council set development targets for these properties of at least one-third affordable rental, one-third market rental, and no more than one-third ownership housing. The Housing Now initiative is coordinated through a steering committee comprised of senior staff from the Affordable Housing Office (AHO), City Planning and CreateTO. The first four properties deemed most ready for market offering this fall by the Steering Committee are: 140 Merton St., 777 Victoria Park Avenue, 705 Warden Avenue, and 50 Wilson Heights…
