What the item proposed

On January 31, 2019, City Council approved a signature affordable housing initiative, "Housing Now," to leverage City-owned land for the purpose of creating new mixed-use, mixed-income communities. Through the initiative, 11 properties were identified for development, with the potential to create a total of 10,000 new residential units. City Council set development targets for these properties of at least one-third affordable rental, one-third market rental, and no more than one-third ownership housing. The Housing Now initiative is coordinated through a Steering Committee comprised of senior staff from the Housing Secretariat, City Planning and CreateTO.
