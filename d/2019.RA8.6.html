The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with By-law 1-2018 to Govern the proceedings of the Board of Directors of CreateTO, regular meetings of the Board are determined by the Board and a schedule of those meetings shall be publicly available by the first meeting of the Board in each calendar year. City Council on July 16, 17 and 18, 2019 approved the 2020 schedule of meetings for City Council and its Committees. The proposed schedule of meetings for CreateTO has been developed to avoid dates, whenever possible, that conflict with the approved City Council schedule; and to avoid meetings on days of cultural or religious significance.
