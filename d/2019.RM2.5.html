What was decided

Various presentations, introductions and announcements were made to City Council on January 30 and 31, 2019. January 30, 2019 Speaker Nunziata, during the morning session of the meeting, acknowledged the City Clerk and her staff for their work on the new Anthem recording and video that started the meeting. Speaker Nunziata advised Council that the Anthem was performed by Singing Out, Toronto's largest LGBTQ plus mixed community chorus, composed of more than 140 members who reflect the diversity of the City. The video images and editing were provided by the City's in-house photo/video production unit of the City Clerk's Office. Mayor Tory, during the morning session of the meeting, welcomed the Toronto Paramedic Services team who won the International Trauma and Life Support Competition to the Chamber.
