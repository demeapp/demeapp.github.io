The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek Board approval on the negotiated terms of the collective agreement with Unifor 2003E.

What was decided

The Human Resources, Compensation and Labour Relations Committee recommends that the Board of Directors of TO Live: 1. Approve the negotiated terms of the collective agreement with Unifor 2003E, as outlined in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (May 24, 2019) from the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live. 2. Direct that the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (May 24, 2019) from the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live remain confidential in its entirety, as it is about labour relations or employee negotiations.
