What the item proposed

At its meeting on May 9, 2019, the Board of Directors of TO Live referred item CT3.1 to the Audit Committee with the request that the Audit Committee review the proposal for a hospitality organization to occupy space within the Sony Centre in CT3.1a and make recommendations to the June 13, 2019 Board meeting.
