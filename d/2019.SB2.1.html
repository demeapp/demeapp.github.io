The official record

What the item proposed

This report addresses an appeal concerning one variance sought respecting a proposal for one non-illuminated first party roof sign displaying static copy (the "Proposed Sign"). The Proposed Sign is to be located at the north-easterly corner of the roof, cantilevered over a newer two-storey addition at an existing brewery located at 164 Evans Avenue. The Proposed Sign has two sign faces in a "v-shaped" configuration, directed towards traffic on the Gardiner Expressway. Each of the two sign faces measure 4.35 metres horizontally by 5.1 metres vertically, and will function to identify the brewery located at the subject property. The height will not exceed 15.4 metres. Cool Brewery, through their agent Bousfields Inc., was the initial applicant. What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Granted the requested variance, subject to the specified conditions, to §694-26I required for the Signage Master Plan consisting of the Proposed Sign at the premises municipally known as 164 Evans Avenue, as described in Attachment 1 to the report (March 25, 2019) from the Supervisor, Tax, Variance & Permits, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building.
