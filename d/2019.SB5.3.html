Application by Outfront Media for One Third Party Electronic Ground Sign at 400 Eastern Avenue

AdoptedNov 28, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The Sign Variance Committee was asked to refuse the requested variances, subject to the specified condition(s), required to allow the Applicant to obtain a permit for the erection and display of one third party electronic ground sign at 400 Eastern Avenue. This report reviews a proposal by Outfront Media, on behalf of the property owner (the "Applicant"), to seek four variances, each subject to specified condition(s), required to allow for one third party electronic ground sign displaying electronic static copy (the "Proposed Sign"), as described in further detail in Attachment 1.

What the item proposed

This report reviews a proposal by Outfront Media, on behalf of the property owner (the "Applicant"), to seek four variances, each subject to specified condition(s), required to allow for one third party electronic ground sign displaying electronic static copy (the "Proposed Sign"), as described in further detail in Attachment 1. The Proposed Sign would be located along the south frontage of the property municipally known as 400 Eastern Avenue (the "Subject Premises"), which contains a two storey building intended for office and retail use. The Proposed Sign would have a height of 7.33 metres from grade, and contain two sign faces in a v-shape configuration, with a sign face area of 19.99 square metres on each side. The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Refused to grant the four requested variances to sections 694-22E, 694-25C(2)(e), 694-25C(2)(f), and 694-25C(2)(k) each subject to the specified condition, required to allow the Applicant to obtain a permit for the erection and display of one third party electronic ground sign at 400 Eastern Avenue, as described in Attachment 1 of the report (November 14, 2019) from the Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building.
