All-Way Stop Control Review - Havendale Road and Scotland Road/Roker Crescent

Adopted with changesNov 5, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council was asked to not authorize all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Havendale Road and Scotland Road/Roker Crescent.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report recommends that the Community Council not authorize the installation of an all-way stop control at the intersection of Havendale Road and Scotland Road/Roker Crescent as the existing conditions do not meet the warrant for the installation of such a traffic control device. This intersection currently has a stop control for the northbound movement on Roker Crescent and the southbound movement on Scotland Road at Havendale Road.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Havendale Road and Scotland Road/Roker Crescent.
