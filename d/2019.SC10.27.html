U-Turn Prohibition - Sedgemount Drive, between Pandora Circle and Daventry Road

AdoptedNov 5, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council was asked to prohibit eastbound and westbound U-turn movements at all times on Sedgemount Drive, between Pandora Circle and Daventry Road.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report recommends a U-turn prohibition on Sedgemount Drive between Pandora Circle and Daventry Road. This prohibition will reduce conflicts between all users and improve safety along this section of Sedgemount Drive which is a local road in the vicinity of Tredway Woodsworth Public School.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Prohibited eastbound and westbound U-turn movements at all times on Sedgemount Drive, between Pandora Circle and Daventry Road.
