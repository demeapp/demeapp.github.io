School Zone Review - Bernadine Street

AdoptedDec 3, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council was asked to rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect from 9:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m., Monday to Friday on both sides of Bernadine Street, between Packard Boulevard and a point 100 metres west.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision. This report recommends prohibiting U-turns on Bernadine Street and to revise parking, stopping, and school bus loading zone regulations on Bernadine Street in the vicinity of St. Victor Catholic School. These changes are required due to the recent implementation of an all-way stop control at the intersection of Bernadine Street and Doerr Road.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 9:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m., Monday to Friday on both sides of Bernadine Street, between Packard Boulevard and a point 100 metres west. 2. Rescinded the existing stopping prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday except July, August and Public Holidays on the south side of Bernadine Street, between Brimley Road and a point 204 metres east. 3. Rescinded the existing maximum 30 minute parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday on the north side of Bernadine Street, between Brimley Road and a point 107.6 metres east. 4.
