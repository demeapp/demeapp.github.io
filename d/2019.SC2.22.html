The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report recommends the installation of revised relocations for parking, standing, stopping and school bus loading zone regulations on Southlawn Drive in the vicinity of the Lynnwood Heights Junior Public School. These changes are recommended due to the approval of an unwarranted All-Way Stop Control that was recently installed at the intersection of Southlawn Drive and Belgreen Avenue.

Rescinded the existing School Bus Loading Zone designation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., on the north side of Southlawn Drive, between a point 86 metres east of Buena Vista Avenue and a point 62 metres further east. 5.
