Councillor Karygiannis asks for a report on no-stopping rules for three Scarborough streets.

Official title: Parking Restrictions on Bonis Avenue, Bamburgh Circle. and Redlea Avenue.

AdoptedJan 15, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Require staff to report back to the March 2019 Scarborough Community Council meeting with a report on no-stopping prohibitions on Bonis Avenue, Bamburgh Circle. and Redlea Avenue.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Directed the Acting Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, Scarborough District to report to the March 19, 2019 Scarborough Community Council meeting with a report on: a. Prohibiting stopping at all times on the south side of Bonis Avenue, between Kennedy Road and a point 475 metres west; b. Rescinding the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Bonis Avenue, between Kennedy Road and a point 475 metres west; c. Prohibiting stopping at all times on the south side of Bamburgh Circle, between Warden Avenue (north intersection) to Wintermute Boulevard; d. Rescinding the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Bamburgh Circle, between Warden Avenue (north intersection) and Wintermute Boulevard; and, e. Prohibiting stopping at all times on both sides of Redlea Avenue,…
