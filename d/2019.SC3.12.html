Staff recommend removing an unused school bus loading zone on Brimley Road.

Official title: School Bus Loading Zone - 1965 Brimley Road

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends removing the existing school bus loading zone on the east side of Brimley Road, fronting C.D. Farquharson Junior Public School. The school no longer requires the school bus loading zone. Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) will be installing a new bus stop in the area of the existing school bus loading zone.

What was decided

City Council on February 26, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council rescind the existing school bus loading zone in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., on the east side of Brimley Road, between a point 167 metres north of Pitfield Road and a point 21 metres further north.
