The official record

What the item proposed

Please accept this letter as my request to add a new item of business to the February 14th, 2019 Scarborough Community Council agenda, entitled: Updated Ridership Projection along Sheppard Ave East Corridor. I would like to move the motion below that would update ridership numbers along the Sheppard Ave East Corridor. As you may know, there is significant development planned for this area to be built in the near future, almost over 70 towers containing upwards of 18 thousand new residential units. The motion is to request updated ridership projections to get a better understanding of whether the imminent intensification may require a higher order of transit to service this area.

What was decided

City Council on February 26, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the City Manager, in consultation with Toronto Transit Commission staff, the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning and Metrolinx, to report back in the third quarter of 2019 to the Executive Committee with updated ridership projections along the proposed Sheppard Avenue East Corridor taking into account the intensification coming along the Sheppard corridor and surrounding corridors in the next 2 to 4 years.
