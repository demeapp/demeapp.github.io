The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report recommends the installation of no stopping prohibitions for a restricted period of time and days on both sides of Pachino Boulevard in the vicinity of Terraview-Willowfield P.S. driveway. Currently, there is a default three hour parking on both sides of Pachino Boulevard.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Prohibited stopping from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, except public holidays, from September 1 of one year to June 30 of the next following year, inclusive, on both sides of Pachino Boulevard, between Crosland Drive and the east end of Pachino Boulevard.
