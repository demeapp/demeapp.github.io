The official record

What the item proposed

Seeking endorsement of an event for liquor licensing purposes.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Under its delegated authority under Chapter 27, Council Procedures, declared the following to be an event of municipal significance for liquor licensing purposes: a. the "5th Annual Scarborough (Toronto) Community Multicultural Festival" event to be held on Friday, August 30, 2019 from 12:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m.; Saturday, August 31, 2019 from 10:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.; and Sunday, September 1, 2019 from 10:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. at Confederation Park, 250 Don Varden Boulevard.
