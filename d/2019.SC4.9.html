Staff recommend a community consultation meeting on a zoning by-law amendment for 2075 Kennedy Road, 26 and 50 Village Green Square.

Official title: 2075 Kennedy Road, 26 and 50 Village Green Square - Zoning By-law Amendment Application - Preliminary Report

AdoptedMar 19, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information and identifies a preliminary set of issues regarding the application located at 2075 Kennedy Road, 26 and 50 Village Green Square. Staff are currently reviewing the application. It has been circulated to all appropriate agencies and City divisions for comment. Staff will proceed to schedule a community consultation meeting for the application with the Ward Councillor.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council directed that: 1. Staff schedule a community consultation meeting for the application located at 2075 Kennedy Road, 26 and 50 Village Green Square together with the Ward Councillor. 2. Notice for the community consultation meeting be given to landowners and residents within 120 metres of the application site, and to additional residents, institutions and owners to be determined in consultation with the Ward Councillor, with any additional mailing costs to be borne by the applicant.
