Introduction of Overnight On-Street Permit Parking - Pringdale Gardens Circle

AdoptedApr 24, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Transportation Services asks approval from Scarborough Community Council to introduce overnight on-street permit parking on Pringdale Gardens Circle, east of Danforth Road, on a street name basis, under the operating hours of 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., seven days a week.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting approval from Scarborough Community Council to introduce overnight on-street permit parking on Pringdale Gardens Circle, east of Danforth Road, on a street name basis, under the operating hours of 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., seven days a week. Area residents requested the implementation of overnight on-street permit parking on Pringdale Gardens Circle to accommodate their long term parking needs without being subject to tagging by Parking Enforcement Officers.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking except by permit from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily, on a street-name basis, on the south side of the northerly east-west leg of Pringdale Gardens Circle, from Danforth Road to the east side of the easterly north-south leg of Pringdale Gardens Circle, and on the south side of the southerly east-west leg of Pringdale Gardens Circle, from the west side of the westerly north-south leg of Pringdale Gardens Circle to the east side of the easterly north-south leg of Pringdale Gardens Circle. 2.
