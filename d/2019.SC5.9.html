Stopping Prohibition - Kingston Road at Harewood Avenue

AdoptedMay 14, 2019 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to rescind the existing stopping prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Kingston Road, between a point 21 metres west of Harewood Avenue and a point 36 metres west of Harewood Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

An operational review conducted in 2015 at the intersection of Kingston Road and Harewood Avenue, recommended that parking be prohibited on Kingston Road at the southwest corner of the above-noted intersection by removing one parking spot from the parking lay-by. It was recently identified by Transportation staff that the distance of the parking prohibition was excessive and resulted in the loss of four on-street parking spaces. To prevent the loss of four on-street parking spaces, staff recommends changing the existing stopping prohibition at all times on the south side of Kingston Road between Harewood Avenue and a point 36 metres west to 21 metres west.

What was decided

City Council on May 14 and 15, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council rescind the existing stopping prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Kingston Road, between a point 21 metres west of Harewood Avenue and a point 36 metres west of Harewood Avenue. 2. City Council authorize maximum one-hour parking at all times on the south side of Kingston Road, between a point 21 metres west of Harewood Avenue and a point 36 metres west of Harewood Avenue.
