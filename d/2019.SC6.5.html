Standing Prohibitions - Thatcher Avenue, Larwood Boulevard, St. Quentin Avenue, Gradwell Drive, Redland Crescent East and West

AdoptedMay 22, 2019 · Toronto City Council

This report recommends prohibiting standing from 9:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., Saturday, Sunday, and public holidays from May 1 to September 30 of each year, on one side of residential streets in the Bluffers Park neighbourhood, namely: Thatcher Avenue, Larwood Boulevard, St. Quentin Avenue, Gradwell Drive, Barkdene Hills, Redland Crescent East and Redland Crescent West.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report recommends prohibiting standing from 9:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., Saturday, Sunday, and public holidays from May 1 to September 30 of each year, on one side of residential streets in the Bluffers Park neighbourhood, namely: Thatcher Avenue, Larwood Boulevard, St. Quentin Avenue, Gradwell Drive, Barkdene Hills, Redland Crescent East and Redland Crescent West.
