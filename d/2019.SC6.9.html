The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision. Transportation staff have reviewed the need for traffic calming on Vradenberg Drive, between Lowcrest Boulevard and Wishing Well Drive and on Lowcrest Boulevard, between Warden Avenue and Cloverleaf Gate. The request is as a result of the Councillor and residents' concerns regarding the speed of vehicles. Our assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the Traffic Calming Policy has not been satisfied on either street. Therefore, traffic calming should not be installed on Vradenberg Drive or Lowcrest Boulevard.
