The Community Council was asked to designate fire routes at 25 Halbert Place.

Official title: Designation of Fire Routes and amendment to Chapter 880 - Fire Route - 25 Halbert Place

AdoptedJun 25, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

To obtain Scarborough Community Council approval for the amendment of the Fire Route By-law to amend the designation of a certain portion of the property municipally known as 25 Halbert Place as a fire route within the meaning of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 880, as amended. The fire route in question was applied for and designated under its previous address: 31 McCowan Road (Halbert Jr. Public School). This municipal address is no longer in use. The driveway that serviced this school was adopted as a street and the school's municipal address changed to 25 Halbert Place. As a result, the address listed in Schedule A, Fire Route Designations to Chapter 880 requires correcting. Fire Services uses designated fire routes as a key mechanism in regulating fire prevention, including the prevention of spreading of fires and the delivery of fire protection services.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Amended Municipal Code Chapter 880, Fire Routes, to amend the address of the private roadway located at the property currently listed in Schedule A, Fire Route Designations of Chapter 880, Fire Routes as 31 McCowan Road to 25 Halbert Place. 2. Authorized the Fire Chief and City Solicitor to take the appropriate action to make a designated Fire Route.
