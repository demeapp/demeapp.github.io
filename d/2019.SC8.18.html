The official record

What the item proposed

East Avenue is a collector road with sidewalk on the west side and partial sidewalk on the east side. The speed limit is 50 km/hr with a reduction to 40 km/hr in a school zone. The road connects two collector roads: Island Road and Lawrence Ave East. Currently, there are no-all-way-stop controls on East Avenue between Lawrence Ave East and Kirkdene Drive. These intersections are located 1.7 kilometers apart. My office has been contacted by local residents and the West Rouge Community Association who are requesting an all-way-stop control at East Avenue and Fanfare Drive. The grassroots community association, which represents approximately 3,400 households, is concerned about excessive speed on East Avenue. What was decided

Scarborough Community Council directed that: 1. Transportation Services undertake a traffic study on East Avenue, between Island Road and Lawrence Avenue East, determine if any stop signs or other traffic management devices are warranted to improve traffic flow and safety for both vehicles and pedestrians, and report back to the June 9, 2020 meeting of the Scarborough Community Council.
