What the item proposed

The application for a Zoning By-law Amendment proposes to amend the former City of Scarborough Birchcliff Community Zoning By-law No. 8786 and City of Toronto Zoning By-law No. 569-2013 to permit the construction of an 11 storey building at 1478-1496 Kingston Road. The proposed redevelopment would consist of 198 residential dwelling units and include 486.9 square metres of commercial ground floor area on the first floor. The proposal would result in a Floor Space Index (FSI) of 6.93 times the lot area. The proposal is consistent with the Provincial Policy Statement (2014) and conforms with the Growth Plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe (2019).
