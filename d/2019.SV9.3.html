What was decided

The Property Standards Committee, Etobicoke York Panel: 1. Confirmed the Order to Comply, dated September 25, 2019, for 2500 Keele Street, and in doing so granted an extension of time to December 2021. 2. Directed that the retaining wall be secured with emergency fencing, in consultation with Municipal Licensing and Standards staff, within 60 days.
