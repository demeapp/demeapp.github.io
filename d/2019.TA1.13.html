The official record

What the item proposed

A Direct Investment of up to $1,150,000 is recommended. The proposed investment will not be advanced unless it meets risk/reward and greenhouse gas (GHG) impact expectations, and appropriate terms, including security and other conditions, are approved by the Investment Committee.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Rescinded its approval of the confidential instructions in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (January 26, 2018) from the Vice President, Impact Investing (2018.TA19.7). 2. Approved an investment of up to $1,150,000 in subordinated debt as set out in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (February 8, 2019) from the Vice President, Impact Investing, to be advanced once final terms, amount, structure, conditions and required due diligence outlined by the Investment Committee are met, and upon approval of the final Binding Offer to Finance by the designated Investment Committee sub-committee. 3.
