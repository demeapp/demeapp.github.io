The official record

What the item proposed

Remote participation in Toronto Atmospheric Fund (TAF) Board meetings through electronic or telecommunications means has been approved by the TAF Board and will be supported and implemented by the City Clerk's office using the City of Toronto's current web conferencing application and protocols. Operational Guidelines have been developed to ensure remote participation is aligned with governance requirements and desired group dynamics for decision-making.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Approved the Operational Guidelines for Remote Participation in Toronto Atmospheric Fund Board meetings, as amended in the following, in Attachment 1 to the report (February 7, 2019) from the Chief Executive Officer. a. That Clause 13a of Operational Guidelines be revised to read as follows: "13. PROCEDURE FOR REMOTE PARTICIPATION a. A member of the Board who intends to participate remotely is requested to notify the Board Chair, City Clerk's office, and TAF administrator within 24 hours of the agenda being published. b. That the Operational Guidelines include the following: "Video conferencing is preferred method for remote participants." c.
