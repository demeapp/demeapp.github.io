Toronto City Council · Decision

Appointments to Business Improvement Area Boards of Management

AdoptedNov 5, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The purpose of this report is to appoint directors to the Forest Hill Village, Ossington Avenue, St. Clair Gardens, and Trinity Bellwoods BIA boards of management and remove directors from the Forest Hill Village, Ossington Avenue, and Regal Heights Village BIA boards of management.

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to appoint directors to the Forest Hill Village, Ossington Avenue, St. Clair Gardens, and Trinity Bellwoods BIA boards of management and remove directors from the Forest Hill Village, Ossington Avenue, and Regal Heights Village BIA boards of management.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. In accordance with the City's Public Appointments Policy, appointed the following nominees to the Business Improvement Area (BIA) boards of management set out below at the pleasure of Toronto and East York Community Council, and for a term expiring at the end of the term of Council or as soon thereafter as successors are appointed: Forest Hill Village: Singh, Gillian Ossington Avenue: Chalmers, Scott St. Clair Gardens: Tavora, Carlos Trinity Bellwoods: Reynolds, Andrea 2. Removed the following directors from the Business Improvement Area (BIA) boards of management set out below: Forest Hill Village: Kassam, Farhan Zhou, Hang Ossington Avenue: Smith, Kate Regal Heights Village: Tran, Annie
