The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has assessed an appeal for Commercial Boulevard Parking (CBP) from the owner of 170 Bedford Road. Commercial boulevard parking at this location is not recommended because on-street metered parking is in effect along the street fronting the proposed parking stalls. The regulations of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 743 do not permit CBP due to the installation of ramp to access the parking stalls will be contrary to municipal purpose due to loss of revenue from Toronto Parking Authority (TPA). The owner will be given an opportunity to make a deputation before Community Council.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council referred the item to Transportation Services staff to report back to the December 3, 2019 Toronto and East York Community Council meeting on: 1. a detailed breakdown on the recommended fee associated with these spots; and 2. the feasibility of installing a two car loading zone on Bedford Road at this location.
