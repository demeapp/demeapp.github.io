What the item proposed

This report concerns a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting the Toronto and East York Community Council to extend existing approval of the temporary closure to vehicular traffic of a portion of the westerly southbound curb lane of Duncan Street, south of Richmond Street West until April 14, 2023 to facilitate the proposed extended operations of a curb lane parklet café at The Fifth Pub House, Café and Market located at 225 Richmond Street West. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the temporary closure to vehicular traffic of the westerly southbound curb lane of Duncan Street between a point 4.2 metres south of Richmond Street West and a point 27.1 metres further south from January 1, 2020 to April 14, 2023, inclusive, for the operation of a parklet cafe. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Duncan Street, between Richmond Street West and a point 31.3 metres south. 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the west side of Duncan Street, between a point 4.2 metres south of Richmond Street West and a point 27.1 metres further south. 4. Rescinded the 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and 6:00 p.m. to 12:00 midnight Monday to Saturday and 1:00 p.m. to 12:00 midnight Sunday parking machine regulation on the west side of Duncan Street, between Adelaide Street West and…
