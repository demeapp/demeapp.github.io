Extending Approval for Year-Round Operation of Parklets and Parklet Cafés on King Street and Duncan Street - Non-Delegated

AdoptedNov 26, 2019 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to authorize the continuation from January 1 to April 14, and from November 15 to December 31 each year until April 14, 2023, all dates inclusive, of outdoor parklet cafés, parklets and public installations and the temporary closure, to vehicular traffic. This report requests Council approval to permit cafés and public space installations to continually remain on a portion of King Street from Bathurst Street to Jarvis Street, as shown in Attachment 1, and on a portion of Duncan Street south of Richmond Street West, as shown in Attachment 2, until April 14, 2023, subject to annual permitting.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

This report requests Council approval to permit cafés and public space installations to continually remain on a portion of King Street from Bathurst Street to Jarvis Street, as shown in Attachment 1, and on a portion of Duncan Street south of Richmond Street West, as shown in Attachment 2, until April 14, 2023, subject to annual permitting. Under existing authorities, the curb lanes occupied by cafés and parklets are closed to vehicular traffic until December 31, 2019 on Duncan Street and until April 14, 2020 on King Street. This includes seeking authority to extend the temporary closure to vehicular traffic of the portions of King Street until April 14, 2023.
