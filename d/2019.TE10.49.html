The official record

What the item proposed

To the Chair and Members of Toronto East York Community Council: I am asking for your support on two issues that have a very real impact on residents in Beaches-East York every day: parking pad permissions and the need to access electric vehicle charging infrastructure on residential streets. As you know, the City’s parking pad bylaw contains varying standards and requirements within the Toronto-East York Community Council boundary. Currently the rules surrounding parking pad permissions in Ward 19 are complicated and confusing for residents. We also know that we have a real challenge with building out infrastructure for electric vehicle charging, particularly for residents relying on on-street parking. The recommendations before you are meant to help us make meaningful progress on improving parking pad rules and building out EV infrastructure. First, they make the parking pad rules…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Referred the item and all amendments considered by the Community Council, to the General Manager, Transportation Services, in consultation with City Planning; Toronto Water; Office of Environment and Energy; and Urban Forestry, and requested a report back to the February 5, 2020 Toronto and East York Community Council meeting on the following: a. right-of-way management; b. capacity of Toronto's electricity system; c. tree canopy; d. stormwater management; e. urban design; f. pedestrian safety; g. staff resources required to monitor the use of parking pads to ensure that they are only used by an electric vehicle.
