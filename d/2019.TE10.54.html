What the item proposed

At the Waterfront Neighbourhood Centre Annual General Meeting held on October 23, 2018, the membership nominated the individuals listed above for appointment by Toronto and East York Community Council to the Board of Management of the Waterfront Neighbourhood Centre. The Board of Management struck a Nominations committee early in 2018 to fill upcoming vacancies on the WNC Board. The Nominations Committee among other duties reviewed the role, responsibilities and required skills of a Board member and for those who have served prior, reviewed their record of attendance, diligence and oversight. The committee also identified additional desirable skills, particularly those being: business and finance and/or fundraising and resource development, with final recommendations presented to the Membership for election at the Annual General Meeting. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1.
