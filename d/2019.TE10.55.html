The official record

What the item proposed

As our downtown communities grow and become more dense with residents, jobs, and visitors, it is increasingly important that we are making the most of our existing parks and public spaces. Downtown already has a parkland provision 67% below the City-wide average, and the residential population is projected to more than double by 2041. The Council-approved Downtown Parks and Public Realm Plan includes four objectives to support liveable and healthy downtown communities: acquire and expand; improve; share; and connect. Victoria Memorial Square Park is a heavily-used public park near the intersection of Wellington Street West and Bathurst Street, which has at its centre the earliest known cemetery associated with Fort York and the Town of York. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Public Realm Section, Transportation Services, to report back to the Toronto and East York Community Council on: a. the temporary closure, to vehicular traffic, of the southerly curb lane of Wellington Street West, west of Portland Street and adjacent to Victoria Memorial Square Park for near-term public realm and pedestrian improvements; and b. the permanent closure of the southerly curb lane of Wellington Street West, west of Portland Street, for the purpose of enlarging Victoria Memorial Square Park.
