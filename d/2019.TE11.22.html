Assigning the Ceremonial Name "Karen Kain Way" to Part of Queen Street West

AdoptedDec 3, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The purpose of this report is to recommend that the ceremonial name of "Karen Kain Way" be assigned to Queen Street West between University Avenue and York Street.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend that the ceremonial name of "Karen Kain Way" be assigned to Queen Street West between University Avenue and York Street. A ceremonial street dedication does not change the official street name or address information. It does result in the installation of special signage along the selected street. Transportation Services was consulted during the preparation of this report.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the ceremonial name "Karen Kain Way" to Queen Street West between University Avenue and York Street. 2. Directed Transportation Services to erect signage marking "Karen Kain Way".
