Toronto City Council · Decision

Parking Amendments - Pape Avenue

AdoptedDec 17, 2019 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to prohibit stopping at all times on the east side of Pape Avenue, between a point 15.7 metres north of Strathcona Avenue and a point 13.8 metres south of Strathcona Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Pape Avenue, City Council approval of this report is required. At the request of Councillor Paula Fletcher's office, Transportation Services is recommending that stopping be prohibited on the east side of Pape Avenue, between a point 15.7 metres north of Strathcona Avenue and a point 13.8 metres south of Strathcona Avenue, in order to ensure sightlines are not obstructed by accessible parking permit holders.

What was decided

City Council on December 17 and 18, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit stopping at all times on the east side of Pape Avenue, between a point 15.7 metres north of Strathcona Avenue and a point 13.8 metres south of Strathcona Avenue. 2. City Council amend the existing community safety zone on Pape Avenue, between Withrow Avenue and Poucher Street, to be in effect between Withrow Avenue and a point 62.7 metres south of Langley Avenue.
