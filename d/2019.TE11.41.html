Vehicle Width Restriction - First East-West Public Lane, North of King Street East and West of Bright Street

Adopted with changesDec 3, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to prohibit vehicles over two metres in width from travelling the first east-west lane, north of King Street East and west of Bright Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is recommending that vehicles greater than two metres in width be prohibited from travelling the first east-west public lane, north of King Street East and west of Bright Street. Prohibiting vehicles over two metres in width from travelling the subject lane would be beneficial in reducing the incidence of damage to adjacent private property.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited vehicles over two metres in width from travelling the first east-west lane, north of King Street East and west of Bright Street. 2. Approved the installation of additional bollards on the North and South sides of the Bright Street entrance to the laneway to prevent further damage to existing properties.
