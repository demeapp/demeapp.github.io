Toronto City Council · Decision

Permanent Closure of a Portion of the Public Lane extending between 1789 and 1791 St. Clair Avenue West

AdoptedDec 17, 2019 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to authorize the permanent closure of the portion of the public lane abutting 1789 and 1791 St. Clair Avenue West designated as Parts 7, 8 and 9 on Reference Plan 66R-30869, and also shown as Part 3 on Sketch No. PS-2019-006 (the "Lane"), attached to the report dated November 25, 2019. Transportation Services recommends that the portion of the public lane abutting 1789 and 1791 St. Clair Avenue West (the "Lane") be permanently closed for incorporation into the adjacent development site.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services recommends that the portion of the public lane abutting 1789 and 1791 St. Clair Avenue West (the "Lane") be permanently closed for incorporation into the adjacent development site. Pursuant to a land transfer agreement, a new public lane will be constructed in the east-west direction outlet to Ford Street and will be transferred to the City and subsequently dedicated as a public highway.

What was decided

City Council on December 17 and 18, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the permanent closure of the portion of the public lane abutting 1789 and 1791 St. Clair Avenue West designated as Parts 7, 8 and 9 on Reference Plan 66R-30869, and also shown as Part 3 on Sketch No. PS-2019-006 (the "Lane"), attached to the report (November 25, 2019) from the Director, Transportation Planning and Capital Program, Transportation Services. 2. City Council enact a By-law substantially in the form of the draft By-law attached as Appendix A to the report (November 25, 2019) from the Director, Transportation Planning and Capital Program, Transportation Services.
