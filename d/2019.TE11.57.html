Toronto City Council · Decision

Parking Amendment - Dovercourt Road

AdoptedDec 17, 2019 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to amend the existing maximum 15-minute parking regulation in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In November 2019, the Bloorcourt Village Early Learning Centre, located at 823 Dovercourt Road, contacted my office to report that the current pick-up/drop off zone, which operates from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, in front of the centre can no longer accommodate their needs. According to the Centre, parents often park in the adjacent 'no standing' zones when they pick-up/drop-off their children. As a result, parents are often ticketed. The purpose of this motion is to extend the period of the pick-up/drop off zone from 7:00 a.m. 10:00 a.m.

What was decided

City Council on December 17 and 18, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend the existing maximum 15-minute parking regulation in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Dovercourt Road, between a point 87 metres north of Northumberland Street and a point 13 metres further north, to be in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday.
