The official record

What the item proposed

A growing concern for residents in the Garden District has been the impact of new development on available permit parking spaces within the 6G area. New condominiums are approved under the assumption that sufficient parking will be available on-site. However, the city has received and granted parking permits to residents in newly constructed buildings within the 6G area that were not specifically omitted. Permit parking is meant primarily to allow residents who live in residential areas, where driveways and/or garages are not common, the ability to park their vehicle near their home. While underground parking is expensive to build, developers should be expected to provide enough capacity on site without impacting parking in the neighbourhood. The 6G area has 100 parking spots. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council directed: 1. Transportation Services to examine and to report back to the February 5, 2010 Toronto and East York Community Council meeting regarding the realignment of Permit Parking Area "6G" and the exclusion of the subject site, at 203 Jarvis Street, from the permit parking area.
