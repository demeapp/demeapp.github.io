Parking Amendments - Gladstone Avenue and Shanly Street

AdoptedDec 3, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing 15-minute maximum parking regulation in effect from 8:15 a.m. to 8:45 a.m. and 3:15 p.m. to 3:45 p.m.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Parents and teachers from St. Anthony's Catholic School reached out to my office and informed me that the hours at their school recently changed. In addition, they asked that there be space allocated within the existing school pick-up/drop-off area on Gladstone Avenue for larger school buses to pick-up/drop-off, instead of on Shanly Street. I am by way of this motion requesting that the school pick-up/drop-off hours be adjusted to reflect the new school hours and that space be allocated for school bus pick-up/drop-off on Gladstone Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing 15-minute maximum parking regulation in effect from 8:15 a.m. to 8:45 a.m. and 3:15 p.m. to 3:45 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Gladstone Avenue, between a point 15 metres north of Shanly Street and a point 82.5 metres further north. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 8:45 a.m. to 3:15 p.m. and 3:45 p.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Gladstone Avenue, between a point 15 metres north of Shanly Street and a point 82.5 metres further north. 3. Prohibited parking from 8:00 a.m. to 8:30 a.m. and from 3:00 p.m. to 3:15 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Gladstone Avenue, between a point 55 metres north of Shanly Street and a point 42.5 metres further north. 4.
