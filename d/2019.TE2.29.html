The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. In response to concerns regarding pick-up and drop-off activity in the vicinity of Oriole Park Public School, located at No. 80 Braemar Avenue, this report recommends amendments to the existing time periods pertaining to the parking for a maximum period of 10 minutes on Braemar Avenue, College View Avenue and Elmsthorpe Avenue. In addition, this report also corrects inconsistencies between on-street parking regulations posted on Braemar Avenue, College View Avenue, Elmsthorpe Avenue, and the Traffic and Parking By-laws as listed in Chapter 950 of the Toronto Municipal Code.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking at all times on the west side of Braemar Avenue, between Eglinton Avenue West and a point 36 metres south. 2. Rescinded the existing parking for a maximum period of one hour in effect from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Braemar Avenue, between Eglinton Avenue West and a point 36 metres south. 3.
