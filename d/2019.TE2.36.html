What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to rescind the current parking prohibitions on the west sides of Fraser Avenue, Jefferson Avenue and Pardee Avenue to provide additional parking spaces around Lamport Stadium located at 1155 King Street West. City of Toronto's Shelter Support and Housing Administration (SSHA) reviewed city properties to provide 24-hour respite service and choose a location at 1155 King Street, the parking lot of Lamport Stadium. The new structure will occupy approximately 100 parking spaces at this location. The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Fraser Avenue, between King Street West and Liberty Street, between a point 69.5 metres south of Liberty Street and between a point 55.5 metres further south and between a point 143 metres south of Liberty Street and a point 66 metres further south. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Jefferson Avenue, between King Street West and a point 126.5 metres south. 3. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Pardee Avenue, between Liberty Street and a point 36 metres south. 4.
