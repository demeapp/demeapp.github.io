Toronto City Council · Decision

Staff recommend parking regulation changes on Clinton Street.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Clinton Street

AdoptedJan 15, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to implement a "No Stopping, Anytime" regulation on the east side of Clinton Street, near Jersey Street. This is in response to complaints about vehicles parking in the existing parking prohibition area, thus creating sightline issues between motorists and pedestrians crossing Clinton Street at Jersey Avenue. This proposal would not result in the loss of any parking spaces.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited stopping at all times on the east side of Clinton Street, between a point 3.3 metres north of a point opposite the south side of Jersey Avenue and a point 27.5 metres south. 2. Prohibited parking from 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Clinton Street, between a point 27.4 metres south of Jersey Avenue and a point 24.2 metres south of Jersey Avenue. 3. Amended the existing permit parking regulation on the odd (east) side of Clinton Street, from College Street to Harbord Street, to be in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., except "No Standing" anytime from College Street to a point 38.6 metres north, and no stopping anytime from a point 24.2 metres south of a point opposite the south side of Jersey Avenue to a point 3.3 metres north of a point opposite the south side of Jersey Avenue.
