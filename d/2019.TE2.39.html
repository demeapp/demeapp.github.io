Staff recommend parking regulation changes on Crawford Street.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to rescind the alternate-side parking regulations on Crawford Street, between Dewson Street and Harbord Street. Parking will be provided on the west side of the street year round and will provide additional on-street parking spaces. The proposed changes will not impact traffic operations, but will have a negative impact on mechanical street cleaning.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from the 16th day to the last day of each month from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the east side of Crawford Street, from Dewson Street to Harbord Street. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from December 1 of one year to March 31 of the following year, inclusive and from the first day to the 15th day of each month from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the west side of Crawford Street, from Dewson Street to Harbord Street. 4. Prohibited parking at all times on the east side of Crawford Street, between Dewson Street and Harbord Street. 5.
