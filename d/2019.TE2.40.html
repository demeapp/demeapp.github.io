Staff recommend temporary parking changes on 33 Dunfield Avenue and 44 Lillian Street during construction.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. A review of the traffic regulations on Dunfield Avenue and Lillian Street has determined a discrepancy between the on-street parking signage and the by-laws. In view of the above, it is recommended that installation of a stopping prohibition to be in effect at all times on the subject section of Dunfield Avenue and Lillian Street. The above recommendation will facilitate safe and efficient flow of two-way traffic and accommodate construction activity.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council approved the following parking amendments during construction until May 31, 2019: 1. Rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Dunfield Avenue, between Soudan Avenue and a point 109 metres north. 2. Prohibit stopping at all times on the east side of Dunfield Avenue, between Soudan Avenue and a point 109 metres north. 3. Rescind the existing parking prohibition at all times in effect on the east side of Lillian Street, between Soudan Avenue and a point 88 metres north. 4. Prohibit stopping at all times on the west side of Lillian Street, between Soudan Avenue and a point 93.5 metres north. 5. Prohibit stopping at all times on the east side of Lillian Street, between Soudan Avenue and a point 56 metres north. 6.
