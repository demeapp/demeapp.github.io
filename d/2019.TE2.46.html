Staff recommend parking regulation changes on Chapman Avenue.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Chapman Avenue

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services staff is requesting approval to designate two maximum 15-minute pick-up/drop-off areas on the north side of Chapmen Avenue, between Dawes Road and Munford Crescent, in order to facilitate parents and guardians to pick-up/drop-off students attending George Webster Elementary School, located at 50 Chapman Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Chapman Avenue, between a point 177 metres west of Dawes Road and a point 318 metres west of Dawes Road. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Chapman Avenue, between Dawes Road and George Webster Road. 3. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 15 minutes from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., and from 3:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Chapman Avenue, between a point 115 metres west of Dawes Road and a point 60 metres further west, and a point 9 metres east of Munford Crescent (east intersection) and a point 103 metres further east. 4.
