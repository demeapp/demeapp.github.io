The official record

What the item proposed

At the Cecil Community Centre Board of Management meeting on November 29, 2018, the Board members unanimously voted in favour of the appointment of Andrew Reddin, Nicolas Cole and Adam Mamdani by the Toronto and East York Community Council to the Board of Management of Cecil Community Centre. Upon learning of Ms. Yeung, Mr. Breckon and Ms. Li’s resignations, the Governance Committee of the Board of Management commenced a search for suitable candidates to fill these vacancies. The Governance Committee, among other duties, reviewed the role, responsibilities and required skills of a Board member and identified additional desirable skills, those being: fundraising, children, youth and seniors’ issues, legal, and human resources. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Appointed, at pleasure of Council, the following nominees to the Board of Management of Cecil Community Centre for a term of office from November 29, 2018 and ending June 30, 2020, or until a successor is appointed.: -Andrew Reddin, -Nicolas Cole -Adam Mamdani
