Councillor Fletcher recommends removing a planter on Busy Street.

Official title: Verral and Busy St - Planter

AdoptedJan 15, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on November 14, 2017, Toronto and East York Community Council adopted Item TE28.63, approving the installation of a planter box on the south side of Busy Street, west of Verral Avenue. However, residents have reported that the planter is not working as anticipated and has attracted litter and graffiti. They have requested that the planter be removed and alternate solutions considered.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the removal of the planter box on the south side of Busy Street, west of Verral Avenue. 2. Directed Transportation Services to investigate modifying the corner of Verral and Busy using flexible posts and line painting.
