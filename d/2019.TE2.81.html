Toronto City Council · Decision

Staff recommend parking regulation changes on Russell Street.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Russell Street

AdoptedJan 15, 2019 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

With the introduction of pay-and-display parking on the south side of Russell Street, the University of Toronto has been experiencing difficulties entering and exiting their loading docks located on the north side of the street as well as conducting other delivery/shipping activities. As a result, two-way traffic on this section of Russell Street is being impacted.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking machine regulation on the south side of Russell Street, between Spadina Crescent and Huron Street, operating from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, and from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday, for a maximum period of three hours, at a rate of $4.00 per hour. 2. Rescinded the existing parking machine regulation on the north side of Russell Street, between Spadina Crescent and Huron Street - east branch, operating from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, for a maximum period of three hours, at a rate of $4.00 per hour. 3. Rescinded the existing parking machine regulation on the north side of Russell Street, between Spadina Crescent and Huron Street - east branch, operating from 6:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, and from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday, for a maximum period of three hours,…
