Alterations to Heritage Properties, Intention to Designate under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act and Authority to Enter into a Heritage Easement Agreement - 15 Duncan Street and 158 Pearl Street

AdoptedFeb 26, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend approving alterations to the heritage property at 15 Duncan Street and 158 Pearl Street in accordance with Section 33 of the Ontario Heritage Act to allow for the construction of a tower and base building with alterations to the heritage building.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve the alterations proposed for the properties at 15 Duncan Street and 158 Pearl Street in accordance with Section 33 of the Ontario Heritage Act to allow for the construction of a 60-storey tower on the properties and per the settlement offer submitted to the City in conjunction with and appeal to the Ontario Municipal Board (now the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal ("LPAT") and the subsequent mediation of the application for a Zoning By-law Amendment. This report also recommends that City Council state its intention to designate 15 Duncan Street and 158 Pearl Street under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act and give authority to enter into a Heritage Easement Agreement for these properties

What was decided

City Council on February 26, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve the alterations to the heritage properties at 15 Duncan Street and 158 Pearl Street in accordance with Section 33 of the Ontario Heritage Act to allow for the construction of a tower and base building with alterations to the heritage buildings in conjunction with an appeal to the Local Planning Appeals Tribunal and subsequent mediation of the application for a Zoning By-law Amendment comprised of the plans and drawings prepared by Richmond Architects Ltd. dated November 15, 2018 and the Heritage Impact Assessment prepared by GBCA Architects dated November 16, 2018, both on file with the Senior Manager, Urban Design/Heritage, City Planning, all subject to and in accordance with a Conservation Plan satisfactory to the Senior Manager, Urban Design/Heritage, City Planning and subject to the following…
