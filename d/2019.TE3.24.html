Staff recommend re-installing the Temperance Street parklet cafés for 2019.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report is required to permit the re-installation of the Temperance Street parklet cafés and on-street bicycle parking for the outdoor café season for 2019. Modifications to Temperance Street between Sheppard Street and Bay Street were piloted from April 15 to November 14 in 2018 with positive success and no issues reported. Positive results included: enabling customers to dine outdoors in an aesthetically pleasing area; providing features that generate visual interest when looking in at Temperance from Bay Street in the city's Financial District; and providing plantings that softened the streetscape and enhanced pedestrian activity. Local businesses - Boxcar Social and John & Sons Oyster House - are requesting the re-installation for April 15 to November 14 in 2019. No changes are proposed from the 2018 installation.
