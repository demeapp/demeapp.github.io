The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Westbank Projects Corp. is constructing a 57-storey high mixed-use development at 219 and 223 Adelaide Street West and 19 Duncan Street. Construction of the development will be undertaken in several phases. This report deals with matters related to Phase 1 of construction only on Duncan Street, which includes drilling, shoring and installation of façade retention steel structure. The sidewalk on the east side of Duncan Street abutting the site is currently closed to accommodate construction staging operations for the development. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the continuation of the closure of the east sidewalk on Duncan Street, between Adelaide Street West and a point 31 metres south, from March 1, 2019 to April 30, 2019. 2. Directed the Acting Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, to add as a condition of permit, a requirement provision for a Traffic Control Person to be stationed at the intersection of Adelaide Street West and Duncan Street from March 1, 2019 to April 30, 2019. 3. Directed the applicant to continue posting a 24-hour monitored construction hotline number on the hoarding board, which must be prominently placed and legible from 20 metres and on all elevations from the construction site. 4.
