What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. EllisDon Corporation is constructing a 17-storey institutional building at the lands located at 11 and 33 Centre Avenue and 80 Chestnut Street. The site is bounded by an existing commercial building (55 Centre Avenue and 108-110 Chestnut Street to the north, Chestnut Street to the east, Armoury Street to the south and Centre Avenue to the west. Operating under a monthly Street Occupation Permit, a construction staging area has been setup on the north sidewalk and a portion of the westbound curb lane on Armoury Street, between Centre Avenue and Chestnut Street.
