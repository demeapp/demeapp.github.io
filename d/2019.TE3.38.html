Staff recommend removing an accessible loading zone on Springhurst Avenue.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on this segment of Springhurst Avenue, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting approval to remove a designated on-street accessible loading zone, located on the north side of Springhurst Avenue, between Cowan Avenue and Dunn Avenue. The accessible permit holder who originally requested the accessible loading space has moved and no longer requires the designated space.

What was decided

City Council on February 26, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council rescind the existing on-street accessible loading zone that operates from 6:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Springhurst Avenue, between a point 40.8 metres west of Cowan Avenue and a point 8 metres further west.
