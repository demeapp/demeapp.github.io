The official record

What the item proposed

The Agnes Macphail Award was established by the Borough of East York. It continues today as an annual event to recognize an outstanding volunteer who lives within the boundaries of the former borough. The Agnes Macphail Selection Committee will be meeting on February 11th, 2019 to make their recommendation on the recipient of the 2019 Agnes Macphail Award. The Agnes Macphail Selection Committee will be submitting their recommendation on the recipient of the 2019 Agnes Macphail Award prior to the Toronto and East York Community Council meeting and the North York Community Council meeting on February 14, 2019 so that it can be considered on that date by the respective Community Council. This timing would allow the award ceremony to take place on March 24, 2019 which is the anniversary of Agnes Macphail's birth.

What was decided

City Council on February 26, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve Karen Fraser as the recipient of the 2019 Agnes Macphail Award.
