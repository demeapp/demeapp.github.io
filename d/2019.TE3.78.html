The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes 52 rental residential dwelling units with retail uses at grade. A preliminary report came forward in July 2018 (TE34.98) and City Planning was directed to schedule a consultation meeting. I am asking Community Council to support a larger notice area for this meeting than was initially proposed to allow local residents a chance to comment before the final report comes forward later this year.

What was decided

Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed staff to schedule a community consultation meeting for the lands at 1151 Queen Street East together with the Ward Councillor. 2. Notice for the community consultation meeting be given to landowners and residents within 120 metres of the site, and to additional residents, institutions and owners to be determined in consultation with the Ward Councillor, with any additional mailing costs to be borne by the applicant. 3. Directed notice for the public meeting under the Planning Act be given according to the regulations of the Planning Act.
