The official record

What the item proposed

Carlaw-Dundas is a former industrial area in the City's east end that has been undergoing a transformation, emerging as a mixed-use creative and cultural hub of workers and residents. The Wrigley Building and many of the other buildings in the Carlaw – Dundas area were identified in 2014 as potentially having significant heritage value. The property at 235 Carlaw Avenue contains a building complex commissioned as the Canadian branch of Chicago’s William Wrigley, Jr. Company Limited (afterward William Wrigley, Jr. Company Limited of Canada), producers of chewing gum. The five-storey North Building was completed in 1916 according to the plans of Toronto architects, Prack and Perrine, followed by the near-identical South Building in 1919, with the design purportedly modelled on the parent company’s Chicago factory. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the Senior Manager, Heritage Preservation Services, complete the evaluation of the property at 235 Carlaw Avenue according to provincial criteria Regulation 9/06 and finalize recommendations to designate The Wrigley Building to conserve the cultural heritage value of the landmark property and maintain the historic industrial character of the Carlaw-Dundas area.
