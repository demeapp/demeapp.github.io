The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Toronto and East York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This is a report on the refusal to issue a permit by Municipal Licensing and Standards in the matter of an application for a boulevard café permit located at 1975 A Queen Street East, Waverley Road flankage

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the Boulevard Café Permit Application located at 1975 A Queen Street East, Waverley Road Flankage, with the following conditions: a. The café patio shall be closed and cleared by 10:00 p.m. daily. b. The Licensee shall undergo a review of the permit operations in 1 year's time by Municipal Licensing and Standards.
