What the item proposed

On November 24, 2017 an application to amend the Zoning By-law was submitted to permit the conversion and enlargement of an existing vacant building on a lot fronting a public laneway at 596 R Rushton Road into a detached house. The proposed dwelling is two-storeys and 6.82 metres in height. A parking pad for one vehicle and an amenity space is proposed to be located on a portion of 598 Rushton Road. The existing single detached house at 598 Rushton Road will be maintained. On March 29, 2018 the applicant appealed the Zoning By-law Amendment application to the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal (LPAT), citing City Council's failure to make a decision within the allotted time in the Planning Act.
