What the item proposed

The purpose of this staff report is to seek City Council approval of 440, 444, 450 and 462 Front Street West; 425,439, 441 and 443 Wellington Street West; 6-18 Spadina Avenue and 1 and 19 Draper Street - Public Art Plan. The Plan, which is included as Attachment 1 of this report, outlines the method by which the owner will commission public art on a publicly-accessible area of the development site. The Public Art Plan provides a framework and description of the commissioning of public art including; introduction and background; public art plan; site context; project team; public art site description; artwork selection criteria; artist selection process; competition process; the jury; maintenance obligation; mentorship program; artwork budget and project schedule.
