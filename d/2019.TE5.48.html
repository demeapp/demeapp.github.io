What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Sentinel (Church) Holdings Inc. is constructing a 52-storey mixed-use development at 215-229 Church Street and 117 Dundas Street East. The site is located in the south-east corner of Dundas Street East and Church Street. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the sidewalk and a portion of the southbound lane on the west side of Dalhousie Street for a period of 48 months. In addition, approval is requested to temporarily convert a section of Dalhousie Street abutting the site from a two-way traffic operation to a one-way northbound traffic operation.
