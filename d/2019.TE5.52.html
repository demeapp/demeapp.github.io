The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services is requesting approval from City Council to prohibit the General Manager from accepting applications from residents of, visitors to, and tradespersons at the development properties municipally known as 871 – 899 College Street from residential on-street permit parking. Transportation Services has assessed the realignment of permit parking area 3J and the exclusion of the development property located at 871 – 899 College Street as requested by Toronto and East York Community Council, Item TE4.83, on March 19, 2019. Transportation Services is recommending approval of the exclusion as it will not impact negatively on College Street and neighbouring residents.

What was decided

City Council on May 14 and 15, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend Schedule B of the City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 925, Permit Parking, to incorporate a revised map of permit parking area 3J, as attached in Appendix A to the report (April 2, 2019) from the Manager, Permits and Enforcement, Parking, Transportation Services.
