Toronto City Council · Decision

Parking Amendments - Hendrick Avenue

AdoptedApr 24, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Transportation Services asks Toronto and East York Community Council to prohibit standing at all times on the west side and prohibit parking at all times on the east side of Hendrick Avenue, south of St. Clair Avenue West.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to prohibit standing at all times on the west side and prohibit parking at all times on the east side of Hendrick Avenue, south of St. Clair Avenue West. This is in response to concerns received from the condominium board of 835 St. Clair Avenue West about vehicles parking illegally on west side of Hendrick Avenue which adding difficulty to vehicle entrance or egress manoeuvres.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited standing at all times on the west side of Hendrick Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and the first lane south. 2. Prohibited parking at all times on the east side of Hendrick Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and the first lane south. 3. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, except each Thursday, from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the west side of Hendrick Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and the first lane south. 4. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect on each Thursday, from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the east side of Hendrick Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and the first lane south.
