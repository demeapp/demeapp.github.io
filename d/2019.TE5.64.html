Parking Amendments - Parkwood Avenue

AdoptedApr 24, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Transportation Services asks Toronto and East York Community Council to implement a "No Stopping Anytime" regulation on the west side of Parkwood Avenue, fronting the entrance to the condominium complex at No. 6 Parkwood Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to implement a "No Stopping Anytime" regulation on the west side of Parkwood Avenue, fronting the entrance to the condominium complex at No. 6 Parkwood Avenue. This proposal would provide residents clear access to the road and would result in the loss of one parking space on the west side of Parkwood Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing maximum one hour parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., daily, on the west side of Parkwood Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and Montclair Avenue. 2. Prohibited stopping at all times on the west side of Parkwood Avenue, between a point 25.2 metres north of St. Clair Avenue West and a point 5.1 metres further north. 3. Authorized parking for a maximum period of one hour from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., daily on the on the west side of Parkwood Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 25.2 metres north and between a point 30.3 metres north of St. Clair Avenue West and Montclair Avenue.
