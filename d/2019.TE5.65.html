Parking Amendments - Carlaw Avenue

AdoptedMay 14, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Transportation Services is requesting approval to prohibit standing at all times on the east side of Carlaw Avenue fronting the driveway servicing No. 53 Carlaw Avenue (Gensco Equipment Inc.).

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Carlaw Avenue, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting approval to prohibit standing at all times on the east side of Carlaw Avenue fronting the driveway servicing No. 53 Carlaw Avenue (Gensco Equipment Inc.). The proposed standing prohibition is being requested to prevent motorist from blocking the driveway, and impacting access during loading/unloading activities.

What was decided

City Council on May 14 and 15, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, except public holidays, on the east side of Carlaw Avenue, between a point 222 metres north of Lake Shore Boulevard East and a point 9 metres further north. 2. City Council prohibit standing at all times on the east side of Carlaw Avenue, between a point 222 metres north of Lake Shore Boulevard East and a point 9 metres further north.
