What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has assessed a request from Aquavista Bayside Toronto Inc., the owners of the property currently known as 261 Queens Quay East, for the installation of two temporary free-standing pillar signs fronting the premises at 261 Queens Quay East, near the east and west intersections of Merchants Warf. The signs will identify the retail uses which are located out of view on Edgewater Drive and Merchants Wharf adjacent to the subject property. As the signs are temporary in nature, and will not pose a significant negative impact on the public right of way, Transportation Services recommends approval of the encroachment(s).
