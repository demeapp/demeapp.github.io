The official record

What the item proposed

Residents in Cabbagetown South have come together to organize Hello Seaton Street - a street event to encourage neighbours to get to know one another, promote a sense of inclusivity and sharing among the community. It is a significant step towards making Seaton Street more safe and inviting. Residents hope that this event will contribute to a more positive perception of their neighbourhood. The community has expressed a lack of social cohesion and bonds, as there are limited opportunities for neighbours to come together locally and there have been challenges with litter and hazardous waste improperly disposed. These and other issues have made the street feel unsafe. Neighbours have expressed that these challenges have made it uncomfortable for them to idle outdoors talking to one another or spend time in their front yards. What was decided

City Council on May 14 and 15, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Transportation Services to waive the permit fee charged for the 2019 Hello Seaton Street event (fee reference number 61.09, Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 441, Fees and Charges, Appendix C – Schedule 2, Transportation Services).
