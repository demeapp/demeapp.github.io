The official record

What the item proposed

The intersection of Pape and Danforth is a busy intersection used by pedestrians, cyclists, cars and TTC busses daily. Immediately north of the intersection is Pape Subway Station, which recently had a traffic light installed. Residents have expressed concerns about the safety of these two intersections, and in particular the way the two traffic lights operate together. Transportation Services is undertaking a review of the two intersections. I am asking that the review be brought to Community Council to ensure that the community has the opportunity to give feedback.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council requested: 1. Transportation Services to report to the September 16, 2019 meeting of the Toronto and East York Community Council with the results of the intersection safety and signal timing/phase review at Pape Avenue and Danforth Avenue, and Pape Avenue and Lipton Avenue.
