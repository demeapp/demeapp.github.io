What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Toronto and East York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. To report on the refusal to issue a permit by Municipal Licensing and Standards in the matter of an application for a boulevard café permit located at 356 College Street, Brunswick Avenue flankage.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council approved the application for the proposed boulevard café permit located at 356 College Street, Brunswick Avenue flankage, with the following conditions: 1. To the satisfaction of Parks, Forestry, and Recreation, and Transportation Services, and the Ward Councillor, and in consultation with the local Residents' Association, the applicant will create a landscaping plan, including but not limited to: a. Removal of any trees in poor condition and their boxes, with any associated costs and repaving to be the responsibility of the applicant b. Retention of any healthy trees in their boxes c. Removal of any remaining trees in the event that they die, and in consultation with Forestry and Municipal Licensing and Standards, relocation or removal of the two additional planters as required d.
