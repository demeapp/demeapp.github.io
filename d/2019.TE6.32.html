What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. TMG Builders is constructing a 56-storey residential condominium building at 1 Yorkville Avenue. The site is located in the south-west corner of Yonge Street and Yorkville Avenue. The sidewalk and a portion of the eastbound curb lane on the south side of Yorkville Avenue fronting the site are currently closed to accommodate construction staging operations for the development. Toronto and East York Committee Council, at its meeting on April 5, 2016, approved the subject construction staging area on Yorkville Avenue from June 1, 2017 to May 31, 2019. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the continuation of the closure of the sidewalk and the eastbound curb lane on Yorkville Avenue, between Yonge Street and a point 45.3 metres west of Yonge Street, from July 1, 2019 to May 31, 2020. 2. Directed the applicant to continue posting a 24-hour monitored construction hotline number on the hoarding board, which must be prominently placed and legible from 20 metres and on all elevations from the construction site. 3. Directed the applicant to continue providing and installing public art, including mural artwork, onto every elevation of the hoarding board with adequate spotlighting for night-time illumination, at their sole cost, to the satisfaction of the Ward Councillor. 4.
