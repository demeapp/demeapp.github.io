What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Tribute Communities is constructing a 36-storey condominium at 77- 83 Mutual Street. The site is located in the north-east quadrant of Carlton Street and Church Street. The site is bounded by Mutual Street to the west, existing residential uses (190 - 194 Jarvis Street) to the east and public laneways to the north and south. Toronto and East York Community Council, at its meeting on November 14, 2017, approved the subject construction staging areas on Mutual Street and the public laneway, south of the site, from November 21, 2017 to June 1, 2019.
