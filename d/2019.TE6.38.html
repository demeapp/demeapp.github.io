Transportation Services asks Toronto and East York Community Council to close the west sidewalk and curb lane on Dewhurst Boulevard for a period of 20 months.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the west sidewalk and curb lane on Dewhurst Boulevard for a period of 20 months. Laughlin Solutions Incorporated is building a 4-storey condominium with a total of 32 units, a stacked parking garage for approximately 24 cars and one surface parking space. The site is located on the west side of Dewhurst Boulevard, just north of Danforth Avenue and south of Strathmore Boulevard. To enable construction, the applicant requires the aforementioned closures.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the closure of the west sidewalk and 1.5 metres of the southbound parking lane on Dewhurst Boulevard, from a point 33 metres north of Danforth Avenue to a point 63.4 metres further north, from May 31, 2019 to January 1, 2021. 2. Amended the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., on the west side of Dewhurst Boulevard, from Danforth Avenue to a point 45.1 metres north of Milverton Boulevard, to exclude the section from a point 33 metres north of Danforth Avenue to a point 63.4 further north. 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the west side of Dewhurst Boulevard, from a point 33 metres north of Danforth Avenue to a point 63.4 metres further north. 4.
