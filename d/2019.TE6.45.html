Parking Amendments - Pirandello Street

AdoptedMay 22, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Transportation Services asks Toronto and East York Community Council to prohibit standing at all times on the west side of Pirandello Street, near the access to the fire route for Liberty Village Town Homes (TSCC 1660, 1673, 1705, 1746).

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to prohibit standing at all times on the west side of Pirandello Street, near the access to the fire route for Liberty Village Town Homes (TSCC 1660, 1673, 1705, 1746). This is in response to complaints about vehicles blocking the fire route access on the west side of Pirandello Street. This proposal would not result in the loss of any parking spaces. This report will address the fire route access for Liberty Village Town Homes on Pirandello Street. The fire route access and underground parking access for Liberty Village Town Homes on the north side of East Liberty will be addressed in a separate report to Toronto and East York Community Council, entitled…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited standing at all times on the east side of Pirandello Street, between a point 69.1 metres north of East Liberty Street and a point 12.5 metres further north. 2. Rescinded the parking machine regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday; from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday for a maximum period of three hours, on the east side of Pirandello Street, between a point 69.1 metres north of East Liberty Street and a point 12.5 metres further north
