Traffic Control Signals - Pape Avenue at Strathcona Avenue and Pape Avenue at Bain Avenue

Deferred indefinitelyMay 22, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Transportation Services does not support the replacement of the existing pedestrian crossovers (PXO) with traffic control signals at the following intersections: Pape Avenue at Strathcona Avenue Pape Avenue at Bain Avenue The technical justification for these installations are not satisfied.

Council put off deciding this item.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Pape Avenue, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services does not support the replacement of the existing pedestrian crossovers (PXO) with traffic control signals at the following intersections: Pape Avenue at Strathcona Avenue Pape Avenue at Bain Avenue The technical justification for these installations are not satisfied. Therefore, the installation of traffic control signals is not recommended.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council deferred consideration of the item indefinitely.
