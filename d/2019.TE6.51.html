Encroachment - Bremner Boulevard Barrier Gate and Swing Gate

AdoptedMay 22, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Transportation Services asks Toronto and East York Community Council to enter into an encroachment agreement with Maple Leaf Sports and Entertainment Partnership (MLSEP) to install, maintain and repair an 8 metre wide by 1.26 metre high permanent, manually operated, barrier gate located on Bremner Boulevard approximately 11 metres north of Lake Shore Boulevard West.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision provided it is in keeping with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting authority to enter into an encroachment agreement with Maple Leaf Sports and Entertainment Partnership (MLSEP) to install, maintain and repair an 8 metre wide by 1.26 metre high permanent, manually operated, barrier gate located on Bremner Boulevard approximately 11 metres north of Lake Shore Boulevard West. This barrier gate is being proposed to enhance pedestrian safety in this area of Bremner Boulevard during events at the Scotiabank Arena, and will allow the flexibility to open the roadway to general traffic when no events are scheduled, as this section of roadway has been permanently closed (since April 2018) for security reasons.
