The official record

What the item proposed

The Leslieville area currently has three Accessible Pedestrian Signals (APS). There are numerous senior's homes in close proximity in this area and seniors make up a significant percentage of pedestrian traffic. Leslieville's proximity to the downtown core, high vehicular traffic and high percentage of senior residents make it a prime candidate for further APS installation. Two local students undertook a study and identified six locations where the installation of APS is crucial to ensure the safety of residents, especially seniors, in the community. This motion includes recommendations to protect pedestrians, improve road safety and ensure that residents feel safe in their neighbourhood.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested Transportation Services to install Accessible Pedestrian Signals (APS) at the following intersections in the next round of installations: Queen Street and Carlaw Avenue (PX0545) Gerrard Street and Jones Avenue (PX0370) Dundas Street and Carlaw Avenue (PX0289) Gerrard Street and Carlaw Avenue (PX0372) Gerrard Street and Greenwood Avenue (PX0369) Queen Street and Greenwood Avenue (PX1393)
