What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Toronto and East York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This is a report on the refusal to issue a permit by Municipal Licensing and Standards in the matter of an application for a boulevard café permit located at 744 St. Clair Avenue West, Rushton Road flankage.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the proposed boulevard café permit application located at 744 St. Clair Avenue West, with the following conditions: a. The Licensee shall ensure that the boulevard café is closed and cleared of patrons, food and drink, at or before 10:00 p.m. 7 days a week. b. The Licensee shall undergo a review of the permit and conditions in one year's time by the local Councillor and Municipal Licensing and Standards. c. Should transfer of business ownership occur for the premises, the patio licence shall not be transferred. Municipal Licensing and Standards shall report on any transfer applications.
