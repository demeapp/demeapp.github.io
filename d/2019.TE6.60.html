What the item proposed

At the Swansea Town Hall Association Annual General Meeting on May 16, 2019, the membership nominated the individuals listed above for appointment by Etobicoke York Community Council to the Board of Management of the Swansea Town Hall Community Centre. Appointments to the Swansea Town Hall Community Centre Board of Management is a matter for which the Community Council has been delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision, provided that the recommendations do not vary from the Public Appointments Policy. All the nominees are eligible and qualified to serve on the Board of Management pursuant to the relevant provisions of the City of Toronto By-law (Chapter 25 of the former City of Toronto Municipal Code), the Relationship Framework for Board run Community Centers and the Public Appointments Policy.
