Parking Amendments - Gladstone Avenue, Havelock Street, and Rusholme Park Crescent

AdoptedMay 22, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to prohibit parking on the east side of Gladstone Avenue between College Street and a point 15 metres north.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In July 2018, traffic control signals were approved for the intersections of College Street, at Gladstone Avenue and College Street, at Havelock Street and Rusholme Park Crescent. When these traffic control signals were approved, parking was also removed to a point of 30.5 metres on the approaches of these intersections. Area residents have since voiced concerns over the loss of parking. The purpose of this motion therefore is to reduce the previously approved parking reductions 30.5 metres to 15 metres on most approaches in order to maximize on-street parking.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking on the east side of Gladstone Avenue between College Street and a point 15 metres north. 2. Prohibited parking on the west side of Gladstone Avenue between College Street and a point 15 metres south. 3. Prohibited parking on the west side of Havelock Street between College Street and a point 15 metres north. 4. Prohibited parking on the west side of Rusholme Park Crescent between College Street and a point 35 metres south. 6. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition on the west side of Gladstone Avenue, between College Street and the first lane south, in effect from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. daily. 7.
