The official record

What the item proposed

In recent months, several local residents have reached out with concerns regarding pedestrian, cyclist, and motorist safety at the intersection of Gerrard Street East and Pembroke Street. Residents have sent communications to my office, stating that they frequently observe cars, passing through the intersection along Gerrard, at high speeds. This speeding activity is often observed to be in violation of an activated pedestrian crossing. Residents have also brought forward serious sight line and visibility concerns, affecting all intersection users. There have, additionally, been numerous complaints regarding the location of the pedestrian crossing activation button on Pembroke, which is located several feet south of the crossing. Pedestrians who activate the crosswalk, are not visible to cyclists and motorists approaching the intersection. Pedestrians must then take time to walk forward…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services, to study strategies to improve safety at the Gerrard Street East and Pembroke Street intersection, in consultation with the local residents' association, and to report back to the September 16, 2019 meeting of Toronto and East York Community Council.
