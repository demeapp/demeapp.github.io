What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Toronto and East York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This is a report on the refusal to issue a permit by Municipal Licensing and Standards in the matter of an application for a boulevard café permit located at 1588 Dupont Street, Franklin Avenue Flankage.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the application for the proposed boulevard café permit located at 1588 Dupont Street, Franklin Avenue flankage.
