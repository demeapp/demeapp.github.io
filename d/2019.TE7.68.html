Vehicle Width Restriction - Public Lane, First South of College Street, Between Manning Avenue and Clinton Street

AdoptedJun 25, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to prohibit vehicles over two metres in width from travelling the public lane, first south of College Street, between Manning Avenue and Clinton Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is seeking authority to prohibit vehicles greater than two metres in width from travelling the public lane, first south of College Street, between Manning Avenue and Clinton Street. Prohibiting vehicles over two metres in width from travelling the subject lane will be beneficial in reducing the incidence of damage to adjacent private property.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited vehicles over two metres in width from travelling the public lane, first south of College Street, between Manning Avenue and Clinton Street.
