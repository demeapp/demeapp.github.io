What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has assessed a request from Menkes Waterfront Holdings Inc. to construct an overhead pedestrian bridge, to connect two office buildings being constructed at 125 and 155 Queens Quay East, which will encroach within the public right-of-way over Dockside Drive.
