The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is recommending that an on-street accessible loading zone be designated on Melita Crescent. The establishment of this accessible loading zone will provide unimpeded curb side access to TTC Wheel-Trans service for residents of the TCHC building located at 470 Melita Crescent.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Designated an accessible loading zone to operate at all times on the west side of Melita Crescent, between a point 44 metres south of Wychcrest Avenue and a point 11 metres further south.
