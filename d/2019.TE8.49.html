What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is recommending that the operation of the short-term parking area and the passenger loading zone on the east side of Brownlow Avenue for the Eglinton Junior Public School (223 Eglinton Avenue East) be amended. This proposal will amend the operating times in order to accommodate school zone pick-up/drop-off activity and will extend the existing short-term parking area by 24 metres further south. These parking regulation changes will add about four additional parking spaces to the existing pick-up/drop-off area and will not adversely impact traffic or parking operations on Brownlow Avenue.
