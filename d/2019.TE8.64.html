Speed Limit Reduction on Glenholme Avenue

AdoptedSep 16, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to reduce the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on Glenholme Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 59.2 metres north.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Parents and teachers of Rawlinson Community School, along with the local School Trustee met with the Ward Councillor and Transportation Services staff and requested that the speed limit on Glenholme Avenue be reduced to 30 km/h. While the section between a point of 59.2 metres north of St. Clair Avenue West and Rogers Road is 30 km/h, the section to the south between St. Clair Avenue West and a point of 59.2 metres further north is unsigned at 50 km/h. The purpose of this motion is to harmonize the speed limit on Glenholme Avenue to 30 km/h, and therefore improve safety for local residents, as well as parents and students when they walk to and from school.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on Glenholme Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 59.2 metres north.
