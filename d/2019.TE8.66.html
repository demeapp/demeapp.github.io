The official record

What the item proposed

In June 2019, I received a petition from residents and business owners on Pelham Avenue requesting parking changes on that street. According to business owners and residents, the demographics have changed and the lack of parking has been impacting clients and visitors that are trying to access the area. The requested changes are the removal of the existing 'no parking' regulation that is in effect between, 9 a.m. and 5 p.m., Monday to Friday, and the introduction of a one hour parking limit between, 9 a.m. and 6 p.m., Monday to Friday.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Pelham Avenue, between Osler Street and a point 35.1 metres west of Uxbridge Avenue. 2. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 1 hour from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Pelham Avenue, between Osler Street and a point 35.1 metres west of Uxbridge Avenue.
