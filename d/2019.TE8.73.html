The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to you today regarding the truncated portion of Gerrard Street, between Carlaw and Badgerow. This portion of Gerrard has houses, but does not connect to the rest of Gerrard, instead ending at Badgerow. Due to the fact that it connects to Carlaw but does not allow access to any other potion of Gerrard Street residents are concerned about the number of wrong turns by large trucks and other vehicles through their neighbourhood. Transportation Services has tried different solutions to solve this problem including turn restrictions and signage. Residents are wondering about a more permanent solution. I would ask that you support this request for a report back on potential options.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed Transportation Services staff to report to the December 3, 2019 meeting of Toronto and East York Community Council on the options for restricting vehicular access on the portion of Gerrard Street between Carlaw Avenue and Badgerow Avenue, including the feasibility of converting this stretch of Gerrard Street into parkland or open space.
