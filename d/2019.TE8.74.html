The official record

What the item proposed

Dear Chair and Members of the Toronto and East York Community Council, Knox Avenue is a one-way street in Ward 14 that connects Eastern Avenue and Queen Street. As such it experiences high volumes of traffic. It also has a contraflow cycle lane on it that connects to Eastern Avenue. The street has speed humps, but residents are interested in additional measures to slow down traffic and keep people safe.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested Transportation Services staff to report to the December 3, 2019 meeting of the Toronto and East York Community Council on the following items: Reviewing signage; Additional Separation between the Cycle Lane and Traffic; Investigate if reversing the direction of Knox Avenue would reduce cut-through traffic; Curb Radius Reduction at Queen Street Eastd and Knox Avenue; and Additional speed humps between the existing humps.
