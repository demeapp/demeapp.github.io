The official record

What the item proposed

Permit Parking Area 9G has 250 on-street permit parking spaces and 257 Permits have been issued. Area 9A has 2,207 on-street permit parking spaces. To date 1,789 permits have been issued. Cedarvale Avenue, between Danforth Avenue and the 9G boarder has 6 spaces and 4 permits issued. Gledhill Avenue between Danforth Avenue and the 9G boarder has 5 spaces and 1 permit issued.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services, to review and to report back to Toronto and East York Community Council on the realignment of Permit Parking Area "9G" to relocate the existing southern boundary line south to Danforth Avenue.
