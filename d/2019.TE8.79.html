Encroachment Agreement - 1006 Bloor Street West

AdoptedSep 16, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The owner of 1006 Bloor Street West is proposing encroachments in the municipal boulevard abutting the Westmoreland Avenue flank of their property for the purpose of building an enclosed boulevard café. Toronto and East York Community Council is asked to consider: Encroachment Agreement - 1006 Bloor Street West.

What the item proposed

The owner of 1006 Bloor Street West is proposing encroachments in the municipal boulevard abutting the Westmoreland Avenue flank of their property for the purpose of building an enclosed boulevard café.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services, to enter into an Encroachment Agreement with the property owner at 1006 Bloor Street West to maintain encroachments in the municipal boulevard abutting the Westmoreland Avenue flank of the property.
