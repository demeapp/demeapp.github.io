Toronto City Council · Decision

Permanent Closure of the Boulevard Lands on the North-West Corner of Phoebe Street and Soho Square

AdoptedOct 2, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Transportation Services recommends that the boulevard lands on the north-west corner of Phoebe Street and Soho Square be permanently closed to allow for the transfer of Operational Management of the lands to Parks, Forestry and Recreation for a future enhanced green space. City Council is asked to consider: Permanent Closure of the Boulevard Lands on the North-West Corner of Phoebe Street and Soho Square.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services recommends that the boulevard lands on the north-west corner of Phoebe Street and Soho Square be permanently closed to allow for the transfer of Operational Management of the lands to Parks, Forestry and Recreation for a future enhanced green space.

What was decided

City Council on October 2 and 3, 2019, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the permanent closure of the boulevard lands on the north-west corner of Phoebe Street and Soho Square, shown as Part 1 on Sketch PS-2017-025 (the "Highway") and Part 1 on Reference Plan 66R-30774, attached to the report (August 28, 2019) from the Director, Transportation Planning and Capital Program, Transportation Services. 2. City Council enact a By-law substantially in the form of the Draft By-law attached as Appendix A to the report (August 28, 2019) from the Director, Transportation Planning and Capital Program, Transportation Services.
