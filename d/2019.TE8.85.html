The official record

What the item proposed

The Regent Park Neighbourhood refers to the area bordered by River Street to the east, Shuter Street to the south, Gerrard Street to the north, and Parliament Street to the west. Regent Park is primarily comprised of local and minor arterial roads, with a major arterial road, Dundas Street East, running through the center of the neighbourhood. The land is primarily residential, with a few schools, parks/green space, a community centre, and an aquatic centre. Over the last sixteen years, Regent Park has been undergoing a Revitalization project. Once the project is fully completed, the Regent Park revitalization will have built: 2,083 replacement rent-geared-to-income (RGI) units, 448 new affordable rental units, and 5,400 new market condominium units. What was decided

Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed Transportation Services staff, to work with the Regent Park community to evaluate their traffic concerns and to develop a Traffic Safety Plan that improves traffic flow and safety for both pedestrians and vehicles, and to report back to the December 3, 2019 meeting of Toronto and East York Community Council.
