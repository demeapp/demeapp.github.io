What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. In accordance with the City's Public Appointments Policy, appointed the following nominee to the Business Improvement Area (BIA) board of management set out below at the pleasure of Toronto and East York Community Council, and for a term expiring at the end of the term of Council or until successors are appointed: College Promenade: Giavedoni, Paola 2. Removed the following directors from the Business Improvement Area (BIA) board of management set out below: Korea Town: Lee, Carrol Lee, Matt Park, Choong Ho
