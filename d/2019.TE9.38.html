The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the east sidewalk and a portion of the northbound lane on St. Patrick Street for a period of 38 months. Lanterra Construction Management LTD. is building three towers, 17, 36 and 39-storey condominium. The project includes three and a half levels below grade with the P1 level for Toronto Parking Authority (TPA). The site is located on the east side of St. Patrick Street, between Dundas Street West to the north side, Simcoe Street to the east and Michael Sweet Avenue to the south. To enable construction, the applicant requires the aforementioned closures.

What was decided
