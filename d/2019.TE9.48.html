The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting approval to remove a designated on-street accessible loading zone, located on the east side of Chisholm Avenue, between Gates Avenue and Harris Avenue. The accessible permit holder who originally requested the accessible loading space no longer requires the designated space.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing accessible loading zone for persons with a disability, in effect from 5:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Chisholm Avenue, between a point 6 metres south of Gates Avenue and a point 11 metres further south. 2. Rescinded the two existing accessible parking spaces for persons with a disability, in effect from 5:30 p.m. of one day to 5:30 a.m., of the next following day, Monday to Friday, and at all times on Saturday and Sunday, on the east side of Chisholm Avenue, between a point 6 metres south of Gates Avenue and a point 11 metres further south. 3.
