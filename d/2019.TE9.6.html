What the item proposed

The Common Elements Condominium application proposes to establish the drive aisle, driveway, landscape area, parking area, and a shared mechanical and electrical room as a common element for nine townhouses and one commercial unit at 3258-3260 Dundas Street West. The Common Elements Condominium will ensure shared ownership and maintenance of the common elements by the condominium corporation. The requested exemption from the Part Lot Control provisions of the Planning Act is to permit the creation of conveyable lots for the nine townhouses fronting Gilmour Avenue and the commercial unit fronting Dundas Street West, which will become the Parcels of Tied Land (POTLs) to the Common Elements Condominium.
