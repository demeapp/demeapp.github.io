Speed Limit Reduction - Niagara Street

AdoptedOct 10, 2019 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to reduce the speed limit from 40 km/h to 30 km/h on Niagara Street, between King Street West and Bathurst Street.

What the item proposed

There is no number of deaths or serious injuries on our streets that is acceptable. Each one is preventable. As a City, we must prioritize and accelerate measures to make our streets safe and accessible for all road users, especially vulnerable pedestrians and cyclists. In 2015, Toronto and East York Community Council directed that all roads within the district classified as local streets be reduced to a 30 km/h speed limit. Despite its local residential character, Niagara Street is technically classified as a collector roadway, and as a result the speed limit remained at 40 km/h. I am writing to recommend that the speed limit on Niagara Street be reduced to 30 km/h. In addition, my office has received a petition requesting additional traffic calming measures be implemented on Niagara Street.
