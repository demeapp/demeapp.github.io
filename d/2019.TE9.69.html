The official record

What the item proposed

On January 15, 2019, Toronto and East York Community Council approved the installation of speed humps on Pendrith Street, as per Item TE2.59. The original Recommendation for Item TE2.59 incorrectly indicated "four speed humps". However, the accompanying drawing 421G-2789 correctly showed "five speed humps" to be installed. This error must be corrected in order for the installation of traffic calming/speed humps to proceed and a highway alteration bylaw enacted. The cost to install five speed humps will be $20,000, rather than the $16,000 as previously indicated.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item TE2.59 with respect to Recommendation 1 only. 2. Amended recommendation 1 of Item TE2.59 by changing the reference to "four speed humps" to read as "five speed humps".
