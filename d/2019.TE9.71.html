The official record

What the item proposed

The Greater Yorkville Residents Association requested amendments to the existing parking regulation on the east side of McMurrich Street, between Davenport Road and Belmont Street. The intent of this amendment is to prohibit standing at all times, in order to deter any illegal parking and provide safe and efficient two-way traffic on McMurrich Street. Transportation Services staff have been consulted and have no objection to the proposed amendment.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the east side of McMurrich Street, between Davenport Road and Belmont Street. 2. Prohibited standing at all times on the east side of McMurrich Street, between Davenport Road and Belmont Street.
