Parking Amendments - Edwin Avenue, Ruskin Avenue to Dupont Street

AdoptedOct 10, 2019 · Toronto City Council

Last year, the member received a petition from residents requesting the introduction of a 1 hour parking limit on Edwin Avenue, between Ruskin Avenue and Dupont Street, Monday to Friday, between 10 a.m. and 6 p.m., and the extension of overnight permit parking hours, which currently end at 7:00 a.m., to end at 10 a.m.

The official record

What the item proposed

Last year, I received a petition from residents requesting the introduction of a 1 hour parking limit on Edwin Avenue, between Ruskin Avenue and Dupont Street, Monday to Friday, between 10 a.m. and 6 p.m., and the extension of overnight permit parking hours, which currently end at 7:00 a.m., to end at 10 a.m. (Monday to Friday) instead. Residents submitted this petition because they were finding it difficult to secure on-street parking. I reviewed the petition in detail and asked Traffic Operations staff to comment on the feasibility of introducing the requested parking regulation changes. City staff did not object to the parking change, but the petition did not demonstrate sufficient support - minimum 60 percent of houses on the street - to introduce the change. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 1 hour from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Edwin Avenue, between Ruskin Avenue and Dupont Street. 2. Extended the overnight on-street permit parking hours currently in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily on Edwin Avenue, between Ruskin Avenue and Dupont Street, to be in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., Monday to Friday and from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., Saturday and Sunday.
