The official record

What the item proposed

In August 2019, I received a petition from the majority of Westmoreland Avenue residents requesting a one hour parking limit on Westmoreland Avenue, between Bloor Street West and Shanly Street, between the hours of 10 a.m. and 6 p.m., Monday to Friday. The current parking limit on this stretch of Westmoreland Avenue is the statutory unsigned three hour parking limit. The purpose of this motion is to have these regulations put in place.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized parking for a maximum period of one hour from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, from the first day to the 15th day of each month, April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the east side of Westmoreland Avenue, between a point 44.3 metres north of Bloor Street West and Shanly Street. 2. Authorized parking for a maximum period of one hour from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, from December 1 of one year to March 31 of the next following year, inclusive, and from the 16th day to the last day of each month, April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the west side of Westmoreland Avenue, between a point 44.3 metres north of Bloor Street West and Shanly Street. 3.
