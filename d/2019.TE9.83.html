Improving Pedestrian Safety - Pedestrian Pathway between Strathcona Avenue and Bain Avenue

AdoptedOct 10, 2019 · Toronto City Council

At a recent safety walk with residents there were questions about the status of a well-used pedestrian path that runs along the eastern ends of four dead end streets Wroxeter, Frizzell, Dingwall, and Bain. Establishing the ownership of this path will allow for improvements to be considered.

The official record

What the item proposed

At a recent safety walk with residents there were questions about the status of a well-used pedestrian path that runs along the eastern ends of four dead end streets Wroxeter, Frizzell, Dingwall, and Bain. Establishing the ownership of this path will allow for improvements to be considered. This pathway was once part of a now closed portion of Kiswick Avenue, but there are other sections where ownership is less clear.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed Transportation Services and Engineering and Construction to report to the December 3, 2019 meeting of the Toronto and East York Community Council on the ownership, management, and status of the pedestrian path, made up of the closed Kiswick Street that runs along the Eastern Terminus' of Wroxeter, Frizzell, Dingwall, and Bain, including Plan R3816 Part 3 & Plan R 3816 Part 2.
