What the item proposed

The City of Toronto (City) maintains portfolios of sinking funds in order to build up funds used to repay City debt when it matures. Sinking fund contributions are invested; the investment earnings help reduce the amount of City own-source cash used to repay amounts borrowed. Sinking funds form part of the City's consolidated cash balances. This report presents the City's Sinking Funds Audited Financial Statements for the year ended December 31, 2019 and provides highlights of the Sinking Funds' 2019 financial performance as of December 31, 2019. The Sinking Funds Audited Financial Statements have been prepared by management who is responsible for ensuring that proper internal controls have been applied to the presentations in each statement.
