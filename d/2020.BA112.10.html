Award of Ariba Doc Number 2509964355 to BFS Technologies Inc., o/a Taff-guard Filtration for Supply and Delivery of New Drive Belts and Filters for HVAC Equipment for Seniors Services and Long-Term Care Division

AdoptedDec 2, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The Bid Award Panel was asked to award a contract for Award of Ariba Doc Number 2509964355 to BFS Technologies Inc., o/a Taff-guard Filtration for Supply and Delivery of New Drive Belts and Filters for HVAC Equipment for Seniors Services and Long-Term Care Division. The recommended bid is $575,219 (including HST).

The official summary names a figure of $575,219.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
