No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Civic Appointments Committee to select candidates for interviews for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board. Following the interviews, the Civic Appointments Committee will recommend one candidate for appointment to City Council to fill a vacancy on the board.

What was decided

The Civic Appointments Committee: 1. Selected the following candidates to be interviewed at its next meeting for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board: INB2-4 INB2-23 INB2-85 2. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1, 2 and 3 to the report (July 13, 2020) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board.
