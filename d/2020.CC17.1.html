The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide Council with the results of the collective bargaining negotiations with TCEU, Local 416 - CUPE and to seek ratification and approval of the successfully negotiated Memorandum of Settlement between the City of Toronto and TCEU, Local 416 - CUPE.

What was decided

City Council on March 6, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council ratify and approve the Confidential Attachment 1, Memorandum of Settlement dated February 28, 2020, between the City of Toronto and TCEU, Local 416 - CUPE to effect a new collective agreement, as attached to the report (March 4, 2020) from the City Manager and the Chief People Officer. 2. City Council authorize staff to make the necessary amendments to rates of pay, benefits and other issues identified as agreed changes in the new Memorandum of Settlement. 3. City Council authorize staff to make the necessary adjustments to the 2020 Non-Program Expenditure Budget and Operating Budgets of the Divisions to reflect provisions of the Memorandum of Settlement. 4.
