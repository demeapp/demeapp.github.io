The official record

What the item proposed

In December 2019, the provincial and federal governments announced the Canada-Ontario Housing Benefit (COHB) program, a new funding program under the National Housing Strategy. The Province has confirmed Toronto's funding allocation for the first two years of the program, $7.5M in 2020-2021 and $9.9M ($2.4M new funding) in 2021-2022. This is estimated to provide 1,300 households with a housing benefit to assist with rental affordability challenges. Benefit amounts are estimated to range from $500 to $1,200 per month, based on a formula set by the Province that uses information such as household income, household composition, and the local average market rent. Housing benefits under COHB will be offered to households who have been on the centralized waiting list for rent-geared-to-income housing for a long period of time and households that fall within one of the priority groups.…
