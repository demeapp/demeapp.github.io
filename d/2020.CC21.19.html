The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek further instruction in relation to a development application for a properties at 599-631 Kennedy Road and related ongoing appeals at the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal. The LPAT has requested the parties schedule a case management conference for the summer of 2020, although no dates have been set at the time of this report. The purpose of this report is to seek City Council's direction with respect to newly filed revised plans for the site and to seek instruction with respect to the ongoing LPAT process. City Planning has reviewed the revised application and was consulted in the preparation of this report.
