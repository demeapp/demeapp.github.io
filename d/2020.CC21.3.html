The official record

What the item proposed

On December 13, 2018, City Council approved the Housing Now Initiative to activate 11 City-owned sites ("Phase One") for the development of affordable housing within mixed-income, mixed-use and transit-oriented communities. Phase One of the Housing Now Initiative is estimated to create approximately 10,750 new residential units with approximately 7,800 being purpose-built rental housing, including almost 3,900 affordable rental units. Work is well underway to advance Phase One of the Housing Now Initiative. This report recommends Council's approval of six additional sites (outlined in Appendix I) to the Housing Now Initiative. The additional six sites (the "Phase Two Sites") are estimated to add between 1,455 and 1,710 new residential units to the program, including approximately 1,060 to 1,240 purpose-built rental units with approximately 530 to 620 being affordable rental units.
