What the item proposed

In October 28, 2015, a Zoning By-law Amendment was submitted in regards to the land municipally known as 30 and 44 Zorra Street. On May 26, 2017, City Council's failure to make a decision was appealed to the Ontario Municipal Board, now known as the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal (the "LPAT"). On July 23, 2018, City Council adopted a recommendation from the City Solicitor to accept a settlement offer dated March 2, 2018 (the "Settlement Offer") from the applicant Zorra Developments Limited (the "Owner") and authorized the City Solicitor and appropriate City Staff to attend the LPAT hearing in support of the revised proposal of a 35-storey residential building, as detailed in the Settlement Offer.
