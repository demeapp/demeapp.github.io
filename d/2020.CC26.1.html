The official record

What the item proposed

Pursuant to section 170(2) of the City of Toronto Act, 2006 (COTA), Ombudsman Toronto has concluded an Enquiry to review how the City of Toronto handles homeowners' third-party liability claims for basement flooding and sewer backups. We hereby table with City Council for its November 25 and 26, 2020 session the Enquiry Report, dated November 12, 2020.

What was decided

City Council on November 25 and 26, 2020, referred Item CC26.1 to the General Government and Licensing Committee for consideration.
