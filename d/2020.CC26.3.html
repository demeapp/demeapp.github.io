What the item proposed

At its meeting on March 26, 2018, City Council adopted the ConsumersNext Secondary Plan ("OPA 393"), which was the recommended Official Plan Amendment resulting from the ConsumersNext Planning Study (the "Study"). The Study reviewed the policy context for lands generally between Highway 404, Highway 401, Sheppard Avenue East and Victoria Park Avenue, but also included lands designated Mixed Use Area north of Sheppard Avenue East and East of Victoria Park Avenue (the "Study Area"). The Study examined ways to manage growth anticipated by recent policy changes for lands in the Study Area. OPA 393 was appealed by several appellants: 2450 Victoria Park Incorporated, the Armenian Community Centre, Elad Canada Incorporated ("Elad") Hydin Limited and Fontmil Investments Limited.
