What the item proposed

City Council adopted Official Plan Amendment No. 231 policies for the entire city following the Five-Year Official Plan and Municipal Comprehensive Reviews regarding employment lands. First Capital REIT, the owner of the property municipally known 85 Hanna Avenue, has appealed Official Plan Amendment 231 regarding the land use designation for 85 Hanna Avenue (the Lands) to the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal. The appeal sought to have the Mixed Use Areas designation on the Lands while Official Plan Amendment No. 231 re-designates the Lands as General Employment Areas. First Capital REIT also appealed Policy 3.5.2.6 of Official Plan Amendment 231 respecting cultural industry uses within the King Parliament Secondary Plan area, the King Spadina Secondary Plan area and the Liberty Village area of the Garrison Common North Secondary Plan on a city-wide basis.
