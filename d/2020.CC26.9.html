The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to the request for the General Manager, Parks Forestry and Recreation ("PFR") to report back to the November 25 and 26 meeting of City Council on options for keeping park washrooms open and accessible, including clearing pathways of snow and ice, through the upcoming winter. In addition, it provides preliminary information on the potential to retrofit park washrooms to withstand freezing temperatures and remain open year-round. The COVID-19 pandemic resulted in heavy use of parks and outdoor spaces during the summer months. Given the current public health advice to limit trips outside the home to essential activities only, including fresh air and exercise, it is expected that this higher use will continue through the colder months. What was decided

City Council on November 25 and 26, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council receive the report (November 18, 2020) from the General Manager, Parks Forestry and Recreation for information. 2. City Council request the Medical Officer of Health to review Toronto’s Public Health guidelines regarding access to public washroom facilities, and to work with the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation and the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management to support safely re-opening washrooms in all open community centres and parks. 3. City Council request the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation to take the necessary steps to ensure that in parks with outdoor skating facilities, washroom access in the parks is maintained between the time the rink buildings are closed and the opening of the seasonal washrooms. 4.
