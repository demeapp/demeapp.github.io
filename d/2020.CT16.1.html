The official record

What the item proposed

The St. Lawrence Centre Redevelopment Committee is interested in adding some additional members. The purpose of expanding the composition of the committee is to add additional members who would better represent the diversity of the City, as well as having a skill set in either architecture, theatre, redevelopment or real estate which would enhance the Committee’s deliberations in a much richer and more productive way. The current committee composition is 6 members to be drawn from the Board of Directors and the current membership is: Councillor Gary Crawford, Councillor John Filion, Robert J. Foster, Steven Levitan, myself as Chair, and there is one vacancy. (The current terms of reference and composition are attached as Appendix A). What was decided

The Board of Directors of TO Live: 1. Increased the membership of the TO Live St. Lawrence Centre Redevelopment Committee from up to 6 members to 11 members, by adding five (5) individuals who may be non-members of the Board. 2. Appointed the three (3) individuals identified in Confidential Attachment 1 to the letter (October 9, 2020) from the Chair, St. Lawrence Centre Redevelopment Committee - TO Live Board of Directors to the TO Live St. Lawrence Centre Redevelopment Committee. 3. Directed that the names of the individuals, and their biographies, be made public once appointed by the Board. 4. Directed the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live to bring forward a report to fill the remaining vacancies, in early 2021.
