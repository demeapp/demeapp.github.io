Toronto City Council · Decision

The accessibility committee receives a presentation on accessible recreation programs during COVID-19.

Official title: COVID-19 Response and Accessibility - Parks, Forestry and Recreation Programs

Adopted with changesNov 20, 2020 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Howie Dayton, Director, Community Recreation, and Sharon Butler, Manager, Community Recreation, Parks, Forestry and Recreation will give a presentation on COVID-19 Response and Accessibility related to various Parks, Forestry and Recreation programs. The presentation will include information on SwimTO, CampTO and any new or existing recreational programs and initiatives, detailing any efforts and modifications to accommodate persons with disabilities, and any employment opportunities for persons with disabilities.

What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee recommends that: 1. City Council request the Medical Officer of Health to review Toronto’s public health guidelines regarding access to public washroom facilities, and to work with the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation and the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management to support safely re-opening washrooms in all open community centres and parks. 2. Economic and Community Development Committee recommend that City Council direct the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, in collaboration with the Chief Communications Officer, to update the City of Toronto website to include all City recreation programs operating through the COVID-19 pandemic, indicating which programs are accessible, and communicate changes through all appropriate City channels, and report back to the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee…
