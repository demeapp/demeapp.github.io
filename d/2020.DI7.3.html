The official record

What the item proposed

Janet Lo, Senior Project Manager, Transportation Services will provide a presentation on the development of an oversight and management program for e-scooters, which are electric kick-scooters. The presentation will include information on recent provincial and city regulations, the status of the process to date, and will seek the Committee's feedback on accessibility considerations for the oversight of e-scooters.

What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee recommends to the Infrastructure and Environment Committee that: 1. City Council prohibit e-scooters for use in public spaces including sidewalks and roads, and direct that any City permission granted to e-scooter companies be guided by public safety, in robust consultation with people living with disabilities, and related organizations serving this population.
