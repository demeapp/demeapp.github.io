What the item proposed

The City of Toronto has undergone rapid changes, over the past three months, brought on by the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic, and the subsequent declaration of a State of Emergency. This situation is unprecedented, and the Emergency Operations Centre - led by Mayor Tory, Medical Officer of Health Eileen de Villa, and Toronto Fire Chief Matthew Pegg - has moved swiftly to bring forward new programs to safeguard public health and safety, and aid in economic recovery. The need for accelerated, emergency action is understandable, however it must also be acknowledged that the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee (TAAC) has a mandated role in providing advice to City staff, regarding policies, programs and services affecting people with disabilities. What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee recommends to the Executive Committee that: 1. City Council direct the Executive Director, Social Development, Finance and Administration, the General Manager, Shelter, Support and Housing Administration, and the Executive Director, Housing Secretariat to bring forward presentations on how the City is specifically supporting vulnerable people with disabilities in Toronto during and beyond COVID-19, to the September 24, 2020 meeting of the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee. 2. City Council direct the General Manager, Transportation Services, to bring forward presentations on CurbTO, ActiveTO, CafeTO, and any other new transportation programs and initiatives to address COVID-19, to the September 24, 2020 meeting of the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee. 3.
