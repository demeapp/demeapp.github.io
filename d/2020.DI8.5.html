The official record

What the item proposed

According to multiple media reports, Mayor John Tory announced that the City of Toronto may be forced to implement “terrible and devastating” service cuts to a multiplicity of essential city services including emergency, fire, recreation, shelter and transit services, as well as reducing spaces in city-run long-term care facilities as early as Fall 2020 in response to a $1.5 billion dollar financial shortfall brought about by the COVID-19 pandemic. Please see the below CBC news report for further details: https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/toronto/toronto-covid-19-may-22-1.5580045 These “terrible and devastating” service cuts will have significant implications for people with disabilities and seniors in particular as the cuts include a 73.6 per cent reduction of the TTC's Wheel-Trans service resulting in a loss of more than four million rides and a 50 per cent reduction of spaces in…

What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee recommends to the Executive Committee that: 1. City Council direct the City Manager and the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer to ensure that people with disabilities are consulted and/or have opportunity to provide input prior to Fall 2020 on any decisions related to service cuts. 2. City Council direct the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer to apply a disability policy lens to all City of Toronto budget processes to ensure that accessibility and inclusion become central to budget planning and that accommodation costs and resources are addressed organizationally as per human rights legislation and Ontario Human Rights Commission Policy on Ableism 9.3: “…The costs of accommodation must be distributed as widely as possible within the organization so that no single department, employee, customer or subsidiary is burdened with the expense.…
