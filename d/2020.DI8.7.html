Economic Advancement Opportunities for Persons Living with Disabilities

Adopted with changesJul 3, 2020 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to direct the City Manager in partnership with Economic Development, and Employment and Social Services examine the City of Toronto’s role in and provide a report on opportunities to support the economic advancement for persons living with disabilities.

The official record

What the item proposed

Striving to obtain, and sustain meaningful employment is a barrier regularly faced by people living with disabilities. A supportive and ongoing employment program is essential for the vitality and longevity of persons with disabilities who are seeking to access employment and training opportunities for their daily living goals and achievements as citizens within our society. An exploration into the feasibility of a partnership with the Province of Ontario and the City of Toronto to allocate a socio-economic development strategy for persons with disabilities in relation to job creation and advancement opportunities would greatly impact the success of these individuals. What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee recommends to the Economic and Community Development Committee that: 1. City Council direct the City Manager, in partnership with Economic Development and Culture and Toronto Employment and Social Services, to examine the City of Toronto’s role in, and provide a report on, opportunities to support the economic advancement for persons living with disabilities, including but not limited to: a. Support services and vendors relevant to the safety, success, and well-being of persons with disabilities for employment provisions which have been researched, and reviewed. b. Internal employment opportunities to help foster inclusion, diversity and accessibility for persons with disabilities by providing internal job vacancies with the City of Toronto. 2.
