What the item proposed

At its meeting on February 11, 2020, the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee considered Item FA2.6, Draft Work Plan for the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee 2019-22.

What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee: 1. Requested the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, to invite City officials, together with representatives from l'Université de l'Ontario français, to present to the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee in 2020 on the City's role in the implementation and start-up phase of the project. 2. Requested the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, to provide an overview of current partnerships with other cities and countries, including any Memorandums of Understanding currently in place, at the June 12, 2020 meeting of the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee. 3.
