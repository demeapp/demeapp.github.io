The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on February 20, 2020, the Toronto Music Advisory Committee considered Item MA3.1, City-Owned Spaces for DIY (Do-It-Yourself) Music Organizations.

What was decided

City Council on June 29 and 30, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, to work with the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, to develop the terms of a pilot program for DIY (Do-It-Yourself) music organizations to use City-owned spaces at lower than market value and report to the September 24, 2020 meeting of the Toronto Music Advisory Committee. 2. City Council direct the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, to work with the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, to develop an inventory of City-owned properties suitable for tenancy by DIY (Do-It-Yourself) music organizations at lower than market value and report to the September 24, 2020 meeting of the Toronto Music Advisory Committee.
