Collaborating to Enhance Pathways to Innovation and Growth in Toronto's Food and Beverage Manufacturing Sector

The Economic and Community Development Committee was asked to consider collaborating to Enhance Pathways to Innovation and Growth in Toronto's Food and Beverage Manufacturing Sector.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an update on the City's support for Toronto's food and beverage sector. Over 60,000 people are employed in the food and beverage manufacturing sector in the Greater Toronto Area, and there are more than 2,000 food and beverage manufacturing companies in the Toronto CMA and over 1,000 in Toronto alone (Source: Statistics Canada). In consultation with industry partners, City staff commissioned an outside consultant, through a competitive Request for Quotations (RFQ) process, to develop a road map to support the growth of the sector over the next 10 years (see Attachment 1). What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee: 1. Directed the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, to report in the third quarter of 2021 with an update on the progress of the inter-divisional working group in support of the food and beverage sector. 2. Requested the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, to re-engage the Food and Consumer Products of Canada on the concept of a food and beverage manufacturing campus in Toronto that works with post-secondary institutions and report in the second quarter of 2021.
