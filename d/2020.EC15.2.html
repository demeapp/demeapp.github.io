The official record

What the item proposed

Cabbagetown Youth Centre informed the City in late fall 2019 that they were experiencing a funding crisis that would result in the closure of the organization in early 2020 and requested emergency operating funding to continue operations throughout the year. Council approved one-time funding of $160,900 gross and $0 net, fully funded from the Tax Rate Stabilization reserve. This report provides the Council-directed update on the organization's use of these funds and the result of funding-related conditions to complete a program and financial audit and a transition plan. Cabbagetown Youth Centre worked to compile with all City funding requirements. The Neighbourhood Group provided trusteeship support and retained the services of a reputable consultant.
