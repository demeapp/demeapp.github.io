What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Blanket Contract 47020330 awarded to Sani Gear Incorporated, as a result of the Request for Quotation (RFQ) 0105-16-0062for the non-exclusive provision of a care and maintenance program for Firefighting Structural Bunker Suits for the City of Toronto's Fire Services. The total amendment being requested is for an additional $1,306,958 net of all applicable taxes and charges, ($1,329,961 net of HST recoveries) for the third option year period (January 1, 2020 to December 31, 2020), and for the fourth and final option renewal year period commencing on January 1, 2021, and ending on December 31, 2021, revising the total current Blanket Contract value from $1,458,183 to $2,765,141 net of all applicable taxes and charges. What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 - Financial Control By-law, granted authority to amend Contract 47020330 with Sani Gear Incorporated, for the non-exclusive provision of a care and maintenance program for Firefighting Structural Bunker Suits for the City of Toronto's Fire Services, by an additional amount of $1,306,958 net of all taxes and charges, ($1,329,961 net of HST recoveries), revising the current contract value from $1,458,183 net of all taxes and charges, ($1,483,847 net of HST recoveries) to $2,765,141 net all taxes and charges ($2,813,807 net of HST recoveries).
