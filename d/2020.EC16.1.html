The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this staff report is to bring forward an implementation action plan for the City's COVID-19 response for homelessness services for the next 12 months, as part of critical second wave planning efforts to ensure the shelter system is positioned to respond effectively if there is a resurgence of COVID-19 this fall. The shelter recovery strategy supports the City's broader COVID-19 resurgence plan, which includes supporting vulnerable populations as one of five key priority areas for ensuring preparedness for future resurgences of COVID-19. The report also provides information on Shelter, Support and Housing Administration's 2021 Shelter Infrastructure Plan.
