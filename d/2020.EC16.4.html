The official record

What the item proposed

The Fire Chief and General Manager, Toronto Fire Services, is reporting back to the Economic and Community Development Committee on a process to review and implement potential measures concerning misadventures along the Scarborough Bluffs including cost recovery measures for rescues. Parks, Forestry and Recreation, Municipal Licensing and Standards, and Toronto Fire Services will continue to work collaboratively to develop proactive strategies to prevent these misadventures. The responsibility for promoting safety and preventing misadventures of the Scarborough Bluffs relies on the efforts of multiple City divisions working in collaboration. Current mitigation efforts include signage and fencing, public education focused on increasing awareness of safe park usage, and by-law enforcement. What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee: 1. Requested the Fire Chief and General Manager, Toronto Fire Services, the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, and the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards, to provide signage and education materials in traditional Chinese language to promote safety and prevent misadventures at the Scarborough Bluffs.
