The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of the report is to provide an overview of the process that was used to establish the temporary shelter at 30 Norfinch Drive as requested by City Council through MM25.29, Process to Select and Open Temporary Sites for Physical Distancing in Shelters. Shelter, Support and Housing Administration conducted a comprehensive search for physical distancing spaces for shelter residents as part of the City's COVID-19 response. As part of these ongoing efforts, the City identified the Holiday Inn Express North York located at 30 Norfinch Drive for use as a temporary shelter for physical distancing. Properties were assessed by an interdivisional team against By-law and program requirements. The site met all requirements of the selection process and the City signed a lease for the property commencing May 17, 2020.

What was decided

City Council on December 16, 17 and 18, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council receive the report (November 18, 2020) from the Deputy City Manager, Community and Social Services, for information. 2. City Council request the General Manager, Shelter, Support and Housing Administration to undertake the process outlined in Item 2017.CD19.6 headed "Proposed New Engagement and Planning Process for Emergency Shelters" which was approved by City Council on April 26, 27 and 28, 2017, prior to entering into negotiations for a lease extension for the temporary shelter located at 30 Norfinch Drive.
