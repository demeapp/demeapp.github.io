What the item proposed

In the context of COVID-19, when many households are facing economic uncertainty, eviction prevention programs are critical strategies to minimize the flow to people into homelessness. Eviction prevention is also identified in the City's HousingTO Action Plan as a key strategy in ensuring positive outcomes for households in Toronto and reducing pressure on the shelter system. In alignment with the Interim Shelter Recovery and Infrastructure Implementation Plan, the City has already made a significant commitment to expanding supports for households at risk of eviction to prevent people from becoming homeless due to eviction during the pandemic.
