This is an information item for Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: This report provides an update and information regarding Exhibition Place business continuity planning for the restart, recovery, and rebuild plans, as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an update and information regarding Exhibition Place business continuity planning for the restart, recovery, and rebuild plans, as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic. The financial impact to the Board's 2020 Operating and Capital Budget from the cancellation of events and closure of tenants operations due to the global pandemic is the subject of a separate report.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Received the report (June 10, 2020) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place for information.
