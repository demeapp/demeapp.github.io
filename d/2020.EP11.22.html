Toronto Police Services Board Mounted Unit Occupancy Arrangement - 15 Nova Scotia Avenue (Horse Palace)

AdoptedJun 24, 2020 · Toronto City Council

Board of Governors of Exhibition Place was asked to consider toronto Police Services Board Mounted Unit Occupancy Arrangement - 15 Nova Scotia Avenue (Horse Palace).

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that the Board approve a five (5) year occupancy arrangement with The City of Toronto for the Police Services Board ("PSB") for its Mounted Unit commencing on January 1, 2020 and expiring on December 31, 2024; for the premises within the Horse Palace which is presently occupied by the Toronto Police Service Mounted Unit Head Quarters, substantively on the terms and conditions of the existing lease and the terms outlined in Appendix A of this report.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved a five (5) year occupancy arrangement with the City of Toronto for the Police Services Board's Mounted Unit commencing on January 1, 2020 and expiring on December 31, 2024; for the premises located within the Horse Palace occupied by the Toronto Police Service Mounted Unit on the same terms and conditions as the existing lease and the terms outlined in Appendix A to the report (June 10, 2020) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place.
