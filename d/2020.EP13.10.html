The official record

What the item proposed

On October 22, 2019, Exhibition Place received an Ontario Labour Relations Board (OLRB) ruling approving the termination of Bargaining Rights with CUPE Local 5116 and Exhibition Place Security Guards. As a result, the employees in this former Bargaining Unit were now required to become non-union Board employees. At its meeting of February 11, 2020, the Board of Governors authorized the Chief Executive Officer to engage a consultant to conduct a Market Analysis of compensation levels for the employees in the Security Group, and directed the Chief Executive Officer to report back in early 2020 on the design and financial implications of the implementation of the Market Analysis design.
