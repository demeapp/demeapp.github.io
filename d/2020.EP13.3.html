The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends entering into a ten (10) year sign agreement with Branded Cities Company Canada ("BCC") (formerly, "Clear Channel Outdoor Company Canada") for two digital signs at Lakeshore Boulevard which are proposed to replace the two existing static displays billboards signs. The agreement is conditional on obtaining the necessary City approvals, including any amendments, if applicable, to the City's Municipal Code Chapter 694, Signs, General. The agreement is to be effective on the date the new signs are installed and becomes operational, which is anticipated to be prior to the expiry of BCC current agreement. The current 15-year agreement with BCC is set to expire on July 31, 2022.
