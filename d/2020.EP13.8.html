What the item proposed

The City of Toronto made a decision in March to cancel all programs and events up to June 30, 2020 due to COVID-19 pandemic risk to public health and safety. Provincial Public Health Officials have further restricted gathering numbers to a limit of 50 persons indoors. Since March, nearly every Exhibition Place Show Organizer and Meeting Planner client have invoked their Force Majeure clause in their License Agreement to cancel or postpone their 2020 events. The same meeting and event cancellation is occurring in every convention centre across Canada. In addition to the cancellation of shows/meetings, Tenants such as Liberty Grand, QE Theatre, Aqua Dolce Resto Venue and Medieval Times have been completely closed while other tenants have opened in very limited capacity and therefore the loss of related ancillary revenue, such as Parking and Show Services from their closure.
