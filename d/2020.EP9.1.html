The official record

What the item proposed

A number of Exhibition Place employees should be recognized by the Board for recent outstanding accomplishments: 1. 2019 Corporate Social Responsibility Employee of the Year Mr. Terry Day is recognized as the 2019 Corporate Social Responsibility Employee of the Year. Mr. Day in achieving Platinum Level status has demonstrated his personal commitment to social responsibility and environmental sustainability at Exhibition Place. The Corporate Social Responsibility Program commenced in 2014 and provides to each staff member their own report card on their individual everyday use and practices, so that each employee can see and set their personal goals for improvements. The areas focused on are: mode of transportation to work; energy savings in the office; paper consumption; participation in our recognition program; and volunteer participation in various staff committees and community…

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Applauded and extended congratulations to Mr. Terry Day as the 2019 Corporate Social Responsibility Employee of the Year. 2. Applauded and extended congratulations to Ms. Ina Toncescu on being recognized as the 2019 Employee of the Year. 3. Applauded and extended congratulations to Ms. Debbie Sanderson for her outstanding efforts on the 2019 United Way City of Toronto Campaign.
