The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of December 17 and 18, 2019, City Council referred Item EX11.25 back to the Executive Committee for further consideration at its January 23, 2020 meeting. Speaker Nunziata also ruled that the Confidential Attachment on this Item was a public document.

What was decided

City Council on January 29, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council endorse the replacement of the St. Lawrence Centre for the Arts, located at 27 Front Street East with a new re-imagined centre as a state-of-the-art cultural and civic hub for the City’s creative communities and the community at large, in particular not-for-profit performing arts organizations, centred on its historic role as a landmark centre for economic and cultural activities, subject to satisfactory programmatic, community building, business and funding plans being developed. 2.
