What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to correct issues related to the delegation of authority for the delivery of emergency social services by granting the Director, Office of Emergency Management the authority to enter into a service agreement with the Canadian Red Cross for the provision of emergency social services. In 2010, City Council authorized the General Manager, Shelter, Support and Housing Administration to enter into an agreement with the Canadian Red Cross for the provision of emergency social services (formerly referred to as emergency human services). City Council on January 29, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the Director, Office of Emergency Management to provide emergency social services for curbside incidents and emergencies. 2.
