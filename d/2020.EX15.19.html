What the item proposed

At its meeting on January 22, 2020, the Toronto Police Services Board was in receipt of a report from Chief Mark Saunders with regard to the City of Toronto Council Decisions – 2017 Annual Statistical Report. The Board received the Chief’s report and agreed that a copy of this report be forwarded to the City of Toronto Executive Committee. A copy of the Board minute P9/2020 regarding this matter is attached.

What was decided

City Council on July 28 and 29, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Chair, Toronto Police Services Board to report to the December 10, 2020 meeting of the Executive Committee on the progress made by the Toronto Police Services Board to release its Open Data Sets to the City of Toronto's Open Data Portal.
