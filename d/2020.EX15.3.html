Management/Non-union Employees, Accountability Officers and Elected Officials Benefit Plans

AdoptedJul 28, 2020 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to direct that the Active Benefits Plan for Management/Non-union employees, Accountability Officers and Elected Officials, be amended as outlined in Attachment 1 to this report.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide recommendations regarding the proposed changes to the benefits plan for Management/Non-union employees, Accountability Officers and Elected Officials. The proposed changes outlined in this report arise from staff's review of the benefit plan changes, following the recently concluded collective bargaining with TCEU, Local 416 and CUPE, Local 79.

What was decided

City Council on July 28 and 29, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend the Active Benefits Plan for Management/Non-union employees, Accountability Officers and Elected Officials, as outlined in Attachment 1 to the report (July 7, 2020) from the City Manager and the Chief People Officer. 2. City Council approve the requirements to provide medical certificates for Management/Non-Union employees as outlined in Attachment 1 to the report (July 7, 2020) from the City Manager and the Chief People Officer. 3. City Council amend the Pre-65 Retiree Benefit Plan for Management/Non-union employees and Accountability Officers, where applicable, for employees who retire on or after August 1, 2020, as outlined in Attachment 1 to the report (July 7, 2020) from the City Manager and the Chief People Officer.
