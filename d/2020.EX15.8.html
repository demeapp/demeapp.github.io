What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide City Council with the Operating Variance for the five months ended May 31, 2020 as well as projections to year-end. This report also requests City Council's approval for amendments to the 2020 Approved Operating Budget that have no impact on the City's 2020 Approved Net Operating Budget. Since mid-March, the City of Toronto, consistent with other major Canadian and Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area municipalities has been experiencing significant financial impacts, both in the form of added costs and revenue losses as a direct result of the COVID-19 pandemic.
