What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide an update on the draft Surface Transit Network Plan (STNP) and seek Council feedback and endorsement on the proposal for conducting public consultation on the draft STNP. Following this consultation, the final STNP will come forward to Executive Committee and City Council for approval in June 2021. In 2018 Transportation Services Division, City Planning Division and the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) jointly initiated the Surface Transit Network Implementation Study (STNIS) through a consulting assignment. The study aims to provide a roadmap of policies, governance, evaluation and prioritization along with an actionable implementation plan - the Surface Transit Network Plan (STNP) - for enhancing surface transit priority across the City.
