Official title: Approval of Temporary Signage to Support Community Information Meetings for the RBC Canadian Open in June 2020

AdoptedJan 8, 2020 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Golf Canada will hold the 2020 RBC Canadian Open at St. George's Golf and Country Club from June 8 -14, 2020. Previously St. George's hosted the event in 2010. In order to accommodate the event, there are temporary road closures, parking restrictions and permits required. The venue also includes a partnership with the Toronto District School Board, at Richview Collegiate. Golf Canada are hosting a number of information meetings for residents and stakeholders. Although there are notification procedures, one or more temporary mobile signs would be a beneficial supplement in providing notice of the meetings to the public, and to advise of the road closures. Municipal Code Chapter 693 outlines the requirements for signs being used to advertise community events, and does not have a procedure to permit variance or appeal to the criteria which regulate temporary signage. What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Recognized the public information meetings held by Golf Canada in advance of the RBC Canadian Open 2020 as community events, and notwithstanding the requirements of Chapter 693- Signs: Subsection 17 d.5, 24 a.1, and 24 c, permit Golf Canada to erect temporary mobile signs in more than one location and in the right of way, subject to the satisfaction of the General Manager, Transportation Services, with the purpose to promote citizen participation in the meetings.
