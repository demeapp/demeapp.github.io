What the item proposed

This report provides information and identifies a preliminary set of issues regarding the application to amend the Official Plan, the former City of York Zoning By-law No. 1-83 (as amended by By-law No. 244-2005) and City-Wide Zoning By-law No. 569-2013, for the lands municipally known as 1956, 1966, 1970, 1972, 1974, 1976, 1978, 1980, 1982, 1984 and 1986 Weston Road and 1 Little Avenue. The application proposes two tall buildings with heights of 29-storeys (100 m in height, including the mechanical penthouses) connected by a 12-storey podium (41 m in height). The buildings would contain 3,991 m 2 of non-residential floor area on the first and second floors and 592 residential units above.
