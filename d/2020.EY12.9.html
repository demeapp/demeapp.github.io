Standing Prohibition - Viella Street

PostponedJan 8, 2020 · Toronto City Council

Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south and west side of Viella Street, between Symes Road and Tarragona Boulevard.

Council put off deciding this item.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to recommend that the existing parking prohibition, in effect at all times, on the south and west side of Viella Street be amended to prohibit standing at all times. The proposed prohibition was requested by area residents, in consultation with staff and the Ward Councillor, to address concerns regarding excessive and long-term parking and congestion in the area.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council deferred consideration of this Item to its meeting on February 5, 2020.
