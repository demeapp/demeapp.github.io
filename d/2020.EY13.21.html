Status Update for Comprehensive Traffic Study in Mimico

AdoptedFeb 5, 2020 · Toronto City Council

Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to request the General Manager, Transportation Services to provide an update on the status of Item EY32.85 - Request for Comprehensive Traffic Study in Mimico, including work completed to date, parties responsible, immediate next steps, estimated completion date, and opportunities to accelerate completion.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In July 2018, I requested a comprehensive traffic study for the Mimico neighbourhood of the former Ward 6, roughly bounded by Royal York Road to the west, Cavell Avenue to the north, Louise Street to the east, and Lake Shore Blvd West to the south, to assess the appropriateness and feasibility of further speed limit reductions, turning restrictions, vertical deflection traffic calming measures (e.g. speed humps, raised intersections, raised pedestrian crossings), Horizontal deflection traffic calming measures (e.g. chicanes, curb extensions, mid-block pinch-points, traffic islands, traffic circles, raised median islands, curb radius reductions, directional closures to narrow existing one way streets), improved pedestrian crossings and navigation throughout affected area (in conjunction with or complementary to traffic calming measures), and any other measures that may be appropriate in…

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services to provide an update on the status of Item EY32.85 - Request for Comprehensive Traffic Study in Mimico, including work completed to date, parties responsible, immediate next steps, estimated completion date, and opportunities to accelerate completion, to the March 12, 2020 meeting of Etobicoke York Community Council.
