Speed Limit Amendment - Glenhaven Street (Strathnairn Avenue to Yore Road)

Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to reduce the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on Glenhaven Street, between Strathnairn Avenue and Yore Road.

The official record

What the item proposed

Glenhaven Street is classified in the City's Road Classification System as a local road. This street is located in the residential community east of Keele Street, north of Eglinton Avenue West. The legal speed limit on Glenhaven Street is 50km/h. Sidewalks are present on both sides of Glenhaven Street from Strathnairn Avenue to Lonborough Avenue. A missing section of sidewalk exists on the east side of Glenhaven Street, between Lonborough Avenue and Yore Road. A request has been submitted to Transportation Services to program this missing section of sidewalk for construction. With the adoption of the Vision Zero Road Safety Plan, the speed limits on local roads will eventually be reduced to 30 km/h, with reports from Transportation Services on this initiative to begin in 2021. What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on Glenhaven Street, between Strathnairn Avenue and Yore Road.
