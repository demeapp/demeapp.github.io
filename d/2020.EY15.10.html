The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to prohibit parking at all times on the east side of Dayton Avenue, between Sinclair Street and the first laneway south of The Queensway. This is in response to a petition received from affected residents on Dayton Avenue and in consultation with the Ward Councillor.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, on the east side of Dayton Avenue, between Sinclair Street and the first laneway south of The Queensway, running east from Dayton Avenue. 2. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Etobicoke York District, to consult with the appropriate Toronto Parking Authority officials to explore additional parking opportunities for businesses along The Queensway in the vicinity of Royal York Road and Dayton Avenue.
