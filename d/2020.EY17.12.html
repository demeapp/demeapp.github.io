The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is regarding a matter that the Etobicoke York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to obtain approval from Etobicoke York Community Council to grant an appeal and enter into an encroachment agreement for a proposed fence and existing hedges at 51 Mimico Avenue.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Approved the appeal of the proposed fence at 51 Mimico Avenue, and approved entering into an Encroachment Agreement contingent on the conditions provided by the Transportation Services Division, as well as all recommendations and requirements set out by other divisions within the City of Toronto.
