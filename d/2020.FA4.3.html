The official record

What the item proposed

The President, Université de l'Ontario français, will provide an update and overview of the implementation and start-up phase of the university. On March 10, 2020, the Economic and Community Development Committee, on the recommendation of the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee (Item FA2.6), requested the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, to invite City officials, together with representatives from l'Université de l'Ontario français, to present to the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee in 2020 on the City's role in the implementation and start-up phase of the project.

What was decided

The Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee received the Item for information.
