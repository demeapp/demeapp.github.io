What was decided

The Film, Television and Digital Media Advisory Board recommends that the Economic and Community Development Committee: 1. Direct that the Film Commissioner and Director of Entertainment Industries report back to the next meeting of the Film, Television and Digital Media Advisory Board on April 14, 2020, on current green initiatives already underway in the city, as well as the top ten park and public realm locations that could be considered for power drops.
