Metropolitan Toronto Pension Plan - Termination/Wind-Up and Surplus Distribution

AdoptedJan 29, 2020 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to amend Schedule A to By-law 159-2018, as amended, governing the Metropolitan Toronto Pension Plan (Metro Plan), to provide for the distribution of the surplus to the members of the Metro Plan, in accordance with the Surplus Sharing Agreement dated May 11, 2018.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks authority from City Council to: 1. amend the By-law governing the Metropolitan Toronto Pension Plan (Metro Plan) to provide for surplus distribution to the members of the Metro Plan, in accordance with the Surplus Sharing Agreement dated May 11, 2018; 2. terminate the Metropolitan Toronto Pension Plan (Metro Plan), effective January 31, 2020, and distribute the surplus assets remaining following the January 8, 2020 merger of the Metro Plan with OMERS ("remaining assets"); and 3. introduce a Bill to repeal By-law 159-2018, as amended, upon completion of the distribution of the remaining surplus assets of the Metro Plan.

What was decided

City Council on January 29, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend Schedule A to By-law 159-2018, as amended, governing the Metropolitan Toronto Pension Plan, to provide for the distribution of the surplus to the members of the Metro Toronto Pension Plan, in accordance with the Surplus Sharing Agreement dated May 11, 2018. 2. City Council approve the termination of the Metropolitan Toronto Pension Plan, outlined in Schedule A to By-law 159-2018, as amended, effective January 31, 2020, and authorize the City Solicitor, following the distribution of the Metro Toronto Pension Plan's remaining assets in accordance with the Surplus Sharing Agreement dated May 11, 2018, to introduce a Bill to repeal By-law 159-2018, as amended.
