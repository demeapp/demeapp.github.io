What the item proposed

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place, as landlord, and 1309320 Ontario Inc. ("1309320"), as tenant, entered into a lease dated December 7, 2000, for premises known as the Ontario Government Building and certain adjacent lands. The lease was subsequently assigned by 1309320 to Liberty Grand Entertainment Complex Inc. with the Board's consent (such lease, as assigned, is hereinafter the "Existing Lease"). The term of the Existing Lease is twenty (20) years, commencing on January 1, 2001 and expiring on December 31, 2020 (the "Existing Term"). Throughout the Existing Term, the Tenant has undertaken significant renovations in the leased premises at a value of approximately $4.650 million excluding food and beverage equipment.
