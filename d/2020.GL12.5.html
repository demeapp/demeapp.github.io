Potential Download of Responsibility from the Province of Ontario for the Prosecution of Provincial Offences Act Offences

AdoptedJun 29, 2020 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to consider potential Download of Responsibility from the Province of Ontario for the Prosecution of Provincial Offences Act Offences.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Ministry of the Attorney General (MAG) in January 2020 commenced a confidential consultation process with municipalities across Ontario in relation to the current Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) and Local Side Agreements (LSA) which exist between municipalities and the Province of Ontario in relation to the responsibility for prosecuting Provincial Offences Act offences. The existing MOU and LSA between the Province and the City of Toronto was entered into at the time of the initial download of certain prosecutions formerly conducted by the Province commencing in 2002. Confidential Attachment 1 provides further information regarding this matter.

What was decided

City Council on June 29 and 30, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (February 24, 2020) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council direct that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (February 24, 2020) from the City Solicitor be made public after the negotiations with the Province are completed and at the discretion of the City Solicitor. Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (February 24, 2020) from the City Solicitor remains confidential at this time in accordance with the City of Toronto Act, 2006 as it concerns a position to be applied to negotiations carried on by or on behalf of the City of Toronto.
