What the item proposed

The Rexdale Community Hub located at 21 Panorama Court plays an important role in developing solutions to the complex issues in the North Etobicoke community by creating opportunities for engagement, and equipping local residents and stakeholders with the supports and services necessary to restore the health of the community. The purpose of this report is to obtain City Council authority to enter into Community Space Tenancy lease agreements with seven local community agencies collectively occupying 66,331 square feet within the Rexdale Community Hub facility located at 21 Panorama Court, Toronto, and to have each of the respective leased premises designated as a Municipal Capital Facility.
