The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks authority for the Director, Transaction Services, Corporate Real Estate Management, to approve certain licenses, easements, and consents required for the relocation of existing utilities, and amendments to existing leases with community boat clubs, as necessary for the Algonquin Island Bridge Rehabilitation Project (the Project").

What was decided

City Council on July 28 and 29, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the granting of licenses, easements and consents, and entering into of other agreements, as outlined below, on terms satisfactory to the Director, Transaction Services, Corporate Real Estate Management, in accordance with the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (June 22, 2020) from the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, to facilitate temporary and permanent utility relocations as may be required in connection with the Algonquin Island Bridge Rehabilitation Project (the "Project"): a. Licences to existing utility providers (the "Utility Providers"); b.
