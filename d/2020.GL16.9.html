The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek authority to amend the existing Ground Lease Agreement at 419-425 Coxwell Avenue, between the City of Toronto and New Frontiers Aboriginal Residential Corporation dated January 26, 2002, enabling the tenant to obtain refinancing to redevelop their property. This residential property currently provides 74 affordable rental units and financial restructuring will allow the tenant to construct 12 additional affordable rental units. In July 2018, City Council authorized this affordable housing project as a part of Open Door Program's competitive call for affordable rental housing applications. The project supports Open Door Program's objectives and supports the City's efforts to achieve targets of approving 5,000 affordable rental and 2,000 affordable ownership homes for low- and moderate-income residents by 2020.
