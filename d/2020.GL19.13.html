The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek authority from City Council to commence expropriation proceedings to acquire permanent easements on part of the properties municipally known as 429 Port Union Road, 433 Port Union Road, 437 Port Union Road, 28 Rozell Road, 33 Rozell Road, 2 Cameron Glen Boulevard, 446 Lawson Road and 24 Fanfare Avenue (collectively, the "Properties"). The acquisition of these easements is essential for the construction of the proposed Port Union Road Widening project and ancillary works to improve vehicular traffic as well as cyclist and pedestrian safety in the area. This report is the first stage of the expropriation process. After the application for approval to expropriate is authorized, staff will serve and publish notice on the registered owners of the Properties.
