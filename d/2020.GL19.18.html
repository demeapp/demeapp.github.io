The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek authority for the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, and the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, to amend eleven (11) non-competitive bridging blanket contracts to increase the contract values and to extend the contract expiry dates to May 31, 2021. The cumulative total increase in contract value is $3,950,000, net of Harmonized Sales Tax ($4,019,520 net of Harmonized Sales Tax Recoveries). This is required to ensure continuity of critical inspection and maintenance services related to Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning systems, boilers, water chillers, and cooling towers.
