The board receives a three-hour training session on anti-Black racism and equity in governance.

Official title: Training Session on Anti-Black Racism, Understanding Equity from Africentric Values and Principles, and Embedding Equity and Anti-Black Racism in Governance and Leadership

Adopted with changesOct 5, 2020 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Liben Gebremikael, Executive Director, TAIBU Community Health Centre, Dr. Kisha McPherson, Founder, Creating Consulting Group, and Kimberley N. Tull, Director, Community Development and Engagement, University of Toronto Scarborough, will provide a three-hour training session on Anti-Black Racism, Understanding Equity from Africentric Values and Principles, and Embedding Equity and Anti-Black Racism in Governance and Leadership in order to build the Board's capacity to provide equitable space for racialized and marginalized representation, further to the Board's decision on Item 2020.HL17.9 , Addressing Anti-Black Racism as a Public Health Crisis in the City of Toronto. The Board of Health will meet in closed session on this item in accordance with the provisions of the City of Toronto Act, 2006 which permits the closing of a meeting for the education or training of the Directors.

What was decided

The Board of Health: 1. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Attachment 2 remain confidential in their entirety, as they pertain to the education or training of the Directors.
