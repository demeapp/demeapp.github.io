What the item proposed

The Minister of Health publishes the Ontario Public Health Standards: Requirements for Programs, Services and Accountability as per Section 7 of the Health Protection and Promotion Act. The Standards identify the minimum expectations for public health programs and services to be delivered by Ontario's 34 boards of health. Boards of health are accountable for implementing the Standards, including the requirement to have a self-evaluation process of its governance practices and outcomes. This report contains the results of the self-evaluation of Toronto's Board of Health (BOH), conducted in February 2020. Overall, the BOH self-evaluation survey results were positive, including: - Verifying that there is strong governance on the BOH; - Highlighting effective relationships and informed decision-making; and - Identifying that the BOH is responsive to the immediate and long-term public health…

What was decided

The Board of Health: 1. Requested the Medical Officer of Health to conduct the next Board of Health self-evaluation in 2021 and report by the end of 2021 with the results of the self-evaluation.
