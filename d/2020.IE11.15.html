The official record

What the item proposed

This report is in response to a letter submitted to the Public Works and Infrastructure Committee, Item PW29.10, requesting the General Manager Transportation Services to consider the implementation of a local traffic vehicle identification program to alert traffic enforcement to allow local residents to access their communities, using otherwise prohibited routes. Transportations Services has consulted with Toronto Police Services and, after taking into consideration the ability to enforce such exemptions, the potential impacts on equal access to transportation infrastructure and the administration of such a program, does not recommend implementing a pilot local traffic vehicle identification program.

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. Received the report (December 20, 2019) from the General Manager, Transportation Services for information.
