What the item proposed

Since 2014 the City Of Toronto has experienced an increase of pedestrian injuries and fatalities. According to available data from the Toronto Policy Database, the majority of these collisions have taken place at intersections and pedestrian crossings. As the Vision Zero action plan moves forward, Toronto should consider exploring as many safety measures as possible to strengthen the comprehensive 5 year plan. Feedback and fresh ideas from the community can be used to guide responsible change in our neighbourhoods as we strive to make our City safer. A group of students from Bayview Glen School Independent School recently approached my office to share their innovative ideas on how to further combat pedestrian injuries and fatalities in Toronto.
