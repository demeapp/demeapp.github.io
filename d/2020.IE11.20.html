Establishing a Park at Dufferin Transfer Station

AdoptedJan 29, 2020 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to direct the General Managers of Solid Waste Management Services and Parks, Forestry and Recreation, to explore the feasibility of transforming "Parkland Location number 8" into public parkland and/or green space.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Duke Heights BIA (DHBIA) in Ward 6 is home to over 2500 businesses, employs over 31,000 people and hosts one of the largest concentration of manufacturing enterprises in Toronto. Throughout the years the DHBIA has moved away from the old perceptions of the area and created a new and vibrant community that’s connected and committed to growth. In August 2017 the DHBIA released a report highlighting parkland deficiencies within their boundaries. The report focuses on several pockets of land within employment areas that could be transformed into public parkland space. The BIA has then focused their initial efforts on transforming a piece of City owned land at the Dufferin Transfer Station, which they have labelled as "Parkland Location #8" (see Attachment #1). What was decided

City Council on January 29, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Solid Waste Management Services and the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, to explore the feasibility of transforming Parkland Location 8 identified in Attachment 1 to the letter (January 6, 2020) from Councillor James Pasternak into public parkland and/or green space, and report back by the end of the third quarter of 2020 with the findings.
