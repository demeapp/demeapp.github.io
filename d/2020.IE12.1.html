What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to advise of the results of Request for Proposal (RFP) No. 9117-19-7165, for detailed design services during construction and post construction for the Midtown Toronto Relief Storm Sewer Project and request the authority to enter into an agreement with Hatch Ltd., (Hatch) in the amount of $28,653,248 net of all taxes, $32,378,170 including HST and all applicable charges ($29,157,545 net of HST recoveries).

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1.
