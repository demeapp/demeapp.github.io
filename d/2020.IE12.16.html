Prohibiting Snow Clearing Activity within Commercial Properties Located 100m or less from Residential Areas between 12am to 6am

AdoptedMar 11, 2020 · Toronto City Council

This item concerns prohibiting Snow Clearing Activity within Commercial Properties Located 100m or less from Residential Areas between 12am to 6am.

A citywide decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I received a letter from a number of my constituents who are concerned about the noise generated by snow clearing activity on commercial properties located adjacent to residential areas during nighttime hours. These constituents would like the City to amend its by-laws to restrict snow clearing or any other winter maintenance activity involving mechanical equipment, between the hours of 12am to 6am, on commercial properties that are located 100m or less from residential areas.

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee requested that: 1. The Deputy City Manager, Infrastructure and Development Services report to the Infrastructure and Environment Committee on September 22, 2020 on the feasibility of prohibiting snow clearing activity within commercial properties located 100 metres or less from residential areas between 12 a.m. and 6 a.m.
