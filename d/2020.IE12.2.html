Contract Awards for Tender Ariba Doc #2155284733 and Tender Ariba Doc #2155392775 for the 2020 Local Road Resurfacing Program

AdoptedMar 11, 2020 · Toronto City Council

Infrastructure and Environment Committee was asked to consider contract Awards for Tender Ariba Doc #2155284733 and Tender Ariba Doc #2155392775 for the 2020 Local Road Resurfacing Program.

The official summary names a figure of $28,136,230.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to: (1) advise of the results of Tender Ariba Doc #2155284733, Contract No. 20ECS-TI-13LR, for local road resurfacing in Wards 4 through 6, 8, 9, and 12, and request authority to award the contract to D. Crupi & Sons Limited, in the amount of $28,136,230 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($28,631,430 net of HST recoveries); and, (2) advise of the results of Tender Ariba Doc #2155392775, Contract No. 20ECS-TI-14LR, for local road resurfacing in Wards 10, 11, 13 through 16, and 19 through 22, and request authority to award the contract to Sanscon Construction Ltd., in the amount of $32,902,199 net of applicable taxes and charges ($33,481,279 net of HST recoveries).

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environmental Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 195-8.4A of the Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 195 (Purchasing By-Law), granted authority to award Contract No. 20ECS-TI-13LR, Tender Ariba Doc #2155284733, for local road resurfacing in Wards 4 through 6, 8, 9, and 12, in the amount of $28,136,230 net of all taxes ($28,631,430 net of HST recoveries) to D. Crupi & Sons Limited, having submitted the lowest compliant bid and meeting the specifications in conformance with the Tender requirements. 2. In accordance with Section 195-8.4A of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 195 (Purchasing By-Law), granted authority to award Contract No. 20ECS-TI-14LR, Tender Ariba Doc #2155392775, for local road resurfacing in Wards 10, 11, 13 through 16, and 19 through 22, in the amount of $32,902,199 net of all taxes ($33,481,279 net of HST recoveries) to Sanscon Construction Ltd.,…
