The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend the value and extend the term of the Purchase Order 6041832 with GHD Limited (GHD), formerly Conestoga-Rovers and Associates Ltd. for engineering services related to the contract administration for the design, build and commissioning of the City’s Dufferin Organics Processing Facility Expansion. GHD was originally awarded a contract in 2013 to provide contract administration and technical support for the expansion of the Organics Processing Facility at the Dufferin Solid Waste Management Facility. There have been delays in the completion of the expansion project by W.S. Nicholls, the vendor responsible for the design, build and commission of the facility and as a result of the Contractor's delay, an amendment is required to the Purchase Order to increase the agreement value and to further extend the term to 2022. What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1.C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Financial Control), granted authority to amend and extend Purchase Order No. 6041832 with GHD Ltd. (GHD) formerly Conestoga-Rovers & Associates Ltd., as a result of Request for Proposal No. 9117-13-3073 by an additional amount of $490,772.63, excluding taxes and charges ($499,410.23 net of HST recoveries), revising the current total value of $1,809,984.99, excluding taxes and charges to $2,300,757.62, excluding taxes and charges ($2,341,250.95 including HST recoveries) and authorize an extension of the agreement with GHD Ltd. from December 31, 2021 to December 31, 2022.
