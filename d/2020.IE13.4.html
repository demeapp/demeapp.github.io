The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek Council approval for the time extension of existing temporary road closures and traffic regulation amendments within the tunnelled and at-grade section of the Eglinton Crosstown Light Rail Transit (ECLRT) project corridor. A number of road/lane closures have been implemented along the corridor to assist with on-going construction activities for the construction of the Keelesdale, Fairbank, Forest Hill, Chaplin, Eglinton and Laird stations as well as at-grade work along the corridor. A time extension of several of these closures is required to complete construction of the LRT project. City staff have reviewed Metrolinx's requests for extension and are satisfied that they are reasonable. Staff will continue to work with Metrolinx to maximize the effectiveness of traffic management and construction scheduling on the project corridor.
