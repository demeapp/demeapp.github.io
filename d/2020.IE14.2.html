What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend the value and extend the term of Purchase Order 6037723 with Cole Engineering Group Ltd. for professional engineering services for the installation of non-emergency generators at the Ellesmere Pumping Station awarded under Request for Proposal 9117-12-7279. Cole Engineering Group Ltd. was originally awarded a contract in June 2013 to provide contract administration, and site supervision related to construction of non-emergency generator upgrades at the Ellesmere Pumping Station. What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1.C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Financial Control), granted authority to amend and extend Purchase Order No. 6037723, issued to Cole Engineering Group Ltd., for the provision of professional engineering services for the installation of non-emergency generators at the Ellesmere Pumping Station by an additional amount of $272,696 net of all taxes and charges ($277,495 net of HST recovery) revising the current purchase order value from $1,789,319 net of all taxes and charges ($1,820,811 net of HST recovery) to $2,062,015 net of all taxes and charges ($2,098,306 net of HST recovery) and extend the delivery date from December 31, 2021 to December 31, 2023.
