What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to negotiate and enter into a new or amending agreement with Aecon Construction and Materials Limited (Aecon), the contractor for the F.G. Gardiner Expressway Rehabilitation Project: Section 1 – Jarvis Street to Cherry Street (Contract No. 18ECS-TI-01GE), to construct a new sanitary sewer and watermain (Water Works) along Lower Jarvis Street, intersecting Aecon's Gardiner Expressway construction work zone. The terms and conditions of the proposed agreement are set out in Confidential Attachment 1 of the report.
