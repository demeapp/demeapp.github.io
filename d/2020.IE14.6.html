The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend the value of Contract No. 47021416 (GFL Environmental Inc.) and Contract No. 47022273 (Renewi Canada Ltd, now Convertus Canada Ltd.) for Organics (formerly Source Separated Organics or SSO) processing services. The increased funds are required for continued processing of the City's Organics by external contractors as a result of delays in the commissioning of the Dufferin Organics Processing Facility (DOPF) and also higher tonnages of Organics being collected through the Green Bin Program.
