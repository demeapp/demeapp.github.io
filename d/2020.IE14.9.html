What the item proposed

This report is in response to a direction from the Infrastructure and Environment Committee on December 5, 2019, to report back on an analysis of options the City could employ to assist residents looking to replace private side lead water service pipes. The options identified for analysis included consideration of assistance via the Toronto Renovates program, grants and loans. A companion request for information centered on the potential cost of adding the pipe replacement cost to a homeowner borne line of credit with a financial institution is also considered in this report. The City has previously studied lead pipe replacement initiatives on several occasions in the past and had prepared implementation steps for a lead pipe replacement loan program in 2015.
