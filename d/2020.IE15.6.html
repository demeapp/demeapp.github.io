The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to establish non-competitive bridging contracts with the current Arboricultural Services providers Davey Tree Expert Company of Canada (Blanket Contract # 47021964), Ontario Line Clearing & Tree Experts (Blanket Contract # 47021967) and W.M. Weller Tree Service (Blanket Contract # 47021966) for the continued non-exclusive supply of all labour, equipment and material necessary to perform Arboricultural Services at various locations for the Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division from January 1, 2021 to June 30, 2021. This work is required to mitigate public liability and to maximize the tree canopy potential by ensuring healthier and long-lived trees through a proactive, systematic maintenance regime.
