The committee was asked to award a sewer upgrade contract to Memme Excavation for flood protection Area 22.

Official title: Award of Contract Number 20ECS-LU-02FP to Memme Excavation Company Limited for Construction of Sewer Upgrades for Basement Flooding Protection Program Study Area 22

AdoptedOct 6, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The official summary names a figure of $24,103,194.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to advise of the results of Ariba Document Number 2418616021, Contract Number 20ECS-LU-02FP, for the construction of storm and sanitary sewers, watermains and other related improvements in Basement Flooding Protection Program Study Area 22, which is bounded by Lawrence Avenue East, Eglinton Avenue East, the CN railway and Droxford Avenue and request authority to award the contract to Memme Excavation Company Limited, in the amount of $24,103,194 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($24,527,411 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries).

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 195-8.4 of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 195 (Purchasing), granted authority to award Contract Number 20ECS-LU-02FP, Ariba Document Number 2418616021, for the construction of storm and sanitary sewers, watermains and other related improvements in the area bounded by Lawrence Avenue East, Eglinton Avenue East, the CN railway and Droxford Avenue, in the amount of $24,103,194 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($24,527,411 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) to Memme Excavation Company Limited, having submitted the lowest compliant bid and meeting the specifications in conformance with the Request for Tenders requirements.
