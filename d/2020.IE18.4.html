What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to respond to a number of requests made by City Council of the General Manager, Transportation Services concerning the following road safety items: - evaluation of city-wide major arterial speed limit reductions; - review of student project idea: Pedestrian Avoidance Warning System (P.A.W.S.); - MOVE update and sharing traffic volume and collision data; - Right Turn on Red Prohibition Strategy, including research on the impact of driver frustration on road safety; - improving road safety of school age children through Automated Speed Enforcement; and - 2020 collision trends to date.
