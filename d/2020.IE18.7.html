The official record

What the item proposed

At its virtual meeting held on September 17, 2020, the Toronto Police Services Board was in receipt of a report dated August 26, 2020, from Ryan Teschner, Executive Director & Chief of Staff, regarding a City of Toronto Council Decision with respect to Free Floating Car-Share Pilot – Review. At its meeting, the Board approved the foregoing report and the following recommendations: 1. That the Board refer this report to the Chief of Police for consideration and direct the Chief to liaise with City officials and others, as deemed appropriate. 2. That the Board forward a copy of this report to the Infrastructure and Environment Committee.

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee received the letter (October 22, 2020) from the Toronto Police Services Board for information.
