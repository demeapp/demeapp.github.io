Strategies for Assisting Live Music Venues

Adopted with changesFeb 20, 2020 · Toronto City Council

This is an information item for Toronto Music Advisory Committee: City staff made a presentation on exploring options to assist the long-term sustainability of live music venues in Toronto.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City staff will make a presentation on exploring options to assist the long-term sustainability of live music venues in Toronto. The Toronto Music Advisory Committee Chair will lead a discussion with members to shape further direction and work on this issue and on criteria for identifying live music venues.

What was decided

The Toronto Music Advisory Committee recommends that: 1. The Economic and Community Development Committee request the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture to provide an update on the status of the Strategies for Assisting Live Music Venues at the May 13, 2020 meeting of the Toronto Music Advisory Committee.
