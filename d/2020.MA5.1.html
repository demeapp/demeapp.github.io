COVID-19 Impacts on Toronto's Music Community

Adopted with changesMay 13, 2020 · Toronto City Council

This is an information item for Toronto Music Advisory Committee: The Chair of the Toronto Music Advisory Committee will lead a discussion with the members of the Committee to identify impacts of COVID-19 on different parts of Toronto's music ecosystem, including areas of urgency and threat, gaps in existing support and resources, and members' recommendations for the City's role in mitigation and recovery across the sector.

How the vote went

The official record

What the item proposed

The Chair of the Toronto Music Advisory Committee will lead a discussion with the members of the Committee to identify impacts of COVID-19 on different parts of Toronto's music ecosystem, including areas of urgency and threat, gaps in existing support and resources, and members' recommendations for the City's role in mitigation and recovery across the sector.

What was decided

The Toronto Music Advisory Committee: 1. Requested the Co-Chairs to summarize and document the Toronto Music Advisory Committee's concerns and suggestions on mitigating the impacts from COVID-19 to the music sector as well as ideas and strategies for recovery, and submit the communication to Councillor Crawford in his capacity at the Culture Roundtable.
