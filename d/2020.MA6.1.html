The official record

What the item proposed

Members of the Toronto Music Advisory Committee and staff from Economic Development and Culture will make a presentation on the current conditions affecting musicians in Toronto, including COVID-related impacts, available financial assistance, and career development programs. The Chair of the Toronto Music Advisory Committee will lead a discussion with Committee members to shape their recommendations on these issues.

What was decided

The Toronto Music Advisory Committee recommends to the Economic and Community Development Committee that: 1. City Council request the Government of Canada to: a. ensure that adequate financial support continues to be accessible for self-employed and gig workers; and b. extend the Canada Emergency Wage Subsidy at current funding levels for music-industry employers and workers. 2. The Economic and Community Development Committee request the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture to explore developing further online resources for artists through the Music Office, Film and Entertainment Industries, and report to the next meeting of the Toronto Music Advisory Committee. 3.
