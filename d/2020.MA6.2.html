Current Impacts on Live Music Venues

Staff from Economic Development and Culture and Toronto Public Health provided an update on the current impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic on live music venues in Toronto, including related public health guidelines and insurance concerns.

The official record

What the item proposed

Staff from Economic Development and Culture and Toronto Public Health will provide an update on the current impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic on live music venues in Toronto, including related public health guidelines and insurance concerns. The Chair of the Toronto Music Advisory Committee will lead a discussion with Committee members to shape their recommendations on these issues.

What was decided

The Toronto Music Advisory Committee recommends to the Economic and Community Development Committee that: 1. City Council request the Government of Ontario to: a. provide long-term financial support for live music venues and music companies working in live music; b. apply a fair and more flexible reopening guidelines established for other businesses and venues to live music venues, while ensuring all necessary and rigorous health protocols are accounted for and in place; c. convene a meeting to discuss insurance concerns from live music venues; d. institute an immediate ban on evictions for music businesses; and e. enforce mandatory participation in Ontario-Canada Emergency Commercial Assistance (OCECRA) program for property owners. 2. City Council request the Government of Canada to: a. extend the Canada Emergency Commercial Rent Assistance (CECRA) program and make it more effective…
