Enhanced Support for Toronto Live Music Venues

Adopted with changesDec 3, 2020 · Toronto City Council

Staff from Economic Development and Culture and Finance and Treasury Services provides an update on work being done to explore the feasibility of the City of Toronto's role in supporting an insurance program for live music venues.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Staff from Economic Development and Culture and Finance and Treasury Services will provide an update on work being done to explore the feasibility of the City of Toronto's role in supporting an insurance program for live music venues. Staff will also provide an update on the property tax reduction program for live music venues.

What was decided

The Toronto Music Advisory Committee recommends that: 1. The Economic and Community Development Committee request the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer and the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture to continue to explore City of Toronto options for supporting an insurance program for live music venues, and to bring forward a report with potential options directly to the Economic and Community Development Committee in March 2021.
