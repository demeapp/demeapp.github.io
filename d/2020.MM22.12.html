What the item proposed

This Motion seeks to release $3,200 of Section 37 funds to Heritage Toronto for a heritage plaque commemorating William (Billy) John McKay Jr. born in York, September 15, 1862 - April 15, 1943. William (Billy) McKay (1862-1943) was a farmer born in York Township. Between the World Wars, he helped establish the community of East York when he donated and sold significant portions of his land in the vicinity of Coxwell Avenue and Mortimer Avenue. The East York Municipal Building, R. H. MacGregor School and East York General Hospital were built on his former land. In the 1930's, however, tensions arose between Mr. McKay and East York, which saw his farm as an obstacle to the town’s growth. Mr. McKay was determined to keep his property and rejected purchase offers. In old age, he became unable to maintain his farm and he died in Lakeshore Psychiatric Hospital in 1943.
