The official record

What the item proposed

In December 2017, Toronto City Council adopted regulations regarding short-term rentals that were designed to balance the need to protect existing housing stock and the quality of life in residential communities with the desire of Torontonians to partake in the short-term rental market. The City’s Zoning By-Law amendment was appealed to the Local Planning and Appeal Tribunal. In November 2019, the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal issued a ruling that dismissed the appeals and upheld City Council’s adopted Zoning By-Law amendments for short-term rentals. Fairbnb Canada reported in February of 2020 that there were more than 23,000 short-term rental listings advertised on Airbnb’s website pre-COVID-19.
