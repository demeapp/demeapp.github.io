The official record

What the item proposed

Under the leadership of the Mayor, the Medical Officer of Health and the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards, the City of Toronto urged Landlords and Owners of multi-tenanted buildings to adopt new health and safety measures and a rigorous cleaning routine to reduce the risk of residents from COVID-19. Unfortunately, several months later, those that were applauding the City's initiative to protect tenants from COVID-19 are now reporting that very little has been done to implement these critical guidelines. In a recent poll, the Federation of Metro Toronto Tenants' Associations found that 55 percent of Landlords had provided any new cleaning practices to address COVID-19.
