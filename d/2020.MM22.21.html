What the item proposed

Construction is a critical industry in Toronto. It creates jobs, with many corollary benefits that are part of a strong economy. Without exaggeration, now more than ever it is critical to make sure our construction industry can thrive as we begin the post-COVID-19 recovery period. For the duration of the COVID-19 shutdown no new Committee of Adjustment applications were accepted or approved. Committee of Adjustment meetings resumed on June 3, 2020, but at a reduced capacity. There are currently 1,250 active Committee of Adjustment applications, which can only be expected to increase now that property owners and developers can submit applications again. Typically, each Committee of Adjustment panel reviews an average of 40 applications per hearing, meeting twice per month.
