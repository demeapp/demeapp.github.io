What the item proposed

In response to the ongoing COVID-19 crisis which has emphasized the pre-existing need to help people experiencing homelessness access permanent housing with supports, it is critical that the City continue to take immediate action to increase the supply of permanent affordable housing. This includes improving the condition of our existing housing stock to make it safe, secure and suitable for the needs of residents. This Motion seeks Council’s approval to transfer $6,000,000 to the Shelter, Support and Housing Administration’s Operating Budget, on a one time basis, in order to transfer funds to Toronto Community Housing Corporation for the renovation and modernization of affordable rental homes at 502-508 Parliament Street, fully funded from Section 37 (Planning Act Reserve Fund) community benefits from various developments within Ward 13.
