What the item proposed

At the conclusion of the City Council meeting scheduled for July 28 and 29, 2020, City Council will be recessing until its meeting of September 30 and October 1, 2020. During that period it is likely that there will be decisions arising from applications to the Committee of Adjustment which will be matters of concern to the City and would warrant the City's attendance at the Toronto Local Appeal Body (or the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal where there is a related appeal) either on an appeal by the City itself or on the applicant's appeal to the tribunal.
