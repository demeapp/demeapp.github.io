What the item proposed

At its meeting of January 31, 2017, Toronto City Council adopted Item TE20.7, thereby approving the recommendations in the report from the Director, Community Planning, Toronto and East York District to enact a proposed Zoning By-law amendment for the lands at 440, 444, 450 and 462 Front Street West; 425, 439, 441 and 443 Wellington Street West; 6 - 18 Spadina Avenue and 1 and 19 Draper Street to permit a large-scale mixed-use development ("The Well"). The comprehensive redevelopment proposal provides for seven mixed-use buildings with residential, office and retail uses, as well as public parkland and privately-owned publicly-accessible open space. Overall, 1,537 dwelling units are proposed, with a mix of residential tenure including rental and ownership housing, as well as 105,364 square metres of office space and 44,824 square metres of retail space.
