What the item proposed

City Council decided in October 2019 to authorize Toronto Water to enter into necessary agreements with Noventa Energy, Inc. ("Noventa") for Noventa to facilitate a pilot for a wastewater energy transfer project at Sunnybrook Hospital and, following the result of the pilot, to determine whether to continue the project for much longer duration. However, with the following three developments – a change to the pilot site to Toronto Western Hospital (after Council amended its October 2019 decision), a decision among City staff that the Energy and Environment Office should lead this effort, and a new approach that would require a longer term commitment from the City -- City staff realized that new Council authority would be needed.
