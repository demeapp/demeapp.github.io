The official record

What the item proposed

This Motion seeks authorization to release $15,000 in Section 45(9) funds to Heritage Toronto for two heritage plaques to recognize both the heritage and cultural significance of City Park Co-op in the Church Wellesley Village. City Park was the first Modern, multi-building apartment complex in Toronto and, at the time of its construction in 1954, the biggest single residential project in the country. The buildings, designed by architect Peter Caspari, replaced a block of housing considered substandard during a post-war push to improve living conditions in the City. Caspari’s City Park, located between Church, Wood, and Alexander streets, consisted of three, 14-storey buildings with large open spaces between each one, which allowed more light to reach individual units.
