219-231 Dundas Street East - Northbound Left Turn Prohibition - by Councillor Kristyn Wong-Tam, seconded by Councillor Mike Layton

AdoptedNov 25, 2020 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Kristyn Wong-Tam, seconded by Councillor Mike Layton, asks City Council to prohibit northbound left turns at all times from the driveway at 219-231 Dundas Street East, located on the south side of Dundas Street East at a point approximately 41 metres east of George Street. A mixed-use development consisting of a tower height of 21 storeys with a three storey podium is in the process of being constructed by Menkes Development Ltd. at the municipal addresses of 219-231 Dundas Street East.

The official record

What the item proposed

A mixed-use development consisting of a tower height of 21 storeys with a three storey podium is in the process of being constructed by Menkes Development Ltd. at the municipal addresses of 219-231 Dundas Street East. The subject site is located on the south side of Dundas Street East, east of George Street and west of Pembroke Street. As a condition of the Site Plan Agreement (Site Plan Application 18 145012 STE 27 SA), a northbound left-turn prohibition at all times is required at the driveway access at 219-231 Dundas Street East and the developer is required to advise the City in advance of the anticipated date of first occupancy of the project, so that the required By-law can be enacted. The expected occupancy of the project will be June 2021. The subject turn prohibition is required to improve operations and safety by prohibiting outbound left turns at all times onto Dundas Street…

What was decided

City Council on November 25 and 26, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit northbound left turns at all times from the driveway at 219-231 Dundas Street East, located on the south side of Dundas Street East at a point approximately 41 metres east of George Street.
