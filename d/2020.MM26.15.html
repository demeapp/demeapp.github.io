What the item proposed

The HousingTO 2020-2030 Action Plan identifies the critical need to protect the existing supply of affordable rental housing across the City. This Motion seeks Council’s approval to transfer $150,000 to the Shelter, Support and Housing Administration’s Operating Budget, on a one time basis, to provide funding to St. Clare's Multifaith Housing Society, fully funded from Section 37 (Planning Act Reserve Fund) community benefits from the developments at 124 and 128 Pears Avenue, 275 Albany Avenue and 420 Dupont Street and 126-128 Hazelton Avenue within Ward 11. This funding will support modernization of kitchens in 10 existing affordable rental housing units at 138 Pears Avenue. This work will improve the quality of homes for existing and future residents.
