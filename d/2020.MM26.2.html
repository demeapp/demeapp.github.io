The official record

What the item proposed

A Sole Proprietor has submitted an application for a new liquor licence to the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario for a premises at 1375 Danforth Road, Units 11 and 12, operating under the name Tasty Jolluf (the “Premises”). The applicant is seeking a liquor licence for a capacity of 91 patrons indoors. Due to the premises' close proximity to a residential area, and close proximity to another problematic licenced establishment, impacts associated with the operation of the Premises may disturb neighbouring residents. This application for a liquor licence is not in the public interest unless certain conditions, addressing the concerns of the community, are attached to the licence. Under no circumstance should a liquor sales licence be granted at the Premises without appropriate conditions attached. This matter is deemed urgent as the placard posting for objections will occur any day…

What was decided

City Council on November 25 and 26, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the City Clerk to advise the Registrar of the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario that the liquor licence application for the proposed establishment located at 1375 Danforth Road, Units 11 and 12 (the "Premises”), is not in the public interest having regard to the needs and wishes of the residents, and that the Registrar should issue a Proposal to Review or Refuse the liquor licence. 2. City Council request the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario or the Licence Appeal Tribunal to provide the City with an opportunity to participate in any proceedings with respect to the Premises. 3.
