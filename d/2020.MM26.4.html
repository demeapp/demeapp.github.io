The official record

What the item proposed

The Dupont Street Regeneration Study, conducted in 2014, determined that certain lots on the north side of Dupont Street between Kendal Avenue and Ossington Avenue could appropriately accommodate a change from Employment Area to a Mixed Use designation on the front portion of the lots. As a result, the Dupont Street Corridor is experiencing a transformation as lots on north side of the street, which were previously industrial sites, are now being redeveloped with a mix of uses including restaurants, retail stores and residential uses. In addition, the built form in the area is also changing from one of industrial/commercial and low-density residential into more high-density residential and offices. What was decided

City Council on November 25 and 26, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the installation of signalized traffic control at the intersection of Dupont Street and Kendal Avenue. 2. City Council authorize and direct that installation of the signalized traffic control be co-ordinated with the development of 316-332 Dupont Street sites to the satisfaction of the General Manager, Transportation Services. 3. City Council direct the General Manager, Transportation Services to take all necessary action to implement signalized traffic control at the intersection of Dupont Street and Kendal Avenue, including adding it to the capital budget.
