The official record

What the item proposed

As part of the Second Exit Project, the Toronto Transit Commission is proposing to make Donlands Subway Station safer and convenient by constructing a new second exit building. City Council authorization is required to acquire temporary property interests (the "Easements") to temporarily relocate gas utility lines in support of the Project. Subsequent to City Council's authorization in 2019 to expropriate property interests for the construction of the Project, the Easements are new property interests, identified by the Toronto Transit Commission, and are necessary to install temporary gas utility lines to further facilitate the Project and provide power to the owners of the property on which the Easements are located during construction of the Project.
