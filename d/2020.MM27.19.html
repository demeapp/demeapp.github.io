Worldwide Christmas Eve Jingle 2020 - by Councillor Paula Fletcher, seconded by Councillor Brad Bradford

AdoptedDec 16, 2020 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Paula Fletcher, seconded by Councillor Brad Bradford, asks City Council to request the City Manager to work with City staff to promote Worldwide Christmas Eve Jingle 2020 festivities in Toronto.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Earlier in the pandemic, there was a world-wide movement to bang pots and pans in the evening to show support for front line workers. A similar campaign has been launched for Christmas Eve. Many Torontonians will be joining this a world-wide bell-ringing event at 6:00 p.m. on December 24, 2020 to bring some community joy to the holiday season. Torontonians have been asked to only celebrate the holidays with people in their household so that the current surge in COVID-19 cases in Toronto does not get worse. This event will allow them to follow public health measures and at the same time join their neighbours in a world-wide celebration.

What was decided

City Council on December 16, 17 and 18, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the City Manager to work with City staff to promote Worldwide Christmas Eve Jingle 2020 festivities in Toronto.
