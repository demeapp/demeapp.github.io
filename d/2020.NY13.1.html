What the item proposed

This report reviews and recommends approval of the application to amend the City's Zoning By-law 569-2013 and Zoning By-law 7625 for the former City of North York for the property at 2788 Bathurst Street and 515 Glencairn Avenue. The application proposes to construct a mixed-use mid-rise 9 storey building with a height of 33.5 metres (excluding mechanical penthouse). The building would have a total floor area of approximately 24,700 square metres, consisting of 1,000 square metres of street related retail space, approximately 1,600 square metres of hotel space with 23 suites, and 22,110 square metres of residential space with a total of 120 residential dwelling units resulting in a net density 5.05 times the area of the lot.
