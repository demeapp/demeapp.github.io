Toronto City Council · Decision

Re-opening of Items NY9.21 and NY8.20 with respect to Parking Amendments - Doris Avenue and Beecroft Road

AdoptedFeb 5, 2020 · Toronto City Council

North York Community Council was asked to re-open Item NY9.21 headed "Re-opening Item NY8.20 - Parking Amendments - Doris Avenue and Beecroft Road" and rescind its previous decision with respect to Item NY9.21.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Following the September 16, 2109 meeting of North York Community Council, staff from Transportation Services advised that technical amendments were needed with respect to Item NY8.20. These amendments were adopted on October 10, 2019 under Item NY9.21 but due to an administrative error, the reference numbers for parts of North York Community Council's September 16 decision to be deleted/amended were incorrect. As such, a further re-opening and amendment is required.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item NY9.21 headed "Re-opening Item NY8.20 – Parking Amendments - Doris Avenue and Beecroft Road" and rescinded its previous decision with respect to Item NY9.21. 2. Re-opened Item NY8.20 headed "Parking Amendments – Doris Avenue and Beecroft Road" for further consideration. 3. Deleted Part 27 of the Decision of North York Community Council Item NY8.20 and replaced it with the following new Part 27: "27. Authorized the pay parking regulation in effect from 10:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Saturday and from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday, at a rate of $2.00 per hour, for a maximum parking period of 3 hours and from 6:30 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, at a rate of $2.00 per hour, for a maximum parking period of 2.5 hours, west side of Beecroft Road, between a point 30.5 metres north of Park Home Avenue…
