The North York Community Council was asked to amend the existing maximum 30-minute parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Glen Park Avenue

AdoptedSep 10, 2020 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the maximum 30-minute parking regulations in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on both sides of Glen Park Avenue, east of Dufferin Street, be amended to be in effect for a maximum period of one-hour during the same hours. The longer duration was requested by local businesses for their customers. The proposed one-hour maximum parking regulations will address the concerns of local businesses and have a negligible effect on traffic operations.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing maximum 30-minute parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Glen Park Avenue, between a point 9 metres east of Dufferin Street and a point 36 metres further east, to be in effect for a maximum period of one hour. 2. Amended the existing maximum 30-minute parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Glen Park Avenue, between a point 9 metres east of Dufferin Street and a point 38 metres further east, to be in effect for a maximum period of one hour.
