The community council was asked to re-open and correct the Section 37 funds item for 2772–2778 Keele Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this motion is to request a re-opening of item NY14.26 adopted by North York Community on March 12th, 2020. The reopening seeks to correct the previous recommendations to address the scope of S.37 funds to be used.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item NY14.26, headed "2772-2778 Keele St. - Request for Zoning By-law Amendment to Reallocate Section 37 Funds Received from the development" and amended Recommendation 1 to read as follows instead: "1. Request the Director, Community Planning, North York District to bring forward a Zoning By-law Amendment report to the North York Community Council pertaining to 2772-2778 Keele St., to amend the Section 37 provisions of By-law No. 3-2005(OMB), and authorize that the Section 37 provisions of the site-specific zoning by-law be amended to specify that uncommitted funds be used for parkland and streetscape improvements in the vicinity of the development site".
