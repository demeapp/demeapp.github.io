The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting held on January 8, 2020 North York Community Council amended the parking regulations on Briar Hill Avenue, east of Dufferin Street, to allow parking on the north side of the street in front of newly constructed townhouses. http://app.toronto.ca/tmmis/viewAgendaItemHistory.do?item=2020.NY12.8. Subsequently, residents of Briar Hill Avenue complained that visibility through the curved section of the street fronting the new townhouses is obstructed by vehicles parked on the curve. Also, delivery drivers regularly park illegally on the south side of the street to make deliveries to the townhouses since they do not have an on-site loading area. As a result, two-way traffic is obstructed. Transportation Services staff observed operations on the street and concur with the residents' concerns. To address the above situation, it is recommended that stopping be prohibited at all…

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item NY12.8 - "Parking Amendments – Briar Hill Avenue". 2. Amended its decision with respect to Item NY12.8 by adding the following new Recommendations 4, 5, and 6: "4. Prohibit stopping at all times on both sides of Briar Hill Avenue, between a point 85 metres east of Dufferin Street and a point 70 metres further east. 5. Rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Briar Hill Avenue, between a point 85 metres east of Dufferin Street and a point 155 metres east of Dufferin Street. 6. Prohibit parking from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., daily, on the north side of Briar Hill Avenue, between a point 74 metres east of Dufferin Street and a point 11 metres further east."
