What the item proposed

Call Issued: August 21, 2019 Call Closed: September 27, 2019, Noon, Toronto time Call Number: Request for Proposal Number 20190821 Number of Addenda Issued: Four (4) Number of Bids: Four (4) Description: The purpose of this Request for Proposal (RFP) is to select a qualified company to partner with Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) for the operation and administration of the Bike Share Toronto system. Through a competitive bid process, TPA selected and contracted with Ward O'Farrell Consultants Inc., a Government of Ontario Vendor of Record consulting firm specializing in preparing, writing, and managing government procurement RFPs. What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Granted authority to award the contract for the management of the Bike Share Toronto system to Shift Transit Inc., providing the highest ranking proponent score and the lowest bid price, for a term of five years, plus two optional one-year extensions, at the sole option of the President, Toronto Parking Authority, in the estimated amount (five years) of $42,244,200.
