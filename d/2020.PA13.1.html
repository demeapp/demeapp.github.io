What the item proposed

This report seeks approval from the Board of Directors, Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) for an $11,250,000 purchase of new bicycle and system equipment, including the supply, delivery, and installation of approximately 1,850 bicycles, 160 stations, including 300 E-FIT pedal assist bikes, and 10 e-charging stations and ancillary elements, from PBSC Urban Solutions Inc. (PBSC), pursuant to the terms and conditions of an established Equipment Supply Agreement between TPA and PBSC. With the purchase of this equipment in 2020, Toronto will have a network of 6,850 bikes, including 300 e-bikes and 625 stations extending beyond the existing service area to west of High Park, north of Lawrence Avenue, and east of Victoria Park.
