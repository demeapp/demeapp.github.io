What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) has worked closely with the City of Toronto (City) since the activation of the Emergency Operations Centre (EOC) on March 12. TPA is participating in the Planning Task Force, Corporate Leadership Team and Human Resources (HR) Leadership Team. As the COVID-19 pandemic situation evolves, all City Agencies and Divisions, including TPA, have been aligned with the City's direction to scale back all non-essential services. Essential services that have remained operational are those that: - are required to meet legislative requirements. - support employee and public health and safety. - enable critical community services and supports. - support services necessary to keep essential services operating. - protect and operate vital infrastructure. - fulfill critical legal and financial obligations.
