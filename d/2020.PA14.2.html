What the item proposed

The rate structure established for each Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) Car Park is applicable to all users, regardless of special circumstances. Requests for special parking considerations are generally discouraged except those requests that meet the criteria outlined in Policy Resolution 2-11 - Special Parking Arrangements For Organizations (Appendix A). On a historical basis, TPA has provided special parking arrangements for a number of organizations that include Places of Worship and organizations involved in charitable work, such as the Lawyers Feed the Hungry Program operated by The Law Society Foundation. Throughout the COVID-19 pandemic, TPA has received multiple requests for special arrangements for parking at specific Car Parks and Bike Share rides.
