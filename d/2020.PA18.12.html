What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) has established a range of Board-enacted Policies which form the guiding principles and procedures by which it operates. This report seeks approval of recommended revisions to TPA Policy Resolution 4-1 Appendix 3: Vacations with Pay. The change in service milestone from 22 years to 21 years of service for vacation entitlements has been in effect since 2006. As 22 years is no longer a milestone for purposes of vacation entitlements, the reference to the 22-year milestone is no longer required. It is recommended that references to the 22-year milestone be removed. Vacation leave is an important part of work-life balance for employees, supporting both health and wellness.
