The Parking Authority board defers its on-street parking rate review to 2021.

Official title: 2020 On-Street Rate Review

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Every three (3) years Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) undertakes a comprehensive review of the hourly parking rates and the hours of operation of its on-street paid parking program. The purpose of the rate review is to evaluate performance of the program and ensure rates and hours of operation are set to encourage the turnover in parking throughout the day. The last comprehensive review was undertaken in October 2017. Given the widespread impacts that the Covid-19 pandemic has had on the commercial retail sector and in turn on parking demands in the city, TPA’s on-street paid parking program has seen a significant reduction in both the total number of transactions and revenues generated. What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Deferred the 2020 comprehensive On-Street Rate Review to 2021. 2. Forwarded the report (October 20, 2020) from the Acting President, Toronto Parking Authority to City Council for information.
