The official record

What the item proposed

Call Number: Request for Quotation Number FM2020-01 - Winter Maintenance Services at Toronto Parking Authority Call Issued: June 29, 2020 Call Closed: August 9, 2020 Number of Addenda Issued: One (1) Number of Bids: Four (4) Description: The non-exclusive provision of winter maintenance service for TPA off-street parking facilities from November 1, 2020 for a term of two (2) years ending May 1, 2022, plus one (1) optional one (1)-year extension at the sole option of TPA. Should the option be exercised, the Director, Parking Services, will request the Vice President of Operations to process the renewals under the same terms and conditions. TPA Parking Services team developed the operational and performance requirements and prepared the procurement documents. The contractual scope includes the following changes from the previous procurement document: - Pricing tables organized per month…

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Granted authority to award the contract for the provision of winter maintenance services (snow clearing, snow removal and salting) for Toronto Parking Authority off-street parking facilities to Across Canada Construction Ltd., having submitted the lowest bid and meeting all specifications in conformance with the Request for Qualification winter maintenance requirements, for a term of two (2) years, plus an optional extension of one (1) year, at the sole option of the President, Toronto Parking Authority, in the amount (two years) of $2,521,378 plus $252,138 as a contingency allowance (10 percent of the contract value over the two (2) year period), being the sum total amount of $2,773,516, net of all taxes and charges.
