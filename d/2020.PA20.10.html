The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with Section 27-3.6. of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 27, Council Procedures, public notice is hereby given that the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority, at its next regular meeting, intends to consider passing a procedure by-law for governing the calling, place, and proceedings of meetings in accordance with Section 189 of the City of Toronto Act, 2006.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority received the item for information.
