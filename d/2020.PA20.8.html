Contract Award - City of Toronto Request for Proposal Number 3401-16-3008 for Credit/Debit Card Payment Processing Services

AdoptedDec 14, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto Parking Authority was asked to award a contract for City of Toronto Request for Proposal Number 3401-16-3008 for Credit/Debit Card Payment Processing Services. The recommended bid is $30,168,984 (including HST).

The official summary names a figure of $30,168,984.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Call Number: City of Toronto Request for Proposal Number 3401-16-3008 Call Issued: July 8, 2016 Call Closed: August 5, 2016 Number of Bids: Two Description: The total contract award for credit/debit payment processing services for a five-year period, including all option years with the option to renew the contract for five additional separate terms of one year each at the sole discretion of the Treasurer was estimated to be $30,168,984 net of all applicable taxes. Costs were estimated as they are dependent on the volume of transactions paid by credit and debit cards. The contract commenced on February 1, 2017.

What was decided
