What the item proposed

This report recommends City Council approval of $4,846,556 in Section 37 funds to be provided to Riverdale Co-operative Homes Inc. ("Riverdale Co-operative") for the redevelopment of 685 Queen Street East in Ward 14. The report also provides an update on pre-development work completed to-date. City Council, at its meeting of June 18 and 19, 2019, authorized the Executive Director, Housing Secretariat, to provide $500,000 in funding to Riverdale Co-operative to be used for pre-development work at 685 Queen Street East. Council also directed the Housing Secretariat to report to the Planning and Housing Committee on the results of the pre-development work, including any capital and operating funding required to undertake the redevelopment project.
