The official record

What the item proposed

On September 30, 2020, City Council adopted Item No. "PH16.8 - Addressing Housing and Homelessness Issues in Toronto through Inter-governmental Partnerships" and directed the Executive Director, Housing Secretariat and the General Manager, Shelter, Support and Housing Administration to report to the October 20, 2020 meeting of the Planning and Housing Committee on a plan to shift future capital and operating funding from shelters to create permanent supportive housing and to include the plan as part of the 2021 Capital and Operating Budget submission.
