The official record

What the item proposed

The Keele Finch Plus Study was initiated in 2016 to develop a plan to guide future growth and change for the area around Keele Street and Finch Avenue West. The study leverages recent and planned rapid transit investment and encourages transit-supportive development. Following extensive review and consultation on the study, this report recommends adoption of a Secondary Plan for the Keele Finch study area and associated amendments to the Official Plan. The OPA provides a balanced plan to encourage growth, place-making and community building, leverages transit investment, and provides for compatible land uses and built form. Approximately 10,500 people currently live in the Keele Finch area, and there are approximately 8,000 jobs.
