What the item proposed

As the City of Toronto’s real estate agency, CreateTO manages the City’s real estate portfolio, develops City buildings and lands for municipal purposes and delivers client-focused real estate solutions to City divisions, agencies and corporations. For the year ending December 31, 2019, CreateTO generated revenue of $12.49 million, had expenditures of $12.49 million, and net income of $0.
