What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to inform the Board of Directors of CreateTO on the ongoing conversations between staff of CreateTO and the Toronto Public Library (“TPL”) with Wittington Properties Limited ("Wittington") and Capital Developments (“Capital”), on options relating to the City owned Deer Park Library site at 40 St. Clair Avenue East (the "Subject Property") provided a new and expanded library can be relocated within the abutting Wittington development at 1485 Yonge St ( the “Development”), currently the subject of an official plan amendment and rezoning application. CreateTO has been working with the Toronto Public Library and the City Planning Department to understand the opportunities that exist within this proposal for the Subject Property itself, and within the broader Yonge and St. Clair public realm and neighbourhood.
