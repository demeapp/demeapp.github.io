Toronto City Council · Decision

One Year Extension of Electronic Meeting Rules

AdoptedAug 25, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The Board of Directors of CreateTO was asked to amend By-law 1-2018 and adopt the draft bill in Attachment 1 to extend the remote electronic participation rules for a period ending one year following the termination of the COVID-19 emergency declaration under section 4 of the Emergency Management and Civil Protection Act.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of July 28 and 29, 2020, City Council adopted Item EX15.21, and in so doing has authorized its local boards to extend or adopt remote electronic meeting rules for a period of one year following the later of the termination of the Provincial emergency and the termination of the municipal emergency related to the issue of COVID-19.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Amended By-law 1-2018 and adopted the draft bill in Attachment 1 to the report (August 11, 2020) from the City Clerk to extend the remote electronic participation rules for a period ending one year following the termination of the COVID-19 emergency declaration under section 4 of the Emergency Management and Civil Protection Act.
