The CreateTO board delegates recruitment of a new CEO to its Vice-Chair.

Official title: Position of Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO

Adopted with changesOct 9, 2020 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As previously announced, Chief Executive Officer Brian Johnston is stepping down from his position on October 16, 2020. The Board should discuss a recruitment process to fill the position. In order to facilitate the search for, and appointment of, a Chief Executive Officer, it is proposed that the recruitment process be initiated under delegated authority to the Vice Chair of the Board, with any additional instructions from the Board.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Confirmed the appointment of Steven Trumper as the Interim Chief Executive Officer of CreateTO as of October 16, 2020 for a term that is the lesser of 18 months or until a successor is appointed. 2. Approved the replacement of Brian Johnston with Interim Chief Executive Officer Steven Trumper as an authorized signing officer and bank signing officer for CreateTO effective on October 16, 2020, and that effective October 16, 2020, the Board delegate the signing authorities of the Chief Legal Counsel and Corporate Secretary to the Associate General Counsel on an interim basis until the Interim Chief Executive Officer appoints a new Chief Legal Counsel and Corporate Secretary. 3.
