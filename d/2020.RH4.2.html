What the item proposed

The City of Toronto is a city where almost half the households are renters. According to the 2016 Census, Toronto’s housing market was comprised of 1,112,930 households with 53% living in ownership housing and 47% living in rental housing[1]. In December 2019, City Council approved the HousingTO 2020-2030 Action Plan ("HousingTO Plan") based on extensive public consultations and feedback. The HousingTO Plan is the City's blueprint to address issues across the housing spectrum including housing affordability, poor living conditions, displacements due to illegitimate evictions and lack of affordable housing choices. The HousingTO Plan also sets targets and outlined actions to preserve the City's existing rental housing stock.
