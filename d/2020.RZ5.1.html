What was decided

The TO Live Audit Committee recommends that the Board of Directors of TO Live: 1. Approve the 2019 fourth quarter financial operating results in Confidential Attachments 1, 2 and 3 to the report (January 28, 2020) from the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live. 2. Direct the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live to work with the City of Toronto's Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer on exploring options including the use of the Sony Centre Stabilization Reserve (XQ2031) and the Toronto Centre for the Arts Stabilization Reserve (XQ1060) to address the 2019 operating deficit. 3. Direct the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live to request funds from the TO Live Foundation Board to address the 2019 operating deficit. 4.
