The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of re-opening this item is to give members an opportunity to ask further questions on the very important safety concerns on our roads, and to allow for a broader discussion and an opportunity for members to move recommendations and amendments on speed limit reductions.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item SC11.8 - "Scarborough Minor Arterial and Collector Road Speed Limit Reductions as Part of Vision Zero Speed Management Strategy", adopted by Scarborough Community Council at its meeting on December 3, 2019. 2. Amended Attachment 3 to Item SC11.8 as follows: a. Removed the line: "Add - Galloway Road between Lawrence Avenue East and Guildwood Parkway (40 km/hr speed limit)"; and b. Added the following lines: i. "Add - Galloway Road between Lawrence Avenue East and CN Rail (30 km/hr speed limit)"; and ii. "Add - Galloway Road between CN Rail and Guildwood Parkway (40 km/hr speed limit)". 3. Amended Item SC11.8 by adding the following new Recommendations: "1. Added the following minor arterial road segments in Ward 24, Scarborough-Guildwood and reduced the speed limits to 40 km/h on: a. Orton Park Road from Ellesmere Road to Lawrence Avenue…
