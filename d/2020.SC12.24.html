The official record

What the item proposed

Port Union Road is classified as a minor arterial road with a speed limit of 60km/hr. The road is a key commuter route to the Rouge Hill GO Station for both local residents and individuals living in Pickering and northeast Scarborough. In 2021/2022, the City of Toronto will be widening Port Union Road from Lawrence Avenue East to Island Road. Work will include the addition of a second northbound lane, complete sidewalks on both sides, bicycle lands, landscaped median between Lawrence Avenue East and Clappison Boulevard and centre-turn lanes in some locations. When completed, this important project will address traffic congestion and improve conditions for all road users. Currently, City Transportation staff are advancing detailed design for this project. What was decided

Scarborough Community Council directed: 1. Transportation Services to undertake a traffic study on Port Union Road, between Island Road and Lawrence Avenue East, to determine if any advance green lights or arrows are warranted for improved safety and report back to Scarborough Community Council in Q4 2020.
