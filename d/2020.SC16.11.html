U-Turn Prohibition - Timberbank Boulevard

AdoptedJul 17, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The Scarborough Community Council was asked to prohibit east/westbound and north/southbound u-turn movements at all times on Timberbank Boulevard, between Carondale Crescent (east intersection) and Chaldean Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that the Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision. This report recommends a u-turn prohibition on Timberbank Boulevard, between Carondale Crescent (east intersection) and Chaldean Street. Prohibiting u-turns will provide a safer environment for all road users along this section of Timberbank Boulevard located adjacent to Timberbank Junior Public School.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Prohibited east/westbound and north/southbound u-turn movements at all times on Timberbank Boulevard, between Carondale Crescent (east intersection) and Chaldean Street.
