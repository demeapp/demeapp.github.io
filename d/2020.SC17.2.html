4620 Finch Avenue East - Zoning Amendment - Final Report

City Council was asked to approve a zoning change allowing a student residence at the rear of a private school site at 4620 Finch Avenue East.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes to amend the Zoning By-law to permit the development of a student residence building at the rear of an existing private school site at 4620 Finch Avenue East. The proposed development is consistent with the Provincial Policy Statement (2020) and conforms with A Place to Grow: Growth Plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe (2019). It conforms with relevant provisions of the Toronto Official Plan. The proposed use is provided for on lands designated Institutional Areas and compatible with the adjacent land uses. The development is appropriately integrated with the existing buildings and fits within the area context. It improves the site condition adjacent to the natural feature to the north of the site resulting in no adverse impacts.
