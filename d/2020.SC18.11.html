The official record

What the item proposed

In response to concerns brought forward by members of the community, I am bringing forward a motion to Scarborough Community Council to authorize the installation of two all-way stop controls, on East Avenue at Golders Green Avenue and East Avenue at Broadbridge Drive, respectively. East Avenue is a collector road with sidewalk on the west side and partial sidewalk on the east side. The speed limit is 50 km/hr with a reduction to 40 km/hr in a school zone. The road connects two collector roads: Island Road and Lawrence Ave East. Currently, there are no-all-way-stop controls on East Avenue between Lawrence Ave East and Kirkdene Drive. These intersections are located 1.7 kilometers apart. The West Rouge Community Association, which represents approximately 3,400 households, is concerned about excessive speed on East Avenue. What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized all-way stop control at the intersection of East Avenue and Golders Green Avenue. 2. Authorized all-way stop control at the intersection of East Avenue and Broadbridge Drive.
