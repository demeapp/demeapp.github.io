What the item proposed

A 2020 Business Plan and Budget are presented for Board consideration and approval. It provides an overview of our priorities for the coming year, and outlines specific activities to be undertaken to achieve the stated objectives. The plan is guided by Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s Strategic Directions and Theory of Change, understanding of the greenhouse gas emission sources and reduction opportunities across the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area, perspectives on opportunities for climate policy implementation, and analysis of the successes and challenges experienced in our past work. It was designed and developed by the full staff team. Implementation of the Business Plan will be supported by the proposed 2020 Operating Budget of $5.6 million.
