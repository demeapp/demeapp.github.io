The official record

What the item proposed

The Audit Committee met on January 28, 2020 to review the scope of work for the 2019 audit as presented in the Audit Engagement Letter from Welch LLP, which was approved. This report provides minutes from the Committee meeting and recommends one amendment to the Audit Committee Terms of Reference.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Received Attachment 1 to the report (January 31, 2020) from the Chief Executive Officer - Minutes of the Audit Committee Meeting Held January 28, 2020 for information. 2. Approved amendment of the Audit Committee Terms of Reference as set out in Attachment 2 to the report (January 31, 2020) from the Chief Executive Officer.
