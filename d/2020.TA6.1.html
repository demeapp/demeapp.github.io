Ratification of the Calling of a Meeting of the Members of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund, May 21, 2020

AdoptedMay 21, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The Board of Directors of Toronto Atmospheric Fund confirm was asked to consider ratification of the Calling of a Meeting of the Members of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund, May 21, 2020.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The meeting of the Members of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund (TAF) scheduled for April 28, 2020 could not be held as a result of COVID-19 restrictions on in-person meetings. At its meeting on April 30, 2020, City Council authorized the Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund to meet virtually during this emergency period. The purpose of this report is to confirm the decision of the Chair to call a meeting of the Members of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund for May 21, 2020.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Atmospheric Fund confirmed: 1. The annual general meeting of the members of Toronto Atmospheric Fund shall be held on Thursday, May 21, 2020 at 9:45 a.m. or immediately following the conclusion of the meeting of the Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund. 2. The calling of the annual general meeting by the Chair by notice given on May 8, 2020 is ratified and approved. 3. The meeting will be conducted with members participating by electronic means, as authorized by City Council on April 30, 2020.
