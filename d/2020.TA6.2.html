Draft Audited Consolidated Financial Statements for the Year Ended December 31, 2019

AdoptedMay 21, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund was asked to consider draft Audited Consolidated Financial Statements for the Year Ended December 31, 2019.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The 2019 Audited Consolidated Financial Statements and Report have been prepared by Welch LLP and are presented to the Board of Directors of Toronto Atmospheric Fund for approval. These statements provide separate fund accounting for both the City of Toronto and the Province of Ontario endowment funds.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Approved transfers of $3,004,823 to the Toronto Stabilization Fund and $1,814,782 to the Ontario Stabilization Fund. 2. Approved the 2019 Audited Consolidated Financial Statements.
