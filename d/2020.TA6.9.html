What the item proposed

Toronto Atmospheric Fund (TAF) currently has twelve investments in its Direct Investment portfolio and two investments in Private (pooled) Equities, totalling approximately $8 million. TAF also operates two financing lines for ESPAs, totalling $8 million. An additional $8.8 million has been approved but not advanced for five Direct Investments and one Private (pooled) Equity Fund. Given the change in business circumstances due to the COVID-19 crisis, staff stress tested each of these using risk weighting matrix and identified reserve percentages for each risk category (Confidential Attachment 1). This will be improved as appropriate in consultation with the Investment Committee and used to continue monitoring the portfolio. There are no areas of immediate concern.
