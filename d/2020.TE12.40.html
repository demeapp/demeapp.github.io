Toronto City Council · Decision

Parking Amendments - Heath Street West

AdoptedJan 8, 2020 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to prohibit parking at all times on the south side of Heath Street West, between Avenue Road and a point 48 metres east.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is recommending that parking be prohibited at all times on the south side of Heath Street West, between Avenue Road and a point 48 metres east. This will address concerns regarding parked vehicles obstructing sightlines for drivers exiting the rear parking lot of 575 Avenue Road and improve traffic flow along Heath Street West. This proposal will result in the loss of three parking spaces.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking at all times on the south side of Heath Street West, between Avenue Road and a point 48 metres east. 2. Rescinded the existing maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., daily, on the south side of Heath Street West, between Avenue Road and a point 30.5 metres east of Oriole Road.
