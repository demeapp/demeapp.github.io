The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing stopping prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.

Official title: Laughton Avenue Pick-up and Drop-off Zone

AdoptedJan 8, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

Parents and neighbours in the area surrounding Saint Paul VI Catholic Elementary School contacted my office to voice safety and traffic management concerns. In response to these concerns, my office met onsite with City of Toronto Traffic Operations staff, School Parent Council and the School Principal in order to discuss potential solutions. After reviewing this matter in detail, Traffic Operations staff reported back to my office and recommended the installation of a pick-up and drop-off zone along the east side of Laughton Avenue, across from the school, in order to help ensure safety for students and compliance by parents. The purpose of this motion is therefore to install a pick-up and drop-off zone along the east side of Laughton Avenue, across from the school.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing stopping prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Laughton Avenue, between Talbot Street and a point 41.1 metres south of St. Clair Avenue West. 2. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 10 minutes from 8:30 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., from 11:45 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., and from 3:15 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Laughton Avenue, between Talbot Street and a point 41.1 metres south of St. Clair Avenue West. 3. Prohibited parking from 9:00 a.m. to 11:45 a.m. and from 1:00 p.m. to 3:15 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Laughton Avenue, between Talbot Street and a point 41.1 metres south of St. Clair Avenue West.
