Toronto City Council · Decision

Parking Amendments - Wallace Avenue

AdoptedJan 8, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing 15-minute maximum parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

It has been brought to my attention that as the result of changes in school hours and the continued growth of the Wallace Emerson neighbourhood, the current pick-up and drop-off area along Wallace Avenue between Pauline Avenue and Brock Avenue is being used by school buses as a loading zone. Principals from both Saint Sebastian Elementary School and Pauline Junior Public School contacted my office to formally change the current pick-up and drop-off area to a bus loading zone to better service the schools.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing 15-minute maximum parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., 11:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., and 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Wallace Avenue, between Brock Avenue and a point 48 metres east. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 9:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. and 1:00 p.m. to 3:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Wallace Avenue, between Brock Avenue and a point 48 metres east. 3. Designated a school bus loading zone to operate between the hours of 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Wallace Avenue, between Brock Avenue and a point 48 metres east.
