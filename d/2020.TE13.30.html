What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Cresford Developments is constructing a 68-storey mixed-used development and a 42-story residential condominium building at 33 Yorkville Avenue and 50 Cumberland Street, respectively. The development site will have frontage on both Yorkville Avenue and Cumberland Street. The site is bounded by Yorkville Avenue to the north, existing commercial uses and public laneway to the east, Cumberland Street to the south and existing commercial uses and a private laneway to the west. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the closure of the south sidewalk and a 2.0 metre wide portion of the eastbound curb lane on Yorkville Avenue, between a point 47 metres east of Bay Street and a point 32 metres further east and provision of a temporary pedestrian walkway within the closed portion of the eastbound curb lane, from March 1, 2020 to May 31, 2024. 2. Authorized the closure of the north sidewalk and a 1.5 metre wide portion of the eastbound curb lane on Cumberland Street, between a point 31.5 metres east of Bay Street and a point 86 metres further east, from March 1, 2020 to May 31, 2024. 3. Designated Cumberland Street, between Bay Street and a point 61 metres east, as one-way for eastbound traffic only. 4.
