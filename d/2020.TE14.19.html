The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with city-wide residential demolition control under the Toronto Municipal Code Ch. 363, under the authority of Section 33 of the Planning Act, the application for the demolition of three (3) existing detached dwellings located at 2, 4, and 6 Humber Hill Avenue (Application No(s). 20-108275 DEM, 20-108306 DEM, 20-108312 DEM) is being referred to the Toronto and East York Community Council for consideration to refuse or grant the application, including any conditions, if any, to be attached to the permit application because a building permit has not been issued for a replacement building. This staff report is regarding a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council deferred consideration of the item until its April 7, 2020 meeting.
