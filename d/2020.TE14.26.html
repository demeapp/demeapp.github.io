Toronto City Council · Decision

Appointments to Business Improvement Area Boards of Management

AdoptedMar 12, 2020 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve appointments to Business Improvement Area Boards of Management.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to appoint directors to the Cabbagetown, College West, Greektown on the Danforth, and Junction Gardens BIA boards of management and remove directors from Church-Wellesley Village, Greektown on the Danforth, Kensington Market, Liberty Village, Ossington Avenue, Queen Street West, Regal Heights Village, and Roncesvalles Village BIA boards of management.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. In accordance with the City's Public Appointments Policy, appointed the following nominees to the Business Improvement Area (BIA) boards of management set out below at the pleasure of Toronto and East York Community Council, and for a term expiring at the end of the term of Council or as soon thereafter as successors are appointed: Cabbagetown: Michaud, Gerald College West: Bordokas, Alexander Greektown on the Danforth: Vassos, Helene Junction Gardens: Di Bernardo, Janet Yurchuk, Dan 2.
