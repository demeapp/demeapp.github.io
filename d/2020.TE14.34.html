The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information on a 4-storey stacked, back-to-back townhouse development at 1165 O'Connor Drive and recommends City Council classify the subject lands as a Class 4 Noise Area pursuant to Publication NPC-300 - Ministry of Environment and Climate Change Environmental Noise.

What was decided

City Council on May 28, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council classify the lands at 1165 O'Connor Drive as a Class 4 Noise Area pursuant the Ministry of Environment (now Ministry of Environment, Conservation and Parks) Environmental Noise Guideline - Stationary and Transportation Sources - Approval and Planning Publication NPC-300, August 2013.
