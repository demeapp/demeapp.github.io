The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is recommending extending the existing corner parking prohibition on the east side of Oak Park Avenue, between Everett Crescent and a point 9 metres south, to be in effect between Everett Crescent and a point 32 metres south. This is in response to concerns received by Traffic Operations, via 311, regarding a sightline obstruction for westbound turning motorists at the intersection of Oak Park Avenue and Everett Crescent. This proposal will result in the loss of four on-street parking spaces on the east side of Oak Park Avenue, south of Everett Crescent.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking at all times on the east side Oak Park Avenue, between Everett Crescent and a point 32 metres south. 2. Prohibited parking at all times on the east side of Oak Park Avenue, between Everett Crescent and a point 24 metres north. 3. Rescinded the parking prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Everett Crescent, between Wallington Avenue and Oak Park Avenue. 4. Rescinded the parking prohibition in effect at all times on the east side of Everett Crescent, between Wallington Avenue and Oak Park Avenue.
