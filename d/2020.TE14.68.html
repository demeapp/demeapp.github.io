The official record

What the item proposed

The General Manager of Transportation Services requires authority to temporarily close to vehicular traffic any highway or portion of a highway beyond four consecutive days for social, recreational, community, athletic or cinematographic purposes in accordance with Municipal Code Chapter 937-3, Temporary Closing of Highways. Approval to temporarily close a portion of Bremner Blvd for 79 days is a delegated authority to Toronto and East York Community Council. Maple Leaf Sports and Entertainment (MLSE) will be hosting viewing parties on Bremner Blvd outside of Scotiabank Arena’s Gate 6 during the 2020 Toronto Maple Leafs and Toronto Raptors playoff season. MLSE has hosted viewing parties in this area for a number of years during home opener and playoff events. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the General Manager of Transportation Services to temporarily close Bremner Boulevard between Lake Shore Boulevard West to a point east of the parking garage accesses servicing 15 and 25 York Street respectively, from Sunday April 5, 2020 to Tuesday June 23, 2020, to facilitate Maple Leafs Sports and Entertainment's viewing area during the Toronto Maple Leafs and Toronto Raptors 2020 playoff season and to amend the necessary traffic regulations; and 2. Authorized and direct the appropriate City Officials to take the necessary action to give effect to the Community Council decision, including the introduction of any Bills that may be required.
