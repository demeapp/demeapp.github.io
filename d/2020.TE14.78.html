Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to consider no Right Turn at O'Connor Drive and Four Oaks Gate.

Official title: No Right Turn at O'Connor Drive and Four Oaks Gate

AdoptedMar 12, 2020 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Dear Chair and Members of the Toronto and East York Community Council, Four Oaks Gate is a residential street just north of O'Connor Drive near Don Mills Road. A high volume of commuter traffic uses O'Connor Drive to access the Don Valley Parkway via Don Mills Road every morning and many of these vehicles use Four Oaks Gate as a cut-through to bypass the traffic waiting to turn right at Don Mills Road onto the DVP. I have heard from residents that they are concerned about the safety of their community because of this cut-through traffic.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested Transportation Services conduct a study for the implementation of a right turn prohibition at O'Connor Drive and Four Oaks Gate between 7:00 a.m. and 9:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, and report back to Toronto and East York Community Council by September 15, 2020.
