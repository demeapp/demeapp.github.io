The official record

What the item proposed

The City will be resurfacing Shaw Street between Dupont and Dewson Street this year. Along with this work, the existing bikeway on Shaw Street will be renewed and upgraded to address various safety concerns that have been raised by area residents. As part of the upgrades, parking will be shifted to the west side of the street south of Essex resulting in the loss of 11 permit parking spaces on this stretch. Changes will be necessary to help alleviate the exacerbated parking pressure residents on this stretch will face when the changes are implemented this year.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services to review and to report back to the April 7, 2020 meeting of Toronto and East York Community Council on the extension of the overnight on-street permit parking hours on Shaw Street, between Essex Street and Bloor Street West from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., 7 days a week to 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m., 7 days a week. 2. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services to review and to report back to the April 7, 2020 meeting of Toronto and East York Community Council on the implementation of a 1-hour parking maximum between 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., 7 days a week.
