Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to consider speed Limit Reduction - Niagara Street.

Official title: Speed Limit Reduction - Niagara Street

AdoptedJun 18, 2020 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

There is no number of deaths or serious injuries on our streets that is acceptable. Each one is preventable. As a City, we must prioritize and accelerate measures to make our streets safe and accessible for all road users, especially vulnerable pedestrians and cyclists. In 2015, Toronto and East York Community Council directed that all roads within the district classified as local streets be reduced to a 30 km/h speed limit. Despite its local residential character, Niagara Street is technically classified as a collector roadway, and as a result the speed limit remained at 40 km/h. I am writing to recommend that the speed limit on Niagara Street be reduced to 30 km/h.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 40 km/h to 30 km/h on Niagara Street, between King Street West and Queen Street West.
