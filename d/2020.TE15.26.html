Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to consider parking Amendments - Barton Avenue.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Barton Avenue

AdoptedJun 18, 2020 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

St. Raymond Catholic School has been under construction, with occupancy projected only in January 2022. Currently, there are various parking restrictions on Barton Avenue in front of the school, preventing residents and visitors from using this area to park, though the school is currently unoccupied. On behalf of residents living on the northern edge of Christie Pits, as well as visitors to the area, I request that Transportation Services review the parking restrictions and rescind them until the school becomes operational, so that there are more parking options for residents and visitors throughout the day.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 9:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m., 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. and 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday on the north side of Barton Avenue, between a point 94.5 metres west of Pendrith Lane and a point 50.5 metres further west. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 8:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday on the north side of Barton Avenue, between Pendrith Lane and a point 94.5 metres west. 3. Rescinded the existing maximum ten-minute parking regulation in effect from 8:30 a.m. to 9:30 a.m., 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m., 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, except public holidays, on the north side of Barton Avenue, between a point 94.5 metres west of Pendrith Lane and a point 50.5 metres further west.
