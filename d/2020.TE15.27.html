Toronto and East York Community Council decides on Traffic By-law Amendments for Essex Street.

The official record

What the item proposed

In April 2020, City Council adopted the Item IE12.8 and in so doing, authorized the installation of contra-flow bicycle lanes and cycle tracks including the associated traffic and parking amendments at certain locations including Shaw Street, Harbord Street to Dupont Street (http://app.toronto.ca/tmmis/viewAgendaItemHistory.do?item=2020.IE12.8). Additional technical amendments are required for Essex Street to address an omission to the traffic regulations previously submitted. The changes proposed on Essex Street are a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This matter is urgent on account of work on Shaw being completed shortly.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the amendments to traffic regulations on Essex Street, as described in Attachment 1 - Amendments to Traffic Regulations, attached to the Letter (June 12, 2020) from Councillor Layton.
