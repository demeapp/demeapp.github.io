What the item proposed

This report recommends that the name "Giuseppe Rauti Lane" be approved to identify an existing public lane located west of Euclid Avenue, extending southerly from Lennox Street. Community Councils have delegated authority to decide street naming matters which comply with the City of Toronto Street Naming Policy.
