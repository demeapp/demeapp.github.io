What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. PricewaterhouseCoopers Inc., LIT is constructing a 38-storey residential condominium building with retail uses at 480-494 Yonge Street. A portion of St. Luke Lane abutting the site and the south sidewalk and a portion of the eastbound curb lane on Grosvenor Street are currently closed to accommodate construction staging operations for the development. Toronto and East York Committee Council, at its meeting on May 2, 2018, approved the subject construction staging areas on St. Luke Lane from October 1, 2018 to July 31, 2020; and Grosvenor Street, from October 1, 2018 to June 30, 2021.
