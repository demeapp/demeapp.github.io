Parking Amendments - Gough Avenue

AdoptedJul 16, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to amend the existing maximum 10-minute parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., from 12:00 p.m. to 2:30 p.m. and from 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is recommending that the existing maximum 10-minute parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., from 12:00 p.m. to 2:30 p.m. and from 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Gough Avenue, between Danforth Avenue and a point 29.5 metres south, amended to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., from 12:00 p.m. to 2:30 p.m. and from 4:00 p.m. to 6:30 p.m., Monday to Friday. Play Loft Preschool has received several complaints from parents receiving parking tickets while picking up their children between the hours of 4:00 p.m. and 5:00 p.m., currently designated as "no parking anytime".
