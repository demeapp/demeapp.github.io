The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes revitalization of a Toronto Community Housing Corporation (TCHC) site including a 10 storey residential building ("the TCHC Building") and a 17 storey and 16 storey mixed-use building (the "Market Building"), collectively containing approximately 771 residential units with a variety of housing tenures including rent-geared-to-income, affordable rental, market rental, and condominiums. The proposed development will include 100 net new affordable residential rental dwelling units and 120 replacement social housing units with rents geared-to-income. All 120 existing units will be replaced in the new development and tenant relocation and assistance will be provided.
