Requests for a Confidential Briefing Re: Staging Area Permits

Adopted with changesJul 16, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

Many Councillors in the Toronto and East York district are met with construction plans on numerous development sites which request the use of the public realm for an extended period of time, often putting residents at an inconvenience for many years. Before we can engage in discussions with developers about the potential to change or refuse staging permits, it would be prudent to have this discussion with City Legal.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the City Solicitor to provide a confidential briefing, if necessary, or legal opinion to the members of the Toronto and East York Community Council at the September 15, 2020 meeting, regarding any legal implications that may arise from the refusal of a construction staging area permit in the municipal right of way for a private development.
