All-Way Stop Control - Wales Avenue and Augusta Avenue

AdoptedJul 16, 2020 · Toronto City Council

This item, "All-Way Stop Control - Wales Avenue and Augusta Avenue," is before the Toronto and East York Community Council. The published agenda material provides no further detail.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents of Kensington Market have requested a stop sign be installed at Augusta and Wales for northbound traffic to improve safety and calm traffic in the neighbourhood along Augusta Ave. Many people have observed wrong-way driving on Wales from northbound traffic turning left onto the street, and adding a stop sign would allow the one-way sign to be more visible. In addition, it will help slow the speed of through traffic on Augusta Ave. The installation of a stop sign here would help improve road safety and is strongly supported by the community.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized compulsory all-way stop control at the intersection of Wales Avenue and Augusta Avenue.
