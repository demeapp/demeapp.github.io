Parking Amendment - Markdale Avenue

AdoptedJul 16, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to prohibit parking at all times on both sides of Markdale Avenue, between Strathearn Road and the west end of Markdale Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to you to request Community Council's support for a motion that is important to the constituents of the Toronto-St. Paul's ward, but one that could not be brought forward by staff due to the impacts of the pandemic on staff redeployments. My office has received a petition from the residents on the stretch of Markdale Avenue, between Strathearn Road and Cedarvale Park, expressing their desire to have a no parking by-law in place at all times. The residents shared their concerns over the current setup at the location, which allows for up to 3 hours of parking on each side of the street. As this is a gateway into Cedarvale Park, the residents have noted numerous close calls between drivers doing three point turns and pedestrians trying to access the park. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking at all times on both sides of Markdale Avenue, between Strathearn Road and the west end of Markdale Avenue.
