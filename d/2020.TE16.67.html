The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to authorize all-way stop control at the intersection of Vesta Drive and Robinwood Avenue.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to you to request Community Council's support for a motion that is important to the constituents of the Toronto-St. Paul's ward, but one that could not be brought forward by staff due to the impacts of the pandemic on staff redeployments. The residents of Vesta Drive came to my office last June with concerns over safety at the intersection with Robinwood Avenue. My office requested Transportation Services to review the location, which resulted in some adjustments to the signage and roadway markings. However, this did not address the concerns of the community fully and the residents followed up this year with a strong petition requesting improvements to the safety at the intersection, with a specific ask for all-way stop control. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized all-way stop control at the intersection of Vesta Drive and Robinwood Avenue.
