What the item proposed

I am writing to request your support for re-opening this item that we had discussed last month but did not make a decision on. There have been new developments that inform my understanding of the situation and I would like to propose a way forward that will help the community while allowing the construction to go ahead. Since the June Community Council meeting, my office has helped facilitate dialogue between the tenants' association at 45 Dunfield Avenue, City staff, and the developer to find a manageable solution that would both minimize the impact of the work on the community and provide the safest possible conditions on site. While I have fundamental concerns over the public right-of-way being occupied for private development, City staff have advised us that there are no safe alternatives to the proposed staging.
