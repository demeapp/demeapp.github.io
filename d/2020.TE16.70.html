What the item proposed

The 519 continues to meet the required catchment residency provision and these recommended appointments comply with the Public Appointments Policy and the Relationship Framework between the City of Toronto and Community Centres. The 519’s Nominations Committee short-listed seven candidates for an open election for six available positions. The Committee interviewed all candidates using a standard template of questions focused on how the nominees see themselves contributing in supporting our work at The 519; and how their skills, expertise and lived experience advances our commitment to our Strategic Priorities. Elections were held via secret ballot at the Annual General Meeting 2019. The 519 Membership elected these candidates for appointment by Toronto and East York Community Council.
