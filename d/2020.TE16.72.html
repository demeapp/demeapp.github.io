Removal of Bollards from an unnamed laneway between Ontario Street and Seaton Street without Consulting the Local Community or Councillor’s Office

AdoptedJul 16, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to direct the General Manager of Transportation to undertake the reinstallation of updated, movable bollards by no later than the end of Q3 2020 to the unnamed laneway that begins between 216 Ontario Street and 218 Ontario Street and extends westward to Seaton.

The official record

What the item proposed

At the beginning of May 2020, city contractors removed at least 8 concrete bollards, which have been in place for over 50 years, from an unnamed laneway that begins between 216 Ontario Street and 218 Ontario Street and extends westward to Seaton Street, without providing any notice to my office or the local residents and without any consultation. These bollards were first identified as an issue during a safety walk that I co-hosted with the Cabbagetown South Residents’ Association on December 16, 2019, along with various stakeholders present including local residents, Toronto Hydro, Toronto Police 51 Division, and city staff from Transportation, Social Development Finance and Administration, and Shelter Support and Housing Administration. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the General Manager, Transportation to undertake the reinstallation of updated, movable bollards by no later than the end of the third quarter of 2020 to the unnamed laneway that begins between 216 Ontario Street and 218 Ontario Street and extends westward to Seaton Street in consultation with Toronto Hydro, local residents, and the Ward Councillor.
