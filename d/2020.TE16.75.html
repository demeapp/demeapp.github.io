Temporary Sidewalk Installation on Mayfield Avenue

AdoptedJul 16, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to authorize Transportation to install a temporary sidewalk on Mayfield Avenue in Ward 4.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision. Installation of a missing link sidewalk is needed to ensure safety remains a top priority. The missing link sidewalk is on Mayfield Avenue between Armadale Avenue and Willard Gardens Parkette.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized Transportation Services to install a temporary sidewalk on Mayfield Avenue in Ward 4. 2. Removed fencing along western edge of Willard Gardens Parkette, to permit the installation of temporary missing link sidewalk. 3. Installed a temporary missing link sidewalk on Mayfield Avenue, from Armadale Avenue to Willard Gardens Parkette. 4. Altered the reconstruction of Mayfield Avenue roadway to accommodate the permanent installation of the missing link sidewalk as part of the road resurfacing projected to take place as part of the Capital Repair list for 2021/2022.
