Toronto City Council · Decision

Car-share Vehicle Parking Areas - Emily Street and Bastion Street

AdoptedSep 16, 2020 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the car-share vehicle parking area for up to two Maven vehicles on the east side of Emily Street, between a point 31 metres north of Wellington Street West and a point 11 metres further north.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is requesting authorization to rescind car-share vehicle parking areas (CVPAs) on Emily Street and on Bastion Street.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the car-share vehicle parking area for up to two Maven vehicles on the east side of Emily Street, between a point 31 metres north of Wellington Street West and a point 11 metres further north. 2. Prohibited parking at all times on the east side of Emily Street, between a point 31 metres north of Wellington Street West and a point 11 metres further north. 3. Rescinded the car-share vehicle parking area for up to two Maven vehicles on the east side of Bastion Street, between a point 16.7 metres south of Sloping Sky Mews and a point 11 metres further south. 4. Prohibited parking at all times on the east side of Bastion Street, between a point 16.7 metres south of Sloping Sky Mews and a point 11 metres further south.
