What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting approval from Toronto and East York Community Council to introduce overnight on-street permit parking on the streets listed in Attachment A of this report, on an area name basis, under the operating hours of 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., 7 days a week. The implementation of overnight on-street permit parking is a means to accommodate residents' long term parking needs without being subject to tagging by Parking Enforcement Officers. Recommendation 2 of this report addresses housekeeping amendments associated with the introduction of overnight on-street permit parking in Wards 4, 9 and 14.
