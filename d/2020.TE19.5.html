The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes to amend the Zoning By-law to permit two 8 to 13-storey buildings and one 11-storey building with a total 80,800 square metres of gross floor area consisting of: 870 residential units, of which 261 are affordable; 3,129 square metres of retail gross floor area; and a 481 square metre community space at 373 Front Street East and 90 Mill Street. The proposal also provides for privately-owned publicly accessible spaces in the form of a pedestrian walkway connecting Front Street East to the future east-west local road, and two courtyards on either side of the pedestrian walkway. An application to lift the "(h)" holding symbol in the Zoning By-law for the subject lands has also been applied to permit the development as proposed.
