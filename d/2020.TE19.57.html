City Council was asked to let Coxwell Avenue get permit parking without the usual resident poll.

Official title: Implementation of Permit Parking on Coxwell Avenue, between Eastwood Road and Dundas Street East

AdoptedOct 27, 2020 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I have heard from constituents of Coxwell Avenue from Eastwood Road and Dundas Street East who are experiencing difficulty parking outside of their homes overnight. I am requesting to implement legal on-street permit parking in this area by expanding the existing 9C zone to include the above noted section of Coxwell Avenue. Implementing this change would allow residents in the area to apply for a permit parking sticker and park legally overnight. The proposed change will not affect daytime stopping conditions along the east side of the street. While permit holders will be able to park beyond the 1HR currently in place for 9:00am-4:00pm on weekdays, the No Stopping during the 4:00-6:00pm rush hour will remain the same. What was decided

City Council on October 27, 28 and 30, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council exempt the implementation of Permit Parking on Coxwell Avenue, between Eastwood Drive and Dundas Street East from the requirements of subsection 925-4B of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 925, Permit Parking, until such time as Toronto and East York Community Council considers a request for implementation of Permit Parking at this location.
