What the item proposed

This Official Plan Amendment application proposes to add portions of City owned land at 40 and 50 Service Road, 446 Lakeshore Avenue, 425 Lakeshore Avenue, 1 Mugg's Island Park, 339 Queens Quay West, 318 and 330X Queens Quay, and 350 Lake Shore Boulevard West to Official Plan Site and Area Specific Policy 265 so they are exempt from Official Plan Policy 4.3.8, which prohibits the disposal of City-owned land in areas designated as Parks and Open Spaces. The Enwave Energy Corporation is proposing an expansion of the existing Deep Lake Water Cooling supply, which provides an environmentally friendly air conditioning alternative to buildings in the downtown core. To facilitate the expansion, Enwave Energy Corporation is proposing to construct a new intake in Lake Ontario and a new raw water bypass pipe from the Island Water Treatment Plant to the John Street Pumping Station.
