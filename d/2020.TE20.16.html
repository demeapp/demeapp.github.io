City Council was asked to take over the Sudbury Street extension and its services.

Official title: Assumption of Services, Sudbury Street Extension (from Queen Street West to Abell Street) - S37 Agreement - AT2483854, Core Servicing Agreement, City of Toronto By-Law No. 1169-2009 (OMB), Plan 66R-24748.

AdoptedNov 25, 2020 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that the municipal services and the road construction under the terms of the Section 37 Agreement (AT2483854 dated August 23, 2010) and the Core Servicing Agreement (dated August 29, 2008) for Plan 66R-24748 (dated March 9, 2010) between the West Queen West Triangle Landowners Group Limited and the City of Toronto be assumed by the City.

What was decided

City Council on November 25 and 26, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council: a. assume the services installed within Sudbury Street (from Queen Street West to Abell Street) and that the City formally assume the road within the Registered Plan 66R-24748; b. authorize the Director, Engineering Review to release the Letter of Credit associated with this work; c. direct that an assumption By-law be prepared to assume the public highway and municipal services within the Registered Plan 66R-24748; d. authorize and direct the City Solicitor to register the assumption By-law in the Land Registry Office, at the expense of the Owner; e. authorize the appropriate City Officials to take the necessary action to give effect to the City Council's decision; and f. authorize the appropriate City Officials to transfer ownership of the street lighting system constructed with Registered Plan 66R-24748…
