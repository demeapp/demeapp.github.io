What the item proposed

The purpose of this staff report is to seek Community Council approval about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has assessed a request from The Hospital For Sick Children (the "Owner") to replace a recently demolished bridge that was connected to the recently demolished Elizabeth McMaster Building with a new, larger bridge to be situated over the City-owned public highway known as Elizabeth Street (the "Right of Way"). The new overhead pedestrian bridge will provide pedestrian access between Sick Kids Hospital, located at 555 University Avenue, and the new Patient Care Centre, located at 175 Elizabeth Street (collectively, the "Buildings").
