The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information on a development at 390 to 440 Dufferin Street composed of three mixed-use buildings (13 storeys, 10 storeys and 9 storeys) containing 397 residential units with retail at grade in two of the buildings and with employment/office/light industrial space in the first 5 storeys the third building, and recommends City Council classify the subject lands as a Class 4 Noise Area pursuant to Ministry of the Environment, Conservation and Parks (MECP) Publication NPC-300 (Environmental Noise Guideline).

What was decided

City Council on December 16, 17 and 18, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council classify the lands at 390 to 440 Dufferin Street as a Class 4 Noise Area pursuant to the Ministry of Environment (now the Ministry of the Environment, Conservation and Parks) Environmental Noise Guideline - Stationary and Transportation Sources - Approval and Planning Publication NPC-300, August 2013.
