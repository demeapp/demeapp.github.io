City Council was asked to grant authority to enter into a new three-year agreement with Green Savoree Toronto.

Official title: Honda Indy Toronto Race 2021-2023 - Temporary Street Closures

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services is requesting approval to temporarily close and amend the necessary traffic regulations on Lake Shore Boulevard West, between Strachan Avenue and Ontario Drive and on Strachan Avenue, between Fleet Street and Lake Shore Boulevard West from July 8, 2021 to July 11, 2021 and July 14, 2022 to July 17, 2022 and July 13, 2023 to July 16, 2023. In the event of a delay or cancellation of the Sunday event, the final race will be held on Monday, July 12, 2021, July 18, 2022 or July 17, 2023. This temporary closure to vehicular traffic is required to facilitate the Honda Indy Toronto hosted by Green Savoree Toronto, ULC. The proposed street event does not meet the requirements of the City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 937, Temporary Closing of Highways.

What was decided

City Council on December 16, 17 and 18, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the entering into of a new three-year agreement with Green Savoree Toronto ULC, to permit the holding of the Honda Indy Toronto events in the years 2021, 2022 and 2023 on terms and conditions satisfactory to the General Manager, Transportation Services, and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor. 2. City Council amend Section 937-3.1 of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 937, Temporary Closing of Highways, subject to the City's execution of the agreement between the City and Green Savoree Toronto ULC described in Part 1 above, to delegate authority to the General Manager, Transportation Services to temporarily close to vehicular and pedestrian traffic any highway or portion of a highway for such periods as required for the purposes of the Honda Indy Toronto automobile race in…
