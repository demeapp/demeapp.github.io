What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision The proposed pedestrian and cycling bridge commences at the back edge of the sidewalk on Strachan Avenue and will connect to the proposed park at the south side of the property, at the on-going development of 11-25 Ordnance Street. On behalf of the owners, Bentall Kennedy (Canada) Limited has submitted an application which proposes to construct the new bridge. The owner of the property is BK Prime Ontario I LP c/o BentallGreenOak (Canada) LP. Transportation Services has reviewed the application. The Applicant and Owner are required to satisfy all conditions of approval set out in the recommendations of this report and must comply with Transportation Services procedure for granting approval of bridges.
