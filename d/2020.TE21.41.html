What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on College Street, City Council approval of this report is required. SKYGRiD Construction Incorporated is constructing mixed-use commercial retail and residential suites at the lands municipally known as 547, 549 and 555 College Street, east of Manning Avenue. This development includes three-story building and a six-story commercial building. The site is bounded by College Street to the north, a public laneway to the south and existing commercial buildings to the east and west. The sidewalk on the south side of College Street and six parking machine spaces on College Street, within the lay-by abutting the development, are currently closed for construction staging operations. What was decided

City Council on December 16, 17 and 18, 2020, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the continuation of the closure of the sidewalk approximately 30.0 metres east of Manning Avenue and a point approximately 30.0 metres further east, from November 1, 2020 to April 30, 2021. 2. City Council authorize the continuation of the closure of the lay-by lane on the south side of College Street from Manning Avenue to a point approximately 36.0 metres east of Euclid Avenue from November 1, 2020 to April 30, 2021. 3. City Council direct the applicant to continue posting a 24-hour monitored construction hotline number on the hoarding board, which must be prominently placed and legible from 20 metres and on all elevations from the construction site. 4.
