The Toronto and East York Community Council considers a parking or traffic change.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Western Battery Road

AdoptedDec 2, 2020 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Construction staging on the north-south portion of Western Battery Rd is removed and residents have taken occupancy at 19 Western Battery Rd, resulting in traffic flow issues on the north-south portion of the street just north of East Liberty St. The current bylaws are the ones that were in place during construction which replaced the Permit Parking and Pay and Display on the east side and No Parking Anytime on the west side. The construction bylaws are going to be reverted back to pre-construction status. Due to the current traffic flow issues, Traffic Operations is recommending that the no parking anytime regulation on the west side of the street be bumped up to No Standing Anytime.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Western Battery Road, between East Liberty Street and a point 68.1 metres north. 2. Prohibited standing at all times on the west side of Western Battery Road, between East Liberty Street and a point 68.1 metres north.
