The Toronto and East York Community Council considers a parking or traffic change.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Camden Street

AdoptedDec 2, 2020 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents on Camden street have noted serious problems with parking on the south curbside of Camden, as well as parking on the south sidewalk. This has serious consequences, as emergency vehicles and solid waste vehicles cannot pass between the cars parked on the north and south side of the street. Staff have advised that changing the existing conditions will serve to improve compliance and enable safe conditions for all road users.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Camden Street, between Spadina Avenue and Brant Street. 2. Prohibited stopping at all times on the south side of Camden Street, between Spadina Avenue and Brant Street.
