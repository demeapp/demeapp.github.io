City Council was asked to appoint five public members, including a Chair, to the Toronto Local Appeal Body.

Official title: Appointment of a Public Member to the Toronto Local Appeal Body

Adopted with changesNov 9, 2020 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Nominating Panel – Toronto Local Appeal Body will conduct interviews and recommend five candidates, including one as Chair, to City Council for appointment to the Toronto Local Appeal Body.

What was decided

The Nominating Panel – Toronto Local Appeal Body recommends that: 1. City Council appoint the following candidates to the Toronto Local Appeal Body, including one as Chair, for a term of office ending November 27, 2024 and until successors are appointed. Sabnavis Gopikrishna Christine Kilby Dino Lombardi, Chair Stanley Makuch Ted Yao 2. City Council direct that Confidential Attachments 1 to 4 to the report (October 23, 2020) from the Interim City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Local Appeal Body.
