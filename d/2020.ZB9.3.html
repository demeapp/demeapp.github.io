The official record

What the item proposed

This report requests that City Council convey ownership of James Pearse House to the Rouge Valley Foundation, a non-for-profit organization, at nominal consideration, plus grant them permission over Zoo Road to access to the house. City Council owns the Zoo’s assets and must consent to the sale. Once this transfer has occurred, the Tripartite Agreement can be executed, thereby completing the lengthy Tripartite Agreement process.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Requested City Council approve and enter into an agreement with the Rouge Valley Foundation that vests ownership of Pearse House to the Rouge Valley Foundation for nominal consideration and releases the City of Toronto and the Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo from all future associated obligations and liabilities, in a form satisfactory to the City Manager and City Solicitor, and to provide the Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo with a fully executed copy of the agreement. 2. Terminated the lease over Part of Lot 4, Concession 3, Scarborough in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor promptly upon receipt of the fully executed agreement authorized by Part 1, above. 3.
