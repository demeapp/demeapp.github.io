The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends the award of the tender for the Polar Bear Exhibit Modifications For Walrus project. The project will upgrade the Life Support systems for the habitat and provide suitable holding and exhibit space for walrus

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Awarded the tender for the Polar Bear Exhibit Modifications for Walrus project to Midome Construction Services Ltd. in the amount of $638,000 (including 10 percent owner’s contingency) plus HST.
