What the item proposed

This report describes the rationale for developing new Memorandums of Understanding (MOU) with other like minded institutions and discusses current and future likely memorandums. Currently, the Toronto Zoo has long standing MOU’s with Parks Canada and Centennial College. New collaborators and the supporting MOUs are a critical tool to help us achieve our mission of connection people, animals and conservation science to fight extinction.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Received the report (May 8, 2020) from the Senior Director, Corporate Projects and Human Resources, for information.
