The official record

What the item proposed

Jeffrey Cantos, Manager, Official Plan, City Planning, and Stephanie Burnham, Planner, Dillon Consulting, will give a presentation on the Municipal Comprehensive Review, including steps to amplify Indigenous voices through this review process.

What was decided

The Aboriginal Affairs Advisory Committee: 1. Requested the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, to incorporate the Aboriginal Affairs Advisory Committee's suggestions for amplifying Indigenous voices into the engagement process of Our Plan Toronto, the Municipal Comprehensive Review.
