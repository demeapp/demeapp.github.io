The official record

What the item proposed

The Manager, Business Services, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, the Project Officer Development, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, and the Senior Public Consultation Coordinator, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, will give a presentation on Golf Operational Review. The City of Toronto is conducting an operational review of our five City-operated golf course locations to better understand how to maximize golf and non-golf experiences. As part of the consultation process that will inform recommendations to Council, the project team will provide the Aboriginal Affairs Advisory Committee with a project overview and collect feedback on current and potential future uses of the five City-operated golf courses.

What was decided

The Aboriginal Affairs Advisory Committee: 1. Requested the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, to incorporate the Committee's feedback into the work on Golf Operational Review.
