What the item proposed

The attached report summarizes the results of the Auditor General's review of allegations of fraud. The case involved two BIAs resulting from the actions of a consultant who worked for each Business Improvement Area (BIA) at different times beginning in 2017. This investigation was initiated based on concerns raised by the City's BIA office of the Economic Development and Culture Division. After concluding our investigation but before our public report was issued, we were apprised by law enforcement that "the police conducted their investigation independently of the Auditor General. The suspect has been identified and arrested by police, and has been charged with fraud." The consultant was not interviewed as part of the Auditor General's work in order to retain separation of the work conducted by the Auditor General's Office and the criminal investigative process.
