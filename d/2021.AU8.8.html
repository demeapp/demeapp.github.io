Information Technology Projects Implementation: Information Privacy and Cybersecurity Review of Human Resource System

AdoptedApr 7, 2021 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to strengthen cybersecurity and privacy controls in IT project governance after an incident involving the new HR system.

The official record

What the item proposed

The City of Toronto implemented a new human resource (HR) system in 2019 to replace its old HR modules in human resource management and administration. This new integrated HR system provides an end-to-end workflow, from recruitment to hiring and onboarding for new staff. It collects and stores a significant amount of human resources information for employees and elected officials. Because of this, it is extremely important that the system has strong cybersecurity and privacy controls in place. In early 2020, the Auditor General became aware of a cybersecurity incident related to the implementation of this new system. Given the importance of information privacy and cybersecurity, the Auditor General immediately initiated a review of the system implementation process in the context of overall information security at the City.
