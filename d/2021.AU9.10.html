The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to a recommendation adopted at the Audit Committee's February 2021 meeting to report on ways to ensure adequate performance measures and oversight of all City tree maintenance service contracts, including methods to guard against payments for work not stipulated in the contracts or for work falsely reported, and for the termination of contracts due to evidence of performance that does not meet the terms and conditions of the contract. The City has implemented a number of approaches to strengthen oversight and management of tree maintenance service contracts.
