What the item proposed

This report summarizes the changes impacting the City of Toronto (the City)'s obligatory reserve funds (deferred revenues), and reserves and Council-directed reserve funds (accumulated surplus) as at December 31, 2020. The City's deferred revenues (obligatory reserve funds) and reserves and Council-directed reserve funds (accumulated surplus) are subject to annual changes based on funding contributions, transfers, and drawdowns, which are authorized by legislation, third-party agreements, and Council's approvals. The tables and appendices reflect information by major category.
