What the item proposed

On February 9, 2021, the Auditor General released a report entitled "Getting to the Root of the Issues: A Follow-Up to the 2019 Tree Maintenance Services Audit", which was tabled at the Audit Committee meeting on February 16, 2021. The report included 18 recommendations for consideration by City Council, that are aimed to strengthen Parks, Forestry and Recreation's (PFR) oversight, monitoring and contract management related to tree maintenance services to ensure value for money for the City.
