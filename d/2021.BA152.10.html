Award of Tender Ariba Doc Number 2938303660 to Midome Construction Services Ltd., for the Site Improvements Works at Disco Transfer Station for Solid Waste Management Services Division

AdoptedSep 9, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The Bid Award Panel was asked to award the Site Improvements Works at Disco Transfer Station for Solid Waste Management Services Division contract to Midome Construction Services Ltd.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (September 2, 2021) from the Chief Procurement Officer: Solicitation Number: Request for Tender, Ariba Doc Number 2938303660 Description: The Project involves reconstruction of inbound/outbound and haulage ramps, replacement of indoor catch basins, replacement of watermain / sewer lines, concrete floor/ceiling repairs and parking area pavement repairs at Disco Transfer Station. Recommended Supplier: Midome Construction Services Ltd. Contract Award Value: $2,051,025 net of all applicable taxes and charges $2,317,658 including HST and all applicable charges $2,087,123 net of HST recoveries Contract is expected to start from date of the written order to commence work for a period of one hundred and nighty-eight (198) working days.
