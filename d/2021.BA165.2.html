What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (November 18, 2021) from the Chief Procurement Officer: Solicitation Number: Request for Proposal (RFP), Ariba Document Number 2988100469 Description: The provision of consulting and engineering services for Traffic Signal Left Turn Phase Studies to determine where new left turn phases should be installed or when an existing left turn phase should be enabled for 65 study cases every year from 2022 to 2025. The term of the contract will be from January 01, 2022 to December 31, 2022, with options in favour of the City to extend the agreement for up to three (3) separate one (1) year periods.
