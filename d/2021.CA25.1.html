Appointment of Public Member to the Toronto Transit Commission Board

AdoptedDec 15, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The Civic Appointments Committee conducted interviews and recommend one candidate to City Council for appointment to the Toronto Transit Commission Board.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Civic Appointments Committee will conduct interviews and recommend one candidate to City Council for appointment to the Toronto Transit Commission Board.

What was decided

City Council on December 15, 16 and 17, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council appoint Ron Lalonde to the Toronto Transit Commission Board, at pleasure of Council, for a term of office ending on February 4, 2025, and until a successor is appointed. 2. City Council direct that Confidential Attachments 1 to 4 to the report (November 8, 2021) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Transit Commission Board. Confidential Attachments 1 to 4 to the report (November 8, 2021) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety in accordance with the provisions of the City of Toronto Act, 2006, as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Transit Commission Board.
