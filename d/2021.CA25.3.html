Selection of Candidates for Interview for the Toronto Investment Board

Adopted with changesNov 18, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The Civic Appointments Committee selected candidates for interviews for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board. Following the interviews, the Civic Appointments Committee will recommend three candidates for appointment to City Council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

The Civic Appointments Committee will select candidates for interviews for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board. Following the interviews, the Civic Appointments Committee will recommend three candidates for appointment to City Council.

What was decided

The Civic Appointments Committee: 1. Selected the following candidates for interview at its next meeting for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board: INB2-39 INB2-50 INB2-52 INB2-79 INB2-178 INB2-212 INB2-243 INB2-251 INB2-257 2. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1 to 3 to the report (November 8, 2021) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board.
