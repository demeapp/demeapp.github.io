The official record

What the item proposed

City Council adopted the Garden District Heritage Conservation District and Garden District Heritage Conservation District Plan (the "Garden District HCD") at its meeting of January 31, 2017. The Garden District HCD was subsequently appealed to the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal (the "LPAT" or "Tribunal"). The LPAT scheduled a 15 day hearing on the merits of the Garden District HCD beginning August 17, 2020. At the request of the parties the hearing was adjourned to allow further discussion of outstanding issues. This report requests direction from City Council with respect to the results of that discussion and the continuing LPAT appeal process.

What was decided

City Council on February 2, 3 and 5, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (January 27, 2021) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (January 27, 2021) remain confidential as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
