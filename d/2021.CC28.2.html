What the item proposed

On March 28, 2017, City Council adopted item CC27.2, directing the City Solicitor to attend the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal ("LPAT") in support of a joint settlement in principle of the Official Plan and Zoning By-law amendment applications for 31R Parliament Street, 370 Cherry Street and 370R Cherry Street ("31R Parliament Street") and the Official Plan and Zoning By-law amendment application at 60 Mill Street. On July 6, 2017, the LPAT issued its decision approving the joint settlement but withheld its final Order until the satisfaction of a number of conditions (the "2017 Settlement"). Following the 2017 Settlement the lands at 60 Mill Street have gone through changes of ownership and are now owned by 2575867 Ontario Inc.
