The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide City Council with the results of the collective bargaining negotiations between the Association of Community Centres (AOCC) and Canadian Union of Public Employees (CUPE) Local 2998 and to seek ratification by Council of the negotiated agreement.

What was decided

City Council on April 7 and 8, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council ratify and approve the attached Memorandum of Settlement dated February 27, 2021, between the Association of Community Centres and Canadian Union of Public Employees, Local 2998, in order to effect the new collective agreement. 2. City Council authorize staff to make the necessary amendments to rates of pay, benefits and other issues identified as agreed changes in the new Memoranda of Settlement. 3. City Council authorize staff to make the necessary adjustments to the 2020 Non-Program Expenditure Budget and Operating Budgets of the Association of Community Centres to reflect the provisions of the Memorandum of Settlement. 4.
