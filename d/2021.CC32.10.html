The official record

What the item proposed

On December 20, 2017, the City received an application to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law for 41 Chatsworth Drive (the "Current Application"). The Current Application proposed a 4 storey residential condominium building containing 43 units and 8,667 square metres of gross floor area resulting in an overall site density of 1.96 times the site area. The proposed building would be 4-storeys facing Chatsworth Drive and 5-storeys facing Duplex Parkette. The Current Application was deemed complete on January 18, 2018. The applicant appealed City Council's neglect or failure to make a decision on its application for Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendments (the "Appeals") to the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal (the "LPAT") on October 21, 2019. What was decided

City Council on May 5 and 6, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (April 28, 2021) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Appendix A to the report (April 28, 2021) from the City Solicitor at the discretion of the City Solicitor. 3. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (April 28, 2021) from the City Solicitor remain confidential at the discretion of the City Solicitor, as it contains advice and information that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
