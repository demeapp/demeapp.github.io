What the item proposed

On February 9, 2021, the Auditor General released a report entitled "Getting to the Root of the Issues: A Follow-Up to the 2019 Tree Maintenance Services Audit", which was tabled at the Audit Committee meeting on February 16, 2021. This report was in response to City Council's request that the Auditor General report further to the Audit Committee on Parks, Forestry and Recreation Division's (PFR) review of work performed by tree maintenance vendors. The Auditor General's report provided 18 recommendations for the consideration of City Council, that are aimed to strengthen PFR's oversight, monitoring and contract management related to tree maintenance services to ensure value for money for the City.
