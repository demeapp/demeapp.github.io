What the item proposed

The Owner of the property at 2915-2917 Bloor Street West (the Site) made an application to amend the Zoning By-law to permit an 8-storey mixed use building. The application was appealed to the Ontario Municipal Board, subsequently the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal ("LPAT") and now continued as the Ontario Land Tribunal ("OLT"), citing Council's failure to make a decision within the prescribed timeframe in the Planning Act. A contested LPAT hearing took place over 12 days between early June and early December, 2018. In a decision issued on May 19, 2019 (the Decision), the LPAT determined that the proposal was not appropriate for the Site.
