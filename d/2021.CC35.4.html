The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend to City Council the appointment of a Deputy City Manager.

What was decided

City Council on July 14, 15 and 16, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council appoint Paul Johnson to the position of Deputy City Manager for the City of Toronto, with such appointment to be effective on September 7, 2021, subject to written acceptance by Paul Johnson, of the terms and conditions of a contract of employment offered by the City. 2. City Council authorize the City Manager to negotiate terms and conditions of employment with Paul Johnson. 3. City Council authorize the public release of the name and curriculum vitae summary of Paul Johnson in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (July 6, 2021) from the City Manager.
