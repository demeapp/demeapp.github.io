The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to facilitate the transmission of the CreateTO Board decision to City Council in a timely manner and to seek City Council's ratification of the Board's choice for the position of Chief Executive Officer. The CreateTO Board will meet on Friday November 5, 2021 to appoint the candidate to the position of Chief Executive Officer. Following its meeting, the Board will transmit its decision and recommended candidate to City Council for ratification.

What was decided

City Council on November 9, 10 and 12, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council ratify the appointment of Vic Gupta to the position of Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO, subject to an agreement of employment terms. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the name and candidate summary of Vic Gupta once employment terms are agreed, and City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the Transmittal (November 5, 2021) from the Board of Directors of CreateTO remain confidential as it contains personal information about an identifiable individual. Candidate Summary of Vic Gupta: Vic Gupta Mr. Gupta joined CreateTO in 2019 as Senior Vice President, Strategic Development and has most recently held the position of Executive Vice President, Strategic Development.
