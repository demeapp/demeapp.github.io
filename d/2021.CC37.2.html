What the item proposed

Wells Gordon Limited (the "Applicant") is the owner of the property municipally known as 700-730 Mount Pleasant Road, 214-226 Soudan Avenue, and 19-21 Brownlow Avenue (the "Subject Site"), located on the north side of Soudan Avenue, west of Mount Pleasant Road. The Subject Site currently contains the Briton House Retirement Centre (the "Briton House"), which is comprised of a two-tower building, being 10 and 12 storeys in height, and an above ground Toronto Parking Authority parking garage at 730 Mount Pleasant Road. On November 2, 2017, the Applicant submitted a Zoning By-law Amendment application to the City (the "Application") to permit the development of a new 25-storey building (88.6 metres including mechanical penthouse), including a 7-storey base podium building (the "Original Proposal").
