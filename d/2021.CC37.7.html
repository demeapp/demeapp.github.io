The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority executed an agreement (the "Agreement") to sell the lands municipally known as 20 Castlefield Avenue and 565 Duplex Avenue (the "City Lands") to 2500 Yonge Street Limited (the "Purchaser"). The City of Toronto did not approve the sale of the City Lands as provided for in the Agreement. Confidential Attachment 1 contains information pertaining to potential litigation and contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.

What was decided

City Council on November 9, 10 and 12, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (November 1, 2021) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council direct that the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (November 1, 2021) from the City Solicitor remain confidential at the discretion of the City Solicitor, and City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (November 1, 2021) from the City Solicitor remain confidential, as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege. 3.
