What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek further instruction regarding a development application at 2793, 2795-2799 Bathurst Street. This report has been prepared in consultation with City Planning. At its meeting on December 4, 5 and 13, 2018, City Council endorsed a settlement from Aird and Berlis LLP, dated October 1, 2018, on behalf of the owner for a 9 storey mixed-use development with residential and retail uses at 2795-2799 Bathurst Street. City Council's decision is available at: Agenda Item History - 2019.CC1.18 (toronto.ca) A Local Appeal Planning Tribunal hearing was held on January 17, 2019 to consider the proposed settlement (PL170696) for 2795-2799 Bathurst Street.
