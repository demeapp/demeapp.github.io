The official record

What the item proposed

There are currently a number of vacancies on the Board's committees. As four new members were recently appointed to the Board, I would like to appoint members to the committees and review the size of committees. In addition, I would like to review the status of the Digital Transformation Committee, as it is near completion of its mandate.

What was decided
