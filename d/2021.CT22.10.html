The TO Live board was asked to authorize a grant application to the Canada Arts Presentation Fund for the 2022 season.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek authorization from the Board of Directors of TO Live to submit a grant application to the Department of Canadian Heritage – the Canada Arts Presentation Fund to assist with the presentation of programming for TO Live's 2022 season at the Meridian Arts Centre.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of TO Live: 1. Authorized and directed Management to submit a grant application to the Canada Arts Presentation Fund together with all necessary supporting documentation, that will assist with the presentation of programming for TO Live's 2022 season at the Meridian Arts Centre, provided that it meets the eligibility criteria of the grant application.
