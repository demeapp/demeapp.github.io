The official record

What the item proposed

Jodi Callan, Project Manager, Policy and Innovation Unit, Transportation Services, will give a presentation on CaféTO 2021. The presentation is to discuss the upcoming CaféTO program for 2021, including proposed program improvements related to accessibility for those dining in the cafés and using the road and sidewalks near cafés as well as new educational resources for restaurant and bar operators about steps to take to make their spaces accessible.

What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee: 1. Communicates its support to the Executive Committee, for consideration with the next staff report on CaféTO, for the enhanced 2021CaféTO Guidebook and its ongoing refinement through consultation with persons living with disabilities.
