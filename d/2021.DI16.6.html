The official record

What the item proposed

Andrew Robinson, Manager, Corporate Security, Corporate Real Estate Management will provide a presentation on Impacts on Access and Accessibility to waiting areas at City Hall. As requested by City Council, Corporate Real Estate Management held public consultations, with specific outreach to people with disabilities on proposed changes to the waiting area/bus shelters at City Hall and other locations that are used by Toronto Transit Commission Wheel-Trans or other customers with disabilities. This presentation provides the results of the consultations, including the steps Corporate Real Estate Management is taking to address the feedback received.

What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee received the item for information.
