What the item proposed

This report brings forward Business Improvement Area (BIA) annual Operating Budgets for approval by City Council as required by the City of Toronto Act, 2006. City Council approval is required to permit the City to collect funds through a special tax levy on the commercial and industrial properties within the respective BIA boundaries. There are currently 84 established BIAs in the City of Toronto. The approval by City Council of the 2021 Operating Budgets for 84 BIAs takes multiple phases. City Council previously approved the 2021 Operating Budgets for 23 BIAs through Report 1 (Item 2020.EC18.11) at its meeting on December 16, 17, and 18, 2020. Included in this Report 2 are the 2021 Operating Budgets for 21 BIAs for City Council approval.
