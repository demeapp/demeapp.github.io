What the item proposed

At its meeting on February 25, 2021, the Toronto Music Advisory Committee considered Item MA8.4, Toronto Music Advisory Committee Priorities for 2021-2022.

What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee: 1. Requested the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning and the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards, in consultation with other relevant City staff, to review zoning, licensing, or other measures to allow live music venues to operate as dance studios, gyms, or other businesses that have been allowed to open under different measures or restrictions than live music venues, pursuant to Ontario's COVID-19 response framework, and report on the possible implementation to the Toronto Music Advisory Committee. 2.
