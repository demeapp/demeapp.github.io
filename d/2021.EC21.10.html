What the item proposed

This report responds to an October 2020 City Council directive, requesting Municipal Licensing and Standards (MLS) and Toronto Fire Services (TFS) undertake a review of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 466, Fireworks. Chapter 466, Fireworks regulates the use and sale of fireworks in Toronto. Fireworks on private property may only be used without a permit on Victoria Day and Canada Day. To use fireworks on private property any other day of the year, residents must have a permit from TFS. Discharging or igniting fireworks in a city park by members of the public is also prohibited. The sale of fireworks in Toronto requires a vendor permit issued by MLS. Staff undertook a review of Chapter 466, Fireworks in early 2021.
