What the item proposed

At its meeting on March 29, 2021, the Film, Television and Digital Media Advisory Board considered Item FB7.5, Sustainability and Green Infrastructure Updates.

What was decided

City Council on May 5 and 6, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, in collaboration with the General Manager, Transportation Services and the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, to develop a business and operational case for a pilot project to install power drops at popular filming locations for consideration as part of the 2022 Budget process or before, wherever possible.
