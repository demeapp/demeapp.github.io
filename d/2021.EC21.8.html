What the item proposed

This report is to request authority from the Economic and Community Development Committee to amend seven (7) existing non-competitive blanket contracts established for emergency services for the ongoing shelter operations in response to the COVID-19 pandemic for the homeless residents of Toronto as outlined in the recommendations below. Shelter, Support and Housing Administration Division (SSHA) operates an emergency shelter system for people facing homelessness and continues to experience a high demand for services that near exceed the regular system capacity. To meet the Medical Officer of Health's order to physically distance and self-isolate if experiencing symptoms of COVID-19, SSHA was directed to open additional shelter facilities. SSHA continues to meet the needs of homeless clients and, since the beginning of the pandemic, has opened 40 plus additional shelters across the City.…
