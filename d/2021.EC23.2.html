What the item proposed

This report outlines the progress on the workplan for the coordinated Downtown West Service and Facility Review. The Review includes advancements in three areas: (1) use of St. Patrick's Market; (2) planning for the Grange Precinct; and (3) regularizing support for Alexandra Park Community Centre. The Downtown West area is bounded by Bay Street to the east, College Street to the north, Adelaide Street West to the south, and Bathurst Street to the west. Through the Review, opportunities for service improvements and coordinated planning have been identified by the Downtown West Interdivisional Working Group. This work applies a property intelligence lens to decision-making and long-term asset planning, including accommodating planned service interruptions or changes to achieve service improvements.
