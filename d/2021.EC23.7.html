The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority from the Economic and Community Development Committee to amend Blanket Contract Number 47022957 issued to R Courier for the provision of courier services for COVID-19 related documents and specimens from various Toronto Public Health (TPH) locations to City of Toronto residents' home locations and local area hospitals and assessment centres. This report requests an amendment to increase the contract value by $850,000 net of Harmonized Sales Tax ($864,960 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries), increasing the contract value from $950,000 to $1,800,000 net of Harmonized Sales Tax ($1,831,680 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries). On March 23, 2020, the Mayor declared a state of emergency for the City of Toronto due to the COVID-19 pandemic. TPH is currently responding to active COVID-19 cases. What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1C of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71, Financial Control, authorized an amendment to Blanket Contract Number 47022957, issued to R Courier for the provision of courier services for Toronto Public Health's COVID-19 related services, by an additional $850,000 net of Harmonized Sales Tax ($864,960 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries), revising the current contract value from $950,000 net of Harmonized Sales Tax ($966,920 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) to $1,800,000 net of Harmonized Sales Tax ($1,831,680 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries).
