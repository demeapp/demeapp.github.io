The Economic and Community Development Committee was asked to order a report on a proposed Reptilia reptile zoo at 245 Queens Quay West.

The official record

What the item proposed

Harbourfront Centre is currently in discussions with a prospective tenant, Reptilia, for the north building located at 245 Queens Quay West, and has asked that I bring this matter forward on their behalf, as a member of the Harbourfront Centre Board and local Councillor representing the central waterfront. Reptilia is a reptile zoo and visitation centre with established locations in Whitby and Vaughan. The proposed tenant would provide the waterfront with another key tourist attraction complementing the CN Tower, Toronto Islands and the Aquarium, when post-COVID-19 tourism returns. Reptilia's proposed program includes animals that fall under the Prohibited Animals list in City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 349, Animals. What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee: 1. Requested the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards to report by the end of the fourth quarter of 2021 on the proposed Reptilia facility at 245 Queens Quay West, including the following: a. a recommendation on whether or not to include the facility under the Prohibited Animals exceptions in Section 349-4 of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 349, Animals; b. the specific prohibited animal species that would be permitted, if City Council grants the exception; and c. health and safety considerations for staff and the public, including but not limited to, access to antivenin and consultation with local hospitals.
