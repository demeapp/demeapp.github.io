What the item proposed

The Dawes Road Library and Community Hub capital project was initiated as response to the locally identified need for more community spaces to address service gaps and to support a consolidation of service and program investments into the community. The Dawes Road branch is located within the Taylor Massey neighbourhood, one of 31 Neighbourhood Improvement Areas identified in the Toronto Strong Neighbourhoods Strategy 2020 approved by City Council in 2014. As part of this Strategy, City Council also approved a one-time investment of $12 million for the Partnership Opportunities Legacy Fund to increase and enhance City assets for community use. $1.2 million of the Partnership Opportunities Legacy funding was allocated to build a 2,000 square foot Community Hub in the Toronto Public Library's Dawes Road capital project.
