What the item proposed

The City of Toronto Heat Relief Strategy supports the operation of Emergency Cooling Centres from May 15 to September 30 during heat warnings where consecutive days have maximum temperatures of 31 degrees or warmer as outlined on the website: Staying Healthy in Hot Weather – City of Toronto . "Environment and Climate Change Canada issues Heat Warnings across Canada. A Heat Warning is issued for southern Ontario (including Toronto) when there is a forecast of two or more consecutive days with daytime maximum temperatures of 31 degrees Celsius or warmer, together with nighttime minimum temperatures of 20 degrees Celsius or warmer or when there is a forecast of two or more consecutive days with humidex values expected to reach 40 degrees Celsius or higher."
