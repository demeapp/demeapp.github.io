What the item proposed

This report provides an update on Toronto's efforts to support the retail sector over the past eight months, requests authority from City Council to implement certain Toronto Main Street Recovery and Rebuild Initiative ("MRRI") programs funded by the Government of Canada, and describes modifications to some existing City retail and BIA programs to better meet business operator and community needs. This report is a follow-up to the report entitled "Support for the Retail Sector - Update", received by the Economic and Community Development Committee on November 12, 2020, which detailed the initial impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic on Toronto's retail sector and small main street businesses.
