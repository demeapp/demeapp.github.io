Amendment to Non-Competitive Blanket Contract Number 47022679 with Star Security Inc. for Security Guard Services at Shelter Sites

AdoptedDec 1, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Non-Competitive Blanket Contract Number 47022679 with Star Security Inc. for the provision of security guard services at various locations managed by the Shelter, Support and Housing Administration Division, in the amount of $4,223,205 net of all taxes and charges ($4,297,534 net of Harmonized Sales Tax (H.S.T.) recoveries), for the period from October 1, 2021 to December 23, 2021.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Non-Competitive Blanket Contract Number 47022679 with Star Security Inc. for the provision of security guard services at various locations managed by the Shelter, Support and Housing Administration Division, in the amount of $4,223,205 net of all taxes and charges ($4,297,534 net of Harmonized Sales Tax (H.S.T.) recoveries), for the period from October 1, 2021 to December 23, 2021. With the requested amendment, the total contract value will be increased from $22,477,606 net of all taxes and charges ($22,873,212 net of H.S.T. recoveries) to $26,700,811 net of HST ($27,170,745 net of H.S.T. recoveries). The amendment is required due to unexpected administrative delays associated with finalizing a competitive award package for Bid Award Panel approval, resulting from Request for Proposal Ariba Document Number 2991705089 provision…

What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1C of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71, Financial Control, authorized an amendment to Non-Competitive Blanket Contract Number 47022679 with Star Security Inc. for the provision of contracted security guard services at various locations managed by Shelter, Support and Housing Administration in the amount of $4,223,205 net of all taxes and charges ($4,297,534 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) for the period from October 1, 2021 to December 23, 2021, increasing the current blanket contract value from $22,477,606 net of all taxes and charges ($22,873,212 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) to $26,700,811 net of all taxes and charges ($27,170,745 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries).
