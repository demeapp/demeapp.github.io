The official record

What the item proposed

Jennifer Quinn, Chief Operating Officer, Nieuport Aviation, Owner and Operator of the Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport Passenger Terminal, will give a presentation on the Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport - Economic Impact Study. The recently commissioned Study quantifies the future economic impact associated with further investments at the Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport, including the planned construction of a United States preclearance facility, and reaffirms the Airport's critical role in supporting post-COVID-19 pandemic economic recovery for the City of Toronto and region.

What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee: 1. Received the presentation (December 1, 2021) from Neil Pakey, Chief Executive Officer, Nieuport Aviation and Jennifer Quinn, Chief Operating Officer, Nieuport Aviation, headed "Billy Bishop Airport: Unlocking Economic Growth in a Post-COVID World", for information.
