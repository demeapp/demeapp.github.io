What the item proposed

The global pandemic has created the need to enforce strict public health precautions affecting the meeting, exhibition, and convention industry. While events (mass gatherings) have been cancelled and/or postponed for the last 12 months, a tremendous amount of work by staff has been occurring during this time to prepare and ensure that when events resume, Exhibition Place is positioned for success. Staff has been participating on focus groups and industry panels globally to identify best practices on a return to business. Based on industry research, it has been identified clients will expect that the venues will be prepared to receive their events and attendees in a safe, clean environment.
