What the item proposed

This report provides an update on the Board's 2021 operating budget forecast due to COVID-19. Since the start of the pandemic back in March 2020 and the cancellation of events from City of Toronto and Provincial Public Health Officials decision, Exhibition Place staff have worked closely with City of Toronto, Provincial Government, and industry partners to actively monitor COVID-19 and the continued impact on the trade, consumer show, and conference centre business. Exhibition Place as well as the City and our industry partners are having to adjust and adapt as the situation and risk factors evolve. In addition to the impact to our business from the loss of events, the Board also continues to be impacted from a loss of ancillary revenue, such as Parking and tenant's services revenue from their closure of business operations.
