The official record

What the item proposed

The Collective Agreement between the Board of Governors of Exhibition Place and Labourers' International Union of North America, Local 506 (LiUNA Local 506) expired on March 31, 2021. In 2020, the parties resolved a number of long-standing issues that were pending arbitration, and negotiations for a new collective agreement commenced in January 2021. This report outlines the terms of a Memorandum of Agreement attached as Confidential Appendix A that was ratified by the Labourers' International Union of North America, Local 506 on August 19, 2021, and pending Board approval, would be the basis of a renewed Collective Agreement with the Board.
