The official record

What the item proposed

The Board and 16730801 Ontario Ltd. (the "Tenant") entered into a lease for the Fountain Dining Room ("FDR"), being part of the Queen Elizabeth complex, dated November 1, 2010, which expires on April 30, 2020. Fountainblu is formerly known as the Fountain Dining Room. At its meeting of September 19, 2019 the Board approved a new lease between the City of Toronto as Landlord and the Tenant for a term of ten (10) years commencing May 1, 2020 and ending April 30, 2030. The Tenant on January 9, 2020 wrote to the CEO of Exhibition Place requesting a term of twenty (20) years expiring April 30, 2040, instead of the ten (10) years, which was approved by the Board on February 11, 2020.
