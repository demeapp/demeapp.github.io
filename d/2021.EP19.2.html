2022 Proposed Capital Works Budget as Part of Ten-Year (2022-2031) Program

The Board was asked to approve the Proposed 2022 Above the Line Capital Works State of Good Repair Budget (Sections A and B of Appendix A) of $20.005 million, funded by City Debt, as part of the Ten-Year (2022-2031) proposed program submission to the City of Toronto.

What the item proposed

On June 30, 2021, the Executive Director of the City's Financial Planning Division provided the preliminary 2022 Budget Process, Directions, and Guidelines for all City Divisions and Agencies, which is still subject to changes. Exhibition Place worked with the City Financial Planning staff and have developed a Capital Works Budget as part of a Ten-Year Program that meets the City directive. The City practice has recently changed from a set budget based on debt target to a budget based on the organization capacity to spend combined with the City's ability to fund. Annually, staff review the 10-year capital plan; this annual review procedure allows Exhibition Place to adjust its current State of Good Repair program each subsequent year based on changing and operational needs, building assessments, legislative changes, mandates and priorities. What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the Proposed 2022 Above the Line Capital Works State of Good Repair Budget (Sections A and B of Appendix A to the report (September 14, 2021) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place) of $20.005 million, funded by City Debt, as part of the Ten-Year (2022-2031) proposed program submission to the City of Toronto. 2. Directed the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place to submit the State of Good Repair 2022 Budget and any changes or directions by the Board to City of Toronto Financial Planning as part of the 2022 Budget Process.
