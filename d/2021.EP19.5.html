The official record

What the item proposed

Hotel X Phase Two Development - Minutes of Settlement between the International Alliance of Theatrical Stage Employees and Moving Picture Technicians, Artists and Allied Craft of the United States, its Territories and Canada, Local 58 (IATSE), Overactive Media Corporation (OAM), and Princes Gates Hotel Limited Partnership (Hotel X), and the Board of Governors of Exhibition Place. The purpose of this report is to provide the Board of Governors with results of the collective bargaining negotiations between Exhibition Place, Hotel X, OAM and IATSE, as well as, a multi-party MOS that includes Exhibition Place. It is a precondition of the above-noted Collective Agreement and E-Sport Performance Venue completion/operations at Exhibition Place. This report seeks approval by the Board for two particular clauses in the Minutes of Settlement which pertain specifically to Exhibition Place.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved Clause 7 and Clause 8 of the Minutes of Settlement as set out in Confidential Attachment 2 to the report (September 14, 2021) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place. 2. Authorized the public release of the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (September 14, 2021) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place at the discretion of the Chief Executive Officer. 3.
