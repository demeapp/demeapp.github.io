What the item proposed

This report is to seek the Board authority to extend the Parking Services Agreement with Toronto Parking Authority ("TPA") for an additional term additional term of one (1) year, six (6) months and sixteen (16) days to end on March 31, 2023. This agreement is for TPA to maintain, repair, and operate Exhibition Place sixteen (16) Pay and Display parking equipment at eight (8) car parks, and also to provide, operate, and maintain the TPA Mobile Payment Program for the grounds parking areas. The proposed extension with TPA will be in accordance with the same commercial terms and conditions of the existing Agreement. The agreement extension is also subject to TPA Board's approval at their next Board meeting.
