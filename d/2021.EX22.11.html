City Council was asked to approve a procedures by-law for the Toronto Parking Authority board.

Official title: Procedures By-law for the Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority

AdoptedApr 7, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority currently conducts its meetings according to the procedures in Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 27, as they apply to Committees of Council.

What was decided

City Council on April 7 and 8, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve the Toronto Parking Authority Procedures By-law in Attachment 1 to the report (January 25, 2021) from the Interim City Clerk, amended by the Board as follows: Subsection 12. (5) (b) (9) is amended, with the words "Motion to resolve into Committee of the Whole" deleted and replaced with "Motion to meet in closed session".
