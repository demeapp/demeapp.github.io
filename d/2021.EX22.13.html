City Council was asked to require open data provisions in all standing committee reports.

Official title: Open Data Requirement at All City of Toronto Standing Committees Boards and Agencies

Adopted with changesApr 7, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

As Chair of the General Government and Municipal Licensing Committee, I am requesting that all city Standing Committees, Boards and Committees participate in the Open Data Master Plan by ensuring that all tabled items incorporate text allowing for the Open Data Portal Team to support the City’s commitment to Open Government. The data is to be provided in the suitable format to be utilized by the Open Data Team.

What was decided

City Council on April 7 and 8, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the City Manager to implement mandatory open data requirements in all reports submitted to standing committees for the purposes of ensuring the City can continue to build its open data platform, and City Council request Standing Committee Chairs to monitor their agendas for compliance with this requirement. 2. City Council request the City Clerk to consider adding additional data sets about the City's legislative process to the open data portal, including data that would provide information about the performance of the City's governance system, including origin and disposition of items.
