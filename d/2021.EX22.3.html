What the item proposed

This report details the proposed approach for public consultation of the Surface Transit Network Plan (STNP) and responds to Recommendation #2 of EX18.1 - Surface Transit Network Plan Update, adopted by City Council with amendments on November 25-26, 2020. The Surface Transit Network Plan is a roadmap to guide the planning, implementation, operation, maintenance, and monitoring of Toronto's transit priority measures. The development and delivery of the Surface Transit Network Plan will involve a three-phased consultation approach. Consultation in Phases 1 and 2 will focus on the evaluation framework used to identify and prioritize surface transit corridors and develop the plan.
