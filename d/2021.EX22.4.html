What the item proposed

At its meeting on February 10, 2021, the TTC Board considered the attached report titled “Transit Expansion Network”. The Board adopted the recommendations in the report, as follows: It is recommended that the TTC Board: 1. Authorize the Chief Executive Officer to execute a Subway Program Resource Agreement between the TTC and Metrolinx, including terms to have Metrolinx fund temporary staff positions in support of the Subway Program for the period from 2020 to 2025 and renewable in subsequent years. 2. Request City Council authorization to amend the 2021 TTC Operating Budget to: a. City Council on April 7 and 8, 2021, adopted the following: 1.
