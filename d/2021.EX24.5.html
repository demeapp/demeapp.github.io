The official record

What the item proposed

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund (TAF) requests City Council authorization to receive $2 million in funding from Natural Resources Canada's Zero Emission Vehicle Infrastructure Program. TAF will use the funding to provide grants to eligible stakeholders throughout the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area to install electric vehicle charging infrastructure. The funding will support deployment of approximately 300 electric vehicle chargers, including 15 fast chargers, in public places, on streets and in multi-unit residential buildings. The TAF project aligns with the City's strategic priorities, including those of the TransformTO Climate Action Strategy and the Electric Vehicle Strategy. City Council on June 8 and 9, 2021, adopted the following: 1.
