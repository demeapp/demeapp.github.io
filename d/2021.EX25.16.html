Official title: Comparison of Campaign Spending Limits in Ontario Municipal, Provincial and Federal Elections

AdoptedJul 6, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides a comparison of campaign spending limits in Toronto-area wards/electoral districts across City of Toronto municipal elections, Ontario provincial elections, and Canada federal elections. It also examines the use of the Elections Ontario's voter's list in the 2018 City of Toronto general election and concludes that small increases in candidate spending limits resulted from the increased accuracy of the voter's list.

What was decided

The Executive Committee received the report (June 18, 2021) from the City Clerk for information.
