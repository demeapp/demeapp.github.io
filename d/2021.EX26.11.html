Toronto City Council · Decision

Request for Presentation on the City Budgeting Process and Initiatives Relevant to the Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee

AdoptedSep 23, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The Executive Committee was asked to request the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer to present to the November 18, 2021 meeting of the Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee on how the City’s budgeting process works and how funds are allocated to programs and initiatives.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee would like the Executive Committee to request the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer to make a presentation to the Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee on how the City’s budgeting process works and how funds are allocated to programs and initiatives of relevance to the Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee and Black Torontonians.

What was decided

The Executive Committee: 1. Requested the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer to present to the November 18, 2021 meeting of the Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee on how the City’s budgeting process works and how funds are allocated to programs and initiatives of relevance to the Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee and Black Torontonians.
