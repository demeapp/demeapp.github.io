What the item proposed

The Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender, Queer and Two-Spirit Advisory Committee, at its June 22, 2021 meeting, considered Item CC30.2, deferred by City Council at its March 10, 2021 meeting.

What was decided

City Council on October 1 and 4, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, the General Manager, Transportation Services and other impacted divisions, to review facility booking policies, procedures, guidelines, terms/conditions and complaints processes to ensure compliance with the City's standardized approach set out in the Section of the report (March 3, 2021) from the City Manager and the Chief People Officer titled "Supporting Policy, Application and Process Alignment across Divisions" that is aligned with the City of Toronto's Vision Statement on Access, Equity, and Inclusion, the Corporate Facilities Display and Fundraising Policy, as well as Human Rights and Anti-Harassment/Discrimination and Hate Activity…
