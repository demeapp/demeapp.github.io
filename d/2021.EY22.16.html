The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates bus service on Hullmar Drive, City Council approval of this report is required if all-way stop control is approved at the northeast intersection of Hullmar Drive and Hisey Crescent. Transportation Services has reviewed the need for all-way stop control at the northeast intersection of Hullmar Drive and Hisey Crescent. Based on the assessment undertaken, Transportation Services does not recommend the installation of all-way stop control at this intersection as the warrant criteria was not met.

What was decided

City Council on March 10, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize all-way compulsory stop control at the northeast intersection of Hullmar Drive and Hisey Crescent.
