The official record

What the item proposed

The Islington Avenue and Rexdale Boulevard re-development at 2200 Islington Avenue will include over 50,000m2 of retail space in 18 buildings. At a time where business activity and investments in our community have been hindered due to the COVID-19 pandemic, the benefits of this exciting project could not come at a better time. First and foremost, this project will bring hundreds of well-paying jobs to Etobicoke North in the retail, construction and consumer goods sectors. For residents that have been waiting years for this re-development, this project will connect them to a variety of businesses and services, including a Costco which will spur increased economic growth and investments in our community. What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Community Planning, Etobicoke York District report to the next meeting of Etobicoke York Community Council, April 19, 2021, with an update on the holding provision for 2200 Islington Avenue, including a complete list of the conditions required to lift the holding provision, and an update on the status of those conditions.
