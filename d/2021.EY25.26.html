Etobicoke York Community Council decides on short-term parking changes on George Street for school pick-up and drop-off.

Official title: Parking Amendment - George Street

AdoptedJun 22, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that the existing parking prohibition, in effect at all times, on the east side of George Street be amended. The proposed amendment will provide additional short-term parking to facilitate pick-up and drop-off of school children at St. John the Evangelist Catholic School.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing stopping prohibition in effect at all times, on the east side of George Street from King Street and a point 202 metres north of King Street, to be in effect at all times on the east side of George Street from King Street to a point 160 metres north. 2. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, on the east side of George Street from a point 202 metres north of King Street and Church Street, to be in effect at all times on the east side of George Street, between Fern Avenue and Church Street. 3. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 10 minutes from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., and 2:30 p.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of George Street, between Fern Avenue and a point 83 metres south. 4.
