Etobicoke York Community Council decides on adding more flexible in-road signs on Prince Edward Drive South.

Official title: Install Additional Flexible In-Road Signs on Prince Edward Drive South

AdoptedJun 22, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Flexible in-road traffic calming signs are installed in the centre of the road, between opposing traffic lanes, and have a narrowing effect on the roadway which can give drivers the perception of the need to slow down. They are designed to withstand impacts from, and avert damage to, vehicles if struck by collapsing and rebounding. The use of these signs is currently in a pilot phase, but Prince Edward Drive South is one of the pilot locations. This motion directs staff to install as many additional signs as can fit on Prince Edward Drive South.

What was decided

Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services, to install as many additional flexible in-road signs as can fit on Prince Edward Drive South, by July 31, 2021.
