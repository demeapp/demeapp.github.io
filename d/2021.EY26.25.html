Toronto City Council · Decision

Introduction of Overnight On-Street Permit Parking - Eastwood Park Gardens and Long Branch Avenue

AdoptedSep 10, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Transportation Services recommends overnight on-street permit parking be introduced on Eastwood Park Gardens between Long Branch Avenue and the west-end of Eastwood Park Gardens and Long Branch Avenue between Eastwood Park Gardens and the north end of Long Branch Avenue from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., as part of newly created Permit Parking Area 21B; Etobicoke York Community Council makes the final decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that overnight on-street permit parking be introduced on Eastwood Park Gardens between Long Branch Avenue and the west-end of Eastwood Park Gardens and Long Branch Avenue between Eastwood Park Gardens and the north end of Long Branch Avenue from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., as part of newly created Permit Parking Area 21B. Residents requested the implementation of overnight on-street permit parking on Eastwood Park Gardens and Long Branch Avenue to accommodate their long-term parking needs.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Authorized parking by permit only on an area basis, within permit parking area 21B, from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily on the south side of Eastwood Park Gardens, between Long Branch Avenue and the west end of Eastwood Park Gardens 2. Authorized parking by permit only on an area basis, within permit parking area 21B, from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily on the east side of Long Branch Avenue, between Eastwood Park Gardens and the north end of Long Branch Avenue.
