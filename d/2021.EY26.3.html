What the item proposed

On November 28, 2019, a Zoning By-law Amendment application was submitted to permit a 38-storey (124.7 m in height, including the mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building including a 6-storey podium and containing 446 dwelling units and 427 m2 of non-residential floor area. The application proposes to amend the former City of York Zoning By-law 1-83 and City-wide Zoning By-law 569-2013 to establish zoning standards to facilitate the development of the proposal. On April 19, 2021, the applicant appealed the Zoning By-Law Amendment application to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) (formerly the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal), citing City Council's failure to make a decision on the application within the prescribed timelines of the Planning Act.
