Right-Turn-on-Red Prohibition - Jane Street at Pritchard Avenue

AdoptedDec 15, 2021 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to prohibit northbound right-turns on a red signal at all times on Jane Street at Pritchard Avenue (north intersection).

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services is requesting authority to prohibit right-turn-on-red movements for northbound and southbound motor vehicle traffic on Jane Street at Pritchard Avenue. This prohibition is recommended to improve safety of the intersection for people walking, cycling, taking transit, or driving. As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Jane Street, City Council approval of this report is required.

What was decided

City Council on December 15, 16 and 17, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit northbound right-turns on a red signal at all times on Jane Street at Pritchard Avenue (north intersection). 2. City Council prohibit southbound right-turns on a red signal at all times on Jane Street at Pritchard Avenue (south intersection).
