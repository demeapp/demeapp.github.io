The official record

What the item proposed

On December 9, 2020, an application was submitted to amend the former City of North York Zoning By-law 7625 and city-wide Zoning By-law 569-2013 for lands at 41-75 Four Winds Drive. The application proposes to permit three buildings of 9, 11 and 32 storeys consisting of 560 residential units and 1,422 square metres of commercial gross floor area, with a total gross floor area of 43,745 square metres and a Floor Space Index of 4.1 times the area of the lot. Vehicular parking is proposed at grade and within a one and four-level underground parking garage consisting of 350 spaces. The proposal does not conform to the built form policies of the Official Plan, and the emerging policies of the Council adopted Keele Finch Secondary Plan.
