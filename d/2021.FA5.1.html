What the item proposed

On November 29, 2019, the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee elected a Co-chair for a term of office ending December 31, 2020 and until a successors is appointed. The Committee will elect a Co-chair for a term of office ending November 14, 2022.

What was decided

The Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee: 1. Elected Serge Paul as Co-Chair of the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee for a term of office ending on November 14, 2022.
