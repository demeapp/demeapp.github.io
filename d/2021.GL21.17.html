What the item proposed

The pandemic has led to an increase in the demand for tools that enable remote work, and real-time collaboration. As more City services are delivered digitally and staff work remotely, there is a greater requirement for enhanced equitable and inclusive collaboration tools to improve operational efficiency within the City, with Agencies, Boards and Commissions, and other external organizations. Microsoft 365 is a leading cloud-based collaboration platform that will enable the continuous remote work and is a critical technology that will be foundational for the delivery of the ModernTO Workplace Modernization Program benefits and outcomes. The ModernTO Program will accelerate the City's transition to a modernized employee experience and improve the overall efficiency of the office footprint.
