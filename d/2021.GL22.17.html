What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to "close out" the Financial Planning Analysis and Reporting System (FPARS) Project, as requested by Council and the Auditor General's recommendations. The FPARS project was established in 2006 to introduce a new budgeting system to support a multi-year, performance and service-based budget. FPARS was a large, complex and transformational project, with multiple stakeholders and corporate partners. It also envisioned full automation through technology with change impacts on people, processes and ultimately, the culture at the City by enhancing accountability and transparency. The FPARS project goal was to implement a multi-year, service-based, citizen-centric budgeting system.
