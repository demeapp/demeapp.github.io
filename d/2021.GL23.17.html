2053 Dufferin Street - Municipal Car Park 670

AdoptedJun 8, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority recommends that City Council remove Car Park 670 located at 2053 Dufferin Street from the list of municipal parking facilities identified in Chapter 950 of the Toronto Municipal Code.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority operates a 23-space, surface car park at 2053 Dufferin Street known as Car Park 670. The car park is comprised of both lands owned by the City of Toronto, as well as, a 2.47 metre wide strip of the northern portion of 2045 Dufferin Street (Private Premises). The Private Premises was operated under a Parking Management Agreement with the owner of the Private Premises, Dufferin Street Professional Clinic Incorporated (Owner), authorized by Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors in 2007. As part of its ongoing review of underperforming locations, Toronto Parking Authority has identified Car Park 670 as a candidate location for disposal. Demands for parking at this location have been consistently low for a number of years and Toronto Parking Authority has realized net losses in each of the 14 years prior. What was decided

City Council on June 8 and 9, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council remove Car Park 670 located at 2053 Dufferin Street from the list of municipal parking facilities identified in Chapter 950 of the Toronto Municipal Code.
