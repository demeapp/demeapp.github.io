The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to the request for the Chief Technology Officer to report back to the General Government and Licensing Committee on public and stakeholder consultations related to the development and procurement of digital infrastructure; and on a request to consult with appropriate stakeholders on the inclusion of a commitment to the City's control and autonomy of its core digital infrastructures. Digital autonomy is related to some key concepts that are already embedded within the Digital Infrastructure Plan including equity, democracy, transparency, and privacy. To-date, staff have developed a definition for digital autonomy that applies to the municipal government context; and have identified a range of issues that could position the City on a path towards achieving digital autonomy.
