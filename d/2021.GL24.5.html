The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide the annual summary of the City's employer contributions submitted to OMERS and to provide information of the City's total members and contributions relative to the overall OMERS plan members and contributions. We are reporting on years 2019 and 2020 together as a result of the COVID 19 pandemic.

What was decided

City Council on July 14, 15 and 16, 2021, adopted the following: 1.
