What the item proposed

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place and Muzik Clubs Inc. entered into a lease dated February 16, 2004, for premises consisting of approximately 37,820 square feet of leasable area, 2,920 square feet of basement space and 86,758 square feet of patio space, in and around the building known as Horticulture Building at Exhibition Place (the "Leased Property"). The lease was subsequently assigned by Muzik to Toronto Event Centre Inc. (the "Tenant") on April 27, 2017, and expires on February 15, 2024 (the "T.E.C. Lease"). This report recommends that City Council ("Council") approve entering into an agreement with the Tenant with respect to the T.E.C.
