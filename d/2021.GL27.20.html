What the item proposed

In accordance with the Relationship Framework approved by City Council for the Toronto Licensing Tribunal, the Chair's 2020 Annual Report is being communicated to the General Government and Licensing Committee for information. The Toronto Licensing Tribunal (the Tribunal) is an independent quasi-judicial body comprised of seven citizens chosen by City Council for a four-year term. The Tribunal receives administrative support from the City of Toronto's Court Services Division. The Tribunal makes decisions about business licensing matters that are brought forward by Municipal Licensing and Standards, or at the request of a licensee or applicant, in accordance with the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 545, Licensing, and Chapter 546, Licensing of Vehicles-for-Hire.
