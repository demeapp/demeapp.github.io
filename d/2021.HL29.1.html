What the item proposed

The COVID-19 pandemic continues to have a significant impact on our city, with variants of concern requiring continued public health measures to decrease the risk of transmission and the risk of serious illness or death. While COVID-19 cases and hospitalization rates are trending downward, it is imperative that all people in Toronto adhere to the recommended public health measures and draw on lessons learned in waves 1, 2, and 3 of the COVID-19 pandemic. The City's vaccination campaign throughout the month of May has continued to be a critical strategy to mitigate the COVID-19 pandemic. In particular, the expansion of pop-up and mobile vaccine clinics has been especially successful in facilitating vaccine delivery and ensuring those at greatest risk have access to vaccines.
