What the item proposed

This report outlines the Toronto Public Health (TPH) 2022 Operating Budget Submission for consideration by the Board of Health. Toronto Public Health's 2022 Operating Budget Submission totals $362,678.1 thousand gross, $128,753.0 thousand net and 2,736.7 positions. This submission is $17,933.9 thousand gross, $4,361.6 thousand net and 324.1 positions higher than the 2021 Operating Budget as approved by City Council on February 18, 2021 (referred to as "TPH 2021 Council Approved Operating Budget" for the purpose of this report). The 2022 Operating Budget Submission includes in-year changes, base changes, and service enhancements pending City Council approval as detailed in the recommendations included in this report.
