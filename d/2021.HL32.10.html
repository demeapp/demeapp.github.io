Climate Change and the Role of Public Health

AdoptedDec 6, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The Board of Health was asked to request the Medical Officer of Health to review the potential to declare a public health emergency as it relates to the phasing out of natural gas if the Province of Ontario refuses to implement such policy in the coming year.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

With Toronto set to debate its path to a 2040 Net Zero Climate Action Plan, it is imperative we look to ensure all of the City's efforts are geared toward achieving the targets our plan will set out. The targets included in the City's plan are great, but the City of Toronto lacks the explicit powers to do what is necessary - a full phase out of natural gas in existing and new buildings imminently. There are pathways the City can look to implement from other jurisdictions in Canada, but Toronto lacks the power to implement a phase out of natural gas equipment compared to Vancouver and other municipalities with greater powers granted to them by the province. As a result, the recommendations going to Council are requesting a phasing down of natural gas, but we are not able to institute it ourselves. What was decided

The Board of Health: 1. Requested the Medical Officer of Health to review the potential to declare a public health emergency as it relates to the phasing out of natural gas if the Province of Ontario refuses to implement such a policy in the coming year.
