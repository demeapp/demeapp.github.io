The Investment Board was asked to extend its investment consultant contract with Aon for two more years.

Official title: Investment Consultant Contract - Option to Extend

AdoptedMar 9, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides a review and recommendation with regard to the investment consultant (Aon) for the Toronto Investment Board. The original contract with Aon was for three years with an option to extend for another two years. The initial three year period ended on January 14, 2021. City staff are recommending to extend this contract for another two years based on the Evaluation Scorecard found in Confidential Attachment 1 of this report.

What was decided

1. The Toronto Investment Board directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (February 22, 2021) from the Director, Capital Markets remain confidential in its entirety, as it contains information that involves the security of property belonging to the City or one of its agencies or corporations. 2. The Toronto Investment Board extended the contract for the investment consultant (Aon) for an additional two years and authorized the Chair or Vice-Chair of the Investment Board to execute such agreements on behalf of the Investment Board.
