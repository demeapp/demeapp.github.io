Investment Manager and Other Updates

The Toronto Investment Board was asked to authorize the Chair or Vice-Chair of the Toronto Investment Board to sign the necessary documentation to appoint StonePine Asset Management as sub-advisor to the Fiera Global Equity Fund as requested by Fiera Capital Corporation.

What the item proposed

This report provides the Toronto Investment Board with an update with regards to activities involving the investment managers and related parties. It includes changes to the investment manager mandates, contracts, and funding along with other related items. The first item involves the Fiera Global Equity Fund which the Board contracted and funded in February 2020. Fiera Capital Corporation (Fiera) announced that it has agreed to establish a sub-advisory partnership with StonePine Asset Management (StonePine) who will now manage this fund. StonePine was created by portfolio manager Nadim Rizk and his global equities team who are currently managing the fund under the Fiera banner. The same people and strategies will continue to be used to manage the Fiera Global Equity Fund and this is viewed as more of an administrative change.
