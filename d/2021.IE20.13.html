What the item proposed

The Cycling Network Plan and the associated Near Term Implementation Plan (2019-2021), adopted by Council in July 2019 seeks to build on the existing network of cycling routes to connect gaps in the current network, grow the network into new parts of the city, and renew existing parts of the network to improve safety. This report is a first quarter update on the initial group of cycling infrastructure projects that are proposed to be installed in 2021 for which design and consultation have been completed. Additional cycling infrastructure projects proposed for installation in 2021 will be included in upcoming reports.
