The committee receives a report on a dedicated inspection and maintenance program for bike lanes.

Official title: Dedicated Bikeways Infrastructure Inspection and Maintenance Strategy

AdoptedMar 23, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On November 5, 2020, Infrastructure and Environment Committee endorsed IE17.3 - Reducing the State of Good Repair Backlog and Enhancing Cycling Lane Maintenance which would update the plan to reduce the backlog of road rehabilitation needs, discuss best practices in dealing with the management and repair of potholes, and define the plan for the maintenance and refresh of cycle tracks. The purpose of this report is to respond to a request for the General Manager, Transportation Services to establish and implement a dedicated inspection, repair and maintenance program that is specific to bicycle lanes (bikeways) by leveraging existing in-house staff resources and newly acquired pavement repair equipment.

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee received the report (March 9, 2021) from the General Manager, Transportation Services for information.
