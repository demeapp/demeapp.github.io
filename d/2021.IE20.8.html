The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Blanket Contract Number 47020198 issued to Municipal Maintenance Incorporated for an additional amount of $900,000 net of all taxes and charges, ($915,840 Net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) increasing the contract value from $8,268,979 ($8,414,513 Net of Harmonized Sales Tax Recoveries) to $9,168,979 net of all applicable taxes and changes ($9,330,353 Net of Harmonized Sales Tax Recoveries). This amendment is required to address the underestimate in the overall contract value for the provision of winter service hours that was required for the duration of the five (5) years ending on December 31, 2021. The increase to the total contract target value is required to process the outstanding invoices for sidewalk and laneway cleaning through the continuation of the five (5) year contract. What was decided

City Council on April 7 and 8, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize an amendment to Blanket Contract Number 47020198 with Municipal Maintenance Incorporated, for the provision of mechanical litter removal services, in the amount of $900,000 net of all taxes and charges ($915,840 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries), revising the current blanket contract value from $8,268,979 ($8,414,513 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) to $9,168,979 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($9,330,353 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries).
