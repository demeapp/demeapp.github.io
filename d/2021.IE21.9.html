Traffic Concerns and Parking Amendments - Construction Hub Haul Routes on Broadway Avenue, Roehampton Avenue and Redpath Avenue

Adopted with changesMay 5, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The General Manager, Transportation Services recommends that City Council designate Roehampton Avenue, between Yonge Street and Mount Pleasant Avenue as one-way for eastbound traffic only at all times.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report is submitted to Infrastructure and Environment Committee as Broadway Avenue forms a shared boundary between the Toronto and East York Community Council and the North York Community Council. Roehampton Avenue, Broadway Avenue and Redpath Avenue are residential streets located within the recently created area known as the Yonge/Eglinton Construction Hub. The Yonge/Eglinton Construction Hub was created to provide coordination amongst the large number of redevelopment projects that are currently taking place and are proposed within a small geographical area, as a way to improve safety, mitigate the impacts of construction traffic and ensure consultation between the parties involved. There are a number of sites on both Broadway Avenue and Redpath Avenue that are currently undergoing redevelopment, with several more sites proposed to come online in the next few months.
