What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to advise on the results of the Negotiated Request for Proposal Doc2865468161 for the provision of Traffic Electrical Maintenance and Emergency Services; and Traffic Electrical Planned Capital Works Roster (Capital Works Roster), and to request authority for the General Manager, Transportation Services to enter into agreements with the top-ranked Suppliers meeting the requirements set out in the Negotiated Request for Proposal . There are essentially three components comprising this Negotiated Request for Proposal .
