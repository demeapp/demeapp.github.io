The official record

What the item proposed

In 2016 City Council adopted TransformTO, the City of Toronto's (the "City") comprehensive climate action strategy to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 2050. Further, in 2019, City Council declared a climate emergency and adopted a stronger emissions reduction target for Toronto, net zero emissions by 2050, or sooner. This report outlines Corporate Real Estate Management's Net Zero Carbon Plan (the "Plan") for the City's building portfolio in response to this declaration. The Plan provides a road map to achieve net zero emissions in City buildings, first and foremost through changes to facility utilities[1] consumption.
