What the item proposed

The Province is adopting a Consolidated Linear Infrastructure Permissions Approach to replace the current Ontario environmental compliance approvals framework for low risk projects related to municipal sanitary collection and stormwater management, and is now rolling out implementation of the new permissions approach inviting the City of Toronto to apply on or before January 21, 2022 for a Consolidated Linear Infrastructure Permissions Approach Environmental Compliance Approval (Consolidated Linear Infrastructure Permissions Approach Environmental Compliance Approval) for these projects.
