Toronto City Council · Decision

Revisiting Permits for Utility Companies to Rectify Safety Issues at Job Sites

AdoptedNov 9, 2021 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to request the General Manager, Transportation Services, to report back in April of 2022 to the Infrastructure and Environment Committee on Item IE14.16 , Increasing the Oversight in the Utility Cut Process.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Utility companies undertake contracts, and in many cases, the process leads to incomplete and substandard restoration work, as is the case in Snowood Drive (pictures attached). Through motions and my communication with City staff, I have asked to review temporary stream utility permits. The City of Toronto has no capacity to fully supervise or fully inspect these job sites, and because these jobs are not finished to a satisfactory level, they are causing the deterioration of the City's capital assets.

What was decided

City Council on November 9, 10 and 12, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services to report to the Infrastructure and Environment Committee in April 2022, on Item IE14.16 , headed "Increasing the Oversight in the Utility Cut Process".
