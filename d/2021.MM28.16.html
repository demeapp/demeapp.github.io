What the item proposed

This Motion is for the allocation of $0.540 M of Section 37 funds towards a capital project that will secure a permanent home for SKETCH Working Arts for Street Involved and Homeless Youth (SKETCH), a community arts organization serving street involved and homeless youth through arts programs. SKETCH, is a registered charity. Since 1996, it has engaged marginalized young people in the transformative power of the arts, building their leadership and life skills. SKETCH also celebrates these young people as culture makers, perception changers, and collaborators in creative city and community building.
