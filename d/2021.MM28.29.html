Business Licenses and COVID-19 Related Closures - by Councillor Anthony Perruzza, seconded by Councillor James Pasternak

PostponedFeb 2, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Anthony Perruzza, seconded by Councillor James Pasternak, asks City Council to direct the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer and the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards to report through the 2021 Budget Process on options to aid licensed businesses impacted due to COVID-19 closures, including, but not. Through the COVID-19 pandemic small businesses have closed due to Provincial orders and suffered great financial hardship.
