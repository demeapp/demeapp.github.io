What the item proposed

Canadian Stage, a registered charity, has provided performances in the amphitheatre in High Park since 1983. Over the years, these programs have appeared under various names including the Dream in High Park and Shakespeare in High Park. These programs have been enjoyed by thousands of Torontonians and visitors. Due to COVID-19 which had devastating effects on the cultural sector, and other trends in the performing arts, Canadian Stage has approached the City with a proposal for a revised High Park program, again titled The Dream in High Park. It would lengthen the season beyond the current performance period, introduce a wider range of artistic offerings including music, dance and original Canadian drama, and include more opportunities for community-based programming.
