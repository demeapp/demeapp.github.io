City Council was asked to fix the address and drop a spent beautification condition for the demolition at 911X Castlefield Avenue.

Official title: Request to Demolish Non-Residential Building at 911X Castlefield Avenue - by Councillor Mike Colle, seconded by Councillor James Pasternak

AdoptedMar 10, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting held on July 28 and 29, 2020, City Council adopted Item NY16.15 without amendment. Part 1.c. of Council's decision requires the owner to register the beautification agreement on title to the property and to provide proof of registration to the City Solicitor. Since the owner has already submitted their beautification plan to the satisfaction of the City Solicitor, this condition is no longer necessary to achieve the requirements in the beautification agreement and can be deleted. Hydro One and/or Infrastructure Ontario commits to do other walkway and landscaping improvements as set out in the letter (December 22, 2020) from Hydro One to Councillor Mike Colle. In order to ensure Hydro One and/or Infrastructure Ontario is held accountable to honour their commitment, City Council is requested to add a recommendation to that effect. What was decided

City Council on March 10, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend its decision on Item NY16.15 by: a. correcting the municipal address of “911X Castlefield Drive” to “911X Castlefield Avenue”; b. deleting the following Part 1.c: 1.c. the owner registering the beautification agreement on title to the property and providing proof of registration to the City Solicitor; and c. adding the following new Part 3: 3. City Council approve the application to demolish the existing single-storey building at 911X Castlefield Avenue, and in doing so hold Hydro One and/or Infrastructure Ontario accountable to honor their commitment to do the other walkway and landscaping improvements set out in the letter (December 22, 2020) from Hydro One to Councillor Mike Colle. so that City Council's decision will now read as follows: 1.
