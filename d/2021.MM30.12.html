What the item proposed

The Mount Dennis area of Toronto, near the intersection of Weston Road and Eglinton Avenue West, will soon be home to one of the largest transit hubs in the City, second only to Union Station. Mount Dennis Station will connect the Eglinton Crosstown Light Rail Transit, Toronto Transit Commission buses, UP Express, and GO Transit's Kitchener Line all in one location. Building on the significant transit infrastructure investment in the immediate vicinity, Economic Development and Culture completed an economic development strategy for the Mount Dennis Area. The study recommended a range of actions the City should undertake or support to accelerate business investment, job creation and a vibrant creative sector in the area. What was decided

City Council on March 10, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct CreateTO, in consultation with the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, in partnership with the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, to identify City-owned, other publicly-owned and privately-owned land in the Mount Dennis area that would be suitable for a post-secondary campus and report back in the 4th quarter of 2021 with potential locations. 2. City Council direct the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture to continue to engage post-secondary institutions both within Toronto on the creation of a post-secondary campus or presence in the Mount Dennis area, with engagement to include the facilitation of focus groups and/or brainstorming sessions with interested institutions as appropriate. 3.
