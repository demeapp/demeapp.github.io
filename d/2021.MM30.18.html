The official record

What the item proposed

In 2019, the City of Toronto received an unsolicited proposal from the Friends of University Avenue, a non-profit citizen-led organization. The proposed public realm improvement project consisted of temporary lighting and landscaping enhancements to University Median Park and was envisioned to lift the spirits of patients under care in nearby hospitals. In its decision on September 30, 2020, City Council adopted Motion MM24.24 that provided support for the Friends of University Avenue's proposal and authorized City staff to negotiate and sign a Memorandum of Understanding with the organization for the temporary lighting and landscaping enhancements. Furthermore, on February 18, 2021, City Council amended the 2021-2030 Capital Budget and Plan for Parks, Forestry and Recreation in order to provide cash flow commitments of $50,000 in 2021 and $50,000 in 2022, fully funded by Section 37…

What was decided

City Council on March 10, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the General Manager, Parks Forestry and Recreation to negotiate, in consultation with the General Manager, Transportation Services as necessary, and execute on behalf of the City, a Construction and License Agreement and any other necessary ancillary agreements with the Friends of University Avenue to complete, maintain and remove temporary enhancements to University Avenue, on terms and conditions satisfactory to the General Manager, Parks Forestry and Recreation and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor.
