Support for New Non-Profit Affordable Housing on City Lands at 25 Bellevue Avenue - Section 37 Funds for Pre-Development Work - by Councillor Mike Layton, seconded by Councillor Joe Cressy

Adopted with changesMar 10, 2021 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to spend up to $150,000 in development funds on pre-development work for affordable housing on the Green P lot at 25 Bellevue Avenue.

The official summary names a figure of $150,000.00.

The official record

What the item proposed

In July 2020, City Council designated the Toronto Parking Authority lot at 25 Bellevue Avenue (Green P Carpark 71) in Kensington Market for new non-profit affordable rental housing development. To prepare the site, City staff need funds for due diligence and pre-development work, such as environmental and engineering studies. This Motion recommends up to $150,000.00 in Section 37 funds from developments in Ward 11 to pay these costs. This work in conjunction with a competitive affordable housing proposal call to select a strong non-profit developer/operator, this pre-development funding will ready the site for the construction of much-needed safe, secure and affordable new homes.
