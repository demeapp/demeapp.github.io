The official record

What the item proposed

Anishnawbe Health Toronto is a health centre promoting traditional Indigenous practices recognizing that there is a need for a comprehensive approach to health care for the Indigenous community. Since 1989, Anishnawbe Health Toronto has been recognized and funded by the Ministry of Health as a community health centre. Anishnawbe Health Toronto has secured a brand new site in the West Don Lands at 425 Cherry Street as part of Block 10 in the West Don Lands precinct plan. While Anishnawbe Health Toronto is not a public hospital under the Public Hospitals Act, it does provide similar primary care to a public hospital and receives annual funding from the same source, the Ministry of Health. Anishnawbe Health Toronto has benefitted from significant support from both the City’s Planning Division and the Ministry of Health. What was decided

City Council on March 10, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer, in consultation with the City Solicitor and the Chief Building Official and Executive Director, Toronto Building to report to the April 7 and 8, 2021 meeting of City Council on the feasibility of deferring the payment of development charges in the amount of $624,243.36 for Anishnawbe Health Toronto Health Centre, Indigenous Hub, a proposal to construct a new 4 storey medical office building at 425 Cherry Street, as long as it is maintained and operated as a non-profit Indigenous health care centre.
