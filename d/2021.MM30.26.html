City Council was asked to add resident and business protections to the Broadview watermain and TTC construction contracts.

Official title: Protecting Residents and Businesses During the Broadview Watermain and Toronto Transit Commission Construction - by Councillor Paula Fletcher, seconded by Councillor Joe Cressy

Adopted with changesMar 10, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

Engineering and Construction will be managing two major projects on behalf of Toronto Water and the Toronto Transit Commission on Broadview Avenue between Gerrard Street East and Danforth Avenue. One is scheduled for 2021 and one for 2022. Each of these projects will create disruption for residents and local businesses. These local residents and businesses have already been under pressure due to the ongoing Don Valley and Central Waterfront project in Riverdale Park and of course the pressures that all businesses are facing due to the COVID-19 Pandemic. Incorporating these requirements into the initial contract will give the businesses and residents the security of knowing that the contractor is incorporating their concerns into their planning from the earliest stage. What was decided

City Council on March 10, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the Chief Engineer and Executive Director, Engineering and Construction Services to include an addendum to the Request for Quotation for the Broadview Watermain Construction and the Toronto Transit Commission Track Replacement with requirements for the contractor to ensure that businesses, including the Broadview/Danforth Business Improvement Area, are supported during both phases of work, with the hiring of a dedicated staff and that the hiring be done in consultation with the City's Business Improvement Office and the Broadview Danforth Business Improvement Area. 2.
