The official record

What the item proposed

This Motion seeks authorization to release $1,625 in Section 45 funds to Heritage Toronto for a heritage plaque to commemorate the Elihu Pease house at 20 Harrison Garden Boulevard. The Elihu Pease house at 20 Harrison Garden Boulevard is one of the oldest surviving residential buildings in North York. The one-and-a-half-storey home was built in 1834, the same year the City of Toronto to the south was incorporated, on Yonge Street near Sheppard Avenue. Elihu Pease, the likely builder and first owner of the house, was born around 1780 in the United States and moved to Canada in 1810. He married Katherine Cummer, daughter of a prominent North York family, and operated a tannery near the southeast corner of Yonge and Sheppard.
