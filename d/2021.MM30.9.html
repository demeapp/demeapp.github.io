What the item proposed

This Motion seeks approval to release Section 37 funds of in the amount of $50,000 to the Ossington Business Improvement Area for the completion of public art and streetscape improvements. The Business Improvement Area will maintain all streetscape improvements once constructed. The Ossington Business Improvement Area has been working in partnership with the City, through the Business Improvement Area Office and the community, on this comprehensive streetscape renewal and beautification project for many years. The plan includes the installation of public art and two parkettes. Each parkette will increase the pedestrian clearway on side street flankages and include new trees, landscaping, seating and public art.
