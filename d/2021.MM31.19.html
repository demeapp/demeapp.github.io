The official record

What the item proposed

In Toronto's current real estate market, affordable multi-unit rental buildings are rapidly being converted into expensive rentals, or demolished to make way for new condominium development. There is a critical need to permanently protect the affordability of existing rental housing across the city, and partnerships with the non-profit housing sector provide a viable pathway towards doing so. This Motion presents such an opportunity: to help a land trust acquire a property in Kensington Market and maintain its 12 units as affordable rental housing for the next 99 years. The building's tenants have already been faced with the threat of renoviction, and protecting their homes is urgent. What was decided

City Council on April 7 and 8, 2021, adopted the following: 1.
