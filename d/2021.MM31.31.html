City Council was asked to clarify No Exit signs where pedestrians and cyclists can still pass through.

Official title: I'm Walking Here - Clarifying No Exit Signage for Pedestrians - by Councillor Paula Fletcher, seconded by Councillor Brad Bradford

Adopted with changesApr 7, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

There has been talk recently of a number of instances in our City where No Exit signs give the impression that pedestrians need to find an alternate route, only to later find out that pedestrians can indeed get through. A crowd-sourced map has generated over 350 locations all across the City where No Exit signs exist but pedestrian access is still possible. Many of these locations are neighbourhood 'secrets' – known only to residents who walk there every day, but hidden to anyone who is not as intimately familiar to the area. Finding a simple solution will make our City more pedestrian friendly, accessible, and welcoming.

What was decided

City Council on April 7 and 8, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services to implement changes to clarify No-Exit signage where pedestrian access is still permitted and to begin installation of the new signs in 2021.
