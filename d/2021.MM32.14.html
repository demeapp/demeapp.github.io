City Council considers: Extend the appointment term of the Auditor General to December 16, 2022.

The official record

What the item proposed

COVID-19 has had a profound impact on the City, including to the work of the City's Accountability Officers. The Auditor General has faced this pressure on many fronts, with a diversion of resources to the pandemic response, constraints to delivering her work plan, and new priority focus areas. The Toronto Public Service has redeployed resources which has in some cases impacted the ability to respond to ongoing audits or the implementation of prior recommendations. Additionally the pandemic has highlighted significant social pressures, new circumstances, priorities, and risks, creating a crucial time for Council to rely on the continuity of the Auditor General's work. The City of Toronto's Auditor General was appointed by Council in 2014 for a seven year term, ending December 16, 2021, under item 2014.CC52.7. http://app.toronto.ca/tmmis/viewAgendaItemHistory.do?item=2014.CC52.7 In…

What was decided

City Council on May 5 and 6, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council extend the appointment term of the Auditor General to December 16, 2022. 2. City Council authorize the Chief People Officer to negotiate a limited employment agreement of up to 3 months to permit a transition period with the successive Auditor General, as determined necessary through the recruitment process.
