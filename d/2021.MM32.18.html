What the item proposed

The Toronto Seniors’ Forum is a City-sponsored and supported group of seniors (60 and over) who are Toronto residents. It was established in 2004 “to facilitate civic engagement of seniors in Toronto” and “to ensure that Toronto meets its commitment to providing equitable services to all seniors living in or visiting Toronto.” Members meets to examine, discuss and advise on seniors issues. It provides an opportunity for senior residents to have input into the City of Toronto’s operations and policies. Currently, The Toronto Seniors’ Forum is running a Show Us That You Care Campaign with the following requests: 1. To the Provincial Government It is estimated that $2 billion annually will support the program infrastructure to enable Ontario seniors to age in their homes or in their community in dignity and respect. What was decided

City Council on May 5 and 6, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council endorse the requests of the Toronto Seniors' Forum 'Show Us That You Care' Campaign. 2. City Council request the City Manager to advise the appropriate Federal and Provincial Ministries, and all Toronto MPs and MPPs, about the Toronto Seniors' Forum 'Show Us That You Care' Campaign requests and that the Campaign has the support of Toronto City Council.
