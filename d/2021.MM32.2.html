The official record

What the item proposed

This Motion is for the allocation of $156,287.06 of Section 37 funds towards a memorial commemorating the life and contributions of former Deputy Mayor Pam McConnell. At its meeting of July 23, 2018, City Council directed staff to honour the late Pam McConnell by renaming the Aquatic Centre in Regent Park after her, and incorporating a memorial bench and commemorative statue in her memory. http://app.toronto.ca/tmmis/viewAgendaItemHistory.do?item=2018.MM44.120 In 2019, work was begun on the project, and then the impact of the Covid-19 pandemic created delays through 2020. In 2021, staff reengaged with the project. While the budget for the bench and signage is being covered by the Parks, Forestry and Recreation Capital Budget, the commemorative artwork is unfunded. Economic Development and Culture staff have begun drafting the terms of reference for a public art commission for the site,…

What was decided

City Council on May 5 and 6, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council increase the 2021- 2030 Council Approved Capital Budget and Plan for Economic Development and Culture by $156,287.06 gross and $0 debt in project costs with cash flow funding of $25,000 in 2021 and future year commitments of $131,287.06 in 2022 for the new Public Art – Pam McConnell memorial project, fully funded by Section 37 funds (Planning Act Reserve Funds) received from the development at 75, 77, and 83 Mutual Street, secured for community/cultural/recreational space improvements in the local area (Source Account XR3026-3700877) for the purpose of commissioning, fabricating and installing the Pam McConnell commemorative artwork at 640 Dundas Street West, as directed by City Council.
