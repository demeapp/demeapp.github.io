The official record

What the item proposed

This Motion is to reallocate Section 37 funds secured from the development at 14 Trent Avenue from 2015 to improve the public laneways around the Main Street Toronto Transit Commission station. In the 2014-2018 term of Council, $400,000.00 of Section 37 funds were secured for this site. In May 2018, a Member’s Motion was passed to allocate $300,000.00 in funds for the Transportation Services Neighbourhood Improvement Program and $89,383 was allocated to The Laneway Project to make laneway enhancements near Trent Avenue, Kelvin Avenue and Luttrell Avenue (2018.MM41.17). Due to limited staff resources, designs for these improvements have not been advanced and the funds remain unspent more than five years after the original development was approved.
