What the item proposed

This Motion seeks approval to release Section 37 funds of in the amount of $50,000 to The Queensway Business Improvement Area for the completion of streetscape improvements. The Queensway Business Improvement Area has been working in partnership with the City, through the Business Improvement Area office on the reconstruction of the sidewalk on the north side of The Queensway just east of Islington Avenue. The sidewalk is safe to walk on, but stepped. By making the sidewalk flush with landscaping, is consistent with the shoptheQueensway Business Improvement Area's vision for a safe, pedestrian-oriented area. As such, the City request the Business Improvement Area's consideration. The reconstruction will incorporate sustainable green infrastructure to assist in storm-water management and reduce heat island effects.
