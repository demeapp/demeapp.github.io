The official record

What the item proposed

In 2019, City Council directed the Executive Director, Social Development, Finance and Administration to determine the suitability for the North St. James Town neighbourhood, focusing on the neighbourhood east of Sherbourne Street, to be identified as a Neighbourhood Improvement Area when the next City-wide review to identify Neighbourhood Improvement Areas is complete and report back to the Economic and Community Development Committee in the fourth quarter of 2020 as part of this process. As a result of COVID-19, this review has been delayed and the work is now not scheduled to be completed before 2022. St. James Town is Canada’s most densely populated areas, one of the most culturally diverse communities within Toronto and one of Toronto’s poorest neighbourhoods. What was decided

City Council on May 5 and 6, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the Executive Director, Social Development, Finance and Administration to accelerate the review to determine the suitability for the North St. James Town neighbourhood, focusing on the neighbourhood east of Sherbourne Street, to be identified as a Neighbourhood Improvement Area and report back to the Economic and Community Development Committee as soon as possible.
