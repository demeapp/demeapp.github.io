What the item proposed

At its meeting of March 24, 2021 the Toronto Preservation Board adopted Item PB22.2, a revised report recommending the Inclusion on the City of Toronto's Heritage Register, Intention to Designate under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act, Alterations to a Heritage Property, and Authority to Enter into a Heritage Easement Agreement - 260 Church Street, which included revised conditions. At its meeting of April 21, 2021, the related Zoning Amendment Final Report, Item TE24.9, was adopted by the Toronto and East York Community Council which included the revised conditions consistent with Item PB22.2.
