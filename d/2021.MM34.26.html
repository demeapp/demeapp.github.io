What the item proposed

The creation of modular supportive housing is a key element of the HousingTO 2020-2030 Action Plan. The HousingTO Plan has a target of approving 40,000 affordable rental homes over the next 10 years, including 18,000 supportive homes, at least 1,000 of which will be modular homes. By creating modular supportive homes for those living outside or in our shelter system, the City is creating permanent housing solutions to help some of our most marginalized residents live in dignified housing with supports. During the COVID-19 pandemic, these homes will also save lives by providing individuals with safe spaces to exercise physical distancing and self-isolation.
