City Council considers: Help for Fontbonne Place.

The official record

What the item proposed

Fontbonne Ministries, the operator of Fontbonne Place, in their discussions with Metrolinx have become aware that running the Ontario Line aboveground in the rail corridor in Riverside/Leslieville poses serious problems for the residents of Fontbonne Place, both during and after construction. Fontbonne Place is located at 791 Queen Street East and has 18 one-bedroom apartments. They are provided to older women, on a rent-geared-to-income basis who otherwise would be homeless or inadequately housed, in response to Toronto’s lack of affordable housing. Women applying to live there must be in one of the following situations: in need of rent-geared-to-income housing, or currently homeless or under-housed, or in danger of losing housing, or living in an untenable situation. What was decided

City Council on June 8 and 9, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Executive Director, Housing Secretariat to work with the Executive Director, Transit Expansion Office and Fontbonne Ministries to review the impacts of the Ontario Line, in its currently proposed configuration, on the ongoing viability of the supportive housing at Fontbonne Place, and the health and well-being of its residents; and include the initial findings of this review in the report on the Ontario Line to be considered at the July 6, 2021 Executive Committee meeting.
