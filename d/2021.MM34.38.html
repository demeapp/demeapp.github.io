The official record

What the item proposed

A fatal crash on the Don Valley Parkway in late May 2021, highlights the need to take action against stunt driving, street racing and other dangerous driving practices. This crash, involving multiple motorcycles travelling at high speed, resulted in one death and sent two others to hospital. Tragedies like this, of which there have been far too many on our roads, behooves us to develop new practices and policies to prevent similar incidents from happening again. For the past few years' residents living along the Don Valley Parkway, one of the busiest arteries in our City, have been reporting stunt driving and racing on the Don Valley Parkway. Since the summer of 2020, the number of complaints has increased. What was decided

City Council on June 8 and 9, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Toronto Police Services Board to increase their efforts to take action against stunt drivers and street racers in order to end these dangerous practices. 2. City Council request the Toronto Police Services Board to prepare in advance on how measures in Bill 282, Moving Ontarians More Safely Act, 2021 might be used to improve road safety in Toronto. 3. City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services to report to the September 14, 2021 meeting of the Infrastructure and Environment Committee on how Bill 282, Moving Ontarians More Safely Act, 2021 might make Toronto's roads safer; and to include in that report what authority would be required to conduct automated enforcement of dangerous street racing. 4.
