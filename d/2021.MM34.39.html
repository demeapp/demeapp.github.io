The official record

What the item proposed

On June 7, 2016, the Broadview Avenue Planning Study was adopted by Council as Official Planning Amendment 343 but was appealed to the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal. On August 15, 2018, the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal approved Official Planning Amendment 343 except for three sites, one being the Salvation Army site located at 1132 Broadview Avenue. In addition to appealing Official Planning Amendment 343, the Salvation Army submitted its own application to amend the City of Toronto Official Plan. Therefore, the Salvation Army has two appeals before Local Planning Appeal Tribunal, one for Official Planning Amendment 343 and one for its site-specific application. The site-specific application to amend the Official Plan is to redesignate the lands from Neighbourhoods to Mixed Use Areas and for a site specific policy to apply to the site that would amend Official Planning Amendment 343…

What was decided

City Council on June 8 and 9, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council request The Governing Council of the Salvation Army in Canada to engage in meaningful discussions with the City of Toronto about the future development plans for its property located at 1132 Broadview Avenue, through mediation at the Ontario Land Tribunal, especially the important issue of affordable housing. 2. City Council direct the Deputy City Manager, Community and Social Services, the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning and the Executive Director, Housing Secretariat to immediately reach out to the leadership of the Salvation Army to discuss how their property at 1132 Broadview Avenue can continue to contribute to the social mission of the Salvation Army and City Council request CreateTO to support City staff in this endeavor.
