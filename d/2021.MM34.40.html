What the item proposed

The Economic Development and Culture Division plans to apply for a Municipal Asset Management Program grant for up to $50,000, through the Federation of Canadian Municipalities for the Toronto History Museums Digitization Initiative. A Council resolution or decision is required as part of the grant application process. Should Economic Development and Culture be successful in the application, the Municipal Asset Management Program grant funding will support the Toronto History Museums Digitalization Initiative. The Toronto History Museums Digitalization Initiative will develop the asset management process for Toronto History Museums through the digitization of the City of Toronto's Fine Art Collection. This project intends to develop processes for capturing visual images and comprehensive records of the entire Fine Art Collection (more than 3,000 pieces), standardizing asset circulation…
