What the item proposed

As we enter these warm, hopeful summer months, it’s been incredible to see Torontonian’s enjoying the City’s extensive, beautiful parks and public spaces in numbers we’ve never seen before. Like many City Council colleagues, I am hopeful the pandemic will bring renewed attention to the value of investing in and supporting our public spaces for generations to come. Unfortunately, many of the communities surrounding our most popular parks and public spaces have been inundated by large crowds and the associated litter, traffic and parking impacts, and at its worst, safety issues. In my own Beaches-East York community, the areas in and around Woodbine Beach and the eastern beaches have been overwhelmed by inaccessible streets, large gatherings and a deluge of litter and garbage. What was decided

City Council on June 8 and 9, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services, working with the Toronto Police Service’s Parking Enforcement Unit, to initiate a towing pilot program to remove illegally parked vehicles on public streets South Queen Street East from Kingston Road to Victoria Park Avenue starting as soon as possible and running at least until Fall 2021. 2. City Council request the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards, working with the Toronto Police Service, to increase by-law enforcement to the maximum possible extent, with particular focus on gatherings, littering and noise violation at Woodbine Beach and the eastern beaches after 10:00 p.m. on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays until Fall 2021. 3.
