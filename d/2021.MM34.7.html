What the item proposed

This Motion seeks authority to enter into a Construction Project Agreement with the Developer, with Lanterra Development (Bay Wellesley) Limited (the "Owner"), and for the authority to transfer Section 37 funds up to $0.750 million from the 951 - 971 Bay Street and 36 Wellesley Street West development for the construction of a portion of Breadalbane Street at Dr. Lillian McGregor Park. The executed Section 37 Agreement between the City and Lanterra Development (Bay Wellesley) Limited dated August 27, 2014, for their development at 11 Wellesley Street, details the obligations for Lanterra to design, construct and project-manage the delivery of the new City park (Dr. Lillian McGregor Park) that will be located on the north side of Breadalbane Street between Yonge and Bay Streets. City Council on June 8 and 9, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the City of Toronto to enter into, and the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation to execute on behalf of the City, a Construction Project Agreement with Lanterra Development (Bay Wellesley) Limited for streetscape improvements on Breadalbane Street up to the eastern and western limits of the currently-under-construction Dr. Lillian McGregor Park, on terms and conditions satisfactory to the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, the General Manager, Transportation Services and the City Solicitor, and in accordance with City policies applicable to capital projects. 2.
