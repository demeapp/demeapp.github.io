The official record

What the item proposed

In 2020, Zoning By-laws 438-86 and 569-2013 were amended to permit a mixed-use development on the property municipally known as 2 Bloor Street West and 820 Yonge Street, consisting of three towers on two development parcels, divided by a new public park. The applicant has since requested that the phasing of loading spaces on the development site, and the location of the office uses required by the by-laws within Building A and Building B be amended, and is seeking variances. This Motion is urgent because construction is set to begin and this change is needed to be able to proceed to the next stage of the Site Plan Application process.

What was decided

City Council on July 14, 15 and 16, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council declare, pursuant to Subsection 45(1.4) of the Planning Act, an application for variances is permitted with respect to the property municipally known as 2 Bloor Street West and 820 Yonge Street for relief from the requirements of Former City of Toronto Zoning By-law 438-86, as amended by By-law 661-2020, to amend the associated development standards only as they relate to the phasing of loading spaces on the development site, and that the location of the office uses required by the by-laws are to be provided within Building A and Building B. 2.
