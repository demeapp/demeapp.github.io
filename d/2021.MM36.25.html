The official record

What the item proposed

Many Toronto residents were shocked and concerned by the actions taken during the encampment clearings in parks this summer across the City. The videos and images that circulated in the media showed a use of force that is unprecedented and unacceptable. There is a critical need to examine what happened during those encampment clearings, and the impact on vulnerable Torontonians and housing advocates. A tent in a park is not the solution to providing permanent, safe, affordable housing. However, forcibly and violently removing encampment residents is not a human rights approach to housing. This approach traumatized many already vulnerable unhoused residents and pushed many who did not want to accept shelter spaces out of one park and into another park, underneath a bridge, or onto a sidewalk or laneway. What was decided

City Council on October 1 and 4, 2021, did not adopt Motion MM36.25.
