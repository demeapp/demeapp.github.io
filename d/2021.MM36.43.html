What the item proposed

City Council at its meeting on July 14, 15 and 16, 2021 adopted the recommendations in the supplementary report (July 13, 2021) from the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning to amend former City of Toronto Zoning By-law 438-86 and By-law 569-2013, approving a zoning by-law amendment application for the lands at 1075 Bay Street. Recommendation 5 required the owner to enter into a Section 37 Agreement with the City to secure community benefits that included a $500,000 cash contribution towards public art to be located on the development site. The City's Public Art Program provides that when public art is to be provided on private property, it is commissioned by the owner, therefore no cash contribution is made to the City.
