Councillor Jennifer McKelvie asks City Council to consider accelerating Vision Zero.

Official title: Accelerating Vision Zero - by Councillor Jennifer McKelvie, seconded by Councillor Gary Crawford

AdoptedNov 9, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The City of Toronto currently uses multiple methods for collecting speed data. Our 187 mobile Watch Your Speed signs and 50 Automated Speed Enforcement units are examples of systems that independently collect speed data. The Toronto Police Service also collects data on the speeds of vehicles. Through this Motion, we are hoping to further understand the management of speed data in the City, how it is used, and how City Staff can better use this data to prioritize the implementation of Vision Zero, and improve safety. We are also seeking recommendations from City of Toronto Transportation Staff regarding options to accelerate the roll-out of Vision Zero through the 2022 budget process, to improve safety for the most vulnerable users of our transportation system — pedestrians, school children, older adults and cyclists.

What was decided

City Council on November 9, 10 and 12, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Transportation Services to report back to the Infrastructure and Environment Committee in the first quarter of 2022 on how vehicle speed data is collected, managed and actioned in the City of Toronto, as well as the key performance indicators and metrics around speed management. 2. City Council direct the General Manager, Transportation Services to develop options to accelerate the implementation of Vision Zero for consideration through the 2022 budget process.
