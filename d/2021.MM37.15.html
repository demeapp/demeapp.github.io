The official record

What the item proposed

A recent pedestrian fatality on O'Connor Drive near Pepler Avenue has underscored the concern that the community has about the speed of vehicles along O'Connor Drive. In 2019, Transportation Services was requested to undertake a safety review of the intersections and the corridor generally. A community petition, signed by over 600 residents, is asking for this review to be expedited. This Motion accelerates some of the more basic requests that the community has made and asking for the larger report to come back as soon as possible. This matter is urgent as a recent pedestrian fatality has deeply concerned the local community and immediate action is required.

What was decided

City Council on November 9, 10 and 12, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Transportation Services to report to the January 6, 2022 meeting of Toronto and East York Community Council on Item TE7.130 headed "O'Connor Drive Intersection and Traffic Safety Review". 2. City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services to consider installing a Red Light Camera at Pape Avenue and O'Connor Drive and to report back to the January meeting of the Infrastructure and Environment Committee on the feasibility of this installation. 3. City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services to consider placing an Automated Speed Enforcement device on O'Connor Drive between Pape Avenue and Greenwood Avenue and to report back to the January meeting of the Infrastructure and Environment Committee on the feasibility of this installation. 4.…
