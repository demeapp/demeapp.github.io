The official record

What the item proposed

The Off-Leash Area at Allan Gardens is one of the oldest in the City and, as a destination park, the park has a very high usage rate year-round. The deterioration of the Off-Leash Area quickened due to the rapid growth of the number of dogs adopted by residents during COVID-19 lockdowns and the numerous maintenance challenges over the past several years now must be addressed urgently. The Ward 13 office has been engaged with the Allan Gardens Off-Leash Dog Area stakeholders since 2019. All maintenance complaints have been forwarded to Park Operations staff and there has been ongoing work as a result, but clearly it is not enough. Outstanding issues raised that have long-term health and safety impacts include grading, surfacing, irrigation, inadequate lighting, broken gates, and gaps in and under the existing fencing.
