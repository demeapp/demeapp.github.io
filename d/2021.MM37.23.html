The official record

What the item proposed

From libraries to recreation centres and community hubs, caregivers of young children often struggle to find safe and sanitary places to change their child's diaper. When changing stations are available, they are disproportionately found in women’s restrooms. Given that many couples share the responsibility of parenting, and the role of primary caregiver to their children, the lack of access to safe and sanitary places to change their child’s diaper forces an inequitable distribution of the parental role. Moreover, it is prohibitive to many same sex couples and single parents. What was decided

City Council on November 9, 10 and 12, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management in consultation with City divisions to report back by February 2022 on the feasibility of establishing a City-wide guideline for adding infant changing stations in all City-owned public buildings including those undergoing substantial renovations, and to report all proposed guidelines through the Executive Committee. 2. City Council request the Province of Ontario to review the current Ontario Building Code requirements for washrooms to better accommodate gender identity and expression and that the Code require accessible infant changing stations in all public washrooms and washrooms added in any new public buildings or during a substantial renovation.
