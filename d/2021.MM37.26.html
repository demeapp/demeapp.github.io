The official record

What the item proposed

As part of the redevelopment of 22 John Street authorized by City Council in 2015, a lease for the Weston Cultural/Community Hub space at adjacent 33 King Street ("Weston Hub") was entered into between the owner of 33 King Street as landlord, the developer, and Toronto Artscape Inc. ("Artscape") as tenant, for a term of fifty (50) years less one day. The Weston Hub, operated by Artscape, is a cultural hub in the heart of Weston. It provides a platform for local creative talent to thrive, as well as reflecting the rich history and diversity of the Weston community. The adjacent City-owned community outdoor space (“Weston Common”) is also the home of the Weston Farmers’ Market, the second-oldest market in the City of Toronto, opening annually for over forty years. What was decided

City Council on November 9, 10 and 12, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the entering into of a Licence Agreement of a term of twenty (20) years with Toronto Artscape Inc. as licensee, which includes a 20-year commitment to provide access to the Weston Common for the Weston Farmers' Market, and substantially on the terms and conditions outlined in Appendix A to Motion MM37.26, with such revisions thereto and such other terms acceptable to the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, or their designate from time to time, and in a form acceptable to the City Solicitor. 2. City Council authorize the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, or their designate, to administer and manage the Licence Agreement referenced in Part 1 above, including the provision of any consents, amendments, approvals, waivers, notices, and notices of termination,…
