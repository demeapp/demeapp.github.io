Authorization to convert a Section 37 cash payment to an in-kind contribution for the construction and finishing of community space by 5200 Yonge Limited Partnership (Sorbara Development Group) - by Councillor John Filion, seconded by Councillor Mike Colle

Councillor John Filion asks City Council to consider authorization to convert a Section 37 cash payment to an in-kind contribution for the construction and finishing of community space by 5200 Yonge Limited Partnership (Sorbara Development Group).

The official summary names a figure of $600,000.

The official record

What the item proposed

Pursuant to a Section 37 Agreement between 5200 Yonge Limited Partnership (Sorbara Development Group) (the "Developer") and the City of Toronto, dated August 28, 2014, the Developer agreed to transfer to the City a minimum of 545 square metres (5,866 square feet) of Social Facility Space, as defined in the Zoning By-law Amendment. The key terms of the arrangement pertaining to the space are as follows: The Social Facility Space shall be provided to the stage of base building completion, plus finished turn-key conditions, to the satisfaction of the Chief Corporate Officer (City of Toronto) or designate. The Developer's cost to finish the ground floor portion of Social Facility Space pursuant to the approved plans shall not exceed $600,000. Any change in plans that increase total costs of the finishing work are to be approved by the Chief Corporate Officer and paid by the City based on…

What was decided

City Council on November 9, 10 and 12, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council increase the Approved 2021 Capital Budget for Corporate Real Estate Management by $434,036.99 gross, $0 net, (WBS Element CCA252-03 – State of Good Repair Leased Facilities/Properties) fully funded by Section 37 funds received from the development at 5182-5192, 5200 and 5218 Yonge Street (Source Account: XR3026-3700740) in order to fund and complete the on-site social facility space. 2. City Council direct that the $434,036.99 be forwarded to the Sorbara Development Group directly, subject to the Deputy City Manager, Corporate Services, signing off on the additional costs.
