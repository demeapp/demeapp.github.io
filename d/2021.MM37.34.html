What the item proposed

The property municipally known as 411 Victoria Park Avenue, 2510 and 2530 Gerrard Street East, Toronto, Ontario (the “Property”) was purchased by the developer from Build Toronto in accordance with an agreement of purchase and sale dated February 23, 2018 (as same has been amended, restated, supplemented, revived or otherwise modified, from time to time) between Build Toronto Holdings (Victoria Park) Inc. (“Build Toronto”) and the developer (the “Agreement of Purchase and Sale”). As a requirement of the Agreement of Purchase and Sale, the developer was obligated to re-convey to Build Toronto a parcel of land for the purposes of developing affordable housing. The Agreement of Purchase and Sale acknowledges that these lands would comprise risk assessed lands, subject to a Certificate of Property Use.
