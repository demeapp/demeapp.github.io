The official record

What the item proposed

The Eglinton-West Little Jamaica community is continuing to see an explosion of development applications with seven active developments currently underway. These projects only call for market value housing, well beyond the reach of local residents in need of an affordable place to live. The upcoming completion of the Eglinton Crosstown LRT, and the opening of the nearby Fairbank and Oakwood Stations, will result in extreme development in this area because of the new provincial transit station development policies. In March 2021, City Council passed MM30.24 which requested that City staff start the process of transforming the Shortt Street Green P parking lot (located north-west of the Dufferin St and Eglinton Avenue West intersection) into a community service hub, including an affordable housing development.
