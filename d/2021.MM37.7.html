What the item proposed

The 26th United Nations Climate Change Conference of the Parties (COP26) will take place in Glasgow, Scotland from October 31, 2021 to November 12, 2021. The COP26 summit will convene parties from across the world to accelerate meaningful action towards the goals of the Paris Agreement and the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change. The four main goals of the Glasgow Conference are to: 1. Secure global net zero by mid-century and keep 1.5 degrees within reach; 2. Adapt to protect communities and natural habitats; 3. Mobilize finance; and 4. Work together to deliver. It is recognized that climate change represents a significant threat to the world and the impacts of climate change are increasing more rapidly than ever before. What was decided

City Council on November 9, 10 and 12, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the City Manager to convene an ad hoc working group which includes the Chair of the Planning and Housing Committee and the Chair of the Infrastructure and Environment Committee to explore having the City of Toronto host a future United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change in our City. 2.
