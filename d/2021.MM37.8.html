The official record

What the item proposed

This Motion seeks Council authorization for the City to receive a $150,000 grant from the Trottier Family Foundation on behalf of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund. The grant is composed of $100,000 for Toronto Atmospheric Fund to deliver a building retrofit program in the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area and $50,000 for Toronto Atmospheric Fund to provide advisory services to the Greater Montreal Climate Fund. Greater Montreal Climate Fund is a partner with Toronto Atmospheric Fund in its Low Carbon Cities Canada initiative that launched in 2020 including funding to Toronto Atmospheric Fund from the Federal Government under an agreement approved by City Council. City staff confirm that the City can receive grants from charitable foundations and transfer them directly to Toronto Atmospheric Fund. What was decided

City Council on November 9, 10 and 12, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the City Manager or designate to negotiate and enter into an agreement with the Trottier Family Foundation for a $150,000 grant to the Toronto Atmospheric Fund for a building retrofit program with activities in the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area and in Montreal, Quebec, on terms and conditions acceptable to the Controller and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor. 2. City Council authorize the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer to receive the $150,000 from the Trottier Family Foundation and transfer the funds to the Toronto Atmospheric Fund, in accordance with the Toronto Atmospheric Fund Act, 2005. 3.
