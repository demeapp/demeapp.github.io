The official record

What the item proposed

The Province of Ontario introduced Bill 27, 2021 , An Act to amend various statutes with respect to employment and labour and other matters on October 25, 2021. It is imperative it include Toronto Transit Commission drivers as they conduct their work to serve the general public. The Bill addresses public access to restroom for those whose employment involves external deliveries. Bill 27 – Duties of owners – washroom access - 29.1 (1) Schedule 5, occupations Health and Safety Act, Duties of owners – washroom access: The Occupational Health and Safety Act is amended to require the owner of a workplace to provide access to a washroom to persons making deliveries to or from the workplace. Exceptions are provided for. Access to washrooms is a health and safety issue that affects everyone. The inability to access public washrooms has been a challenge for Toronto Transit Commission drivers,…

What was decided

City Council on November 9, 10 and 12, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Province of Ontario to amend Bill 27, An Act to amend various statutes with respect to employment and labour and other matters to include Toronto Transit Commission drivers to the list of permitted professions to access restrooms at commercial establishments as outlined under Section 29.1 (1) Schedule 5, Occupations Health and Safety Act, Duties of Owners - Washroom Access.
