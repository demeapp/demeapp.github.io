The official record

What the item proposed

On October 30, 2017, the applicant submitted a Zoning By-law Amendment Application for the lands municipally known as 2450 Victoria Park Avenue. The Zoning By-law Amendment Application proposed to redevelop the lands with a total of four mixed use buildings ranging in height from 11 to 44 storeys. A new 2,915 square metre onsite public park and two new east-west private roads were also proposed as part of the application. The existing seven-storey (7,755 square metres) office building on the lands was also proposed to be demolished. On March 23, 2018, the applicant appealed their proposal to the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal due to Council's failure to make a decision within the statutory timeframe.
