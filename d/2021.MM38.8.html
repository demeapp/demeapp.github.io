What the item proposed

The purpose of this Motion is to seek City Council authority to amend the terms of the lease agreement with Young People’s Theatre (the “Tenant”) and the City (the “Landlord”) to allow the Tenant to complete the necessary Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning system repair through their own hired contractor and be compensated by the City. Young People's Theatre is a charitable, not-for-profit arts organization providing professional theatre programming for children, youth and families, and one of the most respected companies in North America. They have continuously occupied the purpose-built theatre at 165 Front Street East since 1977 and steward the facility since then. Young People's Theatre has a below market rent lease with the City that was entered into in August 2010 and renewed in 2020.
