The community council was asked to allow three-hour parking in front of Lescon Park on Nymark Avenue.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Nymark Avenue

AdoptedApr 20, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending an amendment to the parking prohibition on the south side of Nymark Avenue to allow parking for the statutory, unsigned three-hour limit in front of Lescon Park. This will provide approximately 16 convenient parking spaces for park visitors and have a negligible effect on traffic flow on Nymark Avenue. During the review of this matter it was revealed that part of the parking prohibition on the south side of Nymark Avenue is signed without a supporting regulation. The recommendation in this report will correct this inconsistency.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, on the south side of Nymark Avenue, between Glentworth Road and a point 112 metres north, to be in effect between Glentworth Road and a point 50 metres north.
