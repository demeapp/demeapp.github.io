The official record

What the item proposed

I received a request from the owner of Blood Brothers Brewery, located at 300 Supertest Road, advising that in order to obtain a "By the Glass" - Manufacturer's Limited Liquor Sales Licence, a resolution from the local Municipal Council is required.

What was decided

City Council on May 5 and 6, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council advise the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario of its support of the application from Blood Brothers Brewery, 300 Supertest Road, for a Manufacturer's Limited Liquor Sales Licence for sales by the glass.
