The official record

What the item proposed

On June 8 2020, a Zoning By-law Amendment and Rental Housing Demolition Application were submitted for the properties at 1718 to 1734 Bayview Avenue. A preliminary report was adopted by North York Community Council on September 10, 2020. Since the time of the original application submission, the proposal has been revised to include five additional properties at 1710, 1712, 1714, 1716 and 1736 Bayview Avenue. The revised address now includes the properties municipally known as 1710 to 1736 Bayview Avenue and the revised application now proposes a nine-storey residential building fronting onto Bayview Avenue and 12 live-work units also facing Bayview Avenue. This report provides information and identifies a preliminary set of issues regarding the revised application located at 1710 to 1736 Bayview Avenue. What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Referred this Item back to Community Planning staff for discussions with the applicant, the local Councillor and local community, on how to make the application compliant with the applicable planning standards and policies, including the Mid-Rise Building Performance Standards.
