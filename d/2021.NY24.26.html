Re-Opening Item NY23.38: Request for Fence Exemption - 24 Swiftdale Place

AdoptedMay 21, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Deputy Mayor Denzil Minnan-Wong recommends that North York Community Council re-open Item NY23.38, titled 'Request for Fence Exemption - 24 Swiftdale Place', amended by North York Community Council on April 20, 2021.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Following the April 20, 2021 North York Community Council meeting, I received correspondence from the property owner of 24 Swiftdale Place and their agent in relation to their Fence Exemption Application. They asked me to re-open this Item as they believe that they did not receive appropriate information from City Staff, about the specific chapter of the Fence Bylaw that their construction was non complaint with, before the April 20th North York Community Council Meeting. As a result, they feel that they were unable to adequately prepare to present their Fence Exemption Application to the North York Community Council on April 20th.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item NY23.38, titled 'Request for Fence Exemption - 24 Swiftdale Place', amended by North York Community Council on April 20, 2021. 2. Directed that consideration of this Item be at the next North York Community Council meeting on June 23, 2021, and that Notice be issued in accordance with Municipal Code Chapter 447, Fences.
