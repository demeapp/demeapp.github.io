Request for a report on the feasibility of constructing a sidewalk on Fraserwood Avenue from the Allen Road to Shermount Avenue

Adopted with changesMay 21, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Mike Colle recommends that North York Community Council authorize all-way stop control at the intersection of Fraserwood Avenue/Chater Court and Englemount Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In light of recent most tragic event involving a 7 year-old child struck by a motor vehicle on Fraserwood Avenue, the community requests that action be taken and safety measures for all road users, especially pedestrians are put in place to better protect local neighbours and residents. My office has been in touch with Transportation Services' staff and asks on behalf of the residents along Fraserwood Avenue and its neighbouring community to have a safety audit be conducted and measures implements as soon as possible to ensure safety of all road users.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized all-way stop control at the intersection of Fraserwood Avenue/Chater Court and Englemount Avenue. 2. Authorized all-way stop control at the intersection of Fraserwood Avenue and Shermount Avenue. 3. Directed Transportation Services to investigate speeding and safety concerns on Fraserwood Avenue, between Englemount Avenue and the west end, to enhance safety for vulnerable road users, including pedestrians. 4. Directed Transportation Services to investigate the feasibility to install a sidewalk on Fraserwood Avenue, between Englemount Avenue and the west end. 5. Directed Transportation Services to undertake a traffic study to consider and recommend additional traffic calming and pedestrian safety measures in the area bounded by Lawrence Ave. to Glencairn Ave. and from Englemount Ave. to Shermount Ave. 6.
