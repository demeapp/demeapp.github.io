North York Community Council decides on a new stop sign on Elvina Gardens.

Official title: Stop Control - North-south segment of Elvina Gardens and east-west segment of Elvina Gardens

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting approval to install southbound stop control on the north-south segment of Elvina Gardens at the intersection with the east-west segment of Elvina Gardens. Currently there is no stop control at the intersection. The installation of southbound stop control will clearly designate the right-of-way at the intersection and thereby enhance safety for all road users.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized a compulsory stop control for southbound traffic on Elvina Gardens (north/south segment) at Elvina Gardens (east/west segment).
