The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report concerns a matter for which the North York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to advise Council concerning an application submitted by the owner of 24 Swiftdale Place for a site-specific Fence Exemption, pursuant to Section 447- 1.5 B. (1) of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447- Fences. The property owner is seeking Council's permission to exempt the location of the Swimming Pool Enclosure Gate in the rear yard which does not comply with the location restrictions in Section 447-1.3. E. (1) (a) a violation as detailed below in the following chart: The subject property, 24 Swiftdale Place, is located in Ward 16, in a residential zone property. What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Granted the application for the proposed fence thereby to be constructed at 24 Swiftdale Place. Direct and require that at such time as replacement of the fence is required that such installation will comply with Municipal Code Chapter 447, Fences, or its successor by-law.
