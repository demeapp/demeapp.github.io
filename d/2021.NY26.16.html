Construction Staging Area - 146 to 150 Laird Drive

Referred back for more workSep 13, 2021 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to authorize the closure of the west sidewalk on Laird Drive, between a point 38 metres south of McRae Drive/ Wicksteed Avenue and a point 135 metres further south, from October 1, 2021 to December 31, 2024.

Council sent it back for more work.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Laird Drive, City Council approval of this report is required. Maystar General Contractors Inc. is constructing an eight-storey retirement home at 146 to 150 Laird Drive. The site is located on the west side of Laird Drive between McRae Drive/ Wicksteed Avenue and Commercial Road. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the west sidewalk on Laird Drive for a period of 39 months (i.e. October 1, 2021 to December 31, 2024) to facilitate construction staging operations. Pedestrian movements on the west side of Laird Drive abutting the site will be restricted and pedestrians will be directed to the east side sidewalk of Laird Drive. What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Referred the Item back to staff and directed the Acting Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services to: a. report back once the developer establishes a Construction Liaison Committee with neighbourhood representatives; b. explore options to shorten the timeline for the proposed sidewalk and lane closures, with a view to prioritizing pedestrian safety and eliminating the sidewalk closure if possible; and c. work with the community and developer to establish an immediate strategy to mitigate potential traffic infiltration on nearby residential streets and improve traffic flow on Laird Drive for the duration of the lane and sidewalk closure.
