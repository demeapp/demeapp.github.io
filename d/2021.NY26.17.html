Pedestrian Crossing Protection - Greenfield Avenue between Yonge Street and Doris Avenue

AdoptedSep 13, 2021 · Toronto City Council

North York Community Council was asked to authorize the installation of a pedestrian crossover on Greenfield Avenue at a point approximately 120 metres west of Doris Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. As per conditions of approval for the development at 4841 to 4881 Yonge Street, 2 and 50 Sheppard Avenue East, and 2, 4 and 6 Forest Laneway, a pedestrian crossover (PXO) is to be installed on Greenfield Avenue, approximately 120 metres west of Doris Avenue, with the cost associated with the installation of the PXO to be shared by the City and the proponent. Transportation Services can support the installation of a PXO on Greenfield Avenue, approximately 120 metres west of Doris Avenue, as it will provide enhanced safety for vulnerable road users and is justified based on the assessment undertaken.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of a pedestrian crossover on Greenfield Avenue at a point approximately 120 metres west of Doris Avenue.
