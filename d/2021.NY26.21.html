Traffic and Pedestrian Safety Measures - Stanley Greene Neighbourhood

Adopted with changesSep 13, 2021 · Toronto City Council

North York Community Council was asked to authorize all-way compulsory stop control at the following intersections: a. Downsview Park Boulevard and William Duncan Road; b. Downsview Park Boulevard and Frederick Tisdale Drive; and c. Frederick Tisdale Drive and Thomas Mulholland Drive.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Stanley Greene subdivision approaches completion and the new residential units become occupied, vehicular and pedestrian traffic within the neighbourhood has rapidly increased. As a result, my office has heard from several members of the community which are requesting more effective road safety measures. The request comes after collisions and close calls became more frequent in the neighbourhood as of late. The roads and sidewalks within the Stanley Greene subdivision have yet to be assumed by the City. While we wait for the assumption process to unfold, my office believes that these pressing traffic safety issues must be addressed as soon as possible.
