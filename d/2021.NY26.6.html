The official record

What the item proposed

An application for a Rental Housing Demolition and Conversion permit under Chapter 667 of the Municipal Code has been filed for the residential and commercial properties at 1894, 1898-1908, 1914 to 1918, and 1920 Eglinton Avenue West. This Rental Housing Demolition and Conversion application proposes to demolish ten (10) existing residential rental units and replace them on-site as part of a new development. The proposed development includes all properties from 1886-1920 Eglinton Avenue West and would result in a 9-storey residential building with one-hundred and ninety-four (194) purpose-built rental dwelling units, inclusive of the replacement of the ten rental replacement units. This report reviews and recommends approval of the Rental Housing Demolition Permit under Chapter 667 of the Toronto Municipal Code.
