Toronto City Council · Decision

Ernest Avenue - Traffic Calming

Referred back for more workOct 13, 2021 · Toronto City Council

North York Community Council was asked to authorize the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Ernest Avenue, between Edmonton Drive and Victoria Park Avenue, despite poll results that did not satisfy the polling criteria for the installation of speed humps pursuant to the City's Traffic Calming Policy.

Council sent it back for more work.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office has been working with local residents along Ernest Avenue for several years on measures to address speeding and safety issues along this street. Unfortunately, the pandemic precluded us from taking follow-up action until now. Given the return of pre-pandemic traffic levels and local construction, we have seen a marked increase in speeding through Ernest Avenue and onto surrounding streets such as Edmonton Drive, Faye Drive, Cherokee Boulevard, and Lucifer Drive. I am therefore requesting that North York Community Council approve the installation of five speed humps, generally as shown in the attached drawing.

What was decided

North York Community Council referred Item NY27.13 back to the General Manager, Transportation Services for further review and to bring a report to North York Community Council.
