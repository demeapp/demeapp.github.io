The official record

What the item proposed

This is a report on the status of the legal action commenced by Toronto Parking Authority on January 17, 2019 against BSaR (Eglinton) Ltd. and BSaR (Eglinton) LP (both together referred to as BSaR), and BSaR's principals, Tarek Sobhi, and Tyler Hershberg. In the legal action, Toronto Parking Authority is claiming damages in the amount of $7.0 million for breach of contract related to an option held by Toronto Parking Authority to purchase the retail unit (Retail Component) of a joint venture development at 935 Eglinton Avenue West (the Property).

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Adopted the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 19, 2021) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority, as amended by the Board in closed session. 2. Authorized the public release of the confidential instructions to staff contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 19, 2021) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority, as amended, at the discretion of the City Solicitor. 3. Directed that the balance of the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 19, 2021) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority remain confidential in its entirety, as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
