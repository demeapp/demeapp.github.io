The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority retained Our Cool Blue Architects Inc. to provide engineering design and contract administration services needed to make modifications to operations at Car Park 26 (Queen/Victoria Garage), Car Park 36 (Nathan Phillips Square Garage) and Car Park 125 (Richmond/Sherbourne Garage). Planned improvements include the replacement of in-lane attendant booths with customer service booths, traffic flow adjustments, installation of pay on foot machines and relocating payment equipment. The total contract amount approved in 2019 for these modifications was $70,000, excluding Harmonized Sales Tax. Following the award of the contract, the scope of work for the design was updated to reflect new information technology and operational requirements. What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to increase the contract value of Purchase Order Number 38191 to address changes in the scope of work to be carried out by Our Cool Blue Architects Inc., for the planned improvements to Car Park 26, 36, and 125 from $70,000 to $92,838, excluding Harmonized Sales Tax.
