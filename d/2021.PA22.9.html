The Parking Authority board was asked to approve temporary parking arrangements during the Donlands Station construction.

Official title: Temporary Parking Arrangements - 31 Langford Avenue (Car Park 142)

The official record

What the item proposed

As part of its Easier Access and Second Exit Programs, Toronto Transit Commission will be modernizing its Donlands Subway Station (Donlands Station), located at the southwest corner of Donlands Avenue and Strathmore Boulevard. The project includes the construction of two elevators, as well as, a second entrance / exit to the station at Dewhurst Boulevard south of Strathmore Boulevard (Project). As part of the Project, Toronto Transit Commission has identified a construction staging area on Strathmore Boulevard, which will obstruct access to eighteen (18) on-street residential permit parking spaces and a further four (4) spaces located on private driveways for a three (3) year period. At its meeting of February 24, 2021 (Item TE23.62) Toronto and East York Community Council approved the construction staging area for the Project and directed Toronto Transit Commission to make arrangements…

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority to negotiate, enter into and execute a Memorandum of Understanding or other form of agreement with the Toronto Transit Commission, as may be acceptable to the City Solicitor, to accommodate the loss of parking spaces due to Toronto Transit Commission's construction at Donlands Subway Station at 31 Langford Boulevard (Car Park 142) in accordance with the Terms and Conditions outlined in the report (March19, 2021) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority, and any such other amended terms and conditions as may be acceptable to the President, Toronto Parking Authority and the City Solicitor.
