The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide the Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority with an update regarding the sale of the former City-owned above-grade lands at 50 Cumberland Street/37 Yorkville by PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP, as receiver and manager of the assets 33 Yorkville Residences Inc. and 33 Yorkville Residences Limited Partnership.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (April 28, 2021) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority and the City Solicitor remain confidential in its entirety at the discretion of the City Solicitor, as it contains information and advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege, including communications necessary for that purpose.
