2020 Toronto Parking Authority Annual Report

AdoptedMay 12, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The President, Toronto Parking Authority recommends that the Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority approve the 2020 Toronto Parking Authority Annual Report for distribution.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Each year, Toronto Parking Authority prepares an Annual Report, which presents the overall accomplishments of the prior year, the consolidated financial statements of Toronto Parking Authority and a message from the Chair, Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors. The draft 2020 Annual Report (refer to Attachment 1) presents all of these materials with the exception of the financial statements. The 2020 financial statements are being submitted to Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors for approval at this meeting through a separate report (Item PA23.2). Once approved, Toronto Parking Authority will incorporate the financial statements into the 2020 Annual Report and finalize for publishing on Toronto Parking Authority's website.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Approved the 2020 Toronto Parking Authority Annual Report for distribution.
