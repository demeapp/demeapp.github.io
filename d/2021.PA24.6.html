Toronto City Council · Decision

Proposed Settlement with Bike Share Contractors

Adopted with changesMay 14, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The City Solicitor and the President, Toronto Parking Authority recommend that Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors confirm the settlement set out in Confidential Attachment 1.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Through a detailed review of the Bike Share Toronto revenue collection procedures, Toronto Parking Authority has worked with its third-party partners to agree on the reconciliation of under reported receivables. The purpose of this report is to provide an update on a proposed settlement with Toronto Parking Authority's contractors involved in the Bike Share Toronto program. Confidential Attachment 1 contains information pertaining to potential litigation and contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Confirmed the settlement set out in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (April 28, 202), from the President, Toronto Parking Authority. 2. Adopted the confidential instructions to staff set out in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (April 28, 202), from the President, Toronto Parking Authority. 3. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (April 28, 202), from the President, Toronto Parking Authority remain confidential in its entirety, as it pertains to litigation or potential litigation that affects the Toronto Parking Authority and advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
