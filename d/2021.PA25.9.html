The Parking Authority board is told a settlement report on unpaid Dufferin Street parking fees is coming.

Official title: Proposed Settlement with 2261016 Ontario Corporation

AdoptedJul 19, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to confirm instructions regarding a settlement of monies owed by numbered company, 2261016 Ontario Corporation, who operates Vescio Funeral Homes Inc., to Toronto Parking Authority for the use of parking spaces at Car Park No. 658 located at 2054 Dufferin Street, Toronto, Ontario. This report is to advise that the complete report is being compiled and will be submitted upon completion for consideration by the Board of Directors at its scheduled July 19, 2021 meeting.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Approved the conditional settlement set out in Confidential Attachment 1 to the supplementary report (July 14, 2021) from the City Solicitor and the President, Toronto Parking Authority. 2. Adopted the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the supplementary report (July 14, 2021) from the City Solicitor and the President, Toronto Parking Authority. 3. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the supplementary report (July 14, 2021) from the City Solicitor and the President, Toronto Parking Authority remain confidential in its entirety, as it pertains to litigation or potential litigation that affects Toronto Parking Authority and contains advice or communications that are subject to solicitor-client privilege.
