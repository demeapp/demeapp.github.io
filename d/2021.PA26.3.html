City Building Activity - 2021 Third Quarter

AdoptedSep 29, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The purpose of this report is to provide Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors with an update on the status of Toronto Parking Authority facilities that have been identified for City building opportunities and initiatives or which are otherwise being redeveloped through joint venture projects.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors with an update on the status of Toronto Parking Authority facilities that have been identified for City building opportunities and initiatives or which are otherwise being redeveloped through joint venture projects. Further updates about City building activities involving Toronto Parking Authority facilities will continue to be provided to the Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors on a quarterly basis moving forward.

What was decided

The Board of Directors, Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (September 15, 2021) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority remain confidential in its entirety, as it deals with a proposed or pending acquisition or disposition of land by the City of Toronto.
