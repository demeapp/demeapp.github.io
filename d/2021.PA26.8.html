The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority is carrying out construction activities to upgrade the electrical switchboard equipment at Car Park 29, located at 75 Holly Street and Car Park 43, located at 2 Church Street. The main switchboard and electrical distribution equipment at both of the Car Parks are over 40 years old and are past their service life, making these upgrades necessary. The construction contract for the Electrical Switchboards Upgrades was awarded on July 2, 2019 to Beckett Electrical Inc. (Beckett) in the amount of $363,500.00, plus $16,500 in contingency, resulting in a total approved amount of $380,000, excluding Harmonized Sales Tax. Work at these car parks commenced on July 2, 2019. Following findings during construction by Beckett, Toronto Parking Authority was provided with change orders for unforeseen changes that occurred during the course of the project. What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to increase the value of Toronto Parking Authority contract number 5000-240, as a result of operational changes to the scope of work, unforeseen site conditions, and generator rental from $380,000 to $564,500 net of all taxes and charges to Beckett Electrical Inc., to complete the work.
