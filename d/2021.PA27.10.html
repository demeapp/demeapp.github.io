The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority seeks to retain Toronto Hydro-Electric System Limited to deploy and operate thirty-two electric vehicle chargers at Toronto Parking Authority-operated facilities. Toronto Parking Authority is requesting authority to enter into two (2) agreements, one (1) for the retention of Toronto Hydro-Electric System Limited with respect to nine (9) Electric Vehicle chargers that will replace the existing Electric Vehicle charging equipment that Toronto Parking Authority has in place at three (3) Toronto Parking Authority facilities; and the other for Toronto Hydro-Electric System Limited 's retention with respect to twenty-three (23) Electric Vehicle charges at other locations within Toronto Parking Authority facilities.
