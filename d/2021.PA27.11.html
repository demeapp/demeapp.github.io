The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority executed an agreement (the Agreement) to sell the lands municipally known as 20 Castlefield Avenue and 565 Duplex Avenue (the City Lands) to 2500 Yonge Street Limited (the Purchaser). The City of Toronto did not approve the sale of the City Lands as provided for in the Agreement. Confidential Attachment 1 contains information pertaining to potential litigation and contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Adopted the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (October 25, 2021) from the City Solicitor. 2. Directed that the confidential instructions in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (October 25, 2021) from the City Solicitor remain confidential at the discretion of the City Solicitor and the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (October 25, 2021) from the City Solicitor remain confidential in its entirety, as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege. 3. Forwarded its decision to City Council for information. 4.
