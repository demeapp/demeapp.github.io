The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority is undertaking upgrades to the electrical switchboard equipment at Car Park 29 (CP29), located at 75 Holly Street and Car Park 43 (CP43), located at 2 Church Street. The main switchboard and electrical distribution equipment at both of car parks are over 40 years old and are past their service life, making these upgrades necessary. Construction of the project is being carried out by Beckett Electrical Inc., with consultant services, including engineering design, tender preparation and contract administration, provided by AECOM Canada Ltd., (AECOM). Purchase Order Number 37520 was issued to AECOM on July 21, 2017 in the amount of $34,000, including $2,350 for disbursements and a further $4,000 in contingency, excluding Harmonized Sales Tax. What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to increase the total value of the contract with AECOM Canada Ltd., for consultant services for Electrical Switchboard Upgrade at 75 Holly Street (Car Park 29) and 2 Church Street (Car Park 43) from $34,000, to $133,000, excluding Harmonized Sales Tax.
