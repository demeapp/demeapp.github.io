The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council include the following nine properties on the City of Toronto’s Heritage Register for their cultural heritage value: 55 Elm Street (entrance address at 650 Bay Street) 57 Elm Street 59 Elm Street 61 Elm Street 63 Elm Street 65 Elm Street 67 Elm Street 69 Elm Street 71 Elm Street The subject properties are situated on the south side of Elm Street, between the southwest corner of Bay & Elm streets and Elizabeth Street. The group contains a 3-storey corner commercial building featuring Renaissance Revival styling and built together with five attached 2.5-storey Bay-n-Gable dwellings in 1891. Completing the row of house-form buildings at the west end are three Edwardian brick dwellings built c.1903-1909 to replace earlier wood-frame structures on the site.
