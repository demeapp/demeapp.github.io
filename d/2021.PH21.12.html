What the item proposed

What was decided

The Planning and Housing Committee: 1. Requested the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards to report to the May 20, 2021 meeting of the Planning and Housing Committee to outline their approach to the new system Council approved in December 2020 for RentSafeTO. 2. Requested the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards examine the performance data of the RentSafeTO Program to determine whether any additional operational procedures or other measures are required during RentSafeTO Bylaw Enforcement Officers investigations of service requests and building audits.
