What the item proposed

At its meeting of October 27, 2020 City Council adopted Item MM25.32 - "Implementation of the Federal Rapid Housing Initiative" and authorized staff to enter into an agreement with Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation to secure $203.3 million in federal capital funding to create new permanent, affordable rental homes for people experiencing homelessness. In accordance with the Rapid Housing Initiative ("RHI") program requirements, the City is investing its funding allocation to develop modular housing and acquire buildings in various wards across the city, and ensure that the new homes are ready for occupancy within twelve months.
