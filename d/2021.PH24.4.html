What the item proposed

This report brings forward updates on the work completed to date by the Concept 2 Keys team, highlighting preliminary results of Phase 1, updates on preparatory work for Phase 2, and additional City-wide development process improvements. The Concept 2 Keys (C2K) program is transforming, enhancing and improving how the City delivers effective and coordinated development review services. The objective is to design and build a development review process that is consistent, transparent, collaborative and accountable across City staff and applicants.
