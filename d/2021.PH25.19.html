What the item proposed

Councillor Paula Fletcher has submitted the following Administrative Inquiry: "City Council has been trying to deal with an ongoing homelessness crisis and housing affordability crisis for several years. We have taken important steps like Housing Now, which leverages City land to fund affordable housing, to try to address the latter and especially during the pandemic have worked to increase the housing solutions available to help people coping with homelessness, including the construction of new supportive housing on City lands. The surplus lands in Toronto of the federal and provincial government would help a great deal with efforts to improve the housing situation for many Torontonians.
