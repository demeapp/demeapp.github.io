The official record

What the item proposed

The Interim General Manager, Economic Development and Culture will present preliminary information on the development of a new cultural district designation for the City of Toronto to preserve and protect intangible cultural heritage in the city. This presentation seeks to gain strategic feedback and advice from the Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender, Queer and Two-Spirit Advisory Committee.

What was decided

The Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender, Queer, and Two-Spirit Advisory Committee: 1. Requested the Interim General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, to incorporate the Committee's feedback into the development of a cultural district plan for the City of Toronto.
