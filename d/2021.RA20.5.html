The official record

What the item proposed

Further to the recommendations approved by the Board of Directors of CreateTO on October 9, 2020 pursuant to RA17.7, a request for proposals for an executive search consultant to assist with the recruitment of a Chief Executive Officer for CreateTO will be issued on January 6, 2021. I will provide a verbal update on the recruitment process at the January 11, 2021 meeting of the Board.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Received the letter (December 24, 2020) from the Chair, CreateTO for information.
