What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to update the Board of Directors of CreateTO (the “Board”) on the on-going collaboration between CreateTO and Senior Services and Long-Term Care Division (“SSLTC”) to identify opportunities that enable the timely delivery of City operated long-term care beds. The Ministry of Long-Term Care (“MOLTC”) has identified the mandatory redevelopment of Class “B” and “C” long-term care homes in order to bring these facilities into conformance with the design standards issued by the Ministry of Long-Term Care (MOLTC) to improve the physical and service needs of residents. To date, there are about 15,200 long-term care beds operated out of 84 facilities within Toronto. What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Endorsed the proposal to build a new Senior Services and Long-Term Care facility on the City owned property located at 4610 Finch Avenue East, as identified in Attachment A to the report (June 22, 2021) from the Vice President, Client Relationship Management, CreateTO. 2. Directed the Interim Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO, in consultation with the General Manager, Senior Services and Long-Term Care, Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, and the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management to explore the feasibility to potentially locate a new Senior Services and Long-Term Care facility at the City owned property at the Don Valley Parkway and York Mills Road, as outlined in Attachment B to the report (June 22, 2021) from the Vice President, Client Relationship Management, CreateTO. 3.
