What the item proposed

The Basin Media Hub is a development proposal being advanced by CreateTO on behalf of Toronto Port Lands Company (TPLC), for a purpose-built and state-of-the-art film, television, and digital media complex in the Film Studio District of the Port Lands. This development will significantly contribute to ongoing growth of Toronto’s screen-based industries and represent a major step forward in the revitalization of the Port Lands. At its meeting on October 12, 2021, the TPLC Board of Directors will be considering a term sheet (the "Term Sheet") which Management has negotiated with the preferred proponent.
