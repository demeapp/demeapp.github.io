The Sign Variance Committee was asked to grant the requested variances for Metrolinx.

Official title: Application for Five Variances, subject to One Condition, respecting One Third Party Electronic Ground Sign located in the Galt Subdivision (40 metres west of Highway 427)

The official record

What the item proposed

Metrolinx (the "Applicant") has made an application for five variances, one of which is subject to one condition, required to allow for a permit to be issued to allow the erection and display of one third party electronic ground sign with electronic static copy (the "Proposed Sign") to be located on the premises functioning as a railway corridor known as Galt Subdivision, (the "Proposed Location"), as further described in Attachment 1. The Proposed Sign will be located on an existing elevated structure above the railway, approximately 300 metres north of North Queen Street, between The East Mall and The West Mall and 40 metres west of Highway 427 and facing in a north-easterly and south-easterly direction along Highway 427. The Applicant has retained Van Horne Outdoor to act as their representative with respect to this application. The Proposed Location is a Utility ("U") Sign District,…

What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Refused to grant any of five requested variances, consisting of the four requested variances to sections 694-24.A(17), 694-25D(3)(a), 694-25D(3)(b), and 694-25D(3)(e) or the one requested variance to section 694-25D(3)(k), subject to one condition, as required to allow for the issuance of a permit respecting the erection and display of the Proposed Sign, as further described in Attachment 1 to this report (May 19, 2021) from Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building.
