What the item proposed

This application proposes to amend the Official Plan to add a Site and Area-Specific Policy ("SASP") for the property at 1960 Eglinton Avenue East (see Attachment 1: Location Map) to permit a mixed-use development including a new public street and parkland. The subject lands are located within the Golden Mile Secondary Plan ("GMSP") area approved through Official Plan Amendment No. 499 ("OPA 499"), and are also subject to new Golden Mile Urban Design Guidelines, as adopted by City Council on October 30, 2020. The application was submitted on February 4, 2020 and has been appealed to the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal ("LPAT") citing City Council's failure to make a decision within the prescribed time frames set out in the Planning Act.
