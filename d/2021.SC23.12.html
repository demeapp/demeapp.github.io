What the item proposed

Scarborough Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision on requests for exemptions to Chapter 447- Fences. The purpose of this report is to advise Scarborough Community Council concerning an application by the owner(s) of 25 Conference Blvd for a site-specific Fence Exemption, pursuant to Section 447-2 of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447- Fences. The property owner(s) is seeking Scarborough Community Council's permission to be exempt concerning a proposed rear yard fence which will not comply with restrictions contrary to Section 447-1.3 of the bylaw as detailed below in the following chart: The subject property 25 Conference Blvd is located in Ward 25, in a residential zone property.
