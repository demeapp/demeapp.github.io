The official record

What the item proposed

Residents have voiced their concerns regarding pedestrian safety due to the lack of sidewalks on Falaise Road. The local road is a 600 metre stretch from Kingston Road to Warnsworth Street neighbouring two schools, West Hill Collegiate and St. Margaret’s Public School, and is heavily utilized by pedestrians as a means of exercise, recreation and a passway to facilities and transportation means. Area residents have submitted a petition, receiving 30% resident support, requesting Transportation Services install a sidewalk on this Falaise Road. Transportation Services staff confirmed their support and have placed the project on a list to be studied and programmed, but report that delivery will take up to 5 years to implement as current projects and programming are subscribed into 2023. What was decided

City Council on May 5 and 6, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council request Transportation Services and Engineering and Construction Services and required Divisions to install a Pedestrian Sidewalk on Falaise Road from Kingston Road to Warnsworth Street and that: a. all required planning and design be completed by 2022 for the installation, construction and completion in 2022; and b. all required divisions add this project to their 2022 project list for completion in 2022.
