The official record

What the item proposed

Bluffer's park has become a beautiful destination spot for many visitors from near and far. As a result, the surrounding residential area is subject to severe traffic congestion and parking issues during the summer months. I have directed Transportation Services to review the parking by-laws on Sunnypoint Crescent, and support their recommendation that a no standing by-law be implemented, as per the following report. I am requesting this be submitted as a new business item.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council directed that: 1. Transportation Services amend the traffic and parking by-law by enacting No Standing on the west, north, and south side of Sunnypoint Crescent between Brooklawn Avenue and Eastville Avenue from 9:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., Saturday, Sunday, and public holidays from May 1 to September 30 of each year.
