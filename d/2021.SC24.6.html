The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending short-term parking be provided on the south side, and no stopping on the north side of Stamford Square South in the vicinity of Our Lady of Fatima Catholic School. The proposed changes will allow for parents/guardians to pick-up/drop-off students near the schools frontage and prevent students from crossing the road after getting picked-up/dropped-off by parents. The proposed changes will allow for two-way traffic flow and reduce congestion on Stamford Square South during the schools start and end times.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized maximum 15-minute parking on the west and south side of Stamford Square South, between Stamford Square North and a point 141 metres further south and east, from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., and from 2:30 p.m. to 3:30 p.m. Monday to Friday, except public holidays, from September 1 of one year to June 30 of the next year. 2. Authorized maximum 15-minute parking on the south, and east side of Stamford Square South, between Stellarton Road and a point 95 metres south and west, from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., and from 2:30 p.m. to 3:30 p.m. Monday to Friday, except public holidays, from September 1 of one year to June 30 of the next year. 3. Prohibited stopping on the north, east, and west side (opposite to school side) of Stamford Square South, between Stamford Square North and Stellarton Road from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., and from 2:30 p.m. to 3:30 p.m.…
