Lane Designation - Ellesmere Road at Victoria Park Avenue

AdoptedJun 8, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The Acting Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services recommends that City Council designate the northerly westbound lane on Ellesmere Road, between Victoria Park Avenue and a point 95.5 metres east, for westbound right-turns only, buses exempted.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Ellesmere Road, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting approval to designate the westbound right-turn lane on Ellesmere Road at Victoria Park Avenue for right-turns only, buses excepted. This by-law will clearly define traffic lane usage and eliminate confusion as TTC vehicles continue to travel through the intersection in the westbound direction from the right-turn lane.

What was decided

City Council on June 8 and 9, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council designate the northerly westbound lane on Ellesmere Road, between Victoria Park Avenue and a point 95.5 metres east, for westbound right-turns only, buses exempted.
