Toronto City Council · Decision

Overnight Parking Review - Roads in Ward 22

WithdrawnMay 18, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Nick Mantas recommends that Scarborough Community Council allow overnight parking from 2:00 am to 6:00 am on both sides of all streets in the Ward 22 area bounded by Victoria Park Avenue, Highway 401, Midland Avenue and Finch Avenue East.

The item was withdrawn before a final decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The prohibition of on-street overnight parking south of Finch Avenue East was considered and approved by Scarborough Community Council on September 16, 2019. Subsequently, the previous Councillor received numerous emails from residents expressing their concerns that there was insufficient off-street parking available. COVID-19 only served to exacerbate the problem as family members consolidated their living arrangements to look after one another and unemployed family members returned home due to financial pressures. Therefore, I am asking the members of Scarborough Community Council to support the revocation of the prohibition of overnight on-street parking south of Finch Avenue East, east of Victoria Park, west of Midland Avenue and north of Highway 401.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council withdrew the Item.
