Morningside Avenue, St. Clair Avenue East, and Warden Avenue - Speed Limit By-law Revision

AdoptedOct 1, 2021 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to reduce the speed limit from 60 km/h to 50 km/h on Morningside Avenue between Kingston Road and Tams Road/Pan Am Drive.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about speed limit reductions on major arterial roadways requiring City Council approval. The purpose of this report is to recommend speed limit "housekeeping" by-law amendments to correct by-law amendments previously presented to Scarborough Community Council in January 2020 so that they properly align with the by-laws for speed limit reduction recommendations previously presented to City Council in July 2019 for the following locations: Morningside Avenue between Kingston Road and Tams Road/Pan Am Drive, and between Milner Avenue and Finch Avenue East; St. Clair Avenue East between Kingston Road and Danforth Road, and between Kennedy Road and Birchmount Road; and Warden Avenue between Steeles Avenue East and Arkona Drive/Clover Leaf Gate, and between Metropolitan Road a point 305 metres north of Mack Avenue.

What was decided

City Council on October 1 and 4, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council reduce the speed limit from 60 kilometres per hour to 50 kilometres per hour on Morningside Avenue between Kingston Road and Tams Road/Pan Am Drive. 2. City Council reduce the speed limit from 60 kilometres per hour to 50 kilometres per hour on Morningside Avenue between Milner Avenue and Finch Avenue East. 3. City Council reduce the speed limit from 60 kilometres per hour to 50 kilometres per hour on St. Clair Avenue East between Kingston Road and Danforth Road. 4. City Council reduce the speed limit from 60 kilometres per hour to 50 kilometres per hour on St. Clair Avenue East between Kennedy Road and Birchmount Road. 5.
