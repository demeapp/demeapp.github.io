The official record

What the item proposed

City Council at its meeting of July 23, 2018 adopted item TE34.148 entitled, "Traffic Management Plan – Glen Road" with amendments. City Council ruled that only Toronto and East York Community Council Recommendation 2 of this report was in order, as the balance of the recommendations on compulsory all way stop regulations, and speed limits, should be resubmitted to the Toronto and East York Community Council for consideration under its delegated authority. The following recommendations are resubmitted to the Toronto and East York Community Council for consideration under its delegated authority.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing all-way stop control at Glen Road and Roxborough Drive, to be in effect at Glen Road and Roxborough Drive (south intersection). 2. Rescinded the compulsory stop controls for southbound and northbound traffic on Glen Road at Bin-Scarth Road. 3. Reduced the speed limit from 40km/h to 30km/h on Glen Road, between Elm Avenue and Summerhill Avenue.
