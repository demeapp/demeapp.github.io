Gough Avenue Speed Humps

AdoptedJan 12, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to request Transportation Services investigate traffic calming measures, such as speed humps, for Gough Avenue and report back by the June 24.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Gough Avenue is a one-way northbound residential street near Pape Avenue and Danforth Avenue. Residents of Gough Avenue contacted my office expressing serious concern for safety on their street having witnessed having witnessed many collisions and vehicles exceeding the speed limit. Local residents are interested in having speed humps installed on Gough Avenue between Danforth Avenue and Canning Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested Transportation Services staff to investigate traffic calming measures, such as speed humps, for Gough Avenue and to report back by the June 24, 2021 Toronto and East York Community Council Meeting.
