The official record

What the item proposed

The Plan of Subdivision proposes to create four blocks and two new public roads. Block 1 (the south-east block), Block 2 (the north-east block) and Block 4 (the north-west block) will be developed with five mixed-use buildings and one office/commercial building ranging in height from 25 storeys to 90 storeys. A total gross floor area (GFA) of 424,705 square metres is permitted of which approximately 80% is residential and 20% is non-residential. A child care and an elementary school are planned in the base of the building in Block 4. Block 3 (the south-west block) is anticipated to be a new public park. Respecting streets, the extension of Harbour Street from Yonge Street to Freeland Street as part of the 1 to 7 Yonge Street development to the west of the subject site is continued through the centre of the lands.
