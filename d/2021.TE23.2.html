City Council was asked to permanently close and sell the public lane known as Brigden Place for a mixed-use redevelopment.

Official title: Permanent Closure of the City-owned Public Lane Known as Brigden Place

AdoptedMar 10, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services recommends that the public lane known as Brigden Place, located west of Ontario Street and south of Queen Street East, as shown in Appendix "A", be permanently closed. Once closed the lane will be sold to Richmond GP INC., as general partner for and on behalf of, Richmond Residential Limited Partnership to be incorporated into the mixed-use redevelopment of the block generally bounded by Queen Street East to the north, Ontario Street to the east, Richmond Street East to the south and McFarrens Lane to the west.

What was decided

City Council on March 10, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the permanent closure of the public lane known as Brigden Place (the "Lane"), shown as Part 1 on Sketch No. PS-2016-125, attached as Appendix A to the report (February 5, 2021) from the Director, Transportation Planning and Capital Program, Transportation Services. 2. City Council enact a By-law substantially in the form of the draft by-law attached as Appendix B to the report (February 5, 2021) from the Director, Transportation Planning and Capital Program, Transportation Services.
