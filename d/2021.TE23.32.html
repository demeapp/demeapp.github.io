What the item proposed

This staff report is in regards to a matter for which the Toronto and East York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to consider an application submitted by the owner(s) of 237 Warren Rd for a site-specific Fence Exemption, pursuant to the Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447- Fences. The property owner(s) is seeking Council's permission to be exempt from Chapter 447–1.3.C(3) –'If the wall of any building, or any portion of it, forms part of the pool enclosure, there shall be no access to the enclosed pool area through the wall', contrary to Section 447-1.3.C(3) of the bylaw, as detailed in the following chart: The subject property 237 Warren Rd is located in Ward 12, on a residentially zoned lot.
