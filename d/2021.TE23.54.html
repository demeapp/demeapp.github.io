510-528 Yonge Street and 7 Breadalbane Street - Official Plan Amendment, Zoning By-law Amendment and Rental Housing Demolition Applications - Preliminary Report

The community council considers a preliminary report on planning applications for 510-528 Yonge Street and 7 Breadalbane Street, which staff do not support in their current form.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information and identifies a preliminary set of issues regarding the Official Plan Amendment, Zoning By-law Amendment, and Rental Housing Demolition applications for 510-528 Yonge Street and 7 Breadalbane Street. Staff are currently reviewing the applications, which are not supportable in their current form, and will continue working with the applicant to address the issues outlined in this report in an attempt to achieve a proposal that could be supported by staff. The applications have also been circulated to all appropriate agencies and City divisions for comment and staff will proceed to schedule a community consultation meeting with the Ward Councillor.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council directed: 1. Staff schedule a community consultation meeting for the lands pertaining to 510-528 Yonge Street and 7 Breadalbane Street, which includes the provision of accessibility resources including closed captioning and, upon request, sign language interpretation at the sole cost of the applicant, in consultation with the Ward Councillor. 2. Notice for the community consultation meeting be given to landowners and residents within 120 metres of the application site, and to additional residents, institutions and owners to be determined in consultation with the Ward Councillor, with any additional mailing costs to be borne by the applicant. 3.
