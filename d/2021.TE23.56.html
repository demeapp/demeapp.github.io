Construction Staging Area - 898-900 St. Clair Avenue West (Alberta Street)

AdoptedFeb 24, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The community council was asked to approve a construction staging closure on Alberta Avenue for a 12-storey condo at 898-900 St. Clair Avenue West.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Canderel Residential Incorporated is constructing a 12-storey condominium building with ground floor retail at 898-900 St. Clair Avenue West. The site is located on the northeast corner of St. Clair Avenue West and Alberta Avenue. Transportation Services is requesting authorization to close the east sidewalk and a 7.3-metre-wide portion of the northbound curb lane and adjacent lane on Alberta Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 65 metres north, for a period of 12 months (i.e., February 25, 2021 to February 28, 2022) to accommodate a construction staging area. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the closure of the east sidewalk and a 7.3-metre-wide portion of the northbound curb lane and adjacent lane on Alberta Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 65 metres north, from February 25, 2021 to February 28, 2022. 2. Rescinded the existing parking machine regulation in effect from 10:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday and 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday, at a rate of $2.00 per hour and for a maximum period of 3 hours, on the both sides of Alberta Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and the north city limit of the road. 3. Rescinded the existing permit parking in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., on both sides of Alberta Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 54.9 metres further north. 4.
