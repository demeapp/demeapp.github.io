Construction Staging Area - 5 Scrivener Square, 4-10R Price Street and 1095-1107 Yonge Street

The community council was asked to approve a construction staging closure on Scrivener Square for a 21-storey development, March 2021 to March 2025.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Diamond Corporation is constructing a 21-storey mixed use building with ground and second floor non-residential uses and 141 residential units, at 5 Scrivener Square, 4-10R Price Street and 1095-1107 Yonge Street. The site is an "L" shaped property located on the southeast corner of Yonge Street and Scrivener Square. Transportation Services is requesting authorization to close the south sidewalk on Scrivener Square, between Yonge Street and the east limit of the roadway, for a period of 49 months (i.e., March 1, 2021 to March 31, 2025) to accommodate a construction staging area. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the closure of the south sidewalk on Scrivener Square, between Yonge Street and a point 81 metres east, from March 1, 2021 to March 31, 2025. 2. Rescinded the existing standing prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Scrivener Square (east-west leg), between Yonge Street and Scrivener Square (north-south leg). 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the south side of Scrivener Square (east-west leg), between Yonge Street and Scrivener Square (north-south leg). 4. Prohibited stopping at all times on both sides of Scrivener Square (north-south leg), between Price Street and Scrivener Square (east-west leg). 5.
