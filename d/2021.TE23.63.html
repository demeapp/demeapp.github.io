Removal of Accessible Loading Zone - Laughton Avenue

AdoptedFeb 24, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The community council was asked to remove an accessible loading zone on Laughton Avenue that the property's new resident says is no longer needed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is recommending the removal of a designated on-street accessible loading zone located on the west side of Laughton Avenue, between Pelham Avenue and Davenport Road. Staff has been advised by the new resident of the property that the accessible loading zone is not required and can be removed.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council recommends that: 1. Rescinded the existing accessible loading zone in effect at all time, on the west side of Laughton Avenue, between a point 94.6 metres north of Pelham Avenue and a point 9 metres further north.
