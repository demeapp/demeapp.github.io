The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. Transportation Services is recommending removal of car-share vehicle parking areas (CVPAs) at various locations in the Toronto and East York District.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council : 1. Rescinded the car-share vehicle parking area for up to two Zipcar vehicles on the east side of Brunel Court, between a point 33 metres south of Forth York Boulevard and a point 11 metres further south. 2. Prohibited stopping from 7:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Brunel Court, between a point 33 metres south of Fort York Boulevard and a point 11 metres further south. 3. Authorized the installation of parking machines to be in effect from 4:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Friday; from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. on Saturday, and from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. on Sunday, for a maximum period of three hours at a rate of $3.00 per hour on the east side of Brunel Court, between a point 33 metres south of Fort York Boulevard and a point 11 metres further south. 4.
