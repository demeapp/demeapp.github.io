City Council was asked to exclude the 1249-1251 Queen Street East development from Permit Parking Area 8D.

Official title: Realignment of Permit Parking Area 8D to exclude the development located at 1249-1251 Queen Street East

AdoptedMar 10, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services is requesting approval from City Council to prohibit the General Manager from accepting applications from residents of, visitors to, and tradespersons at the development properties municipally known as 1249-1251 Queen Street East from residential on-street permit parking.

What was decided

City Council on March 10, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend Schedule B of the City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 925, Permit Parking, to incorporate a revised map of permit parking area "8D", as shown in Attachment 1 to the report (January 29, 2021) from the Director, Permits and Enforcement, Transportation Services.
