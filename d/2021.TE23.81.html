Toronto City Council · Decision

The community council was asked to approve stop controls at two intersections on Housey Street in CityPlace.

Official title: Traffic Regulations - Housey Street

AdoptedFeb 24, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

CityPlace is a neighbourhood that continues to see growth in its community and with new developments. Part of that growth must include the necessary road safety measures. Over the past number of years, I have worked closely with CityPlace residents to identify street safety concerns and have worked to implement the necessary improvements. Housey Street is an east/west street located between Bathurst Street and Dan Leckie Way in the CityPlace neighbourhood. Currently, there is no westbound traffic control at the intersection of Bauthurst Street and Housey Street and no all-way stop at the intersection of Housey Street and Queens Wharf Road. Additionally, there are no parking regulations on Housey Street. Transportation Services staff have advised on the installation of the necessary traffic control and parking regulations to Housey Street.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized an all-way stop control at the intersection of Queens Wharf Road and Housey Street. 2. Authorized a stop control for westbound traffic on Housey Street at Bathurst Street. 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the south side of Housey Street, between Bathurst Street and Dan Leckie Way. 4. Authorized the installation of "Pay-and-Display" parking machines to operate from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday and 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday for a maximum period of three hours, at the rate of $3.00 per hour, on the north side of Housey Street between Bathurst Street and Dan Leckie Way.
