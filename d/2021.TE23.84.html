Toronto City Council · Decision

The community council was asked to seek a report on excluding the 500 Dupont Street development from Permit Parking Area 5A.

Official title: Realignment of Permit Parking Area "5A" to exclude the development at 500 Dupont Street

AdoptedFeb 24, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The exclusion of the development located at 500 Dupont Street from the 5A permit parking area is a means to ensure that current permit holders have sufficient parking space. The issuance of parking permits to residents of this development would negatively impact on the already limited supply of on-street parking spaces in the area. Councillor Layton is requesting approval from Toronto and East York Community Council to prohibit the General Manager, Transportation Services, from accepting applications from residents of, visitors to, and tradespersons at the subject development property. Residents have reported that it is difficult to find a parking space close to home. The request is to ensure the balance of parking within area 5A from the potential influx from another development in the neighbourhood.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services, to review and report back to Toronto and East York Community Council on the realignment of Permit Parking Area "5A" to exclude the development located at 500 Dupont Street.
