The official record

What the item proposed

Over the last several months, our office has received a series of correspondence from constituents and Toronto Police Service Parking Enforcement about ongoing parking concerns along several streets south of Dundas Street East in Regent Park. Most pressing, are complaints from Toronto Fire Services about fire trucks being unable to access the roads as vehicles are parked on both sides. For the last number of years, as part of the ongoing development several streets in Regent Park have been under the jurisdiction of the developer. A number of these streets have now been dedicated to the City of Toronto allowing for a review of traffic and parking regulations. Transportation Services have reviewed the following streets: Nicholas Avenue, Wyatt Avenue, Tubman Avenue, Dreamers Way, and Oak Street. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the traffic and parking regulations on Nicholas Avenue, Wyatt Avenue, Tubman Avenue, Dreamers Way and Oak Street, as described in Attachment 1: Traffic and Parking Regulations-Nicholas Avenue, Wyatt Avenue, Tubman Avenue, Dreamers Way and Oak Street to the Letter (February 22, 2021) from Councillor Wong-Tam. 2. Directed Transportation Services, in consultation with Toronto Community Housing, Regent Park Neighbourhood Association and the Ward Councillor, to engage in a community wide communication campaign in multiple languages to notify Regent Park residents and business owners to the upcoming traffic and parking regulation changes in the immediate area.
