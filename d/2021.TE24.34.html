471-479 Queen Street East - Zoning By-law Amendment Application - Preliminary Report

Adopted with changesApr 21, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The community council considers a preliminary report on a proposed 15-storey building at 471-479 Queen Street East, which staff do not support in its current form.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information and identifies a preliminary set of issues regarding the application located at 471-479 Queen Street East. The application proposes a 15-storey (51.1 metres plus a 3.7-metre mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building with ground floor retail uses and 143 residential dwelling units. The proposal involves the demolition of the fire-damaged building at 479 Queen Street East. Staff are currently reviewing the application. It has been circulated to all appropriate agencies and City divisions for comment. The proposal is not supportable in its current form. Staff will work with the applicant to address the issues outlined in this report. A community consultation meeting is scheduled for March 29, 2021.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council directed: 1. Staff schedule a community consultation meeting for the lands pertaining to 471-479 Queen Street East, which includes the provision of accessibility resources including closed captioning and, upon request, sign language interpretation at the sole cost of the applicant, in consultation with the Ward Councillor. 2. Staff continue to work with the applicant to address issues identified in this report and additional issues that may be identified through the continuing review of the application, agency comments and further community consultation processes. 3.
