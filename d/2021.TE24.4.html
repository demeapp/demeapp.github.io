What the item proposed

This Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment application proposes to permit the lands at 646-648 Dufferin Street and 1-3 Boland Lane to be redeveloped with an 8-storey residential building containing 124 dwelling units. The proposal has been significantly revised since the original submission in response to feedback from staff and the local community. This application is also being reviewed concurrently and considered with the Zoning By-law Amendment application at 1494-1502 Dundas Street West (File No. 19 101840 STE 09OZ), as this application is proposing a shared loading space with the development proposal to the south on Dundas Street West which would be accessed via the public laneway.
