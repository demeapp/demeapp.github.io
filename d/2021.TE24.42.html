What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City policy or by-laws. Peter Adelaide Limited Partnership is constructing a 47-storey residential condominium building that includes two-levels of retail space with four-levels of underground parking at 102-118 Peter Street and 350-354 Adelaide Street West. The site is an "L" shaped lot located on the northwest corner of Adelaide Street West and Peter Street. Transportation Services is requesting authorization to close the sidewalk and a 3.1 metre wide portion of the southbound curb lane/parking lane on the west side of Peter Street for a period of 20 months (i.e. May 1, 2021 to December 31, 2022) to accommodate a construction staging area.
