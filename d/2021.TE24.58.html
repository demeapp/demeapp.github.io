The community council was asked to ban parking at all times near two blocked driveways on Montclair Avenue.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Montclair Avenue

AdoptedApr 21, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that parking be prohibited at all times on the north side of Montclair Avenue, between Bathurst Street and a point 24.1 metres east of Bathurst Street. This is in response to multiple instances where a parked vehicle would partially block the driveway access for residents at 194 and 196 Montclair Avenue. This proposal will improve driveway access for residents at these premises and will not result in the loss of any parking spaces on Montclair Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 2:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., daily, on the north side of Montclair Avenue, between Bathurst Street and Lonsmount Drive. 2. Authorized parking prohibition to be in effect at all times, on the north side of Montclair Avenue, between Bathurst Street and a point 24.1 metres east of Bathurst Street. 3. Authorized parking prohibition in be in effect from 2:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., daily, on the north side of Montclair Avenue, between a point 24.1 metres east of Bathurst Street and Lonsmount Drive.
