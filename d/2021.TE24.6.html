What the item proposed

This application proposes to permit a 9-storey, 188-bed student residence with a food hall on the ground floor at 40-56 Harbord Street. The proposed development is consistent with the Provincial Policy Statement (2020), conforms with the Growth Plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe (2020) and conforms with the Official Plan. This report reviews and recommends approval of the application to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law. The application proposes a compact built form on a site that is appropriate for further intensification, within a built-up area, that would take advantage of existing infrastructure, provide new institutional student housing, and contribute to the vitality of the campus and surrounding area.
