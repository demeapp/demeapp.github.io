The community council was asked to restrict Rosevear Avenue permit parking to the north side of the street year-round.

Official title: Permit Parking on Rosevear Avenue

AdoptedApr 21, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents have reached out to my office to request the on street parking designation on Rosevear Avenue be amended so that parking is only available on the north side of the street. Rosevear Avenue is a local road with a speed limit of 30km/h. Currently on the north side there is no parking, except by permit, 12:01am - 7am daily in January, February, March, April, June, August, October, and December. On the south side there is no parking, except by permit, 11pm - 5am daily in May, July, September, and November. Three hour maximum parking is in effect at all other times. By amending the parking designation so that permit parking is permitted on the north side of the street only, Traffic Operations have found this would result in seven additional parking spaces being made available to residents throughout the year. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times in January, February, March, April, June, August, October and December, on the south side of Rosevear Avenue, between Dawes Road and Avonlea Boulevard, to be effective for the whole year. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times in May, July, September and November, on the north side of Rosevear Avenue, between Dawes Road and Avonlea Boulevard. 3. Rescinded the existing permit parking in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., daily, in May, July, September and November, on the odd side of Rosevear Avenue, between Dawes Road and Avonlea Boulevard. 4.
