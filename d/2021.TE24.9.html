What the item proposed

This application proposes to amend the Zoning By-law to permit a 52 storey mixed use development including at grade commercial uses and 615 dwelling units above with a total gross floor area of 38,365 square metres at 244-260 Church The proposed building would have a height of 166.1 metres including the mechanical penthouse. The proposal includes commercial units at grade and the retention, in-situ, of the existing 3-storey Nishnawbe Homes Inc. aboriginal supportive housing facility (244 Church Street). A portion of the proposed tower would cantilever over the northern side of the 244 Church property while the remaining portions of the Nishnawbe building will be restricted to an 11 metre height limit in order to secure adequate tower separation to the south.
