The official record

What the item proposed

In 2017 Transportation Services began a project to stabilize the slope behind the north side of Ivy Avenue, approximately around 42 Ivy Avenue. This is a long standing issue for these residents and I hope to be able to provide them a more fulsome update on this project.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Transportation Services, Project Design and Management, to report to the September 9, 2021 meeting of Toronto and East York Community Council with an update on the project to stabilize the slope behind Ivy Avenue.
