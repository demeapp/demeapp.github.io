The official record

What the item proposed

The condominium at 55 Lombard Street, also known as The Bentley, was originally built in 1925 as the St. James Parking Garage, one of Toronto’s first purpose-built parking garages before it was converted into private residences. Due to its unique history and land use conversation, private garbage pick-up occurs on Lombard Street in front of The Bentley rather than internally serviced like all new condominium residences. Across the street at 89-99 Church Street at the intersection of Lombard, a 45-storey mixed-use building being developed by Minto, has been under excavation for months without a final construction staging area permit. However, due to existing traffic concerns related to the construction site, Transportation Services has provided a temporary staging area for 89-99 Church Street while they work with Minto to bring a report forward to a future Toronto and East York Community…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the Manager, Construction Coordination and Traffic Mitigation, Transportation Services, to consult with the developer of 89-99 Church Street and the board of directors and property manager at 55 Lombard Street, and to report back to the May 19, 2021 Toronto and East York Community Council meeting on temporary parking changes needed to ensure safe pedestrian passage and to accommodate private garage pick-up from 55 Lombard Street while the site at 89-99 Church Street await their construction staging area permit from the City.
