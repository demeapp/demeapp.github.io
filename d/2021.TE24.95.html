What the item proposed

Exhibition Place is a unique and important public resource for Toronto as a large publicly-owned property close to the downtown core, Lake Ontario, and major transportation links. It is of immense value to Torontonians today, and we must also be wise stewards of Exhibition Place for the continued benefit of future generations. In support of making the most of the present and future of Exhibition Place, on May 19, 2019, City Council adopted the recommendations of the Exhibition Place Cultural Heritage Landscape Assessment (CHLA) and directed staff to utilize these recommendations in the development of a Master Plan for Exhibition Place. On September 30, 2020, Council approved the Exhibition Place Master Plan - Phase 1 Proposals Report. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning Division, to work with the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place, and the Ward Councillor to undertake public consultation for the proposed Phase 2 Hotel X development at Exhibition Place and report back at an early stage of the Site Plan review process.
