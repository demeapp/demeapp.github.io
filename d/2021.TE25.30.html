Toronto City Council · Decision

Construction Staging Area - 409 Front Street East (Cooperage Street)

Adopted with changesMay 19, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The Acting Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, recommends that Toronto and East York Community Council authorize the closure of the west sidewalk on Cooperage Street, between Front Street East and Mill Road, from May 20, 2021 to September 30, 2024.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision. EllisDon Corporation is constructing two towers with a total of 400 rental and condominium units connected by an eight-story podium with one level on underground parking and a row of townhomes along Cooperage Street at 409 Front Street East. In addition, a five-storey training and education centre will also be constructed on the subject site. The site encompasses the entire block bounded by Front Street East to the north, Cooperage Street to the east, Mill Street to the south and Cherry Street to the west. Transportation Services is requesting authorization to close the west sidewalk on Cooperage Street, between Front Street East and Mill Street, for a period of 41 months (i.e., May 20, 2021 to September 30, 2024) to accommodate a construction staging area.
