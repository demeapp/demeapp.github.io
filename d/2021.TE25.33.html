Accessible Parking Space - Shaw Street (Non-Delegated)

AdoptedJun 8, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The Acting Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services recommends that City Council designate an on-street accessible parking space on the east side of Shaw Street, between a point 51 metres south of Queen Street West and a point 5.5 metres further south.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Shaw Street, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting approval for the installation of an on-street accessible parking space on the east side of Shaw Street.

What was decided

City Council on June 8 and 9, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council designate an on-street accessible parking space on the east side of Shaw Street, between a point 51 metres south of Queen Street West and a point 5.5 metres further south.
