It is recommended that Toronto and East York Community Council re-open item TE24.69: Traffic Calming Poll Results – Cottingham Street.

Official title: Re-Opening Item TE24.69: Traffic Calming Poll Results - Cottingham Street

AdoptedMay 19, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City staff have advised me that there has been a clerical error made on item TE24.69 at last month's Community Council meeting. This led to the adoption of an incomplete alternate recommendation. Staff have asked me to re-open this item so that necessary amendments can be made and the by-law corrected. Transportation Services and City Clerk's staff have been consulted and have provided the corrected recommendation wording.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened item TE24.69: Traffic Calming Poll Results – Cottingham Street. 2. Deleted Recommendation 1 in TE24.69 and replaced it with the following new recommendations: a. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Cottingham Street, between Avenue Road and Gange Avenue, despite poll results that did not satisfy the polling criteria for the installation of speed humps pursuant to the City's Traffic Calming Policy. b. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway to install four speed humps on Cottingham Street, between Avenue Road and Gange Avenue, generally as shown on Attachment 1, Drawing No 421G - 3771, dated August 2020, attached to the report entitled "Traffic Calming Poll Results - Cottingham Street " from the Acting Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services."
