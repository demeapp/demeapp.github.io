Toronto City Council · Decision

Amend Parking Regulations on Church Street, between Wood Street and Alexander Street

AdoptedMay 19, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Wong-Tam recommends that Toronto and East York Community Council rescind the parking prohibition in effect from 7:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Church Street, between Wood Street and Alexander Street.

The official summary names a figure of $1,051,744.54.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Over the past four years, my office has been in deep conversation with City staff, Toronto District School Board, Church Wellesley Village BIA, Church Wellesley Neighbourhood Association, local business and community members about opportunities for public realm improvements in the area, especially on Church Street. Central to our discussions was the community ambitions to produce a significant placemaking opportunity by replacing the failing and outdated fence of the school. To that effect and through City Council approval, my office provided $1,051,744.54 to the Church Street Public School through s.37 and s.45(9) funding for the the creation, design and installation of a customized decorative fence and public art installation around the perimeter of the school. The central piece of this new public work will be prominently featured on the west fence at the Church Street Public School.…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the parking prohibition in effect from 7:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Church Street, between Wood Street and Alexander Street. 2. Prohibited standing at all times on the east side of Church Street, between Wood Street and Alexander Street.
