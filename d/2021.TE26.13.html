The official record

What the item proposed

A Part-Lot Control Exemption application has been submitted to create three conveyable parcels on the lands municipally known as 48-110 Eastdale Avenue. The application is to facilitate the separate legal ownership of three parcels created through the development of new 35-storey and 7-storey residential apartment buildings and a 612 square metre privately-owned publicly accessible space, while maintaining an existing 24-storey apartment building. The three parcels will share a driveway, amenities, certain servicing areas, ramps, and an underground parking garage. The proposed development is consistent with the Provincial Policy Statement (2020) ("PPS"), conforms with the Growth Plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe (2020) ("Growth Plan"), and conforms to the City of Toronto Official Plan ("the Official Plan").
