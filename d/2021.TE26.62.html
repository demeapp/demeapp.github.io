The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Front Street East, Lower Sherbourne Street, and The Esplanade, City Council approval of this report is required. The purpose of this report is to respond to Toronto and East York Community Council's direction to review and report back on the realignment of Permit Parking Area 6K to exclude the development located at 177, 183 and 197 Front Street East, and 15-21 Lower Sherbourne Street, and 200 The Esplanade. The exclusion of the development located at 177, 183 and 197 Front Street East, and 15-21 Lower Sherbourne Street, and 200 The Esplanade is a means to ensure that current permit holders have sufficient parking space. City Council on July 14, 15 and 16, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend Schedule B of the City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 925, Permit Parking, to incorporate a revised map of Permit Parking Area "6K", as shown in Attachment 1 of the report (June 4, 2021) from the Director, Permits and Enforcement, Transportation.
