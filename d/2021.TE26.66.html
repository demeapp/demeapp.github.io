Toronto and East York Community Council corrects an error in the 1998-2000 Bathurst Street construction staging item.

Official title: Re-Opening Item TE25.26: Construction Staging Area - 1998-2000 Bathurst Street - Amendments to traffic operations

AdoptedJun 24, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

It was brought to my attention during a Community Construction Liaison Committee meeting for the Peveril Hill North area that an error in the recommendation to item TE25.26 had occurred at last month's Community Council meeting. Subsequent to the meeting, my office reviewed the error with Transportation Services staff and they advised of the need to re-open the previous item and adjust the recommendations. Staff have been consulted and have provided the corrected wording.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened item TE25.26: Construction Staging Area – 1998-2000 Bathurst Street. 2. Amended item TE25.26 by replacing the roadway "Peverill Hill North" in Part 2 with the roadway "the lane first west of Bathurst Street", so that limits of the two-way operations on Dewbourne Avenue now reads as follows: "Approve implementation of two-way traffic operations on Dewbourne Avenue, between Bathurst Street and the lane first west of Bathurst Street."
