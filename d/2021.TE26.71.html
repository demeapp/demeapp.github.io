Toronto City Council · Decision

Toronto and East York Community Council decides on reinstating a school drop-off zone on Clendenan Avenue.

Official title: The Clover School - Designated Drop-off Zone

AdoptedJun 24, 2021 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Clover School at 411 Clendenan Avenue is asking for the pick-up / drop-off zone to be re-instated on Clendenan Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 15 minutes, from 7:30 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and from 3:30 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Clendenan Avenue, between a point 34.5 metres north of Annette Street and a point 18 metres further north. 2. Prohibited parking from 7:00 a.m. to 7:30 a.m. and from 3:00 p.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Clendenan Avenue, between a point 34.5 metres north of Annette Street and a point 18 metres further north.
