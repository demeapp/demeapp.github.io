Parking Amendments - Bay Street

AdoptedOct 1, 2021 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to rescind the existing taxi stands for two taxis operating at all times except from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Bay Street, at a point 30.5 metres north of Wellesley Street West.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Bay Street, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is recommending that the existing taxi stands for two taxis on the east side of Bay Street at a point 30.5 metres north of Wellesley Street West be removed. The existing two taxi stands were installed for the Sutton Place Hotel previously located at 955 Bay Street. The hotel is no longer at this location, and the taxi stands are not required.

What was decided

City Council on October 1 and 4, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council rescind the existing taxi stands for two taxis operating at all times except from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Bay Street, at a point 30.5 metres north of Wellesley Street West.
