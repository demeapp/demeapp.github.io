Parking Amendments - Westwood Avenue

Deferred indefinitelySep 9, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to prohibit parking at all times on the north side of Westwood Avenue, between a point 31 metres east of Burley Avenue to a point 27 metres further east.

Council put off deciding this item.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending prohibiting parking on the north side of Westwood Avenue, between a point 31 metres east of Burley Avenue to a point 27 metres further east. This proposal will legalize the existing posted signage. During the course of the investigation, it was noted that there is a discrepancy between posted signs and the existing parking regulations. The recommendations will correct this inconsistency.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council deferred consideration of the item indefinitely.
