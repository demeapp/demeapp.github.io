The official record

What the item proposed

Residents have reached out to my office with concerns about speeding along their street and requested that traffic calming measures (speed humps) be installed on the street as a method of addressing these concerns. My office polled the homes and there was strong support from the residents for making this change. I am requesting that speed humps be installed on Bowmore Road to Bellhaven Road.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorize the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Fairmount Crescent from Bowmore Road to Bellhaven Road; and 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway on Fairmount Crescent from Bowmore Road to Bellhaven Road, for traffic calming purposes, generally as shown on the copy of Drawing No. 421G3915 (August 2021), from the Acting Director, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District.
