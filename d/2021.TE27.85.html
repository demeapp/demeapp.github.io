Parking Amendments - Ordnance Street

AdoptedSep 9, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing accessible parking space in effect at all times on the north side of Ordnance Street, between a point 12.8 metres east of Strachan Avenue and a point 6.2 metres further east.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In consultation with Transportation Services, I am requesting approval to amend the existing parking regulations on Ordnance Street, between Strachan Avenue and the south end of Ordnance Street. The proposed on-street parking regulations will help facilitate the implementation of future bike lanes, include a commercial loading zone that will provide for curb-side loading/unloading of merchandise, and help improve safe traffic operations on this street. With the recent completion of three new developments along Ordnance Street, the existing roadway has been narrowed considerably, and cannot safely accommodate two traffic lanes, parking and bike lanes. For the most part, the proposed cross-section on Ordnance Street will comprise of two traffic lanes (one lane per direction) and bike lanes on both sides of the roadway. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing accessible parking space in effect at all times on the north side of Ordnance Street, between a point 12.8 metres east of Strachan Avenue and a point 6.2 metres further east. 2. Rescinded the existing parking machine regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, and from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday, for a maximum period of three hours, at the rate of $3.00 per hour, on both sides of Ordnance Street, between Strachan Avenue and the south end of Ordnance Street. 3. Amended the existing 30km/h speed limit on Ordnance Street, between Strachan Avenue and a point 179.50 metres east, to be in effect from Strachan Avenue and the south end of Ordnance Street. 4. Prohibited stopping at all times on the north side of Ordnance Street (east/west leg), between Strachan Avenue and Ordnance Street…
