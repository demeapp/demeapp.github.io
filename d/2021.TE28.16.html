The official record

What the item proposed

On October 17, 2019, Councillor Wong-Tam on behalf of the Winchester Park Residents' Association submitted a Naming or Renaming an Existing application to name a City park at 149 Bleecker St., identified as the north portion of Winchester Park to Bleecker Parkette. This naming proposal meets the criteria outlined in the City of Toronto Property Naming Policy. Further this naming request, although not subject to the moratorium under the Dundas Street Petition, has been reviewed by the Recognition Review team as not honorific in nature and therefore can proceed with renaming. The Ward Councillor, local Business Improvement Area (BIA) and area residents believe that naming this portion of parkland would provide a unique identifier that would enhance the parks identity and community safety.
