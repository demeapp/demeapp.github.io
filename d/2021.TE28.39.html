Speed Limit Reduction - Lourdes Lane and Homewood Avenue

AdoptedOct 14, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to reduce the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on Lourdes Lane, between Sherbourne Street and Wellesley Place.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending speed limit reduction to 30 km/h on Lourdes Lane, from Sherbourne Street to Wellesley Place, and on Homewood Avenue, from Lourdes Lane to Wellesley Street East. Reducing the speed limit on these streets to 30 km/h will promote lower operating speeds for motorists and will be consistent with the posted speed limit on adjacent local streets in the surrounding neighbourhood.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on Lourdes Lane, between Sherbourne Street and Wellesley Place. 2. Reduced the speed limit from 40 km/h to 30 km/h on Homewood Avenue, between Lourdes Lane and Wellesley Street East. 3. Amended the existing 40 km/h speed limit on Homewood Avenue, between Carlton Street and the north end of Homewood Avenue, to be in effect between Carlton Street and Wellesley Street East.
