Speed Bump Installation on First East-West Public Lane South of Eversfield Road, West of Dufferin Street and the First North-South Public Lane East of Boon Avenue, North of Rogers Road

AdoptedOct 14, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to authorize the installation of speed bumps in the first east-west public lane south of Eversfield Road, west of Dufferin Street, at the locations shown on Drawing 421G-3956, dated September 2021, from the Acting Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Recently, my office has heard from concerned residents abutting the first east-west public lane south of Eversfield Road, west of Dufferin Street regarding vehicles speeding and racing on the laneway. On a few occasions this has resulted in cars losing control and causing significant damage to properties that back on to this laneway. In 2017, prior to my representing the area, a traffic study was conducted to determine whether speed bumps were warranted on both the east-west and north-south sections of the public lane. The criteria were met in both cases, but no additional action was taken at the time. Because of these reports and having consulted with Traffic Operations, I am requesting that speed bumps be installed as per the recommendations.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of speed bumps in the first east-west public lane south of Eversfield Road, west of Dufferin Street, at the locations shown on Drawing 421G-3956, dated September 2021, from the Acting Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District. 2. Authorized the installation of speed bumps in the first north-south public lane east of Boon Avenue, north of Rogers Road, at the locations shown on Drawing 421G-3959, dated September 2021, from the Acting Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District.
