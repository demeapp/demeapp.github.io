Parking Amendments - Queens Wharf Road

AdoptedOct 14, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west and north side of Queens Wharf Road, between Housey Street and Dan Leckie Way.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Traffic Operations recently received a request from an inquirer regarding the existing parking conditions on the north/west side of Queens Wharf Road, between Fort York Blvd. and Dan Leckie Way. Residents have reported illegal parking activity within the existing No Parking Anytime area along this roadway. A field investigation undertaken by Transportation Services staff confirmed that there are various vehicles parked illegally for long periods of time within this No Parking Anytime area. As Queens Wharf Road carries a road width of only approx. 8.5m having vehicles parked on the north/west side reduces the road width and impacts safe two-way traffic. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west and north side of Queens Wharf Road, between Housey Street and Dan Leckie Way. 2. Prohibited standing at all times on the west and north side of Queens Wharf Road, between Housey Street and Dan Leckie Way.
