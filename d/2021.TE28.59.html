Change Parking Regulations on Foxbar Road to Facilitate the Installation of a Median

AdoptedOct 14, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to prohibit standing at all times on the west side of Foxbar Road, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 83 metres south.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to request your support in changing parking regulations on Foxbar Road, south of St. Clair Ave West. This is to facilitate the installation of a median along the centreline on Foxbar Road to address long-standing traffic infiltration concerns and issues on Foxbar Road. This arrangement would lead to a loss of pay & display parking spots frequently used by visitors to the Bradgate Arms Retirement Residence. In order for the median to work without negative impacts on the retirement home, my office has worked with the developer's team at 129 St. Clair Ave W to open up parking space for commercial use at 129 St Clair Ave W to offset the loss of parking on the street. My office worked on this plan with Transportation Services, the Foxbar Neighbourhood Association (FNA), and Bradgate Arms, who have all confirmed their support. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited standing at all times on the west side of Foxbar Road, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 83 metres south. 2. Prohibited standing at all times on the east side of Foxbar Road, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 67 metres south. 3. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Foxbar Road, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 83 metres south. 4. Rescinded the existing parking machine regulation in effect from 10:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. Sun., for a maximum period of 3 hours and at a rate of $3.00 for 1 hour on the west side of Foxbar Road, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 83 metres south. 5.
