The official record

What the item proposed

Infrastructure Ontario, a Provincial crown agency, is currently advancing five Transit Oriented Community (TOC) proposals for mixed-use development on lands associated with the Ontario Line near planned stations at Exhibition, King-Bathurst, Queen-Spadina, Corktown, and East Harbour. All of these TOC proposals are in rapidly-growing areas where communities, with local councillors and Planning staff, have extensive experience constructively managing change and growth to build complete neighbourhoods. In a number of critical instances there are also recently updated planning frameworks in place, or active City-building projects underway. However, the TOC proposals are not going through the typical process of submitting a re-zoning application to the City for each site, and as a result the standard steps of consultation and review have not automatically been initiated.
