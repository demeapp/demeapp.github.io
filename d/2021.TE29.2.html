The official record

What the item proposed

As this report proposes permanently closing a portion of a public highway, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services recommends that the east-west portion of Guest Avenue be permanently closed and sold to Marlin Springs Limited Partnership and Dandaw Developments Limited (the "Applicant"). Part 1 on the Sketch will remain as public highway, Guest Avenue.

What was decided

City Council on December 15, 16 and 17, 2021, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the permanent closure of the east-west portion of Guest Avenue, designated as Part 2 on Sketch No. PS-2021-0023 and containing a land area of 344.3 square metres (the "Highway"), attached to the report (November 3, 2021) from the Director, Transportation Planning and Capital Program, Transportation Services. 2. City Council enact a By-law substantially in the form of the draft By-law, provided as Attachment 1 to the report (November 3, 2021) from the Director, Transportation Planning and Capital Program, Transportation Services. 3.
