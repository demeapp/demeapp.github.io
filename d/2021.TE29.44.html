Parking Amendments for Wards: Parkdale High Park (4), Davenport (9) and Toronto-Danforth (14)

WithdrawnNov 24, 2021 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to authorize the parking regulations identified in Attachment 1 to the report dated November 3, 2021 from the Director, Permits and Enforcement, Transportation Services.

The item was withdrawn before a final decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As amendments to Chapter 925 and 950 of the Municipal Code are being requested, City Council approval of this report is required. The purpose of this report is to resolve by-law amendments missed in ItemTE11.35 "Residential On-street Permit Parking - Status Update on the Expansion of On-Street Permit Parking in the Toronto and East York Community Council Area" Transportation Services is recommending the approval of the parking regulations listed in Attachment 1 of this report. These proposed by-law amendments will ensure that the intent of Item TE11.35, as adopted with amendments by City Council on January 29, 2020 can be properly installed and enforced?

What was decided

The Item was withdrawn from the agenda.
