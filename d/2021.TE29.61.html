Toronto City Council · Decision

Improvement of Pedestrian Cross Time at Parliament Street and Shuter Street

AdoptedNov 24, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to direct staff to provide more crossing time in the north-south and east-west directions at the Shuter and Parliament street intersection by reducing the default walk speed to 0.9 m/s.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

A petition was submitted to the City of Toronto with over 100 signatures expressing concern for the safety of pedestrians at this intersection. The petition requests the improvement of cross time on the pedestrian countdown signals at Parliament Street and Shuter Street. The current default walk speed at this intersection is 1.0 m/s. Given that this intersection is used by many older pedestrians, there is a need to provide more crossing time in the north-south and east-west directions by reducing the default walk speed to 0.9 m/s.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed staff to provide more crossing time in the north-south and east-west directions at the Shuter and Parliament street intersection by reducing the default walk speed to 0.9 m/s. 2. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services, to reduce the default walk speed as outlined in Recommendation 1 above, as soon as possible.
