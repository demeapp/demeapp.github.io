What the item proposed

My office recently attended an onsite meeting at St. Mary of the Angels Catholic School. In attendance were the Trustee, Superintendent, the Principal of the school, the Catholic School Parent Council (CSPC), and Traffic Operations staff from the City of Toronto. The purpose of the meeting was to discuss ongoing concerns about parking at the school, specifically during pick-up and drop-off times. St. Mary of the Angels is located at the intersection of Dufferin Street and Davenport Road. The front of the school faces Dufferin Street and so to avoid traffic and for the safety of the students, parents drop-off their children on Bristol Avenue which is located at the back of the school where parking is prohibited from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and from 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and from 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Bristol Avenue, between a point 38 metres south of Davenport Road and a point 70 metres further south, to be in effect between a point 83 metres south of Davenport Road and a point 25 metres further south. 2. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 15 minutes from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and from 2:45 p.m. to 3:45 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Bristol Avenue, between a point 38 metres south of Davenport Road and a point 45 metres further south.
