No Stopping on St. Clarens Avenue between St. Clair Avenue West and Joseph Deleo Lane

AdoptedNov 24, 2021 · Toronto City Council

My office recently received a request to have the parking regulations on St. Clarens Avenue between St. Clair Avenue West and Joseph Deleo Lane amended to "No Stopping." The proposal would improve sightlines and safety at the laneway in addition to the intersection as well as provide clear access to the fire hydrant on this stretch of road.

What the item proposed

My office recently received a request to have the parking regulations on St. Clarens Avenue between St. Clair Avenue West and Joseph Deleo Lane amended to "No Stopping." The proposal would improve sightlines and safety at the laneway in addition to the intersection as well as provide clear access to the fire hydrant on this stretch of road. Having consulted with Traffic Operations staff, I am requesting that parking on St. Clarens Avenue between St. Clair Avenue West and Joseph Deleo Lane be amended as follows.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the east side of St. Clarens Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 28 metres south. 2. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect from the 16th day to the last day of each month, from April 1 to November 30, inclusive on the east side of St. Clarens Avenue, between a point 28 metres south of St. Clair Avenue West and Davenport Road (west intersection) to be in effect from Joseph Deleo Lane and Davenport Road (west intersection). 3.
