The official record

What the item proposed

There are a series of properties along Bloor which have been brought to my attention as they are in need of review as shared by the Annex Residents' Association Heritage Committee Chair. These properties are located in the heart of the Annex neighbourhood, adjacent to the East Annex Heritage Conservation District, the Madison Avenue Heritage Conservation District and the forthcoming West Annex Heritage Conservation District. These properties comprise a portion of a submission for a Zoning By-Law Amendment Application that was submitted to the City Planning Division for the north-east corner of Spadina and Bloor, spanning the properties of 320-332 Bloor St W. The Annex is an area with distinct heritage considerations and districts and a rich architectural history. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Heritage Planning - City Planning, to evaluate the Registered Properties at 320 Bloor Street West, 322 Bloor Street West, 324 Bloor Street West and 326 Bloor Street West for designation under Part IV of the Ontario Heritage Act and to report to the Toronto Preservation Board, Toronto East York Community Council and City Council.
