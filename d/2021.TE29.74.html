Additional Speed Hump on Hillsdale Avenue East, between Yonge Street and Mount Pleasant Road

AdoptedNov 24, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The community council was asked to consider installing an additional speed hump on Hillsdale Avenue East, between Yonge Street and Mount Pleasant Road.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to request your support in installing an additional speed hump on Hillsdale Avenue East, between Yonge Street and Mount Pleasant Road. Despite the existing speed humps, speeding is a major problem on a section of the street, especially between nos. 176 and 244 Hillsdale Avenue East due to an inadequate number of speed humps on that section. Traffic Operations staff visited the location, and identified that it is feasible to install an additional hump in the vicinity of nos. 226 and 227 Hillsdale Avenue East to address the speeding and safety concerns.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement and authorize the installation of one additional traffic calming speed hump on Hillsdale Avenue East, between Yonge Street and Mount Pleasant Road. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway on Hillsdale Avenue East, for traffic calming purposes, generally as shown on the copy of Drawing No. 421G-3963, dated October 2021, from the Acting Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, Toronto.
