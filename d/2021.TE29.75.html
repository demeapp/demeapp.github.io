Toronto City Council · Decision

Parking Amendments on Dewbourne Avenue

AdoptedNov 24, 2021 · Toronto City Council

The community council was asked to consider prohibiting parking on Dewbourne Avenue, between Glen Cedar Road and Strathearn Avenue, between 8:00am and 10:00am.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to request your support in prohibiting parking on Dewbourne Avenue, between Glen Cedar Road and Strathearn Avenue, between 8:00am and 10:00am. Residents of the street petitioned my office to undertake these changes to help improve traffic flow, and ease congestion in view of the astronomical amount of cut through traffic plying Cedarvale Street, a result of the ongoing LRT construction.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Dewbourne Avenue, between Strathearn Road and a point 37 metres west of Bathurst Street, to be in effect between Strathearn Road and Glen Cedar Road, and between Chiltern Hill Road and a point 37 metres west of Bathurst Street. 2. Prohibited parking from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Dewbourne Avenue, between Glen Cedar Road and Chiltern Hill Road. 3. Prohibited parking from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Dewbourne Avenue, between Glen Cedar Road and Chiltern Hill Road.
