Requesting Pick-Up and Drop-Off Parking Amendments at the St. Michael’s Choir School, 67 Bond Street

AdoptedNov 24, 2021 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing maximum 10-minute parking regulation in effect from 7:30 a.m. to 8:30 a.m., 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m., 3:30 p.m.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Parents, administrators and the local Toronto Catholic District School Board Trustee of St. Michael’s Choir School, located at 67 Bond Street expressed concerns about the Pick-Up and Drop-Off (PUDO) conditions for the students at the school. The Bond Place Hotel is located at 65 Bond Street and is currently leased by the City as part of the temporary hotel shelter program in efforts to ensure physical distancing among clients that were difficult to achieve in traditional City shelters during the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic. The Bond Place Hotel is across the street from St. Michael’s Choir School and parents have expressed safety concerns for their students citing at-risk behaviour displayed by some clients of the temporary hotel shelter program.
