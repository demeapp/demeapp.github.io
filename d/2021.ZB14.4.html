What the item proposed

This report discusses the recommendation on Phase II of the Network Re-build project. The Network Re-build project will establish a new network at the Toronto Zoo providing internet access to staff for operations and Wi-Fi services to guests across 80% of Zoo site as shown in Appendix A. This network upgrade will significantly improve operating efficiency and guest experiences. Internet access is especially important during the current COVID-19 pandemic situation when staff rely heavily on communications through digital platforms and when the Zoo engages guests through virtual experiences. The existing network, built in 1999 with a speed of 80 megabits per second, is insufficient to support efficient operations for all staff and guests. What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Authorized the Chief Executive Officer of the Toronto Zoo to purchase the equipment, professional services and supporting licenses for the Phase II of the Network Re-build project within a budget of $1.95 million dollars (including a contingency of $0.1 million dollars) from OnX, which is the vendor of record at the City of Toronto. 2. Authorized the Chief Executive Officer of the Toronto Zoo to apply up to $0.85 million dollars of designated funds from the Toronto Zoo Wildlife Conservancy to this project.
