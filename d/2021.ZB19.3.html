What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Adopted the 2022 calendar of meeting dates for the Toronto Zoo Board and its Committees, as set out in the report (November 25, 2021) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Zoo, and as amended by motion 1 by Councillor McKelvie. The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo 2022 meeting dates are as follows: Monday, February 14 - 2:00 p.m. Thursday, April 14 - 3:00 p.m. Thursday, June 9 - 2:00 p.m. Thursday, September 15 - 2:00 p.m. Thursday, October 13 - 2:00 p.m. Thursday, December 8 - 2:00 p.m. 2. Directed sub-committee meetings to be scheduled at the call of the Chief Executive Officer of the Toronto Zoo. 3. Changed the name of the Animal Acquisition Committee to further be known as the Animal Committee. 4.
