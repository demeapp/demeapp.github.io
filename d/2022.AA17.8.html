The official record

What the item proposed

The Indigenous Tkaranto Collective Consultants and the Museum Administrator, Fort York National Historic Site, will give a presentation on the work plan for the development of a three-year strategy for Indigenous Arts Festival and Indigenous Peoples Month at Toronto History Museums. The strategy will advise on governance, scale, partnerships, and programming approaches.

What was decided

The Aboriginal Affairs Advisory Committee deferred consideration of the item until the June 13, 2022 meeting of the Aboriginal Affairs Advisory Committee.
