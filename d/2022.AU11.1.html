What the item proposed

This report provides information on the Auditor General's Office 2021 activities and financial and non-financial benefits achieved by the City through its implementation of report recommendations. In 2021, the Auditor General's Office presented 14 audit, review and investigation reports, three follow-up reports and seven administrative reports. Additionally, our Fraud and Waste Hotline was as busy as ever managing hundreds of complaints and conducting several complex investigations. We also continued important reviews of the Toronto Police Service, the Toronto Transit Commission's IT infrastructure, the Toronto Building Division, and of Toronto's shelter system. Every year, we demonstrate the value our Office brings with a quantified return on investment.
