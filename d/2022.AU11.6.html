This is an information item: Council receives a confidential update on the City's cybersecurity governance framework.

Official title: Status of the Governance Framework for Effective Cyber Security Management

AdoptedApr 6, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an update on the approach the Office of the CISO (OC) has taken in developing a governance structure to effectively identify, plan for and mitigate cybersecurity risks across the City of Toronto, including all City divisions, agencies and corporations, and the governance framework to ensure that City divisions, agencies and corporations are effectively managing their cybersecurity risks and responding as new risks arise.

What was decided

City Council on April 6 and 7, 2022 adopted the following: 1. City Council direct that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (February 4, 2022) from the Chief Information Security Officer remain confidential in its entirety, as it pertains to the security of the property of the City of Toronto. Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (February 4, 2022) from the Chief Information Security Officer remains confidential in its entirety in accordance with the provisions of the City of Toronto Act, 2006, as it pertains to the security of the property of the City of Toronto.
