What the item proposed

Kevin Travers, Lead Audit Engagement Partner, KPMG LLP and Maria Khoushnood, Project Management Partner, KPMG LLP will give a presentation on The City of Toronto Audit Findings Report for the Year Ended December 31, 2021.

What was decided

City Council on July 19, 20, 21 and 22, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Auditor General to direct the City's external auditor to promptly report to the Audit Committee any identified control deficiencies determined to be of significant importance to merit the attention of management or those charged with governance. 2. City Council direct the City Manager to implement the 55 recommendations in the Management letter (December 15, 2021) from KPMG LLP (for the year 2020), in alignment with implementation timelines. 3.
