The official record

What the item proposed

The Budget Committee will hear public presentations on the 2022 Capital and Operating Budgets on January 25, 2022 from 6:00 p.m. onwards. Meetings of the Budget Committee to hear Public Presentations on the 2022 Capital and Operating Budgets have been scheduled as follows: Monday, January 24, 2022: 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.; 1:30 p.m. to 4:30 p.m.; and 6:00 p.m. onwards. Tuesday, January 25, 2022: 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.; 1:30 p.m. to 4:30 p.m.; and 6:00 p.m. onwards. Register to speak to the Budget Committee by emailing buc@toronto.ca or calling 416-392-4666. Individuals may only make one presentation on either January 24 or January 25. Please register to speak by 4:30 p.m. on Friday, January 21. Registered public speakers will be provided with instructions on how to appear at the Committee meeting. The meeting will be streamed live online at toronto.ca/council.

What was decided

The Budget Committee: 1. Received the public presentations on the 2022 Capital and Operating Budgets for information.
