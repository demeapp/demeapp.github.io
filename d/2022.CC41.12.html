The official record

What the item proposed

The City Solicitor has received a with-prejudice settlement offer regarding lands at 169 The Donway West (the "Settlement Offer" attached as Public Appendix "A"). This report will outline the Settlement Offer, its associated challenges, the relationship between 169 The Donway West and 966 Don Mills, and offer confidential advice from the City Solicitor related to the Settlement Offer.

What was decided

City Council on March 9, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 2, 2022) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 2, 2022) from the City Solicitor. 3. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 2, 2022) from the City Solicitor remain confidential, as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege. The confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 2, 2022) from the City Solicitor, were adopted by City Council and are now public as follows: 1.
