What the item proposed

2457938 Ontario Limited (the "Applicant") is the owner of the property municipally known as 3100-3200 Bloor Street West and 4-8 Montgomery Road (the "Subject Site"). The Subject Site currently contains a car dealership, car rental establishment, and a paved parking lot along Bloor Street, as well as three two-storey detached residential dwellings fronting onto Montgomery Road. To the north of the Subject Site are the Toronto Transit Commission ("TTC") subway tracks for the Bloor-Danforth Line 2. A portion of the Subject Site is found north of the TTC subway tracks. To the west of the Subject Site is the Mimico Creek.
