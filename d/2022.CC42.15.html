The City Solicitor asks Council for confidential directions on the Ontario Land Tribunal case for a seven-storey building at 2810-2814 Bayview Avenue.

The official record

What the item proposed

Dormer Bay Inc. (the "Applicant") is the owner of the property municipally known as 2810, 2812 and 2814 Bayview Avenue (the "Subject Site"). The Applicant applied for an Official Plan and zoning by-law amendment application (the "Applications") in order to facilitate a seven storey residential building on the Subject Site. The Applicant has appealed the application to the Ontario Land Tribunal (the "OLT") due to City Council's failure to make a decision within the timeframes prescribed in the Planning Act. On December 15, 2021 City Council adopted recommendations directing the City Solicitor to attend the OLT to oppose the proposal's vehicular access and parking spaces. The OLT has scheduled a Case Management Conference for May 3, 2022. This report has been prepared in consultation with City Planning and Transportation Services staff.
