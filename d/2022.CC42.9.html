The City Solicitor asks Council for confidential directions on the Ontario Land Tribunal case for 234-250 King Street East and 162 Princess Street.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek further instruction in relation to a development application that has been appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal (the "OLT") as Tribunal case number OLT-21-001198. A 9-day hearing relating to the development proposal is scheduled to commence on December 5, 2022.

What was decided

City Council on April 6 and 7, 2022 adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 29, 2022) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council authorize the public release of confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Appendices A and B to the report (March 29, 2022) from the City Solicitor. 3. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 29, 2022) from the City Solicitor remain confidential at the discretion of the City Solicitor as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
