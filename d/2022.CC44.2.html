Council appoints a new councillor for Ward 13, Toronto Centre.

Official title: Appointment to fill the vacancy in the Office of Councillor, Ward 13, Toronto Centre

Adopted with changesJun 1, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to appoint a qualified person to the office of Councillor, Ward 13, Toronto Centre.

What was decided

City Council on June 1, 2022, appointed Robin Buxton Potts to the Office of Councillor, Ward 13, Toronto Centre, for the remainder of this term of Council. City Council also appointed Robin Buxton Potts to the following, effective June 1, 2022: a. the Planning and Housing Committee for a term of office until November 14, 2022 and until a successor is appointed; b. the Board of Health for a term of office until November 14, 2022 and until a successor is appointed; c. the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee, as Chair, for a term of office until November 14, 2022 and until a successor is appointed; and d. the Two-Spirit, Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender and Queer Advisory Committee, as Chair, for a term of office ending November 14, 2022.
