The official record

What the item proposed

The applicant appealed its zoning by-law amendment application and site plan control application to the Ontario Land Tribunal (the "Tribunal") on April 7, 2022. The Tribunal has scheduled the first case management conference in this matter for August 4, 2022. The Tribunal's rules expect parties to the appeal to be prepared with issues in respect of the appeal by the first case management conference. The applicant has gone on the public record indicating their willingness to mediate the appeals. Further direction from City Council is required on this matter.
