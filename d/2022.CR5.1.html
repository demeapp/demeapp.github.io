The official record

What the item proposed

At the invitation of the Confronting Anti-Black Racism Unit, Social Development, Finance and Administration, the Chair, Black Scientists' Task Force and Special Advisor for Vaccine Equity Strategy in Community Resources Section, Social Development, Finance and Administration, will give a presentation on the impacts of COVID-19 on Black communities, and outline the ways the pandemic has negatively affected the mental health and employment outcomes for Black residents.

What was decided

The Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee received the item for information.
