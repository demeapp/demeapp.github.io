Ticketing Services Agreement

AdoptedSep 29, 2022 · Toronto City Council

The Board of Directors of TO Live was asked to approve the extension of TO Live's current agreement for ticketing with the vendor and on the terms set out.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide the Board with an update on TO Live's ticketing services agreement.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of TO Live: 1. Approved the extension of TO Live's current agreement for ticketing with the vendor and on the terms set out in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (September 8, 2022) from the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live. 2. Authorized the public release of the name of the vendor and terms after the execution of the agreement and direct that the remainder of the information in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (September 8, 2022) from the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live remain confidential as it is about a position or plan to be applied to any negotiations carried on or to be carried on by or on behalf of TO Live.
