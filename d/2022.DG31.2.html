The official record

What the item proposed

Appeal of Dangerous Dog Order, issued June 18, 2022, Appeal DDRT2022-12.

What was decided

The Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal: 1. Deferred consideration of Appeal DDRT2022-12 to its meeting on November 10, 2022, or failing that, to a date to be set by the Tribunal, in consultation with the parties. 2. Such deferral to be granted on a peremptory basis, meaning that no further adjournment requests will be granted to the Appellant except in the most exceptional circumstances or with the consent of all parties. 3. Directed further that the Order and its conditions remain in effect until and unless the Order is rescinded by the Tribunal.
