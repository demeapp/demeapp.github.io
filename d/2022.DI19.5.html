The official record

What the item proposed

Annette Synowiec, Director, Policy, Planning and Outreach, and Michael Skaljin, Manager, Solid Waste Policy and Planning; Solid Waste Management Services will give a presentation on the City's proposed Single-Use and Takeaway Items Reduction Strategy. The presentation will cover the specific considerations of the needs of people with disabilities that have been taken into account, as well as information about the public online survey that has been launched to collect feedback from the public.

What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee received the item for information.
