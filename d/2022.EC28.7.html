What the item proposed

The Imagination, Manufacturing, Innovation and Technology Financial Incentive Program (the "Imagination, Manufacturing, Innovation and Technology Program") provides incentives in the form of property tax increment grants to support the renovation or construction of buildings in targeted employment sectors and uses throughout Toronto. This report provides an overview of the economic and financial impacts of the Imagination, Manufacturing, Innovation and Technology Program and proposed Terms of Reference for the upcoming review of the Imagination, Manufacturing, Innovation and Technology Program.
