What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority from the Economic and Community Development Committee to amend Blanket Contract Number 47023417 issued to R Courier for the provision of courier services for the distribution and transportation of vaccine and non-medical supplies from the central distribution and storage warehouse to mass immunization clinics across the City of Toronto. This report requests an amendment to increase the contract value by $700,000 net of Harmonized Sales Tax ($712,320) net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries), increasing the contract value from $500,000 to $1,200,000 net of Harmonized Sales Tax ($1,221,120) net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) and also to extend the Contract from June 30, 2022 to December 31, 2022.
