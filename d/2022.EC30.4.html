What the item proposed

Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 591, Noise (the Noise Bylaw) provides standards for noise, and applies to all properties in Toronto. Chapter 591 regulates episodic types of noise, which generally relate to the day-to-day activities of residents and businesses, including temporary events and construction. It must balance the desires of all residents to enjoy their homes and environments, and in a City as large and vibrant as Toronto, certain levels of noise are reasonable and reflect life in a densely populated city. This report responds to outstanding directives from Toronto City Council since amendments to the Noise Bylaw were adopted in 2019. These directives relate to the feasibility of restricting noise from two-stroke engine leaf blowers and similar equipment and strategies to reduce excessive vehicle noise in Toronto, including automated noise radar.
