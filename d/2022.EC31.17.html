The official record

What the item proposed

Chief People and Strategy Officer, Exhibition Place, will provide a presentation on the Exhibition Place 2022-2026 Strategic Plan. The 2022-2026 Strategic Plan outlines the future direction, focal points, and goals for Exhibition Place in the next four and a half years. With a renewed vision that builds on the legacy of the past, Exhibition Place aspires to become Canada’s premier destination for conventions, exhibitions, events, and entertainment. Over 5.5 million guests visit the 192-acre site located near the shore of Lake Ontario annually, generating significant economic benefits for Toronto. The Strategic Plan ensures that Exhibition Place continues to be thriving hub for Canada’s largest conventions and exhibitions, entertainment and sporting events, and cultural attractions.

What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee deferred the consideration of this item to the first meeting of the Economic and Community Development Committee in 2023.
