The official record

What the item proposed

At the October 29 and 30, 2019 City Council meeting, the Deputy City Manager, Community and Social Services and the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation were directed to: 1) to allocate funding from TO Wards Peace to support youth in the Jane Falstaff community who have lived experience with gun violence; 2) consult with youth from Jane Falstaff community and to report back on a local plan for the provision of recreation program and services. The Jane Falstaff community is located at Jane Street and Falstaff Avenue within the boundaries of the Rustic Neighbourhood Improvement Area. The Jane Falstaff community is comprised of three Toronto Community Housing high-rise apartment buildings. This community has experienced a high level of violent incidents in recent years.
