What the item proposed

This staff report provides an overview of the implementation of a strategic vision at Toronto History Museums to advance reconciliation, equity and inclusion and counter anti-Black racism as outlined in the Toronto History Museums Strategic Plan: Laying a New Foundation contained in Attachment 1. The vision's goals include working towards decolonization, creating inclusive community spaces, sharing stories from multiple perspectives, providing equitable access, building sustainability, and championing the creation of a Museum of Toronto. What was decided

City Council on July 19, 20, 21 and 22, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve the Toronto History Museums Strategic Plan: Laying a New Foundation contained in Attachment 1 to the report (June 20, 2022) from the Interim General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, as a framework for continuing to advance reconciliation, equity and inclusion at the City's Toronto History Museums. 2.
