Toronto Newcomer Strategy - Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee

AdoptedJul 19, 2022 · Toronto City Council

The Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee asks Council to give Toronto's francophone community an active role in the 2022-26 Newcomer Strategy.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on June 3, 2022, the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee considered Item FA8.3 and made a recommendation to City Council.

What was decided

City Council on July 19, 20, 21 and 22, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the Manager, Toronto Newcomer Office to ensure that Toronto's Francophone population plays an active role in the implementation of the Toronto Newcomer Strategy 2022-26 and to report back in 2023 to the Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee or the Economic and Community Development Committee, as appropriate, on the involvement of Francophone communities.
