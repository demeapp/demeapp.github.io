The official record

What the item proposed

I would like the Board to consider a request to the Province of Ontario regarding the Ontario Line station located at Exhbition Place.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Requested the Province of Ontario to consider changing the name of the train station at Exhibition Place for the Ontario Line, from "Exhibition" to "Exhibition Place". 2. Directed the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place to convey the Board's request.
