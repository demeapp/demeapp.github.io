What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of travel expenses for Exhibition Place Chief Executive Officer, Don Boyle to attend the 89th UFI Global Congress. UFI is the international organization representing trade and exhibition centers. The Enercare Centre has been a member of this association since 2000 and has been one of the few North American venues accepted into this association. This year's Congress is the first UFI Global Congress in ten years to take place in the Middle-East. The Sultanate of Oman is a diverse nation, ideally situated on the Arabian Peninsula, and rich in both history and culture. As the sector continues its speedy recovery from the impact of the global pandemic, leaders from the regional and international exhibition sector will gather in Muscat. What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Authorized meeting, convention and travel expenses for Don Boyle to attend the 89th UFI Global Congress in Muscat, Sultanate of Oman from November 14 - 17, 2022 at a cost not to exceed $7,000 for registration fees, flights, accommodations and incidental expenses.
