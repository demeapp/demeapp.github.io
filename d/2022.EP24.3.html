The official record

What the item proposed

An Exhibition Place employee is recognized by the Board for a recent outstanding accomplishment. The International Association of Venue Managers Foundation (IAVM) has announced the 30 Under 30 Class of 2022. Anthony Monteforte, Event & Meeting Coordinator has officially been named to the 2022 Class on May 13, 2022, and is only one of three Canadians to be named in this year’s class. The International Association of Venue Managers is a global association of public assembly venues and has 7000+ active members around the world. This program in its seventh year is designed to engage the best and brightest young professionals in the venue management industry to accelerate and carry the industry into the future. The Class of 2022 will convene at VenueConnect 2022 in Phoenix, Arizona, July 18 - 21 to be recognized on stage at the association’s annual conference. "The 2022 Class of 30 UNDER 30…

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Applauded and extended congratulations to Mr. Anthony Monteforte for his outstanding achievement to the 30 Under 30 Class of 2022.
