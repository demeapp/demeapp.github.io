The official record

What the item proposed

The Collective Agreement between the Board of Governors of Exhibition Place and The International Union of Painters and Allied Trades, District Council 46, expired on December 31, 2018. This report outlines the terms of a Memorandum of Agreement attached as Confidential Attachment 1 - Appendix A that was ratified by The International Union of Painters and Allied Trades, District Council 46 on April 4, 2022, and pending Board approval would be the basis of a Collective Agreement with the Board, with an expiry date of December 31, 2022. The bargaining process was positive with the key principles of transparency, focus on sustainability of Exhibition Place businesses, quality assurance, and the highest level of customer service being the priority. The union-management relationship was cordial and cooperative due to the transparent nature of the process.
