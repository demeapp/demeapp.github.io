The official record

What the item proposed

Due to the postponement of the scheduled November 2, 2022 Board meeting to December 9, 2022, and in order to meet critical timelines for the assignment closing between the parties, legal documentation and operational needs of the parties, staff seek the consent of the Board. This report recommends that the Board approve of the assignment of the named License Agreements in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Attachment 1 - Appendix A of this report with the Consumer show clients because of an asset purchase partnership agreement between the parties.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the assignment of the five (5) license agreements with the Consumer Show Client for Beanfield Centre and Enercare Centre, as set out in the Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Attachment 1 - Appendix A to the report (September 27, 2022) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place, commencing in each of 2023, 2024, and 2025 on the terms and conditions of the existing License Agreement and such other terms and conditions satisfactory to the Chief Executive Officer and the City Solicitor. 2.
