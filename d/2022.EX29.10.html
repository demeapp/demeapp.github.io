What the item proposed

The City Clerk has continually worked to improve the municipal election experience by providing reliable digital services that assist City staff in effectively administering the election while also simplifying election participation for voters and candidates. To reach this objective, the City Clerk uses the DataFix platform, an externally hosted solution that provides both public facing web applications and internal back-end election administration components to conduct municipal elections in the City. The purpose of this report is to seek authority to amend Purchase Order Number 6044628 issued to DataFix, Division of Comprint Systems Incorporated by $563,035, net of all applicable taxes and charges ($572,944, net of Harmonized Sales Tax (HST) recoveries), revising the current purchase order value from $2,036,200, net of all applicable taxes and charges ($2,072,037, net of HST…

What was decided

City Council on February 2 and 3, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the City Clerk to amend Purchase Order Number 6044628 with DataFix, Division of Comprint Systems Incorporated, and increase the value of the contract by $563,035, net of all taxes and applicable charges ($572,944, net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries), increasing the total contract value from $2,036,200, net of all applicable taxes and charges ($2,072,037, net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) to $2,599,235, net of all taxes and applicable charges ($2,644,981, net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries), and to extend the validity date until June 1, 2023, under the same pricing, terms and conditions of the existing agreement and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor.
