The official record

What the item proposed

The City of Toronto proactively leads with a sense of purpose and makes positive impacts across key Environment, Social, and Governance priorities through commitment, innovation, and transparency. The City has demonstrated its commitment and accountability through proper performance disclosure and consideration of Environmental, Social, and Governance risks and opportunities. It was the first government in Canada to issue an annual Environmental, Social, and Governance performance report in early 2021. As part of the commitment contained in the inaugural issue, Attachment 1 provides the second annual Environmental, Social, and Governance performance report. The objective of this report is to communicate the City's environmental, social, and governance commitments; define targeted outcomes; and report on the City's progress towards sustainability and positive socioeconomic outcomes.…

What was decided

The Executive Committee: 1. Received the report (January 12, 2022) from the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer for information.
