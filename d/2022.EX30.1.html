Toronto City Council · Decision

2022 Property Tax Rates and Related Matters

Adopted with changesFeb 17, 2022 · Toronto City Council

The Executive Committee considers the 2022 property tax rates, as recommended by the Budget Committee: an average budgetary tax increase of 2.11 percent — 2.90 percent for residential, 1.45 percent for commercial, 0.97 percent for industrial — with no increase for multi-residential properties and a 1.5 percent City Building levy increase.

Decided by a recorded vote, 21 in favour and 2 against — every member's position is public.

What the item proposed

At its meeting on February 7, 2022, the Budget Committee considered Item BU45.2 headed "2022 Property Tax Rates and Related Matters" and made recommendations to the Executive Committee. The Executive Committee will make recommendations on this Item to City Council for its consideration at its meeting on February 17, 2022. Summary from report (January 14, 2022) from the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer This report recommends the 2022 municipal tax ratios and tax rates arising from the concurrent adoption of the City of Toronto's 2022 tax supported Operating and Capital Budgets.
