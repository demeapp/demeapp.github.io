What the item proposed

The Province of Ontario is advancing the planning and delivery of the Ontario Line from the Science Centre to Exhibition Place through central Toronto, one of its four priority subway projects in Toronto. As part of this subway project, a number of stations have been identified as part of the Transit Oriented Communities (TOC) program, which leverages new development permission on transit station sites for a contribution to the future transit facilities. The TOC proposals covered in this report have been advanced by the Province within the context of the legislative framework provided by the Building Transit Faster Act, the Transit Oriented Communities Act, and Minister's zoning powers under the Planning Act.
