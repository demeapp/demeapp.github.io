The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends City Council adopt the Digital Infrastructure Strategic Framework (DISF) attached to this report to guide a standardized, integrated approach to decision-making related to the City's Digital Infrastructure. The Digital Infrastructure Strategic Framework was developed in response to Council's February 26, 2019 direction to "develop a City-wide policy framework and governance model associated with digital infrastructure and data". The Digital Infrastructure Strategic Framework includes five Working Principles that City Council adopted in January 2020 to advance this work; and reflects feedback gathered from two phases of community consultation and stakeholder engagement held in the summer and fall of 2021.
