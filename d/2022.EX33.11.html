The Accessibility Committee asks for a report on accommodating at-home voters in the municipal election.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on May 5, 2022, the Toronto Accessibility Committee considered Item DI20.3 and made a recommendation to the Executive Committee. Fiona Murray, Deputy City Clerk, Toronto Elections gave a presentation on the 2022 Election Accessibility Plan, providing an overview of initiatives aimed at improving accessibility for voters and candidates with disabilities during an election cycle.

What was decided

The Executive Committee: 1. Requested the City Clerk to report back to the June 13, 2022 meeting of the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee on how those who participated in home visit voting can vote in the upcoming municipal election.
