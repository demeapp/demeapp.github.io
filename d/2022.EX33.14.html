The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on May 9, 2022, the Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee considered Item CR7.5 and made recommendation to City Council.

What was decided

City Council on June 15 and 16, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Executive Director, Social Development, Finance and Administration, to work with the UNESCO Coalition of Inclusive Municipalities to engage other large Canadian municipalities on their efforts to advance the 2015-2024 United Nations International Decade for People of African Descent, to share and amplify learnings and Black experience, evaluate progress, and identify shared strategic actions to achieve and sustain long term outcomes for Black Canadians.
