The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide City Council with an update on the City's priority transit expansion projects, namely the Eglinton East Light Rail Transit (EELRT) and the Waterfront East LRT (WELRT) and seek required authorities to continue advancing both projects. This report also provides an update on the Province's Durham-Scarborough Bus Rapid Transit project. Eglinton East LRT The EELRT is a proposed 15 kilometre LRT in Scarborough extending from the terminus of the Eglinton Crosstown LRT (ECLRT) to Malvern Town Centre via the University of Toronto Scarborough Campus (UTSC). In 2020, City Council approved the updated alignment and concept for the EELRT as an extension of the Metrolinx-owned ECLRT by extending the ECLRT tracks underground at Kennedy Station, which would enable through-service at Kennedy Station.
