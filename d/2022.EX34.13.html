What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide City Council with the City of Toronto capital spending for the twelve month period ended December 31, 2021. As illustrated in Table 1 below, City's 2021 capital expenditure was $3.545 billion or 67.6% of the 2021 capital budget of $5.245 billion for the period ended December 31, 2021. - Tax Supported Programs and Agencies reported capital expenditures of $2.319 billion representing 61.3% of their collective 2021 Approved Capital Budget of $3.783 billion. - Rate Supported Programs reported capital expenditures of $1.226 billion, representing 83.9% of their collective 2021 Approved Capital Budget of $1.462 billion.
