The official record

What the item proposed

Housing is a central focus for the City of Toronto as it is for municipalities across Ontario and Canada. With the adoption of the HousingTO 2020-2030 Action Plan ("HousingTO Plan") in 2019, Toronto established a blueprint for action to improve housing outcomes for residents across the housing continuum. From the provision of emergency shelters and homelessness services to managing the social housing system, the City is working with other governments to provide supportive housing and associated wrap-around assistance such as for mental health and addictions, ensure more affordable rental housing development, deliver long-term care and advance other policies and incentives related to improving the quality and affordability of housing.
