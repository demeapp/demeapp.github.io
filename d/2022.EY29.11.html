The official record

What the item proposed

This report requests that City Council deny the request for a permit to remove one privately owned tree located at 171 John Street. The application indicates the reason for removal is due to concerns that the tree is destabilized due to the impact of landscaping work near the tree's roots. The subject tree is a Colorado blue spruce tree (Picea pungens), measuring 51.5 cm in diameter. The Tree By-laws do not support the removal of this tree as it is healthy and maintainable.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Deferred consideration of the item until February 24, 2022 meeting of the Etobicoke York Community Council.
