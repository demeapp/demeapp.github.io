The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes an exemption from the Part Lot Control provisions of the Planning Act to permit the creation of 32 parcels of tied land ("POTLs") associated with a common element condominium, as well as 14 freehold townhouse units at 3002, 3004, 3006, 3010 and 3014 Islington Avenue. This report reviews and recommends approval of the Part Lot Control Exemption application. In addition, this report recommends that the owner of the lands be required to register a Section 118 Restriction under the Land Titles Act agreeing not to convey or mortgage any part of the lands without prior consent of the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning or designate.

What was decided
