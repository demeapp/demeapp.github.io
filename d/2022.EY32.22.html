Community Council was asked to move permitted ice cream truck spots on Marine Parade Drive away from residences.

Official title: Amending Chapter 740, Schedule D, Ice Cream Vending Permit Locations

AdoptedMay 27, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I have received numerous complaints from local residents about ice cream trucks idling for hours in front of residences along Marine Parade Drive. Residents have repeatedly reported strong smells of propane, noisy generators, and illegal parking. This motion seeks to shift the permitted operating areas of ice cream trucks along Marine Parade Drive away from local residences.
