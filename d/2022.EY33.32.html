City Council was asked to permanently close part of the rear laneway at 127 King Street to fix a garage encroachment.

Official title: Permanent Closure of a Portion of the Untraveled Laneway at the Rear of 127 King Street

The official record

What the item proposed

As this report proposes to permanently close a portion of untraveled public laneway, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services recommends that a portion of the untraveled public laneway at the rear of 127 King Street, as shown in Attachment 1 be permanently closed. Once closed, it is intended that Part 2 on the Plan will be sold to the adjacent property owners, Ekaterini Allagas and Steven Da Silva, in order to resolve the current situation where part of a garage structure is occupying the property.

What was decided

City Council on July 19, 20, 21 and 22, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the permanent closure of a portion of untraveled laneway, shown as Part 2 on the R-Plan 66R-32634 set out in Attachment 1 to the report (June 9, 2022) from the Director, Transportation Planning and Capital Program, Transportation Services. 2. City Council enact a by-law substantially in the form of the draft by-law in Attachment 2 to the report (June 9, 2022) from the Director, Transportation Planning and Capital Program, Transportation Services.
