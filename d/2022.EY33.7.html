The official record

What the item proposed

These applications propose to revitalize the existing Toronto Community Housing Corporation's (TCHC) lands to permit four residential buildings with heights of four to 25 storeys, two mixed-use buildings of 25 storeys, a community hub, and a public park at 3 Marsh Grassway, 7 and 11 Blue Grassway, 1, 2 and 8 Dune Grassway, 4 and 17 Cane Grassway, and 5, 10 and 40 Turf Grassway. The existing 12-storey apartment building at 5 Needle Firway and the two rows of two-storey townhouses at 2-14 and 22-36 Needle Firway form part of this application and are to be retained. Collectively, the subject site is called the Firgrove Grassways community.
