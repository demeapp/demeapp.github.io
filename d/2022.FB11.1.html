This is an information item: the advisory board receives staff updates on film permits, workforce programs, and production volumes.

Official title: Staff Updates

Adopted with changesApr 4, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Economic Development and Culture staff will provide updates on film permit fees adoption and implementation timelines, Power Drops implementation, Workforce Development programs update and recent film production volumes.

What was decided

The Film, Television and Digital Media Advisory Board recommends that: 1. The Economic and Community Development Committee request the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, and the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, in consultation with the Chair and the Co-Chair of the Film, Television and Digital Media Board and the Directors Guild of Canada, report to June 6, 2022 meeting of the Toronto Film, Television and Digital Media Board on any changes necessary to existing contracts for filming on city sites that would facilitate easier access and expedite the process; such report to include the list of current city properties that are being used for film purposes as well as those that have been requested but not currently permitted.
