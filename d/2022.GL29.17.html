The City Clerk asks Council to add a dedicated email retention schedule to the City's records by-law.

Official title: Amendments to Municipal Code Chapter 217, Records, City (Corporate)

AdoptedApr 6, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to amend the City's records retention by-law support of the City's transition to digital recordkeeping by establishing a new records retention schedule specifically for email, which would prevail over any other retention schedule.

What was decided

City Council on April 6 and 7, 2022 adopted the following: 1. City Council amend Municipal Code Chapter 217, Records, Corporate (City) by establishing a new records retention schedule for email set out in Attachment 1 to the report (March 8, 2022) from the City Clerk.
