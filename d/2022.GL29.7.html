What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek authority for the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management to amend the contract value of three (3) non-competitive procurements with fire and life safety vendors providing service to the City of Toronto. The cumulative amendment value requested of all three (3) contracts is $1,150,000 net of all taxes ($1,177,938, net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries). The contracts were originally established in 2019 to provide temporary coverage of fire and life safety inspection, testing, maintenance and monitoring services for the City of Toronto, following the Auditor General's investigation and recommendations (see item AU13.11 ) that resulted in several fire and life safety contracts being terminated due to non-compliance with the Ontario Fire Code.
