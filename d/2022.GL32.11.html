What the item proposed

This report provides information on non-union employee separation costs for 2021. In 2014, the City's Auditor General reviewed the City of Toronto's non-union employee separation costs. The review affirmed that separation costs had been awarded in accordance with City of Toronto policies, procedures, applicable legislation and jurisprudence. The Auditor General recommended that separation costs continue to be monitored and that the costs be reported out regularly. The City of Toronto has statutory and legal obligations to provide separation pay when the employment relationship is terminated by the City of Toronto without just cause. Administering separation payments for non-union employees whose employment is terminated without cause falls under the authority of the City Manager.
