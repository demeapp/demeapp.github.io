The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on June 15 and 16, 2022, referred Motion MM45.25 to the General Government and Licensing Committee. Summary from Member Motion: Since being selected as the operator of Casa Loma, the Liberty Entertainment Group has unquestionably revitalized the heritage site. However, there is still a need for community consultation to create a vision for the North of Austin Terrace site. The item at City Council that awarded the Request for Proposal to Liberty in 2013 was EX35.2 - Casa Loma Request for Proposals - Operator for Main House and Grounds. The Staff Recommendations for the item clearly delineated the North of Austin Terrace as a separate entity, intended for a different use and/or operator: 6. City Council request that the Casa Loma Corporation review options for the north portion of the Casa Loma complex, including consideration of issuance of an Request for Expressions of…

What was decided

The General Government and Licensing Committee deferred the consideration of this item indefinitely.
