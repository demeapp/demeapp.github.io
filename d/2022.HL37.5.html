The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an update to the Board of Health on the Toronto Public Health Capital Budget Variance for the twelve months ended on December 31, 2021. As of December 31, 2021, Toronto Public Health spent $1,217.9 thousand representing 24.2 percent of the 2021 Approved Capital Budget of $5,032.0 thousand. The underspending reflects the planned projects for 2021 which were affected by the impact of COVID-19 on the availability of resources within Toronto Public Health and its partners.

What was decided

The Board of Health: 1. Received the report (May 2, 2022) from the Medical Officer of Health for information.
