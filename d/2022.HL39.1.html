What the item proposed

The Deputy Medical Officers of Health will give a presentation on Update on COVID-19 Response and Other Public Health Issues.

What was decided

The Board of Health: 1. Requested the Medical Officer of Health to report to the Board of Health in the winter of 2023 with an update on the respiratory season in Toronto, including information on COVID-19 and Influenza. 2. Requested the Medical Officer of Health urgently explore all avenues toward re-issuing mask mandates, starting with schools, to reduce the spread of respiratory viruses and protect the capacity of pediatric health care services.
