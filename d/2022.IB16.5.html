The Board receives the City's Reconciliation Action Plan 2022-2032.

Official title: Actions to Advance Truth and Justice for Indigenous People in the City of Toronto: Reconciliation Action Plan 2022-2032

Adopted with changesJun 23, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on April 6 and 7, 2022, adopted Executive Committee Item EX31.1, and in so doing, adopted the Reconciliation Action Plan 2022-2032 in Attachment 1 to the report (March 16, 2022) from the City Manager, as amended. City Council has also directed the Boards of City Service Agencies (excepting the Toronto Library Board and the Toronto police Services Board) to: 1. Adopt the values and principles set out in Attachment 3 to the report (March 16, 2022) from the City Manager as a guide for future reconciliation efforts. 2. Submit to the Indigenous Affairs Office every two years, details of the work being undertaken for and with Indigenous People and organizations in Toronto, as input into the Indigenous Affairs Office bi-annual reporting to City Council.

What was decided

The Toronto Investment Board: 1. Adopted the values and principles set out in Attachment 3 to the report (March 16, 2022) from the City Manager as a guide for future reconciliation efforts. 2. Requested the Director, Capital Markets to prepare a report to the Board in the new term on the work being undertaken for and with Indigenous People and organizations in Toronto as it pertains to the mandate of the Toronto Investment Board.
