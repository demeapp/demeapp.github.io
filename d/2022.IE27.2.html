What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend the value and extend the term to February 22, 2023 of Purchase Order 6044699 issued to Air Products Canada Ltd. (the "Supplier") for the supply of liquid oxygen, the lease of liquid oxygen equipment and the provision of specialized maintenance services at the F.J. Horgan Water Treatment Plant (the "Purchase Order"). This report further seeks delegated authority to the General Manager, Toronto Water, to enter into and execute an amending agreement to the City's current contract with the Supplier to give effect to these Purchase Order amendments. The City is in the process of procuring a new liquid oxygen supply contract for these specialized services, however, the new contract will not be awarded prior to the expiration of the City's current contract with the Supplier on February 22, 2022.
