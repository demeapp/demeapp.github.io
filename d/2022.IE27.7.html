The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an update on the one-year Residential and Downtown On-Street Electric Vehicle Charging Station pilots that were launched in October of 2020, including information on charging station utilization, greenhouse gas impacts, costs, revenues and measures taken to improve compliance by non-Electric Vehicle operators with the existing Electric Vehicle parking regulations. At the request of Toronto Hydro, the report seeks City Council approval to continue collecting data on the sites that were part of the Downtown and Residential Electric Vehicle Charging Station pilots by eight (8) months. While uptake of the pilots’ charging stations has increased over the past 12 months, the pandemic has had an impact on some of the findings.
