What the item proposed

The City of Toronto has many unique neighbourhoods and different physical attributes to the design of its streets and public infrastructure. Many of these features are products of the era in which they were built. Most of Etobicoke Centre, Ward 2, was developed in the post-World War II decades, and features many streets of detached homes on rural-cross section roads. These particular streets have ditches and soft shoulders, instead of curbs, gutters and continuous storms sewers, which are found in the earlier and later eras. While ditches are excellent performers when it comes to capturing and conveying storm water, and offer water-absorbing green infrastructure, they also must be maintained just like other municipal infrastructure. What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. Requested Transportation Services to report to the Infrastructure and Environment Committee in the first quarter of 2023 on the ditches and ditch culvert repair and maintenance program, and to identify opportunities and resources required to meet service demands in a reasonable time frame and to reach a state of good repair.
