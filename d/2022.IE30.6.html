What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Purchase Order Number 6042338 and 6042339 with Black & Veatch Canada Company ("Black & Veatch") for additional project management and professional engineering design services for the Integrated Pumping Station Project at the Ashbridges Bay Treatment Plant in the total amount of $2,454,712 net of all taxes and charges ($2,497,915 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) and to reallocate project costs and cashflows in Toronto Water's Approved 2022 Capital Budget and 2023-2021 Capital Plan in the amount of $1,950,000 to fund increased costs of the proposed amendment. This amendment is required to complete the project, resolve claims and accommodate an extended timeline for completion of the remaining work.
