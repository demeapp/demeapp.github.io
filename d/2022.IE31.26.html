Councillors Perruzza and Colle ask the Infrastructure and Environment Committee to study emergency grass-cutting and tree-trimming teams.

Official title: Investigating the Feasibility of Emergency Grass Cutting Teams

AdoptedJul 7, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents requests for City staff to address overgrown grass often results in lengthy delays. These issues can often arise in specific problem areas on an annual basis that cannot wait for the regular schedule for seasonal work to be addressed.

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services to investigate the feasibility of establishing emergency response teams to address grass cutting and tree trimming.
