What the item proposed

At its meeting on May 28, 2020, City Council adopted the Recommendations in the Report (February 25, 2020) from the Director, Community Planning, Scarborough District approving amendments to Zoning By-law 569-2013 and the Highland Creek Community Zoning By-law 10827, to permit the development of 8 detached homes, 20 semi-detached homes and 16 townhouse units. After the statutory public meeting held at Scarborough Community Council on March 12, 2020, and after the May 28, 2020, City Council meeting it was determined by Toronto Buildings that the draft Zoning By-law Amendments found in Attachments 7 and 8 to the Planning Report required minor amendments to correct technical deficiencies that brought about by continued refinement of the proposal through concurrent review of the Plan of Subdivision application.
