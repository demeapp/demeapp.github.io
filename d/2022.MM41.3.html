What the item proposed

The City of Shenzhen, China has requested an extension to the Memorandum of Understanding between Shenzhen and Toronto. The City of Toronto has enjoyed a strong, productive relationship with the Municipality of Shenzhen for several years, supported most recently by a 5-year Memorandum of Understanding. Unlike agreements with other cities that fall within the scope of the City's International Alliance Program which are open-ended, a sunset clause was included in Toronto's Memorandum of Understanding with Shenzhen: after 5 years both cities must make the decision to renew the relationship agreement. This mechanism encourages both cities to innovate and helps to ensure that our continued engagement yields tangible mutual benefits which take account of current circumstances and market opportunities.
