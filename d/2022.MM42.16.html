The official record

What the item proposed

The Legacy Art Project is a citizen-funded public space initiative dedicated to the spirit of courage, determination, and action that Terry Fox embodied. In July 2018, City Council endorsed (MM44.38) the use of 439 Queens Quay West, between the Spadina Wetlands and the Toronto Music Garden, for a memorial to Terry Fox proposed by Legacy Art Project. Since that time, a design has been chosen for the installation and surrounding public realm improvements: "We Are Shaped by the Obstacles We Face" by Jon Sasaki and DTAH. From the installation’s west end, it appears as a single figure – the iconic silhouette of Fox, mid-stride. Private donations have raised hundreds of thousands of dollars to date, and fundraising continues.
