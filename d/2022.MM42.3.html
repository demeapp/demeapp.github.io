The official record

What the item proposed

In June 2021, the City and the Aki Wiidookaagewin (Ontario Earth Helpers), an Indigenous organization local to the Spadina neighbourhood, were among several parties to a Memorandum of Understanding in respect of the Indigenous Kitchen Garden Project. The Project's objectives include among other things, to create raised bed gardens at the City's Spadina Museum site to demonstrate food growing practices and create employment and skills training opportunities for Indigenous peoples, to activate the museum space and provide educational programming activities available to the public. The City, as part of its commitments under the Memorandum of Understanding entered into an agreement with Aki Wiidookaagewin (Ontario Earth Helpers) for a one year term expiring on June 30, 2022.
