What the item proposed

Opened in 1894, Massey Hall is the most celebrated music venue in Canada and a critical component of Toronto's culture and economy. Massey Hall embarked upon an ambitious revitalization project in 2018, to expand its capacity to serve audiences, develop artists and act as a cultural and tourism hub for the city. Between construction and the first five years of operations, the revitalization of Massey Hall will contribute $348 million to the local economy and create 4,000 jobs. Going forward, the renewed facility will contribute to the economy with $42.6 million in direct and indirect impacts, 430 Full Time Equivalent jobs, and 500,000 visitors annually. Massey Hall advances Toronto's reputation as a Music City, thereby contributing to tourism and the development of Toronto's creative industries.
