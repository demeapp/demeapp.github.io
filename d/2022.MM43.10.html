Council was asked to support federal Bill C-233, “Keira's Law”, on domestic-violence protections.

The official record

What the item proposed

Violence against women is a Canadian public health crisis that demands urgent action. Sadly, one in four women experience domestic violence in their lifetime; and one woman or girl is killed every other day, on average, somewhere in our country. The COVID-19 pandemic has only exacerbated the domestic violence crisis. According to the National Judicial Institute, there is no mandatory education for Judges on domestic violence. The most dangerous time for a victim of abuse is when she separates from her partner. A formal education program regarding domestic violence or coercive control and other measures proposed in Private Member’s Bill C-233 would ensure another line of defense for victims, as well as preventing violence and abuse before it happens. What was decided

City Council on May 11 and 12, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council support Member of Parliament Anju Dhillon's Private Member’s Bill C-233, “Keira’s Law” which would amend the Criminal Code and Judges Act to strengthen measures to protect women and children from domestic violence. 2. City Council request that a copy of this Motion and City Council's decision be provided to all Toronto Members of Parliament and the leaders of all political parties with Members of Parliament.
