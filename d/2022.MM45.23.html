The official record

What the item proposed

This Motion requests authority from Council to amend the 2022-2031 Council Approved Capital Budget and Plan for Parks, Forestry and Recreation in the amount of $0.650 million for improvements to Osler Playground and the adjacent Senhor Santo Cristo Catholic School lands located at 30 Humbert Street. Additional funds are required to support the scope of work that meets the needs of the community. The total project cost would be amended from $1.100 million to $1.750 million, with cash flow commitments in 2023. The improvements will be fully funded from the following sources: $0.050 million from the Section 37 funds obtained from the development at 700 King Street West (Source Account: XR3026-3700112); $0.200 million from the Section 37 funds obtained from the development at 99 Atlantic Avenue, 38-40 Hanna Avenue, 2-24A Liberty Street and 1 Snooker Street (Source Account: XR3026-3700112);…
