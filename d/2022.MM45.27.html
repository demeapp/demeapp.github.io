Council was asked to fund $175,000 in basketball court improvements at Walter Saunders Memorial Park.

The official record

What the item proposed

This Motion requests authority from City Council to amend the 2022-2031 City Council Approved Capital Budget and Plan for Parks, Forestry and Recreation to create a new capital sub-project for Walter Saunders Memorial Park Basketball Court Improvements in the amount of $0.175 million with cash flow in 2022. The project will be fully funded from the Section 37 funds obtained from the development at 2270-2280, 2296 Eglinton Avenue West, 6 Sanderstead Avenue and 9 Croham Road (Source Account: XR3026-3701169); in order to improve the existing courts and add a new half court with lighting. The funds have been received, are eligible for these purposes and support the needs of the community.

What was decided

City Council on June 15 and 16, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend the Parks, Forestry and Recreation 2022-2031 Approved Capital Budget and Plan to create a new capital sub-project known as Walter Saunders Memorial Park Basketball Court Improvements in the Outdoor Recreation Centre project, with a project cost and cash flow of $0.175 million in 2022, fully funded by Section 37 funds obtained from the development at 2270-2280, 2296 Eglinton Avenue West, 6 Sanderstead Avenue and 9 Croham Road (Source Account: XR3026- 3701169) for the purpose of basketball court improvements.
