What the item proposed

For the past several years, Museum and Heritage Services in the Economic Development and Culture division has been reimagining and rethinking what "community heritage" means in the context of the City museums. In 2019, Museum and Heritage Services sought and received federal funding as part of the Museums Assistance Program for a project as part of Museum and Heritage Services' ongoing efforts to indigenize the City of Toronto’s heritage collections (the "Project"). The initiative began and then paused due to the COVID-19 pandemic. Ongoing commitment to advancing and supporting Reconciliation and equity led to more emphasis on collecting and amplifying voices that have historically not been represented in Toronto’s museums, including Indigenous ones.
