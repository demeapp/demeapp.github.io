What the item proposed

The City of Toronto, in collaboration with the University of Toronto, is working to create a monumental mural honouring iconic Canadian hero Terry Fox. The project will bring a significant public realm enhancement to the University Avenue corridor, beautifying the area and creating a cultural tourism destination. Authority is being sought to release $200,000 in Section 37 funds to ArtworxTO: Toronto’s year of Public Art to provide one-time funding to support the creation and installation of a large-scale mural celebrating the legacy of Terry Fox. In commemoration of this inspirational Canadian hero, the City of Toronto, the University of Toronto and The Legacy Project are collaborating on a community-engaged Terry Fox Tribute Mural - a landmark mural to be painted on panels on the north side of the University of Toronto's Rehabilitation Sciences Building at 500 University Avenue.
