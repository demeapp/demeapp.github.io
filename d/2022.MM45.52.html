The official record

What the item proposed

An application for Site Plan Approval has been filed proposing a 30 storey, 382 suite hotel which is connected to a 7,000 seat venue that will host E-sports events, concerts and other large gatherings, located on the Exhibition Grounds. A surface parking lot of 108 parking spaces is proposed together with 358 parking spaces located 1 level below grade. The proposed development is located over a portion of the New Fort archeological site (AjGu-32). In response to the on-site archeological resources, the development intends to remove the findings located directly beneath the site, with the exception of the men’s barracks’ foundations, to facilitate the underground parking while introducing a heritage interpretation piece that displays a portion of the excavated former men’s barracks’ foundations. What was decided

City Council on June 15 and 16, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning to continue to expedite the review of the Site Plan Application Number 22 120103 STE 10 SA for the Hotel X Phase 2 – 2 Strachan and 135 Princes’ Boulevard, and City Council direct the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning to report directly to the July 19 and 20 meeting of City Council on the status of the site plan application.
