The official record

What the item proposed

This Motion seeks authorization to release $22,500 in Section 16 funds to Heritage Toronto for the creation and installation of three heritage plaques to recognize John M Tinsley, Peggy, and James Mink, persons of historic significance identified through research by Ontario Black History Society and Heritage Toronto. John Tinsley John Tinsley (1782-1892) was born a free Black man in Richmond, Virginia. He trained as a carpenter and millwright and came to Canada in 1842. In Toronto, Tinsley was a building contractor and he ran a successful construction company. Tinsley would often hire and provide vital paid work to newcomers to Canada who arrived via the Underground Railroad, the secret network of travel routes that allowed enslaved people to find comparative freedom in the northern United States and Canada.
