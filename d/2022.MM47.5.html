Council was asked to have staff report on expanding the Oriole Community Centre gymnasium through the 2023 budget.

Official title: Finishing the Project - Potential Expansion of Oriole Gymnasium - by Councillor Shelley Carroll, seconded by Councillor Paul Ainslie

AdoptedJul 19, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In 2005-2006, Parks, Forestry, and Recreation conducted a feasibility study for the expansion of Oriole Community Centre, including the onsite gymnasium. Due to lack of available funding, this aspect of the expansion was de-scoped from the expansion project that was completed in 2017. Due to the ongoing subway intensification along Sheppard Avenue East, there is a pressing need for an expanded community centre and, in particular, gymnasium space in the Don Valley Village community. Additionally, there is now available funding as a result of that intensification. This Motion requests that the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation report through the 2023 Budget process on adding this much-needed project to the 10-Year Capital Plan, to be funded through community benefit funding.

What was decided

City Council on July 19, 20, 21 and 22, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation to report back through the 2023 Budget process on the inclusion of an expansion of the Oriole Community Centre gymnasium, to be funded from local Section 37 and Section 42 funding, as part of the 10-Year Capital Plan. 2. City Council direct the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation and the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning to identify, in collaboration with the local Councillor, potential sources of funding from local community benefit funds for the expansion of the Oriole Community Centre gymnasium.
