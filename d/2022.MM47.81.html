What the item proposed

The requirement for completion of the Section 37 Agreement is being amended from prior to the enactment of Bills to prior to the issuance of a building permit. As a result, the indexing provisions are proposed to be changed to the date that the By-law is enacted by City Council, for consistency. These matters are addressed in Parts 1 and 2. Part 3 is proposed to provide for consistency in the implementation of the Toronto Green Standard across development applications. This Motion is urgent because City Council directed that City staff secure community benefits and matters of legal convenience pursuant to Section 37 of the Planning Act.
