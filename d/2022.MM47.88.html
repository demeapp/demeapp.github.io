The official record

What the item proposed

In 2021, an Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment was approved by the Ontario Land Tribunal to permit a mixed use development of the former TDSB lands located at 90 Croatia Street, 980 Dufferin Street, and 1141 Bloor Street West. The approved redevelopment includes affordable and new market housing, retail, a new daycare, community space, a new park, a new public street, and conservation of heritage at 980 Dufferin Street. This Motion responds to a request from the owner to seek changes to the gross floor area and height above and beyond what had been approved by the Ontario Land Tribunal. Other performance standards related to the site-specific by-law may also need amending.
