What the item proposed

City Council on December 15, 16 and 17, 2021, referred Administrative Inquiry IA38.1 from Councillor Shelley Carroll, Ward 17, Don Valley North regarding the Thomas Clark Way Extension and Answer IA38.1a from the City Manager to the North York Community Council for consideration.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Community Planning, North York District to ensure the Thomas Clark Way extension is not precluded or negatively impacted as part of the review of the Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment Applications for 589 Sheppard Avenue East and 7 to 9 Barberry Place in accordance with the Provincial Policy Statement (2020) and A Place to Grow: Growth Plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe (2020). 2.
