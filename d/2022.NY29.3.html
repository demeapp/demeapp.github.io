The official record

What the item proposed

In a report dated February 1, 2021, for consideration at the March 1, 2021 North York Community Council meeting, City Planning recommended refusal of the application to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law for the lands at 12 to 24 Leith Hill Road to permit three blocks of townhouse units, containing 48 dwelling units, at the western limit of the site, and an 11-storey residential building, proposed with 121 dwelling units, to the east of the existing 16-storey apartment building. The application was not consistent with relevant policies of the Provincial Policy Statement (2020) and did not conform with A Place to Grow: Growth Plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe (2020). Additionally, the proposed amendments were not consistent with the Official Plan, the Secondary Plan and relevant guidelines.
