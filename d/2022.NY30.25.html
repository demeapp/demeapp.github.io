Staff ask to extend a school bus loading zone on Wilmington Avenue.

Official title: School Bus Loading Zones - Wilmington Avenue

AdoptedMar 9, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Wilmington Avenue, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is recommending the extension of the existing school bus loading zone on the west side of Wilmington Avenue, in the vicinity of 200 Wilmington Avenue, to accommodate additional school buses that load and unload students at The Anne & Max Tanenbaum Community Hebrew Academy of Toronto.

What was decided

City Council on March 9, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend the existing designated school bus loading zone in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday on the west side of Wilmington Avenue, between a point 32.6 metres south of Overbrook Place and a point 60.2 metres further south, to be in effect between a point 32.6 metres south of Overbrook Place to a point 90 metres further south. 2. City Council amend the existing stopping prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Wilmington Avenue, between Kennard Avenue and Overbrook Place, to be in effect at all times.
