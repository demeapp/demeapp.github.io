Staff recommend against permanently closing the Blue Ridge Road cul-de-sac.

Official title: Permanent Closure to Vehicular Traffic of the Blue Ridge Road Cul-de-sac

Adopted with changesFeb 23, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of November 4, 2020, North York Community Council directed the General Manager, Transportation Services to investigate the permanent stop-up and closure to vehicular traffic of the cul-de-sac at the northerly terminus of Blue Ridge Road. Further to our review, Transportation Services does not recommend closing the cul-de-sac to vehicular traffic, and recommends that this report be received for information.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services to develop a plan to close the cul-de-sac to vehicular traffic, in consultation with operating divisions. 2. Requested the Chief Engineer and Executive Director, Engineering and Construction Services, Land and Property Surveys, to prepare in consultation with the General Manager, Transportation Services a Reference Plan of Survey to identify the sections of cul-de-sac being closed to vehicular traffic. 3. Upon registration of the Plan of Survey, requested that the General Manager, Transportation Services report back to North York Community Council on the closure to vehicular traffic of the Blue Ridge Road cul-de-sac and append to that report a draft bylaw, accordingly; and authorize the General Manager, Transportation Services, to give notice to the public of a proposed by-law to close the cul-de-sac…
