Staff ask to ban parking at all times on part of Harrison Garden Boulevard.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Harrison Garden Boulevard

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that the existing parking prohibition between 7:00 a.m. and 10:00 a.m. on the south side of Harrison Garden Boulevard be amended to prohibit parking at all times. The proposed amendment will deter parking at this location and will improve the sightlines of the motorists entering and exiting the driveway access at property number 80 Harrison Garden Boulevard. During the course of the investigation, it was noted that Harrison Garden Boulevard has been extended further north to Oakburn Crescent and that the existing winter parking maintenance regulation is not updated. Recommendation 4 will correct this regulation.

What was decided

Prohibited parking in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, on the south and east sides of Harrison Garden Boulevard, between a point 148 metres east of Everson Drive and Oakburn Crescent. 3.
