The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes exemption from the Part Lot Control provisions of the Planning Act for a portion of the subject lands municipally known as 2995 Keele Street, Block "P", of the Stanley Greene District. The purpose of the application is to permit the creation of 38 conveyable lots to facilitate the development of 38 townhouse dwelling units in 6 buildings. This proposal would work in conjunction with a separate application for a Plan of Common Elements Condominium on another portion of the subject lands. The purpose of the Common Elements Condominium application is to allow the development of a number of common element features. The 38 conveyable lots of the townhouse development would become Parcels of Tied Land ("POTL) to the Common Elements Condominium.
