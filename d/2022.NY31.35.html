North York Community Council decides whether to declare Armenian Summerfest 2022 an event of municipal significance for liquor licensing.

Official title: Endorsement of Event for Liquor Licensing Purposes

AdoptedMay 11, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The City Clerk's Office has received a request for municipal endorsement for a temporary liquor licence extension that does not meet the criteria of the delegated authority.

What was decided

City Council on May 11 and 12, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council endorse the application from Armenian Summerfest 2022 - Armenian Community Centre for an extension to licensed area as set out in Attachment 1 to the report (March 30, 2022) from the City Clerk and City Council direct the City Clerk to issue a letter of municipal significance and non-objection to the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario.
