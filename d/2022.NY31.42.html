Demolition Applications for 51 Drewry Avenue and 8 to 28 Inez Court

Adopted with changesMay 11, 2022 · Toronto City Council

North York Community Council was asked to have the Chief Building Official report directly to Council on demolition applications for 51 Drewry Avenue and 8-28 Inez Court.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

To facilitate a land exchange between the applicant and the City, it would be helpful if a demolition approval for the above-noted properties is approved as soon as possible. My office has been advised that the staff report will not be ready in time for this meeting of NYCC. I am recommending that North York Community Council direct the Chief Building Official to report directly to Council on this matter
