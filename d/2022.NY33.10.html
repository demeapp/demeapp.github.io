What the item proposed

The Part Lot Control Exemption Application (the "Application") applies to the lands identified as Blocks 1 and 2 on Plan 66M-2577 and known as Phase 1E of the Lawrence Heights Revitalization Project (Attachment 4) (related Site Plan Application No: 17 222387 NNY 15 SA). What was decided
