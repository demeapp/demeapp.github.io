The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting approval to install all-way stop control at the north intersection of Godstone Road and Kempsell Crescent. Based on the assessment undertaken, the installation of all-way stop control is recommended at this intersection and should enhance safety for all road users.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Godstone Road and Kempsell Crescent (north intersection).
