The official record

What the item proposed

Thomas Clark Way is a planned street that will serve the community located to the southeast of the Bayview Avenue and Sheppard Avenue East intersection. The street will extend approximately 200 metres (m) between Kenaston Gardens in the west and Rean Drive in the east, with a two-way stop-controlled intersection at Barberry Place. The street will have a right-of-way width varying between 16.5m and 20.0m with a paved surface of 8.5 m, including wide sidewalks on both sides of the street and grassed boulevard space. Portions of Thomas Clark Way have been constructed between Rean Drive and Barberry Place and between Barberry Place and Kenaston Gardens. These sections are dedicated as a future public road but are not yet assumed by the City. The remaining segment of Thomas Clark Way will be delivered by the City.
