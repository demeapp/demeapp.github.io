What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting approval for the installation of on-street paid parking on local streets to the east and west of Yonge Street in Ward 18. This request resulted from a review following the December 16, 2020 City Council endorsement of the recommended design for the reconstruction of Yonge Street between Florence Avenue / Avondale Avenue to the Finch Hydro Corridor. The preferred design will result in a reduction from six to four traffic lanes between Sheppard Avenue and Finch Avenue and thus result in the loss of several on-street paid parking spaces along Yonge Street.
