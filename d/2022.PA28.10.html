What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority’s history goes back nearly 70 years to June 24, 1952 when the Council of the City of Toronto passed a bylaw establishing the Parking Authority of Toronto. Toronto Parking Authority’s use of the Green P logo dates back to at least 1956 and is now a central component of Toronto Parking Authority’s branding of all Toronto Parking Authority parking facilities, parking equipment, the Green P Mobile App, Toronto Parking Authority’s online and social media presence and third-party parking lots operated by Toronto Parking Authority as Green P parking. The use of the Green P Logo has come to be known as an indicator of affordable, safe, and secure public parking in the City of Toronto.
