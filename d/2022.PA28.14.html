The official record

What the item proposed

This report details the legal services commenced by Toronto Parking Authority pertaining to five (5) projects including, 30 Alvin Avenue (Car Park 12), 50 Cumberland Street (Car Park 15), 1445 Bathurst Street (Car Park 217), 16 John Street (Car Park 650), and 1607 Eglinton Avenue West (Car Park 664) (collectively referred to as the Projects). Confidential Attachment 1 contains information pertaining to potential litigation and contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Adopted the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (February 4, 2022) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority. 2. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1 to 7 to the report (February 4, 2022) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority, remain confidential in their entirety, as they contain advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
