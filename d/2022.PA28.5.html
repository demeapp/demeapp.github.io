The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 3-6: Residential Overnight Use (refer to Appendix A) identifies ungated car parks located in residential areas that can accommodate overnight parking by local residents displaying valid residential on-street permits. Under the policy, permit holders may park overnight at the designated car parks at no charge. The car parks and applicable hours where residential on-street permit holders can park are identified in Annex A to the policy. This report seeks approval for several amendments to the administration of Policy Resolution 3-6, which will provide Toronto Parking Authority the flexibility to manage residential overnight parking based on the capacity of car parks. The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Referred the item to the President, Toronto Parking Authority, and requested the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to consult with local Councillors impacted by the proposed changes.
