The official record

What the item proposed

This report includes an update on staffing matters and the organization structure, including a review of Toronto Parking Authority's human resources, as of the first quarter of 2022. Arlene Yam Fritz, Vice President, Human Resources and Transformation will provide an update to the Board at its meeting of April 20, 2022, on staffing matters and the organization structure. These matters will require a closed session discussion, as they relate to labour relations or employee negotiations and/or deal with personal matters about identifiable persons.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Directed that the confidential presentation from the Vice-President, Human Resources and Transformation, Toronto Parking Authority, remain confidential in its entirety, as it pertains to labour relations and employee negotiations and personal matters about identifiable individuals.
