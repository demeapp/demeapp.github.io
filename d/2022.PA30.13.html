The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks authority from Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors to direct City of Toronto Corporate Real Estate Management to enter into a Lease Extension Agreement (the “Agreement”) with Karelada Investments Limited (the “Owner” or “Karelada”) for the parcel of land located at 5665 Yonge Street. These lands have been leased by Toronto Parking Authority since 2006 and are currently incorporated into Car Park 419 (the “Property”) (refer to Appendix A - Site Location Map). Car Park 419 is comprised of both the leased lands at 5665 Yonge Street and a City-owned parcel located at 5667 Yonge Street. Should the recommendations of this report be adopted, Corporate Real Estate Management will negotiate, enter into and execute the Agreement and extend the lease of the land until May 31, 2024 with three (3) optional renewal terms of one (1) year each.
