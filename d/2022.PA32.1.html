What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide the Board of Directors with Management’s recommended 2023 Operating and Capital Budgets. As part of the approval process (and in recognition that this is an election year), Management will seek Board approval to receive the proposed budget at the September 29, 2022 Board Meeting. Management will subsequently discuss the budget in further detail with the Toronto Parking Authority Board’s Audit and Risk Management Committee on November 8, 2022 and thereafter, request formal approval of the 2023 Budget at the November 23, 2022 Board meeting. This timeline affords both the Board and Management sufficient opportunity to discuss the 2023 Annual Operating Plans while adhering to the City’s Budgetary process.
