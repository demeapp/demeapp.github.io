The official record

What the item proposed

Bike Share Toronto is a sustainable, healthy, and affordable mobility program that is an integral component of Toronto’s transportation network. Designed to improve mobility options for Torontonians, the program has seen tremendous success and growth in the first half of 2022, including record ridership, growth in annual memberships, an increased deployment of e-bikes, and marked progress towards securing a strategic sponsor. As North America's 4th largest bike share system in size, it is projected to reach 4.3 million rides by the end of 2022, a 23 percent increase in ridership versus 2021. The program also continues to enjoy broad support amongst its customers with a 92 percent overall satisfaction rating reported in the summer of 2022.
