The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve the alterations proposed for the heritage property located at 330 University Avenue (designated under Part IV of the Ontario Heritage Act). The proposal is for replacement of three matching "Canada Life" illuminated fascia signs on the north, west, and south elevations of the tower of the Canada Life Building at 330 University Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto Preservation Board recommends to the Toronto and East York Community Council that: 1. City Council approve alterations to the heritage property at 330 University Avenue in accordance with Section 33 of the Ontario Heritage Act, to allow for the replacement of three "Canada Life" signs on the lands known municipally as 330 University Avenue, in accordance with the plans and drawings prepared by Pattison Sign Group, dated November 2, 2020 and October 29, 2020 and on file with the Senior Manager, Heritage Planning, Urban Design, City Planning and subject to the following conditions: a.
