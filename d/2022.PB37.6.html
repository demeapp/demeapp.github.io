The official record

What the item proposed

Heritage Planning of the City Planning Division brings forward for City Council approval reports recommending the addition to the City’s heritage register of "batch listings" of properties. Such properties may share, for example, similar age and characteristics, such as "two-storey main-street commercial." The reports are first presented to the Toronto Preservation Board, which makes recommendations to City Council regarding their approval. If the listings are adopted by City Council, they are added to the Heritage Register. In due course, they are considered by Heritage Staff for designation under the Ontario Heritage Act (OHA), often in association with development applications that include all or some of the buildings in a particular batch. What was decided

The Toronto Preservation Board: 1. Requested the Senior Manager, Heritage Planning, Urban Design, City Planning to review the current process and make a presentation to the Toronto Preservation Board regarding any recommended process changes, including public engagement, with respect to listed properties that are subsequently deemed not worthy of designation under the Ontario Heritage Act (OHA). 2. Requested the presentation at the September 20, 2022, meeting of the Toronto Preservation Board.
