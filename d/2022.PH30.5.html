The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto is largely built-out and there is fierce competition for land for both residential and employment space. Toronto must find room via infill and redevelopment of existing land and buildings to accommodate growth. At the same time, the city’s Employment Areas face considerable pressure to convert to permit a broader range of uses, including sensitive residential uses. Due to the operational needs of many businesses and given differences in development economics, once land designated Employment Areas is converted, it is unlikely that these lands will be developed for significant future industrial, manufacturing or distribution employment activity.
