The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends City Council approval for staff to create 23 new transitional and supportive housing opportunities at 1430 Gerrard Street East (the "Property") for a minimum period of five years, by way of extending the current two-year nominal rent sublease agreement with WoodGreen Community Housing Inc. ("WoodGreen") for an additional three years. In addition, this report recommends City Council approval to exempt property taxes for the duration of the sublease term which will help deepen affordability for future residents. The Property is currently leased by the City and was previously operated as a temporary shelter location for the Red Door Shelter which moved into its new permanent location in June 2021.
