Dundas-Sherbourne Neighbourhood Revitalization - Update

AdoptedMay 11, 2022 · Toronto City Council

This is an information item: the committee receives an update on the Dundas-Sherbourne neighbourhood revitalization around 251 Sherbourne Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Given the persistent, systemic and interconnected housing, health, social, economic and safety challenges faced by many residents of 251 Sherbourne Street ("Dan Harrison Community Complex") and the wider Dundas-Sherbourne community, on March 10, 2021, City Council adopted Item PH21.2 and directed the City, Toronto Community Housing ("TCHC") and CreateTO staff to report back to the Planning and Housing Committee with a proposed plan to improve the built-form and the operations of the Dan Harrison Community Complex and to create new supportive housing opportunities to better meet the needs of existing and future residents. Through Item PH21.2 , staff were also directed to examine opportunities to fund the acquisition of the properties at 214, 218, 220, 222, 224, 226 and 230 Sherbourne Street, should they become available for purchase.
