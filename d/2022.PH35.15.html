The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends adoption of Official Plan Amendment 591 (OPA 591) that amends the Official Plan's economic health and Employment Area policies contained in Chapters 2, 3 and 4 as part of the phased Municipal Comprehensive Review (MCR) and Growth Plan Conformity Exercise named "Our Plan Toronto". This report also recommends adoption of several site or area specific Official Plan policies resulting from local area studies and review of Official Plan Amendment applications and conversion requests that propose the introduction of uses that are not current permitted, including residential uses, on lands designated either Core Employment Areas or General Employment Areas in the Official Plan.
