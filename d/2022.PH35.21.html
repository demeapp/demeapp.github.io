What the item proposed

Increasing the supply of affordable and supportive housing is one of the key objectives of the HousingTO 2020-2030 Action Plan ("HousingTO Plan"). The City continues to move rapidly to implement or advance a number of key programs, projects and initiatives to scale up the supply of a range of new homes in all areas across Toronto. Concurrently, there is a keen focus on maintaining our existing supply of homes and taking action to improve housing conditions for current and future residents. This report recommends City Council approval for staff to take a number of actions over the upcoming months to advance these objectives. Specifically, this report recommends Council approval to enter into long-term leases/sub-leases with non-profit housing providers to operate a number of new affordable and supportive housing sites.
