This is an information item: the 2SLGBTQ+ Advisory Committee hears an update on library and facility use policies.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Director, Equity and Human Rights and the City Librarian, will provide an update to the Committee on the recommendations in Item EX26.14, Reviewing City of Toronto Policies Governing the Use of Libraries and City Facilities by Individuals and Groups Promoting Intolerance or Discrimination.

What was decided

The Two-Spirit, Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender and Queer Advisory Committee recommends that: 1. City Council request the Toronto Public Library Board to request the City Librarian, in consultation with the Director, Equity and Human Rights, to explore opportunities to expand upon the Toronto Public Library trans affirming care benefit package to other City of Toronto departments. 2. City Council request the Toronto Public Library Board to include in their 2023 budget submission, an increase to the Toronto Public Library budget for improvements to 2SLGBTQI collections and a Rainbow Presence in every library in the Toronto Public Library system.
