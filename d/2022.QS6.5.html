Toronto City Council · Decision

The 2SLGBTQ+ Advisory Committee asks Council to study creating a dedicated City unit for 2SLGBTQ+ issues.

Official title: Feasibility of Establishing a City of Toronto Unit Responsible for 2SLGBTQ+ Issues

AdoptedMay 19, 2022 · Toronto City Council

A citywide decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Currently, many of the equity-deserving groups included in the Equity Lens Toolkit that was presented to our Committee have a dedicated Unit or Office (e.g., Indigenous Affairs Office, Gender Equity Unit, Confronting Anti-Black Racism Unit); however, this appears to be lacking with respect to 2SLGBTQ+ communities. Having dedicated staff support is necessary to: a. enable follow through on the recommendations made by our Committee; b. ensure that individual 2SLGBTQ+-identified City employees are not informally called upon to do the work of representing 2SLGBTQ+ communities; c. allow for collaboration with other equity Units and Offices to allow for an intersectional approach to issues that impact diversely situated members of our communities; and d. support sustainability of 2SLGBTQ+-related initiatives, for example, through changes in government.

What was decided

The Two-Spirit, Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender and Queer Advisory Committee recommend that: 1. City Council request the City Manager to investigate the feasibility of having a dedicated City of Toronto Unit responsible for 2SLGBTQ+ issues, and report their findings back to either the Two-Spirit, Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender and Queer Advisory Committee or the Executive Committee, as appropriate, by the second quarter of 2023.
