The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of January 20, 2020, City Council adopted EX12.6 which directed CreateTO, in consultation with TO Live, to prepare a building program, Class D cost estimate, business model and funding strategy for a reimagined St. Lawrence Centre for the Arts ("STLC") a civic asset, managed by TO Live, located at 27 Front Street East in the heart of the St. Lawrence Neighbourhood. The purpose of this report is to provide the Board of Directors of CreateTO (the "Board") with an update on the work, directed by City Council and recommendations for next steps. The report will also be considered by the TO Live St. Lawrence Centre for the Arts Redevelopment Board Committee and the TO Live Board on April 5 and April 14 respectively.
