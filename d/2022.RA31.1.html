Toronto City Council · Decision

The CreateTO Board was asked to make the work plan update a quarterly report.

Official title: Quarterly CreateTO Work Plan - Update

AdoptedMay 17, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report will replace the regular report on action items arising out of prior meetings of the CreateTO Board of Directors and new matters that City Council has directed CreateTO to become involved in. This new report will provide the Board of Directors a quarterly update on progress towards the objectives in the CreateTO 2022 Work Plan and a full CreateTO Projects List.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Directed the Chief Executive Officer to report back on progress towards the objectives outlined in the CreateTO 2022 Work Plan at the end of each quarter of the year. 2. Directed that the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Attachment 2 to the report (May 2, 2022) from the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO remain confidential in their entirety, as they contain information that deals with a position, plan procedure, criteria or instruction to be applied to negotiations to be carried on by or on behalf of the City and/or the Board of Directors of CreateTO, and they deal with proposed or pending acquisitions or dispositions of land by the City and/or the Board of Directors of CreateTO.
