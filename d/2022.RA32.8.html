What the item proposed

On June 8, 2021, City Council adopted item MM34.6 - "Exploring Development Opportunities at 1169 Weston Road and 4 Hollis Street" and requested CreateTO, in consultation with City staff and the Toronto Parking Authority, to explore the development opportunities of the City-owned real estate assets known as 1169 Weston Road and 4 Hollis Street. What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Authorized the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO to negotiate a conditional term sheet and conditional agreement of purchase and sale of the City-owned Property at 1169 Weston Road, with the adjacent Private Landowner based on the strategic plan outlined in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (June 6, 2022) from the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO; and report back to the Board of Directors of CreateTO on the outcomes of these negotiations by the first quarter 2023. 2.
