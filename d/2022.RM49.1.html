What was decided

CALL TO ORDER: 1:52 p.m. Speaker Nunziata took the Chair and called the Members to order. Speaker Nunziata advised Members that as Council has not yet completed Meeting 48, Meeting 49 is now recessed until the completion of Meeting 48. Speaker Nunziata reconvened Meeting 49 at 2:03 p.m. and proposed that Council dispense with the National Anthem and the Moment of Silence, Council having done them in the morning meeting. Speaker Nunziata gratefully acknowledged that City Council was meeting on the traditional territory of many nations including the Mississaugas of the Credit, the Anishnabeg, the Chippewa, the Haudenasaunee and the Wendat peoples and is now home to many diverse First Nations, Inuit and Métis peoples.
