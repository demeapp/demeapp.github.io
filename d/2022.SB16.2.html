Application for Seven Variances, each subject to three Conditions, Respecting One Third Party Electronic Ground Sign - 476 Evans Avenue

Adopted with changesApr 1, 2022 · Toronto City Council

The Sign Variance Committee decides whether to grant seven variances for an electronic sign at 476 Evans Avenue, which staff recommend refusing.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Xmedia (the "Applicant") has stated that they have been authorized by the owner of the property municipally known as 476 Evans Avenue (the "Subject Premises"), to apply for seven variances, each subject to three conditions, required to allow for a permit to be issued for one third party electronic ground sign with two sign faces, displaying electronic static copy (the "Proposed Sign") at 476 Evans Avenue. The Subject Premises is a relatively unique location within the City, as it is one of the few locations that is subject to more than one of the 74 separate area specific restrictions enacted by City Council, which specifically prohibit any third party signs from being erected on the premises. What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Refused to grant any of the seven requested variances to sections 694-24.A(1), 694-24.A(17), 694-25.C(2)(a), 694-25.C(2)(b), 694- 25.C(2)(d), § 694-25.C(2)(f), and § 694-25.C(2)(h), each subject to conditions, which would be required to allow for the issuance of a permit respecting the erection and display of the proposed Sign at the property municipally known as 476 Evans Avenue as described in Attachment 1 to this report (March 9, 2022) from the Manager, Sign By-Law Unit, Toronto Building, and such additional conditions included in April 1, 2022, Presentation from Rav Banwait, Xmedia Outdoor.
