Appeal to Sign Variance Decision Respecting Two First Party Wall Signs (21-172811): 3410 Sheppard Avenue East

Adopted with changesApr 1, 2022 · Toronto City Council

The Sign Variance Committee hears an appeal of a refused sign variance for two wall signs on an office building at 3410 Sheppard Avenue East.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Zip Signs Ltd. (the "Appellant") has stated that they have been authorized by both Desjardins Financial Security (the "Sign Owner") and the owner of the property municipally known as 3410 Sheppard Avenue East (the "Subject Premises"), to appeal a refusal decision made to a Sign Variance application (21–172811). The application required one variance from the Sign By-law in order to allow for permits to be issued for each of the two first party wall signs (the "Proposed Signs") to be erected and displayed above the second storey of an office building. The Proposed Signs would be erected on the fourth storey of the building on the Subject Premises. One on the east elevation ("Proposed Sign East") and one on the west elevation ("Proposed Sign West"). What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Granted the requested variances to section 694-21.D(5), required to allow for the issuance of a permit respecting the erection and display of each of the Proposed Signs at the property municipally known as 3410 Sheppard Avenue East as described in Attachment 1 to the report (March 11, 2022) from the Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building; and further that the sign be constructed with illuminated channel letters on a non-illuminated background.
