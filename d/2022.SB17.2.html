Staff recommend refusing sign variances for an illuminated wall sign at 2 St. Clair Avenue East.

Official title: Application for Three Variances Respecting One Third Party Illuminated Wall Sign Displaying Static Copy - 2 St. Clair Avenue East

The official record

What the item proposed

Norm Girdhar (the "Applicant") has stated that they have been authorized by the owner of the property municipally known as 2 St. Clair Avenue East (the "Subject Premises"), to apply for three variances, required to allow for a permit to be issued for one third party illuminated wall sign with a single sign face, displaying static copy (the "Proposed Sign") at 2 St. Clair Avenue East. The Subject Premises is a relatively unique location within the City, as it is one of the few locations that is subject to one of the 74 separate area specific restrictions enacted by City Council, which specifically prohibits any third party signs from being erected within 100 metres of St. Clair Avenue between Bathurst Street and Inglewood Drive. The Proposed Sign will be approximately 60 metres north of St. Clair Avenue. The Proposed Sign would be located at 9th to 13th storey of north elevation of the…

What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Refused to grant any of the three requested variances to Sections 694-24.A(26), 694-25.B(1)(a) and 694-25.B(1)(b), which would be required to allow for the issuance of a permit respecting the erection and display of the Proposed Sign at the property municipally known as 2 St. Clair Avenue East as described in Attachment 1 of the report (May 19, 2022) from the Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building .
