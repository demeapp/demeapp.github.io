Staff recommend granting sign variances for a Metrolinx electronic ground sign north of Ellesmere Road.

Official title: Application for Two Variances, Each Subject to Four Conditions, Respecting One Third Party Electronic Ground Sign - Belleville Subdivision, North of Ellesmere Road, East of Warden Avenue

Adopted with changesJun 3, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Metrolinx (the "Applicant") has made an application for two variances (the "Subject Premises"), subject to four conditions, required for a permit to be issued to allow the erection and display of one third party electronic ground sign displaying electronic static copy (the "Proposed Sign") to be located on a specific portion of the premises defined by the Sign By-law as the Belleville Subdivision, north of Ellesmere Road and east of Warden Avenue (the "Proposed Location"), as further described in Attachment 1. The Proposed Location is a portion of the Belleville Subdivision, an area which functions as a railway corridor, and is a Utility ("U") Sign District, which allows third party electronic ground signs. The Proposed Sign would have a height of 10 metres from the grade, and would contain one rectangular sign face, facing to the east and measuring 5.79 metres horizontally by 2.90…

What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Granted the requested variances to sections 694- 18E(5), 694- 25D(3)(f) and 694-25D(3)(g) each subject to conditions, as required to allow for the issuance of a permit respecting the erection and display of the Proposed Sign on the Belleville Subdivision, North of Ellesmere Road, East of Warden Avenue, as further described in Attachment 1 to the report (May 19, 2022) from the Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building, and as amended below: 4. That the third party electronic ground sign which would be no closer than 295.0 metres to the Proposed Sign is located on Warden Avenue - which is a street which forms an intersection with the street (Ellesmere Road) on which the Proposed Sign is located.
