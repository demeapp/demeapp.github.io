Staff recommend granting sign variances for a Metrolinx electronic ground sign at 1416 Warden Avenue.

Official title: Application for Three Variances, Each Subject to Conditions, Respecting One Third Party Electronic Ground Sign - 1416 Warden Avenue

The official record

What the item proposed

Metrolinx (the "Applicant") has made an application for three variances (the "Subject Premises") each subject to five conditions, for a permit to be issued to allow the erection and display of one third party electronic ground sign displaying electronic static copy (the "Proposed Sign") to be located on the premises known as 1416 Warden Avenue (the "Subject Premises"). The Subject Premises is within a Utility ("U") Sign District, which allows third party electronic ground signs. The Proposed Sign will have a height of 10 metres from the grade, and will contain two rectangular sign faces in a back to back configuration and northerly – southerly direction. Both sign faces are measuring 5.8 metres horizontally by 2.90 metres vertically, with a sign face area of 16.85 square metres. The Proposed Sign is described further in Attachment 1. What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Granted the requested variances to sections 694- 18E(5), 694- 25D(3)(f) and 694-25D(3)(g) each subject to conditions, as required to allow for the issuance of a permit respecting the erection and display of the Proposed Sign at 1416 Warden Avenue, as further described in Attachment 1 to the report (May 19,2022) from the Manager, Sign By-law Unit, Toronto Building, and as amended below: That condition 5 of Attachment 1 be replaced with the following: 5. That the third party electronic ground sign which would be no closer than 295.0 metres to the Proposed Sign is located on Ellesmere Road - which is a street which forms an intersection with the street (Warden Avenue) on which the Proposed Sign is located.
