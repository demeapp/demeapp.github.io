Councillor McKelvie asks staff to study moving the school crosswalk on Generation Boulevard.

Official title: Relocation of School Crossing - Generation Boulevard

AdoptedJan 10, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In response to concerns brought to my attention by members of the community, I am bringing forward a motion to Scarborough Community Council to ask Transportation Services staff to conduct a study to relocate the existing pedestrian crosswalk location by Chief Dan George Public School (GR. JK-08) on Generation Boulevard, and look into the feasibility of having a crossing guard at the new location. This motion asks that staff report back by June 2022. I have heard from the parents of students at Chief Dan George PS and the school Principal about the current placement of the crosswalk. The crosswalk is set between the driveway of the school parking lot and the boulevard curb of 180 Generation Blvd. Currently there is no crossing guard and the signage and lines of the marked pedestrian crossing create a false sense of confidence on the part of pedestrians who may enter the crosswalk…

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services to review the location of the crosswalk located fronting Chief Dan George Public School, 185 Generation Boulevard, and to report back to the June 30, 2022 meeting of Scarborough Community Council with recommendations for the relocation of the crosswalk to a safer location and the feasibility of having a School Crossing Guard at the new location.
