Councillor Lai asks the community council to relocate a school bus loading zone on Heather Road.

Official title: School Bus Loading - Heather Road

AdoptedJan 10, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The location of an existing school bus loading zone (SBLZ) on Heather Road requires a slight relocation to accommodate a new school driveway at 51 Heather Road, in front of St. Bartholomew Catholic School. We now need to formalize the relocation of the SBZL in the traffic by-law and permanent signs will be installed. The current by-law states the SBLZ is in effect on the south side of Heather Road, between a point 105 metres west of Brimley Road and a point 38 metres further west. The by-law should be amended to be in effect on the south side of Heather Road, between a point 127 metres west of Brimley Road and a point 36 metres further west.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Amended the existing school bus loading zone in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on the south side of Heather Road, between a point 105 metres west of Brimley Road and a point 38 metres further west, to be in effect between a point 127 metres west of Brimley Road and a point 36 metres further west.
