Staff ask to add overnight permit parking on Linton Avenue.

Official title: Introduction of Overnight On-Street Permit Parking - Linton Avenue

AdoptedFeb 25, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that overnight on-street permit parking be introduced on Linton Avenue, between Victoria Park Avenue and Blantyre Avenue from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., daily. Residents requested the implementation of overnight on-street permit parking on Linton Avenue to accommodate their long-term parking needs.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized parking by permit only on a street name basis, from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily on both sides of Linton Avenue, between Victoria Park Avenue and Blantyre Avenue.
