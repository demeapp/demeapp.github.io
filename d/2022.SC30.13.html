Councillor McKelvie asks for safety improvements at Steeles Avenue East and Reesor Road.

Official title: Improving Road Safety at Steeles Avenue East and Reesor Road

AdoptedFeb 25, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In response to safety concerns raised by residents, and municipal colleagues at the City of Markham, I am requesting that Transportation Services staff evaluate options to improve road safety at Steeles Avenue East and Reesor Road. I ask that staff report their findings to the June 2022 Scarborough Community Council meeting, and share their findings with the City of Markham so that we can work collaboratively to implement the proposed safety improvements.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services to evaluate measures to improve road safety, including the installation of a traffic control signal and/or other intersection improvements to address sightlines and safety of road users at the intersection of Steeles Avenue East and Reesor Road and report back to Scarborough Community Council at its meeting on June 30, 2022. 2. Requested the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services to undertake best efforts to have City of Markham share the capital cost of installing traffic control signals and/or other intersection improvements at the intersection of Steeles Avenue East and Reesor Road and report back to Scarborough Community Council at it's meeting on June 30, 2022.
