Councillor Crawford asks to extend a parking ban on Parkland Road for school safety.

Official title: Parking By-law Amendment - Parkland Road

AdoptedFeb 25, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Parkland Road is a residential street without sidewalks that runs between Clonmore Drive and Blantyre Avenue. It is heavily used by pedestrians as a route to Blantyre Public School. Local residents have expressed concerns over the years with regard to traffic and pedestrian safety on this street, especially at the beginning and end of school days. The speed limit will be reduced to 30km/hr in the future, and my office is in the process of investigating the feasibility of sidewalk installation to improve safety. That being said, after meeting onsite with residents, it is clear that the need for effective safety measures is more immediate. Recently there was a near miss when a student stepped out from between 2 parked cars to cross the street. Speed was not a factor, and thankfully she was fine. What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, on the north side of Parkland Road between North Drive and a point 30.6 metres west of North Drive to be in effect between Blantyre Avenue and Clonmore Drive.
