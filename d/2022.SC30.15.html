Toronto City Council · Decision

Councillor Crawford asks for school-hour stopping bans on Cathedral Bluffs Drive.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Cathedral Bluffs Drive

AdoptedFeb 25, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

St Agatha Catholic School is located on Cathedral Bluffs Drive, which is a collector road running between Sloley Road and Kingston Road. In order to ensure the safety of the students entering and exiting the school at peak hours, I along with the local community, Parent Council and school administration, am seeking measures to reduce traffic congestion in front of the school during peak hours. A stopping prohibition on the west side of the street opposite the school will help to achieve this objective. During the course of the investigation, Transportation Services staff noted a discrepancy between posted signs and the existing parking regulations. Recommendation 2 will correct this inconsistency.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Prohibited stopping from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Cathedral Bluffs Drive, between a point 38 metres south of Earl Road and a point 63 metres further south. 2. Amended the existing stopping prohibition in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Cathedral Bluffs, between a point 120 metres south of Kingston Road and a point 70.2 metres further south, to be in effect on the east side of Cathedral Bluffs Drive, between a point 247 metres south of Kingston Road and a point 43 metres further south.
