Staff ask for a community consultation meeting on a proposed 93-unit building at Kingston Road.

Official title: Preliminary Report - 1552, 1554, 1562, 1564, 1570 and 1572 Kingston Road - Zoning By-law Amendment Application

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information and identifies a preliminary set of issues regarding the application to amend City-wide Zoning By-law No. 569-2013 and the Birchcliff Community Zoning By-law 8786, as amended, for the lands municipally known as 1552, 1554, 1562, 1564, 1570 and 1572 Kingston Road. The application proposes an 8-storey residential building (27.3 metres in height, inclusive of the mechanical penthouse and habitable 8th floor amenity space) containing 93 units. The proposed 7,511 square metres of gross floor area results in an Floor Space Index (FSI) of 3.88 times the area of the lot. Staff are currently reviewing the application. It has been circulated to all appropriate agencies and City divisions for comment. Staff will proceed to schedule a community consultation meeting for the application with the Ward Councillor.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council directed that: 1. Staff schedule a community consultation meeting for the application located at 1552, 1554, 1562, 1564, 1570 and 1572 Kingston Road together with the Ward Councillor. 2. Staff provide notice for the community consultation meeting to landowners and residents within the area bounded by Warden Avenue to the west, Birchmount Road to the east, Hollis Avenue to the north and the lakefront to the south, and to additional residents, institutions and owners to be determined in consultation with the Ward Councillor, with any additional mailing costs to be borne by the applicant.
