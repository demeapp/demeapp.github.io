The official record

What the item proposed

Dear Scarborough Community Council, With traffic and pedestrian safety being of utmost importance, over the years I have consistently advocated for safety to be prioritized by directing Transportation Services staff to conduct the necessary studies and to follow up with the necessary changes required to provide a safer environment for residents. Measures have been initiated along Clonmore Drive and Gerrard Street East, from Warden Avenue to Victoria Park Avenue, to improve traffic and pedestrian safety. Conditions continue to be assessed to determine additional improvements that will be part of future development in the area. What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services, in consultation with the Ward Councillor, to review and report back to Scarborough Community Council on the need for an additional protected pedestrian crossing on Gerrard Street East between Clonmore Drive and Blantyre Avenue, associated with the development at 411 Victoria Park Avenue and 2510 and 2530 Gerrard Street East. The review is to be undertaken upon completion of the future public park fronting on to Gerrard Street East between Clonmore Drive and Blantyre Avenue.
