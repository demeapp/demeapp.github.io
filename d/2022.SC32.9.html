Toronto City Council · Decision

Community Council was asked to install speed humps on Janet Boulevard to curb speeding.

Official title: Traffic Calming - Janet Boulevard

AdoptedMay 27, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Speeding and not stopping at the Stop sign has created a dangerous situation on Janet Boulevard. Toronto Police have regularly enforced both offences. Numerous tickets have been issued but speeding and not stopping at the stop sign continue. I am very concerned for the safety of residents specifically children and seniors. I hosted a community consultation meeting, TPS reported that numerous tickets were issued, and residents expressed their concerns relating to community safety. The installation of speed humps is one of the tools to assist with discouraging speeding. Based on the numerous tickets issued and community safety concerns, I am requesting an expedited installation of speed humps on Janet Blvd.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Janet Boulevard, between Victoria Park Avenue and Pharmacy Avenue. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway on Janet Boulevard, between Victoria Park Avenue and Pharmacy Avenue, to install six speed humps, generally as shown on the copy of 'Drawing No. 421G-4026' dated April 2022 from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services attached to the letter (May 6, 2022) from Councillor Michael Thompson, Ward 21, Scarborough Centre.
