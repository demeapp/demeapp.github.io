Scarborough Community Council was asked to send a proposed 440-home, 44-storey tower at 1151 Markham Road to a community meeting.

Official title: 1151 Markham Road - Zoning Amendment Application - Preliminary Report

AdoptedJun 30, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information and identifies a preliminary set of issues regarding the application for the site located at 1151 Markham Road. The application proposes to amend the Zoning By-law to permit a 44-storey building with 440 dwelling units and 223 square meters of retail gross floor area. The proposed gross floor area is approximately 31,348.9 square metres resulting in a Floor Space Index of 15.89 times the area of the lot. A total of 149 vehicular parking spaces and 367 bicycle parking spaces are proposed. The proposal also contemplates a parkland dedication. Staff are currently reviewing the application. It has been circulated to all appropriate agencies and City divisions for comment. Staff will proceed to schedule a community consultation meeting for the application with the Ward Councillor.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council directed that: 1. Staff schedule a community consultation meeting for the application located at 1151 Markham Road together with the Ward Councillor. 2. Staff provide notice for the community consultation meeting to landowners and residents within 120 metres of the application site, and to additional residents, institutions and owners to be determined in consultation with the Ward Councillor, with any additional mailing costs to be borne by the applicant.
