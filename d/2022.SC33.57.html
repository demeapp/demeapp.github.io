The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report requests that Scarborough Community Council deny the request for a permit to remove one privately owned tree located on the boundary line between the properties of 18 Rodarick Drive and 20 Rodarick Drive. The application indicates the reason for removal is due to safety related to falling branches, and the roots of the tree damaging the driveway. The subject tree is a Norway maple tree (Acer platanoides), measuring 77.5 cm in diameter. The Tree By-laws do not support the removal of this tree as it is healthy and maintainable.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Approved the request for a permit to remove one privately owned tree located on the boundary line between the properties of 18 and 20 Rodarick Drive, and required the applicant to provide five replacement trees which can be achieved in a combination of on-site planting and cash-in-lieu of planting to the satisfaction of the General Manager of Parks, Forestry and Recreation.
