What the item proposed

At its June 17, 2022 meeting, the TAF Grants and Programs Committee recommended that the Board approve four grants with a combined value of $478,000 and one internal program allocation with a total value of $90,000. This report provides recommendations and minutes from the Committee meeting.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Approved the following grants to be funded jointly from the relevant endowment(s) indicated: a. $150,000 over 12 months to Hamilton Community Enterprises to complete a comprehensive report on the feasibility of a low-carbon thermal corridor that will enable large-scale carbon reductions through the decarbonization of heating in new and existing buildings in Hamilton, subject to the condition set out by the Committee that HCE provide written confirmation at least $150,000 in additional funding from external sources has been secured (funded from the Canada and Ontario endowments). b. $100,000 over 12 months to Pembina Institute to coordinate dialogue and knowledge sharing between organizations working on promoting deep retrofits and the government (funded from the Canada, Ontario, and Toronto endowments). c. $50,000 over 10 months…
