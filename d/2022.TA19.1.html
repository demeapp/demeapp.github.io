The Atmospheric Fund board was asked to approve a revised intercreditor agreement for a direct investment.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Direct Investment Committee is recommending the Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund approve entering into a revised Intercreditor Agreement based on negotiations demonstrating the best potential outcome for risk-adjusted return for TAF. The Direct Investment Committee is also recommending the Intercreditor Agreement be conditional upon a side letter specifying TAF receives access to all information and reporting available to all investors regardless of investment seniority.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Directed the Chief Executive Officer to implement a revised Intercreditor Agreement for the direct investment as set out in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (August 10, 2022) from the Director, Impact Investing, Toronto Atmospheric Fund, to the satisfaction of Toronto Atmospheric Fund's Solicitor. 2. Directed the Chief Executive Officer to enter into a side letter for the direct investment set out in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (August 10, 2022) from the Director, Impact Investing, Toronto Atmospheric Fund, specifying Toronto Atmospheric Fund receives access to all information and reporting available to any other investor, regardless of seniority. 3.
