Councillor Fletcher asks staff to report on the zoning status of the rented-out soccer field at 49 Felstead Avenue.

Official title: 49 Felstead Avenue - Zoning Matter

AdoptedJan 6, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I hope to have your support in getting to the bottom of an issue that has troubled many of my residents for over three years. The soccer field at the TCDSB St. Patrick's School, at 49 Felstead Avenue has been licensed/leased to a commercial enterprise who rents out the field to individuals, groups and sports leagues. This field backs directly onto the houses all along the north side of Torbrick Road with most separated from the field by only a few metres. Additionally there is a seniors residence immediately west of the field. Partially due to the proximity of the field to the homes and backyards in this Old Riverdale neighbourhood and partially due to the level and duration of activity on the field the impacts on local residents are considerable and they date back to summer of 2018. They include: • Permitting field use to 11 p.m. rather than the 9 pm cut off for other TCDSB fields •…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the Deputy Chief Building Official, the City Solicitor, the Director of Investigation Services, Municipal Licensing and Standards, and the Acting Director, Zoning to report to the February 16, 2022 meeting of the Toronto and East York Community Council on the current zoning status of 49 Felstead Avenue and what actions have been taken by the city between 2018 and 2021 to ensure compliance, and what further action the city can take to bring any non-compliant zoning elements into compliance.
