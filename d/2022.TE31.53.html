What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Church Street Limited Partnership is constructing a 39-storey mixed-use development at 191-201 Church Street. The site is located on the east side of Church Street, between Shuter Street and Dundas Street. A portion of the southbound curb lane and west sidewalk on Dalhousie Street is currently closed to accommodate construction staging operations for the development. Toronto and East York Community Council, at its meeting on February 24, 2021, approved the subject construction staging area on Dalhousie Street from March 1, 2021 to March 31, 2022. At the time, the developer indicated they would require the staging area for a total of 45 months, from March 1, 2021 to November 30, 2024.
