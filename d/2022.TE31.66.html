The official record

What the item proposed

The Board confirms that each of these nominees is qualified to serve under the provisions of the City of Toronto's Public Appointments Policy and the Relationship Framework for Community Centre Boards of Management. These appointments are a result of the following public member's terms coming to an end: - Jennifer Benedetti, resigned effective September 13,2021,

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Appointed the following candidates to the Central Eglinton Community Centre Board of Management, at pleasure of Council, for the terms of office noted below and until successors are appointed: - Elizabeth Reid, for a term of office ending June 27, 2023. - Eric Saarvala, for a term of office ending June 27, 2023.
