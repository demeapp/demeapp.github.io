The official record

What the item proposed

371 Bloor Street West is located on the south side of Bloor Street West, between Spadina Avenue and Huron Street and is owned and also occupied by the University of Toronto (U of T). The proposal from the owner is to renovate and expand the east portion of the building at Huron Street currently occupied by University of Toronto Schools (UTS) to modernize their facilities and to accommodate a new gymnasium and fitness centre, auditorium and three storeys of classroom space. The expansion represents an increase of approximately 3,400 square metres in gross floor area (GFA), and will include building on a portion of the existing public lane connecting to Washington Avenue.
