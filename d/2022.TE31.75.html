The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek City Council direction on two requests received by the City Clerk's Office for municipal endorsement for temporary liquor licence extensions that do not meet the criteria of the delegated authority.

What was decided

City Council on March 9, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council endorse the application for extensions to licensed hours or areas for the establishments as listed in the chart below with the dates for the Do West Fest changed to June 3, 4, and 5. 2. City Council direct the City Clerk to issue letters of municipal significance and non-objection to the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario for the applications in Part 1 above.
