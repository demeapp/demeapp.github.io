Toronto City Council · Decision

Councillor Perks asks to fix parking rules at the Parkdale Road and Glendale Avenue intersection.

Official title: Amendments to the existing parking regulation on Parkdale Road at Glendale Avenue

AdoptedFeb 16, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The intersection of Parkdale Road and Glendale Avenue is located in the Sunnyside neighbourhood in Ward 4. During a neighbourhood walk with residents, it was noted that a vehicle could be park in the intersection of Parkdale Road and Glendale Avenue due to current parking regulations. Transportation Staff did a review of the intersection and recommend changes to the parking regulation to improve the safety and visibility of the intersection.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on both sides of Parkdale Road, between Indian Road and Glendale Avenue (south intersection). 2. Authorized parking for a maximum period of one hour from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on the south side of Parkdale Road, between Indian Road and Glendale Avenue (south intersection). 3. Authorized parking for a maximum period of one hour from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on the north side of Parkdale Road, between Indian Road and a point 160 metres east. 4. Prohibited parking at all times on the north side of Parkdale Road from a point 160 metres east of Indian Road to a point 24.8 metres further east.
