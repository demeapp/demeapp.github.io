What the item proposed

A staff report was presented and adopted at the September 9, 2021 Toronto and East York Community Council meeting entitled Parking Machines - Various Streets Intersecting Bloor Street West TE27.38. Subsequent to the above presentation and approval, a technical error has been discovered (see attachment 2 and 3 of the report) and requires correction.
