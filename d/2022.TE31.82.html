Councillor Matlow asks to install speed humps on Gormley Avenue.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to request your support in authorizing the installation of speed humps on Gormley Ave, between Oriole Parkway and Lascelles Boulevard, to enhance safety on the street. A preponderance of residents on Gormley Ave petitioned my office for speed humps, as a way of mitigating reckless speeding and irresponsible driver behaviour on their street. A lot of residents have personally recounted "close shaves" with their kids as a result of reckless speeding on Gormley. In addition, adjacent streets to Gormley have speed humps. As such, drivers tend to avoid those streets and instead use Gormley and that comes with the attendant safety hazards. Traffic Operations staff conducted a traffic study, with all the warrants being met except two. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Gormley Avenue, between Oriole Parkway and Lascelles Boulevard. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway on Gormley Avenue, between Oriole Parkway and Lascelles Boulevard, to install three speed humps, generally as shown on the copy of 'Drawing No. 421G-3989' dated January 2022 from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
