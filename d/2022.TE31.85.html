Councillor Wong-Tam asks for speed bumps in Reverend Porter Lane to protect pedestrians.

Official title: Updating Signage to Ensure Pedestrian and Public Safety on Reverend Porter Lane

AdoptedFeb 16, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Reverend Porter lane is a one way northbound lane, from Carleton Street to Wood Street. Pedestrians using the sidewalk along Wood Street are constantly at risk of being hit by a vehicle exiting Reverend Porter Lane due to a lack of safety features for both motorists and pedestrians at the mouth of the lane’s exit on Wood Street. My office has consulted with Transportation Staff about the feasibility of adding new road safety features. The installation of these mechanisms will improve pedestrian safety along this stretch of Wood Street.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of speed bumps in Reverend Porter Lane, between Carlton Street and Wood Street, at the locations shown on Drawing 421G-3987, dated January 2022, from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District. 2. Authorized a compulsory stop control for northbound traffic on Reverend Porter Lane at Wood Street.
