Councillor Bailão asks to install speed bumps on Cosmos Nature Lane.

Official title: Speed Bump Installation on Cosmos Nature Lane

AdoptedFeb 16, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In February 2021, residents reached out to my office with an urgent concern about speeding on the north-south segment of Cosmo Nature Lane near Susan Tibaldi Parkette. In November of last year, residents requested via petition that traffic calming measures (speed bumps) be installed as a method of addressing these concerns. Of the residents that responded, there was unanimous support for making this change. I am requesting that speed bumps be installed on Cosmos Nature Lane.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of speed bumps in Cosmos Nature Lane, bounded by Bloor Street West, Margueretta Street, Wallace Avenue and Brock Avenue, as shown on Drawing 421G-3992, dated February 2022, from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
