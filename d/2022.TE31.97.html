Councillor Bailão asks to install a speed hump on Stonehouse Crescent.

Official title: Speed Hump Installation on Stonehouse Crescent

AdoptedFeb 16, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In September 2020, residents reached out to my office with an urgent concern about speeding along their street and requested via an online survey that traffic calming measures (speed humps) be installed as a method of addressing these concerns. Of those that responded, a strong majority were in support of making this change. I am requesting that speed humps be installed on Stonehouse Crescent Avenue from Dufferin Street to Gladstone Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed hump) on Stonehouse Crescent, between Dufferin Street and Gladstone Avenue. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter a section of the roadway on Stonehouse Crescent, between Dufferin Street and Gladstone Avenue, to install one speed hump, generally as shown on the copy of 'Drawing No. 421G-3991' dated February 2022 from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
