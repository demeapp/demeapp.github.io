Stopping Prohibition - 192 Jarvis Street

AdoptedApr 21, 2022 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to prohibit stopping on Jarvis Street near 192 Jarvis Street after repeat driveway blockages.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office was advised about repeat parking and illegal stopping situation at the driveway of 192 Jarvis that is seriously impacting the residents of this building. Customers from J San Sushi, the adjacent restaurant, are constantly parking their cars in front of this specific driveway to go pick up their takeout. As a result, residents from 192 Jarvis are unable to access their residential building because vehicles are blocking the driveway. Residents report that they must wait for extended periods of time until they are able to enter the building as it is dependent upon vehicles moving from the driveway. Even more problematic is the fact that residents are forced to ask customers and Uber drivers who are parked or stopped in front of the driveway to move their vehicles. Prohibited stopping at all times on the west side of Jarvis Street, between a point 58 metres south of Dundas Street East and a point 19 metres further south. 2.
