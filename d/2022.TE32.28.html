101 - 105 Spadina Avenue and 363 Adelaide Street West - Zoning Amendment Application - Preliminary Report

This is an information item: Toronto and East York Community Council gets a preliminary look at a 39-storey, 394-unit proposal with a new city park for 101-105 Spadina Avenue.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information and identifies a preliminary set of issues regarding the Zoning By-law Amendment application for a 39-storey mixed-use building with commercial uses on the ground floor and 394 residential dwelling units above. A new 1,000 square metre City park is proposed on the northern portion of the site at the southeast corner of Spadina Avenue and Adelaide Street West with a public parking garage beneath. A total of 70 of the 149 parking spaces proposed are to be owned and operated by the Toronto Parking Authority (TPA). The application has been circulated to all appropriate City divisions and public agencies for comment. In consultation with the Ward Councillor, City Planning staff have scheduled a Community Consultation Meeting for the application on April 4, 2022.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Received the report (March 28, 2022) from the Director, Community Planning, Toronto and East York District, for information.
