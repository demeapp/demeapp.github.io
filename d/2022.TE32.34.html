906 Yonge Street and 25 McMurrich Street - Zoning By-law Amendment Application - Preliminary Report

AdoptedApr 21, 2022 · Toronto City Council

This is an information item: Toronto and East York Community Council gets a preliminary look at a two-tower mixed-use proposal for 906 Yonge Street and 25 McMurrich Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information and identifies a preliminary set of issues regarding the Zoning By-law Amendment application for a mixed-use complex with two tall buildings connected by a 2-storey base building. The tall building fronting Yonge Street is 33 storeys, containing 213 dwelling units and 75 square metres of grade-related retail space. The tall building fronting McMurrich Street is 19 storeys containing 188 dwelling units. Staff are currently reviewing the application. It has been circulated to all appropriate agencies and City divisions for comment. Together with the Ward Councillor, City Planning staff held a Community Consultation Meeting on February 1, 2022.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Endorsed the arrangements made between City Planning staff and the applicant to give notice for the February 1, 2022, Community Consultation Meeting to landowners and residents within 120 metres of the site, and to additional residents, institutions and owners as was determined in consultation with the Ward Councillor, with any additional mailing costs to be borne by the applicant.
