1233 Yonge Street and 9 Woodlawn Avenue East - Official Plan Amendment, Zoning By-law Amendment, and Rental Housing Demolition Applications - Preliminary Report

AdoptedApr 21, 2022 · Toronto City Council

This is an information item: Toronto and East York Community Council gets a preliminary look at a 13-storey proposal involving 40 rental units at 1233 Yonge Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information and identifies a preliminary set of issues regarding the Official Plan Amendment, Zoning By-law Amendment, and Rental Housing Demolition applications to demolish 40 rental dwelling units and allow for the construction of a 13-storey (45.5-metre excluding the mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building, containing 110 dwelling units (including 40 replacement rental units) and 600 square metres of grade-related retail space. Staff are currently reviewing the applications. It has been circulated to all appropriate agencies and City divisions for comment. City Planning staff have tentatively scheduled a Community Consultation Meeting for the application with the Ward Councillor on April 26, 2022.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council directed: 1. Staff schedule a community consultation meeting for the applications pertaining to 1233 Yonge Street and 9 Woodlawn Street East together with the Ward Councillor. 2. Notice for the community consultation meeting be given to landowners and residents within 120 metres of the application site, and to additional residents, institutions and owners to be determined in consultation with the Ward Councillor, with any additional mailing costs to be borne by the applicant.
