545 Lake Shore Boulevard West Zoning Amendment - Final Report

Adopted with changesMay 11, 2022 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend approving a mixed-use development with 13- and 21-storey buildings at 545 Lake Shore Boulevard West, conserving the heritage Crosse and Blackwell building.

The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes to amend the Zoning By-law to permit a mixed-use development with residential, office and retail uses. There is a designated heritage building on the north portion of the site known as the Crosse and Blackwell building that is proposed to be conserved and re-used for office purposes. Residential and retail uses are proposed to be located in a new 13 and 21-storey building on the south portion of the site. The proposal includes a total of 412 dwelling units, 8,938 square metres of office space, 146 vehicle parking spaces and 468 bicycle parking spaces. A new 70 square metre Privately-Owned and Publicly-Accessible Open Space (POPS) is proposed at the southwest corner of the site.
