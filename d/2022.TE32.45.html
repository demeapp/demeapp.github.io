The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Madison Group is constructing a residential development (Nobu Residences) consisting of two 45-storey condominium buildings at 15-35 Mercer Street. The site is located midway on Mercer Street, between John Street and Blue Jays Way. A portion of the City's right-of-way on the south side of Mercer Street abutting the site is currently closed to accommodate a construction staging area for the development. Toronto and East York Community Council, at its meeting on July 4, 2018, approved the temporary closure of a 3.3 metre-wide portion of the westbound lane on Mercer Street from July 20, 2018 to May 31, 2022. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the continued closure of the south sidewalk on Mercer Street, between a point 46.8 metres west of John Street and a point 86.2 metres further west, from June 1, 2022 to May 31, 2023. 2. Authorized the continued closure of a 3.3 metre-wide portion of the westbound lane on the south side of Mercer Street, between a point 46.8 metres west of John Street and a point 66.2 metres further west, from June 1, 2022 to May 31, 2023. 3. Directed the applicant to provide a sufficient number of traffic control persons as determined by the Work Zone Traffic Coordinator and Toronto Police Construction Liaison Officer, on a daily basis to control construction vehicle access and egress to and from the site and maintain a safe environment for the public.
