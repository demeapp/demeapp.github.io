The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. 462 Developments Inc. is building an eight-storey condominium with a total of 286 units at 462 Eastern Ave. The site is located on the north side of Eastern Avenue, between Logan Avenue and Booth Avenue. The north sidewalk and westbound curb lane of Eastern Avenue, from the west side of Logan Avenue to the east side of Booth Ave, are closed to accommodate construction staging operations for the development. In addition, the west sidewalk and a portion of the southbound lane on Logan Avenue is closed, from Eastern Avenue to a point 90 metres north. Toronto and East York Community Council, at its meeting on December 3, 2019, approved the construction staging areas on Eastern Avenue and Logan Avenue from December 2, 2019 to March 31, 2022. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the continuation of the closure of the north sidewalk and westbound curb lane of Eastern Avenue, from the west side of Logan Avenue to the east side of Booth Ave, from April 22, 2022 to September 1, 2022. 2. Authorized the continuation of the closure of the west sidewalk and a 3 metre-wide portion of the southbound lane on Logan Avenue from Eastern Avenue to a point 90 metres further north, from April 22, 2022 to September 1, 2022. 3. Directed the applicant to continue posting a 24-hour monitored construction hotline number on the hoarding board, which must be prominently placed and legible from 20 metres and on all elevations from the construction site. 4.
