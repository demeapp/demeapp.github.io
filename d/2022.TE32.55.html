Parking Amendments - Munro Street, Matilda Street and Sammon Avenue

AdoptedApr 21, 2022 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Toronto and East York Community Council fixes missed permit-parking by-law amendments on Munro Street, Matilda Street, and Sammon Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to resolve by-law amendments missed in Item TE11.35 "Residential On-street Permit Parking - Status Update on the Expansion of On-Street Permit Parking in the Toronto and East York Community Council Area". Transportation Services is recommending that overnight on-street permit parking be introduced on Munro Street, between Dundas Street East and Matilda Avenue/Kintyre Avenue from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., daily and to move permit parking on Sammon Avenue, from the odd to even side of the street. These proposed by-law amendments will ensure that the intent of Item TE11.35, as adopted with amendments by City Council on January 29, 2020 can be properly installed and enforced.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized parking by permit only on an area name basis, within permit parking area 7F, from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily on odd side of Munro Street, between Dundas Street East and Thompson Street. 2. Authorized parking by permit only on an area name basis, within permit parking area 7F, from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily on odd side of Matilda Street, between Munro Street and Davies Avenue. 3. Amended the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., daily on the odd side of Sammon Avenue, between Pape Avenue to Woodycrest Avenue, to be in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., daily on the even side of Sammon Avenue, from Pape Avenue to Woodycrest Avenue. 4.
