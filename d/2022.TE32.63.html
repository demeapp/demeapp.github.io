This is a procedural item: Toronto and East York Community Council appoints members to the Applegrove Community Complex board.

Official title: Appointments to the Board of Management of the Applegrove Community Complex

AdoptedApr 21, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Board further confirms that each of these nominees is qualified to serve under the provisions of the City of Toronto's Public Appointments Policy and the Relationship Framework for Community Centre Boards of Management. On behalf of the Board, I respectfully request that the City take the necessary steps to appoint these nominated candidates. The following public member's terms are coming to an end: • Timothy McNab, term ending March 31, 2022, and until a successor is appointed. • Jean Lim-O’Brien, resigned effective March 31, 2022.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Appointed the following candidates for appointment to the Board of Management of the Applegrove Community Complex, at pleasure of Council, for the terms of office noted below and until successors are appointed: Eadit Rokach, for a term of office ending March 31, 2024 Jim Valentine, for a term of office ending March 31, 2024 Moneca Yardley, for a term of office ending March 31, 2024 Shirin Karim, for a term of office ending March 31, 2024 Vai Teng Law, for a term of office ending March 31, 2023
