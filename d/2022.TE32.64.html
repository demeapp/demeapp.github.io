Toronto City Council · Decision

Endorsement of Events for Liquor Licensing Purposes

AdoptedMay 11, 2022 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council decides whether to declare Big on Bloor and Taste of Little Italy events of municipal significance for liquor licensing.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The City Clerk's Office has received two requests for municipal endorsement for temporary liquor licence extensions that do not meet the criteria of the delegated authority.

What was decided

City Council on May 11 and 12, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council endorse the applications listed below for extensions to licensed areas as set out in Attachment 1 to the report (March 21, 2022) from the City Clerk and City Council direct the City Clerk to issue a letter of municipal significance and non-objection to the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario: - Big on Bloor - Taste of Little Italy.
