The official record

What the item proposed

Chester Hill Road is a residential street in East York which dead ends to the west at the Chester Hill Lookout. In 2019, I held a site visit with City staff and residents and moved a motion to address safety concerns which were brought to my attention. I have since been working with local residents to have speed humps and a CCTV camera installed as well as road design improvements. The CCTV camera was installed several months ago but remains unconnected and non-functional. City staff also have not provided an update on the street redesign since the 2019 site visit. I am writing today to request an update on these initiatives.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested Transportation Services provide an update on the street redesign and installation of the CCTV camera at the Chester Hill Lookout and report back to Toronto and East York Community Council at the May 26, 2022 meeting.
