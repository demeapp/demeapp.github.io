Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to re-open and revise its 2020 decision on speed humps for St. Clarens Avenue.

Official title: Re-Open TE27.59 Speed Hump Installation on St. Clarens Avenue

AdoptedApr 21, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In August 2020, residents reached out to my office with an urgent concern about speeding along their street and requested via a community-led petition that traffic calming measures (speed humps) be installed as a method of addressing these concerns. Unanimously, the residents surveyed were in support of making this change. By way of re-opening this item, I am requesting that Traffic Operations staff amend TE27.59, as per the recommendations, to include information that was omitted in the Community Council decision regarding the installation of speed humps on St. Clarens Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened TE27.59 entitled "Speed Hump Installation on St. Clarens Avenue", with respect to Recommendation 2 of the Toronto and East York Community Council's decision. 2. Deleted Recommendation 2 of its decision with respect to Item TE27.59 and adopt the following: "2. Toronto and East York Community Council direct the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway on St. Clarens Avenue, to install seven speed humps, generally as shown on the copy of Drawing No. 421G-3910 and Drawing No. 421G-3920, dated August 2021, from the Acting Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District."
