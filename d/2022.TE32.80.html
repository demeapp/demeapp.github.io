Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve speed humps on Highbourne Road.

Official title: Authorize the Implementation of Speed Humps on Highbourne Road, between College View Avenue and Tranmer Avenue

AdoptedApr 21, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to respectfully request the implementation of speed humps on Highbourne Road, between College View Ave and Tranmer Ave. Residents of the street presented a petition to my office requesting this more than two years ago, which was referred to staff for a study. Due to pandemic delays, nothing has been forthcoming and residents have continued to complain of speeding and other forms of reckless and dangerous driving on the street. Upon further consultation with the Oriole Park Association, through periodic traffic safety meetings, we identified this as a high priority location for traffic calming and we have worked with Traffic Operations staff in making this request.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorize the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Highbourne Road, between College View Avenue and Tranmer Avenue. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway on Highbourne Road, between College View Avenue and Tranmer Avenue, to install three speed humps, generally as shown on the copy of 'Drawing No. 421G-4008’ dated April 2022 from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
