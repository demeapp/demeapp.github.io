Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to add a No Standing prohibition on Durham Avenue.

Official title: Implementation of a "No Standing" prohibition on Durham Avenue, between Winnett Avenue and Arlington Avenue

AdoptedApr 21, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to respectfully request the implementation of a "No Standing" prohibition on the south side of Durham Avenue, between Winnett Avenue and Arlington Avenue. Residents of the street presented a petition to my office requesting the change in order to help ease congestion. Residents have noted high incidences of parents, who have kids attending schools in the area, using the street as a standing zone, while waiting to pick up their kids.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Durham Avenue, between Atlas Avenue and a point 61 metres east of Arlington Avenue, to be in effect between Atlas Avenue and Winnett Avenue. 2. Prohibited standing at all times on the south side of Durham Avenue, between Winnett Avenue and Arlington Avenue. 3. Prohibited parking at all times on the south side of Durham Avenue, between Arlington Avenue and a point 61 metres east.
