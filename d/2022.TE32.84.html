Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to amend parking rules on Melita Crescent for alternate-side parking.

Official title: Parking Amendments on a section of Melita Crescent

AdoptedApr 21, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to respectfully request a number of parking changes on Melita Crescent that would provide alternate side parking to residents on the north/south segment of Melita Crescent, south of Wychcrest Avenue (west intersection). Residents presented a petition to my office requesting this in order to promote efficient flow of traffic, as well as to allow street sweepers and snowplows to reach the curb without parked cars impeding their progress. My office has worked closely with both Traffic Operations staff and Permit Parking staff in coming up with these recommendations.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect each Thursday, April 1 to November 30, inclusive on the east, north and west side of Melita Crescent between Lambertlodge Avenue (west intersection) and Lambertlodge Avenue (east intersection) to be in effect between a point 61 metres south and east of Wychcrest Avenue (west intersection) and Lambertlodge Avenue (east intersection). 2. Prohibited parking each Thursday, April 1 to November 30, inclusive on the east side of Melita Crescent between Lambertlodge Avenue (west intersection) and Wychcrest Avenue (west intersection) 3.
