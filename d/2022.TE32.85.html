Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to re-open and rescind a morning parking prohibition on Dewbourne Avenue.

Official title: Re-Open Item TE29.75 - Parking Amendments on Dewbourne Avenue

AdoptedApr 21, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to respectfully re-open Item TE29.75, "Parking Amendments on Dewbourne Ave" and to rescind the authority for the prohibition of parking on the north side of Dewbourne Ave, between Glen Cedar Road and Chiltern Hill Road, between 8:00am and 10:00am. After adoption, residents of the street brought it to my office's attention that their request was only for the south side of that section of Dewbourne Ave. The north side is to remain unchanged. This action would help improve traffic flow, and ease congestion in view of the astronomical amount of cut through traffic plying Cedarvale streets, a result of the ongoing LRT construction.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened TE29.75 entitled "Parking Amendments on Dewbourne Avenue, with respect to Part 1 and 2 of the Toronto and East York Community Council's decision. 2. Deleted Recommendation 1 of its decision with respect to Item TE29.75 and adopt the following: "1. Toronto and East York Community Council amend the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Dewbourne Avenue, between Strathearn Road and Glen Cedar Road, and between Chiltern Hill Road and a point 37 metres west of Bathurst Street, to be in effect between Strathearn Road and a point 37 metres west of Bathurst Street." 3.
