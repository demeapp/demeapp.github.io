The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this letter is to temporarily close Bellair Street from Cumberland Street to Yorkville Avenue from May 1, 2022 until November 7, 2022, inclusive, to vehicular traffic to permit six eating establishment operators who have applied under the CaféTO program to operate a curb lane/parklet café under that program on the street. Bellair Street is a one-way local road, with no TTC service and is a mix of commercial properties. The Toronto and East York Community Council is delegated authority to temporarily close roads of this type under Chapter 27, Council Procedures. The notice of this closure has been circulated to Transportation Services, Solid Waste Management and Emergency Services. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Temporarily closed Bellair Street from Cumberland Street to Yorkville Avenue to vehicular traffic from May 1, 2022 until November 7, 2022, inclusive, to permit the installation of curb lane/parklet cafés under the CafeTO program.
