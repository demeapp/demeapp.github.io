Toronto City Council · Decision

Blake Street Parking Amendments

AdoptedApr 21, 2022 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to amend parking on Blake Street after the Jones Avenue Jewish Cemetery reported blocked driveways.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Jones Avenue Jewish Cemetery is one of the oldest in the city, with some plots dating to 1885. Their access for vehicles is off Blake Avenue. They have contacted my office about the habitual blocking of their driveway by vehicles. Transportation Services has prepared language to modify the existing No-Parking area to cover the cemetery frontage. This change will not impact permit parking.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the east side of Blake Street, between Boultbee Avenue and a point 318 metres north to be in effect between Boultbee Avenue and a point 378 metres north. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on the east side of Blake Street, between a point 347 meters north of Boultbee Avenue and a point 11 meters further north.
