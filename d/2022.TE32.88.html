Toronto City Council · Decision

Councillor Fletcher asks the community council to seek a review of proposed Toronto Parking Authority overnight parking policy changes.

Official title: Proposed Amendments to Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 3-6: Residential Overnight

AdoptedApr 21, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On February 18, 2022, the Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) Board considered a Staff report that recommended delegating to staff the authority to adjust the hours when overnight parking is permitted in TPA lots in permit parking areas. That item was deferred and TPA staff have reached out to Councillors to get their feedback. The suggested changes will likely have an impact on the City's Permit Parking program. This program already faces many challenges including oversubscription in many areas.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services to undertake a review of PA28.5 Proposed Amendments to Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 3-6: Residential Overnight Use, as it pertains to the Toronto and East York Community Council District; and to report back to the May 26, 2022 meeting of Toronto and East York Community Council on the impacts to the permit parking system before a final report is submitted to the Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) Board.
