The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that the short-term parking zone for Bowmore Public School on Wrenson Road be amended. The proposed parking changes will provide eight additional short-term parking spaces on the north side of Wrenson Road and will further facilitate student pick-up and drop-off activities.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition from 9:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m., from 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m., and from 6:00 p.m. of one day to 7:30 a.m. of the next following day, Monday to Friday; and anytime Saturday and Sunday, on the north side of Wrenson Road, between a point 30 metres west of Bowmore Road and a point 79 metres west of Bowmore Road, to be in effect between a point 30 metres west of Bowmore Road and a point 124 metres west of Bowmore Road. 2.
