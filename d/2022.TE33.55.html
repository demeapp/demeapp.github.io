Community Council was asked to replace damaged bollards protecting 19 Silverthorn Avenue.

Official title: Bollard Installation - 19 Silverthorn Avenue

AdoptedMay 26, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Recently, I have heard from the homeowner of 19 Silverthorn Avenue requesting bollard repair and replacement along the flankage of their property. Bollards act as both a physical and visual barrier, protecting people and property from damage without impeding the public right of way. In the reported case, the resident has a fence around their front yard and bollards along its flankage, which have been damaged because of vehicle impact. As a result, I am recommending that the damaged bollards be replaced in order to protect property and enhance community safety.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services, to replace the bollards in the first public lane north of St. Clair Avenue West and east of Silverthorn Avenue along the flankage of 19 Silverthorn Avenue.
