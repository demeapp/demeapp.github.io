The official record

What the item proposed

Traffic safety and calming in the North Corso Italia neighbourhood of Ward 9 has been an ongoing issue. Being in close proximity to arterial roadways such as Rogers Road, Dufferin Street, and St. Clair Avenue West, this neighbourhood is often subject to vehicles travelling at high speeds, cut-through traffic, congestion, and noise. With an existing aging population along with many young families living in the neighbourhood, a group of local residents representing various streets in North Corso Italia, established a Traffic Management Committee to develop a complete street plan and implement a series of coordinated traffic calming and road safety measures to alleviate the concerns that are negatively impacting the neighbourhood. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council directed: 1. The General Manager, Transportation Services to establish a comprehensive neighbourhood streets plan for the North Corso Italia neighbourhood, bounded by Rogers Road, Dufferin Street, St. Clair Avenue West and Prospect Cemetery, including a review and analysis of the recommendations made by the North Corso Traffic Management Committee and consideration of any other appropriate measures to address the issues raised by the community or identified by City staff.
