Council was asked to secure emergency vehicle access to St. Joseph's Health Centre's driveway on The Queensway.

Official title: Curb depressions and emergency vehicle full access to St Joseph Hospital's Melynk Driveway from the Queensway

AdoptedJun 15, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As a part of the King / Queen / Queensway / Roncesvalles (KQQR) road reconstruction work, St. Joseph's Health Centre (SJHC) has requested that emergency service vehicles have full access to the SJHC Melynk driveway, located on The Queensway between Glendale Ave and Sunnyside Ave. Engineering & Construction Services (ECS), in consultation with TTC and Transportation Services Traffic Operations, have reviewed and agree with installing curb depressions on The Queensway to accommodate emergency service vehicles. To deter regular vehicular traffic from entering the TTC track allowance, the curb depressions will be offset with the south side curb depression located further west.

What was decided

City Council on June 15 and 16, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit eastbound and southbound left turns at all times at the intersection of The Queensway and the private driveway at 30 The Queensway, located on the north side of The Queensway, approximately 90 metres west of Sunnyside Avenue, emergency vehicles excepted.
