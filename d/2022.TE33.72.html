The official record

What the item proposed

In August 2020, Transportation Services began to review the need for traffic calming measures on London Street, between Markham Street and Bathurst Street after my office received a request from neighbours to have the City address safety concerns on the street. London Street is a local road that operates one-way in the eastbound direction from Markham Street and Bathurst Street. It has a posted regulatory speed limit of 30 km/h and an average daily traffic volume of about 1252 between Markham Street and Bathurst Street. The pavement width on London Street is about 7.3 metres and there are sidewalks provided on both east and west side of the road. Heavy vehicles are prohibited at all times and there is no TTC service provided on London Street. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on London Street, between Markham Street and Bathurst Street. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway on London Street, between Markham Street and Bathurst Street, to install one speed hump, generally as shown on the copy of 'Drawing No. 421G-4015' dated April 2022 from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, attached to the letter (May 26, 2022) from Councillor Mike Layton, Ward 11, University - Rosedale.
