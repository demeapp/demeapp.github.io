The official record

What the item proposed

In 2016, staff started to look at the potential to transition Cumberland Road to become at least partially 2-way to ensure safety within the Yorkville neighbourhood at the request of the local resident associations. Converting a section of Cumberland from its current one-way eastbound direction to a two-way operation is possible. In preparation of doing so, parking will need to be eliminated on Cumberland within the 2-way portion in order to safely accommodate traffic flow. This would mean No Stopping Anytime on both sides of the street. The limit for the 2-way portion on Cumberland would start from Avenue Rd to approx. the pedestrian crossover. This would give vehicles exiting driveways along this stretch the 2-way option. Westbound left-turns at Avenue would be prohibited for safety.
