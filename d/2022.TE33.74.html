Community Council was asked to make the Murray Street passenger loading zone near Princess Margaret permanent.

Official title: Passenger Loading Zone - Murray Street

AdoptedMay 26, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

During the pandemic, a passenger loading zone was installed on Murray Street as a part of the CurbTO initiative. This loading zone was intended to help with parking challenges around Princess Margaret and congestion with pick up/drop off activities in the vicinity of their Murray Street driveway. Staff were previously planning to remove this zone and re-instate the pay and display parking spaces that were removed. However, Princess Margaret staff have reached out and requested that this zone remain, and made permanent. According to hospital staff, the zone has helped with parking congestion in the area, and provided an alternative space where patients can safely load/unload. Making this area a permanent Passenger Loading Zone is possible, but will require Community Council approval to amend the current parking by-laws. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Designated a passenger loading zone to operate at all times on the east side of Murray Street, between a point 8.3 metres south of Orde Street and a point 57 metres further south. 2. Amended the existing parking machine regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. at a rate of $4.00 per hour and for a maximum period of 3 hours, on the east side of Murray Street, between Orde Street and a point 90 metres south, to be in effect between a point 65.3 metres south of Orde Street and a point 24.7 metres further south.
