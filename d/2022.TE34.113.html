The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Avenue Road, City Council approval of this report is required. Hazelton On The Avenue Inc. is constructing a 20-storey residential condominium building at 89 Avenue Road. The site is located on the east side of Avenue Road between Yorkville Avenue and Webster Avenue. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the east sidewalk and a portion of the east side northbound curb lane on Avenue Road for a period of 29 months, from July 20, 2022 to November 30, 2024 to facilitate construction staging operations. Pedestrian operations on the east side of Avenue Road will be maintained in a 2.1 metre-wide covered and protected walkway within the closed portion of the existing lane.
