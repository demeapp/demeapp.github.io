The community council decides whether to close part of Asquith Avenue for construction at 771-775 Yonge Street.

Official title: Construction Staging Area - 771 and 775 Yonge Street

AdoptedJun 29, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Menkes 771 Yonge Street Inc. is constructing a 25-storey condominium building at 771 and 775 Yonge Street. The site is located at the south-west corner of Yonge Street and Asquith Avenue. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the south sidewalk and a 2.5 metre-wide portion of the south side eastbound curb lane on Asquith Avenue for a period of 26 months, from October 1, 2022 to November 30, 2024, to facilitate construction staging operations.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the closure of the south sidewalk and a 2.5 metre wide portion of the south side eastbound curb lane on Asquith Avenue, between a point 8.3 metres east of Yonge Street and a point 39.0 metres further east, and provision of a temporary 2.5 metre-wide pedestrian walkway within the closed portion of the eastbound curb lane, from October 1, 2022 to November 30, 2024. 2. Rescinded the existing no standing prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Asquith Avenue, between a point 8.3 metres east of Yonge Street and a point 39.0 metres further east. 3. Rescinded the existing no parking anytime prohibition on the south side of Asquith Avenue, between a point 8.3 metres east of Yonge Street and a point 39.0 metres further east. 4.
