The community council considers appointing new members to the Swansea Town Hall board.

Official title: Appointments to the Swansea Town Hall Board of Management

AdoptedJun 29, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Board confirms that each of these nominees is qualified to serve under the provisions of the City of Toronto's Public Appointments Policy and the Relationship Framework for Community Centre Boards of Management. Please note that the terms of the following members have now come to an end/will be ending: - Glenn Dunphy, term ending May 19, 2022 - Marcia Smith, term ending May 19, 2022 - Sarah Doucette, resigned effective February 25, 2021 On behalf of the Board, I respectfully request that the City take the necessary steps to appoint these nominated candidates.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Appointed the following individuals for appointment to the Swansea Town Hall Board, at pleasure of Council, for the terms of office noted below and until their successors are appointed: - Ayan Kailie, for a term of office ending May 31, 2024 - Andrea Beitel, for a term of office ending May 31, 2024 - Madhu Bhargava, for a term of office ending May 31, 2024 - Meg Meng, for a term of office ending May 31, 2024 - Justine Montgomery, for a term of office ending May 31, 2024 - Brian O’Rourke, for a term of office ending May 31, 2023 - Nicholas Setacci, for a term of office ending May 31, 2024 - Phil Vlach, for a term of office ending May 31, 2023
