1540-1550 Bloor Street West - Rental Housing Demolition Application - Final Report

AdoptedJul 19, 2022 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to approve demolishing 12 rental units at 1540-1550 Bloor Street West for a 27-storey tower with 26 affordable rentals.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes to demolish the existing two-storey mixed-use buildings, which collectively contain 12 rental dwelling units, to permit the redevelopment of the lands with a 27-storey apartment building comprised of 354 dwelling units, including 12 affordable replacement rental units and 14 new affordable rental units. As of the date of this report, the total Gross Floor Area (GFA) of the proposed development would be 23,987 square metres, including 663 square metres of non-residential GFA, resulting in a Floor Space Index (FSI) of 9.73 times the site area. The properties are also the subject of a Zoning By-law Amendment (ZBA) application (19 263422 STE 04 OZ), which was appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT), formerly the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal (LPAT), in June 2020 (Case No. PL200248) on the basis of City Council's failure to make a decision on the application…

What was decided

City Council on July 19, 20, 21 and 22, 2022, adopted the following: 1.
