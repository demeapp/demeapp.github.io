A councillor asks to re-open and correct a Durham Avenue parking rule that missed residents' actual request.

Official title: Re-open Item TE32.83 - Implementation of a "No Standing" prohibition on Durham Avenue, between Winnett Avenue and Arlington Avenue

AdoptedJun 29, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to respectfully request your support in re-opening Item TE32.83 in order to change the "No Standing" prohibition on Durham Ave, between Winnett Ave and Arlington Ave, to a "No Stopping" prohibition. After adoption, residents of the street brought it to my office's attention that their petition had actually requested for a "No Stopping" prohibition. This measure would help ease congestion on their street by deterring parents, who have kids attending schools in the area, from using the street as a standing/stopping zone while waiting to pick up their kids.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item TE32.83 titled “Implementation of a "No Standing" prohibition on Durham Avenue, between Winnett Avenue and Arlington Avenue", with respect to Recommendation 2. 2. Replaced Recommendation 2 with the following: 2. Prohibit stopping at all times on the south side of Durham Avenue, between Winnett Avenue and Arlington Avenue
