Toronto City Council · Decision

A councillor asks to move parking permanently to one side of Humberview Road after a residents' petition.

Official title: Parking Amendments on Humberview Road from Jane Street to Halford Avenue

AdoptedJun 29, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Parking currently alternates on Humberview Road between Jane Street and Halford Avenue. A petition asking for the parking to be changed permanently to one side was received from residents along this section of Humberview Road. Transportation Staff have provided their recommendations below

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 1st day of each month to the 15th day of each month, April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the north side of Humberview Road, between Halford Avenue and a point 38.4 metres west of Jane Street 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, from December 1 to March 31, inclusive, on the north side of Humberview Road, between Halford Avenue and a point 38.4 metres west of Jane Street 3. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Humberview Road, between Jane Street and a point 38.6 metres west of Jane Street 4.
