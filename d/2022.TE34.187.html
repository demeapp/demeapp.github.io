A councillor asks to install speed bumps on College Place after a 2021 resident petition.

Official title: Traffic Amendments - College Place

AdoptedJun 29, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In 2021, residents submitted a petition to my office requesting speed bumps along College Place between College St and Dundas St W. Residents have reported they feel the speeds in the lane are unsafe. The installation of speed humps may assist in calming traffic and making the conditions safer for all users of the lane, including pedestrians and vulnerable road users such as seniors and children.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of speed bumps on College Place, between College Street and Dundas Street West, at the locations shown on Drawing Nos. 421G-4068 and 421G-4069, dated June 2022, from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, Toronto and East York District.
