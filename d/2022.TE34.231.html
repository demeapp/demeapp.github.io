The community council decides whether to re-open and correct a speed bump item with wrong lane boundaries.

Official title: Re-Open TE32.76 Speed Bump Installation on East-West Public Lane bounded by Wallace Avenue, Emerson Avenue, Armstrong Avenue and Dufferin Street

AdoptedJun 29, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

TE32.76, which was moved at the April meeting of Toronto East York Community Council contained incorrect boundary information related to the proposed installation of speed bumps. By way of re-opening this item, I am requesting that Traffic Operations staff amend TE32.76, as per the recommendations, regarding the installation of speed bumps on the east-west public lane bounded by Wallace Avenue, Emerson Avenue, Armstrong Avenue and Dufferin Street.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened item TE32.76 – "Speed Bump Installation on the East-West Public Lane bounded by Wallace Avenue, Emerson Avenue, Armstrong Avenue and Dufferin Street." 2. Rescinded the decision on item TE32.76.
