The community council decides whether to extend Commissioners Street and realign part of Cherry Street in the Port Lands.

Official title: Street Naming Initiatives - Port Lands Flood Protection - Phase 1

AdoptedJun 29, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of the westerly extension of Commissioners Street, west of Cherry Street. This report recommends the realignment and naming of a portion of Cherry Street between Polson Street and Commissioners Street (westerly extension). This naming initiative complies with the City of Toronto Street Naming Policy which can be found at https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/planning-development/street-naming/ .

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the westerly extension of Commissioners Street, west of Cherry Street, shown as PART Number 1 on Revised Attachment Number 1 Sketch Number PS-2022-025 attached to the report (June 24, 2022) from the Director, Engineering Support Services, Engineering and Construction. 2. Approved the realignment and naming of a portion of Cherry Street extending between Polson Street and Commissioners, shown as PART Number 2 on Revised Attachment Number 1 Sketch Number PS-2022-025 attached to the report (June 24, 2022) from the Director, Engineering Support Services, Engineering and Construction.
