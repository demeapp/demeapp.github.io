The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report requests that Toronto and East York Community Council deny the request for a permit to remove one privately owned tree located at 57 Alcorn Avenue. The application indicates the reason for removal is due to concerns that the falling nuts are causing damage to the property and pose a significant risk of injury to people in the vicinity. The subject tree is a black walnut tree (Juglans nigra), measuring 93 cm in diameter. The Tree By-laws do not support the removal of this tree as it is healthy and maintainable.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Denied the request for a permit to remove one privately owned tree located at 57 Alcorn Avenue.
