Toronto City Council · Decision

The community council endorses the consultation notice for the 11-storey proposal at 1255-1311 Yonge Street.

Official title: 1255-1311 Yonge - Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment Application - Preliminary Report

AdoptedJun 29, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information and identifies a preliminary set of issues regarding the Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application to allow for the construction of an 11-storey (40-metre excluding the mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building, containing 102 residential dwelling units and 5,000 square metres of non-residential gross floor area (including 3,885.9 square metres of office uses). Staff are currently reviewing the application. It has been circulated to all appropriate agencies and City divisions for comment. Together with the Ward Councillor, City Planning staff have scheduled a Community Consultation Meeting for June 13, 2022.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Endorsed the arrangements made between City Planning staff and the applicant: notice for the June 13, 2022, Community Consultation Meeting to landowners and residents within 120 metres of the site, and to additional residents, institutions and owners as was determined in consultation with the Ward Councillor, with any additional mailing costs to be borne by the applicant.
