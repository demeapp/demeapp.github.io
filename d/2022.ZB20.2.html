The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto Zoo 2020 Master Plan was developed through extensive research and consultation from staff, volunteers, members, guests, donors, stakeholders, and community partners. This Master Plan will provide a roadmap for The Board of Management and the Toronto Zoo for future growth and development over the next 20 years. The Master Plan aligns with the Zoo's mission and vision, ensures that we continue to live our 4 cares, and brings us to the forefront as one of the top zoos in the world.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Approved the Toronto Zoo Master Plan.
