What the item proposed

Since our last meeting of the Board of Management, the Ontario government released its plan to lift COVID-19 restrictions and regulations as part of its re-opening plan. On March 1, vaccination mandates were lifted for certain businesses that were previously required to have mandates under Regulation 364/20. On March 21, mandatory masking requirements were also eased. As these Government directives pertaining to certain COVID-19 health and safety measures were lifted, individual organizations have been provided the authority to keep health and safety policies in place. Your Toronto Zoo endeavours to ensure a safe, inclusive and inviting experience for our team, animals, guests and community. What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Approved the continued implementation of the following policies until May 30, 2022 or until the high-risk animals in the Zoo's care have been inoculated: a. requiring proof of vaccination for persons 12 years and older to enter the Zoo; b. requiring mandatory vaccination of all staff; and c. requiring the wearing of masks in sensitive areas of all staff and contractors.
