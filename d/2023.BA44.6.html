The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (August 31, 2023) from the Chief Procurement Officer: Solicitation Number: Request for Tender, Ariba Document Number 4005444130 Description: For the supply, manufacture, delivery, assembly, and installation of all elements required to construct a parkette at the intersection of Dufferin Street and Finch Avenue in the Duke Heights Business Improvement Area, on behalf of the Economic Development and Culture Division of the City of Toronto.
