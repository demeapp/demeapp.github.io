Appointment of Members of the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee

AdoptedOct 11, 2023 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to appoint 8 individuals to the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee for a term ending November 14, 2026, or until their mandate is completed, whichever comes first. The nominees are named in a confidential attachment.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

This report recommends the appointment of 8 individuals to the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee.

What was decided
