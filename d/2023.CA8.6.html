Appointment of Members to the Toronto Film, Television and Digital Media Board

AdoptedOct 11, 2023 · Toronto City Council

City Council was asked to appoint 22 individuals to the Toronto Film, Television and Digital Media Board for the remainder of the current term of City Council for a term ending November 14, 2026. The nominees are named in a confidential attachment.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
