The official record

What the item proposed

The Civic Appointments Committee must select candidates to interview for appointment to the Housing Rights Advisory Committee. Following the interviews, the Civic Appointments Committee will recommend eleven candidates, including one as Chair, for appointment to City Council.

What was decided
