What the item proposed

City Council adopted Official Plan Amendment 231 on December 18, 2013, following the Five-Year Official Plan and Municipal Comprehensive Reviews regarding employment lands. In addition to setting out city-wide economic policies, Official Plan Amendment 231 also amended the land use designations applicable to many parcels of land, in some cases by adding new or amending existing site and area specific policies or secondary plans. Through Council's adoption of Official Plan Amendment 231, Area 3 of the in-force Garrison Common North Secondary Plan was amended to include only those lands bound by Dufferin Street to the west, King Street West to the north, Hanna Avenue to the east, and the Lakeshore Rail Corridor to the south, corresponding to the western portion of the neighbourhood known as Liberty Village.
