What the item proposed

On May 8, 2019 the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal, now the Ontario Land Tribunal ("OLT"), issued its Decision and Order (the "Decision" or "Order") on the Phase 3 Hearing of the appeals of the city-wide Zoning By-law 569-2013 ("By-law 569-2013") concerning the regulations of the Chapter 15 Residential Apartment Zone Category and the Chapter 30 Commercial Zone Category. The Conservatory Group was the lone appellant at the Hearing (the "Appellant" or "Conservatory Group"). The main issue in the Phase 3 Hearing concerned whether townhouses are a permitted building type in the Residential Apartment (“RA”), Residential Apartment Commercial (“RAC”) and Commercial Residential (“CR”) Zones.
