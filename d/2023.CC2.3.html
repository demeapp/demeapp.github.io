Legislative Changes to City Governance - Bill 3 and Bill 39

Adopted with changesDec 14, 2022 · Toronto City Council

Bill 3, Strong Mayors, Building Homes Act, 2022 Legislative and regulatory changes that provide new powers to the Mayors of the City of Toronto and City of Ottawa came into force on November 23, 2022, with the proclamation of the Ontario government's Bill 3, Strong Mayors, Building Homes Act, 2022 ("Bill 3"). Associated regulations were also released by the Province on November 23, 2022. The City Manager, City Clerk and City Solicitor recommends that City Council amend Municipal Code Chapter 169, City Officials to provide that the City Solicitor and City Clerk are authorized to submit and process bills.

Decided by a recorded vote, 16 in favour and 10 against — every member's position is public.
