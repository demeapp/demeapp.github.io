The official record

What the item proposed

On December 23, 2020, a Zoning By-law Amendment application was submitted to the City for the lands at 277 Wellington Street West. The application was to permit a 66-storey (232.3 metres, including mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building containing 116 square metres of retail space, 9,169 square metres of office uses, and 645 residential units. The proposed 9-storey (42.90 metres) base building would contain retail and office uses. The application was appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) on May 25, 2022 for Council's failure to make a decision on the application within the prescribed timelines in the Planning Act. A Case Management Conference was held at the OLT on August 31, 2022.
