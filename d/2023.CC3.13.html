The official record

What the item proposed

On November 19, 2020, a Zoning By-law Amendment application was submitted to the City for the lands at 471 - 479 Queen Street East. The application was to permit a 15-storey (54.8 metres tall including mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building with ground floor retail uses and 143 residential dwelling units. The application was appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal on November 5, 2021 for City Council's failure to make a decision on the application within the prescribed timelines in the Planning Act. Case Management Conferences were held at the Ontario Land Tribunal on February 4, 2022 and May 9, 2022 and a 10-day hearing has been scheduled to commence on March 13, 2023.
