40 and 42 Tuxedo Court - Zoning Amendment Application - Request for Direction Report

AdoptedMar 29, 2023 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Council was asked to adopt confidential legal instructions for the Tribunal hearing on the two-tower development at 40 and 42 Tuxedo Court.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On May 28, 2021, the City received a Zoning By-law Amendment application for 40 - 42 Tuxedo Court (the "Site") to permit the construction of a mixed-use building with two towers. The applicant appealed City Council's neglect or failure to make a decision on its application (the "Appeal") to the Ontario Land Tribunal on January 21, 2022. The City Solicitor requires further directions for the upcoming Ontario Land Tribunal hearing scheduled for July 10, 2023.

What was decided

City Council on March 29, 30 and 31, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 15, 2023) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Appendices A and B to the report (March 15, 2023) from the City Solicitor. 3. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 15, 2023) from the City Solicitor remain confidential at the discretion of the City Solicitor, as it contains advice and information that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
