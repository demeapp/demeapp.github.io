Development Charges - Local Planning Appeal Tribunal Decision - 2157 Lake Shore Boulevard West (Humber Bay Shores)

AdoptedMar 29, 2023 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Council was asked to adopt confidential legal instructions on the Humber Bay Shores development charges legal challenge.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information regarding a legal challenge to a decision of the Ontario Divisional Court released February 8, 2023 concerning a decision issued by the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal on August 7, 2020 in respect of development charge complaints filed by certain land owners in the Humber Bay Shores area of the City pursuant to section 20 of the Development Charges Act, 1997.

What was decided

City Council on March 29, 30 and 31, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 20, 2023) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 20, 2023) from the City Solicitor at the discretion of the City Solicitor. 3. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 20, 2023) from the City Solicitor remain confidential as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege. Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 20, 2023) from the City Solicitor remains confidential at this time in accordance with the provisions of the City of Toronto Act, 2006, as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
