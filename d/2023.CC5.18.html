801 York Mills Road - Ontario Land Tribunal Hearing - Request for Directions

AdoptedMar 29, 2023 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Council was asked to adopt confidential legal instructions for the Tribunal hearing on two 21-storey towers and a 9-storey building at 801 York Mills Rd.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On November 4, 2019, a Zoning By-law Amendment application was submitted for 801 York Mills Road and 1855 Leslie Street to permit two, 21-storey towers (69 metres each), and a 9-storey apartment building (31.9 metres). The applicant appealed City Council's neglect or failure to make a decision on its application for the Zoning By-law Amendment (the "Appeal") to the Ontario Land Tribunal on April 25, 2022. The City Solicitor requires further direction for the upcoming Ontario Land Tribunal hearing scheduled for July 31, 2023.

What was decided

City Council on March 29, 30 and 31, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 16, 2023) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Appendix A to the report (March 16, 2023) from the City Solicitor. 3. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 16, 2023) from the City Solicitor remain confidential at the discretion of the City Solicitor, as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
