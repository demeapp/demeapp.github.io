The official record

What the item proposed

On March 5, 2019, the City of Markham received applications for an Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment to permit the development of 27- and 6-storey residential buildings on the lands located at 36, 38, 40, 42, 44, 46, 48 Steeles Avenue East and 37, 39, 41, 43, 45, 47, 49 Highland Park Boulevard ("Proposed Development"). The applicant has appealed the City of Markham's failure to make a decision on the Proposed Development to the Ontario Land Tribunal. The Ontario Land Tribunal held the first Case Management Conference on February 6, 2023, at which the City of Toronto was granted party status. A hearing is scheduled for November 6, 2023, for which the City Solicitor requires instructions.
