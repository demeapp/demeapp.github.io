The official record

What the item proposed

On January 20, 2019, the Applicant submitted an application to amend Zoning By-law 569-2013 and former City of North York Zoning By-law 7625 (the "Application") for the property municipally known as 20 Godstone Road (the "Site" or "Lands"). The purpose of the January 2019 Application (the "Original Proposal") was to permit the development of two additional residential buildings on the site. The site currently contains a 15-storey rental residential building that is proposed to remain, and none of the existing 174 dwelling units in the existing building are proposed to be removed or demolished. The existing rental building has a gross floor area of 16,050 square metres and a Floor Space Index of 1.44 times the area of the lot.
