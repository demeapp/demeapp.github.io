The official record

What the item proposed

On December 18, 2020, the City received a Zoning By-law Amendment application for 1-11 Bloor Street West and 768-784 Yonge Street (the "Site") to permit an increase in the maximum permitted building height by 9 storeys, totalling 94-storeys (338.3 metres). The applicant appealed City Council's neglect or failure to make a decision on its application for the Zoning By-law Amendment (the "Appeal"), to the Ontario Land Tribunal on September 22, 2022. A Zoning By-law Amendment Application (Application 15 128261 STE 27 OZ) had been approved by the Ontario Municipal Board (now continued as the Ontario Land Tribunal) on April 18, 2017 permitting an 85-storey (306.9 metres, including mechanical penthouse) mixed use building on the Site.
