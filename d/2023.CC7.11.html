The official record

What the item proposed

On July 15, 2021, the City received a Zoning By-law Amendment application for the lands at 1-3 Concorde Gate and 10-12 Concorde Place (the "Site"). The application sought to permit the redevelopment of the Site with five residential buildings consisting of nine towers ranging in height from 40 to 52 storeys. The applicant appealed City Council's neglect or failure to make a decision on its application for the Zoning By-law Amendment (the "Appeal"), to the Ontario Land Tribunal on January 28, 2022. A with prejudice offer to settle the appeal of the Zoning By-law Amendment was provided to the City on March 16, 2023 (the "March 16 Offer"). City Council did not accept the March 16, 2023 Offer at the March 29-31, 2023 City Council meeting.
