What the item proposed

The Governing Council of the University of Toronto (the "University") applied in September, 2016 to amend the Official Plan to establish a new Secondary Plan for the University of Toronto St. George Campus area. The proposal was to replace the existing University of Toronto Secondary Plan that was adopted in 1997, to provide an updated policy framework that would manage change and guide new development in the area.
