The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek further instruction in relation to a development application for an appeal at the Ontario Land Tribunal.

What was decided

City Council on June 14 and 15, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential recommendations in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (May 29, 2023) from the City Solicitor, as amended by Motion 1 by Councillor Stephen Holyday. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Appendices A and B to the report (May 29, 2023) from the City Solicitor. 3. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to Motion 1 by Councillor Holyday. 4. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (May 29, 2023) from the City Solicitor remain confidential, as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
