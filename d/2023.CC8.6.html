The official record

What the item proposed

On July 29, 2021, the City received a Zoning By-law Amendment application to permit a 21-storey residential building at 537-547 Eglinton Avenue East. On October 27, 2021, a related Official Plan Amendment application was received. On September 23, 2021 and December 9, 2021, City Council refused the applications and directed that staff oppose any appeal to the Ontario Land Tribunal (the "Tribunal"). The applicant appealed City Council's refusal of the Zoning By-law Amendment and Official Plan Amendment applications to the Ontario Land Tribunal on October 29, 2021 and March 3, 2022 respectively (the "Appeals"). The 537-547 Eglinton Avenue East lands were subsequently sold to the owner of 59 and 61 Hoyle Avenue, and the new owner assumed the applications and Appeals.
