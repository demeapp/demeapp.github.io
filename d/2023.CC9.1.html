The official record

What the item proposed

This report outlines the options for filling the vacancy in the office of Councillor in Ward 20 - Scarborough Southwest as a result of Gary Crawford's resignation. The City of Toronto Act, 2006, and the Municipal Elections Act, 1996, together prescribe rules and impose deadlines with respect to filling a vacancy. The former Councillor resigned as the Councillor for Ward 20 - Scarborough Southwest effective July 26, 2023. Consequently, Council must declare the seat vacant at its next meeting. The City of Toronto Act, 2006, also provides that Council, within 60 days of the office being declared vacant, must decide whether to fill the vacancy by appointment or pass a by-law requiring that a by-election be held. The person appointed or elected to fill the vacancy holds office for the remainder of the term, ending November 14, 2026.

What was decided

City Council on August 10, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council declare a vacancy in the Office of Councillor, Ward 20 - Scarborough Southwest. 2. City Council fill the vacancy by requiring a by-election be held for Ward 20 - Scarborough Southwest, and: a. Approve an in-year budget adjustment to increase the City Clerk's Office 2023 Operating Budget by $500,000 gross and $0 net, fully funded from the Election Reserve Fund (XR1017) to reflect the funds required to conduct the by-election.
