What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide detailed information on the 2024 Plan and Budget to the Finance and Audit Committee.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of TO Live: 1. Approved the 2024 Plan and Budget, with a City investment of $5.599 million, as outlined in Attachment 1 and Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (August 31, 2023) from the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live. 2. Approved the reallocation and repurpose of existing cash flow estimates totaling $47 million, currently budgeted towards St. Lawrence Centre for the Arts (STLC) State of Good Repair and AODA projects, to the St. Lawrence Centre for the Arts Redevelopment project as part of the funding strategy, and direct TO Live to reflect this in the 2024 budget submission to the City of Toronto for consideration during the 2024 budget process. 3.
