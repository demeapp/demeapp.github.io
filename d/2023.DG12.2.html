The official record

What the item proposed

Appeal of Dangerous Dog Order issued November 13, 2022, Appeal DDRT2023-16.

What was decided

The Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal: 1. Affirmed that as the Appellant Alexander Orlovsky has failed to appear before the Hearing of the Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal under file DDRT2023-16, which was marked peremptory, he is deemed to have abandoned the subject Appeal. 2. Confirmed the determination of a dangerous dog and directed that the conditions of the Order remain in effect, and that the Tribunal forward the reasons for this decision to Alexander Orlovsky and the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards on or before October 27, 2023.
