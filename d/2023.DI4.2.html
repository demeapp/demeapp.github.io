The official record

What the item proposed

Noah Slater, Director, Housing Development, Renewal and Improvement, Housing Secretariat will give a presentation on the update on the work of the Accessible Housing Working Group who is tasked by Council to review accessibility requirements in City-led housing development projects. A report is due back from the group in the new year, with recommendations.

What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee received the item for information.
