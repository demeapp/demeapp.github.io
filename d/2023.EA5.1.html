The official record

What the item proposed

The Municipal Elections Act, 1996 requires the Compliance Audit Committee ("Committee") to appoint an auditor if it grants a compliance audit application requiring an audit of a candidate's election campaign finances. To date, the Committee has granted applications for two candidates in the 2022 municipal election. To comply with statutory requirements, the Committee must appoint external auditors to conduct the compliance audits of these candidates, as well as any other candidates or registered third party advertisers for whom a compliance audit application may be granted during the 2022-2026 term of office. The Committee previously authorized the City Clerk to manage the procurement process to hire an external auditor when it adopted its Purchasing Policy, and the City Clerk issued a competitive Request for Services Quotation (RFSQ) tender to auditing firms in March 2023. What was decided

The Compliance Audit Committee: 1. Appointed: a. William Molson; b. BDO Canada LLP; c. Chaplin & Co LLP; d. Delta Group Canada Limited; and e. MNP LLP to conduct compliance audits of election campaign finances of candidates or third party advertisers during the 2022 to 2026 term of office.
