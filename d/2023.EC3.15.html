Respecting Deceased Homeless in Toronto

WithdrawnApr 25, 2023 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Ainslie recommends the City resume publishing monthly data on deaths of people experiencing homelessness, retroactive to January 2023.

The item was withdrawn before a final decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On March 17, 2023, Toronto Public Health released data related to the deaths of individuals experiencing homelessness in 2022. The data reported 187 deaths, with the data collected from Participating agencies who serve the homeless and under-housed communities and the City of Toronto's Shelter, Support and Housing Administration (SSHA) division. Deaths of People Experiencing Homelessness – City of Toronto Data on homeless deaths was previously released monthly, this practice enabled our divisions to recognize any patterns, address urgent needs and respectfully grieve individuals at the time of their deaths. What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee withdrew item EC3.15 from the agenda.
