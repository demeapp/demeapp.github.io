What the item proposed

The Chair of the Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal must submit an annual report on the Tribunal's activities to the appropriate standing committee in accordance with its governance structure. Attached to this report is the Tribunal Chair's 2022 Annual Report. The Tribunal is an adjudicative board and city and local board of the City of Toronto under the authority of the City of Toronto Act, 2006. Tribunal hearings are conducted in accordance with the Statutory Powers Procedure Act, 1990 and the Tribunal's rules of procedure. The Tribunal provides an independent consideration of appeals to Dangerous Dog Orders issued by Municipal Licensing and Standards under the authority of Section 349-15 of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 349, Animals.
