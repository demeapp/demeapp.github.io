City Council receives a presentation on why manufacturing matters to Toronto's economy.

Official title: Why Manufacturing Is Essential To Toronto

Adopted with changesOct 11, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This presentation will provide an overview of the current status of Toronto's manufacturing sector, highlighting existing issues, opportunities, and competitiveness challenges. The presentation will illustrate how manufacturing supports good jobs across the city, and how it is a critical part of a diverse economy with high economic multipliers.

What was decided

City Council on October 11 and 12, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture and the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning to report back in fourth quarter of 2024 on the state of the manufacturing sector, including the impacts of recent legislative and regulatory changes and related processes. 2. City Council request the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning and the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture to consider opportunities to promote and incentivize the development of Wet Lab manufacturing space through any upcoming Official Plan policy changes, and in addition to consider any potential Zoning By-law Amendments and program changes that may help facilitate and enable the development of Wet Lab manufacturing space across the City.
