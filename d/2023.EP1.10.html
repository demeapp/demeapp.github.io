What the item proposed

This report provides a further update since June 2022 on the year-end forecast from the impact of COVID-19. The Boards operations are highly dependent on the cash flow and net income from our trade and consumer shows, conferences, conventions, socials, galas, meetings, and tenants' operations. Since early in 2021, given the rise in concern over COVID-19, staff have been monitoring its cash flow from operations and continue to monitor all non-essential costs and discretionary expenditures such as travel, training, etc. At the time of our budget submission there were certain assumptions that were used with respect to the Board 2022 Operating Budget. Some of the key assumptions are: - The pandemic phase of COVID-19 was ending in 2022.
