What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of travel expenses for Exhibition Place General Manager, Laura Purdy, to attend the UFI European Conference 2023 and the co-located Women in Exhibitions (WIE) International Summit, at which she is a keynote speaker. UFI is the international organization representing trade and exhibition centers. The UFI European Conference is the leading European exhibition industry event, a trusted platform for bringing colleagues together from across Europe and beyond. The European Conference puts a spotlight on important industry topics and challenges faced by exhibition and event industry professionals. The event attracts over 300 industry leaders from organizers, venues, and service providers, and offers keynote sessions, interactive workshops, and invaluable networking opportunities. What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Authorized meeting, convention, and travel expenses for Laura Purdy to attend the 2023 UFI European Conference from June 14 - 16 in Maastricht, The Netherlands at a cost not to exceed $4,500 for registration fees, flights, accommodations, and incidental expenses.
