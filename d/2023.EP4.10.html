The Exhibition Place board was asked to award a $1.64 million Dufferin Sub-Station relocation contract to Black and McDonald.

Official title: Relocation of Dufferin Sub-Station

AdoptedJun 8, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approving a contract with Black & McDonald Limited for the Relocation of Dufferin Sub-Station. The tender call was issued on April 20, 2023 through Exhibition Place Purchasing and Materials Stores via Bonfire Online Procurement System, and the recommendation is to award the contract to the lowest compliant bidder meeting tender requirements and specifications within the available budget.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the award of Contract No. 23-096-39675 (RFT No. EP105-2023) to Black and McDonald Limited for the Relocation of Dufferin Sub-Station in the amount of $1,639,519.02 which includes a cash allowance of $30,000.00 but excludes HST; this being the only acceptable tender submission received following the public tendering of the project.
