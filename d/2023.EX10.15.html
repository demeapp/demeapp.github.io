What the item proposed

During the COVID-19 pandemic, the sidewalk and curb lane dining components of the CaféTO program animated Toronto's commercial main streets and supported thousands of local restaurants, food suppliers and hospitality sector jobs. CaféTO is the City's program that (i) permits use of public property for cafés on sidewalks, (ii) permits use of public property for cafés in the curb lane of roadways, and (iii) allows, through zoning provisions, for dining on private property (no permit required). After operating (at no cost to participants) in its first three years as a temporary initiative focused primarily on the needs of restaurants, City Council made the curb lane dining stream of CaféTO permanent in February 2023.
