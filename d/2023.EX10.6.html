What the item proposed

Development charges are collected from new development and redevelopment for the purposes of recovering growth-related capital infrastructure costs. This report provides a statement of the Development Charge Deferred Revenues for the year ended December 31, 2022, as required by the Development Charges Act, 1997 (DC Act). The City’s financial statements are prepared in accordance with Public Sector Accounting Standards (PSAS). Under PSAS, development charges are recognized as liabilities called “deferred revenues” on the City's Statement of Financial Position when received and are recognized as earned revenue on the City's Statement of Operations and Accumulated Surplus when the capital expenditures these funds are intended to support are incurred.
