What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide City Council with the City of Toronto capital spending for the Nine-months period ended September 30, 2023, as well as the year-end projected expenditures to December 31, 2023. Furthermore, this report seeks Council's approval for in-year budget adjustments to the 2023-2032 Approved Capital Budget and Plan. What was decided

City Council on December 13, 14 and 15, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve in-year budget adjustments to the 2023-2032 Approved Capital Budget and Plan as detailed in Appendix 4 to the report (November 20, 2023) from the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer.
