The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide City Council with the Operating Variance for the nine months ended September 30, 2023 as well as projections to year-end. This report also requests City Council's approval for amendments to the 2023 Approved Operating Budget and recommended expenditure authority that have no impact on the City's Net Budget. In 2023, the City continues to experience significant financial impacts, both in the form of added costs and revenue losses as a direct result of the lasting impact of the COVID-19 pandemic. As a result, the 2023 Operating Budget was balanced based on the expectation of continued COVID-19 support funding from the Government of Canada and Province of Ontario with a total budget amount of $932.8 million.
