What the item proposed

City Council on July 19, 20, 21 and 22, 2022, adopted Item AU13.13 as amended, and in so doing, has forwarded the report (June 24, 2022) from the Auditor General to the Budget Committee for consideration during the 2023 Budget process. In recent years, the Auditor General has completed a number of comprehensive and complex audits and investigations that covered a broad range of programs and services across the City and its agencies and corporations. The Auditor General's 2022 Work Plan included 20 in-progress projects and upcoming projects for 2022 and another 16 projects to be initiated in 2022 or 2023, contingent upon availability of audit resources and consideration of emerging risks. What was decided

The Executive Committee referred the Item to the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer for consideration and report back to the Executive Committee prior to the 2024 budget process.
