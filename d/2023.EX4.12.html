What the item proposed

As an appointee by the City of Toronto the Board of Directors of the Good Roads Association, I attended the Annual Conference held on Sunday April 16 through to Wednesday April 19, 2023. This year's conference focused on the latest developments in transportation infrastructure, including new technologies and funding models.
