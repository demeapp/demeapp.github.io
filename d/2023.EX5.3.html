What the item proposed

The Province of Ontario's Subways Program, being delivered by Metrolinx, is a significant transit expansion project within the City of Toronto. The Subways Program consists of the Ontario Line, Scarborough Subway Extension, Eglinton Crosstown West Extension and Yonge North Subway Extension (YNSE) projects. This report provides updates on procurement, design, construction, and engagement milestones achieved by Metrolinx since staff last reported to City Council on the entirety of the Subways Program in June 2022 and addresses related City Council directives. Cummer Station Metrolinx’s business case for the YNSE considered a total of six stations, Cummer, Steeles, Clark, Royal Orchard, Bridge and High Tech. What was decided

City Council on June 14 and 15, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Province of Ontario to fund all associated costs required to deliver Cummer Station as part of the Yonge North Subway Extension. 2. City Council request the Executive Director, Transit Expansion to explore Federal infrastructure funding opportunities to contribute towards Cummer Station and to report back to the Executive Committee as part of the next transit expansion update report. 3.
