What the item proposed

I would like to express my gratitude to the City of Toronto’s, FCM Conference Liaison Table, in demonstrating exceptional efforts in preparing for and managing the conference. Special recognition should be given to Josie Scioli, Deputy City Manager Corporate Services; Aretha Phillips, Chief of Protocol; Karen Jones, Director Corporate Intergovernmental & Agency Relation; and Caphan Lieu, Senior Corporate Management Policy, as well as the numerous volunteers who contributed to the event's success. The City of Toronto hosted 1,500 municipal leaders from coast to coast-to-coast. The conference program included a wide range of activities, such as workshops, keynote speeches, panel discussions, plenaries, and innovative sessions.
