The official record

What the item proposed

The Beaches-East York Pilot Project is part of the Expanding Housing Options in Neighbourhoods (EHON) initiative. The purpose of the Pilot Project is to review appropriate City-owned sites in Beaches-East York (Ward 19) and work with the development industry and in consultation with the community to build a “missing middle” demonstration project. The processes and approaches developed through this initiative will inform how missing middle projects may be built on other sites, both publicly- and privately-owned, within the city. The purpose of this report is to seek City Council authority for the transfer of the City-owned property at 72 Amroth Avenue (“the Property”) from the City to a CreateTO managed corporation, Build Toronto Inc., or an affiliate of Build Toronto Inc. What was decided

City Council on July 19 and 20, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct that the City-owned property at 72 Amroth Avenue be allocated to the Beaches-East York Pilot Project for the purposes of developing missing middle housing solutions on City-owned, transit-oriented properties. 2.
