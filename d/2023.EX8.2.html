What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide City Council with the City of Toronto capital spending for the Six months period ended June 30, 2023, as well as the year-end projected expenditures to December 31, 2023. Furthermore, this report seeks Council's approval for in-year budget adjustments to the 2023 Adopted Capital Budget and Plan. What was decided

City Council on October 11 and 12, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve in-year budget adjustments to the 2023-2032 Capital Budget and Plan as detailed in Appendix 4 to the report (September 18, 2023) from the Interim Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer.
