The official record

What the item proposed

City Council, at its meeting of December 15 – 17, 2021, considered item EX28.2 – Final Tax Design and Steps to Implement a Vacant Home Tax in Toronto and approved the implementation of a Vacant Home Tax (VHT), with direction to report back in the fourth quarter of 2023 with findings from the first year of tax collections as part of an annual reporting requirement. This report includes an update on the implementation of the VHT, learnings from other jurisdictions that have implemented a similar tax, and recommendations for required changes to the VHT program. This report also details the communication plan to promote awareness of the VHT from its approval in July 2021 to its implementation throughout 2022 and building towards 2023.
