The official record

What the item proposed

On March 14, 2022, an application to amend the Official Plan, the former City of York Zoning By-law 1-83 and City of Toronto Zoning By-law 569-2013 for the property at 1-9 Oxford Drive was submitted to permit a residential development with a height of 26-storeys (88 metres in height, including mechanical penthouse). The proposal would result in a gross floor area of 18,989 square metres with a total of 278 residential units. On August 29, 2022, the combined Official Plan and Zoning By-law amendments application was appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) citing City Council’s failure to make a decision on the application within the prescribed timelines after the application was deemed complete.
