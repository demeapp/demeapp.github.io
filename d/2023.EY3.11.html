Clouston Avenue - Traffic Calming Poll Results

Adopted with changesFeb 27, 2023 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Etobicoke York Community Council is asked not to install speed humps on Clouston Avenue, as the resident poll did not reach the required response rate.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is reporting on the results of the traffic calming poll undertaken on Clouston Avenue, between Denison Road West and Weston Road. Polling ballots were mailed to the affected residents to determine the level of support in the community for the proposed installation of speed humps. The minimum response rate of 50% plus 1 votes needed to proceed with the installation was not achieved. Therefore, staff do not recommend the installation of speed humps on Clouston Avenue.

What was decided

That the Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Clouston Avenue, between Denison Road West and Weston Road, despite poll results that did not satisfy the polling criteria for the installation of speed humps pursuant to the City's Traffic Calming Policy. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway to install four speed humps on Clouston Avenue, between Denison Road West and Weston Road, generally as shown on Attachment 1, dated April 2022 attached to the report titled "Traffic Calming Poll Results - Clouston Avenue" from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
