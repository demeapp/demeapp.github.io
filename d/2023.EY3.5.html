This is a procedural item: Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to name a public lane west of Royal York Road 'Natale Bozzo Lane'.

Official title: Naming of an existing Public Lane west of Royal York Road, extending between Hay Avenue and Simpson Avenue

AdoptedFeb 27, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that the name "Natale Bozzo Lane" be approved to identify an existing public lane located west of Royal York Road, extending between Hay Avenue and Simpson Avenue. This naming proposal complies with the City of Toronto Street Naming Policy which can be found at https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/planning-development/street-naming/ This naming proposal is in alignment with the objectives of the Guiding Principles for Commemoration in the Public Realm.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Approved the name "Natale Bozzo Lane" for an existing public lane located west of Royal York Road, extending between Hay Avenue and Simpson Avenue.
