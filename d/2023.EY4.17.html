Thomas Fisher Drive, Edward Horton Crescent and Augustus Terrace - Heavy Truck Prohibition

AdoptedApr 3, 2023 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to ban heavy trucks on Thomas Fisher Drive, Edward Horton Crescent and Augustus Terrace.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting approval to implement heavy truck prohibitions, to be in effect at all times, on Thomas Fisher Drive, between Norseman Street and Edward Horton Crescent (south intersection), all of Edward Horton Crescent and all of Augustus Terrace. The introduction of these regulations is intended to reduce heavy truck traffic infiltrating the residential neighbourhood bounded by Norseman Street to the north and Islington Avenue to the east.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Prohibited heavy trucks at all times on Thomas Fisher Drive, between Norseman Street and Edward Horton Crescent (southeast intersection). 2. Prohibited heavy trucks at all times on Edward Horton Crescent, between Thomas Fisher Drive (northwest intersection) and Thomas Fisher (southeast intersection). 3. Prohibited heavy trucks at all times on Augustus Terrace, between Edward Horton Crescent and Thomas Fisher Drive.
