Toronto City Council · Decision

Councillor Perruzza asks that a rush-hour westbound left-turn signal be installed at Sheppard Avenue West and Abraham Welsh Road by Q3 2024.

Official title: Westbound Left Turn Signal at Sheppard Avenue West and Abraham Welsh Read

AdoptedApr 3, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office have received numerous calls from residents advising of the dangerous situation at making a left turn onto Abraham Welsh Rd while travelling Westbound on Sheppard Ave W. Residents have advised of long wait times to turn left thus creating conditions where cars are rushing to make the turn and putting pedestrians at risk. To ensure the safety of the community, I recommend implementing a Westbound Left Turn Signal during the morning and afternoon rush period at this location.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, continue preparations to implement a westbound left-turn phase that will operate during the morning and afternoon rush period at the intersection of Sheppard Avenue West and Abraham Welsh Road by the third quarter of 2024.
