The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending numerous amendments to the parking regulations on Chalkfarm Drive, in the area of Chalkfarm Pubic School. The proposed amendments will provide an area for short term parking for parents/guardians to accompany students to/from the school and improve safety/reduce congestion in the area by prohibiting stopping opposite the school. In addition, it is recommended that northbound "U" turns be prohibited in the area of the school.
