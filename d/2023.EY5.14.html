Etobicoke York Community Council receives a report on snow plough damage to sod and landscaping from the 2022-23 winter.

Official title: Snow Plough Damage To Sod Along Sidewalks and Other Landscape Features within the Road Right of Way

Adopted with changesMay 15, 2023 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

Following the recent 2022-23 winter season, there has been concerns over snow plough damage to property and sod along sidewalks and other landscape features within the public road right of way. The purpose of this report is to respond to the April 3, 2023 Etobicoke York Community Council's direction for an update concerning the extent of damage, the plan to make repairs, an overview of the responsibilities of the City, the snow clearing contractor, and of residents, and on progress toward making repairs this spring.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Operations and Maintenance, Transportation Services to review the findings of the report of the property and sod damage provided to Etobicoke York Community Council Members with the snow clearing contractor. 2. Requested the Director, Operations and Maintenance, Transportation Services provide a communication on timelines to the members of the Etobicoke York Community Council that can be shared with the public. 3. Directed the Director, Operations and Maintenance, Transportation Services to identify the source of complaints/reports in future communications to Councillors. 4. Requested the Director, Operations and Maintenance, Transportation Services to develop a visual marker that can be placed on locations that are designated for repair to inform the public of the work.
