Etobicoke York Community Council is asked not to install speed humps on Earlington Avenue after a failed poll.

Official title: Earlington Avenue - Traffic Calming Poll Results

AdoptedSep 18, 2023 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is reporting on the results of the traffic calming poll undertaken on Earlington Avenue, between Dundas Street West and Government Road. Polling ballots were mailed to the affected residents to determine the level of support in the community for the proposed installation of speed humps. The results of the traffic calming poll indicates that both the response rate and support for the installation of traffic calming measures fail to satisfy the minimum requirements. Furthermore, the technical requirements to warrant the installation were not satisfied. Therefore, staff do not recommend the installation of speed humps on Earlington Avenue.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Denied the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Earlington Avenue, between Dundas Street West and Government Road.
