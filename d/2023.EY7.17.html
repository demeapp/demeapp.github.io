Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to designate one EV-only parking space on Kings Lynn Road for a charging station.

Official title: On-Street Electric Vehicle Charging Station on Kings Lynn Road - Parking Amendment (Delegated Location)

AdoptedSep 18, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report outlines the parking by-law amendment required to implement the parking area for one (1) of the Electric Vehicle charging station location, Kings Lynn Road, for the roll out of one (1) charging station for which this Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This location has been reviewed and supported by the local Councillor.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Designated on-street electric vehicle charging station parking area for the following location: a. Kings Lynn Road, west side, from a point 9.9 metres north of Bloor Street West and a point 6 metres further north (1 space), for the exclusive use of electric vehicles and amend City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 950, Traffic and Parking, as described in Attachment 2 to the report (August 28, 2023) from the Director, Policy and Innovation, Transportation Services.
