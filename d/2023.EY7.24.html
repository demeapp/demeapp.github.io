Etobicoke York Community Council was asked to install an all-way stop at Habitant Drive and Bradstock Road.

Official title: Habitant Drive and Bradstock Road - All-Way Stop Control

AdoptedSep 18, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents have contacted me concerned about the safety on Habitant Dr and Bradstock Rd. There have been many near misses and recently an accident at this location. Staff have not yet conducted traffic counts however, given the precariousness of the situation, I am recommending the committee authorize an All-Way stop at this location.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Authorized an all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Habitant Drive at Bradstock Road.
