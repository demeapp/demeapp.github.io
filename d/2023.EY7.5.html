The official record

What the item proposed

This application is requesting exemption from the Part Lot Control provisions of the Planning Act for the lands municipally known as 111 Plunkett Road, formerly known as 135 Plunkett Road. The purpose of this application is to facilitate the creation of 42 separately conveyable parcels, each containing one semi-detached residential dwelling unit. The remainder of the subdivision, including the existing public right-of-way, public walkway, and separate development block (Phase 2, currently addressed as 135 Plunkett Road) would remain unchanged. The proposal is consistent with the Provincial Policy Statement (2020), conforms to the Growth Plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe (2020), and conforms to the Official Plan.
