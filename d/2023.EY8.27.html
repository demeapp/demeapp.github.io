The community council was asked to prohibit stopping on Strathburn Boulevard near a school.

Official title: No Stopping on Strathburn Boulevard

AdoptedOct 16, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents and the local school Principal have contacted me concerned about the safety on Strathburn Boulevard outside of St. Simon Catholic School. Strathburn Boulevard is lined with cars each morning and afternoon as many parents drop off and pick up their children and this has created a hectic and dangerous situation. Recently I moved a motion to install an All-Way Stop control at Melody and Strathburn to work to make the situation safer for families going to and from the school. With the addition of the stop sign, the parking regulations in front of the school must be re-visited. Therefore I am recommending the committee rescind the existing patchwork of parking regulations and prohibit stopping directly in front of St. Simon Catholic School.

What was decided

Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Strathburn Boulevard, between a point 48 metres south of Wallasey Avenue and a point 30 metres further south. 2.
