Amendments to Various Drinking Water Treatment Chemical Blanket Contracts

AdoptedJan 20, 2023 · Toronto City Council

General Government Committee was asked to grant authority to the General Manager, Toronto Water to amend existing Blanket Contract Number 47021759.

The official summary names a figure of $4,182,336.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend various blanket contracts for the supply and delivery of critical chemicals used in the purification of water at the City's four drinking water treatment plants. These amendments are requested in response to escalating unit costs resulting from increased raw material costs, global supply chain challenges and the war in Eastern Europe. The amendment also covers the costs associated with increased chemical usage to further enhance the treatment of water. The amendments requested total $4,182,336 (net of HST recoveries) and would increase the current target value of five (5) Blanket Contracts.

What was decided
