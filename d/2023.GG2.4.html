Toronto City Council · Decision

Amendment to Blanket Contract Number 47024566 with London Machinery Inc. (McNeilus Canada) for the supply and delivery of Mack Cab-over Truck Chassis, Tandem Axle, and Refuse Loading Packer Body

AdoptedMar 3, 2023 · Toronto City Council

The General Government Committee was asked to add $5,512,287 to the garbage truck chassis contract with London Machinery Inc. to cover a funding shortfall.

The official summary names a figure of $5,512,287.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek authority to increase the target value of Blanket Contract Number 47024566 issued to London Machinery Inc. (McNeilus Canada) for the supply and delivery of thirty-eight (38) latest model Mack cab-over truck chassis 28,123 kilograms (62,000 LBS) GVWR tandem axle, complete with 32 yd3 (24.47 m3) single stream, rear loading refuse packer bodies for a fixed period of two (2) years from September 8, 2022 to September 6, 2024. Fleet Services Division completed a review of the 2023 replacement plan and concluded that there will be a funding shortfall. The additional funding will allow Fleet Services to continue to support vehicle requisitions on behalf of Solid Waste Management. What was decided

The General Government Committee: 1. Granted authority to amend Blanket Contract Number 47024566 by increasing the total Blanket Contract target value by $5,512,287 net of HST and all applicable charges ($5,609,304 net of HST recoveries), revising the current Blanket Contract value from $25,380,787 ($25,827,489 net of HST recoveries) to $30,893,074 ($31,436,793 net of HST recoveries).
