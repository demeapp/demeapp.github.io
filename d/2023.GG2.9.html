Amendments to Various Purchase Orders for Rehabilitation and Upgrades at the Ashbridges Bay and Humber Treatment Plants

AdoptedMar 3, 2023 · Toronto City Council

The General Government Committee was asked to amend purchase orders for the Ashbridges Bay and Humber treatment plant upgrades to cover COVID-19 delays and replenish contingency.

The official summary names a figure of $4,196,394.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend various purchase orders related to the rehabilitation and upgrades being carried out at the Ashbridges Bay and Humber Wastewater Treatment Plants. These amendments are required for additional construction administration services to accommodate the revised construction schedules submitted by the general contractors as a result of delays due to COVID 19 issues. The amendments also include replenishing the contract contingency for the Operations Control Centre Rehabilitation and Upgrades Project at the Humber Treatment Plant that was depleted early in the contract schedule due to the implementation of COVID-19 protocols. The amendments requested total $4,196,394 (net of HST recoveries) and would increase the current value of three (3) purchase orders.
