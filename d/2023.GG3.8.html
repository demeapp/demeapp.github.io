Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Ariba Document Number 3775384972 to Dependable Truck and Tank Limited for the Non-exclusive Supply and Delivery of up to 30 Rescue-Pumper Fire Trucks for Toronto Fire Services

AdoptedApr 20, 2023 · Toronto City Council

The General Government Committee was asked to award the contract for up to 30 rescue-pumper fire trucks to Dependable Truck and Tank Limited for $33,590,760.

The official summary names a figure of $33,590,760.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to advise of the results of Request for Quotation Ariba Document Number 3775384972, for the non-exclusive supply and delivery of up to thirty (30) Rescue-Pumper Fire Trucks for Toronto Fire Services and to request authority to award the contract to Dependable Truck and Tank Limited, in the amount of $33,590,760 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($34,181,957 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries).

What was decided

The General Government Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 195- 8.4.A of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 195 (Purchasing), granted authority to award Request for Quotation, Ariba Document Number 3775384972 for the non-exclusive supply and delivery of up to thirty (30) Rescue-Pumper Fire Trucks to Dependable Truck and Tank Ltd., in the amount of $33,590,760 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($34,181,957 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries).
