What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide information on the results of a Negotiated Request for Proposal (nRFP) Doc3518771019. for the provision of administrative and underwriting services for employee benefit plans for the City of Toronto, (including Public Health) and the Agencies and Corporations along with Toronto Police Services (TPS) and Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) included in its benefits administration. The Agencies and Corporations (ABC’s) which provide benefits to employees under the City's umbrella are as follows: The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place; TOLive; Toronto Zoo; Toronto Public Library; and Community Centres and Arenas.
