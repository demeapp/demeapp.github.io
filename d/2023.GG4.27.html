What was decided

City Council on June 14 and 15, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Deputy City Manager, Corporate Services to report to the July 10, 2023 meeting of the General Government Committee on: a. the number of cleaners who have had their full time hours restored; and b. any further plans to restore the Full Time Cleaner complement to the 2012 level of 120.
