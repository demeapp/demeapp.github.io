The official record

What the item proposed

This report is to request authority from the General Government Committee to amend Non-Competitive Blanket Contract Number 47024284 issued to Canadian Red Cross Society for the provision of emergency services, including emergency lodging; staff and volunteers to conduct initial contact, assessment and service delivery; and subcontractor costs for security. The Canadian Red Cross provides shelter services at a Toronto hotel site near the airport (hereafter referred to as “site”) for Ukrainian arrivals needing emergency shelter. The site currently has a block of 70 rooms. As of June 16, 2023 there are 181 individuals from 68 households at the Toronto hotel site.
