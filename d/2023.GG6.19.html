The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek City Council authority to enter into a non-competitive contract with Sanscon Construction Ltd., for the replacement of the existing 300-millimetre cast iron watermain on O'Connor Drive between Sandra Road and Bermondsey Road in the estimated amount of $5,500,000 net of all taxes and charges ($5,596,800 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries). The total estimated cost of $5,500,000 net of all taxes and charges ($5,596,800 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) will be funded by Toronto Water. A non-competitive contract is necessary to enable an accelerated replacement of the existing watermain and water services on O'Connor Drive, given that the new green infrastructure under the current Contract 20ECS-LU-03FP is planned to be constructed directly above the existing watermain.
