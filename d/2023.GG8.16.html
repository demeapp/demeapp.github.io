The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks Council's authority for the adoption of the necessary by-laws to designate portions of three properties leased by the City of Toronto for use as City Councillor's constituency offices as Municipal Capital Facilities and to provide exemptions for municipal taxes and education taxes. The municipal capital facility agreements authorized by the by-laws will provide tax exemptions for approximately 3,163 square feet in total of combined space. The three properties and their respective square footage are provided below. 641B The Queensway, Ward 3: 1,592 square feet 329 Parliament Street / 0 Regent Street, Ward 13: 919 square feet 895 Lawrence Avenue East, Ward 16: 652 square
