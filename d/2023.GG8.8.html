The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to advise Toronto City Council, pursuant to Chapter 195 of the Toronto Municipal Code (Purchasing By-Law, Section 195-7.4), of a non-competitive contract with EllisDon Civil Limited, to procure construction services to repair damaged elements of the temporary Dufferin Street bridges over the Metrolinx rail corridor, south of Springhurst Avenue, for a total value of $1,320,000 excluding Harmonized Sales Tax ($1,343,232 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries). The issuance of this non-competitive contract was a matter of urgency to accelerate the work required to re-open Dufferin Street following an emergency closure on July 13, 2023, due to damage to the bridge structures.
