The official record

What the item proposed

At its November 8, 2022 meeting, the Board of Health requested additional information pertaining to the 2023 Toronto Public Health Operating Budget Submission. This report provides further details on the proposed confidential program efficiency included in that submission, as well as further information on Toronto Public Health's 2023 Service Levels (see Attachment 1). Toronto Public Health staff will address other requests for information via a presentation to the Board of Health.

What was decided

The Board of Health: 1. Authorized the public release of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (December 30, 2022) from the Medical Officer of Health, once City Council's decision on Item 2022.HL39.2 has been communicated to Unions and affected staff. 2. Authorized the public release of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (October 28, 2022) from the Medical Officer of Health, under Item 2022.HL39.2, once City Council's decision on Item 2022.HL39.2 has been communicated to Unions and affected staff. 3. Requested the Medical Officer of Health to review existing Toronto Public Health service levels in light of lessons learned over the COVID-19 pandemic and report the findings to the Board of Health Budget Committee in the second quarter of 2023.
