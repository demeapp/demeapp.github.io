The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with Section 25 of the Board of Health Procedures By-law , regular meetings of the Board are determined by the Board and a schedule of those meetings shall be publicly available by the first meeting of the Board in each calendar year. On November 23 and 24, 2022, City Council considered Item ST1.2 , 2023 Schedule of Meetings, and approved the 2023 schedule of meetings for City Council and its Standing Committees. The schedule also included proposed 2023 meeting dates for the Board of Health. The updated meeting schedule can be viewed on the City of Toronto's website at www.toronto.ca/council .
