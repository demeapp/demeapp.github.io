2023 Board of Health Committees and Appointments

Adopted with changesFeb 21, 2023 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: the Board of Health appoints its members to its Budget Committee, the MOH performance appraisal committee and the alPHa board for 2023.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report outlines the need for appointments of Board of Health members to two Committees of the Board of Health and one external Board for 2023: - the Board of Health - Budget Committee, - the Board of Health - Performance Appraisal of the Medical Officer of Health Committee, and - the Association of Local Public Health Agencies' (alPHa) Board of Directors and Board of Health Section. Toronto Public Health will report back to the Board of Health with recommendations for the appointment of members to the Toronto Drug Secretariat Implementation Panel pending the release of the updated Toronto Drug Strategy later this year.
