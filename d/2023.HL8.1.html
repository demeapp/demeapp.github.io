What the item proposed

With a diverse and growing population, the City of Toronto offers an extraordinary wealth of cultural, social, and economic opportunities, making it one of the most livable cities in the world. To keep Toronto vibrant and welcoming, individuals and communities in the city must have equitable access to opportunities to optimize their health and achieve their full potential. In June 2022, the Board of Health requested the Medical Officer of Health to develop a Mental Health and Addictions Strategy for Toronto. At the same time, the Board of Health directed the Medical Officer of Health to engage with stakeholders throughout summer and fall 2022 to refresh the Toronto Drug Strategy. This staff report presents Our Health, Our City: A Mental Health, Substance Use, Harm Reduction and Treatment Strategy for Toronto. What was decided

The Board of Health: 1. Endorsed Our Health, Our City: A Mental Health, Substance Use, Harm Reduction and Treatment Strategy for Toronto attached as Attachment 1 to the report (November 13, 2023). 2.
