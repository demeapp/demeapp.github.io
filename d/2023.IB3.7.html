The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on March 21, 2023, the Executive Committee considered Item EX3.7 and made recommendations to City Council. Item EX3.7 was considered and amended by City Council at its meeting on March 29, 30, and 31, 2023. Summary from the report (March 7, 2023) from the City Clerk: The purpose of this report is to recommend amendments to the Council Procedures to make electronic participation a permanent option for meetings of City Council and its Committees, and to authorize local boards to make amendments to their own rules of procedure.

What was decided

The Toronto Investment Board received the letter (June 7, 2023) from the City Clerk for information.
