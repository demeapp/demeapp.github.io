Infrastructure and Environment Committee was asked to request the General Manager, Solid Waste Management Services, the General Manager.

Official title: Operational response and mitigation strategies to address illegal dumping on roadways in the City of Toronto

AdoptedJan 30, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto residents have voiced their concerns to local councillors, 311 and the media about garbage that has been illegally dumped on local residential streets and major roadways. The types of garbage ranges from household waste to furniture to construction materials. Not only is illegal dumping unsightly but it infringes upon residents' enjoyment of their neighbourhoods and can also pose health and safety concerns. While the pandemic did force a redeployment of City staff, residents now expect that City services will have returned to pre-pandemic levels. Eliminating waste from roadways goes beyond just a coordinated cleanup effort and also requires preventative measures through education and by-law enforcement. We must ensure we are taking all available measures to maintain clean and safe roadways in the City of Toronto.

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. Requested the General Manager, Solid Waste Management Services, the General Manager, Transportation Services and the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards, to report back to an Infrastructure and Environment Committee meeting in the second quarter of 2023 on the City's operational response and mitigation strategies to address illegal dumping on local and major roadways.
