Ravine Slope Stabilization - Update

AdoptedJun 28, 2023 · Toronto City Council

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee receives a report on options for sharing the cost of ravine slope stabilization with benefiting property owners.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At the March 20, 2023 meeting of the Infrastructure and Environment Committee, the Infrastructure and Environment Committee requested the City Solicitor, in consultation with Finance and other City divisions and the Toronto Region Conservation Authority as appropriate, to report back to the Infrastructure and Environment Committee on options for: a. Requiring the owners of adjacent private property, that would directly benefit from ravine slope stabilization, to contribute to the cost of such work, and/or to transfer ravine land to the city or the Toronto Region Conservation Authority in lieu of such payment, b. Notifying insurers of the adjacent private property of the risk of slope instability, and c. Registering the risk of slope instability against the title of directly affected adjacent lands.
