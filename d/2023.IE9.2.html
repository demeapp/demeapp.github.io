The official record

What the item proposed

The General Manager, Transportation Services, is seeking City Council approval to decommission all of the 176 on-street single-space meters which are currently located in various Wards across the City of Toronto and accordingly delete all entries in Schedule III, Parking Meters, of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 910, Parking Machines, Parking Meters and Mobile Only Zones. Transportation Services is making this proposal after consulting with the Toronto Parking Authority with respect to the Toronto Parking Authority's experience with the City's on-street single-space meter paid parking program. Single-space meters have been in operation for a number of decades; however, the equipment is at end-of-life and requires extensive maintenance to continue operation.
