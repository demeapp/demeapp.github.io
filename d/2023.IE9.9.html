The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on October 16, 2023, the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee considered item DI3.2 - Ensuring Safe and Accessible Pedestrian Routes in Construction Zones, and made recommendations to City Council.

What was decided

City Council on December 13, 14, and 15, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services to work with all constructors working in the transportation right-of-way, to ensure there is clear notification and signage, on which both 311 phone number and the construction zone number are listed, for all residents of Toronto regarding construction projects and the planned accommodations for safe, accessible, and clearly marked pedestrian routes through them. 2. City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services to report back on related measures to the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee in the first quarter of 2024. 3.
