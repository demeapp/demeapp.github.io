The official record

What the item proposed

A new West Park Healthcare Centre facility is currently under construction and near completion at 82 Buttonwood Avenue as contemplated in City site specific By-laws 1001-2010 and 837-2017 together with a related Section 37 Agreement, as amended. Application for a plan of subdivision was also submitted within a portion of the hospital lands which has been approved and a Subdivision Agreement entered into with the City. The new roads and services have now been constructed. Both the Section 37 Agreement and the Subdivision Agreement contemplate registration of the plan of subdivision prior to occupancy of the new facility. The hospital is seeking to satisfy all contractual requirements and achieve occupancy in November 2023.
