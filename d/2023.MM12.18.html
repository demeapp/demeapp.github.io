What the item proposed

Due to the closure of the St. Clair Avenue West streetcar right of way, the Toronto Transit Commission is observing congestion along St. Clair Avenue West, slowing down the 512 St. Clair bus replacement service and general traffic. Toronto Transit Commission is recommending removing parking spaces and adding no stopping restrictions in strategic locations and times along St. Clair Avenue West, between Old Weston Road and Westmount Avenue, to alleviate these traffic concerns. Toronto Transit Commission has expressed that some of these locations have the potential to become dangerous for buses when there is snow accumulation without these measures being introduced. The proposed parking removal and no stopping during peak periods would take place effective immediately, and last until regular streetcar service is resumed.
