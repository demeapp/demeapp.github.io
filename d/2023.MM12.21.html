The official record

What the item proposed

At it's meeting of May 10, 11 and 12, 2023, Toronto City Council adopted Item TE4.6 approving a Rental Housing Demolition Application to demolish 12 rental dwelling units located at 152-164 Bathurst Street and 621-627 Richmond Street West, which included conditions that must be met prior to issuance of the rental housing demolition permit and residential demolition permit. These pre-conditions included a requirement that permits for excavation and shoring (conditional or full permit) for the approved development on the site had issued. Due to work associated with the required retention of the heritage façade of 164 Bathurst Street, the applicant would like to demolish the 3 rental dwelling units located at 164 Bathurst Street before the issuance of excavation and shoring permits for the approved development.
