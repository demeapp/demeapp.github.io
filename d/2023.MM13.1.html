City Council was asked to change parking rules on Welland Avenue so TTC buses can turn safely.

Official title: Welland Avenue - Parking That Blocks Toronto Transit Commission - by Councillor Dianne Saxe, seconded by Councillor Stephen Holyday

AdoptedDec 13, 2023 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto Transit Commission bus route “88 South Leaside” operates in part west on Moore Avenue, south on Welland Avenue, and west on St. Clair Avenue East. The Toronto Transit Commission advises that their bus operators are experiencing difficulty making a westbound left-turn from Moore Avenue onto Welland Avenue because private vehicles are frequently parked on the west side of Welland Avenue, in close proximity to Moore Avenue. This prevents buses from making this turn safely. Transportation Services, in consultation with the The Toronto Transit Commission, are recommending that stopping be prohibited at all times on the west side of Welland Avenue, between Moore Avenue and a point 43 metres further south. What was decided

City Council on December 13, 14 and 15, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, except public holidays, on the west side of Welland Avenue, between Inglewood Drive and Moore Avenue. 2. City Council prohibit parking from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, except public holidays, on the west side of Welland Avenue, between Inglewood Drive and a point 43 metres south of Moore Avenue. 3. City Council prohibit stopping at all times on the west side of Welland Avenue, between Moore Avenue and a point 43 metres further south.
