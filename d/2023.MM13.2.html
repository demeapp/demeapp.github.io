City Council was asked to oppose an appeal seeking to legalize 7 basement suites at 8 Adams Drive.

Official title: 8 Adams Drive - Request for City Solicitor to Attend at the Toronto Local Appeal Body - by Councillor Paul Ainslie, seconded by Councillor Jennifer McKelvie

AdoptedDec 13, 2023 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

The applicant applied to the Committee of Adjustment requesting variances from City-wide Zoning By-law 569-2013 (Application Number A0161/23SC), including seeking to legalize 7 secondary suites in the basement of single-detached dwelling. On October 18, 2023, the Scarborough District Panel of the Committee of Adjustment refused to grant the requested minor variances (Decision in Attachment 1). The applicant has appealed the refusal to the Toronto Local Appeal Body. In its report to Committee of Adjustment dated October 6, 2023, Scarborough District Community Planning staff determined that the proposal does not maintain the general intent and purpose of the Official Plan and Zoning By-law and is not in keeping with the existing physical character of the neighbourhood and recommended that the minor variance application be refused (Staff Report in Attachment 2). What was decided

City Council on December 13, 14 and 15, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the City Solicitor to attend the Toronto Local Appeal Body hearing with appropriate City staff in order to oppose the Appeal and to uphold the Committee of Adjustment’s refusal of Application A0161/23SC, respecting 8 Adams Drive. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to attempt to negotiate a resolution of the Appeal for 8 Adams Drive (Application A0161/23SC), and City Council authorize the City Solicitor to settle the matter on behalf of the City at the City Solicitor's discretion, after consultation with the Ward Councillor and the Director, Community Planning, Scarborough District.
