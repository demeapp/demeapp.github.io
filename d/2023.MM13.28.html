City Council was asked to oppose a Committee of Adjustment decision on a new home at 19 Forest Glen Crescent.

The official record

What the item proposed

The applicant applied to the Committee of Adjustment requesting variances from Zoning By-law 569-2013 (Application Number A0579/23NY), including variances to front yard setback, maximum driveway width, rear yard setback, front yard landscaping area, front yard soft landscaping area, lot coverage, and canopy encroachment, to construct a new detached dwelling at 19 Forest Glen Crescent. In its report dated November 30, 2023, Community Planning Staff noted concerns with the variances relating to the proposed reduction in front yard setback and the proposed increase in lot coverage. Staff recommended that the variances relating to front yard setback and lot coverage be refused, should the Committee of Adjustment approve the Application. A copy of the Planning Staff Report is attached. On December 7, 2023, the North York Panel of the Committee of Adjustment approved a revised front yard…

What was decided

City Council on December 13, 14 and 15, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to both appeal and attend the Toronto Local Appeal Body as a party, with appropriate City staff, in order to oppose the Committee of Adjustment’s decision regarding the proposed development at 19 Forest Glen Crescent (Application A0579/23NY). 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to retain outside consultants as necessary. 3. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to attempt to negotiate a resolution regarding Application A0579/23NY respecting 19 Forest Glen Crescent, and City Council authorize the City Solicitor to resolve the matter on behalf of the City, at the City Solicitor's discretion, after consultation with the Ward Councillor and the Director, Community Planning, North York District.
