What the item proposed

Participatory Budgeting is a system of direct democracy where residents suggest and then vote on how public funds are spent within their community. In May 2023, the local Councillor initiated a Participatory Budgeting process in Bayview Village community by hosting an Information Session and Ideas Generation Meeting. Local residents suggested over 70 project ideas which were then evaluated by City Staff in Parks, Forestry and Recreation and Transportation Services to assess costs and determine feasibility. In Fall 2023, the local Councillor advertised the November 3, 4, and 5, 2023 Participatory Budgeting Vote Days through various channels, including over 10,000 mailed flyers. Hundreds of votes were cast and the following projects were successful: 1. $90,000 to improve the pathway in Blue Ridge Park 2. $100,000 to improve the stairs at Clarinda Park 3. $100,000 to add fitness equipment…
