What the item proposed

During the pandemic ensuring that our small businesses survived was a key shared goal of all levels of government. From providing resources to allow for safe shopping, CafeTO, to financial supports many, but not all, small businesses survived. However for months local businesses have been raising the alarm about the upcoming deadline for the repayment of the Canada Emergency Business Account. Many businesses are having to take out loans to cover the repayment that is due, further increasing the financial pressure on them. Many of them are still feeling the pressure and effects of the pandemic. They have been clear that the Federal Government’s deadline will have a serious impact. Extending the repayment period would keep our local small businesses on a secure footing, helping them to continue to animate our neighbourhoods for years to come. What was decided

City Council on December 13, 14 and 15, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Federal Government to extend the repayment deadline for the Canada Emergency Business Account to December 31, 2024. 2. City Council forward this Item to all Toronto area Members of Parliament, for information.
