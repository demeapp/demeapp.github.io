Joint Appointment of Waterfront Toronto Chair - by Mayor John Tory, seconded by Councillor Ausma Malik

AdoptedFeb 7, 2023 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Council was asked to reappoint Stephen Diamond as Chair of Waterfront Toronto for three months, from March 21 to June 30, 2023.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am recommending that City Council, together with the Provincial and Federal governments, reappoint Stephen Diamond as a Director and Chair of Waterfront Toronto for a three month period, commencing on March 21, 2023 until June 30, 2023. Stephen Diamond has served as the Chair of the Board for the past four years. I understand that our government partners are moving forward in parallel to confirm the extension to Stephen Diamond's term, provided that City Council also agrees. An extension would provide continuity and continued leadership as the Board searches for and transitions to a new Chair. Under the Toronto Waterfront Revitalization Corporation Act, the Board of Directors of Waterfront Toronto is composed of up to 13 members. Each government appoints up to four members (12 total) and then may jointly appoint one member, who serves as the Chair.…

What was decided

City Council on February 7 and 8, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council reappoint Stephen Diamond as Director and Chair of Waterfront Toronto for a three month term commencing on March 21, 2023 until June 30, 2023.
