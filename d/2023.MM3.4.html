Street Vending Permit Transfer - by Councillor Ausma Malik, seconded by Councillor Alejandra Bravo

AdoptedFeb 7, 2023 · Toronto City Council

Council was asked to allow the transfer of a hot dog cart vending permit on Lake Shore Boulevard despite the moratorium on new and transferred sidewalk vending permits.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

David Roland Schooley has been the operator and Sidewalk Vending Permit holder of a hotdog cart on the north side of Lakeshore Blvd (30 meters east of Bay St) for many years. There is a current moratorium in place which does not allow applications for a Sidewalk Vending Permit to vend refreshments and/or vehicles from a non-motorized vehicle. COVID-19 has added immense financial pressures to families, particularly to those who rely on these types of businesses which are somewhat precarious in nature, and given that this is the family's main income they cannot afford to lose it. This Motion is urgent because if the permit is not transferred as soon as possible, they will be unable to operate the street vending unit and continue losing income needed to support the family.
