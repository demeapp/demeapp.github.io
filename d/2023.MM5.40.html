What the item proposed

This motion requests authority from City Council to amend the 2023-2032 Council Approved Capital Budget and Plan for Parks, Forestry and Recreation in the amount of $1,477,000 for improvements to Topham Park Clubhouse. Additional funds are required to proceed with the award of contract in order to start construction. The total project cost would be amended from $306,000 to $1,783,000, with cash flow commitments of $1,037,173 in 2023 and $439,827 in 2024. The improvements will be fully funded from the following sources: $1,037,173 from the South District Parkland Development Reserve Fund (XR2209) and $439,827 from the Section 42 Above 5% Cash-in-lieu generated from developments in proximity to Topham Park.
