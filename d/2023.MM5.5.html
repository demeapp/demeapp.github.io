What the item proposed

On February 27, 2023, Oshawa City Council adopted a motion in support of The City of Montreal’s motion of February 20, 2023 in support of a high-speed train instead of a high-frequency train. The federal government recently made an important decision to modernize the rail network in the Quebec-Toronto corridor. The federal government is instead aiming for a high-frequency train project rather than a high-speed train project, which will only slightly reduce the travel time between the major cities located in this corridor and therefore make the project much less attractive to the population.
