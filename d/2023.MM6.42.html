City Council was asked to remove a Danforth Avenue loading zone so a restaurant can open a CafeTO curb lane patio.

Official title: Removal of a Commercial Loading Zone to Accommodate a CaféTO Curb Lane Café on Danforth Avenue - by Councillor Paula Fletcher, seconded by Councillor Shelley Carroll

AdoptedMay 10, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

CaféTO has transformed the public realm and is a critical source of revenue for businesses and restaurants, many of which are still feeling the crushing impacts of the pandemic. An existing commercial loading zone near a Danforth restaurant limits their ability to secure a CaféTO curb lane café permit. The removal of this loading zone is required in order to support them to continue operating a successful curb lane café. This matter is urgent to ensure that the restaurant in question can receive their CaféTO permit at the same time as other local establishments.

What was decided

City Council on May 10, 11 and 12, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council rescind the existing commercial loading zone in effect at all times, on the south side of Danforth Avenue, between a point 33.8 metres east of Chester Avenue and a point 10 metres further east.
