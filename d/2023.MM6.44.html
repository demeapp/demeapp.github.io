The official record

What the item proposed

City staff have completed the design for the reconstruction of Peel Avenue and Gladstone Avenue, north of Queen Street West (formerly the "Dufferin Jog"). This project is an important capital improvement project for the City and residents. The design is a reflection of community expectations generated following the completion of an Environmental Assessment (EA) in 2010. With elimination of the Dufferin Jog in 2010, Peel Avenue and Gladstone Avenue were no longer required to accommodate all through traffic movement between Dufferin Street and Queen Street East. The proposed design widens the pedestrian boulevard area, narrows the roadway, improves accessibility and road safety, and incorporates cycling infrastructure and boulevard greening.
