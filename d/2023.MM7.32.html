Council was asked to waive the permit parking rule for a CaféTO patio at 1122 Queen West.

Official title: 1122 Queen Street West - CaféTO Curb Lane Café - Waiving Permit Parking Requirement in Municipal Code Chapter 742 - by Councillor Alejandra Bravo, seconded by Councillor Ausma Malik

AdoptedJun 14, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Hello 123, located at 1122 Queen St W, had submitted an application for a curb lane café on the flankage street Lisgar Street. Following a feasibility assessment by CaféTO staff it was determined that the café on Lisgar Street would not be compliant with City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 742, Sidewalk Cafés, Parklets and Marketing Displays. The curb lane café would be located on a local road with permit parking and would have an impact on existing alternate side parking. My office has worked with the CaféTO team and a solution has been developed that would ensure that the alternate side parking adjacent to the curb lane café would remaining unaffected. However, the requirement related to a curb lane café not being on a local road with permit parking would need to be waived. What was decided

City Council on June 14 and 15, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards or the General Manager, Transportation Services to exempt curb lane café permit area on the flankage of 1122 Queen Street West, Hello 123, from the requirements of Section 742-10.2A(2) of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 742, Sidewalk Cafés, Parklets and Marketing Displays.
