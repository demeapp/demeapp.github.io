What the item proposed

As part of the redevelopment of 260 Adelaide Street West, the City of Toronto will be required to relocate the current Adelaide Fire Hall from 260 Adelaide Street West to Metro Hall at 55 John Street. This move necessitates the reconfiguration of the existing uses on the ground floor of Metro Hall, including the space currently being occupied by the YMCA of Greater Toronto (the "YMCA"). Given the long-standing partnership between the City of Toronto and the YMCA in running many social programs including the Hospitality Training Program currently being operated at Metro Hall, staff have identified the building located at 238 Queen Street West (also known as St. Patrick's Market) as the most suitable location given its size and proximity to the Metro Hall location.
