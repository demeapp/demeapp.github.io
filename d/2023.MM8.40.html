City Council was asked to reopen and correct wording in its approval releasing development funds from 316 Bloor Street West.

The official record

What the item proposed

In June 2023, City Council approved a Section 37 payment for urgent bike infrastructure on Bloor Street between Avenue Road and Spadina. That motion contained an error, which has caused distress to some constituents. This motion will correct that error.

What was decided

City Council on July 19 and 20, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council reopen MM7.39 to replace the words “neighbouring residents' association” with the word “community”.
