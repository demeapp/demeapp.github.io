The official record

What the item proposed

The Corporations Nominating Panel must select candidates to interview for appointment to the Toronto Hydro Board. Following the interviews, the Corporations Nominating Panel will recommend one candidate for appointment to City Council.

What was decided

The Nominating Panel - Corporations: 1. Selected the following candidates for interview at its next meeting for appointment to the Toronto Hydro Board: THY2-379 THY2-320 THY2-318 THY2-352 THY2-426 THY2-366 2. Directed that the balance of the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (February 24, 2023) remain confidential in its entirety as it relates to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Hydro Board.
