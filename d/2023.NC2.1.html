Toronto City Council · Decision

Appointment of Public Member to the Toronto Hydro Board

Adopted with changesMar 20, 2023 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: the Corporations Nominating Panel interviews candidates and recommends one person for appointment to the Toronto Hydro Board.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Nominating Panel - Corporations will conduct interviews and recommend one candidate to City Council for appointment to the Toronto Hydro Board.

What was decided

The Nominating Panel - Corporations recommends that: 1. City Council appoint the following candidate to the Toronto Hydro Board, at the pleasure of Council, for a term of office ending March 31, 2025, and until a successor is appointed: Earl Davis 2. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Attachments 2 to 4 to the report (March 6, 2023) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety, as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Hydro Board.
