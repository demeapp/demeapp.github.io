Official title: Selection of Candidates for Interview for the Toronto Hydro Board

Adopted with changesMay 17, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Nominating Panel - Corporations must select candidates for interviews to fill a vacancy on the Toronto Hydro Board. Following the interviews, the Nominating Panel - Corporations will recommend one candidate for appointment to City Council.

What was decided

The Nominating Panel - Corporations: 1. Selected the following candidates for interview at its next meeting on June 2, 2023 for appointment to the Toronto Hydro Board: THY2-306 THY2-321 THY2-406 THY2-412 2. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1, 2 and 3 to the report (May 3, 2023) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Hydro Board.
