The official record

What the item proposed

The Nominating Panel - Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal must select candidates to interview at its next meeting for appointment to the Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal. Following the interviews, the Nominating Panel will recommend two candidates to City Council for appointment to the Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal.

What was decided

The Nominating Panel - Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal: 1. Selected the following candidates for interview at its next meeting for appointment to the Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal: Candidate DDR1-156 Candidate DDR1-129 Candidate DDR1-148 Candidate DDR1-157 Candidate DDR1-151 Candidate DDR1-117 Candidate DDR1-144 Candidate DDR1-143 2. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (October 31, 2023) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal.
