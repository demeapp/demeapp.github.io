The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report concerns a matter for which the North York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to consider an application by the owner(s) of 14 Langton Avenue to maintain an existing fence/privacy screen in the rear yard on the north side which exceeds the maximum height of 2.0 meters as specified in the City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447-1.2B(1) Table 1 – Maximum Height of Fences Item 9. As well as a wooden lattice fence that contains climbable material that does not comply with the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447-1.3E(1C) Construction Standards. A Notice of Violation was issued on May 6, 2021 and April 28, 2022.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Refused to grant the application for an exemption permit, by the owner(s) of 14 Langton Avenue, to allow them to maintain the existing fence/privacy screen with a height of greater than 2.0 meters, which does not comply with the Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447, Fences, specifically section Chapter 447-1.2B(1) Table 1 – Maximum Height of Fences Item 9. As well as a wooden lattice fence that contains climbable material that does not comply with the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447-1.3E(1C) Construction Standards.
