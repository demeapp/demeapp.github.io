The official record

What the item proposed

On May 30, 2017 an Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application was submitted to permit a long-term mixed use development plan for portions of the Yorkdale Shopping Centre site. The existing Yorkdale Shopping Centre is to remain on site and in operation. The proposed development is intended to occur in a phased manner over the next 20 years and beyond. This proposal introduces new streets, parks and open spaces, internal pedestrian connections, new residential uses in the form of mid-rise buildings and tall buildings, additional commercial and office uses, and a hotel. The master plan consists of eleven new development blocks, three new park blocks, one Privately-Owned, Publicly Accessible Space block.
