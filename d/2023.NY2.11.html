The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending changes to the parking regulations on Greenfield Avenue, between Yonge Street and Doris Avenue to reflect changes in the field with the newly constructed mid-block pedestrian crossover located approximately 120 metres west of Doris Avenue. Pay and Display parking and No Stopping regulations on both sides of the street need to be amended as per conditions of approval for the development at 4841 to 4881 Yonge Street, 2 and 50 Sheppard Avenue East, and 2, 4 and 6 Forest Laneway.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing stopping prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Greenfield Avenue, between Yonge Street and a point 87 metres east of Yonge Street. 2. Prohibited stopping at all times on the south side of Greenfield Avenue between Yonge Street to a point 20 metres east of Yonge Street and between a point 62 metres east of Yonge Street and a point 73 metres further east. 3.
