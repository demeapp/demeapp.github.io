The official record

What the item proposed

This report requests that North York Community Council deny the request for a permit to remove one privately owned tree located at 20 Pinemore Crescent. The applicant is seeking permission to remove the tree in order to widen the shared driveway. The subject tree is a bur oak (Quercus macrocarpa), measuring 111 centimetres in diameter. The Tree By-laws do not support the removal of this tree as it is healthy and maintainable.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Denied the permit to remove one privately owned tree located at 20 Pinemore Crescent.
