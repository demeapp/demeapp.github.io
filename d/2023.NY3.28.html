Traffic Control Signals at Wilson Avenue and Champlain Boulevard

AdoptedMar 29, 2023 · Toronto City Council

Council was asked to approve new traffic signals at Wilson Avenue and Champlain Boulevard, paid for by the developer of 470-530 Wilson Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

There is construction underway of a new development on the north side of Wilson Avenue east of Wilson Heights Boulevard. The addresses are 470-530 Wilson Avenue. There were 110 rental units. The new development will consist of 161 rental units, 394 condo units and 387 parking spaces. There will also be office and retail space at grade along Wilson Avenue. Transportation Services is requesting the approval of a traffic control signal at the intersection of Wilson Avenue and Champlain Boulevard to accommodate the future increased pedestrian and vehicular traffic coming from the site. Furthermore, there will be no financial impact as the developer has agreed to pay for and install the traffic control signal and all related infrastructure at no cost to the City of Toronto.

What was decided

City Council on March 29, 30 and 31, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the installation of new traffic control signals at the intersection of Wilson Avenue and Champlain Boulevard/Private Access. 2. City Council direct that the installation of traffic control signals at the intersection of Wilson Avenue and Champlain Boulevard be co-ordinated with the development site located at 470 - 530 Wilson Avenue and adjacent planned roadway and infrastructure improvements, to the satisfaction of the General Manager, Transportation Services.
