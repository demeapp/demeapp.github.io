The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. 2500 Yonge Street Limited is constructing a 14-storey mixed-use condominium building with ground floor retail space at 2490-2514 Yonge Street. The site is located at the north-west corner of Yonge Street and Castlefield Avenue. Transportation Services is requesting approval to temporarily close the north sidewalk and a portion of the westbound curb lane on Castlefield Avenue, and temporarily close a portion of the right-of-way on Yonge Street abutting the site, for a period of 34 months from April 5, 2023 to February 1, 2026, to facilitate construction staging operations.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing no parking prohibition in effect during all times on the north side of Castlefield Avenue, between Yonge Street and a point 46 metres west, from April 5, 2023 to February 1, 2026. 2. Rescinded the existing no parking prohibition in effect during all times except Sunday on the south side of Castlefield Avenue, between Yonge Street and a point 46 metres west, from April 5, 2023 to February 1, 2026. 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on both sides of Castlefield Avenue, between Yonge Street and a point 46 metres west, from April 5, 2023 to February 1, 2026. 4.
