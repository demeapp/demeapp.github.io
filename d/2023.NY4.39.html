Councillor Colle asks North York Community Council to re-open the Melrose Avenue decision and poll residents on traffic calming.

Official title: Melrose Avenue - Traffic Calming - Amendment to NY3.10

AdoptedApr 4, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On February 22nd, 2023 North York Community Council adopted recommendations from staff to not install speed humps along Melrose Avenue between Yonge Street and Jedburgh Road. The purpose of this motion is to re-examine this study and ask City Staff conduct a poll of eligible householders on this block of Melrose Avenue to see determine if there is support for traffic calming measure to be instated.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item NY3.10 titled: Melrose Avenue – Traffic Calming. 2. Deleted its prior decision on Item NY3.10 and adopted instead the following: That North York Community Council: 1. Directed the Director of Traffic Management, Transportation Services, to request the City Clerk to poll eligible householders on Melrose Avenue, between Yonge Street and Jedburgh Road to determine whether residents support the installation of traffic calming, in accordance with the City of Toronto Traffic Calming Policy. 2. Subject to favourable results of the poll: a. authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Melrose Avenue, between Jedburgh Road and Yonge Street. b. directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway to install 4 speed humps on Melrose Avenue, between Jedburgh Road and Yonge Street, for traffic calming…
