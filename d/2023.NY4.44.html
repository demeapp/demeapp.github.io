Toronto City Council · Decision

Councillor Burnside asks North York Community Council to cut the speed limit on Langbourne Place from 50 to 40 km/h.

Official title: Langbourne Place - Speed Limit Reduction

AdoptedApr 4, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Langbourne Place is a local road with two way traffic extending west from The Donway West to a dead end. The speed limit for roads of similar nature in the vicinity is 40 km/h. I have heard from concerned residents about vehicles regularly exceeding the speed limit on Langbourne Place and I am requesting that the speed limit be reduced from 50 km/h to 40 km/h, to bring it in line with its other local roads in North York.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 50 kilometres per hour to 40 kilometres per hour on Langbourne Place, between The Donway West and the west end of Langbourne Place.
