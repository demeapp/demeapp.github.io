Toronto City Council · Decision

Councillor Robinson asks that the damaged median on Teddington Park Avenue be restored in 2023.

Official title: Teddington Park Avenue - Restoring the Median

AdoptedApr 4, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The median on Teddington Park Avenue, particularly between Yonge Street and Bocastle Avenue, is in a significant state of disrepair. Due to the lower-than-regulated height, vehicles routinely drive over the median, causing damage to the sod and greenery and inhibiting growth in certain areas. The median is not only a community amenity, but also an important safety feature that helps narrow lanes and reduce vehicle speeds on a residential street. While I have reported this issue to Transportation Services on multiple occasions, including as recently as 2022, a long-term solution to restore and maintain the median has not been identified and it continues to deteriorate.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services to restore the median on Teddington Park Avenue in 2023 and consult with the local Councillor on long-term solutions.
