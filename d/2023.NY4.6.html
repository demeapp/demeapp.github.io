City Council was asked to send the City Solicitor to oppose the appeal for a 35-storey, 472-unit building at Lyon Court and Roselawn.

The official record

What the item proposed

On February 25, 2022, a complete application to amend the Zoning By-law (21 235591 NNY 08 OZ) was submitted to permit a 35-storey (111.40 metres, excluding mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building with 472 dwelling units and 397.5 square metres of retail uses at grade. A complete site plan control application in support of the same was submitted on November 9, 2021. A separate Rental Housing Demolition application (21 235748 NNY 08 RH) has also been submitted which proposes to demolish the existing 53 rental dwelling units on the site and replace them in the new building. On September 14, 2022, the applicant appealed the Zoning By-law amendment application to the Ontario Land Tribunal due to Council not making a decision within the time frame specified in the Planning Act. A Case Management Conference was held on February 23, 2023. Notice of the case management conference was provided…

What was decided

City Council on May 10, 11 and 12, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the City Solicitor and appropriate City staff to attend the Ontario Land Tribunal in opposition to the current Application regarding the Zoning By-law Amendment appeal for the lands at 131 Lyon Court and 836, 838 Roselawn Avenue and to continue discussions with the Applicant in an attempt to resolve outstanding issues. 2.
