The official record

What the item proposed

The Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment application proposes to redevelop the lands at 22-36 Greenbriar Road with a 22-storey (68.4 metres) residential building containing 320 dwelling units. The proposed building has a total gross floor area of 24,246 square metres. Vehicular access to the site would be provided by a single driveway via Talara Drive. The proposed development would provide a total of 145 parking spaces located in a two-level underground garage and a total of 241 bicycle parking spaces. The Rental Housing Demolition application proposes to demolish two rental dwelling units within eight existing semi-detached houses on the lands.
