Council staff were asked to install a bigger Do Not Block Driveway sign at 1900 Sheppard East.

Official title: 1900 Sheppard Avenue East (Crossroads Complex) - Addressing Congestion

AdoptedJun 20, 2023 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

Sheppard Avenue East is one of the busiest roadways in Don Valley North. Over the last several years, residents who reside in the condominium complex at 1900 Sheppard Avenue East also known as 'Crossroads' have seen a significant increase in vehicular congestion, and have expressed traffic safety concerns about access and egress into and out of the complex. A "Do Not Block Driveway" sign currently in place has been insufficient to address these issues and this motion recommends the installation of a larger sign and to bring it into sightline to address these concerns. The entrance to the condominium complex is located right next to a busy Toronto Transit Commission bus stop, with the bus lane also being utilized by vehicles turning right to enter Highway 404 located one intersection over. The current 'Do Not Block Driveway' sign is installed on a hydro pole with multiple other signs…

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services, to replace the "Do Not Block Driveway" sign for the driveway to 1900 Sheppard Avenue East with a sign at least 50 per cent larger.
