What the item proposed

On June 1, 2023 Transportation Services advised that the installation of all-way stop control is warranted at the intersection of Hillmount Avenue and Shermount Avenue, based on the study results summarized in the table below. Staff advised that a report recommending installation could be submitted to the September 7, 2023 meeting of North York Community Council.
