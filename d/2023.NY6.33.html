3441 Yonge Street - Trattoria Otto - CaféTO Curb Lane Café - Waiving Intersection Setback Requirement in Municipal Code Chapter 742

AdoptedJun 20, 2023 · Toronto City Council

North York Community Council was asked to exempt Trattoria Otto at 3441 Yonge Street from the 15-metre intersection setback so its CaféTO curb lane café can be approved.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Trattoria Otto restaurant, located at 3441 Yonge Street, had submitted an application for a curb lane café. Following a feasibility assessment by CaféTO staff, it was determined that the café would not meet the 15 metre setback requirement from the intersection at Yonge Street, between Glen Echo Road and Teddington Park Avenue, as per City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 742, Sidewalk Cafés, Parklets and Marketing Displays. The curb lane café would only be setback 12.9 metres and on this basis the application was refused. After further discussions with Transportation staff, they examined the corner setback more closely and feel that a reduced intersection setback of 12.6 metres would not compromise safety and would support the applicant's request for curb lane café. This motion would provide the pathway to exempt Trattoria Otto restaurant from the setback requirement of Municipal Code…

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards, or the General Manager, Transportation Services, to exempt curb lane café permit area at 3441 Yonge Street, Trattoria Otto, from the requirement of § 742-10.4. A(4) of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 742, Sidewalk Cafés, Parklets and Marketing Displays.
