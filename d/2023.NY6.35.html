Westgate Boulevard - Replace a "No Parking" Sign with an Amended Parking Restriction

Adopted with changesJun 20, 2023 · Toronto City Council

North York Community Council was asked to replace a No Parking sign on Westgate Boulevard with an amended 8 a.m. to 6 p.m., Monday to Thursday restriction.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Over the past several months, our office has been attempting to resolve an issue with respect to the posted "No Parking" sign that is currently in place along Westgate Boulevard near the intersection with Armour Boulevard. As a compromise, I proposed that the aforementioned sign to be replaced with a sign that states an amended parking restriction of no parking between 8:00am to 6:00pm from Monday to Thursday. This proposed amended parking restriction will be along Westgate Boulevard between Armour Boulevard and Edinburgh Drive.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Monday to Thursday, on the west side of Westgate Boulevard, between Armour Boulevard and Edinburgh Drive.
