North York Community Council was asked to correct a measurement in its June 2023 Bridle Path stopping prohibition.

Official title: Updating Parking Signage on The Bridle Path

AdoptedSep 7, 2023 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

North York Community Council at its meeting on June 20, 2023, adopted Item NY6.34 entitled "The Bridle Path - Changing Parking Signage" approved amendments to parking regulations on the Bridle Path. The purpose of this motion, with support from Transportation Services, is to request a reopening of Item NY6.34 to amend the adopted stopping prohibition measurement from 57 metres to 47 metres east of Bayview Avenue on the south side of The Bridle Path to correspond with the current signage placement.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item NY6.34, entitled "The Bridle Path - Changing Parking Signage" with respect to Part 3 of the decision of the North York Community Council. 2. Deleted Part 3 of its decision with respect to Item NY6.34 and adopted instead the following: "3. North York Community Council prohibit stopping in effect at all times on the south side of The Bridle Path, between Bayview Avenue and a point 47 metres east."
