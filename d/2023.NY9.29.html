The community council was asked to reverse parking changes on St. Clements Avenue.

The official record

What the item proposed

At our the June 20th meeting of North York Community Council, my motion was passed for new parking regulations and the installation of new parking signs along St. Clements Avenue between Yonge Street and Duplex Avenue. These changes were necessary to accommodate the opening of a new school at the southwest corner of Yonge Street and St Clements since there were already two schools on the same block. These changes were requested by the community and existing schools in order to ensure the orderly and efficient pick-up and drop-off for students, as well as their parents and residents of the area. Since these changes were made, the third school indicated that it would no longer open as planned. What was decided

North York Community Council : 1. Re-opened item NY6.32. 2. Rescinded its prior decision on item NY6.32, including all applicable by-law changes and reverted to the pre-existing parking restrictions on St. Clements Avenue between Duplex Avenue and Yonge Street and for Transportation Services to make the related sign changes.
