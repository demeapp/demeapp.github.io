Toronto City Council · Decision

The community council was asked to remove outdated no-left-turn signs on St. Clements Avenue.

Official title: St. Clements Avenue - Removing No Left Turn Signs

AdoptedNov 14, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The St. Clements School in my ward has undergone an extensive construction project in order to accommodate the school community. Part of this construction included the removal of the circular driveway that was used on the old school site for pick-up and drop off. At this time, there were two no left turn signs installed on St. Clements Avenue to prevent cars from accessing this circular drive via the exit. Since the site had changed with the addition to the school, this circular drive is no longer in use, but the no left turn signs have remained posted. These signs are now causing confusion for drivers and local police who read the signs to mean no left turn on to Duplex Avenue for westbound traffic. I request that North York Community Council vote to remove these signs to clear up the confusion as they are no longer needed by the school community to access or exit the property.

What was decided

North York Community Council : 1. Rescinded the westbound left-turn prohibition in effect at all times at St. Clements Avenue and the driveway to premises numbers 21-45 St. Clements Avenue located on the south side of St. Clements Avenue, at a point 20 metres east of Duplex Avenue (north intersection).
