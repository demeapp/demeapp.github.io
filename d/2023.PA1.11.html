The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto Parking Authority is seeking to obtain board approval to complete extensive concrete repairs at Car Park 58, located at 9 Bedford Road. Subsequent contracts will be tendered for service improvements in later phases of the project development. Car Park 58 was built in 1987 and is a combined surface lot and four (4) level underground parking garage, providing approximately 399 parking spaces total. The underground parking garage is integrated with the Yorkville Royal Sonesta Hotel facing 220 Bloor Street West and shares hotel maintenance rooms residing within the car park. Pre-pandemic (2019) gross revenues were $2.8 Million, and it is consistently amongst our top five performers. October year-to-date revenues are $1.85 Million or 67percent of 2019 levels. What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Provided authority to the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to award the Construction Contract for the 5058-41 Garage Restoration at 9 Bedford Road (Car Park 58) to Complete Concrete Restoration Ltd., having submitted the lowest compliant bid and meeting all specifications in conformance with the Construction Tender requirements, in the amount of $5,876,500 plus $823,500 as a contingency allowance, being the sum total amount of $6,700,000 excluding Harmonized Sales Tax.
