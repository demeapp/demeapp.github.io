What the item proposed

As part of our vision to become the world’s best provider of sustainable parking, bike share and last mile mobility experiences, Toronto Parking Authority is executing an ambitious rollout plan for Electric Vehicle charging across the City of Toronto. The build-out of Toronto Parking Authority’s Electric Vehicle charging network is well underway and is planned to grow to more than 650 Electric Vehicle chargers in off-street (garages and lots) and on-street Green P locations by the end of 2024. The provision of Electric Vehicle charging services is a key component of Toronto Parking Authority’s transformation to a mobility services provider, supports the City of Toronto’s Net Zero Strategy and will support Toronto Parking Authority’s growth and financial returns over the long-term.
