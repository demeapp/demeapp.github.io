The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority operates an extensive inventory of 307 off-street parking facilities containing over 40,000 spaces. 145 of these locations, including more than 24,000 parking spaces are managed and operated by Toronto Parking Authority under arrangements collectively described as "Parking Management Agreements" or "PMA’s". Through Parking Management Agreements, Toronto Parking Authority provides some, or all of the following parking management services: undertaking of capital improvements, provision of payment equipment including use of the Green P Mobile App, coin collection services, enforcement and maintenance. In most cases, Toronto Parking Authority provides a full range of services, however, a more limited arrangement may be provided in some cases at the request of the contracting party.
