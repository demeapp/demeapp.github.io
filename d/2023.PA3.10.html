The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek authorization from the Board of Directors, Toronto Parking Authority, for the garage restoration project located at 20 St. Andrew Street (Car Park 68) valued at $6,538,450 plus $1,000,000 as a contingency allowance, being the sum total of $7,538,450 excluding HST. Car Park 68, located at 20 St. Andrew Street (Kensington Garage), is a 45-year-old, six (6) story parking structure with 417 spaces in the Kensington Market neighborhood. This facility is one of the top 15 most productive garages in Toronto Parking Authority’s portfolio delivering a forecasted revenue of $1.65 Million in 2023; +$500,000 better than 2019 pre-pandemic performance. As such, this investment is consistent with our strategy of prioritizing investments for the purposes of safety, financial sustainability and brand reputation. What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority to award the Garage Restoration project located at 20 St. Andrew Street (Car Park 68) for 2023-CON-CP68-B10 to Restorers Group, having submitted the lowest compliant bid and meeting all specifications in conformance with the Construction Tender requirements, in the amount of $6,538,450 plus $1,000,000 as a contingency allowance, being the sum total of $7,538,450 excluding Harmonized Sales Tax.
