What the item proposed

At its meeting on March 28, 2023, the Toronto Parking Authority – Audit and Risk Management Committee considered Item PR1.6 and made recommendations to the Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority. Summary from the report (March 6, 2023) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority The purpose of this report is to provide a recommendation for the performance targets to be utilized in the Toronto Parking Authority's 2023 Short-Term Incentive Program. Toronto Parking Authority is on its accelerated journey towards its vision of becoming the world's best provider of sustainable parking, bike share and last mile mobility experiences. To support this effort, Toronto Parking Authority has been addressing our significant capability gaps with a particular emphasis on acquiring new talent in an extremely competitive labour market. What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Directed the President, Toronto Parking Authority to implement the 2023 Corporate Goals and Key Performance Indicator targets for the Short-Term Incentive Plan as identified in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 6, 2023) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority. 2. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 6, 2023) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority remain confidential in its entirety, as it deals with personal matters about identifiable persons.
