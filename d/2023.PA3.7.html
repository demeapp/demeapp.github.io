What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide an update to the Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors about the City - Toronto Parking Authority Relationship Framework, which was adopted by City Council, with amendments, at its meeting of March 29, 30 and 31, 2023. Amendments to the Relationship Framework include a recognition that Toronto Parking Authority will support the City's Housing Action Plan and assist the City to reach net-zero climate pollution by 2040, in a manner consistent with TransformTO. These changes, as well as other minor changes made to reflect input from City Divisions and Agencies are summarized in Table 1 and included in an edited version of the document appended to this report (refer to Attachment 1).
