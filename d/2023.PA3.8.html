What the item proposed

A key priority of Management’s approved 2023 Annual Operating and Capital Plan is to initiate the modernization of its' parking payment equipment. As a critical first step in our multi-year journey, Management is seeking authorization from the Board of Directors, Toronto Parking Authority, to purchase 225 pay-by-plate on-street payment machines valued at $2,056,961.25. The current pay and display machines, which have been in the market since 1998, are approaching their end-of-serviceable-life and require replacement. As background, The Toronto Parking Authority's current on-street parking portfolio includes approximately 21,500 parking spots and accepts payment via 2,728 pay-and-display parking machines, 180 single-space parking meters, and our Green P App. In 2022, Toronto Parking Authority’s on-street paid parking program generated $44.7 million in revenues with 30 percent generated…

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized payment of $2,056,961.25 (excluding Harmonized Sales Tax) for the purchase of 225 Pay by Plate parking machines from Precise Parklink Inc., which will be made pursuant to the terms and conditions of the Master Services Agreement between the Toronto Parking Authority and Precise Parklink Inc. dated July 1, 2016.
