What the item proposed

At its meeting on March 21, 2023, the Executive Committee considered Item EX3.4 and made recommendations to City Council. Item EX3.4 was considered and amended by City Council at its meeting on March 29, 30, and 31, 2023. Summary from the report (March 7, 2023) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority: The purpose of this report is to transmit the City's Relationship Framework for the Toronto Parking Authority to City Council for approval. A Relationship Framework is City Council's governing instrument used for setting out the specific mandate, delegations of authority, legislative framework, policy directions and accountability for City Agencies, and their relationship to the City of Toronto. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority deferred consideration of item PA4.10 - City of Toronto Relationship Framework for the Toronto Parking Authority to its next meeting on July 18, 2023.
