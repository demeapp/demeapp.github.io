The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority has prepared its 2022 Annual Report, which outlines the accomplishments of the prior year and includes the consolidated audited financial statements and a joint message from Toronto Parking Authority's Board Chair and President. The 2022 draft Annual Report included in Attachment 1 presents all of these materials with the exception of the financial statements. The 2022 financial statements are being submitted to the Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors for approval at this meeting through a separate report (PA4.1). Once approved, Toronto Parking Authority will incorporate the financial statements into the 2022 Annual Report and finalize for distribution and publishing on Toronto Parking Authority's website.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Approved the 2022 Toronto Parking Authority Annual Report for distribution.
