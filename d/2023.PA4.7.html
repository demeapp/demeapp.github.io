What the item proposed

As part of our vision to become the world’s best provider of sustainable parking, bike share and last mile mobility experiences, Toronto Parking Authority is executing an ambitious rollout plan for Electric Vehicle charging across the City of Toronto. Responding to City Council direction that Toronto Parking Authority assume responsibility for the operation, maintenance and continued expansion of the City’s on-street Electric Vehicle charging program, Toronto Parking Authority began to mobilize plans to deliver its first 50 on-street Electric Vehicle chargers at 30 locations across eight (8) Wards in 2023.
