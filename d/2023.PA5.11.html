Litigation Update Regarding Option to Purchase Retail Component of Development at 935 Eglinton Avenue West (Municipal Car Park 655)

AdoptedJul 18, 2023 · Toronto City Council

The Parking Authority board was asked to adopt confidential instructions on its $7 million lawsuit over the 935 Eglinton Avenue West development.

The official summary names a figure of $7.0 million.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This is a report on the status of the legal action commenced by Toronto Parking Authority on January 17, 2019 against BSaR (Eglinton) Ltd. and BSaR (Eglinton) LP (both together referred to as BSaR), and BSaR's principals, Tarek Sobhi, and Tyler Hershberg (the Action). In the Action, Toronto Parking Authority is claiming damages in the amount of $7.0 million for breach of contract related to an option held by Toronto Parking Authority to purchase the retail unit (Retail Component) of a joint venture development at 935 Eglinton Avenue West (the Property).

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Adopted the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (June 29, 2023) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority and the City Solicitor. 2. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (June 29, 2023) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority and the City Solicitor remain confidential in its entirety, as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
