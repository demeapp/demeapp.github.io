What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors has been requested by City Council to report on the feasibility of providing parking discounts to Canadian Veterans. Toronto Parking Authority currently has a targeted program that provides free parking to Canadian Veterans at all Toronto Parking Authority off-street parking facilities located on lands owned by the City of Toronto. This program supports Canadian Veterans on four (4) commemorative days each year and applies to all vehicles that display a valid Ontario Veteran’s license plate (Poppy Plate). In addition, Toronto Parking Authority also provides free localised parking in coordination with the Royal Canadian Legion in support of events honouring or commemorating a significant military event that may occur from time to time throughout the city.
