The official record

What the item proposed

As part of its vision to become the world’s best provider of sustainable parking, bike share and last mile mobility experiences, Toronto Parking Authority is executing an ambitious rollout plan for Electric Vehicle (EV) charging across the City of Toronto. In recognition of Toronto Parking Authority’s extensive assets and executional capabilities, City Council directed Toronto Parking Authority in June of 2022 to assume the responsibility for the operation, maintenance and continued expansion of the on-street EV Charging Program and further requested Toronto Parking Authority to deliver a minimum of 50 on-street EV chargers in 2023. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority to negotiate, enter into and execute a Purchase Agreement with Toronto Hydro-Electric System Limited for the purchase of the forty-seven (47) existing on-street EV charging stations, inclusive of all supporting electrical equipment, generally in accordance with the terms and conditions contained in Attachment A to the report (September 12, 2023) and any such other or amended terms and conditions as may be acceptable to the President, Toronto Parking Authority, and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor. 2.
