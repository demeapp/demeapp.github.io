The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority has established a range of Board-enacted Policies which form the guiding principles and procedures by which it operates. The Occupational Health and Safety Act requires that two policies relating to the Act be reviewed on an annual basis. These policies were most recently reviewed and affirmed by the Board at its meeting of September 29, 2022. This report confirms that Toronto Parking Authority's Joint Health and Safety Committee concurs with the policies, with no recommended changes to the following Toronto Parking Authority policy resolutions for 2023, namely: · Policy Resolution 4-19: Occupational Health and Safety Policy; and · Policy Resolution 4-22: Workplace Harassment and Violence Policy.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Reviewed and endorsed Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolutions as follows: a) Policy Resolution 4-19 - Occupational Health and Safety Policy, attached as Appendix A; and b) Policy Resolution 4-22 - Workplace Harassment and Violence Policy, attached as Appendix B; and directed staff to enact these Policy Resolutions, as approved.
