The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council amend Chapter 667 of the Toronto Municipal Code, the Residential Rental Property Demolition and Conversion Control By-law (“Chapter 667”), in response to recommendations made by the Auditor General respecting tenant access to affordable rental replacement housing based on financial need, and to improve administrative coordination with Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 363 (Building Construction and Demolition) (“Chapter 363”) and to improve the clarity of the process for the public. Chapter 667 prohibits the demolition or conversion of residential rental units in buildings containing six or more residential units, of which at least one is rental, without obtaining a permit from the City.
