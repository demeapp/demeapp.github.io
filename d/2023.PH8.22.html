City Council was asked to seek a provincial zoning order to relocate Iqbal Foods to 100 Thorncliffe Park Drive.

Official title: Saving Iqbal Foods and Supporting Ontario’s Halal Food Supply Chain

The official record

What the item proposed

Iqbal Foods has been a fixture in the Thorncliffe Park and Flemingdon Park communities for more than 30 years, providing access to culturally-necessary foods that are not available elsewhere in the area. This business is also a vital part of the Halal food supply chain, providing 80% of Ontario’s supply of Halal food products that meet the strictest religious requirements. Iqbal Foods’ long standing retail and meat processing facility at 2 Thorncliffe Park Drive has been expropriated to allow Metrolinx to build a maintenance and storage facility for the Ontario Line. Metrolinx says this location must be vacated by April 2024. Iqbal Foods has secured a new location nearby at 100 Thorncliffe Park Drive, where it has entered into a lease with the landowner.
