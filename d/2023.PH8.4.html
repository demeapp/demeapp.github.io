The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to report back to the Planning and Housing Committee on the stakeholder and public consultation for the draft updated Rear Transition performance standards for mid-rise buildings, as well as the other existing Mid-Rise Buildings Performance Standards, and a timeline for implementation through Zoning By-law Amendment. This Performance Standards review is part of the Housing Action Plan to achieve or exceed the provincial housing target of 285,000 new homes of the next 10 years, adding to the 166,739 new homes completed in the past 10 years. Mid-rise buildings are the 'in between' scale of building, and have heights generally no greater than the width of the right-of-way that it fronts onto, up to 11 storeys.
