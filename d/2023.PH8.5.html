The official record

What the item proposed

City Council, at its meeting on November 8 and 9, 2023, referred the draft Zoning By-law Amendments in Attachment 1 to the report (September 13, 2023) from Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning and the Executive Director, Transit Expansion Office, as they relate to the following properties, to the Planning and Housing Committee for further consideration at its meeting of November 30, 2023: 891, 885 and 897 Pape Avenue; 1012-1052 Pape Avenue; 240 and 242 First Avenue; 495 and 497 Pape Avenue; 716 Pape Avenue; 676-692 Danforth Avenue; 5-11 Gertrude Place; 1, 3, 5, 7, 11, and 13 Lipton Avenue; 855 Gerrard Street East; 15 and 17 Minton Place and 156 and 158 Hopedale Avenue; and 449 Carlaw Avenue.
