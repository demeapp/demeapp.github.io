Toronto City Council · Decision

Potential Additional Land for Affordable Housing on the Waterfront

AdoptedJun 20, 2023 · Toronto City Council

CreateTO was asked to explore with Waterfront Toronto whether more land for affordable housing can be found on the north side of the Keating Channel.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Last night Waterfront Toronto has a very well attended meeting regarding Affordable Housing on the Portlands and the city’s new goal of 30%. There was overwhelming support for the goal of a minimum of 30% affordable housing in this new neighbourhood, with most residents wanting the percentage increased. There was a discussion of a potential number of acres available for affordable housing on the north side of the Keating Channel. There is an existing precinct plan for Keating and the city does own a number of acres on the north side of the channel. Given the interest in affordable housing it would be helpful if our CEO could have a discussion with WaterfronToronto CEO to determine if any options exist to increase affordable housing in Keating North.

What was decided

The Board of Directors CreateTO: 1. Requested the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO to have discussions with the Chief Executive Officer, Waterfront Toronto to determine if any options exist to increase affordable housing in Keating North.
