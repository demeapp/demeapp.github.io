The official record

What the item proposed

On January 30, 2019, City Council approved a signature affordable housing program, the "Housing Now Initiative," to leverage City-owned land for the purpose of creating new mixed-use, mixed-income communities near transit. Through the Initiative, 11 properties were initially identified for development, with the potential to create a total of 10,000 new residential units. On May 28, 2020, Council approved the addition of six more sites to the Housing Now Initiative with the potential to create 1,455 to 1,700 additional residential units. On November 9th and 10th, 2021 City Council endorsed the addition of four Phase Three sites to the Initiative. These sites are estimated to add between 1,150 and 1,400 net new homes.
