The official record

What the item proposed

The Beaches-East York Pilot Project (BEY Pilot Project) is a City Council endorsed priority project identified through the Expanding Housing Options in Neighbourhoods (EHON) work program. The work program consists of multiple priority projects intended to expand permissions to allow low-rise housing in neighbourhoods. The purpose of the BEY Pilot Project is to build “missing middle” housing on City-owned lands in Beaches-East York (Ward 19). On January 19, 2021, the Planning and Housing Committee requested City Staff, in consultation with CreateTO, to review appropriate City-owned sites and facilitate, through an appropriate development procurement process, the design and construction of demonstration projects, as part of the EHON initiative.
