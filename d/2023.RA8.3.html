The CreateTO board receives a confidential update on the Mount Dennis campus opportunity.

Official title: Mount Dennis Campus Opportunity

AdoptedNov 14, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The CreateTO Board and City Council directed the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO to report back to the CreateTO Board in 2023 on the status of the viability of the three sites identified in the Mount Dennis area, pursuant to RA30.6 - Mount Dennis Campus Opportunity , referred by the CreateTO Board to City Council as GL30.16 - Mount Dennis Campus Opportunity . This report provides a further update on this review and confirms the addition of one site after a thorough review and the continued due diligence on the first three potential sites. A Memorandum of Understanding was executed between CreateTO and George Brown College to continue this due diligence work which speaks to the feasibility of a post-secondary campus and City-building opportunities in the Mount Dennis area, feeding into the George Brown College lead Campus Master Plan review.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Directed that the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (October 31, 2023) from the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO remain confidential in its entirety, as it is about a position, plan, procedure, criteria, or instruction to be applied to any negotiations carried on or to be carried on by or on behalf of the Board of Directors of CreateTO.
