June 14, 2023 Speaker Nunziata, during the morning session of the meeting, recognized two members of the City Clerk's Office, Carol Kaustinen and Ellen Devlin, for their retirement from the City of Toronto. Speaker Nunziata acknowledged that Carol Kaustinen and Ellen Devlin have both had distinguished careers with the City and the City Clerk's Office, providing support to Members of Council, staff and the public, in their roles as Administrators. Speaker Nunziata remarked that many Chairs and senior officials have benefitted from their advice, calmness, and dedication to ensuring open and transparent decision making. Speaker Nunziata, on behalf of City Council, thanked Ms. Kaustinen and Ms. Devlin for their service to the City and its residents, and wished each a wonderful retirement.
