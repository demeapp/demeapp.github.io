The Sign Variance Committee was asked to permanently allow electronic participation in its meetings.

Official title: Making Electronic Participation Permanent for the Sign Variance Committee

AdoptedJun 23, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend amendments to the Sign Variance Committee Procedural By-Law to make electronic participation a permanent option for meetings, and modify provisions concerning the time to achieve quorum.

What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Amended the Sign Variance Committee By-Law No. 1, Rules of Procedure for the Sign Variance Committee, by adopting a by-law in the form or substantially in the form, attached as Attachment 1 to the report (June 14, 2023) from the City Clerk to: a. Repeal and re-enact the various amendments previously adopted concerning electronic participation in all meetings; and b. Allow 30 minutes instead of 15 minutes to achieve quorum.
