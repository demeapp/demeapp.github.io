642 McCowan Road - Residential Demolition Application

Adopted with changesDec 1, 2022 · Toronto City Council

The Scarborough Community Council was asked to refuse the application to demolish the residential building at 642 McCowan Road because there is no permit.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Scarborough Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. In accordance with Section 33 of the Planning Act and the former City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 363, Article 6.3, Subsection D(1) "Demolition Control", the application for the demolition of a dwelling at 642 McCowan Road is referred to the Scarborough Community Council to refuse or to grant the application as a building permit has not been issued for a replacement building. If the Scarborough Community Council grants issuance of the demolition permit, it may impose conditions if any, to be attached to the demolition permit.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Approved the application to demolish the residential building at 642 McCowan Road with the following conditions: a. that a construction fence be erected in accordance with the provisions of the Municipal Code, Chapter 363, Article 7, if deemed appropriate by the Chief Building Official; b. that all debris and rubble be removed immediately after demolition; c. be maintained free of garbage and weeds, in accordance with the Municipal Code Chapter 629-10, paragraph B and 629-11; d. that any holes on the property are backfilled with clean fill.
