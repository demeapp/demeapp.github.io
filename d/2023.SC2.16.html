Through Highways - Belinda Square, Sandyhook Square and Sylvestre Square

AdoptedJan 27, 2023 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council was asked to designate Belinda Square, between Wintermute Boulevard and a point 150 metres east, as a through highway.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report recommends that Belinda Square, Sandyhook Square and Sylvestre Square be designated as through highways in order to clearly define the right-of-way and to regulate traffic flow for the three subject uncontrolled P-Loop intersections.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Designated Belinda Square, between Wintermute Boulevard and a point 150 metres east, as a through highway. 2. Designated Sandyhook Square, between Wintermute Boulevard and a point 150 metres east, as a through highway. 3. Designated Sylvestre Square, between Wintermute Boulevard and a point 100 metres east, as a through highway.
