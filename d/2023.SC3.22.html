U-Turn and Parking Amendments - Chartland Boulevard South

AdoptedFeb 24, 2023 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Scarborough Community Council was asked to tighten parking and U-turn rules on Chartland Boulevard South near Iroquois Junior Public School.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that the existing school bus loading zone on the south side of Chartland Boulevard South, in front of Iroquois Junior Public School, be enhanced with a no standing prohibition east and west of the existing school bus loading zone, amending the duration time of the existing no stopping prohibition on the north side of Chartland Boulevard South and prohibit U-turn movements in the vicinity of this school zone. The proposed recommendations will improve the safe movement of the large number of school buses and further facilitate a safe student pick-up and drop-off activities of school children in front of this school zone.

What was decided

Scarborough Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing stopping prohibition in effect from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Chartland Boulevard South, between a point 59 metres south of Hartleywood Drive and a point 160 metres southwest of Briarscross Boulevard. 2. Prohibited stopping to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Chartland Boulevard South, between Briarscross Boulevard and a point 88 metres northeast, and between Briarscross Boulevard and a point 173 metres southwest. 3. Prohibited standing to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Chartland Boulevard South, between Dibgate Boulevard and a point 66 metres northeast, and between a point 122 metres northeast of Dibgate Boulevard and a point 15 metres further northeast. 4.
