Scarborough Community Council was asked to install an all-way stop at Denton Avenue and Robinson Avenue.

Official title: All-Way Stop Control - Denton Avenue and Robinson Avenue

AdoptedApr 5, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

For some time, residents have been contacting my office with right of way concerns at the intersection of Denton and Robinson, and recently there was an accident there. Having spoken in person with many in the area, I've found there is overwhelming support for implementation of an all way stop control at this location. I am requesting installation of an all way stop control at the intersection of Denton Ave. and Robinson Ave.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized a compulsory all-way stop control at the intersection of Denton Avenue and Robinson Avenue.
