This is a procedural item: Scarborough Community Council was asked to exempt bicycles from the one-way left-turn lane on Farmcrest Drive.

Official title: Traffic Amendments - Farmcrest Drive and Meadowacres Drive

AdoptedApr 5, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This staff report concerns the feasibility of enacting a "Bicycles Excepted" regulation in an existing one-way eastbound left turning lane along Farmcrest Drive at its intersection with Meadowacres Drive.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Amended the existing one-way traffic lane on Farmcrest Drive, between Meadowacres Drive and a point 24 metres west of Meadowacres Drive, for eastbound left turns only at all times to be in effect from Meadowacres Drive and a point 24 metres west, bicycles excepted. 2. Prohibited eastbound right turns, bicycles excepted, at all times on Farmcrest Drive and Meadowacres Drive. 3. Prohibited eastbound through movements, bicycles excepted, at all times at Farmcrest Drive and Meadowacres Drive.
