Toronto City Council · Decision

Scarborough Community Council was asked to approve speed humps on Clonmore Drive near Blantyre Park.

Official title: Traffic Calming (Speed Humps) - Clonmore Drive

AdoptedMay 18, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This segment of Clonmore Drive runs along the northwest side of Blantyre Park. As the park is a popular destination for people of all ages in both winter and summer, there is an increased amount of both pedestrian and vehicular traffic. Over the years, residents of Clonmore Drive have repeatedly expressed serious concerns for the safety of pedestrians, especially children, due to speeding on the street. Therefore, I support the implementation of traffic calming.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorize the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Clonmore Drive, between Blantyre Avenue and Fallingbrook Road. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway to install three speed humps on Clonmore Drive, between Blantyre Avenue and Fallingbrook Road, generally as shown on the attached Drawing TC-139, dated April 2023 from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services. 3. Requested Transportation Services to investigate the feasibility of installation of traffic calming measures (speed humps) on Clonmore Drive between Fallingbrook Road and Gerrard Street East and report back to Scarborough Community Council.
