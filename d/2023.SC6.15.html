Scarborough Community Council was asked to designate a two-way centre left-turn lane on Bridletowne Circle.

Official title: Bridletowne Circle, Northeast Leg - Two-Way Centre Left-Turn Only Lane

AdoptedJun 27, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting Scarborough Community Council to approve the designation of a two-way centre left-turn only lane on Bridletowne Circle (northeast leg), between a point 57.5 metres north of Finch Avenue East and a point 54.5 metres east of Warden Avenue. Due to the existing wide lane widths and lane configuration on Bridletowne Circle (northeast leg), it is appropriate to narrow the lane widths and designate a two-way centre left-turn only lane, to allow left-turns into driveways on both sides of Bridletowne Circle (northeast leg). This safety and operational improvement would be completed in 2023 in conjunction with road resurfacing on Bridletowne Circle (northeast leg), from Finch Avenue East to Warden Avenue.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Designated a two-way centre left-turn only lane on Bridletowne Circle (northeast leg), between a point 57.5 metres north of Finch Avenue East and a point 54.5 metres east of Warden Avenue.
