Scarborough Community Council was asked to close part of Military Trail for 31 months for construction staging at 1625 Military Trail.

Official title: 1625 Military Trail - Construction Staging Area

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Highland Trail Limited Partnership is constructing two 8-storey residential condominium buildings at 1625 Military Trail. The site is located on the south side of Military Trail, between Old Kingston Road and Kingston Road. Transportation Services is requesting approval to temporarily close the south sidewalk and portion of the eastbound curb lane on Military Trail, for a period of 31 months from June 30, 2023 to January 31, 2026, to facilitate construction staging operations. Pedestrian movements will be maintained on the south side of Military Trail within a covered and protected walkway.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized the closure of the south side sidewalk and a 5.4 metre wide portion of the eastbound curb lane on Military Trail, between a point 52.3 metres east of Old Kingston Road and a point 179.2 metres further east, and provision of a temporary pedestrian walkway within the closed portion of Military Trail, from June 30, 2023 to January 31, 2026. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Military Trail, between a point 52.3 metres east of Old Kingston Road and Kingston Road. 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the south side of Military Trail, between a point 52.3 metres east of Old Kingston Road and Kingston Road. 4. Directed the applicant to sweep the construction site and adjacent sidewalks and roadways daily, or more frequently as needed to be cleared of any construction debris and made safe.…
