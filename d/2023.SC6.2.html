Scarborough Community Council was asked to approve the name "White Tail Crescent" for a private street in a Kingston Road development.

Official title: Naming of Proposed Private Street for a Development at 6175, 6183 Kingston Road, 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 10 and 11 Franklin Avenue

AdoptedJun 27, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that the name "White Tail Crescent" be approved to identify a Proposed Private Street for a development located at 6175, 6183 Kingston Road, 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 10 and 11 Franklin Avenue. This naming proposal complies with the City of Toronto Street Naming Policy which can be found at https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/planning-development/street-naming/

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Approved the name “White Tail Crescent” to identify a Proposed Private Street for a development at 6175, 6183 Kingston Road, 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 10 and 11 Franklin Avenue shown as PART 1 on Attachment 1, Sketch No. PS-2023-015, to the report (May 26, 2023) from the Director, Engineering Support Services, Engineering and Construction Services. 2. Requested the applicant to pay the costs, estimated to be $500.00, for the fabrication and installation of the street names signage; and 3. Requested the owner and successors of the subject lands to maintain, at their own expense, the street names signage installed under Recommendations 2, above.
