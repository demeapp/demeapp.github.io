Toronto City Council · Decision

Scarborough Community Council was asked to extend a standing prohibition on Belanger Crescent to improve snow clearing.

Official title: Belanger Crescent - Parking Amendments

AdoptedJun 27, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

A number of residents have contacted me with regard to snow clearing issues on Belanger Crescent due to parked cars. I reached out to Transportation staff, and they advised the following: "Currently, standing is prohibited at all times on the south side of Belanger Crescent between Edhouse Avenue and the east end of Belanger Crescent. With Belanger Crescent now extending and intersecting with Lily Cup Avenue., it is recommended that the standing prohibition be extended to Lily Cup Avenue." I support this recommendation, as it will mitigate the snow clearing issues, and will help to ensure traffic and pedestrian safety on the street.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Amended the existing standing prohibition in effect at all times, on the south side of Belanger Crescent, between Edhouse Avenue and the east end of Belanger Crescent, to be in effect on the south and east side of Belanger Crescent, between Edhouse Avenue and Lily Cup Avenue.
