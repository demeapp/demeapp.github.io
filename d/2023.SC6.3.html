The official record

What the item proposed

This Rental Housing Demolition application proposes to demolish 7 rental dwelling units at 2328, 2230, 2334, 2340, 2342, 2344, 2346 and 2350 Kingston Road and replace them with 7 new rental units within the proposed redevelopment of a new 10-storey mixed-use building comprising 208 new dwelling units. A Minor Variance application (23 106635 ESC 20 MV) was submitted to obtain a series of variances necessary to facilitate the proposed redevelopment. The Minor Variance application was approved, with Conditions, by the Committee of Adjustment on June 1, 2023. One of the conditions necessary to be fulfilled to authorize the approved variances was City Council’s approval of this Rental Housing Demolition application.
