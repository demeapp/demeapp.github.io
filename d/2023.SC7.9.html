The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the amendment of parking regulations on Orchard Park Drive. The proposed amendment will provide staff and visitors of a sheltered housing site at 4626 Kingston Road additional parking opportunities.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on both sides of Orchard Park Drive, between Kingston Road and West Hill Drive. 2. Prohibited parking at all times on the south side of Orchard Park Drive, between Kingston Road and West Hill Drive.
