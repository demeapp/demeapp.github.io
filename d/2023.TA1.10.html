What the item proposed

This report recommends the appointment of members to the Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s (TAF) Investment Committee, Direct Investment Committee, and Grants and Programs Committee.

What was decided

Established the composition of the Direct Investment Committee as seven members, and appointed individual C listed in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (January 27, 2023) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Atmospheric Fund as a member. 3. Established the composition of the Grants and Programs Committee as ten members, and appointed the individuals D, E, F, G and H listed in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (January 27, 2023) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Atmospheric Fund as members. 4.
