The Toronto Atmospheric Fund Board was asked to approve $5.75 million in investments across two infrastructure funds.

Official title: Alternative Investment Request

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On the recommendation of the Investment Committee, the Vice President Impact Investing recommends an investment in two Infrastructure Funds. These investments will diversify Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s portfolio in keeping with the asset allocation targets in the Statement of Investment Objectives and Principles and provide risk-adjusted returns and mandate-related impact.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Approved a total investment of $5,750,000 in two Alternative Investment Infrastructure Funds as detailed in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (April 13, 2023) from the Vice President, Impact Investing, Toronto Atmospheric Fund. 2. Adopted the Consumer Price Index plus 4 percent as the appropriate benchmark to assess the performance of the Infrastructure Funds within Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s Alternative Investments asset class. 3. Directed staff to implement the investment transactions subject to the satisfaction of Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s Solicitor and execution by the City Chief Financial Officer. 4.
