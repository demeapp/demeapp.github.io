What the item proposed

This report recommends the appointment of three members to the Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s (TAF) Direct Investment Committee.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Established the composition of the Direct Investment Committee as nine members, and appoint the individuals listed in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (June 29, 2023) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Atmospheric Fund as members. 2. Instructed the City Clerk to make the appointee names public once appointed by the Board, and directed that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (June 29, 2023) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Atmospheric Fund remain confidential as it deals with personal matters about identifiable people.
