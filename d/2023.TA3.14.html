The Toronto Atmospheric Fund board receives a verbal update on succession planning.

Official title: Human Resources Matters - Succession Planning

Adopted with changesJul 14, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Chair of the Human Resources Committee will provide a verbal update to the Board on succession planning, to be received for information, and to solicit Board input on the planning process before a succession plan is developed. These matters will require a closed session discussion, as they relate to personal information about identifiable individuals.

What was decided

The Board of Director of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Directed the Chair, Human Resources Committee to work with the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Atmospheric Fund and the Vice President, Strategy and Partnerships, Toronto Atmospheric Fund on a workplan for a succession plan, and report back to the Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund at its next meeting on November 21, 2023.
