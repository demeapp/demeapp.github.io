The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends the appointment of one member to the Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s (TAF) Grants and Programs Committee and Audit Committee.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Established the composition of the Grants and Programs Committee as eleven members, and appoint the individual listed in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (November 7, 2023) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Atmospheric Fund as a member. 2. Appointed the individual listed in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (November 7, 2023) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Atmospheric Fund as a member of the Audit Committee. 3. Instructed the City Clerk to make the appointee names public once appointed by the Board, and direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (November 7, 2023) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Atmospheric Fund remain confidential as it deals with personal matters about identifiable people.
