The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Broadview Avenue, City Council approval of this report is required. The purpose of this report is to respond to City Council's direction to review and report back on the realignment of Permit Parking Area 8A to exclude the development located at 838-844 Broadview Avenue. Staff have determined the realignment of the Permit Parking Area 8A is feasible and has no objections to excluding the development located at 838-844 Broadview Avenue. If City Council excludes this address from the subject Permit Parking Area, residents and visitors of this address will be prohibited from participating in the on-street permit parking program.

What was decided

City Council on December 14 and 15, 2022, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve the amendment to Schedule B of the City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 925, Permit Parking, to incorporate a revised map of Permit Parking Area "8A", as shown in Attachment A to the report (November 14, 2022) from the Director, Permits and Enforcement, Transportation Services, excluding the development currently located at 838-844 Broadview Avenue.
