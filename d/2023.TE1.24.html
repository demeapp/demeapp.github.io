Toronto City Council · Decision

Implementation of a No Standing Prohibition on the west side of Rushton Road, between St. Clair Avenue West and Charley Roach Lane

AdoptedNov 30, 2022 · Toronto City Council

Toronto East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, on the west side of Rushton Road.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to respectfully request the prohibition of standing on the west side of Rushton Road, between St. Clair Ave West and Charley Roach Lane. This is in response to concerns received from the Community regarding parked vehicles causing potential sight line issues between vehicles travelling north on Rushton Road and vehicles travelling west on Charley Roach Lane. Pedestrian safety has also been noted.

What was decided

The Toronto East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, on the west side of Rushton Road, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 53.4 metres north of St. Clair Avenue West. 2. Prohibited standing at all times, on the west side of Rushton Road, between St. Clair Avenue West and Charley Roach Lane. 3. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect from the 1st day of each month to the 15th day of each month, April 1 to November 30, inclusive on the west side of Rushton Road between a point 53.5 metres north of St. Clair Avenue West and Vaughan Road to be in effect between Charley Roach Lane and Vaughan Road. 4. Amended the existing maximum one hour parking in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., from the 16th day of each month to the last day of each month, April 1 to November 30, inclusive on the west side of Rushton Road…
