Toronto East York Community Council was asked to waive the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy.

Official title: Implementation of Speed Humps on Winona Drive, between Glenhurst Avenue and Barrie Avenue

AdoptedNov 30, 2022 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to respectfully seek your support on this request for the installation of speed humps on Winona Drive, between Glenhurst Ave and Barrie Ave. An overwhelming number of residents of the street presented a petition requesting this over two years ago as a result of speeding and other forms of reckless driver behavior on the street that endangers kids. In consultation with the previous Councillor for Ward 9, we referred the petition to staff for a study. While the staff report did not warrant the installation of speed humps, residents continued to report close shaves and produced vide evidence to that effect. As a result, my office consulted further with traffic operations staff, who assisted us in coming up with the recommendations. My office has also consulted with the new Councillor for Ward 9, who has no objections.

What was decided

The Toronto East York Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorize the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Winona Drive, between Glenhurst Avenue and Barrie Avenue. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter a section of the roadway on Winona Drive, between Glenhurst Avenue and Barrie Avenue, to install two speed humps, generally as shown on the copy of 'Drawing No. TC-109' dated November 2022 from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
