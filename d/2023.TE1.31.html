Councillor Fletcher moves that delete the recommendations approved for item TE34.155.

Official title: Traffic Calming on Walpole Avenue

The official record

What the item proposed

Dear Toronto and East York Community Council, In June 2022, speed humps were approved on Walpole Avenue under item TE34.155 with the following recommendations: 1. Directed the Director of Traffic Management, Transportation Services, to request the City Clerk to poll eligible householders on Walpole Avenue, between Greenwood Avenue and Hiawatha Road, to determine whether residents support the installation of traffic calming, in accordance with the City of Toronto Traffic Calming Policy. 2. Subject to favourable results of the poll: a. authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Walpole Avenue, between Greenwood Avenue and Hiawatha Road. b. directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway to install five speed humps on Walpole Avenue, between Greenwood Avenue and Hiawatha Road, for traffic calming purposes, generally as shown on…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Deleted the Recommendations approved for Item TE34.155. 2. Adopted the following new Recommendations: The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Waive the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorize the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Walpole Avenue; and 2. Authorize the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Walpole Avenue, between Greenwood Avenue and Hiawatha Road. 3. Direct the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway to install five speed humps on Walpole Avenue, between Greenwood Avenue and Hiawatha Road, for traffic calming purposes, generally as shown on Attachment 2, dated May 2022, attached to the report (May 9, 2022) from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
