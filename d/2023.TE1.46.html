What the item proposed

I am writing to request your support in directing Traffic Operations Staff to introduce permit parking on the south side of Manor Road West between Duplex Avenue and the Manor Road West Foot Bridge. Residents of the apartment building at 5 Manor Road West have, until April 2022, have had access to overnight permit parking spots on the eastern portion of Manor Road West, in front of their building. However, due to the construction, and future pedestrianization of Manor Road West as part of the development at 2100 Yonge Street, the short and long term future of their parking situation has been jeopardized. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing no parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Manor Road West between Yonge Street and Duplex Avenue to be in effect between Yonge Street and a point 41 metres west 2. Authorized a 1-hour parking regulation to be in effect from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM on the south side of Manor Road West, from Duplex Avenue and a point 96 metres east. 3. Authorized parking by permit to be in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. daily on the odd (south) side of Manor Road West, from Duplex Avenue and a point 96 metres east.
