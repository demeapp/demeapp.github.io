What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on King Street West, City Council approval of this report is required. Great Gulf Duncan Inc is constructing a 73-storey residential condominium building at 266-270 King Street West. The building will include a total of 864 units, together with ground floor retail, several levels occupied by the Ontario College of Design (OCAD) and 4 basement levels used for services, storage, and bicycle lockers (no car parking). All ingress and egress will be from Ed Mirvish Way and King Street West. The site is located on the north east corner of Ed Mirvish Way and King Street West.
