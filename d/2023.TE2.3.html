The official record

What the item proposed

On November 4, 2021, an Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application was submitted to permit a 29-storey mixed use building containing 143 dwelling units. This report reviews and recommends refusal of the application to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law as the site is not an appropriate location for a tall building. The site is located in the low-rise village of Yorkville and Mixed Use Area 4 of the Downtown Plan. The proposal does not conform with the Official Plan, Downtown Plan, or Site and Area Specific Policy 211 and should be refused.

What was decided

City Council on February 7 and 8, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council refuse the application for the Zoning By-law Amendment at 69 Yorkville Avenue for the reasons set out in the report (January 10, 2023) from the Acting Director, Community Planning, Toronto and East York District. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor, together with appropriate staff, to appear before the Ontario Land Tribunal in support of City Council's decision to refuse the application, in the event the application is appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal. 3.
