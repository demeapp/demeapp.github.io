Rhodes Avenue - Parking Amendments

PostponedJan 26, 2023 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to amend the existing parking prohibition in effect from the first day to the 15th day of each month from April 1.

Council put off deciding this item.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending overnight on-street permit parking be permitted on both sides of Rhodes Avenue, between Gerrard Street East and Fairford Avenue. The proposed amendment will increase the number of available on-street parking spaces while maintaining the one-way traffic flow on Rhodes Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council deferred consideration of the Item until its February 23, 2023 meeting.
