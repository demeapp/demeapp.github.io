Beech Avenue - Two-Way Traffic Operation

AdoptedJan 26, 2023 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to amend the existing one-way northbound designation on Beech Avenue, between Kingston Road and Park Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that the one-way traffic operation on Beech Avenue, immediately south of Kingston Road be amended to a two-way operation. This proposal will provide access from Kingston Road to the new development at 907 Kingston Road. The two-way operation will reduce development-generated traffic using Beech Avenue and other local streets in the neighbourhood.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing one-way northbound designation on Beech Avenue, between Kingston Road and Park Avenue, to be in effect from Park Road and a point 47 metres further south of Kingston Road.
