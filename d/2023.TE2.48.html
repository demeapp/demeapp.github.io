The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended so that it varies with City Policy or by-laws. The purpose of this report is to recommend "housekeeping" amendments to permit parking regulations at the following locations: - Marlborough Avenue between Yonge Street and Marlborough Place - Penrose Road between De-Savery Crescent and Mount Pleasant Road - Thome Crescent between Burlington Crescent and Mount Royal Avenue - Burlington Crescent between Oakwood Avenue and Alberta Avenue - Jameson Avenue between King Street West and Queen Street West The proposed parking amendments will correct discrepancies between posted signs and the existing parking regulations.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing permit parking provision in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily on the even (north) side of Marlborough Avenue, from a point 317.3 metres west of Yonge Street to a point 40.5 metres further west. 2. Prohibited parking except by permit from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. daily on the even (north) side of Marlborough Avenue, from a point 319 metres west of Yonge Street to a point 42.2 metres further west. 3. Authorized parking for a maximum period of one hour from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., daily on the north side of Marlborough Avenue, between a point 319 metres west of Yonge Street and a point 42.2 metres further west. 4.
