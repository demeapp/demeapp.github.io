Toronto City Council · Decision

Motion to Re-Poll Eligible Voters on Strathearn Road toward installing Speed Humps on their Street

AdoptedJan 26, 2023 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to direct the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services to request the City Clerk to poll eligible.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to respectfully request your support to re-poll residents of Strathearn Road, between Dewbourne Ave and Eglinton Avenue West, in order to install speed humps on their street. Residents requested for the installation of speed humps more than a couple of years ago; however, the process was truncated due to an unfavourable poll. Recently, there have been renewed concerns around speeding and other forms of reckless driver behavior that endanger kids. Following confirmation that the moratorium date has elapsed, my office has worked with traffic operations staff to poll eligible residents once again.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services to request the City Clerk to poll eligible householders on Strathearn Road, between Eglinton Avenue West and Dewbourne Avenue, to determine whether residents support the installation of traffic calming, in accordance with the City of Toronto Traffic Calming Policy. 2. Subject to favourable results of the poll: a. authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Strathearn Road, between Eglinton Avenue West and Dewbourne Avenue; and b. directed the City Solicitor to prepare a By-law to alter sections of the roadway on Strathearn Road, between Eglinton Avenue West and Dewbourne Avenue, to install one additional speed hump, generally as shown on the attached copy of Drawing No. 421G-3758, dated June 2020.
