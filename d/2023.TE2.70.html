No Stopping Prohibition on Hendrick Avenue south of Benson Avenue

AdoptedJan 26, 2023 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to amend the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, on the west side of Hendrick Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to respectfully request a "No Stopping" prohibition on Hendrick Avenue south of Benson Avenue, to enhance safety for students at the McMurrich Junior Public School. Over a year ago, a driver hit a 3-year old McMurrich student, while driving a car at the intersection of Benson and Hendrick Avenues. This incident brought to light serious safety flaws at that particular intersection and around the school zone in general. One of the safety issues identified was parents dropping off and picking up their kids on Hendrick Avenue, which is dead end Street, and is not a designated loading area – creating gridlock and causing significant sightline issues for cars reversing. As a result, that strip has become very unsafe for students walking to school through the back entrance, designated only for pedestrians. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, on the west side of Hendrick Avenue, between the south end of Hendrick Avenue and the first lane south of St. Clair Avenue West to be in effect between Benson Avenue and the first lane south of St. Clair Avenue West. 2. Prohibited stopping to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and from 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Hendrick Avenue, between the south end of Hendrick Avenue and Benson Avenue. 3. Prohibited parking to be in effect from 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. and from 4:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. of the next following day, Monday to Friday, and from 4:00 p.m. Friday to 8:00 a.m. of the following Monday, on the west side of Hendrick Avenue, between the south end of Hendrick Avenue and Benson Avenue.
