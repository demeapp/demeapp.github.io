Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to authorize the installation of parking machines on the south side of Eastern Avenue.

Official title: Parking Machines - Eastern Avenue

AdoptedJan 26, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting held on June 29, 2022, Toronto and East York Community Council adopted item TE34.133 and approved the installation of on-street paid parking on a number of local streets in Wards 11, 13 and 14. The south side of Eastern Avenue, between Cherry Street and Trinity Street was selected as a location for the introduction of pay and display parking. However, the recommendation to approve pay and display parking on this street was inadvertently omitted in the previous report. The motion provided will address this matter.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of parking machines on the south side of Eastern Avenue, between Cherry Street and a point 30.5 metres east of Trinity Street, to be in effect from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., 6:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. Monday to Friday; 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. Saturday and from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. Sunday, for a maximum period of three hours and at a rate of $3.00 per hour.
