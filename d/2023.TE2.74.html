Toronto City Council · Decision

Compulsory Stop Control - Durant Avenue and Judith Drive

AdoptedJan 26, 2023 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to rescind the existing yield control on Durant Avenue at the intersection with Judith Drive.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Durant Avenue and Judith Drive are two local roads located in a residential neighbourhood immediately south of O'Connor Drive. This intersection currently has a yield control in place. I have heard from residents who are concerned about traffic safety and have requested that the existing yield sign be changed to a stop sign. The installation of a stop sign at this intersection will improve traffic safety on Durant Avenue and Judith Drive. I hope you will support this recommendation.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing yield control on Durant Avenue at the intersection with Judith Drive. 2. Authorized a compulsory stop control for northbound traffic on Durant Avenue at Judith Drive.
