79-85 Shuter Street - Construction Staging Area Time Extension

This is a procedural item: Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to extend the construction staging closure on Shuter Street for the 79-85 Shuter Street development.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Hyde Park Homes Limited Partnership is constructing a 32-storey residential development building at 79-85 Shuter Street. The site is located on the southeast corner of Shuter Street and Mutual Street. At its meeting on November 10, 2020, Toronto and East York Community Council approved the construction staging areas on Shuter Street and Mutual Street, from November 12, 2020 to April 30, 2022. The south sidewalk and a 2.1-metre-wide portion of the eastbound curb lane on Shuter Street, between Mutual Street and a point 35 metres east, were closed for construction staging operations, with the bike lane on Shuter Street maintained at 1.6 metres in width and pedestrian operations on the south side of Shuter Street maintained in a 1.5-metre-wide covered and protected…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the continuation of the closure of the south sidewalk on Shuter Street, between Mutual Street and a point 35 metres east, and provision of a temporary pedestrian walkway within the closed portion of the eastbound curb lane from February 24, 2023 to October 31, 2023. 2. Authorized the continuation of the closure of a 2.1-metre-wide portion of the most southerly eastbound curb/bike lane on Shuter Street, between Mutual Street and a point 35 metres east, from February 24, 2023 to October 31, 2023. 3. Authorized the continuation of the closure of the east sidewalk on Mutual Street, between Shuter Street and a point 37 metres south, from February 24, 2023 to October 31, 2023. 4.
