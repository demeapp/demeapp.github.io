The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to respond to Toronto and East York Community Council's direction to review and report back on the realignment of Permit Parking Area 6C to exclude the development located at 17 St. Andrew Street. Staff have determined the realignment of the Permit Parking Area 6C is feasible and has no objections to excluding the development located at 17 St. Andrew Street. If Toronto and East York Community Council excludes this address from the subject Permit Parking Area, residents and visitors of this address will be prohibited from participating in the on-street permit parking program.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended Schedule B of the City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 925, Permit Parking, to incorporate a revised map of Permit Parking Area 6C as shown in Attachment 1 to the report (February 6, 2023) from the Director, Permits and Enforcement, Transportation Services, excluding the development currently located at 17 St Andrew Street.
