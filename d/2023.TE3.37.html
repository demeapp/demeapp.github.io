Warland Avenue - Traffic Calming (Speed Humps)

Adopted with changesFeb 23, 2023 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Toronto and East York Community Council is asked not to install speed humps on Warland Avenue because the City's traffic-calming criteria were not met.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has reviewed the need for traffic calming (speed humps) on Warland Avenue, between Donlands Avenue and O'Connor Drive. The assessment indicates the warrant criteria as set out in the City of Toronto's Traffic Calming Policy has not been satisfied. Therefore, staff do not recommend the installation of speed humps on Warland Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Warland Avenue. 2. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Warland Avenue, between Donlands Avenue and O'Connor Drive. 3. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter sections of the roadway to install four speed humps on Warland Avenue, between Donlands Avenue and O'Connor Drive, for traffic calming purposes, generally as shown on Drawing No. TC-119, dated January, 2023 and attached as Attachment 2 to the report (February 6, 2023) from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
