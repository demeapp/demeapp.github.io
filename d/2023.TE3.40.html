Permanently Closing Market Street between Front Street East and The Esplanade

AdoptedFeb 23, 2023 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to close Market Street to vehicles from June 1 to October 15, 2023, after strong community support for pedestrianization.

The official record

What the item proposed

Market Street, between Front Street East and The Esplanade, was temporarily closed in 2022 at the request of the St. Lawrence Neighbourhood Association and the St. Lawrence Market Neighbourhood Business Improvement Area (BIA). This closure last summer was very well received by the local community, with a survey indicating that approximately 80% of participants were interested in seeing Market Street close permanently to accommodate pedestrians and programming. Market Street, between Front Street East and The Esplanade was designed as a flexible street without curbs in 2014. Adjacent to the historic St. Lawrence Market, Market Street is within a heavily pedestrianized and historic part of Toronto known for its many restaurants, shops and boutiques. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the General Manager, Transportation Services to temporarily close Market Street between Front Street East and The Esplanade from June 1, 2023 to October 15, 2023. 2. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services to work with stakeholders and the St. Lawrence Business Improvement Area to come up with solutions for deliveries and waste collection, as required, through the permitting for the closure. 3. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services to consult with the Manager, St Lawrence Market Complex, Corporate Services, St. Lawrence Market Neighbourhood Business Improvement Area, the St. Lawrence Neighbourhood Association, and other City divisions and stakeholders as identified about the permanent closure of Market Street, between Front Street East and The Esplanade, and to report back to the June 22, 2023…
