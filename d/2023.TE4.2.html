Naming of Proposed Private Streets for a Development at 1245 Dupont Street

AdoptedApr 12, 2023 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve Record Lane, Graphophone Grove and Album Lane at 1245 Dupont Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that the names "Record Lane", “Graphophone Grove” and “Album Lane” be approved to identify three (3) proposed private streets located at 1245 Dupont Street. This naming proposal complies with the City of Toronto Street Naming Policy which can be found at https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/planning-development/street-naming/

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the name "Graphophone Grove", for a proposed private street at 1245 Dupont Street shown as Part 1 on Attachment 1, Sketch No. PS-2023-005, to the report (March 17, 2023) from the Director, Engineering Support Services, Engineering and Construction Services. 2. Approved the name "Record Lane" for a proposed private street at 1245 Dupont Street shown as Part 2 on Attachment 1, Sketch No. PS-2023-005, to the report (March 17, 2023) from the Director, Engineering Support Services, Engineering and Construction Services. 3. Approved the name "Album Lane" for a proposed private street at 1245 Dupont Street shown as Part 3 on Attachment 1, Sketch No. PS-2023-005, to the report (March 17, 2023) from the Director, Engineering Support Services, Engineering and Construction Services. 4.
