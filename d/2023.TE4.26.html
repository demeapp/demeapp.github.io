85 Raglan Avenue - Residential Demolition Application

This is a procedural item: Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to refuse demolishing the house at 85 Raglan Avenue with no replacement planned.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which the Toronto and East York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council make a final decision. In accordance with city-wide residential demolition control under the Toronto Municipal Code Ch.363, under the authority of Section 33 of the Planning Act, the application for the demolition of an existing three-storey detached single family dwelling, at 85 Raglan Avenue (Application No. 23 105375 DEM 00 DM) is being referred to the Toronto and East York Community Council for consideration to refuse or grant the application, including any conditions, if any, to be attached to the permit application because a building permit has not been issued for a replacement building.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the application to demolish the three-storey detached single family dwelling and detached garage buildings without any conditions.
