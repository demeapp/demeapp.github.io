This is a procedural item: Toronto and East York Community Council reviews a noise exemption appeal for extended construction hours at 1 Bloor Street West.

The official record

What the item proposed

1 Bloor Street West is an 85 Storey condominium. The matter before the Community Council is an appeal application for a construction noise exemption permit submitted by Mizrahi Development Group (The One) Inc. through their solicitor Dentons Canada LLP. The application requests construction activities to between 5:00 am to 11:00 pm from Monday through to Sunday and statutory holidays. The requested hours outside the bylaw time limits are to conduct low-noise activities (concrete-pouring and crane operation (electric crane, minimal noise), no jackhammering or demolition). The application further seeks exemption from the Noise Bylaws Loading and Unloading time limits, to allow for loading and unloading operations outside of the allowable time limits as per Section 2.4 (A) of the Noise Bylaw. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1.
