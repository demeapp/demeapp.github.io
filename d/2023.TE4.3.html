Toronto City Council · Decision

Naming of an existing Public Lane west of Shaw Street extending between Dundas Street West and Halton Street

AdoptedApr 12, 2023 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to name a public lane west of Shaw Street Rocco Mandalfino Lane.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that the name "Rocco Mandalfino Lane" be approved to identify an existing public lane located west of Shaw Street extending between Dundas Street West and Halton Street. This naming proposal complies with the City of Toronto Street Naming Policy which can be found at https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/planning-development/street-naming/ This naming proposal is in alignment with the objectives of the Guiding Principles for Commemoration in the Public Realm.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the name "Rocco Mandalfino Lane" for an existing public lane located west of Shaw Street extending between Dundas Street West and Halton Street.
