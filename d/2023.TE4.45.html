This is a procedural item: Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve an encroachment agreement for entrances and garbage boxes at 225 Brunswick Avenue.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has reviewed an application to construct an encroachment within the public right-of-way at 225 Brunswick Avenue. The encroachments consist of two sunken building entrances and seven garbage storage boxes which contravenes City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks, Use Of. Although the proposed encroachment on the public right-of-way contravenes the requirements under Code Chapter 743, staff is of the opinion that it will not have a negative impact on the public right-of-way for pedestrians or for traffic on the affected streets. Therefore, Transportation Services is requesting authority to enter into an encroachment agreement with the property owner, subject to conditions.
