60 Yorkville Avenue - Encroachment Appeal - Canopy and Terrace Addition

This is a procedural item: Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve an encroachment agreement for a canopy and terrace at 60 Yorkville Avenue.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has reviewed an application from the property owners at 60 Yorkville Avenue to construct and maintain an encroachment fronting Bay Street within the public right-of-way. The encroachment consists of a glass and steel canopy structure and a raised terrace which is in contravention of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks, Use Of. Specifically, the stainless steel canopy support structure and terrace structure does not fall within the list of delegated permitted encroachments.
