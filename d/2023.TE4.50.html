Construction Staging Area Time Extension and Traffic Amendments - 375-381 Queen Street West (Peter Street)

This is a procedural item: Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to extend the Peter Street construction staging area to December 31, 2023.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. 375-381 Queen Street West Inc., is constructing a seven-storey mixed-use office building with ground floor retail at 375-381 Queen Street West. A portion of the southbound curb lane on Peter Street and west sidewalk, abutting the site, is currently closed to accommodate construction staging operations for the development. Toronto and East York Community Council, at its meeting on September 16, 2020, authorized the subject construction staging area from September 16, 2020 to October 31, 2022. The developer has requested an extension of the duration of the construction staging area on Peter Street, as the site has experienced a number of construction delays due to inclement weather and labour interruptions.
