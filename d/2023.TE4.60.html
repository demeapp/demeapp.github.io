Traffic Calming (Speed Humps) - Jackman Avenue

Adopted with changesApr 12, 2023 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Toronto and East York Community Council is asked not to install speed humps on Jackman Avenue, as warrant criteria were not met.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has reviewed the need for traffic calming (speed humps) on Jackman Avenue, between Browning Avenue and Hurndale Avenue. The assessment indicates the warrant criteria as set out in the City of Toronto's Traffic Calming Policy has not been satisfied. Therefore, staff do not recommend the installation of speed humps on Jackman Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Jackman Avenue; and a. authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Jackman Avenue, between Browning Avenue and Hurndale Avenue; and b. directed the City Solicitor to prepare a By-law to alter sections of the roadway to install seven speed humps on Jackman Avenue, between Browning Avenue and Hurndale Avenue, for traffic calming purposes, generally as shown on Attachment 2, Drawing TC-122 dated March 2023, to the report (March 23, 2023) from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
