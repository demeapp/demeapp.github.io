Re-opening and Technical Amendment Item to TE2.42 - Accessible Parking Spaces - January 2023 (Delegated)

AdoptedApr 12, 2023 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Bravo asks Toronto and East York Community Council to re-open the January accessible parking decision for Bartlett and Westmount Avenues.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This item is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision, provided that it is not amended. This change will improve accessibility for residents who require these spaces. The proposed revision to the accessible parking spaces on the west and east sides of Bartlett Avenue and on the west side of Westmount Avenue are to be replaced with the recommendations below.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item TE2.42, headed "Accessible Parking Spaces - January 2023 (Delegated)" for further consideration, only as it pertains to the accessible parking spaces on the west and east sides of Bartlett Avenue and on the west side of Westmount Avenue. 2. Deleted the entries in Attachment 1, On-street Accessible Parking Spaces to be Installed/Removed, at the locations identified in Table 1 to the report (January 10, 2023) from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, only as it pertains to the accessible parking spaces on the west and east sides of Bartlett Avenue and on the west side of Westmount Avenue, and replaced them with the following: On-street accessible parking spaces to be installed: Ward 9 Location Bartlett Avenue, east side, between a point 70.3 metres south of Craftsman Lane and a point 5.5 metres further…
