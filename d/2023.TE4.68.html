Councillor Malik asks Toronto and East York Community Council to install speed bumps on Tom Longboat Lane.

Official title: Speed Bumps - Tom Longboat Lane

AdoptedApr 12, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to respectfully request the implementation of speed bumps on Tom Longboat Lane. In 2021 The Longboat Area Residents Association (LARA) reached out to former Councillor Joe Cressy's office over speeding concerns, and requested Transportation Services to review the need for speed bumps in Tom Longboat Lane. The study findings indicated that the operational characteristics satisfy the criteria set out in the guidelines adopted by City Council. In addition, former Councillor Joe Cressy received a petition from a majority of residents in favour of having speed bumps installed in Tom Longboat Lane.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of speed bumps in Tom Longboat Lane (east-west branch), between Tom Longboat Lane (westerly north-south branch) and Tom Longboat Lane (easterly north-south branch), as shown on Drawing TC-116, dated December 2022, from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
