The official record

What the item proposed

The development at 462 Eastern is nearing occupancy. The development contains 307 residential units, with 323 total parking spaces and 361 bicycle parking spaces. There is adequate parking in the development to accommodate the demand from the new residents.

What was decided

City Council on May 10, 11 and 12, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve an amendment to Schedule B of the City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 925, Permit Parking, to exclude the development located at 462 Eastern Avenue, including the address at 150 Logan and any other addresses for the development at 462 Eastern.
