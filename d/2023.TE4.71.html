City Council was asked to change parking rules on College Street to make room for CafeTO patios near the West End YMCA.

Official title: Parking Amendments to Support CaféTO on College Street between Dovercourt Road to Ossington Avenue

AdoptedMay 10, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this letter is to amend parking regulations on the north side of College Street, between Dovercourt Road to Ossington Avenue and on the east side of Dovercourt Road just north of College Street to support CaféTO. As the TTC operates a route on College Street, City Council approval is required. The proposed amendments shift a day care pick-up/drop-off area for the local YMCA on the north side of College Street to the east side of Dovercourt Road. This is to allow for the permitting of curb lane cafés under the CaféTO program on College Street while continuing to allow for a pick-up/drop-off area. The local BIA and the West End YMCA have been consulted on these changes.

What was decided

City Council on May 10, 11 and 12, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council rescind the existing parking for a maximum period of 10 minutes, from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of College Street, between a point 30.5 metres east of Dovercourt Road and a point 33 metres further east. 2. City Council rescind the existing parking machine regulation in effect from 9:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. Saturday, and from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. on Sunday, at a rate of $2.00 per hour and for a maximum period of three hours, on the north side of College Street, between a point 30.5 metres east of Dovercourt Road and a point 33 metres further east. 3.
