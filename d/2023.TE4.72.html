Councillor Saxe asks Toronto and East York Community Council to re-open the Shaw Street accessible parking space decision to fix an error.

Official title: Re-opening the Accessible Parking Space installation on Shaw Street

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on February 23, 2023, Toronto and East York Community Council approved the implementation of an accessible parking space on Shaw Street. The Community Council decision can be found at: Agenda Item History - 2023.TE3.18 Transportation Services is requesting that this item be re-opened in order to adjust an incorrect entry pertaining to this location.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item TE3.18, headed "Accessible Parking Spaces -February 2023 (Delegated)" for further consideration, only as it pertains to the accessible parking spaces on the west side of Shaw Street. 2. Deleted the entry in Attachment 1 to the report (January 10, 2023) from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, only as it pertains to the accessible parking space the west side of Shaw Street, and replaced it with the following: On-street accessible parking spaces to be installed: Ward 11 Location Shaw Street, west side, between a point 14 metres south of Essex Street and a point 5.5 metres further south.
