Toronto City Council · Decision

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to extend overnight permit parking hours on Mountjoy Avenue to 10 a.m.

Official title: Permit Parking Amendments on Mountjoy Avenue

AdoptedApr 12, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

L'Ecole Viamonde will soon be opening a new school at the intersection of Mountjoy Avenue and Ladysmith Avenue. I have heard from residents of Mountjoy who are concerned about the impacts of traffic to and from the school on permit parking in their neighbourhood and they have requested an extension of the permit parking hours on Mountjoy Avenue. This letter includes recommendations to extend permit parking hours to ensure access to parking for local residents.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the extension of the overnight on-street permit parking hours on Mountjoy Avenue, between Greenwood Avenue and 37 Mountjoy Avenue from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., 7 days a week to the new hours of 12:01 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., 7 days a week.
