What the item proposed

On February 23, 2023, TEYCC received a presentation on the Night Economy, which is a shared Divisional initiative among Economic Development and Culture, Municipal Licensing and Standards, and City Planning. The presentation resulted in two motions being moved at Committee that request a report back at the April 12 Toronto East York Community Council meeting: 1. Request the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture to request Vivelab to present their consultation plan on the Night Economy for the Toronto and East York district at the April 12, 2023 meeting of the Toronto and East York Community Council. 2. Request the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture to engage in thorough and robust consultation with resident, neighbourhood and community association in the Toronto and East York Community Council boundaries, on the Night Economy Plan and provide a update on…
