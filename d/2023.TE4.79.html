Toronto City Council · Decision

Appointments to the Applegrove Community Complex Board of Management

AdoptedApr 12, 2023 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to appoint new directors to the Applegrove Community Complex board.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On behalf of the Applegrove Community Complex I am submitting the names of the new board of directors which have been submitted to the City but did not make the agenda closing for this meeting. A letter from the Executive Director is attached, and approving them at this meeting will allow the new board to commence work. I wish to thank the outgoing board members for their service and thank the new members for putting their names forward. I look forward to working with them.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Appointed the following individuals to the Applegrove Community Complex Board of Management, at pleasure of Council, for a term of office ending March 31, 2025 and until successors are appointed: - Ann McKechnie - Brian Buchan - Camille Bettonville - Sarah Lasch - Susan Munn - Tammy Rogers
