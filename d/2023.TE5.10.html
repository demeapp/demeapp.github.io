The official record

What the item proposed

This report reviews and recommends refusal of the application to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law to permit a 94-storey mixed-use tower containing 1,262 dwelling units and 272 square metres of retail space. The application has not demonstrated that the proposed tall building can be accommodated on the site in a manner that is appropriate within the existing context. The development as proposed, does not conform with the Official Plan, the Downtown Secondary Plan, and, does not meet the intent of the Tall Building Design Guidelines.

What was decided

City Council on June 14 and 15, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council refuse the Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application at 15-19 Bloor Street West for the reasons set out in the report (May 5, 2023) from the Acting Director, Community Planning, Toronto and East York District. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor, together with appropriate staff, to appear before the Ontario Land Tribunal in support of City Council's decision to refuse the application, in the event the application is appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal. 3.
