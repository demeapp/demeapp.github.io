What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Church Street Limited Partnership is constructing a 39-storey mixed-use development at 191-201 Church Street. The site is located on the east side of Church Street, between Shuter Street and Dundas Street. A portion of the southbound curb lane and west sidewalk on Dalhousie Street is currently closed to accommodate construction staging operations for the development. Toronto and East York Community Council, at its meeting on February 16, 2022, approved the subject construction staging area on Dalhousie Street from April 1, 2022 to May 31, 2023. At the time, the developer indicated they would require the staging area for a total of 26 months, from April 1, 2022 to May 31, 2024. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the continuation of the closure of the west sidewalk and a 2.8 metre wide portion of the southbound lane on Dalhousie Street, between a point 52 metres north of Shuter Street and a point 46 metres further north, from June 1, 2023 to December 31, 2023. 2. Directed the applicant to continue posting a 24-hour monitored construction hotline number on the hoarding board, which must be prominently placed and legible from 20 metres and on all elevations from the construction site. 3. Directed the applicant to continue providing and installing public art, including mural artwork, onto every elevation of the hoarding board with adequate spotlighting for night-time illumination, at their sole cost, to the satisfaction of the Ward Councillor. 4.
