The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make the final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the conversion of an existing advisory courier loading zone to an enforceable Delivery Vehicle Parking Zone on the north side of Balliol Street, east of Yonge Street. During the course of the investigation, it was noted that there is a discrepancy between the posted signage and the existing parking regulations on the south side of Balliol Street, between Yonge Street and Mount Pleasant Road (west intersection). Recommendations 5 and 6 in this report will correct this inconsistency.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Designated a Delivery Vehicle Parking Zone to be in effect at all times for a maximum period of 20 minutes, on the north side of Balliol Street, between a point 17.7 metres east of Yonge Street and a point 19.1 metres further east. 2. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, on the north side of Balliol Street, between Yonge Street and a point 93.3 metres east, to be in effect between Yonge Street and a point 17.7 metres east, and between a point 36.8 east of Yonge Street and a point 56.5 metres further east. 3. Rescinded the existing maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Balliol Street, between Yonge Street and a point 179.5 metres west of Mount Pleasant Road (west intersection). 4.
