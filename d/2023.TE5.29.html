The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the designation of a school bus loading zone on the east side of Ladysmith Avenue, adjacent to Greenwood School, located at 24 Mountjoy Avenue, to provide a safe pick-up/drop-off area for students. This designation will result in loss of five on-street parking spaces on Ladysmith Avenue during the weekday, daytime periods.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Designated a school bus loading zone to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Ladysmith Avenue, between a point 20.5 metres north of Mountjoy Avenue and a point 30 metres further north. 2. Amended the existing maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, on the east side of Ladysmith Avenue, between Danforth Avenue and Mountjoy Avenue to be in effect, between Danforth Avenue and a point 50.5 metres north of Mountjoy Avenue. 3. Authorized parking for a maximum period of one hour from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Saturday, on the east side of Ladysmith Avenue, between a point 20.5 metres north of Mountjoy Avenue and a point 30 metres further north. 4.
