Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to adjust parking rules around the new courthouse at 10 Armoury Street.

Official title: Parking and Traffic Regulations - 10 Armoury Street (New Ontario Court of Justice)

AdoptedMay 24, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Ontario government is opening a new courthouse in Toronto as part of its plan to modernize and improve access to the justice system. The fully accessible state-of-the-art courthouse will bring six Ontario Court of Justice (OCJ) criminal courts under one roof, including specialty courts, such as those for drug treatment, youth and mental health. Located at 10 Armoury Street, the new courthouse is bounded by Armoury Street, Centre Avenue and Chestnut Street. As part of this development, various on-street parking and traffic amendments have been proposed on these roadways in order to improve barrier free parking, curbside management and also to enhance safe traffic operations in this area. The following recommendation will address this matter.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the amendments to City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 903, Chapter 910 and Chapter 950, parking regulations on Centre Avenue, Armoury Street, and Chestnut Street, as described in Attachment 1 to the letter (May 4, 2023) from Councillor Ausma Malik.
