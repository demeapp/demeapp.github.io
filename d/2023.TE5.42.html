Toronto City Council · Decision

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve a speed hump on Winona Drive near Earlsdale Avenue.

Official title: Install Traffic Calming Measures to Improve Pedestrian Safety in Humewood and Oakwood Neighbourhood Line

AdoptedMay 24, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to request your support in directing Traffic Operations Staff to install a speed hump on Winona Drive before the intersection with Earlsdale Avenue. Local residents and concerned parents reach out to both my office and Councillor Bravo's office, advising that this intersection should have additional traffic calming measures. There is a stop sign, but as this section of Winona Drive is one-way and downhill between Vaughan Road and St. Clair Avenue, there are many vehicles passing through – many of which fail to stop properly. At a site visit with concerned residents, my staff were informed that a speed hump prior to the intersection would be a start in getting adequate traffic calming measures at this intersection.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Waive the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed hump) on Winona Drive, between Holland Park Avenue and Earlsdale Avenue (north intersection). 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a By-law to alter the roadway to install one speed hump on Winona Drive, between Holland Park Avenue and Earlsdale Avenue (north intersection), generally as shown on Drawing TC-145 dated May, 2023, from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
