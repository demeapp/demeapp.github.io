Toronto City Council · Decision

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to ban stopping on Logan Avenue south of Eastern Avenue to protect a loading area.

Official title: No Stopping West Side of Logan Avenue south of Eastern Avenue

AdoptedMay 24, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I have been contacted by a local businesses, a recording studio, who have been having persistent issues with their loading area being blocked. Their loading area is a legal boulevard parking spot that is often blocked by illegal parking. Transportation Services has provided language to change the regulations on this short stretch to “No-Stopping” which would ensure that this access is maintained. I look forward to your support for these recommendations.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited stopping at all times on the west side of Logan Avenue, between Eastern Avenue and a point 30 metres south.
