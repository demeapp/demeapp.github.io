Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to add permit parking spaces on Sammon Avenue between Coxwell and Durant.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Sammon Avenue between Coxwell Avenue and Durant Avenue

AdoptedMay 24, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I have been contacted by a local residents of Sammon Avenue who have been having ongoing issues with access to permit parking on their street. This section of Sammon Avenue, between Coxwell and Durant is also used for student pick-up and drop-off at the adjacent elementary school and is often used for short term parking for visitors to Michael Garron Hospital too. Transportation Services has provided language to change the regulations on this block of Sammon Avenue to allow for a few additional permit parking spaces for residents. I look forward to your support for these recommendations.

What was decided
