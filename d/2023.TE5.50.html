Toronto City Council · Decision

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to ban standing on part of Sammon Avenue east of Coxwell near the hospital layby.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Sammon Avenue between Coxwell Avenue and Knight Street

AdoptedMay 24, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As a part of construction, Michael Garron Hospital has installed a temporary parking layby on the north side of Sammon Avenue, east of Coxwell Avenue. In order for the bylaw to reflect these changes, staff suggest the below recommendations.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited standing at all times on the north side of Sammon Avenue, between a point 42.5 metres east of Coxwell Avenue and a point 65 metres further east. 2. Amended the existing parking machine regulation in effect from 6:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Friday and from 9:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Saturday and Sunday, at a rate of $3.00 per hour and for a maximum period of three hours, on the north side of Sammon Avenue, between a point 30.5 metres east of Coxwell Avenue and point of 26 metres further east, to be in effect between 30.5 metres east of Coxwell Avenue and point 12 metres further east. 3.
