Toronto City Council · Decision

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve speed humps on Wolverleigh Boulevard between Glebemount and Woodbine.

Official title: Traffic Calming - Wolverleigh Boulevard, between Glebemount Avenue and Woodbine Avenue

AdoptedMay 24, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents have reached out to my office with concerns about road safety and speeding along Wolverleigh Blvd. My office polled local residents and the majority were in favour. In order to make our community safer for all, I recommend installing speed humps on this street.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Waived the poling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Wolverleigh Boulevard, between Glebemount Avenue and Woodbine Avenue. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway to install three speed humps on Wolverleigh Boulevard, between Glebemount Avenue and Woodbine Avenue, generally as shown on the attached Drawing 421G-3902, dated August 2021, from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
