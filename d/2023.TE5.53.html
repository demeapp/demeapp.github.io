Toronto City Council · Decision

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to lower minor arterial roads in six wards to 40 km/h.

Official title: 40 kilometre per hour Speed Limit on Minor Arterial Roads

AdoptedMay 24, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In our respective wards, Wards 4 (Parkdale-High Park), 9 (Davenport), 11 (University-Rosedale), 12 (Toronto-St. Paul’s), 13 (Toronto Centre) and 14 (Toronto-Danforth), there are sections of arterial roads that are already 40 km/h due to the proximity to schools or because of the 2016 Council approval of the Road Safety Plan to reduce the number of road fatalities and serious injuries. However, there are still arterial roads that are 50 km/h. With staff support, we are looking to have all arterial roads, except Lake Shore Blvd, in our wards, be reduced to 40 km/h. The intent of this motion is to improve safety and provide consistency.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the reduction in the speed limit of all roads designated as minor arterials that currently have a speed limit above 40 kilometres per hour down to 40 kilometres per hour within the confines of Ward 4 (Parkdale-High Park), Ward 9 (Davenport), Ward 11 (University-Rosedale), Ward 12 (Toronto-St. Paul’s), Ward 13 (Toronto Centre) and Ward 14 (Toronto-Danforth), excluding any minor arterial roads that bound more than the Toronto and East York Community Council area, and excluding minor arterial roads that bound Ward 10 (Spadina-Fort York) or Ward19 (Beaches-East York). 2. Amended City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 950, Traffic and Parking, generally as outlined in Attachment 1 to the letter (May 17, 2023) from Councillor Perks. 3.
