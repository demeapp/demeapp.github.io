What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve the revised heritage permit application for the heritage properties at 1404-1420 Yonge Street under Part IV, Section 33 and Section 34 of the Ontario Heritage Act. The revised heritage permit application is in conjunction with Official Plan and Zoning Bylaw Amendment applications that were approved by Council in 2022 for a 50-storey building at the subject lands. Approval was granted by City Council in July 2022 for alterations to the heritage property at 1420 Yonge Street and for the demolition of the building at the heritage property at 1406 Yonge Street in connection with the above noted Official Plan and Zoning Bylaw Amendment applications.
