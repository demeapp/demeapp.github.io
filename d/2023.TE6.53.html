City Council was asked to make the northbound curb lane on Dundas Street West a right-turn-only lane approaching Bloor Street West.

Official title: Dundas Street West at Bloor Street West - Lane Designations

The official record

What the item proposed

There are two separate intersections of Bloor Street West and Dundas Street West in the City of Toronto, this report concerns the eastern-most intersection, located in the Toronto and East York Community Council area. In support of planned quick-build safety improvements at the intersection of Dundas Street West and Bloor Street West, Transportation Services recommends that the northbound curb lane on Dundas Street West approaching Bloor Street West be designated for right-turns only, bicycles excepted. As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates transit service on Dundas Street West, City Council approval of this report is required.

What was decided

City Council on July 19 and 20, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council designate the easterly northbound lane on Dundas Street West, between Bloor Street West and a point 60 metres south for northbound right-turns only, bicycles excepted.
