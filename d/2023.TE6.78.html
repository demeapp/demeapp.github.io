Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to adjust permit parking hours on part of Campbell Avenue.

Official title: Permit Parking Amendments on Campbell Avenue

AdoptedJun 22, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office received a petition from residents of Campbell Avenue requesting changes to the permit parking hours on Campbell Avenue between Dupont Street and Antler Street, including a rush hour parking prohibition from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. While there was support in favour of this initiative, there is a highly utilized employment building on Campbell Avenue and a concern was raised about customer parking during these hours. As a result, I am recommending that the permit parking hours be implemented on a section of the block instead of the full block. These changes will balance the loading and parking needs of the local businesses in the area, and residents are supportive of this compromise in the changes.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., daily, on the even (west) side of Campbell Avenue, from Antler Street to Dupont Street. 2. Authorized permit parking to be in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. and from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. daily, on the (even) west side of Campbell Avenue, from Antler Street to a point 95 metres north. 3. Authorized permit parking to be in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily, on the even (west) side of Campbell Avenue, from a point 95 metres north of Antler Street to Dupont Street. 4.
