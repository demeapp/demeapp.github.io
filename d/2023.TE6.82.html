Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve a speed hump on Lonsdale Road near Oriole Parkway.

Official title: Install Traffic Calming Measures to Improve Pedestrian Safety by Oriole Parkway and Lonsdale Road Intersection

AdoptedJun 22, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to request your support in directing Traffic Operations Staff to install a speed hump on Lonsdale Road between Oriole Road and Oriole Parkway. Local residents and concerned parents have reached out to my office and have consulted with Traffic Operations, advising that this intersection should have additional traffic calming measures. The unusual nature of this intersection has been a source of strain for the community since a child was hit by a car there while walking home from school in 2021.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorize the installation of traffic calming (speed hump) on Lonsdale Road, between Oriole Road and Oriole Parkway (east intersection). 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway to install one speed hump on Lonsdale Road, between Oriole Road and Oriole Parkway (east intersection), generally as shown on Drawing TC-146, dated June, 2023, from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
