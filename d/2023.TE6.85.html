Rescind Turning Restrictions on Hillsdale Avenue for Vehicles Exiting Art Shoppe Condominium

AdoptedJun 22, 2023 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to remove a turning restriction at Hillsdale Avenue East and the Art Shoppe Condominium driveway.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I'm writing today to request your support in rescinding the turning restrictions that were installed on Hillsdale Avenue East prior to occupancy at 2131 Yonge Street (Art Shoppe Condominium). Just prior to occupancy in 2020, there was concern amongst residents and the South Eglinton and Davisville Ratepayers' Association (SEDRA) that traffic exiting out of the southern driveway of Art Shoppe Condos would cause significant traffic infiltration in the community east of Yonge. However, through site plan approval, the number of vehicular parking spots was decreased and the commercial profile changed to smaller tenants. Upon reviewing the traffic volumes in spring 2022 undertaken by members of the condo found that traffic was 20% less than what was projected by the developer. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing westbound right turn prohibition in effect at all times at the intersection of Hillsdale Avenue East and the private driveway at 2131 Yonge Street located on the north side of Hillsdale Avenue East, approximately 70 metres east of Yonge Street. 2. Rescinded the existing southbound left turn prohibition in effect at all times at the intersection of Hillsdale Avenue East and the private driveway at 2131 Yonge Street located on the north side of Hillsdale Avenue East, approximately 70 metres east of Yonge Street.
