Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to exempt Bar Volo at 17 St. Nicholas Street from CaféTO rules so its patio can continue.

Official title: CaféTO - Permission to Allow Bar Volo, at 17 St. Nicholas Street, to Operate a Curb Lane Café in a Public Lane

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that City Council has directed for action to this Community Council, as it has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The restaurant at 17 St. Nicholas Street, operating as Bar Volo, received a temporary CaféTO permit in 2021 and 2022 to operate an outdoor dining area on St. Nicholas Street, which is designated as a public laneway The patio consists of elements which is in contravention of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 742, Sidewalk Cafés, Parklets and Marketing Displays, § 742-10.4A (1), which specifies that curb lane cafés must be located on a major arterial, minor arterial, collector or local road and therefore beyond staff authority to approve a patio application on this laneway. The business is seeking approval from Toronto East York Community Council to allow a CaféTO patio to be maintained within the public…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the General Manager, Transportation Services to exempt curb lane café permit area at 17 St. Nicholas Street, Bar Volo, from the requirements of § 742-10.4A (1) and § 742-10.4. B(6) and B(7) and § 742-10.4 C(1) and C(3) of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 742, Sidewalk Cafés, Parklets and Marketing Displays, for the 2023 CaféTO season.
