The official record

What the item proposed

On August 17, 2022 the City received a Zoning By-law Amendment application seeking permission for a 49-storey mixed-use building on the site at the northeast corner of Pape and Danforth Avenues. The ground level includes a shared residential and office lobby, as well as retail and commercial uses fronting onto Danforth Avenue and a portion of Pape Avenue. Office uses are proposed from the third to fifth floors with the residential use proposed from the sixth to 49th floors. The proposal includes a total of 496 dwelling units, 29,756 square metres of residential gross floor area, 4,743 square metres of non-residential gross floor area, and three levels of underground parking. On May 12, 2023 the applicant appealed this application to the Ontario Land Tribunal due to Council's failure to make a decision within the time frame prescribed by the Planning Act.
