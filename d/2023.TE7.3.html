The official record

What the item proposed

This Rental Housing Demolition application proposes to demolish 23 rental dwelling units at 2-24 Temple Avenue and replace them with 23 replacement rental dwelling units within the proposed new 14-storey residential building on the site, comprising 268 dwelling units. The properties are also the subject of a Zoning By-law Amendment application (21 105906 STE 04 OZ), which was appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal (Case Number OLT-21-001749) on the basis of City Council's failure to make a decision on the application within the statutory timeline under the Planning Act. City Council endorsed a settlement offer on December 14, 2022 and directed staff to request the Ontario Land Tribunal to withhold its Final Order on the appeals until such time as Council has made a decision on the Rental Housing Demolition application.
