The official record

What the item proposed

This report reviews and recommends approval of the Zoning By-law Amendment application and the Rental Housing Demolition application to permit the demolition of an existing 11-storey apartment building containing 81 rental units and construction of a 159.0-metre (50 storeys, plus mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building, containing 624 dwelling units at 77 Roehampton Avenue. The existing 81 rental dwelling units will be replaced off-site at 136 Broadway Avenue. The proposal also includes a Tenant Relocation and Assistance Plan that addresses the right for existing tenants to return to the off-site replacement rental dwelling units at similar rents and financial compensation to lessen hardship.

What was decided
