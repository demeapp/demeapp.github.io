Safety and Convenience Improvements along Gladstone Avenue

AdoptedSep 19, 2023 · Toronto City Council

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve traffic and parking changes along Gladstone Avenue for the cycling connections project.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

These bylaw changes were developed and informed through the stakeholder and public consultation for the Bartlett-Havelock-Gladstone Cycling Connections project. While City Council approved delegated authority for the bylaws associated with the project, further approval is required as these changes were not included in the delegated authority. Parking is currently on the north side of Cross Street except when prohibited every Thursday between April and November (inclusive). On Thursdays during those months, parking is permitted only on the south side of Cross Street. This motion is to affix parking on the north side of Cross Street at all times, which will allow deliveries to the Fairview Nursing Home any day of the week, and to provide added convenience for on-street permit parking holders by requiring them to move their vehicles on Thursdays.
