The official record

What the item proposed

This Rental Housing Demolition application proposes to demolish 6 rental dwelling units at 5-19 Cosburn Avenue and 8-40 Gowan Avenue and replace them with 7 new rental units within the proposed redevelopment of a new 24-storey residential building comprising 425 new dwelling units. The properties are also the subject of a Zoning By-law Amendment application (20 232879 STE 14 OZ), which was appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal (Case Number OLT-21-001666) on the basis of City Council's failure to make a decision on the application within the statutory timeline under the Planning Act. City Council endorsed a settlement offer on March 29, 2023 and directed staff to bring forward a Decision Report on the Rental Housing Demolition application.
