The official record

What the item proposed

The matter before the Community Council is an appeal application for a noise exemption permit submitted by EllisDon Corporation for Man and Material Hoist Start Up/Safety and Movement of Personnel from 6:30 am to 7:00 am Monday to Friday. The project, located at 45 Strachan Avenue, Toronto, also known as 45 Strachan Avenue is a 39 -storey residential/commercial rental tower with 433 units. The noise exemption permit was requested for the start-up and safety of the Man and Material (M&M) Hoist by having the operator run it and conduct safety checks which includes running the hoist up and down the mast. This operation is to start at 6:30 am (Mondays to Fridays only) and following the completion of the safety check personnel can use the Hoist to their designated floor to start work at 7:00 am. The noise permit is specifically requested to remove the restriction of bylaw § 591-2.3.(1),…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Refused the noise exemption permit application submitted by EllisDon Corporation for Man and Material Hoist Start Up/Safety and Movement of Personnel from 6:30 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. Monday to Friday for the project located at 45 Strachan Avenue.
