The official record

What the item proposed

Residents in my ward have shared their concerns regarding road and pedestrian safety along Geary Avenue, which extends beyond Dufferin Street to the west and ends at Ossington Avenue to the east. A growing number of new small businesses, restaurants, and cultural spaces have opened on Geary Avenue in the past several years – bringing more residents and their families to experience what Geary Avenue has to offer. I have heard numerous concerns about speeding cars, narrow sidewalks, and a lack of safety for pedestrians crossing between businesses. The need for action on safe mobility & public realm design is clear to ensure pedestrians and other vulnerable road users feel safe accessing the neighbourhood and many destinations along Geary Avenue. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services to provide a progress report on the Geary Avenue Mobility and Public Realm Strategy and any additional associated recommended measures to the Toronto and East York Community Council by the third quarter of 2024.
