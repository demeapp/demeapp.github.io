The community council was asked to approve speed humps on Aldwych Avenue.

Official title: Traffic Calming - Aldwych Avenue, between Pape Avenue and Woodycrest Avenue

AdoptedOct 18, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Aldwych Avenue is an east-west residential street in East York. I have heard from local residents concerned about speeding on their street, requesting that speed humps be installed. After polling residents of Aldwych Avenue, 77% of residents support the installation of speed humps. This letter includes recommendations to improve safety on the street by installing speed humps.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Waived the polling requirement under the City's Traffic Calming Policy and authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Aldwych Avenue, between Pape Avenue and Woodycrest Avenue. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway to install three speed humps on Aldwych Avenue, between Pape Avenue and Woodycrest Avenue, generally as shown on Attachment 1 to the letter (October 16, 2023) from Councillor Paula Fletcher.
