Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve parking regulation changes for Robbins Avenue.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Robbins Avenue

AdoptedOct 18, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office received requests from local residents on Robbins Ave to rescind the existing parking prohibition located in front of Moncur Park. This location was unsigned for years, and residents became accustomed to the additional 3-4 parking spots provided. I would like to remove the parking prohibition to increase permit parking in the area.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Robbins Avenue, between a point 54.9 meters east of Coxwell Avenue and a point 24.4 meters further east. 2. Rescinded the parking prohibition in effect from the 16th day to the last day of each month, from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the north side of Robbins Avenue, between a point 79.2 meters east of Coxwell Avenue and the northeast end Robbins Avenue. 3. Rescinded the parking prohibition in effect from the 16th day to the last day of each month, from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the north side of Robbins Avenue, between Coxwell Avenue and a point 54.9 metres east. 4.
