The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is for Toronto and East York Community Council to consider an appeal from the property owner of 2294 Bloor Street West regarding their encroachment application. The encroachment consists of an enclosed marketing structure which is in contravention of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks, Use Of. The property owner is seeking approval from Toronto and East York Community Council to allow an enclosed marketing structure to be maintained within the public right-of-way at 2294 Bloor Street West, which is in contravention of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks, Use Of.
