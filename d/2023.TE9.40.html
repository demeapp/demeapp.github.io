City Council was asked to approve a 9-month construction staging closure on Dundas Street West near Jane Street.

Official title: 3524 Dundas Street West - Construction Staging Area

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Dundas Street West City Council approval of this report is required. Maple Reinders Constructors Ltd is constructing a 6-storey self storage building at 3524 Dundas Street West. The site is located on the north side of Dundas Street West between Priscilla Avenue and Willard Avenue. Transportation Services is requesting approval to close the north sidewalk and the north side westbound curb lane on Dundas Street West for a period of 9 months, from December 31, 2023 to October 1, 2024 to facilitate construction staging operations. Pedestrian movements on the north side of Dundas Street West abutting the site will be maintained in a minimum 2.1 metre-wide covered and protected walkway. The construction staging area will result in the loss of six on-street parking spaces on the north side of Dundas Street West.

What was decided

City Council on December 13, 14 and 15, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the closure of the north sidewalk and the north side westbound curb lane on Dundas Street West, between a point 128 metres east of Jane Street and a point 45.75 metres further east, and provision of a temporary pedestrian walkway within the closed portion of the westbound curb lane, from December 31, 2023 to October 1, 2024. 2. City Council rescind the existing maximum two-hour parking regulation in effect 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday; 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Saturday on north side of Dundas Street West, between a point 103 metres east of Jane Street and a point 69 metres further east. 3.
