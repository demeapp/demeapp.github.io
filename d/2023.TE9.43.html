City Council was asked to approve road or sidewalk closures near 10 Elm Avenue for construction staging.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Eastern Construction is constructing a 4 storey Performance Theatre at 10 Elm Avenue. The site is located at the northeast corner of Mount Pleasant Road and Elm Avenue. Transportation Services is requesting approval to temporarily close a portion of the northbound lane on Mount Pleasant Road and a portion of the sidewalk on the east side of Mount Pleasant Road for a period of 19 months, from November 16, 2023 to May 31, 2025 to accommodate a construction staging area. Pedestrian operations on the east side of Mount Pleasant Road will be maintained in a covered and protected walkway within the closed portion of the existing northbound lane. Two-way traffic movement and the four lanes will be maintained on Mount Pleasant Road.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the closure of the east sidewalk and a 3.8 metre wide portion of the northbound curb lane on Mount Pleasant Road, between Elm Avenue and a point 118 metres further north, and provision of a temporary pedestrian walkway within the closed portion of Mount Pleasant Road, from November 16, 2023 to May 31, 2025. 2. Rescinded the existing no parking prohibition in effect all times, on the east side of Mount Pleasant Road, between Elm Avenue and a point 118 metres north. 3. Rescinded the existing stopping prohibition in effect from 3:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m. Monday to Friday, except public holidays on the east side of Mount Pleasant Road, between Elm Avenue and a point 118 metres north. 4.
