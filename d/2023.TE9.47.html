What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Alterra Group of Companies is constructing a 39 storey residential condominium building at 125 George Street. The site is located at the southeast corner of George Street and Richmond Street East. Transportation Services is requesting approval to temporarily close a portion of the northbound lane on George Street and a portion of the south side westbound curb lane on Richmond Street East for a period of 38 months, from November 16, 2023 to December 31, 2026 to accommodate a construction staging area. Pedestrian operations on the east side of George Street will be maintained in a covered and protected walkway within the closed portion of the existing northbound lane.
