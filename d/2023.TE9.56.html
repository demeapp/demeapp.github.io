The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. At its meeting of October 11 and 12, 2023, City Council approved a pilot project of up to twelve (12) months at on-street paid parking locations to allow Mobile Only Zones to allow customers who are paying for On-Street paid parking to do so through the Green P App, text to pay options, or by scanning a QR code to complete paid parking transactions. As part of City Council's approval, it was identified that thirteen (13) on-street paid parking locations across the City of Toronto be selected for Mobile Only Zones based on the following criteria: - High mobile utilization within that specific area or location (greater than 70 percent utilization), - Low number of parking stalls at minimum five (5) or less parking stalls per one (1) Pay and Display (P&D) parking…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Enacted the parking by-law amendments to convert existing on-street paid parking locations to Mobile Only Zones as set out in Attachments 2 and 3 to the report (October 30, 2023) from the Director, Policy and Innovation, Transportation Services.
