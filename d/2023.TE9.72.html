City Council was asked to update stopping and parking rules on Roncesvalles Avenue after the intersection reconstruction.

Official title: By-law changes to reflect the new conditions after the King Street West, Queen Street West, The Queensway and Roncesvalles Avenue construction

AdoptedDec 13, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

With the completion of the reconstruction of the King Street West, Queen Street West, The Queensway and Roncesvalles Avenue intersection imminent, Transportation staff recommend the following to resolve discrepancies between bylaws and signage in the vicinity.

What was decided

City Council on December 13, 14 and 15, 2023, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend the existing stopping prohibition in effect at all times, on the east side of Roncesvalles Avenue, between Geoffrey Street and Grenadier Road, to be in effect between a point 15 metres north of Geoffrey Street and Grenadier Road. 2. City Council prohibit parking at all times on the east side of Roncesvalles Avenue, between Geoffrey Street and a point 15 metres north. 3. City Council rescind the standing prohibition in effect at all times on the east side of Roncesvalles Avenue, between Grafton Avenue and a point 26.1 metres further south. 4. City Council rescind the stopping prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Roncesvalles Avenue, between Queen Street West and Grafton Avenue. 5.
