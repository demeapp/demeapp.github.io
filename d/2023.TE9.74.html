The community council was asked to restrict evening through-traffic on Wallace Avenue at Lansdowne.

The official record

What the item proposed

Since 2017, the Wallace Emerson Traffic Management Committee— a group of local area residents— has been working with the Councillor's office to introduce road safety improvements in the neighbourhood. One of the suggested improvements from this work is a 'through-traffic' restriction on Wallace Avenue at Lansdowne Avenue in the westbound direction during the evening rush hour. The creation of this restriction would prohibit vehicles travelling westbound on Wallace Avenue from going straight through the intersection at Lansdowne Avenue. Instead, vehicles would be redirected onto Lansdowne Avenue, reducing the utilization of Wallace Avenue as an east-west corridor for non-local traffic. Bicycles are excepted from the restriction. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited westbound through movements in effect from 3:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, at Wallace Avenue and Lansdowne Avenue, bicycles excepted.
