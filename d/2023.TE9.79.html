The community council was asked to adjust parking on Bleecker Street near a care home.

Official title: Parking Amendment - Bleecker Street

AdoptedNov 15, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to request your support in amending the on-street parking regulation on the west side of Bleecker Street, south of Wellesley Street East in order to deter parking fronting the garbage loading zone of Fudger House Long-term Care Home located at 439 Sherbourne Street. Transportation Services staff recommends establishing a “No Standing Anytime” prohibition fronting the garbage loading zone in order to deter illegal parking in this area, and maintain unobstructed access. There will be no loss of on-street parking as a result of this parking amendment.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect at all times, on the west side of Bleecker Street, between Wellesley Street East and Carlton Street. 2. Authorized parking for a maximum period of one hour at all times, on the west side of Bleecker Street, between a point 32 metres north of Carlton Street and a point 87.5 metres south of Wellesley Street East. 3. Authorized parking for a maximum period of one hour at all times, on the west side of Bleecker Street, between Wellesley Street East and a point 55 metres south. 4.
