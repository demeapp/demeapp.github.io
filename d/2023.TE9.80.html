Toronto City Council · Decision

Toronto and East York Community Council was asked to approve parking regulation changes for Rhodes Avenue.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Rhodes Avenue between Fairford Avenue and the north end of Rhodes Avenue

AdoptedNov 15, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Rhodes Avenue is a north-south street located in a residential community. I have heard from local residents requesting the removal of alternate side parking to affix parking to one side of the street. After polling residents of Rhodes Avenue, north of Fairford Avenue, residents have voted in favour of affixing the parking to one side of the street year round.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., at all times, except no parking from 16th day to the last day of each month, from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the odd (east) side of Rhodes Avenue, from Fairford Avenue to a point 240.8 metres north. 2. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00, at all times, except no parking from the first day to the 15th day of each month, inclusive, and no parking anytime from December 1 of one year to March 31 of the next following year, inclusive, on the even (west) side of Rhodes Avenue, from Fairford Avenue to a point 240.8 metres north. 3.
