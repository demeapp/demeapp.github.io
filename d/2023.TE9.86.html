The community council was asked to remove two confusing stop signs near Secord Elementary School.

Official title: Reopening and Amending TE5.51 - All-way stop controls around Secord Elementary School

AdoptedNov 15, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this motion is to re-open and amend TE5.51 and approve removal of two stop signs that have caused confusion in determining who has the right-of-way among drivers and pedestrians.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item TE5.51, All-way stop controls around Secord Elementary School, with respect to Part 1 and Part 2 of the decision of the Toronto and East York Community Council. 2. Replaced Part 1 and 2 with the following: 1. Authorized compulsory stop controls for eastbound and southbound traffic at the intersection of Chisholm Avenue and Lumsden Avenue (north intersection). 2. Authorized compulsory stop controls for westbound and northbound traffic at the intersection of Chisholm Avenue and Lumsden Avenue (south intersection).
