Toronto Zoo Infrastructure Projects

AdoptedFeb 17, 2023 · Toronto City Council

The Zoo board was asked to authorize the CEO to award design and construction contracts for Zoo infrastructure projects within a confidential maximum amount.

What the item proposed

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Authorized the Chief Executive Officer to award the design and construction of required structures as set out and within the maximum amount outlined in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (February 16, 2023) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Zoo. 2. Authorized the public release of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (February 16, 2023) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Zoo following the closing of any purchase transaction.
