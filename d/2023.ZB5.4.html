The Zoo board receives an update on the energy retrofit project's change of scope.

Official title: Energy Retrofit Project Change of Scope

AdoptedSep 22, 2023 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report is to provide an update on the Change of Scope for the Peak Shaving Component of the Energy Retrofit Project. Further work on this project has shown that the existing Generac generators used for site-wide emergency back up are in need of replacement, and therefore not the best equipment to be used for the Peak Shaving Conservation Measure of the original Energy Retrofit Project. At this time, the Zoo will be replacing the Peak Shaving Conservation Measure as approved by the Board with a heating and cooling system replacement conservation measure in Africa Pavilion and Indo Malaya Pavilion which will include heat pumps and new boilers similar to the Americas Pavilion Energy Retrofit Project. This alternative measure will realize an additional 409 tonnes of annual greenhouse gas emission savings of the original scope of the Energy Retrofit Project.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo received the report (August 16, 2023) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Zoo, for information.
