What the item proposed

The Auditor General’s 2024 Work Plan included an audit on how the City acquires and manages its applications, corporate-wide software licences, and cloud subscriptions. In 2023, the City spent $44 million on software acquisitions and $34 million on software maintenance. Over the last five years from 2019 to 2023, the City’s total expenditures for software acquisition, maintenance and support were approximately $235 million. The City currently uses over 7,500 software titles; over 1,200 titles are paid software. As the City continues to automate its processes and systems, the investment in technology systems and applications will continue to grow. This audit focused on assessing whether tracking of software licences, cloud subscriptions, and maintenance is accurate, and that unused software is identified and addressed in a timely manner.
