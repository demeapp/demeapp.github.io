The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Rescind its July 31, 2024, Item BA90.4 decision to award Collaborative Procurement from Kinetic GPO Request for Standing Offers Number 23-15 to Sweet Pete’s Bike Shop, due to stating incorrect Supplier name; and 2.
