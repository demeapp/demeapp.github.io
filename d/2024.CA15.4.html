The committee picks candidates to interview for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board.

Official title: Selection of Candidates for Interview for the Toronto Investment Board

Adopted with changesApr 12, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Civic Appointments Committee will select candidates to be interviewed for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board. The Civic Appointments Committee will conduct interviews at its next meeting and recommend three candidates to City Council for appointment.

What was decided

The Civic Appointments Committee: 1. Selected the following candidates for interview at its next meeting for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board: INB2-273; INB2-274; INB2-300; INB2-301; INB2-333; INB2-340; INB2-357; INB2-359; INB2-365; and INB2-370. 2. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (April 3, 2024) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board. 3. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the supplementary report (April 10, 2024) from the City Clerk remains confidential in its entirety as it relates to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board.
