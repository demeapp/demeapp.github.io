The official record

What the item proposed

On November 8, 2021, the City received a Zoning By-law Amendment application for 49-51 Yonge Street (the "Site") to permit the construction of a 60 storey mixed-use building with 1,757 square meters of non-residential gross floor area on the first five storeys, and 256 dwelling units above. Portions of the south and west facades of the existing designated heritage buildings were proposed to be integrated into the development. The applicant appealed City Council's neglect or failure to make a decision on its application for the Zoning By-law Amendment (the "Appeal"), to the Ontario Land Tribunal on April 17, 2023. The City Solicitor requires further directions after receiving a With Prejudice settlement offer on February 23, 2023, which is conditional upon the offer being considered at the March 20, 21 and 22 meeting of Toronto City Council.
