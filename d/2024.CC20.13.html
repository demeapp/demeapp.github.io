The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on July 10, 2024 the Nominating Panel - Committee of Adjustment considered Item NA7.1 and made recommendations to City Council. Summary from the report (June 28, 2024) from the City Clerk: The Nominating Panel - Committee of Adjustment will conduct interviews and recommend 3 candidates to City Council for appointment to the Committee of Adjustment.

What was decided

City Council on July 24 and 25, 2024, adopted the following: 1. City Council appoint the following candidates to the Committee of Adjustment for a term of office ending November 14, 2026, and until successors are appointed, as follows: North York Panel: Carl Knipfel; and Toronto and East York Panel: Ryan Bissonnette. 2. City Council appoint the following candidate to the Committee of Adjustment - Scarborough Panel to fill a vacancy created by the leave of absence, for a term of office until the end of a leave, including up to the end of the term, November 14, 2026, and until a successor is appointed: Eric Hou 3.
