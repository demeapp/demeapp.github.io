The official record

What the item proposed

On July 22, 2022, the City enacted By-law 975-2022, adopting Official Plan Amendment 571, implementing the Mount Dennis Secondary Plan for the lands generally bounded by the Humber River, Clouston Avenue, the CN rail line, Trethewey Drive, Keelesdale Park, Humber Boulevard, Rockcliffe Boulevard, Lambton Avenue, Jane Street and Eglinton Avenue. Official Plan Amendment 571 was appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal by five landowners. Of the five original appeals, two appellants remain as parties in the Official Plan Amendment 571 appeal. The two remaining appellants and the City participated in Ontario Land Tribunal-led mediation in the fall of 2023. The City Solicitor requires further directions for a hearing scheduled to commence on October 1, 2024.
