The official record

What the item proposed

On February 23, 2022, the City received a Zoning By-law Amendment application for 1 York Gate Boulevard to permit the construction of a 22-storey residential rental building with a gross floor area of 17,930 square metres and 294 dwelling units. On March 29, 2022, a Site Plan Control application was submitted for the proposed development. The applicant appealed City Council's neglect or failure to make a decision on its applications for the Zoning By-law Amendment and Site Plan Control to the Ontario Land Tribunal on September 22, 2023. On July 10, 2024, the City received a With Prejudice, not confidential, settlement offer from the applicant's lawyers, Kagan Shastri DeMelo Winer Park LLP, which is attached as Public Attachment 1 (the "Settlement Offer").
