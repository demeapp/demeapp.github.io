The official record

What the item proposed

On July 27, 2023, the City received a Zoning By-law Amendment application for 710 The West Mall to permit the construction of a 22-storey residential building containing 282 new dwelling units. The applicant appealed City Council's refusal of its application for the Zoning By-law Amendment, to the Ontario Land Tribunal on March 6, 2024. The City Solicitor requires further directions for upcoming Ontario Land Tribunal Case Management Conference scheduled for February 10, 2025.

What was decided

City Council on November 13 and 14, 2024, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (October 31, 2024) from the City Solicitor, as amended by Motion 1 by Councillor Stephen Holyday. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Appendices A and B to the report (October 31, 2024) from the City Solicitor, as amended by Motion 1 by Councillor Stephen Holyday. 3. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (October 31, 2024) from the City Solicitor remain confidential at the discretion of the City Solicitor, as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
