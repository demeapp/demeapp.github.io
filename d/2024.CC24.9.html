The official record

What the item proposed

On November 20, 2017, the City received Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment applications for 145 Sheppard Avenue East to permit the construction of an 11-storey mixed use building with at grade retail and an underground parking garage with 47 vehicular parking spaces. The applicant appealed City Council's neglect or failure to make a decision on its application for the Zoning By-law Amendment to the former Ontario Municipal Board on March 29, 2018, and at its meeting on June 26, 27, 28 and 29, 2018, City Council refused the Official Plan Amendment application. The refusal of the Official Plan Amendment application was appealed to the former Local Planning Appeal Tribunal on July 26, 2018.
