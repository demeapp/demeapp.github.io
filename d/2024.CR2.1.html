The official record

What the item proposed

Staff from the Confronting Anti-Black Racism Unit will give a presentation that provides an overview of the process to develop a Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee workplan, issues and desired impacts to be considered and advanced during the Committee's term and key considerations to inform forthcoming meeting agendas.

What was decided

The Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee adopted the 2024-2026 Work Plan taking into account the suggestions made by members of the Committee at the meeting.
