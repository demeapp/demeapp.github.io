The Accessibility Advisory Committee considers a Seniors Strategy presentation on accessibility.

Official title: Toronto Seniors Strategy

AdoptedDec 11, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The City of Toronto is developing a third Toronto Seniors Strategy to better support aging in place, particularly for seniors who are Indigenous, Black, and who belong to equity-deserving groups. The City of Toronto will be implementing a community consultation process to enhance the City’s understanding of the priorities of seniors and older adults in Toronto. These consultations will be a key input into the development of the next Toronto Seniors Strategy. The experiences of seniors with disabilities must be included in this strategy, and people with disabilities should be a specific priority group for consultations. The strategy should be designed with an accessibility lens.

What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee recommends that the Executive Committee: 1. Request the Toronto Seniors’ Strategy team, in the Seniors Services and Long-Term Care Division, to present to the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee at its February 28, 2025 meeting, on: a. an update on the strategy and outreach to and consultation with disability communities in the community consultation process; and b. how the team is using/will use an accessibility lens in preparing this strategy.
