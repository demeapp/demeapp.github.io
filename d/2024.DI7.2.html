The official record

What the item proposed

Peter Athanasopoulos, Director, Public Policy, Spinal Cord Injury Ontario (SCIO) will give a presentation. SCIO is a community organization supporting people with disabilities for almost 80 years. This presentation intends to provide insight from the community on how people with disabilities are struggling and adapting to on demand accessible vehicle for hire services, including recommendations to improve services and processes towards becoming a gold standard in accessible transportation services in Toronto.

What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee received the item for information.
