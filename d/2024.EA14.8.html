The official record

What the item proposed

Final Compliance Audit Report (November 26, 2024) from Delta Consulting Group for candidate Giorgio Mammoliti.

What was decided

The Compliance Audit Committee considered the apparent contraventions identified in the audit report prepared by Delta Consulting Group (“the Auditor’s Report”), of the election campaign finances of Giorgio Mammoliti and has unanimously decided to commence a legal proceeding against the candidate for the apparent contraventions. 1. The Compliance Audit Committee (the “Committee”) met on December 13, 2024, to hear Item EA14.8 - Compliance Audit Report for the City of Toronto Re: Giorgio Mammoliti. Giorgio Mammoliti (the “Candidate”) was a candidate in the City of Toronto’s 2023 Mayoral By-Election that took place on June 26, 2023. 2.
