What the item proposed

The unexpected and unprecedented surge in the number of refugee claimants arriving in Toronto has placed significant strain on the City's shelter and other systems. These pressures spiked in late spring and summer 2023, resulting in groups of refugees staying outside the City’s assessment and referral centre on Peter Street, with no space available in the shelter system. In response, many Black-led churches and community organizations stepped up, opening their doors to provide emergency shelter and supports to hundreds of refugee claimants who were arriving in Toronto homeless. In late summer, the City provided $150,000 to three leading churches. Additionally, in September 2023, City Council authorized that up to $750,000 be provided to churches and community organizations for eligible expenses incurred by September 30.
