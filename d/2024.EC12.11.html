Toronto City Council · Decision

The Francophone Affairs committee asks the committee to support more French resources at municipal museums.

Official title: French Services at Municipal Museums

AdoptedApr 30, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on April 8, 2024, the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee considered item FA2.1 and made recommendations to the Economic and Community Development Committee. Summary from the Item at the April 8, 2024 meeting of the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee: As invited by the Chair, Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee, the Director of La Societe d'histoire de Toronto (La SHT) will give a presentation on French Services at Municipal Museums.

What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee: 1. Requested the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, to explore ways to include support for the development of additional French language resources for City-owned and operated Museums and Cultural Centres in future applications for relevant programs with the Federal Government. 2. Requested the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture to explore a plan for a French language pilot at one City-owned and operated Museum and/or Culture Centre, which includes translated materials and resources for visitors including students and report back to the October 17, 2024 meeting of the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee.
