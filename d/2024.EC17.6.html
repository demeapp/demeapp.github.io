What the item proposed

This report provides a response to City Council’s request that Seniors Services and Long-Term Care (SSLTC) work with specific City divisions to develop an approach to supporting Naturally Occurring Retirement Communities (NORCs) in Toronto. Toronto’s population of adults over the age of 65 is growing, with increasing diversity and evolving priorities for aging in place. To respond to these priorities, the first and second Toronto Seniors Strategies advanced key City initiatives that supported the quality of life, social participation, access to services, and well-being of seniors in Toronto.
