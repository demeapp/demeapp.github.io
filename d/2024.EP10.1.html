What the item proposed

The auditor of record for the Board, KPMG, audits our financial statements in accordance with Canadian public sector accounting standards and summarizes issues of audit significance, and provides the communications required by their professional standards. KPMG presents for the information of the Board for the year ended December 31, 2023 audit, the following: 1. Draft Audited Report and Financial Statements (Appendix A), 2. Management Representation Letter (Appendix B), and 3. Audit Findings Report (Appendix C). The Audit Findings Report (Appendix C) is the section of the audit results where the auditors make their suggestions and recommendations based on audit findings to improve the accounting and internal control procedures. What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the Draft Financial Statements (Appendix A), Management Representation Letter (Appendix B) and Audit Findings Report (Appendix C) to the report (May 22, 2024) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place, for the year ended December 31, 2023 audit. 2. Directed the transfer of the 2023 Operating Budget Deficit Subsidy funding of $2,200,000 to the City of Toronto. 3. Directed the transfer of the Operating Surplus of $1,817,000 into the Conference Centre Reserve Fund held by the City of Toronto for Exhibition Place.
