The Exhibition Place Board was asked to approve a long-term licence agreement for Better Living Centre space.

Official title: Long Term Licence Agreement with an Exhibition Client

Adopted with changesSep 19, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends the Board approve, subject to the necessary City of Toronto authorization, a licence agreement between an Exhibition Client identified in Confidential Attachment 1 (the "Exhibition Client"), as licensee, the City as licensor and The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place for space within the Better Living Centre, substantially on the terms and conditions set out in Confidential Attachment 1.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Deleted Item 5 in the Proposed Terms and Conditions in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (September 16, 2024) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place. 2. Subject to obtaining the necessary City of Toronto authorization, approved a licence agreement (the "Licence Agreement") between the City of Toronto, (the "City"), as licensor, Exhibition Client, as licensee, and the Board, substantially on the terms and conditions set out in Confidential Attachment 1, as amended above, and such other and amended terms and conditions deemed appropriate by the City of Toronto's approving authority, the Chief Executive Officer of Exhibition Place (the "CEO"), and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor. 3.
