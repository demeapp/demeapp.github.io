The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approving business travel expenses for Laura Purdy, General Manager, to attend UFI Executive Committee Meetings, and UFI Board of Directors Meetings in 2025. Union des Foires Internationales, (UFI) is the global association of the Exhibition Industry. In November of 2023 Laura was appointed as the inaugural Chair of the newly established North American chapter of UFI. As Chapter Chair, Laura represents the region on both the UFI Board of Directors and the Executive Committee.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Authorized business travel expenses for Laura Purdy, General Manager, to attend the UFI Executive Committee Meetings and UFI Board of Directors Meetings as listed below.
