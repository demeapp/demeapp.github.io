The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting held December 13, 14 and 15, 2023, City Council considered Item CA9.1 - Appointment of Public Members to the Exhibition Place Board, and appointed the following two public members to the Exhibition Place Board of Governors for a term of office starting December 16, 2023 and ending December 16, 2027, and until successors are appointed: - Malcolm Bromley - Aakanksha Verma As part of this process, Peter Leon and Tess Romain terms of office on the board came to an end effective December 15, 2023.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place received the item for information.
