What the item proposed

On a trial basis, this report recommends identifying events held from May to September at Exhibition Place as Municipally Significant for tenants applying for a temporary extension of operating hours for serving alcohol. Toronto City Council adopted a report from the General Manager of Economic Development and Culture outlining a comprehensive plan to “Strengthen Toronto’s Nighttime Economy.” “Nighttime Economy” describes the City’s social, cultural, and economic activities between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Nightlife refers to entertainment-related activities and is vital to the nighttime economy. It encompasses enjoying Toronto’s nightlife amenities and support for those who work at night, including individuals who help manage life at night, such as hospitality industry workers, bar owners, shift workers, and emergency services professionals. What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Requested staff to provide a letter of support to tenants and event organizers at Exhibition Place for events held between May 1 and September 30, 2024, on a trial basis, such as those specifically listed in Attachment 1 of this Report, to be considered as Municipally Significant for the purpose of applying for a Special Occasion Permit or for a temporary extension of premises and/or hours for the sale of alcohol at permanently licensed locations, and staff report back with an update on the pilot at the December 12, 2024 meeting of the Board.
