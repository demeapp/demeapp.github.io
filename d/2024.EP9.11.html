What the item proposed

In 2018, the 2026 World Cup was awarded to a joint bid by Canada, United States, and Mexico. The World Cup is the largest single sporting event held in the world and rivals the Summer Olympics as the largest overall. It is held every four years over six weeks. In 2026, the World Cup will be expanded to include 48 countries and 104 matches from the current 36 countries, playing 64 games. There will be 6 matches held at Exhibition Place - BMO Field. The event is the world's most-watched sporting event, with a global viewership of four billion people for the entire competition and upwards of 200 million for each match. Overseen by the International Federation of Football Association (FIFA), the World Cup generates significant economic and cultural benefits for host cities.
