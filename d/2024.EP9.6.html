What the item proposed

Exhibition Place employee recognized by the Board for recent outstanding accomplishment: 1. 2023 United Way City of Toronto Campaign Each year in an effort to build a better city, the City of Toronto Public Service Staff, along with all Agencies, Boards, and Commissions participate in the annual city-wide fundraising program in support of United Way Toronto and York Region campaigns. Ms. Catherine de Nobriga is recognized for her dedication and hard work managing the 2023 United Way City of Toronto Campaign for Exhibition Place. This year, Exhibition Place raised a grand total of $4,100.86 for the United Way of Toronto Campaign. In 2023 we focused our efforts on a few of the City Programs - BINGO and the Cash Raffle, along with our events that have been successful in the past Ghost Walks, Old Technology Sales, and Dress Down days.
