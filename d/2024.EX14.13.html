What the item proposed

As the City of Toronto appointee to the Board of Directors of the Ontario Good Roads Association, I attended their annual conference in April 2024. The conference delegates were able to hear from Mayor Olivia Chow, Premier Doug Ford, Provincial Opposition Party Leaders Marit Stiles, and Bonnie Crombie, as well as several Provincial Cabinet Ministers The conference theme, "Connecting Global Change to Local Impacts and Solutions," allowed for the holding of over 100 study/technical seminars which reviewed topics critical to the future success of our transportation infrastructure. The topics which were covered included: Ontario's electricity future, harnessing the power of camera technology, innovations in infrastructure service delivery, and smart city innovations.
