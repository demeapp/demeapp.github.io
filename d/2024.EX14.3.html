What the item proposed

This report responds to a request from City Council for the Chief Information Security Officer to report on the progress of developing an implementation plan for an independent and centralized information technology risk and compliance, privacy, and cyber security function, as per item #15 of 2021.AU8.8 . Additionally, this report recommends extending the scope of the authority of the Chief Information Security Officer to mitigate cyber security risks across all City agencies and corporations. In 2020, City Council established the Office of the Chief Information Security Officer, an independent cyber security division based on the Auditor General’s recommendation.
