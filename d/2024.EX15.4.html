What the item proposed

This report responds to City Council's request to conduct a governance and operational review of Sankofa Square[1], including but not limited to considerations such as publicly operating Sankofa Square in the same manner as Nathan Phillips Square (NPS). The review included a jurisdictional scan and engagement with Board members. Key findings of the review include: -Sankofa Square has a broader mandate than the City's civic squares which includes economic development and community and commercial objectives. -Sankofa Square's governance and operations model is similar to other North American squares that have broad mandates. -Sankofa Square is mandated to be financially self-sufficient while the civic squares are not and therefore Sankofa Square can generate revenues from advertisements, sponsorships and commercial events.
