What was decided

City Council on July 24 and 25, 2024, adopted the following: 1. Due to the absence of any further 2023 intergovernmental funding support, City Council approve the one-time withdrawal of $739.3 million COVID-19 backstop funding from the Tax Rate Stabilization Reserve to offset the remaining unfavourable variance to the 2023 operating year-end results. 2. City Council direct that $200 million generated from the 2023 operating year-end Toronto Shelter and Support Services one-time underspending, as a result of New Deal intergovernmental funding support, to be transferred to the Working Capital Reserve for addressing expected future shelter demand requirements. 3. City Council approve the recommended expenditure authority as detailed in Appendix D to the report (July 2, 2024) from the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer.
