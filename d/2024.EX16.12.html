What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide City Council with the City of Toronto capital spending for the four-month period ended April 30, 2024, as well as the projected 2024 year-end expenditures. Furthermore, this report seeks Council's approval for in-year budget adjustments to previous approved Capital Budget and Plan as outlined in the Appendix 3 of this report. What was decided

City Council on July 24 and 25, 2024, adopted the following: 1.
