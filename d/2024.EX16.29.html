What the item proposed

At its meeting held on June 24, 2024, the Toronto Police Service Board (Board) considered a report dated May 16, 2024, regarding “Toronto Police Service applications to the 14 programs under the Province of Ontario Community Safety Project Grants” from Chief Demkiw. Minute number P.2024-0624-11.0 is attached along with the Chief’s report. The Board approved the foregoing report and agreed to forward a copy of the report to Toronto City Council via the City of Toronto Executive Committee for information.

What was decided

The Executive Committee: 1. Received the report (May 16, 2024) from the Chief of Police for information.
