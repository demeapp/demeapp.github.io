What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide City Council with the Operating Variance for the six months ended June 30, 2024, as well as projections to the year-end, December 31, 2024. This report also requests City Council's approval for amendments to the 2024 Approved Operating Budget that have no impact on the City's Net Budget. What was decided

City Council on October 9 and 10, 2024, adopted the following: 1.
