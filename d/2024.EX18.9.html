The official record

What the item proposed

This report requests Council authority to adopt the necessary by-law to levy interim property taxes for all property classes for 2025 and to prescribe applicable interim bill payment due dates. The 2025 interim levy will raise approximately $2.85 billion for City purposes and will provide for the cash requirements of the City until such time as the 2025 Operating Budget and 2025 final property tax levy are approved by Council.

What was decided

City Council on November 13 and 14, 2024, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize that the 2025 interim levy for all property classes be based on 50 percent of the total 2024 taxes billed for each property, adjusted, as necessary, to reflect any additional taxes added to the previous year's taxes as a result of assessment added to the tax roll. 2. City Council authorize that the interim levy apply to assessments added to the tax roll for 2024 that were not on the assessment roll when the By-law was passed. 3.
