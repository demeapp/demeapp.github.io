This is a procedural item: Etobicoke York Community Council decides on demolishing two vacant buildings at 140 The Queensway; staff recommend refusal.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is for a matter for which the Etobicoke York Community Council has the delegated authority to make a final decision. In accordance with Residential Demolition Control, as specified under the City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 363, Article 6 'Demolition Control' and pursuant to the authority of Section 33 of the Planning Act, the application for the demolition of two existing detached residential buildings, a single-storey building and a two-storey building located at 140 The Queensway (Application No. 24 167946 DEM), is being referred to the Etobicoke York Community Council. Etobicoke York Community Council has the authority to either refuse or approve the demolition applications. The reason for this report is that a building permit has not yet been issued for a replacement building at 140 The Queensway.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Approved the application to demolish the two vacant residential buildings at 140 The Queensway without any conditions.
