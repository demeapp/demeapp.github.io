What was decided

The General Government Committee: 1. Harris Water Treatment Plant, in the amount of $54,920,037 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($55,886,630 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) to Alberici Constructors Ltd, having submitted the lowest compliant bid and meeting the specifications in conformance with the Tender requirements. 2.
