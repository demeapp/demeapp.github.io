Council was asked to permanently close part of Eglinton Avenue West for the new Caledonia GO station.

Official title: Permanent Road Closure - Eglinton Avenue West, West of Croham Road

AdoptedApr 17, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report proposes to permanently close an unused portion of a public highway located in the northwest quadrant of Croham Road and Eglinton Avenue West to support new infrastructure for the new Caledonia GO station on the Barrie rail corridor. City Council approval of this report is required to stop-up and close this unused portion of the Eglinton Avenue West right-of-way. Transportation Services recommends the permanent closure of the land legally described as Part 1 on Plan 66R-31588. This closure would allow this land to be nominally transferred to Metrolinx to become part of the Caledonia GO station, through the delegated authority of the Deputy General Manager, Internal Corporate Services and the Director, Real Estate Services.

What was decided

City Council on April 17 and 18, 2024, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the permanent closure of the unused public road known as Eglinton Avenue West, shown as Part 1 on Plan 66R-31588 and Property Plate, attached as Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (March 12, 2024) from the General Manager, Transportation Services. 2. City Council enact a by-law substantially in the form of the draft by-law attached as Attachment 3 to the report (March 12, 2024) from the General Manager, Transportation Services.
