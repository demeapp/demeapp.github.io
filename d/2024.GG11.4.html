The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Blanket Contract Number 47024406 issued to Guild Electric Limited for Part A, for the Provision of Traffic Paint Repainting and Custom Painting Service as Required on Roads and Expressways within the City of Toronto. This amendment is necessary to ensure the continuity of required services under Blanket Contract Number 47024406 and to facilitate the processing of all outstanding invoices. The contract is overspent due to the refreshment of all priority locations within the scope of work. Unlike previous years, Transportation Services was able to complete the entire paint program, including the application of the second coat. That resulted in unanticipated additional costs, which were not initially budgeted for, crucial for maintaining program quality and integrity. What was decided

The General Government Committee: 1. In accordance with section 71-11.1C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Financial Control), authorized the amendment of Blanket Contract Number 47024406 issued to Guild Electric Limited by an additional amount of $650,000 net of all taxes and charges ($661,440 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries), revising the current Blanket Contract Number value from $1,316,318 ($1,339,485 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) to $1,966,318 net of all taxes and charges ($2,000,925 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) to cover the over expenditures and facilitate the processing of all outstanding invoices.
