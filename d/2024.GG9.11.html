What the item proposed

On May 11, 2022, City Council authorized the initiation of expropriation proceedings for the partial fee simple taking and temporary easements from the freehold and leasehold property located at 2 Bloor Street East and 90 Bloor Street East, and on July 19, 2022, City Council further authorized the initiation of expropriation proceedings for the full fee simple taking of the freehold property located at 2 Bloor Street East, for the purposes of expanding the Bloor Yonge interchange station as part of the Toronto Transit Commission Bloor Yonge Capacity Improvement Project (the "Project"). This report relates to the second stage of the expropriation process for both expropriations.
