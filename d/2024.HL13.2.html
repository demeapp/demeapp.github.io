This is a health item: Council was asked to authorize food security contracts with Working Women Community Centre.

Official title: Service Agreements Awarded and Executed by the Medical Officer of Health for 2024

AdoptedJun 26, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide information on the purchase of service contracts awarded and executed by the Medical Officer of Health for 2024, according to the delegation of authority by City Council. This report also requests approval for the Medical Officer of Health to award, execute and amend, on an ongoing basis, purchase of services contracts with Working Women Community Centre to deliver food handler training and certification, food literacy, and employment skills development to low-income community participants, including newcomers and refugees.

What was decided

City Council on June 26 and 27, 2024, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the Medical Officer of Health to award, execute, and amend, on an ongoing basis, purchase of service agreements for food security initiatives with Working Women Community Centre, within the approved Toronto Public Health budget, in accordance with applicable program and funding guidelines, on terms and conditions satisfactory to the Medical Officer of Health and in a form approved by the City Solicitor.
