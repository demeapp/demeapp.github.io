What the item proposed

Since 2022, mpox has been circulating in Toronto and globally. It is spread person to person through direct or indirect contact with infected lesions or blisters or with bodily fluids, or respiratory secretions. In Toronto, it is mostly transmitted between people who have had close, intimate, or sexual contact, and it has been disproportionately impacting men who have sex with men. The strain circulating in Toronto, known as clade II mpox, is rarely associated with serious illness. TPH has a comprehensive mpox response that focuses on collaboration across the organization and in the community. What was decided

The Board of Health: 1. Requested the Ministry of Health and Health Canada to explore opportunities to increase access and reduce barriers to health care providers providing the mpox vaccine to their patients.
