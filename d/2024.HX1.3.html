The official record

What the item proposed

The Director, Public Health, Strategy and Clinical Services, Toronto Public Health will give a presentation on Developing a New Strategic Plan for Toronto Public Health.

What was decided

The Board of Health – Strategic Plan Development Committee recommends that: 1. The Board of Health request the Medical Officer of Health develop a strategic plan that: a. has a length of four years, from 2024 to 2028, to ensure consistency and direction between terms; b. follows the timeline for development as outlined in Attachment 1 to the motion by Councillor Alejandra Bravo; and c. prioritizes embedding the Indigenous cultural safety principles (respect, truth, humility, engagement, and reciprocity). 2. The Board of Health request the Medical Officer of Health further engage the public, stakeholders, City Divisions and Toronto Public Health staff on the Strategic Plan development between February and April 2024 and share the results of this consultation at the April 24, 2024 Board of Health – Strategic Plan Development Committee meeting. 3.
