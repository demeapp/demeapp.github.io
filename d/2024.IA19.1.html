What the item proposed

Councillor Chris Moise, Ward 13, Toronto Centre, has submitted the following Administrative Inquiry: I am submitting this Administrative Inquiry under Municipal Code S27-7.11 for the June 26, 2024 meeting of City Council to seek information on the missing video archive of the Toronto Transit Commission Board Meeting held on Thursday, February 22, 2024. As a Commissioner, I submitted an Administrative Inquiry to the Toronto Transit Commission Board and received a response at our meeting on April 11, 2024. In the response from Toronto Transit Commission Staff, they explained: On the morning of Friday, February 23, Toronto Transit Commission staff became aware that the archived video feed from the February 22, 2024, Toronto Transit Commission Board meeting was unavailable on YouTube.
