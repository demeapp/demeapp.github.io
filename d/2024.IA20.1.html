What the item proposed

Councillor Michael Thompson, Ward 21, Scarborough Centre, has submitted the following Administrative Inquiry: I am submitting this Administrative Inquiry under Municipal Code S27-7.11 for the July 24, 2024 meeting of City Council. City Council 2023.AU2.7 adopted the Auditor General’s June 21, 2023 report with 19 recommendations to strengthen the planning, oversight, tracking, monitoring, management and reporting of project costs of all Phase One and Phase Two Modular Housing projects. A future work plan would see the AG examine whether supportive housing is effective in meeting the needs of tenants. 39 Dundalk Drive, included in Phase Two of the Modular Housing Initiative, opened 57 units last November.
