What the item proposed

This report summarizes Toronto Water’s administrative compliance and enforcement responsibilities and activities for 2023 under the City of Toronto's Municipal Code Chapter 681 ("Sewers By-law") and Municipal Code Chapter 851 ("Water Supply By-law"). In 2023, Toronto Water conducted 967 tasks under the Sewers By-law - including inspections, field / site analysis, stakeholder meetings and 177 inspections under the Water Supply By-law; completed 3,076 sampling events resulting in 15,478 laboratory analyses; issued 335 Notices of Violation for non-compliance with the Sewers By-law and 15 with the Water Supply By-law; and responded to 689 pollution complaints. Legal action moved forward against 25 companies for violations of both By-laws.
