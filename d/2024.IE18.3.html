City Council considers selling clean fuel credits from the City's natural gas waste fleet.

Official title: Compressed Natural Gas Environmental Attributes

AdoptedDec 17, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The City's Solid Waste Management Services has been transitioning from diesel-powered trucks to quieter and more environmentally conscious natural-gas-powered trucks since 2010, with 96 percent of the City’s in-house residential waste collection fleet now using natural gas. To support the move away from diesel, Solid Waste Management Services had compressed natural gas fuelling stations installed at three of its sites. As a result of its compressed natural gas use and ownership of any related environmental attributes, Solid Waste Management Services has an opportunity to benefit from the creation and sale of compliance credits pursuant to the Clean Fuel Regulations under the Canadian Environmental Protection Act and may be eligible to generate environmental attributes under other programs as well. What was decided

City Council on December 17 and 18, 2024, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the General Manager, Solid Waste Management Services and/or designate, to negotiate, enter into, and execute any new agreements, amendments of existing agreements, or acknowledgements - including on the basis of a non-competitive procurement under Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 195, Procurement, necessary in connection with the efforts to generate, manage, market, distribute, use and sell in a manner beneficial to the City the environmental attributes associated with Solid Waste Management Services' compressed natural gas use in vehicles, on terms and conditions acceptable to the General Manager, Solid Waste Management Services and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor.
