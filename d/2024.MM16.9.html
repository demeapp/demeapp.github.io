The official record

What the item proposed

This motion is to assist the Toronto District School Board and ABC Residents Association to upgrade the outdoor running track at Jesse Ketchum School, which is available for community use outside school hours, with $300,000 in Section 37 funds. In 2020, $2,190,000 of Section 37 funds from 1-11 Bloor Street West, 768-784 Yonge Street, and 760-762 Yonge Street were earmarked, by By-Law 685-2020, for new or existing cultural and community facilities. The Jesse Ketchum School outdoor running track and schoolyard provide important green space for community and recreational activities. There is no comparable track in any public park in the area. The current track is inadequate and does not meet the needs of either the school or the local community.
