What the item proposed

City Council, at its meeting on July 19, 2022, adopted Item 2022.EY33.8, approving a Zoning By-law amendment for the lands located at 337-349 Queens Drive to allow for the construction of 20 townhouses and eight semi-detached homes. The Bills were held pending the applicant satisfactorily addressing the requirements of Engineering and Construction Services and Transportation Services. During the review of the Site Plan Control application for 337-349 Queens Drive, staff identified two regulations that were inadvertently omitted from the draft Zoning By-law Amendment attached to Item EY33.8 that are necessary to permit the proposed development.
