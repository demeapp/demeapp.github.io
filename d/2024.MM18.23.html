What the item proposed

The lands at 57-77 Wade are being re-developed for the purpose of constructing a 7-storey commercial office building. The project is well underway having been re-zoned and having received initial Notice of Approval Conditions for Site Plan Control on June 18, 2020, with an amendment on September 23, 2021. One of the conditions related to the Notice of Approval Conditions was the conveyance of a 3-metre sub-strata piece of land surrounding the Toronto Transit Commission tunnel which is approximately 9 metres below grade. The Developer has agreed to provide the lands as per the Notice of Approval Conditions. However, City policy is that it cannot accept land that has not been tested and shown to be free of environmental contaminants.
