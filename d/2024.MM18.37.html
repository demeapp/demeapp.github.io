What the item proposed

On October 11, 2023, Toronto City Council adopted Item 2023.MM11.32, Responding to Toronto Artscape Inc. Receivership Proceedings, which provided information and sought direction regarding a pending receivership proceeding that was anticipated at that time to be commenced against Toronto Artscape Inc. Throughout the fall, City staff worked with Artscape to develop comprehensive transition plans for Artscape’s 14 properties ahead of the receivership, with the objective of ensuring continuity for Artscape’s 390 residential and commercial tenants. A Receiver was appointed over Artscape’s operations by a decision of the Ontario Superior Court of Justice on January 11, 2024. Of Artscape’s 390 residential and commercial tenants at its various properties, the vast majority were excluded from the scope of the Receivership.
