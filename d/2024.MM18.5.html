What the item proposed

Toronto recently earned the title of most congested city in North America, outranking perennial contenders like New York, Los Angeles and Mexico City. Travel times on the Gardiner Expressway have doubled in recent weeks and it’s killing our local businesses, choking our economy, and compromising quality of life for millions of Torontonians. We are quickly becoming a city where people do not want to live or work. People are looking for relief and results. It’s time City Hall took the impacts of traffic congestion more seriously. The purpose of this motion is to request that Transportation Services bring forward a comprehensive action plan to accelerate construction of the Gardiner Expressway and alleviate the traffic impacts that have brought our city to a grinding halt and hurt our economy.
