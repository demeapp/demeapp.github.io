What the item proposed

This motion requests authority from City Council to increase the 2024 Operating Budget for Housing Secretariat by $500,000 in order to support The Elizabeth Fry Society’s affordable housing development at 215 Wellesley Street East. In 2022, Elizabeth Fry received approval to construct an addition to their existing building at 215 Wellesley Street East in order to create 32 new transitional housing units, as well as new space for community services, including counselling services, court diversion programs, employment supports, and other multi-purpose spaces that can meet the evolving needs of Elizabeth Fry Toronto's operations in the future. Construction is well underway and the building is expected to open this year.
