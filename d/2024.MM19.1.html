What the item proposed

This motion seeks approval to release Section 37 Funds in the amount of $90,000.00 to the Toronto District School Board to facilitate the inclusion of stationary fitness equipment at Don Mills Collegiate Institute. Don Mills Collegiate Institute is undertaking a park project to rejuvenate the facilities offered. Support for this initiative is required to direct funds to facilitate the inclusion of stationary exercise equipment which will be enjoyed by all ages, including seniors. The funds will be forwarded to the Toronto District School Board once the Toronto District School Board has entered into a Community Access Agreement with the City, governing the purpose of the funds, the financial reporting requirements, and addressing community access to the improved facilities, to the satisfaction of the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation and the City Solicitor, prepared in…
