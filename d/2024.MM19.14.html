What the item proposed

This Motion seeks to contribute $100,000 of Section 37 funds towards the Dewson Street Junior Public School schoolyard revitalization project. The scope of the improvements includes a multi-use sports court, track and field, new playground, and outdoor learning spaces. The Dewson Street Junior schoolyard is a valuable and heavily used green space. In the absence of a City playground nearby, the school grounds serve that purpose for the wider community.
