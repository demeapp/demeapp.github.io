The official record

What the item proposed

This motion is aimed at allowing for the partial demolition of a site as certain properties within the site are seeing increased vandalism and safety concerns. It also makes provisions for public art onsite. No tenants are currently onsite on these particular properties. At its meeting of May 10, 2023, Toronto City Council adopted Item TE4.6 approving a Rental Housing Demolition Application to demolish 12 rental dwelling units located at 152-164 Bathurst Street and 621-627 Richmond Street West, which included conditions that must be met prior to issuance of the rental housing demolition permit and residential demolition permit. These pre-conditions included a requirement that permits for excavation and shoring (conditional or full permit) for the approved development on the site had been issued.
