What the item proposed

A Member Motion is required at City Council to re-open and amend the decision on NY17.11 from the October 9 and 10, 2024 City Council meeting, because Recommendation 7 from the staff report (September 16, 2024 ) from the Director, Community Planning, North York District was not presented properly on the agenda at that time of the North York Community Council meeting. This motion is required to amend and correct the decision on NY17.11, so it accurately reflects the recommendations in the revised report (September 16, 2024) from the Director, Community Planning, North York District. REQUIRES RE-OPENING: North York Community Council NY17.11 (October 9 and 10, 2024 City Council decision), as it relates to Part 7 of the City Council decision only.
