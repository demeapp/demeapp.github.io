What the item proposed

Municipalities in Ontario are considering how to support members of their communities most effectively in actions that will help mitigate climate change, especially regarding Greenhouse Gas emissions from buildings. Many commercial building owners and operators encounter financial barriers to energy and water retrofits and new construction components due to high debt loads, competing needs for other capital improvements, split incentives between building operators who must invest in energy efficiency improvements and their tenants who benefit from the lower energy and water bills, as well as by the longer payback periods that deep energy and emissions retrofits often require to be profitable.
