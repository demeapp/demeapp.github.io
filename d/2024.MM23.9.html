What the item proposed

At its meeting on April 17, 2024, Toronto City Council adopted item NY12.8 approving a Rental Housing Demolition Application to demolish 47 rental dwelling units located at 680 and 688 Sheppard Avenue East, which included conditions that must be met prior to issuance of the rental housing demolition permit and residential demolition permit. The building at 680 Sheppard contains a total of 30 dwelling units, of which 12 are rental dwelling units, and all of which are currently vacant. Due to significant health and safety concerns, the applicant would like to demolish the dwelling units located at 680 Sheppard before a building permit for a replacement rental building is issued. This motion also modifies the conditions upon which demolition permit can be issued by requiring public art to be included on the construction hoarding for the duration of construction.
