What the item proposed

Guildwood Community has a long and rich history starting with Rosa and Spencer Clark who created Canada’s first artists’ community, the Guild of All Arts. The couple welcomed artists of all backgrounds to their property and eager visitors came to see painters, sculptors, weavers and others in action. The site became so popular that the Clarks converted their home into the Guild Inn. In their later years, the Clarks sold their Guild property which now operates as the Guild Park and Gardens. This is the heart of Guildwood Village and contains many architectural artefacts which were saved during the 1960s when Toronto’s downtown was undergoing a major building boom. These treasures such as the Greek Theatre would have been lost forever had it not been for the preservationist mentality of the Clarks. What was decided

City Council on December 17 and 18, 2024, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the Executive Director, Development Review, and the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning to undertake a thorough review of existing development guidelines and zoning regulations within the Guildwood Community. 2.
