The official record

What the item proposed

This motion recommends directing $1,000,000 in local s. 37 funding to enhance capital upgrades at the Yorkville Library. Located at 22 Yorkville Avenue, Yorkville is the oldest library branch in the Toronto Public Library network. Opened in 1907 funded by a grant from the Carnegie foundation, the Yorkville branch became a listed Heritage property in 1973. Yorkville is the most historically significant branch in the Toronto Public Library system. Improving the accessibility of this location is an important signal of our commitment to celebrate and modernize our unique heritage buildings by providing modern, accessible library service. The Yorkville branch serves a community of 61,000 at its current location. Population in the immediate area is growing rapidly with the construction of new residential towers.
