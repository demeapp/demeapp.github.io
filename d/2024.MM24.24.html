What the item proposed

The site is comprised of the land on the North-West Corner of Yonge Street and Wilson Ave and includes portions of the Don River. In 2022, a Zoning By-law amendment ( By-law 788-2022 (OLT) ) was finalized permitting two residential towers as part of a mixed-use development containing office and retail uses and associated accessible connection to the existing below grade pedestrian tunnels connecting to York Mills subway station. The Zoning By-law secured the conveyance of the defined “Conservation Land” which includes part of an existing pedestrian tunnel connection to York Mills subway station that serves an existing office complex to the north and includes a 10 metre buffer from the established long-term stable top of slope as identified and confirmed by the Toronto Region Conservation Authority.
