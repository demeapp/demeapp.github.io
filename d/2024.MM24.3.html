What the item proposed

The Queensway Market Gardens ran on both sides of the Queensway from the Humber River to the west of Kipling Avenue. The soil of south Etobicoke attracted farmers who specialized in growing food and flowers to supply the city. The main produce was vegetables with a few farms having apple and pear trees as well as strawberry and raspberry bushes. Typically market gardens were family-centred operations that were passed down through multiple generations. For market gardeners to sell their produce, an informal farmers’ market existed on the Queensway at Park Lawn Road from 1915 to 1918. The road was only paved in 1925, so farmers would use horses and wagons to transport crops, but they would often get caught or bogged down due to the mix of rain and loose soil on their way to market.
