The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission operates a transit service on Yonge Street, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting approval to prohibit northbound and southbound U-turn movements at all times on Yonge Street at Kempford Boulevard. This proposed prohibition will address traffic safety concerns in the area.

What was decided

City Council on May 22 and 23, 2024, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit northbound and southbound U-turn movements at all times on Yonge Street at Kempford Boulevard.
