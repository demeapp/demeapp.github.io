The official record

What the item proposed

This Zoning By-law Amendment application proposes to permit 39-storey (133.0 metre), 33-storey (115 metre) and 14-storey (53 metre) buildings on top of a shared 5-storey (21.5 metre) base building with a 6-storey (27 metre) component of the base building along Steeles Avenue West. The application proposes a total of 960 dwelling units, including the replacement of 120 existing rental units on-site at 1875 Steeles Avenue West. The proposed development is consistent with the Provincial Policy Statement (2020) and conforms with A Place to Grow: Growth Plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe (2020). The proposed development also conforms to the City's Official Plan. This report reviews and recommends approval of the application to amend the Zoning By-law, with a holding provision.
