The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law 569-2013 to permit an 11-storey mixed-use building (37.5 metres excluding a 5.0 metre mechanical penthouse) with 256 dwelling units and 209 underground parking spaces on three levels at 665, 667, 669 and 671 Sheppard Avenue West. The application proposes a total gross floor area of 16,980 square metres, including 154 square metres of retail GFA, resulting in a density of 6.0 times the lot area. The redevelopment proposal is in keeping with the objectives of the Sheppard West / Dublin Secondary Plan to encourage a diversity of commercial and residential uses along Sheppard Avenue West, while protecting the abutting lower density residential uses and improving the streetscape.
