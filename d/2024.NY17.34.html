The council was asked to lower the speed limit to 40 km/h on The Donway East.

Official title: Speed Limits - The Donway between Don Mills Road (south intersection) and Don Mills Road (north intersection)

AdoptedSep 24, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Donway is a ring road that encircles Don Mills Road. to the north and south of Lawrence Avenue East and to the east and west of Don Mills Road. Numerous residents have expressed concerns over the posted speed limits which go back and forth between 40 kilometres per hour and 50 kilometres per hour. The area includes several educational facilities, seniors’ residences, retail locations including the Shops at Don Mills and two community safety zones. A change will provide a consistent speed limit for the entire length of The Donway, improve pedestrian and vehicular safety and end confusion. I am requesting a uniform speed limit of 40 kilometres per hour for the entirety of The Donway.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Reduced the speed limit from 50 kilometres per hour to 40 kilometres per hour on The Donway East, between Don Mills Road (south intersection) and Don Mills Road (north intersection). 2. Reduced the speed limit from 50 kilometres per hour to 40 kilometres per hour on The Donway West between Don Mills Road (south intersection) and Don Mills Road (north intersection).
