Toronto City Council · Decision

The council was asked to replace the parking ban with a standing ban on part of Edith Drive.

Official title: Edith Drive - Parking Amendments

AdoptedSep 24, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that the existing parking prohibition on the west side of Edith Drive, in front of the condominium at 2 Edith Drive, be changed to a standing prohibition. The proposed amendment will deter unloading in the vicinity of the driveway at 2 Edith Drive, which obstructs visibility for exiting vehicles. During the course of the investigation, it was noted that there is a discrepancy between posted signs and the existing parking regulations on Edith Drive. Recommendation 3 will correct this inconsistency.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Edith Drive, between Eglinton Avenue West and a point 48.8 metres north. 2. Prohibited standing at all times on the west side of Edith Drive, between Eglinton Avenue West and a point 48.8 metres north. 3. Amended the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. at all times, except no parking anytime from Eglinton Avenue West to a point 48.8 metres further north on the even (west) side of Edith Drive, to be in effect 12:01 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. at all times, except no standing anytime from Eglinton Avenue West to a point 48.8 metres further north.
