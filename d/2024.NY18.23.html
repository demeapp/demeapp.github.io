North York Community Council was asked to approve speed humps on Millersgrove Drive.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of speed humps on Millersgrove Drive, between Hidden Trail and Fisherville Road. Staff's assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the updated Traffic Calming Policy has been satisfied. Therefore, speed humps should be installed on Millersgrove Drive.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of speed humps on Millersgrove Drive, between Hidden Trail and Fisherville Road. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway for the installation of five speed humps on Millersgrove Drive, between Hidden Trail and Fisherville Road, generally as shown on Attachment 2, Drawing TC-358 and TC-359 dated September 2024, attached to the report (October 10, 2024) from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
