The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes to demolish a total of 60 existing townhouse rental dwelling units at 150 - 160 Cactus Avenue. The 60 units will be replaced as part of the new development in two blocks of back-to-back stacked townhouse units. The proposal also includes a tenant relocation and assistance plan that addresses the right of existing tenants to return to replacement rental units at similar rents and provides financial compensation to mitigate hardship. The properties are also the subject of Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment applications (21 226750 NNY 18 OZ) which were appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal.
