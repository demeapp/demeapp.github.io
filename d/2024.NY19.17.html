City Council considers registering a sanitary sewer easement from Parc Downsview Park.

Official title: Easement from Parc Downsview Park for a Sanitary Sewer to Service the Stanley Greene Subdivision, Registered Plan 66M-2520

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council authorize the City Solicitor to register an easement conveyed by Parc Downsview Park for a sanitary sewer to service the Stanley Greene Subdivision.

What was decided

City Council on December 17 and 18, 2024, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to register an easement conveyed by Parc Downsview Park with terms and conditions satisfactory to Acting Director, Engineering Review, for a sanitary sewer located on Parc Downsview Park lands that currently services the Stanley Green subdivision. 2. City Council authorize the appropriate City Officials to take the necessary action to give effect to City Council’s decision.
