The official record

What the item proposed

This motion proposes to add permit parking on additional streets in the Stanley Green neighbourhood. Five years ago, a motion to include a new permit parking area (Permit Area 18A) along various streets in this neighbourhood was adopted (Item 2019.NY11.13). John Perkins Bull Drive, William Duncan Road, Franco Basille Grove, and Maria lacampo Gardens were not fully developed roads at that time and did not meet the requirements to be included in this amendment. Further transportation developments have taken place throughout Stanley Greene since 2019, and the community continues to grow in density and population.
