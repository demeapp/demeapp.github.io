What the item proposed

On March 31, 2023, an Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application (23 129052 NNY 06 OZ), and Site Plan Control application (23 129057 NNY 06 SA) were deemed complete to permit a 11 storey (40.5 metres including mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building with 156 residential dwelling units and 388.82 square metres of at-grade commercial uses. The current revised proposal was received on June 4, 2024. The applicant currently proposes an 11-storey mixed-use building with a height of 44.3 metres, including a mechanical penthouse. It comprises 186 units, 615 square metres of at-grade commercial space, and 324 square metres of a Privately Owned Publicly Accessible Space. The gross floor area has increased from 13,700 square metres to approximately 14,926 square metres due to changes in building massing, layout, and configuration.
