The official record

What the item proposed

This is a report on the status of the legal action (the "Action") commenced by Toronto Parking Authority on January 21, 2019 against BSaR (Eglinton) Ltd., BSaR (Eglinton) LP, and BSaR's principals, Tarek Sobhi, and Tyler Hershberg (all together referred to as BSaR). In the legal action, Toronto Parking Authority is claiming damages in the amount of $7.0 million for breach of the terms of an Agreement of Purchase and Sale (APS) between BSAR and the Toronto Parking Authority that provided Toronto Parking Authority an option to purchase the retail unit (Retail Component) of a joint venture development at 935 Eglinton Avenue West (the Property).

What was decided

The Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (October 4, 2024) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority and the City Solicitor remain confidential in its entirety, as it contains advice which is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
