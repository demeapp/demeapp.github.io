What the item proposed

During the past three years, the Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) has established Canada’s largest municipally owned electric vehicle (EV) charging network, with 445 charging ports across 84 locations. During this time, we have provided over 140,000 EV charging sessions to more than 23,000 customers, and we expect to deliver close to 200,000 sessions in 2025. This blend of incremental parking and charging revenue makes the Toronto Parking Authority EV charging program one of the few profitable EV charging programmes in North America. In 2025, and consistent with our growth strategy, the Toronto Parking Authority will advance its off-street EV charging portfolio by installing an additional 60 to 85 EV chargers at 13 new locations.
