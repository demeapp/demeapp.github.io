What the item proposed

At its meeting on November 5, 2024, the Toronto Parking Authority - Audit and Risk Management Committee considered Item PR8.3 and made recommendations to the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority. Summary from the report (October 21, 2024) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority: In today's digital age, cyber threats have become increasingly sophisticated, presenting unprecedented challenges to organizations worldwide. The Toronto Parking Authority stands at a crucial intersection where protecting our digital infrastructure is not just about preventing attacks - it's about safeguarding our operational integrity and maintaining public trust. As we navigate this complex landscape, our proactive approach to cybersecurity has become more vital than ever. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (October 21, 2024) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority remain confidential in its entirety, as it involves the security of property belonging to the City or one of its agencies or corporations.
