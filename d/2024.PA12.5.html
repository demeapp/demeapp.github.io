What the item proposed

At its meeting on Tuesday, November 5, 2024, the Toronto Parking Authority – Audit and Risk Management Committee considered Item PR8.4 and made recommendations to the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority. Summary from the report (October 22, 2024) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority: The Board received the proposed budget for information at its meeting on October 18, 2024. The purpose of this report is to provide the Audit and Risk Management Committee of the Board of Directors with Management’s recommended 2025 Operating Budget and 2025 – 2027 Capital Budget in further detail. Formal approval will be requested at the November 29, 2024 Board meeting where Management will also present the 2025 Annual Operating Plan.
