What the item proposed

As part of our vision to become the world’s best provider of sustainable parking, bike share and last mile mobility experiences, Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) has been executing an ambitious rollout plan for Electric Vehicle (EV) charging across the City of Toronto. This plan supports both Management's strategy to grow Toronto Parking Authority’s market share for its core parking business by attracting incremental B2C and B2B customers, while at the same time delivering against the City's TransformTO objectives. Toronto Parking Authority’s off-street EV charging rollout plan is informed by the 2022 EV Charging Strategy and Deployment Plan prepared by Dunsky on behalf of Toronto Parking Authority.
