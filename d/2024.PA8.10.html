What the item proposed

During the past two years and consistent with our strategy, Toronto Parking Authority has designed and built Canada's largest municipally-owned Electric Vehicle (EV) charging network with 407 charging ports at 80 locations. In 2024, Toronto Parking Authority Management will deploy an additional 38 chargers at four (4) car parks. The purpose of this report is two-fold. The first is to seek authority from Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors to award a $1,270,000.00 contract to Precise ParkLink Inc., having submitted the lowest compliant bid, for the construction services required to complete the installation of 38 Electric Vehicle chargers across four (4) car parks in 2024. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to award the Electric Vehicle Infrastructure Construction Contract for 2024-CON-VAR-D50.4 - Car Park 53 (803 Richmond Street West), Car Park 82 (9 Emerson Avenue), Car Park 11 (21 Pleasant Boulevard) and Car Park 26 (33 Queen Street East) to Precise ParkLink Inc., having submitted the lowest compliant bid and meeting all specifications in conformance with the Construction Tender requirements. 2. Authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to issue a Purchase Order to Toronto Hydro Electrical Systems Limited in the amount of $800,000 excluding Harmonized Sales taxfor electrical upgrades required for the development of Toronto Parking Authority’s off-street Electric Vehicle charging network in 2024.
