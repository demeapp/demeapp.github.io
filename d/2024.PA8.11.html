What the item proposed

In our pursuit of our vision to become the world’s best provider of sustainable parking, bike share, and last-mile mobility experiences, our management remains unwavering in its commitment to executing operational fundamentals with excellence each day. To maintain the natural environmental elements around our facilities, the Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) is seeking board approval to award two (2) landscaping maintenance service contracts that will be separated into east and west areas of the city. These contracts will address our landscaping needs including flower bed clean up, cultivation of flower beds, fertilizing, weekly maintenance, watering, and the planting of shrubs and trees at off-street parking facilities.
