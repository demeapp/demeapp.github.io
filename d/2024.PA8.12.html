What the item proposed

In pursuit of Toronto Parking Authority's vision to become the world's best provider of sustainable parking, bike share, and last-mile mobility experiences, management remains focused on elevating its performance against creating a more safe and secure experience for our customers and staff. The purpose of this paper is to secure Board approval to award a Monthly Fire and Life Safety Inspection Services Contract. This contract will cover the inspection of fire extinguishers, emergency lighting units, fire alarm systems, and sprinkler systems in our parking facilities. Ensuring the integrity of these systems is crucial to delivering not only our regulatory obligations but maintaining a safe and secure environment for everyone. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to award the monthly fire and life safety inspection services contract for FM 2023-06 to Eurotech Safety Inc. who having submitted the only compliant bid and meeting all specifications in conformance with the Request for Proposal requirements, in the amount of $808,690.50 including contingency, plus $640,961.00 including contingency for two (2) optional one (1) year extensions. Bringing the total sum to $1,449,651.50 excluding Harmonized Sales Tax.
