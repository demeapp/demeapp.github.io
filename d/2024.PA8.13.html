The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority has been providing a range of parking management, equipment and revenue collection services to the Owner at ten (10) Exhibition Place car parks totaling 5,668 spaces, within the Exhibition Place grounds since 2016 (refer to Appendix A – Site Location Map). Management has worked with Exhibition Place to negotiate terms and conditions in principle that will extend the arrangement with Toronto Parking Authority for at least a further five (5) years through a consolidation of contracts. Should the recommendations of this report be adopted by the Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority, the Parking Management Agreement will extend the services provided by Toronto Parking Authority for Exhibition Place for a term of five (5) years with the option to extend for one (1) additional term of five (5) years.
