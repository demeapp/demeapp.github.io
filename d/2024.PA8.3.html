The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with Section 2 of the Terms of Reference of the Toronto Parking Authority - Audit and Risk Management Committee, the Board will appoint members to the Committee and appoint a member of the Committee as Chair, for a term ending on March 26, 2025.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Appointed Maureen Farrow, Zeshan Khan, and Namby Vithiananthan to the Audit and Risk Management Committee for a term ending on March 26, 2025. 2. Appointed Zeshan Khan as Chair of the Audit and Risk Management Committee for a term ending on March 26, 2025.
