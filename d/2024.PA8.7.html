What the item proposed

The cyber security landscape continues to evolve rapidly, with emerging threats posing significant risks to our organization. Cyber resiliency and intelligence are keys to success in helping to secure the Toronto Parking Authority against emerging threats. Toronto Parking Authority conducts independent audits on an ongoing basis to strengthen its cyber security practices and closely collaborates with the City of Toronto’s Chief Information Security Office (CISO) to manage and mitigate identified risks. Cyberattacks are unauthorized attempts to gain access to a system and confidential data, modify it somehow, or delete or render information in the system unusable. These are some of the biggest threats facing organizations today.
