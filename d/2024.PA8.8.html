The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to secure approval from the Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority to renew an agreement with Moneris Solution Corporation (Moneris) for Credit/Debit Card processing services for a period four (4) years on favorable terms and reduced rates for certain categories, particularly digital transactions. The Toronto Parking Authority has always provided its customers with choice, speed, and convenience. As a result, our customers have widely adopted digital payment methods as a primary source of transacting. This Board has previously authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to enter into an agreement with Moneris for a term of three (3) years with an option, to be exercised at the sole discretion of the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to renew the agreement for four (4) further one (1) one-year terms. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to amend the existing agreement with Moneris Solution Corporation for credit/debit card processing services and extend it by a term of four (4) years until February 29, 2028.
