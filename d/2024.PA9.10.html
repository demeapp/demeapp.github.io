The official record

What the item proposed

This is a report on the status of the legal action commenced by Toronto Parking Authority on January 21, 2019 against BSaR (Eglinton) Ltd. and BSaR (Eglinton) LP (both together referred to as BSaR), and BSaR's principals, Tarek Sobhi, and Tyler Hershberg. In the legal action, Toronto Parking Authority is claiming damages in the amount of $7.0 million for breach of contract related to an option held by Toronto Parking Authority to purchase the retail unit (Retail Component) of a joint venture development at 935 Eglinton Avenue West (the Property).

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (May 14, 2024) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority and the City Solicitor remain confidential in its entirety, as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
