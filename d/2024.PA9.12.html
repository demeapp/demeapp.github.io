The official record

What the item proposed

As part of our vision to become the world’s best provider of sustainable parking, bike share and last mile mobility experiences, Toronto Parking Authority is executing an ambitious rollout plan for Electric Vehicle (EV) charging across the City of Toronto. With over 407 Electric Vehicle chargers deployed over the last twenty-four months, Toronto Parking Authority's network of Electric Vehicle chargers has quickly scaled to become the largest municipally-owned network in Canada. Despite this progress, Toronto remains materially undeveloped versus its North American peers. The Toronto Parking Authority plans to address this situation over the next five years.
