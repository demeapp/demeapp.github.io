The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority has prepared a draft of the 2023 Annual Report, which outlines our accomplishments in 2023 and includes a joint message from the Toronto Parking Authority's Board Chair and President (refer to Attachment 1). Through Item PA9.1, the 2023 Audited Financial Statements are also being submitted to the Board for approval. Should the Board approve both the draft Annual Report and the Audited Financial Statements, Toronto Parking Authority will incorporate the Audited Financial Statements into the 2023 Annual Report and finalize for distribution and publishing on Toronto Parking Authority's website.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Approved the 2023 Toronto Parking Authority Annual Report for distribution.
