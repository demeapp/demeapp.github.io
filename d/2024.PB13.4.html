The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve the alterations proposed for the heritage properties at 127 (121-123) Church Street and 89 Queen Street East under Section 33 of the Ontario Heritage Act and approve the full removal and partial reconstruction of the building on the property at 119 Church Street under Section 34 of the Ontario Heritage Act, in connection with the development of the subject site. On December 13, 14 and 15, 2023 City Council adopted the staff report recommending designation of the subject properties under Part IV of the Ontario Heritage Act. All of the subject properties were designated for their design and contextual value, with 119 and 123 Church Street and 89 Queen Street East also having historical or associative value.
