The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve the alterations under Section 33 of the Ontario Heritage Act proposed for the heritage properties at 414, 416 and 418 Church Street and 79 and 81 Granby Street in connection with the redevelopment of the site. Located in the McGill-Granby Village community, the properties at 414-418 Church Street, known as the Stephen Murphy Houses and Store, contain two house form buildings adjoining a commercial unit. The properties at 79 and 81 Granby Street, known as the Robert Kidney Houses, contain two semi-detached residential house-form buildings. The proposal involves the construction of a new 32-storey mixed-use development with retail uses at grade and residential uses in the upper storeys.
