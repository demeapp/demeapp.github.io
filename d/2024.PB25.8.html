The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this final report is to summarize the findings of the Weston (Phase 2) Heritage Conservation District (HCD) Study ("the Study") conducted by City staff, and to recommend that the Toronto Preservation Board endorse staff's recommendation that the Study not proceed to the Plan Phase because the study area does not meet the provincial criteria to merit Part V designation under the Ontario Heritage Act. On January 31, 2018, City Council adopted item PG25.4 directing that a Heritage Conservation District study be initiated for the Weston (Phase 2) HCD Study Area as identified on the map in Attachment 1 to this report. In June 2019, City staff initiated a study of the Weston (Phase 2) study area to determine whether it merited designation under Part V of the Ontario Heritage Act (OHA). What was decided

The Toronto Preservation Board: 1. Received for information the report (November 18, 2024) from the Senior Manager, Heritage Planning, Urban Design, City Planning, entitled Weston (Phase 2) Heritage Conservation District Study - Final Report. 2. Endorsed the list of heritage potential properties recommended for further research and evaluation in Attachment 7 – Properties with Potential Cultural Heritage Value Recommended for Further Research and that staff bring forward recommendations for any properties that merit inclusion on the City's Heritage Register.
