The official record

What the item proposed

On February 2, 2022, City Council approved Official Plan Amendment 554 (OPA 554), related Site and Area Specific Policy 670 (SASP 670) and Zoning By-law Amendment 101-2022 to permit garden suites on properties in most residential zones across the City. The amendments were appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT), although the Tribunal dismissed the appeals after determining that it did not have jurisdiction to hear the appeals. The amendments adopted by Council to permit garden suites are rooted in the principles to create complete communities and provide a full range of housing in terms of form, tenure, and affordability for residents across the City in a form that makes efficient use of land. A "garden suite" is an additional self-contained living accommodation, usually located in the rear yard, but not on a public lane, and is separate or detached from the main house on the lot.…
