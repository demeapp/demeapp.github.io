The official record

What the item proposed

This report is the sixth in a series of Final Reports that provide staff recommendations on Employment Area conversion requests as part of the Municipal Comprehensive Review (MCR) of the Official Plan. In 2023, Toronto’s Employment Areas were home to over 21,900 establishments employing nearly 398,000 people (26% of all jobs in the city). Businesses within our Employment Areas create about 27% of the City’s estimated 2021 GDP of $195.7 billion and contribute about 12% of all property taxes paid in the City (municipal and education taxes). This report also highlights the social and economic role that Employment Areas play in the health and growth of the city supporting Toronto’s diverse economic base and providing jobs with living wages for the City’s diverse labour pool.
