What the item proposed

There is a continuous stream of people entering and leaving the city annually, creating varied demand for housing of all types and tenures. The Housing Occupancy Trends bulletin examines the influence of demographic, social and market trends on housing occupancy in Toronto between 2001 and 2021. Ongoing monitoring of household occupancy trends provides background information to support the continued monitoring, review and development of Official Plan policies and can assist other Divisions within the City in the consideration of their programs. Many of the findings reported in this bulletin are a continuation of trends reported in the previous Housing Occupancy Trend bulletin, published in 2019.
