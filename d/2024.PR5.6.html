What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority aspires to be the world's best provider of sustainable parking, Bike Share and last mile mobility experiences. As part of this journey, Management is continually seeking opportunities to streamline its policies, systems, and decision-making processes to provide greater agility for Team Toronto Parking Authority to deliver real-time outcomes and compete in the marketplace. The purpose of this report is to provide the Toronto Parking Authority - Audit and Risk Management Committee and the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority with a preview of amendments that Management will be proposing to Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 5-10: Delegation of Authority and a proposed delegation of operationally focused policies to the President, Toronto Parking Authority. Formal approval will be requested at the July 19, 2024, meeting of the Board.…

What was decided

The Toronto Parking Authority – Audit and Risk Management Committee recommends that: 1. The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority receive the report (April 6, 2024) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority for information.
