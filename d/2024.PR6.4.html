What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to reconcile an inconsistency between Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolutions with respect to the authority of the President and certain Management to award a contract through a non-competitive procurement process or limited solicitation. Under Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 5-7: Procurement, the President and certain Management are provided the authority to award a contract through a non-competitive procurement process or limited solicitation. These same authorities are not reflected in Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 5-10: Delegation of Authority.
