The official record

What the item proposed

The Access Plan for Two-Spirit, Trans and Non-Binary Youth (ages 12-29) is the City’s first coordinated initiative to improve health and social outcomes for gender-diverse people in Toronto. Social Development, Finance and Administration will be presenting an update on the development and implementation of the 56 actions outlined in the Plan. Social Development, Finance and Administration previously brought a draft of the Plan to the 2SLGBTQ+ Advisory Committee in 2021 ( 2021.QS4.3 ).

What was decided

The Two-Spirit, Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender and Queer Advisory Committee received the item for information.
