The official record

What the item proposed

This report addresses an appeal respecting a Sign Variance application (the “Sign Variance”). The Sign Variance contains one first party electronic ground sign, displaying both static copy and electronic moving copy (the "Proposed Sign"), which would be located on the north frontage of the property municipally known as 427 Bloor Street West (the “Subject Premises”). The Proposed Sign would have two sign faces, in a “v-shaped” configuration, each of the sign faces are proposed to measure 2.53 metres vertically by 1.93 metres horizontally, and height of 3.66 metres. The Proposed Sign would identify and provide information for Trinity - Saint Paul’s Centre located on the Subject Premises. A Sign variance was originally applied by Jordan’s Sign Permits on behalf of Steel Art Sign Corp (collectively known as the "Applicant"), as authorized by Trinity Saint Paul’s United Church (the “Owner”).…
