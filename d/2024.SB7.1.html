The official record

What the item proposed

This report addresses an appeal of the decision of the Chief Building Official and Executive Director, Toronto Building (“CBO”) concerning an application for a Signage Master Plan regulating a portion of the premises municipally known as 2075 Bayview Avenue (“2075-Bayview”) consisting of the majority of the northern elevation of a the building known as “S-Wing” (the "Specific Location") concerning three variances sought for three first party illuminated wall signs displaying static copy (the “Proposed Signage Master Plan”) . Two of the wall signs in the Proposed Signage Master Plan (“Proposed Sign 1” and “Proposed Sign 2”) would have been located on the fifth storey, and the other (“Proposed Sign 3”) would have been located on the sixth storey of the northerly elevation of the S-Wing building at 2075-Bayview.
