The official record

What the item proposed

This report addresses an appeal concerning one variance sought respecting a Signage Master Plan proposal (the “Proposed Signage Master Plan”). The Proposed Signage Master Plan addresses a defined area consisting of only a northeast portion of the roof of the building (commonly known as the Eaton Centre) on the premises, municipally known as 33 Dundas Street West (the "Subject Premises"). The Proposed Signage Master Plan contains one first party illuminated roof sign displaying static copy (the "Proposed Roof Sign"). The Proposed Roof Sign would have one sign face, displaying static copy, and would be identifying the Bank of Montreal (“BMO”) on the Subject Premises. A Signage Master Plan was originally applied for by Zip Signs (the "Applicant") on behalf of Cadillac Fairview (the “Owner”).
