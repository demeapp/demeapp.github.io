The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this staff report is to seek City Council approval of the Public Art Plan (“The Plan”) for 25 Borough Drive. The Plan, which is included as Attachment 1 of this report, outlines the method by which the owner will commission public art within the privately-owned publicly accessible space (“POPS”) on the site. The Plan provides a framework and description of the commissioning of public art including: introduction; site overview; potential collaborators; community context, art consultant role; the section 37 agreement; public art approach; public art objectives; public art location; art selection strategy; jury composition; mentorship opportunities; proposed public art budget; and timeline. The attached Plan meets the objectives of the City's Percent for Public Art Guidelines and is supported by the Toronto Public Art Commission (“TPAC”).

What was decided

City Council on June 26 and 27, 2024, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve the Public Art Plan for 25 Borough Drive attached as Attachment 1 to the report (May 24, 2024) from the Director, Urban Design, City Planning.
