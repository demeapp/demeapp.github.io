This is a procedural item: Councillor Ainslie asks the committee to add two speed humps on Pegasus Trail.

Official title: Pegasus Trail - Traffic Calming (Speed Humps)

AdoptedJul 12, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On behalf of area residents, I am writing to address ongoing traffic safety concerns on Pegasus Trail, which were raised during the community meeting held on February 27, 2024. Residents have reported excessive speeding still occurring on Pegasus Trail, particularly in sections where no speed humps are installed. This high rate of speed poses a safety hazard to the community. In response to these reports, I have contacted the Traffic Operations department to investigate the feasibility of installing additional speed humps on Pegasus Trail. Their recommendation regarding this matter is before you for consideration. Thank you for carefully review on the matter. The recommendations align with the concerns raised by residents, and I urge you to support its implementation to ensure the safety of all residents on Pegasus Trail.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of two additional speed humps on Pegasus Trail, between Portico Drive and Netheravon Road. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway to install two additional speed humps on Pegasus Trail, between Portico Drive and Netheravon Road, generally as shown on Drawing 421G-4087, to the letter (June 21, 2024) from Councillor Paul Ainslie.
