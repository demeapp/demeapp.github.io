Toronto City Council · Decision

This is a procedural item: Councillor Myers asks the committee for parking bans and speed humps on Scottfield Drive for safety.

Official title: Scottfield Drive - Increasing Traffic Safety

AdoptedJul 12, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The importance of safety for our seniors and families as they walk and drive on our streets is paramount. Given the number of seniors’ residences, long term care facilities, townhomes and businesses that line Scottfield Drive, there is ongoing concern that the vehicular traffic in this area needs to be slowed in order to increase safety. This motion is a direct response to community concerns by calling for safety adjustments on Scottfield Drive between Middlefield Road and Finch Avenue East to ensure the safety of local residents and all road users.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking at all times on the west side of Scottfield Drive between a point 136 metres south of Finch Avenue East and a point 65 metres further south. 2. Authorized the installation of speed humps on Scottfield Drive between Middlefield Road and Finch Avenue East. 3. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway for the installation of seven speed humps on Scottfield Drive, between Middlefield Road and Finch Avenue East, generally as shown on the attached Drawing Nos. TC-312 and TC313, to the letter (June 25, 2024) from Councillor Jamaal Myers.
