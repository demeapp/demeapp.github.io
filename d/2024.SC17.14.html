Toronto City Council · Decision

The council was asked to approve speed humps on Waldock Street.

Official title: Waldock Street - Traffic Calming/Speed Humps

AdoptedNov 1, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

There have been increasing concerns about speeding along Waldock Street, which compromises the safety of all road users, especially young children, seniors, and families. Eastview Junior Public School, as well as the entrance to the playground and splash pad in Eastview Park, are located on Waldock Street, emphasizing the need for safer conditions. Drivers are also using Waldock Street during the morning and evening rush hours to access the Guildwood GO station. The installation of speed humps on Waldock Street would help address the safety concerns created by motorists speeding on this street. I recommend that traffic calming measures be implemented to mitigate these risks. I hope you will support this recommendation to install speed humps.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of speed humps on Waldock Street, between Galloway Road and Poplar Road. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway for the installation of six speed humps on Waldock Street, between Galloway Road and Poplar Road, generally as shown on the attached Drawing No. TC-367 to the report (October 9, 2024) from Councillor Paul Ainslie.
