The council was asked to approve speed humps on Torrance Road.

Official title: Torrance Road - Traffic Calming

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The issue at John McCrea Public School involves pedestrian safety with regards to vehicles travelling on McCowan Road and Torrance Road, particularly during school pick-up and drop-off hours. This situation poses a risk to the students, families, and residents who frequently walk or cross these streets. In September, I met with members of the school’s parent-teacher council and local community representatives, who voiced serious concerns about the safety of children navigating these busy roads. They emphasized the frequent speeding and lack of adequate traffic calming as a threat to pedestrian safety, especially in a school zone. The installation of speed humps is a proven measure that can effectively deter speeding, ensuring a safer, slower pace of traffic in these residential streets.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Torrance Road, between Trudelle Street and Radisson Street. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway for the installation of seven speed humps on Torrance Road, between Trudelle Street and Radisson Street, generally as shown on the attached Drawing Nos. TC-384 and TC-385 to the letter (October 29, 2024) from Councillor Michael Thompson. 3. Subject to approval of recommendations 1 and 2 above, reduced the speed limit from 40 km/h to 30 km/h on Torrance Road, between Trudelle Street and Radisson Street, in conjunction with the installation of speed humps.
