The council was asked to amend parking rules on Fairfax Crescent for pedestrian safety.

Official title: Fairfax Crescent - Parking Amendment

AdoptedNov 1, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This proposed motion is intended to address pedestrian safety concerns for students and residents. The subject area is a tight cul-de-sac affecting six houses near a local high school. The high volume of student drop-offs and students parking their vehicles in the cul-de-sac obscure site lines for residents backing out their vehicles from adjacent driveways. The proposed bylaw amendments will add an important safety mechanism for pedestrians.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Fairfax Crescent, between a point 415 metres west of Warden Avenue and Clairlea Crescent, to be in effect at all times between a point 505 metres west of Warden Avenue and Clairlea Crescent. 2.
