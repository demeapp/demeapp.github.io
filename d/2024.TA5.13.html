What the item proposed

This report recommends the Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s internal program allocation request of $946,000 and grant budget allocation request of $100,000 for Board approval.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Approved the allocation of $946,000 to support Toronto Atmospheric Fund programs in 2024 as outlined below, to be funded from the Canada, Ontario, and Toronto endowments except where otherwise indicated: a. Accelerate deployment of electric vehicles (EV) and charging: $14,000 to expand EV charging for multi-family building residents and vehicle for hire drivers; b. Decarbonizing Ontario’s electricity system: $94,000 support the region’s transition to a net-zero grid; c. Retrofit Accelerator: $265,000 to build the market for deep retrofits by collaborating with leading building operators and industry solutions providers to deliver deeper retrofits faster and more cost-effectively; d.
