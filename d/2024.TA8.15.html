What the item proposed

The Direct Investment Committee (DC) recommends an investment of up to $1,650,000 to finance Multi-Unit Residential Building (MURB) upgrades for comprehensive Electric Vehicle (EV) charging, referred to here as ‘The Program’. The investment will occur as project financing agreements with 11 Multi-Unit Residential Buildings, previously identified with conditions set out in signed Letters of Intent (LOIs). This project builds on the Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s previous experience delivering the EV Station Fund and prior work contracted with Dunsky Energy + Climate Advisors and is expected to showcase the value of Multi-Unit Residential Buildings undertaking comprehensive EV charging upgrades. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Approved an investment of up to $1.65 million via project financing agreements, and subject to: a. acceptance of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s application for grant funding; b. individual building upgrades contracted under terms and conditions not materially different to the Letters of Intent entered into by the building representatives as part of the grant application; c. a total Program Internal Rate of Return being above a 10 percent minimum target rate; and d. Legal Review. 2. Approved financing the upfront cost of working capital expenses which are recoverable through grant funding. 3.
