What the item proposed

This report recommends adding new opportunities to allocate unspent Section 37 funds from site specific Zoning By-laws within Ward 11. The sites include: -86-100 Bloor Street West (By-law 133-1999); -1, 3 Sultan Street, 11 St Thomas Street, and 76-98 Charles Street West (By-law 1090-2002); -77 Charles Street West (By-law 954-2005); -13-21 Balmuto Street (By-law 226-2007); -590 Jarvis Street (By-law 1018-2005) - now known as 110 Charles Street East; -4, 6, 8 St. Thomas Street and 100-110 Charles Street West (By-law 60-2008(OMB)); and, -192A, 194, and 200 Bloor Street West (By-law 1131-2010).
