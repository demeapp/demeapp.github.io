Councillor Perks asks Toronto and East York Community Council to approve an all-way stop at Mayfield and Willard Avenues.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Transportation Staff are recommending an all-way stop control at the Mayfield Avenue and Willard Avenue intersection. The all-way stop control will complement the upcoming Missing Sidewalk Program installation on this section of Mayfield Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Mayfield Avenue and Willard Avenue.
