Toronto City Council · Decision

Councillor Fletcher asks Toronto and East York Community Council to limit the Torrens Avenue bus zone to school days.

Official title: Parking Amendments - William Burgess School Bus Loading Zone: Torrens Avenue

AdoptedMay 7, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Torrens Avenue is a local road, south of William Burgess Elementary School and east of Pape Avenue. Residents have received tickets for parking in the school bus loading zone on weekends and holidays. The principal and TDSB trustee are not aware of programming on weekends that would require school buses utilizing the loading zone. Amending the school bus loading zone to the school calendar would allow residents to utilize their street for parking.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing school bus loading zone in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., on the north side of Torrens Avenue, between a point 85 metres east of Pape Avenue and a point 127 metres east of Pape Avenue, to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, from September 1 of one year to June 30 of the next following year, inclusive, except public holidays. 2. Amended the existing no parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., except Saturday and Sunday and school holidays, on the north side of Torrens Avenue between Pape Avenue and a point 30 metres east of Pape Avenue to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, from September 1 of one year to June 30 of the next following year, inclusive, except public holidays. 3.
