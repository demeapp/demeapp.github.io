Encroachment Appeal - 308 Delaware Avenue

This is a procedural item: the committee decides whether to allow a privacy fence in the public right-of-way at 308 Delaware Avenue; staff recommend approval.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is for Toronto and East York Community Council to consider an appeal from the property owner of 308 Delaware Avenue regarding their encroachment application. The property owner is seeking authority from Toronto and East York Community Council to permit a 1.98m high wooden privacy fence to be constructed and maintained within the public right-of-way on the Shanly Street flank with a vision splay provided for pedestrians and motorists at the intersection of the existing driveway and the sidewalk at 308 Delaware Avenue using open screen fencing.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1.
