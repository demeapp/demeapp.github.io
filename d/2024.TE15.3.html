Staff recommend approving a City-initiated 10-storey mass-timber building with 94 units, including 28 affordable rentals, at 1113-1125 Dundas Street West.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This City-initiated application proposes to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law to permit a 10-storey (35.5 metres in height inclusive of the mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building with 94 dwelling units and approximately 194 square metres of non-residential space and a one-storey (4.5 metres in height) rear ancillary building on the City-owned site located at 1113-1125 Dundas Street West. A minimum of 30% of the units are required to be affordable rental units, which means 28 affordable rental units will be provided through this development. The 10-storey building would be the first mass-timber building on a City-owned site, part of the Mass Timber Pilot Project that was adopted by Council in May 2022.
