This is an appointments item: the committee was asked to appoint three new members to the Ralph Thornton Community Centre board.

Official title: Appointment of New Board Members of the Ralph Thornton Community Centre

AdoptedJul 10, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I’m forwarding the following names for appointment to our Board of Management, having been elected by our members at our Annual General Meeting on Wednesday, June 12th, 2024. The following Board members did not stand for re-election and are no longer Board members: - Lisa Pottie - Martin Bryan We look forward to the contributions of our new and returning Board members as we continue to serve our community. We also thank our departing Board members for their service to our organization.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Appointed the following individuals to the Ralph Thornton Community Centre Board of Management, at pleasure of Council, for a term of office ending June 2027 and until successors are appointed: - Michelle Markwart - Maggi Redmonds - Sarah Reyes - Gabrielle Totesau - Wairimu Wamugo 2. Appointed Ejay Tupe to the Ralph Thornton Community Centre Board of Management, at pleasure of Council, for a term of office ending June 2026 and until a successor is appointed.
