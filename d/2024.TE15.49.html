Toronto City Council · Decision

This is a procedural item: the committee adjusts stopping rules on Harbour Street to accommodate the new development's valet lay-by.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Harbour Street

AdoptedJul 10, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

EllisDon is constructing a 57-story non-residential development at 30 Bay Street and 60 Harbour Street. The existing heritage building is known as the Toronto Harbour Commission Building. The site is bound by an existing mixed-use building to the west, Harbour Street to the south, Bay Street to the east, and Lake Shore Boulevard West/Gardiner Expressway to the north. As part of the site plan application of this development, a lay-by is scheduled to be constructed in front of the property on Harbour Street to meet the market needs of Harbour Sixty, which allows for valet service. The lay-by will help facilitate pick-up/ drop-off demands, without impacting traffic flow on Harbour Street. The on-street parking regulation within the lay-by will need to be amended to a “No Standing Anytime” regulation. This will deter parking in this area but allow for the pick-up/drop-off of passengers only.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing stopping prohibition in effect at all times on both sides of Harbour Street, between Lower Simcoe Street and Yonge Street. 2. Prohibited stopping at all times on the north side of Harbour Street, between Lower Simcoe Street and a point 77 metres west of Bay Street. 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the north side of Harbour Street, between a point 52 metres west of Bay Street and Yonge Street. 4. Prohibited stopping at all times on the south side of Harbour Street, between Lower Simcoe Street and Yonge Street. 5. Prohibited standing at all times on the north side of Harbour Street, between a point 52 metres west of Bay Street and a point 25 metres further west.
