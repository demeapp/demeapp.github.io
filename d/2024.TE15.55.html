This is a procedural item: the committee considers installing speed humps on Heathdale Road between Raglan and Humewood.

Official title: Install Traffic Calming Measures to Improve Safety along Heathdale Road

AdoptedJul 10, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to request your support in directing Traffic Operations to install traffic calming measures along Heathdale Road between Raglan Avenue and Humewood Drive. There has been sustained community concern dating back to 2022 over vehicles speeding throughout this residential street, with documented crashes occurring since the community’s initial request for traffic calming. With the installation of speed humps throughout this street, I am confident that this measure will help to slow down drivers along Heathdale Road.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Heathdale Road, between Raglan Avenue and Humewood Drive. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a By-law to alter the roadway on Heathdale Road, between Raglan Avenue and Humewood Drive, to install eight speed humps, generally as shown on Attachments 1 and 2 to the letter (July 8, 2024) from Councillor Josh Matlow.
