This is a procedural item: the committee considers installing speed humps on Maxwell Avenue between Eglinton West and Anderson.

Official title: Install Traffic Calming Measures to Improve Safety along Maxwell Avenue

AdoptedJul 10, 2024 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to request your support in directing Traffic Operations to install traffic calming measures along Maxwell Avenue between Eglinton Avenue West and Anderson Avenue. The Maxwell Avenue community has shared sustained concern since 2019 over the speeds at which vehicles drive through this residential street. In 2022, the community organized and submitted a petition with a large portion of households on the street asking that traffic calming measures be implemented. With the passage of this motion, the community will receive the installation of traffic calming measures they have been organizing for.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Maxwell Avenue, between Eglinton Avenue West and Anderson Avenue. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a By-law to alter the roadway on Maxwell Avenue, between Eglinton Avenue West and Anderson Avenue, to install four speed humps, generally as shown on Attachment 1 to the letter (July 8, 2024) from Councillor Josh Matlow.
