Toronto City Council · Decision

This is a procedural item: the committee considers converting parking to no-stopping on Lisgar Street to keep a business's driveway clear.

Official title: No Stopping - Lisgar Street near Dundas Street West

AdoptedJul 10, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

A local business located near Dundas Street West and Lisgar Street contacted my office to share challenges with accessing their valid boulevard parking. Vehicles often park in front of the openings and exits of their boulevard parking spaces, resulting in delays for clients and disruptions for parked delivery vehicles. Following an investigation by Transportation Services, it was determined that the existing no parking prohibition along the boulevard parking space can be converted to a no stopping to better prevent vehicles from blocking access. This change would maintain the existing permit parking spaces between the opening and exit of the boulevard.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Lisgar Street, between Dundas Street West and a point 15 metres south. 2. Prohibited stopping at all times on the west side of Lisgar Street, between Dundas Street West and a point 23 metres south. 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the west side of Lisgar Street, between a point 33 metres south of Dundas Street West and a point 6 metres further south.
