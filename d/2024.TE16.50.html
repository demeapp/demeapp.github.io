The council was asked to remove an unneeded commercial loading zone on St. Helen's Avenue.

Official title: Commercial Loading Zone Removal - St. Helen’s Avenue

AdoptedSep 25, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services advised that the existing commercial loading zone on St. Helen’s Avenue, south of Dublin Street, is no longer required by the original applicant. This removal will result in this area operating a one-hour parking regulation as currently in place for St. Helen’s Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the designated commercial loading zone, in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, on the west side of St. Helen’s Avenue, between a point 35 metres south of Dublin Street and a point 69 metres further south. 2. Rescinded the existing maximum one-hour parking regulation, in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of St. Helen’s Avenue, between a point 104 metres south of Dublin Street to a point 25 metres north of Whytock Avenue. 3. Rescinded the existing on-street accessible parking space designation, in effect at all times, on the west side of St. Helen’s Avenue, between a point a point 49 metres north of Whytock Avenue and a point 5.5 metres further north. 4.
