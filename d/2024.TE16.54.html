The council was asked to approve speed humps on Perth Avenue.

Official title: Installation of Speed Humps on Perth Avenue, between Kingsley Avenue and Davenport Road

AdoptedSep 25, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

For several years, residents on Perth Avenue, just south of Davenport Road, have requested traffic calming measures to reduce speeding and other dangerous driving behaviour in the area. This part of Perth Avenue is frequently used by residents, parents, and children walking towards Charles-Sauriol Elementary School or Pelham Avenue Playground – highlighting the urgent need for immediate traffic safety improvements. Residents in the area voted strongly in support of speed humps as a traffic calming measure in a survey conducted by my office. The proposed six speed humps, which extend from Davenport Road to Kingsley Avenue, will help to significantly improve neighbourhood safety.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Perth Avenue, between Kingsley Avenue and Davenport Road. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a By-law to alter the roadway for the installation of six speed humps on Perth Avenue, between Kingsley Avenue and Davenport Road, generally as shown on Attachment 1 to the letter (September 23, 2024) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo.
