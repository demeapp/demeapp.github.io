The council was asked to extend parking time to 20 minutes near ALPHA Alternative Junior Public School.

Official title: Parking Amendments - Brant Street

AdoptedSep 25, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

ALPHA Alternative Junior Public School has specific needs around parking around the school. An adjustment to existing parking regulations aims to prevent parents from receiving unnecessary parking tickets during drop-off and pick-up times, ensuring a smoother and more flexible process for the school community. Proposed changes involve extending the parking duration to 20 minutes during designated periods.

What was decided
