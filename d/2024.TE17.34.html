What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of speed humps on four streets and the installation of speed bumps in one lane in the West Parkdale Neighbourhood. Staff's assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the updated Traffic Calming Policy has been satisfied.
