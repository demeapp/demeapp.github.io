What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that overnight on-street permit parking be introduced on an area basis (Area 7L), within the area bounded by: Gerrard Street East to the north, River Street to the east, Shuter Street to the south, and Parliament Street to the west. The Ward Councillor requested the implementation of overnight on-street permit parking in Regent Park to accommodate the long-term parking needs of Toronto Community Housing Corporation (TCHC) residents.
