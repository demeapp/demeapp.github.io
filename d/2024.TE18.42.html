Toronto and East York Community Council fixes a by-law error for the accessible loading zone on Delaware Avenue North.

Official title: Delaware Avenue North - Parking Amendments

AdoptedDec 4, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

During an investigation to install pavement markings, Transportation Services discovered a discrepancy in the distance for an on-street accessible loading zone on Delaware Avenue North. The wording in the by-law incorrectly set an accessible loading zone on a different section of the street, separate from where the current signage and poles are installed. The below amendments to the parking regulations on Delaware Avenue North are required to maintain use of the existing accessible loading zone for residents and visitors on the street.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing accessible loading zone in effect from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., daily, on the west side of Delaware Avenue North, between a point 63 metres south of Davenport Road and a point 18.5 metres further south. 2. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., daily, except no parking from a point 117 metres south of Davenport Road to a point 7 metres further south on the even (west) side of Delaware Avenue from the first lane north of Geary Avenue to Davenport Road. 3. Designated an accessible loading zone to be in effect from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., daily, on the west side of Delaware Avenue North, between a point 101 metres south of Davenport Road and a point 20.5 metres further south. 4.
