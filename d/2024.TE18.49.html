Toronto and East York Community Council considers removing the daycare drop-off parking zone on Harrison Street.

Official title: Harrison Street - Parking Amendments

AdoptedDec 4, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

A pick-up and drop-off area was installed on Harrison Street to provide short-term parking for parents of children attending the daycare at Harrison Street and Ossington Avenue. Earlier this year, the daycare closed, and the property was later listed for sale. Transportation Services determined there is no longer a need for a short-term parking zone on Harrison Street and has recommended its removal. The spaces will be converted to permit parking for residents and visitors in the area.

What was decided
