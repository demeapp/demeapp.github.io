Toronto and East York Community Council considers removing alternate side parking rules on Ward Street.

Official title: Ward Street - Parking Amendments

AdoptedDec 4, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Ward Street, which extends from Wallace Avenue to Lappin Avenue, has homes located on the east side and a large office complex with boulevard parking on the west side. During alternate side parking days, residents on Ward Street are required to move their vehicles to the east side of the street, where there are significantly fewer spaces – resulting in difficulty locating parking spaces in the area. In a survey conducted by my office, a majority of residents supported the removal of alternate side parking and maintaining parking on the east side in front of homes.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition regulation in effect each Thursday from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the east side of Ward Street, between Lappin Avenue and Wallace Avenue. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times from December 1 of one year to March 31 of the next following year, inclusive and anytime, except each Thursday from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the west side of Ward Street, between Lappin Avenue and a point 15 metres north of Wallace Avenue. 3. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., each Thursday from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the even (west) side of Ward Street from Lappin Avenue to a point 15 meters north of Wallace Avenue. 4.
