Toronto and East York Community Council considers speed humps on two Regent Park streets.

Official title: Implementing Traffic Calming in Regent Park - Nicholas Avenue and Sumach Street

AdoptedDec 4, 2024 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to request speed humps on Nicholas Avenue, between Shuter Street and Wyatt Avenue as well as Sumach Street, between Shuter Street and Dundas Street East. Pedestrian safety has been a critical focus of my office. While reviewing existing traffic calming in Regent Park, my staff, in discussion with community members and with lived experience, identified these two streets for potential traffic calming measures. Future traffic calming measures will be considered as Regent Park Phases 4 and 5 moves forward.

What was decided

Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Nicholas Avenue, between Shuter Street and Wyatt Avenue. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a By-law to alter the roadway on Nicholas Avenue, between Shuter Street and Wyatt Avenue, to install three speed humps, generally as shown on Attachment 1 to the letter (December 2, 2024) from Councillor Chris Moise. 3. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Sumach Street, between Shuter Street and Dundas Street East. 4. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a By-law to alter the roadway on Sumach Street, between Shuter Street and Dundas Street East, to install four speed humps, generally as shown on Attachment 2 to the letter (December 2, 2024) from Councillor Chris Moise.
