The official record

What the item proposed

Althea Hutchinson, Director of Operations and Business Services, Toronto Public Health will give an overview of Student Nutrition Programs in the City of Toronto.

What was decided

The City-School Boards Advisory Committee recommends to the Executive Committee that: 1. City Council consider establishing new food nutrition programs for students in marginalized priority neighbourhoods with new provincial funding. 2. City Council request the Provincial Government continue to fund food nutrition programs and to fund newly established food nutrition programs. 3. City Council request the Province of Ontario to increase its financial commitment to Student Nutrition Programs in Toronto at $0.55 per Serving per meal in alignment with the Northern Fruit and Vegetable Alliance allowance in order to honour the rising cost of food in the City of Toronto. 4. City Council request the Federal and Provincial Governments to work with Toronto’s Food Nutrition Program so more children can receive nutritious food at school through a Universal Nutrition Program. 5.
