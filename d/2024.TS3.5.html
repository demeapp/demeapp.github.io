What the item proposed

Cricket has been a popular sport in many countries. There are 27 cricket pitches in the City of Toronto. Cricket is popular in Toronto and is one of the fastest growing sports. As part of the 20-year Facilities Master Plan (FMP), City staff reviewed Toronto’s existing cricket facilities to determine if improvements and/or new facilities are required to keep up with demand. Cricket Strategy. The review recommended the following action: • Create up to five new cricket pitches. ◦ new pitches will be regulation size and may be shared with other recreational activities; and ◦ accommodating new pitches may require the City to explore opportunities for land use partnerships, acquiring new land and or redesigning existing parks Partnerships with School Boards There are opportunities for partnerships with school boards for new cricket facilities. The City-School Boards Advisory Committee: 1. Referred the item to the General Manager, Parks, Forestry and Recreation, and requested them to continue the engagement of school boards on this item.
