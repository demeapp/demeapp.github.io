Extending the Mandate of the City’s Chief Information Security Officer

Received for informationJul 19, 2024 · Toronto City Council

This is an information item: the Zoo board notes Council's decision extending the Chief Information Security Officer's mandate to City agencies.

Received for information — Council took no action on it.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on May 22 and 23, 2024, adopted Item EX14.3 and, in so doing, has: 1. Directed the Boards of the Agencies and, as Shareholder, directed the Boards of the Corporations set out in Part 2 [of Council’s decision] to: a. provide the necessary information, access, and visibility into their cyber security programs to facilitate the cyber security risk management partnership with the Chief Information Security Officer; b. operationalize the Chief Information Security Officer’s recommendations to mitigate cyber risks identified in the cyber security risk management partnership; and c. engage in consultation with the Chief Information Security Officer on all initiatives that could potentially affect cyber security, including but not limited to rates of compliance, remediation plans and strategies aimed at reducing risks and promoting compliance.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Received the letter (June 14, 2024) from the City Clerk for information.
