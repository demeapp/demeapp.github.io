What the item proposed

Resolution to be passed to appoint Auditor.

What was decided

The Members of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund adopted the following resolution: “Be It Resolved that the Doane Grant Thornton LLP be reappointed and ratified as auditors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund until the next Annual General Meeting of the Members, and be confirmed at the remuneration fixed by the Directors and that the Directors be authorized to fix such remuneration."
