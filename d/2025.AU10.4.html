The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of June 29, 2020, in item 2020.AU5.7, City Council appointed KPMG LLP as the auditor licensed under the Public Accounting Act, 2004 responsible for annually auditing the accounts and transactions of the City and certain of its local boards and expressing an opinion on the financial statements of these bodies based on the audit. KPMG LLP submits this Management Letter for the City of Toronto with management responses for period ended December 31, 2024.

What was decided

The Audit Committee: 1. Received the KPMG Management Letter including Management Responses - for the year 2024 ending December 31, 2024 (October 22, 2025) from KPMG LLP for information.
