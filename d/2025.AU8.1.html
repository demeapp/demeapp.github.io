Auditor General's 2024 Annual Report - Demonstrating the Value of the Auditor General's Office

Adopted with changesMar 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Auditor General's 2024 annual report goes to Council as information. Acting on her recommendations has saved over $376 million over five years, against about $33.3 million in office costs — about $11 back for every dollar invested.

What the item proposed

This report provides information about the Auditor General's Office 2024 activities and the financial and non-financial benefits achieved by the City through its implementation of report recommendations. In 2024, the Auditor General's Office presented 10 audit, investigation, and cybersecurity reports with 86 recommendations, as well as a consolidated report on the status of previous Auditor General recommendations to City divisions, agencies and corporations. Additionally, our Forensic Unit managed 679 complaints (representing approximately 1,050 allegations) received through our Fraud and Waste Hotline, and conducted and / or provided oversight of some complex investigations. Every year, we demonstrate the value our Office brings with a quantified return on investment.
