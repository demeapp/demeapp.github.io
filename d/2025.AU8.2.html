Auditor General's 2024 Annual Report on the Fraud and Waste Hotline

The 2024 annual report on the Fraud and Waste Hotline, covering 679 complaints. It goes to Council for information. The program prevented $2.0 million in actual losses and $1.1 million in potential losses from 2020 to 2024.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report represents the 2024 annual report on fraud, waste and wrongdoing at the City including the activities of the Fraud and Waste Hotline Program (the Hotline Program). It highlights the complaints that have been communicated to the Auditor General’s Office. It does not represent an overall picture of fraud or other wrongdoing across the City. In 2024, 679 complaints, representing approximately 1,050 allegations, were received by the Auditor General’s Office. This is a reduction from 2023 by approximately 36 per cent or 375 complaints, primarily the result of receiving fewer complaints outside of our jurisdiction (not related to the City of Toronto), as well as subsidy fraud complaints.
