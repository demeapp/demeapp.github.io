Community Centres - 2024 Audited Financial Statements

AdoptedJul 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: council was asked to accept the checked 2024 financial statements for nine community centres (one audit was still underway). No policy change is proposed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide the Audit Committee and City Council with the 2024 audited financial statements of Community Centres. The 2024 audited financial statements for the ten Community Centres are presented to Audit Committee after approval by their respective Boards of Management. At this time, the audits of nine Community Centres have been completed, and one was still in progress. Depending on when the remaining audit is completed, the Independent Auditor's Report, accompanying financial statements and management control letter (where applicable) of that Community Centre will be presented at a subsequent meeting of the Audit Committee.

What was decided

City Council on July 23 and 24, 2025, adopted the following: 1.
