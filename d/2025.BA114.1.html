Award of Doc4527040342 to Midome Construction Services Ltd. for Watermain Replacement, Sewer Replacement, Laneway Reconstruction and Neighbourhood Improvements on Various Roads for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedJan 8, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $18,088,400 contract with Midome Construction Services for replacing water pipes and sewers and rebuilding laneways in North York and Toronto-East York, January 2025 to August 2027 ($20,439,892 including HST; $18,406,756 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $16,444,000.00, $16,490,860.00, $16,660,000.00, $16,865,100.00 and $20,382,130.00.

The official summary names a figure of $16,444,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
