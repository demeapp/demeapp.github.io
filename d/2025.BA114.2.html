Award of the nRFP0009 to Skidata, Inc. for the Provision of Turnstiles for the World Cup Hosting 2026 Secretariat

AdoptedJan 8, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $ 571,467 contract with Skidata, Inc. for temporary turnstiles and handheld ticket scanners for the Toronto stadium during FIFA World Cup 2026, through September 2026 ($ 645,758 including HST; $ 581,524 net of HST recoveries) is up for approval by the city's contract panel.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (January 2, 2025) from the Chief Procurement Officer: Solicitation Number: nRFP009, MERX # 0000272694 Description: For the supply, delivery, set-up, commissioning, maintenance, and removal of temporary turnstiles, and handheld ticketing scanning devices to be utilized at the Toronto Stadium for the FIFA World Cup 2026 games in Toronto. Recommended Supplier: Skidata, Inc. Contract Award Value: $ 571,467 net of all applicable taxes and charges, $ 645,758 including HST and all applicable charges, $ 581,524 net of HST recoveries Contract is expected to start on the award date, and end on Sep 1, 2026.
