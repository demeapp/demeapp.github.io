Award of Doc4683164573 to Allied Medical Instruments Inc. for the Non-Exclusive Supply and Delivery of Disposable Bag Valve Masks for Purchasing and Materials Management (Stores)

AdoptedJan 15, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A contract with Allied Medical Instruments for breathing-assist masks for city stores, one year plus four optional renewals, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Award: $153,888 ($173,893; $156,596); option years: $158,505 ($179,111; $161,295), $163,260 ($184,484; $166,133), $168,158 ($190,019; $171,118), $173,203 ($195,719; $176,251); five-year total: $817,014 ($923,226; $831,393). Bids: $128,240.00, $137,580.00, $183,910.00, $216,145.00, $269,993.00, $277,610.00.

The official summary names a figure of $128,240.00.
