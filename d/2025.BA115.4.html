Award of Sourcewell Solicitation Number 011723-CNH-1 to Nors Construction Equipment Canada ST, LP for Fifty-Two (52) Various Case Heavy Construction Equipment with Related Accessories, Attachments and Supplies for Fleet Services

AdoptedJan 15, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A contract with Nors Construction Equipment Canada for heavy construction equipment for the city fleet, using a Sourcewell buying deal, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Award: $11,732,533 ($13,257,762; $11,939,025); option year 1: $5,693,336 ($6,433,469; $5,793,538); combined values: $19,691,231, $17,425,869, $17,732,563.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

Should the option year renewal be extended by Sourcewell for the final year, Fleet Services may purchase fourteen (14) units in 2028.
