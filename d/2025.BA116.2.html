Award of Doc4609558102 to Chem-Aqua, Div. of NCH Canada Inc. for the Non-Exclusive Supply of Labour, Equipment, Delivery, Materials and Services Necessary to Provide Boiler Hot Water Testing and Heating Glycol Testing for Toronto Water

AdoptedJan 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A one-year contract with Chem-Aqua for boiler water and heating fluid testing for Toronto Water, with up to four optional renewals, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Award: $127,056 ($143,573; $129,292); option years: $130,868 ($147,881; $133,171), $134,794 ($152,317; $137,166), $138,838 ($156,887; $141,282), $143,003 ($161,593; $145,520); five-year total: $674,559 ($762,251; $686,431). Bids: $127,056.37, $179,538.33, $238,624.90.

The official summary names a figure of $127,056.37.

What was decided

The Chief Procurement Officer recommends that the Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the following contract: Solicitation Number: Request for Quotation Doc4609558102 Description: For the non-exclusive supply of all labour, equipment, delivery, materials and services necessary to provide boiler hot water testing and heating glycol testing for the City of Toronto's Water Division. The term of the Contract is to be for a period of one (1) year from the date of award, with an option in favour of the City to extend the agreement on the same terms and conditions for an additional term of up to four (4) additional separate one (1) year periods at the sole discretion of the City and subject to budget approval.
