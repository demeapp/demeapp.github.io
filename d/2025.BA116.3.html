Award of Doc4637168478 to EQUIPMENT MEDICAL RIVE-NORD (EMRN) and ALLIED MEDICAL INSTRUMENTS INC. for the Non-Exclusive Supply and Delivery of Sharps Containers for Purchasing and Materials Management (Stores)

AdoptedJan 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Contracts with two suppliers for safe bins for needles and medical waste for city stores through December 2025, with up to four optional renewals, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Lot A award: $199,168 ($225,060; $202,674); option years: $205,143 ($231,812; $208,754), $211,298 ($238,766; $215,016), $217,636 ($245,929; $221,467), $224,166 ($253,307; $228,111); total: $1,076,021 ($1,194,874; $1,057,411). Lot B award: $65,801 ($74,355; $66,959); option years: $67,775 ($76,586; $68,968), $69,808 ($78,883; $71,037), $71,902 ($81,250; $73,168), $74,059 ($83,687; $75,363); total: $355,494 ($394,760; $349,345). Lot A bids: $ 165,973.50, $ 169,767.50, $ 189,103.60, $ 191,148.00, $ 219,460.50, $ 279,132.80, $ 312,304.30, $ 96,514.50, $ 115,232.50, $ 153,457.00. Lot B bids: $ 54,834.00, $ 60,762.00, $ 63,973.00, $ 70,642.00, $ 71,383.00, $ 86,944.00, $ 90,155.00 and $ 19,760.00.
