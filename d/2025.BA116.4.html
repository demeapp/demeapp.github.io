Award of Doc4755542784 to 2281610 Ontario Inc for the Supply and Delivery of Twenty (20) Latest Model Cab and Chassis Class 4-5 Trucks, Gasoline Engine, with Various Mini Dump Body Configurations for Fleet Services

AdoptedJan 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Buying 20 gasoline dump trucks for the city vehicle fleet from Ford Downtown/Lincoln Downtown, with options to buy more through 2029-30, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Award: $2,507,458 ($2,833,428; $2,551,589); optional purchase values: $3,208,064 ($3,625,112; $3,264,526), $859,179 ($970,872; $874,300), $2,177,737 ($2,460,843; $2,216,065), $2,488,788 ($2,812,331; $2,532,591), $4,188,056 ($4,732,504; $4,261,766); combined total: $15,429,283 ($17,435,089; $15,700,838). Bids: $2,507,458.00, $2,765,294.00.
