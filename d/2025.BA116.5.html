Award of Doc4787073195 to 614128 Ontario Ltd. O/A Trisan Construction for Grading, Excavating and Hauling Services for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedJan 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $191,713 contract with Trisan Construction for grading, digging and hauling for Parks and Recreation, one year with two optional renewals ($216,635 including HST; $195,087 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel.
