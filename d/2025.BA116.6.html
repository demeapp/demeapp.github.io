Award of Doc4790896092 to Pegah Construction Limited for the Building Renovation of Upper Yonge Village Daycare Centre, Located at 14 St. Clement Avenue, Toronto, for Corporate Real Estate Management

AdoptedJan 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $5,474,021 contract with Pegah Construction for renovating the Upper Yonge Village Daycare Centre at 14 St. Clement Avenue, finishing December 2026 ($6,185,643 including HST; $5,570,363 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $4,974,020.73, $5,407,535.60, $5,487,671.20, $6,077,472.36, $6,198,231.00, $6,608,000.00, $7,119,400.00.

The official summary names a figure of $4,974,020.73.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
