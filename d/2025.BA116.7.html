Award of Doc4805503099 to 614128 Ontario Ltd. o/a Trisan Construction for Load, Transport and Disposal of Fill Concrete and Asphalt Material from Construction District Yards for Toronto Water

AdoptedJan 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A contract with Trisan Construction for loading, hauling and removing fill, concrete and asphalt from Toronto Water construction yards, one year with four optional renewals, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Award: $2,363,250 ($2,670,473; $2,404,843); option years: $2,434,148 ($2,750,587; $2,476,989), $2,507,172 ($2,833,104; $2,551,298), $2,582,387 ($2,918,097; $2,627,837), $2,659,859 ($3,005,641; $2,706,673); five-year total: $12,546,816 ($14,177,902; $12,767,640). Bids: $1,969,375.00, $2,529,075.00, $2,555,350.00, $2,673,375.00, $3,714,219.25.

The official summary names a figure of $1,969,375.00.
