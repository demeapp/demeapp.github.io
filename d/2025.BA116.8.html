Award of Doc4872799414 to 2489960 Ontario Inc. o/a Kore Infrastructure Group for Watermain Replacement, Storm Sewer Replacement, Storm and Sanitary Sewer Rehabilitation for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedJan 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $6,580,200 contract with Kore Infrastructure Group for replacing water pipes on Yonge Street and sewers on Culford Road, through December 2026 ($7,435,626 including HST; $6,696,012 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $5,982,000.00, $6,871,620.00, $8,295,000.00, $8,308,494.50, $12,380,952.00.

The official summary names a figure of $5,982,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
