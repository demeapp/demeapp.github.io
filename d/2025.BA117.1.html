Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Collaborative Procurement from Kinetic GPO Request for Standing Offers Number 21-12 to CDW Canada for the provision of Information Technology Solutions and Services for Office of the Chief Information Security Officer

AdoptedJan 28, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A three-year contract with CDW Canada for IT products and services for the city's cybersecurity office, using a Kinetic GPO buying deal, with up to two optional extensions, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Award: $13,350,000 ($15,085,500; $13,584,960); option-year-1 value: $3,325,000 ($3,757,250; $3,383,520); combined values: $20,000,000 ($22,600,000; $20,352,000).

A citywide decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
