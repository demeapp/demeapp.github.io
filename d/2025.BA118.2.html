Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Document Number 4558290882 to 373044 Ontario Limited o/a Trans Canada Construction for William Bolton Arena Roof Replacement and State of Good Repair for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedFeb 5, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,137,246 contract with Trans Canada Construction for roof replacement and cladding work at William Bolton Arena, finishing by September 2025 ($1,285,088 including HST; $1,157,261 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $937,246, $946,357, $1,005,690, $1,029,500, $1,088,668, $1,114,400, $1,154,689.

The official summary names a figure of $937,246.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
