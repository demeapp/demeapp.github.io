Award of Document Number 4746117483 to Martinway Contracting Ltd. for Dufferin Maintenance Yard Building Repairs for Solid Waste Management Services

AdoptedFeb 5, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,317,170 contract with Martinway Contracting for building repairs at the Dufferin Maintenance Yard, through September 2025 ($1,488,402 including HST; $1,340,352 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel.
