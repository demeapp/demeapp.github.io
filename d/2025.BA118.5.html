Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Document Number 4802162515 to Alliance Verdi Civil Inc. for Culvert Replacement for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedFeb 5, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $7,420,739 contract with Alliance Verdi Civil for replacing the culvert carrying Albion Creek under Silverstone Drive, through December 2029 ($8,385,436 including HST; $7,551,344 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $6,183,949.50, $6,669,304.81, $6,804,522.94, $7,278,527.20, $7,349,169.72, $7,974,345.30, $8,330,552.70, $8,464,293.55, $10,408,556.30.

The official summary names a figure of $6,183,949.50.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
