Award of Document Number 4926756164 to OnX Enterprise Solutions Ltd. for OpenText (Formerly Microfocus) Application Lifecycle Management (ALM) SaaS Products

AdoptedFeb 5, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A three-year, $1,088,302 contract with OnX Enterprise Solutions for OpenText software for Technology Services, through December 2027 ($1,229,781 including HST; $1,107,456 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $147.00 (ruled non-compliant) and $1,088,302.32.

The official summary names a figure of $147.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
