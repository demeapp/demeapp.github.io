Award of Document Number 4445799952 to Kipling Realty Management Inc. for Property Management Services for Canoe Landing for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedFeb 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $2,434,704 contract with Kipling Realty Management for property management of the Canoe Landing community facility at 20 Brunel Court ($2,751,216 including HST; $2,477,555 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Costs are shared with the two school boards and the child care centre.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
