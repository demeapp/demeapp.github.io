Award of Document Number 4811351377 to Kudlak-Baird (1982) Limited for Multi Transfer Station Upgrade Interior Lighting at Haulage Bay Area for Solid Waste Management Services

AdoptedFeb 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,414,834 contract with Kudlak-Baird for interior lighting upgrades in the truck bay area of a waste transfer station, through February 2026 ($1,598,762 including HST; $1,439,735 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $1,058,117.32 (non-compliant) and $1,179,027.96.

The official summary names a figure of $1,058,117.32.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
