Award of Document Number 4885106821 to N.S.J. Waterworx Group Ltd. for the Installation of New Residential Water and Sewer Service Connections and Associated Work at Various Locations for Toronto Water

AdoptedFeb 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A contract with N.S.J. Waterworx Group for installing new residential water and sewer connections, through February 2026 with one optional renewal year, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Award: $3,179,350 ($3,592,666; $3,235,307); option year: $3,274,731 ($3,700,446; $3,332,366); combined total: $6,454,081 ($7,293,112; $6,567,673). Bids: $3,029,350, $3,232,700, $3,683,050, $3,942,350, $3,957,850, $4,787,712, $5,745,950, $6,005,075.

The official summary names a figure of $3,029,350.
