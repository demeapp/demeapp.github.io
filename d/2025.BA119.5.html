Award of Document Number 4847094809 to Parsons Inc. for the Update Downsview Phases 3 - 4 Municipal Class Environmental Assessment for Transportation Services

AdoptedFeb 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A contract with Parsons Inc. for an environmental assessment of streets and servicing for the Downsview redevelopment phases 3 and 4, three years with two optional extensions, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Award: $2,951,124 ($3,334,770; $3,003,064); option years: $88,534 ($100,043; $90,092), $91,190 ($103,044; $92,795); combined total: $3,537,858, $3,130,847, $3,185,950.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
