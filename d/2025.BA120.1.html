Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Document Number 4530586174 to Cardinal Health Canada Inc. for the Non-Exclusive Supply and Delivery of Blood Pressure Cuffs for Purchasing and Materials Management (Stores)

AdoptedFeb 19, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A contract with Cardinal Health Canada (the lowest compliant bidder) for blood pressure cuffs for city stores through November 2025, with up to four optional renewals, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Award: $120,725 ($136,419; $122,850); option years: $124,347 ($140,512; $126,535), $128,077 ($144,727; $130,331), $131,919 ($149,069; $134,241), $135,877 ($153,541; $138,268); five-year total: $640,945 ($724,268; $652,226). Bids: $35,290.00, $75,990.30, $100,604.10, $105,732.50, $106,815.00, of which $35,290.00, $75,990.30 and $106,815.00 were non-compliant.

The official summary names a figure of $35,290.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
