Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Document Number 4879606963 to Gensler Architecture & Design Canada Inc. for the Feasibility Study to Re-imagine the City of Toronto's Civic Centre Public Spaces for Corporate Real Estate Management (CREM).

AdoptedFeb 19, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $936,780 contract with Gensler for a study on redesigning the public spaces around Toronto's civic centres, for one year with an optional one-year extension ($1,058,561 including HST; $953,267 net of HST recoveries) is up for approval by the city's contract panel.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (February 13, 2025) from the Chief Procurement Officer: Solicitation Number: Request for Proposal Doc4879606963 Description: For the feasibility study to re-imagine the City of Toronto's Civic Centre public spaces, for Corporate Real Estate Management (CREM). The term of the agreement is to be for a period of one (1) year from date of award, with an option in favour of the City to extend the agreement on the same terms and conditions for an additional term of up to one (1) year. Should the option be exercised, then the Executive Director, CREM will request the Chief Procurement Officer to process the renewal under the same terms and conditions, with no increase in the contract value.
