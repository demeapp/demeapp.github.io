Award of Document Number 4885245457 to OJCR Construction Ltd. and Rabcon Contractors Ltd. for Installation of New Residential Water, Sewer Service Connections and Associated Work at Various Locations for Toronto Water

AdoptedFeb 19, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Contracts with OJCR Construction and Rabcon Contractors for installing new residential water and sewer connections in three city service areas, through February 2026 with one optional renewal year, is up for approval by the city's contract panel.
