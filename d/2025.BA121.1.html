Award of Doc4706190304 to Martinway Contracting Ltd., for Compactor Replacement and Watermain Rehabilitation at Disco Road Transfer Station for Solid Waste Management Services

AdoptedFeb 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $7,896,000 contract with Martinway Contracting for replacing compactors and repairing the water pipe at the Disco Road Transfer Station, through March 2026 ($8,922,480 including HST; $8,034,970 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $6,558,000.80, $6,618,713.45 (non-compliant), $6,627,919.00 (non-compliant).

The official summary names a figure of $6,558,000.80.
