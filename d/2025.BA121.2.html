Award of Doc4784736229 to Dayco Distributing Ltd., for the Provision of Supply, Delivery, Installation, and Warranty of Food Service Equipment for Seniors Services and Long-Term Care

AdoptedFeb 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $2,245,112 contract with Dayco Distributing for kitchen equipment for long-term care homes, one year with one optional renewal year ($2,536,977 including HST; $2,284,626 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Option year 1: $2,312,465 ($2,613,086; $2,353,164); two-year total: $4,557,577 ($5,150,062; $4,637,790). Bids: $1,870,926.34, $1,976,190.72.

The official summary names a figure of $1,870,926.34.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
