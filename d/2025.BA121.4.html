Award of Doc4881022646 to Biochem Environmental Solutions Inc., for Sanitary Waste Collection and Disposal Services of Feminine Hygiene Waste and Diapers at Select City of Toronto Locations

AdoptedFeb 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A three-year contract with Biochem Environmental Solutions for collecting sanitary waste, such as feminine hygiene products and diapers, at selected city buildings, with two optional one-year extensions, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Award: $879,412 ($993,735,; $894,889); option years: $301,931 ($341,182,; $307,245), $310,989 ($351,418,; $316,463); five-year total: $1,492,332 ($1,686,335,; $1,518,597). Bids: $ 244,281, $ 316,765.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
