Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4892284211 to Hansler Smith Ltd., for the Non-Exclusive Supply and Delivery of Particulate Masks and Respirators for Purchasing and Materials Management (Stores)

AdoptedFeb 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A contract with Hansler Smith (the lowest compliant bidder) for dust masks and respirators for city stores through December 2025, with up to four optional renewals, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Award: $344,805 ($389,630; $350,874); option years: $355,150 ($401,319; $361,400), $365,804 ($413,359; $372,242), $376,778 ($425,759; $383,409), $388,081 ($438,532; $394,912); five-year total: $1,830,619 ($2,068,599; $1,862,837). Bids: $287,337.81, $298,363.00, $300,575.17, $309,960.76, $334,458.70, $358,622.55, $395,480.16.

The official summary names a figure of $287,337.81.
