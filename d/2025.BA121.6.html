Award of Doc4913219634 to Waste Reduction Group Inc., for the Non-Exclusive Supply of the Waste, Recycling, and Organics Collection at Select Locations Within the City of Toronto for Corporate Real Estate Management

AdoptedFeb 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A three-year contract (March 2025 to February 2028) with Waste Reduction Group for waste, recycling and organics collection at selected city buildings, with two optional one-year extensions, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Award: $2,065,547 ($2,334,068,; $2,101,901); option years: $709,171 ($801,363,; $721,653), $730,446 ($825,404,; $743,302); five-year total: $3,505,165 ($3,960,836,; $3,566,856). Bids: $573,763.10, $702,880.60.

The official summary names a figure of $573,763.10.
