Award of Doc4787310157 to Joe Pace & Sons Contracting Inc. for the provision of Interior Upgrades for Seniors Services and Long-Term Care

AdoptedMar 5, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $3,142,150 contract with Joe Pace and Sons for interior upgrades at three long-term care homes, through March 2026 ($3,550,630 including HST; $3,197,452 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $2,856,500.00, $3,456,000.00, $3,586,865.00, $4,016,700.00 (non-compliant).

The official summary names a figure of $2,856,500.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
