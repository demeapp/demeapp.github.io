Award of Doc4948192738 to Belor Construction Ltd. for Bridge Rehabilitation for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedMar 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $6,861,511 contract with Belor Construction for repairing two bridges over Black Creek (Jane Street and Black Creek Drive), through December 2028 ($7,753,508 including HST; $6,982,274 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $5,717,926.20, $5,876,000.00, $6,993,456.00, $7,544,422.00, $8,306,542.20, $8,896,713.30, $9,402,973.70.

The official summary names a figure of $5,717,926.20.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
