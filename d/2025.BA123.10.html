Award of Doc4904612774 to Environics Research Group Ltd. for Public Opinion Research for Solid Waste Management Services

AdoptedMar 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A one-year, $397,199 contract with Environics Research Group for public opinion research on waste services, April 2025 to March 2026, with up to four optional renewals ($448,835 including HST; $404,190 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Option years: $409,115 ($462,300; $416,315), $421,388 ($476,169; $428,805), $434,030 ($490,454; $441,669), $447,051 ($505,167; $454,919); five-year total: $2,108,782 ($2,382,924; $2,145,897).

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
