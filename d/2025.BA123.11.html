Award of Doc4906209604 to Lomco Limited for Terraview Park Playground and Splash Pad Improvements for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedMar 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,637,982 contract with Lomco Limited for playground and splash pad improvements at Terraview Park, finishing December 2025 ($1,850,920 including HST; $1,666,811 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel.
