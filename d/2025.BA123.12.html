Award of Doc4921312998 to Rafat General Contractor Inc. for Minor Bridge Repairs, Bridge Deck Flushing, Chipping Loose Concrete from Bridges and Minor Retaining Wall Repairs for Transportation Services

AdoptedMar 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $768,310 contract with Rafat General Contractor for minor bridge repairs and wall repairs in Wards 6, 8 and 15 to 18, through December 2025 ($868,190 including HST; $781,832 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $708,310.00, $710,485.00, $734,606.00, $743,707.90.

The official summary names a figure of $708,310.00.
