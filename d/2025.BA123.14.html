Award of Collaborative Procurement from Ontario Education Collaboration Marketplace Document Numbers 2021-390-01 to Gerrie Electrical Wholesale Ltd. and 2021-390-03 to Westburne for the Supply of Electrical Products for Various City Divisions

AdoptedMar 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Contracts with Gerrie Electrical Wholesale and Westburne for electrical supplies for seven city divisions, using an Ontario Education Marketplace agreement starting April 2025, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Gerrie: $3,589,000 ($4,055,570; $3,652,166); Westburne: $5,460,000 ($6,169,800; $5,556,096).

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
