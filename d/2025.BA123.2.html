Award of Doc4961972802 to D. Crupi & Sons Limited for Local Roads Resurfacing in Various Wards for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedMar 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $15,220,455 contract with D. Crupi and Sons for repaving local roads in Wards 1 through 9, through December 2026 ($17,199,114 including HST; $15,488,335 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $13,836,777.00, $13,936,888.70, $15,467,768.50.

The official summary names a figure of $13,836,777.00.
