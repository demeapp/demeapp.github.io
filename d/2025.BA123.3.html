Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4969145825 to Dynex Construction Inc. for the North Toronto Treatment Plant Outfall, Submerged Crossings and Don River Watercourse Restoration for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedMar 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $4,251,819 contract with Dynex Construction for the North Toronto Treatment Plant's outflow pipe, underwater crossings and restoration of the Don River waterway, through December 2026 ($4,804,555 including HST; $4,326,651 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $3,674,474.00, $6,456,697.50, $6,664,937.83, $6,765,197.24.

The official summary names a figure of $3,674,474.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
