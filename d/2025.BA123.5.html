Award of Doc4731760915 to Pine Valley Corporation for Grand Avenue Park Construction of Phase 2 Park Improvements for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedMar 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,514,982 contract with Pine Valley Corporation for Phase 2 park improvements at Grand Avenue Park, finishing December 2025 ($1,711,930 including HST; $1,541,646 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $1,314,982.00, $1,486,729.96, $1,542,897.00, $1,819,719.00, $1,929,800.00, $1,943,570.00, $2,268,863.58, $2,259,168.56.

The official summary names a figure of $1,314,982.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
