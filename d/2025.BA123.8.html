Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4873092396 to BDA Inc. for Centennial Park Venue Specific Training Site Building for FIFA World Cup 2026 for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedMar 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $19,247,700 contract with BDA Inc. to build the Centennial Park training-site building for FIFA World Cup 2026, finishing by May 2027 ($21,749,901 including HST; $19,586,460 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $16,233,000.00 (non-compliant), $16,323,775.00 (non-compliant), $17,247,700.00, $19,037,542.77, $20,992,700.00, $21,529,352.00.

The official summary names a figure of $16,233,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
