Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4887167167 to Hank Deenen Landscaping Ltd. for Flemingdon Park Improvements for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedMar 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,107,008 contract with Hank Deenen Landscaping for park improvements at Flemingdon Park, through December 2027 with a two-year warranty ($1,250,919 including HST; $1,126,140 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $779,736.00, $895,861.70, $986,200.00, $999,900.00, $1,180,646.48, $1,241,670.00, $1,673,344.10.

The official summary names a figure of $779,736.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
