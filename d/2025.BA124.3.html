Award of Doc4821124325 to Joe Pace and Sons Contracting Inc. for George Bell Arena State of Good Repair for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedMar 19, 2025 · Toronto City Council

An $11,889,225 contract with Joe Pace and Sons for repair and upkeep work at George Bell Arena, through September 2026 ($13,434,824 including HST; $12,098,475 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $9,069,549.00, $12,950,000.00.

The official summary names a figure of $9,069,549.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
