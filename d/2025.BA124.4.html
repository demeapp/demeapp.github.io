Award of Doc4915589102 to 9030-5814 Québec Inc. o/a Spectralite/Signoplus for the Supply of Aluminum Sign Blanks for Transportation Services

AdoptedMar 19, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,626,574 contract with Spectralite/Signoplus for the metal panels used to make road signs, for two years through March 2027 ($1,838,029 including HST; $1,655,202 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $1,478,703.75, $1,765,639.50, $2,033,600.25, $4,545,756.25, $12,710,335.50.

The official summary names a figure of $1,478,703.75.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
