Award of Doc4973924816 to Almon Equipment Ltd. and Guild Electric Limited for the Application, Re-Application and Warranty of Traffic and Custom Pavement Markings on Roads and Expressways for Transportation Services

AdoptedMar 19, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Contracts with Almon Equipment and Guild Electric for painting lane lines and road markings across Toronto for two years, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Area 1: $3,527,976 ($3,986,613; $3,590,069); Area 2: $4,024,769 ($4,547,989; $4,095,605); combined totals: $8,534,602 (including HST), $7,685,674 (net of HST recoveries). Area 1 bids: $3,052,806.50, $3,556,799.98, $4,423,329.50. Area 2 bids: $2,557,082.32, $3,484,799.92, $3,893,281.44.

The official summary names a figure of $3,052,806.50.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
