Award of Doc4975974141 to Midome Construction Services Ltd. for Road Resurfacing on Various Streets for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedMar 19, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,074,100 contract with Midome Construction for repaving Stephenson Avenue, Cosburn Avenue, Main Street, Westlake Avenue and Haldon Avenue, ending August 2025 ($1,213,733 including HST; $1,093,004 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $934,000.00, $1,031,777.00, $1,139,170.02, $1,333,333.33, $1,387,858.46, $1,689,349.59.

The official summary names a figure of $934,000.00.
