Award of Doc4998124539 to Aqua Tech Solutions Inc. for Hot-In-Place Asphalt Patching Within East York and Scarborough Districts for Transportation Services

AdoptedMar 19, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $606,949 contract with Aqua Tech Solutions for pothole patching done by reheating the existing pavement in East York and Scarborough, through December 2025 ($685,852 including HST; $617,631 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. The bid received was $596,948.50.

The official summary names a figure of $596,948.50.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
