Award of Doc4837489444 to WSP Canada Inc. for the Preliminary, Detailed Design, Construction and Post Construction Services for the Queensway Project for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedMar 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $5,307,875 contract with WSP Canada for engineering design and construction oversight of the Queensway project, through December 2031 ($5,997,899 including HST; $5,401,294 net of HST recoveries) is up for approval by the city's contract panel.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
