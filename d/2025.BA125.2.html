Award of Doc4913561938 to 614128 Ontario Ltd. O/A Trisan Construction for Bluffer's Park Washroom Improvements: Sanitary Forcemain Replacement for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedMar 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,661,803 contract with Trisan Construction to replace the sanitary sewer line serving the Bluffer's Park washroom, finishing August 2025 ($1,877,837 including HST; $1,691,051 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $1,326,992.90, $1,694,478.00, $2,974,974.00, $3,329,260.00, $3,605,426.25.

The official summary names a figure of $1,326,992.90.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
