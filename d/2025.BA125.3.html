Award of Doc4987617254 to PNJ Engineering Inc. for Material Specification Compliance Testing and Project Quality Assurance Services for Transportation Services

AdoptedMar 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $326,565 contract with PNJ Engineering for testing construction materials and quality checks on road projects in Toronto-East York and Scarborough through December 2025, with one optional renewal year ($369,018 including HST; $332,313 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Option year: $336,062 ($379,750; $341,977); two-year total: $662,627 ($748,768; $674,289). Bids: $316,565.00, $546,885.00, $662,705.00.

The official summary names a figure of $316,565.00.
