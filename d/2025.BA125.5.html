Award of Ariba Doc5006610288 to Kudlak-Baird 1982 Limited for the Non-exclusive Supply of Maintenance and Repair of Electrical Systems at Various Toronto Locations for Corporate Real Estate Management

AdoptedMar 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A three-year contract (April 2025 to March 2028) with Kudlak-Baird for electrical system maintenance and repairs at various city buildings, with one optional renewal year, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Award: $9,681,642 ($10,940,255; $9,852,039); option year: $3,324,030 ($3,756,154; $3,382,533); four-year total: $13,005,672 ($14,696,410,; $13,234,572). Bids: $2,689,345, $2,977,262, $5,272,395.

The official summary names a figure of $2,689,345.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (March 20, 2025) from the Chief Procurement Officer: Solicitation Number: Request for Quotation Doc5006610288 Description: For the Maintenance and Repair of Electrical Systems at Various Toronto locations for Corporate Real Estate Management, Fleet Services and Solid Waste Management Divisions. The award is for an initial contract period of three (3) years from April 1, 2025, to March 31, 2028, with the City having the option to extend the contract on the same terms and conditions for up to an additional one (1) year period. Should the option(s) be exercised, the Executive Director of Corporate Real Estate Management, will request the Chief Procurement Officer to process the renewals under the same terms and conditions.…
