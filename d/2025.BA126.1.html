Award of Doc5005887622 to VBN Paving Limited for the Repairs to Roads, Arterial, Collector and Laneways at Various Locations for Transportation Services

AdoptedApr 2, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $ 4,600,268 contract with VBN Paving for road and sidewalk repairs in the Etobicoke York district, through December 2025 ($ 5,198,303 including HST; $ 4,681,233 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Bids: $ 4,572,267.50, $ 4,672,239, $ 5,053,058.95, $ 5,223,777, $ 5,255,046.75, $ 5,323,504.90.
