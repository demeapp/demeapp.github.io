Award of Doc4979801915 to OJCR Construction Ltd. for the Repair or Replacement of Water Services and Replacement of Curb Boxes, Stops and Large Diameter Valve Box by Vacuum Excavation for Toronto Water

AdoptedApr 2, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $2,920,305 contract with OJCR Construction for repairing and replacing water service connections and valve boxes across Toronto using vacuum-digging equipment, July 2025 to June 2026 with two optional renewal years ($3,299,945 including HST; $2,971,702 net of HST recoveries), is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Option years: $3,007,914 ($3,398,943; $3,060,853), $3,098,152 ($3,500,911; $3,152,679); three-year total: $9,026,371 ($10,199,799; $9,185,235). Bids: $2,830,305.00, $2,908,123.00, $2,978,948.00.

The official summary names a figure of $2,830,305.00.
