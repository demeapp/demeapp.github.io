Award of Doc4328291470 to Lift North America Inc., operating as Masterlift for On-Site Safety Inspections, Testing, Certification, Maintenance Scheduling, Maintenance and Repairs for Powered Lift Trucks for Various City Divisions

AdoptedApr 2, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A one-year contract (April 2025 to March 2026) with Masterlift for safety checks, testing and repairs of forklifts across city divisions, with four optional renewal years, is up for approval by the city's contract panel. Award: $222,848 ($251,818; $226,770); option years: $229,534 ($259,373; $233,573), $236,420 ($267,154; $240,581), $243,512 ($275,169; $247,798), $250,818 ($283,424; $255,232); five-year total: $1,183,132 ($1,336,938; $1,203,954). Bids: $185,706.81, $234,721.46, $247,759.99.

The official summary names a figure of $185,706.81.
