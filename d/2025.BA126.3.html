Award of Doc4739743766 to M.E. Andrews and Associates Limited for Engineering Services for the Mid Toronto Interceptor Trunk Sewer Chamber Drain Repairs for Toronto Water

AdoptedApr 2, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,298,575 contract with M.E. Andrews and Associates for engineering work to repair drains on the Mid Toronto Interceptor main sewer line, through December 2029 ($1,467,390, including HST; $1,321,430 net of HST recoveries) is up for approval by the city's contract panel.
