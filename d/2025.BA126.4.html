Award of Doc4811298215 to EXP Services Inc. for Professional Consulting Services for the Rehabilitation of Bridges ID077 and ID076 for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedApr 2, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $6,122,848 contract with EXP Services for consulting on the repair of the Prince Edward Viaduct and Rosedale Valley Viaduct bridges, through December 2031 ($6,918,818 including HST; $6,230,610 net of HST recoveries) is up for approval by the city's contract panel.
