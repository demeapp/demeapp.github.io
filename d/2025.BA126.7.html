Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Document Number 4913427082 to Anacond Contracting Inc., for the Union Station Third Floor Security Offices Renovation at 65 Front Street West, Toronto, for Corporate Real Estate Management

AdoptedApr 2, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding a $1,636,250 contract ($1,848,963 including HST; $1,665,048 net of HST recoveries) to Anacond Contracting for renovating the third-floor security offices at Union Station, 65 Front Street West. The two bids were $1,309,000 and $1,568,500.

The official summary names a figure of $1,309,000.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
