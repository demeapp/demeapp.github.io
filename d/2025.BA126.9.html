Award of Doc4951011283 to Clearway Construction Inc. for the New Connection and Incoming Sewer at the Scott Street Sewage Pumping Station for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedApr 2, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding a $2,770,161 contract ($3,130,282 including HST; $2,818,916 net of HST recoveries) to Clearway Construction for a new sewer connection at the Scott Street Sewage Pumping Station. The three bids were $2,462,365.50, $2,929,000.00 and $2,929,327.00.

The official summary names a figure of $2,462,365.50.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
