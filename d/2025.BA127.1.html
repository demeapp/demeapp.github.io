Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4681378101 to Ecotex Healthcare Linen Services for the Provision of Laundry Services for Seniors Services and Long-Term Care and Toronto Paramedic Services

AdoptedApr 9, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding a two-year, $9,357,278 laundry and linen contract ($10,573,725 including HST; $9,521,966 net of HST recoveries) to Ecotex Healthcare Linen Services for long-term care homes and paramedic services. With the optional two-year period ($9,637,997 / $10,890,936 / $9,807,625), the total is $18,995,275 / $21,464,661 / $19,329,592.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
