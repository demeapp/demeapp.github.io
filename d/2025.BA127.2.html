Award of Doc4702380227 to Dependable Emergency Vehicles, DIV Dependable Truck and Tank Ltd. for the Non-exclusive Supply and Delivery of Two (2) Air/Light Emergency Response Vehicles for Toronto Fire Services

No action takenApr 9, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding a $1,343,805 contract ($1,518,500 including HST; $1,367,456 net of HST recoveries) to Dependable Emergency Vehicles for two air/light emergency vehicles for Toronto Fire Services. The bids were $1,286,000.00 and $1,317,456.00.

The official summary names a figure of $1,286,000.00. No action was taken on this item.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
