Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4751539950 to AECOM Canada Ltd. for Spring Creek Ponds Reaeration Demonstration Project for Toronto Water

No action takenApr 9, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding a $1,744,662 contract ($1,971,468 including HST; $1,775,368 net of HST recoveries) to AECOM Canada for the Spring Creek Ponds water-oxygenation demonstration project, through December 2030. AECOM was the only bidder.

No action was taken on this item.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
