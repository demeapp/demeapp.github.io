Award of Doc4794193248 to Joe Pace and Sons Contracting Inc. for Centennial Park Artificial Ice Skating Trail for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedApr 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

An $8,015,009 contract ($9,056,960 including HST; $8,156,073 net of HST recoveries) with Joe Pace and Sons for an artificial ice skating path at Centennial Park, through December 2026, is up for approval by the contract panel. The two bids were $7,000,224.87 and $7,761,500.00.

The official summary names a figure of $7,000,224.87.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
