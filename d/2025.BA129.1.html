Award of Doc4845512607 to Multiple Suppliers for the Development of Consolidated Sanitary and Combined Sewer Hydraulic Models for Toronto Water

AdoptedApr 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding three sewer-modeling contracts: $2,430,462 / $2,746,422 / $2,473,238 to Stantec, $3,003,511 / $3,393,967 / $3,056,372 to Arcadis and $2,670,134 / $3,017,251 / $2,717,128 to AECOM. Combined, the three total $9,157,640 including HST and $8,246,738 net of HST recoveries.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
