Award of Doc4963994971 to Tree Doctors Inc. for Arboricultural Services throughout the City of Toronto for Environment, Climate and Forestry

AdoptedApr 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $747,000 tree-care contract ($844,110 including HST; $760,147 net of HST recoveries) with Tree Doctors, with four optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $624,700.00, $654,685.00, $888,984.05 and $970,280.00; the option years are $769,410 / $869,433 / $782,952, $792,492 / $895,516 / $806,440, $816,267 / $922,382 / $830,633 and $840,755 / $950,053 / $855,552, for a five-year total of $3,965,924 / $4,481,495 / $4,035,725.

The official summary names a figure of $624,700.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
