Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5003461334 to Trinity Construction Management Ltd. for Full Roof Replacement and Roof Top Unit Replacement of the Papermill Theatre building at 67 Pottery Road for Economical Development and Culture

AdoptedApr 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,093,939 contract ($1,236,151 including HST; $1,093,939 net of HST recoveries) with Trinity Construction Management for a new roof and rooftop units at the Papermill Theatre, 67 Pottery Road, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $929,939.00, $1,180,799.88, $1,258,000.00 and $1,313,900.00.

The official summary names a figure of $929,939.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
