Award of Doc5055777749 to Midome Construction Services Ltd. for Watermain and TTC Track Replacement in Toronto for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedApr 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $2,728,800 contract ($3,083,544 including HST; $2,776,827 net of HST recoveries) with Midome Construction for watermain and TTC track replacement at King Street East and Church Street, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $2,274,000.00, $2,879,768.00 and $4,186,379.36.

The official summary names a figure of $2,274,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
