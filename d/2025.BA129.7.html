Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Collaborative Procurement Contract from Ontario Education Collaborative Marketplace Master Agreement OECM-2024-453-01 to Deloitte LLP for Tax Recovery and Tax Advisory Services for Accounting Services Division

AdoptedApr 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,000,000-per-year deal ($1,130,000 including HST; $1,017,600 net of HST recoveries) with Deloitte LLP for tax recovery and advice, June to December 2025, with two optional one-year extensions, is up for approval by the contract panel. The three-year total is $3,000,000 / $3,390,000 / $3,052,800.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
