Award of Doc4898593115 to 2246706 Ontario Inc. O/A Tiki Taxi for Marine Water Taxi Services for Toronto Water Division

AdoptedApr 30, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding a $161,943 water-taxi contract ($182,996 including HST; $164,793 net of HST recoveries) to Tiki Taxi for trips to the Island Water Treatment Plant, May 2025 to April 2026, with four optional renewal years.
