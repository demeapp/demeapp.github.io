Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4919130062 to South Central Inc. for the Union Station Procurement and Installation of Hostile Vehicle Mitigation Barriers Located at Front Street West for Corporate Real Estate Management

AdoptedApr 30, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $5,458,623 contract ($6,168,243 including HST; $5,554,694 net of HST recoveries) with South Central Inc. for anti-vehicle barriers, trench drains and granite pavers at Union Station, 65 Front Street West, is up for approval by the contract panel. The four bids were $4,548,922.56, $5,262,057.00, $5,380,075.53 and $6,938,900.00.

The official summary names a figure of $4,548,922.56.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
