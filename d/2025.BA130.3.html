Award of Doc5020378835 to Weston Forest Products Inc. for the Non-exclusive Supply and Delivery of Various Lumber Items for Various Divisions

AdoptedApr 30, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A one-year, $948,291 lumber-supply contract ($1,071,569 including HST; $964,981 net of HST recoveries) with Weston Forest Products, with four optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel.
