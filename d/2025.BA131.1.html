Award of Doc4325600649 to Anaplan, Inc for the Provision of a Financial Planning and Analysis Software-as-a Service Solution for the Financial Planning Division

AdoptedMay 7, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A five-year, $4,328,445 contract ($4,891,142 including HST; $4,404,625 net of HST recoveries) with Anaplan for online financial planning software for the city's budget process, November 2025 to November 2030 is up for approval by the contract panel.
