Award of Doc5052385955 to 7850891 Canada Inc O/A Egnatia Paving for the Roadway Crack Repairs Within Toronto for Transportation Services

AdoptedMay 7, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,151,835 contract ($1,301,574 including HST; $1,172,107 net of HST recoveries) with Egnatia Paving for road crack repairs in Toronto, East York and Scarborough, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $1,136,835, $1,386,750 and $1,473,370.

The official summary names a figure of $1,136,835.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
