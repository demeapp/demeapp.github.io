Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5063061527 to 2489960 Ontario Inc. O/A Kore Infrastructure for the Watermain Replacement and Road Resurfacing for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedMay 7, 2025 · Toronto City Council

An $11,842,600 contract ($13,382,138 including HST; $12,051,030 net of HST recoveries) with Kore Infrastructure for watermain replacement and repaving on Sheppard Avenue from Kennedy Road to Victoria Park Avenue, is up for approval by the contract panel. The five bids were $10,766,000, $12,384,432.75, $12,529,051, $13,133,000 and $16,640,859.80.

The official summary names a figure of $10,766,000.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
