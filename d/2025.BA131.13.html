Award of Doc5069651675 to Defina Haulage Ltd. for the Repair of Distressed Asphalt Within Toronto for Transportation Services

AdoptedMay 7, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $908,060 contract ($1,026,108 including HST; $924,042 net of HST recoveries) with Defina Haulage for fixing damaged asphalt in Toronto and East York, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $887,400, $888,060, $932,500 and $934,150.

The official summary names a figure of $887,400.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
