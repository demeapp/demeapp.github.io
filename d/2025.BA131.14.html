Award of Doc5086159751 to Maple-Crete Inc. for Builders Files and Road Maintenance Within Scarborough District for Transportation Services

AdoptedMay 7, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $709,535 contract ($801,775 including HST; $722,023 net of HST recoveries) with Maple-Crete for road maintenance in the Scarborough district, is up for approval by the contract panel. The six bids were $699,535.00, $757,910.75, $761,549, $878,424, $1,124,215 and $1,297,368.50.

The official summary names a figure of $699,535.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
