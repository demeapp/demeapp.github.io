Award of Doc4797919445 to ProSpect Scientific Incorporated for the Supply and Delivery of Various Chemical and Gas Detection Equipment for Toronto Fire Services

AdoptedMay 7, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A one-year, $196,082 contract ($221,573 including HST; $199,533 net of HST recoveries) with ProSpect Scientific for chemical and gas detection gear for Toronto Fire, with four optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $657,030.00, $93,480.00 and $163,402.00; the option years are $177,025 / $200,038 / $180,141, $182,336 / $206,039 / $185,545, $184,527 / $208,516 / $187,775 and $190,063 / $214,772 / $193,408, for a five-year total of $930,034 / $1,050,938 / $946,402.

The official summary names a figure of $657,030.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
