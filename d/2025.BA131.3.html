Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Ariba Doc4887066699 to Centennial College and Cortech Safety Solutions Inc., for the Provision of Technical and Continuous Education and Health and Safety Related Training for Fleet Services Division

AdoptedMay 7, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Two training contracts for Fleet Services staff: $100,806 per year ($113,911 including HST; $102,580 net of HST recoveries) with Centennial College and $28,560 per year ($32,273 including HST; $29,063 net of HST recoveries) with Cortech Safety Solutions, is up for approval by the contract panel. The five-year totals are $504,030 / $569,554 / $512,901 and $142,800 / $161,364 / $145,313; the bids were $84,005.00, $23,800, $30,500 and $35,500.

The official summary names a figure of $84,005.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
