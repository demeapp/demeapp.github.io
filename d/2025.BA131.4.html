Award of Doc4908094740 to Martinway Contracting Ltd. for 329 Chaplin Crescent Roof Restoration, Roof Top Unit Replacement and North Elevation Mock-Up Project for Economic Development and Culture

AdoptedMay 7, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $693,292 contract ($783,420 including HST; $705,494 net of HST recoveries) with Martinway Contracting for roof work at 329 Chaplin Crescent, is up for approval by the contract panel. The five bids were $619,648.89, $674,800.00, $725,827.50, $748,000.00 and $872,000.00.

The official summary names a figure of $619,648.89.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
