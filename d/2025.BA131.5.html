Award of Doc5017706330 to StormFisher Environmental Ltd. and Seacliff Energy Corp. for Processing Services for Up to 50,000 Tonnes Annually of Organic Material for Solid Waste Management Services

AdoptedMay 7, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Two organic-waste processing contracts: $9,013,064 / $10,184,763 / $9,171,694 with StormFisher (30,000 tonnes) and $6,676,344 / $7,544,269 / $6,793,848 with Seacliff Energy (20,000 tonnes), each for three years with one optional year, is up for approval by the contract panel. The option years are $3,186,392 / $3,600,623 / $3,242,472 and $2,360,290 / $2,667,128 / $2,401,831; four-year totals are $13,785,386 and $12,414,167 (StormFisher) and $10,211,397 and $9,195,679 (Seacliff Energy); the combined four-year total is $21,236,091 / $23,996,782 / $21,609,846. The bids were $2,661,000.00, $3,857,700.00, $4,064,700.00, $1,834,000.00, $2,271,800.00, $2,471,480.00 and $2,709,800.00.

The official summary names a figure of $2,661,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
