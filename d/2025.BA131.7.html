Award of Doc5023827634 to PTR Paving Inc. for Repairs to Culverts, Driveways, Ditches and Shoulders at Various Locations for Transportation Services

AdoptedMay 7, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,526,600 contract ($1,725,057 including HST; $1,553,468 net of HST recoveries) with PTR Paving for culvert and ditch repairs in Etobicoke York, is up for approval by the contract panel. The eight bids were $1,486,599.50, $1,597,575.00, $1,620,296.00, $1,869,694.00, $1,880,077.70, $1,888,031.00, $2,017,315.00 and $2,174,826.46.

The official summary names a figure of $1,486,599.50.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
