Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5029493464 to HN Construction Ltd for the Interior Renovation of 95 The Esplanade, Toronto, Ontario for Corporate Real Estate Management

AdoptedMay 7, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,575,600 contract ($1,780,428 including HST; $1,603,331 net of HST recoveries) with HN Construction for interior renovations at 95 The Esplanade, is up for approval by the contract panel. The eight bids were $1,313,000.00, $1,317,000.00, $1,485,228.82, $1,558,265.00, $1,583,000.00, $1,656,664.90, $1,908,280.00 and $1,946,806.30.

The official summary names a figure of $1,313,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
