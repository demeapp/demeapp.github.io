Award of Doc4925861999 to Acart Equipment Ltd. for Supply, Delivery, and Warranty of Service Carts, Hampers and Linen Carts for Seniors Services and Long-Term Care

AdoptedMay 14, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A two-year, $465,636 contract ($526,169 including HST) with Acart Equipment Ltd. for service carts, hampers and linen carts for long-term care homes, with three optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel. Three other category awards go to MIP Inc. ($363,169), Five Star Enterprises ($104,222) and Stevens Company Ltd. ($396,348), all net of taxes including option years; the total potential award across all categories is $1,206,842 net of taxes ($1,363,732 including taxes). Acart was the lowest compliant bidder in its categories; the rival bids there were non-compliant.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
