Award of Doc4957288342 to Melfer Construction Inc. for Earl Bales Park New Skateboard Park for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedMay 14, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,293,240 contract ($1,461,361 including HST; $1,316,001 net of HST recoveries) with Melfer Construction for a new skateboard park at Earl Bales Park, is up for approval by the contract panel. The five bids were $999,847.00, $1,077,700.00, $1,167,700.00, $1,250,354.73 and $1,594,495.00.

The official summary names a figure of $999,847.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
