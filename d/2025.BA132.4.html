Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5020168499 to 2489960 Ontario Inc. o/a Kore Infrastructure Group for Road Resurfacing, Watermain Replacement and New Sidewalk Construction on Various Streets for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedMay 14, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $2,268,200 contract ($2,563,066 including HST; $2,308,120 net of HST recoveries) with Kore Infrastructure for road and watermain work on several streets, is up for approval by the contract panel. The five bids were $2,062,000.00, $2,188,982.00, $2,377,777.00, $2,414,414.69 and $2,444,000.00.

The official summary names a figure of $2,062,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
