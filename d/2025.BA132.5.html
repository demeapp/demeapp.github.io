Award of Doc5092408869 to D. Crupi & Sons Limited for Critical Interim Repairs on Roadways Within Toronto for Transportation Services

AdoptedMay 14, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,720,000 contract ($1,943,600 including HST; $1,750,272 net of HST recoveries) with D. Crupi & Sons for urgent road repairs across Toronto, is up for approval by the contract panel. The six bids were $1,600,000.00, $1,627,174.50, $1,710,716.25, $1,770,502.88, $1,874,802.50 and $1,878,200.91.

The official summary names a figure of $1,600,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
