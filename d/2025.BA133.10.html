Award of Doc5059734237 to Aqua Tech Solutions Inc., for Hot-in-Place Asphalt Patching Repairs to Various Streets for Transportation Services

AdoptedMay 21, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $479,681 contract ($542,040 including HST; $488,123 net of HST recoveries) with Aqua Tech Solutions for pothole patching by reheating pavement in Etobicoke York and North York, with two optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bid was $477,681.00; the option years are $494,011 / $558,233 / $502,706 and $508,772 / $574,912 / $517,726, for a three-year total of $1,482,464 / $1,675,185 / $1,508,556.

The official summary names a figure of $477,681.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
