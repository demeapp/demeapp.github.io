Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5102518578 to 2198271 Ontario Ltd., operating as Metro Asphalt for Repairs to Ditches, Culverts, Shoulders, Driveways and Guide Rails for Transportation Services

AdoptedMay 21, 2025 · Toronto City Council

An $818,694 contract ($925,124 including HST; $833,103 net of HST recoveries) with Metro Asphalt for ditch and culvert repairs in the Scarborough district, is up for approval by the contract panel. The seven bids were $778,694.00, $779,135.10, $837,777.77, $868,370.50, $871,743.40, $889,333.00 and $924,948.80.

The official summary names a figure of $778,694.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
