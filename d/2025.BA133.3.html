Award of Doc4892449484 to Acapulco Pools Limited for the Repair and Maintenance for City Water Slides for the Parks and Recreation

AdoptedMay 21, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A one-year, $108,618 contract ($122,738 including HST; $110,530 net of HST recoveries) with Acapulco Pools for water slide repairs, with four optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $90,515.00 and $958,210.00; the option years are $111,877 / $126,420 / $113,846, $115,233 / $130,213 / $117,261, $118,690 / $134,119 / $120,779 and $122,251 / $138,143 / $124,402, for a five-year total of $576,668 / $651,635 / $586,817.

The official summary names a figure of $90,515.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
