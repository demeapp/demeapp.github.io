Award of Doc4930010603 to GIP Paving for Basement Flooding Protection Program, Phase 4 Various Assignments for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedMay 21, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $17,797,115 contract ($20,110,740 including HST; $18,110,344 net of HST recoveries) with GIP Paving for basement flooding protection work, is up for approval by the contract panel. The five bids were $15,475,752.12, $17,429,375.02, $17,986,939.84, $18,459,646.19 and $19,919,924.50.

The official summary names a figure of $15,475,752.12.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
