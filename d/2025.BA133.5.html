Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4953590192 to Lomco Limited for Tennis Courts State of Good Repair Rehabilitation for Seneca Hill Park for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedMay 21, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,408,097 contract ($1,591,150 including HST; $1,432,880 net of HST recoveries) with Lomco Limited for tennis court repairs at Seneca Hill Park, is up for approval by the contract panel. The five bids were $1,153,997.00, $1,252,521.00, $1,252,480.50, $1,298,525.00 and $1,289,766.58.

The official summary names a figure of $1,153,997.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
