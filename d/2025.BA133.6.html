Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4956515637 to 614128 Ontario Ltd., operating as Trisan Construction for Basement Flooding Protection Program, Phase 4 Various Assignments for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedMay 21, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $16,556,539 contract ($18,708,889 including HST; $16,847,934 net of HST recoveries) with Trisan Construction for basement flooding protection work, is up for approval by the contract panel. The seven bids were $14,396,990.23, $15,789,347.26, $16,561,068.24, $16,986,167.71, $17,948,110.00, $20,796 and $27,981,140.50.

The official summary names a figure of $14,396,990.23.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
