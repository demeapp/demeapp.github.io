Award of Doc4890584788 to Kudlak-Baird (1982) Limited for Dufferin Transfer Station Exterior Lighting Improvements for Solid Waste Management Services

AdoptedMay 28, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,525,571 contract ($1,723,895 including HST; $1,552,421 net of HST recoveries) with Kudlak-Baird for lighting upgrades at the Dufferin Transfer Station, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $1,271,309.00 and $1,357,380.00.

The official summary names a figure of $1,271,309.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
