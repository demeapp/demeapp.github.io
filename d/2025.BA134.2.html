Award of Doc5048607075 to Robert B. Somerville Co. Limited for the Watermain CIPP Lining at Various Locations for Toronto Water

AdoptedMay 28, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $10,849,713 contract ($12,260,176 including HST; $11,040,668 net of HST recoveries) with Robert B. Somerville Co. for dig-free watermain relining in Wards 16, 21 and 24, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $ 9,863,375 and $ 9,958,866.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
