Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4611820536 to GFL Environmental Services Inc. for Preventive Maintenance of Catch Basins, Grease Traps and Oil Interceptors for Various Divisions

AdoptedJun 4, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A three-year, $6,023,348 contract ($6,806,383 including HST; $6,129,358 net of HST recoveries) with GFL for upkeep of catch basins, grease traps and oil separators, with two optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $439,490, $23,140, $1,740,830, $142,323, $2,116,470, $5,837,931, $1,904,537, $6,623,645 and $774,735; the option years are $2,068,016 / $2,336,858 / $2,104,413 and $2,130,056 / $2,406,964 / $2,167,545, for a five-year total of $10,221,420 / $11,550,205 / $10,401,317.

The official summary names a figure of $439,490.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
