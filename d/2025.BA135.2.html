Award of Doc4985288483 to Forest Ridge Landscaping Inc. O/A Urban Life Solutions for Tree Pit and Planter Maintenance for Environment, Climate and Forestry

AdoptedJun 4, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A one-year, $132,840 contract ($150,109 including HST; $135,178 net of HST recoveries) with Forest Ridge Landscaping for tree pit and planter care, with three optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $132,840.00, $184,500.00, $196,800.00, $287,820.00, $306,270.00, $307,500.00 and $322,875.00; the option years are $136,825 / $154,612 / $139,233, $140,930 / $159,251 / $143,410 and $145,158 / $164,028 / $147,713, for a four-year total of $555,753 / $628,001 / $565,534.

The official summary names a figure of $132,840.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
