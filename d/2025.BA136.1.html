Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Collaborative Procurement from Kinetic GPO Request for Standing Offers Number 23-02 to AssetWorks Inc. for Fuel and Fleet Management and Related Solutions and Services for Fleet Services Division

AdoptedJun 11, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $650,353 contract ($734,898 including HST; $661,799 net of HST recoveries) with AssetWorks for fuel and fleet tracking software through a Kinetic GPO buying deal, to May 2026, with two optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel. The option years are $669,863 / $756,945 / $681,653 and $689,959 / $779,654 / $702,102, for a three-year total of $2,010,175 / $2,271,497 / $2,045,554.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
