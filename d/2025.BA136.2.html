Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5120847019 to Midome Construction Services Ltd. for Road and Cycling Infrastructure Improvements for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedJun 11, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,443,040 contract ($1,630,635 including HST; $1,468,438 net of HST recoveries) with Midome Construction for road, sidewalk and bike lane work on Brunswick Avenue, Havelock Street and Macdonell Avenue, is up for approval by the contract panel. The eight bids were $1,244,000.00, $1,532,704.00, $1,556,155.65, $1,668,994.00, $1,677,777.00, $1,750,778.00, $1,862,799.80 and $2,219,726.65.

The official summary names a figure of $1,244,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
