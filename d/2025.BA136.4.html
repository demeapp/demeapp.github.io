Award of Doc5092149316 to Alliance Verdi Civil Inc. for the Rehabilitation of Two (2) Bridges for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedJun 11, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $7,797,364 contract ($8,811,022 including HST; $7,934,598 net of HST recoveries) with Alliance Verdi Civil for fixing two bridges, is up for approval by the contract panel. The six bids were $6,497,803.50, $6,497,922.00, $6,773,313.00, $7,119,422.00, $7,272,963.00 and $8,878,784.00.

The official summary names a figure of $6,497,803.50.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
