Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4913561945 to Zprocis Solutions Inc., for the implementation of the IBM Maximo Work and Asset Management solution for the Playground Service Area of Parks and Recreation Division

AdoptedJun 11, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A three-year, $2,169,056 contract ($2,451,033 including HST; $2,207,231 net of HST recoveries) with Zprocis Solutions to set up IBM Maximo work-tracking software for Parks and Recreation playgrounds, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $2,169,055.74, $3,167,564.00 and $23,298,800.00.

The official summary names a figure of $2,169,055.74.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
