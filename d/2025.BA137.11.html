Award of Doc5107248039 to Midome Construction Services Ltd. for TTC Track Replacement for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedJun 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,466,400 contract ($1,657,032 including HST; $1,492,209 net of HST recoveries) with Midome Construction for TTC track work at College Street and McCaul Street, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $1,222,000.00, $1,327,700.00 and $2,667,662.00.

The official summary names a figure of $1,222,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
