Award of Doc5115979779 to Bridgecon Construction Ltd. for Bridge Rehabilitation for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedJun 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $5,757,228 contract ($6,505,668 including HST; $5,858,555 net of HST recoveries) with Bridgecon Construction for fixing the Governors Road bridge over Moore Park Ravine, is up for approval by the contract panel. The four bids were $4,797,690.00, $6,157,602.50, $6,817,728.00 and $16,262,944.00.

The official summary names a figure of $4,797,690.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
