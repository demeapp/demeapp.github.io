Award of Doc5158501413 to PTR Paving Inc. for Permanent Maintenance Repairs of Roads and Sidewalk Accessibility Ramps within Toronto for Transportation Services

AdoptedJun 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $2,860,400 contract ($3,232,252 including HST; $2,910,743 net of HST recoveries) with PTR Paving for road and sidewalk repairs in Toronto and East York, is up for approval by the contract panel. The four bids were $2,785,400.00, $2,990,977.50, $3,275,593.26 and $3,895,264.00.

The official summary names a figure of $2,785,400.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
