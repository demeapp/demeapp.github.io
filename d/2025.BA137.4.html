Award of Doc4630717900 to Joe Pace and Sons Contracting Inc. for Centennial Park District Playground and Waterplay for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedJun 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $6,100,626 contract ($6,893,707 including HST; $6,207,997 net of HST recoveries) with Joe Pace and Sons for a playground and waterplay area at Centennial Park, is up for approval by the contract panel. The four bids were $5,500,625.57, $6,208,000.78, $6,298,828.00 and $8,496,161.00.

The official summary names a figure of $5,500,625.57.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (June 12, 2025) from the Chief Procurement Officer: Solicitation Number: Request for Tenders, Doc4630717900, Contract Number 24-PFR-031 Description: For the supply and delivery of all labour, materials, equipment, and supervision required to complete Centennial Park District Playground and Waterplay for Parks and Recreation. Recommended Supplier: Joe Pace and Sons Contracting Inc. Contract Award Value: $6,100,626 net of all applicable taxes and charges. $6,893,707 all applicable taxes and charges. $6,207,997 net of HST Recoveries The contract is expected to start on the date of award and end by December 31, 2027. This includes a two (2) year warranty period.
