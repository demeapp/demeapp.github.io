Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4880914220 to Joe Pace and Sons Contracting Inc. for Cummer Lodge Building Automation Systems Upgrades and Roof Repairs for Seniors Services and Long-Term Care

AdoptedJun 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $6,074,115 contract ($6,863,750 including HST; $6,181,019 net of HST recoveries) with Joe Pace and Sons for building-system upgrades and roof repairs at Cummer Lodge long-term care home, is up for approval by the contract panel. The six bids were $ 5,521,922.75, $ 5,564,484.00, $ 5,850,000.00, $ 6,292,403.00, $ 6,633,611.30 and $ 6,766,300.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
