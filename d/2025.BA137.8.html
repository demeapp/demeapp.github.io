Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5008094921 to various Suppliers to provide Asphalt and Concrete Paving Services for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedJun 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Five suppliers would share 18-month asphalt and concrete paving contracts for Parks and Recreation sites — $8,000,000 contracts to each of the three lowest bidders and $4,000,000 to the fourth and fifth for capital projects, plus $1,000,000 per supplier for parks operations — a combined $37,000,000 ($41,810,000 including HST), up for award by the contract panel.

The official summary names a figure of $3,347,005.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
