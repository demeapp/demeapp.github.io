Award of Doc4844777854 to Stantec Consulting Ltd. for Professional Engineering Services for the Proposed New Sections of Commissioners Street Sanitary Trunk Sewer for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $6,163,256 contract ($6,964,479 including HST; $6,271,729 net of HST recoveries) with Stantec Consulting for design work on new sections of the Commissioners Street main sewer line is up for approval by the contract panel.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
