Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4864874854 to Develotech Inc. for the Non-Exclusive Supply and Delivery of Bike Lane Bollards and Related Items for Purchasing and Materials Management (Stores)

AdoptedJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A one-year, $ 235,692 contract ($ 266,332 including HST; $ 239,840 net of HST recoveries) with Develotech for bike lane posts for city supply stores, with four optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $196,410.00, $235,692.00, $280,597.50, $282,425.55, $301,785.00 and $553,839.15; the option years are $242,763 / $274,322 / $247,035, $250,046 / $282,552 / $254,446, $257,547 / $291,028 / $262,080 and $265,273 / $299,759 / $269,942, for a five-year total of $1,251,321 / $1,413,993 / $1,273,344.

The official summary names a figure of $196,410.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
