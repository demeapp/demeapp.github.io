Award of Doc4953550456 to Infosys Public Services Canada Inc., for the Phase 2 Implementation of the IBM Maximo Work and Asset Management (EWMS) for Solid Waste Management Services Division

AdoptedJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A three-year, $2,199,951 contract ($2,485,944 including HST; $2,238,670 net of HST recoveries) with Infosys for Phase 2 of the IBM Maximo work-tracking system for Solid Waste Management, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $2,199,950.57 and $2,666,230.67.

The official summary names a figure of $2,199,950.57.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
