Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5078321208 to 2489960 Ontario Inc. o/a Kore Infrastructure Group for Watermain Replacement and Road Resurfacing for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $10,987,900 contract ($12,416,327 including HST; $11,181,287 net of HST recoveries) with Kore Infrastructure for water pipe and sewer work plus road repaving in several districts, is up for approval by the contract panel. The five bids were $ 9,989,000.00, $10,337,576.25, $10,723,990.00, $14,482,725.00 and $14,717,916.00.

The official summary names a figure of $10,337,576.25.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
