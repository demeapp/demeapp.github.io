Award of Doc5117247719 to Chemtrade Chemicals Canada Ltd. for the Non-Exclusive Supply and Delivery of Aluminum Sulphate for Toronto Water

AdoptedJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $2,231,620 contract ($2,521,731 including HST; $2,270,897 net of HST recoveries) with Chemtrade Chemicals for aluminum sulphate to treat drinking water, is up for approval by the contract panel. Chemtrade was the only bidder.

The official summary names a figure of $2,231,620.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
