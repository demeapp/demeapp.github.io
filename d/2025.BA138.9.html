Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Sourcewell Solicitation Number 082923-DAC to Green Tractors Inc. for the Supply and Delivery of Fifty Two (52) Various Agriculture Tractors with Related Accessories, Attachments and Supplies for Fleet Services

AdoptedJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $10,894,006 deal ($12,310,227 including HST; $11,085,741 net of HST recoveries) with Green Tractors for 52 diesel farm tractors, with an option to buy seven more in 2026, is up for approval by the contract panel. The 2026 option is $1,875,706 / $2,119,548 / $1,908,718; with it, the total is $12,769,712 / $14,429,775 / $12,994,459.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
