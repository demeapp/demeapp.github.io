Award of Doc5163872820 to Aqua Tech Solutions Inc. for Roadway and Roadside General Maintenance for Transportation Services

AdoptedJul 9, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $776,023 contract ($876,906 including HST; $789,681 net of HST recoveries) with Aqua Tech Solutions for road upkeep in Wards 20 to 25, is up for approval by the contract panel. The six bids were $746,022.90, $759,795.75, $878,699.28, $892,220.50, $910,546.39 and $946,130.00.

The official summary names a figure of $746,022.90.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
