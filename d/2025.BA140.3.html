Award of Doc5177952812 to Vaughan Paving Ltd. for the Construction of Asphalt Speed Humps for Transportation Services

AdoptedJul 9, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $2,120,800 speed-hump contract ($2,396,504 including HST; $2,158,126 net of HST recoveries) with Vaughan Paving, through December 2025, with one optional renewal year, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $2,050,800.00, $2,253,997.80, $2,490,040.00 and $3,395,172.00; the option year is $2,181,724 / $2,465,348 / $2,220,122, for a two-year total of $4,302,524 / $4,861,852 / $4,378,248.

The official summary names a figure of $2,050,800.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
