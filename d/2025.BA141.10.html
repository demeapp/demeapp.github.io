Award of Doc4781406813 to Lunar Contracting Inc. for the Supply and Delivery of Fire Station Appliances for Toronto Fire Services

AdoptedJul 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A one-year, $378,834 contract ($428,082 including HST; $385,501 net of HST recoveries) with Lunar Contracting for fire station appliances, with four optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $315,695.00, $543,772.00 and $591,387.35; the option years are $390,199 / $440,925 / $397,067, $401,905 / $454,153 / $408,979, $413,962 / $467,777 / $421,248 and $426,381 / $481,811 / $433,885, for a five-year total of $2,011,281 / $2,272,748 / $2,046,680.

The official summary names a figure of $315,695.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
