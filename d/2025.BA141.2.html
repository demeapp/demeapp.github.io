Award of Doc4883472183 to Hank Deenen Landscaping Ltd. for Huron Washington Parkette Playground Improvements for Parks and Recreation

WithdrawnJul 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,198,603 contract ($1,354,421 including HST; $1,219,698 net of HST recoveries) with Hank Deenen Landscaping for playground upgrades at Huron Washington Parkette, is up for approval by the contract panel. The six bids were $1,078,791.00, $1,155,000.00, $1,198,603.00, $1,200,320.00, $1,280,251.00 and $1,499,332.00.

The official summary names a figure of $1,078,791.00. The item was withdrawn before a final decision.
