Award of Doc4997853816 to Monarch Mechanical Ltd. for the Provision of Preventive and Corrective Maintenance of Refrigeration Equipment for Seniors Services and Long-Term Care

AdoptedJul 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A one-year, $241,332 contract ($272,705 including HST; $245,579 net of HST recoveries) with Monarch Mechanical for fridge upkeep in long-term care homes, with four optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bid was $229,839.71; the option years are $248,572 / $280,886 / $252,947, $256,029 / $289,313 / $260,535, $263,710 / $297,992 / $268,351 and $271,621 / $306,932 / $276,401, for a five-year total of $1,281,263 / $1,447,827 / $1,303,813.
