Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5195551450 to 2489960 Ontario Inc. O/A Kore Infrastructure Group for the Reconstruction of Greenwood Avenue for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedJul 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $2,561,900 contract ($2,894,947 including HST; $2,606,989 net of HST recoveries) with Kore Infrastructure for rebuilding Greenwood Avenue from Mortimer Avenue to Strathmore Boulevard, is up for approval by the contract panel. The seven bids were $2,329,000.00, $2,389,062.93, $2,538,749.92, $2,559,816.92, $2,624,000.00, $3,129,024.92 and $3,477,259.59.

The official summary names a figure of $2,329,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
