Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5074031122 to Aqua Tech Solutions Inc. for Full Length Trenchless Rehabilitation of Existing Gravity Sewers and Related Works at Various Locations for Toronto Water

AdoptedJul 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $4,539,525 contract ($5,129,663 including HST; $4,619,421 net of HST recoveries) with Aqua Tech Solutions for dig-free sewer repairs in northeast Toronto, is up for approval by the contract panel. The four bids were $3,887,426.17, $5,417,427.87, $5,887,887.00 and $6,194,983.30.

The official summary names a figure of $3,887,426.17.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
