Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5086270005 to Joe Pace and Sons Contracting Inc. for St. Lawrence Community Recreation Centre and Daycare Chiller and Roof Replacement for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedJul 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $2,276,998 contract ($2,573,008 including HST; $ 2,317,073 net of HST recoveries) with Joe Pace and Sons for cooling-system and roof replacement at the St. Lawrence Community Recreation Centre and daycare, is up for approval by the contract panel. The six bids were $54,000.00, $2,098,497.50, $2,135,79.00, $2,167,800.00, $2,304,660.00 and $2,519,359.00.

The official summary names a figure of $54,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
