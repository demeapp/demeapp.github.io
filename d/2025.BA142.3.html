Award of Doc4558834641 to Allied Medical Instruments Inc. for the Non-Exclusive Supply and Delivery of Cervical Collars for Purchasing and Materials Management (Stores)

AdoptedJul 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A one-year, $250,200 contract ($282,726 including HST; $254,604 net of HST recoveries) with Allied Medical Instruments for neck braces for city supply stores, with four optional renewals, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $208,500.00, $229,350.00, $295,027.50, $297,190.50, $298,502.50, $456,558.50 and $612,603.50; the option years are $257,706 / $291,208 / $262,242, $265,437 / $299,944 / $270,109, $273,400 / $308,942 / $278,212 and $281,602 / $318,211 / $286,559, for a five-year total of $1,328,346 / $1,501,031 / $1,351,725.

The official summary names a figure of $208,500.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
