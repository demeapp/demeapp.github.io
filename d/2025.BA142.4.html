Award of Doc5006610301 to Drainstar Contracting Ltd. for Sanitary Forcemain Replacement Project Group 2 - Phase 2B for Toronto Water

AdoptedJul 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $4,731,006 contract ($5,346,037 including HST; $4,814,272 net of HST recoveries) with Drainstar Contracting for twin sewer pressure pipes and pump station upgrades at three sewage pumping stations, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $4,143,861, $5,496,496 and $5,675,432.

The official summary names a figure of $4,143,861.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
