Award of Doc5074518176 to Profire Emergency Equipment Inc for the Supply and Delivery of Device Components and Parts for Smeal Aerial and Pumper Trucks to the City of Toronto's Fire Services (TFS) Division

AdoptedJul 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A one-year, $338,621 contract ($382,642 including HST; $344,581 net of HST recoveries) with Profire Emergency Equipment for fire truck parts, with four optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bid was $282,184.26; the option years are $348,780 / $394,121 / $354,918, $359,243 / $405,945 / $365,566, $370,020 / $418,123 / $376,533 and $381,121 / $430,667 / $387,829, for a five-year total of $1,797,785 / $2,031,498 / $1,829,426.
