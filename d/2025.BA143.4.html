Award of Doc5162427972 to Midome Construction Services Ltd. for Improvements to Roads and Pedestrian Infrastructure for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedJul 30, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,896,400 contract ($2,142,932 including HST; $1,929,777 net of HST recoveries) with Midome Construction for road repaving and new sidewalks and crossings, is up for approval by the contract panel. The eight bids were $1,724,000.00, $1,770,107.51, $1,838,000.00, $2,081,587.00, $2,149,926.00, $2,269,891.00, $2,274,397.00 and $3,026,108.50.

The official summary names a figure of $1,724,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
