Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4839866409 to Gio Crete Construction Ltd. for Watermain and Substandard Water Service Replacements, Bikeway Improvements and Local Road Resurfacing for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedAug 6, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $15,662,546 contract ($17,698,677 including HST; $15,938,207 net of HST recoveries) with Gio Crete Construction for water pipe, bike lane and repaving work on Queens Park Crescent East and Grosvenor Street, is up for approval by the contract panel. The seven bids were $12,700,753.00, $13,401,654.00, $13,407,777.77, $14,238,677.75, $14,499,011.00, $14,530,205.00 and $15,488,236.40.

The official summary names a figure of $12,700,753.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
