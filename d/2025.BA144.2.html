Award of Doc4882900765 to Harco Co Ltd. for a Heavy Duty Washer Extractor, Two (2) Product Liquid Chemical Dispenser, Drying Cabinet; and On-Site Training for Toronto Fire Services

AdoptedAug 6, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A one-year, $350,656 contract ($396,241 including HST; $356,828 net of HST recoveries) with Harco Co Ltd. for heavy-duty laundry machines and training for Toronto Fire, with four optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bid was $350,656.00; the option years are $83,166 / $93,978 / $84,630, $85,661 / $96,797 / $87,169, $88,231 / $99,701 / $89,784 and $90,878 / $102,692 / $92,478, for a five-year total of $698,593 / $789,410 / $710,888.
