Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5065962940 to Kingsgate Restoration Inc. for the Podium Deck, Ramp and Foundation Walls Waterproofing Project at 5100 Yonge Street, Toronto for Corporate Real Estate Management

AdoptedAug 6, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $2,899,313 contract ($3,276,224 including HST; $2,950,341 net of HST recoveries) with Kingsgate Restoration for waterproofing at 5100 Yonge Street, is up for approval by the contract panel. The eight bids were $ 2,436,927.39, $ 3,159,187.00, $3,241,775.00, $3,475,555.00, $3,824,091.00, $3,895,250.00, $4,268,857.03 and $4,475,000.00.

The official summary names a figure of $3,241,775.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
