Award of Doc5092150643 to Bell Canada for the Next Generation Firewall Acquisition, Premium Support and Implementation Services for Technology Services

AdoptedAug 6, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A five-year, $2,574,710 USD contract ($2,909,422 USD including HST; $2,620,025 USD net of HST recoveries) with Bell Canada for new network security equipment and support, is up for approval by the contract panel. The four bids were $2,574,709.41, $2,926,797.44, $2,998,085.20 and $431,612,248.22.

The official summary names a figure of $2,574,709.41.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
