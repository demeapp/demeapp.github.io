Award of Doc5180419688 to D. Martino Construction Limited for Road Resurfacing and Reconstruction for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedAug 6, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $2,035,000 contract ($2,299,550 including HST; $2,070,816 net of HST recoveries) with D. Martino Construction for repaving Winona Drive, is up for approval by the contract panel. The six bids were $1,850,000.00, $1,878,769.00, $1,970,000.00, $2,008,008.00, $2,028,595.00 and $2,515,656.00.

The official summary names a figure of $1,850,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
