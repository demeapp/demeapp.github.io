Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5167641635 to 2489960 Ontario Inc. O/A Kore Infrastructure Group for Laneway Reconstruction, Local Road Rehabilitation, Sidewalks and Watermain Replacement for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedAug 13, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $7,773,700 contract ($8,784,281 including HST; $7,910,517 net of HST recoveries) with Kore Infrastructure for laneway rebuilding, road repairs, new sidewalks and water pipe replacement, is up for approval by the contract panel. The six bids were $7,067,000.00, $8,270,342.12, $8,895,111.00, $8,905,448.00, $9,444,000.00 and $12,239,937.00.

The official summary names a figure of $7,067,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
