Award of Sourcewell Solicitation Number 112624-KBA to Kooy Brothers Lawn Equipment Ltd. for the Supply and Delivery of Eighteen (18) Grounds Maintenance Equipment and Related Attachments for Fleet Services

AdoptedAug 13, 2025 · Toronto City Council

An $8,743,135 deal ($9,879,743 including HST) with Kooy Brothers Lawn Equipment Ltd. for 18 diesel grounds-maintenance machines for Fleet Services, under a four-year Sourcewell purchasing agreement with an option for one more year, is up for approval by the contract panel. The city could buy 29 more units in 2026, 16 in 2027, 3 in 2028, and 5 in 2029, pending budget approval. With the option year exercised, the total potential value is $9,393,135 net of taxes ($10,614,243 including HST).

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
