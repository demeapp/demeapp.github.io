Award of Doc4892361105 to Joe Pace & Sons Contracting Inc. for Irving Chapley Community Centre State of Good Repair for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedAug 20, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $4,832,100 contract ($5,460,273 including HST; $4,917,145 net of HST recoveries) with Joe Pace & Sons for repairs at the Irving W. Chapley Community Centre, 205 Wilmington Avenue, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $4,082,100.00 and $4,094,800.00.

The official summary names a figure of $4,082,100.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (August 14, 2025) from the Chief Procurement Officer: Solicitation Number: Request for Tender Doc4892361105, Contract Number 22-PFR-024 Description: For the supply and delivery of all labour, materials, equipment, and supervision required to complete the proposed State of Good Repair work at Irving W. Chapley Community Centre at 205 Wilmington Avenue, Toronto for Parks and Recreation. Recommended Supplier: Joe Pace & Sons Contracting Inc. Contract Award Value: $4,832,100 net of all applicable taxes and charges. $5,460,273 including all applicable taxes and charges. $4,917,145 net of HST Recoveries The contract is expected to start on the date of award and end by December 31, 2028. This includes a two (2) year warranty period.
