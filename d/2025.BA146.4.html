Award of Doc5091005897 to Forest Ridge Landscaping Inc. for the Collection, Removal and Disposal of Waste, Refuse, Debris, Hoarded Items and Hazardous or Contaminated Material for Municipal Licensing and Standards

AdoptedAug 20, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A one-year, $333,288 contract ($376,615 including HST; $339,153 net of HST recoveries) with Forest Ridge Landscaping for removing waste and hazardous materials from properties where ordered, with four optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel.
