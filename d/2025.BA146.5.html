Award of Doc5104833666 to Direct Underground Inc. for Watermain Replacement and Road Resurfacing for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedAug 20, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $2,198,809 contract ($2,484,654 including HST; $2,237,508 net of HST recoveries) with Direct Underground for water pipe and repaving work on Goddard Street, is up for approval by the contract panel. The seven bids were $1,558,690.55, $1,928,688.41, $1,998,917.68, $2,044,000.00, $2,060,000.00, $2,117,441.97 and $2,196,956.54.

The official summary names a figure of $1,558,690.55.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
