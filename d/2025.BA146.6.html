Award of Doc5196624876 to Ernst & Young LLP for Upgrading SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding 1.0 Module to Onboarding 2.0 For Technology Services Division

AdoptedAug 20, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $685,000 contract ($774,050 including HST; $697,056 net of HST recoveries) with Ernst and Young for upgrading the city's employee onboarding software, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $685,000.00 and $879,638.00.

The official summary names a figure of $685,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
