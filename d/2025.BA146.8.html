Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Province of Ontario's Vendor of Record Tender-12075 - Managed Services Provider for Contingent IT Resources for Purchasing and Materials Management Division

AdoptedAug 20, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $20,000,000 contract ($22,600,000 including HST; $20,352,000 net of HST recoveries) with Flextrack Inc. for temporary IT staffing through April 2026 is up for approval by the contract panel.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The solicitation was issued by the Ministry of Government and Consumer Services of the Province of Ontario as " Tender-12075 - Managed Services Provider for Contingent IT Resources " and the opportunity was awarded on April 6, 2020.

What was decided

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the contract in accordance with the Contract Details set out in the report (August 14, 2025) from the Chief Procurement Officer: Solicitation Number: Province of Ontario's Vendor of Record Tender-12075 Sourcing Project WS5298848515 Description: To adopt the Vendor of Record Tender-12075 for Managed Services Provider for Contingent IT Resources. The Vendor of Record arrangement was arrived at through a negotiated competitive procurement process to provide contingent IT services staff where the work is short term and/or specific expertise is required to supplement current staff in program delivery for all ministries and approved agencies of the Government of Ontario for a contract term beginning April 6, 2020, and ending April 5, 2026.
