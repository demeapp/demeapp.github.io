Award of Doc4883472183 to Hank Deenen Landscaping Limited for Huron Washington Parkette Playground Improvements for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedAug 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,399,803 contract ($1,581,777 including HST; $1,424,439 net of HST recoveries) with Hank Deenen Landscaping for playground upgrades at Huron Washington Parkette, is up for approval by the contract panel. The six bids were $990,041.00, $1,052,000.00, $1,084,798.00, $1,094,320.00, $1,149,876.00 and $1,387,682.00.

The official summary names a figure of $990,041.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
