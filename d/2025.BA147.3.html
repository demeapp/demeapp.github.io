Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5031633091 to Heritage Restoration Inc. for Masonry and Window Restoration at the Assembly Hall for Economic Development and Culture

AdoptedAug 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,729,150 contract ($1,953,940 including HST; $1,729,150 net of HST recoveries) with Heritage Restoration for fixing the brickwork and windows of the Assembly Hall, is up for approval by the contract panel. The five bids were $1,261,535.00, $1,312,058.00, $1,403,275.00, $1,419,044.00 and $1,513,967.00.

The official summary names a figure of $1,261,535.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
