Award of Doc5092847122 to Turf Care Products Canada Limited for the Non-exclusive Supply of All Labour, Materials, and Equipment Necessary for Toro Products for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedAug 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

An $800,000 deal ($904,000 including HST; $814,080 net of HST recoveries) with Turf Care Products for Toro equipment parts and maintenance, with four optional renewal years, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bid was $554,037.59; the option years are $824,000 / $931,120 / $838,502, $848,720 / $959,054 / $863,657, $874,182 / $987,825 / $889,567 and $900,407 / $1,017,460 / $916,254, for a five-year total of $4,247,309 / $4,799,459 / $4,322,059.
