Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5200162004 to Gio Crete Construction Ltd. / Nelli Construction for Watermain Replacement and State of Good Repair for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedAug 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $4,947,520 contract ($5,590,698 including HST; $5,034,596 net of HST recoveries) with Gio Crete Construction/Nelli Construction for water pipe replacement on Sorauren Avenue and Virtue Street, is up for approval by the contract panel. The five bids were $4,280,558.10, $4,497,745.10, $5,331,000.00, $5,844,000.00 and $8,404,404.00.

The official summary names a figure of $4,280,558.10.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
