Award of Sourcewell Contract Number 020223-CEC to Oaken Holdings Inc. Doing Business as Bobcat of Toronto for Supply and Delivery of Six (6) Medium Duty and Compact Construction Equipment with Related Attachments for Fleet Services

AdoptedAug 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $750,000 deal ($847,500 including HST) with Bobcat of Toronto for six diesel compact construction machines for Fleet Services, under a two-year Sourcewell agreement with an option for one more year, is up for approval by the contract panel. The city could buy 27 more units in 2025-2026 and 22 in 2026-2027, pending budget approval. With the optional purchases, the total potential value is $7,482,343 net of taxes ($8,455,048 including HST); exercising the option year would bring it to $9,734,749 net of taxes ($11,000,266 including HST).

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
