Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4885106821 to N.S.J. Waterworx Group Ltd. for the Installation of New Residential Water and Sewer Service Connections and Associated Work at Various Locations for Toronto Water

AdoptedSep 3, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $3,664,725 contract ($4,141,139 including HST; $3,729,224 net of HST recoveries) with N.S.J. Waterworx for new home water and sewer hookups, to September 2026, with one optional renewal year, is up for approval by the contract panel. The six bids were $3,055,581.00, $3,514,725.00, $3,569,000.25, $4,178,725.00, $4,189,500.00 and $6,290,602.00; the option year is $3,774,667 / $4,265,374 / $3,841,101, for a two-year total of $7,439,392 / $8,406,513 / $7,570,325.

The official summary names a figure of $3,055,581.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
