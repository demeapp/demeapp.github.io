Award of Doc4927701805 to Insituform Technologies Limited for Full Length Trenchless Rehabilitation of Existing Gravity Sewers and Related Works at Various Locations for Toronto Water

AdoptedSep 3, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $14,260,653 contract ($16,114,538 including HST; $14,511,640 net of HST recoveries) with Insituform Technologies for dig-free sewer repairs in central-south Toronto, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $12,099,542 and $13,386,227.

The official summary names a figure of $12,099,542.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
