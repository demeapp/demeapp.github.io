Award of Doc4969068503 to Insituform Technologies Limited for Full Length Trenchless Rehabilitation of Existing Gravity Sewers and Related Works at Various Locations for Toronto Water

AdoptedSep 3, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $9,877,296 contract ($11,161,344 including HST; $10,051,136 net of HST recoveries) with Insituform Technologies for dig-free sewer repairs in central-north Toronto, is up for approval by the contract panel. The bids were $8,988,779 and $9,234,234.

The official summary names a figure of $8,988,779.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
