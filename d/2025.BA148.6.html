Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5144891537 to Mopal Construction Ltd. for Elie Wiesel Park Improvements for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedSep 3, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $1,065,064 contract ($1,203,522 including HST; $1,083,809 net of HST recoveries) with Mopal Construction for a new playground, seating, fitness area, basketball court, drainage and paths at Elie Wiesel Park, is up for approval by the contract panel. The six bids were $879,627.46, $887,553.87, $988,000.00, $988,065.00, $1,369,896.87 and $1,567,353.87.

The official summary names a figure of $879,627.46.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
