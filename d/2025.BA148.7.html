Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5163540070 to Clearway Construction Inc. for the Basement Flooding Protection Program, Phase 4, Assignment 36-18 for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedSep 3, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding Clearway Construction Inc. a $25,468,023 contract ($28,778,866 including HST; $25,916,260 net of HST recoveries) for Phase 4 of the Basement Flooding Protection Program (Assignment 36-18), running through December 31, 2028. The five bids ranged from $22,146,106.95 (Clearway) to $27,997,869.10 (GIP Paving), with Drainstar at $25,556,209.00, Varcon at $25,787,348.51 and Kapp at $27,866,398.70.

The official summary names a figure of $22,146,106.95.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
