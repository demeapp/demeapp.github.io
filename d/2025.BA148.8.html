Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5249504537 to Il Duca Contracting Inc. for Reconstruction and Resurfacing on Roadways for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedSep 3, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding Il Duca Contracting Inc. a $4,068,680 contract ($4,597,608 including HST; $4,140,289 net of HST recoveries) to rebuild Hopewell Avenue, Gibson Street, Clovelly Avenue, Amherst Avenue and Jones Avenue, running through December 31, 2026. The four bids were $3,698,800.00 (Il Duca), $3,971,835.00 (CRCE), $4,160,348.00 (Sanscon) and $4,238,000.00 (Kore Infrastructure Group joint venture).

The official summary names a figure of $3,698,800.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
