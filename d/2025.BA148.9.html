Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5257015875 to D. Crupi & Sons Limited for Major Road Resurfacing and Bikeway Construction for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedSep 3, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding D. Crupi & Sons Limited a $1,257,177 contract ($1,420,610 including HST; $1,279,303 net of HST recoveries) to resurface York Gate Boulevard from Jane Street to Finch Avenue West and build a bikeway, running through December 31, 2025. The six bids ranged from $1,142,888.00 (D. Crupi) to $1,538,176.00 (Pave-Al), with D. Martino at $1,250,000.00, Sanscon at $1,276,435.00, Four Seasons at $1,490,633.00 and CRCE at $1,509,260.00.

The official summary names a figure of $1,142,888.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
