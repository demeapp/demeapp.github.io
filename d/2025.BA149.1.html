Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Document Number 5162400439 to Optiv Canada Inc., for Recorded Future Subscription Services for the Office of the Chief Information Security Officer

AdoptedSep 10, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding Optiv Canada Inc. a one-year $386,254 contract ($436,467 including HST; $393,052 net of HST recoveries) for Recorded Future threat-intelligence services, starting September 1, 2025, with four renewal options. The three bids were $120,000.00 (Empirical, non-compliant), $300,000.00 (Certkor, non-compliant) and $2,134,291.39 (Optiv). Option years are $405,565 / $458,289 / $412,703, $425,844 / $481,203 / $433,338, $447,136 / $505,263 / $455,005 and $469,493 / $530,527 / $477,756, totalling $2,134,292 / $2,411,749 / $2,171,854 over five years.

The official summary names a figure of $120,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
