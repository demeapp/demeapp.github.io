Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Document Number 5042102186 to Bevcon Construction & Paving Ltd. for Water Supply Valve Chamber Road Adjustments for Toronto Water

AdoptedSep 10, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding Bevcon Construction & Paving Ltd. a one-year $407,500 contract ($460,475 including HST; $414,672 net of HST recoveries) to adjust water valve chambers on roads, with four renewal options. The two bids were $393,600.00 (Gio Crete, non-compliant) and $407,500.00 (Bevcon). Option years are $419,725 / $474,289 / $427,112, $432,317 / $488,518 / $439,926, $445,287 / $503,174 / $453,124 and $458,646 / $518,270 / $466,718, totalling $2,163,475 / $2,444,727 / $2,201,552 over five years.

The official summary names a figure of $393,600.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
