Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Document Number 5207452368 to 1460973 Ontario Ltd. Operating as C.P. Systems for Cathodic Protection of Existing Iron Watermain Pipes Using Exclusively Magnesium Anodes for Toronto Water

AdoptedSep 10, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding C.P. Systems a $2,619,278 contract ($2,959,784 including HST; $2,665,377 net of HST recoveries) to protect iron watermains from corrosion using magnesium anodes, running through December 31, 2027. The two bids were $2,387,207.00 (C.P. Systems) and $6,324,849.62 (Global Cathodic Protection).

The official summary names a figure of $2,387,207.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
