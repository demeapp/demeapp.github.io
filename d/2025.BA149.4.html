Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Document Number 5213373859 to 373044 Ontario Limited Operating as Trans Canada Construction for Roof Replacement at the Danforth Child Care Centre for Children's Services

AdoptedSep 10, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding Trans Canada Construction a $620,004 contract ($700,605 including HST; $630,916 net of HST recoveries) to replace the roof at the Danforth Early Learning & Child Care Centre, 1125 Danforth Avenue, starting September 15, 2025. The six bids ranged from $620,004.00 (Trans Canada) to $1,092,850.00 (Joe Pace), with Anacond at $695,948.00, H.N. Construction at $749,073.00, BDA at $848,659.00 and Pegah at $933,669.00 (non-compliant).

The official summary names a figure of $620,004.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
