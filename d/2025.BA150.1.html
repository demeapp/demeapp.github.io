Toronto City Council · Decision

Rescind and Re-Award of Doc4826552842 for Category 2, 3 and 4 to Arjo Canada Inc. and Cornerstone Medical Inc. for Various Lifts for Seniors Services and Long-Term Care

AdoptedSep 17, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Cancelling its July 2, 2025 award of Category 2 of the long-term-care lifts contract to Cornerstone Medical Inc. (the wrong supplier was picked), and re-awarding Categories 2 and 4 to Arjo Canada Inc. and Category 3 to Cornerstone. It goes to the contract panel for a decision. Category 2 figures are $1,676,822 / $1,894,809 / $1,706,334, $1,727,127 / $1,951,654 / $1,757,524 and $1,778,941 / $2,010,203 / $1,810,250, totalling $5,182,890 / $5,856,666 / $5,274,108; Category 3 figures are $121,752 / $137,580 / $123,895, $125,405 / $141,707 / $127,612 and $129,167 / $145,958 / $131,440, totalling $376,323 / $425,245 / $382,947. Also listed are $6,190,389, $5,856,665 and $6,299,340, and the combined total with unchanged Category 1 is $18,627,701 / $21,049,301 / $18,955,548.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
