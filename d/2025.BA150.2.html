Award of Doc5005063142 to LifeLabs LP for the Provision of Mobile Laboratory and Diagnostic Services for Seniors Services and Long Term Care

AdoptedSep 17, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding LifeLabs LP a two-year $1,845,000 contract ($2,084,850 including HST; $1,877,472 net of HST recoveries) for mobile lab services at the city's long-term care homes, December 1, 2025 to November 30, 2027, with three renewal options. LifeLabs was the only bidder. Option years are $950,175 / $1,073,698 / $966,898, $978,680 / $1,105,909 / $995,905 and $1,008,041 / $1,139,086 / $1,025,782, totalling $4,781,896 / $5,403,542 / $4,866,057 over five years.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
