Award of Doc5080269661 to DJ Industrial Sales for Design, Machining and Fabrication of Parts for Toronto Water

AdoptedSep 17, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding DJ Industrial Sales a one-year $495,500 contract ($559,915 including HST; $504,221 net of HST recoveries) to make replacement parts for Toronto Water, running to July 31, 2026, with four renewal options. The two bids were $300,000.00 (Snell, non-compliant) and $495,500.00 (DJ Industrial). Option years are $510,365 / $576,712 / $519,347, $525,676 / $594,014 / $534,928, $541,446 / $611,834 / $550,976 and $557,690 / $630,189 / $567,505, totalling $2,630,677 / $2,972,665 / $2,676,977 over five years.

The official summary names a figure of $300,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
