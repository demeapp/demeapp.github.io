Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5198346953 to Ritestart Limited for the Cooling Tower Replacement, Scarborough Civic Centre at 150 Borough Drive, Toronto for Corporate Real Estate Management

AdoptedSep 17, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding Ritestart Limited an $877,440 contract ($991,507 including HST; $892,883 net of HST recoveries) to replace the cooling tower at the Scarborough Civic Centre, 150 Borough Drive, running through March 31, 2026. The six bids ranged from $ 751,200.00 (Ritestart) to $ 1,114,500.00 (Trans Canada), with Anacond at $ 794,997.00, BDA at $ 870,678.93, Joe Pace at $ 877,700.00 and HN Construction at $ 929,500.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
