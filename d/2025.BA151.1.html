Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc4995777966 to Orin Contractors Corp. for Market Lane Park Improvements for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedSep 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding Orin Contractors Corp. a $4,243,572 contract ($4,795,237 including HST; $4,318,259 net of HST recoveries) for improvements to Market Lane Park and the adjacent street, running through December 31, 2028. The three bids were $3,313,030.00 (Loc-Pave, non-compliant), $3,691,205.57 (South Central, non-compliant) and $4,082,000.00 (Orin).

The official summary names a figure of $3,313,030.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
