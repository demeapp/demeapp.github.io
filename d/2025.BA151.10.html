Award of Doc5251713435 to 416 Vactors Inc. for the Hydraulic Flushing, Cleaning and Closed-Circuit Television Inspection of Service Lateral Drains and Main-Line Sewers for Emergency Work at Various Locations for Toronto Water

AdoptedSep 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding 416 Vactors Inc. a one-year $1,831,133 contract ($2,069,180 including HST; $1,863,361 net of HST recoveries) for emergency sewer flushing, cleaning and camera inspections in 2026, with four renewal options. The four bids were $1,701,133.00 (416 Vactors), $2,377,770.00 (Aqua Tech), $2,517,150.00 (Clean Water Works) and $3,259,074.00 (Clearwater). Option years are $1,886,067 / $2,131,256 / $1,919,262, $1,942,649 / $2,195,193 / $1,976,840, $2,000,928 / $2,261,049 / $2,036,144 and $2,060,956 / $2,328,880 / $2,097,229, totalling $9,721,733 / $10,985,558 / $9,892,836 over five years.
