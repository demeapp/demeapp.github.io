Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5256775968 to Alliance Verdi Civil Inc. for Culvert Rehabilitation Over East Highland Creek for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedSep 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding Alliance Verdi Civil Inc. a $ 2,249,073 contract ($ 2,541,452 including HST; $ 2,288,657 net of HST recoveries) to fix the East Highland Creek culverts under Invergordon Avenue and Milner Avenue, running through December 31, 2028. The six bids ranged from $1,874,227.40 (Alliance Verdi) to $3,597,401.00 (Marbridge), with Bronte at $2,683,285.68, Clearwater at $2,690,700.00, Graham Bros. at $2,984,025.42 and Belor at $3,587,079.58.

The official summary names a figure of $1,874,227.40.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
