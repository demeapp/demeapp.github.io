Award of Doc5264867023 to 416 Vactors Inc. for the Hydraulic Flushing, Cleaning and Closed-Circuit Television Inspection of Service Lateral Drains and Main-Line Sewers for Planned Work at Various Locations for Toronto Water

AdoptedSep 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding 416 Vactors Inc. a one-year $1,163,187 contract ($1,314,401 including HST; $1,183,659 net of HST recoveries) for planned sewer flushing, cleaning and camera inspections in 2026, with four renewal options. The four bids were $1,023,187.00 (416 Vactors), $1,567,130.00 (Aqua Tech), $1,608,500.00 (Pipetek) and $2,305,230.00 (Clearwater). Option years are $1,198,083 / $1,353,834 / $1,219,169, $1,234,025 / $1,394,448 / $1,255,744, $1,271,046 / $1,436,282 / $1,293,416 and $1,309,177 / $1,479,370 / $1,332,219, totalling $6,175,518 / $6,978,335 / $6,284,207 over five years.
