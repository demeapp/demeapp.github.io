Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5276373158 to Alliance Verdi Civil Inc. for Culvert Rehabilitation Within Scarborough for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedSep 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding Alliance Verdi Civil Inc. a $ 4,053,788 contract ($ 4,580,780 including HST; $ 4,125,134 net of HST recoveries) to fix three Scarborough culverts (St. Clair Avenue over Massey Creek, Brimley Road over West Highland Creek, Penworth Road over Massey Creek), running through December 31, 2028. The five bids ranged from $3,378,156.40 (Alliance Verdi) to $4,678,999.66 (Clearwater), with Belor at $3,383,362.80, Bronte at $4,220,853.23 and Graham Bros. at $4,281,640.80.

The official summary names a figure of $3,378,156.40.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
