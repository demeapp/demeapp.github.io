Award of Collaborative Procurement Contract From Kinetic GPO Request for Standing Offers Number 24-10 to Club Car LLC., / Albion Golf Cars, A Division of C.J. McDonald Co. Ltd. for Fourteen (14) Various Utility Vehicles for Various City Divisions

AdoptedSep 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Buying 14 utility vehicles for city divisions through the Kinetic GPO purchasing agreement, from Club Car LLC / Albion Golf Cars (C.J. McDonald Co. Ltd.). It goes to the contract panel for approval. The award is $955,572 / $1,079,796 / $972,390; also listed are $97,844 / $110,563 / $99,566 and totals of $1,300,923 / $1,151,259 / $1,171,521. The Kinetic GPO contract runs February 1, 2025 to January 31, 2028, with two renewal options.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The Bid Award Panel adopted the following: 1. Bid Award Panel grant authority to award the following contract as per the Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 195-6.6. Procurement of same goods and services as a public body: Solicitation Number: Kinetic GPO Request for Standing Offers Number: 24-10 Sourcing Project WS5209370375 Description: Kinetic GPO is a cooperative purchasing organization established for Broader Public Sector and MASH entities across Canada, with the specific purpose of reducing procurement costs by leveraging the purchasing power of group buying and saving our members time by simplifying steps to procure. Kinetic GPO issued the Request for Standing Offers to result in a national contract Request for Standing Offers 24-10 with Club Car for the procurement of Utility, Transportation, Golf Vehicles and Related Accessories, Equipment, Parts and Services.
