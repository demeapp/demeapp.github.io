Award of Doc5005151030 to Rutherford Contracting Ltd. for Blantyre Park Outdoor Pool Improvements for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedSep 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding Rutherford Contracting Ltd. an $11,146,874 contract ($12,595,968 including HST; $11,343,059 net of HST recoveries) to upgrade the Blantyre Park outdoor pool and pool building plus streetscape and bike-share work on Clonmore Drive and Fallingbrook Road. The six bids ranged from $8,888,000.00 (ORIN, non-compliant) and $9,157,465.00 (Joe Pace, non-compliant) to $14,082,962.00 (Canada Construction), with Rutherford at $9,679,754.30, Anacond at $10,522,893.00 and Trans Canada at $10,560,348.75.

The official summary names a figure of $8,888,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
