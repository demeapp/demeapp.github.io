Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5036068314 to ROC Corporation for Mobile Crane Services for Toronto Water and Solid Waste Management Services

AdoptedSep 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding ROC Corporation a one-year $473,088 contract ($534,589 including HST; $481,414 net of HST recoveries) for mobile crane services for Toronto Water and Solid Waste, with four renewal options. The four bids were $235,555.00 (J. Noble, non-compliant), $394,240.00 (ROC), $407,980.00 (B.W. Haggart) and $477,445.50 (Amherst). Option years are $487,281 / $550,627 / $495,857, $501,899 / $567,146 / $510,732, $516,956 / $584,160 / $526,054 and $532,465 / $601,685 / $541,836, totalling $2,511,688 / $2,838,208 / $2,555,894 over five years.

The official summary names a figure of $235,555.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
