Award of Doc5098489666 to WSP Canada Inc. for High Park Movement Strategy - West Road and Colborne Lodge Drive Redesign for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedSep 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding WSP Canada Inc. a $1,100,154 contract ($1,243,174 including HST; $1,119,517 net of HST recoveries) to design the High Park Movement Strategy for West Road and Colborne Lodge Drive, running through December 31, 2032 including a two-year warranty. Five firms bid, with scores from 42.94 to 85.53.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
