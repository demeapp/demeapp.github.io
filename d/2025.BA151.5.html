Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5135370669 to Cummins Canada ULC for Performing Repairs, Maintenance, Overhauls, Diagnostic Repairs and Service Calls on Cummins Engines for Toronto Fleet Services Division, Toronto Fire Services, and Toronto Paramedic Services

AdoptedSep 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding Cummins Canada ULC a one-year $1,945,882 contract ($2,198,847 including HST; $1,980,130 net of HST recoveries) for Cummins engine repairs on city fleet, fire and paramedic vehicles, October 1, 2025 to September 30, 2026, with four renewal options. The four bids were $1,515,744.70 (Ontario Hino, non-compliant), $1,621,568.30 (Cummins), $1,665,037.60 (Mississauga Bus) and $1,890,836.80 (City View). Option years are $2,004,258 / $2,264,812 / $2,039,533, $2,064,386 / $2,332,757 / $2,100,719, $2,126,318 / $2,402,740 / $2,163,741 and $2,190,108 / $2,474,823 / $2,228,654, totalling $10,330,952 / $11,673,976 / $10,512,777 over five years.

The official summary names a figure of $1,515,744.70.
