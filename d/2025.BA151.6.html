Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5150712134 to Drainstar Contracting Ltd. for Toronto Ravine Sewer Replacements on Various Roads and Ravine Areas for Engineering and Construction Services

AdoptedSep 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding Drainstar Contracting Ltd. a $6,824,329 contract ($7,711,492 including HST; $6,944,437 net of HST recoveries) to replace sewers in ravines and on roads in North York and East York, October 15, 2025 to June 15, 2026. The four bids were $5,996,875.00 (Trisan, non-compliant), $6,203,935.04 (Drainstar), $7,626,000.00 (Kore joint venture) and $8,941,763.00 (Comer Group).

The official summary names a figure of $5,996,875.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
