Award of Doc5174088881 to 2324523 ONTARIO INC. for the Supply, Delivery and Warranty of IBM Power Servers, Related Peripherals and Accessories, and Tape Library Systems for Technology Services Division

AdoptedSep 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding FAHM Technology Partners a five-year $6,500,000 contract ($7,345,000 including HST; $6,614,000 net of HST recoveries) for IBM Power servers and tape library systems for Technology Services, running through August 31, 2030. The four bids were $4,196,505.00 (FAHM), $4,673,900.00 (OnX), $5,291,440.00 (Blair) and $6,300,755.00 (M&A IT Tech).

The official summary names a figure of $4,196,505.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
