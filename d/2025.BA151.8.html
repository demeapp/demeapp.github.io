Toronto City Council · Decision

Award of Doc5198107987 to Anacond Contracting Inc., for Toronto Accessibility Upgrades (AODA), Group 3, at 7 Locations for Corporate Real Estate Management

AdoptedSep 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding Anacond Contracting Inc. a $7,732,668 contract ($8,737,914 including HST; $7,868,763 net of HST recoveries) for accessibility upgrades at seven city locations (AODA Group 3), running through December 31, 2027. The six bids ranged from $6,787,223.00 (Anacond) to $11,223,848.00 (BDA), with Pegah at $7,585,420.00, Joe Pace at $9,303,923.00, Ritestart at $9,371,900.00 and MJ Dixon at $9,533,000.00.

The official summary names a figure of $6,787,223.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
