Award of Doc5235769753 to Sanscon Construction Ltd. for Intersection Upgrades and Sidewalk Improvements at Various Streets for Transportation Services

AdoptedSep 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The contract panel decided on awarding Sanscon Construction Ltd. a $2,226,469 contract ($2,515,910 including HST; $2,265,654 net of HST recoveries) for intersection upgrades and new sidewalks at Galloway Road, Power Street and Martin Grove Road, running through December 31, 2025. The four bids were $ 1,956,069.00 (Sanscon), $ 2,203,980.00 (D. Martino), $ 3,043,063.00 (IL Duca) and $ 3,508,008.00 (Aqua Tech).

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
