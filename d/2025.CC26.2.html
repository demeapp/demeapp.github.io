86 Lombard Street - Alterations to a Designated Property and Removal of a Building on a Designated Property under Sections 33 and 34(1)2 of the Ontario Heritage Act

AdoptedFeb 5, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The City Solicitor asks council for private directions on a tribunal case about a proposed 59-storey, 480-unit tower at 86 Lombard Street, including changes to a heritage building. The legal strategy is confidential.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On March 26, 2024, the owner of 86 Lombard Street appealed Council's failure to make a decision within the legislated timelines for Zoning By-law Amendment applications for 86, 100-108 Lombard Street and 103 Richmond Street East to the Ontario Land Tribunal. The amendments to the Zoning By-law proposed a 59-storey (216 metres, including mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building containing 480 dwelling units, 4,321 square metres of office space, and 616 square metres of retail space. The former City Morgue building at 86 Lombard Street is designated under Part IV of the Ontario Heritage Act and proposed to be relocated to the eastern portion of the site. A mid-block connection between Richmond Street East and Lombard Street and a public open space are proposed.
