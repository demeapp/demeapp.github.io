Toronto City Council · Decision

Budget Implementation Including Property Tax Rates, User Fees and Related Matters

Adopted with changesFeb 11, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council sets 2025 property tax rates and user fees, with an average tax-rate increase of 4.37%. The rates would raise a $6.063 billion property tax levy, including an extra $250.8 million from the budget increase and $69.7 million for transit and housing projects. The average home would pay $4,160 in municipal property tax.

A citywide decision. Decided by a recorded vote, 23 in favour and 1 against — every member's position is public.

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend the additional approvals required to give effect to the 2025 Budget and includes the following matters: 2025 Property Tax Rates and Related Matters : City Council must enact a by-law to levy property tax rates that generate the tax revenue set out in the City’s budget. This report recommends the 2025 municipal tax rates, reflected in the 2025 Mayor's Proposed Budget. For 2025, an average budgetary tax rate increase of 4.37 percent is proposed, as detailed in Table 1. This includes a 5.40 percent increase for residential and industrial properties and a 2.70 percent increase for multi-residential and commercial properties. Additionally, this report recommends an incremental tax rate increase for the City Building Fund Levy to support critical capital investments in transit and housing.
