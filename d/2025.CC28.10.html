137-141 Isabella Street - Ontario Land Tribunal Hearing - Request for Directions

AdoptedMar 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The City Solicitor asks council for private directions on a tribunal case about a proposed 69-storey tower with 823 homes at 137-141 Isabella Street. The legal strategy is confidential.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On February 27, 2024, the City received a Zoning By-law Amendment application for 137-141 Isabella Street (the "Site") to permit the construction of a 69-storey residential building containing 823 dwelling units, including 61 replacement rental units. The applicant appealed City Council's neglect or failure to make a decision on its application for the Zoning By-law Amendment (the "Appeal"), to the Ontario Land Tribunal on June 19, 2024. The City Solicitor requires further directions for the upcoming Ontario Land Tribunal hearing scheduled for June 2, 2025. This matter is urgent and cannot be deferred.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 12, 2025) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Appendices A and B to the report (March 12, 2025) from the City Solicitor. 3. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 12, 2025) from the City Solicitor remain confidential at the discretion of the City Solicitor, as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
