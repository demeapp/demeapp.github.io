Update on Electric Ferries Shoreside Infrastructure and Ferry Terminal Improvement Projects

Adopted with changesMar 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council receives for information a notice that an update on electric ferries and the Jack Layton Ferry Terminal will come to the March 26-28, 2025 meeting. The shoreside project has a base budget of $42,535,680 (net of HST recoveries); full financial details will come in a supplementary report. No decision is proposed.

Decided by a recorded vote, 20 in favour and 4 against — every member's position is public.

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to advise that the City Manager will be submitting a report that provides an update on the Electric Ferries Shoreside Infrastructure and Jack Layton Ferry Terminal improvement projects to City Council for its meeting on March 26, 27 and 28, 2025.

What was decided
