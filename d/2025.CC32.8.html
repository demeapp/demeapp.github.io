11 Polson Street - Appeal of Licence Appeal Tribunal Decision

AdoptedJul 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The City Solicitor asks council for directions on whether to appeal a tribunal decision about liquor licence conditions for the Rebel Nightclub and Cabana Pool Bar at 11 Polson Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On June 13, 2025, the Licence Appeal Tribunal issued a decision on the appeals of a Notice of Proposal to add conditions and a Notice of Proposal to remove conditions (heard together) for the liquor licence of Maya Corp for the establishment operating as Rebel Nightclub and Cabana Pool Bar at 11 Polson Street (the "Premises"). The Licence Appeal Tribunal Act allows the parties to a hearing to appeal a Licence Appeal Tribunal decision to the Divisional Court. The City Solicitor requires further directions.

What was decided

City Council on July 23 and 24, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (July 9, 2025) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council authorize the public release of Part 1 of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (July 9, 2025) from the City Solicitor. 3. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (July 9, 2025) from the City Solicitor remain confidential as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege. The confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (July 9, 2025) from the City Solicitor were adopted by City Council and Part 1 is now public as follows: 1.
