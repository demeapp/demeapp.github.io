The City Solicitor asks council for private directions on an appeal hearing about converting office space into a pool and billiards hall at 4466 Sheppard Avenue East. The legal strategy is confidential.

The official record

What the item proposed

On September 27, 2024, the applicant submitted a Minor Variance application for 4466 Sheppard Avenue East (the "Site") seeking variances to the Former City of Scarborough Employment Districts Zoning By-law 24982 in order to permit interior alterations to convert the second-floor office space of the existing two-storey building into a pool and billiards hall. The variances sought to reduce the number of parking spaces, reduce the minimum distance of the pool and billiards hall from a residential zone, and to permit the pool/billiard hall fronting Sheppard Avenue East. The City appealed the Committee of Adjustment's decision to approve the application for the Minor Variance (the "Appeal"), to the Toronto Local Appeal Body on September 30, 2025.
