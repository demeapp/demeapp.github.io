Appointment of Chief Building Official and Executive Director, Toronto Building

AdoptedDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Appointing a new Chief Building Official and Executive Director of Toronto Building, effective January 1, 2026 is on council's agenda. The candidate's name is confidential until they accept.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend to City Council an appointment to the position of Chief Building Official and Executive Director, Toronto Building.

What was decided

City Council on December 16 and 17, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council appoint Kamal Gogna as Chief Building Official and Executive Director, Toronto Building for the City of Toronto, with such appointment to be effective January 1, 2026, subject to written acceptance by the candidate of the terms and conditions of a contract of employment offered by the City. 2. City Council authorize the appropriate City officials to take the necessary action to give effect to City Council's decision, including the introduction in Council of the necessary bills to appoint the approved candidate as the Chief Building Official and Executive Director, Toronto Building under the Building Code Act, 1992. 3.
