Upcoming Changes Impacting Access to Social Infrastructure

Adopted with changesJun 9, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The committee gets a presentation on the plan for upcoming changes affecting access to social infrastructure, including how the committee will be engaged on community communications. No decision is proposed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Presentation by the Deputy Chief of Staff, City Manager's Office on the implementation plan for the upcoming changes impacting access to social infrastructure. The presentation will provide an update on the next steps for the upcoming changes impacting access to social infrastructure, including engaging the Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee on the community communication plan.

What was decided

The Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee: 1. Requested the Confronting Anti-Black Racism Unit, Social Development invite the City Manager’s Office to present an update on the Access to Social Infrastructure by-law implementation by Q4 2025. The presentation should include the following: a. an overview of the implementation plan, including staff resources; b. equity implications and strategies to prevent harm to Indigenous, Black and other equity-deserving communities affected; and c. the monitoring and reporting plans on the use and impact of the by-law.
