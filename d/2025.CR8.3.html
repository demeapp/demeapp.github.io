The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting held November 13 and 14, 2025, City Council appointed the following members to the Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee for a term of office ending on November 14, 2026, or until their mandate is completed, whichever comes first: • Kendall Forde • Nicholas Marcus Thompson; and • Mahkeba Diana Walters These new appointments fill the positions previously held by Efia Tekyi-Annan whose term of office came to an end on April 8, 2025; Shannon Lawrence, whose term ended on June 3, 2025; and Dr. Beverly-Jean Daniel, whose term ended on September 22, 2025.
