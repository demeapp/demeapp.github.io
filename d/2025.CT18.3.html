Grant Application

AdoptedDec 11, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A federal Canada Cultural Spaces Fund grant application for rehearsal halls at the Meridian Arts Centre and Meridian Hall, with matching funds would be authorized by the TO Live board.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek authorization from the Board of Directors of TO Live to submit a grant application to the Government of Canada's Canada Cultural Spaces Fund.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of TO Live: 1. Authorized and directed the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live, to submit a grant application to the Canada Cultural Spaces Fund program, together with all necessary supporting documentation, to support improvements to the rehearsal halls at the Meridian Arts Centre and Meridian Hall. 2. Endorsed TO Live's commitment to provide matching funds equivalent to the contribution requested from the Canada Cultural Spaces Fund.
