The official record

What the item proposed

The City Clerk's Office has received notice that Dr. Hayley Glaholt has resigned from the Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal effective December 31, 2024. Staff from the Public Appointments Secretariat in the City Clerk's Office are working on a process to recruit applicants and help bring a nominated candidate to City Council for their consideration in the coming months.

What was decided

The Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal: 1. Received the (January 7, 2025) letter from the Acting Manager, Public Appointments Secretariat on behalf of the City Clerk.
