The official record

What the item proposed

The Rick Hansen Foundation Accessibility Certification (RHFAC) program recently partnered with the City of Vancouver to build technical capacity, assess, and improve the accessibility of public buildings and spaces. Through this collaboration, several city-owned facilities - including community centres, libraries, and civic buildings - are being rated using the RHFAC methodology, which evaluates sites based on their meaningful access for people with varying disabilities. This partnership has helped Vancouver identify barriers, prioritize upgrades, and promote inclusive design across the city, aligning with its broader goals of becoming a more accessible and equitable community for everyone. What was decided
