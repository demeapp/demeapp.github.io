York-Eglinton Business Improvement Area - Name Change

AdoptedMar 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The York-Eglinton Business Improvement Area would be renamed to the Little Jamaica Business Improvement Area, as voted by its board and endorsed by its members, if council agrees.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend that the name of the York-Eglinton Business Improvement Area (BIA) be changed to Little Jamaica Business Improvement Area. The BIA Board of Management voted to support the renaming process and request the City of Toronto to formally change the name of the BIA. This name change request was also endorsed by the BIA's general membership at its November 12, 2024 Annual General Meeting.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve the renaming of the York-Eglinton Business Improvement Area, as defined by the boundaries described in Attachment 1 to the report (February 10, 2025) from the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, to the Little Jamaica Business Improvement Area. 2. City Council amend the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 19, Business Improvement Areas, as required, to reflect the name change of the York-Eglinton Business Improvement Area to the Little Jamaica Business Improvement Area.
