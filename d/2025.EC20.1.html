Access to Outdoor Ovens in Toronto Parks

AdoptedMay 21, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The booking fee for community use of outdoor ovens in city parks would be removed, making the fee zero, and a revised policy to reduce barriers to their use goes to council for approval.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to City Council's direction ( MM20.11 ) to review the 2011 Outdoor Ovens in City Parks Policy to identify opportunities, if any, and to reduce administrative or financial barriers to the community use of outdoor ovens, while also ensuring public health, safety, and liability considerations continue to be met. Toronto maintains the largest outdoor public oven network in Canada and facilitates public enjoyment of these amenities both through City-delivered programming and opportunities for individuals and groups to book the ovens for their own use.
