Business Licence and Permit Applications Action Plan: Chapter 545, Licensing (2025 to 2029)

AdoptedJul 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council would overhaul business licensing: drop four application requirements, stop licensing seven trades (boats for hire, school bus drivers, transient traders, building cleaners, chimney repairmen, fallout-shelter builders, second-hand-goods collectors), let licensing staff add conditions to licences, and require an email contact from applicants. Removed licence fees ranged from $281.87 to $758.01. A new $239.11 zoning review fee would apply. Changes start January 1, 2026.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

Municipal Licensing and Standards is continuously optimizing the administration of the Division's business licence and permit services to reduce regulatory burden for business operators and trade practitioners while upholding the City's mandates to mitigate potential community nuisances, protect consumers, and support public health and public safety objectives. Examples of business licences issued by Municipal Licensing and Standards include but are not limited to those for eating or drinking establishment operators, building renovators, and motorized refreshment vehicle operators. In addition to business licences, the Division administers business-related permits, including but not limited to permits to display marketing signs and permits to use public-rights-of-way.
