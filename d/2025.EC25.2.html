Establishing a Toronto Community Crisis Service Training Centre to Strengthen Toronto’s Fourth Emergency Service

AdoptedDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council would approve a lease with Family Service Toronto for a crisis-response training centre at 355 Church Street, with a $2.5 million figure attached to the project. It would train the city's non-police mental health crisis team.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto is advancing its commitment to non-police-led mental health crisis response by establishing a dedicated Toronto Community Crisis Service (TCCS) Training Centre. This investment marks a critical step in expanding the City’s emergency response system and advances SafeTO, Toronto’s ten-year community safety plan’s vision to prioritize prevention, equity, and community wellbeing. The establishment of the TCCS Training Centre marks a critical step in embedding TCCS as a permanent and professionalized component of the City’s emergency response system, alongside the Toronto Police Service, Toronto Fire Services, and Toronto Paramedics Services, each of which operates its own dedicated training facility.
