Toronto City Council · Decision

Asphalt and Concrete Repair Services

AdoptedFeb 4, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The board would hire Tony Battista Paving for on-call asphalt and concrete repairs at Exhibition Place: one year plus up to three more, worth up to $250,000 a year.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approving a contract with Tony Battista Paving Inc. ("Battista") for Asphalt and Concrete Repair Services on an as required basis at various locations on Exhibition Place grounds, for a period of one (1) year, with an option to renew for an additional three (3) years. The Request for Quotation was issued on December 9, 2024, through Exhibition Place Purchasing Department via Bonfire Online Procurement System, and prices per linear meter on various type of work were obtained from a number of vendors. The recommendation is to award the contract to the lowest compliant bidder meeting Request for Quotation requirements and specifications within the available budget. This project is both time and weather sensitive and to meet construction timelines, as well as, to leverage window of opportunity available during between scheduled events. What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved a contract with Tony Battista Paving Inc. for asphalt and concrete repair services at various locations on Exhibition Place grounds for a one-year term, with an estimated annual amount of up to $250,000 (excluding HST) based on projected and historical requirements with an option to renew for an additional three (3) years on the terms and conditions set out in the report (January 21, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place and such other terms and conditions required by the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place and the City Solicitor.
