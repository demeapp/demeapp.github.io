Nighttime Economy - Municipally Significant Designation

Adopted with changesFeb 4, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The board would keep calling May-to-September events at Exhibition Place Municipally Significant, so organizers can apply for extra alcohol-serving hours at licensed spots. The report cites a $594 million figure.

The official summary names a figure of $594 million.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to the Board recommendation from report EP9.10 adopted at its meeting of April 25, 2024, where the Board directed staff to provide a letter of support to tenants and event organizers at Exhibition Place for events held between May 1 and September 30, 2024, to be considered as Municipally Significant to apply for a Special Occasion Permit or a temporary extension of premises and/or hours for the sale of alcohol at permanently licensed locations on a trial basis, and that staff report back to the Board with an update on the pilot. Exhibition Place is Canada’s most extensive entertainment venue, hosting more than 1,761 events and attracting more than 5.5 million visitors annually. What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Designated the events held from May to September at Exhibition Place listed in Attachment 1 to the report (January 21, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place as Municipally Significant, in order to apply for a temporary extension of operating hours to sell alcohol at licensed tenanted locations. 2. Requested the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place to report annually to the Board on the Nighttime Economy - Municipally Significant Designation.
