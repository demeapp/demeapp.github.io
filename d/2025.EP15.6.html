Toronto City Council · Decision

Community Partnership Policy Update

Adopted with changesFeb 4, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The board would make the Community Partnership Policy permanent, starting February 2025, to support partnerships and community use of the grounds year-round.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends Board approval to implement Exhibition Place's Community Partnership Policy on a permanent basis detailed in Appendix A. The report will highlight the policy's key successes, including notable partnerships, attendance, community impact, and in-kind support. By formalizing the Community Partnership Policy, Exhibition Place can enhance the partnership and community experience and support its goal of promoting site animation 365 days a year.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the Community Partnership Policy attached as Appendix A to the report (January 21, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place on a permanent basis effective February 4, 2025. 2. Directed the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place to explore further partnerships with local and community groups for the Community Partnership policy and to report back by the end of 2025.
