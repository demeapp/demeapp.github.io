Proposed Collective Agreement with The United Association of Journeymen and Apprentices of the Plumbing and Pipe Fitting Industry of the United States and Canada, Local Union 46

AdoptedApr 28, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The board would approve a new three-year deal (2025-2027) with the plumbers and pipefitters union, Local 46, based on the agreement the union approved in March 2025.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Collective Agreement between the Board of Governors of Exhibition Place and The United Association of Journeymen and Apprentices of the Plumbing and Pipe Fitting Industry of the United States and Canada, Local Union 46, expired on December 31, 2024. This report outlines the terms of a Memorandum of Settlement attached as Confidential Attachment 1 - Appendix A, that was ratified by the United Association of Journeymen and Apprentices of the Plumbing and Pipe Fitting Industry of the United States and Canada, Local Union 46 on March 24, 2025, and pending Board approval, would be the basis of a Collective Agreement with the Board, for a three-year (3) term, from January 1, 2025, expiring on December 31, 2027.
