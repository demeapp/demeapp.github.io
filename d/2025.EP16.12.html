2024 Capital Works Program - Year End Report

AdoptedApr 28, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Staff report the final 2024 building program results: a $49,268,618 budget covering repairs and BMO Field upgrades for the FIFA World Cup. The budget splits into $20,080,000 for city-managed repairs, a $3,791,143 carry-forward, and $25,397,475 for BMO Field improvements. The board just receives it.

The official record

What the item proposed

This year-end report provides an update to the Board on the final progress of the 2024 Capital Works Program State of Good Repair Budget. The 2024 Capital Works Program budget consists of two parts totalling $49,268,618: 1. Exhibition Place Managed Projects which includes the “State-of-Good-Repair” ("SOGR") 2024 program with a budget of $20,080,000 (Table 1 - Appendix A); and the 2023 total cash flow carry forward of $3,791,143 (Table 2 - Appendix A). 2. Non-Exhibition Place Managed Projects which includes $25,397,475 for Service Improvements relating to BMO Field improvements in support of Toronto's hosting of FIFA World Cup 2026. A cash flow carry forward in this report is defined as the combination of unspent mostly committed costs from prior year through signed contracts and agreements, and any surplus from the above SOGR program projects.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Received the report (April 10, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place, for information.
