License Agreement West Annex Team Space

AdoptedApr 28, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The board would approve a nearly 11-year team-space licence with Maple Leaf Sports and Entertainment in the Enercare Centre's West Annex, running August 2024 to June 2035, subject to city approval.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

This report recommends that, subject to obtaining the necessary City approvals, The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place (the "Board") enter into a ten (10) year and eleven (11) month license agreement (the "Licence") with the City of Toronto, as Licensor, and Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment Partnership (the "Licensee") for certain premises (the "Team Space") in the West Annex of the Enercare Centre, commencing August 1, 2024, and ending on June 30, 2035. The License will be coterminous with the sublease between BPC Coliseum Inc. and the Tenant dated June 16, 2005, as amended and extended by a Sublease Extension and Amending Agreement dated July 1, 2025, and consented to by the City and the Board, as head landlord (collectively, the "Sublease").
