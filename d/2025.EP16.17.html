What the item proposed

Announced by the Province of Ontario in 2019, the Ontario Line is one of four priority transit projects being led by Metrolinx in the Greater Toronto Area (GTA). It represents the largest single expansion in Toronto’s subway history, aimed at reducing congestion on existing transit lines and extending service to underserved neighbourhoods across the city. The Ontario Line will add 15.6 kilometres of new subway service to Toronto, improving the speed, convenience, and connectivity of transit across the city. Stretching from Exhibition Place in the west to the future Eglinton Crosstown LRT at Don Mills Road and Eglinton Avenue East, the line will include 15 stations—six of which will serve as interchange stations—and provide more than 40 new connections to GO trains, existing subway lines, streetcars, and bus services.
