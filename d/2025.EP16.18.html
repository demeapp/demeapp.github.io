What the item proposed

The F.G. Gardiner Expressway Strategic Rehabilitation Plan was developed by the City of Toronto in 2014 and sets the framework for lifecycle renewal of the expressway from Highway 427 to the Don Valley Parkway (DVP). Section 1 of the Rehabilitation Project from Jarvis Street to Cherry Street was completed in 2021. Section 2 of the Rehabilitation Plan from Dufferin Street to Strachan Avenue commenced in late 2023 and is under construction. The Ontario-Toronto New Deal, approved on December 13, 2023, included a provincial commitment to upload the Gardiner Expressway and the Don Valley Parkway to the Government of Ontario subject to due diligence by the province, unlocking approximately $1.9 billion over the next decade for investment into priorities such as transit and housing.
