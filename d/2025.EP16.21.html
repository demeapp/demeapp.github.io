Toronto City Council · Decision

Re-open Item EP15.3, Titled: “Nighttime Economy - Municipally Significant Designation"

AdoptedApr 28, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The board would undo its earlier nighttime-economy alcohol decision and send a support letter instead, since the board can only support, not officially designate, events for liquor licensing.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its last meeting on February 4, 2025, the Board of Governors of Exhibition Place made the following decisions: 1. Designated the events held from May to September at Exhibition Place listed in Attachment 1 to the report (January 21, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place as Municipally Significant, in order to apply for a temporary extension of operating hours to sell alcohol at licensed tenanted locations. 2. Requested the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place to report annually to the Board on the Nighttime Economy - Municipally Significant Designation. It has come to my attention that the Board cannot designate an event as municipally significant for liquor licensing purposes. They can only write a letter supporting the event as being designated as municipally significant. By re-opening this item, I am requesting that the Board of Governors of Exhibition…

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Re-opened Item EP15.3 , titled: “Nighttime Economy - Municipally Significant Designation” 2. Deleted Decision 1, and adopted the following instead: “1. Request the Chief Executive Officer to provide a letter of support to tenants and event organizers at Exhibition Place for events held between May 1 and September 30, 2025, such as those specifically listed in Attachment 1 of this Report, to be considered as Municipally Significant for the purpose of applying for a Special Occasion Permit or for a temporary extension of premises and/or hours for the sale of alcohol at permanently licensed locations.”
