Toronto City Council · Decision

2026 - 2028 Rentals Price List for Show Management

AdoptedApr 28, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The board would approve the 2026-2028 price list for show management rentals and services, raised to reflect higher labour and material costs. The detailed prices stay private.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of the Rentals and Services Price List for the years 2026 - 2028 as outlined in Confidential Attachment 1. A comprehensive review and update of the price list is done annually to factor in the increase in labour and material costs to assure a competitive return to the Board on various show services offered. The increases that are being recommended either directly reflect the increase in general labour rates associated with the supply of the service that were included in the collective agreements approved by the Board or increases that are in keeping with the marketplace surveys that were undertaken.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the 2026 - 2028 Show Management Price List for third party Rentals and Services, outlined in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (April 10, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place, effective January 1, 2026. 2. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (April 10, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place remain confidential, as it contains financial information that belongs to the Board of Governors of Exhibition Place and has monetary value or potential monetary value.
