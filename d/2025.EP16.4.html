Exhibition Place Hotel X - Phase 1 - Consent to Change of Control of Tenant

AdoptedApr 28, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The board would approve a change in who controls the Hotel X tenant after a sale of Henry Kallan's stake, and related lease changes, subject to city approval.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Following the completion of a successful Request for Proposal process held in 2007, the City of Toronto and The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place (the "Board"), collectively as Landlord, executed a lease for a 49-year term, with two options to renew, each for a 25-year term (the "Phase 1 Lease"), with Princes Gates Hotel Limited Partnership (the "Phase 1 Tenant") for the development and operation of a new hotel on the Exhibition Place grounds. The first phase of the hotel, located at 111 Princes' Boulevard and known as "Hotel X", opened for operations on March 20, 2018. By letter dated January 15, 2025, the Phase 1 Tenant formally advised the Board and the City, as Landlord, of a pending sale of the interest of Henry Kallan, his affiliates and related parties in the Hotel X property to the party set out in in Confidential Attachment 1 (the "Proposed Transaction"), and to request…

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Subject to obtaining the necessary authorization from City, consented to the change in effective control of the Phase 1 Tenant resulting from the Proposed Transaction on the terms and conditions contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (April 10, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place, and such other terms and conditions as may be satisfactory to the Chief Executive Officer and the City's approving authority, and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor. 2. Subject to obtaining the necessary authorization from City, consented to the Phase 1 Lease amendments on the terms and conditions contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (April 10, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place, and such other and amended terms and conditions as may be satisfactory to the Chief Executive Officer…
