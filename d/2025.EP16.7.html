What the item proposed

Exhibition Place employee recognized by the Board for recent outstanding accomplishment: 1. 2024 United Way City of Toronto Campaign Each year, to build a better city, the City of Toronto Public Service Staff, along with all Agencies, Boards, and Commissions participate in the annual city-wide fundraising program in support of United Way Toronto and York Region campaigns. Exhibition Place raised a grand total of $1,797.92 for the United Way of Toronto Campaign. In 2024 we focused our efforts on Old Technology Sales and the annual Ghost Walk Program. Over the last 20 years Exhibition Place have contributed $100,769.43 to the United Way Toronto and York Region campaigns. 2. 2024 Community Events Program Exhibition Place ran a collection of low-cost community events, where attendees would pay what they can to attend these events during the spring and summer months.
