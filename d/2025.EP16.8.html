Grievance Update and 2024 Year-end Grievance Summary

AdoptedApr 28, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Staff report 2024 grievance and arbitration activity: six grievances across seven union deals, down from 23 in 2023. The board just receives it.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides a summary and analysis of grievance and arbitration activity for 2024, and a comparison to previous years. Exhibition Place has seven (7) collective agreements that govern its operations on the grounds. In 2024, a total of six (6) grievances were filed by all bargaining units on Exhibition Place grounds, in contrast to twenty-three (23) in 2023. There were three (3) arbitration dates held in 2024. LiUNA 506 filed one (1) grievance, which was withdrawn. Carpenters 27 filed one (1) grievance, which was resolved. IATSE 58 filed four (4) grievances, and one (1) was resolved. All but one (1) IATSE grievance originated in a tenanted facility. The outstanding grievances are related to the assignment of work and scope of jurisdiction at tenanted work locations.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Received the report (April 10, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place, for information.
