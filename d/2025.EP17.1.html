What the item proposed

In 2018, Canada, the United States, and Mexico were awarded the 2026 World Cup. The World Cup is the largest single sports event held in the world, rivalling the Summer Olympics as the largest overall. It is held every four years over a period of six weeks. In 2026, the World Cup will be expanded to 48 countries and 104 matches from the current 36 countries, playing 64 games. There will be six matches held at BMO Field. The event is the world's most watched sporting occasion, attracting a global viewership of four billion people throughout the competition and over 200 million for each match. Overseen by the International Federation of Football Association (FIFA), the World Cup provides significant economic and cultural benefits to host cities.
