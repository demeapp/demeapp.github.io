The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with Board of Governors of Exhibition Place Procedure By-law, The Board determines the place, day and hour of its regular Meetings and the schedule of those Meetings shall be publicly available by the first Meeting of the Board in each calendar year. City Council on July 23 and 24, 2025 approved the 2026 schedule of meetings for City Council and its Committees. The schedule of meetings for the Board of Governors of Exhibition Place has been developed to avoid dates, whenever possible, that conflict with the approved City Council schedule; and to avoid days of cultural or religious significance. The following meetings of the Board of Governors of Exhibition Place have been scheduled for 2026: Date: Monday, February 2, 2026 Time: 9:30 a.m. Location: Automotive Building, 105 Princes' Boulevard, Room 204/Video Conference Date: Thursday, April 16, 2026 Time: 9:30 a.m. Location:…
