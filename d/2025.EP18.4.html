Toronto City Council · Decision

Business Travel GSX - Global Security Exchange Conference

AdoptedSep 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The board would approve travel costs of up to $5,100 plus tax for security manager Vasilis Androutsos to attend a security conference in New Orleans in late September 2025.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of travel expenses for Vasilis Androutsos, Security Manager, to attend the Global Security Exchange (GSX) Conference hosted by ASIS International.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Authorized travel expenses for Vasilis Androutsos, Security Manager, to attend the Global Security Exchange (GSX) Conference hosted by ASIS International in New Orleans, Louisiana, USA, from September 26, 2025, to October 1, 2025, at a cost not expected to exceed $5,100 plus HST for flights, accommodation, and incidental expenses.
