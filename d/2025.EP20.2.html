Business Travel for UFI Executive Committee Meetings and UFI Board of Directors Meetings in 2026

AdoptedDec 5, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The board would approve travel costs for General Manager Laura Purdy to attend events industry meetings in 2026 in Vienna, Izmir, France, and Bahrain, as chair of the industry group's North American chapter.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approving business travel expenses for Laura Purdy, General Manager, to attend UFI Executive Committee Meetings and UFI Board of Directors Meetings in 2026. Union des Foires Internationales, (UFI) is the global association of the Exhibition Industry. In November of 2023 Laura was appointed as the inaugural Chair of the newly established North American chapter of UFI. As Chapter Chair, Laura represents the region on both the UFI Board of Directors and the Executive Committee.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Authorized business travel expenses for Laura Purdy, General Manager, to attend the UFI Executive Committee Meetings and UFI Board of Directors Meetings as listed below. The cost for each business travel event is not expected to exceed $8,500 CDN plus HST, for registration, flight, accommodation, and incidental expenses, with the exception being the Global Congress in Bahrain, for which expenses will not exceed $18,000 CDN. a. Global CEO Summit, January 28-30, 2026, taking place in Vienna, Austria b. European Conference, June 3-5, 2026, taking place in Izmir, Türkiye c. Executive Committee meeting, September 2026, taking place in France d. Global Congress, November 2-5, 2026, taking place in Manama, Bahrain
