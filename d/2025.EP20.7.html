The official record

What the item proposed

The three-year Collective Bargaining Agreement between the Board of Governors of Exhibition Place and the Canadian Union of Public Employees, Local 2840 (“CUPE 2840”) expired on December 31, 2024. This report outlines the terms of a Memorandum of Agreement attached as Confidential Attachment 1 - Appendix A, which was ratified by CUPE 2840 members on November 6, 2025. Pending Board approval, it will form the basis of a Collective Agreement with the Board for a three-year (3) term, from January 1, 2025, to December 31, 2027.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the terms and conditions of the Memorandum of Agreement, outlined in Confidential Attachment 1 - Appendix A to the report (November 21, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place, between the Board of Governors of Exhibition Place and the Canadian Union of Public Employees, Local 2840. 2. Authorized staff to make the necessary amendments to pay rates and other issues identified as agreed changes to the collective agreement in the Memorandum of Agreement (Confidential Attachment 1 - Appendix A) and outlined in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (November 21, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place. 3.
