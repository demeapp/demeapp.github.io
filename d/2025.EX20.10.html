The official record

What the item proposed

The Waterfront East Light Rail Transit (WELRT) is a City of Toronto priority transit project. According to growth projections, when fully built, this 3.8-kilometre planned transit line will provide over 50,000 daily trips, bringing higher-order transit to support an estimated 100,000 residents and 50,000 jobs along its alignment. The WELRT will provide improved transit options to the Central Waterfront, Lower Yonge, East Bayfront, Quayside, Keating Precinct, and the Port Lands communities, and is an integral part of the future development on Ookwemin Minising (formerly known as Villiers Island).
