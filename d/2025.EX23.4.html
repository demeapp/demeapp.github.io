The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an update to City Council on a series of recommendations made by the Auditor General (AG) to improve 9-1-1 operations and responses to calls for service by the Toronto Police Service (TPS). The status of these recommendations is subject to formal review and verification by the Auditor General. In July 2022, City Council adopted two reports from the AG, which focused on: 1) Enhancing the efficiency of 9-1-1 operations ( AU13.5 ) 2) Supporting more effective responses to calls for service ( AU13.6 ) In total, the AG posed 51 recommendations across the two reports to improve and modernize the TPS. Of these, 16 recommendations involved the City Manager's Office and other City Divisions, including Toronto Paramedic Services, Toronto Fire Services (TFS), Social Development, Municipal Licensing and Standards (MLS), Customer Experience Division (CXD), and others.
