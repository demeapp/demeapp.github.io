Toronto City Council · Decision

Assessment of City Impacts from Bill 17, Protect Ontario by Building Faster and Smarter Act, 2025 and Provincial Budget

Adopted with changesJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council would ask Ontario to consult staff and the public before writing Bill 17 rules, to allow zoning with conditions, and to drop mandatory development charge increases in 2025 and 2026. The province moved the bill to third reading without committee review or public hearings, and it became law before the public comment period closed. The changes could cost the city about $1.9 billion over 10 years, and council also asks Ontario to make the city whole and reconfigure the Building Faster Fund.

Decided by a recorded vote, 19 in favour and 1 against — every member's position is public.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an assessment of impacts to the City arising from Bill 17, Protect Ontario by Building Faster and Smarter Act, 2025 and the 2025 Ontario Budget. An overview of the 2025 Ontario Budget tabled on May 15, 2025 is included as Attachment 1 to this report. The provincial government introduced Bill 17 on May 12, 2025, and fast tracked it through the legislature, including its referral directly to 3rd Reading without further debate, amendments, public hearings, or standing committee review. The provincial government passed Bill 17 and it received Royal Assent on June 5, 2025 - before the end of the public commenting period on June 11, 2025. Bill 17 is an omnibus bill that amends eight separate statutes and introduces several non-legislative related actions, all of which are intended to speed up the construction of new homes and infrastructure by streamlining development…

What was decided

City Council on June 25 and 26, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Government of Ontario to: a. make the City whole for the financial impacts of Bill 17, including the deferral of residential development charge payments until the time of occupancy, estimated to be $1.9 billion in cash flow over the next 10 years; and b. reconfigure the Building Faster Fund in future years to provide funding upfront to help the City provide development charge waivers or other financial supports to reach the Province’s homebuilding target. 2. City Council request the Government of Ontario to meaningfully consult with City of Toronto staff and the public in advance of drafting Regulations that implement Bill 17. 3. City Council request the Government of Ontario to enact a Regulation to permit the use of zoning with conditions, pursuant to Section 113 of the City of Toronto Act 2006,…
