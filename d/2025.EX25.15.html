The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to City Council’s directive on November 25 and 26, 2020 ( 2020.EX18.6 ), to provide key findings from Phase One of the Data for Equity Strategy (see Attachment 1). It also responds to City Council’s directive on October 9 and 10, 2024 ( 2024.MM22.15 ) to report back, as part of the Data for Equity Strategy in the second quarter of 2025, on how the City can best collect and appropriately use disaggregated workforce data to better understand the experiences of First Nations, Inuit and Métis, Black and equity-deserving staff and enable the delivery of more equitable and transparent human resource services throughout the employee journey.
