RapidTO: Transit Priority on Dufferin Street and Bathurst Street

Adopted with changesJul 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council would set aside bus-and-bike lanes on Dufferin Street between Bloor and King, plus bus-only lanes on other Dufferin and Bathurst sections, to make transit more reliable. The lanes would cost about $8 million, paid from the TTC's capital budget.

Decided by a recorded vote, 20 in favour and 3 against — every member's position is public.

What the item proposed

RapidTO is a joint initiative by the City of Toronto and the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) that aims to improve bus and streetcar travel time reliability for surface transit priority roadways along arterial roads using reserved lanes, intersection signal improvements, and regulatory changes. In February 2024, City Council endorsed, in principle, the RapidTO: Surface Transit Network Plan (RapidTO) as the basis of Transportation Services' planning and programming of surface transit priority projects in Toronto. The report directed staff to initiate four new roadway-specific studies in the near term (2024-2025), one of which was the RapidTO: Dufferin Street project.
