Toronto City Council · Decision

Cuts to Federal and Provincial Support for Housing

AdoptedOct 8, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council would ask the federal government for $107 million to help pay for refugee claimants and asylum seekers in Toronto's shelters, and ask both governments to restore $54 million in housing benefit funding for Toronto. The province cut Toronto's housing benefit to $7.95 million for 2026, from $19.75 million in 2025 and $38 million in 2024.

The official summary names a figure of $7.95 million.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The City of Toronto is facing two funding cuts that threaten our ability to bring people off the streets, out of encampments, and into shelter and housing. On September 18, the Province of Ontario informed the City that our allocation of the Canada-Ontario Housing Benefit (COHB) for 2026 has been reduced to $7.95 million, compared to $19.75 million for 2025 and $38 million for 2024. COHB is the single most effective tool we have for freeing up beds in our shelter system so that more people can come indoors from streets and parks. COHB helps people leave shelters, afford their own housing, become independent, and eventually no longer rely on the government to stay housed. Earlier this year, facing provincial delays and uncertainty, City Council decided to front some of the COHB funding. That was $4.815 million to help 570 households move into housing, therefore freeing up more shelter…

What was decided

City Council on October 8 and 9, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Federal Government to ensure that transitional funding of $107 million is flowed for 2025-2026 to support housing costs for refugee claimants and asylum seekers already in the City’s emergency shelter system. 2. City Council reiterate the request to the Provincial and Federal governments in 2024.MM20.38 to increase Toronto’s allocation through the Canada-Ontario Housing Benefit to $54 million to allow 300 households to continue to secure permanent housing each month.
