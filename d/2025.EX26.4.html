What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide City Council with the Operating Variance for the six months ended June 30, 2025, as well as projections to the year-end, December 31, 2025. This report also requests City Council's approval for amendments to the 2025 Approved Operating Budget that have no impact on the City's Net Budget. What was decided

City Council on October 8 and 9, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve the budget adjustments and any associated complement changes detailed in Appendix D to the report (September 15, 2025) from the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer to amend the 2025 Approved Operating Budget, with no impact on the Net Operating Budget of the City. 2. City Council direct that the Active Benefits Plan for Management/Non-union employees, Accountability Officers and Elected Officials, be amended as outlined in Appendix 1 to motion 1 by Councillor Shelley Carroll, and that the Pre-65 Retiree Benefit Plan for Management/Non-union-employees, Accountability Officers and Elected Officials, be amended, where applicable, for employees who retire on or after November 1, 2025.
