Toronto Water 2025 Capital Budget and 2026-2034 Capital Plan Adjustments

AdoptedOct 8, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council would move money within Toronto Water's budget and 10-year plan: $38.7 million in timing shifts and $7.9 million from under-budget or finished projects to ones needing more. The total budget stays the same.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report requests City Council's authority to amend Toronto Water's 2025 Capital Budget and 2026-2034 Capital Plan by adjusting project cash flows contained within the 10-Year Capital Budget and Plan, respectively, to align forecasted project accelerations and deferrals. Additional reallocations to project cashflows and project costs are requested where project expenditures exceed the current approved cashflows and project costs. These reallocations will allow Toronto Water to continue to deliver projects within its 10-Year Capital Plan. The adjustments will have a zero dollar impact on the 2025 Capital Budget and 2026-2034 Capital Plan and will align the budget and plan with Toronto Water's capital project delivery schedule and program requirements.

What was decided

City Council on October 8 and 9, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the reallocation of cashflows and corresponding funding within Toronto Water’s 2025 Capital Budget and 2026-2034 Capital Plan in the amount of $38.720 million, for acceleration and deferral of projects, as presented in Schedule A (Parts A and B) to the report (September 12, 2025) from the General Manager, Toronto Water, with a zero Budget impact. 2. City Council authorize the reallocation of project costs, cashflows and corresponding funding in Toronto Water's 2025 Capital Budget and 2026-2034 Capital Plan in the amount of $7.870 million from projects that have been awarded under budget or completed to those requiring additional funding in the same amount as presented in Schedule A (Part C) to the report (September 12, 2025) from the General Manager, Toronto Water, with a zero Budget impact.
