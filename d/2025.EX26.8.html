Western Beaches Breakwaters Action Plan Progress Update

AdoptedOct 8, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council would have staff work with the Toronto and Region Conservation Authority on repairs and funding for high-risk breakwaters along the western beaches, from the Humber River to Ontario Place, and report back in spring 2026.

What the item proposed

This report provides a status update on the Western Beaches Breakwaters Action Plan, including identification of immediate repair needs and funding requirements as requested by City Council in June 2025. Since then, staff have advanced Council directions on identifying repair needs and consulting with other levels of government on ownership, rehabilitation funding and coordinated investment. This report is specific to the Western Beaches breakwaters, stretching from the Humber River to Ontario Place. Staff will bring forward a separate report in 2026 on the broader dock walls work required across the Toronto waterfront. In summary, based on a visual condition assessment completed in July and August by Toronto and Region Conservation Authority (TRCA) and its consultants, there are no locations where imminent failure risk is extreme and immediate stabilization is required.
