2026 Budget Allocation for Social Development Plan Implementation

Referred back for more workDec 9, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council would have the Mayor consider setting aside $250,000 to $500,000 per year per neighbourhood in the 2026 budget, so community groups can develop and run Social Development Plans in priority neighbourhoods by 2027.

The official summary names a figure of $250,000. Council sent it back for more work.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
