Toronto City Council · Decision

Towards Implementing a Maximum Indoor Temperature Requirement for Rental Units and Cooling Rooms

Adopted with changesDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council would pass a rule capping apartment indoor temperatures at 26 degrees: buildings with an amenity room but without unit-wide cooling would keep one amenity room at 26 degrees or cooler from June to September, starting 2026.

Decided by a recorded vote, 23 in favour and 1 against — every member's position is public.

The official record

What the item proposed

Extreme heat is Toronto’s most urgent climate threat, and excessive indoor temperatures are of particular concern for tenants in leased residential premises without cooling equipment like air conditioning (A/C) or heat pumps. Unlike heating, cooling is not considered a vital service[1] by the Government of Ontario. This report responds to City Council’s endorsement in December 2024 ( 2024.PH17.5 ) of a maximum indoor temperature standard of 26 Degrees Celsius for leased residential premises and cooling rooms, and direction for staff to report back on implementation and next steps. In 2025, staff conducted comprehensive stakeholder engagement and procured C40 Cities to examine this topic in Toronto and other North American cities.
