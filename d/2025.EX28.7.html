29 Basin Street - Update to Ground Lease Amendments

AdoptedDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council, as owner of the Toronto Port Lands Company, would approve changes to the ground lease for Basin Studios, a film and TV hub at 29 Basin Street, an approximately $300 million project with 12 sound stages.

The official summary names a figure of $300 million.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek City Council's approval to change certain terms previously approved by Council to amend the Ground Lease between Toronto Port Lands Company (TPLC), legally the Toronto Economic Development Corporation, and Basin Media Studios GP Inc. (the "Tenant") related to the Basin Studios project at 29 and 75 Basin Street. This report has been prepared by staff from the City of Toronto and CreateTO. The Basin Studios project is an approximately $300 million, purpose-built, state-of-the-art film, television, and digital media hub in Toronto’s Port Lands. The development encompasses over 485,000 square feet of new purpose-built studio and support space, including 12 new sound stages.
