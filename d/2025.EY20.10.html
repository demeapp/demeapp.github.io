Council would decide whether to allow a clothing donation drop box at 345 Driftwood Avenue: staff refused the permit, so council must choose to uphold the refusal or approve the permit.

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council deny the application for the proposed Clothing Drop Box Location permit at 345 Driftwood Avenue.
