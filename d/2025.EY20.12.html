Refusal of a Sidewalk Cafe Permit Application Located at 2372 Lake Shore Boulevard West, Local Road Flankage

Adopted with changesFeb 19, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The local council would decide whether to allow a sidewalk café at 2372 Lake Shore Boulevard West: staff refused the permit, so council must choose to uphold the refusal or approve the permit.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this staff report is to report on the refusal to issue a permit by Municipal Licensing & Standards in the matter of an application for a Sidewalk Café permit located at 2372 Lake Shore Boulevard West, local road flankage.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Approved the application for the proposed Sidewalk Cafe permit located at 2372 Lake Shore Boulevard West, local road flankage. 2. Required the permit holder to post signs, at least 11x17 inches in dimension, clearly visible from all parts of the sidewalk cafe, that remind patrons to respect their neighbours by keeping outdoor noise to a minimum.
