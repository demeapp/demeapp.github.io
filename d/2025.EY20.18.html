Lake Shore Boulevard West between Royal York Road and Lake Crescent - On-Street Accessible Loading Zone

AdoptedMar 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council would approve an on-street accessible loading zone on the north side of Lake Shore Boulevard West east of Royal York Road, giving a resident clear curbside access to TTC Wheel-Trans service.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Lake Shore Boulevard West, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting approval to designate an on-street accessible loading zone on the north side of Lake Shore Boulevard West, east of Royal York Road. The on-street accessible loading zone will provide unimpeded curbside access to Toronto Transit Commission Wheel-Trans service for a resident of Lake Shore Boulevard West.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council designate an accessible loading zone to operate at all times, on the north side of Lake Shore Boulevard West, between a point 104 metres east of Royal York Road and a point 9 metres further east.
