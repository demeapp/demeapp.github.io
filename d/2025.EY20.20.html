Haynes Avenue and Leitch Avenue - Parking Amendments

AdoptedFeb 19, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The local community council would approve 15-minute short-term parking on Haynes Avenue and Leitch Avenue for nearby shops, while banning stopping elsewhere between The Pond Road and Assiniboine Road to ease congestion.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending short term parking (maximum 15 minutes) be permitted at all times on a portion of Haynes Avenue and Leitch Avenue. In addition, that stopping be prohibited at all times on these two streets, between The Pond Road and Assiniboine Road, outside of the short-term parking areas. The proposed regulations will mitigate concerns with congestion and excessive parking while ensuring the abutting retail units have a designated area for short term parking for patrons.
