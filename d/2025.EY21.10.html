Wild Rose Gardens, Creekbank Road and Blackstone Street - Parking and Traffic Amendments

AdoptedApr 2, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The local community council would set 30 km/h speed limits on Wild Rose Gardens and Creekbank Road, ban heavy vehicles, and add a stop sign for southbound traffic on Wild Rose Gardens at Creekbank Road.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting approval for various parking and traffic regulations, proposed under the Draft Plan of Subdivision Application for the development at 1780 Lawrence Avenue West, to be implemented on Wild Rose Gardens, Creekbank Road and Blackstone Street. Wild Rose Gardens and Creekbank Road have recently been dedicated as public road allowance. Currently there are no traffic or parking regulations on these two streets. The proposed traffic regulations are consistent with City policies and guidelines given the operation of the roadways and surrounding residential nature.
