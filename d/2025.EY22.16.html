Toronto City Council · Decision

8-16 Locust Street - Construction Staging Area

AdoptedApr 30, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The local community council would approve closing the north sidewalk and part of the westbound lane on Locust Street from April 2025 to April 2028 for a 37-storey condo build, sending pedestrians to the south sidewalk.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Pomerleau Inc is constructing a 37-storey residential condominium building at 8-16 Locust Street. The site is located on the north side Locust Street and 62 metres east of Weston Road. Transportation Services is requesting authorization to close the north sidewalk and a portion of the westbound curb lane on Locust Street for a period of 36 months, from April 9, 2025 to April 30, 2028, in order to facilitate construction staging operations. Pedestrian movements on the north side of Locust Street abutting the site will be restricted and pedestrians will be directed to the south side sidewalk of Locust Street. Two-way traffic flow will be maintained on Locust Street at all times.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Authorized the closure of the north sidewalk and a 3.7 metre wide portion of the westbound curb vehicle traffic lane on Locust Street, between a point 62 metres east of Weston Road and a point 70 metres further east, from April 9, 2025 to April 30, 2028. 2. Rescinded the existing 1 hour parking in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on south side of Locust Street, between a point 61 metres northeast of Weston Road and the northeast end of Locust Street from April 9, 2025 to April 30, 2028. 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the south side of Locust Street, between a point 61 metres east of Weston Road and the northeast end of Locust Street from April 9, 2025 to April 30, 2028. 4. Directed the applicant to sweep the construction site and adjacent sidewalks and roadways daily, or more frequently as needed to be cleared of any construction debris…
