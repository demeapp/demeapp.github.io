6 Locust Street - Residential Demolition Application

Adopted with changesJun 4, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council would approve tearing down one half of the semi-detached home at 6 Locust Street, with no replacement permit issued, on condition the owner beautifies the site with clean fill, landscaping, and a construction fence.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that shall be referred to City Council for consideration and final decision in respect of the former City of York By-law 3102-95. In accordance with City of York, Special Demolition Control By-law 3102-95 and the City of York Act 1994 (Number 2), the application for demolition of the existing one half of the semi-detached residential building located on the lands municipally known as 6 Locust Street is being referred to City Council to grant or grant with conditions the demolition application, including any conditions to be attached to the permit, because a building permit has not been issued for a replacement building.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Approved the application to demolish the existing one half of the semi-detached residential building without any conditions.
