Elms-Old Rexdale Neighbourhood Streets Plan

AdoptedJun 4, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The local community council would approve the Elms-Old Rexdale Neighbourhood Streets Plan so it can be put into action, including new all-way stops, speed humps, and no-standing zones to improve road safety in the neighbourhood.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to conclude the Elms-Old Rexdale Neighbourhood Streets Plan (NSP) and enable staff to proceed to the implementation phase. The Elms-Old Rexdale Neighbourhood Streets Plan study encompassed an assessment of existing conditions in the study area, analyses to determine appropriate changes to the streets and a multi-staged engagement process with the public and community organizations. This report summarizes the study findings and recommends road safety and traffic management changes for implementation in the Elms-Old Rexdale neighbourhood.
