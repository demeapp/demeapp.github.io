Sunnylea and The Kingsway Neighbourhoods - Traffic Calming (Speed Humps) and All-Way Stop Control

Adopted with changesJun 4, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council decided whether to install 56 speed humps on nine streets in Sunnylea and The Kingsway, plus an all-way stop, since staff found the streets meet the city's safety criteria. The humps would cost about $4,000 each, or about $224,000 in total.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of speed humps on nine (9) streets to reduce motor vehicle operating speeds and improve roadway safety for everyone, particularly pedestrians, by reducing the severity and likelihood of speed-related collisions. The assessment completed indicates the criteria as set out in the updated Traffic Calming Policy has been satisfied.
