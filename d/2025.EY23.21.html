Poynter Drive and Stapleton Drive - U-Turn Prohibitions and Parking Amendments

AdoptedJun 4, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council decided whether to ban U-turns on Poynter Drive and Stapleton Drive near Westway Junior Public School, and to allow short-term parking on part of Poynter Drive for school drop-offs.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that U-turns be prohibited on Poynter Drive, between Stapleton Drive and a point 178 metres west, and on Stapleton Drive, between Poynter Drive and Fletcher Place. The proposed prohibitions are intended to improve safety for all road users in the vicinity of Westway Junior Public School, located at 25 Poynter Drive. In addition, Transportation Services is recommending that the existing parking prohibition on the south side of Poynter Drive, between a point 137 meters west of Stapleton Drive and a point 77 meters further west, be amended to allow for short-term parking. The proposed short-term parking amendment will provide an additional area to park for parents/guardians accompanying students to/from the school.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Prohibited eastbound and westbound U-turn movements at all times on Poynter Drive, between Stapleton Drive and a point 178 metres west. 2. Prohibited northbound and southbound U-turn movements at all times on Stapleton Drive, between Poynter Drive and Fletcher Place. 3. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Poynter Drive, between Stapleton Drive and a point 214 metres west, to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Poynter Drive, between Stapleton Drive and a point 137 metres west. 4. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 15 minutes, from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Poynter Drive, between a point 137 metres west of Stapleton Drive a point 77 metres further west.
