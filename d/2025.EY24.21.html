Photography Drive, between Black Creek Drive and a Point 310 Metres Northwest - Parking Regulation Amendments

AdoptedJul 3, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council voted on banning stopping at all times on both sides of Photography Drive near Black Creek Drive, following complaints about long-term parked vehicles.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am seeking your support to amend the parking regulations on Photography Drive, from Black Creek Drive to a point 310 metres northwest. My office has received numerous complaints regarding vehicles parked for extended periods along this section of the roadway. Despite repeated enforcement efforts, illegal and prolonged on-street parking continues to be a concern. To address these ongoing issues, I am recommending the implementation of a "No Stopping Anytime" prohibition on both sides of Photography Drive. This change will enhance the flow of two-way traffic, improve safety, and discourage long-term on-street parking violations.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Prohibited stopping at all times on both sides of Photography Drive, between Black Creek Drive and a point 310 metres northwest.
