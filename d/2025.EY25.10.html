Secondary Uses in the Islington Avenue to Royal York Road Hydro One Corridor

AdoptedSep 17, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council gets a progress report on opening a 7.3-hectare hydro corridor between Islington Avenue and Royal York Road for formal neighbourhood recreation use.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The City of Toronto leases approximately 485 hectares (1200 acres) of Hydro One corridors from Infrastructure Ontario for recreation purposes across the city. Under the Master Licence Agreement, as managed by the Parks and Recreation division, these lands support a network of multi-use trails and a variety of recreational uses as well as meadow re-naturalization projects. Parks and Recreation were directed by City Council in December 2024 to explore opportunities to improve a 7.3 ha Hydro One corridor in Ward 3 between Islington Avenue and Royal York Road, which is currently informally used by the neighbourhood, and add these lands to the Master License Agreement. Staff have met with Hydro One to discuss formalizing access for local recreation in this corridor if all hydro infrastructure safety setbacks and maintenance access can be assured.
