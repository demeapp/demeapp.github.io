Homeview Avenue - Introduction of Overnight On-Street Permit Parking

AdoptedSep 17, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council voted on allowing overnight permit parking on Homeview Avenue between Jane Street and Florence Crescent, as residents requested.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that overnight on-street permit parking be introduced on Homeview Avenue from Jane Street to Florence Crescent, 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., daily. Residents requested the implementation of overnight on-street permit parking on Homeview Avenue from Jane Street to Florence Crescent, to accommodate their long-term parking needs.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Authorized parking by permit only to be in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily on the even (north) side of Homeview Avenue from Jane Street to Florence Crescent.
