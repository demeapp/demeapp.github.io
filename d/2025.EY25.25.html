Grovedale Avenue, between Erie Street and Blue Springs Road - Traffic Calming

AdoptedSep 17, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Etobicoke York Community Council decided whether to install seven speed humps on Grovedale Avenue, after residents voted 22 to 4 in favour.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The residents of Grovedale Avenue, between Erie Street and Blue Springs Road, submitted a petition to my office requesting the installation of speed humps. Following this, my office conducted an internal poll, in which residents voted overwhelmingly in favor of the installation, with a result of 22–4. The proposed traffic calming (Speed Humps) measures will improve safety and promote the orderly flow of traffic for the community.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Grovedale Avenue between Blue Springs Road and Erie Street. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a By-law to alter the roadway to install seven speed humps on Grovedale Avenue between Blue Springs Road and Erie Street, generally as shown on Attachment 1, Drawing ATP-SH-120 and Attachment 2, Drawing ATP-SH-121 dated August 2025 and attached to the letter (September 11, 2025) from Councillor Frances Nunziata.
