Assumption of Services, Registered Plan 66M-2503, 3035 Weston Road, Damaris Development Incorporated

AdoptedNov 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is procedural: City Council voted on taking over the municipal services in the 3035 Weston Road subdivision built by Damaris Development.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends City assumption of the municipal services installed under the terms of the Subdivision Agreement, dated August 17, 2011, between Damaris Development Incorporated and the City of Toronto relating to registered Plan of Subdivision 66M-2503. The Subdivision Agreement was subsequently amended October 3, 2014.

What was decided

City Council on November 12 and 13, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council assume the municipal services relating to Plan of Subdivision 66M-2503. 2. City Council authorize the Director, Engineering Review, Development Review, to release the performance guarantee held with respect to the municipal services in accordance with the Subdivision Agreement, dated August 17, 2011, between Damaris Development Incorporated and the City of Toronto. 3. City Council authorize and direct the City Solicitor to prepare an assumption By-law to assume the public highway within Plan of Subdivision 66M-2503 and to bring it forward for enactment. 4. City Council authorize the appropriate City Officials to take the necessary action to give effect to City Council's decision. 5.
