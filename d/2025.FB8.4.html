The official record

What the item proposed

The Terms of Reference for the Film, Television and Digital Media Advisory Board states that the Board will hold up to four meetings per year at the call of the Chair or Vice-Chair on delegation of the Chair. City Council on July 23 and 24, 2025, approved the 2026 schedule of meetings for City Council and its Committees. The schedule of meetings for Film, Television and Digital Media Advisory Board has been developed to avoid dates, whenever possible, that conflict with the approved City Council schedule; and to avoid days of cultural or religious significance. The Chair has scheduled the following meetings for 2026: Date: Tuesday February 3, 2026 Time: 9:30 a.m. Location: Committee Room 1, City Hall/Video Conference Date: Monday June 29, 2026 Time: 9:30 a.m. Location: Committee Room 1, City Hall/Video Conference

What was decided

The Film, Television and Digital Media Advisory Board received the item for information.
