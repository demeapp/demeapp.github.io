Potential Litigation Arising from the Construction of Certain Sanitary Sewer Upgrades in the Vicinity of 1215 York Mills Road

AdoptedMar 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City Council voted on confidential legal recommendations about potential litigation over sanitary sewer upgrade costs near 1215 York Mills Road.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report concerns potential litigation arising from the costs associated with the construction by Alit Don Valley Property Inc. ("Alit"), of certain planned upgrades to the City's sanitary sewers in the vicinity of its development at 1215 York Mills Road. Confidential Attachment 1 contains legal advice from the City Solicitor regarding the potential litigation and seeks instructions.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (February 10, 2025) from the City Solicitor and the General Manager, Toronto Water. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (February 10, 2025) from the City Solicitor and the General Manager, Toronto Water, at the discretion of the City Solicitor. 3. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (February 10, 2025) from the City Solicitor and the General Manager, Toronto Water remain confidential in its entirety, as it relates to potential litigation involving the City and contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
