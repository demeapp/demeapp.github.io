Amendment to Purchase Order Number 6043796 with WSP Canada Inc. and Purchase Order Number 6052068 with Maple Reinders Constructors Limited for Rehabilitation and Upgrades of Seven Group Six Sewage Pumping Stations at Various Locations

AdoptedFeb 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The General Government Committee voted on two sewage pumping station amendments: WSP Canada up $1,258,123 ($1,280,266 net of HST recoveries) from $1,664,968 ($1,694,271 net of HST recoveries) to $2,923,091 ($2,974,537 net of HST recoveries), and Maple Reinders up $1,100,000 ($1,119,360 net of HST recoveries) from $26,546,800 ($27,014,024 net of HST recoveries) to $27,646,800 ($28,133,384 net of HST recoveries).

The official summary names a figure of $1,258,123.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

This report is seeking authority to amend Purchase Order Number 6043796 issued to WSP Canada Inc., and Purchase Order Number 6052068 issued to Maple Reinders Constructors Limited for work awarded through Request for Tender Doc2666227009 for the Rehabilitation and Upgrade of Seven (7) Group 6 Sewage Pumping Station across Toronto.
