Amendment to Purchase Order Number 6047783 with Hatch Corporation and Purchase Order Number 6053327 with W.S. Nicholls Construction Inc., for the Rehabilitation and Upgrades at Four Water Pumping Stations

AdoptedApr 7, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The General Government Committee voted on two water pumping station amendments: Hatch up $1,458,000 ($1,483,661 net of HST recoveries) from $2,622,539 ($2,668,695 net of HST recoveries) to $4,080,539 ($4,152,356 net of HST recoveries), and W.S. Nicholls up $5,300,000 ($5,393,280 net of HST recoveries) from $16,080,829 ($16,363,852 net of HST recoveries) to $21,380,829 ($21,757,132 net of HST recoveries).

The official summary names a figure of $1,458,000.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

This report is seeking authority to amend Purchase Order Number 6047783 issued to Hatch Corporation (Hatch), and Purchase Order Number 6053327 issued to W.S. Nicholls Construction Inc. (WSN) for work awarded through Request for Tender Doc 3134470485 for the Rehabilitation and Upgrades at Four (4) Water Pumping Stations for Toronto Water. The amendment for Hatch is required to provide additional engineering services related to construction administration of two separate construction contracts over a longer period of time compared with the original scope. The total value of the requested amendment for Purchase Order Number 6047783 is $1,458,000 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($1,483,661 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries).
