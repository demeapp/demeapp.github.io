What the item proposed

The Customer Experience Division is committed to the continuous improvement of the 311 online service experience, as well as the introduction of new features and capabilities, where feasible, that support efficient service delivery and enhance the customer experience. Improvements are informed by ongoing feedback from stakeholders, including the public and elected officials, as well as proactive and continuous reviews by City staff. At its meeting in February 2025, a letter was submitted to the General Government Committee, outlining specific, targeted areas of functionality and usability of the City of Toronto's 311 service for review.
