What the item proposed

This report requests City Council authority to amend purchase order number 4701547 (the "Contract") with Bell Canada, a Canadian supplier. The amendment will add five (5) years to the contract duration and update the total ten year cost. The City is intentionally pursuing a contract amendment five years prior to expiry to strategically leverage favorable terms and rates, accelerate and complete planned technology modernization, and reduce ongoing maintenance costs through the use of industry-standard solutions. The City is unable to achieve these benefits under the existing Contract. The Contract supports the operation of the City's telecommunication and networking systems, including network cabling installation and completion of state of good repair projects, and advances the City's Integrated Telecommunications Infrastructure modernization strategy.
