Below Market Sublease / Lease Agreements with Various Child Care Services’ Operators - 15 York Garden Way, 135 Kyle Lowry Road, and 8450 Sheppard Avenue East

AdoptedJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City Council voted on ten-year nominal leases with three child care operators for new centres at 15 York Garden Way, 135 Kyle Lowry Road and 8450 Sheppard Avenue East.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks authority to enter into 10-year nominal sublease / lease agreements (collectively the “Agreements”) with the following child care operators: (1) Canadian Mothercraft Society as subtenant at 15 York Garden Way; (2) Child Development Institute as subtenant at 135 Kyle Lowry Road; and (3) Red Apple Day Care (Overlea) as tenant at 8450 Sheppard Avenue East, for the purpose of delivering child care services at newly constructed child-care centres. The child care centres at 15 York Garden Way and 135 Kyle Lowry Road are located in condominium buildings while the child care centre at 8450 Sheppard Avenue East is located in the North East Scarborough Community Recreation Centre.
