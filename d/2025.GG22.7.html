Toronto City Council · Decision

Amendment to Purchase Order Number 6053180 with Frank Pellegrino General Contracting Limited for the State of Good Repair Upgrades at the Ted Reeve Community Arena and Artificial Ice Rink Inflatable Dome for the Parks and Recreation

AdoptedJun 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The General Government Committee voted on adding $223,810 ($227,750 net of HST recoveries) to the Ted Reeve Arena upgrade order, raising it from $8,110,445 ($8,253,189 net of HST recoveries) to $8,334,255 ($8,480,938 net of HST recoveries).

The official summary names a figure of $223,810.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Purchase Order number 6053180 issued to Frank Pellegrino Contracting Limited, under the Request for Tender Doc2877856212 (Contract Number 20-PFR-067) for the State of Good Repair upgrades at the Ted Reeve Arena and Artificial Ice Rink inflatable dome by an additional $223,810 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($227,750 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries), revising the current Purchase Order value from $8,110,445 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($8,253,189 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) to $8,334,255 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($8,480,938 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) and extending the delivery date from May 31, 2024 to September 30, 2025 to complete the required work. This Purchase Order Amendment is necessary for additional work related to the original scope to complete…

What was decided

The General Government Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Financial Control By-law), authorized the General Manager, Parks and Recreation to amend Purchase Order Number 6053180 with Frank Pellegrino General Contracting Limited for the State of Good Repair upgrades at the Ted Reeve Arena and Artificial Ice Rink Inflatable Dome by an additional $223,810 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($227,750 net of Harmonized Sales Tax Recoveries), revising the current Purchase Order value from $8,110,445 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($8,253,189 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) to $8,334,255 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($8,480,938 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) and extending the delivery date from May 31, 2024 to September 30, 2025 to complete the required work.
