What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to enter into a non-competitive contract with Nederman Canada Limited for the supply of Nederman Manufactured Parts and Maintenance and Repair Services for the City of Toronto’s Corporate Real Estate Management Division in the amount of $636,000 net of Harmonized Sales Tax ($647,194 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) for an initial period of three years, commencing on August 1, 2025 to July 31, 2028, with two separate single year option terms. A non-competitive procurement is required, as the Nederman Magna System utilizes proprietary technology for vehicle exhaust extraction and is available exclusively from Nederman Canada Limited, the sole manufacturer and seller of the required parts.
