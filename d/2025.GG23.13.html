The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend blanket contract 47024834, issued to Graham Alarm Ltd, for the non-exclusive supply of all labour, material, supervision, and any associated equipment required by the CAN/ULC-S561 standard, covering the installation and services for fire signal receiving centres and systems. This amendment is necessary to bring the contract into financial controls compliance and proceed with its closure. Over the past several years, Corporate Real Estate Management has successfully standardized and consolidated all fire monitoring for all City of Toronto Divisions. The centralization initiative has progressed ahead of schedule and has consumed the full fire alarm monitoring contract value prematurely.
