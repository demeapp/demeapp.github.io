Toronto City Council · Decision

Amendment to Purchase Order Number 6053584 to Crestline Coach Ltd., for the Provision of a Ministry of Health Certified Multi-Patient Ambulance for Toronto Paramedic Services

AdoptedOct 8, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City Council voted on adding $2,406,813 ($2,449,173 net of HST recoveries) to the Crestline multi-patient ambulance order, raising it from $685,105 ($697,163 net of HST recoveries) to $3,091,918 ($3,146,336 net of HST recoveries, $3,146,335 also listed).

The official summary names a figure of $2,406,813.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek authority to amend and extend the Non-Competitive Purchase Order Number 6053584, awarded to Crestline Coach Ltd., for the provision of Ministry of Health certified Multi-Patient Ambulances. The total value of the requested Purchase Order amendment is $2,406,813 net of all taxes and charges ($2,449,173 net of Harmonized Sales Tax Recoveries), revising the current Purchase Order value from $685,105 net of all taxes and charges ($697,163 net of Harmonized Sales Tax Recoveries) to $3,091,918 net of all taxes and charges ($3,146,336 net of Harmonized Sales Tax Recoveries). In addition, the expiration date of the Purchase Order will be extended from March 31, 2026 to December 31, 2027. The amendment is requested to accommodate an increase in unit pricing due to a change in chassis requirements, as well as higher costs resulting from project delays,…

What was decided

City Council on October 8 and 9, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council, in accordance with the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 195, Purchasing, Section 7.3(D) and Section 11.1(C), of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71, Financial Control, authorize the Chief, Toronto Paramedic Services, to amend Purchase Order 6053584 with Crestline Coach Ltd., for the provision of two (2) Ministry of Health certified Multi-Patient Ambulances for Toronto Paramedic Services, increasing the value of the Purchase Order by a value of $2,406,813 net of all taxes and charges ($2,449,173 net of harmonized sales tax recoveries) from $685,105 net of all taxes and charges to $3,091,918 net of all taxes and charges ($3,146,335 net of Harmonized sales tax recoveries). In addition, to recommend to extend the expiry date from March 31, 2026 to December 31, 2027.
