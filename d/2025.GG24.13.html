What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek Council authority to enter into a nominal sublicence agreement (the "Sublicence Agreement") with the sublicensee, FoodShare Toronto, to permit the non-exclusive use of up to approximately 0.874 acres of land within the East Flemingdon Park Hydro Corridor at 150 Grenoble Drive (the “Sublicensed Area”),in the vicinity of Don Mills Road and Eglinton Avenue East, as more particularly outlined in Appendix A of this report. The proposed Sublicence Agreement will have a five-year term, with one option to extend for an additional five years and will allow FoodShare Toronto to continue operating and potentially expand a community garden under the City’s Community Engagement and Entrepreneurial Development Gardens Program.
