Amendment to Purchase Order 6046601 with Graham Construction and Engineering LP and Purchase Order 6046595 with AECOM CANADA ULC, for the Ashbridges Bay Treatment Plant UV Disinfection Facility Construction Project

AdoptedNov 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City Council voted on two Ashbridges Bay UV disinfection amendments: Graham up $9,680,000 ($9,850,368 net of HST recoveries) from $209,363,500 ($213,048,298 net of HST recoveries) to $219,043,500 ($222,898,666 net of HST recoveries), and AECOM up $4,591,755 ($4,672,570 net of HST recoveries) from $16,214,983 ($16,500,367 net of HST recoveries) to $20,806,738 ($21,172,936 net of HST recoveries, $16,500,366 also listed).

The official summary names a figure of $9,680,000.

What the item proposed

This report is seeking authority to amend Purchase Order Number 6046601 issued to Graham Construction and Engineering LP (Graham) and Purchase Order Number 6046595 issued to AECOM CANADA ULC. The Purchase Order 6046601 issued to Graham through Request for Tender 235-2017, Contract Number 17ECS-MI-03AB for construction of the Effluent Disinfection System Upgrades Project at the Ashbridges Bay Treatment Plant. The amendment is needed for completion of the work and for resolution of claims.
