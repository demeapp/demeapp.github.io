The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks Council's authority for the adoption of the necessary By-laws to designate eight properties leased to the City of Toronto for use as shelters as Municipal Capital Facilities, and to provide exemptions for municipal taxes and education taxes. The Municipal Capital Facility Agreements authorized by the by-laws will provide exemptions for the entire property for each of the eight properties, with the exemptions ending concurrently with the expiry of the respective lease or licence.
