Award of Negotiated Request for Proposal Doc 5331229053 to Sure General Contractors Inc., Tribro Group Ltd., and Cosar GC PM Suppliers for the Provision of General Trades and Minor Building Repairs at Various City of Toronto Locations

AdoptedDec 8, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The General Government Committee voted on awarding three general trades repair contracts totalling $61,155,158 ($62,231,488 net of HST recoveries): Sure at $27,304,135 ($27,784,688 net of HST recoveries), Tribro at $23,066,378 ($23,472,346 net of HST recoveries) and Cosar at $10,784,645 ($10,974,455 net of HST recoveries).

The official summary names a figure of $27,304,135.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
