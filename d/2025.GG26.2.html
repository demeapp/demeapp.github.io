Summary of Open Competitive Awards Made by the Chief Procurement Officer from October 1, 2025, to November 14, 2025

AdoptedDec 8, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is procedural: the General Government Committee received a summary of competitive contract awards made October 1 to November 14, 2025 under the new $30 million authority; source documents list $30 million.

The official summary names a figure of $30 million.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Effective October 1, 2025, the Bid Award Panel was eliminated, and the Chief Procurement Officer was authorized to make awards resulting from open competitive solicitations valued up to $30 million and with a term of up to five years, including option periods, or the projected term of capital funding for a project as approved by Council. This authority is set out in Section 8.1(D) of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 195, Procurement. The purpose of this report is to inform the General Government Committee of open competitive solicitations awarded during the period from October 1, 2025, to November 14, 2025, under the authority of the Chief Procurement Officer.

What was decided

The General Government Committee: 1. Received the report (November 24, 2025) from the Chief Procurement Officer for information.
