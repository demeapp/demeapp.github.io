What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to negotiate and enter a non-competitive contract with Grascan Construction Limited / Torbridge Construction Limited for the design and construction of repairs of select F.G. Gardiner Expressway concrete bents at Bay Street and Yonge Street. The estimated value of the non-competitive contract is $4,838,911 net of Harmonized Sales Tax, $4,924,076 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries. The proposed repair work will start immediately upon approval and be completed before the FIFA World Cup begins in June 2026. This project is urgently needed due to the severe deterioration of concrete bents at Bay Street and Yonge Street, which are in critical condition.
