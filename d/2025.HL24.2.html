2025 Student Nutrition Program Service Subsidies

AdoptedMay 21, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City Council voted on 2025 school food funding of $31,020,579: $5,508,378 to the Angel Foundation for Learning and $25,512,201 to the Toronto Foundation for Student Success, with $1,448,575 kept in reserve for new programs.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report makes recommendations for the 2025 Student Nutrition Program service subsidy allocations. The proposed combined allocations to the Angel Foundation for Learning and the Toronto Foundation for Student Success are for the purpose of administering the 2025 approved municipal funding to eligible student nutrition programs across the City of Toronto. These allocations total $31,020,579, which includes $1,448,575 held in reserve for fall applications and appeals, including equipment and start-up supplies for new programs submitting fall applications.

What was decided

City Council on May 21 and 22, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the Acting Medical Officer of Health to enter into agreements and other suitable arrangements, on behalf of the City of Toronto, for the transfer of funds to the Angel Foundation for Learning ($5,508,378) and the Toronto Foundation for Student Success ($25,512,201) for the purpose of administering the 2025 approved municipal funding (totalling $31,020,579) to eligible Toronto student nutrition programs on terms satisfactory to the Acting Medical Officer of Health and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor.
