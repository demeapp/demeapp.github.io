Service Agreements Awarded and Executed by the Medical Officer of Health for 2025

AdoptedMay 21, 2025 · Toronto City Council

For information only. Council gets the list of Toronto Public Health's 2025 contracts with community groups for programs like food security, sexual health, and dental care. No policy change is proposed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Public Health currently has service contracts with partner agencies that support service delivery in the community for the Healthy Babies Healthy Children program, Food Security initiatives, Sexual Health program, Dental Care to Street Involved Youth and Low-Income Adults program, and the Ontario Seniors Dental Care program. Purchasing services from community agencies can be an effective and efficient way to provide essential public health services. This approach builds on existing service infrastructure in the community, facilitates community partnerships, supports continuity of service to clients, and can increase service efficiencies. The purpose of this report is to provide information on the purchase of service contracts awarded and executed by the Acting Medical Officer of Health for 2025, according to the delegation of authority by City Council.

What was decided

City Council on May 21 and 22, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council receive the report (April 17, 2025) from the Acting Medical Officer of Health for information.
