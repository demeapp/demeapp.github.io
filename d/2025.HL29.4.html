Promoting and Delivering School Based Vision Testing in Selected Toronto High Needs Schools - by Councillor James Pasternak, seconded by Councillor Lily Cheng

Received for informationNov 24, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A plan for a pilot mobile eye-testing program in schools, offered on an opt-in basis to school-age children, by early 2026 would be considered by the Board.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on October 8 and 9, 2025, adopted Item MM33.22 and, in so doing, has requested the Board of Health to consider developing a road map to provide a pilot school-based mobile eye testing program on an opt in basis to school-age children to ensure everyone has an equal opportunity to excel in the classroom by the first quarter of 2026.

What was decided

The Board of Health received the Item for information.
