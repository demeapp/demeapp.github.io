What the item proposed

In alignment with HRAC’s mandate to provide advice to City Council, to ensure that the interests and needs of those with living/lived experiences of housing precarity, discrimination and homelessness are reflected in City programs and service delivery, I’m pleased to share the following updates: In September 2024, five HRAC members attended the Mayor’s Renter’s Action Committee Forum of tenants, tenant advocates, and non-profit housing providers to gather feedback on the formation of the committee, including its structure, mandate, priorities, and membership. In January 2025, HRAC members were invited to a consultation on the City’s Rental Replacement Policy, of which five HRAC members attended.
