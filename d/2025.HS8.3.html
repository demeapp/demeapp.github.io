Centralized Access System for Affordable Rental and Rent-Geared-to-Income Homes

Received for informationNov 19, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City staff present on the single system for applying to affordable rental and rent-geared-to-income homes, which adds affordable rentals in 2026. No new rules are proposed.

Received for information — Council took no action on it.

The official record

What the item proposed

In response to Item HS7.8 , Yolisa de Jager, Manager, Access to Housing and Sarah Blackstock, Director, Tenants Services and Support, Housing Secretariat, will give a presentation on centralized access system for affordable rental and Rent-Geared-to-Income (RGI) homes. The presentation will provide an overview of the City’s centralized access system for RGI homes, which will also include affordable rental homes in 2026; an explanation of the differences between RGI and affordable rental homes; an overview of how human rights principles are informing the design, implementation and plans for continuous improvement of the access system and responses to questions previously posed by the Housing Rights Advisory Committee.

What was decided

The Housing Rights Advisory Committee received the item for information.
