The official record

What the item proposed

I would like to recommend that the Housing Rights Advisory Committee (HRAC) endorse the recommendations of the Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee in Item CR7.3 - Addressing Evictions through Disaggregated Race-Based Data in Social Housing , which were adopted by the Economic and Community Development Committee at its meeting on October 28, 2025 ( Item EC24.6 ). Disaggregated data collection is a central accountability and monitoring mechanism which can be used to assess progress in the fulfillment of the right to adequate housing and identify potential systemic barriers to housing. Equity-based data collection tools for vulnerable tenants in social housing would support HRAC’s fulfillment of its mandate by ensuring that the needs of those with living/lived experiences of housing precarity, discrimination, and homelessness are reflected in City programs and service…

What was decided

The Housing Rights Advisory Committee: 1. Endorsed the Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee’s recommendations in Item CR7.3 - Addressing Evictions through Disaggregated Race-Based Data in Social Housing.
