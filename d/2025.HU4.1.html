What the item proposed

The Director of Operations and Business Services, Toronto Public Health will give a presentation on Preliminary Toronto Public Health 2026 Operating Budget and 2026-2035 Capital Budget and Plan.

What was decided

The Board of Health - Budget Committee recommends that: 1. The Board of Health request the Province of Ontario to increase funding for Toronto Public Health to cover an annual cost of living increase equivalent to the annual average Ontario Consumer Price Index.
