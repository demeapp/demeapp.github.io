Environmental, Social, and Governance Reporting Update

AdoptedMar 17, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto Investment Board kept four private documents secret and have staff look at more options for checking environmental, social, and governance scores, then report back.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto Investment Board (Board) engaged MSCI Inc. (MSCI) and its subsidiary MSCI ESG Research LLC as its ESG Ratings Service provider. The agreement was in response to City Council's request to the Toronto Investment Board and the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer to monitor and report on the performance of the external investment firms hired by the Board with respect to ESG factors according to the Investment Policy. In addition, the agreement includes the MSCI Carbon Portfolio Analytics reporting package that is part of MSCI's Climate Lab Enterprise, a comprehensive set of climate data and analytics. Carbon Portfolio Analytics reporting from MSCI ESG Research helps investors understand, measure and manage carbon risk at the portfolio, sector and company level. What was decided

The Toronto Investment Board: 1. Directed that the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachment 1, Confidential Attachment 2, Confidential Attachment 3, and Confidential Attachment 4 remain confidential in its entirety, as it contains information that involves the security of property belonging to the City or one of its agencies or corporations. 2. Directed that the Director, Capital Markets investigate and review further Environmental, Social, and Governance ratings solutions and enhancements under the terms of the current contract with Aon Solutions Canada and report back to the Toronto Investment Board at the next meeting with an outline of the additional information to be provided, terms of service, additional costs, and any other relevant details.
