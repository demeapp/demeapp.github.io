The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with Section 25 of the Toronto Investment Board Procedures By-law, regular meetings of the Board are determined by the Board and a schedule of those meetings shall be publicly available by the first Board meeting in each calendar year. The Toronto Investment Board's meetings will be held as hybrid meetings with members participating in person and remotely. On July 23 and 24, 2025, City Council approved the 2026 annual schedule of meetings for City Council and its Committees. The meeting schedule is available on the City of Toronto's website at www.toronto.ca/council.

What was decided

The Toronto Investment Board: 1. Approved the following meeting dates for 2026: Monday, March 23, 2026 – City Hall, Committee Room 2/Video Conference Monday, June 22, 2026 – City Hall, Committee Room 3/Video Conference Monday, September 28, 2026 – City Hall, Committee Room 2/Video Conference Monday, December 14, 2026 – City Hall, Committee Room 2/Video Conference
