The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an Investment Manager analysis per the directive of the Toronto Investment Board ("Board") at their meeting held on June 24, 2025. The Board's investment consultant, Aon, will provide an update and discuss the results of the analysis. The confidential attachment to this report details the analysis.

What was decided

The Toronto Investment Board: 1. Directed that the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to this report (September 8, 2025) from the Director, Capital Markets remain confidential in its entirety, as it contains information that involves the security of property belonging to the City and the Toronto Investment Board. 2. Requested the Director, Capital Markets and the investment consultant (Aon) to report to the March 23, 2026 meeting of the Toronto Investment Board on an asset liability strategy, and a passive versus active investing strategy for the portfolio.
