Investing in Infrastructure

Adopted with changesDec 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Board got a report on whether to invest in infrastructure, like bridges and roads, with a presentation from its consultant Aon. No decision yet.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to further the discussion of the Toronto Investment Board's ("Board") potential investment in the infrastructure asset class as part of the Real Assets category of the asset mix established in the Council adopted Investment Policy (Attachment 1). The investment consultant (Aon) will provide a presentation (Attachment 2) that provides further information in relation to investing in Infrastructure.

What was decided

The Toronto Investment Board: 1. Received the report (November 28, 2025) from the Director, Capital Markets for information. 2. Requested the investment consultant (Aon), in consultation with the Director, Capital Markets, to proceed with a manager search and evaluation for the infrastructure asset class, within the guidelines of the Council-approved Investment Policy, and to report back with recommendations at the March 23, 2026 meeting of the Toronto Investment Board.
