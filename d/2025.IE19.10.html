Toronto City Council · Decision

Options to Fund and Expand the HTO To Go Water Trailer Program

AdoptedMar 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The HTO To Go water trailer program would become a regular part of the City's heat relief, with trailers spread more evenly and a contractor hired to run them, if council agrees. Council would also approve up to $220,000 to buy two stainless steel water trailers with ten taps each.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to City Council's direction to explore options for expanding the HTO To Go Water Trailer Program (the Program) and the City’s network of water bottle filling stations to both align with the City of Toronto's Heat Relief Strategy by improving access to drinking water for vulnerable populations during heat warnings and ensure an equitable distribution of Program resources at events across all Community Council Areas. Toronto Water is expanding the criteria for the Program to ensure a more balanced distribution of water trailers at events across the City's Community Council Areas. Additionally, Toronto Water will collaborate with Toronto Shelter and Support Services Division to support the City's Heat Relief Strategy. City Council on March 26 and 27, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Toronto Water and the General Manager, Toronto Shelter and Support Services to establish and formalize roles, responsibilities, and deployment procedures for the HTO To Go Water Trailer program as part of the City’s Heat Relief Strategy response protocol, with an implementation target of end of the first quarter of 2025. 2. City Council direct the General Manager, Toronto Water to establish and administer a contract with a third-party water trailer service provider to supply water trailer services for deployment at City sites, as selected by Toronto Shelter and Support Services, that meet the operational and logistical requirements for water trailer deployment as part of the City’s Heat Relief Strategy. 3.
