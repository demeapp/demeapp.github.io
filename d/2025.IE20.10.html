Update on Site Selection for Proposed Tennis Canada Donation

AdoptedApr 9, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The committee noted that Centennial Park is the top pick for a Tennis Canada-backed, year-round community tennis centre with up to two million dollars in funding.

The official record

What the item proposed

Tennis Canada has expressed interest in collaborating with the City of Toronto to enhance access to year-round tennis by contributing up to two million dollars towards the installation of community tennis bubble. This report responds to City Council’s direction ( 2024.MM18.21 ) for Parks and Recreation to identify an appropriate site to advance the delivery of an accessible and affordable year-round tennis facility with Tennis Canada. This report identifies Centennial Park as the preferred site and provides an update to Council on next steps.

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. Received the report (March 27, 2025) from the General Manager, Parks and Recreation for information.
