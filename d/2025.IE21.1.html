Delegation of Development Review Related Authorities for Consolidated Linear Infrastructure Environmental Compliance Approvals

AdoptedMay 21, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The development review boss could name staff who can sign environmental forms for the City under the province's new approval process for sewer and stormwater systems, if council agrees.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends the delegation of authority to the Executive Director, Development Review, to designate in writing the appropriate staff authorized to sign, on behalf of the City, any application and notification forms, as may be required under the Ministry of the Environment, Conservation and Parks new Consolidated Linear Infrastructure Environmental Compliance Approval application and notification process for municipal wastewater collection and stormwater management systems, related to development review and third-party projects as provided under the partnership agreement.
