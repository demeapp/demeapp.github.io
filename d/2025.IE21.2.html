Recommended Strategies to Address Prematurely Failing Water Meter Transmission Units

Adopted with changesMay 21, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A plan for replacing the City's broken water meter readers, which come from a single supplier, would take effect if council adopts it. Details are private because talks are ongoing.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide an assessment of the costs and benefits associated with two implementation options for a strategy to replace the City's rapidly failing water Meter Transmission Units. Aclara, a United States-based company, remains the sole supplier of Meter Transmission Units compatible with the City’s existing water metering technology. Aclara Meter Transmission Units are manufactured in Mexico and shipped directly to Canada. Confidential Attachment 1 to this report contains additional information for Council's consideration, along with recommended confidential instructions to staff.
