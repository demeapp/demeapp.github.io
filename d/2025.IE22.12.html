The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on May 9, 2025, the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee considered Item DI11.4 and made a recommendation to the Infrastructure and Environment Committee. Summary from the Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee: The City of Toronto’s inadequate and excruciatingly slow snow removal process following February's major snowfall had a disproportionate impact on people with disabilities and left our streets and sidewalks impassable for far too long. For people with disabilities, this failure is not just an inconvenience - it is a barrier to mobility, independence, and safety. When sidewalks remain covered in snow and ice for days, wheelchair users, seniors with mobility challenges, and those who rely on assistive devices are effectively trapped in their homes.
