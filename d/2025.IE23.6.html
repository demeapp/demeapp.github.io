Cycling Network Plan - 2025 Cycling Infrastructure and Missing Sidewalk Installation - Second Quarter Update

Adopted with changesJul 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Bike lanes would be upgraded to protected cycle tracks on Blue Jays Way, Peter Street, and Davenport Road, with one stretch on Jones Avenue downgraded, and staff could tweak these projects through 2028, if council agrees. The bike lane work would cost $500,000 and the new sidewalks $2.0 million, all from the transportation capital budget.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks City Council authority to upgrade bikeway projects identified in the Cycling Network Plan Near-Term Implementation Program (2025-2027). The Cycling Network Plan and the associated Near-Term Implementation Plan adopted by City Council in June 2024 seeks to build on the existing network of cycling routes to Connect gaps in the current network, Grow the network into new parts of the city, and Renew existing parts of the network to improve safety. Through this report, Transportation Services is seeking authority for bikeway projects that are proposed to be installed or under construction in the near-term (2025-2027), for which design and consultation have been completed.
