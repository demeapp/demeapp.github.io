Weston Loop Master Plan

AdoptedDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A councillor wants Council to adopt the Weston Loop Master Plan, a community vision for 74 acres of connected parkland across five parks along the Humber River in Weston, built over 25 years.

The official record

What the item proposed

Last winter, Mayor Chow joined me to launch a community-led visioning process for the 74 acres of connected parkland spanning five parks along the Humber River in Weston. Using a design charrette for the first time, we initiated the development of what has become the Weston Loop Master Plan. Over several months, an iterative engagement process was undertaken, including focus groups, in-person and one-on-one consultations, the establishment of a local stakeholder advisory group with regular meetings, engagement with treaty rights holders and numerous pop-up engagements at community events. Dedicated digital platforms - such as a project website, Frequently Asked Questions, and online postings - were also used to ensure broad participation and accessibility.
