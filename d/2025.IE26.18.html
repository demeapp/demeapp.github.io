Maintaining Accessibility During Short Duration Work in the Right of Way

AdoptedDec 4, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A councillor wants staff to write guidance for keeping sidewalks clear and accessible around short-term work like tree pruning, especially for people with vision loss, and to deal with unpermitted blockages.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Each day across Toronto there are countless obstructions in the right of way. Whether they are created by construction, sidewalk and boulevard work or tree pruning, these obstructions present a risk for Torontonians with disabilities - in particular, for those with visual impairments who navigate with the use of a cane. Requirements for the demarcation of short duration work in the right of way - such as tree pruning or utilities work - are established through the Ontario Traffic Manual and corresponding City guidelines on construction management. Despite this, I have heard from a number of constituents who have accidentally entered areas where work is actively taken place due to insufficient measures taken on site to alert those with visual impairments to the risk present.
