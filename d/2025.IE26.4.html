The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on June 25 and 26, 2025, City Council requested the General Manager of Transportation Services to develop a pilot for improved parking signage. Transportation Services proposes launching a comprehensive Parking Signage Pilot to address longstanding challenges with clarity, visibility, and consistency in Toronto’s parking regulations. Current signage often meets provincial standards but can create confusion when multiple signs are clustered, leading to interpretation delays and high violation rates. In 2024 alone over 1.8 million parking violations notices were issued for curbside infractions. What was decided

City Council on December 16 and 17, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve a parking signage pilot project of up to eighteen (18) months to evaluate the four (4) signage options, generally as described in the report (November 20, 2025) from the Acting General Manager, Transportation Services and City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services to report back to City Council on the outcome of the pilot project and recommended next steps.
