Cycling Network Plan - 2025 Cycling Infrastructure Fourth Quarter Update

Adopted with changesDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Approval of 20.5 kilometres of new bike lanes and trails at several spots, including Keele Street, Kingston Road, Kipling Avenue, and Albion Road, is before council. No car lanes would be removed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks City Council authority to install new, and upgrade existing, bikeway projects identified in the Cycling Network Plan Near-Term Implementation Program (2025-2027). The Cycling Network Plan, and the associated Near-Term Implementation Plan adopted by City Council in June 2024, seek to build on the existing network of cycling routes to Connect gaps in the current network, Grow the network into new parts of the city, and Renew existing parts of the network to improve safety. Through this report, Transportation Services is seeking authority for bikeway projects that are proposed to be installed or be tendered for construction in the near-term (2025-2027), for which design and consultation have been completed.
