The Long-Term Care Committee picked a Vice Chair from its members.

Official title: Election of Vice Chair

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with Section B3 of the City of Toronto Long-Term Care Committee of Management Governance Structure, the Vice Chair of the Committee is elected from among its Members. At its first meeting or as soon as possible after, or upon vacancy of the office of Vice Chair, the Committee will elect a Vice Chair.

What was decided

The Long-Term Care Committee of Management: 1. Elected Councillor Amber Morley as Vice-Chair of the Long-Term Care Committee of Management for a term of office ending November 14, 2026.
