Toronto City Council · Decision

Approval of Property Tax Exemptions for new Affordable Rental Homes at 253 Markham Road - Building A - by Councillor Paul Ainslie, seconded by Councillor Gord Perks

AdoptedFeb 5, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A councillor wants Council to approve property tax breaks for the affordable rental and rent-controlled homes at 253 Markham Road Building A, built by the Cedars Non-Profit Housing Co-operative. The project would create 49 affordable and 196 rent-controlled homes. The tax break is worth about $10.25 million over 50 years: $7.95 million in city taxes, $2.19 million in education taxes, and $110,000 in the building levy.

The official summary names a figure of $7,946,724.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

The Rental Housing Supply Program was approved by City Council at its meeting of June 26 and 27, 2024, to support the delivery of a range of new rental homes, including rent-geared to income, affordable, rent-controlled, and purpose-built rental homes, and build the capacity of the community housing sector. A Call for Applications for financial incentives under this program opened in November 2024, and an application for the affordable rental and rent-controlled housing development at 253 Markham Road - Building A has been received. This project represents the first phase of a larger redevelopment project. The development is led by The Cedars Non-Profit Housing Co-operative Corporation who will lease the property from Cedars Development Corporation.
