Authorization to Reallocate Section 37 Funds to STEPS for a Mural at Wellesley Station - Councillor Chris Moise, seconded by Councillor Shelley Carroll

AdoptedMar 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A councillor wants Council to move developer community benefits funding for a planned Wellesley Station mural from the Church-Wellesley Village business group to the non-profit STEPS Public Art. The funding totals $80,000.00 in Section 37 community benefits.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Participatory Budgeting is a democratic process that empowers residents to shape their neighbourhoods. Following a process to brainstorm potential neighbourhood improvements, projects were evaluated by City staff for cost and feasibilty. These proposals were presented to the local community for a vote to determine which projects received funding. In 2023, the Wellesley Station Mural Project emerged as the second most popular choice in Participatory Budgeting for the Bay Cloverhill, Church-Wellesley and Upper Jarvis neighbourhoods, garnering 40 percent of the votes. However, further discussion with the private property owner and a shift in the direction of the Church-Wellesley Village Business Improvement Area, the project's leadership will now transition to STEPS Public Art and the Toronto Transit Commission. This motion is necessary to redirect the previously approved funds to STEPS…
