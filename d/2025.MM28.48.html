What the item proposed

Established in 1950, Hollywood Public School has been a part of the landscape of Willowdale for a long time. Over the years, as the surrounding area has developed and population density has increased, the school has expanded to accommodate the growing needs of the community. Today, it serves approximately 375 students, ranging from Junior Kindergarten to Grade 8, with one single childcare room. In response to the evolving needs of the neighborhood, the school has undertaken another expansion to include additional space for essential childcare services, a larger gymnasium to accommodate a growing student body, and seven additional classrooms.
