200 Queens Quay West - Encumbrance and Limiting Distance Agreements - by Deputy Mayor Ausma Malik, seconded by Councillor Gord Perks

AdoptedApr 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A councillor wants Council to let the parkland at 200 Queens Quay West have up to seven underground concrete supports under it, with Parks and Recreation approval and payment to the City.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on July 19, 20, 21 and 22, 2022, City Council adopted Item TE34.35, in connection with a site-specific zoning amendment relating to redevelopment of lands at 200 Queens Quay. The application was approved for a mixed-use development and included an on-site parkland dedication having a minimum area of 488 square metres as contemplated by Section 42 of the Planning Act. The proposed development was approved and site- specific By-law 1012-2022 was enacted by Council which secured the parkland conveyance as a matter in support of the development through Section 37 Agreement requirements. What was decided
