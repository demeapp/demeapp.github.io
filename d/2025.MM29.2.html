Authorization to Release Section 37 Funds for Public Realm and Streetscape Improvements Within the Highland Creek Community Area - by Councillor Paul Ainslie, seconded by Councillor Nick Mantas

AdoptedApr 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A councillor wants Council to add $29,000.00 to the capital budget, paid by developer community benefits, for decorative paving and community signs in the Highland Creek neighbourhood. That includes $24,000 for paving and banners, plus $5,000 also listed in the motion.

The official summary names a figure of $29,000.00.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

This motion seeks approval to release Section 37 funds in the total amount of $29,000.00 to Transportation Services for the purpose of funding various public realm and streetscape improvements including the supply and installation of decorative paving fronting the historic Wesleyan Cemetery and associated Highland Creek Community Association signage. In partnership with the local Councillor and Transportation Services staff, the resident’s association has identified an opportunity to upgrade a section of existing asphalt paving fronting the historic Wesleyan Cemetery to create an appealing public space for the local community. This project is a partnership with the Highland Creek Community Association who are fully funding adjacent historic cemetery fence repairs.
