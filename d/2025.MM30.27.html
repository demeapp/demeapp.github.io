Administrative Penalty Administration During Possible Postal Strike - by Councillor Dianne Saxe, seconded by Councillor Stephen Holyday

AdoptedMay 21, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Drivers would get 60 days instead of 30 to respond to red-light and speed-camera tickets if a postal strike disrupts mail, pending a council vote. The change only applies during a mail disruption.
