What the item proposed

At its meeting of April 6 and 7, 2022, City Council adopted Item 2022.NY29.1, approving an Official Plan Amendment, Zoning By-law Amendment and a Rental Housing Demolition Application to demolish 15 rental dwelling units located at 824 Sheppard Avenue West, which included conditions that must be met prior to issuance of the rental housing demolition permit and residential demolition permit. These pre-conditions included a requirement for the issuance of permits for excavation and shoring for the approved development on the site. The applicant would like to demolish the 15 rental dwelling units before the issuance of Notice of Approval Conditions and excavation and shoring permits for the approved development.
