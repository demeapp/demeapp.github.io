The official record

What the item proposed

Pride Month is an opportunity to celebrate a remarkable milestone: the 50th anniversary of the Cabbagetown Group Softball League. Half a century ago, a group of trailblazers came together to form a baseball league where the gay community could gather in joy, camaraderie, and competition in a well-loved sport. The original constitution of the league spelled out its values to “provide and protect the opportunity for individuals who support the bonds of gay fellowship to play softball in an atmosphere of friendly competition, free of discrimination on the basis of age, race, creed, sex, ability and sexual orientation.” They had wanted to name the league Cabbagetown Gay Softball League, but instead had to choose a name that would allow them to secure field permits at a time when the City of Toronto would not issue a baseball permit to an openly gay league. So, the initials Cabbagetown Group…

What was decided

City Council on June 25 and 26, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council extend warmest congratulations to the Cabbagetown Group Softball League on 50 years of Pride, Perseverance and Play.
