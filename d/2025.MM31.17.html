Speeding Up Streetcars: Traffic Amendments on Adelaide Street, King Street and York Street - by Mayor Olivia Chow, seconded by Deputy Mayor Ausma Malik

Adopted with changesJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Approval of temporary parking and traffic changes on Adelaide, King and York streets starting July 2, 2025, until streetcars return to King and Church is before council. The changes would keep the 504 King moving during construction. The streetcar track contract would also grow by $350,000, to about $3.43 million, to pay for extra paid-duty officers near the King and Church work site.

Decided by a recorded vote, 22 in favour and 1 against — every member's position is public.

What the item proposed

The closure of King and Church for combined Toronto Transit Commission and watermain work is having an impact on commuters and drivers in our downtown. At the last meeting I moved a Member Motion titled “Speeding Up Streetcars: Getting People Moving Faster During King and Church Construction” directing staff to prioritize looking at additional ways to keep people moving during the construction. Currently, the Financial District Business Improvement Area and their stakeholders use loading zones on the south side of Adelaide Street West, from Yonge Street to York Street, between 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., Monday to Friday. Based on the travel time data, streetcar operations are negatively impacted when the loading operations are in effect.
