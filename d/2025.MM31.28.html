Action to Address Speed Racing on Expressways - by Councillor Jon Burnside, seconded by Councillor Shelley Carroll

AdoptedJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A call on Toronto police to consider joint action on late-night speed racing on highways like the Don Valley Parkway goes to council. The parkway would also be added to noise enforcement work.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Recently, Councillor offices along the Don Valley Parkway and Highway 404 corridor have begun to receive a significant increase in complaints about loud overnight vehicle noise. This reckless behaviour has adverse health and safety impacts on communities and requires immediate action. City Council has previously taken action that was effective in stemming the instances of noisy speed racing our expressways. In 2020, City Council adopted MM25.24 - Too Fast, Too Furious: Addressing Street Racing and in 2021, adopted MM34.38 - Too Fast, Too Furious 2 - More Action Against Stunt Driving and Speeding on the Don Valley Parkway . As in the past, collaborative action between police services and the City is the most effective approach. This motion is urgent since action is required in order for the Toronto Police Service Board to consider this item for the summer while the issue is most severe.

What was decided

City Council on June 25 and 26, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Toronto Police Service Board to request the Chief of Police to consider opportunities for collaborative action on speed racing on expressways and nearby streets with the City of Toronto Noise Team and other police services. 2. City Council request the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards to include the Don Valley Parkway in any upcoming enforcement and education initiatives by the City's Noise Team and Toronto Police on the acceptable noise levels for vehicles on Toronto roadways.
