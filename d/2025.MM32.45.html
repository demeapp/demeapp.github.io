Accepting In-Kind Donation from Scarborough Cricket Association for Facility Improvements at Milliken Park - by Councillor Jamaal Myers, seconded by Councillor Mike Colle

AdoptedJul 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Up to $150,000 ($0.150 million) in donated cricket-facility work from the Scarborough Cricket Association for Milliken Park would be accepted, if council agrees. The park could get a cricket pitch and/or batting cages.

The official summary names a figure of $0.150 million. Its recorded vote was unanimous — 22 in favour, none against.

The official record

What the item proposed

The total in-kind donation amount for the project is valued up to $0.150 million. Under City Council’s Donations to the City of Toronto for Community Benefits Policy, Council authority is required to accept donations over $0.050 million. No costs will be incurred by Parks and Recreation for the court refurbishment, and ongoing maintenance will be covered through annual Parks and Recreation operating budget. Reason for urgency: This matter is time sensitive and urgent given ongoing consultations, future design and planning work needed to open such a facility in 2026. Delays in Council approval will jeopardize timelines, donor commitments and other work items supporting the project.

What was decided

City Council on July 23 and 24, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council increase the 2025 Capital Budget for Parks and Recreation by $0.150 million, funded by an in-kind donation from the Scarborough Cricket Association, for the purpose of constructing a cricket pitch and/or batting cages at Milliken Park, in compliance with the Donations to the City of Toronto for Community Benefits Policy. 2. City Council authorize the Interim General Manager, Parks and Recreation to negotiate and execute an in-kind donation agreement with Scarborough Cricket Association to facilitate construction of a cricket pitch and/or batting cages at Milliken Park in 2025-2026, pending consideration of the results of a summer community consultation led by Parks and Recreation, on terms and conditions satisfactory to the General Manager, Parks and Recreation, in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor, and…
