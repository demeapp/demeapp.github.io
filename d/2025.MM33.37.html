The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of November 7, 8 and 9, 2017, City Council adopted Item TE27.10, approving a Rental Housing Demolition Application to demolish 12 rental dwelling units located at 263 to 267 Adelaide Street West, which included conditions that must be met prior to issuance of the rental housing demolition permit and residential demolition permit. These pre-conditions included a requirement for the issuance of permits for excavation and shoring for the approved development on the site. Due to timing of the construction phases for the proposed development, the applicant wishes to demolish the 12 rental dwelling units located at 263 to 267 Adelaide Street West before the issuance of excavation and shoring permits.
