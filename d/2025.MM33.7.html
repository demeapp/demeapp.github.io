Toronto City Council · Decision

Authority to Waive Permit Fees for Winchester Junior and Senior Public School for Use of Winchester Park - by Councillor Chris Moise, seconded by Councillor Rachel Chernos Lin

AdoptedOct 8, 2025 · Toronto City Council

$1,017.75 in park permit fees so Winchester Junior and Senior Public School can use Winchester Park while its schoolyard is under construction would be waived, if council agrees.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Winchester Junior and Senior Public School has requested the use of Winchester Park on a short-term, temporary, basis for additional student outdoor space while the school yard is under construction. Currently, there is no shared use agreement in place between the City and the Toronto District School Board that covers use of Winchester Park. This motion seeks City Council authority to waive the permit fees on a one-time basis to accommodate the outdoor recreation space needed for the duration of the construction.

What was decided

City Council on October 8 and 9, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Parks and Recreation to waive permit fees totaling $1,017.75 (excluding Harmonized Sales Tax), on a one-time basis, for permits taken out by the Toronto District School Board until the end of 2025 for use of Winchester Park during construction at Winchester Junior and Senior Public School.
