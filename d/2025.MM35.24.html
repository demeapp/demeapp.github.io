17 St. Andrew Street - Technical Amendment to By-law 447-2023 - by Councillor Dianne Saxe, seconded by Councillor Josh Matlow

AdoptedDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a technical fix: council was asked to fix small errors in the zoning rules for 17 St. Andrew Street so building work can continue. No new rules here.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting held on February 7 and 8, 2023 City Council adopted Item 2023.TE2.1 which recommended amendments to Zoning By-law 569-2013 to permit a 5-storey mixed-use building. City Council enacted By-law 447-2023 to permit the development. It came to Development Review’s attention that there are minor errors in the implementing site specific zoning by-law that must be corrected for the applicant to continue ongoing construction activities. The corrections reflect the development concept previously considered by City Council, but recent detailed review identified details that should have been originally included in Site Specific Zoning By-law 447-2023.
