Toronto City Council · Decision

369 Cummer Avenue - Request for City Solicitor to Attend at the Toronto Local Appeal Body - by Councillor Lily Cheng, seconded by Councillor James Pasternak

AdoptedDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The city's lawyer would go to the local appeals board to support the committee's refusal of a lot split and rule exceptions at 369 Cummer Avenue, if council agrees. The committee refused the plan in October 2025.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The applicant applied to the Committee of Adjustment requesting consent to sever the subject lands into two (2) residential lots, and submitted two (2) related minor variance applications requesting relief from City-wide Zoning By-law 569-2013 in order to allow the construction of a new dwelling and garden suite on each new lot (Applications B0034/24NY, A0435/24NY and A0436/24NY). Each new lot would contain three (3) dwelling units and require variances to the lot frontage and area. On October 23, 2025, the North York District Panel of the Committee of Adjustment refused the Applications (Decisions in Attachment 1). The applicant has appealed the refusals to the Toronto Local Appeal Body. In its report dated May 5, 2025, City Planning Staff opined that the consent application failed to satisfy the consent criteria under s. 51(24) and that the minor variances do not meet the four tests…

What was decided

City Council on December 16 and 17, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the City Solicitor to seek party status at the Toronto Local Appeal Body, and to attend the Toronto Local Appeal Body hearing, with appropriate City staff, in order to support the Committee of Adjustment’s decision to refuse Applications B0034/24NY; A0435/24NY and A0436/24NY, respecting 369 Cummer Avenue. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to attempt to negotiate a resolution of the Appeal of the decision in Application (Applications B0034/24NY; A0435/24NY and A0436/24NY), respecting 369 Cummer Avenue and to resolve the matter on behalf of the City at the City Solicitor's discretion in consultation with the Ward Councillor and the Director, Community Planning, North York District.
