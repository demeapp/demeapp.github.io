Toronto City Council · Decision

Additional Applications for the Toronto Hydro Board

Adopted with changesMay 29, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: a city panel was asked to consider extra applicants for the Toronto Hydro Board and select four candidates to interview. No new rules here.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on April 29, 2025, the Corporations Nominating Panel deferred the selection of candidates for interview for appointment to the Toronto Hydro Board to its meeting on May 29, 2025. Since that meeting, additional candidates have applied for appointment to the Toronto Hydro Board. These applications are being submitted to the Panel for its consideration. Additionally, this supplementary report includes a skills matrix for all highly qualified candidates who have applied for appointment to the Toronto Hydro Board to fill the vacancy.

What was decided

The Corporations Nominating Panel: 1. Selected the following candidates for interview for appointment to the Toronto Hydro Board: Candidate THY2-496; Candidate THY2-590; Candidate THY2-624; Candidate THY2-686; and Candidate THY2-689. 2. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1a, 1b, and 2 to the report (April 16, 2025) from the City Clerk and Revised Confidential Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (May 13, 2025) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Hydro Board.
