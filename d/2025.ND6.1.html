Appointment of a Public Member to the Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal

Adopted with changesFeb 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: a city panel was asked to recommend one person to council for the Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal, for a term starting March 2025 and ending November 2026. No new rules here.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Nominating Panel - Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal will conduct interviews and recommend one candidate to City Council for appointment to the Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal.

What was decided

The Nominating Panel - Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal recommends that: 1. City Council appoint the following candidate to the Dangerous Dog Review Tribunal for a term of office beginning March 28, 2025, and ending on November 14, 2026, and until a successor is appointed: Sophia Davis
