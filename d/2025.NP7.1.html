Toronto City Council · Decision

Selection of Candidates for Interview for the Property Standards Committee

Adopted with changesJun 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: a city panel was asked to select 16 candidates to interview for the Property Standards Committee. No new rules here.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Nominating Panel - Property Standards Committee will select candidates to be interviewed for appointment to the Property Standards Committee. The Nominating Panel will conduct interviews at its next meeting and will recommend eight candidates to City Council for appointment.

What was decided

The Nominating Panel - Property Standards Committee: 1. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (June 3, 2025) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Property Standards Committee.
