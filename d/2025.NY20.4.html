Green Gates Court (9 Fairmeadow Avenue) Subdivision, North York - Assumption of Services, Registered Plans 66M-2531

AdoptedFeb 5, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: council was asked to take over the roads and city services in the Green Gates Court subdivision. No new rules here.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that the municipal services under the terms of the Subdivision Agreements for Plan 66M-2531 dated June 30, 2015, between Orchard Ridge (Fairmeadow) G.P. Inc., and the City of Toronto be assumed by the City.

What was decided

City Council on February 5, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council assume the services installed within Green Gates Court and that the City formally assume the road within the Registered Plan of Subdivision 66M-2531. 2. City Council authorize the Acting Director, Engineering Review to release the performance guarantee held with respect to the municipal services in accordance with the Subdivision Agreement. 3. City Council direct that an assumption By-law be prepared to assume the public highway and municipal services within the Registered Plan of Subdivision 66M-2531. 4. City Council authorize and direct the City Solicitor to register the assumption By-law in the Land Registry Office at the expense of the Owner. 5.
