Varna Drive - Parking Amendments

AdoptedFeb 19, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Parking rules on Varna Drive to allow one-hour parking from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday would change, if council agrees. Drivers there would be affected.

Ward 8 is named in the record.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that the existing 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday parking prohibition on the west side of Varna Drive, between Ranee Avenue and the north end of the street, be amended to a one-hour maximum parking limit during these times. The proposed change will provide short-term parking for nearby businesses and residents.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on both sides of Varna Drive, between Ranee Avenue and the north end of Varna Drive. 2. Prohibited parking from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Varna Drive, between a point 30.5 metres north of Ranee Avenue and the north end of Varna Drive. 3. Approved a one-hour maximum parking limit in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Varna Drive, between a point 30.5 metres north of Ranee Avenue and the north end of Varna Drive.
