Assigning the Ceremonial Name of “Heroes' Way” to Canterbury Place, North York

AdoptedFeb 19, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Giving Canterbury Place in North York the extra ceremonial name "Heroes' Way" on special signs goes to council. Street names and addresses would not change.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend that the ceremonial name of "Heroes' Way" be assigned to Canterbury Place, North York. A ceremonial street dedication does not change the official street name or address information. It does result in the installation of special signage along the selected street. Economic Development and Culture consulted with Transportation Services during the preparation of this report.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Assigned the ceremonial name "Heroes' Way" to Canterbury Place, North York. 2. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services to erect signage marking "Heroes' Way".
