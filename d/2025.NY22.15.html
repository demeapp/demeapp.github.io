Exbury Road - Traffic Calming (Speed Humps)

AdoptedApr 2, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Six speed humps on Exbury Road between Monclova Road and Troutbrooke Drive would be added, if council agrees. Drivers there would be affected.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of speed humps on Exbury Road, between Monclova Road and Troutbrooke Drive. Staff's assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the updated Traffic Calming Policy has been satisfied. Therefore, speed humps should be installed on Exbury Road.

What was decided

North York Community Council 1. Authorized the installation of speed humps on Exbury Road, between Monclova Road and Troutbrooke Drive. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway for the installation of 6 speed humps on Exbury Road, between Monclova Road and Troutbrooke Drive, generally as shown on Attachment 2, Drawing TC-453 and TC-454 dated January 2025, attached to the report (March 17, 2025), entitled "Traffic Calming (Speed Humps) - Exbury Road", from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services. 3. Subject to approval of Parts 1 and 2 above, reduced the speed limit from 40 kilometres per hour to 30 kilometres per hour on Exbury Road, between Monclova Road and Troutbrooke Drive, in conjunction with the installation of speed humps.
