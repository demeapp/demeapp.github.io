William Sylvester Drive and Shinleaf Terrace - Parking and Traffic Amendments

AdoptedApr 30, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Parking and traffic rules for the new streets William Sylvester Drive and Shinleaf Terrace in the 243-255 Consumers Road area would be set, if council agrees. Drivers there would be affected.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services and Development Review are requesting approval for various parking and traffic regulations, proposed under the Assumption of services report for the development at 243-255 Consumers Road, to be implemented on William Sylvester Drive and Shinleaf Terrace. William Sylvester Drive and Shinleaf Terrace have been dedicated as public road allowances. Currently there are no traffic or parking regulations on these two streets. The proposed traffic regulations are consistent with City policies and guidelines given the operation of the roadways and surrounding commercial / employment nature.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking at all times on both sides of William Sylvester Drive between Shinleaf Terrace and at a point 30.5 metres west of Consumers Road. 2. Prohibited stopping at all times on both sides of William Sylvester Drive between Consumers Road and a point 30.5 metres west. 3. Prohibited parking at all times on both sides of Shinleaf Terrace between William Sylvester Drive and Yorkland Boulevard. 4. Authorized a compulsory stop control for northbound traffic on Shinleaf Terrace at Yorkland Boulevard. 5. Authorized an all-way stop control at Shinleaf Terrace and William Sylvester Drive. 6. Amended the existing traffic control signal at the intersection of Consumer Road and a point 90 metres south of Yorkland Boulevard / Private Access to be in effect at the intersection of Consumers Road and William Sylvester Drive.
