Maniza Road - Parking Restrictions

Referred back for more workJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Parking rules on Maniza Road near St. Norbert Catholic School during school hours, September to June, would be tightened, if council agrees. Drivers there would be affected.

Council sent it back for more work.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents in the area surrounding St. Norbert Catholic School have long had issues with parking. During school pickup and drop-off hours, vehicles parked on the street can cause major disruptions to the flow of traffic, and can cause conflicts between parked vehicles on both sides of the road. Being able to stop for a brief period of time is necessary for parents who drive their kids to school, so no parking is being recommended rather than no stopping.

What was decided

City Council on June 25 and 26, 2025, referred Item NY23.28 to the July 3, 2025, meeting of the North York Community Council for consideration.
