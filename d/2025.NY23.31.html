Maxome Avenue and Bishop Avenue - Report Back on Removal of Turn Prohibitions

AdoptedApr 30, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A councillor asks council to have staff study whether to remove evening rush-hour turn bans at Maxome Avenue and Bishop Avenue. Drivers there would be affected.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The T-intersection at Maxome Avenue and Bishop Avenue requires drivers to make a right turn during the hours of 3:30 pm to 7:00 pm. If residents then need to head northbound on Maxome Avenue, they are making a U-turn or turning into neighbourhood streets to accomplish the same outcome. My office conducted a survey of the neighbouring community and based on the results of the survey, there is support for the removal of the existing turn prohibitions at the subject intersection. I would like to request that a staff report be written examining the existing turn prohibitions at the intersection of Maxome Avenue and Bishop Avenue and whether their removal would allow motorists to better navigate the area.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services report back on the feasiblity and impact of rescinding the eastbound left-turn prohibition in effect from3:30 p.m. to 7:00 p.m., Monday to Friday at the intersection of Bishop Avenue and Maxome Avenue. 2. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services report back on the feasiblilty and impact of rescinding the southbound right-turn prohibition in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., Monday to Friday at the intersection of Maxome Avenue and Bishop Avenue.
