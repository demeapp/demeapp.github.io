1350 Sheppard Avenue West - Zoning Amendment Application - Decision Report - Approval

Adopted with changesJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

At 1350 Sheppard Avenue West, the developer wants to raise the unit count from 1,400 to 1,700 and shrink minimum two- and three-bedroom sizes. City Council is asked to approve the change.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This application proposes to amend site-specific Zoning By-law 1082-2022 that was enacted by Council on July 22, 2022 for the William Baker / Arbo District Phase 1. The proposed amendments to the by-law include an increase of the maximum unit count from 1,400 units to 1,700 units, and a reduction in the minimum size of 2-bedroom and 3-bedroom units, as well as several technical amendments to Schedule A of site specific Zoning By-law 1082-2022. The application does not propose any change to maximum total gross floor area, minimum non-residential gross floor area, parking, bicycle parking, loading, setbacks or height. Zoning By-law 1082-2022 secured housing equal to the greater of 220 units or at least 20 percent of the total residential units would be delivered as affordable units.
