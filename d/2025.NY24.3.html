690-720 Sheppard Avenue East Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment Applications - Appeal Report

AdoptedJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Two mixed-use buildings (32 and 28 storeys, 705 homes) are proposed at 690-720 Sheppard Avenue East. The applicant appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal, and North York Community Council is asked to have the City Solicitor oppose the appeal.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment applications were submitted and deemed complete as of March 8, 2024, to construct 32-storey (100.65 metres) and 28-storey (92.25 metres) mixed-use buildings (705 units) with 8-storey podiums. The proposed total gross floor area is 54,700 square metres (7.28 Floor Space Index), with the residential gross floor area occupying 54,512 square metres and the non-residential gross floor area (4 retail units) occupying 1,221 square metres. The application includes the proposed demolition of 15 rental dwelling units, and a related submitted Rental Housing Demolition application. On February 24, 2025, the applicant appealed the Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment applications to the Ontario Land Tribunal ("OLT") citing Council's failure to make a decision within the timeframes specified in the Planning Act.
