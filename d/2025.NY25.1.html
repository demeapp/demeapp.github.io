369 Soudan Avenue - Application for Fence Exemption

Adopted with changesJul 3, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The owner at 369 Soudan Avenue wants an exemption from the city's fence-height rules. North York Community Council has the final say and is asked to decide whether to grant it.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 369 Soudan Avenue has requested North York Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447-1.2B(1) and Chapter 447-1.2C(1) B. Fence height. 1. No fence described in the following Table shall, when measured at any point along its length from the average grade level measured perpendicular to and one meter away on either side of the fence, exceed the height set out in the Table for the fence. What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Granted the fence exemption, to the property owner of 369 Soudan Avenue, to maintain a maximum height of 1.54 metres and to be exempt from the requirement of open construction for the first 2.4 metres from the front line.
