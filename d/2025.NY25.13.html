30-36 Hendon Avenue - Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment - Decision Report - Refusal

AdoptedJul 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend refusing a 46-storey, 433-home building at 30-36 Hendon Avenue, saying its height and scale do not fit the site. Council is asked to refuse it.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The application proposes to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law to permit a 46-storey (152.55 metres including mechanical penthouse) residential building containing 433 dwelling units at 30-36 Hendon Avenue. The proposed building has a gross floor area of 30,696.3 square metres and a Floor Space Index (“FSI”) of 22.9 times the lot area. The proposed development is of an inappropriate height, scale and massing and represents an overdevelopment of the site. The proposal does not conform to the North York Centre Secondary Plan (“NYCSP”) and the application has not demonstrated adequate justification for the proposed variation from the height and density policies of the NYCSP. What was decided

City Council on July 23 and 24, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council refuse the application for the Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment (Application 24 252430 NNY 18 OZ) in its current form, for the lands municipally known as 30-36 Hendon Avenue. 2. In the event the applications are appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal, City Council, pursuant to subsections 22(8.1) and 34(11.0.0.1) of the Planning Act, use mediation, conciliation or other dispute resolution techniques in an attempt to resolve the Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment applications, to the satisfaction of the Executive Director, Development Review and the City Solicitor. 3.
