Harlandale Avenue - Construction Staging Area Time Extension

AdoptedJul 3, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The TTC needs more time for utility and road work on Harlandale Avenue, between Yonge Street and Beecroft Road. North York Community Council is asked to extend the temporary road closure.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The Toronto Transit Commission has undertaken the construction of a utility duct bank and pavement rehabilitation on the south side of Harlandale Avenue, between Yonge Street and Beecroft Road. At its meeting of December 3, 2024, North York Community Council authorized the continuation of the temporary closure of the south sidewalk, eastbound traffic lane and the on-street parking spaces on both sides of Harlandale Avenue, from December 4, 2024 to May 31, 2025. Toronto Transit Commission has requested a second time extension of the duration of the construction staging area on Harlandale Avenue, as the site has experienced another setback to the construction schedule due to underground site conditions.
