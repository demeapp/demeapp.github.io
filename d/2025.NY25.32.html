Van Horne Avenue and Silas Hill Drive - All-Way Stop Control

AdoptedJul 3, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Stop signs are proposed for Van Horne Avenue and Silas Hill Drive, in front of Divine Mercy Catholic School. Council is asked to approve them after safety concerns raised by the school community.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This motion brings forward a necessary and time-sensitive request to improve safety at the intersection of Van Horne Avenue and Silas Hill Drive, directly in front of Divine Mercy Catholic School. Over the past year, I’ve heard repeated concerns from the school community about safety risks at this location, particularly during drop-off and pick-up hours. In response, I visited the site alongside the school principal and our Transportation Services team to assess conditions on the ground. As outlined in the attached memo from the Director, Traffic Management, staff intend to recommend an all-way stop at this intersection as part of the forthcoming Peanut Streets Plan. While that plan will come forward later this year, this motion seeks to implement the stop control now to enhance safety ahead of the new school year.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized an all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Van Horne Avenue and Silas Hill Drive.
