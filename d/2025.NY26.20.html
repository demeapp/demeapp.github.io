Allegrezza Court - Traffic and Parking Amendments

AdoptedSep 17, 2025 · Toronto City Council

New parking rules and stop signs are proposed for Allegrezza Court, a new public road with no rules today. North York Community Council is asked to approve them.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Review is recommending the implementation of parking regulations and stop control for the development at 36 and 37 Jane Osler Boulevard (Allegrezza Court). Allegrezza Court has been dedicated as a public road but has not been assumed by the City of Toronto. Currently there are no traffic or parking regulations on this street. The proposed parking and traffic regulations are consistent with City policies and guidelines given the surrounding residential nature.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking at all times on both sides of Allegrezza Court between Jane Osler Boulevard and the west end of Allegrezza Court. 2. Authorized a compulsory stop for eastbound traffic on Allegrezza Court at Jane Osler Boulevard.
