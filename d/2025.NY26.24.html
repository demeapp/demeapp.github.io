Bayview Avenue, between Moore Avenue and Pottery Road (North Intersection) Safety Review

Adopted with changesSep 17, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Staff completed the safety review of Bayview Avenue between Moore Avenue and Pottery Road. North York Community Council is asked to receive the report for information; no new changes are proposed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

North York Community Council requested Transportation Services to undertake a safety review of Bayview Avenue, between Moore Avenue and the northerly intersection with Pottery Road. This report provides an overview of the assessments and improvements undertaken and proposed by Transportation Services.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Directed the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services to investigate solutions in line with Vision Zero design principles to make Pottery Road North (from Bayview Avenue to Moore Avenue) safer, including the potential implementation of interim measures such as painted markings or flexible posts, in advance of permanent safety improvements to the street, and report back on their findings to North York Community Council in the second quarter of 2026. 2. Directed the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services, to undertake an operational efficiency and safety review of the intersection of Bayview Avenue, Bayview Heights Drive and Evergreen Gardens and report back on their findings to North York Community Council in the second quarter of 2026.
