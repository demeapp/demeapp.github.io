Becky Cheung Court - Parking Regulations

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Review is recommending the implementation of parking regulations for the development at 162 - 200 Cummer Avenue (Becky Cheung Court). Becky Cheung Court has been dedicated as a public road but has not been assumed by the City of Toronto. Currently there are no parking regulations on this street. The proposed parking regulations are consistent with City policies and guidelines given the surrounding residential nature.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Deferred the consideration of this item until the October 21, 2025 meeting of the North York Community Council.
