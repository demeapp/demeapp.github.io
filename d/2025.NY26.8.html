3138 Bayview Avenue - Zoning Amendment Application - Decision Report - Approval

AdoptedOct 8, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend approving five three-storey townhouses at 3138 Bayview Avenue, each with one parking space. City Council is asked to change the zoning to allow them.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This Report recommends approval of the application to amend the City-wide Zoning By-law 569-2013 to permit the development of five, three-storey townhomes at 3138 Bayview Avenue. The units would front onto Bayview Avenue, have an overall density of 976.25 square metres of residential gross floor area with a Floor Space Index (FSI) of 1.44 and a lot coverage of 53.75 percent. Each unit will include one parking space, accessed via a private rear driveway from Byng Avenue.

What was decided

City Council on October 8 and 9, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend City of Toronto Zoning By-law 569-2013 for the lands municipally known as 3138 Bayview Avenue substantially in accordance with the draft Zoning By-law Amendment included as Attachment 5 to the report (August 28, 2025) from the Director, Community Planning, North York District. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to make such stylistic and technical changes to the draft Zoning By-law Amendment as may be required. 3.
