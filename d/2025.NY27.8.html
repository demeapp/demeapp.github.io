4800 Yonge Street - Encroachment Agreement - TTC Pedestrian Tunnel

Adopted with changesOct 21, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The owner of 4800 Yonge Street wants to build a pedestrian tunnel under Yonge Street to the TTC. North York Community Council is asked to approve the agreement covering the tunnel under city property.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The registered owners of the property, 4800 Yonge Limited (the “Owner”), has submitted an application for the property municipally known as 4800 Yonge Street (the “Owner’s Lands”) to construct a subsurface pedestrian Tunnel Connection under Yonge Street (“Public Highway”) which lies between the owners’ new development at the Owner’s Lands and Toronto Transit Commission’s Sheppard Subway Station, which will encroach within the public right-of-way. The tunnel will be accessible to the public during the operating hours of Toronto Transit Commission’s Subway System. North York Community Council: 1. Authorized the City to enter into an encroachment agreement with 4800 Yonge Limited, to permit 4800 Yonge Limited, to construct, maintain, repair and operate a publicly accessible underground pedestrian tunnel connection, located underneath the west side of Yonge Street and south of Sheppard Avenue West, connecting 4800 Yonge Limited’s Lands and Toronto Transit Commission’s Sheppard Subway Station, as shown in Attachments 1 and 2 (the “Tunnel Connection”) to the report (September 30, 2025) from the Director, Street Permits, Transportation Services, substantially on the major terms and conditions set out in Attachment 3 to the report (September 30, 2025) from the Director, Street Permits, Transportation Services, and including such other terms and conditions or such amendments to the major terms and conditions set out in Attachment 4 to the report…
