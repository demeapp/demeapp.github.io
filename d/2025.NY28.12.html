241-243 Finch Avenue East - Part Lot Control Exemption Application - Decision Report - Approval

AdoptedDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend freeing 241-243 Finch Avenue East from a rule that blocks lots from being split, so eight townhouses can be sold as separate lots. City Council is asked to approve.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of the application to exempt the lands from the Part Lot Control provisions of the Planning Act at 241-243 Finch Avenue East to permit the creation of conveyable lots for eight townhouse units which would ultimately be held under freehold ownership and become the Parcels of Tied Land (POTLs) to the Common Elements Condominium. A related Common Elements Condominium application has been submitted to establish eight (8) residential dwelling units with shared common elements such as a private access driveway and site servicing under File Number 25 134479 NNY 18 CD.
