Metrolinx’s Ontario Line Construction within the North York District - Fourth Quarter 2025 Update

AdoptedDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A routine update: staff report on Metrolinx's Ontario Line construction and transit-oriented projects in North York for the fourth quarter of 2025. North York Community Council is asked to receive it; no decision is needed.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council, in March 2024, through MM16.31, directed City staff to report to North York Community Council (NYCC) with regular updates on the status and progress of Metrolinx’s Ontario Line construction and the Province’s Transit-Oriented Communities (TOC) projects within the boundaries of North York. The purpose of this report is to provide updates and project details on the three Ontario Line stations and a Transit-Oriented Communities proposal located in North York, and includes updates on: - Ontario Line North Segment Construction Updates and Impacts; - Metrolinx’s Community Benefits and Supports Program; - Public Engagement; and - Transit-Oriented Communities Update - Thorncliffe Park. City staff will provide the next annual report to North York Community Council in the third quarter of 2026.
