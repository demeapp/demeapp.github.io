1125 Sheppard Avenue East (Block 2) - Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment Application - Decision Report - Refusal

AdoptedDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend refusing a 52- and 48-storey, 1,238-home development at 1125 Sheppard Avenue East (Block 2). Council is asked to refuse it, keeping the site's current rules.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report reviews and recommends refusal of the application to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law to permit a mixed-use development consisting of 52 and 48-storey residential towers on a shared 6-storey podium, a privately owned publicly accessible space at the southeast corner of the site and landscaped mid-block connection connecting Sheppard Avenue East to Singer Court. The proposal includes a total of 1,238 residential units, with 78,265.24 square metres of gross floor area, including 2,881.8 square metres of non-residential gross floor area for a maximum Floor Space Index ("FSI") of 11.50 times the lot area. The application does not conform with the Official Plan and the Sheppard Subway Corridor Secondary Plan. The proposal is in its current form does not appropriately respond to the Council-adopted Renew Sheppard East Secondary Plan.

What was decided

City Council on December 16 and 17, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council refuse the application for the Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment (Application Number 24 128320 NNY 17 OZ) for the lands municipally known as 1125 Sheppard Avenue East. 2. In the event the applications are appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal, City Council, pursuant to subsections 22(8.1) and 34(11.0.0.1) of the Planning Act, use mediation, conciliation or other dispute resolution techniques in an attempt to resolve the Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment applications, to the satisfaction of the Executive Director, Development Review and the City Solicitor. 3.
