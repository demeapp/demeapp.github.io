Toronto City Council · Decision

Bike Share for Everyone: Allowing Young Torontonians to access Safe, Sustainable Commutes

PostponedMar 4, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City Council asked the Parking Authority to let 16- and 17-year-olds ride regular Bike Share bikes on their own, and to consider letting 14- to 16-year-olds ride with a parent or guardian. The board is asked to consider the change.

Council put off deciding this item.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on July 24 and 25, 2024, adopted Item MM20.7 and, in so doing, has: 1. Requested the Toronto Parking Authority Board to allow Bike Share users ages 16 and 17 to ride classic (non-electronic) bikes without being accompanied by a parent or a guardian. 2. Requested the Toronto Parking Authority Board to consider allowing Bike Share users between 14 and 16 years of age to ride classic bikes if being accompanied by a parent or a guardian.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority deferred consideration of the item until the May 15, 2025 meeting of the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority.
