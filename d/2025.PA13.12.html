101 Cedarvale Avenue - Future De-designation as a Municipal Parking Facility - Toronto Transit Commission's Line 2 Capacity Enhancement Program

Received for informationMar 4, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City Council wants the parking lot at 101 Cedarvale Avenue to eventually stop being a public lot so the TTC can use it for its Line 2 capacity program. The Parking Authority board is asked to take appropriate action.

Received for information — Council took no action on it.
