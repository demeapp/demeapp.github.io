The official record

What the item proposed

Our colleagues are the cornerstone of Toronto Parking Authority's success. For 73 years, thousands of staff have delivered world class Parking, Bike Share and EV Charging solutions for our customers, the city, and its residents. This report provides an update to the Board on the Toronto Parking Authority's People and Cultural transformation as we continue our journey towards becoming one of the world's premiere mobility companies. For context, the pandemic had a devastating impact on the Toronto Parking Authority. Customer trips and revenues were more than halved, profitability collapsed, 65 percent of unionized staff were laid off and 81 percent of non-union staff were downsized and/or departed since 2021.
