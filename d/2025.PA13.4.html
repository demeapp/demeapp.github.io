The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek approval from the Board of Directors, Toronto Parking Authority for the purchase of 1,900 Pay-by-Plate (PbP) parking meters, valued at $15.6 million, as part of management’s multi-year strategy to replace end-of-life parking payment equipment. This purchase represents the third (3) and final phase of a modernization initiative that began in 2023. This investment is included in Toronto Parking Authority’s 2025–2027 capital plan, with procurement and installation scheduled over that period. As background, on April 21, 2023, the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) approved the purchase of 225 Pay-by-Plate on-street parking meters valued at $2.06 million, marking Phase One (1) of Toronto Parking Authority’s parking equipment modernization strategy. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized payment of $15.6 million excluding Harmonized Sales Tax (HST) for the purchase of 1,900 Pay by Plate (PbP) parking machines from Precise Parklink Inc., which will be made pursuant to the terms and conditions of the Master Services Agreement between the Toronto Parking Authority and Precise Parklink Inc. dated July 1, 2016.
