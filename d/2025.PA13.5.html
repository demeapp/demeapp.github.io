The official record

What the item proposed

Over the past two (2) years, consistent with our strategy, the Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) has designed and built Canada’s largest municipally owned Electric Vehicle (EV) charging network, featuring 461 charging ports across 85 locations. Over the next two (2) to three (3) years, Toronto Parking Authority will continue expanding its EV charging network, further solidifying its position as Toronto’s premier EV charging provider. The purpose of this paper is to seek approval for the extension of two (2) key agreements with EV charging equipment providers, ChargePoint Inc. and Services FLO Inc. These agreements were initially approved by the Board on December 12, 2022, as an extension of a City of Toronto agreement with the cooperative purchasing organization, Kinetic GPO that was tendered in the fall of 2022 and remains in effect today.
