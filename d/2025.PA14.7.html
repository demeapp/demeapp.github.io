Summary of Legal Disputes - 2025 First Quarter Update

AdoptedMay 15, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A routine update: a summary of the Parking Authority's legal disputes for the first quarter of 2025. The board receives it for information; no decision is needed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on April 24, 2025, the Toronto Parking Authority - Audit and Risk Management Committee considered Item PR9.7 and made recommendations to the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority. Summary from the report (April 2, 2025) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority: As part of Toronto Parking Authority’s operation of commercial parking, the management of tenanted properties and Bike Share Toronto, Toronto Parking Authority is dealing with a range of legal matters related to supplier disputes, personal injuries, commercial tenancy disputes and the protection of Toronto Parking Authority’s registered trademarks. Toronto Parking Authority has suitable insurance in relation to such concerns, such as general liability insurance for all of its facilities; and has assigned/retained legal counsel and insurance adjusters to manage any claims. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Directed that the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (April 2, 2025) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority remain confidential in its entirety, as it contains advice about litigation or potential litigation that affects Toronto Parking Authority and contains advice or communications that are subject to solicitor-client privilege.
