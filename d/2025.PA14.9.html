What the item proposed

Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) is North America's largest municipal operator of public parking with over 25 million transactions in 2024 contributing to $ in revenue. In addition to its parking operations, Toronto Parking Authority also operates Bike Share Toronto, North America’s third largest Bilke Share program. In 2024, Bike Share Toronto attracted over 40,000 members (+5,000 v 2023) and achieved ridership of 6.9 million (+1.2 m v 2023) marking a nearly 300 percent increase in ridership since 2019. Toronto Parking Authority’s electric vehicle (EV) charging network, which now comprises over 450 chargers, places TPA as the operator of the largest municipally owned EV charging network in Canada.
