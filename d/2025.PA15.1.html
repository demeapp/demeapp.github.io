What the item proposed

The City of Toronto has developed a Strategic Parking Framework that is intended to guide the City-wide management of parking in a manner that supports city-building objectives and fosters effective collaboration across the City's divisions and agencies. By adopting a system-wide approach, the City aims to better position itself to leverage parking policy and management as strategic tools to optimize space usage, support multiple modes of transportation, and meet the diverse needs of residents and visitors. Development of the Strategic Parking Framework was initiated by Transportation Services in early 2023, but very much a collective effort between several City partners, including Toronto Parking Authority (TPA), that have mandates requiring innovation related to Toronto's parking systems and infrastructure.
