The official record

What the item proposed

As part of the Toronto Parking Authority’s State of Good Repair (SOGR) strategy to systematically assess and restore each facility, garage modernization (refurbishment) work is scheduled for Car Park 58, located a 9 Bedford Road. The purpose of this paper is to seek authorization from the Board of Directors, Toronto Parking Authority, award the garage modernization project located at 9 Bedford Road (Car Park 58) for 2025-CON-CP58-B10 to Ritestart Limited, having submitted the lowest compliant bid and meeting all specifications in conformance with the Construction Tender requirements, in the amount of $633,400.00, plus $126,700.00 as a contingency allowance, being the sum total of $760,100.00 excluding Harmonized Sales Tax (HST). What was decided

The Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors: 1. Provided authority to the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to award the garage modernization project located at 9 Bedford Road (Car Park 58) for 2025-CON-CP58-B10 to Ritestart Limited, having submitted the lowest compliant bid and meeting all specifications in conformance with the Construction Tender requirements, in the amount of $633,400.00, plus $126,700.00 as a contingency allowance, being the sum total of $760,100.00 excluding Harmonized Sales Tax (HST).
