What the item proposed

This report seeks the approval of the Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) to award a contract for the implementation of new inbuilding Distributed Antenna System (DAS) cell phone repeater systems at the following eight (8) car parks: Car Park 13: 20 Delisle Avenue Car Park 36: 110 Queen Street West Car Park 42: 91 Via Italia Car Park 49: 30 Roehampton Car Park 52: 40 York Street Car Park 58: 9 Bedford Road Car Park 125: 323 Richmond Street East Car Park 261: 45 Abell Street This initiative is part of the Toronto Parking Authority's ongoing commitment to deliver a superior customer experience in our parking facilities, emphasizing convenience, reliability, and operational excellence. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to negotiate, enter and execute a contract with Rogers Communications Canada Inc., to deliver the Distributed Antenna System Cell Phone Repeater Project (RFP-ITDAS-202503-01), in an amount not exceeding $4,218,077.90, inclusive of contingency but excluding Harmonized Sales Tax.
