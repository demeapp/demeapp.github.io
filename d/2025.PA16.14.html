Proposed Amendments to Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 3-6 - Residential Overnight Use

AdoptedOct 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Changing its overnight parking policy. It goes to the board for a decision. New RapidTO bus lanes on Bathurst and Dufferin are reducing paid parking spaces, which affects overnight permit holders.

The official record

What the item proposed

As part of Toronto’s preparations for hosting the 2026 FIFA World Cup, the City of Toronto and the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) have rolled out the RapidTO initiative, focused on implementing transit priority measures to enhance the speed, reliability, and overall performance of surface transit services. As a result of RapidTO, Toronto Parking Authority's (TPA) inventory of paid parking will be reduced along Bathurst and Dufferin for priority bus lanes to more efficiently move people along these key corridors. Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) Policy Resolution 3-6 - Residential Overnight Use allows TPA to designate specific off-street parking lots for temporary overnight parking at no charge to applicable residential parking permit holders. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Approved the amendments to Policy Resolution 3-6, to add Car Park 241 (9 Bonar Place) to Schedule A, as outlined in Attachment 1 to the report (October 1, 2025) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority.
