Toronto City Council · Decision

Waiving of On-Street Paid Parking Fees on Bond Street

AdoptedOct 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Waiving $115,230 in fees after St. Michael's Hospital's expansion removed three paid parking spaces on Bond Street and required two parking machines to be moved. It goes to the board for approval. The fee covers the lost meter revenue.

The official summary names a figure of $115,230.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As part of a hospital expansion project at St. Michael’s Hospital, three paid on-street parking spaces are being removed, and two associated parking machines will need to be relocated. In accordance with Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) Policy Resolution 2-12: Cost Recovery – Permanent Removal of On-Street Meter Locations, Toronto Parking Authority has assessed the financial impact of this removal at $115,230. Recognizing both the value of the redevelopment and the critical role St. Michael’s Hospital plays in delivering healthcare services to the surrounding community and the City as a whole, City Council at its June 2025 meeting requested that Toronto Parking Authority waive all associated parking removal and equipment relocation fees. What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Exempted St. Michael’s Hospital from Policy 2-12 and waived fees totaling $115,230 associated with the permanent removal of three on-street paid parking spaces and relocation of two parking machines on Bond Street between Queen Street East and Shuter Street, as part of the St. Michael’s Hospital Redevelopment project (Application: 16 148963 STE13 SA) as outlined in Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (October 1, 2025) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority.
