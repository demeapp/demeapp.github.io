A Strategic Parking Framework for the City of Toronto

WithdrawnOct 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Endorsing the city's Strategic Parking Framework. It goes to the board for approval. City Council also wants a report on extending free parking to other times of religious significance, beyond Sunday mornings.

The item was withdrawn before a final decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on June 25 and 26, 2025, adopted Item IE22.2 as amended and, in so doing, has requested the Toronto Parking Authority board to request the President, Toronto Parking Authority, in consultation with the Director, Equity and Human Rights, to report in the first quarter of 2026 on the feasibility of extending free parking at other times of religious significance in addition to Sunday mornings.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority withdrew Item PA16.17 from the agenda.
