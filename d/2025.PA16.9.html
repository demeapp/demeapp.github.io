Contract Award 2025-CON-CP52-D3060 Vent Shaft Repairs at 40 York Street (Car Park 52)

AdoptedOct 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Awarding an emergency vent shaft repair contract at 40 York Street (Car Park 52) to Structural Contracting Ltd. It goes to the board for approval. The contract is $1,493,355.00 plus $113,850.00 in provisional items plus a $500,000.00 contingency, totalling $2,107,205.00.

The official record

What the item proposed

As part of the Toronto Parking Authority's (TPA) ongoing State of Good Repair (SOGR) strategy to ensure continued safe operations of existing parking facilities, Toronto Parking Authority must undertake emergency vent shaft repairs at Car Park 52 located at 40 York Street. This emergency work aligns with Toronto Parking Authority's established State of Good Repair process and reflects our continued commitment to proactive infrastructure maintenance and public safety. The purpose of this paper is to seek authorization from the Board of Directors, Toronto Parking Authority, to award Structural Contracting Ltd. for the vent shaft repair project located at 40 York Street (Car Park 52) valued at $1,493,355.00 in base scope, plus $113,850.00 in provisional items, plus $500,000.00 as a contingency allowance, being the sum total of $2,107,205.00 excluding Harmonized Sales Tax (HST). What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to award the vent shaft repair project located at 40 York Street (Car Park 52) for 2025-CON-CP52-D3060 to Structural Contracting Ltd., having submitted the lowest compliant bid and meeting all specifications in conformance with the Construction Tender requirements, in the amount of $1,493,355.00 in base scope, plus $113,850.00 in provisional items, plus $500,000.00 as a contingency allowance, being the sum total of $2,107,205.00 excluding Harmonized Sales Tax.
