10-Year Circular Economy Road Map

Received for informationDec 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City Council adopted a 10-year plan to cut waste through reuse and recycling, and asked city agencies to adopt similar actions. The Parking Authority board is asked to consider it.

Received for information — Council took no action on it.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on November 12 and 13, 2025, adopted Item IE25.1 as amended and, in so doing, has requested the City’s Agencies and Corporations to review the report of the Executive Director, Environment, Climate and Forestry and the General Manager, Solid Waste Management Services, dated October 15, 2025, titled "10-Year Circular Economy Road Map," including all attachments, along with City Council's directions, and adopt similar actions where possible.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority received the item for information.
