Property Included on the City's Heritage Register - Consideration of Objection for 1751 Bayview Avenue

AdoptedMay 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The owner of 1751 Bayview Avenue objects to the property being on the Heritage Register. The board is asked to review the objection and recommend whether the listing should stay.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to a notice of objection received by the City of Toronto under Section 27 (7) of the Ontario Heritage Act (the "OHA") from the property owner of 1751 Bayview Avenue objecting to the inclusion of the subject property on the City's Heritage Register. Located on the east side of Bayview Avenue between Eglinton Avenue East and Parkhurst Boulevard, in the Leaside-Bennington neighbourhood, the property at 1751 Bayview Avenue contains a two-storey, brick-clad, residential apartment building constructed in the mid-1930s. On December 18, 2024, City Council adopted the staff report NY19.12 that included the grouping of similar properties at 1747, 1751, 1759, 1763, 1767, 1771, 1773, and 1775 Bayview Avenue on the City's Heritage Register. What was decided

The Toronto Preservation Board recommends that: 1. In consideration of the objection made under subsection 27(7) of the Ontario Heritage Act, City Council continue to include the property at 1751 Bayview Avenue on the City of Toronto's Heritage Register.
