15 Glen Morris Street - Alterations to and Demolition of Heritage Attributes of a Designated Property under Sections 33 and 34 of the Ontario Heritage Act and Authority to Enter into a Heritage Easement Agreement

AdoptedMay 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Recommending that council approve changes to the protected heritage property at 15 Glen Morris Street, where an 8-storey residential building is proposed behind the old facades. It goes to the board for a decision.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve the alterations and attribute demolitions proposed under Sections 33 and 34(1)1 of the Ontario Heritage Act for the Part IV designated heritage property at 15 Glen Morris Street in connection with a proposal to construct an 8-storey residential structure set behind the altered and partially reconstructed facades of the designated heritage property. The report also seeks approval to enter into a Heritage Easement Agreement. The subject property at 15 Glen Morris Street is designated under Part IV, Section 29, of the Ontario Heritage Act. The property, which is known as the Samuel Bennett House, was constructed in 1878 and is an example of the classic one-and-a-half storey Ontario house that incudes a verandah and a gable dormer on its principal elevation.
