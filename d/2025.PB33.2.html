324 Old Yonge Street - Notice of Intention to Designate a Property under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act

Adopted with changesJun 30, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The board votes on whether to recommend starting heritage protection for 324 Old Yonge Street. Starting designation would limit future demolition and changes to the property.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council state its intention to designate the property at 324 Old Yonge Street under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act for its cultural heritage value according to the Statement of Significance and description of heritage attributes found in Attachment 1. The subject property, known as the Jephcott -Sanderson Residence, is located in the St. Andrews - Windfields neighbourhood in the North York-York Mills area of Toronto. It is situated on the west side of Old Yonge Street, north of York Mills Road, east of Yonge Street, and south of Highway 401. A location map and current photograph of the heritage property are found in Attachment 2. The property contains a two-and-one-half-storey brick house and a detached two-storey brick garage and was constructed in 1935 in the Georgian Revival style. What was decided

The Toronto Preservation Board recommends that: 1. City Council state its intention to designate the property at 324 Old Yonge Street under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act in accordance with the Statement of Significance for 324 Old Yonge Street (Reasons for Designation) attached as Attachment 1 to the report (June 11, 2025) from the Senior Manager, Heritage Planning, Urban Design, City Planning. 2. If there are no objections to the designation, City Council authorize the City Solicitor to introduce the Bill in Council designating the property under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act. 3.
