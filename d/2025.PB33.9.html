126,128 and 130 Avenue Road - Demolition and New Construction on Designated Heritage Properties Under Part V, Section 42 of the Ontario Heritage Act - Approval

Forwarded without a recommendationJun 30, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The board votes on whether to recommend letting three non-heritage buildings in the East Annex heritage district be torn down, clearing the way for a new 11-storey building with 60 homes and shops at 126-130 Avenue Road.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve the demolition of three category "X" buildings within the East Annex Heritage Conservation District (EAHCD) and approve the design of a replacement structure located at 126, 128 and 130 Avenue Road in accordance with Section 42(1) 2 and 42(1) 4 of the Ontario Heritage Act. The application is to demolish three existing buildings that form part of a row of buildings located on the west side of Avenue Road, between Bernard Avenue to the south and Davenport Road to the north. The EAHCD Plan allows for the demolition of category "X" buildings provided that the replacement building design complies with the EAHCD plan guidelines for new buildings. The demolition would allow for the construction of a new 11-storey building that meets the general intent of the EAHCD Plan's guidelines and would not detract from rated heritage buildings to the west…

What was decided

The Toronto Preservation Board forwards the Item without recommendations.
