306 Lawrence Avenue East - Alteration and Demolition of Heritage Attributes of a Designated Heritage Property under Section 33 and 34 of the Ontario Heritage Act

AdoptedSep 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Recommending that council approve changes and partial demolition at the protected heritage properties at 294-318 Lawrence Avenue East, now the Toronto French School campus. It goes to the board for a decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve the alterations and demolition of heritage attributes proposed under Sections 33 and 34 of the Ontario Heritage Act for the Part IV designated heritage property at 294-318 Lawrence Avenue East. The properties at 294 (including 296), 306 and 318 Lawrence Avenue East, which now constitute the campus of the Toronto French School (TFS), are designated under Part IV of the Ontario Heritage Act by By-law No. 688-2024, enacted by City Council on June 27, 2024. The Heritage Permit application is being made to allow for the construction of a new 350-seat Performing Arts Centre (PAC) and the conversion of the house form structure at 306 Lawrence Avenue East into the offices of the Head of School and Registrar.
