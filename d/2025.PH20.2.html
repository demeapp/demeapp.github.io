The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to City Council’s request for the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning to evaluate the current approach to calculating rent gap assistance, report back on compliance with tenant assistance policies, and publish updated implementation guidelines for Rental Housing Demolition applications submitted under Chapter 667 of Toronto Municipal Code (“Chapter 667”) that require replacement of existing rental housing. Throughout November and December 2024, staff undertook consultations with tenants, development industry and tenant advocacy stakeholder groups. What was decided

City Council on April 23 and 24, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the Rental Replacement Handbook in Attachment 1 to the report (March 27, 2025) from the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, as the guidelines for Rental Housing Demolition applications and direct the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, in consultation with the Executive Director, Development Review, to use the Guidelines in the evaluation of new and under review Rental Housing Demolition applications. 2.
