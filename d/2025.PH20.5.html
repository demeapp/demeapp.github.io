Development Review Timeline Metrics - Q1 2025

AdoptedApr 10, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the Planning and Housing Committee was asked to receive for information the Q1 2025 report on how long development applications take to review. No policy change is proposed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to Planning and Housing Committee's direction for staff to report on development review timeline metrics to the Planning and Housing Committee on a quarterly basis ( 2023.PH6.2 ). Quarterly reports include the following: a. Average timeline to complete the Pre-Application Consultation Process; b. Average timeline to reach a decision on a Zoning By-law Application; c. Average timeline to reach a decision on a combined Official Plan Amendment/Zoning By-law Application; and d. Average timeline to approve a Site Plan Application. In addition to a description of the metrics listed above, the report provides context on how ongoing improvements to the development review process impact application review timelines in the medium to long term.
