Housing Action Plan: Incorporating Low-rise Residential Lands into Zoning By-law 569-2013 - North York District - Final Report

AdoptedJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council votes on bringing about 323 North York properties under the city-wide zoning by-law. These lots would then follow the same rules as the rest of the city, including new permissions for more housing.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report outlines the analysis, consultation process, and recommended zoning amendments to advance the first phase of work to incorporate low-rise residential lands in North York District into Zoning By-law 569-2013 (the city-wide Zoning By-law). Staff recommend bringing low-rise residential lands into the city-wide Zoning By-law with zoning standards that are consistent with the applicable former municipal zoning by-law, consistent with recent city-wide zoning amendments, and in conformity with the Official Plan. These properties had active zoning by-law amendment or site plan control applications in 2012 and were excluded from the city-wide Zoning By-law through the transition protocol. What was decided

City Council on June 25 and 26, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend Zoning By-law 569-2013, substantially in accordance with the draft Zoning By-law Amendment attached as Attachment 2 to the report (May 26, 2025) from the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to make such stylistic and technical changes to the draft Zoning By-law Amendment(s) as may be required.
