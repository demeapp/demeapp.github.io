Toronto City Council · Decision

Open and Accessible In-Person Public Transit Oriented Community Open Houses

AdoptedOct 8, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council asks Infrastructure Ontario to hold all Transit Oriented Community meetings in person, after residents asked for face-to-face answers about the Ontario Line.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The lengthy and complex Ontario Line and Transit Oriented Community processes continue to impact many residents in our communities. The litany of ongoing changes and updates including provincially imposed Municipal Zoning Orders (MZOs) add to the frustration and confusion experienced by residents. Residents have repeatedly requested an in-person public open house as a vehicle to support their efforts to have their questions answered.

What was decided

City Council on October 8 and 9, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council request Infrastructure Ontario to hold all Transit Oriented Community meetings in person.
