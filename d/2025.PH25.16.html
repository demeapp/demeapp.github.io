Toronto City Council · Decision

Summary of Development Application Fee and Related Resources for New Live Music Venues

Adopted with changesOct 30, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Staff would keep studying lower development fees for new live music venues, with a report back in early 2026, if the Planning and Housing Committee agrees.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on October 7, 2025 the Toronto Music Advisory Committee considered item MA8.2. Summary from the presentation (October 7, 2025) from Colin Wolfe, Senior Planner, Community Planning: Development Review will provide an update on planning-related application fees and related resources in response to Item MA7.1 at the May 15, 2025 meeting of the Toronto Music Advisory Committee.

What was decided

The Planning and Housing Committee: 1. Request the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, the Executive Director, Development Review, and the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, to continue to explore options to support the sustainability of existing and new live music venues, including a review of development application fees and options to reduce and/or subsidize those fees, through the inter-divisional City “Sound Check” team’s efforts as endorsed by the Toronto Music Advisory Committee.
