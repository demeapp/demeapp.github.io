Proposed Amendments - Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolutions 2-1: Parking Rates - Off-Street Facilities and 2-3: Monthly Parking Permits

AdoptedApr 24, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto Parking Authority board votes on letting its President change off-street parking rates by up to 25 percent as needed, instead of only once a year.

The official record

What the item proposed

As North America's largest municipally-owned commercial parking operator with ample competition in the off-street parking arena, Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) uses rate setting or pricing as a tool to respond to evolving consumer behaviour and remain competitive while continuing to meet its mandate to provide short-stay, high turnover parking. Toronto Parking Authority's approach to rate setting at its off-street parking facilities is guided by Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 2-1: Parking Rates - Off-Street Facilities. Under the current framework, Management carries out a comprehensive review of Toronto Parking Authority's off-street facilities at least once a year with the results of the review and recommended rate changes then brought forward to Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors for approval as part of the annual budget process. What was decided

The Toronto Parking Authority - Audit and Risk Management Committee recommends that the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Approve the amendments to Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 2-1 - Parking Rates - Off-Street Facilities in Revised Attachment 1 to the report (April 2, 2025) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority, and direct the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to enact this Policy Resolution, as amended. 2. Approve the amendments to Toronto Parking Authority Policy Resolution 2-3 - Monthly Permits in Revised Attachment 2 to the report (April 2, 2025) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority.
