Transferring Secretary Role for Property Standards Committee to Court Services

Adopted with changesNov 28, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the Property Standards Committee was asked to appoint the Executive Director of Court Services as its Secretary starting January 1, 2026.

A citywide decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on October 8 and 9, 2025, adopted Item EX26.9 and, in so doing, has requested the Property Standards Committee to appoint the Executive Director, Court Services as Secretary of the Property Standards Committee effective January 1, 2026, with the authority to delegate the role of Secretary for each Panel.

What was decided

The Property Standards Committee: 1. Appointed the Executive Director, Court Services, as Secretary of the Property Standards Committee effective January 1, 2026, with the authority to delegate the role of Secretary for each Panel. 2. Amended Section 6 of By-law No. 1 City of Toronto Property Standards Committee, Rules of procedure for the Property Standards Committee, as amended, so that it now reads as follows: Committee Secretary. The Executive Director, Court Services is appointed as the Secretary of the Committee and may delegate administrative responsibilities to a Deputy Administrator or Secretary assigned to administer a Panel. 3. Amended By-law No. 1 City of Toronto Property Standards Committee, Rules of procedure for the Property Standards Committee, as amended, to replace all references to “the City Clerk” with “the Executive Director, Court Services”. 4.
