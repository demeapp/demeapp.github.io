What the item proposed

At its meeting held July 23 and 24, 2025, City Council appointed the following members to the Two-Spirit, Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender, and Queer (2SLGBTQ+) Advisory Committee for a term of office ending on November 14, 2026, or until their mandate is completed, whichever comes first: Felipe Del Campo Donoso Susan Gapka leZlie lee kam Leyla Shahid These new appointments fill the positions previously held by Apt’sqi’gnn ᐊᑉᑦ ᔅᑭ ᒃᓐᓐ, whose term of office came to an end on February 4, 2025; Mark Toffoli, whose term ended on April 10, 2025; and Kay Mehriz and Bamboo Ren, whose terms ended on April 30, 2025.

What was decided

The Two-Spirit, Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender and Queer Advisory Committee received the item for information.
