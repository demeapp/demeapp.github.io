Policy Framework - City Response to Demonstrations

Received for informationJan 30, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the CreateTO board is noting city council's adoption of the Policy Framework on the city's response to demonstrations. No policy change.

Received for information — Council took no action on it.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on December 17 and 18, 2024, adopted Item CC24.2 as amended and, in so doing, has: 1. Adopted the Policy Framework - City Response to Demonstrations in Attachment 1 to the report (December 10, 2024) from the City Manager and direct the City Manager to update the Framework as necessary to ensure the Framework remains current and responsive to the City's needs and priorities. 2. Requested City agencies and corporations to refer to the Policy Framework - City Response to Demonstrations in Attachment 1 to the report (December 10, 2024) from the City Manager as a model and align applicable policies and procedures to be consistent with the City's approach where appropriate.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO received the item for information.
