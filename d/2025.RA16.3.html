Updated Business Case for 158 Borough Drive

The CreateTO board votes on endorsing the updated business case for 158 Borough Drive and offering the 1.57-acre site to the market, with a fairness monitor watching the process.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends the CreateTO Board of Directors endorse the updated business case in support of a market offering for the site located at 158 Borough Drive. A business case for this site was previously considered and approved at the April 12, 2022 meeting of the Board of Directors. However, due to unprecedented cost escalations and cost uncertainty in the real estate market at that time, a market offering for the 1.57-acre site was not issued. Since that time, CreateTO staff have worked closely with their partners in the Housing Secretariat to revisit the business case for the property to ensure it was appropriately structured and funded to address new Council priorities and direction. What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Endorsed the updated business case for 158 Borough Drive, as presented in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (January 16, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO and, subject to further approvals from relevant City officials, directed the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO to: a. release to market 158 Borough Drive, conditional on approval by relevant City authorities; and b. retain the services of a fairness monitor to oversee and ensure an open, fair and transparent market offering process. 2. Authorized the public release of the information in Confidential Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (January 16, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO following the expiry of the lease with the successful proponent, and at the discretion of the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO.
