2025 CreateTO Annual Objectives and Work Plan

Adopted with changesJan 30, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The CreateTO board votes on approving its 2025 annual goals, the CEO's performance goals and the 2025 work plan, with the goals kept private.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks approval from the Board of Directors of CreateTO on the organization's Annual Objectives and performance objectives for the Chief Executive Officer. This report also provides CreateTO's anticipated 2025 Work Plan. CreateTO's 2025 Annual Objectives and the corresponding performance objectives of the Chief Executive Officer for 2025 are provided in Confidential Attachment 1. CreateTO's 2025 Work Plan, with key milestones and target deliverables, is provided in Confidential Attachment 2.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Approved CreateTO's 2025 Annual Objectives and the performance objectives for the Chief Executive Officer for 2025, as detailed in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (January 16, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO. 2. Directed the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO to replace Confidential Attachment 2 to the report (January 16, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO, to replace the names of the appropriate housing programs in the Description column. 3. Requested the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO to report back to the Board of Directors of CreateTO in 2025 if there are substantive updates to the Work Plan outlined in Confidential Attachment 2 to the report (January 16, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO, such as additional City Council direction on the implementation of the City's housing plan. 4.…
