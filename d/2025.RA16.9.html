Commuter Parking Opportunities for 777 Victoria Park Avenue

Adopted with changesJan 30, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the CreateTO board is noting council's direction to study commuter parking options for 777 Victoria Park Avenue, including a possible off-site Green P lot.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
