Request for Information on Public Consultations Regarding the City's Policy Framework - City Response to Demonstrations - Status Update

AdoptedApr 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the CreateTO board receives a status update on public consultations around the city's Policy Framework on responses to demonstrations. No policy change.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report is in response to the motion passed at the CreateTO Board meeting on March 3, 2025 (Item RA17.3 ). CreateTO submitted a letter to the City Manager (March 5, 2025) requesting information about (i) the nature and extent of public consultations conducted and planned in relation to the City of Toronto’s (the "City") Policy Framework – City Response to Demonstrations, and (ii) the level and scope of the City’s consultation and engagement with Indigenous Peoples as part of this process, and (iii) the City's overall policy framework concerning engagement with Indigenous communities. The City Manager's provided a response letter dated April 4, 2025.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Received the report (April 4, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO for information.
