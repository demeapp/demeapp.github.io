Master Services Agreement with City of Toronto for Housing Development

The CreateTO board votes on signing a Master Services Agreement between CreateTO and the City of Toronto, setting out how CreateTO will do housing development work for the city.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides information about the development of a Master Services Agreement ("MSA") between CreateTO and the City of Toronto (City), details key components of the MSA, and seeks authority to execute CreateTO's MSA and carry out its terms. To meet the complex and evolving housing needs of its residents, the City is pursuing a more proactive and hands-on approach to housing development. As detailed in EX19.1 - Strengthening the City of Toronto's Housing Development Capacity to Deliver Housing Faster , in December 2024, City Council directed the Deputy City Manager, Development and Growth Services to negotiate and execute an MSA with CreateTO to support these housing needs. A similar MSA will be entered into between the City and Toronto Community Housing Corporation ("TCHC").
