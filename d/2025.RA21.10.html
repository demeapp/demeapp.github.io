The official record

What the item proposed

The City Clerk's Office has received notice that Marni Dicker has resigned from the CreateTO Board effective August 26, 2025. Staff from the Public Appointments Secretariat in the City Clerk's Office are planning to lead a recruitment and selection process for this board in the coming months and this vacant position will be considered as part of that process. If you have any questions about this update, please contact the Public Appointments Secretariat at appoint@toronto.ca or 416-397-0088.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Received the letter (August 28, 2025) from the City Clerk for information.
