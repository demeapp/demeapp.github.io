Building Faster: Streamlining Housing Delivery and Strengthening the City’s Development Capacity

Adopted with changesOct 15, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the CreateTO board is noting council's direction for CreateTO to act as a development partner for housing projects on city lands.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on July 23 and 24, 2025, adopted Item EX25.1 as amended and, in so doing, as part of Council's previous request in Item EX19.1, Part 3, adopted by City Council in December 2024, requested the Board of Directors, CreateTO to direct the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO to advance work on housing opportunities at the direction of the City, including having CreateTO provide development and construction management services and act as a development partner for projects on City lands as part of the terms of the Master Services Agreement to be executed by CreateTO.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Directed the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO to advance work on housing opportunities at the direction of the City, including having CreateTO provide development and construction management services and act as a development partner for projects on City lands as part of the terms of the Master Services Agreement to be executed by CreateTO.
