Potential Future Community Recreation Centre in Liberty Village - by Deputy Mayor Ausma Malik, seconded by Councillor Amber Morley

Adopted with changesOct 15, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the CreateTO board is noting council's request to assess city-owned land in Liberty Village for a possible future community recreation centre.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on July 23 and 24, 2025, adopted Item MM32.51 and, in so doing, has requested the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO, in consultation with the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, the General Manager, Parks and Recreation, and other relevant agencies and divisions, to assess available City-owned real estate in Liberty Village, including parking lots, for the purposes of accommodating future community infrastructure, including a community recreation centre.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Directed the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO, in consultation with the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, the General Manager, Parks and Recreation, and other relevant agencies and divisions of the City of Toronto, to assess available City-owned real estate in Liberty Village, including parking lots, for the purposes of accommodating future community infrastructure, including a community recreation centre.
