3725 Bloor Street West - City-initiated Zoning By-law Amendment - Decision Report - Approval

Adopted with changesOct 15, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the CreateTO board is noting council's direction to advance the detailed design of the housing development at 3725 Bloor Street West.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on July 23 and 24, 2025, adopted Item PH23.2 and, in so doing, has requested the Board of Directors of CreateTO to direct the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO, in consultation with the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning and the Executive Director, Development Review, to engage City partners and the development partner to advance the detailed design of the development requirements identified in Attachment 7 to the report (June 30, 2025) from the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning and the Executive Director, Development Review, to the satisfaction of the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning and the Executive Director, Development Review, and to secure the delivery of the development requirements on terms, including details and timing, in a Lease Agreement with the development partner.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Directed the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO, in consultation with the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning and the Executive Director, Development Review, to: a. engage City partners and the development partner, to advance the detailed design of the development requirements identified in Attachment 7 to the report to City Council dated June 30, 2025, from the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning and the Executive Director, Development Review, to the satisfaction of the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning and the Executive Director, Development Review; and b. secure the delivery of the development requirements on terms, including details and timing, in a Lease Agreement with the development partner.
