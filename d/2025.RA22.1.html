Toronto City Council · Decision

Third Quarter Financial Results

Adopted with changesNov 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The CreateTO board votes on switching its financial reporting from every three months to every six months, along with receiving the third-quarter 2025 results. Spending was $0.88 million below budget, with annual spending expected at $15.39 million.

The official summary names a figure of $0.88 million.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

For the nine months ended September 30, 2025, CreateTO continues to operate on a net zero basis. Total expenditures are tracking $0.88 million lower than the budget, with annual expenditures expected to be in line with the approved budget of $15.39 million gross, $0 net. This report also proposes a change in the reporting frequency of financial results to the Board. Management is recommending CreateTO move from quarterly to semi-annual reporting to the Board.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO: 1. Directed the Chief Executive Officer to provide financial reporting to the Board of Directors on a semi-annual basis for only the Second Quarter results and Year End results. 2. Directed the Chief Executive Officer to provide each Board member with First Quarter and Third Quarter financial results by email, consistent with the material submitted to the City, with management available to address any questions at the Board meeting following. 3. Directed the Chief Executive Officer to continue presenting to the Board in a timely manner any significant transactions impacting the financial statements or not approved through the annual budget process.
