Application for Five Variances, Each Subject to Three Conditions, Respecting One Third Party Electronic Ground Sign - 10 Morse Street

PostponedMay 2, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A company wants a rule exception to put up a two-sided electronic billboard at 10 Morse Street. City staff say the Sign Variance Committee should say no.

Council put off deciding this item.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Zelinka Priamo Ltd (the "Applicant") has made an application for three variances, each subject to three conditions, required to allow the Chief Building Official ("CBO") to issue a sign permit for one third party electronic ground sign with two sign faces, displaying electronic static copy (the "Proposed Sign") at 10 Morse Street (the “Subject Premises”). The Proposed Sign would be located along the southern frontage of the Subject Premises, perpendicular to, and viewed by traffic travelling eastbound and westbound along Lake Shore Boulevard East. The Proposed Sign would have a maximum height of 8.0 metres from grade and is proposed to contain two sign faces in a back-to-back configuration, each measuring 3.05 metres by 10.70 metres, with sign face area of 32.65 square metres. What was decided

That the Sign Variance Committee: 1. Adjourned, at the request of the Applicant, Zelinka Priamo Ltd, the matter of an Application for Five Variances, Each Subject to Three conditions, Respecting One Third Party Electronic Ground Sign - 10 Morse Street, to its September 12, 2025 meeting.
