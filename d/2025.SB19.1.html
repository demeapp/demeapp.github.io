Supplementary Report: Application Respecting One Third Party Electronic Ground Sign at 10 Morse Street

AdoptedNov 28, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a follow-up on a billboard plan at 10 Morse Street with a revised proposal from the company. City staff say the committee should say no, with six conditions attached.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its September 12, 2025 meeting, the Sign Variance Committee (the "SVC") considered an application submitted by Zelinka Priamo Ltd (the "Applicant") seeking five variances, each subject to three conditions, required to allow the issuance of a sign permit for one third party electronic ground sign with two sign faces, displaying electronic static copy at 10 Morse Street (the “Subject Premises”). On September 5, 2025 the Applicant submitted a communication for the SVC consideration proposing substantial changes to the proposal, including the removal of one of the requested variances. For the purposes of reviewing and preparing a supplementary report in response to the revised proposal, the SVC adjourned the matter to the November 28, 2025 meeting and referred the matter back to the Chief Building Official and Executive Director, Toronto Building (the "CBO"). What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Refused to grant the requested variances to sections 694-22D, 694-22E, 694-25C(2)(f) and 694-25C(2)(h), each subject to six conditions, required to allow for the issuance of a permit respecting the erection and display of the Proposed Sign, as further described in Attachment 1 to the report (October 15, 2025) from the Project Director, Business Transformation and Citywide Priorities, Toronto Building.
