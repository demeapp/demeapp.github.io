Toronto City Council · Decision

Assumption of Services, Registered Plans 66M-2540, 67 Kirkdene Drive

AdoptedMar 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The city would take over roads and underground pipes built for the subdivision at 67 Kirkdene Drive, including Bluebird Place, now that the builder is done, if approved. The city's guarantee would be released. Council is asked to approve.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that the municipal services under the terms of the Subdivision Agreements for Plan 66M-2540, dated October 20, 2017, between Your Home Developments (Kirkdene) Inc. and the City of Toronto be assumed by the City.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council assume the services installed in the subdivision and that the City formally assume Bluebird Place road within the Registered Plan of Subdivision 66M-2540. 2. City Council authorize the Acting Director, Engineering Review to release the performance guarantee held with respect to the municipal services in accordance with the Subdivision Agreement. 3. City Council direct that an assumption By-law be prepared to assume the public highway and municipal services within the Registered Plan of Subdivision 66M-2540. 4. City Council authorize and direct the City Solicitor to register the assumption By-law in the Land Registry Office at the expense of the Owner. 5.
