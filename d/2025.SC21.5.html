1925 Victoria Park Avenue - Public Art Plan

AdoptedApr 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Approval of the public art plan for the building at 1925 Victoria Park Avenue, which says how the builder will add artwork to the north-facing wall is before council. Approval is a required step for the project.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this staff report is to seek City Council approval of the 1925 Victoria Park Avenue - Public Art Plan (“The Plan”). The Plan, which is included as Attachment 1 of this report, outlines the method by which the owner will commission public art that is integrated into the north-facing building facade. The Public Art Plan provides a framework and description of the commissioning of public art including: an overview of the site context; project background; vision for public art; proposed location; selection process; mentorship program; communications and maintenance strategies; budget and schedule. The attached Plan meets the objectives of the City's Percent for Public Art Guidelines and is supported by the Toronto Public Art Commission (TPAC).

What was decided

City Council on April 23 and 24, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve 1925 Victoria Park Avenue Public Art Plan in Attachment 1 to the report (March 17, 2025) from the Director, Urban Design, City Planning.
