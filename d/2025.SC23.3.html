3434 Lawrence Avenue East, Zoning By-law Amendment Application - Decision Report - Approval

City staff suggest changing zoning rules to allow two L-shaped buildings of 10 to 23 storeys at 3434 Lawrence Avenue East, with 855 homes, some shops, a new public road, and a new park.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of the application to amend Zoning By-law 569-2013, as amended, to permit the construction of two L-shaped residential buildings with heights ranging from 10 to 23 storeys at 3434 Lawrence Avenue East. A total of 855 units are proposed within a total gross floor area of approximately 59,794 square metres. A total of 559.5 square metres of ground floor non-residential floor area is also proposed. A new north-south public road located on the western boundary of the site and a new public park located to the east of the site would also form part of the proposed redevelopment. The proposed development is consistent with the Provincial Planning Statement (2024) and conforms to the City's Official Plan.
