Holswade Road - Traffic Calming (Speed Humps)

AdoptedNov 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Staff suggest adding seven speed humps on Holswade Road between Victoria Park Avenue and Pharmacy Avenue. The street passed the city's safety test; community council is asked to approve.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of speed humps on Holswade Road, between Victoria Park Avenue and Pharmacy Avenue. Staff's assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the Traffic Calming Policy has been satisfied. Therefore, speed humps should be installed on Holswade Road.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (seven speed humps) on Holswade Road, between Victoria Park Avenue and Pharmacy Avenue, generally as shown on Attachments 2 and 3, Drawings TC-593 and TC-593-1, to the report (November 10, 2025) from the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services.
