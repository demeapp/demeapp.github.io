Tom Wells Crescent - Parking Amendments

AdoptedNov 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Staff suggest changing overnight parking rules on Tom Wells Crescent: the current 2:00-6:00 a.m. ban on both sides would become a ban on only the east, north, and west sides, freeing the south side. Community council is asked to approve.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting the approval of amendments to the parking regulations on the south side of Tom Wells Crescent, between a point 91 metres north of Sanwood Boulevard (west intersection) and a point 49 metres further east. The proposed amendment will change the timing of existing parking prohibition, currently in effect from 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m., to be in effect at all times. The proposed amendments will enhance safety and facilitate easier vehicle movements for residents exiting their driveways on this section of the street. During the course of the investigation, Transportation Services identified a bylaw adopted in 2013 containing an entry error. Recommendation 6 in this report rescinds this bylaw.
