Bellamy Road South - Parking Amendments

AdoptedNov 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Staff suggest changing the short-term parking area for Bliss Carman Senior Public School on Bellamy Road South to help student pick-up and drop-off, adding a stopping ban during school hours. Community council is asked to approve.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that the existing short-term parking area, designated for Bliss Carman Senior Public School, on the west side of Bellamy Road South, be adjusted to better support student pick-up and drop-off activities for the school. During the course of the investigation, it was noted that there are discrepancies between the posted signs and the by-lawed parking regulations. Recommendations 1, 2 and 3 will correct these inconsistencies.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 10 minutes from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Bellamy Road South, between a point 66 metres north of Kingston Road and a point 63 metres further north. 2. Prohibited stopping from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east side of Bellamy Road South, between Kingston Road and a point 121 metres north. 3. Prohibited parking at all times on the west side of Bellamy Road South, between Kingston Road and a point 42 metres north.
