Toronto City Council · Decision

Placement of a Temporary Crossing Guard at the Intersection of Warden Avenue and Freeman Street

AdoptedNov 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A councillor asks community council to tell staff to place a temporary school crossing guard at Warden Avenue and Freeman Street until a safety study is done by end of 2026. Parents of Birchcliff Public School students raised concerns.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to respectfully request the placement of a temporary school crossing guard at the intersection of Warden Avenue and Freeman Street. The school parent community has continued to raise serious concerns about safety at this intersection. The location was flagged by residents as having an accident in previous years, in addition to this crossing being a main pedestrian pathway for students leaving Birchcliff Public School. Parents and school staff report that driver behaviour, including aggressive driving and failure to yield, further increases the risk for children and families walking to and from school. Given these ongoing concerns, and in light of the unique design and operating conditions at this intersection, I am requesting that Scarborough Community Council override the technical analysis and authorize the placement of a temporary school crossing guard at Warden Avenue…

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services, to install a temporary school crossing guard at the intersection of Warden Avenue and Freeman Street until a subsequent warrant analysis can be conducted by the end of the fourth quarter of 2026.
