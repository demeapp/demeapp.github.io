What the item proposed

The Customer Experience Division, through 311, provides access to non-emergency City services, programs, and information 24 hours a day, seven days a week. 311 offers assistance in over 180 languages over the phone, and online through self-service options using the City’s website and the 311 mobile app, and through email specifically for general inquiries or requests for information. This report responds to Council direction to provide an overview of trending issues received by 311 on an annual basis. In 2024, Customer Experience Division continued to provide timely, supportive, and effective customer service. Customer Experience Division received 1.29 million interactions, comprising mostly of general inquiries (62 percent) and service requests (32 percent), with fewer requests for status updates on existing service requests (6 percent).
