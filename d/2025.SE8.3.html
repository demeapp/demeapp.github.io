Transportation Services Salesforce and Maximo Integration

Adopted with changesJun 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the head of Transportation Services presents on joining up its software systems and improving customer service. Nothing is decided.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The General Manager, Transportation Services, will give a presentation on Transportation Services Salesforce and Maximo integration and provide an update on initiatives aimed at improving the customer service experience.

What was decided

The Service Excellence Committee: 1. Requested the Deputy City Manager, Corporate Services to report back to the Service Excellence Committee on the plan to integrate the customer relationship management system and operational work order management systems, and the history of the setup in 311 in 2009 by the end of the third quarter of 2025.
