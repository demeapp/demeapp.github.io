Council Member Appointments to the Canadian National Exhibition Association, Municipal Section and Board of Directors

AdoptedApr 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the Striking Committee suggests five councillors for the Canadian National Exhibition Association board, for a one-year term starting spring 2025. Council makes the final choices.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend to City Council the appointment of Council Members to the Canadian National Exhibition Association, Municipal Section and Board of Directors.

What was decided

City Council on April 23 and 24, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council appoint the following Members of Council to the Canadian National Exhibition Association, Municipal Section and Board of Directors for a term of office starting from the Annual Meeting in the Spring of 2025 to the Annual Meeting in the Spring of 2026, and until successors are appointed: Councillor Paul Ainslie Councillor Stephen Holyday Councillor Nick Mantas Deputy Mayor Ausma Malik Councillor Chris Moise
