Council Member Appointments to Fill Vacancies on Committees, Boards and External Bodies

AdoptedNov 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the Striking Committee suggests councillors to fill empty seats on several committees and boards, including the Executive and Budget Committees and Board of Health, until November 2026. Council makes the final choices.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend to City Council the appointment of Council Members to fill vacancies on the Executive Committee, Budget Committee, Striking Committee, Aboriginal Affairs Advisory Committee, Board of Health, Toronto Preservation Board, Toronto Symphony Orchestra Board, and Toronto and Region Conservation Authority Board of Directors.

What was decided

City Council on November 12 and 13, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council appoint Councillor Neethan Shan to the Executive Committee for a term of office until November 14, 2026, and until a successor is appointed. 2. City Council appoint Councillor Jamaal Myers to the Budget Committee for a term of office until November 14, 2026, and until a successor is appointed. 3. City Council appoint Councillor Neethan Shan to the Striking Committee for a term of office until November 14, 2026, and until a successor is appointed. 4. City Council appoint Councillor Rachel Chernos Lin to the Board of Health for a term of office until November 14, 2026, and until a successor is appointed. 5. City Council appoint Councillor Neethan Shan to the Toronto and Region Conservation Authority Board of Directors for a term of office until November 14, 2026, and until a successor is appointed. 6.
