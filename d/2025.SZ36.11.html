Proposed January 2026 Schedule of Property Standards Committee, Toronto and East York Panel Meetings

AdoptedDec 8, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Property Standards Committee's Toronto and East York Panel set its January 2026 meeting schedule for hearing property appeals. Meetings are held remotely; no rules are changing.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with Section 9A of the Consolidated Rules of Procedure for the Property Standards Committee, as amended, each Panel shall adopt a schedule of meetings to hear appeals for each year. Please note that meetings for the Property Standards Committee, Toronto and East York Panel will be held remotely until further notice.

What was decided

The Property Standards Committee, Toronto and East York Panel: 1. Approved the following meeting date for January 2026: a. Monday, January 26, 2026.
