Re-Open Item TA11.5 titled “Direct Investment Request 2025 C”

AdoptedNov 24, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the board re-opens its vote on the $3,500,000, unfunded loan guarantee (Direct Investment Request 2025 C) to fix the wording. The guarantee covers part of a $3.5 million loan facility; no new spending is proposed.

The official summary names a figure of $3,500,000.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
