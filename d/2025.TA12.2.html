New Appointments to the Toronto Atmospheric Fund Board

Received for informationNov 24, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City Council appointed Jehad Aliweiwi and Elizabeth McCallion to the board for October 2025 to October 2029, replacing two members, and reappointed Jean Olemou. The board just notes the appointments.

Received for information — Council took no action on it.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting held on October 8 and 9, 2025, City Council appointed the following members to the Toronto Atmospheric Fund Board for a term of office from October 10, 2025, to October 11, 2029, and until successors are appointed: Jehad Aliweiwi; and Elizabeth McCallion These new appointments fill the positions previously held by Stuart Galloway and Kim Marshall whose terms of office came to an end on July 15, 2025. City Council also reappointed Jean Olemou for a term of office from October 10, 2025, to October 11, 2029, and until a successor is appointed.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund received the item for information.
