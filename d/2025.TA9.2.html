Peak Power

The CEO of Peak Power, a company the fund invested in back in 2021, updated the board on its progress. This is for information only; no policy change is proposed.

The official record

What the item proposed

In 2021, along with other co-investors, the Toronto Atmospheric Fund made a direct investment in Peak Power based on due diligence that indicated the Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s investment would enable this local company with a strong mandate alignment to realize its growth potential. A presentation by Peak Power’s Chief Executive Officer, Derek Sim Loo, will showcase the company’s progress since the Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s initial investment and our work together to achieve mutual objectives.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Received the report (February 3, 2025) from the Vice President, Impact Investing, Toronto Atmospheric Fund for information.
