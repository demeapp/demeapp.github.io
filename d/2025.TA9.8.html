What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of the balance of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s endowment-funded program budget for 2025.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Approved an allocation of $733,162 to support the Toronto Atmospheric Fund programs in 2025 as outlined below, to be funded from the Canada, Ontario, and Toronto endowments except where otherwise indicated: a. Accelerating EV Uptake: $56,250 to expand the availability of affordable public EV charging; b. Accelerating Net-Zero-Ready New Construction: $75,000 to progressively strengthen codes and standards across the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area and advance supportive policy frameworks to ensure all new construction is net-zero ready by 2030; c. Accelerating Clean Electrification: $127,500 to scale adoption of local energy solutions and clean electricity supply in support of the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area’s transition to a net-zero grid; d.
