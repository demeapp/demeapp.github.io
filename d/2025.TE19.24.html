182-184 Spadina Avenue - Construction Staging Area

AdoptedFeb 5, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council voted on closing parts of Spadina Avenue and Cameron Street for four years for hotel construction at 182-184 Spadina Avenue. The item lists a $4.00 fee.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As Spadina Avenue is classified as a major arterial street, City Council approval of this report is required. Manga Hotels is constructing a 15-storey hotel at 182-184 Spadina Avenue. The site is located on the west side of Spadina Avenue, north of Queen Street West. Transportation Services is requesting authorization to close the west sidewalk and a portion of the parking lay-by on Spadina Avenue, from a point 85 metres north of Queen Street West to a point 15 metres further north. Pedestrian movements on the west side of Spadina Avenue will be accommodated in a temporary pedestrian walkway within the closed parking layby. Additionally, authorization is being requested to close the east sidewalk and northbound lane on Cameron Street.
