Community council decided on extending the lane and sidewalk closure on Maitland Street to December 31, 2026, for construction at 20-26 Maitland Street.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. TMG Builders has undertaken the construction of a 40-storey residential building with two levels of underground parking. The site is located on the north side of Maitland Street, between Yonge Street and Church Street. At its meeting of January 24, 2024, Toronto and East York Community Council authorized the temporary closure of the westbound lane on Maitland Street and a portion of the sidewalk on the north side of Maitland Street, from January 25, 2024 to January 31, 2025. At the time, the developer indicated they would require the staging area for a total of 36 months, from January 25, 2024 to December 31, 2026, however, the occupation was only approved for a 12 month period. The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the continuation of the closure of the north sidewalk and a 4-metre-wide portion of the westbound north curb lane on Maitland Street, between a point 78 metres east of Yonge Street and a point 90 metres further east, and provision of a temporary pedestrian walkway within the closed portion of Maitland Street, from February 1, 2025 to February 1, 2026. 2. Directed the applicant to continue to sweep the construction site, adjacent sidewalks and roadways daily, or more frequently as needed to be cleared of any construction debris and made safe. 3.
