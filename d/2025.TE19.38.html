Toronto City Council · Decision

Installation of a Stop Sign at Ramp Merge of Don Valley Parkway (DVP) Ramp to Bayview Avenue Southbound

AdoptedJan 14, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Community council voted on a temporary stop sign where the DVP southbound off-ramp meets Bayview Avenue. It stays until bridge work finishes.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Engineering Construction Services has undertaken rehabilitation work on the Don Valley Parkway interchange at Bayview Avenue. The project involves rehabilitation of bridge and consists of multiple phases near the Don Valley Parkway southbound off-ramp at Bayview Avenue. Prior to construction, vehicular traffic from the Don Valley Parkway off-ramp would merge free flow on to the southbound acceleration lane on Bayview Avenue. The southbound lane is currently closed to facilitate construction staging operations. As a result, vehicular traffic can longer merge on to Bayview Avenue safely without stopping. Therefore, Transportation Services recommends the installation of a stop control at the southbound off-ramp of Don Valley Parkway at Bayview Avenue. The stop control will serve as a temporary condition during construction and will be removed following the completion of the project phase…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized compulsory stop control for southbound traffic at the Don Valley Parkway Bayview Avenue South off-ramp at Bayview Avenue until reopening of the southbound acceleration lane of Bayview Avenue.
