Temporary Traffic and Parking Amendments - Lake Shore Boulevard West, Stadium Road and Eireann Quay

AdoptedFeb 20, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Community council voted on temporary turn and lane changes around Lake Shore Boulevard West for watermain and track work. The work runs February to August 2025.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Engineering and Construction Services (ECS) and Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) are planning to replace the watermain and streetcar tacks at the intersection of Bathurst Street and Lake Shore Blvd West/Fleet Street, from February 17, 2025 to August 1, 2025. The project involves multiple construction phases where traffic lanes will be reduced to one lane in each direction. In order to improve traffic operations at the subject intersection, all turns will be prohibited during construction. These turn restrictions will impact access/egress for area residents and Billy Bishop Airport. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing northbound left-turn prohibition in effect 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. Monday to Friday, except public holidays, on Stadium Road and Lake Shore Boulevard West. 2. Rescinded the existing eastbound right-turn prohibition in effect 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. Monday to Friday, except public holidays on Stadium Road and Lake Shore Boulevard West. 3. Rescinded the existing northbound left-turn prohibition in effect at all times on Eireann Quay and Queens Quay West. 4. Prohibited standing at all times on the west side of Stadium Road, between Bishop Tutu Boulevard and Queens Quay West.
