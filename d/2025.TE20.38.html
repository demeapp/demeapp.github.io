Temporary Left-Turn Prohibition - Elm Street at Yonge Street

AdoptedFeb 20, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Community council voted on banning eastbound left turns at Elm Street and Yonge Street until November 2027. A covered walkway blocks drivers' view.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. By way of background, at its meeting on November 8 and 9, 2023, City Council adopted Item 2023.TE8.24 entitled “Construction Staging Area – 8 Elm Street” and in so doing, authorized a closure of the west sidewalk and southbound curb lane, between Elm Street and a point 40 metres north, with a 2.1 metre covered and protected walkway in the closed curb lane, from November 30, 2023, to November 30, 2027. Transportation Services has received sightline obstruction concerns from area residents due to the above mentioned covered and protected walkway on the northwest corner of Yonge Street and Elm Street. Based on a site investigation, staff has determined that the covered walkway creates a sightline obstruction for eastbound motorists on Elm Street, turning left at…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited eastbound left-turns at all times on Elm Street at Yonge Street, from January 15, 2025 to November 30, 2027.
