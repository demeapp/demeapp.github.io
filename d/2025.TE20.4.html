Toronto City Council · Decision

Council voted on permanently closing a public laneway between 10A and 16 Eastern Avenue. The land would be sold to a neighbouring owner.

Official title: Permanent Closure of the Public Laneway between 10A Eastern Avenue and 16 Eastern Avenue

AdoptedMar 26, 2025 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As this report recommends the permanent closure of a public laneway, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services recommends that the public laneway known as Ln W Gilead Pl N Eastern Ave and between 10A Eastern Avenue and 16 Eastern Avenue, shown as Part 5 on the registered plan of survey attached as Attachment 1 of this report, be permanently stopped up and closed. Once closed, it is intended that the former public laneway will be sold to an adjacent property owner to improve short-term access to 10A and 16 Eastern Avenue and to facilitate future re-development of the broader site. The transaction is approved pursuant to the delegated authority set out in Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 213.

What was decided

City Council on March 26 and 27, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the permanent closure of the public laneway shown as Part 5 on Plan 66R-32512 attached as Attachment 1 to the report (February 3, 2025) from the Director, Planning, Design and Management, Transportation Services. 2. City Council enact a By-law substantially in the form of the draft By-law attached as Attachment 2 to the report (February 3, 2025) from the Director, Planning, Design and Management, Transportation Services.
