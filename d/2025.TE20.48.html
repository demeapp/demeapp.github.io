Community council voted on banning stopping at all times on Jarvis and Lower Jarvis south of Queen. The current ban is rush hours only.

Official title: Parking Amendment - No Stopping Anytime on Jarvis Street and Lower Jarvis Street

AdoptedFeb 20, 2025 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I’m writing to request support to shift from the current "No Stopping 7:30-9:30 AM and 3:30-6:30 PM" to a more effective "No Stopping Anytime" along Lower Jarvis and Jarvis Street, South of Queen. This change aims to deter illegal parking and enhance traffic flow. Moreover, with the imminent completion of St. Lawrence Market North, we anticipate increased pedestrian and vehicular traffic. By ensuring smoother traffic flow on the ground level and directing parking to the future Green P Parking garage to the north, we can keep traffic along Jarvis and Lower Jarvis flowing smoothly and safely.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Jarvis Street, between The Esplanade and a point 61 metres north of Queen Street East. 2. Rescinded the stopping prohibition in effect from 7:30 a.m. to 9:30 a.m., and from 3:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, except public holidays, on the west side of Jarvis Street, between Front Street East and a point 77 metres south of Dundas Street East. 3. Prohibited parking at all times on the west side of Lower Jarvis Street, between The Esplanade and Front Street East. 4. Prohibited parking at all times on the west side of Jarvis Street, between King Street East and a point 61 metres north of Queen Street East. 5.
