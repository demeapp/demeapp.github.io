Toronto City Council · Decision

Community council voted on extending permit parking hours on Nursewood Road. Residents would get priority parking.

Official title: Re-Opening of Item TE17.55 - Amendment of Permit Parking hours of operation on Nursewood Road, Parking Permit Area 9D

AdoptedFeb 20, 2025 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office has received ongoing concerns from residents on Nursewood Road regarding traffic and parking challenges on their street. To help alleviate these issues and better support residents, I am requesting an extension of permit parking hours to prioritize residential parking and improve accessibility for those who live in the area.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item TE17.55, entitled “Parking Amendments - Nursewood Road” with respect to Part 5 of the decision of Toronto and East York Community Council. 2. Deleted Part 5 of its decision with respect to Item TE17.55 and adopt the following new Recommendations: 1. Toronto and East York Community Council rescind the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily on the odd (east) side of Nursewood Road, from 235 metres south of Queen Street East to Queen Street East. 2. Toronto and East York Community Council authorize parking by permit only to be in effect from 4:00 p.m. to 9:00 a.m. daily on the odd (east) side of Nursewood Road, from the south end of Nursewood Road to Queen Street East.
