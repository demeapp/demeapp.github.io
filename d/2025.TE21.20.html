120 Peter Street, 122-124 Peter Street, 126-128 Peter Street and 357-359 Richmond Street West - Alterations to, Demolition and Construction of, Buildings under Sections 42 of the Ontario Heritage Act and Authority to Amend a Heritage Easement Agreement

AdoptedApr 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council voted on changes to heritage properties on Peter Street and Richmond Street West for a new 40-storey building. One heritage house would be altered and others demolished.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve alterations, demolition and new construction works for the properties at 120, 122-124, 126-128 Peter Street and 357-359 Richmond Street West under Part V, Section 42 of the Ontario Heritage Act, in connection with the development of the site consisting of a new 40-storey mixed-use structure integrated with new and heritage base buildings. This report also recommends that City Council grant authority to amend the existing Heritage Easement Agreement (or execute a new Heritage Easement Agreement if needed), in connection with the proposed development of the property. The subject property is situated in the King-Spadina Heritage Conservation District (KSHCD).
