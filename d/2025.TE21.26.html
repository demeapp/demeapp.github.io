1 Roxborough Street West - Construction Staging Area

Community council decided on closing Paul Hahn Lane for 7 months for condo construction at 1 Roxborough Street West. The item lists a $4.25 fee.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. North Drive is constructing a 12-storey residential condominium building at 1 Roxborough Street West. The site is located on the southwest corner of Yonge Street and Roxborough Street West. The project will be undertaken in multiple phases, with varying degrees of sidewalk and traffic lane closures on Roxborough Street West, as well as a full closure of Paul Hahn Lane. Phase 1 of the project will require a full closure of Paul Hahn Lane, between Roxborough Street West and a point 49 metres south for a period of 7 months, to facilitate excavation and shorting activities.
