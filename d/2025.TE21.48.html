Community council voted on speed bumps for the laneway south of Davenport Road east of Salem Avenue. Residents surveyed supported them.

Official title: Installation of Speed Bumps on Public Laneway South of Davenport Road East of Salem Avenue

AdoptedApr 3, 2025 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Speeding vehicles travelling down the public laneway south of Davenport Road and east of Salem Avenue has been a constant issue for residents in the neighbourhood. With residents and their families in this laneway, there have been many unfortunate experiences involving dangerous drivers. In a neighbourhood survey conducted by my office, residents in the area overwhelmingly confirmed their support for the installation of traffic calming measures that will reduce speeding and improve safety.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of speed bumps in the public lane south of Davenport Road, east of Salem Avenue (north-south branch), between lane south of Davenport Road, east of Salem Avenue North (east-west branch) and the south end, as shown on Attachment 1 to the letter (March 18, 2025) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo.
