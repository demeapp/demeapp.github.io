Bellevue Avenue and Denison Square - Accessible Parking Spaces

Adopted with changesMay 1, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Community council voted on rejecting two accessible parking spaces near the Kiever Synagogue. Staff say they don't meet the City's guidelines.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The Ward Councillor, in consultation with The Kiever Synagogue, is requesting approval for two on-street accessible parking spaces in the vicinity of the synagogue. These installations are intended to replace off-street spaces lost through the redevelopment of the adjacent Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) lot on Bellevue Avenue, they are not intended to serve a specific applicant. Accordingly, they are not supported under Transportation Services’ guidelines for the installation of accessible parking spaces.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Designated an on-street accessible parking space in effect at all times on the west side of Bellevue Avenue, from a point 21 metres north of Denison Square to a point 5.5 metres further south. 2. Designated an on-street accessible parking space in effect at all times on the north side of Denison Square, from a point 20 metres east of Bellevue Avenue to a point 5.5 metres further west.
