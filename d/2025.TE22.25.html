Breadalbane Street - Streetscape Improvements and Parking Bylaw Amendments

AdoptedMay 1, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Community council voted on parking changes on Breadalbane Street for road and streetscape work. Paid parking would drop from 19 to 15 spaces.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Breadalbane Street, extending between Yonge Street and Bay Street, is scheduled for road resurfacing and streetscape improvements in 2025. The project includes curb extensions with integrated green infrastructure and seating features associated with a midblock pedestrian connection, between the Opera Place Park and Dr. Lilian McGregor Park. Community Council approval of on-street parking by-law amendments along the south side of Breadalbane Street is required to facilitate proposed streetscape improvements, which will be delivered in conjunction with the road resurfacing work in 2025. The proposed amendments would reduce the existing 19 paid on-street parking spaces in this section of Breadalbane Street to 15 spaces.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved amendments to the Traffic and Parking By-laws for Breadalbane Street between Yonge Street and Bay Street as described in Attachment 2 to the report (April 10, 2025) from the Director, Planning, Design and Management, Transportation Services.
