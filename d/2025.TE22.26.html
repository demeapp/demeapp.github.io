Community council voted on banning standing on Albany Avenue north of Dupont Street. The change keeps two-way traffic moving.

Official title: Albany Avenue - Parking Amendments

AdoptedMay 1, 2025 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that the existing parking prohibition on both sides of Albany Avenue, north of Dupont Street, be amended to a standing prohibition. The proposed amendment will deter parking on this section of Albany Avenue which will improve unobstructed two-way traffic flow and provide a safer environment, particularly for vulnerable road users.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the east side of Albany Avenue, between Dupont Street and a point 61.6 metres north of Dupont Street. 2. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Albany Avenue, between Bloor Street West and a point 61.9 metres north of Dupont Street, to be in effect between Bloor Street West and Dupont Street. 3. Prohibited standing at all times on the east side of Albany Avenue, between Dupont Street and a point 62 metres north. 4. Prohibited standing at all times on the west side of Albany Avenue, between Dupont Street and a point 62 metres north.
