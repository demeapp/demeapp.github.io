Toronto City Council · Decision

Tracy Street - Traffic Calming (Speed Hump)

AdoptedMay 1, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Community council voted on replacing two old speed humps on Tracy Street with one speed hump. The old humps are often damaged during snow removal.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of a speed hump on Tracy Street, between Sydenham Street and Shuter Street. This report will replace the existing two speed bumps on this street with a single speed hump. This change will continue to provide traffic calming on this roadway and eliminate the frequent damage and dislodging of the existing speed bumps during snow removal operations.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing speed bumps on Tracy Street, between Sydenham Street and Shuter Street, as shown on Attachment 2 to the report (April 11, 2025) from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services. 2. Authorized the installation of a speed hump on Tracy Street, between Sydenham Street and Shuter Street. 3. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a By-law to alter the roadway for the installation of one speed hump on Tracy Street, between Sydenham Street and Shuter Street, generally as shown on Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (April 11, 2025) from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services.
