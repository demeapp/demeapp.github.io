Toronto City Council · Decision

Vehicle Width Restriction - Lane north of Lappin Avenue, east of Emerson Avenue

AdoptedMay 1, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Community council voted on banning vehicles wider than two metres from the lane north of Lappin Avenue. The ban aims to reduce property damage.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is seeking authority to prohibit vehicles greater than two metres in width from travelling on the lane first north of Lappin Avenue, east of Emerson Avenue. Prohibiting vehicles which have a width of two metres or more from travelling on the subject lane section would be beneficial in reducing the incidence of damage to the adjacent private property.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited vehicles over two metres in width from travelling the lane first north of Lappin Avenue, east of Emerson Avenue, between Emerson Avenue and the lane south of Dupont Street, east of Emerson Avenue (north-south segment).
