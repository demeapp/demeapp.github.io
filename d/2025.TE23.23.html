Toronto City Council · Decision

Use of Nathan Phillips Square for Various Events - October 1, 2025 - April 30, 2026

AdoptedJun 25, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council voted on letting event organizers at Nathan Phillips Square serve alcohol in wine gardens from October 2025 to April 2026. Provincial and health approvals are required.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The City of Toronto provides opportunities to access Nathan Phillips Square for eligible events through a permitting process administered by the Economic Development and Culture Division. The purpose of this report is to request exemptions from Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 636, "Public Squares", and former City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 237, “Nathan Phillips Square”, for events that are proposed to be held on Nathan Phillips Square from October 1, 2025 - April 30, 2026.

What was decided

City Council on June 25 and 26, 2025, adopted the following: 1.
