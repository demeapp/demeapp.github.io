The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Yonge Street, City Council approval of this report is required. Mizrahi Development Group (The One) Inc. (the "developer") is currently the owner of a mixed-use development at 1 Bloor Street West (Mizrahi Project). The site is located on the southwest corner of Yonge Street and Bloor Street West. The development will consist of an eight-storey high retail podium and an 86-storey high residential tower. By way of background, Toronto and East York Community Council, at its meeting on November 25 and 26, 2020, approved the construction staging area on Balmuto Street from November 30, 2020 to November 30, 2022. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Deferred consideration of the Item until the July 8, 2025 meeting of the Toronto and East York Community Council.
