Traffic Circulation Changes, Traffic Calming (Speed Humps) and Parking Amendments - West Parkdale Neighbourhood

AdoptedJun 5, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Community council voted on West Parkdale traffic changes. Macdonell Avenue would become one-way northbound and speed humps would be added on Wabash Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting approval to convert Macdonell Avenue from Rideau Avenue to Fern Avenue from one-way southbound for motor vehicles to one-way northbound for motor vehicles, while maintaining two-way travel for cyclists. The purpose of this change is to address neighbourhood traffic infiltration concerns as part of the West Parkdale Cycling Connections project. In conjunction with this change, on-street parking is recommended to be moved from the west side of Macdonell Avenue to the east side between Rideau Avenue and Fern Avenue. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of speed humps on Wabash Avenue between Sorauren Avenue and Macdonell Avenue in the West Parkdale neighbourhood. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended traffic and parking regulations required in Toronto Municipal Code Chapters 925 and 950, as generally described in Attachment 1 to the report (May 20, 2025) from the Director, Planning, Design and Management, Transportation Services. 2. Authorized the installation of speed humps on Wabash Avenue between Sorauren Avenue and MacDonell Avenue. 3. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a By-law to alter the roadway for the installation of speed humps, generally as shown on Attachment 3 to the report (May 20, 2025) from the Director, Planning, Design and Management, Transportation Services.
