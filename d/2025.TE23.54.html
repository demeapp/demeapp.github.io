Speed Humps on Stanhope Avenue between O’Connor Drive and Hopedale Avenue

AdoptedJun 5, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Community council voted on temporarily installing four speed humps on Stanhope Avenue from July 2025 to December 2028. Nearby Ontario Line construction has raised traffic concerns.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents on Stanhope Avenue, between O’Connor Drive and Hopedale Avenue, have expressed repeated concerns about speeding vehicles and occasional truck traffic on their local street. While Stanhope Avenue is not a designated haul route for the Metrolinx’s Ontario Line construction, it is located in close proximity to Metrolinx’s Minton Portal construction site, where major works are underway and are expected to continue until 2028. Residents have reported that construction-related activity in the surrounding area has led to increased traffic volumes and speeding, impacting their safety and livability. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the temporary installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Stanhope Avenue between O’Connor Drive and Hopedale Avenue from July 1, 2025 to December 31, 2028. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a By-law to alter the roadway to temporarily install four speed humps on Stanhope Avenue between O’Connor Drive and Hopedale Avenue, generally as shown on Attachment 2 to the letter (May 20, 2025) from Councillor Paula Fletcher.
