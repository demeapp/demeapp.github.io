Toronto City Council · Decision

Location of Hoverlink terminal adjacent to the Bathurst Quay Neighbourhood at Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport

AdoptedJun 5, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Community council voted on asking staff to report on a proposed private hovercraft service between Toronto and St. Catharines. The terminal would be near Billy Bishop airport.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

There is a proposal by Hoverlink Ontario, a private entity, to operate a rapid hovercraft transit service between Toronto and St. Catharines in the Niagara region. The proposed 30-minute service is to start in Spring 2027, at the earliest. The proposed Toronto landing pad and passenger waiting area would be located in Ward 10, Spadina-Fort York on the south side of the western channel at Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport. Hoverlink has entered into an agreement with PortsToronto. Passengers would use the Billy Bishop Airport tunnel or ferry to access the mainland and local transportation options in Bathurst Quay.
