The official record

What the item proposed

I am kindly asking for your support of my letter that will re-install on-street parking options to residents on Austin Terrace. In December 2024, Toronto and East York Community Council adopted TE18.66 addressing traffic concerns that resulted from an installation of a new sidewalk on Austin Terrace. This motion is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending making amendments to TE18.66 to reinstate parking on the south side of Austin Terrace between Lyndhurst Court and a point 20 metres west, where there is space available to reinstate parking on a portion of the south side of Austin Terrace.

What was decided

Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item TE18.66, titled "Addressing Traffic Concerns on Austin Terrace.” 2. Deleted Parts 9 and 10 of its decision with respect to Item TE18.66 and adopted the following new additional recommendations: 1. Prohibit parking at all times on the south side of Austin Terrace, between a point 20 metres west of Lyndhurst Court and a point 33 metres further west. 2. Prohibit parking at all times on the south side of Austin Terrace, between Lyndhurst Court and a point 74 metres east. 3. Authorize parking for a maximum period of one hour from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Austin Terrace, between Lyndhurst Court and a point 20 metres west. 4.
