Borden Street, between Bloor Street West and Harbord Street - Traffic Calming

AdoptedJun 5, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Community council voted on six speed humps on Borden Street between Bloor and Harbord. Residents asked for measures to slow speeding drivers.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents on Borden Street are frustrated by frequent speeding on their street endangering pedestrians and cyclists. They have requested the installation of speed humps, which Transportation Services agree would be feasible. Speed humps have made surrounding streets safer and residents are eager to see them installed on Borden Street.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of speed humps on Borden Street, between Bloor Street West and Harbord Street. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a By-law to alter the roadway for the installation of six speed humps on Borden Street, between Bloor Street West and Harbord Street, generally as shown on Attachment 1 to the letter (June 4, 2025) from Councillor Dianne Saxe.
