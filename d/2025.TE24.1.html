1930-1938 Bloor Street West and 3, 5 and 21 Quebec Avenue - Rental Housing Demolition Application - Decision Report - Approval

AdoptedJul 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Council voted on demolishing 12 rental homes at 1930-1938 Bloor Street West. The owner would build 12 replacement rentals kept for at least 20 years. If the owner doesn't finish the replacement building on time, the City can charge $20,000.00 per demolished unit.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of a Rental Housing Demolition application which proposes to demolish 12 rental dwelling units located at 1930-1938 Bloor Street West and 3, 5 and 21 Quebec Avenue. The 12 rental units are proposed to be replaced as part of the new development on the site. The proposal includes a Tenant Assistance Plan that addresses the right of existing tenants to return to replacement rental units at similar rents and provides financial compensation to mitigate hardship. The proposed development on the site is the subject of a related Zoning By-law Amendment application (24 121126 STE 04 OZ) which was appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT). A settlement offer was endorsed by City Council on February 5, 2025.
