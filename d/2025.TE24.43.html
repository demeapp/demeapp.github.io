Council voted on closing parts of Yonge and Colborne streets for 10 months for demolition at 53-55 Yonge Street.

Official title: 53-55 Yonge Street - Construction Staging Area

AdoptedJul 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As Yonge Street is classified as a major arterial street, City Council approval of this report is required. PCL Constructors Canada Inc. is demolishing the properties at 53 and 55 Yonge Street in preparation for a future development. The site is located on the southeast corner of Yonge Street and Colborne Street. In order to facilitate construction staging operations for the development, Transportation Services is requesting authorization to temporarily close the following: - A portion of the east sidewalk and northbound curb lane of Yonge Street between Wellington Street East and Colborne Street, for a period of 10 months, from July 28, 2025 to May 1, 2026; and - A portion of the south sidewalk and eastbound curb lane on Colborne Street between Yonge Street and Scott Street, for a period of 10 months, from July 28, 2025 to May 1, 2026. City Council authorize the closure of a 3.3 metre wide portion of the eastbound curb lane on Colborne Street, between a point 35 metres west of Scott Street and a point 16 metres further west, from July 28, 2025 to May 1, 2026, inclusive. 4.
