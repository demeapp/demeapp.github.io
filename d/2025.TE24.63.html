Council voted on banning northbound left turns from Foundry Avenue onto Davenport Road during rush hours. The ban aims to reduce backups.

Official title: Davenport Village Traffic Safety Plan - Left Turns on Foundry Avenue

AdoptedJul 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

As part of the Davenport Village Traffic Safety Plan, a prohibition on northbound left turns on Foundry Avenue at Davenport road was proposed. Daily northbound vehicle backups along Foundry Avenue, caused by attempts to make left turns, create noise and dangerous intersection movements. This prohibition will reduce traffic conflicts and improve overall safety. As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Davenport, City Council approval of this recommendation is required.

What was decided

City Council on July 23 and 24, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit northbound left-turns, from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and 3:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, on Foundry Avenue at Davenport Road.
