St. George Street - No Standing within the On-Street Logistics Mini-hub Zone

AdoptedJul 8, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Community council voted on banning standing on the west side of St. George Street at a delivery mini-hub. Illegally parked vehicles block delivery trucks.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The current dedicated On-Street Logistic Mini-hub Parking zone on St. George Street, which was approved by City Council in June 2024 and currently operated by Purolator Incorporated, is experiencing frequent instances of vehicles parking illegally within this designated area. This mini-hub zone, located in a parking lay-by, provides room for the re-purposed shipping container and short-term truck parking to allow Purolator's loading and unloading activities. The illegally parked or stopped vehicles are impacting Purolator’s daily operations. Implementing a ‘No Standing’ zone in this location will clearly communicate that stopping or waiting in these areas is prohibited at all times, thereby discouraging illegal parking behavior and allow enforcement officers with a clear legal basis to issue tickets or remove vehicles.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited standing at all times, on the west side of St. George Street, between a point 88.7 metres north of Ursula Franklin Street and a point 15.3 metres further north.
