27 Grosvenor Street and 26 Grenville Street - Zoning By-law Amendment and Amendment to Section 37 Agreement

AdoptedOct 8, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City Council voted on updating the deal for a two-tower project at 27 Grosvenor Street and 26 Grenville Street: fewer one-bedroom but new studio affordable rentals, a longer 99-year affordability period, and a relocated child care centre.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On May 5 and 6, 2021, City Council approved a Zoning By-law Amendment application to permit a mixed-use building with two towers, 32 and 46 storeys in height, connected by a shared podium at 27 Grosvenor Street and 26 Grenville Street. As part of the approval, Council directed staff to enter into an agreement under Section 37 of the Planning Act to secure the provision of 231 affordable rental units and a non-profit childcare centre, including specific terms related to unit mix and the location of the childcare centre. The proposal has been modified since the original approval, including changes to the affordable rental unit mix and the location of the child care centre.
