60 Queen Street East - Construction Staging Area Time Extension

Adopted with changesSep 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council voted on extending the Dalhousie Street construction closure near Queen Street East to September 30, 2028, widening it for a 57-storey building at 60 Queen Street East.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Deltera Inc. has undertaken the construction of a 57-storey mixed use building at 60 Queen Street East. The site is located on the northwest corner of Queen Street East and Dalhousie Street. At its meeting of November 15, 2023, Toronto and East York Community Council authorized the temporary closure of the west sidewalk and a 4.2 metre wide portion of the southbound lane on Dalhousie Street, from November 16, 2023 to December 31, 2024, to facilitate construction staging operations. At the time of this report, the below grade construction is underway. The next phase of construction will require a larger footprint to accommodate a material hoist and additional equipment. Authorized the closure of the west sidewalk and a 5.5 metre wide portion of the southbound on Dalhousie Street, between Queen Street East and a point 33 metres north, from September 19, 2025 to February 31, 2027, inclusive. 2.
