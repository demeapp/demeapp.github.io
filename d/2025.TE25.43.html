Pay-and-Display and On-Street Residential Permit Parking - Dovercourt Road - September 2025 (Delegated)

AdoptedSep 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council voted on adding $3.00 paid parking on the west side of Dovercourt Road south of Dundas Street West, replacing free one-hour parking, with overnight permit parking kept in place.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) has worked with Transportation Services to identify a new on-street paid parking opportunity in Ward 9 that will support the local community and area businesses by encouraging a regular turnover in the on-street parking supply. Through this review, Transportation Services is requesting approval for the installation of on-street paid parking on Dovercourt Road in Ward 9. The proposed parking changes will replace the existing parking restrictions currently in effect, including restricted one-hour unpaid parking. The proposed changes will not impact the flow of traffic and will encourage parking turnover. Transportation Services is also requesting approval to amend the existing permit parking hours on the west side of Dovercourt Road…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing one-hour parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, and from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Saturday, on the west side of Dovercourt Road, between a point 30.5 metres south of Dundas Street West and a point 15 metres north of Mackenzie Crescent. 2. Authorized the installation of parking machines to be in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Sunday, at a rate of $3.00 per hour for a maximum period of 2 hours, on the west side of Dovercourt Road, between Dundas Street West and Mackenzie Crescent. 3. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily, except no parking anytime Monday to Saturday from Dundas Street West to Harrison Street, and no parking from 8:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, from St. Annes Road to a point 122 metres…
