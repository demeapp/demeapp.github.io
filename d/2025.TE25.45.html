Howard Street and Redrocket Lane - Traffic and Parking Amendments

AdoptedSep 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council voted on parking and stopping changes on Howard Street and Redrocket Lane for a new building at 603-611 Sherbourne Street. The report lists parking rates of $3.00 and $2.00 in the affected regulations.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting approval for traffic and parking amendments on Howard Street and Redrocket Lane for a new development located at 603-611 Sherbourne Street and 4-6 Howard Street. The proposed traffic and parking amendments will address requirements of the site plan agreement on Howard Street and Redrocket Lane.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited stopping at all times on the south side of Howard Street, between Bleecker Street and a point 15 metres west. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., and from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, except public holidays, on the south side of Howard Street, between Sherbourne Street and Glen Road. 3. Prohibited parking from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., and from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, except public holidays, on the south side of Howard Street, between Sherbourne Street and a point 15 metres west of Bleecker Street. 4. Prohibited parking from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., and from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, except public holidays, on the south side of Howard Street, between Bleecker Street and Glen Road. 5.
