Toronto City Council · Decision

Introduction of Overnight On-Street Permit Parking - Permit Area 9A Thyra Avenue

AdoptedSep 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council voted on overnight permit parking on the west side of Thyra Avenue between Danforth Avenue and Dentonia Park Avenue, replacing the monthly side-switching rules, at residents' request.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that overnight on-street permit parking be introduced on the west side of Thyra Avenue from Danforth Avenue to Dentonia Park Avenue, 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily, within Permit Parking Area 9A and that the current no parking regulations alternating from the east and west sides of Thyra Avenue be removed, with no parking regulations introduced on the east side of Thyra Avenue between Danforth Avenue and Detonia Park Avenue. Residents requested the implementation of overnight on-street permit parking on Thyra Avenue from Danforth Avenue to Dentonia Park Avenue within Permit Parking Area 9A, to accommodate their long-term parking needs.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized parking by permit only to be in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily on the even (west) side of Thyra Avenue, from Danforth Avenue to Dentonia Park Avenue, within Permit Parking Area 9A. 2. Rescinded the parking prohibition in effect May, July, September and November on the east side of Thyra Avenue, between Danforth Avenue and Dentonia Park Avenue. 3. Rescinded the parking prohibition in effect January, February, March, April, June, August, October and December, on the west side of Thyra Avenue, between Danforth Avenue and Dentonia Park Avenue. 4. Prohibited parking at all times on the east side of Thyra Avenue, between Danforth Avenue and Dentonia Park Avenue.
