Appointment of New Board Members of the Ralph Thornton Community Centre

AdoptedSep 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is routine paperwork: the Toronto and East York Community Council appointed three new members to the Ralph Thornton Community Centre board, for terms ending June 30, 2028.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Board confirms that the appointment of these individuals complies with the provisions of the City of Toronto’s Public Appointments Policy and the relevant provisions of the Relationship Framework for Board – Operated Community Centres, as well as, in accordance with our bylaws. The following individuals did not stand for re-election and are no longer on the RTCC Board of Management. - Maggi Redmonds - Hongmei Cai We look forward to the contributions of our new and returning Board members as we continue to serve our community. We also thank our departing Board members for their service to our organization.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Appointed the following individuals to Ralph Thornton Community Centre's Board of Management, at pleasure of Council, for a term of office ending June 30, 2028 or until their successors are appointed: - Allison Macdonald-Buchanan - Amanda Munday - Brittany Rodrigues
