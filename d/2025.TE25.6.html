City Council voted on zoning changes to allow a 49-storey building with 576 homes at 135 St. Clair Avenue West, with a walkway linking Foxbar Road and Avenue Road.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of the application to amend the Zoning By-law to permit a 164.3-metre (49-storeys, excluding mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building. The proposed building includes 576 residential units, with a total gross floor area of 48,548 square metres, including 948 square metres of gross floor area for non-residential uses. The proposal includes a mid-block connection at the southern end of the site between Foxbar Road and Avenue Road.

What was decided

City Council on October 8 and 9, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend City of Toronto Zoning By-law 569-2013 for the lands municipally known as 135 St Clair Avenue West substantially in accordance with the draft Zoning By-law Amendment included as Attachment 7 to the revised report (September 9, 2025) from the Acting Director, Community Planning, Toronto and East York District. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to make such stylistic and technical changes to the draft Zoning By-law Amendment as may be required. 3.
