Installation of Speed Bumps in the Public Laneway South of Corby Avenue and West of Caledonia Road

AdoptedSep 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council voted on speed bumps for the laneway south of Corby Avenue west of Caledonia Road, used by families at F.H. Miller Junior Public School, after speeding concerns.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The public laneway south of Corby Avenue and west of Caledonia Road is frequently used by parents and their children attending F.H. Miller Junior Public School. A school exit and the playground entrance both open on to this laneway, making this area heavily used by residents. Residents and school staff have raised concerns over speeding vehicles using this laneway as a cut-through between Gilbert Avenue and Caledonia Avenue. Previous near misses involving speeding vehicles and students at the school highlight the urgent need for traffic safety improvements. The installation of speed bumps in this laneway will help to reduce speeding and improve safety for all residents.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of speed bumps in the public lane south of Corby Avenue, west of Caledonia Road, between Gilbert Avenue and Caledonia Road, as shown on Attachment 2 to the letter (August 29, 2025) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo
