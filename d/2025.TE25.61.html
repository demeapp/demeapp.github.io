Installation of Speed Bumps in the Public Laneway East of Gladstone Avenue and South of Collahie Street

AdoptedSep 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council voted on speed bumps for the laneway east of Gladstone Avenue south of Collahie Street, used by Fairview Nursing Home residents and staff, after reports of speeding.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Fairview Nursing Home provides housing to over 100 long-term care residents and is located in the public laneway east of Gladstone Avenue and south of Collahie Street. Many of the Fairview residents can be found outside the building on walks, meetings with neighbours, or out grabbing fresh air with their families. This public laneway is an important connection for residents and the dedicated staff members working at the nursing home. Over the past few years, Fairview Nursing Home staff and their residents have experienced an increase in the number of speeding vehicles travelling near their building. Many residents feel unsafe walking down the laneway, which has impacted Fairview’s mission of creating a happy and vibrant community. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of speed bumps in the public lane east of Gladstone Avenue, south of Collahie Street, between Cross Street and Collahie Street, as shown on Attachment 2 to the letter (September 2, 2025) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo.
