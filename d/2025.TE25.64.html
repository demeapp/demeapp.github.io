Charles Street West Two-Way

AdoptedSep 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council asked staff to study making the one-way westbound block of Charles Street West, between Yonge and Balmuto, two-way again, and report back in late 2025.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Charles Street West is a busy street close to Yonge Street and Bloor Street, which is currently restricted to one-way westbound for one short block between Yonge and Balmuto. For more than four years, the Bloor Yorkville BIA and the Greater Yorkville Residents’ Association have requested that this portion of Charles Street West be reopened to two-way traffic, to help manage congestion at the Bloor/ Yonge intersection, including severe, prolonged impacts from long-term TTC and private sector construction. In 2021, by TE26.83 - Two-way Operation of Charles Street West between Balmuto and Yonge Street , Toronto and East York Community Council directed the General Manager, Transportation Services, to review and report back to Toronto and East York Community Council in the first quarter of 2022 on the feasibility of permitting two-way traffic flow on Charles Street West, between Yonge and…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services to evaluate re-opening Charles Street West to two-way traffic between Yonge Street and Balmuto Street, and to report back to the Toronto and East York Community Council in the fourth quarter of 2025 with all necessary details for implementation in the first quarter of 2026.
