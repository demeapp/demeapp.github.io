Toronto City Council · Decision

Speed Humps and Safety Improvements - Benlamond Avenue (Between Main Street and Norwood Road)

AdoptedSep 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council considered asking staff to draw up speed hump plans for Benlamond Avenue between Main Street and Norwood Road, after residents petitioned over pedestrian safety.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents have reached out to my office with concerns about pedestrian safety on Benlamond Avenue and on surrounding neighbourhood streets. Our office has received a petition from residents in support of the installation of speed humps on Benlamond Avenue (between Main Street and Norwood Road) in January 2025. My office directed staff to prepare a report to Toronto and East York Community Council to authorize the installation. Unfortunately, this action was not completed. Therefore, I am requesting staff to report back to our next Community Council meeting with the required details to authorize the installation of speed humps on Benlamond Avenue and to report back the feasibility of accelerating additional road safety improvements.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, to come forward with Recommendations and drawings for traffic calming measures (speed humps) on Benlamond Avenue, between Main Street and Norwood Road, for submission at the Toronto and East York Community Council meeting taking place on October 22, 2025. 2. Directed the Director, Planning, Design and Management, Transportation Services to investigate feasibility of interim geometric safety improvements at the intersection of Benlamond Avenue and Norwood Road / Glen Oak Drive for implementation in 2026.
