Toronto City Council · Decision

Ontario Place Event Traffic Management Strategy

AdoptedSep 18, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council considered requesting a traffic plan for pick-up and drop-off around Ontario Place and the Budweiser Stage, where concert crowds cause jams and unsafe conditions on Lake Shore Boulevard West.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Ontario Place is the site of the Budweiser Stage concert venue and hosts dozens of events every year, with over 70 scheduled in 2025. Budweiser Stage also has plans to expand its capacity in the coming years. The venue generates peak traffic activity on Lake Shore Boulevard West. before and after concerts. In particular, pick-up and drop-off including rideshare is happening on curb lanes, creating congestion and unsafe movements for passengers and pedestrians. As a busy transportation corridor, Lake Shore Boulevard West. experiences large amounts of traffic in general. The site is also close to Exhibition Place which in addition to its trade shows and conventions hosts sporting events and signature events such as the Canadian National Exhibition and Toronto Caribbean Festival that generate significant levels of traffic. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services to work with Live Nation, Ontario Place, Exhibition Place, the Executive Director, Municipal Licensing and Standards, the Toronto Police Service and other relevant partners to develop a strategy for event-related pickup and drop-off in the area surrounding Ontario Place, consider any planned operational and capital projects in the area, conduct trial mitigation measures for the remainder of 2025, and report back to the Toronto and East York Community Council in first quarter of 2026.
