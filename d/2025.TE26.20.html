70 Caledonia Park Road - Construction Staging Area

AdoptedOct 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council voted on closing part of Caledonia Park Road for 8 months, with a protected walkway, while a six-storey self-storage building goes up at 70 Caledonia Park Road.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. PenaltaGroup is constructing a six-storey self storage facility at 70 Caledonia Park Road. The site is located on the west side of Caledonia Park Road, between St. Clair Avenue West and Davenport Road. Transportation Services is requesting authorization to close the west sidewalk and a portion of the southbound curbside traffic lane on Caledonia Park Road for a period of 8 months, from October 31, 2025 to July 1, 2026, in order to facilitate construction staging operations. Pedestrians will be redirected into a temporary protected walkway within the closed portion of the southbound curb lane, around the construction staging area.
