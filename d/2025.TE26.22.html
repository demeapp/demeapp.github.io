610 Bloor Street West - Encroachment Appeal

AdoptedOct 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council must decide an appeal from the owner of 610 Bloor Street West over a marketing structure on city land. Staff recommend letting it stay through a deal with conditions.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is for Toronto and East York Community Council to consider an appeal from the property owner of 610 Bloor Street West regarding their encroachment application. The encroachment consists of an existing enclosed marketing structure which is in contravention of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks, Use Of. The property owner is seeking authority from Toronto East York Community Council to allow the ongoing maintenance of the enclosed marketing structure within the public right-of-way at 610 Bloor Street West, which is in contravention of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks, Use Of.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1.
