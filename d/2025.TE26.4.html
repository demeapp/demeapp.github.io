861 Avenue Road - Zoning By-law Amendment Application - Decision Report - Approval

AdoptedNov 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City Council voted on zoning changes to allow two four-storey buildings with nine townhouses at 861 Avenue Road, with a small road widening along Avenue Road.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of the application to amend the Zoning By-law to permit two 4-storey residential buildings (13.3 and 13.1 metres) with a total of nine townhouse units accessed from a central driveway. The proposed development includes a 0.4-metre road widening on Avenue Road and a 6.0-metre corner rounding conveyance at the northwest corner of the site.

What was decided

City Council on November 12 and 13, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend City of Toronto Zoning By-law 569-2013 for the lands municipally known as 861 Avenue Road substantially in accordance with the draft Zoning By-law Amendment included as Attachment 5 to the report (October 3, 2025) from the Director, Community Planning, Toronto and East York District. 2.
