Appointments to The 519 Church Street Community Centre Board of Management

AdoptedOct 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is routine paperwork: the Toronto and East York Community Council appointed Mohamed Omar to The 519 Church Street Community Centre board through September 2029, replacing Aaron Hewitt, who resigned.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Board confirms that this nominee is qualified to serve under the provisions of the City of Toronto's Public Appointments Policy and the Relationship Framework for Community Centre Boards of Management. On behalf of the Board, I respectfully request that the City take the necessary steps to appoint this nominated candidate. Please note that Aaron Hewitt has resigned, effective July 31, 2025.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Appointed Mohamed Omar to the 519 Church Street Community Centre Board of Management, at pleasure of Council, for a term of office ending September 27, 2029 and until a successor is appointed.
