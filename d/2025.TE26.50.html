St. Clair Avenue West - Parking Amendments

AdoptedNov 12, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City Council voted on removing an unused loading zone on St. Clair Avenue West near Via Italia, since the business that asked for it moved away. That would bring back parking spots.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

A commercial loading zone was previously approved on the south section of St. Clair Avenue West near Via Italia. The business that requested this loading zone moved to a different location and no longer requires these spaces. The local BIA confirmed that none of their members require access to this loading zone and it can be removed. The proposed removal of this commercial loading zone will restore on-street parking and help to welcome visitors to the Corso Italia area.

What was decided

City Council on November 12 and 13, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council rescind the parking prohibition in effect from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, and 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Saturday, except public holidays, on the south side of St. Clair Avenue West, between a point 57 metres east of Via Italia and a point 15.9 meters east.
