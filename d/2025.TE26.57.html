Toronto City Council · Decision

Kensington Orphan Lanes

AdoptedOct 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council considered asking staff to study, by spring 2026, how to bring street lights and garbage pickup to privately owned 'orphan lanes' in Kensington Market serving homes and businesses.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Kensington Market is an important tourist attraction as well as the centre of a thriving multi-ethnic community and one of the most affordable parts of Ward 11. Recent planning changes are accelerating intensification of this area. Kensington Market is crossed by a network of small lanes. Some neighbourhood lanes are owned and maintained by the City of Toronto. Unfortunately, a high concentration of lanes that provide essential access to homes and businesses are registered in the name of individuals who died in the 19th century. This includes Cyril Lane, the emergency fire exit for Kensington Community School, and Ellen Lane, the only vehicular access to a Toronto Community Seniors Housing building. These orphan lanes are broken, unlit and filthy. They are infested with rats and trash. Their visible neglect attracts illegal activities that are profoundly disruptive for the…

What was decided

The Toronto East York Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services to investigate and to report back to the Toronto East York Community Council in the first quarter of 2026 with an appropriate action plan, starting with how to provide street lighting and trash removal to the orphan lanes in Kensington Market that serve public facilities.
