Toronto City Council · Decision

Parking Amendments - Dentonia Park Avenue (Northside between Avonlea Boulevard and Sibley Avenue)

AdoptedOct 22, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council voted on a one-hour parking limit on the north side of Dentonia Park Avenue between Avonlea Boulevard and Sibley Avenue, weekdays 8 a.m. to 6 p.m., after residents petitioned. Permit holders would be exempt.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In response to concerns raised by residents regarding limited parking availability on Dentonia Park Avenue, my office has received a petition from community members requesting changes to the current parking regulations. Specifically, residents are asking that parking on the north side of Dentonia Park Avenue, between Avonlea Boulevard and Sibley Avenue, be limited to a maximum of one hour. On-street permit parking holders would remain exempt from the one-hour limit.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized parking for a maximum period of one hour from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Dentonia Park Avenue, between Avonlea Boulevard and Sibley Avenue.
