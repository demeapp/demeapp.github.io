543 Yonge Street - Demolition of a Building within the Historic Yonge Street Heritage Conservation District and Approval of a Replacement Building

AdoptedDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City Council voted on tearing down a four-storey building at 543 Yonge Street, in the heritage Yonge Street area, and replacing it with a 68-storey building with stores and homes.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve the proposed demolition of the 4-storey commercial building on the site at 543 Yonge Street (543, 545 and 549 Yonge Street) and approve its replacement with a 68-storey mixed-use building with a 2-storey podium, including retail at grade and residential above, in accordance with Section 42(1) 2 and 42(1) 4 of the Ontario Heritage Act, with conditions. The property is designated under Part V of the Ontario Heritage Act as part of the Historic Yonge Street Heritage Conservation District ("HYSCD"). The subject property is identified as 'non-contributing' in the District Plan. The District Plan allows for the demolition of buildings or structures on non-contributing properties.
