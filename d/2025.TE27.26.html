38-50 Park Road - Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment Application - Decision Report - Refusal

Adopted with changesDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City staff want City Council to refuse a 31-storey, 289-home building at 38-50 Park Road, saying it does not fit the low-rise Rosedale Valley area, and to have city lawyers defend the refusal if the builder appeals.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This Report recommends refusal of the application to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law to permit a 31-storey (107.9 metres, mechanical penthouse not included) mixed-use building containing 289 units (including 40 replacement rental dwelling units) and 700 square metres of non-residential gross floor area at 38-50 Park Road. The existing buildings at 38 and 40 Park road, will be demolished, and a corresponding Rental Housing Demolition application has been submitted. The application includes the retention of the designated building at 50 Park Road. The proposed development does not fit the existing or planned context of the site and surrounding low-rise area.
