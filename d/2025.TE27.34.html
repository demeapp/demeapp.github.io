835 Queen Street West - Residential Demolition Application

PostponedNov 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council must decide whether to let the owner tear down a building with shops and homes at 835 Queen Street West before a permit for a new building is issued. Council can refuse or approve the demolition.

Council put off deciding this item.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is regarding a matter for which the Toronto and East York Community Council has delegated authority to make a final decision. In accordance with Section 33 of the Planning Act and the City of Toronto Municipal Code, Ch. 363, Article 6 "Demolition Control", the application for the demolition of the existing mixed-use building, containing ground floor commercial and second floor residential, at 835 Queen Street West (Application No. 25 227295 DEM 00 DM) is being referred to the Toronto and East York Community Council for consideration because Toronto Building received a request letter from the applicant to demolish the building prior to the issuance of a replacement building permit.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Deferred consideration of the Item until the January 13, 2026 meeting of the Toronto and East York Community Council.
