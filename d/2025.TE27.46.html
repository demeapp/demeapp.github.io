1320 Queen Street East - Encroachment Appeal - Basement Entrance

AdoptedNov 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto and East York Community Council must decide an appeal from the owner of 1320 Queen Street East over a basement entrance that would go under city land. Staff recommend allowing it through a deal with conditions.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is for Toronto and East York Community Council to consider an appeal from the property owner of 1320 Queen Street East regarding their encroachment application. The proposed encroachment consists of a basement entrance that extends 2.27m below grade with a 0.10m setback from the sidewalk, which is in contravention of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks, Use Of. The property owner is seeking authority from Toronto and East York Community Council to construct and maintain a basement entrance within the public right-of-way at 1320 Queen Street East, which is in contravention of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks, Use Of.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the General Manager, Transportation Services to enter into an encroachment agreement with property owner of 1320 Queen Street East, to permit the construction and maintenance of a basement entrance, subject but not limited to, the following conditions: a. indemnify the City from and against all actions, suits, claims or demands and from all loss, costs, damages and expenses that may result from such permission granted; b. maintain the basement entrance at their own expense in good repair and a condition satisfactory to the General Manager, Transportation Services and will not make any additions or modifications to the encroachment beyond what is allowed under the Terms of the Agreement; c. accept such additional conditions as the City Solicitor or the General Manager, Transportation Services may deem necessary in the interest of…
