Transit Priority Measures to Support the 505 Dundas 6-Minute Network

Adopted with changesDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

City Council voted on parking and traffic changes so 505 Dundas streetcars can run every six minutes from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. daily. The six-minute service starts in November 2025, with the street changes in January 2026.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services is requesting approval, on behalf of the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC), to install transit priority measures (TPMs) to support 6-minute headways on the 505 Dundas streetcar route from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. daily, with these service improvements to 6-minute headways beginning in November 2025. If approved, the TPMs to support the 6-minute headways would be implemented January 2026. The proposed changes aim to benefit the passengers using the 505 Dundas route, who currently total over 205,000 per week. The purpose of this report is to recommend a suite of TPMs to help improve the speed and reliability of the 505 Dundas route and alleviate the risk of increased bunching and gapping associated with moving to a more frequent headway.
