Re-opening Item TE25.15 - 236-254 Old Weston Road - Residential Demolition Applications

AdoptedNov 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The item proposes to re-open the demolition item for 236-254 Old Weston Road and drop the sod-laying rule, since the site will soon be a fenced construction access point for the Davenport Road Extension project.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City staff have requested the removal of a condition requiring sod to be laid related to the residential demolition applications at 236-254 Old Weston Road. The area will soon be used as a secured construction access point for the Davenport Road Extension project, and will be enclosed with fencing and sediment control measures. The site will support major construction activities including excavation, utility installations, streetlighting, and roadworks. Additionally, utility relocations by Metrolinx and other providers are planned. Given the imminent and intensive use of the site, installing sod would be a temporary and unnecessary expense to the public, as it would be removed shortly after.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item TE25.15, 236-254 Old Weston Road - Residential Demolition Applications. 2. Amended its decision on Part 1c. by deleting the words “sod be laid” and the word “and” so that it now reads: c. that the site be maintained free of garbage and weeds, in accordance with the Municipal Code Chapter 629-10, paragraph B and 629-11; and
