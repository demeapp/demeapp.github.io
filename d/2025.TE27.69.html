Toronto City Council · Decision

80 Mill Street - Installation of an Accessible Loading Zone

AdoptedDec 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The item proposes the 15-minute parking zone outside 80 Mill Street turned into an accessible loading zone, so Wheel-Trans riders and accessibility permit holders can board safely.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to request parking amendments to Mill Street to allow for safe pick up and drop off at 80 Mill Street. In consultation with local residents and the Gooderham and Worts Neighbourhood Association, turning the existing “15-minute parking” zone outside the entrance to 80 Mill Street to an Accessible Loading Zone will ensure that people using Wheel-Trans services or accessibility permits can be picked up and dropped off safely outside 80 Mill Street.

What was decided

City Council on December 16 and 17, 2025, adopted the following: 1. City Council rescind the existing maximum 15-minute parking regulation in effect at all times on the north side of Mill Street, between a point 58 metres west of Cherry Street and a point 11 metres further west. 2. City Council designate an accessible loading zone to be in effect at all times on the north side of Mill Street, between a point 58 metres west of Cherry Street and a point 11 metres further west.
