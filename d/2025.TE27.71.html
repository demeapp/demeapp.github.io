Installation of Traffic Calming Measures and Parking Amendments on Boon Avenue, between Morrison Avenue and Eversfield Road

AdoptedNov 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The item proposes three speed humps on Boon Avenue between Morrison Avenue and Eversfield Road, after residents raised speeding concerns, and an old traffic calming device removed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents on Boon Avenue have shared their concerns regarding speeding vehicles causing accidents and near misses involving residents living in the area. This section of Boon Avenue, located north of Rogers Road, was not considered for traffic calming measures when they were approved for the section south down to St. Clair Avenue West. This oversight creates a significant gap, resulting in drivers speeding up as they exit the traffic calming zone. In a survey hand-delivered to homes by my office, residents further shared their experiences and voted in strong support for the installation of speed humps. I heard similar concerns and support for speed humps at a neighbourhood meeting hosted at St. John Bosco earlier this year. What was decided

Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (three speed humps) on Boon Avenue, between Morrison Avenue and Eversfield Road, generally as shown on Attachment 2 to the letter (November 24, 2025) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo. 2. Rescinded the existing traffic calming (speed humps) on Boon Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 29.4 metres north of Morrison Avenue. 3. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect from the 16th day of each month to the last day of each month, from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the west side of Boon Avenue, between a point 42.1 metres south of Teignmouth Avenue and a point 4.6 metres north thereof. 4.
