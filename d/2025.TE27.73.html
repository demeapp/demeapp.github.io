Toronto City Council · Decision

Installation of Traffic Calming Measures on Morrison Avenue

AdoptedNov 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The item proposes six speed humps on Morrison Avenue between Dufferin Street and Harvie Avenue, after residents and the North Corso Residents' Association raised speeding concerns.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Morrison Avenue, located between St. Clair Avenue West and Rogers Road, is located in the heart of North Corso and connects hundreds of residents to local shops, schools, parks, and public transit. I have heard from many residents and the North Corso Residents’ Association that drivers too often speed excessively down Morrison Avenue, putting them and their families at risk. Residents have also shared their experiences with accidents and near misses as a result of dangerous driving. As this neighbourhood continues to grow, there is an urgent need for traffic calming measures. In a survey hand-delivered to homes by my office, residents along Morrison Avenue overwhelmingly supported the installation of speed humps as a proposed traffic calming measure. The addition of speed humps will help improve traffic safety for residents living in the area and support existing traffic calming…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (six speed humps) on Morrison Avenue, between Dufferin Street and Harvie Avenue, generally as shown on Attachments 2 and 3 to the letter (November 24, 2025) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo.
