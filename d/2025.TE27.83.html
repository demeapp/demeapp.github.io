Toronto City Council · Decision

Installation of Traffic Calming Measures on Eversfield Road

AdoptedNov 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The item proposes five speed humps on Eversfield Road between Earlscourt Avenue and Dufferin Street, after residents raised worries about speeding drivers cutting through from Dufferin Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents on Eversfield Road contacted my office to share concerns regarding traffic safety issues on their street. I have heard about their experiences with near misses involving speeding vehicles travelling off of Dufferin Street and using this local street to avoid traffic. These concerns highlight an immediate need for traffic calming measures on Eversfield Road. In a survey hand-delivered to homes by my office, a strong majority of residents voted in favour of installing speed humps to help reduce speeding and improve safety in this busy neighbourhood.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (five speed humps) on Eversfield Road, between Earlscourt Avenue and Dufferin Street, generally as shown on Attachment 2 to the letter (November 24, 2025) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the south side of Eversfield Road, between a point 18 metres east of Boon Avenue and a point 12 metres east thereof. 3. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:00 midnight to 6:00 a.m., daily, on the odd (south) side of Eversfield Road from Earlscourt Avenue to Dufferin Street. 4. Authorized permit parking to be in effect from 12:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m., daily, on the odd (south) side of Eversfield Road from Earlscourt Avenue to Dufferin Street.
