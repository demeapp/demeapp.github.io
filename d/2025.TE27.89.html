A New Vision for the Washroom/Fieldhouse at Trinity Bellwoods Park

AdoptedNov 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

After a fire badly damaged the washroom and fieldhouse at Trinity Bellwoods Park, Deputy Mayor Malik wants parks staff to run a public consultation on the future of the site.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Following a fire on the weekend of October 25 where the Trinity Bellwoods Park Ravine Fieldhouse/ Washroom Building was significantly damaged, it was determined by City staff that the building needs to be demolished and is beyond repair. Residents and local stakeholders, like the Friends of Trinity Bellwoods Park have had questions and concerns about the building for some time, as it had been out of service for some time. My office has been working closely with these groups and with City staff on improvements to Trinity Bellwoods Park over the past three years and as part of this ongoing work, we should be making a new plan for this site.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Parks and Recreation, to implement a consultation plan with the Ward Councillor and relevant community and internal City stakeholders to review the future of the washroom fieldhouse site at Trinity Bellwoods Park.
