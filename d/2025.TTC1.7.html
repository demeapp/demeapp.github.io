Toronto City Council · Decision

Making Old Mill Station Accessible

AdoptedJan 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Maystar General Contractors, the lowest acceptable bidder, would get a $25,690,748.88 contract for two elevators to make Old Mill Station accessible, with $8.5 million in savings moved to fund it, if the TTC Board agrees.

The official summary names a figure of $25,690,748.88.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to obtain authorization for the award of Contract W45-8, Old Mill Station Easier Access III (EAIII), to Maystar General Contractors Inc. in the amount of $25,690,748.88, inclusive of all taxes, on the basis of the lowest-priced acceptable bid. The contract work provides services for the construction of two elevators at Old Mill Station from street level to each side platform, new tactile attention tiles, station power upgrade, associated signage, mechanical and electrical upgrades and other associated elements to provide a barrier-free path throughout the station. The construction phase of the contract is scheduled to commence in Q2 2025.

What was decided

The TTC Board: 1. Authorized the award of Contract W45-8, Old Mill Station – Easier Access III to Maystar General Contractors Inc. in the amount of $25,690,748.88, inclusive of all taxes, on the basis of the lowest-priced acceptable bid. 2. Approved a reallocation of $8.5 million of permanent savings from the SRT Life Extension project and increase the total project cost for the Easier Access III program to fund the Contract award. 3. Subject to recommendation 2 above, authorized the recommended budget adjustments to the TTC’s 2025-2034 Capital Budget and Plan to be included in the TTC’s Capital Variance Report submission to the City of Toronto for the four months ended April 30, 2025, for City Council consideration and approval. 4.
