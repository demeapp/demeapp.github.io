Procurement Authorization - Greenwood Shop End of Life Replacement - Replace Paint and Sanding Booths

Adopted with changesJan 27, 2025 · Toronto City Council

A $15,029,000.00 contract to Maystar General Contractors, the lowest bidder, to replace paint and sanding booths at the Greenwood maintenance shop would be given, if the TTC Board agrees. Board approval is needed because the contract tops the CEO's $5 million signing limit.

The official summary names a figure of $15,029,000.00.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to obtain authorization for the award of Contract GR1-59 –Greenwood Shop End of Life Replacement – Replace Paint and Sanding Booths to Maystar General Contractors Inc., in the amount of $15,029,000.00, inclusive of HST, based on the lowest total compliant bid price. TTC Board authority is required as this contract award exceeds the Chief Executive Officer’s delegated authority limit of $5 million for the award of contracts for budgeted goods and services. The work under this contract is to supply all labour, equipment, and materials to construct new paint and sanding booth service rooms as a replacement to the existing facilities. The work includes a new painting booth for subway cars, a new small parts painting booth, new sanding and preparation areas, and a new two-storey office space. Authorized the award of Contract GR1-59 – Greenwood Shop End of Life Replacement – Replace Paint and Sanding Booths to Maystar General Contractors Inc. in the amount of $15,029,000.00, inclusive of HST, based on the lowest total compliant bid price. 2. Approved the recommendation contained in the Confidential Attachment 1, as amended, and authorized that the information in the Confidential Attachment remains confidential as it contains information about a position, plan, procedure, criteria or instruction to be applied to any negotiations carried on or to be carried on by or on behalf of the City or local board.
