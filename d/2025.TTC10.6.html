Asset Management - International Association of Public Transport (UITP) Peer Review - Management Response

Adopted with changesNov 3, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Approval of releasing, with security details removed, an international transit group's peer review of TTC subway and streetcar maintenance, along with management's response is before the TTC Board.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As directed by the TTC Audit & Risk Management Committee, at its meeting on September 11, 2024, UITP has completed a Peer Review of the TTC’s subway and streetcar assets and maintenance programs. Key findings and recommendations have been provided to TTC within the attached confidential report. TTC’s management responses are also attached to this report.

What was decided

The TTC Board: 1. Adopted the action taken in camera and authorized the release of the Asset & Maintenance Management – International Association of Public Transport (UITP) Peer Review, with redactions applied to protect security of property and systems, along with the TTC Management Response. 2. Directed the CEO and Interim Chief Operating Officer to work with the General Manager, Transportation Services and report back in Q1 2026 on the development of a plan to accelerate the implementation of enhanced signal priority for all streetcar lines. 3.
