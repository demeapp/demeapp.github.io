Executive Committee Item 19.6 Assessment of Scarborough Rapid Transit Adaptive Reuse and Next Steps

AdoptedFeb 24, 2025 · Toronto City Council

The CEO would report back in 2025 on the old Scarborough RT line's land and buildings, to help the City plan its future use, if the TTC Board agrees.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on December 17 and 18, 2024, adopted Item EX19.6 as amended and, in so doing, has requested the Board of Directors of the Toronto Transit Commission to direct the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Transit Commission to provide an interim report to the Board in the first quarter of 2025 on relevant information to inform the City's public consultation on the land requirements and a final report to the Board in fourth quarter of 2025 on the completed analysis of Scarborough Rapid Transit Line 3 infrastructure and land requirements, and the assets no longer required for transit operation purposes.

What was decided

The Toronto Transit Commission: 1. Directed the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Transit Commission to provide an interim report to the Board in the first quarter of 2025 on relevant information to inform the City's public consultation on the land requirements and a final report to the Board in fourth quarter of 2025 on the completed analysis of Scarborough Rapid Transit Line 3 infrastructure and land requirements, and the assets no longer required for transit operation purposes.
