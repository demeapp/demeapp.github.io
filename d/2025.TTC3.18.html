Procurement Authorization - Supply of Windshield Washer Fluid and Antifreeze

AdoptedApr 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Ultra Clear Engine Fluids would get a contract worth up to $7,055,000 for windshield washer fluid and antifreeze, starting July 2025 for two years, if the TTC Board agrees. The first two years are $2,657,000, with extensions up to $4,398,000. Board approval is needed because it tops the CEO's $5 million signing limit.

The official summary names a figure of $7,055,000.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to obtain authorization to award a contract to Ultra Clear Engine Fluids Inc. for the supply of windshield washer fluid and antifreeze, with a total aggregate contract value of up to $7,055,000, inclusive of HST, for a two-year term commencing July 1, 2025, with up to three optional, one-year extension terms to be exercised at the TTC’s sole discretion. TTC Board approval is required as the contract award, including extension options, exceeds the Chief Executive Officer’s delegated authority limit of $5 million for the award of contracts for budgeted goods and services. A continued supply of antifreeze and windshield washer fluid is required for the safe operation of the TTC’s revenue and non-revenue fleets.

What was decided

The TTC Board: 1. Authorized the award of a contract to Ultra Clear Engine Fluids Inc. in the total amount of $2,657,000, inclusive of HST, for the bulk supply of methyl alcohol (windshield washer fluid) and ethylene glycol (antifreeze) for an initial contract term of two years commencing July 1, 2025, based on lowest-priced compliant bid. 2. Delegated authority to the TTC’s Chief Executive Officer to authorize the execution of up to three one-year extension options, from July 1, 2027 to June 30, 2030, at the TTC’s sole discretion, and to increase the contract value by up to $4,398,000, inclusive of HST, for a total contract value of up to $7,055,000.
