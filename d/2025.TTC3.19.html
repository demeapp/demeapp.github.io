Appointment of a TTC Board member to the TTC-Metrolinx PRESTO Joint Advisory Group

Adopted with changesApr 16, 2025 · Toronto City Council

This is routine business. The TTC Board was asked to appoint one of its members to the TTC-Metrolinx PRESTO joint advisory group, replacing Joanne De Laurentiis.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek the appointment of a TTC Board member to the TTC-Metrolinx PRESTO Joint Advisory Group. The term of appointment for outgoing Vice-Chair Joanne De Laurentiis ended on March 26, 2025. Ms. De Laurentiis previously served as a member of the TTC-Metrolinx PRESTO Joint Advisory Group. The TTC-Metrolinx PRESTO Joint Advisory Group consists of the CEOs for both Metrolinx and TTC, together with two Board members each from Metrolinx and the TTC. The Chair, Jamaal Myers, is currently one of the two TTC Board member representatives. The expiration of the term of Joanne De Laurentiis has created a vacancy which is to be filled by a TTC Board member.

What was decided

The TTC Board appointed Joe Mihevc to the TTC-Metrolinx PRESTO Joint Advisory Group.
