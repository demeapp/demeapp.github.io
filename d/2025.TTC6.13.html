Toronto City Council · Decision

Establishment of a TTC-Metrolinx Joint Advisory Committee - by Vice Chair Joe Mihevc, seconded by Chair Jamaal Myers

AdoptedJun 23, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Vice-Chair Mihevc asks the TTC Board to have staff work with Metrolinx on a formal joint committee with board members from both sides, to line up shared transit priorities.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The TTC and Metrolinx have successfully advanced PRESTO related issues through the TTC-Metrolinx Joint Advisory Group (JAG) Committee. As we work together to open and operate Lines 5 and 6, advance transit expansion projects, continue with the One Fare program, further coordinate service, and modernize fare collection it is critical that there is continued collaboration and coordinated oversight between the two organizations.

What was decided

The TTC Board requested that TTC staff work with Metrolinx staff to establish a formal advisory committee that includes representation of Board Members from both organizations, with the goal of enhancing coordination, fostering strategic alignment, and ensuring effective governance of shared transit priorities.
