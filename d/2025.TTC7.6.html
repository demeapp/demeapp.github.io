The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to the motion adopted by the TTC Board in January 2025 “Addressing Service Delays on the TTC” by providing an update on five key areas: the ongoing external International Association of Public Transport (UITP) review of TTC maintenance with a focus on signalling performance, communication protocols for customers during disruptions, shuttle bus operational improvements, surface transit redundancy, and measures to prevent unauthorized track access. This report responds to the Board’s motion by outlining the TTC’s current and planned initiatives in each area and identifying ongoing improvements to ensure a more resilient, reliable, and rider-focused transit system.

What was decided

The TTC Board received this report for information.
