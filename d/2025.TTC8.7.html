Declaration of Surplus Property - 17 and 19 Dewhurst Boulevard, and 1 and 3 Strathmore Boulevard

AdoptedSep 10, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Declaring parts of properties on Dewhurst Boulevard and Strathmore Boulevard no longer needed, so the City can dispose of them. It goes to the TTC Board for approval.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that the TTC Board declare portions of the rear yards of 17 and 19 Dewhurst Boulevard and the entire properties at 1 and 3 Strathmore Boulevard as surplus to the operational needs of the TTC. The portions of the rear yards of 17 and 19 Dewhurst Boulevard, behind the newly constructed Secondary Entrance/Exit building at Donlands Station, and the entire two properties at 1 and 3 Strathmore Boulevard, are no longer required for TTC use. It is recommended that all lands be released to the City in accordance with TTC Procedural By-Law No. 2, TTC Policy 8.2.0 – Disposal of Surplus Property, Acquisition of Real Property, Office and Short-Term Lease, and the City surplus property process.

What was decided

The TTC Board: 1.
