Toronto City Council · Decision

Procurement Authorization - Supply of Uniforms and Work Clothing

AdoptedSep 10, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Adding $1,736,000 to 24 uniform contracts with nine companies for 2025 needs, covering about 10,000 workers. It goes to the TTC Board for a decision. It is also asked to extend the contracts for 2026-2027 for a combined $17,386,000. Component amounts cited: $1,345,000, $228,000 and $163,000 for 2025; $9,978,000, $4,854,000 and $2,554,000 for 2026-2027.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The TTC procures and distributes uniforms and work clothing annually to approximately 10,000 frontline employees through a network of contracts, systems, and policies. This report is seeking the TTC Board’s approval to: Amend 24 TTC employee uniform and work clothing contracts to ensure sufficient funds are available for 2025 requirements. Extend contracts for the 2026-2027 TTC employee uniform and work clothing requirements to nine current suppliers on a single source basis. The TTC has been engaging stakeholders to improve garment material specifications and redefine uniform requirements to improve uniform quality, inclusivity and operational efficiency. These measures are in alignment with the 2024-2028 TTC Corporate Plan and modernization journey.
