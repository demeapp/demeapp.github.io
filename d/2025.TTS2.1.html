Overview of TTC Service Standards

Approving a plan to review TTC service standards on routes, frequency and service quality, with public input and a final report to the Board. It goes to the committee for approval.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an overview of the TTC’s service standards along with a proposed approach to review and update the standards. The purpose of the service standards is to ensure public transit is accessible, efficient, and cost effective. These standards guide all aspects of service planning through network design standards, quality of service standards and performance targets. TTC Service Standards document can be reviewed here .

What was decided

The Strategic Planning Committee: 1. Endorsed the proposed workplan to undertake a review of TTC Service Standards, which includes the following tasks: a. Background review b. Review of existing standards c. Public and Stakeholder consultation d. Revised Service Standard development e. Final report to TTC Board
