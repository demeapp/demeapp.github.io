Toronto Zoo Community Conservation Centre - Lease With University of Toronto Scarborough

AdoptedApr 4, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Approving the terms of a lease letting the University of Toronto Scarborough use lab and classroom space at the Zoo's Community Conservation Centre for its programs. It goes to the Zoo board for approval.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks approval in principle from the Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo on the terms of a lease arrangement between the City of Toronto, Toronto Zoo and the Governing Council of the University of Toronto for the use of dedicated and shared lab and classroom spaces by University of Toronto Scarborough (UTSc) for the purposes of delivering post secondary education programs at the Toronto Zoo Community Conservation Centre.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Approved in principle the terms and conditions of a lease agreement (the "Lease") with the Governing Council of the University of Toronto (the "Tenant") in respect of the Toronto Zoo Community Conservation Centre at the property municipally known as 2000 Meadowvale Road (the "Leased Premises"), as set out in Confidential Attachment 1 to the revised report (April 1, 2025) from the Chief Transformation Officer, Toronto Zoo. 2. Authorized the Chief Executive Officer of the Toronto Zoo, on behalf of the Board, to negotiate and execute the Lease among the City of Toronto, Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo, and the Tenant, consistent with the terms and conditions contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the revised report (April 1, 2025) from the Chief Transformation Officer, Toronto Zoo, and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor. 3.…
