The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks authorization from the Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo to engage in discussions with a community partner for the purposes of exploring a potential development opportunity at the Toronto Zoo.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Approved the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (April 4, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Zoo. 2. Directed that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (April 4, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Zoo remain confidential in its entirety, as it contains information about an ongoing negotiation that affects the Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo.
