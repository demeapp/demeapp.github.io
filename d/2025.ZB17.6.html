Red Panda Climate Controlled Viewing - Construction Tender Award

AdoptedJul 30, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Approving a construction deal for the Red Panda Climate Controlled Viewing project with a qualified firm. It goes to the Zoo board for approval. The price stays private until the deal closes.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends entering into negotiations for the award of the Red Panda Climate Controlled Viewing Construction Agreement.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Authorized the Director, Capital Program and Sustainability, or her designate, to negotiate the award and enter into a Construction Agreement with a qualified construction firm for the Red Panda Climate Controlled Viewing project at a purchase price as outlined in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (July 16, 2025) from the Director, Capital Program and Sustainability, Toronto Zoo. 2. Authorized the public release of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (July 16, 2025) from the Director, Capital Program and Sustainability, Toronto Zoo, following the closing of any purchase transaction.
