The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo Procedural By-law, the schedule of Board meetings for the year, including dates, time, and locations for meetings, will be approved at or before the First Meeting. On July 23 and 24, 2025, City Council considered Item CC32.3 and adopted the meeting schedule for 2026 for City Council and its Committees. The schedule of meetings for the Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo has been developed to avoid dates, whenever possible, that conflict with the approved City Council schedule; and to avoid days of cultural or religious significance. The following meetings of the Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo have been scheduled for 2026: Date: Wednesday, February 25, 2026 Start: 2:00 p.m. Location: Video Conference Date: Tuesday, May 26, 2026 Start: 2:00 p.m. Location: Toronto Zoo Boardroom, 361A Old Finch Avenue/Video Conference…
