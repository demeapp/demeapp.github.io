Toronto City Council · Decision

2026-2035 Capital Budget

AdoptedOct 15, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Approving the 2026-2035 capital budget, including raising the Community Conservation Centre construction budget from $75.0 million to $77.0 million. It goes to the Zoo board for approval.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report submits the 2026-2035 Capital Budget of your Toronto Zoo for approval. The Policy and Finance Committee reviewed the 2026-2035 Capital Budget on 2025-09-23 and recommended it for approval by the Board.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Approved the 2026-2035 Capital Budget as shown in Attachment 1 to the report (September 24, 2025) from the Chief Transformation Officer. 2. Approved an increase in total construction costs to be tendered under the construction management contract for the Community Conservation Centre (Welcome Area) from $75.0 million up to $77.0 million for PCL in accordance with the approved 2025-2034 capital budget. 3. Authorized the Director, Capital Program and Sustainability or their designate to negotiate and enter into any necessary amendments to the construction management contract for the Community Conservation Centre (Welcome Area) as a result of the adoption of Recommendation 2, on terms acceptable to the Director, Capital Program and Sustainability and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor.
