Toronto Zoo Campus Plan Committee Development

Adopted with changesOct 15, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Creating a Campus Plan Steering Committee and appoint three board members to guide a new 2027 Campus Plan. It goes to the Zoo board for a decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In 2026, your Toronto Zoo will launch a planning process to prepare a new Campus Plan to guide the future growth and development of the Zoo. This report intends to establish a Board committee to guide the development of the new 2027 Toronto Zoo Campus Plan.

What was decided

The Board of Management of Toronto Zoo: 1. Approved the establishment of a Campus Plan Steering Committee to guide the development of the 2027 Toronto Zoo Campus Plan. 2. Adopted the Zoo Board Campus Plan Steering Committee - Terms of Reference as set out in Attachment 1 to the report (September 30, 2025) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Zoo . 3. Appointed Ivan Hon, Sarah Bertollo and Edward McDonnell to the Zoo Board Campus Plan Steering Committee.
