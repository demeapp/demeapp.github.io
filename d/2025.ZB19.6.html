Construction of Perimeter Fence Project - Request for Tenders Award

AdoptedDec 1, 2025 · Toronto City Council

Giving the fence-building contract to Imperial Fence Inc. It goes to the Zoo board for a decision. of Scotland, Ontario, for up to $1,639,615.00 before taxes, the lowest of four bids.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends the award of the Request for Tenders (RFT-42) for the Construction of the Perimeter Fence Project.

What was decided

The Board of Management of Toronto Zoo: 1. Authorized the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Zoo, or their designate, to execute an agreement with Imperial Fence, Inc. (of Scotland, Ontario) for the award of Request for Tender (RFT) 42 for the construction of a perimeter fence project for up to $1,639,615.00 (excluding Harmonized Sales Tax), the lowest bid among all four received, on the terms and conditions of RFT-42 and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor. 2. Adopted the Confidential Instructions to Staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (November 14, 2025) from the Director, Capital Program and Sustainability, Toronto Zoo. 3. Authorized the public release of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (November 14, 2025) from the Director, Capital Program and Sustainability, Toronto Zoo, following the closing of any purchase transaction.
