Toronto City Council · Decision

Indigenous Identity Verification

Adopted with changesFeb 3, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The committee discussed checking Indigenous identity and possible City requests on this topic. It set up a working group to review the issue and write guidelines for the committee to consider.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Committee will have an opportunity to discuss Indigenous identity verification and potential requests from the City on this topic.

What was decided

That the Aboriginal Affairs Advisory Committee: 1. Established a working group to conduct an in-depth review of Indigenous identity verification to support the development of guidelines to be put forward as recommendations for consideration by the Aboriginal Affairs Advisory Committee. 2. Recommended the following members be part of the working group in Part 1 above. Cynthia Bell Suzanne Brunelle Paige Kimiksana-Kreps Millie Knapp Patti Pettigrew Dawn Sutherland, as Chair
