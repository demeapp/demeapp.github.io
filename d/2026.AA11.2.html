Establishment of an Indigenous Table to Guide the Work of Environment, Climate and Forestry Division

City environment staff presented plans for an Indigenous Table to guide the division's work, centred on Indigenous governance and ways of working, and ask the committee for feedback.

The official record

What the item proposed

Staff from Environment, Climate and Forestry Division will provide a presentation to seek feedback from the Aboriginal Affairs Advisory Committee on the development of an Indigenous Table to guide the work of the division while centering Indigenous systems, governance approaches, and relational ways of working.

What was decided

The Aboriginal Affairs Advisory Committee received the item for information.
