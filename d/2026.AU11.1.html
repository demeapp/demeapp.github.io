What the item proposed

This report provides information about the Auditor General's Office 2025 activities and the financial and non-financial benefits achieved by the City through its implementation of Auditor General report recommendations. In 2025, the Auditor General's Office completed 12 impactful audit, investigation, and other subject matter reports, containing 81 recommendations, as well as our 2025 follow-up reports. Every year we share the measurable financial impact of our work through a quantified return on investment. The City has achieved cumulative one-time and annually recurring savings projected over a five-year period totaling over $342 million (as reported in our 2021 to 2025 Annual Reports) by implementing our recommendations.
