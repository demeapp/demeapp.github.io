Future Contract Considerations for SmartTrack and Other Capital Project Agreements

AdoptedMay 1, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is routine business. The Audit Committee receives a report on the City's SmartTrack contracts and on rules, like audit rights, to protect the City in future building deals.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to assess the adequacy of the City’s contract management and project management processes with respect to the SmartTrack Project and whether they ensure value for money and project delivery timelines. The report also identifies requirements that should be considered in future contracts and projects to protect the interests of the City, including the addition of audit clauses. This is in response to the motions made at the February 2026 Audit Committee meeting and the March 2026 City Council meeting.

What was decided

The Audit Committee: 1. Received the report (April 17, 2026) from the Chief Procurement Officer and the Director, Internal Audit, for information.
