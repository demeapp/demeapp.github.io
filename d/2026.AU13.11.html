Continuous Controls Monitoring: City Staff Overtime, Absenteeism, and Other Payroll-Related Expenses

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The Auditor General asks Council to have the Chief People Officer review the City's 2001 attendance program and strengthen oversight. Overtime rose from $109.3 million to $134.1 million and paid sick leave from $109.2 million to $131.9 million between 2023 and 2025; workers earning over $50,000 in overtime were flagged.

The official summary names a figure of $50,000.

What the item proposed

The Auditor General’s 2026 Annual Work Plan[1] included a project on continuous controls monitoring of selected payroll-related expenses at the City. The objective of this continuous controls monitoring, a non-audit project, was to highlight unusual transactions and exceptions in payroll-related expenses, including emerging trends, and areas where internal controls may need to be strengthened. The continuous controls monitoring project uses an exception-based analytics approach to review payroll transactions and trends over multiple years.
