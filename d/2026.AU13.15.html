Arenas - 2025 Audited Financial Statements and Status of Outstanding 2025 Audited Financial Statements

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is routine business. City Council receives the 2025 audited financial statements for five City arenas, plus an update on the three audits still underway, for information. No decision is needed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide the Audit Committee and City Council with the 2025 audited financial statements of Arenas and a status update on those outstanding for 2025. The audited financial statements for the eight City Arenas are presented to the Audit Committee after approval by their respective Boards or Committees of Management. At the time of preparation of this report, there were five 2025 audited financial statements available for Arenas. This is because the 2025 audit for the remaining three entities were in progress. Depending on when the audits are completed, the Independent Auditor's Reports, accompanying financial statements and management control letters (if applicable) will be presented at a subsequent meeting of the Audit Committee.

What was decided
