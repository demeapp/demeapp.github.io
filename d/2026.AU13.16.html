What the item proposed

At its meeting of December 16 and 17, 2025, in item 2025.CC35.3 , City Council appointed Kwok and Associate as the external auditor to conduct an annual compliance audit for the offices of each of the City’s Accountability Officers for a five-year period for calendar years ending December 31st for 2025, 2026, 2027, 2028 and 2029. Kwok and Associate, Licensed Public Accountant, reporting on the Auditor General's Office of the City of Toronto - Report on the Results of Applying Specified Auditing Procedures to Financial Information Other Than Financial Statements for the Year Ended December 31, 2025.

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council receive the letter (May 27, 2026) from Kwok and Associate, Licensed Public Accountant on the 2025 Compliance Audit for the Auditor General’s Office of the City of Toronto for information.
