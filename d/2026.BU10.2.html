Toronto City Council · Decision

Establishing Subcommittees to hear Public Presentations on the 2026 Budget

AdoptedJan 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Setting up four subcommittees to hear public talks on the 2026 budget on January 20 and 21, at City Hall, North York, Scarborough and Etobicoke civic centres and by video. It goes to the Budget Committee for a decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Councillor Shelley Carroll recommends that the Budget Committee establish four Subcommittees in order to meet concurrently in two locations, on each of the two days of public presentations scheduled for January 20 and 21, 2026. These Subcommittees will report to the Budget Committee on their activities as part of the 2026 Budget Process.
