What was decided

The Budget Committee: 1. Recommended that the Mayor: a. Explore opportunities to increase investments in safety in our communities, for example enhancements to the Downtown East Action Plan and opportunities for youth. b. Continue to advocate to the other levels of government for increased investments by both the Federal and Provincial governments in youth violence prevention initiatives, including TOWards Peace. c. As part of the preparation of the 2027 and future budgets, work with the City Manager to: a. Ensure the capital project pipeline is released in draft alongside the staff-recommended budget; b. Continue the Rolling Program Review to realize further in-year savings, and savings to mitigate any 2027 property tax increase; c.
