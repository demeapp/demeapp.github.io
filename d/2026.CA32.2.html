Selection of Candidates for Interview for the Toronto Investment Board

Adopted with changesJan 30, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Picking four candidates to interview for the Toronto Investment Board, then recommend one to City Council. It goes to the Civic Appointments Committee for approval. Candidate details stay private.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Civic Appointments Committee must select candidates to interview for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board. Following the interviews, the Civic Appointments Committee will recommend one candidate for appointment to City Council.

What was decided

The Civic Appointments Committee: 1. Selected the following candidates for interview for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board. INB2-355; INB2-366; INB2-430 and; INB2-438. 2. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1a, 1b, and 2 to the report (January 21, 2026) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Toronto Investment Board.
