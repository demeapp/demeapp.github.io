Selection of Candidates for Interview for the Board of Directors of TO Live

Adopted with changesApr 17, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Picking eight candidates to interview for the TO Live Board, then recommend three to City Council. It goes to the Civic Appointments Committee for approval. Candidate details stay private.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Civic Appointments Committee must select candidates to interview for appointment to the Board of Directors of TO Live. Following the interviews, the Civic Appointments Committee will recommend three candidates for appointment to City Council.

What was decided

The Civic Appointments Committee: 1. Selected the following candidates for interview at its next meeting for appointment to the Board of Directors of TO Live: CTT2-497 CTT2-511 CTT2-540 CTT2-543 CTT2-551 CTT2-556 CTT2-574 CTT2-576 2. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1a, 1b, and 2 to the report (April 7, 2026) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Board of Directors of TO Live.
