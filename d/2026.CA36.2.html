Appointment of Public Members to the Lakeshore Arena Corporation Board

AdoptedJun 24, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Appointing two public members and one tenant representative to the Lakeshore Arena Corporation Board for terms ending June 26, 2028. The nominees' details stay private.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends the appointment of two public members and one tenant representative to the Lakeshore Arena Corporation Board.

What was decided

City Council on June 24 and 25, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council appoint the following candidates as public members to the Lakeshore Arena Corporation Board, at pleasure of Council, for a term office ending June 26, 2028, and until successors are appointed: Julia Alleyne Susanna Han 2. City Council appoint the following candidate as the tenant representative to the Lakeshore Arena Corporation Board, at pleasure of Council, for a term of office ending June 26, 2028, and until a successor is appointed: Elis Valera 3. City Council direct that Confidential Attachments 1 to 3 to the report (May 28, 2026) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal information about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Lakeshore Arena Corporation Board.
