Selection of Candidates for Interview for the Sankofa Square Board of Management

Adopted with changesJun 30, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Picking six candidates to interview for the Sankofa Square Board, then recommend two, including a Chair, to City Council. It goes to the Civic Appointments Committee for approval. Candidate details stay private.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Civic Appointments Committee must select candidates to interview for appointment to the Sankofa Square Board of Management. Following the interviews, the Civic Appointments Committee will recommend two candidates, including a Chair, for appointment to City Council.

What was decided

The Civic Appointments Committee: 1. Selected the following candidates for interview at its next meeting for appointment to the Sankofa Square Board of Management: YDS2-145 YDS2-162 YDS2-172 YDS2-195 YDS2-213 YDS2-225 YDS2-226 2. Directed that Confidential Attachments 1a, 1b, and 2 to the report (June 18, 2026) from the City Clerk remain confidential in their entirety as they relate to personal matters about identifiable individuals being considered for appointment to the Sankofa Square Board of Management.
