Rorschach Brewing Company By-the-Glass Permit Application to the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario

AdoptedFeb 4, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Telling Ontario's Alcohol and Gaming Commission it supports a by-the-glass licence for Rorschach Brewing's new brewery, taproom and retail store in Etobicoke-Lakeshore.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Rorschach Brewing Company opened in 2017 in the Leslieville area (1001 Eastern Avenue) and have been a local hub for food, drinks and community events for the past 8+ years. They recently acquired a secondary location in Etobicoke-Lakeshore that they are planning to open as an innovation brewery, taproom and retail store.

What was decided

City Council on February 4, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council advise the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario of its support of the application from Rorschach Brewing Company (Etobicoke) 815 Kipling Ave, Toronto, ON, M8Z 5G8, for a Manufacturer’s Limited Liquor Sales License for sales by the glass.
