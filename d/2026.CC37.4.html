The official record

What the item proposed

Seaton Park is a small, neglected park between the Bathurst subway station and the Hot Docs cinema, steps from the Bloor Annex shopping/restaurant/entertainment area. Standard levels of Parks Department maintenance have been unable to cope with chronically high level of vandalism and trash in Seaton Park, despite bitter complaints from residents about its dirty, shabby and dangerous condition. In the absence of any other solution, Council added Seaton Park to the territory of the Bloor-Annex Business Improvement Area, which is now voluntarily providing daily maintenance of Seaton Park. This has been an enormous benefit for both business and residential neighbours. The Bloor-Annex Business Improvement Area has also proposed some essential upgrades to the park to make it safer and easier to maintain.
