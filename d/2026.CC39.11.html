Designation of the Kensington Market Heritage Conservation District Under Part V of the Ontario Heritage Act - Ontario Land Tribunal Hearing

AdoptedMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The City Solicitor asks Council for direction on appeals of the Kensington Market heritage district designation, ahead of a June 1, 2026 hearing. The advice stays private as legal advice.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council designated the Kensington Market Heritage Conservation District and adopted the Kensington Market Heritage Conservation District Plan at its meeting of February 5, 2025. The Kensington Market Heritage Conservation District was subsequently appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal. The Ontario Land Tribunal has scheduled a 5-day hearing of the appeals of the Kensington Market Heritage Conservation District beginning June 1, 2026. The City Solicitor requires further direction from City Council on this litigation matter.

What was decided

City Council on March 25 and 26, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 10, 2026) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council direct that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 10, 2026) from the City Solicitor remain confidential at the discretion of the City Solicitor, as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege and information regarding potential litigation. 3. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Appendices 1 to 3 to the report (March 10, 2026) from the City Solicitor, at the discretion of the City Solicitor.
