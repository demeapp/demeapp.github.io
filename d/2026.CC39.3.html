Appointment of Interim Deputy City Managers, Infrastructure Services and Corporate Services

AdoptedMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Appointing two temporary Deputy City Managers, one for Infrastructure Services and one for Corporate Services, starting April 3, 2026. The names are in private documents.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend to City Council the appointment of Interim Deputy City Managers for Infrastructure Services and Corporate Services.

What was decided

City Council on March 25 and 26, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council appoint David Jollimore to the position of Deputy City Manager, Infrastructure Services on an interim basis effective April 3, 2026. 2. City Council appoint Sonia Brar to the position of Interim Deputy City Manager, Corporate Services effective April 3, 2026. 3. City Council authorize the appropriate City officials to take the necessary action to give effect to City Council's decision, including the introduction in City Council of the necessary bills to appoint the candidates as Interim Deputy City Manager, Infrastructure Services and Interim Deputy City Manager, Corporate Services under Chapter 169 of the City of Toronto Municipal Code and the City of Toronto Act, 2006. 4.
