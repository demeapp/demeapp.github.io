Legal Advice Regarding City of Vaughan v. 7553 Islington Holding Inc.

AdoptedMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The City Solicitor asks Council to approve private instructions about legal advice on a Tribunal decision for 155 St. Dennis Drive and a related matter in Vaughan.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting of November 12 and 13, 2025 City Council instructed the City Solicitor to bring an application for judicial review, motion for leave to appeal, and if granted, to appeal to Divisional Court, from the October 10, 2025, decision of the Ontario Land Tribunal regarding the property at 155 St. Dennis Drive. Following the 155 St. Dennis Decision, the Ontario Land Tribunal also rendered a decision on related issues concerning a separate development at 7553 Islington Avenue in the City of Vaughan. The City of Vaughan and Toronto Region Conservation Authority have sought leave to appeal to the Divisional Court from the 7553 Islington Decision. The City Solicitor requires further directions on related time sensitive litigation matters.

What was decided

City Council on March 25 and 26, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 11, 2026) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council direct that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 11, 2026) from the City Solicitor remain confidential in its entirety as it contains advice or communications that are subject to solicitor-client privilege and relates to litigation or potential litigation. 3. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (March 11, 2026) from the City Solicitor, at the discretion of the City Solicitor.
