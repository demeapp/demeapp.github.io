The official record

What the item proposed

On August 10, 2022, the City received Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment applications for 40 - 62 Spadina Avenue and 378 Wellington Street West to permit the construction of a 28-storey (93 metres, including mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building. The proposal included retail at grade, two levels of office, and 521 residential units above. The façade of the existing four-storey factory building was proposed to be retained and incorporated into the new development. On May 15, 2024, the applicant submitted a revised proposal for a 30-storey (99 metres, including mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building with retail at-grade, no office component, and 550 residential units.
