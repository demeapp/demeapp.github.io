Expropriation of 1430 Gerrard Street East - Request for Directions Regarding Ontario Land Tribunal Hearing

AdoptedApr 22, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The City Solicitor asks Council for direction on a Tribunal hearing about the City taking the land at 1430 Gerrard Street East for permanent affordable rental homes. Details stay private as legal advice.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On February 6 and 7, 2024, City Council authorized the expropriation of the property municipally known as 1430 Gerrard Street East (the "Property") which are lands comprised of approximately 9,135 square feet, for municipal purposes including Permanent Affordable Rental Housing (the "Project"). On April 29, 2024, the City registered Plan AT6560961 which expropriated the fee simple interest in the Property. The Property is located on the north side of Gerrard Street East and west of Coxwell Avenue. At the time of the expropriation, the Property was leased by the owner to the City of Toronto and sub-leased to a non-profit provider of transitional housing.
