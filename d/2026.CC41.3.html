Update on 2026 Motorized Watercraft Exclusion Zones

Adopted with changesMay 20, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Council gets an update on marker buoys showing where motorized boats cannot go in the Port of Toronto. It approved a $310,500 transfer to buy and install the buoys in 2026, with the money flowing to the Toronto Port Authority.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The City Manager and the General Manager, Parks and Recreation will be submitting a supplementary report to City Council in advance of its meeting on May 20, 21 and 22, 2026, to provide an update on Council's request to the Toronto Port Authority to fund marker buoys communicating the boundaries of Motorized Watercraft Exclusion Zones within the Port of Toronto.

What was decided

City Council on May 20 and 21, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Director, Waterfront Secretariat to transfer $310,500 for planned 2026 capital work from the Wider Waterfront and Marine Strategy project as part of the next phase of Waterfront Revitalization Initiative within the Waterfront Secretariat's 2026-2035 Capital Budget and Plan and apply the funds towards the purchase and installation of marker buoys for Motorized Watercraft Exclusion Zones in 2026. 2. City Council authorize the City Manager to enter into an agreement with the Toronto Port Authority for the purchase and operation of marker buoys for Motorized Watercraft Exclusion Zones in 2026, on terms and conditions satisfactory to the Director of Waterfront Secretariat, and in a form approved by the City Solicitor, and flow funds to the Toronto Port Authority governed by the agreement. 3.
