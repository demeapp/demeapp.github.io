The official record

What the item proposed

On October 20, 2022, the City received Official Plan Amendment, Zoning By-law Amendment, and Rental Housing Demolition applications to permit the redevelopment of the site at 690-720 Sheppard Avenue East (the "Site") with two mixed-use buildings of 32 and 28 storeys. On February 24, 2025, the applicant appealed City Council's neglect or failure to make a decision on the Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment applications within statutory timeframes (the "Applications") to the Ontario Land Tribunal. On June 1, 2026, the applicant filed revised plans with the Ontario Land Tribunal, which plans are attached as Public Attachment 1 (the "Revised Plans"). The Ontario Land Tribunal hearing will now proceed on the basis of the Revised Plans.
