257, 259 and 291 Lake Shore Boulevard East, 2 Small Street and 200 Queens Quay East - Minutes of Settlement with Redpath Sugar Ltd.

AdoptedJun 24, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is routine business: the City's lawyer asks council to adopt private recommendations about a settlement with Redpath Sugar over its waterfront refinery's environmental impacts near the Quayside development lands. Details stay private as legal advice.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Redpath Sugar Ltd. ("Redpath") operates a refining, processing, packaging, and storage of sugar and sugar-related products facility which, among other things includes the operation of an electrical co-generation system on the lands known municipally as 95 Queens Quay East (the "Redpath Facility"). There is a November, 2011 agreement (the "November 2011 Minutes") between the City, Redpath, and the Toronto Waterfront Revitalization Corporation (whose obligations have been assumed by the owner of 257 to 259 and 291 Lake Shore Boulevard East, 2 Small Street and 200 Queens Quay East ("Quayside Blocks 1 and 2"), which binds the parties to procedural and substantive obligations related to the assessment and mitigation of the Redpath Facility's environmental impacts on nearby sensitive uses.
