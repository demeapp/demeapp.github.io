The official record

What the item proposed

On May 21 and 22, 2025, City Council adopted Official Plan Amendment 804 in response to Provincial legislation (Bill 97 the Helping Homebuyers, Protecting Tenants Act, 2023) and policy changes affecting Employment Areas. Official Plan Amendment 804 aligns the Official Plan with the new definition of “area of employment” in the Planning Act and “employment area” definition in the Provincial Planning Statement 2024. The definition of 'employment areas' in Official Plan Amendment 804 mirrors the definition in the Provincial Policy Statement and does not permit institutional or commercial uses, including retail and office uses, in Employment Areas unless associated with the primary employment use or lawfully established, consistent with the Provincial Policy Statement.
