Renew Sheppard East Secondary Plan - Ontario Land Tribunal Hearing - Request for Directions

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is routine business: the City's lawyer asks council how to handle seventeen landowner appeals of the Renew Sheppard East Secondary Plan land-use rules. Details stay private as legal advice.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council adopted Official Plan Amendment 777, which included the Renew Sheppard East Secondary Plan. Seventeen (17) landowners appealed City Council's decision to adopt Official Plan Amendment 777 to the Ontario Land Tribunal. The Ontario Land Tribunal has conducted three Case Management Conferences and has scheduled a fourth Case Management Conference for September 28, 2026. It is anticipated that the Ontario Land Tribunal will issue a Procedural Order and schedule a hearing of the appeals at the September 28, 2026 Case Management Conferences. The City Solicitor requires further directions for the upcoming Case Management Conferences scheduled for September 28, 2026. This matter is therefore urgent and should not be deferred.

What was decided

City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (July 15, 2026) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Appendix B to the report (July 15, 2026) from the City Solicitor. 3. City Council direct that Confidential Appendices A and C, and the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (July 15, 2026) from the City Solicitor remain confidential at the discretion of the City Solicitor, as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
