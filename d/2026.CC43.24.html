30 to 36 Hendon Avenue - Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment - Ontario Land Tribunal Hearing - Request for Directions

Adopted with changesJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is routine business: the City's lawyer asks council for direction on a hearing about a proposed 46-storey purpose-built rental building at 30 to 36 Hendon Avenue, which council refused. Details stay private as legal advice.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On December 20, 2024, the City received an Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment application for 30 to 36 Hendon Avenue to permit the construction of a 46-storey purpose-built rental residential building. The applicant appealed City Council's refusal of the applications for the Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment, to the Ontario Land Tribunal on August 22, 2025. The City Solicitor requires further directions before an Ontario Land Tribunal hearing is scheduled.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (July 13, 2026) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 and Confidential Appendices A and B to the report (July 13, 2026) from the City Solicitor. 3. City Council direct that the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (July 13, 2026) from the City Solicitor remain confidential at the discretion of the City Solicitor, as it contains advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege. 4.
