The official record

What the item proposed

On February 21, 2025, the City received an Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application for 245 to 251 Marlee Avenue, 1 to 7 Romar Crescent and 16 Stayner Avenue to permit the construction of a 37 and 35-storey mixed-use building. The applicant subsequently appealed the Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application to the Ontario Land Tribunal citing Council’s failure to make a decision within the timeframes specified the Planning Act. City Council has directed the City Solicitor and appropriate staff to attend the Ontario Land Tribunal in opposition to the application. The City Solicitor requires further directions with respect to this ongoing Ontario Land Tribunal appeal. No hearing dates are currently scheduled, although appearances may be scheduled during City Council's election break and instruction is required.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council adopt the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the supplementary report (July 28, 2026) from the City Solicitor. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1 to the supplementary report (July 28, 2026) from the City Solicitor and Confidential Appendices A and B to the report (July 10, 2026) from the City Solicitor. 3. City Council direct that Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (July 10, 2026) from the City Solicitor and the balance of Confidential Attachment 1 to the supplementary report (July 28, 2026) from the City Solicitor remain confidential at the discretion of the City Solicitor, as they contain advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege.
