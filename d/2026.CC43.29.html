87 Highland Avenue - Alterations to a Designated Heritage Property in the North Rosedale Heritage Conservation District

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Council decided whether to refuse changes the owner made without a permit at 87 Highland Avenue, a heritage-protected home in North Rosedale, including removed decorative details and new windows. Staff say the changes do not fit the home's heritage character.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council refuse the alterations to the designated heritage property at 87 Highland Avenue under Section 42 of the Ontario Heritage Act. This is a C-rated property located in the North Rosedale Heritage Conservation District. The property owner has removed architectural elements, including the dentils on the east gable and the brackets on the north gable, along with the Tudor detailing on the north and east gables, and installed new elements in their place. Additionally, a new front porch has been constructed and new windows installed along the front and side elevations. The property owner has also applied a white coating to the masonry on the existing two-storey dwelling and new brick wall at the front of the property. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council refuse alterations to the designated heritage property at 87 Highland Avenue in the North Rosedale Heritage Conservation District under Part 5, Section 42 of the Ontario Heritage Act, comprised of the construction of a new front porch, installation of new windows on the front and side elevations, removal of architectural elements, including the brackets, dentils and Tudor detailing on the north and east gables and their incompatible replacement, and the application of a white coating to the masonry on the two-storey house and brick wall at the front of the property, in accordance with the submission package dated October 3, 2025, submitted by Giamo + Associates Architects Incorporated, on file with the Senior Manager, Heritage Planning. 2.
