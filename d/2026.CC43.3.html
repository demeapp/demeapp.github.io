The official record

What the item proposed

This report describes the Integrity Commissioner’s investigation into the unauthorized disclosure of confidential information from an attachment to the February 12, 2026 Audit Committee agenda. Some of that information appeared in a media article before the material became public. City Council directed the Integrity Commissioner to investigate whether a Member of Council or political staff member was responsible. While the unauthorized disclosure was deliberate, there was insufficient evidence to determine who disclosed this and to conclude that a Member or political staff breached the Code of Conduct. The investigation identified broader concerns about access to confidential information and how it is shared within Members’ offices.
