Appointment of Interim City Solicitor

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Council decided whether to appoint an interim City Solicitor starting September 8, 2026, until a permanent one is chosen. The person's name stays private until the appointment is approved.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend to City Council the appointment of an Interim City Solicitor.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council appoint Sharmila M. Clark to the position of City Solicitor on an interim basis effective September 8, 2026, until such time a permanent City Solicitor for the City of Toronto is appointed. 2. City Council authorize the public release of the name and curriculum vitae summary of the Interim City Solicitor in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (July 22, 2026) from the City Manager.
