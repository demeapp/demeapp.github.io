FIFA World Cup 2026 Toronto: Early Outcomes from the Hosting of the FIFA World Cup 2026

Adopted with changesJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Council got a report on early results from Toronto hosting the FIFA World Cup 2026 and told staff to keep the traffic measures that worked, like the new traffic cameras. Council also asked the Auditor General to audit the value for money of Toronto's hosting. The cost is put at $9.0 million, covering fan festival costs.

Decided by a recorded vote, 25 in favour and 1 against — every member's position is public.

What the item proposed

The City Manager and the Executive Director, FIFA World Cup 2026 Toronto Secretariat will submit a supplementary report to City Council before its meeting on July 29, 2026 that will provide an overview of early outcomes from the hosting of the FIFA Word Cup 2026 in Toronto and respond to direction received from City Council at its meeting on April 22, 2026 ( 2026.EX30.5 ) to identify revenue streams to offset FIFA Fan Festival™ related costs.
