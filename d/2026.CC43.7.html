What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to provide a status update on Canada Post's implementation of the community mailbox program in Toronto, as requested by Council at its meeting on April 22 and 23, 2026. Following Council's direction, Canada Post officials sent a communication addressed to the Mayor and Members of Council (on May 14, 2026 - Attachment 1 to this report) that includes responses to requested information contained in Council's motions ( MM40.36 and MM40.39 ). Discussions with Canada Post and City staff are ongoing, including providing feedback to Canada Post on the proposed community mailbox site locations in Ward 1 Etobicoke North, seeking additional details on the overall conversion project timeline and a city-wide implementation plan. City staff will report back to the appropriate Committee once comprehensive details have been provided by Canada Post and potential impacts…
