What the item proposed

This report responds to Council direction to establish a ward-based maintenance team pilot. The report outlines the City's current approach, recent improvements to related processes and key considerations to inform further analysis and the potential development of a ward-based approach, including the work required to define the model and assess feasibility prior to any pilot. The City currently delivers public realm maintenance through an established interdivisional model. Services are delivered based on geographic operating areas that vary by division, as opposed to ward-based areas. As a result, proactive maintenance, 311 service requests, and planning coordination are all organized and managed according to these geographic areas.
