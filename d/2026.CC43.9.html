Toronto City Council · Decision

Implementation of the Development Charges Reduction Program

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Council decided whether to temporarily cut building fees by 40 to 60 percent and freeze fee increases through 2029, backed by $1.5 billion in federal and provincial funding over 10 years. The goal is to lower the cost of building homes.

The official summary names a figure of $1.5 billion.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On June 23, 2026, the Government of Canada, the Province of Ontario, and the Mayor of Toronto confirmed $1.5 billion in funding for the City over 10 years through the Development Charges Reduction Program, as part of the Canada-Ontario Partnership to Build. The Development Charges Reduction Program will provide the City of Toronto with federal and provincial infrastructure funding to support existing housing-enabling growth-related capital projects already included in the City's 10-Year Capital Plan. The funding will reduce reliance on development charges that would otherwise support these projects, thereby enabling time-limited development charges residential rate reductions of 40 percentt to 60 percent for over a three-year period to reduce development costs, enhance viability and increase housing supply.
