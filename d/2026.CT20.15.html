What the item proposed

Staff from the City Manager’s Office, Corporate Real Estate Management and Economic Development and Culture will lead board members through a governance training. This training will include information about TO Live’s governance framework; key legislation, by-laws and policies that inform the roles and responsibilities of board members; and the role of City staff in supporting TO Live, including support for culture and capital planning.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of TO Live: 1. Deferred consideration of the item until the next meeting of the Board of Directors of TO Live.
