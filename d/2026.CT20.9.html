Toronto City Council · Decision

TO Live Policy 304 - Facility Fee Surcharge

AdoptedJun 15, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Amending its Policy 304 on the Facility Fee Surcharge by raising the surcharge by $1.00 per ticket at applicable TO Live venues, effective for all new contracts signed after board approval. It goes to the TO Live board for approval. It is also asked to double the share of that surcharge revenue allocated to TO Live's operations, from $1.00 to $2.00 per ticket, on the same terms. This matters to ticket buyers because the fee is added to ticket prices at TO Live venues.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek Board approval for updates to TO Live Policy 304 - Facility Fee Surcharge (FFS), including an increase to the Facility Fee Surcharge and an increase in the allocation of Facility Fee Surcharge funds to TO Live’s operations.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of TO Live: 1. Approved the amendments to TO Live Policy 304 Facility Fee Surcharge to increase the Facility Fee Surcharge rates by $1.00 per ticket at applicable TO Live venues, effective for all new contracts executed following Board approval. 2. Approved an increase in the operational allocation of the Facility Fee Surcharge revenues from $1.00 to $2.00 per ticket, effective for all new contracts executed following Board approval. 3. Approved the amended TO Live Policy 304 - Facility Fee Surcharge substantially in the form attached as Attachment 1 to the report (May 25, 2026) from the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live.
