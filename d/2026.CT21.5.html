Grant Applications

AdoptedSep 10, 2026 · Toronto City Council

TO Live's board decided whether to approve grant applications to the federal Canada Arts Presentation Fund for its 2027 season, and to Miziwe Biik Aboriginal Employment and Training for wage help for up to two staff jobs.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek authorization from the Board of Directors of TO Live to submit grant applications to the Department of Canadian Heritage's Canada Arts Presentation Fund and to Miziwe Biik Aboriginal Employment & Training for wage subsidies.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of TO Live: 1. Authorized and directed the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live to submit a grant application to the Canada Arts Presentation Fund together with all necessary supporting documentation, which will assist with the presentation of TO Live's 2027 performance season. 2. Authorized and directed the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live to submit a grant application to Miziwe Biik Aboriginal Employment & Training together with all necessary supporting documentation for wage subsidies for up to two staff positions.
