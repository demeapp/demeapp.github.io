City of Toronto Sustainable Debenture Program

AdoptedApr 14, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is for information only: the City is launching a Sustainable Debenture Program to sell green, social, or sustainability bonds for projects with environmental or social benefits. No decision is required.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides the Debenture Committee with information on the City's new Sustainable Debenture Program. This new program consolidates, updates and builds upon the City’s previously established Green Debenture Framework (2018) and Social Debenture Framework (2020) and enables the City to issue Green, Social, or Sustainability Debentures. In addition, under this program, the City may be able to achieve slightly lower borrowing costs. To establish this new program, a Sustainable Debenture Framework (Framework) provides flexibility to finance eligible capital projects that deliver environmental and/or social benefits, while continuing to align the City’s debt program with evolving global sustainable finance standards and investor demand. The Framework supports the City’s leadership in sustainable finance and reinforces its commitment to transparency, accountability, and impact…

What was decided

The Debenture Committee adopted the following: 1. The information provided by the report (March 23, 2026) be received by the Debenture Committee.
