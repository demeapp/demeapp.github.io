The official record

What the item proposed

Danny Brown, Senior Project Manager, Waterfront, Parks and Recreation, City of Toronto and Mary Anne Young, Senior Project Manager, Special Projects, Toronto and Region Conservation Authority will give a presentation on Tommy Thompson Park Visitor Experience Plan. The City of Toronto and Toronto and Region Conservation Authority are working together on a Visitor Experience Plan (VEP) to study ways to improve how people experience and connect with nature at Tommy Thompson Park. The creation of the VEP was directed by Toronto City Council in 2021 (Council report is online here) and background work to inform the project started in 2025. The development of the VEP presents an opportunity to both retain and deepen relationships with existing users and nurture and welcome newer emerging audiences, while maintaining ecological integrity and the restorative qualities that make TTP special.

What was decided

The Toronto Accessibility Advisory Committee received the item for information.
