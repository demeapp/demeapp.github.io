Ombudsman Toronto Report - No Refunds for Discontinued TTC Tickets and Tokens: An Investigation Behind How the Decision was Made

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The Ombudsman asks council to accept his report on how the TTC decided not to refund or credit discontinued old tickets and tokens. It makes eight suggestions the TTC has agreed to put in place.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On August 6, 2025, I notified the Toronto Transit Commission of my intention to investigate the process leading up to the Toronto Transit Commission's decision not to provide refunds or credits for discontinued legacy fares. I have concluded my investigation and am submitting my report, dated June 17, 2026, to City Council for consideration at its June 24 to 26, 2026, meeting. Toronto Transit Commission leadership and the Toronto Transit Commission Board have had an opportunity to review this report. This report makes eight recommendations which Toronto Transit Commission leadership and the Board have agreed to implement.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council receive the Ombudsman report "No refunds for discontinued Toronto Transit Commission tickets and tokens: An investigation behind how the decision was made" (June 17, 2026) for information.
