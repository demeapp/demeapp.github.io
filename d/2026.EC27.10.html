Request for a Report on Existing Services Available to French-Speaking Torontonians

Adopted with changesApr 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The call on whether to have staff include, in a June 2026 report on services for French-speaking Torontonians, how many City jobs require French and how many of those jobs exist belongs to the committee.

What was decided

The Economic and Community Development Committee: 1. Requested the Chief People Officer to report to the Toronto Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee at its June 18, 2026 meeting on the number of City jobs that have a posted requirement to speak French and how many of those jobs are filled.
