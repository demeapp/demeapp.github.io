Further Direction on Report on Rejoining the Association Francophone des Municipalités de l’Ontario

AdoptedApr 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the committee was asked to have the City Manager include information about Toronto rejoining the French-speaking municipalities' association of Ontario in the report back to the Francophone Affairs Advisory Committee.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
