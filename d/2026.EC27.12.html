Advancing Community Development through Evidence-Based Planning

Referred back for more workApr 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The Confronting Anti-Black Racism Advisory Committee asks council to have staff report on a citywide plan for community development plans in priority neighbourhoods, and asks the Mayor to consider $250,000 to $500,000 per neighbourhood per year in the 2027 budget.
