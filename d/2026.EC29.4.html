Establishing Toronto's Cultural Districts Program

AdoptedJun 24, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Council weighed whether to have staff set up a Cultural Districts Program in phases, starting with formal recognition of culturally distinct areas such as Little Jamaica and Church-Wellesley Village, to celebrate them and help respond to the loss of cultural identity from displacement.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report presents a framework to establish a Cultural Districts Program for Toronto, which would recognize and celebrate distinct cultural areas across the city, while offering supports to respond to displacement and subsequent loss of cultural identity that some areas are facing. Cultural Districts are defined as culturally distinct mixed-use areas that feature a significant concentration of cultural spaces and institutions, culturally significant businesses, and community spaces. Cultural Districts are widely recognized as areas of shared history and identity that offer a sense of belonging. They not only serve the local area but also draw people from across the city and beyond.
