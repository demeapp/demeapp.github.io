What the item proposed

In accordance with the Relationship Framework approved by City Council for the Toronto Licensing Tribunal (TLT), the Chair's 2025 Annual Report is being communicated to the Economic and Community Development Committee for information. Court Services provides administrative support to the TLT and is bringing this report forward for the first time. The decision to have Court Services submit this report was made jointly by Court Services and Municipal Licensing and Standards (MLS). The TLT is an independent quasi-judicial body that makes decisions about business licensing matters brought forward by MLS, or at the request of a licence holder or applicant, in accordance with Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 545, Licensing, Chapter 546, Licensing of Vehicles-for-Hire, and for short-term rental companies, Chapter 547, Licensing and Registration of Short-term Rentals.
