Exploring Options to Recoup City Enforcement Costs for Challenging Properties

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Bravo asks council to have City divisions and the City Solicitor look at options for the City to recover its enforcement costs for challenging properties like 92 Lappin Avenue. City costs there total $306,000, of which $236,000 has been recovered.

The official summary names a figure of $306,000.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Located in Ward 9 – Davenport, the privately-owned property at 92 Lappin Avenue has been the subject of longstanding City interventions dating back to 2009. Over this period, the City has responded to ongoing concerns related to fire and life safety, property standards, and the provision of vital services. There have also been serious concerns regarding the vulnerability of tenants and people visiting the location, and the need for significant support. After extended work by myself, my staff and many City of Toronto employees, in August 2025, Toronto Fire Services and Toronto Building determined that the property posed serious fire and life safety risks, leading to its closure. The building cannot be reoccupied until these unsafe conditions are addressed.
