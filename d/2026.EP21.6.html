Nighttime Economy - Letter of Support

AdoptedFeb 2, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The Exhibition Place board has the final say on whether to have its CEO keep writing support letters for late-night extended-hours events, so tenants can apply for temporary permission to sell alcohol longer at licensed locations. Exhibition Place generates over $594 million in annual economic impact.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to the Board recommendation from Item EP15.3 in February 2025 where the Board directed staff to provide a letter of support to tenants and event organizers at Exhibition Place for events held between May 1 and September 30, to be considered as Municipally Significant to apply for a Special Occasion Permit or a temporary extension of premises and/or hours for the sale of alcohol at permanently licensed locations, and staff report back annually to the Board on the Nighttime Economy - Municipally Significant Designation. Exhibition Place is Canada’s most extensive entertainment venue, hosting more than 2,000 events and attracting over 6 million visitors annually. This iconic entertainment, music, and events site is pivotal in Toronto’s cultural and economic fabric, generating over $594 million in annual economic impact. What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Directed the Chief Executive Officer to continue to provide letters of support to tenants for late-night extended hours corporate and entertainment events held at tenanted facilities, expressing the economic and tourism significance, in support of their application for a temporary extension of operating hours to sell alcohol at licensed locations.
