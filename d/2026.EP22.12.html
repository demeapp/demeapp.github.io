Upgrade Salons 103, 105, 109, and 110 at the Enercare Centre

AdoptedApr 16, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Before the Exhibition Place board for a decision: approve a $1,366,078.15 contract (including a $20,000.00 cash allowance) with Century Group Inc. to upgrade salons 103, 105, 109 and 110 at the Enercare Centre. It was the only compliant bid.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approving a contract with Century Group Inc. ("CGI") of Mississauga, Ontario for upgrades to Salons 103, 105, 109 and 110 at the Enercare Centre. The tender for this contract was issued on January 6, 2026 through the Exhibition Place Purchasing and Materials Stores via the Bonfire Online Procurement System, and the recommendation is to award the contract to the lowest compliant bidder meeting tender requirements and specifications within the available budget.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the award of Contract No. 25-076-57632 (RFT No. EP101-2026) to Century Group Inc. for the upgrades to Salons 103, 105, 109, and 110 at the Enercare Centre in the amount of $1,366,078.15 which includes a cash allowance of $20,000.00 but excludes HST; this being the sole compliant tender submission received following the public tendering of the project.
