Grievance Update and 2025 Year-end Grievance Summary

AdoptedApr 16, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is for information only: the board receives a summary of 2025 grievance activity: 16 grievances were filed across unions, up from 6 in 2024, with no arbitrations. No policy change is proposed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report presents a summary and analysis of grievance and arbitration activity for 2025, along with a comparison with previous years. Exhibition Place has seven (7) collective agreements to which it is bound on the grounds. In 2025, the total number of grievances filed by all bargaining units on Exhibition Place grounds was sixteen (16), compared with six (6) in 2024. There were no arbitrations in 2025. LiUNA 506 filed five (5) grievances, and Carpenters 27 submitted one (1) grievance, with all grievances either resolved or withdrawn. IATSE 58 filed ten (10) grievances, of which one (1) was resolved. Nine (9) of the grievances from IATSE relate to a specific event or originate from a tenanted facility. The remaining grievance concerns the assignment of work and the scope of jurisdiction.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Received the report (March 31, 2026) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place for information.
