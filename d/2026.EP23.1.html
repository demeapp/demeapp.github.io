Draft Financial Statements and Audit Results for the Year Ended December 31, 2025

AdoptedJun 18, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The decision on whether to approve KPMG's audited 2025 financial statements and related transfers rests with the Exhibition Place board. The figures include a $0.450 million operating budget deficit subsidy received from the City and a $2.538 million transfer payable to the City.

The official summary names a figure of $2.088 million.
