Toronto City Council · Decision

Exhibition Place Position on New Requests for Casino License

AdoptedJun 18, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Whether to allow the long-standing temporary casino during the annual fair, and to require that any new casino licence requests at Exhibition Place come to the board for approval now sits with the Exhibition Place board.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report outlines Exhibition Place’s present casino activities. The report also provides public health evidence regarding the social and health impacts associated with gambling expansion in Toronto. Exhibition Place recognizes the historical temporary casino operated by the Canadian National Exhibition Association (CNEA) during the annual fair and proposes that this be grandfathered in. While the City of Toronto does not have a formal policy position on temporary charitable casino operations, City Council and Toronto Public Health have a documented history of opposing the establishment or expansion of permanent casinos in Toronto.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Grandfathered the historical temporary casino operated by the Canadian National Exhibition Association (CNEA) during the annual fair. 2. Directed the Chief Executive Officer to bring any new requests for casino licenses at Exhibition Place to the Board for approval.
