Parking Lot CP853 Rehabilitation

AdoptedJun 18, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The decision on approving a $2,039,985.00 contract (including a $50,000.00 cash allowance) with Tony Battista Paving Inc. to repair Parking Lot CP853, a time-sensitive project to stop further pavement damage rests with the Exhibition Place board.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approving a contract with Tony Battista Paving Inc. of Mississauga, Ontario for the Parking Lot CP853 Rehabilitation project. The tender for this contract was issued on March 23, 2026, through Exhibition Place Purchasing and Materials Stores via the Bonfire Online Procurement System, and the recommendation is to award the contract to the lowest compliant bidder meeting tender requirements and specifications within the available budget. This project is time‑sensitive due to seasonal weather and the need to prevent further site deterioration. Deferring work would accelerate pavement failure, raise long‑term costs, and risk disruptions during major events.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the award of Contract No. 87924-26 (RFT No. EP105-2026) to Tony Battista Paving Inc. of Mississauga, Ontario for the rehabilitation of Parking Lot CP853 in the amount of $2,039,985.00 which includes a cash allowance of $50,000.00 but excludes HST; this being the lowest compliant tender submission received following the public tendering of the project.
