Agreement with the Festival Management Committee for the Toronto Caribbean Carnival 2026

AdoptedJun 18, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The Exhibition Place board is set to decide whether to approve a one-year agreement with the Festival Management Committee for the 2026 Toronto Caribbean Carnival Grande Parade at Exhibition Place on Saturday, August 1, 2026, on the same terms as 2025.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends a one-year agreement with the Festival Management Committee (FMC) for the Grande Parade 2026 at Exhibition Place based on the same terms and conditions as approved by the Board in 2025. The date of the Grand Parade is Saturday August 1, 2026.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the execution of a one-year agreement with the Festival Management Committee for the Grande Parade for 2026, on the terms and conditions of the standard license agreement for Exhibition Place outlined in the report (June 4, 2026) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place, and such other terms satisfactory to the Chief Executive Officer of Exhibition Place and the City Solicitor.
