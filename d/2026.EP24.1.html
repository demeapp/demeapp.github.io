Proposed Parking Rate Adjustment - January 1, 2027

AdoptedSep 17, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A decision on raising daily regular and exhibitor parking rates by $2 starting January 1, 2027, ending the evening rate, plus $1 increases in each of 2028, 2029 and 2030 goes to the Exhibition Place board.

The official summary names a figure of $2.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks the Board’s approval to increase daily regular parking rates by $2, exhibitor rates by $2, commencing January 1, 2027, and the elimination of the evening rate. This report also seeks the Board's approval for a $1 increase to the daily and exhibitor parking rates for the following three years commencing on January 1, 2028, 2029, and 2030. An annual review of parking rates shows that with the increases proposed, Exhibition Place parking rates will cover operating cost increases, maintain its profitability, and remain competitive in the marketplace. The last parking rate increase was implemented on January 1, 2023, and costs have increased since.

What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Approved the adjustments in parking rates at Exhibition Place as set out in Appendix B to the report (September 2, 2026) from the Chief Executive Officer, Exhibition Place, effective January 1, 2027.
