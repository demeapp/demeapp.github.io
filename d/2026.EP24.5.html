What the item proposed

An Exhibition Place employee is recognized by the Board for a recent outstanding accomplishment. Andrew Landrigan, Manager-Event Services successfully earned and was presented with his Certified Venue Professional (CVP) designation on August 9th, 2026 at the IAVM Awards Ceremony in Indianapolis. It takes a dedicated person to complete their CVP and Andrew Landrigan, CEM, CVP is the first employee at Exhibition Place to earn this designation. The Certified Venue Professional (CVP) designation is a distinguished credential that recognizes an individual who has demonstrated operational competence and a deep commitment to the advancement of the venue Management Industry. The CVP designation signifies those venue professionals who have continued to strive for excellence, innovation, and professional growth within venue management. What was decided

The Board of Governors of Exhibition Place: 1. Applauded and extended congratulations to Mr. Andrew Landrigan for successfully achieving the Certified Venue Professional designation from the International Association of Venue Managers and being the first employee to receive this designation at Exhibition Place.
