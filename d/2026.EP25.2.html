What the item proposed

Toronto, being North America’s fourth largest City is home to Exhibition Place. The site currently hosts 2,000 events annually with approximately 5.5 million visitors per year for exhibitions, conventions, events, and entertainment, and we are moving towards becoming a 365-day destination for an impressive range of activities. It is important to note that Exhibition Place is pedestrian friendly, and we are visited by our neighbours to the north, from across the country and around the world. Exhibition Place's vision is to become Canada’s premier destination for conventions, exhibitions, events, and entertainment. We are a unique site in North America, and our goal is to transform Exhibition Place into an intimate space that facilitates unimpeded movement of people.
