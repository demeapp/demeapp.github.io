Port Lands Flood Protection Project: Update and Next Steps

AdoptedMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Council decides whether to authorize a lease or licence with the Toronto Port Lands Company for lands needed for early works on Ookwemin Minising Phase 1, after the Port Lands Flood Protection project reached substantial completion in September 2025.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an update on the Port Lands Flood Protection project, which reached substantial completion on September 30, 2025. Since the last update to City Council in May of 2023 many important project milestones have been reached. These include: - Completion and opening of four new bridges (two on Cherry Street over the Keating Channel, one on Cherry Street Bridge over the new Don River and one bridge on Commissioners Street over the new Don River); - Reconstruction of the new Lake Shore Bridge over the Don River; - Removal of the old Cherry Street lift bridge over Keating Channel; - New water, sanitary and stormwater infrastructure along Cherry Street, Commissioners Street and Don Roadway; - Completion of a Flood Protection Landform underneath the new Don Roadway; - Completion, flooding and opening of the new Don River valley to Lake Ontario, with associated habitats and…
