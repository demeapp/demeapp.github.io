Implementation of O.Reg 1/26 under the Liquor Licence and Control Act, 2019 in the City of Toronto

Adopted with changesApr 22, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Bravo asks council to have the City Clerk and Economic Development and Culture report to the April 22, 2026 meeting on the by-law changes needed to put in place the province's expanded bring-your-own liquor permits for cultural and community events.

The official record

What the item proposed

On March 17, 2026, the Province of Ontario announced an expansion of the liquor licence permit category for “bring-your-own” events (formerly known as tailgate events) to include cultural and community outdoor events such as neighbourhood festivals, movie screenings, art exhibits, and farmer’s markets. This creates new opportunities for local events across Toronto happening this summer, if they wish to participate. In order to implement the expanded permit category created by this new regulation, Toronto City Council needs to establish a process for municipal designation of cultural and community events for the purpose of liquor licensing. City Council update the delegation of authority to the City Clerk to include changes to the administrative process for bring-your-own events as set out in Attachment 2 to the supplementary report (April 22, 2026) from the City Clerk. 3.
