FIFA World Cup 2026 Toronto: By-law Exemptions to Facilitate Planning and Delivery of the FIFA World Cup 2026 Events at Fort York and the Bentway, and at Nathan Phillips Square

AdoptedApr 22, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Council has the final say on whether to authorize exemptions to certain by-laws to deliver FIFA World Cup 2026 events at Fort York, the Bentway and Nathan Phillips Square, including an exemption allowing the sale of plastic bottled water alongside City water trailers.
