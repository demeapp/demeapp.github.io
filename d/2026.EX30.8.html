AdoptedApr 15, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The Service Excellence Committee asks the Executive Committee to have Parks and Recreation, Transportation Services and Municipal Licensing and Standards report back on May 14, 2026, on how they use 311 data to spot trends and improve services.
