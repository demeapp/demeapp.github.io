Exploring Legal Services for the 2SLGBTQ+ community at The 519 Community Centre

AdoptedJun 16, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A councillor asks staff and The 519 to report back by July 21, 2026, on a plan for staff to give legal advice to 2SLGBTQ+ clients who cannot get legal aid.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The 519 Church Street Community Centre has received a three-year grant from the Law Foundation of Ontario to expand legal support services for 2SLGBTQ+ individuals who are unable to access legal aid, with a particular focus on trans women, gender-based violence survivors, newcomers and refugees. The proposed model would allow staff at The 519 to provide legal education and limited, direct legal advice to clients who fall outside the reach of existing legal aid programs, pro bono services, and community legal clinics. The 519 currently operates a program where 519 staff refer clients to approximately 50 independent volunteer pro bono lawyers covering six to eight areas of law; however, 519 staff do not currently provide direct legal advice. What was decided

The Executive Committee: 1.
