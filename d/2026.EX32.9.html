Fair Wage Office - 2025 Annual Report

AdoptedJun 16, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: council was asked to receive the Fair Wage Office's 2025 report on enforcing the city's fair pay rules. Nothing changes.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an overview of Fair Wage Office activities in 2025. Pursuant to Municipal Code Chapter 67, Fair Wage, the Manager, Fair Wage Office, is required to report annually on the administration and enforcement of the Fair Wage Policy, including the identification of contractors and sub-contractors that have violated the Fair Wage Policy or settled Labour Trades Contractual Obligations in the Construction Industry Policy grievances, and any other necessary information. The annual report is transmitted to the appropriate standing committee by the Chief Procurement Officer.

What was decided

The Executive Committee: 1. Received the report (June 2, 2026) from the Chief Procurement Officer for information.
