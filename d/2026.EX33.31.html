What the item proposed

This report provides an overview of my participation in the Federation of Canadian Municipalities (FCM) 2026 Annual Conference and Trade Show, held from June 4 to 7, 2026, in Edmonton, Alberta. The conference, themed Building the Future Together, brought together elected officials, municipal staff, federal representatives and sector partners from across Canada to discuss shared municipal priorities and emerging challenges. During the conference, I participated in discussions with municipal leaders, federal officials, policy experts and industry partners on issues critical to cities and communities, including housing, infrastructure, public safety, economic development, climate resilience and municipal finance.
