Toronto Water 2026 Capital Budget and 2027 to 2035 Capital Plan Adjustments

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The decision on whether to move $32.683 million between Toronto Water capital projects to speed up some and delay others, with no change to the total budget rests with Council. Two projects also get a $245.294 million cost increase so contracts can be awarded ahead of schedule.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report requests City Council's authority to amend Toronto Water's 2026 Capital Budget and 2027 to 2035 Capital Plan by adjusting project cash flows contained within the 10-Year Capital Budget and Plan, respectively, to align forecasted project accelerations and deferrals. Additional reallocations to project cashflows and project costs are requested where project expenditures exceed the current approved cashflows and project costs. These reallocations will allow Toronto Water to continue to deliver projects within its 10-Year Capital Plan.
