What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of an application to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law to permit a multi-phased, mixed-use development at 250 The East Mall (currently occupied by Cloverdale Mall). Phasing of the development would allow for the continuous operation of portions of the mall, including the grocery store, during construction.
