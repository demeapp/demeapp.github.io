Assumption of Services, Registered Plan 66M-2564, 4208, 4210 and 4214 Dundas Street West, 4208 Dundas Development GP Inc.

Taking over the municipal services built for a subdivision near Dundas Street West (registered plan 66M-2564), releasing the builder's performance guarantee, and passing a by-law to assume the subdivision's public highway.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends City assumption of the municipal services installed under the terms of the Subdivision Agreement, dated October 30, 2018, between 4208 Dundas Development GP Inc. and the City of Toronto relating to registered Plan of Subdivision 66M-2564.

What was decided

City Council on February 4, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council assume the municipal services relating to Plan of Subdivision 66M-2564. 2. City Council authorize the Director, Engineering Review, Development Review, to release the performance guarantee held with respect to the municipal services in accordance with the Subdivision Agreement, dated October 30, 2018, between 4208 Dundas Development GP Inc. and the City of Toronto. 3. City Council authorize and direct the City Solicitor to prepare an assumption By-law to assume the public highway within Plan of Subdivision 66M-2564 and to bring it forward for enactment. 4. City Council authorize the appropriate City Officials to take the necessary action to give effect to City Council's decision. 5.
