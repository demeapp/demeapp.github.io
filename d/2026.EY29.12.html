Approving 2 speed humps on Bexley Crescent between Jane Street and Pendeen Avenue, since the criteria were met goes to the community council for a decision.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of speed humps on Bexley Crescent, between Jane Street and Pendeen Avenue. Staff's assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the updated Traffic Calming Policy has been satisfied. Therefore, speed humps should be installed on Bexley Crescent, between Jane Street and Pendeen Avenue.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (3 speed humps) on Bexley Crescent, between Jane Street and East Drive, generally as shown in the Attachment 1 - Drawing No. TC-654 and Attachment 2- Drawing No. TC-654-1, dated January 2026, to the motion by Councillor Nunziata.
