174 Church Street - Application to Remove a Private Tree

Adopted with changesMar 31, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The owner of 174 Church Street wants to cut down a healthy 42 cm Colorado blue spruce for a basement walkout. Staff denied the permit and ask council to uphold the denial.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report requests that Etobicoke York Community Council deny the request for a permit to remove one privately owned tree located at 174 Church Street. The applicant indicates the reason for requesting the removal of the tree is to accommodate the construction of a new basement walkout access associated with a proposed multiplex development. The Colorado blue spruce tree ( Picea pungens ) measures 42 cm in diameter. The City's Tree By-laws do not support the removal of this tree as it is healthy and maintainable. The permit was denied, and the applicant is appealing the decision. The Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision as to whether a permit may be issued when an applicant appeals the City's decision to deny a tree permit.

What was decided

That the Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Approved the request for a permit to remove one privately owned tree located at 174 Church Street and required the applicant to provide 5 replacement trees which can be achieved in a combination of on-site planting and cash-in-lieu of planting to the satisfaction of the Executive Director, Environment, Climate and Forestry.
