Canon Jackson Drive - Parking and Traffic Regulation Amendments

AdoptedApr 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

For a new road at 2175 Keele Street, staff propose basic traffic rules: no parking on either side, a 30 km/h limit, and no heavy trucks at any time.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The Traffic Operations unit in the Transportation Services Division and the Transportation Review unit in the Development Review Division are requesting approval for various parking and traffic regulations, proposed under the Draft Plan of Subdivision Application No. 15 239454 WET 12 SB for the development at 2175 Keele Street, to be implemented on Canon Jackson Drive. Canon Jackson Drive has been dedicated as public road allowance. Currently there are no traffic or parking regulations on this street. The proposed traffic regulations are consistent with City policies and guidelines given the operation of the roadway and surrounding residential nature.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking at all times on both sides of Canon Jackson Drive, between Keele Street and the east limit of Canon Jackson Drive. 2. Designated a 30 km/h speed limit on Canon Jackson Drive, between Keele Street and the east limit of Canon Jackson Drive. 3. Prohibited heavy vehicles at all times on Canon Jackson Drive, between Keele Street and the east limit of Canon Jackson Drive.
