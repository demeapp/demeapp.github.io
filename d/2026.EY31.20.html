Hackett Avenue - Accessible Parking Space

AdoptedApr 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Staff want an on-street accessible parking spot on the west side of Hackett Avenue, open at all times for permit holders. Council is asked to approve it.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting approval for the installation of an on-street accessible parking space on the west side of Hackett Avenue.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Designated an on-street accessible parking space in effect at all times on the west side of Hackett Avenue, from a point 51 metres north of Cook Road to a point 5.5 metres further north.
