Marquis Avenue - Parking Amendments

Adopted with changesApr 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

To make student drop-off safer at Lambton-Kingsway school, staff propose banning stopping on the east side of Marquis Avenue on weekday mornings and afternoons during school hours.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending stopping be prohibited from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday on the east side of Marquis Avenue, opposite Lambton-Kingsway Junior Middle School. The proposed amendment will deter parking at this location during school hours and improve student pick-up/drop off safety.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on both sides of Marquis Avenue between a point 45.5 metres south of Government Road and a point 65.5 metres south and east thereof to be in effect on the east and north side of Marquis Avenue between a point 45.5 metres south of Government Road and a point 65.5 metres south and east thereof. 2. Prohibited stopping in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the east and north side of Marquis Avenue, between Government Road and a point 111 metres south and east thereof. 3. Prohibited parking on the west and south side of Marquis Avenue between a point 60 metres south of Government Road and a point 51 metres south and east thereof.
