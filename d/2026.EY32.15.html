Black Creek Drive and Weston Road - Pedestrian Crossing Protection

AdoptedJun 24, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Staff recommend two pedestrian crossings where Black Creek Drive meets Weston Road, plus yield signs for drivers, to make crossing safer for walkers.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Black Creek Drive and Weston Road, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services has reviewed the need for pedestrian crossing protection at the southbound and westbound right-turn channels at the intersection of Black Creek Drive and Weston Road. Based on the assessment undertaken, Transportation Services is recommending the installation of a pedestrian crossover (PXO) at these locations. The PXOs will provide enhanced safety for vulnerable road users. During the course of the investigation, it was noted that there is a discrepancy between posted signs and the yield sign regulations. Recommendation 2 will correct this inconsistency.

What was decided

City Council on June 24 and 25, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the installation of two pedestrian crossovers at the right-turn channels at the northwest and northeast corners of the intersection of Black Creek Drive and Weston Road. 2. City Council authorize two yield controls on the right-turn channels at the northwest and northeast intersections of Black Creek Drive and Weston Road.
