After concerns about speeding on Liscombe Road, a councillor recommends 4 speed humps between Rustic Road and Maple Leaf Drive.

Official title: Liscombe Road - Installation of Speed Humps

AdoptedMay 26, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

Following community consultations and meetings with concerned residents of Liscombe Road, ongoing issues related to excessive speeding and cut-through traffic have been identified. As part of the broader Maple Leaf and Rustic Neighbourhood Streets Plan—which aims to enhance road safety, improve traffic flow, and increase pedestrian comfort—there is a need to implement targeted traffic calming measures. The installation of speed humps is being requested as an effective tool to reduce vehicle speeds, discourage non-local traffic infiltration, and support safer conditions for all road users.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) on Liscombe Road between Rustic Road and Maple Leaf Drive. 2. Directed the City Solicitor to prepare a By-law to alter the roadway to install four speed humps on Liscombe Road between Rustic Road and Maple Leaf Drive, generally as shown on Attachment 1, Drawing No. ATP-SH-259, to the letter (May 26, 2026) from Councillor Frances Nunziata.
