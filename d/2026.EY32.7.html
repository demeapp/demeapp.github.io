51 Burnhamthorpe Crescent - Application to Remove Two Private Trees

AdoptedMay 26, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The owner of 51 Burnhamthorpe Crescent wants to remove two healthy trees to build parking and a driveway for a fourplex. Staff ask council to deny the appeal.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report requests that Etobicoke York Community Council deny the request for a permit to remove two privately owned trees located at 51 Burnhamthorpe Crescent. The applicant indicates the reason for requesting removal of these trees is for construction of parking spaces, walkway, and driveway associated with a two-storey fourplex building and a garden suite. The black walnut tree (Juglans nigra) measures 58 cm in diameter and the Norway maple tree (Acer platanoides) measures 59 cm in diameter. The City's Tree By-laws do not support the removal of these trees as they are healthy and maintainable. The permit was denied, and the applicant is appealing the decision. Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision as to whether a permit may be issued when an applicant appeals the City's decision to deny a tree permit.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Denied the request for a permit to remove two privately owned trees located at 51 Burnhamthorpe Crescent.
