The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of speed humps on: - Kentroyal Drive, between Dixon Road and Inchcliffe Crescent - Inchcliffe Crescent, between Dixon Road and Riverview Heights Staff's assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the Traffic Calming Policy has been satisfied. Therefore, speed humps should be installed on Kentroyal Drive and Inchcliffe Crescent.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (3 speed humps) on Kentroyal Drive, between Dixon Road and Inchcliffe Crescent, generally as shown in Attachment 3, Drawing No. TC-721 to the report (June 18, 2026) from the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services. 2. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (4 speed humps) on Inchcliffe Crescent, between Dixon Road and Riverview Heights, generally, as shown in Attachment 4, Drawing No. TC-688 to the report (June 18, 2026) from the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services.
