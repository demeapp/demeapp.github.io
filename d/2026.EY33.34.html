The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of speed humps on: - Dalesford Road between Wesley Street and Grand Avenue - Mendota Road between Wesley Street and Gala Lane - Wesley Street between The Queensway and Mendota Road Staff's assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the Traffic Calming Policy has been satisfied. Therefore, speed humps are recommended to be installed on Dalesford Road, Mendota Road and Wesley Street.
