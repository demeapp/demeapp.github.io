The Kingsway Neighbourhood - Traffic Calming (Speed Humps)

Adopted with changesJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Ten speed humps are proposed in The Kingsway: five on Belvedere Boulevard and five on Lynngrove Avenue. Etobicoke York Community Council decides.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of speed humps on two (2) streets to reduce motor vehicle operating speeds and improve roadway safety for everyone, particularly pedestrians, by reducing the severity and likelihood of speed-related collisions. The assessment completed indicates the criteria as set out in the updated Traffic Calming Policy has been satisfied. Speed humps are recommended on the following streets in The Kingsway neighbourhood: - Belvedere Boulevard between Montgomery Road and Royal York Road; and - Lynngrove Avenue between Westrose Avenue and Royal York Road.
