Corrective By-law for the Permanent Closure of Portions of Dundas Street West, Kipling Avenue and Bloor Street West

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: City Council was asked to fix the legal land descriptions in the by-law that closed parts of Dundas Street West, Kipling Avenue, and Bloor Street West in Six Points. One lot was listed by mistake. No policy change.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council authorize a correction to the by-law respecting the permanent closure of portions of Dundas Street West, Kipling Avenue and Bloor Street West in the Six Points area. City Council previously adopted Etobicoke York Community Council item EY24.19 at its meeting of June 8 and 9, 2021, authorizing the permanent closure of portions of Dundas Street West, Kipling Avenue and Bloor Street West. By-law 503-2021 was subsequently enacted; however, the by-law was not registered on title. Following further review, it was determined that the legal descriptions contained in the enacted by-law require a revision before registration can proceed. In particular, Part 4 on Plan 66R-31942 was included in the prior by-law description in error and is not included in the revised legal descriptions recommended through this report.
