The Kingsway and Lambeth Road - Turn Prohibition

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The southbound left-turn ban at The Kingsway and Lambeth Road would be lifted because the rebuilt intersection now handles all turns. Etobicoke York Community Council decides.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting approval to rescind the existing southbound left turn prohibition, at all times, at the intersection of The Kingsway and Lambeth Road. The intersection has been reconstructed and now allows for all movements. The left turn prohibition is no longer required.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing southbound left turn prohibition in effect at all times, at The Kingsway and the ramp from Royal York Road (north of Canterbury Road).
