The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services was directed to report back to Etobicoke York Community Council on the impacts of amending the overnight heavy vehicle prohibition to be in effect at all times. This report will provide the results of traffic studies conducted on Evans Avenue between Islington Avenue and Royal York Road.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Received the revised report (June 18, 2026) from the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services for information.
