Cliveden Avenue - Parking Amendments

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Parking rules on the west side of Cliveden Avenue near Bloor Street West would be updated to match posted signs, including the $4.50 parking machine zone and a new no-stopping area to keep the accessible parking ramp clear. Etobicoke York Community Council decides.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that the existing parking regulations on the west side of Cliveden Avenue, between Bloor Street West and a point 50.5 metres south, be amended to reflect current signage on site. As part of the proposed amendments, designating a no stopping area at the existing curb depression on the west side of Cliveden Avenue located approximately 37 metres south of Bloor Street West will deter parking at this location and will maintain ramp accessibility for patrons utilizing the accessible parking space. During the course of the investigation, it was noted that the spelling of Cliveden Avenue was incorrect for some regulations. Recommendations 7 and 8 will correct these errors.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing angle parking in effect at all times, on the west side of Cliveden Avenue, between Bloor Street West and a point 45.5 metres south, to be in effect between Bloor Street West and a point 36.4 metres south. 2. Permitted angle parking at all times on the west side of Cliveden Avenue, between a point 39.7 metres south of Bloor Street West and a point 7.8 metres further south. 3. Amended the existing parking machine regulation in effect from 9:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, from 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. Sunday, at a rate of $4.50 per hour for a maximum period of three hours, on the west side of Cliveden Avenue, between Bloor Street West and a point 41.5 metres south, to be in effect between Bloor Street West and a point 36.4 metres south. 4.
