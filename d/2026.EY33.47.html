Mabelle Avenue - Parking Amendments

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Most of Mabelle Avenue would switch from no-parking to the stricter no-stopping rule, while $1.50 paid parking stays and a small paid section is added. City Council decides because the TTC runs on Mabelle Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Mabelle Avenue, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is recommending that the existing No Parking Anytime prohibitions on the majority of both sides of Mabelle Avenue, between Dundas Street West and Cordova Avenue, be changed to a stopping prohibition. The proposed amendment will deter parking/stopping on the street and will improve transit operations and vehicle movements by maintaining a clear passage for all vehicles. The existing paid parking areas will remain and a small section on the west side of Mabelle Avenue south of Dundas Street West will be added to provide additional paid parking.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on both sides of Mabelle Avenue, between Cordova Avenue and Dundas Street West. 2. City Council prohibit stopping at all times on the north side of Mabelle Avenue, between Cordova Avenue and a point 164 metres west of Cordova Avenue. 3. City Council prohibit stopping at all times on the east side of Mabelle Avenue, between Dundas Street West and a point 156 metres south. 5.
