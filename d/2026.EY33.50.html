70 Lacey Avenue - Encroachment Appeal - Retaining Wall

The owner of 70 Lacey Avenue appeals for permission to build a tall retaining wall on the public right-of-way. Staff recommend approval with conditions. Etobicoke York Community Council decides.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is for Etobicoke York Community Council to consider an appeal from the property owner of 70 Lacey Avenue regarding their encroachment application. The proposed encroachment consists of a reinforced concrete retaining wall that does not comply with City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks, Use of. The property owner is seeking authorization from Etobicoke York Community Council to allow the construction and maintenance of the encroachment within the public right-of-way at 70 Lacey Avenue. Transportation Services recommends that authority be granted, subject to the conditions set out in this report.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Authorized the General Manager of Transportation Services to enter into an encroachment agreement with the owner of 70 Lacey Avenue to permit the construction and maintenance of a reinforced concrete retaining wall varying from approximately 1.8 metres to 2.8 metres in height, with a footing varying from approximately 1.2 metres to 1.9 metres in depth and located with zero setback from the sidewalk, subject but not limited to, the following conditions: a. The property owner will indemnify the City of Toronto from and against all actions, suits, claims or demands and from all loss, costs, damages, and expenses that may result from such permission granted; b.
