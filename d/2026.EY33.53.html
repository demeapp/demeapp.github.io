Hillside Avenue and Royal York Road Pedestrian Crossing Protection Investigation

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Over 90 Mimico residents want a protected crossing at Royal York Road and Hillside Avenue near a school and park. Etobicoke York Community Council is asked to order a fresh staff study of the idea.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Dear Chair and Members of Etobicoke York Community Council, My office recently received a petition with over 90 signatures from Mimico residents requesting a pedestrian crossing at the intersection of Hillside Ave and Royal York Road to better support access to John English Junior Middle School and Mimico Memorial Park. In 2024, Transportation Services reviewed this request, however the intersection did not meet the City's technical criteria for installing a protected pedestrian crossing. Given continued community interest and changes in the area over the past couple of years, I recommend that Transportation Services conduct a new review of the intersection and report back on the feasibility of installing a crossing at this location.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Requested that the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services, investigate the feasibility of installing pedestrian crossing protection on Royal York Road at Hillside Avenue.
