Toronto City Council · Decision

Manitoba Street Parking Restriction and Review of Traffic Calming

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

After safety complaints on Manitoba Street, the proposal adds no-stopping rules near a driveway and asks staff to study speed cushions between Legion Road North and Grand Avenue. Etobicoke York Community Council decides.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Dear Chair and Members of Etobicoke York Community Council, Residents have expressed concerns about unsafe road conditions on Manitoba Street, between Grand Avenue and Legion Road, caused by both speeding vehicles coming off the Gardiner Expressway and sightline obstructions caused by vehicles parking along both sides of Manitoba Street. The parked vehicles create challenges for motorists exiting the westerly driveway and cause unsafe pedestrian crossing conditions. To mitigate the sightline concerns, I am recommending expanding the “no stopping” regulations around the westerly driveway. To improve pedestrian safety and slow down traffic, I am recommending the installation of speed cushions on Manitoba Street, between Grand Avenue and Legion Road.

What was decided

The Etobicoke York Community Council: 1. Prohibited stopping at all times on the north side of Manitoba Street, from a point 78 metres east of Grand Avenue to a point 12 metres further east. 2. Requested the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services, to investigate the feasibility of installing speed cushions on Manitoba Street between Legion Road North and Grand Avenue.
