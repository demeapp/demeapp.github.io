Improving Traffic Safety on St. Phillips Road

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

After 35 recorded crashes since 2024 on St. Phillips Road, residents petition for a formal safety review and traffic calming. Etobicoke York Community Council is asked to order the review.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office has received a petition signed by representatives of 52 households, along with supporting documentation detailing a pattern of hazardous driving conditions. Residents are requesting urgent intervention to improve safety through the implementation of traffic calming measures, including speed humps, bollards, increased signage and signalization. St. Phillips Road is a residential street with a significant number of families and young children. Documented incidents, including repeated speeding and loss-of-control collisions, indicate a persistent safety risk. Notably, two separate collisions occurred on December 14, 2025, May 24, 2026 in which vehicles struck the same hydro pole in front of 47 St. Phillips Road. These incidents, occurring within a six-month period, resulted in substantial damage and neighbourhood-wide power outages, underscoring the recurring nature of the issue.…
