The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of a City-initiated Official Plan Amendment to delineate the Mount Dennis Protected Major Transit Station Area (PMTSA) as Site and Area Specific Policy 671 (SASP 671) to be contained in Chapter 8 of the Official Plan. SASP 671 would establish the PMTSA policy framework for Mount Dennis Station, including the station area delineation, authorized uses of land, minimum densities for buildings and structures, and a planned minimum population and employment target of 160 residents and jobs combined per hectare. Mount Dennis Station is a major transit interchange served by TTC Line 5 Eglinton Light Rail Transit, GO Transit Kitchener Line and UP Express near Eglinton Avenue West and Weston Road.
