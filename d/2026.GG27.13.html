Extending the High Park trackless train deal with Carla Construction & Maintenance Ltd for the 2026 season without bidding, after a shuttle tender fell through.

What the item proposed

This report seeks City Council authority to enter into a non-competitive agreement with Carla Construction & Maintenance Ltd., to continue operations and maintenance of the trackless train amusement service in High Park. In November 2024, Parks and Recreation issued a Request for Proposals for the operation and maintenance of a passenger transportation shuttle service for High Park and other potential parks, consistent with the High Park Movement Strategy. Carla Construction & Maintenance Ltd., was identified as the preferred supplier. Following Request for Proposal award, the proponent declared that it would not be able to meet the obligations they set through their response to the Request for Proposal, specifically procurement of an accessible electric shuttle vehicle that can safely meet the needs in High Park.
