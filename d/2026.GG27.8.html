Toronto City Council · Decision

Amendment to Purchase Order 6043796 with WSP Canada Inc., and Purchase Order 6052068 with Maple Reinders Constructors Limited for Rehabilitation and Upgrades of Seven Group Six Sewage Pumping Stations at Various Locations

AdoptedMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The city wants to add $1,599,436 ($1,627,586 net of HST recoveries) to WSP's pumping station contract. It would rise from $2,923,091 ($2,974,537 net of HST recoveries) to $4,522,527 ($4,602,123 net of HST recoveries). The city also wants to add $2,000,000 ($2,035,200) to Maple Reinders' contract, raising it from $27,646,800 ($28,133,384 net of HST recoveries) to $29,646,800 ($30,168,584 net of HST recoveries).

The official summary names a figure of $1,599,436.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
