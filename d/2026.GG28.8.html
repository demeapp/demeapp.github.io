Non-Competitive Contract with Microsoft Canada Inc., for M365 Software Licenses, Cloud Subscriptions, and Support Services

AdoptedApr 22, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Approving a no-bid 5-year Microsoft 365 deal directly with Microsoft Canada for about 32,000 staff, since the current deal ends May 31, 2026. A $500,000 figure is cited.

The official summary names a figure of $500,000.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request City Council authority to: - Enter into a non-competitive contract with Microsoft Canada Inc., ("Microsoft"), a Canadian business subsidiary, for software licenses, cloud subscriptions, and support services for five (5) years in total which may include three (3) years initial with the option to extend for two (2) additional one-year periods. - Amend authority previously approved under GG24.9 for Microsoft Unified Support, extending it by an additional five (5) months with no increase in the contract value, to ensure access to the enterprise support service for Microsoft products for the full duration of the proposed non-competitive Microsoft agreement.
