2025 Write-off of Uncollectible Property Taxes and Accounts Receivable

AdoptedMay 20, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The city is writing off 2025 debts it cannot collect: $299,458.16 in property taxes, $1,008,896 in service invoices, and $3,019,028 in provincial offence fines. Council approves the property tax write-off.

The official summary names a figure of $299,458.16.

What the item proposed

This report outlines accounts receivable balances deemed uncollectible and written off in 2025 under the delegated authority of the Controller and Chief Accountant, including property tax amounts owing, outstanding receivables for invoiced services, and fines issued under the Provincial Offences Act. This report recommends that Council deem uncollectible and approve for write-off certain property tax amounts owing on 79 individual property tax accounts, comprising 196 receivable amounts which total $299,458.16, from taxation years 1994 to 2024. Staff are recommending these property tax receivable amounts for write-off as they are no longer reflected on the assessment roll, or for which further collection efforts and recovery of outstanding amounts are extremely unlikely to be successful. What was decided

City Council on May 20 and 21, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council deem the unpaid property taxes levied in all years (including interest and penalties that have accrued on those unpaid taxes up to the time of write-off) on the 196 receivables listed in Attachment 1 to the report (April 27, 2026) from the Controller and Chief Accountant, uncollectible, and direct the Controller and Chief Accountant to remove these amounts from the tax assessment roll by writing them off.
