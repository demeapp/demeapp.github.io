What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request City Council authority to negotiate and enter into non-competitive contracts with Aimsun Inc., for the purchase and provision of maintenance and support of five (5) Aimsun Next transportation modelling software licenses, for a five (5)-year term and PTV Group for the purchase and provision of maintenance and support of ten (10) Vissim transportation modelling software licenses, for a five (5)-year term. Transportation Services has used Aimsun Next and Vissim extensively over the years to conduct traffic operations assessments for a wide range of transportation projects. These software products are proprietary and provided exclusively by their respective vendors.
