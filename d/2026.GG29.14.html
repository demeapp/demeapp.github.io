The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request City Council authority to enter into five (5) separate non-competitive contracts with the suppliers noted below for the supply of proprietary parts and maintenance services required at various water treatment and wastewater facilities. The initial term of the contracts will be from the date of issuance to December 31, 2026, with the option to extend each contract for up to four (4) separate one (1) year periods, at the sole discretion of the City and subject to budget approvals, for the total amount of $7,085,485, net of all applicable taxes and charges ($7,210,190 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries). A non‑competitive procurement is required for the following suppliers: Toromont Power Systems, KSB Pumps Inc. (Canada), APGN Inc., John Brooks Company Limited, and Devine & Associates Limited.
