Amendment to Purchase Order 6054751 with Altas Constructors Inc., for the Construction of the Scarborough Arena Gardens for Parks and Recreation

AdoptedMay 11, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The city wants to add $1,371,983 ($1,396,130 net of HST recoveries) to the Scarborough Arena Gardens rebuild, raising it from $34,601,698 ($35,210,688) to $35,973,680 ($36,606,817), for late extras and a delay claim.

The official summary names a figure of $1,371,983.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request authority to amend Purchase Order 6054751 issued to Atlas Constructors Inc., under the Request for Tender Document Number 3819550702 (Contractor Number 21-PFR-161) for the reconstruction of the Scarborough Arena Gardens. The total value of the current Purchase Order Amendment being requested is $1,371,983 net of all applicable taxes and charges, revising the current Purchase Order value from $34,601,698 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($35,210,688 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) to $35,973,680 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($36,606,817 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries). This Purchase Order Amendment is required to address various additional scope items identified near completion of construction during the inspection and commissioning phases. What was decided

The General Government Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Financial Control Bylaw), authorized the General Manager, Parks and Recreation to amend Purchase Order 6054751 issued to Atlas Constructors Inc., for the reconstruction of the Scarborough Arena Gardens by increasing the value by $1,371,983 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($1,396,130 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries), revising the current Purchase Order value from $34,601,698 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($35,210,688 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) to $35,973,680 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($36,606,817 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries).
