Award of Doc5398199635 to Alberici Constructors Ltd., for the New Ultraviolet Disinfection and Residuals Management Facility at the Island Water Treatment Plant, and Amendment to Purchase Orders 6056811 and 6057463 with AECOM Canada ULC for Contract Administration and Post-Construction Services

AdoptedJun 24, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The city wants to give Alberici Constructors a $333,856,566 deal ($339,732,442 net of HST recoveries; stated as $333,856,56 in the report) for a new UV disinfection facility at the Island water plant, shift $99,966,862 in the water budget, and add $16,368,760 ($16,656,850) and $64,359 ($65,492) to two AECOM deals. Also cited: $3,724,823 ($3,790,380), $20,093,583 ($20,447,230), $275,435 ($280,283), and $339,794 ($345,775).

The official summary names a figure of $333,856,56.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
