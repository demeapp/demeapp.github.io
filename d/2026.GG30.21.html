What the item proposed

This report seeks City Council authority to enter into Community Space Tenancy lease agreements with five not‑for‑profit organizations that will collectively occupy approximately 19,132 square feet within the Bloor–Dufferin Community Hub, a City‑owned facility located at 1141 Bloor Street West and 980 Dufferin Street (the “Leased Premises”). The report further seeks City Council authority to designate the leased premises for each tenant as a Municipal Capital Facility, in accordance with the City of Toronto Act, 2006 and applicable City policy. Consistent with the Community Space Tenancy Policy ( 2017.EX28.8 ), tenant agencies for the Bloor-Dufferin Community Hub were identified through competitive Requests for Expressions of Interest issued in 2021 and 2025.
