The city wants to amend its Veolia deal to fund urgent repairs at the Disco Road organics plant ahead of a planned expansion. No dollar figures are given.

What the item proposed

This report is seeking authority to amend Purchase Order 6049718 issued to Veolia Water Canada Inc. (Veolia). The Purchase Order was issued based on City Council approval of Item 2019.IE5.11, authorizing the General Manager, Solid Waste Management Services and / or designate to negotiate and enter into any agreements necessary to operate, maintain and undertake capital improvements to continue to process source separated organics at the Disco Road Organics Processing Facility, commencing on July 1, 2019, for an approved term of up to 10 years. The Disco Road Organics Processing Facility facility was commissioned in 2014. Through ongoing operations, engineering assessments, and capital work planning, several major capital improvement and maintenance requirements have been identified, some of which are critical to site operations and need to be addressed immediately.
