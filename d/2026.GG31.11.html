Amendment to Purchase Order 6057084 with Vaughan Paving Ltd., for Construction of Asphalt Speed Humps in the City of Toronto

AdoptedJul 20, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Adding $1,570,750 ($1,598,395 net of HST recoveries) to Vaughan Paving's speed hump contract, raising it from $4,278,514 ($4,353,816) to $5,849,264 ($5,952,211), because more humps were approved than planned. It goes to the committee for a decision.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report is seeking authority to amend Purchase Order 6057084 issued to Vaughan Paving Ltd., for work awarded through Request for Tender Doc5177952812, Contract 25TR-OM-411-TEY-SH for the Construction of Asphalt Speed Humps in the City of Toronto. This Purchase Order Amendment is required to install a greater number of speed humps approved through Community Council than had been previously estimated in the contract awarded in July 2025 for installation in 2025 and 2026. The total value of the requested amendment is $1,570,750 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($1,598,395 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries), revising the current Purchase Order value from $4,278,514 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($4,353,816 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries) to $5,849,264 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($5,952,211 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries).

What was decided

The General Government Committee: 1. In accordance with Section 71-11.1.C of the City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 71 (Financial Control By-Law), authorized the General Manager, Transportation Services, to amend Purchase Order 6057084 with Vaughan Paving Ltd., for the Construction of Asphalt Speed Humps in the City of Toronto in the amount of $1,570,750 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($1,598,395 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries), revising the current Purchase Order value from $4,278,514 to $5,849,264 net of all applicable taxes and charges ($5,952,211 net of Harmonized Sales Tax recoveries).
