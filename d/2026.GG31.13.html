Award of Doc5474040786 to Bennett Mechanical Installations (2001) Ltd., for Pumping Station Retrofit Western Beaches Tunnel - Phase 2 and Amendment to Purchase Order 6046087 and 6053027 to Stantec Consulting Ltd., for Services During Construction and Post-Construction Services

AdoptedJul 20, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The city wants to award Bennett Mechanical $52,172,618 ($53,090,856 net of HST recoveries) for the Western Beaches tunnel pumping station retrofit, and add $4,974,258 ($5,061,805) to Stantec's construction services (now $6,629,315, was $1,655,057; $1,684,186 and $6,745,991 net of HST) plus $153,835 ($156,543) for post-construction (now $267,334, was $113,499; $115,497 and $272,039 net of HST).
