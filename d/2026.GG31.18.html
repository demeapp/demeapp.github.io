Award of Doc5471369482 to ASCO Construction (Toronto) Ltd., for Construction of Offline Storage Tank (DWST2) East Don Sanitary Trunk Sewer and Amendment to Purchase Orders 6045927, 6052716 and 6055600 with Associated Engineering (Ont.) AdoptedJul 20, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The city wants to give ASCO $43,694,149 ($44,463,166 net of HST recoveries) to build a sewer storage tank at 1236 and 1240 Sheppard Avenue East, and add $544,151 ($553,728), $1,937,953 ($1,972,061), and $237,877 ($242,064) to three design deals: $2,498,326 ($2,542,296) to $3,042,477 ($3,096,025); $3,051,458 ($3,105,164) to $4,989,411 ($5,077,225); and $50,870 ($51,765) to $288,747 ($293,829).

The official summary names a figure of $43,694,149.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.
