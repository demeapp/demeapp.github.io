The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request City Council authority to enter into two (2) non-competitive contracts with Jenoptik Smart Mobility Solutions, LLC (formerly Traffipax LLC) for the supply, installation, operation, and maintenance of red-light camera systems within the City of Toronto. The red-light camera systems program is an important component of the City of Toronto’s Vision Zero Road Safety Plan. The City of Toronto currently operates 299 red-light camera systems, with equipment and program support provided under two contracts with Jenoptik Smart Mobility Solutions LLC. These contracts, one covering 150 cameras, and the other covering 149 cameras, were both awarded through competitive procurement processes undertaken by the City.
