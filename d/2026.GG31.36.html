What the item proposed

This report seeks authority for the City to enter into a nominal lease agreement with Birchmount Bluffs Neighbourhood Centre as tenant, for a portion of Birchmount Community Centre, located at 93 Birchmount Road as described in Attachment 2 (the “Leased Premises”) for a five-year term and based substantially on the major terms identified in Attachment 1. Birchmount Bluffs Neighbourhood Centre is a non-profit organization that has been providing services for the local community in Ward 20 and has operated at this location since 1994. Staff are recommending approval for the lease agreement to provide Birchmount Bluffs Neighbourhood Centre with exclusive use of a reception office, administrative offices, and programming space for childcare services.
