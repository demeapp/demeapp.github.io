What the item proposed

Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 349, Animals, establishes rules and regulations for responsible pet ownership in the City, including a requirement for every owner of a dog or cat to obtain a pet licence. In 2023, the City entered a no-cost, two-year pilot with DocuPet Inc., a Kingston-based Canadian company that provides online pet licensing services to municipalities, with the goal of improving pet licensing revenue and uptake, streamlining program administration, and enhancing customer experience. The pilot commenced on October 17, 2023, and was originally scheduled to end on October 17, 2025. At its meeting on June 25 and 26, 2025, City Council authorized Municipal Licensing and Standards to negotiate and enter into a non-competitive contract with DocuPet Inc., ( 2025.GG22.11 ).
