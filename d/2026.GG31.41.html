The official record

What the item proposed

In recent years, the City of Toronto has made steady progress in improving the design and delivery of its services, including steps to implement more effective methods to engage residents and collect their feedback. To advance these efforts, this report seeks updated authorities to administer two programs that support the public’s interactions with the City. - The Digital Public Engagement Program, led by the City’s Chief Communications Officer, modernizes how the City of Toronto conducts digital public engagement, consultation, community relationships, and a range of communications activities. - The Modernized 311 Program, led by the Executive Director of the Customer Experience Division, facilitates access to City services and information through new 311 initiatives, such as Closing the Loop and the 311 Frontend Redesign. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the Chief Communications Officer, or their delegate, to implement and administer the Digital Public Engagement Program in accordance with Attachment 1 to the report (July 7, 2026) from the Chief Communications Officer, and the Executive Director, Customer Experience, called “Digital Public Engagement Program.” 2. City Council authorize the Executive Director, Customer Experience, or their delegate, to implement ongoing enhancements to the City’s 311 service as described in Attachment 2 to the report (July 7, 2026) from the Chief Communications Officer, and the Executive Director, Customer Experience, called "Modernized 311 Program."
