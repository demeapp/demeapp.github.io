City Approved Toronto Public Health 2026 Operating Budget

AdoptedMar 30, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Council adopted Toronto Public Health's 2026 budget: $307.421 million gross and $103.489 million net, down $8.846 million gross and $9.038 million net from the board's ask, with 1,865.5 positions. The year-over-year increase is $5.911 million gross and $9.526 million net.
