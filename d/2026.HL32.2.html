What the item proposed

Toronto Public Health currently has service contracts with partner agencies that support service delivery in the community for the Healthy Babies Healthy Children program, Food Security initiatives, Sexual Health services and clinics, the Dental Care to Youth Experiencing Homelessness and Low-Income Adults program, and the Ontario Seniors Dental Care program. Purchasing services from community agencies is an effective and efficient way to provide essential public health services. This approach builds on existing service infrastructure in the community, facilitates community partnerships, supports continuity of service to clients, and can increase service efficiencies.
