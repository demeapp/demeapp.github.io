2026 and 2027 Toronto Urban Health Fund Allocations and Review Process

Adopted with changesJun 1, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Approving $2.157 million in 2026 and $1.051 million in 2027 for the Toronto Urban Health Fund's HIV and substance-use prevention grants to community groups, including up to $0.9 million per year for an Ontario HIV Treatment Network pilot. It goes to the Board of Health for approval.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto Urban Health Fund (TUHF) provides grant funding to community-based organizations (CBOs) to deliver HIV and substance use prevention programs. Toronto Public Health (TPH) has adopted initial findings from a review of the Toronto Urban Health Fund grant program to reduce administrative burden for community-based organizations, including streamlining the reporting process and project requirements. Toronto Public Health will be returning to the Board of Health in 2027 with additional proposed changes to the Toronto Urban Health Fund for the 2028 funding cycle. To facilitate service continuity, Toronto Public Health has implemented an interim approach, approved by the Board of Health. The approach streamlined the approval process to renew funding for twenty-one previously approved projects for the 2026 and 2027 funding period.
