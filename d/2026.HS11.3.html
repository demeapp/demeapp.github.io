The official record

What the item proposed

The Chair will invite Housing Rights Advisory Committee members and members of the public to provide their input and views on the committee’s impact, challenges, and key priorities for its second term if reestablished in the next Council term.

What was decided

The Housing Rights Advisory Committee: 1. Requested the Chair of the Housing Rights Advisory Committee to report back to the June 22, 2026 meeting with a summary of the discussion on Item HS11.3 and identifying the actions and accomplishments of the Housing Rights Advisory Committee during its 2024-2026 term, as well as priorities for the second term of the Housing Rights Advisory Committee should the committee be reestablished in the next Council term.
