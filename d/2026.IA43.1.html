What the item proposed

Councillor Lily Cheng, Ward 18, Willowdale, has submitted the following Administrative Inquiry: I am submitting this Administrative Inquiry under Municipal Code S27-7.11 to clarify several key matters to ensure residents are provided with accurate and factual information as the City advances the development of the new Indigenous Women and Children’s Shelter at 68 Sheppard Avenue West. As the City of Toronto advances the development for the new Indigenous Women and Children’s Shelter at 68 Sheppard Avenue West, significant public discussion has emerged within the surrounding community. While many residents support the provision of critical services for vulnerable women and children, there has also been a considerable amount of misinformation and speculation circulating about the project, both from within the community and from individuals outside of it.
