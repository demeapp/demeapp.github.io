What the item proposed

Over the past several meetings, the Toronto Investment Board has expressed energy to review the global equity mandates to determine whether there are opportunities to enhance the risk adjusted performance of the global equities exposure. During our March meeting, we discussed and made a decision with respect to the passive component of the global equity mandate. However, there are still several additional considerations that should be addressed.
