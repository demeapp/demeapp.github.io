Watermain Cleaning and Relining on Various Streets in the Sheppard Avenue / Chesswood Drive Area

Adopted with changesApr 22, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Pasternak (Ward 6) flags worries that watermain work on Sheppard Avenue and Chesswood Drive could disrupt businesses and Rogers Stadium concert traffic (52,200 seats, 17 May concerts).

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On March 20, 2026 a construction notice was distributed by Toronto Water concerning Contract 26TW-CPI-03CWD. This is a watermain cleaning and relining on various streets in the area. There are growing concerns about the above referenced project. Chesswood Drive was the subject of a major construction project in 2023 which included road resurfacing, off road cycle tracks and sidewalk construction. The project was highly disruptive to local businesses. There were unjustified lane closures and periods of time when the project was abandoned by subcontractors. A town hall with local businesses had to take place the local Councillor received numerous complaints throughout the term of the project. There is deep concern at the possible inconvenience and cost of tearing up the street again with the proposed watermain work.
