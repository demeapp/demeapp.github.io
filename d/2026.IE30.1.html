Enhancing Coordination with Navigation Platforms for Road Closures

Adopted with changesJun 10, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Fletcher asks the committee to get navigation apps (Google Maps, Waze, Apple Maps) to use city road-closure data, with the FIFA World Cup coming this summer.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City of Toronto residents, businesses, and visitors increasingly rely on navigation apps such as Google Maps, Waze, and Apple Maps to guide their daily travel. Unfortunately, there have been recent instances where these platforms have failed to accurately or promptly display road closures related to construction, special events, or other traffic management activities. As a result, motorists have been directed into heavily congested areas or even routed onto streets that are effectively closed, creating confusion and delays for drivers. The City makes significant efforts to provide accurate and timely information through its Road Restrictions and Closures website and an automated data feed for these navigation providers. What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services, in consultation with the Chief Information Officer and the Geospatial Competency Centre to immediately engage with navigation platform providers, including those responsible for Google Maps, Apple Maps, and Waze, to establish a direct communication channel for the consistent, timely, and accurate representation of City road closure, construction, and traffic management data, and official City asset renamings within their platform, and emphasize that, as widely used public-facing services relied upon for mobility, these platforms have a social responsibility to the public to maintain current and accurate information.
