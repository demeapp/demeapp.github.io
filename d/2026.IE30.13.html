Davenport Winter Maintenance Needs

Adopted with changesJun 10, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A member motion asks for winter maintenance tailored to Davenport (Ward 9), citing narrow streets, low service rates, and the city's highest 311 request count this winter.

The official record

What the item proposed

I want to thank the Infrastructure and Environment Committee, along with the City’s Transportation Services staff, for the significant work already undertaken as part of Toronto’s annual Winter Maintenance Review. Important progress has been made to evaluate service delivery, improve operational readiness, and strengthen communication with residents, and I know these changes are appreciated by communities across Toronto. While the annual review of our winter maintenance program is essential to keeping Toronto moving, its citywide standards do not always reflect the unique built form challenges present in older parts of the city. Davenport (Ward 9) is part of the old City of Toronto and retains many of its historic physical characteristics including narrow residential streets, long stretches of monolithic sidewalks, high-density arterial corridors, and very limited space to store snow.…

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services, consider working with the local Councillor prior to the 2026–2027 winter season on targeted winter maintenance improvements for Davenport, University-Rosedale , Etobicoke-Lakeshore, Eglinton-Lawrence, Don Valley West and Toronto-Danforth, including operational adjustments that address snow clearing on narrow streets while maintaining accessibility, and the development of options for increased snow removal, where appropriate. 2. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services, and the Chief Communications Officer consider exploring ways and channels to provide residents with detailed feedback when barriers prevent timely servicing of 311 requests, including explanations of operational constraints and expected timelines for resolution. 3.
