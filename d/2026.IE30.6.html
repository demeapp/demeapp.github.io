Banning southbound right turns on red at Victoria Park and Kingston Road, where TTC streetcar tracks clash with turning cars.

The official record

What the item proposed

As Victoria Park Avenue borders two Community Council areas, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting approval to prohibit southbound right turns on a red signal at all times on Victoria Park Avenue at Kingston Road. This prohibition is intended to mitigate conflicts between eastbound left-turning Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) streetcars on Kingston Road and southbound right-turning vehicles on Victoria Park Avenue. Due to the constrained geometry of the intersection, the streetcar tracks encroach within the path of southbound right-turning vehicles. This creates a conflict point when southbound vehicles proceed on a red signal. Prohibiting right turns on red will eliminate this conflict and improve safety for all road users.

What was decided

City Council on June 24 and 25, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit southbound right turns on a red signal at all times at the intersection of Victoria Park Avenue at Kingston Road. 2. City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services to review and assess measures to improve access to the Bingham TTC Loop driveway, including pavement markings, signage, signal timing modifications, and other operational measures, and implement any appropriate improvements.
