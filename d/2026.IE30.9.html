What the item proposed

This report summarizes Toronto Water’s administrative compliance and enforcement responsibilities and activities for 2025 under the City of Toronto's Municipal Code Chapter 681 (Sewers By-law) and Municipal Code Chapter 851 (Water Supply By-law). These by-laws are in place to provide that the City’s distribution and collection systems are used responsibly, municipal infrastructure is safeguarded, the environment is protected, and regulatory compliance is maintained. Through proactive monitoring and enforcement, Toronto Water staff identify violations, work with industrial customers and residents on compliance issues, prevent contamination of drinking water, and advances nearshore water quality and pollution prevention objectives.
