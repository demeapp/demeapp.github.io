Options to Provide an Inexpensive, Non-polluting Power Supply for Mobile Food Vendors

AdoptedJul 15, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Saxe asks the committee to study clean battery options for food vendors' power, modeled on a New York City pilot, instead of polluting generators.

The official record

What the item proposed

A few mobile food vendors in Toronto use clean, silent, inexpensive grid electricity to power their operations. However, most mobile food vendors in Toronto rely on noisy, polluting, gas or diesel generators to provide the power they need. In areas crowded with mobile food vendors, the cumulative effect of these fossil fuel generators can cause noticeable air pollution, and enough noise to disturb those living or working nearby. The cost of the generator and the fuel can also be a material financial burden for the food vendors. The city of New York has launched a pilot project in Flushing Meadows-Corona Park that allows mobile food cart vendors to inexpensively rent clean, rechargeable batteries instead, according to the Mayor's Office of Climate and Environmental Justice .
