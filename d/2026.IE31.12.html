Road Construction on Keele South of Sheppard

Adopted with changesJul 15, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A member motion raises resident complaints about Canada Lands construction on Keele Street near Sheppard: lane closures with no visible work, no notice to residents, and no contact point.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I bring this motion to the Committee as a matter of urgency as third party construction takes place along Keele Street north and south of Sheppard Avenue. My office has fielded a wide range of calls questioning unjustified lane closures, traffic and project management and project purpose. The project is managed by nearby property owner Canada Lands Corporation and its construction partners. The construction is related to sanitary sewer work and other infrastructure that will connect to the Black Creek Tunnel Project, slated to commence in 2027. There is no indication that a construction notice was sent to residents for this project. Road Occupancy Permits have been issued by the City of Toronto for work South of Sheppard Avenue West, near Wycombe Road (Tor-RD1S2026) and near Maryport Avenue (Tor-RD1S2026-677).
