This is a procedural item: Council was asked to update the policy for accepting potentially contaminated lands conveyed to the city through development approvals.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek Council approval to amend the City Policy entitled "Policy for Accepting Potentially Contaminated Lands to be Conveyed to the City under the Planning Act" (the Policy). This report has been prepared in consultation with Development Review, Transportation Services, Toronto Water, Parks and Recreation, City Planning, TTC, Corporate Real Estate Management, and Legal Services. The amendments are captured in the updated Policy now titled "Policy for Accepting Potentially Contaminated Lands to be Conveyed to the City as a Condition of Approval of a Development Application". The amendments are summarized as follows: - Revision of the Record of Site Condition requirements for Subdivision Applications to be consistent with Ontario Regulation 153/04 as amended. - Renaming of the Policy to better reflect conveyance of land to the City in a land development…

Section 3.3.1.2 is amended by adding the words “does not include bus shelters, garages, or similar at-grade structures”, so it now reads: 3.3.1.2 conveyance of easement interests in favour of the City in respect of that part of pedestrian entrance connections located at and below the surface of the ground which provide access to and from the existing subway and existing light rail transit owned and operated by TTC does not include bus shelters, garages, or similar at-grade structures;…
