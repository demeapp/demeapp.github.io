TTC St. Clair West Station - Sanitary Sewer Structural Lining Cost Recovery Agreement

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: Council was asked to let Toronto Water make the TTC pay the full cost of sewer structural lining work near St. Clair West Station.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks City Council authority for the General Manager, Toronto Water, to negotiate, enter into and execute an agreement with the Toronto Transit Commission for the recovery of all City costs associated with the design and construction of structural lining for sanitary sewer SL50048 near St. Clair West Station. The work is related to Toronto Transit Commission fire ventilation system upgrades at St. Clair West Station under the Line One Capacity Enhancement Program. Toronto Transit Commission has proposed a new below-grade structure above a deep sanitary sewer. Toronto Water has no objection to the proposed structure in relation to the existing Toronto Water infrastructure, provided Toronto Transit Commission funds the structural lining and associated work required to protect the sewer asset.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the General Manager, Toronto Water, to negotiate, enter into and execute on behalf of the City an agreement with the Toronto Transit Commission for the recovery of all City costs for the design and construction of structural lining for sanitary sewer SL50048, including engineering, consulting, permitting, tendering, construction, bypass pumping, mobilization, access works, site restoration, inspection, contract administration, applicable taxes, contingency, and close-out costs, on terms and conditions satisfactory to the General Manager, Toronto Water, and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor. 2.
