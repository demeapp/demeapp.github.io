The official record

What the item proposed

As Victoria Park Avenue borders two Community Council areas, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting authorization to install traffic control signals at the intersection of Victoria Park Avenue and Wakehood Street. Additionally, authorization is being requested to prohibit the east-west through movements between Wakehood Street and the plaza at the west side of Victoria Park Avenue as well as the southbound left turn movement from Victoria Park Avenue onto Wakehood Street. These prohibitions would be in effect at all times. The traffic control signals and turn restrictions will provide enhanced safety for all road users and are justified based on the assessment undertaken.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the installation of traffic control signals at Victoria Park Avenue and Wakehood Street. 2. In conjunction with the installation of new traffic control signals, City Council prohibit eastbound through movement at all times at Victoria Park Avenue from the private driveway access on the west side of Victoria Park Avenue to Wakehood Street. 3. In conjunction with the installation of new traffic control signals, City Council prohibit westbound through movements at all times at Victoria Park Avenue from Wakehood Street to the private driveway access on the west side of Victoria Park Avenue. 4. In conjunction with the installation of new traffic control signals, City Council prohibit southbound left-turns at all times at the intersection of Victoria Park Avenue and Wakehood Street.
