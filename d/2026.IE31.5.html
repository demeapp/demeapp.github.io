The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek City Council authority to extend the existing nominal-fee licensing agreement with Parkrun Canada to continue operating a community-based running program on the Martin Goodman Trail. The extension is recommended based on a successful pilot with strong participation, no identified operational or park impacts, and alignment with City priorities for accessible public recreation. For over 20 years, Parkrun has operated globally as a free, volunteer-led, weekly 5 kilometres run, jog, or walk held on Saturday mornings in communities around the world. Local events are organized by community volunteers and promote healthy, active lifestyles for participants of all abilities. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the General Manager, Parks and Recreation to negotiate and sign an agreement to extend the existing non-exclusive nominal fee licensing agreement to Parkrun Canada, to provide nominal fee permits for a community running program on the Martin Goodman Trail at Budapest Park, Sunnyside Park, and Sir Casimir Gzowski Park ending at Humber Bay Arch Bridge, for a term of up to two (2) years with two optional renewal terms of one year each, in compliance with the Non-Profit/Charitable Special Event Guidelines for City of Toronto Parks and Trails, on terms and conditions satisfactory to the General Manager, Parks and Recreation and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor commencing in the fall of 2026.
