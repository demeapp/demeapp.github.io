The official record

What the item proposed

A number of families have accepted the city’s encouragement to build laneway homes in Ward 11, whose only road access is via a public lane. However, the city does not provide snow clearing services to such lanes, creating serious access issues for their residents during severe winter weather. In 2026.IE29.1 , City staff declined to recommend snow removal for the 2,744 public laneways in the City of Toronto, but did not provide any alternative solution for even pedestrian access to the comparatively small number of laneway houses. This motion is focussed on a small number of laneway houses whose residents had serious access issues during winter weather in 2025 and 2026.

What was decided

The Infrastructure and Environment Committee: 1. Referred the item to the Deputy City Manager, Infrastructure Services and requested that they undertake a broader review of snow clearing options, specific to laneways with laneway housing, with financial implications and include as part of the report back as directed by City Council's decision on item IE29.1.
