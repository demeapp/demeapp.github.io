Authorization to Release Section 37 Funds to Toronto Community Housing Corporation for Affordable Housing Upgrades - by Councillor Dianne Saxe, seconded by Councillor Paula Fletcher

AdoptedMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Dianne Saxe asks council to release $2,366,000 ($2,366,000.00) in developer funds for upgrades at seven Toronto Community Housing buildings in Ward 11: $225,000 at 250 Davenport Rd, $846,000 at 177 Pendrith St, $275,000 at 40 Asquith Ave, $400,000 at 341 Bloor St, $310,000 at 25 Elm St, $220,000 at 72 Clinton St and $90,000 at 34 Oxford St, covering security cameras, landscaping, lighting and solar panels. The report also lists $84,634, $260,722, $110,000, $32,997, $168,420, $474,164, $817,366 and $417,697 as additional listed figures.

The official summary names a figure of $225,000.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

Toronto Community Housing Corporation owns and maintains 12 affordable housing facilities in Ward 11, 8 operated by Toronto Community Housing Corporation, and 4 operated by Toronto Seniors Housing Corporation. Seven of them require upgrades to improve security, landscaping and energy management.
