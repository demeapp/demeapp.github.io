Update of Definition and Reporting of the Delegated Authority on Certain Real Estate Matters - by Councillor Chris Moise, seconded by James Pasternak

AdoptedMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Chris Moise asks council to update the rules and reporting for delegated real-estate decisions, after cost details emerged on the $6 million, 15-year lease at 233 Carlton Street: a $3.7 million initial renovation estimate, over $2 million in rent paid, an $8.7 million contractor award, and $13.5 million in forecasted costs, above the $10 million threshold.

The official summary names a figure of $6 million.

The official record

What the item proposed

In 2019, the City of Toronto entered into a 15-year lease (comprising an initial 10-year term with one 5-year extension option) for 233 Carlton Street, a three-storey commercial building of approximately 13,000 square feet located in Cabbagetown, at a total lease cost of $6 million. The site was selected to replace the Adelaide Women's Resource Centre at 67 Adelaide Street East — a City-owned property — and to be operated as a 24-hour women's drop-in centre, with the lease commencing January 1, 2020. The property owner was also exempted from property taxes for the full duration of the rental period. An initial renovation estimate of $3.7 million was established for the site and was documented on the Delegated Approval Form tied to the lease.
