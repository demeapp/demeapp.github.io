Authorization to Release Section 37 Funds for Accessibility and Performance Upgrades to Cultural Facilities (TRANZAC, Annex and Al Green Theatres) - by Councillor Dianne Saxe, seconded by Councillor Jamaal Myers

Councillor Dianne Saxe asks council to release $550,000 in developer funds for accessibility and performance upgrades at three Ward 11 theatres, TRANZAC, Annex and Al Green, with listed allocations of $300,000, $187,750.51, $112,249.49 and $50,000.

The official record

What the item proposed

Ward 11 has a vibrant and popular collection of small theatres which host a variety of performances, including the heart of the ever-popular Toronto Fringe festival. These small theatres are essential for Toronto’s performing arts community and for everyone who benefits from their performances. Unfortunately, many of these theatres are in desperate need of upgrades, particularly for accessibility, climate resilience, and performance quality. Capital funds for these purposes have been in very short supply for a long time. TRANZAC, at 292 Brunswick Avenue, is a vibrant not-for-profit performance arts organization dedicated to nurturing artistic expression and fostering community connections. The building contains three performance spaces that accommodate audiences of 20 to 150 people each. TRANZAC has a long history of supporting the performing arts. City Council direct that the $550,000 be forwarded to TRANZAC upon the signing of an Undertaking by TRANZAC that governs the use of the funds and the financial reporting requirements. 3.
