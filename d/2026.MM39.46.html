Re-opening and Amending 2025.CC35.19 - 0, 119-125, 160 and 200 Benny Stark Street and 116-122 Turnberry Avenue - Ontario Land Tribunal Hearing - Request for Directions - by Councillor Alejandra Bravo, seconded by Deputy Mayor Ausma Malik

This is procedural: council was asked to fix a drafting error that named the wrong city official in December 2025 directions for a 929-square-metre child care facility at Benny Stark Street and Turnberry Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

There was a drafting error in Confidential Attachment 1 to the Request for Directions Report dated December 8, 2025. Recommendation 3 mistakenly referenced the Executive Director, Social Development, Finance and Administration, rather than the General Manager, Children's Services. Requires Re-opening: City Council Item 2025.CC35.19 (December 16 and 17, 2025 Council Meeting), only as it pertains to Part 3 of the instructions to staff.

What was decided

City Council on March 25 and 26, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend its previous decision on Item 2025.CC35.19 by deleting the words “the Executive Director, Social Development, Finance and Administration” in Part 3 of the instructions to staff and replacing them with the words “the General Manager, Children's Services”, so that Part 3 now reads as follows: 3. City Council allow the owner to design, construct, finish, furnish, equip and convey to the City, at the Owner’s expense, a minimum 929 square metres child care facility, with a minimum 279 square metres outdoor area, ("Child Care Facility") to the satisfaction of the Executive Director, Development Review, the General Manager, Children’s Services, the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, and the City Solicitor, as part of the development, as an in-kind contribution pursuant to subsection 37(6) of…
