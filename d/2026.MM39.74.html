Two Loons Brewing By-the-Glass Permit Application to the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario - by Councillor Alejandra Bravo, seconded by Councillor Josh Matlow

AdoptedMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Alejandra Bravo asks council to support Two Loons Brewing's application for a by-the-glass liquor sales licence at 122 Brock Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Two Loons Brewing, located at 122 Brock Avenue, has advised that in order to obtain a Manufacturer's Limited Liquor Sales Licence, a resolution from the local Municipal Council is required. Reason for Urgency: This Motion is urgent to allow the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario to process the application without further delay and unnecessary hardship to the applicant

What was decided

City Council on March 25 and 26, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council advise the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario of its support of the application from Two Loons Brewing, 122 Brock Avenue for a Manufacturer's Limited Liquor Sales Licence for sales by the glass.
