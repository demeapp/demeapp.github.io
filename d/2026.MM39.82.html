Building a Made-in-Toronto Community Safety Plan Protecting Residents at Bathurst Street and Sheppard Avenue and Across Toronto - by Councillor James Pasternak, seconded by Councillor Michael Thompson

Councillor James Pasternak asks council to develop a made-in-Toronto community safety plan, citing weekly demonstrations at Bathurst and Sheppard since September 2024, resident victim impact statements, and 443 reported hate crimes in 2024, up 80 percent from 2022.

What the item proposed

On or about September 2024 anti-Israel demonstrators arrived at the intersection of Bathurst Street and Sheppard Avenue. They were mostly on the northeast corner. This is a predominantly Jewish neighbourhood. There are no government buildings, no consulates, no embassies and no city squares. Since that time, these demonstrators have used noise amplification systems to harass, demonize and provoke the Jewish community holding a vigil on the west side of Bathurst Street. On occasion, the anti-Israel protestors wander through the local neighbourhood and harassing local residents. They have called for the destruction of Israel, globalizing the Intifada, and making hateful and derogatory remarks about Jews.
