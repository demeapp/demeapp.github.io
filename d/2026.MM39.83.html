Implementing the Cultural Districts Program - by Councillor Chris Moise, seconded by Councillor Josh Matlow

Adopted with changesMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Chris Moise asks council to move the long-promised Cultural Districts Program to implementation, covering Church-Wellesley Village, Little Jamaica and Chinatown, after years of missed deadlines.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council has provided clear and consistent direction on the establishment of a Cultural Districts Program since 2020. In January of that year, Council directed the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture to develop recommendations to support the retention of LGBTQ2S+ small businesses and cultural spaces, with particular attention to the Church-Wellesley Village (Item 2020.MM14.8). In September 2020, Council directed the development of a Cultural District Plan for the Little Jamaica neighbourhood along Eglinton Avenue West, including formal designation, community engagement, and a retail and heritage strategy (Item 2020.MM24.36).
