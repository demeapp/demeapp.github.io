Authority to Provide a Neighbour Authorization Form to the Owners of 53 Lake Shore Drive for Shoreline Retaining Wall Construction - by Councillor Amber Morley, seconded by Councillor Paul Ainslie

AdoptedMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Amber Morley asks council to let Transportation Services sign a neighbour authorization form so the owners of 53 Lake Shore Drive can replace their shoreline retaining wall at their own cost before a late-summer deadline.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The property owners of 53 Lake Shore Drive are seeking to restore the integrity of the shoreline on their property and the land adjacent to their property, by replacing the existing revetment with a new retaining wall. Shoreline restoration is required because the existing revetment is nearing its end of life and must be replaced to satisfy the concerns of the Toronto and Region Conservation Authority with respect to home renovations the property owner plans to complete. A portion of the new retaining wall will be located on public, crown land and the property owner has agreed that they will cover the cost of construction. As part of the review and permitting process, the property owners must include Neighbour Authorization Forms completed by adjacent landowners. What was decided

City Council on March 25 and 26, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Transportation Services in consultation with the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, to complete the Neighbour Authorization Form by April 13, 2026, or as soon as possible thereafter, for the property owners of 53 Lake Shore Drive, in relation to their planned shoreline restoration work, and authorize the General Manager, Transportation Services in consultation with the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management to enter into any other relevant authorization or Agreement as needed.
