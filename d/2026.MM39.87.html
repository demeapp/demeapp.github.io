The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto recently witnessed one of the largest public demonstrations in its history, with more than 350,000 people gathering in the Willowdale neighbourhood to express solidarity with the people of Iran and to call for freedom, democracy, and human dignity. This and several other protests in Toronto emphasize the strong and longstanding connections between Toronto and its Iranian community. Many members of this community have close personal ties to those affected by ongoing violence and unrest in Iran, resulting in heightened anxiety, grief, and uncertainty for families in Toronto. Continued internet shutdowns have only deepened this distress, as many wait for updates from loved ones. The loss of life during recent protests, combined with an increasingly volatile situation, has deeply affected not only the Iranian community but many Torontonians who share a commitment to human rights,…

What was decided

City Council on March 25 and 26, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council formally recognize March 2026 as Persian Heritage Month in the City of Toronto, and encourage the celebration of Persian and Iranian culture, history, and contributions, while also affirming solidarity with the Iranian community during this critical time. 2. City Council direct the Chief of Protocol to illuminate the Toronto sign in Nathan Phillips Square, on a date to be determined in the month of April, in green, white, and red, as an expression of solidarity with the Iranian community, recognizing their pursuit of freedom, democracy, and human dignity in Iran, and to honour the lives lost in this pursuit.
