The official record

What the item proposed

The motion authorizes the release of $367,905.77 in Section 42 funds secured from the development at 1648 Victoria Park Avenue to the Parks and Recreation 2026 – 2035 Capital Budget and Plan for capital improvements at Wigmore Park.

What was decided

City Council on April 22 and 23, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend the 2026–2035 Capital Budget and Plan for Parks and Recreation to increase the project budget for the “Wigmore Park Improvements” project, on a one-time basis, by $367,905.77 gross and $0 debt in 2026, fully funded by local Section 42 parkland dedication funds secured in Ward 16 (Source Account: XR2213-4201714), for the purpose of park improvements at Wigmore Park.
