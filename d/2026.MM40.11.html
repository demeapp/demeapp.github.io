Councillor Jon Burnside asks council to release $439,600 in parkland funds from the 1185 Eglinton Avenue East development for improvements at Ferrand Park.

The official record

What the item proposed

The motion authorizes the release of $439,600 in Section 42 funds secured from the development at 1185 Eglinton Avenue East to the Parks and Recreation 2026-2035 Capital Budget and Plan for capital improvements at Ferrand Park.

What was decided

City Council on April 22 and 23, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend the 2026-2035 Capital Budget and Plan for Parks and Recreation to increase the project budget for the “Ferrand Park Improvements” project, on a one-time basis, by $439,600 gross and $0 debt in 2026, fully funded by local Section 42 parkland dedication funds secured in Ward 16 (Source Account: XR2213-4201076), for the purpose of park improvements at Ferrand Park.
