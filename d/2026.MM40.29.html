Cabbagetown Cycling Connects - Ontario Street Parking By-law Amendments - by Councillor Chris Moise, seconded by Deputy Mayor Ausma Malik

AdoptedApr 22, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Councillor Chris Moise asks council to approve parking by-law changes for the Ontario Street cycling connection in Cabbagetown, adding contra-flow bike lanes and a loading zone while keeping curbside parking.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In February 2025, City Council approved the installation of the Ontario Street cycling connection from Wellesley Street East to Prospect Street, as part of 2025.IE19.1, and in July 2025, City Council approve changes to the bikeway on Ontario Street between Aberdeen Avenue and Carleton Street as part of 2025.IE23.6. In 2026, Ontario Street, between Wellesley Street East and Carlton Street, will undergo watermain, road resurfacing, and public realm improvements, in conjunction with new bike lanes. The project will enhance existing sidewalk and boulevard conditions impacted by tree root upheaving, strengthen connections between existing cycling routes, building on earlier cycling upgrades south of Winchester Square Park through the Cabbagetown Cycling Connections project in 2023. There is no change to existing motor vehicle lanes. Most residents supported the plans during public…

What was decided

City Council on April 22 and 23, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve the parking, bike lane and traffic by-law amendments in Attachment 1 to Member Motion MM40.29 as part of the Cabbagetown Cycling Connections project.
