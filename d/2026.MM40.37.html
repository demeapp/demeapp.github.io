109 Knox Avenue - Request for City Solicitor to Attend at the Toronto Local Appeal Body - by Councillor Paula Fletcher, seconded by Councillor Alejandra Bravo

AdoptedApr 22, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is procedural: council was asked to let the City Solicitor appear before the Toronto Local Appeal Body over the refused third and fourth-storey additions at 109 Knox Avenue.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant applied to the Committee of Adjustment to request variances from City-wide Zoning By-law 569-2013 (Application A0033/24TEY) to alter the existing two-storey detached dwelling by constructing complete third and fourth storey additions to accommodate a total of 6 residential dwelling units on this lot. The variances required to By-law 569-2013 include height, minimum required front yard setback, minimum required rear yard setback, roof eaves projection, maximum permitted height of all side exterior main walls, and minimum rear yard soft landscaping. On January 14, 2026, the Toronto and East York District Panel of the Committee of Adjustment refused the Application. A copy of the Decision is attached as Attachment 1. The applicant has appealed the Decision to Toronto Local Appeal Body. In a report from the Director of Community Planning, Toronto and East York District dated…

What was decided

City Council on April 22 and 23, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the City Solicitor to attend the Toronto Local Appeal Body to oppose the development proposed in application A0033/24TEY. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to retain outside consultants as necessary. 3. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to negotiate a settlement of the appeal, and that the City Solicitor be authorized to resolve the matter on behalf of the City, in her discretion, after consulting with the Ward Councillor and the Director of Community Planning, Toronto and East York District.
