Toronto Island Bike Share Enhancements for the 2026 Season - by Deputy Mayor Ausma Malik, seconded by Councillor Paula Fletcher

AdoptedMay 20, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Deputy Mayor Ausma Malik asks council to implement 2026-season enhancements for Toronto Island Bike Share, after 216,000 trips in the 2025 pilot, including cyclist education and management around residential areas.

The official record

What the item proposed

In May 2025, Bike Share Toronto launched a pilot project on Toronto Island, introducing service to this destination park for the first time. This program aligned with the goal of enhancing access on Toronto Island park for all users, and was implemented in coordination with Parks staff from Toronto Island Park Plan team. Six Bike Share stations at Centre Island, Ward’s Island, Hanlan’s Point Beach and Gibraltar Point were installed for the pilot. The program was successful with users, with over 216,000 trips started on Toronto Island between May and October 2025. In this first year, the Centre Island Ferry Dock and Ward’s Island Ferry Dock station were respectively the first and third most used stations in the entire Bike Share system in 2025.
