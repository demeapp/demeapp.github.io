The official record

What the item proposed

The Township of East York was incorporated on January 1, 1924 with a population of 19,849 and will celebrate its a hundredth anniversary in 2024. In 1953 the Township of East York became one of the local governments as part of the newly created Metropolitan Toronto upper tier level of municipal government. In 1967 the Borough of East York was established through the amalgamation of the former Township of East York and the former Town of Leaside. Known as Canada’s only Borough, East York continued as an semi-autonomous municipality until 1997 when the Government of Ontario amalgamated seven governments into the new City of Toronto. On March 3, 1997, residents of Metropolitan Toronto held a referendum on the idea of amalgamation to which East Yorkers strongly demonstrated their support and commitment for retaining East York’s own local government.
