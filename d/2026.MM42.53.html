What the item proposed

Birchmount Stadium is a premier publicly funded track and field facility in Scarborough, upgraded as part of a $206 million investment in sport infrastructure across the Greater Toronto Area for the 2015 Pan Am Games. Under current agreements, use is restricted to use by local school students during the school year between 8:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m.. Outside those hours, the stadium is heavily permitted by athletic groups, leaving limited windows for general public access during the school year. During the school year (September to June), permitted bookings run Monday to Friday from 6:00–11:00 p.m. and Saturday to Sunday from 8:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., leaving little room for unstructured community use.
