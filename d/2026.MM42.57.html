What the item proposed

The resulting Site Specific By-laws 1173-2022 (OLT) and 1174-2022 (OLT) were enacted on September 16, 2022. The Council approval ( Item 2022.CC39.7 - 10-32 Raglan Avenue - Zoning By-law Amendment Application - Ontario Land Tribunal Hearing - Request for Further Directions ) secured a set of Section 37 Planning Act Community Benefits.
