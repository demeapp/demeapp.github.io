What the item proposed

Yorkville Village is a highly valued tourist attraction whose shops, restaurants, and festivals also serve a dense residential area and the entire Toronto community. In cooperation with the Bloor Yorkville Business Improvement Area, the city has installed 119 street trees in sidewalk pits on Bellair Street, Cumberland Street (west of Bay), Yorkville Avenue (west of Bay), Hazelton Avenue (from Yorkville to approximately 40 Hazelton), Scollard Street (west of Bay) and Bay Street (from St. Mary to just north of Scollard). The trees contribute significantly to the beauty of the area and to making it attractive even in very hot weather. They also contribute to noise abatement. Unfortunately, the health and survival of the trees is being damaged by heavy foot and vehicle traffic through the tree pits.
