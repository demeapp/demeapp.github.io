The official record

What the item proposed

This motion is to provide community benefits secured through section 37 for the purposes of upgrading the streetscape on Richmond Street East adjacent to the development under construction by Tricon Residential Inc. at 261 Queen Street East (also known as ROQ City). Working with the developer, this funding would allow upgrades to the Richmond Street East streetscape and cycle track adjacent to ROQ City, in alignment with the Old Town Toronto Business Improvement Area Street Network Master Plan and beyond the scope of the approved Site Plan Control application for the development.
