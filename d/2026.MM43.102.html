The official record

What the item proposed

At the June 2026 meeting of City Council, City Council confirmed and approved STEPS Public Art as the lead organization for leading artist calls to beautify the local community through public art on Bell utility boxes and prominent picnic benches in Liberty Village Park. Since this agreement, some additional time and resources have been identified as necessary for STEPS to thoroughly liaise with Bell on logistical matters, necessitating some additional costs. The motion is aimed at ensuring STEPS has access to the necessary funds to complete this work. Reason for Urgency: To allow for STEPS Public Art to begin work immediately to liaise with Bell to prepare the utility box sites and undertake public engagement and artist selection as soon as possible. Requires Re-opening: City Council Item 2026.MM42.23 (June 24 and 25, 2026)
