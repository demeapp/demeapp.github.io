Finding safe interim construction access for the 8 to 10 Hyde Park Circle build off Post Road instead of the cul-de-sac.

The official record

What the item proposed

Construction has begun on a development located at 8 to 10 Hyde Park Circle, properties with street addresses facing into a cul-de-sac and with rear and side frontages onto Post Road and Bayview Avenue. As approved, the development will eventually route vehicular access onto Post Road rather than into Hyde Park Circle, as Post Road has greater capacity to accommodate the traffic the project will generate. Similarly, throughout the approval process for this application the City, applicant and community have understood that access to the site during construction would be facilitated through Post Road. As it stands, access to the site in early stages of construction has been routed through Hyde Park Circle due to a narrow reserve along the north side of Post Road, which must be addressed to facilitate access. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the General Manager, Transportation Services to negotiate, enter into and execute a license agreement with North Drive Investments Inc to permit temporary construction access across the reserve running along Post Road for the limited purpose of accessing the rear of the lands municipally known as 8 and 10 Hyde Park Circle via Post Road, all in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor. 2. City Council direct the General Manager, Transportations Services to include in any license agreement entered into further to Part 1 above the following conditions: a. the proposed access be consistent with all approved development agreements and any conditions imposed by the City through the development approval process; b. the proposed access does not adversely impact utilities, existing easements or similar land…
