The official record

What the item proposed

Royal Canadian Legion Scarboro Branch 13 has been a cornerstone of the Birch Cliff community for more than a century. Operating from the Scarboro Veterans Memorial Hall at 1577 Kingston Road, built in 1922 following the First World War, the Branch has provided generations of veterans, their families and local residents with a place of remembrance, fellowship and community service. Reflecting strong advocacy from local residents, veterans, and community members, a new signalized pedestrian crossing at Kingston Road and Kildonan Drive/Eastwood Avenue in front of the Legion is currently being installed. This initiative, intended to improve safety for seniors, veterans, people with disabilities and other vulnerable road users, was successfully approved by Scarborough Community Council and City Council and is now moving toward implementation by this fall. City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council increase the 2026-2035 Capital Budget and Plan for Transportation Services, on a one-time basis by $27,000 gross, $0 debt, from the development at 1478 to 1496 Kingston Road (Source Account: XR3026-3701343) to be utilized for local streetscape improvements, in consultation with the local councillor and community, in the form of decorative pedestrian crossing at Kingston and Eastwood Avenue (CTP726-07).
