What the item proposed

Biidaasige Park is one of Toronto’s largest and most significant new parks and public spaces to open in a generation. Brought to life through the tri-government-funded Port Lands Flood Protection Project, its unique scale, design, ecological restoration, public art, and Indigenous placekeeping elements make it a landmark destination and an important piece of social infrastructure. The park will serve residents and visitors for generations while standing as a testament to Toronto’s commitment to Reconciliation, Indigenous stewardship, and creating world-class public spaces. Given the scale of public investment, the deep engagement with multiple stakeholders and partners and the long-term importance of this asset, it is timely and appropriate to explore governance models that could enhance stewardship, programming, operations, and maintenance.
