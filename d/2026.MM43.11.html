The official record

What the item proposed

There are approximately 3,200 fire departments across Canada. The scale and complexity of emergencies to which Canadian fire services are expected to respond have increased significantly in recent years and are expected to continue growing. All governments have responsibilities related to fire protection, emergency management, disaster response, and public safety. Effective collaboration and strategic coordination among governments and fire services are essential to ensure preparedness for major emergencies and the efficient deployment of resources. The increasing frequency, severity, and duration of wildfires and other climate-related emergencies across Canada demonstrate the need for a coordinated and sustained national approach to prevention, mitigation, response, and recovery. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council express its support for the establishment of a National Fire Administration within Public Safety Canada, developed in collaboration with Canada's fire chiefs and fire service organizations. 2. City Council forward a copy of this motion to the Federal Minister of Public Safety Canada, the Federation of Canadian Municipalities, the Association of Municipalities of Ontario, Toronto's Members of Parliament, and Ontario's Solicitor General.
