What the item proposed

This Motion will give the City Solicitor authority to appeal the Committee of Adjustment’s decision approving the minor variance application at 39 Norfield Crescent (A0178/26EYK); to attend the Toronto Local Appeal Body to oppose the development proposed in application A0178/26EYK; to retain outside consultants as necessary; and to negotiate a settlement of the appeal. The Applicant applied to the Committee of Adjustment to request a variance from City-wide Zoning By-law 569-2013 (Application A0178/26EYK) to construct a second storey addition above the existing dwelling and to maintain the existing one-storey east side addition (the “Application”). The altered dwelling would contain a total of four units. To facilitate the Application, a variance for front yard setback is required. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to appeal the Committee of Adjustment’s decision regarding 39 Norfield Crescent (Application A0178/26EYK) and to attend the Toronto Local Appeal Body to oppose the development proposed in Application A0178/26EYK. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to retain outside consultants as necessary. 3. City Council authorize the City Solicitor to negotiate a settlement of the appeal and to resolve the matter on behalf of the City, in her discretion, after consulting with the Ward Councillor and the Director, Community Planning, Etobicoke York District.
