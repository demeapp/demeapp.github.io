What the item proposed

The proposed developments in the Malvern area, including the Malvern Town Centre, represent a significant opportunity to reshape the community to support major commercial and civic destinations in Scarborough North. Considerations to support new residents and jobs in the Malvern area and how development is phased over time include important matters such as housing, transportation (including new pedestrian and cycling connections), new parks and open spaces, the public realm, and the delivery of community services in Malvern and surrounding neighbourhoods. Malvern is home to a large and diverse population, including significant Black, Indigenous and newcomer communities, as well as seniors, youth and other equity-deserving groups.
