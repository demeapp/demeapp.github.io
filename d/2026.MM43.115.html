The official record

What the item proposed

In 2025, the Toronto Blue Jays had a historic season that culminated in a run to the World Series Championship series. The season not only united our city in a shared celebration of the team's accomplishments but also translated that momentum into significant fundraising success for the team’s official philanthropic body, Jays Care Foundation. Jays Care Foundation uses baseball to teach life skills, build community spaces, and help kids in marginalized communities through programs and grants that increase access to sport. Through its Field of Dreams program, Jays Care Foundation also provides grants to build, enhance, and refurbish safe local baseball diamonds and community infrastructure across Canada. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, in consultation with the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, the General Manager, Parks and Recreation, the Executive Director, Development Review and any other relevant City Official(s), to work with CreateTO to identify potential City-owned sites for a Jays Care Academy, with an emphasis on locations outside of downtown core, and enter into discussions with the Jays Care Foundation to identify city building benefits to the City that would be secured, including those related to community access and public use, and to report to City Council through the appropriate Standing Committee at the earliest opportunity.
