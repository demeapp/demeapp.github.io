What the item proposed

Bedford Park is one of Toronto's oldest established neighbourhoods, with origins dating back to the late 19th century. Initially a rural farming hamlet and popular stop for farmers travelling along Yonge Street to markets in downtown Toronto, the community grew rapidly following the arrival of the Metropolitan Street Railway in 1890, which spurred residential development and helped shape the neighbourhood that exists today. The area is widely believed to have taken its name from the Bedford Park Hotel, which opened in 1873 and became a prominent local landmark, with the name later extending to the surrounding community and post office. Today, Bedford Park remains a well-recognized historic neighbourhood whose identity has endured for more than 150 years.
