The official record

What the item proposed

After 30 years, the green space at 1555 Danforth at Coxwell officially became a city park. It is now formally a park with an official name - Coxwell Common. For all these years it has been a beautiful respite and community gathering space along the busy Danforth. It also hosts the Courtyard Farmers’ Market each week. Unlike most other green spaces, it was built with a full irrigation system that leads to a circle in the middle of the park which was to be a water feature. Since the beginning the circle has only ever had a tree with some plantings. A water feature would improve the overall enjoyment of the park as one of only of two green spaces on the Danforth between Broadview and Victoria Park.
