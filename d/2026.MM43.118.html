What the item proposed

The City of Toronto is developing a long-term real estate strategy for the Grange Precinct in downtown Toronto. This work is led by CreateTO on behalf of the City and in close collaboration with community partners. The precinct includes several important public assets, including St. Patrick's Market, St. Patrick's Market Square, Harrison Pool, University Settlement, and Grange Park. The Grange Precinct is a vital part of Toronto's downtown west community. It is home to significant cultural institutions, community-serving organizations, public spaces, and a diverse population of residents. The area serves as an important gathering place, connecting neighbourhoods while supporting recreation, culture, social services, and community programming. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the General Manager, Parks and Recreation, in consultation with the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO, to review opportunities to expedite the phased delivery of improvements to St. Patrick's Market Square parkette as part of the City's broader Grange Precinct Real Estate Strategy and to include potential enhancements identified through community feedback, including outdoor fitness equipment and other recreational amenities, as part of future public consultations and design considerations.
