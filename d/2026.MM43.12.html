What the item proposed

The Inspector General of Policing serves the public interest by promoting accountability in the policing sector. The Inspector General provides regulatory oversight to ensure compliance with the Community Safety and Policing Act and its regulations. The Inspector General ensures compliance with Ontario's Community Safety and Policing Act and its regulations through inspections, investigations, and advisory services. As of April 1, 2024, the Inspector General of Policing has unique enforcement authorities and can issue measures and directions that are progressive and based on risk to public safety. A Notice of Issue is a mechanism used by the Inspector General of Policing to monitor and call attention to issues relating to the delivery of policing and police governance in Ontario. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council express its support for the findings and recommendations in the Notice of Issue of the Office of the Inspector General of Policing released on July 14, 2026, attached as Attachment 1 to this motion. 2. City Council forward a copy of this motion to the Toronto Police Service Board, the Solicitor General of Ontario, the Attorney General of Ontario and the Minister of Public Safety of Canada.
