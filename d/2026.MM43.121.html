Keeping working on protections for Toronto's live music venues through the Culture Connects plan.

Official title: Protecting Toronto’s Live Music Venues - by Deputy Mayor Ausma Malik, seconded by Councillor Alejandra Bravo

The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto’s live music scene is a cultural engine that has produced local and international stars. While the city boasts a vibrant collection of small and large venues, serving diverse communities, it is also facing major threats including rising rents and insurance and development pressures. Over the past few years, we’ve lost many live music venues in the City of Toronto and many others have been at risk, most recently Sneaky Dee’s. Many divisions at the City of Toronto have been looking at what is possible to support live music venues to continue to thrive. This motion will enable staff to continue this work, reporting back as part of our Culture Connects plan. This motion is urgent to enable staff to begin work to meet the report back timelines.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1.
