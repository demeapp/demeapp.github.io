Taking cash instead of a Dufferin Station tunnel as the community benefit for the Bloor and Dufferin build.

The official record

What the item proposed

Following further review and community consultation, accepting a cash contribution in lieu of the construction of the below-grade connection to Dufferin Station represents the best outcome for the community. This motion directs the City to proceed with the cash contribution option, allowing the value which would otherwise have been allocated to the tunnel's construction to be redirected toward high-priority affordable housing initiatives, community and cultural amenities, and public realm improvements for Dufferin Subway Station identified through consultation with residents and stakeholders. This approach ensures that community benefits associated with the development are delivered in a manner that maximizes public value in a way that is responsive to current local needs.
