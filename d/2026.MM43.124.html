Studying making all food outlets provide customer bins for garbage, recycling and organics.

The official record

What the item proposed

Some quick-serve restaurants do not provide their customers with access to on-site waste receptacles (i.e. garbage, recycling, organics) to dispose of waste after consuming food and drinks. Rather, in these establishments that do not have customer accessible waste receptacles, customers are left with no option other than to dispose of their waste in public space waste receptacles. Public space waste receptacles, such as street litter bins, are not designed nor intended to accept materials generated from food service establishments in lieu of the establishment providing on-site waste receptacles. Council direction is required for Solid Waste Management Services staff to research available regulatory, licensing, operational, and legal mechanisms that could be implemented to require all food service establishments to provide on-site waste receptacles for use by their customers. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Solid Waste Management Services to work with appropriate City Divisions to take the necessary steps to mandate quick-serve restaurants to provide on-site waste receptacles for use by their customers, and report back to City Council if required.
