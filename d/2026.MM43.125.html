Giving Pape Market Foodland exclusive use of the loading zone at 623 Danforth Avenue it funded.

The official record

What the item proposed

With the installation of the Danforth Cycle Track in 2021, the commercial loading zone located adjacent to Pape Market Foodland at 623 Danforth Avenue was established to support the operational needs of the grocery local delivery business and was funded by Pape Market Food Land. Another dedicated loading zone was also established adjacent to the Danforth Music Hall with the installation of a Motor Coach only zone to provide parking for the buses required for the business aspects related to performances at the venue. Pape Market Foodland relies on regular and timely deliveries throughout the day to maintain its operations and serve the surrounding community. Despite funding the Commercial Loading Zone, the business has experienced repeated parking enforcement penalties for delivery vehicles in while in the loading zone. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Transportation Services to designate the existing commercial loading zone adjacent to Pape Market Foodland, 623 Danforth Avenue, for the exclusive use of the business and designate the zone with the appropriate signage.
