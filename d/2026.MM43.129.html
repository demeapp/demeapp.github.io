Letting the City Manager pick and implement a commemoration for former councillor and TTC Chair Howard Moscoe.

Official title: Commemorating Former Councillor and Toronto Transit Commission Chair Howard Moscoe - by Mayor Olivia Chow, seconded by Councillor Shelley Carroll

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The official record

What the item proposed

Following Council direction in 2026.MM42.31 , City staff are working to identify and assess appropriate options to commemorate former Councillor and Toronto Transit Commission Chair Howard Moscoe, including the potential naming of a City asset or to undertake other suitable form(s) of public recognition in his honour. Once identified, staff will consult with the Moscoe family and relevant stakeholders to inform its decision. Work will continue during the Council recess period to support the timely implementation of Council's direction. The motion seeks Council's approval to proceed with a commemorative naming once staff have completed their review, identified a suitable naming and/or commemorative opportunity, and undertake consultation with the Moscoe family, local Councillor, and relevant stakeholders. This motion also seeks authorization to waive any provisions that may be contained…

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the City Manager or designate to identify, approve and implement a suitable commemorative naming of a City asset or other form of public recognition in honour of Howard Moscoe following budget consideration, written consent from the Moscoe family, consultations with the local Councillor, and relevant stakeholders, and in a form as approved by the City Solicitor for any written agreement(s). 2. City Council authorize the commemorative naming of a City asset or other form of public recognition in honour of Howard Moscoe without restriction or delay despite any provision that may be contained in a City policy, procedure or guideline, to give effect to Part 1 above.
