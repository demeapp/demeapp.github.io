The official record

What the item proposed

As part of a plan of subdivision application for the lands at 5799 Yonge Street, a new public road (Olympic Garden Drive) was approved through the lands connecting Cummer Avenue to the north, and Yonge Street in the southwest part of the site, at the intersection of Yonge Street and Narges Drive. The design, construction, and applicable restoration of the road was secured through the executed subdivision agreement. To facilitate the construction of the Olympic Garden Drive, a portion of the adjacent lands municipally known as 5795 Yonge Street was acquired by the City, as authorized by City Council. Development charge credits were previously authorized in respect of the land acquisition costs associated with the expropriation.
