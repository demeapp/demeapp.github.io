What the item proposed

The Central Eglinton Community Centre, located at 160 Eglinton Avenue East, is an integral part of the Yonge-Eglinton community, providing a wide range of services focused on the needs of seniors, families, and newcomers. Central Eglinton Community Centre operates approximately 16,000 square feet of space to serve a growing community. Central Eglinton Community Centre is one of ten community centres known as the Association of Community Centres. Each is managed by a volunteer Board of Management appointed by City Council, and the group receives its funding through the annual Council-approved Operating Budget. These multi-purpose facilities provide inclusive spaces that strengthen civic engagement, build local leadership, and advance equity-focused outcomes.
