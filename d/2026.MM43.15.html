What the item proposed

This Motion seeks authorization to release $10,000 in Section 37 (Planning Act Reserve Account) funds to Heritage Toronto for the creation and installation of a plaque to commemorate Research Enterprises Limited. Research Enterprises Limited was a short-lived Toronto-based Crown Corporation that built electronics and optical instruments during the Second World War. During this time, they became Leaside's largest employer. At its peak, the company's plants covered most of the area between Eglinton Avenue, Laird Drive, and Research Road, along with smaller shops and storage extending to Wicksteed Avenue further south. In total, the company estate covered 22 hectares, had 70,000 square metres of floor space, employed 7,500 men and women, and produced $220 million worth of radar systems and optical instruments that helped the Allies win the war.
