The official record

What the item proposed

Tenant safety remains one of the most frequently raised concerns in Toronto Community Housing Corporation communities across the City of Toronto. Recent community safety meetings hosted by my office and Toronto Community Housing Corporation at Moss Park Apartments and at the Wellesley Community Centre for St. James Town residents brought together tenants, Toronto Community Housing Corporation staff, Toronto Police Service, the City's Violence Intervention and Support Unit, Fred Victor, and Unison Health and Community Services to discuss local safety concerns and identify solutions. Tenants consistently raised concerns regarding building security, unauthorized entry, trespassing, anti-social behaviour, access control, package theft, and recurring safety issues within residential buildings. Many tenants also expressed concern about their ability to identify individuals at their door…

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Board of Directors of Toronto Community Housing Corporation to review current policies governing tenant-installed peephole cameras, door-view cameras, and similar non-invasive security devices and identify whether and under what circumstances such devices may be permitted, accommodated, or otherwise supported, subject to applicable privacy, legal, building, fire/life safety, and operational requirements. 2. City Council request the Board of Directors of Toronto Community Housing Corporation to undertake a portfolio-informed, risk-based review of CCTV camera coverage across its residential portfolio as part of the broader Community Safety and Well-Being Strategy and identify access points and common areas where additional CCTV camera coverage may be warranted due to recurring or documented safety…
