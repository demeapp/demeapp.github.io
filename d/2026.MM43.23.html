What the item proposed

The City of Toronto and Toronto and Region Conservation Authority have initiated the Vale of Avoca Improvement Project, in the Yellow Creek ravine near Yonge Street and St. Clair Ave East. This project will develop a coordinated plan to mitigate erosion hazards, enhance natural features, and improve how people access, move through and experience the Vale of Avoca ravine. The ravine straddles Toronto wards University-Rosedale and Toronto-St. Paul’s. The erosion hazards present risks to municipal infrastructure, trails, access points to the ravine, and adjacent properties. Certain informal trails are in poor condition, and two staircases providing access to the ravine have been closed. Without the construction of adequate erosion control measures, the banks will continue to erode, and slope instabilities will worsen.
