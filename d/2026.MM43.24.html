What the item proposed

This motion requests the authorization to release section 37 funds to Toronto Community Housing to fund new closed-circuit television cameras and related infrastructure at properties in St. James Town and Moss Park. Tenant safety remains one of the most frequently raised concerns in Toronto Community Housing Corporation communities across the City of Toronto. Recent community safety meetings hosted by my office and Toronto Community Housing Corporation at Moss Park Apartments and at the Wellesley Community Centre for St. James Town residents brought together tenants, Toronto Community Housing Corporation staff, Toronto Police Service, the City's Violence Intervention and Support Unit, Fred Victor, and Unison Health and Community Services to discuss local safety concerns and identify solutions.
