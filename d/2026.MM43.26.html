What the item proposed

As Regent Park continues to grow and evolve, the delivery of permanent community infrastructure is critical to building a complete community where residents have access to the spaces, services, and opportunities needed to participate fully in neighbourhood life. Association of Community Centres are community-governed, multi-purpose facilities overseen by volunteer Boards of Management appointed by City Council. These facilities provide affordable space for residents, grassroots groups, community organizations, and local initiatives, while advancing community development and resident leadership. The Council-adopted Association of Community Centre Expansion Framework and Implementation Strategy affirm the Association of Community Centre model as essential community development infrastructure that advances neighbourhood resilience.
