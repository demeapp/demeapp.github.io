The official record

What the item proposed

This Motion seeks authorization to release Section 37 community benefits funds, in the amount of $350,000, to support Indigenous placekeeping capital improvements in Allan Gardens. A portion of this funding will be allocated to the Friends of Allan Gardens to support engagement with relevant Indigenous communities and organizations in collaboration with City staff and relevant partners. These groups will be actively engaged in co-stewardship and the co-development of a long-term vision and will select a community-identified name for the project, replacing the placeholder name included in this motion. Allan Gardens is a historical and contemporary gathering place for many Indigenous peoples and organizations. City Council authorize the General Manager, Parks and Recreation, to allocate $75,000 of the funds described in Part 1 above to Friends of Allan Gardens to support community engagement and related coordination for the Indigenous Placekeeping in Allan Gardens project, on terms and conditions satisfactory to the General Manager, Parks and Recreation, and in a form satisfactory to the City Solicitor. 3.
