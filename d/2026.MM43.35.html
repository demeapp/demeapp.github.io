What the item proposed

The Under Gardiner Public Realm Plan was developed as a collaboration between the City and The Bentway and was approved by Toronto City Council in April 2024 ( https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2024.IE12.5 ). The plan sets out a long-term vision for the 7km elevated Gardiner corridor, establishing new standards for safety, identity, amenity and ecology, and informing future planning of the public realm from Dufferin Street to the Don Valley Parkway. The City is continuing its partnership with The Bentway to implement the Under Gardiner Public Realm Plan by working with BIAs, institutions, and businesses and landowners to advance the Under Gardiner Public Realm Plan, including the goal of removing barriers and making new connections along the elevated Gardiner corridor through public realm improvement projects.
