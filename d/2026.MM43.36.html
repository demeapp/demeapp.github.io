What the item proposed

Transportation Services staff applied for a Green Municipal Fund Grant from the Federation of Canadian Municipalities to support the implementation of the approved road safety project on Ingleton Boulevard as part of the Alton Towers Neighbourhood Connection project ( 2025.IE26.6 ). Stated project goals are improving road safety for everyone, including school children, and enhanced access to schools. Promoting active school travel is also part of the Vision Zero Road Safety Plan. Alton Towers community representatives and school Principals in the area identified the need for additional school travel planning and community engagement, as well as capital enhancements to Ingleton Boulevard including widened sidewalks, improved street crossings, speed humps and on-road cycle tracks.
