Re-opening and Amending Item 2026.TE31.38 - 49 Ontario Street (Phase 1) - Construction Staging Area - by Councillor Chris Moise, seconded by Councillor Dianne Saxe

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Procedural: council was asked to shift the 49 Ontario Street staging dates to September 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027 and raise the rate from $4.25 to $4.50.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council, at its meeting on April 22 and 23, 2026, adopted Item 2026.TE31.38 , and in so doing, authorized a construction staging area on Adelaide Street East and on Ontario Street from April 23, 2026 to September 30, 2027 inclusive. Since then, the developer has revised the construction timeline. The construction staging area on Adelaide Street East is currently in place until September 30, 2027. The construction staging area on Ontario Street will be required from September 1, 2026, to September 30, 2027, inclusive. The purpose of this motion is to request City Council to authorize an amendment to the dates of the construction staging area for Ontario Street. Requires Re-opening: City Council Item 2026.TE31.38 (April 22 and 23, 2026)

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend its previous decision on Item 2026.TE31.38 by: a. deleting in Part 2 the words “April 23, 2026” and replacing them with “September 1, 2026”, so that Part 2 reads as follows: 2. City Council authorize the closure of a 5.5 metre wide portion of the east sidewalk and east boulevard allowance on Ontario Street, between a point 31.5 metres north of Adelaide Street East and a point 32.5 metres further north, from September 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027, inclusive. b. deleting in Part 3 the words “$4.25” and replacing them with “$4.50”, and adding to the end the words “from September 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027”, so that Part 3 reads as follows: 3.
