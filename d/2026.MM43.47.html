What the item proposed

The Rosewood Pollinator Community Garden is a community stewardship initiative operated by the Scarborough Rosewood Community Association on City-owned land in the Agincourt North community. The garden provides native pollinator habitat while promoting environmental education, biodiversity, volunteer stewardship, and community participation. The continued maintenance and development of the garden requires modest capital support for on-site garden infrastructure, including water storage capacity and related improvements that support the operation and sustainability of the pollinator garden. In 2025, City Council enacted By-law 1144-2025, which amended the zoning for the lands at McCowan Road and Finch Avenue. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Toronto Water, working directly with the Scarborough Rosewood Community Association, to identify and implement a permanent solution to provide ongoing and reliable access to water for the operation and maintenance of the Rosewood Pollinator Community Garden, using Toronto Water’s existing authority.
