What the item proposed

This Motion seeks Council authority to release $95,000 in Section 37 funds to the Laneway Project for the purpose of funding streetscape and laneway improvements in the area near Ryerson Ave. An application from the Friends of Alexandra Park group through the Laneway Project coLabs program is proposing to improve and beautify the laneway located adjacent to the Alexandra Hotel and Ryerson Public School. It is an important shared corridor for people with diverse needs and lived experiences. The laneway was identified by the community as a priority site. It is physically well-suited for activation, wider than most downtown laneways and in relatively good condition, but it is currently underused and impacted by several ongoing challenges including poor lighting, frequent illegal dumping, graffiti and lack of consistent stewardship.
