What the item proposed

There is no responsibility of municipal government more fundamental than the safety of the people it serves. No measure of a city's success matters if residents do not feel safe in their homes, on their streets, and on their transit system. The Toronto Police Service Board is the civilian body that employs and holds to account the Chief of Police, sets the objectives and policies that govern the Toronto Police Service, and approves the largest single line item in the City of Toronto’s budget, which in 2026 stands at $1.43 billion. Every day, Toronto’s front-line officers have a demanding and important job to keep our city safe. The city’s leadership must be seized with providing them with the resources and oversight they require.
