The official record

What the item proposed

The South Asian Cultural Health Association for Youth and Seniors operates the TRUPTI Social Café at Milliken Park Field House in its snack bar space under a licence agreement. A commercial kitchen fire suppression system is required to support safe and code-compliant on-site food preparation and enhance the facility’s capacity to deliver community food programming. Section 37 funds secured from the development at 5789 to 5951 Steeles Avenue East were originally allocated toward the expansion of Milliken Park Community Recreation Centre. A portion of these funds remains unspent. What was decided
