What the item proposed

Toronto’s Tamil community is nearing a historic moment. Thanks to relentless hard work, the dream of beginning construction on its community centre is fast becoming a reality. Once completed, the Tamil Community Centre will be a multi-purpose facility serving the Tamil community and the broader Morningside Heights and northeast Scarborough communities. The centre will include a gymnasium, indoor walking track, auditorium, library, museum and archival space, multipurpose programming rooms, outdoor sports facilities, and space for social, educational, cultural and mental health programming. The Tamil Community Centre represents a generational opportunity to support Toronto's vibrant Tamil community, and Scarborough more broadly.
