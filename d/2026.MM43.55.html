The official record

What the item proposed

Liberty Village is a unique Downtown neighbourhood generally bounded by King Street West and Douro Street to the north, the rail tracks to the south, Dufferin Street to the west and Strachan Avenue to the east. The eastern portion of Liberty Village is characterized by mixed-used residential developments with retail, townhouses, and residential towers. The western portion has a concentration of industrial heritage buildings which contribute to the neighbourhood’s distinct character and support a vibrant concentration of commercial, retail and cultural uses. Much of Liberty Village falls within an Area of Parkland Need as defined by the City’s Parkland Strategy. Parkland provision rates are expected to decline over time in this neighbourhood as residential growth continues. Limited inventory and competitive market conditions have posed challenges for acquiring new parkland and expanding…

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the General Manager, Parks and Recreation and the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management to explore all opportunities to pursue the expansion of new parkland in Liberty Village, including the acquisition of unused or underutilized nearby adjacent properties to City-owned lands.
