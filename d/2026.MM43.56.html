What the item proposed

A significant contributing factor to congestion in the City of Toronto is due to construction and maintenance work to repair, maintain or expand our transit, roads and regional transportation assets. This spring and summer, residents and visitors to the City had contrasting experiences when dealing with congestion. During Mother’s Day, the City was difficult to navigate due to sections of the Toronto Transit Commission’s subway network and Don Valley Parkway being concurrently closed for maintenance, road closures to accommodate cultural events and sporting competitions, as well as closures on the UP Express operated by Metrolinx. On the other hand, during the six FIFA 2026 match games held in Toronto the City implemented an incredibly successful transportation and mobility plan through improved coordination of local and regional transit services, road and highway closures, and so on.…
