Starting planning curb management for hire vehicles and self-driving cars, with an update in 2027.

Official title: Planning for Curbside Management when Autonomous Vehicles Arrive - by Councillor Dianne Saxe, seconded by Councillor Rachel Chernos Lin

The official record

What the item proposed

Many Toronto Councillors already receive complaints about inadequate curbside loading space for both passengers and cargo. These complaints may be about to become much more intense. Around the world, autonomous vehicles are increasingly being used to deliver passengers and cargo. These vehicles can offer safety and cost advantages, but they can also exacerbate congestion and the competition for curb space. Experts are therefore recommending that cities should start to plan how they will manage the curbside demands of autonomous vehicles.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services, in consultation with the Chief Congestion Officer and Executive Director, Strategic Capital Coordination Office, to start developing a curbside management strategy that takes vehicles for hire and autonomous vehicles, and the related City-wide spatial distribution of operational supports, including vehicle staging, storage, charging, servicing and empty vehicle movements, into account, and to include a progress update in a Transportation Services report to the Infrastructure and Environment Committee in 2027.
