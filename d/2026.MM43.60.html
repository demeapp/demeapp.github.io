The official record

What the item proposed

Daniel Stopnicki was a beloved resident of Eglinton-Lawrence who was tragically killed in April 2026. He was a beloved husband, a cherished son, a dear brother, and a great companion. He was a dedicated teacher and mentor who was beloved and respected by those who knew him. Daniel is remembered as an enthusiastic and ambitious innovator in real time interactive media, leading numerous exciting companies as Chief Technology and Innovation Officer, and as a dedicated teacher and mentor to many students during his time as a professor and coordinator at Seneca Polytechnic and Centennial College. In his own neighbourhood, Daniel was a beloved and admired member of his community and was a local hero championing a grassroots campaign to create an off-leash park for his human and canine friends. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the General Manager, Parks and Recreation, in consultation with the local Councillor’s office, to review and present recommendations for the renaming of a City-owned park asset in honour of Daniel Stopnicki in alignment with the City's Property Naming Policy, to North York Community Council in 2027.
