The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this Motion is to allocate $1,455,897.98 in Section 37 funds for the development of seventy new affordable rental housing units and fifty-eight new rent-controlled units at 1240, 1246, 1250 Weston Road in Ward 5. The owner, the Learning Enrichment Foundation will also be providing twenty-one rental replacement units. The Section 37 funds are being paid to the City from the development at 8 to 16 Locust Street. The development at 1240, 1246, 1250 Weston Road will be built by Learning Enrichment Foundation. The funds will be provided to the proponent through a municipal capital facility agreement, with disbursements following key construction and occupancy milestones.
