The official record

What the item proposed

Merton Street, from Yonge Street to Mount Pleasant Road, is experiencing a convergence of pressures driven by concentrated construction activity and shifting traffic patterns. The street supports a diverse and unique mix of residential, and businesses uses, including facilities for seniors, individuals with disabilities and a daycare. Situated close to Davisville TTC Station, the Beltline Trail and Mount Pleasant Cemetery, Merton Street also serves as a key connector to local green spaces, cycling infrastructure, and transit. As mobility patterns along Merton Street continue to shift, residents have increasingly raised concerns with pedestrian safety, citing narrow sidewalks, limited sightlines, unsafe mid-block crossings, and a need for enhanced wayfinding. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services, in consultation with the Executive Director, Development Review, Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, local residents, and the local Councillors’ office to report back in the fourth quarter of 2027 with recommendations for streetscape improvements along Merton Street, between Yonge Street and Mount Pleasant Road, in line with the vision identified in Midtown Parks and Public Realm Plan (2018) and Midtown Public Realm Implementation Strategy (2022).
