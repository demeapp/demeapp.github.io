What the item proposed

Toronto is in a housing crisis. The City has committed to a target of 285,000 new homes by 2031 and must deliver the municipal services that support housing approvals in a timely, predictable and accountable manner. This is especially true for affordable housing, where delays add costs that non-profit builders cannot absorb, and for the parks, community facilities and other public benefits that developments deliver alongside new homes. Significant progress has been made speeding up approvals in recent years. In April 2024, the City launched the Development Review Division, consolidating approximately 400 staff from across the organization into a single division with a clear mandate. As reported in EX25.2 , statutory review timelines for complex applications improved by over 80 percent compared to the previous five-year average.
