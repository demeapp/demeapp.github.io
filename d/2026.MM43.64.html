The official record

What the item proposed

This motion is to provide community benefits secured through section 37 to Toronto Metropolitan University for the purposes of implementing their Campus Master Plan . Starting with their former 2008 Campus Master Plan, Toronto Metropolitan University has continued to expand and create an environment for students that feels within–not apart from–the larger downtown. Funds from the 363 to 391 Yonge Street section 37 agreement collected for this very purpose will be directed to Toronto Metropolitan University to allow them to fund new streetscape and public realm improvements.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council increase the 2026 Operating Budget for Non-Program on a one-time basis by $1,837,974.68 gross, $0 net, (Cost Centre: NP2161) for the transfer of funds to Toronto Metropolitan University to fund the construction costs of Public Realm Improvement Projects proposed for portions of Toronto Metropolitan University main Campus, fully funded by Section 37 community benefits from the development 363-391 Yonge Street and 3 Gerrard Street East (Source Account: XR3026-3701436). 2. City Council direct that the $1,837,974.68 be forwarded to Toronto Metropolitan University upon the signing of an Undertaking by Toronto Metropolitan University governing the use of the funds and the financial reporting requirements. 3.
