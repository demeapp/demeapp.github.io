Cutting off city waste pickup at 29 Church Street, where chronic overflow and rodents persist.

Official title: 29 Church Street - Discontinuing City of Toronto Residential Collection Services - by Councillor Frances Nunziata, seconded by Councillor Paula Fletcher

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this motion is to address longstanding waste management issues at 29 Church Street, York, which have persisted since 2013 and have progressively worsened over time. The property is a nine-storey residential apartment building containing 63 dwelling units. As of July 2024, the building received a RentSafeTO score of 82 out of 100, resulting in a "Needs Improvement" classification. The building's designated waste storage area is located along Church Street, within approximately 30 metres of residential properties to the west and north. The existing waste management infrastructure is insufficient to accommodate the volume of waste generated by the building.
