The official record

What the item proposed

Over the last 7 years, City Staff have worked closely with Metrolinx on matters related to the redevelopment of the Christie’s site at Park Lawn Road and Lake Shore Boulevard, the development of a Transportation Master Plan for the broader area, and the design of the planned Park Lawn GO Station. During the Christie’s planning study work in 2019, City Staff worked with Metrolinx to link the redevelopment of the site to the delivery of the GO Station. This collaboration was important for the City to achieve a positive outcome in negotiations with the developer. Additionally, the redevelopment of the site, along with the development in the broader Humber Bay Shores area, were important factors in building a business case for the GO Station. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council, further to its decision on item 2025.EX27.4, request the Provincial government and Metrolinx to form a working group with City Officials, Staff, the Ministries of Infrastructure, Transportation, Municipal Affairs and Housing, and Finance Officials, and other related parties, to examine ways to advance the development of the Park Lawn GO Station and other transit improvements in Humber Bay Shores, recognizing the existing growth and development in the area.
