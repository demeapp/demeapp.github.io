Studying removing the no-left-turn rule at Avenue Road and Eglinton Avenue West.

Official title: Avenue Road and Eglinton Avenue West - Traffic Amendments - by Councillor Josh Matlow, seconded by Councillor Mike Colle

The official record

What the item proposed

This motion requests that the General Manager, Transportation Services, report back in the first quarter of 2027 on recommendations to rescind the northbound left-turn prohibition at Avenue Road and Eglinton Avenue West. Residents have consistently raised concerns that this longstanding turn prohibition has contributed to traffic incursion by concentrating drivers looking to turn on Eglinton Avenue West onto neighbouring streets, including Oriole Parkway. I am asking that City Council support my motion directing Transportation Services to complete the required analysis to determine if this turn prohibition can be removed without overly delaying traffic along Eglinton Avenue West and Avenue Road.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services to report back in the first quarter of 2027 on recommendations to rescind the existing northbound left-turn prohibition at the intersection of Avenue Road and Eglinton Avenue West.
