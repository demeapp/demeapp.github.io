What the item proposed

Toronto adopted the city-wide Multi-Tenant Houses framework in December 2022, effective March 31, 2024, to establish consistent safety and licensing standards, preserve deeply affordable housing, and provide a pathway for previously unlicensed multi-tenant houses to enter legal, regulated operation. Since implementation, Municipal Licensing and Standards, in collaboration with the Housing Secretariat, Toronto Fire Services, Toronto Building and other City divisions, has undertaken significant work to implement the framework, process licence applications, support operators through the licensing process, and coordinate compliance and enforcement activities. As implementation continues, City Council would benefit from additional information on how key components of the framework are operating in practice.
