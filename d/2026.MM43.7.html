The official record

What the item proposed

Councillor Saxe seeks authority to provide a non-profit community land trust, Toronto Chinatown Land Trust , with Section 37 funds in the amount of $700,000 to help purchase a mixed-use building in Ward 11 University-Rosedale, 81 Huron Street, to be operated as an anchor for the recently designated West Chinatown Cultural District . Toronto Chinatown Land Trust is a community-supported non-profit devoted to preserving community space, affordable housing and commercial space in West Chinatown. The demand for culturally appropriate affordable housing in Chinatown far exceeds the supply. Worse, existing affordable housing and retail space is being lost.
