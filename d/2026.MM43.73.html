The official record

What the item proposed

This report presents the findings of a community engagement process undertaken in Spring 2026 to reimagine the future of the former Ontario Science Centre site at 770 Don Mills Road. Informed by this public feedback, the report puts forward a community-informed vision for the site's revitalization, re-opening, and return to public use. This report and engagement process has been led by Save Ontario’s Science Centre, Moriyama Teshima Architects, Councillor Josh Matlow, and Councillor Jon Burnside, and was carried out under the direction of motions approved by City Council including 2024.MM22.3 . In April 2023, the Provincial government announced its decision to relocate the Ontario Science Centre to the waterfront, and in June 2024 it abruptly closed the building. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the City Manager to convene a meeting with representatives of the Province of Ontario, Infrastructure Ontario, the Government of Canada, and the Toronto and Region Conservation Authority, the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO, and relevant community stakeholders, to discuss the future of the Ontario Science Centre site at 770 Don Mills Road and to collaboratively determine a sustainable future use for the site that ensures public benefit and access, consistent with the findings of the “Reimagining the Future of the former Ontario Science Centre report” in Attachment 1 to Member Motion MM43.73.
