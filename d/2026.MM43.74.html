The official record

What the item proposed

BTS is coming to North York, bringing their global phenomenon and passionate ARMY with them. Fans will be given "Permission to Dance" as they gather along Yonge Street, providing North York with an opportunity to showcase Korea Town North that is hosting the largest Korean Festival in Canada at Mel Lastman Square over the same weekend as the BTS concerts happening in North York. BTS proves that "Life Goes On" after their military service with their Arirang World Tour with music that inspires millions and reminds us to "Stay Gold." Fans can make their way along honorary "BTS Boulevard" to take photos with commemorative street signs and enjoy a plethora of Korean restaurants while celebrating one of the most influential musical acts of our time. It's our way of saying "You Never Walk Alone," welcoming BTS and ARMY to Korea Town North with open arms, celebrating the cultural connections…
