What the item proposed

This motion seeks approval to allocate Section 37 funds in the amount of $48,732.87, to Transportation Services to undertake beautification of the traffic island located at Oakwood Avenue and Vaughan Road, in collaboration with the Oakwood Village Business Improvement Area. Planned road work on Vaughan Road in 2028 has provided the City with an opportunity to redesign the traffic island at Oakwood Avenue and Vaughan Road to improve safety and enhance public space. The closure of the Oakwood northbound right slip lane will expand the island and allow for the installation of new public amenities and green space as requested by the community through the Oakwood Village Streets Plan public consultation.
