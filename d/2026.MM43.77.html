The official record

What the item proposed

On June 4, 2026, Prime Minister Mark Carney and Minister of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Innovation Evan Solomon launched "AI for All," Canada's new national artificial intelligence strategy. The strategy commits over $2.3 billion in federal spending over five years, anchored in three guiding principles – Trust, Opportunity, and Sovereignty. Through this strategy, the federal government aims to strengthen Canada’s AI sovereignty by supporting globally competitive Canadian AI companies to ensure that AI systems are developed and governed on Canadian terms. In November 2025, the Government of Canada published the Digital Sovereignty Framework, an approach for improving digital readiness at the Government of Canada. This framework defines digital sovereignty as the Government of Canada’s ability to exercise autonomy over its digital infrastructure, data, and intellectual property,…

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Government of Canada to engage with the City of Toronto, including Technology Services, the Chief Procurement Officer, the Chief Information Security Officer, and the City Clerk to identify opportunities to advance municipal digital sovereignty in alignment with Canada’s National Artificial Intelligence Strategy, including dedicated funding to support municipalities in transitioning to Canadian-based technology and software solutions. 2. City Council forward this Item to the Federation of Canadian Municipalities for their consideration and advocacy.
