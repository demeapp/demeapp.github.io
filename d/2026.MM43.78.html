Advancing Local Priorities in Thorncliffe Park - by Councillor Rachel Chernos Lin, seconded by Mayor Olivia Chow

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Having City Planning set priorities for Thorncliffe Park — better streets, parks, public spaces, and walking and cycling links — as the Ontario Line arrives. The aim is that the growing neighbourhood gains housing, jobs and community spaces along with the new transit.

The official record

What the item proposed

Thorncliffe Park is a densely populated and culturally diverse community and has long served as an important area of arrival for newcomers to Toronto. Currently, the community is undergoing significant change associated with major transit investments, including the Ontario Line and the planned Transit-Oriented Community surrounding the future Thorncliffe Park Station. City staff and the local Councillor’s office have recently engaged with the Urban Land Institute and local stakeholders to consider how the City can optimize the benefits of these investments, and several opportunities have emerged from these discussions around delivering new housing, jobs, community facilities, parks, public spaces, and improving connections within and beyond the neighbourhood.
