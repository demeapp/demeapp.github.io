Exploring Alternative Options to Clarify Complex Intersections - by Councillor Rachel Chernos Lin, seconded by Councillor Dianne Saxe

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Looking for clearer options for confusing intersections like Bayview Avenue and Bayview Heights Drive, where drivers cannot see the no-block zone and keep blocking turns. Clearer signs or layouts would cut crash risk.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Maintaining clarity and understanding of road rules is essential to ensuring safety for all road users. In some areas of the City, road configurations make this challenging. In Don Valley West, the intersection of Bayview Avenue and Bayview Heights Drive is signed “Do Not Block the Intersection” for northbound traffic – however, the intersection is commonly blocked as the area referred to by the sign is not immediately obvious. This makes it nearly impossible for drivers looking to turn north onto Bayview Avenue from Bayview Heights Drive to find a gap, and necessitates drivers to act quickly when a gap does appear, increasing the risk of accidents. On a stretch of road which has already seen several serious collisions in recent years, the present condition is untenable. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services, to undertake a jurisdictional scan for alternative Highway Traffic Act-compliant pavement markings and other options utilized in Ontario municipalities to denote ambiguous intersections for the purpose of increasing driver safety and awareness and to prevent “box blocking”, and report back to the Infrastructure and Environment Committee on findings in the first quarter of 2027.
