The official record

What the item proposed

Allan Gardens is one of Toronto's oldest and most cherished public spaces. Open to the public since 1860, it is a rare downtown commons that has remained open and accessible to all Torontonians across generations, and holds deep cultural and civic significance for the city. As outlined in the Allan Gardens’ Refresh: A Vision Document for Allan Gardens (2017) and the Growing with the City: An Ecosystem of Initiatives for Allan Gardens (2023), a comprehensive vision has been established to expand and modernize the park's conservatory infrastructure and outdoor garden spaces. The proposed Greenhouse Link Building, advanced by the Friends of Allan Gardens, would double the conservatory's footprint from its current 16,000 square feet to over 32,000 square feet, positioning it among the largest conservatories in North America.
