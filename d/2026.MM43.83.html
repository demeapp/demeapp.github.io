What the item proposed

The Church-Wellesley Village Business Improvement Area sits at the geographic and cultural heart of Canada's largest LGBTQ2S+ neighbourhood. Known as The Village, this vibrant and diverse community is bordered by Bloor Street to the north, Carlton Street to the south, Jarvis Street to the east, and Yonge Street to the west. The Business Improvement Area itself is bound by Gloucester Street to the north and Wood Street to the south, with its east-west boundaries encompassing the immediate mixed-use and residential properties along Church Street. For decades, The Village has served as a gathering place for diverse communities, a primary point of contact for tourists, and a welcoming destination for LGBTQ2S+ newcomers to Toronto.
