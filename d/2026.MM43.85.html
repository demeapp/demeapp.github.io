Advancing the Bridletowne Neighbourhood Centre/Community Hub - by Councillor Nick Mantas, seconded by Mayor Olivia Chow

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Moving ahead with the long-planned Bridletowne Neighbourhood Centre by transferring the City-owned 5.2-acre site at 1355 Bridletowne Circle — bought for about $5.9 million in 2011 — to the YMCA of Greater Toronto at nominal cost.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Bridletowne Neighbourhood Centre/Community Hub is a long-standing City-building initiative that will bring much-needed community space, programs and services to the Finch-Warden/Steeles-L’Amoreaux/Bridletowne area. For more than a decade, City Council has supported the use of the City-owned lands at 1355 Bridletowne Circle to create a multi-service community hub. The City acquired the property in 2011 for approximately $5.9 million, and the proposed nominal transfer of the 5.2-acre site to the YMCA of Greater Toronto represents a significant City contribution toward unlocking long-planned community infrastructure in an area of significant need. While Scarborough Health Network is no longer advancing its component of the project, the YMCA of Greater Toronto remains committed to moving the project forward with United Way Greater Toronto and other community partners.
