Amending Item 2018.TE34.48 - 114 and 120 Church Street and 59 Richmond Street East - Amendment of Section 37 Agreement to Address Corner Rounding Property Rights Transfer - by Councillor Chris Moise, seconded by Deputy Mayor Ausma Malik

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: City Council was asked to fix the Section 37 agreement for the 45-storey building at 114 and 120 Church Street and 59 Richmond Street East so the corner-rounding land transfer works as intended. The approved building does not change.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On July 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 and 30, 2018, City Council adopted the settlement offer for the appealed Zoning By-law Amendment application (17 253844 STE 28 OZ) at 114 & 120 Church Street and 59 Richmond Street East allowing a 149.5-metre (45 storeys, excluding mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building. The Council approval (Item 2018.TE34.48 - 114 and 120 Church Street and 59 Richmond Street East - Zoning Amendment Application - Request for Direction Report) secured a set of Section 37 Planning Act Community Benefits. The existing Section 37 Agreement, registered on title to the lands as Instrument Number AT5813856, currently requires that the Owner convey to the City, at the Owner’s sole expense, a 5.0 metre corner rounding at Church Street and Lombard Street, the ("Corner Rounding"), to the satisfaction of the City Solicitor. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend its previous decision on Item 2018.TE34.48 by deleting Part 3.e.viii: Recommendation to be deleted : 3.e.viii. the owner provides a conveyance of lands to the City consisting of a 5.0-metre corner rounding at Church Street and Lombard Street; and adopt instead the following: viii. the Owner provides a pedestrian clearway easement of 5.0 metre corner rounding at the Church Street and Lombard Street corner of the property, being Part 13 on Plan 66R-34868, together with right of support; being Part 14 on Plan 66R-34868. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor and City staff to take all necessary and reasonable actions to implement City Council's decision.
