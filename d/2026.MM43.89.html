Authority to Accept Donations Raised Through Fundraising Campaign for Moss Park Arena Improvements - by Councillor Chris Moise, seconded by Councillor Paula Fletcher

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Letting the City accept donations raised to improve Moss Park Arena, where interior renovations are planned from June 2027 to May 2028. The motion cites figures of $300,000, $238,000, $25,000 and $50,000.

The official summary names a figure of $300,000.

What the item proposed

The City is making improvements to Moss Park Arena’s interior, including the washroom, boardroom, snack bar, pro-shop, Zamboni room and multipurpose rooms. Construction at the City-owned facility is expected to take place from June 2027 to May 2028. Moss Park Arena is one of eight arenas in Toronto that are operated by volunteer Arena Boards of Management (Arena Boards), which are City agencies. The board-run arenas provide indoor ice sport facilities along with programming that can include hockey, figure skating and leisure skating. The Arena Board’s mandate includes: a.
