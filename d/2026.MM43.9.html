2455 St. Clair Avenue West - The Class 4 Noise Area Designation - by Councillor Frances Nunziata, seconded by Councillor Paula Fletcher

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Naming 2455 St. Clair Avenue West a Class 4 noise area so rail noise is managed for the new six-storey building's residents. The approval already required a peer-reviewed rail noise and air quality study.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On February 26, 2026, the Etobicoke York Panel of the Committee of Adjustment approved an application for a minor variance, subject to the conditions, to permit a new six-storey mixed-use building with ten residential units and retail on `the ground floor on the lands municipally known as 2455 St. Clair Avenue West (Application No: A0400/25EYK). As per the Community Planning staff’s recommendation, the Committee of Adjustment Decision included a condition that, due to the site's proximity to the rail yard and rail facilities, a Rail Noise and Vibration Impact Study and Air Quality Study shall be completed by a certified professional engineer and peer-reviewed by a third-party to identify any adverse effects from noise, vibration, and emissions, and required mitigation to prevent or mitigate same. A Rail Noise and Vibration Impact Study and Air Quality Addendum Letter were prepared in…

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council classify 2455 St. Clair Avenue West as a Class 4 Noise Area pursuant to Publication NPC-300 (Environmental Noise Guideline - Stationary and Transportation Sources - Approval and Planning). 2. City Council forward a copy of the City Council Decision to the Ministry of Environment, Conservation and Parks.
