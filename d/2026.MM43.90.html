The official record

What the item proposed

Since the Province first announced their desire to expand the Island airport, we have demanded to see the plans and worked to understand the potential impact it would have on our city. Impacts on new housing, new parks, air and water quality, congestion, wildlife and more. We also sought to learn who would benefit - in particular, if Wall Street bankers at JP Morgan stood to profit. Despite the Toronto Port Authority hiding plans from Torontonians, going so far as asking City officials to sign non-disclosure agreements, the Province passed legislation to expropriate City land. Little Norway Park and the entirety of the Toronto Islands, including Hanlan’s Point Beach, is being taken away from the people of Toronto. City Council has formally opposed this unacceptable overreach and land grab. The Federal government has since shared they would not support the expansion of the Island…

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council demand that the Province of Ontario give back the City of Toronto’s land at Toronto Islands and Little Norway Park by repealing Bill 110, the Building Billy Bishop Airport Act. 2. City Council demand that the Province of Ontario return the City of Toronto to the Tripartite Agreement governing Billy Bishop Airport.
