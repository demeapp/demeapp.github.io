Weekend and Holiday Standing Prohibition on Barkdene Hills - by Councillor Parthi Kandavel, seconded by Councillor Josh Matlow

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Banning standing on Barkdene Hills, a residential street near the Scarborough Bluffs, on weekends and holidays in spring and summer. Parked cars on both sides block traffic, sightlines, and access for buses, emergency vehicles and waste trucks.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Barkdene Hills is a residential street near the Scarborough Bluffs that experiences increased vehicle traffic and parking demand during the spring and summer months, particularly on weekends and public holidays. Residents have raised concerns that vehicles parked along both sides of the street can restrict traffic flow, reduce visibility, create congestion, and impede access for residents, emergency services, waste collection vehicles, and other municipal services. Barkdene Hills is also used as a Toronto Transit Commission bus route. As the proposed parking regulation would affect a roadway on which the Toronto Transit Commission operates, the matter is being directed to City Council for consideration. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit standing from 9:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., Saturday, Sunday, and public holidays from May 1 to September 30 of each year, on the north side of Barkdene Hills, from Brimley Road South to Brooklawn Avenue.
