Toronto City Council · Decision

Amending Item 2025.EX23.3 - Metrolinx Ontario Line - Temporary Lane Closures on the Don Valley Parkway and F.G. Gardiner Expressway and Temporary Delegation of Authority - by Councillor Paula Fletcher, seconded by Councillor Frances Nunziata

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: City Council was asked to allow extra overnight lane closures on the Don Valley Parkway and the Gardiner Expressway in August 2026 so Metrolinx can finish Ontario Line bridge work. Drivers would face more overnight lane closures.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council, at its meeting on May 21 and 22, 2025, adopted Item 2025.EX23.3, entitled “Metrolinx Ontario Line – Temporary Lane Closures on the Don Valley Parkway and F.G. Gardiner Expressway and Temporary Delegation of Authority”. Recommendations 6, 7, 8e, and 8f approved temporary overnight lane closures for up to two days between August 1 and August 31, 2026 facilitate the installation of access scaffolding for the Lower Don Bridge. Additional overnight lane closures in the month of August 2026 are now required to safely complete the previously approved work due to the complexity of the construction activities, site constraints, and the sequencing required to install the access scaffolding over the Don Valley Parkway. Accordingly, this motion recommends amending Item 2025.EX23.3 to permit up to four overnight lane closures, rather than two, for each applicable closure configuration…

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend Item 2025.EX23.3 by deleting the words “two nights between August 1, 2026 and August 31, 2026” wherever they occur in Parts 6, 7, 8e, and 8f and inserting the words “four nights between August 1, 2026 and August 31, 2026”, so that those Parts now read as follows: 6. City Council amend City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 937, Temporary Closing of Highways, to delegate to the General Manager, Transportation Services, to facilitate the installation of access scaffolding in August 2026 and its removal in November 2026 related to the Ontario Line Lower Don Bridge, despite Section 937-4 of Code Chapter 937, the authority to temporarily close to vehicular traffic: a. the eastbound inside lane on the F.G. Gardiner Expressway, between Parliament Street and the Don Valley Parkway-F.G. Gardiner Expressway…
