Advancing the Pakistani Canadian Community Centre - by Mayor Olivia Chow, seconded by Councillor Jon Burnside

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Helping advance a Pakistani Canadian Community Centre for the more than 160,000 people of Pakistani origin in the GTA, concentrated in the city's east. The planned centre would be multicultural and serve all residents.

The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto is home to a vibrant Pakistani community, the largest in Canada. More than 160,000 residents of the Greater Toronto Area are of Pakistani origin. The Pakistani community has a significant population in the eastern part of the City. The community is currently underserved by cultural community facilities and dedicated spaces to support its large and growing population. The Pakistani Canadian Community Centre Project has been working diligently, together with intergovernmental partners, to advance a plan to deliver a dynamic, multicultural and multi-purpose centralized community center space that also serves residents of the City writ large. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the Executive Director, Social Development, Finance and Administration and the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, in consultation with the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO, to work with the Pakistani-Canadian Community and Cultural Centre (PC4) team and stakeholders from the Pakistani-Canadian community to explore potential partnerships for the development of a new community and cultural centre, including consideration of 770 Don Mills Road and other suitable City-owned lands in Toronto, with priority given to locations that are in or near areas with a high concentration of Pakistani-Canadian residents.
