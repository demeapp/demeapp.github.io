What the item proposed

St. Edward Catholic School, located at 1 Botham Road in Willowdale, serves students, families, and the surrounding community as part of the Toronto Catholic District School Board. The schoolyard is an important outdoor space that supports recreation, physical activity, and community connection for students and local residents. The school has identified significant challenges with the existing schoolyard, including poor drainage and uneven grading, which result in the field becoming waterlogged and unusable following rainfall. These conditions limit safe outdoor activities for students and reduce opportunities for community recreation. St. Edward Catholic School and its Catholic School Parent Council have proposed improvements to create a properly drained, levelled, and multi-use artificial turf field that would provide a safe and accessible space year-round.
