Authority to Accept Donations Raised Through Fundraising Campaign for the Restoration of the Sir Casimir Gzowski Monument - by Mayor Olivia Chow, seconded by Councillor Gord Perks

Accepting $50,000 raised by donors to restore the Sir Casimir Gzowski Monument, built in 1968 in a city park of the same name. The monument is part of the City's Public Art and Monuments Collection.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Sir Casimir Gzowski Monument was constructed in 1968 through a close collaboration between the Ontario Institute of Engineers, Toronto’s Polish community, and the City of Toronto. Situated in a City of Toronto park of the same name, this impressive memorial commemorates the life and work of Sir Casimir Gzowski (1813 – 1898), who was knighted by Queen Victoria in 1890 for his vast contributions to Ontario and Canada. The monument is an asset in the City's Public Art and Monuments Collection, managed by Economic Development and Culture. The monument’s substantial structure, designed by architect Richard R. D’Wonnik, symbolizes the major facets of Sir Casimir Gzowski’s professional life; his work as an engineer, in the military, and as a statesman. Features such as railway tracks and I-beams atop the concrete tower allude to the bridges and railroads Sir Gzowski built across Ontario.…

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, in consultation with the General Manager, Parks and Recreation, to engage with the Sir Casimir Gzowski Monument Revitalization Committee regarding a fundraising campaign led by the Committee for the Sir Casimir Gzowski Monument restoration project. 2. City Council authorize the General Manager, Economic Development and Culture, to accept donations raised through the fundraising campaign and collected by the City, and to apply the donated funds toward the Sir Casimir Gzowski Monument restoration project, in compliance with the City of Toronto Donation Policy.
