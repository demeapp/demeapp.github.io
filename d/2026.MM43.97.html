Parking Amendments to Accommodate Emergency Loading for University Health Network, 750 Dundas Street West - by Councillor Dianne Saxe, seconded by Councillor Mike Colle

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Changing parking rules in front of 750 Dundas Street West, a University Health Network clinic across from Toronto Western Hospital, so the curb stays clear for ambulances and a curb cut is added. The motion references a $4.50 figure tied to the parking regulation.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At all hours, paramedics and ambulances must frequently attend 750 Dundas Street West, a medical facility managed by the University Hospital Network. It is located across the street from the Toronto Western Hospital. The curbside in front of the building is currently signed No Parking/afternoon rush-hour No Stopping, and there is no curb cut. Paramedics have urgently requested that the bylaw regulations at this location be amended to ensure that ambulances have room to stop there whenever actively picking up or dropping off patients. That is, the curb must remain clear of parked or stopped vehicles at all times. They have also requested a curb cut, so they are not forced to lift loaded stretchers over the curb.
