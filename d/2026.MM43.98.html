Authorizing Development Charge Refunds for Condominium Projects Converting to Purpose-built Rental Tenure - by Mayor Olivia Chow, seconded by Councillor Nick Mantas

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Allowing development charge refunds when a condo project switches to purpose-built rental before people move in, so it pays the lower rental rate. Rental projects pay lower charges than condos.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 415, Development of Land, allows the City to recover additional development charges where a purpose-built rental development subsequently converts to condominium ownership. However, where a project that has already paid development charges converts from a condominium to purpose-built rental prior to occupancy, there is currently no authority to charge the corresponding rate and issue a refund. Residential developments in the City of Toronto are subject to different development charge rates depending on tenure. Rental developments are eligible for statutory and discretionary discounts, with the discounted amount depending on the number of bedrooms in a unit. Through Bill 17, residential development charges are now payable at occupancy. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer, in the event development charges are paid early at the rates applicable to non-rental residential development and the units are later determined to be Purpose Built Rental prior to first partial occupancy, to refund the difference between the development charge amount paid and the development charge amount payable for Purpose Built Rental Units; the refund shall also include any corresponding adjustment to interest payable under the Development Charges Act, 1997, that would have applied had the dwelling unit or units qualified as Purpose Built Rental Units at the time the development charge was originally paid or payable.
