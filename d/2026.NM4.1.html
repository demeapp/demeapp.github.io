Selection of Candidates for Interview for the Administrative Penalty Tribunal

Adopted with changesMar 13, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: the Nominating Panel interviewed 26 candidates and suggest 13, including a chair, for the Administrative Penalty Tribunal.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Nominating Panel - Administrative Penalty must select candidates to interview for appointment to the Administrative Penalty Tribunal. Following the interviews, the Nominating Panel - Administrative Penalty Tribunal will recommend 13 candidates, including a Chair, to City Council, for appointment.

What was decided
