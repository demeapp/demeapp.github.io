120 Dallimore Circle - Addressing Changes to Parking Provisions, Community Bus Stop

AdoptedMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Limiting the no-stopping rule near the 120 Dallimore Circle bus stop to weekdays — freeing weekend parking — goes to North York Community Council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Multiple residents have contacted my office regarding a new community bus stop in front of 120 Dallimore Circle. This stop removes approximately three on-street parking spaces to accommodate an accessible bus stop. While I understand the need for such a stop, the current implementation restricts on-street parking at all times through a “no stopping” bylaw, even though the community bus only operates Monday to Friday. Given the limited schedule of the community bus, I propose amending the by-law to apply “no stopping” restrictions only on weekdays. This change would allow visitors to park for up to three hours on weekends. Reasons for changing the current ‘no stopping at all times’ provision: - To allow for on-street parking during the weekend as the community bus stop will only be used Monday to Friday.

What was decided

City Council on March 25 and 26, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit standing, Monday to Friday, on the southside of Dallimore Circle, between a point 93 metres southwest of Haldenby Terrace (east intersection) and a point 21 metres further southwest.
