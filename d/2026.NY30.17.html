Tweaking the one-hour parking rule on Dunblaine Avenue near Avenue Road — to help local businesses — goes to North York Community Council.

Official title: Dunblaine Avenue, West of Avenue Road - Parking Regulation Changes

AdoptedFeb 18, 2026 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

After consulting with various businesses in the immediate vicinity in an effort to support them and the continued success and viability of the new Upper Avenue BIA (UBIA), I am proposing the following minor parking regulation changes that my office has consulted on with staff in Transportation Services.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect from 8:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, on the north side of Dunblaine Avenue, between Avenue Road and a point 77 metres west of Avenue Road, to be in effect between a point 36 metres west of Avenue Road to a point 41 metres further west. 2. Prohibited stopping at all times on the north side of Dunblaine Avenue, between Avenue Road and a point 17 metres west. 3. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 15 minutes from 8:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., daily, on the north side of Dunblaine Avenue, between a point 17 metres west of Avenue Road and a point 19 metres further west.
