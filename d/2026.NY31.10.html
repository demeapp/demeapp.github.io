North York Community Council decided whether the owner of 237 Lord Seaton Road may keep a fence that breaks height rules.

Official title: 237 Lord Seaton Road - Application for Fence Exemption

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 237 Lord Seaton Road has requested North York York Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447-1.2B(1) and Chapter 447-1.2C(1): B. Fence height. (1) No fence described in the following Table shall, when measured at any point along its length from the average grade level measured perpendicular to and one meter away on either side of the fence, exceed the height set out in the Table for the fence. Item Description of Fence Fence on Single or Multiple residential property Fence on non-residential property Hedge, shrub, or other vegetation that acts as fence 9 Any other fence 2.0 metres 2.5 metres No maximum The Applicant for 237 Lord Seaton Road is requesting an exemption for: - Erected fence constructed on private portion of the property; - Variable height of 3.28 meters to 3.63 metres; -…

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Granted the fence exemption for the property owner of 237 Lord Seaton Road; directed and required that the enclosure be maintained in good repair without alteration; and at such time as replacement of the enclosure is required that such installation will comply with Municipal Code Chapter 447, Fences, or its successor by-law.
