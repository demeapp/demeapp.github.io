The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends the appointment of one stakeholder representative to the North Toronto Memorial Arena Board.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Appointed Pak Hum as the stakeholder representative for the Avenue Road Eglinton Community Association (ARECA) to the North Toronto Memorial Arena Board, at pleasure of Council, for a term of office ending on April 20, 2030, and until a successor is appointed.
