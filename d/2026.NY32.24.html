Bradgate Road and Chatfield Drive - Parking By-law Amendment to Implement “No Parking” from 12 a.m. to 6 a.m.

Adopted with changesApr 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Banning overnight parking from 12:01 a.m. to 6:00 a.m. on Bradgate Road and Chatfield Drive — after about 70 percent of polled residents backed the ban — goes to North York Community Council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Bradgate Road and Chatfield Drive are two local roads in the Don Mills Neighbourhood of Ward 16. Currently, parking is allowed on both roads with the city-wide 3-hour limit and North York snow removal by-laws in place. Residents contacted my office with concerns of overnight parking all year round (12 a.m. to 6 a.m). After discussions and a poll, a majority of residents (approximately 70 percent) were in favour of prohibiting parking during these times. At the request of the community, I am making the following recommendations.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Prohibited parking from 1:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m., daily, on both sides of Bradgate Road, between Talwood Drive and the north end of Bradgate Road. 2. Prohibited parking from 1:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m., daily, on both sides of Chatfield Drive, between Bradgate Road (northeast intersection) and Bradgate Road (southwest intersection). 3. Rescinded the winter maintenance parking prohibition in effect from 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m., December 1st to March 31st on Bradgate Road, between Talwood Drive and the north end of Bradgate Road. 4. Rescinded the winter maintenance parking prohibition in effect from 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m., December 1st to March 31st on Chatfield Drive, between Bradgate Road (northeast intersection) and Bradgate Road (southwest intersection).
