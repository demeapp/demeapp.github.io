The official record

What the item proposed

This report requests that North York Community Council deny the request for a permit to remove one City-owned tree located at 103 Roslin Avenue. The applicant indicates the reason for requesting removal of the tree is due to nuisance seeds, fruit and sap resulting in bug infestations; as well as concern that the tree or branches have the potential to break creating a safety hazard. The littleleaf linden tree (Tilia cordata) in question measures 44 centimetre in diameter. The City's Tree By-laws do not support the removal of this tree as it is healthy and maintainable. The permit was denied, and the applicant is appealing the decision. Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision as to whether a permit may be issued when an applicant appeals the City's decision to deny a tree permit.

What was decided

North York Community Council took no action on 103 Roslin Avenue - Application to Remove a City Owned Tree, as all motions lost on a tie vote.
