213 Northwood Drive - Application for Fence Exemption

Adopted with changesMay 26, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Whether the owner of 213 Northwood Drive may be exempted from rear-yard fence height and pool-fence rules is up for North York Community Council to decide.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 213 Northwood Drive has requested North York Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: 1. City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 Section 447‑1.2B (1) Restrictions on fences; Height - Rear Yard - Maximum Allowable Height Under Table 1 (447‑1.2), for residential properties: Maximum height: 2.0 metres (6.5 Feet) 2. City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 Section 447‑1.3E (2) - Minimum Standards for Chain‑Link Fence Construction. The Code sets specific minimum construction standards for chain‑link fencing used as a swimming pool enclosure. Mesh Size Requirement for a pool on a single residential property: Maximum mesh opening: 38 millimetres (1.50 inches) The Applicant for 213 Northwood Drive is requesting an exemption to permit the existing rear yard fence to remain as constructed. The fence exceeds the maximum permitted…

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Granted a fence exemption to the property owner of 213 Northwood Drive; directed and required that the enclosure be maintained in good repair without alteration; and at such time as replacement of the enclosure is required that such installation will comply with Municipal Code Chapter 447, Fences, or its successor by-law.
