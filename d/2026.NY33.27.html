The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an overview of Metrolinx’s planned construction activities for the Yonge North Subway Extension Steeles Station at the intersection of Yonge Street and Steeles Avenue. The report outlines the key construction stages, anticipated timelines, and associated long-term temporary lane closures, turning restrictions, pedestrian and cycling impacts, transit adjustments, and access considerations required to facilitate construction. Construction of Steeles Station requires excavation of a large underground station box within the existing Yonge Street right-of-way. Due to the scale and location of the work, it is not feasible to maintain traffic operations within the existing roadway during key stages of construction. To maintain north-south connectivity, Yonge Street will be temporarily realigned onto a newly constructed diversion road west of its existing alignment.…

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Deferred the consideration of Item NY33.27 to the July 7, 2026 meeting of North York Community Council, to allow Metrolinx additional time to undertake community consultation and engagement, in collaboration with the local Councillor’s office, regarding the proposed construction staging, traffic impacts, pedestrian and business access, and associated mitigation measures related to the Yonge North Subway Extension Steeles Station construction works.
