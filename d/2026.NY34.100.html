What the item proposed

This motion highlights the rapid intensification taking place across North York including Ward 18 Willowdale, particularly within and around the North York Centre Secondary Plan area and Yonge North Secondary Plan area, where significant residential and mixed-use growth continues to concentrate along Yonge Street. As population density increases under these Secondary Plans, the area is experiencing growing pressure on local infrastructure, including transportation networks andcurbside parking supply. While parts of Willowdale benefit from proximity to Line 1 subway service, the broader community still relies heavily on cars, surface transit and feeder bus routes, which face ongoing capacity and reliability challenges. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, to review the removal of minimum parking requirements as it applies to North York, including Ward 18 Willowdale, and to report back on the feasibility of pausing the elimination of minimum parking requirements for new developments in areas where access to transit remains constrained, until enhanced and reliable transit infrastructure is in place. 2.
