What the item proposed

This Report recommends approval of the application to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law with a holding provision at 351, 353, 355, 357, 359, 361, 363, 365, 367, 369, 371, 373, 375 and 377 Marlee Avenue, 2, 4, 6 Romar Crescent and 265, 269 Viewmount Avenue to permit a mixed-use development consisting of 37-storey (124.8 metre) and 39-storey (131.1 metre) towers inclusive of 6-metre mechanical penthouses on 6-storey podiums. A total gross floor area (GFA) of 58,447 square metres is proposed of which 778 square metres is non-residential gross floor area inclusive of a 392 square metre community space as an In-kind Contribution), resulting in a floor space index (FSI) of 11.06. A total of 840 residential units inclusive of 26 rental replacement units, 227 vehicular parking spaces, and 961 bicycle parking spaces are proposed.
