What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of a Rental Housing Demolition application which proposes to demolish 16 rental dwelling units located at 351 - 377 Marlee Avenue, 2 - 6 Romar Crescent and 265 - 269 Viewmount Avenue. The 16 rental units are proposed to be replaced with 26 rental dwelling units as part of the new development on the site. The proposal includes a Tenant Assistance Plan that addresses the right of existing tenants to return to replacement rental units at similar rents and provides financial compensation to mitigate hardship. The proposed development on the site at 351 - 377 Marlee Avenue, 2 - 6 Romar Crescent and 265 - 269 Viewmount Avenue is the subject of a related Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application 25 198723 NNY 08 OZ.
