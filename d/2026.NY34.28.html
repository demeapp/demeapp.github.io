The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of a Rental Housing Demolition application which proposes to demolish 8 rental dwelling units located at 23 and 25 Poyntz Avenue. The 8 rental units are proposed to be replaced as part of the new development on the site. A Tenant Assistance Plan is not proposed as all existing rental dwelling units are vacant. The proposed development at 15, 19, 21, 23 and 25 Poyntz Avenue is the subject of a related Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application (25 252388 NNY 18 OZ). The proposed development would permit a 48-storey mixed-use building with 535 dwelling units, including 8 replacement rental units. An approval report for the Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application has been advanced concurrently with this Rental Housing Demolition application approval report.
