The official record

What the item proposed

On August 15, 2025, a complete application to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law was submitted, satisfying the City's minimum application requirements. The application seeks to permit a 48-storey (137.35 metre plus a 7.5 metre mechanical penthouse) building with 505 dwelling units, 93 vehicle parking spaces, 289 bicycle parking spaces, a gross floor area (“GFA”) of 34,123 metres squared, and a floor space index (“FSI”) of 16.72 at 5, 7, 9, 11, and 15 Park Hill Road. Five detached dwellings are proposed to be demolished. On February 25, 2026, the owner appealed the Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment Applications to the Ontario Land Tribunal (“OLT”) due to Council’s failure to make a decision within the time frame in the Planning Act. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the City Solicitor and appropriate City Staff to attend the Ontario Land Tribunal in opposition to the current application regarding the Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment Application appeals for the lands municipally known as 5, 7, 9, 11, and 15 Park Hill Road and to continue discussions with the applicant in an attempt to resolve outstanding issues. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor and City Staff to take any necessary steps to implement City Council's decision, including requesting any conditions of approval that would be in the City's interest, in the event the Ontario Land Tribunal allows the appeal, in whole or in part. 3.
