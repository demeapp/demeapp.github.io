12 Whitefriars Drive - Application for Fence Exemption

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Whether the owner of 12 Whitefriars Drive may keep over-height rear fences and be exempted from pool-safety rules is up for North York Community Council to decide.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 12 Whitefriars Drive has requested North York Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447-1.2B(1) - Fence Height The applicant is seeking permission to maintain existing rear yard fences located on the east and west sides of the property that exceed the maximum permitted height of 2.0 metres. The existing west side fence measures 2.48 metres in height and the existing east side fence measures 2.16 metres in height. AND City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447-1.3C(3) - Swimming Pool Enclosure; Access Protection The applicant is proposing the construction of a permanent 1.2 metre high fence attached to the rear sliding door, with no gate provided, which would eliminate access to the pool enclosure from this access point. AND City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447-1.3E(1)(b) -…

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Refused to grant the application for exemption by the owner of 12 Whitefriars Drive because the fence fails to comply with the provisions of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447 Fences.
