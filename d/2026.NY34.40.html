The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report concerns a matter for which the North York Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The report responds to an application submitted by the property owner of 2 Green Valley Road requesting approval for a site-specific Fence Exemption under Sections 447-1.3E(2)(c)(1), 447-1.3D(1), 447-1.3E(1)(b), and 447-1.3C(1) of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 – Fences. The property owner is seeking Council’s permission to maintain two metal picket fences in the rear yard forming part of a swimming pool enclosure. The fences have horizontal rails spaced approximately 0.89 metres apart, with vertical pickets spaced greater than 38 millimetres apart. What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Referred the item to the District Manager, Municipal Licensing and Standards, East District and requested that they work with the applicant to install a Juliet gate on the door that enters from dwelling to pool area, situated on the rear wall of the house, and report to the first regular meeting of North York Community Council in the 2026-2030 term of Council with recommendations.
