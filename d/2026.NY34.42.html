130 Rykert Crescent - Application for Fence Exemption

Adopted with changesJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The owner of 130 Rykert Crescent brings a fence appeal to North York Community Council over whether they may keep a pool enclosure without a fourth fence along the path from the home.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 130 Rykert Crescent has requested North York Community Council exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 Section 1.3.C(3) Access Protection. The Applicant for 130 Rykert Crescent is requesting an exemption for their request to access from the property’s home to the pool enclosure area in the rear yard remain without a fourth fence. Municipal Licensing and Standards Division has inspected the subject property and issued a notice of violation of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 Section 1.3. C (1) Access Protection.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Granted the application for a fence exemption for the property owner of 130 Rykert Crescent; directed and required that the enclosure be maintained in good repair without alteration; and at such time as replacement of the enclosure is required that such installation will comply with Municipal Code Chapter 447, Fences, or its successor by-law.
