34 Medalist Road - Application for Fence Exemption

Adopted with changesJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A fence appeal from 34 Medalist Road — asking whether the owner may keep rear-yard fence panels taller than the 2.0-metre limit — goes to North York Community Council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 34 Medalist Road has requested North York Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 Section 1.2.B(1) fence in rear yard not to exceed 2.0 metres in height. The Applicant for 34 Medalist Road is requesting an exemption for their existing wooden fence in rear yard to remain: - Rear fence - Eastern lot line 1st fence panel is a height of 1.19 metres - Rear fence - Eastern lot line 2nd and 3rd fence panel is a height of 2.49 metres - Rear fence - Eastern lot line 4th fence panel are a height of 2.8 metres - Rear fence - Eastern lot line 5th fence panel is a height of 2.78 metres - Rear fence - Eastern lot line 6th fence panel is a height of 2.66 metres - Rear fence - Eastern lot line 7th, 8th and 9th fence panel is a height of 2.80 metres - Rear fence - Eastern lot line 10th to 13th fence…

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Granted the application for a fence exemption for the property owner of 34 Medalist Road; directed and required that the enclosure be maintained in good repair without alteration; and at such time as replacement of the enclosure is required that such installation will comply with Municipal Code Chapter 447, Fences, or its successor by-law.
