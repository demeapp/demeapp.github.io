32 Cedarbank Crescent - Application for Fence Exemption

Adopted with changesJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The owner of 32 Cedarbank Crescent brings a fence appeal to North York Community Council over whether they may install a 1.4-metre mesh pool fence.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 32 Cedarbank Crescent has requested North York Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: 1. City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 Section 447‑1.2A(5) - No person shall erect, own or maintain, or cause or permit the erection or maintenance of any fence that uses materials not usually intended for use in permanent fencing, unless specifically permitted by this chapter or another by-law. The applicant for 32 Cedarbank Crescent is requesting an exemption to permit the proposed installation of a 1.4 metres (4.5 feet) climb-resistant mesh, free‑standing rear yard pool fence enclosure designed to separate the dwelling from the pool without drilling into existing surfaces Furthermore, the applicable by-law stipulates that swimming pool enclosures shall be constructed in accordance with prescribed standards and shall be comprised…

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Granted the application for a fence exemption for the property owner of 32 Cedarbank Crescent; directed and required that the enclosure be maintained in good repair without alteration; and at such time as replacement of the enclosure is required that such installation will comply with Municipal Code Chapter 447, Fences, or its successor by-law.
