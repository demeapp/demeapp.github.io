23 Truxford Road - Application for Fence Exemption

Adopted with changesJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Whether the owner of 23 Truxford Road may keep a pool enclosure that includes a garage wall with doors is up for North York Community Council to decide.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 23 Truxford Road has requested North York Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: 1. City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 Section 447 1.3C.(3) - Access protection. If the wall of any building, or any portion of it, forms part of the pool enclosure, there shall be no access to the enclosed pool area through the wall. The applicant for 23 Truxford Road is seeking an exemption to permit the existing rear yard fence to remain in its current constructed state. The fence enclosure has been designed and constructed to include the garage wall, which contains a double access door (walk-out entry), within the enclosed area. Municipal Licensing and Standards Division has inspected the subject property and issued a notice of violation under City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447. - Fences

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Granted the application for a fence exemption for the property owner of 23 Truxford Road; directed and required that the enclosure be maintained in good repair without alteration; and at such time as replacement of the enclosure is required that such installation will comply with Municipal Code Chapter 447, Fences, or its successor by-law.
