Toronto City Council · Decision

North York Community Council decided whether the owner of 59 Hesketh Court may keep a pool fence with shallow posts and climbable spots.

Official title: 59 Hesketh Court - Application for Fence Exemption

Adopted with changesJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The applicant for 59 Hesketh Court has requested North York Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 E.(2)(a)(2) and City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447, 1.3E.(1).(b) The applicant 59 Hesketh Court is requesting an exemption for their existing fence as constructed: The terminal fence posts are not installed to the required depth in concrete as per the bylaw. Furthermore, the swimming pool enclosure, has climbable conditions and thus, the enclosure is less than the minimum required height of 1.8 metres, as an external condition that provides a means of climbing the outside of the enclosure is situated within 1.0 metres. Municipal Licensing and Standards Division has inspected the subject property and issued a notice of violation of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Granted the application for a fence exemption for the property owner of 59 Hesketh Court; directed and required that the enclosure be maintained in good repair without alteration; and at such time as replacement of the enclosure is required that such installation will comply with Municipal Code Chapter 447, Fences, or its successor by-law.
