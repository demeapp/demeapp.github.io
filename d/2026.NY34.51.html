255 Parkview Avenue - Application for Fence Exemption

Referred back for more workJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A fence appeal from 255 Parkview Avenue — asking whether the owner may be exempted from pool-fence access and material rules — goes to North York Community Council.

Council sent it back for more work.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 255 Parkview Avenue has requested North York Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: 1. City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 Section 447 1.3C.(3) - Access protection. If the wall of any building, or any portion of it, forms part of the pool enclosure, there shall be no access to the enclosed pool area through the wall. 2. Section 447‑1.2A(5) - No person shall erect, own or maintain, or cause or permit the erection or maintenance of any fence that uses materials not usually intended for use in permanent fencing, unless specifically permitted by this chapter or another by-law. The applicant for 255 Parkview Avenue is requesting an exemption to permit the existing rear yard fence to remain as constructed. The fence enclosure has been built such that the basement walk-up door is incorporated within the enclosure.…

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Referred the item to the District Manager, Municipal Licensing and Standards, East District, and requested that they work with the applicant to install a Juliet gate on the door that enters from the dwelling to the pool area, situated on the rear wall of the house, and report to the first regular meeting of North York Community Council in the 2026-2030 term of Council with recommendations.
