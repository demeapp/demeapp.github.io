Playfair Avenue - Parking Amendments

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Adjusting school-zone parking times on Playfair Avenue by Regina Mundi Catholic School — freeing about 20 spaces in evenings and weekends — goes to North York Community Council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that the operating times of the 15-minute maximum parking and stopping prohibition on the north side of Playfair Avenue in front of Regina Mundi Catholic School be amended. The proposed changes will align the operating times with the school's hours of operation. This will provide approximately 20 parking spaces with a three-hour maximum limit in the evening and weekends for after school events. During the course of the investigation, it was noted that there are discrepancies between posted signs and the existing parking regulations. Recommendations 2, 3 and 4 will correct these inconsistencies.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing stopping prohibition in effect at all times, on the north side of Playfair Avenue, between a point 60 metres west of Ennerdale Avenue and a point 20 metres west thereof, to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday. 2. Approved a 15-minute maximum parking limit, in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Playfair Avenue, between Ennerdale Street and a point 60 metres west, and between a point 80 metres west of Ennerdale Street and a point 21 metres further west. 3. Amended the existing stopping prohibition in effect at all times, on the north side of Playfair Avenue, between a point 24 metres east of Ennerdale Street, to be in effect between Ennerdale Street and Dufferin Street. 4.
