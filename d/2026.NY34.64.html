St. Cuthberts Road - Introduction of Overnight On-Street Permit Parking

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Bringing in overnight permit parking from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. on St. Cuthberts Road between Bayview Avenue and Bessborough Drive goes to North York Community Council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that overnight on-street permit parking be introduced on St. Cuthberts Road between Bayview Avenue to Bessborough Drive from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. Residents requested the implementation of overnight on-street permit parking on St. Cuthberts Road, between Bayview Avenue and Bessborough Drive to accommodate their long-term parking needs.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized parking by permit only on a street name basis from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily on the odd (south) side of St. Cuthberts Road, from Bayview Avenue to Bessborough Drive. 2. Prohibited parking at all times on the north side of St. Cuthberts Road between Bayview Avenue and Bessborough Drive.
