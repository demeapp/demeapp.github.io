Approving an agreement with Metrolinx over a leftover construction wall at the Crosstown's East Portal near Eglinton Avenue East and Brentcliffe Road.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks City Council authority to enter into an agreement with Metrolinx respecting the Brentcliffe Shotcrete Wall located within the Eglinton Crosstown Light Rail Transit East Portal area on the south side of Eglinton Avenue East, east of Brentcliffe Road. The wall was constructed by Metrolinx as a temporary construction support structure to facilitate excavation and construction of Tunnel Boring Machine Launch Shaft 3 as part of the Eglinton Crosstown Light Rail Transit Project. Following completion of the tunnelling works and backfilling of the launch shaft, the lower portion of the wall became buried while the upper portion remained exposed above grade. The City previously requested that Metrolinx remove the wall in its entirety.
