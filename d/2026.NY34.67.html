The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission operates a transit service on Bayview Avenue, City Council approval of this report is required. North York Community Council directed Transportation Services to investigate solutions in line with Vision Zero design principles to make Pottery Road (north), between Bayview Avenue and Moore Avenue, safer and undertake an operational and safety review of the intersection of Bayview Avenue with Bayview Heights Drive and Evergreen Gardens. This report provides an overview of the assessment undertaken by Transportation Services.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Refer Item NY34.67 titled "Pottery Road (north), Intersection of Bayview Avenue with Bayview Heights Drive and Evergreen Gardens - Operational and Safety Review" back to the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services, to allow time for the local City Councillor to work with staff and community on further consultation efforts.
