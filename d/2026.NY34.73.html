Dallington Drive - Parking Amendments

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Limiting parking to five minutes near Dallington Drive Public School during morning drop-off and afternoon pick-up goes to North York Community Council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending that parking be authorized for a maximum period of five minutes from 7:45 a.m. to 8:30 a.m. and 2:30 p.m. to 3:15 p.m., Monday to Friday on the west side of Dallington Drive, in the vicinity of Dallington Drive Public School. Currently, parking is allowed for the unsigned statutory three-hour maximum limit in this area. The proposed change will promote parking turnover during school arrival and dismissal times for parents / guardians to accompany students to / from the school, thereby mitigating traffic congestion. During the course of the investigation, it was noted that there is a discrepancy between posted signs and the existing parking regulations. Recommendations 3 and 4 will correct this inconsistency.

Amended the existing school bus loading zone, in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., on the west side of Dallington Drive, between a point 91 metres west of Shaughnessy Boulevard and a point 121.9 metres northwest of Shaughnessy Boulevard, to be in effect between a point 106 metres west of Shaughnessy Boulevard and a point 29 metres northwest. 4.
