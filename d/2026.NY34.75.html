What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The Transportation Review unit, in the Development Review Division and the Traffic Operations unit in the Transportation Services Division, are requesting approval for various parking and traffic regulations proposed under the Draft Plan of Subdivision Application for the development at 451 Patricia Avenue. The subject parking and traffic regulations are to be implemented on William Durie Way, which is an unassumed roadway that was recently dedicated as a public highway. The Development Engineering unit of the Development Review Division is preparing a separate report for North York Community Council consideration, which will be recommending assumption of William Durie Way.
