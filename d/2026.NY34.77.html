The official record

What the item proposed

As this report recommends the permanent closure of a portion of the public lane (public highway), City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services has reviewed the feasibility of closing a portion of the public lane south of Sheppard Avenue East, west of Willowdale Avenue, located between 145 and 181 Sheppard Avenue East, shown as Parts 1 and 2 in Attachments 1 and 2, and has determined that the lands can be permanently closed. The proposed closure is required to facilitate the implementation of the settlement approved by City Council through Item 2024.CC24.9 respecting the development applications for 145 Sheppard Avenue East and the related Ontario Land Tribunal Interim Order issued on February 6, 2025. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the permanent closure of a portion of the public lane located between 145 and 181 Sheppard Avenue East, being part of Lane Plan 3596 North York abutting Block A, Plan 3596, City of Toronto, and shown as Parts 1 and 2 on Plan 66R-34984 in the report (June 18, 2026) from the Interim Deputy General Manager, Transportation Services. 2. City Council direct the City Solicitor to prepare and submit for enactment the necessary Bill to permanently close the public lane shown as Parts 1 and 2 on Plan 66R-34984 including the closing By-law substantially in the form of the draft By-law attached as Attachment 3 to the report (June 18, 2026) from the Interim Deputy General Manager, Transportation Services.
