Anewen Drive, Broadleaf Road, and Lynedock Crescent - Traffic Calming Installation

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Adding Anewen Drive, Broadleaf Road and Lynedock Crescent to the traffic-calming installation list — after resident petitions — goes to North York Community Council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am requesting that the three roads titled on this letter be added to a list for traffic calming installation. The provisions are listed below: - Anewen Drive, between Woodthorpe Road and Sweeney Drive. - Broadleaf Road, between Woodcliff Place and The Donway East. - Lynedock Crescent, between Fenside Drive and Sandover Drive. All of these roads have gone through the proper petition process set out by my office with a high majority of residents sharing they are in favour of installation for each road. I am requesting that my colleagues at North York Community Council accept the recommendations prepared by staff below.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (three speed humps) on Broadleaf Road between The Donway East and Woodcliff Place, generally as shown in Drawing Number TC-741, dated June 2026, from the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services, in the attachment to the Letter (June 18, 2026) from Councillor Jon Burnside. 2. Subject to approval of Recommendation 1 above, reduced the speed limit from 50 kilometresper hour to 30 kilometres per hour on Broadleaf Road between The Donway East and Woodcliff Place, in conjunction with the installation of speed humps. 3. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (four speed humps) on Lynedock Crescent between Fenside Drive and Sandover Drive, generally as shown in Drawing Number TC-779, dated June 2026, from the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services,…
