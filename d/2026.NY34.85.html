Review Steeles Avenue East Road Upgrades: Bayview Avenue to Townsend Road

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A review of the 2024 Steeles Avenue East upgrades between Bayview Avenue and Townsend Road — after reports that large vehicles struggle to turn at the narrowed intersections — goes to Transportation Services.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As part of the Steeles Avenue East Road reconstruction project completed in 2024, curb radii reductions were installed at several intersections to improve pedestrian safety and reduce vehicle turning speeds. Since the installation of these measures, residents have raised concerns regarding the ability of larger vehicles, including school buses and waste collection trucks, to safely complete turns from Steeles Avenue East onto local streets. Reports indicate that some vehicles may be mounting the curb while turning, creating potential safety concerns for pedestrians and motorists. A review by Transportation Services will help determine whether the curb radii reductions are functioning as intended, meeting applicable City standards, and providing the desired safety benefits while accommodating the operational needs of larger vehicles.
