The official record

What the item proposed

On May 26, 2026, North York Community Council approved two key amendments to Item NY33.35 for a proposed traffic calming project in the West Lansing neighbourhood in Ward 18. I am requesting to re-open the item and in turn adopt the below recommendations, which will address the technical requirements related to the previous amendments and seek authorization for their implementation. It ensures that staff direction aligns with the North York Community Council’s updated decision, and that the project proceeds as intended.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item 2026.NY33.35, titled "Bogert Avenue, Poyntz Avenue, Johnston Avenue, Cameron Avenue, Franklin Avenue - Traffic Calming (Speed Humps)", with respect to Part 2 and Part 9 of the decision of North York Community Council. 2. Amended Part 2 of its decision on Item 2026.NY33.35 by replacing only Attachment 8, Drawing TC-677-1 with Attachment 1 to the letter (June 18, 2026) from Councillor Lily Cheng, Drawing TC677-1a, dated June, 2026. 3. Deleted Part 9 of its decision on Item 2026.NY33.35. 4. Reduced the speed limit from 40 kilometres per hour to 30 kilometres per hour on Florence Avenue, between Pewter Road and Yonge Street. 5.
