396 Cortleigh Boulevard - Parking Amendments

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Changing parking rules around the daycare at 396 Cortleigh Boulevard — no parking in front for a kiss-and-ride, a 1-hour limit east of the daycare, and no stopping across the street, to ease congestion and improve safety at drop-off and pick-up — goes to North York Community Council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office has received communications from residents expressing concerns regarding traffic congestion and safety around the area of Bathurst Street and Cortleigh Boulevard. The existing three-hour parking limit in front of the daycare results in long-term parking that significantly limits drop-off and pick-up activity. Prohibiting parking in this area to facilitate a kiss and ride would increase drop-off and pick-up capacity. The proposed one-hour limit, east of the daycare will provide short-term parking for parents. The proposed stopping prohibition opposite the daycare and the proposed parking prohibition in front of Cortleigh Parkette will promote safe and efficient two-way traffic.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Approved a one-hour maximum parking limit, in effect from 7:30 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and 3:30 p.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Cortleigh Boulevard, between a point 91.5 metres east of Bathurst Street and a point 49.5 metres further east. 2. Prohibited parking from 7:30 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and 3:30 p.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of Cortleigh Boulevard, between Bathurst Street and a point 91.5 metres further east. 3. Prohibited stopping from 7:30 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and 3:30 p.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Cortleigh Boulevard, between Bathurst Street and a point 91.5 metres further east. 4.
