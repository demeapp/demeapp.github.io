Toronto City Council · Decision

Carmichael Avenue - Speed Hump Relocation

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Moving two approved-but-not-installed speed humps on Carmichael Avenue from in front of 3 and 15 to in front of 4 and 17 — because of conflicts at the approved spots — goes to North York Community Council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services requested my office to present a motion to North York Community Council recommending that the approved but not installed speed humps in front of 3 and 15 Carmichael Avenue be relocated to be in front of 4 and 17 Carmichael Avenue. There are conflicts at the approved locations.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized the relocation of the two approved speed humps on Carmichael Avenue, between Kelso Avenue and Avenue Road, generally as set out in Drawing Number TC-509, dated June 2026, attached, entitled “Carmichael Avenue - Speed Hump Relocation” to the attachment to the letter (June 30, 2026) from Councillor Mike Colle.
