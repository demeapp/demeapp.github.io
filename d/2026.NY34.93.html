Toronto City Council · Decision

Woodsworth Road - Parking Prohibition Change

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Allowing parking on a short stretch of Woodsworth Road (Bannatyne to Elliotwood) — after a resident petition — goes to North York Community Council; the winter 2am–6am ban stays.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office received a request for a change to the parking prohibitions on Woodsworth Drive, between Bannatyne Drive and Elliotwood Court. City staff advised the resident and my office that allowing parking on this short stretch of road was feasible, and the requesting constituent, Mr. Li, successfully petitioned all adjacent residents. Neighbours are aware that the 2 a.m. to 6 a.m. parking prohibition remains in place from December 1 to March 31 for winter maintenance, as on all streets (unsigned) in the former city of North York. City Staff supports these recommendations.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition, in effect at all times, on both sides of Woodsworth Road, between Bannatyne Drive (southeast intersection) and Stubbs Drive. 2. Prohibited parking at all times, on the west side of Woodsworth Road, between Bannatyne Drive (southeast intersection) and Stubbs Drive. 3. Prohibited parking at all times, on the east side of Woodsworth Road, between Elliotwood Court and Stubbs Drive.
