The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. 19 Alexandra Incorporated is constructing a 13-storey retirement residence at 19 Alexandra Boulevard and 2674-2704 Yonge Street. The site is located on the southwest corner of Yonge Street and Alexandra Boulevard. To accommodate construction staging operations for the development, Transportation Services is requesting authorization to close the south sidewalk on Alexandra Boulevard, for a period of 38 months, from December 1, 2026, to January 31, 2030. Pedestrian movements on the south side of Alexandra Boulevard abutting the site will be restricted and pedestrians will be directed to the sidewalk on the north side of Alexandra Boulevard.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized the closure of the south sidewalk on Alexandra Boulevard, between Yonge Street and a point 70 metres west, from December 1, 2026, to January 31, 2030, inclusive. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times from 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m. on the south side of Alexandra Boulevard, between Yonge Street and a point 90 metres west. 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the south side of Alexandra Boulevard, between Yonge Street and a point 90 metres west. 4. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Alexandra Boulevard, between Yonge Street and a point 90 metres west. 5.
