What the item proposed

I am writing regarding the final phase of the Marlee-Glencairn Study to ensure that the final report comprehensively addresses the infrastructure and service needs required to support both current residents and future growth along the Marlee Avenue corridor. The Marlee-Glencairn community continues to attract significant development interest due to its proximity to higher-order transit and its designation within a future Protected Major Transit Station Area (PMTSA), consistent with provincial planning policy and legislative changes that require municipalities to plan for intensified, transit-supportive growth in areas well served by higher-order transit.
