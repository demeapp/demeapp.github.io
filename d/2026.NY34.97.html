Sunnybrook Park Trail Off of Salonica Road - Restricting Driveway Access

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Removing a one-foot reserve at the end of Salonica Road — to create gated emergency access to Sunnybrook Park and construction access for the Wilket Creek Bridge replacement — goes to North York Community Council; a motion also asks that the road stay limited so it does not bring more traffic into the park.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office received the request from staff that the removal of the one-foot reserve at the south end of Salonica Road is required to facilitate an emergency access route into Sunnybrook Park and construction access for the Wilket Creek Bridge replacement project. A gated entrance will be installed to prevent unauthorized vehicular access except during construction. Residents expressed concerns that the new access road could result in increased vehicular activity within the park if used for other purposes, including routine operations. To address these concerns, while supporting the bridge reconstruction project, this amendment will ensure that, except during authorized construction, the access is restricted to emergency vehicles only and will not be available for routine Parks and Recreation Services operations or general public vehicular access. What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Prohibited entry to the driveway access to Sunnybrook Park Trail on Salonica Road, at a point 30 metres south of Suncrest Drive, by inserting into the Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 950 - Traffic and Parking - Schedule XXV (Entry Prohibited) in §950-1324 the following: (In column 1 Highway Not To Be Entered) (In Column 2 From) (In Column 3 Prohibited Time and/or Days) Access driveway to Sunnybrook Park Trail on Salonica Road at a point approximately 30 metres south of Suncrest Drive Salonica Road Anytime (except vehicles actively engaged in construction activities authorized for the site)
