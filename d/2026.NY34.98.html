Hove Street - Traffic Calming (Speed Humps) - Bathurst Manor Neighbourhood Mobility Plan

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A member motion asks North York Community Council to approve speed humps on Hove Street, as recommended by the Bathurst Manor traffic study, after residents raised speeding concerns.

The official record

What the item proposed

Following increasing concerns in the community of high vehicle speeds, and both pedestrian and cyclist safety, Transportation Services developed The Bathurst Manor Neighbourhood Mobility Plan, a comprehensive study of traffic behaviours and travel patterns in the Bathurst Manor Neighbourhood. As part of the Bathurst Manor Neighbourhood Mobility Plan, Transportation Services recommended the installation of speed humps on Hove Street as part of the May Update. Since the installation of speed humps on surrounding streets, the Councillor’s Office has received an increase of concerns from residents regarding speeding on Hove Street, including concerns that Hove Street’s use as a bypass route to avoid the traffic calming measures on surrounding streets.

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (nine speed humps) on Hove Street between Codsell Avenue and Maxwell Street, generally as shown on Attachment 2 to the Letter (July 7, 2026) from Councillor James Pasternak, Drawing numbers ATP-SH-268, ATP-SH-269, and ATP-SH-270, dated July 2026.
