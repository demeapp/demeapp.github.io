Toronto City Council · Decision

Advancing the Bayview Block Plan

AdoptedJul 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A member motion asks North York Community Council to advance a Bayview block plan for Bayview Avenue from Post Road to Highway 401, to guide the area's shift toward midrise buildings.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Bayview Avenue from Post Road to the 401 is an area undergoing significant change. With the implementation of the Major Streets Policy, this area has seen an increasing number of midrise applications which are a departure from the historical low-rise development this area has traditionally seen. The large lots found on this stretch of Bayview Avenue are particularly conducive to this form of development, as plots of land are often large enough to accommodate buildings of a midrise scale without the consolidation that would be required to achieve a similar effect in other areas. At the same time, this is an area with particular needs and nuances that should be taken into consideration as future development is planned. For example, Bayview Avenue is the singular connection point for many cul-de-sacs and dead-end roads, and as such is a high-traffic area prone to congestion and back-ups.…

What was decided

North York Community Council: 1. Forwarded the Central Bayview Block Plan to the Director, Community Planning, North York District, for information and consideration for Planning issues in the review of future development proposals along the portion of Bayview Avenue from Post Road to the 401 alongside existing planning policies and guidelines. 2. Forwarded the Central Bayview Block Plan to the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning, for information and consideration for Planning in the Avenues Policy implementation process in Ward 15, Don Valley West along the portion of Bayview Avenue from Post Road to the 401.
