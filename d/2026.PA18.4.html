2026 On-Street Parking Rate Changes

AdoptedMar 11, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto Parking Authority, which manages over 20,000 on-street spaces and 292 off-street lots, is asking its board to approve a city-wide $0.25 increase in on-street parking rates, except in the lowest-rate areas. Rate changes affect what drivers pay.

The official summary names a figure of $0.25.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) continues its journey to becoming one of the world’s best providers of sustainable parking, Bike Share and last mile mobility experiences. The Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) manages an on-street parking portfolio of over 20,000 spaces in the City of Toronto. Toronto Parking Authority’s on-street parking serves businesses and communities by providing short-stay and high turnover parking. Additionally, the Toronto Parking Authority operates 292 off-street parking facilities throughout the City, including surface lots and garages. Of the facilities that Toronto Parking Authority operates, there are select locations under the operational jurisdiction of other City agencies (i.e., Toronto Transit Commision/Exhibition Place). What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Approved the modified rates for on-street parking locations identified in Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (February 25, 2026) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority. 2. Directed the President, Toronto Parking Authority to request the City Solicitor to submit bills to City Council to make amendments to the appropriate Chapters of the Toronto Municipal Code to implement the rates for on-street parking locations identified in Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (February 25, 2026) from the President, Toronto Parking Authority.
