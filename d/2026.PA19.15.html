What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to obtain approval from the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) for Phase One (1) of a fleet replacement and decarbonization plan. Phase One (1) of the Fleet Replacement and Decarbonization Program (2026–2028) represents the first stage of Toronto Parking Authority’s sustainable fleet transition and replacement strategy, with a focus on replacing the most critical end-of-life fleet assets. Approval is sought for spending authority of $3,300,000, excluding HST, to replace thirty (30) vehicles and to authorize Toronto Parking Authority to procure fleet vehicles and related equipment through the City of Toronto’s Fleet Services Division (FSD).
