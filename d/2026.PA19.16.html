Short Term Parking at the Price Street Green P Lot

Received for informationMay 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Procedural: following City Council's direction, the Toronto Parking Authority board was asked to turn at least 20 spots in the Price Street Green P lot into short-term (3-hour max, non-renewable) parking for local restaurant and business customers.

Received for information — Council took no action on it.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on March 25 and 26, 2026, adopted Item MM39.7 and, in so doing, has requested the Toronto Parking Authority board to designate at least 20 of the Price Street Green P lot parking spots to non-renewable short term (maximum three hours) parking spots, in order to provide parking for customers of local restaurants and businesses.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority received the item for information.
