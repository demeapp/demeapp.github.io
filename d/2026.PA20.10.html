The official record

What the item proposed

The Toronto Parking Authority is committed to delivering clean, safe, secure, and efficient parking facilities that support customers, employees, service providers, and the communities it serves. In partnership with Corporate Security, Toronto Parking Authority applies a risk-based, multi-layered approach to security that integrates physical infrastructure, technology, data-driven analysis, trained personnel, and effective communication. This approach enables Toronto Parking Authority to identify and respond to site-specific risks, strengthen the overall safety and security of its facilities, and support a consistent, reliable customer experience across the parking network. The purpose of this report is to seek approval from the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority to award a non-competitive service contract to Metscan Canada Limited, in an amount not to exceed $360,000,…

What was decided

The Board of Directors Toronto Parking Authority: 1. Authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to enter into and execute a new non-competitive service agreement with Metscan Canada Limited to complete the design and installation of previously purchased Closed-Circuit Television equipment at Car Park 43 and Car Park 68, for a total amount not to exceed $360,000, excluding Harmonized Sales Tax.
