Toronto Island Bike Share Enhancements for the 2026 Season

Received for informationJul 24, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Procedural: following City Council's direction, the Toronto Parking Authority board was asked to start 2026-season Toronto Island Bike Share measures: cyclist education with valet help, avoiding harm to turtle populations when siting stations, and limiting internal-combustion equipment on the Island.

Received for information — Council took no action on it.

The official record

What the item proposed

City Council on May 20 and 21, 2026, adopted Item MM41.13 and, in so doing, has requested the Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors to request the President, Toronto Parking Authority, in consultation with the General Manager, Parks and Recreation starting in the 2026 operating season, to: a. provide clear education for cyclist behavior with Bike Share rental on Toronto Island including valet assisted on-the-ground direction; b. ensure BikeShare takes steps to avoid adverse environmental impacts including to turtle populations when siting BikeShare stations or other infrastructure on Toronto Island; c. minimize the use of internal combustion vehicles for servicing Bike Share stations on Toronto Island; d. coordinate signage that reinforces identification of residential community areas on Toronto Island; and e. provide suggested routes for BikeShare on Toronto Island that avoids…

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority received the item for information.
