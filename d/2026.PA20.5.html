Public Electric Vehicle Charging Program - President’s Authority to Execute Collaboration Agreement

AdoptedJul 24, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Letting its President sign a three-party deal (City, TPA, chosen company) for the public EV charging program, under the city's 2030 public charging plan. It goes to the Toronto Parking Authority board for a decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek approval from the Toronto Parking Authority Board of Directors to authorize the President, Toronto Parking Authority, to negotiate, enter into, and execute a tripartite Collaboration Agreement, with major negotiated terms outlined in Confidential Attachment 1, between the City, Toronto Parking Authority, and the preferred proponent. In October 2024, City Council adopted IE16.5 , Approach to Public Electric Vehicle Charging to 2030, which established the governance framework for city-wide public Electric Vehicle charging. The report identified Environment, Climate and Forestry (ECF) as the lead City division and designated Toronto Parking Authority as the operational delivery partner.
