What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to present Management’s proposed 2027 Operating Budget and 2027-2036 Capital Budget to the Board of Directors of the Toronto Parking Authority for its initial review. The proposed budget has been submitted to the City of Toronto as part of its budget review process and will be brought forward to the Board in December 2026 for approval, after which it will proceed to Toronto City Council for final consideration and approval. Building off a strong performance through the first eight months of 2026, Management is forecasting full year operating income of $63.8 million which is +$2.7 million or 4.4 percent better than budget. Revenues are forecasted to reach a historic high of $184.5 million, exceeding budget by $1.5 million (+0.8 percent).
