What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek approval from the Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) Board of Directors to award a contract for Winter Maintenance Services to Across Canada Construction Ltd. (Across Canada). The proposed contract is for an initial three-year term, from November 1, 2026 to October 31, 2029, with a value of $4,463,025.54, excluding HST. The contract includes two optional one-year extension terms which, if exercised, would extend the agreement to October 31, 2031 and result in a total potential five-year contract value of $7,438,375.90, excluding HST. Management is also recommending a 10 percent contingency of $743,837.59 to address unforeseen service requirements over the potential five-year term, bringing the total Board approval authority requested to $8,182,213.49, excluding HST.
