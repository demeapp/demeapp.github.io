What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek approval from the Board of Directors of Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) for an additional capital investment of $1,931,635, excluding Harmonized Sales Tax (HST), to complete the revised Phase 1 scope of the Security Closed-Circuit Television and Equipment Program. In July 2021, the Board authorized the President, Toronto Parking Authority (TPA), to enter into a contract with the City of Toronto's approved vendor, Met-Scan Canada Limited, for the supply and installation of Closed-Circuit Television cameras, emergency intercoms (duress stations) and access control for secured areas equipment at nine priority Toronto Parking Authority parking facilities, at a total approved value of $4,696,689, including contingency.
