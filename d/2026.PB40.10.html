56 Yonge Street - Alterations to a Designated Heritage Property under Section 33 of the Ontario Heritage Act and Authority to Amend a Heritage Easement Agreement

AdoptedFeb 11, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Approving heritage alterations at 56 Yonge Street and amend the heritage easement, tied to a 65-storey, 648-unit mixed-use tower at 60 Yonge Street that would cantilever 2.6 metres over the heritage building (which stays as is). The plan includes 68 square metres of non-residential space.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve the application for alterations proposed at the designated heritage property at 56 Yonge Street under Section 33 of the Ontario Heritage Act in conjunction with an application to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law to permit the development of a 65-storey (246.2-metres, including mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building, containing 648 residential units and 68 square metres of non-residential space. The project site contains the 8-storey designated heritage property at 56 Yonge Street and a 12-storey building at 60 Yonge Street which is not designated.
