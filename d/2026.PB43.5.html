17-19, 23 and 25 Toronto Street and 55 Adelaide Street East - Alterations to Heritage Properties, Demolition of a Building within the St. Lawrence Neighbourhood Heritage Conservation District, Approval of a Replacement Building and Authority to Enter into a Heritage Easement Agreement

AdoptedApr 27, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Approving heritage alterations and demolition of the non-contributing 23 Toronto Street for a 91-storey mixed-use tower in the St. Lawrence heritage district. The historic buildings at 17–19 Toronto and 57 Adelaide would be kept fully, with façades of 25 Toronto and 55 Adelaide kept in place.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve an application under Section 42 of the Ontario Heritage Act to alter designated heritage properties at 17-19 Toronto Street, 25 Toronto Street and 55 Adelaide Street East. This report also recommends that City Council approve the demolition of 23 Toronto Street under Section 42 of the Ontario Heritage Act in connection with a proposed development of the subject properties. 57 Adelaide Street East is within the development site but is not proposed to be altered. This report also recommends that Council grant authority to enter into a Heritage Easement Agreement for 17-19 Toronto Street, 25 Toronto Street and 55 Adelaide Street East. The proposed development includes the construction of a 91-storey mixed-use tower comprising commercial, office, and residential spaces on the site.
