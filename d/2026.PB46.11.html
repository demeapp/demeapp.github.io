The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve the alterations proposed for the designated heritage properties at 30-32 and 38-40 Huntley Street and 122-124 Isabella Street under Section 33 of the Ontario Heritage Act, in connection with a development on the subject property, and that Council grant authority to enter into a Heritage Easement Agreement. The property contains three properties designated under Part IV of the Ontario Heritage: 30-32 Huntley Street; 38-40 Huntley Street; and 122-124 Isabella Street. The property also contains 34 Huntley Street, a property listed on the City of Toronto’s Heritage Register that staff have determined does not meet the criteria under Ontario Regulation 9/06 of the Ontario Heritage Act and will be demolished as part of the proposal.
