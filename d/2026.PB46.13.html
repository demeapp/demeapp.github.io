1497 and 1501 Queen Street West - Alterations to Designated Heritage Properties and Demolition of Heritage Attributes on a Designated Heritage Property under Sections 33 and 34(1)1 of the Ontario Heritage Act and Authority to Enter into a Heritage Easement Agreement

Adopted with changesJul 6, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council decided whether to allow changes to the heritage buildings at 1497 and 1501 Queen Street West, including some demolition, so the PARC campus can expand with more affordable housing and community services.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve an application under Sections 33 and 34(1)1 of the Ontario Heritage Act (OHA) to alter the Part IV designated heritage properties at 1497 and 1501 Queen Street West, including the selective demolition of heritage attributes of 1501 Queen Street West. This is in connection with an Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment application for the expansion of the existing Parkdale Activity Recreation Centre (PARC) campus to provide additional affordable housing and community services in a new building constructed behind the partially retained façades of the heritage buildings. This report also seeks approval to enter into a Heritage Easement Agreement to ensure the long-term conservation of the heritage properties.
