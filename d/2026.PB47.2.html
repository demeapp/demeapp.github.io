43 Kimbark Boulevard - Notice of Intention to Designate a Property under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act

AdoptedJul 23, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council plans to designate Kimbark House, an Arts and Crafts home at 43 Kimbark Boulevard in Lawrence Park South, as a heritage property.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council state its intention to designate the property at 43 Kimbark Boulevard under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act for its cultural heritage value according to the Statement of Significance which includes a description of heritage attributes found in Attachment 1. The subject property at 43 Kimbark Boulevard is located in the Lawrence Park South neighbourhood, southwest of the intersection of Avenue Road and Lawrence Avenue, on the west side Kimbark Boulevard. It contains Kimbark House, an early 20th century Arts and Crafts two-storey residence and one-and-a-half storey stable linked by a modern two-storey addition. A location map and current photograph of the heritage property is found in Attachment 2. The earliest structure in the neighbourhood, Kimbark House is a unique example of an early-20th century Arts and Crafts suburban estate…

What was decided

The Toronto Preservation Board recommends that: 1. City Council state its intention to designate the property at 43 Kimbark Boulevard under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act in accordance with the Statement of Significance for 43 Kimbark Boulevard - Kimbark House (Reasons for Designation) attached as Attachment 1 to the report (June 30, 2026) from the Senior Manager, Heritage Planning, Urban Design, City Planning. 2. If there are no objections to the designation, City Council authorize the City Solicitor to introduce the Bill in Council designating the property under Part IV, Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act.
