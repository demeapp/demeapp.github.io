2 Whitney Avenue - Application to Erect a Structure in the North Rosedale Heritage Conservation District

Adopted with changesJul 23, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council decided whether to let a partially built masonry wall at 2 Whitney Avenue in North Rosedale, erected without a heritage permit, stay and be finished.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve the proposal to erect a structure on, and adjacent to the property at 2 Whitney Avenue under Section 42 of the Ontario Heritage Act. The majority of the property under consideration is owned by the City of Toronto The subject site is a "B-rated" property located in the North Rosedale Heritage Conservation District (HCD). The applicant is seeking approval for the retention and completion of a partially constructed masonry wall, a structure that was erected without a heritage permit. All public and private lands within the boundaries of a heritage conservation district are designated under Part V of the Ontario Heritage Act. What was decided

The Toronto Preservation Board recommends that: 1. City Council deny the application to erect a structure on the lands adjacent to 2 Whitney Avenue in the North Rosedale Heritage Conservation District.
