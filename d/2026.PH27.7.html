The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve the alterations proposed for the designated heritage property municipally known as 405 Sherbourne Street under Section 42 of the Ontario Heritage Act, in connection with a proposed development of the subject property. The Zoning By-law Amendment application is subject to a Heritage Permit application under the Ontario Heritage Act, as the subject site is designated under Part V of the Ontario Heritage Act as part of the Cabbagetown Northwest Heritage Conservation District. The proposed development application for the subject site contemplates the construction of a 35-storey mixed-use building, replacing an existing surface parking lot. This is a City-owned property that is part of the City Council directed Toronto Builds (formerly Housing Now) initiative to develop housing, including affordable housing, on City-owned land.
