Visitor Parking Requirements for New Development

Adopted with changesFeb 4, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council decided whether to keep visitor parking rules for new buildings unchanged. Staff say a 2024 provincial law already limits what the city can require near transit, and usage data supports the current rules.

Its recorded vote was unanimous — 20 in favour, none against.

The official record

What the item proposed

The recommendations in this report build on work started in 2021 to review the parking standards in the City-wide Zoning By-law 569-2013. These standards were originally adopted by City Council in 2013, based on a series of studies conducted between 2005 and 2007. The first phase of this review resulted in amendments to the standards for automobile and bicycle parking in 2022. The second phase resulted in amendments to accessible parking, bicycle parking, and minor amendments to visitor parking in 2025. City Council directed staff to continue the work to review visitor parking standards. This report does not recommend any changes to the visitor parking standards in Zoning By-law 569-2013.
