Redevelopment Framework for 835-841 Queen Street East

AdoptedMay 7, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the Planning and Housing Committee received a redevelopment framework being explored for the WoodGreen community housing site at 835-841 Queen Street East, with a 6-to-10-storey building under review.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report responds to Council direction in item PH26.17 to complete a redevelopment framework for the WoodGreen Community Housing site at 835-841 Queen Street East, and amend the Local Agency Housing Agreement to incorporate a City-led process for the redevelopment framework at this site. Based on analysis completed by CreateTO on behalf of the Housing Development Office, a building of 6-10 storeys is being explored for further review onsite.

What was decided

The Planning and Housing Committee: 1. Received the report (April 23, 2026) from the Executive Director, Housing Development Office for information.
