The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an update on the work advancing under the Mid-Rise Housing Implementation Initiative (the "Initiative") in response to PH23.6 Missing Middle and Midrise Housing Implementation Initiative , adopted by City Council on July 23, 2025. Enabling new mid-rise housing is a key component of the City's overall housing strategy and the goal of creating a more diverse housing supply. Over the past 2 to 3 years, the City has advanced a number of policy and zoning initiatives, that now permit mid-rise housing in many more locations across the city. The focus of the Mid-Rise Housing Implementation Initiative is to support that policy work by addressing barriers and challenges that impact the viability of delivering new mid-rise housing.
