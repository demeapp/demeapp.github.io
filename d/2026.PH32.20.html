Expiry Date for Protection of Heritage Properties Listed Under the Ontario Heritage Act

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A councillor wants the Planning and Housing Committee to back asking the province to protect listed heritage properties whose register protection expires December 31, 2026.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am writing to request your support for advocating to the Provincial Government to protect heritage properties in Ontario which are at risk of expiring from municipal heritage registers at the end of the year. The protection of listed heritage properties on municipal registers will expire on December 31, 2026. Such properties have only limited, short-term (60 day) protection, and automatically removing listed properties from the registry would permit the demolition of valuable heritage properties across Ontario. As part of the More Homes Built Faster Act, 2022 (Bill 23), the province mandated that any property listed on a municipal register as of December 31, 2022, must be formally designated under the Ontario Heritage Act (OHA) by January 1, 2025, or be removed. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Premier and the Minister of Citizenship and Multiculturalism to make a regulation under the Ontario Heritage Act granting a further extension for three years, from January 1, 2027 to January 1, 2030, for any property listed on a municipal register to be formally designated under the Ontario Heritage Act. 2. City Council request the Mayor to work with other Greater Toronto Hamilton Area municipalities and the Association of Municipalities of Ontario in regard to the matter on seeking an extension to the Ontario Heritage Act timeframe for the designation of properties listed on a municipal register. 3. City Council forward this item to the Greater Toronto Hamilton Area municipalities and the Association of Municipalities of Ontario for information.
