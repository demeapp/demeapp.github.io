The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on December 16 and 17, 2025, City Council considered Item NC27.2 , entitled "Appointment of Public Members to the CreateTO Board" and appointed the following public members to the CreateTO Board, at the pleasure of Council, for the following terms of office beginning on December 18, 2025: • Bruce Davis for a term of office ending on December 19, 2029, and until a successor is appointed; and • Allison Mendes for a term of office ending on December 19, 2029, and until a successor is appointed; and Through adoption of Item NC27.2 , City Council also re-appointed Sheerin Sheikh to the CreateTO Board, at pleasure of Council, for a term of office starting on March 29, 2026, and ending on December 19, 2029, and until a successor is appointed as well as appointed Bruce Davis as Chair of the CreateTO Board.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of CreateTO received the item for information.
