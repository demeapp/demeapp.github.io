What the item proposed

In recognition of the urgent need to expedite efforts to transform Toronto’s housing system, this report provides the Board of Directors, CreateTO with a broad update of 2026 housing-related priority work items: · Sites Under Construction or Targeted to Start Construction in early 2026 (7 sites) · Sites with Secured Proponents (4 sites) · Sites in a Market Offering Process (2 sites) · Sites Being Advanced Under the City’s Public Developer Model (1 site) · Sites That Have Received Initial Planning Approvals (5 sites) · Sites Undergoing Initial Planning Approvals (5 sites) A full list of projects can be found in Attachment 1 - Housing Tracker.
