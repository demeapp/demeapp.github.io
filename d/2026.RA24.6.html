The official record

What the item proposed

As part of CreateTO’s ongoing commitment to maintaining competitive compensation and fostering employee retention, the Chief Executive Officer, CreateTO is recommending the implementation of a cost-of-living adjustment ("COLA") within the Board-approved budget. This annual practice aligns with the City of Toronto’s approved increase and reflects the organization’s responsiveness to economic conditions.

What was decided

The Board of Directors, CreateTO: 1. Approved and directed the Chief Executive Officer to implement a Cost-of-Living Adjustment (COLA) for all CreateTO employees for the 2026 fiscal year, aligned with the percentage provided by the City of Toronto to its non-union employees.
