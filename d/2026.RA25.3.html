What the item proposed

As the City of Toronto’s real estate agency, CreateTO applies a strategic city-wide lens to Toronto’s real estate holdings, develops City buildings and lands for municipal purposes and delivers real estate solutions to advance City Council’s key public policy goals and meet the program needs of City divisions, agencies, and corporations. CreateTO is a self-sustaining agency and operates on a net zero basis to the City of Toronto. Toronto has more than 8,400 properties within its real estate portfolio. CreateTO offers creative and strategic approaches to solving some of our city’s most pressing challenges, from building affordable homes and inclusive communities, to creating cultural and employment opportunities, driving economic prosperity, achieving our climate goals, and increasing our collective quality of life.
