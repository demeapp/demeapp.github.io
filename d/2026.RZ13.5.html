Toronto City Council · Decision

TO Live Policy 304 - Facility Fee Surcharge

AdoptedJun 11, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Raising the Facility Fee Surcharge on tickets by $1.00 per ticket at applicable venues, effective for all new contracts, and increasing the allocation of surcharge funds to TO Live's operations. It goes to TO Live's board for a decision. The board is also asked to raise the share of surcharge revenue going to TO Live's operations from $1.00 to $2.00 per ticket, and to approve the amended Policy 304. The increase would affect ticket buyers at TO Live venues.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to seek Board approval for updates to TO Live Policy 304 - Facility Fee Surcharge (FFS), including an increase to the Facility Fee Surcharge and an increase in the allocation of Facility Fee Surcharge funds to TO Live’s operations.

What was decided

The TO Live - Finance and Audit Committee recommends that the Board of Directors of TO Live: 1. Approve the amendments to TO Live Policy 304 Facility Fee Surcharge to increase the Facility Fee Surcharge rates by $1.00 per ticket at applicable TO Live venues, effective for all new contracts executed following Board approval. 2. Approve an increase in the operational allocation of the Facility Fee Surcharge revenues from $1.00 to $2.00 per ticket, effective for all new contracts executed following Board approval. 3. Approve the amended TO Live Policy 304 - Facility Fee Surcharge substantially in the form attached as Attachment 1 to the report (May 25, 2026) from the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live.
