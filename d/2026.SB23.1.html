What the item proposed

At its meeting on April 22 and 23, 2026, City Council considered Item 2026.CC40.2, Appointment of Public Members to the Sign Variance Committee, and appointed the following members for a term of office beginning April 24, 2026, and ending on April 24, 2030, and until successors are appointed: - Stephen Job - Farzana Jiwani These appointments fill the positions previously held by Janet Ross and Anne Milchberg, whose terms came to an end on April 23, 2026. City Council also appointed Derek Lett as Chair of the Sign Variance Committee for a term of office ending on December 16, 2027, and until a successor is appointed.
