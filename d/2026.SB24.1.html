The Sign Variance Committee decided whether to grant one variance for an electronic sign at 4925 Steeles Avenue West.

The official record

What the item proposed

Permit World Consulting Services Inc. (the "Applicant") has submitted a sign variance application requesting one variance required to allow the Chief Building Official (the "CBO") to issue a sign permit for one third party electronic ground sign with two sign faces displaying electronic static copy (the "Proposed Sign"), on the property municipally known as 4925 Steeles Avenue West (the "Subject Premises"). The Proposed Sign would have a maximum height of 10.0 metres and would contain two rectangular sign faces displayed in a back-to-back configuration, each not exceeding 3.25 metres by 6.05 metres, with a maximum sign face area of 20.0 square metres. The Proposed Sign is described further in Attachment 1 to this report. The Subject Premises is designated Employment ("E") Sign District, which allows third party electronic ground signs. What was decided

The Sign Variance Committee: 1. Granted the requested variance to section 694-25.C(2)(d), subject to two conditions, as required to allow for the issuance of a permit respecting the erection and display of the Proposed Sign, as further described in Attachment 1 to this report (June 8, 2026).
