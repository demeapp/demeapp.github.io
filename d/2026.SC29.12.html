Scarborough Community Council decided whether to install an all-way stop at Brimorton Drive and Amberjack Boulevard; staff recommend against it.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has reviewed the need for all-way stop control at the intersection of Brimorton Drive and Amberjack Boulevard. Based on the assessment undertaken, Transportation Services does not recommend the installation of all-way stop control at this intersection as the warrant criteria was not met.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Brimorton Drive and Amberjack Boulevard.
