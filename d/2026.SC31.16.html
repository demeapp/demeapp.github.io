Ingleton Boulevard (Alton Towers Neighbourhood Connections) - Traffic Calming (Speed Humps)

AdoptedApr 30, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council decided whether to install speed humps on Ingleton Boulevard as part of the Alton Towers Neighbourhood Connections project, to reduce vehicle speeds and improve safety, especially for pedestrians. Road resurfacing is planned in 2027 between Middlefield Road and Alton Towers Circle, offering a chance to bundle the work. The community council has final decision-making authority.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of speed humps on Ingleton Boulevard, as part of the Alton Towers Neighbourhood Connections project, to reduce motor vehicle operating speeds and improve roadway safety for everyone, particularly pedestrians. In 2027, road resurfacing is planned on Ingleton Boulevard between Middlefield Road and Alton Towers Circle. The planned road work provides an opportunity to review the street for changes with the goal to improve safety for all road users. Speed humps are recommended on Ingleton Boulevard for the following segments: - Middlefield Road and Shady Hollow Drive; - 80 metres west of Shady Hollow Drive and Boxdene Avenue; and - Boxdene Avenue and Alton Towers Circle.
