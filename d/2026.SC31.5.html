What the item proposed

This report clarifies the address of the subject site contained in the Revised Report and Attachments 1 to 6 and 8 to 10 from the Director, Community Planning, Scarborough District on 3130 and 3150 Danforth Avenue - Zoning By-law Amendment Application - Decision Report - Approval dated April 23, 2026, is incorrect. The correct address of the subject site is the lands municipally known as 3130 Danforth Avenue and should not include any reference to 3150 Danforth Avenue. Lands known municipally as 3150 Danforth Avenue do not form part of the subject site and is not subject to staff's recommendations. This report recommends approval of the application to amend Zoning By-law 569-2013 for the site municipally known as 3130 Danforth Avenue, to permit the development of a 33-storey mixed use building.
