Bennett Road and Dunstall Crescent - Traffic Calming (Speed Humps)

AdoptedMay 28, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council can approve nine speed humps on Bennett Road and seven on Dunstall Crescent. Staff say both streets meet the rules for traffic calming, which would slow drivers down.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Scarborough Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of speed humps on the two streets sections listed below. - Bennett Road, between Lawrence Avenue East and the south end of Bennett Road - Dunstall Crescent, between Bennett Road and Beechgrove Drive Staff's assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the updated Traffic Calming Policy has been satisfied for both streets. Therefore, speed humps should be installed on the subject sections of Bennett Road and Dunstall Crescent.
