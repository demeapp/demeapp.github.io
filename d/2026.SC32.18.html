Steeles Avenue East and Reesor Road (East Intersection) - Traffic Control Signals

AdoptedMay 28, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council can approve new traffic signals at Steeles Avenue East and Reesor Road (east intersection). Staff say the signals are justified to make the crossing safer.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting approval to install traffic control signals at the intersection of Steeles Avenue East and Reesor Road (east intersection). The traffic control signals will provide enhanced safety for all road users and is justified based on the assessment undertaken.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic control signals at Steeles Avenue East and Reesor Road (east intersection).
