Goldhawk Trail - Pedestrian Crossing Protection and Parking Amendments

AdoptedMay 28, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council can approve a pedestrian crossover on Goldhawk Trail near Banting and Best Public School. It would give students a safer place to cross.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has reviewed the need for pedestrian crossing protection on Goldhawk Trail in the vicinity of Banting and Best Public School. Based on the assessment undertaken, Transportation Services is recommending the installation of a pedestrian crossover (PXO) on Goldhawk Trail at a point 45 metres south of Enchanted Hills Crescent. The PXO will provide enhanced safety for vulnerable road users. Transportation Services is also requesting approval to amend the parking regulations on the west side of Goldhawk Trail, fronting Banting and Best Public School.
