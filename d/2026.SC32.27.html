Lynn Road, Florens Avenue, and McIntosh Street - Traffic Calming (Speed Humps)

AdoptedMay 28, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council can approve speed humps on Lynn Road, Florens Avenue, and McIntosh Street in Ward 20. Residents on all three streets raised speeding concerns and showed strong support.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Lynn Road, Florens Avenue, and McIntosh Street are local residential streets in Ward 20 where residents have raised concerns about speeding and road safety. The Councillor’s office conducted resident flyer drop surveys to better understand support for traffic calming. Strong community support for traffic calming was recorded for: Lynn Road, between Cornell Avenue and Manderley Drive Florens Avenue, between Herron Avenue and Pharmacy Avenue McIntosh Street, between Park Street and Highview Avenue The proposed speed humps are intended to reduce vehicle speeds, improve pedestrian safety, and create a calmer and safer street environment for residents, families, seniors, children, cyclists, and other road users.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services, to study the feasibility of installing traffic calming measures, including speed humps, on Lynn Road between Cornell Avenue and Manderley Drive, Florens Avenue between Herron Avenue and Pharmacy Avenue, and McIntosh Street between Park Street and Highview Avenue, and, if warranted, report back to Scarborough Community Council by the first quarter of 2027 with recommendations to authorize installation.
