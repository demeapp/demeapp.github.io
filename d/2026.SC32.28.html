Cleanside Road and Mendelssohn Street - Traffic Calming

AdoptedMay 28, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council can approve traffic calming on Cleanside Road and Mendelssohn Street. Most residents who were consulted support stronger measures to slow traffic.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Cleanside Road and Mendelssohn Street are local residential streets where residents have raised ongoing concerns about speeding, road safety, and the need for stronger traffic calming measures. These streets are used daily by residents, pedestrians, cyclists, families, seniors, children, and other vulnerable road users travelling through the neighbourhood. Through consultation and flyer drops conducted by the Councillor’s office on the affected streets, a majority of residents expressed support for the installation of speed humps. Residents noted that speeding vehicles create unsafe conditions, reduce comfort for people walking or cycling, and impact the overall safety and livability of the neighbourhood.
