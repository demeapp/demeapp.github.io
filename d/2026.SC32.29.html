Cleanside Road and Mendelssohn Street - Traffic Calming (Speed Limit Designation)

AdoptedMay 28, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council can set a 30 km/h speed limit on Cleanside Road and Mendelssohn Street. Residents asked for calmer, safer streets.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The listed streets are local residential roads where residents have raised concerns about speeding, road safety, and the need for safer neighbourhood conditions. Through consultation and flyer drops conducted by the Councillor’s office in the surrounding area, residents expressed support for measures that would help reduce vehicle speeds and improve safety. The proposed 30 km/h speed limit designation is intended to create a calmer and safer street environment for residents, pedestrians, cyclists, children, seniors, and all road users travelling through the neighbourhood. This item recommends reducing the speed limit to 30 km/h on Cleanside Road, Fusilier Drive, Deans Drive, Ferguson Street, Winkler Terrace, Yates Avenue, Pethick Street, Tranter Trail, Roper Road, Mendelssohn Street, Ayers Crescent, and Gerigs Street.
