The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 33 Falaise Road has requested Scarborough Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 1.2B(1) – Restrictions on Fences; Fence height. City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 1.2C(1) – Restrictions on Fences; Open-fence construction required The fence on the front yard on the south side of the property constructed of concrete and metal picket’s measures approximately 1.95 metres in height. The fence on the front yard North and West sides is constructed of stone pillars with metal picket fencing in between. It is measured at approximately 1.29 metres in height for the metal pickets and approximately 1.95 metres in height for the stone pillars including the lamps. The fence gate is constructed of metal pickets and is measured at approximately 2.5 metres in height at the highest point and…

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Refused to grant an exemption to Chapter 447, Fences, to the property owner of 33 Falaise Road, for the existing fence described in the Summary of the report (April 27, 2026) from the Acting District Manager, Municipal Licensing and Standards, East District.
