1571 Sandhurst Circle - Draft Plan of Subdivision Application - Decision Report - Approval

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

No council vote is needed for the subdivision plan at 1571 Sandhurst Circle: the Executive Director, Development Review, intends to approve it under delegated authority. The plan has mixed-use buildings, new streets, and two park blocks for the City.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The subject Draft Plan of Subdivision application proposes to create a total of sixteen blocks consisting of: five mixed use development blocks with buildings ranging in height from 12 to 33 storeys; two park blocks totaling approximately 7,880 square metres to be conveyed to the City for public park purposes; one block that will contain the existing mall; two private road blocks; two reserve blocks; and four public streets. The Draft Plan of Subdivision facilitates the implementation of Official Plan Amendment 675 (SASP 236) and site-specific Zoning By-law 265-2024 and supports the redevelopment of the surface parking areas surrounding the existing Woodside Square Mall into a mixed use community complete with new public parkland and public street connections.
