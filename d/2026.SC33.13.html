No council vote is needed for the subdivision plan at 9940 Sheppard Avenue East: the Executive Director, Development Review, intends to approve it under delegated authority. The plan includes homes, a new park, and a new public street.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Draft Plan of Subdivision application proposes to create a series of development blocks, a new public park, an environmental buffer, a public street and various road widenings and reserves to facilitate the development of 9940 Sheppard Avenue East with residential uses. The new public street will extend north from Sheppard Avenue East, east of Meadowvale Road, runs between the two proposed residential blocks to the east and the future development block to the west, and connect to Meadowvale Road. The park block will front the newly created street between two residential blocks and back onto lands forming part of the Rouge National Urban Park. The zoning for the subject lands, currently vacant, was approved by the Ontario Municipal Board in 1996, and the current applications assist in activating the development potential of the site.
