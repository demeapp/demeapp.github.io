The official record

What the item proposed

This report outlines the outcomes of the Coronation Drive Employment Area Review ("the Review") and presents a coordinated set of planning, economic development and transportation recommendations intended to support the long-term viability of this Employment Area while aiming to ensure appropriate compatibility with the adjacent residential Neighbourhoods . Initiated in response to issues related to heavy vehicle traffic, the Review draws on technical analysis, policy considerations and extensive stakeholder consultation. The recommendations aim to reinforce the role of the Coronation Drive Employment Area within the City's planning framework, and introduce targeted measures, such as strategic business attraction and road safety improvements, to balance economic viability with the safety and livability of the surrounding area.
