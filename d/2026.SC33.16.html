1891 Eglinton Avenue East - Class 4 Noise Area Classification (NPC-300) - Final Report

This is a procedural item: City Council can classify part of 1891 Eglinton Avenue East as a noise area. That step is needed before the planned mixed-use development can go ahead.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council classify a portion of the lands at 1891 Eglinton Avenue East as a Class 4 Noise Area under the Environmental Noise Guideline, Stationary and Transportation Sources - Approval and Planning (Publication NPC-300) ("Environmental Noise Guideline (NPC-300)") administered by the Ministry of Environment, Conservation and Parks ("MECP"). The purpose of this classification is to implement a mixed-use development on the Future Development Lands ("FDL") area identified in the Official Plan Amendment for the subject lands. A Noise Assessment Study was prepared by Arcadis in support of the development of the FDL area on the lands at 1891 Eglinton Avenue East, which confirms that the site is suitable for a Class 4 Area classification as identified in the Environmental Noise Guideline NPC-300. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council classify a portion of the lands municipally known as 1891 Eglinton Avenue East as a Class 4 Noise Area, pursuant to Publication NPC 300 (Ministry of Environment, Conservation and Parks Environmental Noise Guideline Stationary and Transportation Sources - Approval and Planning) shown as Attachment 2 to the report (June 22, 2026) from the Director, Community Planning, Scarborough District. 2. City Council forward its decision on this classification to the Ministry of Environment, Conservation and Parks.
