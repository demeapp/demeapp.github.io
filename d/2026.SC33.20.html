Metrolinx Scarborough Subway Extension - Authority to Enter into Agreements for Temporary Retaining Walls and Other Support Structures

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council can let Metrolinx build temporary retaining walls in city road space for the Scarborough Subway Extension. The walls support construction work like digging and traffic detours.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks City Council authority to enter into one or more agreements with Metrolinx respecting temporary retaining walls and other similar structures and installations required within the City road right-of-way for construction of the Scarborough Subway Extension (SSE) project. Metrolinx identified several locations where temporary retaining walls are required to facilitate traffic diversions, excavation activities, and associated construction works. The temporary retaining walls will be constructed, maintained and removed by Metrolinx as part of the SSE project, with the affected portion of the road right-of-way restored upon completion of the works.
