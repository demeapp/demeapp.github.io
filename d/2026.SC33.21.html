The official record

What the item proposed

As this report recommends the permanent closure of a portion of a public highway, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services recommends that City Council permanently stop up and close the portions of the public highway municipally known as 6600 Lawrence Avenue East, that are lands north of Lawrence Avenue East and west of Starspray Boulevard, legally described as Parts 1 and 2 on Plan 66R-34623 (the “Subject Lands”). The Subject Lands form part of the Lawrence Avenue East public right-of-way but are not required for current or future municipal transportation purposes. The lands are proposed to be transferred from Transportation Services to Parks and Recreation for the development of a public park and dog off-leash area.
