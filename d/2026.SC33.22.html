The owner of 4 Brooklawn Avenue wants to keep a fence and gate taller than the 2-metre limit. Scarborough Community Council will decide.

Official title: 4 Brooklawn Avenue - Application for Fence Exemption

Adopted with changesJul 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 4 Brooklawn Avenue has requested Scarborough Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 1.2B(1) – Restrictions on Fences; Height. The gate on the north side of the property has a height of 2.17 meters. This is higher than the allowable 2 meters. The fence on the north side of the property has a height ranging between 2.10 meters and 2.39 meters. This is higher than the allowable 2 meters. The fence on the west side of the property has a height ranging between 2.13 meters and 2.39 meters. This is higher than the allowable 2 meters. The fence on the south side of the property has a height ranging between 2.18 meters and 2.3 meters. This is higher than the allowable 2 meters. The gate on the south side of the property is 2.10 meters high. This is higher than the allowable 2 meters.…

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Granted a fence exemption permit to the property owner of 4 Brooklawn Avenue and that at such time as replacement of the fence is required, the installation comply with Municipal Code Chapter 447, Fences, or its successor by-law.
