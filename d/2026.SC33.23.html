10 Whitecap Boulevard - Application for Fence Exemption

AdoptedJul 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The owner of 10 Whitecap Boulevard wants permission to keep a pool fence that is shorter than the required 1.2 metres. Scarborough Community Council will decide.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 10 Whitecap Boulevard has requested Scarborough Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 1.3D(1) – Swimming Pool Enclosures; Enclosure Height. The pool enclosure fence has a height ranging from 1.10 meters to 1.14 meters. This is lower than the required 1.2 meters. AND City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 1.3E(2)(a) – Swimming Pool Enclosures; Construction Standards. The pool enclosure fence posts spacing ranges to a maximum of 3.12 meters. This is greater than the maximum of 2.4 meters. AND City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 1.3E(2)(a) – Swimming Pool Enclosures; Construction Standards. The pool enclosure fence posts utilize a quick detach screw and are not embedded in concrete. Enclosure posts are required to be embedded in 0.76 meters of concrete. Municipal Licensing…

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Refused to grant an exemption to Chapter 447, Fences, to the property owner of 10 Whitecap Boulevard, for the existing fence described in the Summary of the report (May 27, 2026) from the Acting District Manager, Municipal Licensing and Standards, East District.
