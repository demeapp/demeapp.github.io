What the item proposed

The Applicant for 116 Presley Avenue has requested Scarborough Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 1.2B(1) – Restrictions on Fences; Height and 447 1.2C(1) - Open Fence Construction Required The fence in the front yard on the north side of the property is within 2.4 meters of a driveway and is not made of open fence construction. The gate in the side yard on the north side of the property has a height of 2.44 meters. This is higher than the allowable 2 meters. The fence in the side and rear yard on the east side of the property has a height ranging from 2.39 meters to 2.49 meters. This is higher than the allowable 2 meters.
