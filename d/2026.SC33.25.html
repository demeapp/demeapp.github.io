The owner of 125 Presley Avenue wants to keep a rear-yard fence taller than the 2.5-metre limit. Scarborough Community Council will decide.

Official title: 125 Presley Avenue - Application for Fence Exemption

Adopted with changesJul 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 125 Presley Avenue has requested Scarborough Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 1.2B(1) – Restrictions on Fences; Height. The fence in the rear yard on the east side of the property has a height ranging from 2.84 meters to 2.87 meters. This is higher than the allowable 2.5 meters. The fence in the rear yard on the north side of the property has a height ranging from 2.26 meters to 2.87 meters. This is higher than the allowable 2 meters. Municipal Licensing and Standards Division has inspected the subject property and issued a notice of violation of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 1.2(B)(1).

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Granted a fence exemption permit to the property owner of 125 Presley Avenue and that at such time as replacement of the fence is required, the installation comply with Municipal Code Chapter 447, Fences, or its successor by-law.
