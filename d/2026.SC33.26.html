20 Chatterton Boulevard - Application for Fence Exemption

Adopted with changesJul 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The owner of 20 Chatterton Boulevard wants to keep a gate and fence taller than the 2-metre limit. Scarborough Community Council will decide.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 20 Chatterton Boulevard has requested Scarborough Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 1.2B(1) – Restrictions on Fences; Height The gate in the side yard on the west side of the property has a height of 2.12 meters. This is higher than the allowable 2 meters. The fence in the rear yard on the west side of the property has a maximum height of 2.18 meters. This is higher than the allowable 2 meters. The fence in the rear and side yard on the east side of the property has a height ranging from 2.32 meters to 2.35 meters. This is higher than the allowable 2 meters. The gate in the side yard on the east side of the property has a height of 2.32 meters. This is higher than the allowable 2 meters. City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 1.3 E(1)(b) - Swimming Pool Enclosure;…

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Granted a fence exemption permit to the property owner of 20 Chatterton Boulevard and that at such time as replacement of the fence is required, the installation comply with Municipal Code Chapter 447, Fences, or its successor by-law.
