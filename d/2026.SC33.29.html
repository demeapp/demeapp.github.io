The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 2 Deerbrook Trail has requested Scarborough Community Council to allow the proposed fence and exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 1.2B(1) – Restrictions on Fences; Height The proposed fence in the front yard on the south side of the property has a height of 2 meters. This is higher than the allowable 1.2 meters. AND City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 1.2C(1) – Restrictions on Fences; Open-Fence Construction Required The proposed fence in the rear yard on the northeast corner is within 2.4 meters of a driveway and is not made of open-fence construction. The proposed fence in the rear yard on the southeast corner (of the rear yard) is within 2.4 meters of a driveway and is not made of open-fence construction. This is a proposed fence.
