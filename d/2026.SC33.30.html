1 Summerbridge Road - Application for Fence Exemption

AdoptedJul 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The owner of 1 Summerbridge Road wants to build a 1.95-metre front fence, taller than the 1.2-metre limit. Scarborough Community Council will decide.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The owner of 1 Summerbridge Road has applied to Scarborough Community Council for a site-specific exemption from the requirements of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447-1.2B(1) - fence height and Chapter 447-1.2C(1) - open-fence construction requirements. The proposed fence will be located in the front yard along the north, west, and south sides of the property and will be constructed to a height of 1.95 metres, exceeding the maximum permitted height of 1.2 metres. The proposed fence located on the north side of the front yard will be constructed of wood and does not meet the open-fence construction requirements applicable to fences located within 2.4 metres of a driveway.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Refused to grant an exemption to Chapter 447, Fences, to the property owner of 1 Summerbridge Road, for the proposed fence described in the Summary of the report (May 19, 2026) from the Acting District Manager, Municipal Licensing and Standards, East District.
