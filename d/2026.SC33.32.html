21 Mantis Road - Application for Fence Exemption

AdoptedJul 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The owner of 21 Mantis Road wants to keep a rear fence with nails sticking out and a gate taller than the limit. The city has issued a violation notice. Scarborough Community Council will decide.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The owner of 21 Mantis Road has applied to Scarborough Community Council for a site-specific exemption from the requirements of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447-1.2A(2) - prohibited fences and Chapter 447-1.2B(1) - fence height. The existing rear yard fence is constructed with sharp nails projecting outward, which is prohibited under Chapter 447. The existing gate located on the east side of the rear yard has a height of approximately 2.08 metres, exceeding the maximum permitted height of 2.0 metres under Chapter 447. Municipal Licensing and Standards Division inspected the subject property and issued a Notice of Violation respecting the identified contraventions.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Refused to grant an exemption to Chapter 447, Fences, to the property owner of 21 Mantis Road, for the existing fence described in the Summary of the report (May 15, 2026) from the Acting District Manager, Municipal Licensing and Standards, East District.
