The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report concerns a matter for which the Scarborough Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The report responds to an application submitted by the property owner of 34 Rodarick Road requesting approval for a site-specific Fence Exemption under Section 447-1.3E(b) of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447 – Fences. The property owner is seeking Council’s permission to maintain an aluminum metal picket fence approximately 1.24 metres in height surrounding the side means of egress. The fence surrounding the side means of egress is located adjacent to a windowsill approximately 1.07 metres above grade and is not setback a minimum of one metre on either side of the doorway. What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Refused to grant the application for exemption by the owner of 34 Rodarick Road because the fence fails to comply with the provisions of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 447 Fences.
