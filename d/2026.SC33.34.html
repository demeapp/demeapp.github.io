The official record

What the item proposed

The owner of 689 Morrish Road has applied to Scarborough Community Council for a site-specific exemption from the requirements of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447-1.2B(1) respecting fence height and Chapter 447-1.2C(1) respecting open-fence construction requirements. The property owner is seeking Council’s permission to maintain an existing front yard aluminum metal picket fence and mechanical access gates enclosing the front yard and driveway. The fence, including the gates, is approximately 2.0 metres in height and is located within 2.4 metres of the driveway entrance on both the north and south sides, exceeding the maximum permitted height of 1.2 metres under Chapter 447. What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Deferred consideration of the item until the first Scarborough Community Council meeting of 2027.
