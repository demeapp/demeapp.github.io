1650 Military Trail (Phase 2) - Construction Staging Area

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A construction staging area for an 8-storey student rental at 1650 Military Trail goes to City Council for approval. The project needs road and lane closures in two phases.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As Military Trail is classified as a collector street, City Council approval of this report is required. Altree Developments is constructing an 8-storey purpose-built student rental at 1650 Military Trail. The site is located on the northwest corner of Military Trail and Morrish Road. Construction will be undertaken in two phases with varying degrees of boulevard and lane closures taking place on Morrish Road and Military Trail. At the time of this report, Phase 1 (shoring and excavation) is in progress on Morrish Road. Phase 2 (building construction) will require the continuation of the occupation of Morris Road and the additional occupation of Military Trail. This report deals with the matters related to Phase 2. City Council authorize the closure of the north boulevard allowance and a 5.1 metre wide portion of the westbound curb lane on Military Trail, between Kingston Road and a point 72.5 metres west, from September 30, 2026, to July 31, 2028, inclusive. 2. City Council rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the north side of Military Trail, between Kingston Road and a point 72.5 metres west. 3. City Council prohibit stopping at all times on the north side of Military Trail, between Kingston Road and a point 72.5 metres west. 4.
