Two new pedestrian crossovers on Lawrence Avenue East, at Portwine Drive and west of Starspray Boulevard, go to City Council for approval.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Lawrence Avenue East, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting approval to install pedestrian crossovers at the following locations: - Lawrence Avenue East and Portwine Drive (west intersection) - Lawrence Avenue East and a point 280 metres west of Starspray Boulevard Based on the assessments undertaken, the installation of pedestrian crossovers at these locations are recommended and should enhance safety for all road users.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the installation of a pedestrian crossover at the intersection of Lawrence Avenue East and Portwine Drive (west intersection). 2. City Council authorize the installation of a pedestrian crossover at Lawrence Avenue East and a point 280 metres west of Starspray Boulevard.
