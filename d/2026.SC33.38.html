Morrish Road Operational Closure at Highway 2A/Military Trail - Road Alteration

Adopted with changesJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council can close the south end of Morrish Road with a cul-de-sac and shut the Highway 2A off-ramp to Morrish Road. The changes are an interim step in the Highland Creek Village transport plan.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services, in collaboration with Development Review, is requesting City Council authorization to implement road alterations at Highway 2A/Military Trail as an interim measure to achieve the goals of the Highland Creek Village Transportation Master Plan (TMP). This work will comprise the following: - The closure of the south end of Morrish Road through the installation of a cul‑de‑sac - The closure of the Highway 2A/Kingston Road off‑ramp to Morrish Road - Associated roadway and geometric modifications - The delivery of pedestrian connections and landscaping on the closed portion of Morrish Road - Required traffic by-law amendments The TMP was adopted by City Council in May 2021 and provides the policy context for the proposed changes.
