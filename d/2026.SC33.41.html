Terraview Boulevard - Traffic Calming (Speed Humps) and Heavy Truck Prohibition

AdoptedJul 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council can approve speed humps on Terraview Boulevard between Pharmacy Avenue and Victoria Park Avenue. The item also deals with a heavy truck rule.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of speed humps on Terraview Boulevard, between Pharmacy Avenue and Victoria Park Avenue. Staff's assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the Traffic Calming Policy has been satisfied. Therefore, speed humps should be installed on Terraview Boulevard. During the course of the investigation, it was noted that there is discrepancy between the by-lawed heavy vehicle prohibition and the posted signage on Terraview Boulevard. Recommendation 4 will correct this inconsistency.

What was decided
