The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Scarborough Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the installation of speed humps on the two streets sections listed below. - Highcastle Road, between Sealstone Terrace and Military Trail - Pineslope Crescent, between Oakmeadow Boulevard and Highcastle Road Staff's assessment indicates the criteria as set out in the Traffic Calming Policy has been satisfied for both streets. Therefore, speed humps should be installed on the subject sections of Highcastle Road and Pineslope Crescent.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (eight speed humps) on Highcastle Road, between Sealstone Terrace and Military Trail, generally as shown in the Attachments 3 and 4, Drawing Nos. TC-723 and TC-723-1 to the report (June 22, 2026) from the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services. 2. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (five speed humps) on Pineslope Crescent, between Oakmeadow Boulevard and Highcastle Road, generally as shown in the Attachment 5, Drawing No. TC-722 to the report (June 22, 2026) from the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services. 3. Subject to approval of Part 1 above, authorized the amendment of Schedule XLV (Part 1) to City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 950, Traffic and Parking, to remove Highcastle Road between Military Trail and Oakmeadow Boulevard…
