Staff say Rouge River Drive does not meet the rules for speed humps and recommend against them. Scarborough Community Council will decide.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has reviewed the need for speed humps on Rouge River Drive, between Sheppard Avenue East (west intersection) and Sheppard Avenue East (east intersection). The assessment indicates the warrant criteria as set out in the Traffic Calming Policy has not been satisfied. Therefore, staff do not recommend the installation of speed humps on Rouge River Drive.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (15 speed humps) on Rouge River Drive, between Sheppard Avenue East (west intersection) and Sheppard Avenue East (east intersection), generally as shown in Attachments 3, 4, 5, and 6, Drawing Nos. TC-725, TC-725-1, TC-725-2, and TC-725-3, to the report (June 22, 2026) from the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services. 2. Subject to approval of Part 1 above, Scarborough Community Council reduced the speed limit from 40 km/h to 30 km/h on Rouge River Drive, between Sheppard Avenue East (west intersection) and Sheppard Avenue East (east intersection), in conjunction with the installation of speed humps.
