The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Sheppard Avenue East, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting approval to remove the pedestrian refuge islands (PRI) and replace with traffic control signals at the following two locations on Sheppard Avenue East: - At the existing PRI located approximately 10 metres west of Massie Street, to be replaced with traffic control signals at the intersection with Massie Street - At the existing PRI located approximately 440 metres east of McCowan Road, to be replaced with mid-block traffic control signals approximately 390 metres east of McCowan Road The two traffic control signals will provide enhanced safety for all road users and are justified based on the assessments undertaken. City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the installation of traffic control signals at the intersection of Sheppard Avenue East and Massie Street. 2. City Council prohibit pedestrian crossings on Sheppard Avenue East, between the west curb line of Massie Street and a point 30.5 metres east of the east curb line of Massie Street, in conjunction with the installation of traffic control signals as set out in Part 1 above. 3. City Council authorize the installation of traffic control signals on Sheppard Avenue East at a point 390 metres east of McCowan Road. 4. In conjunction with the installation of traffic control signals at the intersection of Sheppard Avenue East and Massie Street, City Council authorize the removal of the existing pedestrian refuge island on Sheppard Avenue East, approximately 10 metres west of Massie Street. 5.
