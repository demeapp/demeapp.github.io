Guildwood Parkway - Traffic Regulations

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

U-turns and some turns would be banned in front of Sir Wilfrid Laurier Collegiate Institute on Guildwood Parkway during school peak times. City Council is asked to approve.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Guildwood Parkway, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is recommending the following traffic amendments on Guildwood Parkway in front of Sir Wilfrid Laurier Collegiate Institute: - Prohibiting eastbound and westbound U-turn movements on Guildwood Parkway, fronting the school - Prohibiting eastbound right turns and westbound left turns during school peak hours at Guildwood Parkway and the school’s western driveway access The proposed changes will improve the traffic flow and safety for all road users in front of the school. These recommendations are being made in consultation with the Sir Wilfrid Laurier Collegiate Institute administration.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit eastbound and westbound U-turn movements at all times on Guildwood Parkway, between Livingston Road and a point 100 metres east of Chancery Lane. 2. City Council prohibit eastbound right turns, from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, at Guildwood Parkway and the driveway access at 145 Guildwood Parkway, located on the south side of Guildwood Parkway at a point 118 metres east of Livingston Road. 3. City Council prohibit westbound left turns, from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, at Guildwood Parkway and the driveway access at 145 Guildwood Parkway, located on the south side of Guildwood Parkway at a point 118 metres east of Livingston Road.
