Expansion of the Free-Floating Car-Share Program into Ward 20

AdoptedJul 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Scarborough Community Council can expand the free-floating car-share program into part of Ward 20. The car-share operator asked for it because of demand.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The report recommends expanding the Free-Floating Car-Share Program operating area into a portion of Ward 20, consistent with the expansion framework approved by City Council in October 2025. The expansion was requested by the City's sole active free-floating car-share operator in response to observed demand and has been developed in consultation with City staff and the local Ward Councillor. If approved, free-floating car-share vehicles operating under a valid permit would be exempt from the City-wide three-hour unsigned parking limit and parking regulations imposing maximum parking periods of one hour or more within the approved expansion area.
