Anson Avenue Area Traffic Calming and Safety Review

AdoptedJul 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A traffic safety review for Anson Avenue near Anson Park Junior Public School is up for Scarborough Community Council to order. Residents raised concerns about speeding and school-area safety.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Safety measures are needed on Anson Avenue and the surrounding area to help ensure the safety of the community, particularly for families, children, students, seniors, pedestrians, cyclists, and other vulnerable road users travelling near Anson Park Junior Public School. Residents have raised ongoing concerns about speeding, cut-through traffic, traffic volumes, pedestrian safety, parking conditions, and school-area safety, especially during student drop-off and pick-up times when pedestrian activity is higher. At recent community consultation, organized by my office, residents identified Oakridge Drive as an example of a nearby street where road safety measures, including all-way stop controls and traffic calming interventions, have helped address similar neighbourhood concerns. Following resident feedback, we recommend that City staff explore comparable safety improvements for Anson…

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services, to study the feasibility of implementing safety improvements along Anson Avenue and the surrounding area, including but not limited to speed humps, all-way stop controls, and report back to Scarborough Community Council in the first quarter of 2027. 2. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services, to review the need for additional signage, speed measurement measures, pavement markings and other appropriate Vision Zero measures, and report back to Scarborough Community Council in the first quarter of 2027.
