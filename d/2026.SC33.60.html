This is a procedural item: Scarborough Community Council can fix the Claremore Avenue speed hump plan by adding a drawing that was left out.

Official title: Claremore Avenue - Traffic Calming (Speed Humps)

AdoptedJul 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services staff identified that one of the drawings intended to be included as part of the approved speed hump location plan for Claremore Avenue was inadvertently omitted from the staff report titled "Claremore Avenue – Traffic Calming (Speed Humps)," adopted by Scarborough Community Council on April 3, 2025. To ensure the by-law accurately reflects the full scope of the approved speed hump location plan, staff are requesting that Scarborough Community Council amend Item 2025.SC21.12 to include the omitted drawing (Drawing No. TC-467). This amendment will correct the record and align the decision with the intended design.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Amended Part 2 of its decision on Item SC21.12 to include Drawing No. TC-467, dated February 2025, so that it now reads as follows: Direct the City Solicitor to prepare a by-law to alter the roadway for the installation of 11 speed humps on Claremore Avenue, between Park Street and McIntosh Street, generally, as shown on Attachment 2, Drawing TC-466, to the report (March 18, 2025) from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services and Attachment 1, Drawing No. TC-467, to the letter (July 9, 2026) from Councillor Parthi Kandavel.
