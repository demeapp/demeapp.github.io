Westbourne Avenue - Traffic Calming

AdoptedJul 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Traffic calming on Westbourne Avenue between Donside Drive and St. Clair Avenue East is up for Scarborough Community Council to order. Residents raised speeding and safety concerns.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Westbourne Avenue is a local residential street that also functions as a neighbourhood throughfare between Donside Drive and St. Clair Avenue East. Residents have raised ongoing concerns about speeding, traffic volumes, and road safety, particularly given the number of pedestrians, cyclists, families, seniors, children, and other vulnerable road users who use this street daily. Through consultation by the Councillor’s office, residents expressed support for stronger traffic calming measures to help reduce vehicle speeds and improve overall safety and comfort in the neighbourhood. The proposed installation of thirteen speed humps is intended to discourage unsafe driving, calm traffic, and create a safer street environment for all road users.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (thirteen speed humps) on Westbourne Avenue, between Donside Drive and St. Clair Avenue East, generally as shown in Attachments 1, 2 and 3, Drawing Nos. TC-754, TC-754-1 and TC 754-2, to the letter (July 9, 2026) from Councillor Parthi Kandavel.
