Kelsonia Avenue and Scarboro Crescent - All-Way Compulsory Stop Controls

AdoptedJul 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council

All-way stop signs at Kelsonia Avenue and Scarboro Crescent are up for Scarborough Community Council, with residents raising concerns about driver behaviour and pedestrian safety.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents in the surrounding neighbourhood have raised ongoing concerns about road safety at the intersection of Kelsonia Avenue and Scarboro Crescent, particularly related to driver compliance with traffic rules, right-of-way issues, and the safety of pedestrians navigating the intersection. The area is close to vulnerable populations, including seniors, children, and families, making safety especially important. Through consultation my office, residents have expressed support for stronger traffic safety measures at this intersection. The installation of all-way compulsory stop controls is intended to improve driver compliance, clarify right-of-way, and enhance safety for pedestrians and other vulnerable road users.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized an all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Kelsonia Avenue and Scarboro Crescent.
