Scarborough Community Council can approve speed humps on Danzig Street. Residents asked for them and a survey showed full support.

Official title: Danzig Street - Traffic Calming

AdoptedJul 9, 2026 · Toronto City Council
No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This letter is a follow-up to the request of community members to implement traffic calming measures on Danzig Street. Staff have completed the necessary drawings for the installation of speed humps. Community members have asked our office for this, and our office surveyed the residents who live on the road who are fully supportive. The recommendations to install speed humps and cushions is another step towards the introduction of additional traffic calming measures in an area that has a need for increased traffic safety.

What was decided

The Scarborough Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (six speed humps) on Danzig Street, between Morningside Avenue and Deekshill Drive, generally as shown in Attachments 1 and 2, Drawing Nos. TC-742 and TC-742-1, to the letter (July 9, 2026) from Councillor Neethan Shan. 2. Subject to approval of Part 1 above, reduced the speed limit from 50 km/h to 30 km/h on Danzig Street, between Morningside Avenue and Deekshill Drive, in conjunction with the installation of speed humps.
