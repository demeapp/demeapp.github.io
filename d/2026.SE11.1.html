What the item proposed

This report provides an overview of the Customer Experience Division's (CXD) 2025 Annual Report, outlining important service issues and trends, as directed by City Council. CXD, through 311, serves as the City of Toronto's primary point of access for 24/7 non-emergency services and information through 311's channels, including phone, email, online, mobile app, live chat, X, and in-person counters. In 2025, CXD supported over 1.58 million customer interactions, a 5.7 percent increase from 2024, reflecting a sustained demand for City services and information. Attachment 1 - 2025 Annual Report - Customer Experience Division (311) (Annual Report) provides a detailed look at CXD's operations in 2025, including 311 data trends, phone line metrics and strategic project updates.
