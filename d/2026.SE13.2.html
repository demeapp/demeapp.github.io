Using 311 and Other Data to Identify Trends and Improve Service Delivery in Solid Waste Management Services

The Service Excellence Committee asked Solid Waste Management to show how it uses 311 data to spot trends and improve garbage and recycling service. This report brings those answers back.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on March 23, 2026, the Service Excellence Committee requested the General Manager, Solid Waste Management Services (SWMS) to report back to the Service Excellence Committee at its meeting on July 6, 2026, on how the Division uses 311 service request (SR) data, as well as other data sources, to identify trends and make service improvements. Solid Waste Management Services utilizes a range of integrated data sources to support informed decision making and ensure safe, efficient and timely delivery of services. This includes tonnage data, asset information, geospatial data, operational performance data and 311 service request data. These data sources play a critical role in planning and allocating sufficient resources, identifying trends, and forecasting budget. They also support the Division with asset lifecycle management, safe and efficient coordination and optimization…

What was decided
