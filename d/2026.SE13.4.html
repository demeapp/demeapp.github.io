Using 311 and Other Data to Drive Service Excellence in Urban Forestry

AdoptedJul 6, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The Service Excellence Committee asked how 311 data is used to improve Urban Forestry services like tree care. This report brings those answers back.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its meeting on March 23, 2026, the Service Excellence Committee requested the General Manager Toronto Water, the General Manager, Solid Waste Management Services and the Executive Director, Environment, Climate and Forestry (ECF) to report back to the Service Excellence Committee, on how they use 311 data, as well as other data sources to identify trends and make service improvements. In response, this report provides an overview of how 311 and other data is used specifically in Urban Forestry to support service delivery. Environment, Climate and Forestry uses this data alongside work management systems, long-term strategic and operations planning, to drive service delivery, pilot service efficiencies and manage issues related to the city’s urban forest.

What was decided

The Service Excellence Committee: 1. Received the report (June 19, 2026) from the Executive Director, Environment, Climate and Forestry, for information.
