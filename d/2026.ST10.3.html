The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to recommend to City Council the appointment of a Council Member to fill a vacancy on the Toronto Preservation Board, resulting from the resignation of Councillor Brad Bradford from the Board, effective January 3, 2025.

What was decided

The Striking Committee referred the item back to the City Clerk and directed the City Clerk to conduct a new canvass of Members of Council for their interest in appointment to the Toronto Preservation Board.
