Proposed 2026 Schedule of Property Standards Committee, Toronto and East York Panel Meetings

AdoptedJan 26, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a procedural item: the Property Standards Committee's Toronto and East York Panel will set its 2026 meeting dates. Meetings stay remote for now.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In accordance with Section 9A of the Consolidated Rules of Procedure for the Property Standards Committee, as amended, each Panel shall adopt a schedule of meetings to hear appeals for each year. Please note that meetings for the Property Standards Committee, Toronto and East York Panel, will be held remotely until further notice.
