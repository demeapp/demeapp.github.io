2026 Annual Plan

AdoptedFeb 13, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto Atmospheric Fund board can approve the fund's 2026 Annual Plan, focused on cutting carbon emissions in the Toronto-Hamilton area.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The 2026 Annual Plan is submitted for Board review and approval. The plan is anchored by TAF’s Strategic Directions 2024-2030 and structured around four Focus Areas representing the largest sources of carbon emissions in the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area. The Annual Plan outlines Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s long-range objectives organized by Focus Area, with in-year targets for 2026, and then specific activities and initiatives undertaken by teams across Toronto Atmospheric Fund in service of those targets.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Approved the 2026 Annual Plan as set out in Attachment 1 to the report (January 30, 2026) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Atmospheric Fund.
