Toronto City Council · Decision

Direct Investment Request: Affordable Housing Guarantee Pool

AdoptedApr 28, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto Atmospheric Fund board can commit $5 million to a guarantee pool that backs loans for affordable, zero-carbon rental housing. Other investors must put in at least $10 million first.

The official summary names a figure of $5 million.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Direct Investment Committee recommends an unfunded amount of $5 million to the affordable housing guarantee pool. This investment is strongly aligned with Toronto Atmospheric Fund's (TAF) mandate to mobilize capital and accelerate delivery of affordable, zero‑carbon, non‑profit multi‑unit residential (MURB) housing. By using a pooled guarantee structure, it has the ability to unlock construction financing while limiting Toronto Atmospheric Fund's (TAF) exposure to its committed amount. The proposed allocation complies with Toronto Atmospheric Fund's (TAF) investment policy and asset allocation framework.
