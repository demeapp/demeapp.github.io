Draft Audited Consolidated Financial Statements for the Year Ended December 31, 2025

Adopted with changesApr 28, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto Atmospheric Fund board can approve its 2025 audited financial statements and move $1,303,938, $561,021, and $1,487,882 into its stabilization fund.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The 2025 Audited Consolidated Financial Statements have been prepared by Doane Grant Thornton and are presented to the Toronto Atmospheric Fund (TAF) Board of Directors for approval. These statements provide separate fund accounting for the endowment funds provided to the Toronto Atmospheric Fund by the City of Toronto, Province of Ontario, and Government of Canada.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Approved the transfer of: a. $1,303,938 from the Toronto General Fund (unrestricted) to the Internally restricted Stabilization Fund; b. $561,021from the Ontario General Fund (unrestricted) to the Internally restricted Stabilization Fund; and, c. $1,487,882 from the Canada General Fund (unrestricted) to the Internally restricted Stabilization Fund. 2. Approved the consolidated financial statements of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund for the year ended December 31, 2025 as presented in Attachment 1 to the report (April 24, 2026) from the Director, Finance, Toronto Atmospheric Fund, together with the related notes thereto, subject to: a. completion of management’s and the auditors’ subsequent event procedures through the date of issuance; and b. the possible inclusion of additional disclosure on the matter to appropriately describe…
