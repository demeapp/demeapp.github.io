Direct Investment Request: Extension of Working Capital Facility (DI.2026.F)

AdoptedJul 13, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The Toronto Atmospheric Fund board can extend its $4.5 million working-capital loan facility with an EV charging company to cover new provincial ChargeON grants.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Direct Investment Committee recommends amending Toronto Atmospheric Fund's bridge financing facility with the Company, a provider of integrated electric vehicle fast-charging and battery energy storage solutions, to extend the facility to accommodate newly awarded grants backed by the provincial governments electric vehicle ChargeON Program.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Approved an amendment to the existing 2025 Credit Agreement governing its $4.5 million revolving working-capital facility with the Company, to accommodate bridge financing of ElectricVehicle ChargeON Round 2 receivables (the Programme), representing Ontario Government financing of up to half of the cost of each of the 45 sites awarded to the Company under the Programme, subject to the following conditions: a. the amendment to be on substantially the same terms as the existing facility, except as required to reflect the Programme, including: i. extension of the advance period; ii. updates to the advance mechanics to reflect the Round 2 Programme structure and eligible site-level funding parameters; and, iii. inclusion of a right for Toronto Atmospheric Fund to substitute financed projects where an originally funded site is…
