The official record

What the item proposed

The Direct Investment Committee recommends the Board approve an investment of up to $400,000 in preferred shares of a Canadian Electric Vehicles charging enablement company as part of a $2,000,000 seed financing. The company’s Artificial Intelligence enabled power management systems supports Level 2 Electric Vehicles charging in capacity-constrained buildings and fleet applications, reducing the need for costly electrical upgrades. The investment aligns with Toronto Atmospheric Fund’s clean transportation, retrofit, and electrification priorities, supports a Toronto-based company with early Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area traction, and is projected to enable approximately 2.19 million tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent in cumulative emissions reductions over 20 years.

What was decided

The Board of Directors of the Toronto Atmospheric Fund: 1. Approved an investment of up to $400,000 in preferred equity in the Company, subject to the following conditions: a. completion of all required documentation to effect Toronto Atmospheric Fund's investment in the Company, on terms satisfactory to the Vice President, Impact Investing; and, b. satisfactory review of all legal documentation by Toronto Atmospheric Fund's legal counsel, as determined by the Vice President, Impact Investing and the Chief Executive Officer. 2.
