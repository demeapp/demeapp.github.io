5 Huntley Street and 2, 4, 6 and 8 Earl Street and a Portion of 10 Earl Street - Rental Housing Demolition Application - Decision Report - Approval

AdoptedFeb 4, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council has the final say on whether to approve demolishing seven rentals at 6 and 8 Earl Street; two were already torn down without a permit in 2024. Nine replacement rentals would be protected for 20 years, and the approval carries a $20,000.00 financial condition.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of a Rental Housing Demolition application which proposes to demolish 7 rental dwelling units located at 6 and 8 Earl street. Two rental units were demolished without a permit in 2024. The 7 existing rental units and 2 previously demolished units are proposed to be replaced as 9 new rental dwelling units part of the new development on the site. The proposal includes a Tenant Assistance Plan that addresses the right of existing tenants to return to replacement rental units at similar rents and provides financial compensation to mitigate hardship. The proposed development on the site at 5 Huntley Street and 2-8 Earl Street and a portion of 10 Earl Street is the subject of a related Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application (24 140234 STE 13 OZ).
