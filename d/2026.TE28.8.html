AdoptedFeb 4, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The decision on whether to approve demolishing 61 rentals at 137 and 141 Isabella Street, to be replaced within a 69-storey development rests with City Council. Tenants get help and a right of return at similar rents, and the approval carries a $20,000.00 financial condition.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of a Rental Housing Demolition application which proposes to demolish a 7-storey apartment building and 3-storey semi-detached dwelling containing a total of 61 rental dwelling units at 137 and 141 Isabella Street. The 61 rental units are proposed to be replaced as part of the new development on the site. The proposal includes a Tenant Assistance Plan that addresses the right of existing tenants to return to replacement rental units at similar rents and provides financial compensation to mitigate hardship. The proposed development on the site at 137 and 141 Isabella Street is the subject of a related Zoning By-law Amendment application 24 119153 STE 13 OZ which was appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT).
