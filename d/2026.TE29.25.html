Use of Nathan Phillips Square for Various Events - May 1 - September 30, 2026

AdoptedMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

It's City Council's call whether to let about 21 events — festivals, markets, and cultural celebrations — use Nathan Phillips Square from May to September 2026, with some serving beer and wine under provincial and health approvals.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The City of Toronto provides opportunities to access Nathan Phillips Square for eligible events through a permitting process administered by the Economic Development and Culture Division. The purpose of this report is to request exemptions from Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 636, Public Squares, and the former City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 237, Nathan Phillips Square, for events that are proposed to be held on Nathan Phillips Square from May 1 - September 30, 2026.
