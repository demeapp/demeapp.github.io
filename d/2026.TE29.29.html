30 Bond Street (St. Michael's Hospital) - Construction Staging Area

Adopted with changesFeb 19, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Whether to close a parking lay-by on Shuter Street from March 2026 to February 2027 for staging during the St. Michael's Hospital renovation now sits with the community council. Sidewalks, traffic lanes, and bike lanes would stay open.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. EllisDon Corporation has undertaken the renovation of St. Michael's Hospital at 30 Bond Street. The site is located at the southeast corner of Victoria Street and Shuter Street. Transportation Services is requesting authorization to temporarily close the parking lay-by on the south side of Shuter Street, between a point 10.5 metres east of Victoria Street and a point 17 metres further east, for a period of 12 months, from March 10, 2026 to February 28, 2027, in order to accommodate a construction staging area for the renovation project. Pedestrian operations will be maintained within the existing sidewalk on the south side of Shuter Street. The existing traffic and bicycle lanes on both sides of Shuter Street will be maintained.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the closure of the lay-by on the south side of Shuter Street, between Victoria Street and a point 40 metres east, from March 10, 2026 to May 31, 2027, inclusive. 2. Rescinded the existing standing prohibition in effect at all times, emergency vehicles excepted, on the south side of Shuter Street, between a point 10.5 metres east of Victoria Street and a point 17 metres further east. 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the south side of Shuter Street, between a point 10.5 metres east of Victoria Street and a point 17 metres further east. 4. Directed the applicant to pressure wash or sweep (weather permitting) the construction site and adjacent sidewalks and roadways daily, or more frequently as needed to be cleared of any construction debris and made safe. 5.
