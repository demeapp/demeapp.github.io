Permanent Closure of Part of the Public Laneway Abutting 1684-1702 Queen Street East

Adopted with changesMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council is set to decide whether to permanently close part of a laneway near 1702 Queen Street East and sell it to the neighbouring developer. Staff say the lane is not needed for public use.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As this report recommends the permanent closure of a public laneway, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services recommends that City Council permanently close a portion of the public laneway located immediately west of Penny Lane and north of 1702 Queen Street East, described as Part of Lane on Registered Plan 435E and designated as Part 1 on Plan 66R-34694 (the “Subject Lane”). Transportation Services has determined that the Subject Lane is not required for public right-of-way or transportation purposes. Once closed, it is intended that the former public laneway will be sold to the applicant, Queen Kingston Holdings Inc., as nominee for and on behalf of Belgrove Beaches Limited Partnership, through the City’s real property disposal process administered by Corporate Real Estate Management, under authority delegated in Article II of Municipal Code Chapter…

What was decided

City Council on March 25 and 26, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the permanent closure of the portion of the public highway being Part of Lane on Plan 435E, designated as Part 1 on Reference Plan 66R‑34694, shown on Attachments 1 and 2 to the report (December 18, 2025) from the Acting Deputy General Manager, Transportation Services. 2. City Council direct the City Solicitor to prepare and submit for enactment any necessary Bills to give effect to City Council’s decision, including the closing By-law substantially in the form of the draft By-law attached as Attachment 3 to the report (December 18, 2025) from the Acting Deputy General Manager, Transportation Services.
