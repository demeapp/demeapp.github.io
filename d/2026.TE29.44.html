Toronto City Council · Decision

Jones Avenue - Parking Amendments

Adopted with changesMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council weighed whether to change no-parking to no-stopping on the east side of Jones Avenue near Ravina Crescent, to improve sightlines. An outdated west-side parking ban would be removed to match the posted signs.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Jones Avenue, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is recommending that the existing parking prohibition on the east side of Jones Avenue, between Ravina Crescent and a point 20 metres further south, be changed to a stopping prohibition. The proposed amendment will further deter parking at this location and will improve sight lines for drivers making southbound and northbound turns from Ravina Crescent. During the course of the investigation, it was noted that there is a discrepancy between the posted signs and the existing parking regulations. Recommendation 2 will correct this inconsistency.

What was decided

City Council on March 25 and 26, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit stopping at all times on the east side of Jones Avenue, between Ravina Crescent and a point 20 metres south. 2. City Council rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Jones Avenue, between Harcourt Avenue and a point 30.5 metres north. 3. City Council direct the General Manager, Transportation Services, to install flexible bollards on the east side of Jones Avenue just south of Ravina Crescent to delineate the No Stopping area and enhance pedestrian crossing safety with installation to occur by the second quarter of 2026, and to design and install a permanent island as soon as possible after that.
