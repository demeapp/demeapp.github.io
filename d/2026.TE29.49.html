College Street/Carlton Street Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) Turning Exemptions

AdoptedMar 25, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Whether to exempt TTC buses from two left-turn bans — at Gerrard/Yonge and Carlton/Jarvis — so buses replacing the 506 Carlton streetcar can run their detour during track repairs starting March 1, 2026 now sits with City Council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Starting as early as March 1, 2026, the City of Toronto and the Toronto Transit Commission will begin critical state of good repair work on streetcar tracks at the intersection of College Street and Bay Street, requiring the 506 Carlton streetcar and 19 Bay bus services to divert. To increase service coverage, the 506 Carlton streetcar will be supplemented with a 506 Carlton bus replacement service which will provide transit coverage closer to the original 506 Carlton streetcar route. For the Toronto Transit Commission to operate the diversions legally and effectively, two Toronto Transit Commission vehicle exemptions to existing left-turn restrictions along the 506 Carlton bus replacement routing are required.
