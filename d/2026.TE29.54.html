Dewbourne Avenue Parking Amendments

AdoptedFeb 19, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A decision on lifting the 8 to 10 a.m. weekday parking ban on the south side of Dewbourne Avenue sits with the community council. Residents say the ban, aimed at Metrolinx workers, is no longer needed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am kindly asking for your support of my motion to rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect on the south side of Dewbourne Avenue, from Chiltern Hill Road to Glen Cedar Road. Dewbourne Avenue residents have requested that this parking prohibition be rescinded, citing the difficulty of finding parking on their street. The prohibition was installed to deter Metrolinx construction workers from parking on Dewbourne Avenue and is no longer required. With the passage of this motion, the community will receive the parking reforms they have been organizing for.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing the parking prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Dewbourne Avenue, between Glen Cedar Road and Chiltern Hill Road.
