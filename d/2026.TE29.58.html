Traffic Calming - Walter Street (between Kingston Road and Lyall Avenue)

AdoptedFeb 19, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council weighed whether to approve three speed humps on Walter Street, between Kingston Road and Lyall Avenue. Residents petitioned for them over pedestrian safety concerns.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents have reached out to my office with concerns about pedestrian safety on Walter Street and on surrounding neighbourhood streets. Our office has received a petition from residents in support of the installation of speed humps on Walter Street. I am requesting that speed humps be installed on Walter Street between Kingston Road and Lyall Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (three speed humps) on Walter Street, between Kingston Road and Lyall Avenue, generally as shown on the Attachment 2 to the letter (February 18, 2026) from Councillor Brad Bradford.
