Reopening TE29.67, 60 and 64 Dundas Street East - Residential Demolition Application

AdoptedFeb 27, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine step: the community council redid its decision on tearing down 60 and 64 Dundas Street East, which accidentally adopted all three options. The fix approves the demolition with conditions like fencing and debris removal.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At its February 19, 2026 meeting, the Toronto and East York Community Council adopted the full set of staff recommendations for Item TE29.67. However, the staff recommendations were a choice between Option 1, Option 2 or Option 3. It is therefore necessary for the Toronto and East York Community Council to re-open the Item in order to clarify its decision. This motion is urgent as Toronto Metropolitan University requires this permission to demolish 60 and 64 Dundas Street East in order to proceed with necessary work before road and construction restrictions related to the FIFA 2026 World Cup come into effect this year.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item TE29.67 entitled "60 and 64 Dundas Street East - Residential Demolition Application." 2. Deleted its decision on Item TE29.67 and adopted Option 3 in the report (February 11, 2026) from the Deputy Chief Building Official and Director, Toronto and East York District as follows: 3.
