Renaming of Chestnut Street Extending between Edward Street and Elm Street

AdoptedApr 1, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council has the final say on whether to rename part of Chestnut Street, between Edward and Elm Streets, 'Heather McGregor Way,' honouring the retiring YWCA Toronto CEO.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that the name "Heather McGregor Way" be approved to rename a section of Chestnut Street extending between Edward Street and Elm Street. This naming proposal complies with the City of Toronto Street Naming Policy which can be found at https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/planning-development/street-naming/ This naming proposal is in alignment with the objectives of the Guiding Principles for Commemoration in the Public Realm.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the name "Heather McGregor Way" for renaming a section of Chestnut Street extending between Edward Street and Elm Street.
