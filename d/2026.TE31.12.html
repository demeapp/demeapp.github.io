1196-1210 Yonge Street and 2-8 Birch Avenue - Rental Housing Demolition Application - Decision Report - Approval

AdoptedApr 22, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A decision on tearing down eight rentals at 1196-1210 Yonge Street and 2-8 Birch Avenue goes to City Council. The eight replacement rentals would be built off-site at Poplar Plains Road with tenant help, and the approval carries a $20,000.00 financial condition.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of a Rental Housing Demolition application which proposes to demolish 8 rental dwelling units located at 1196-1210 Yonge Street and 2-8 Birch Avenue. The 8 rental units are proposed to be replaced offsite within new multiplexes at 257 and 261 Poplar Plains Road. The proposal includes a Tenant Assistance Plan that addresses the right of existing tenants to move directly from their existing unit to one of the replacement units at similar rents and provides financial compensation to mitigate hardship. The properties at 1196-1210 Yonge Street and 2-8 Birch Avenue are the subject of a Zoning By-law Amendment application (21 239174 STE 12 OZ), which was appealed to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT).
