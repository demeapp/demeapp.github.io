11 Lynwood Avenue - Application for Fence Exemption

Adopted with changesApr 1, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The owner of 11 Lynwood Avenue wants to build a rear-yard fence 2.37 metres tall, above the city's two-metre limit. The community council is asked to refuse the exemption.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Applicant for 11 Lynwood Avenue has requested Toronto and East York Community Council to exempt their property from the following by-law requirements: City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 447, Section 1.2.B(1) fence in rear yard not to exceed 2.0 metres in height. The Applicant for 11 Lynwood Avenue is requesting an exemption for their; - Proposed rear yard fence to be constructed - Rear Yard fencing/barrier: Height of 2.37 metres at a length of 7.8 metres - Constructed with steel posts and masonry concrete blocks - Rear Yard Fence separating driveway from backyard: height of 2.37 metres at a length of 12.3 metres - Constructed with steel vertical members

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Granted the application for a fence exemption to the property owner of 11 Lynwood Avenue, thereby allowing the proposed fence as outlined in the report (February 27, 2026) from the Central District Manager, Investigation Services, Municipal Licensing and Standards to be constructed. 2. Directed that at such time when a replacement of the fence is required that such installation will comply with Municipal Code Chapter 447, Fences or its successor by-law.
