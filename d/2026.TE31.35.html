Quayside Infrastructure and Public Realm Project (Phase 2) - Construction Staging Area

AdoptedApr 22, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Approving temporary closures near the waterfront — including part of the Martin Goodman Trail and sections of Queens Quay East — from April to December 2026 for Waterfront Toronto's Quayside infrastructure project goes to City Council for a decision.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As Queens Quay East is a major arterial road, City Council approval of this report is required. Waterfront Toronto is delivering development enabling infrastructure and public space in the Quayside precinct bounded by Lake Shore Boulevard to the north, Bonnycastle Street to the west, Lake Ontario to the south and the Victory Soya Mills Silos to the east under the Quayside Infrastructure and Public Realm (QIPR) project. The QIPR project will realign Parliament Street, extend Queens Quay east to the Silos, normalize the Parliament Street and Queens Quay East intersection, deliver necessary servicing connections and improve Bonnycastle Street and Small Street. The QIPR implementation will unlock development parcels that include commitments for affordable housing and deliver much needed public realm improvements to the area.
