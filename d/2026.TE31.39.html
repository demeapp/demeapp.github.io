53-55 Yonge Street - Construction Staging Area Time Extension

AdoptedApr 22, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The call on whether to extend lane and sidewalk closures on Yonge Street and Colborne Street from May to July 2026 while two buildings are demolished ahead of future development belongs to City Council. The $6.75-per-hour parking rules would also change.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As Yonge Street is classified as a major arterial street, City Council approval of this report is required. H&R REIT is demolishing the properties at 53 and 55 Yonge Street in preparation for a future development. The site is located on the east side of Yonge Street, between Wellington Street East and Colborne Street. At its meeting on July 23 and 24, 2025, City Council authorized the: - Temporary closure of a portion of the east sidewalk and northbound curb lane of Yonge Street between Wellington Street East and Colborne Street, for a period of 10 months, from July 28, 2025 to May 1, 2026; and - Temporary closure of a portion of the south sidewalk and eastbound curb lane on Colborne Street between Yonge Street and Scott Street, for a period of 10 months, from July 28, 2025 to May 1, 2026.
