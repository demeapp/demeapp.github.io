Archer Street - Parking Amendments

AdoptedApr 1, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Whether to ban standing on both sides of part of Archer Street near Cobourg Avenue, and to fix signs that do not match the parking rules now sits with the community council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending prohibiting standing on both sides of the north/south leg of Archer Street, between Cobourg Avenue and the east/west leg of Archer Street. The proposed changes will deter parking at this location and improve safety and traffic operations by maintaining unobstructed sightlines and a clear passage for vehicles on the two-way street. During the investigation, Transportation Services discovered inconsistencies in the traffic by-law related to the current signed parking regulations on Archer Street and Coburg Avenue. Recommendations 2 to 5 will rectify these inconsistencies.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited standing at all times on both sides of the north/south leg of Archer Street, between Cobourg Avenue and the east/west leg of Archer Street. 2. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., daily, on the even (west) side of Archer Street, from Cobourg Avenue to Archer Street. 3.
