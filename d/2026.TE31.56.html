Mid-Block Pedestrian Traffic Control Signal - Jarvis Street, between Carlton Street and Maitland Street/Maitland Place

A decision on installing a mid-block traffic light on Jarvis Street north of Carlton Street, giving pedestrians a protected crossing sits with the community council. Staff say it will improve safety.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has reviewed the need for pedestrian crossing protection on Jarvis Street, between Carlton Street and Maitland Street/Maitland Place. Based on the assessment undertaken, Transportation Services is recommending the installation of a mid-block traffic control signal on Jarvis Street, approximately 190 metres north of Carlton Street. The mid-block traffic control signal will provide enhanced safety for all road users, particularly vulnerable road users.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of a traffic control signal on Jarvis Street, at a point 190 metres north of Carlton Street.
