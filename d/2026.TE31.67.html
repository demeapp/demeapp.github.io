Mirvish Village - Traffic Amendments (Non-Delegated)

AdoptedApr 22, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Up for a decision before City Council: ban northbound left turns and southbound right turns at all times at Bathurst Street and the private driveway at 758 Bathurst Street, as part of the Mirvish Village traffic plan.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Mirvish Village Redevelopment includes properties municipally known as 571-597 Bloor St W, 742-754 and 760-782 Bathurst St, 26-38 Lennox Street, and 581-603 and 588-612 Markham Street with frontage on Bloor Street W, Bathurst Street, Lennox Street, and Markham Street. The owner of the project is Westbank Project Corp, and the corresponding site plan application no 17 187476 STE 19 SA. The proposed mixed-use development includes several mid-rise and tall buildings, ranging from 2 storeys to 27 storeys. This motion is to implement components of the approved 2021 site plan agreement with Westbank Project Corp. What was decided

City Council on April 22 and 23, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit northbound left turns at all times on Bathurst Street and the private driveway at 758 Bathurst Street, located on the west side of Bathurst Street, approximately 72 metres south of Bloor Street West. 2. City Council prohibit southbound right turns at all times on Bathurst Street and the private driveway at 758 Bathurst Street, located on the west side of Bathurst Street, approximately 72 metres south of Bloor Street West.
