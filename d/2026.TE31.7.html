The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of the application to amend the Zoning By-law to convert the existing 25-storey office building into a hotel with 487 suites on the south block of the property at 1 to 7 Yonge Street. The proposed Zoning By-law Amendment application is consistent with the Provincial Planning Statement (2024) and conforms to the City's Official Plan. The adaptive reuse of an existing building is an appropriate and efficient use of land.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Adjourned the public meeting for TE31.7 until the May 28, 2026 meeting of the Toronto and East York Community Council and that no further notice be given.
