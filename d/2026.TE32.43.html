Toronto City Council · Decision

Improving Safety and Vehicle Access on Margueretta Street

AdoptedApr 30, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decides whether to ban standing on the west side of Margueretta Street north of College Street, to keep the curb clear for garbage trucks and delivery vehicles.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Business owners and residents near College Street and Margueretta Street contacted my office to share concerns about parked vehicles impeding access for garbage trucks and delivery vehicles as they maneuver in and out of the area. A site visit by City of Toronto staff confirmed these issues and noted that parked vehicles are creating safety challenges and impacting traffic flow in the area. Transportation Services supports prohibiting standing at all times on the west side of Margueretta Street, north of College Street, to resolve these issues. The proposed parking amendments will improve safety and traffic flow by providing curbside access and clearing passage for all road users.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Margueretta Street, between College Street and a point approximately 60 metres north. 2. Prohibited standing to be in effect at all times on the west side of Margueretta Street, between College Street and a point 60 metres north. 3. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m., daily, except no parking anytime from College Street to a point 66 metres north, from a point 115 metres north of College Street to a point 5.5 metres further north and from a point 168.5 metres south of Bloor Street West to a point 5.5 metres further south, on the odd (east) side of Margueretta Street from College Street to the first laneway south of Bloor Street West. 4.
