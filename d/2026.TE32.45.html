Toronto City Council · Decision

Parking Amendments on Geary Avenue

AdoptedApr 30, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The call on whether to ban standing on the north side of Geary Avenue west of Bartlett Avenue North, so delivery vehicles can reach business driveways belongs to the community council. The $3.25-per-hour parking machines would be adjusted to match.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Business owners on Geary Avenue have contacted my office to share concerns regarding parked vehicles blocking commercial driveway access. These vehicles have prevented delivery vehicles from loading and unloading, causing significant disruption to businesses. Transportation Services conducted a site visit that confirmed the issues impacting businesses and, as a solution, supports prohibiting standing at all times on the north side of Geary Avenue, west of Bartlett Avenue North. This improved parking restriction will better deter illegal parking and maintain driveway access for Geary businesses.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited standing to be in effect at all times on the north side of Geary Avenue, between Bartlett Avenue North and a point 39.5 metres west. 2. Rescinded the existing parking machines on the north side of Geary Avenue, between a point 33 metres east of Dufferin Street and Salem Avenue North, in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, at a rate of $3.25 per hour for a maximum period of three hours. 3. Authorized the installation of parking machines on the north side of Geary Avenue, between a point 33 metres east of Dufferin Street and a point 39.5 metres west of Bartlett Avenue North, to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, at a rate of $3.25 per hour for a maximum period of three hours. 4.
