Cadorna Avenue - Accessible Parking Space - Re-opening Item TE26.26

AdoptedApr 30, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the community council was asked to reopen a 2025 decision and remove an accessible parking spot on Cadorna Avenue, at the resident's request.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

A resident has reached out to the City to request that their accessibility parking spot be removed from their home on Cadorna Avenue. In support of their request I am moving this motion to remove this accessibility parking spot.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council : 1. Re-opened Item TE26.26, headed "Accessible Parking Spaces - October 2025 (Delegated)", from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, for further consideration, pertaining only to the accessible parking space on the east side of Cadorna Avenue. 2. Deleted the entry in Attachment 1: On-street Accessible Parking Spaces to be Installed/Removed, attached to the report (October 22, 2025) from the Director, Traffic Management, Transportation Services, pertaining only to the accessible parking space on the east side of Cadorna Avenue, and replaced it with the following: On-street accessible parking spaces to be removed (Table 2): Ward 14 Location: Cadorna Avenue, east side, between a point 89 metres south of Cosburn Avenue and a point 5.5 metres further south.
