Toronto City Council · Decision

Main Street - Parking Amendments

AdoptedApr 30, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The call on whether to ban stopping on both sides of Main Street's westerly roadway near Stephenson Avenue, and adjust nearby parking, to fix sightline and safety problems belongs to the community council. The $3.25-per-hour parking machines would be removed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office has heard from residents about safety concerns related to vehicles frequently parked in the no-parking zone on the roadway connecting Stephenson Avenue and Main Street, hindering sightlines and narrowing the path of passing vehicles. Following an examination of the issue by Transportation Services staff and continued discussions with residents, I ask that the existing site become a no-stopping zone.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited stopping at all times on both sides of Main Street (westerly roadway) between Stephenson Avenue and Main Street. 2. Rescinded the existing parking machine regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, at a rate of $3.25 per hour and for a maximum period of three hours, on the west side of Main Street (westerly roadway), between Stephenson Avenue and a point approximately 60 metres north. 3. Amended the existing 1-hour parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. daily, on the north and west side of Stephenson Avenue between Main Street and Morton Road, to be in effect on the north side of Stephenson Avenue between the laneway west of Main Street, north of Stephenson Avenue. 4.
