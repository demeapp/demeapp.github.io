33 Yorkville Avenue - Amendment to Pedestrian Tunnel Encroachment Agreement

AdoptedMay 28, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the community council was asked to update the yearly licence fee to $479.02 plus tax for a pedestrian tunnel at 33 Yorkville Avenue. No policy change is involved.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report seeks to amend the licence fee for the encroachment agreement authorized pursuant to Item TE19.27 , adopted by Toronto and East York Community Council at its meeting on October 15, 2020.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended Item TE19.27, adopted by Toronto and East York Community Council on October 15, 2020, by: a. deleting Part 1.h. in its entirety and replacing it with the following: 1.h. the Owner shall pay (i) an annual licence fee in the amount of $479.02 for the 17.51 square metre encroachment area of the pedestrian tunnel, plus applicable Harmonized Sales Tax, and (ii) any applicable real property taxes eligible; the licence fee shall increase annually throughout the term of the Encroachment Agreement in accordance with the City's User Fee Policy, as prescribed under the Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 441, Fees and Charges, Appendix C – Schedule 2, Transportation Services.
