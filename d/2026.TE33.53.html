Oakwood Village Streets Plan - Non-Delegated Road Safety Improvements

Adopted with changesJun 24, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The decision on whether to approve Oakwood Village road safety changes: a new pedestrian light at Oakwood and Clovelly, removal of an old crosswalk, and weekday morning and afternoon turn bans at Oakwood and Earnscliffe rests with City Council.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Toronto and East York Community Council does not have delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. This report is a companion to a report entitled 'Oakwood Village Streets Plan', which documents the findings of the Oakwood Village Streets Plan study and recommended changes, for which authority has been delegated to Toronto and East York Community Council. The Streets Plan assessed existing conditions, engaged with the public and community organizations, and proposed appropriate changes to neighbourhood streets. What was decided

City Council on June 24 and 25, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the installation of a traffic control signal (intersection pedestrian signal) at the intersection of Oakwood Avenue and Clovelly Avenue. 2. City Council authorize the removal of the existing pedestrian crossover on Oakwood Avenue immediately south of Clovelly Avenue, in conjunction with the installation of a traffic control signal (intersection pedestrian signal). 3. City Council prohibit southbound right turns in effect from 7:30 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and from 2:00 p.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, except public holidays, at the intersection of Oakwood Avenue and Earnscliffe Road. 4.
