Transit Priority Measures to Support Transit Diversions during the College Street / Carlton Street Closure Between Bay Street and Church Street

AdoptedJun 24, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A decision on temporary parking and traffic changes so the 506 Carlton streetcar can be rerouted while College and Carlton streets close for sewer and track work from late July to December 2026 goes to City Council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services is requesting approval, on behalf of the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC), to install transit priority measures (TPMs) to support transit diversion routes associated with a sewer and streetcar track replacement project on College Street, between Bay Street and Yonge Street, and on Carlton Street, between Yonge Street and Church Street, scheduled to take place from July 26, 2026 (earliest start date of construction) to December 2026. Due to the nature of the construction and the resulting constraints within the right-of-way, regular streetcar operations will be suspended through the project area. As a result, the 506 Carlton streetcar will be diverted around the construction zone for the duration of the work, and a 506 Carlton replacement bus service will be implemented to supplement transit coverage.
