Accessible Parking Space - Davenport Road (Non-Delegated)

AdoptedJun 24, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council is set to decide whether to add a new accessible parking spot on Davenport Road east of Dufferin Street, and to remove an older marked spot near Bartlett Avenue that is no longer needed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Davenport Road, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting approval for the installation of an on-street accessible parking space on the south side of Davenport Road for persons with disabilities. During the course of the investigation, it was discovered that a by-lawed accessible parking space on the south side of Davenport Road, west of Bartlett Avenue North, no longer had corresponding signage posted in the field. This space has been determined to be no longer required. Recommendation 2 will address the removal of this space from the by-laws.

What was decided

City Council on June 24 and 25, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council designate an on-street accessible parking space to be in effect at all times on the south side of Davenport Road, between a point 49 metres east of Dufferin Street and a point 5.5 metres further east. 2. City Council rescind the existing on-street accessible parking space in effect at all times on the south side of Davenport Road, between a point 31.8 metres west of Bartlett Avenue and a point 5.5 metres further west.
