Heintzman Street (403 Keele Street) - Temporary Parking Amendments

AdoptedMay 28, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council weighed whether to approve temporary parking bans on Heintzman Street from June 2026 to January 2027, so construction vehicles can safely enter and exit the rental apartment site at 403 Keele Street.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Clark Construction Management Inc. is currently developing an 11-storey rental residential building at 403 Keele Street. The property is located on the east side of Keele Street, approximately 95 metres north of Dundas Street West. Access/egress to the construction site is provided from the rear of the property via the west side of Heintzman Street. Currently, across from the construction access gate on-street parking is permitted on the east side of Heintzman Street which conflicts with the turning manoeuvres of construction vehicles accessing/egressing the constriction site. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation in effect at all times (except no parking anytime from Dundas Street West to the lane first north of it; and 1 hour parking from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. from the lane first north of Dundas Street West to the north end) from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. on the east (odd) side of Heintzman Street, between a point 95 metres north of Dundas Street West and the north end of Heintzman Street, from June 1, 2026 to January 30, 2027, inclusive. 2. Rescinded the existing maximum 1-hour parking regulation in effect at all times, from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on the east side of Heintzman Street, between a point 95 metres north of Dundas Street West and the north end of Heintzman Street, from June 1, 2026 to January 30, 2027, inclusive. 3.
