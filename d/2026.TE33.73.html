“Don’t Ride on the Sidewalk” Decals on Ward 11 Sidewalks

AdoptedMay 28, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The decision on letting volunteers put city-designed 'Don't Ride on the Sidewalk' stickers on Ward 11 sidewalks at their own cost rests with the community council. The volunteers would pay $100 each for 12 stickers; the stickers cost about $500 each installed.

The official summary names a figure of $500.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Pedestrians in Ward 11 experience chronic conflicts with motorized vehicles on sidewalks, particularly in the vicinity of food service businesses, which are located in a high density in Ward 11. City staff have experimented with various forms of “walk your wheels” safety sidewalk markings. Painted markings have been used in past years, which last for multiple seasons. Last year, Communication Services and Transportation Services designed, procured and installed a small number of sidewalk decals with approved markings that reminded riders to walk their vehicles on the sidewalk. These decals had some success, but were removed at the end of the summer season.
