Renaming Tichester Park in Honour of Hedy Bohm

AdoptedJun 24, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Renaming Tichester Park to Hedy Bohm Park, honouring a Holocaust survivor and longtime local resident known for Holocaust education and community work heads to City Council for a decision.

The official record

What the item proposed

This letter is to support the renaming of Tichester Park, located at 27 Tichester Road, to Hedy Bohm Park, in recognition of Hedy Bohm’s extraordinary life and enduring contributions to Holocaust education, community building, and anti-discrimination work across Toronto. Born in Romania in 1926, Hedy immigrated to Toronto in 1949 after surviving the Holocaust, including imprisonment at Auschwitz. A resident of Tichester Road since 1992, she has deep roots in the local community and has inspired generations through her advocacy, education, and compassion. Renaming Tichester Park in her honour would provide a meaningful and lasting tribute to Ms. Bohm’s contributions. What was decided

City Council on June 24 and 25, 2026, adopted the following: 1. Despite the provisions of the City of Toronto Naming Policy, City Council approve the renaming of the Tichester Park at 27 Tichester Road Street as Hedy Bohm Park.
