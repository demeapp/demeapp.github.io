What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter for which Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to provide Toronto and East York Community Council with an opportunity to consider an appeal submitted by the owner of 353, 355, and 357 College Street regarding a proposed encroachment within the public right-of-way on College Street. The proposed encroachment would consist of a privately constructed sanitary control maintenance hole, a stormwater control maintenance hole, and a groundwater sampling port. These elements do not comply with certain provisions of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 743, Streets and Sidewalks, Use of.
