Transit Priority Measures to Support Transit Diversions During Construction on King Street West

Before City Council for a decision: approve temporary parking and traffic changes on King Street West from November 2026 to about May 2027, while streetcars are paused for watermain and track work.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services is requesting approval, on behalf of the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC), to install temporary transit priority measures (TPMs) associated with the transit diversion for multiple construction events occurring concurrently on King Street West between Close Avenue and Spadina Avenue. The transit diversion discussed in this report covers the period from November 1, 2026, until approximately May 1, 2027. The ongoing construction, which includes watermain and streetcar track work, will require streetcar operations to be suspended. The 504 King streetcar will be diverted onto Queen Street West, between Roncesvalles Avenue and Spadina Avenue. To maintain coverage on King Street West, a 504 King replacement bus will operate, and the 503 Kingston Rd replacement bus will also be extended further west to Dufferin.
