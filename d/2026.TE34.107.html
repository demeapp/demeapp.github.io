Metrolinx East Harbour Transit Hub - Temporary Closure of Sunlight Park Road

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The decision on approving closing Sunlight Park Road and nearby sidewalks from August 2026 to June 2029, so Metrolinx can build the East Harbour Transit Hub, a major new regional train station rests with the community council.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Metrolinx is constructing the East Harbour Transit Hub (EHTH), a major regional transit interchange station to be located within the Lakeshore East rail corridor, immediately east of the Don Valley Parkway and south of Eastern Avenue. To facilitate construction of the EHTH, Community Council approval is required for the temporary closure of: - Sunlight Park Road, between Broadview Avenue and Eastern Avenue; - The south sidewalk on Sunlight Park Road, between Broadview Avenue and Eastern Avenue; and - The east sidewalk on Broadview Avenue, between Eastern Avenue and Sunlight Park Road. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the temporary closure of the eastbound lanes to vehicular traffic on Sunlight Park Road, between Broadview Avenue and Eastern Avenue, from August 1, 2026 to June 30, 2029, inclusive, to facilitate construction of the East Harbour Transit Hub. 2. Approved the temporary closure of the south sidewalk to pedestrian traffic on Sunlight Park Road, between Broadview Avenue and Eastern Avenue, from August 1, 2026 to June 30, 2029, inclusive, to facilitate construction of the East Harbour Transit Hub. 3. Approved the temporary closure of the east sidewalk to pedestrian traffic on Broadview Avenue, between Eastern Avenue and Sunlight Park Road, from August 1, 2026 to June 30, 2029, inclusive, to facilitate construction of the East Harbour Transit Hub. 4.
