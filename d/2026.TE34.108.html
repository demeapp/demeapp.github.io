What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Queen Street West, City Council approval of this report is required. SG Constructors is building a 27-storey residential condominium building at 1266 Queen St West. The site is located on the northeast corner of Queen Street West and Noble Street. In order to facilitate construction staging operations for the development, Transportation Services is requesting authorization to implement the following temporary measures: - Closure of the east sidewalk / boulevard and the northbound traffic lane on the east side of Noble Street, between Queen Street West and a point 85 metres north; - Conversion of Noble Street from a two-way operation to a one-way traffic operation (northbound to westbound), between Queen Street West and Strickland Avenue; - Installation of a stop control for westbound traffic on Noble Street and…

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the closure of the east sidewalk and the northbound traffic lane on Noble Street, between Queen Street West and a point 85 metres north, from December 1, 2026 to November 30, 2028, inclusive. 2. City Council designate Noble Street, between Queen Street West and Strickland Avenue, as one-way for northbound to westbound traffic only. 3. City Council authorize a compulsory stop control for westbound traffic at the intersection of Noble Street and Strickland Avenue. 4. City Council rescind the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Noble Street, between Queen Street West and a point 50 metres north. 5.
