1464 King Street West and 10 to 12 Maynard Avenue - Rental Housing Demolition Application - Decision Report - Approval

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

A decision on approving tearing down 19 rental homes at 10 Maynard Avenue, if the owner builds 19 replacement rentals and gives tenants compensation and a right to return at similar rents sits with City Council. The approval carries a $20,000.00 financial condition.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of a Rental Housing Demolition application which proposes to demolish 19 rental dwelling units located at 10 Maynard Avenue. The 19 rental units are proposed to be replaced as part of the new development on the site. The proposal includes a Tenant Assistance Plan that addresses the right of existing tenants of the rental units to return to replacement rental units at similar rents and provides financial compensation to mitigate hardship for tenants of the rental units. The proposed development on the site at 1464 King Street West and 10 to 12 Maynard Avenue is the subject of a related Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application (24 253913 STE 04 OZ). The application proposes a 13-storey mixed-use building with 80 dwelling units, including 19 replacement rental units, ground-floor retail and daycare uses, and a temple with religious residences.…
