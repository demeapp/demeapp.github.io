355 Adelaide Street West - Construction Staging Area

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

It's City Council's call whether to approve closing the eastbound curb lane on Adelaide Street West near Charlotte Street from January 2027 to December 2028, for construction of a 58-storey condo at 355 Adelaide Street West.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Adelaide Street West and on Charlotte Street, City Council approval of this report is required. The Hi-Rise Group is constructing a 58-storey condominium building at 355 Adelaide Street West. The site is located on the southwest corner of Adelaide Street West and Charlotte Street. Transportation Services is seeking authorization to temporarily close the eastbound curb lane on the south side of Adelaide Street West for a period of 24 months, from January 1, 2027 to December 31, 2028. The temporary closure of the eastbound curb lane is required to facilitate construction staging operations associated with the below grade and above grade phases of the development.
