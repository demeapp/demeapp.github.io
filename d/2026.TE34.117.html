Centre Avenue and Edward Street (481 University Avenue) - Construction Staging Area Time Extension

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council weighed whether to extend construction staging closures on Edward Street and Centre Avenue until December 2028, to finish the 54-storey condo at 481 University Avenue after heritage-related delays. The report references a $7.00 fee amount.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Davpart Incorporated is constructing a 54-storey condominium building at 481 University Avenue, 481 University Avenue, 210 Dundas Street West, 70 Centre Avenue and 137 Edward Street. The site encompasses the entire block bounded by University Avenue, Dundas Street West, Centre Avenue, and Edward Street. By way of background, Toronto and East York Community Council, at its meeting on November 10, 2020, authorized the subject construction staging area on Edward Street and on Centre Avenue, from November 12, 2020, to September 30, 2026.
