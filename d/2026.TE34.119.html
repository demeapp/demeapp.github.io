65-87 Raglan Avenue - Construction Staging Area Time Extension

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council is set to decide whether to extend the construction closure on Raglan Avenue from December 2026 to December 2027, to finish the 28-storey condo at 65-87 Raglan Avenue after material and construction delays.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Raglan Building Group Inc is constructing a 28-storey residential condominium building at 65-85 Raglan Avenue. The site is located on the east side of Raglan Avenue, between St. Clair Avenue West and Maplewood Avenue. Toronto and East York Community Council, at its meeting on June 11, 2024, approved the subject construction staging area on Raglan Avenue, from September 1, 2024 to November 30, 2026. The developer is requesting an extension for the construction staging area on Raglan Avenue due to project delays, including challenges with materials and additional unexpected work encountered during construction.
