325 Gerrard Street East - Construction Staging Area

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Whether to approve closing the east sidewalk and part of the southbound lane on Dreamers Way from July 2026 to September 2029, for a 26-storey mixed-use building at 325 Gerrard Street East now sits with City Council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As Gerrard Street East is classified as a minor arterial street, City Council approval of this report is required. Deltera Inc. is constructing a 26-storey mixed use building at 325 Gerrard Street East. The site is located on the southeast corner of Gerrard Street East and Dreamers Way. Transportation Services is requesting authorization to temporarily close a portion of the east side southbound curb lane and the east sidewalk on Dreamers Way for a period of 39 months, from July 10, 2026 to September 30, 2029, in order to facilitate construction staging operations for the development.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the closure of the east sidewalk and a 1.9 metre wide portion of the east side southbound curb lane on Dreamers Way, between Gerrard Street East and a point 82.5 metres south, from July 10, 2026, to September 30, 2027, inclusive. 2. City Council rescind the existing standing prohibition at all times on the east side of Dreamers Way, between Gerrard Street East and a point 82.5 metres south. 3. City Council prohibit stopping at all times on both sides of Dreamers Way, between Gerrard Street East and a point 82.5 metres south. 4. City Council rescind the existing stopping prohibition in effect from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, except public holidays, on the south side of Gerrard Street East, between a point 84 metres east of Dreamers Way and a point 18.5 metres further east. 5.
