Grosvenor Street and Grenville Street (Phase 1) - Construction Staging Area

Adopted with changesJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Up for a decision before the community council: approve temporary sidewalk and lane closures on Grosvenor and Grenville streets through March 2028, with walkways, for demolition and early work on two residential towers. Seven parking machine spaces at a $7.00 fee amount would be removed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Deltera Contracting Inc. is undertaking the constructing of two residential towers, 41 and 33 storeys high at the properties municipally known as 27 Grosvenor Street and 26 Grenville Street. The site is located on the east side St. Vincent Lane, between Grenville Street and Grosvenor Street.
