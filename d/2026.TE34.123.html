1134 Queen Street East - Construction Staging Area Time Extension

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Whether to extend the construction closure on Queen Street East near Bertmount Avenue from October 2026 to March 2027, to finish a three-storey condo with shops at 1134 Queen Street East now sits with City Council.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Queen Street East, City Council approval of this report is required. Elcium Construction Management Corp. has undertaken the construction of a 3-storey condominium with ground floor retail at 1134 Queen Street East. The site is located on the north side of Queen Street East, between Bertmount Avenue and Coady Avenue. City Council, at its meeting on July 23 and 24, 2025, adopted Item TE24.44, and in doing so, authorized the temporary closure of the north sidewalk and a portion of the westbound curb lane on Queen Street East, from July 1, 2025 to September 30, 2026. The developer has requested a time extension of the duration of the construction staging area on Queen Street East as a result of delays caused by the issuance of a building permit and approval from Toronto Water for the building's service connections. City Council direct the applicant to continue to pressure wash or sweep (weather permitting) the construction site and adjacent sidewalks and roadways daily, or more frequently as needed to be cleared of any construction debris and made safe. 3.
