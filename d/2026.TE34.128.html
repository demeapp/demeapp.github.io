Avenue Road and Edmund Avenue/Private Access - Traffic Control Signal Amendments

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council decides whether to update the traffic lights at Avenue Road and Edmund Avenue to include a private driveway from De La Salle College, with new turn bans to keep it one-way and limit spillover traffic.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Avenue Road, City Council approval of this report is required. Transportation Services is requesting approval to amend the operation of the existing traffic control signals at the intersection of Avenue Road and Edmund Avenue. The amendments will address the following: - the inclusion of a private access from De La Salle College on the east side of the intersection into the existing traffic control signals - the prohibition of movements at Avenue Road and Edmund Avenue necessary to re-enforce a one-way westbound operation of the private access noted above - the prohibition of westbound straight through movements from the private access to address concerns associated with traffic infiltration into the adjacent community - the addition of a new east-west pedestrian crossing on the south leg of the intersection, which…

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend the existing installation of the traffic control signals at Avenue Road and Edmund Avenue to be in effect at Avenue Road and Edmund Avenue/Private Access. 2. In conjunction with the installation of traffic control signals at Avenue Road and Edmund Avenue/Private Access: a. City Council prohibit northbound right turns at all times at the intersection of Avenue Road and Edmund Avenue/Private Access; b. City Council prohibit eastbound through movements at all times at the intersection of Edmund Avenue/Private Access; c. City Council prohibit southbound left turns at all times at the intersection of Avenue Road and Edmund Avenue/Private Access; and d.
