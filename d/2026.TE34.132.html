What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Broccolini is constructing a 34-storey residential condominium building at 83-97 River Street and 2-4 Labatt Avenue. The site is located at the north-east corner of River Street and Labatt Avenue. As a result, four pay and display parking spaces have been temporarily removed on River Street to accommodate construction. This construction is anticipated to continue until 2029. Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) has worked with Transportation Services to identify new on-street paid parking opportunities in Ward 13 to replace these lost spaces. These new spaces will support the local community and area businesses by encouraging a regular turnover in the on-street parking supply.
