Mercer Street - Streetscape Improvements and Parking By-law Amendments

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Updating parking and loading rules on Mercer Street for a 2026 road rebuild that adds wider sidewalks and curb extensions, cutting paid parking spots from 13 to 9 is on the community council's agenda.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The purpose of this report is to request Community Council approval of amendments to on-street parking and passenger loading regulations along the south side of Mercer Street between Blue Jays Way and John Street, to facilitate proposed streetscape improvements. This section of Mercer Street is scheduled for road reconstruction and streetscape improvements in 2026. The project includes curb extensions, on-street parking laybys and passenger loading zones. The proposed streetscape improvements will provide an enhanced pedestrian environment and a safer roadway for all users. The proposed amendments would reduce the existing 13 on-street paid parking spaces in this section of Mercer Street to nine (9) spaces. The existing passenger loading zones in front of Nobu Hotel and Residence, and Le Germain Hotel will remain.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Approved the amendments to the Traffic and Parking By-laws for Mercer Street between Blue Jays Way and John Street as described in Attachment 2 to the report (June 19, 2026) from the Acting Deputy General Manager, Transportation Services.
