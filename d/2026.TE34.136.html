Oriole Parkway - Parking By-law Amendments

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Changing parking rules on Oriole Parkway for a road resurfacing project that adds a tree-lined centre median, removing three paid parking spots is on City Council's agenda.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Oriole Parkway, City Council approval of this report is required. Oriole Parkway, extending between Eglinton Avenue West and Chaplin Crescent, currently has road resurfacing and streetscape improvements work underway that commenced in 2025 and will be completed in 2026. The project includes replacement of the existing concrete centre median with a sodded median with raised curb, concrete apron, and trees, as well as replacement of existing paver islands with curb extensions at intersections. The proposed on-street parking by-law amendments on Oriole Parkway between Eglinton Avenue West and Chaplin Cresent contained within this report are required to facilitate these streetscape improvements. There would be elimination of three (3) existing paid on-street parking spaces on Oriole Parkway between Eglinton Avenue West…

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council exempt the implementation of permit parking on Oriole Parkway between Anderson Avenue to College View Avenue, from the requirements of subsection 925-4B of City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 925, Permit Parking. 2. City Council approve amendments to the Traffic and Parking By-laws for Oriole Parkway between Eglinton Avenue West and Chaplin Crescent, as generally described in Attachment 3 to the report (June 19, 2026) from the Acting Deputy General Manager, Transportation Services.
