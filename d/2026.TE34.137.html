Farquhars Lane - Parking Amendments

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

It's the community council's call whether to add a commercial loading zone and no-stopping rules in Farquhars Lane, to stop illegal parking that blocks walkers and cars in the laneway.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services has received complaints about illegal parking in Farquhars Lane, which impedes orderly movement of pedestrians and motor vehicles in the laneway. In consultation with the Old Town Business Improvement Association, Transportation Services is recommending installation of a Commercial Loading Zone in Farquhars Lane and that "No Stopping" prohibitions be added on both sides elsewhere in the laneway, between The Esplanade and Front Street.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Designated a Commercial Loading Zone to be in affect at all times on the west side of Farquhars Lane between a point 65 metres north of The Esplanade and a point 45 metres further north. 2. Prohibited stopping at all times in the following locations: a. on the west side of Farquhars Lane between The Esplanade and a point 65 metres north; b. on the west side of Farquhars Lane between a point 110 metres north of The Esplanade and Front Street East; and c. on the east side of Farquhars Lane between The Esplanade and Front Street East.
