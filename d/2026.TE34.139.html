Logan Avenue - Parking Amendments

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

Adding a school bus loading zone on Logan Avenue in front of Frankland Community School for weekday pickup and drop-off, slightly shrinking a short-term parking area is on the community council's agenda.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is recommending the designation of a school bus loading zone on the west side of Logan Avenue fronting Frankland Community School, located south of Danforth Avenue. The proposed amendment will accommodate pick-up and drop-off activities for students arriving at the school by bus and will require the school's existing short-term parking area be shorted by approximately two spaces. These recommendations are being made in consultation with the Frankland Community School representatives and the Councillor’s office.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing maximum 10-minute parking regulation in effect from 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Logan Avenue, between a point 50 metres south of Danforth Avenue and point 38.2 metres further south, to be in effect from between a point 50 metres south of Danforth Avenue and a point 27 metres further south. 2. Designated a school bus loading zone to be in effect from 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Logan Avenue, between a point 77 metres south of Danforth Avenue East and point 11.2 metres further south.
