Temporary Adjustments to Traffic and Parking Regulations for the 2026 Canadian National Exhibition (Delegated)

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council weighed whether to approve temporary traffic and parking rules around Exhibition Place from August 21 to September 7, 2026, to manage traffic during the Canadian National Exhibition.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report concerns a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to seek Toronto and East York Community Council's authority to enact temporary traffic and parking amendments required to help the local community with their parking needs, and enhance traffic operations and pedestrian safety by minimizing traffic infiltration on local roads during the 2026 Canadian National Exhibition (CNE), which takes place from August 21 to September 7, 2026, inclusive. Drawing on lessons learned from previous CNE events, as well as input from The Exhibition Place, the local councillor, local residents, the event organizer, and Transportation Services staff, a set of traffic mitigation strategies was developed to minimize the impacts of increased event-related traffic on the surrounding community. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the temporary traffic and parking regulations on streets in the vicinity of Exhibition Place that are impacted during the Canadian National Exhibition, identified in Attachment 1 to the report (June 19, 2026) from the Director, Congestion and Network Management, Transportation Services; the temporary traffic and parking regulations will override all existing regulations (except with locations as set out in Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 903, Parking for Persons with Disabilities, Schedule II, Designated On-Street Parking for Permit Holders) for the duration of this event (August 21, 2026 to September 7, 2026).
