The official record

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates transit service on Dufferin Street and Springhurst Avenue, City Council approval of this report is required. The purpose of this report is to seek City Council's authority to enact temporary traffic and parking amendments required to help the local community with their parking needs, and enhance traffic operations and pedestrian safety by minimizing traffic infiltration on local roads during the 2026 Canadian National Exhibition (CNE), which will take place from August 21 to September 7, 2026, inclusive. Drawing on lessons learned from previous CNE events, as well as input from The Exhibition Place, the local councillors, local residents, the event organizer, and Transportation Services staff, a set of traffic mitigation strategies was developed to minimize the impacts of increased event-related traffic on the surrounding community. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council authorize the temporary traffic and parking regulations on streets in the vicinity of Exhibition Place that are impacted during the Canadian National Exhibition, identified in Attachment 1 to the report (June 19, 2026) from the Director, Congestion and Network Management, Transportation Services; the temporary traffic and parking regulations will override all existing regulations for the duration of this event (August 21, 2026 to September 7, 2026).
