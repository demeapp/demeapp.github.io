Temporary Adjustments to Traffic and Parking Regulations for 2026 Distillery Winter Village (Delegated)

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report concerns a matter for which the Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The purpose of this report is to seek Toronto and East York Community Council's authority to enact temporary traffic and parking amendments to enhance traffic operations for patrons accessing the event, to help the local community with their parking needs, and to enhance pedestrian safety by minimizing traffic infiltration on local roads during the 2026 Distillery Winter Village event, which will take place from November 12, 2026 to January 3, 2027, inclusive. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the temporary traffic and parking regulations on streets in the vicinity of Distillery District that are impacted during the Distillery Winter Village, identified in Attachment 1 to the report (June 19, 2026) from the Director, Congestion and Network Management, Transportation Services; the temporary traffic and parking regulations will override all existing regulations for the duration of this event (November 12, 2026 to January 3, 2027). 2. Directed the Director, Permits and Enforcement, Transportation Services to communicate these temporary parking and traffic regulations outlined in Part 1 above to residents through a targeted information campaign throughout the duration of the Distillery Winter Village.
