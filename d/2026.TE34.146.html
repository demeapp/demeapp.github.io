227 Gerrard Street East - Proposed Traffic and Parking Amendments

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The decision on whether to convert part of Ontario Street south of Gerrard Street East to two-way traffic and update parking rules, giving drivers access to a new seven-storey building at 227 Gerrard Street East rests with the community council.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. The development site at 227 Gerrard Street East is bounded by Gerrard Street East to the north, Ontario Street to the east, Hagan Lane to the south, and Seaton Street to the west. The development consists of a seven-storey mixed-use building having a residential gross floor area of 8,960 square metres, and a non-residential gross floor area of 1,002 square metres. This development will take access to/from via an existing lane on the west side of Ontario Street, south of Gerrard Street East. Transportation Services is requesting approval to convert a portion of Ontario Street, south of Gerrard Street East, from a one-way southbound operation to a two-way operation. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing one-way southbound traffic designation on Ontario Street, between Gerrard Street East and Dundas Street East, to operate between a point 60 metres south of Gerrard Street East and Dundas Street East. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition on the east side of Ontario Street, between Gerrard Street East and a point 143 metres south. 3. Prohibited stopping on the east side of Ontario Street, between Gerrard Street East and a point 69 metres south. 4. Prohibited parking on the east side of Ontario Street, between a point 69 metres south of Gerrard Street East and a point 74 metres further south. 5. Rescinded the existing maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Ontario Street, between Dundas Street East and Gerrard Street East. 6.
