Pedestrian Crosswalks on Winona Drive between Holland Park Avenue and Glenhurst Avenue

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item: the community council was asked to receive a report on where new marked crosswalks could go on Winona Drive. No policy change.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On May 28, 2026, Toronto and East York Community Council directed Transportation Services to review options to install safe pedestrian crosswalks on Winona Drive, between Holland Park Avenue and Glenhurst Avenue, and to report back with recommendations to the July 8, 2026 meeting of Toronto and East York Community Council. The purpose of this report is to summarize the findings of the review and outline locations where new pedestrian crosswalk markings may be installed.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Received the report (June 17, 2026) from the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services for information.
