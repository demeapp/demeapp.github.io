Shaftesbury Avenue - Parking Amendment

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to fix a parking rule conflict on Shaftesbury Avenue so the original parking signs removed during the Summerhill station elevator project can be put back.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Parking signs were removed from Shaftesbury Avenue years ago to facilitate a TTC “Easier Access” project to add elevators to the Summerhill subway station. Residents have been pressing for restoration of the original signs ever since the TTC relinquished the road and sidewalk space it had occupied for so long. Transportation Services has advised that they cannot restore the original signs until a by-law conflict is resolved by this motion.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect from 9:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, on the north side of Shaftesbury Avenue, between Summerhill Avenue and a point 91.4 metres east of Yonge Street.
