Toronto City Council · Decision

1113-1125 Dundas Street West - Construction Staging Area

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council decided whether to close the south sidewalk and part of Dundas Street West east of Ossington Avenue for 24 months (September 2026 to September 2028) while a 10-storey affordable housing building is built. Five parking spots will be removed and the builder will pay the lost parking revenue at $4.50.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

As the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) operates a transit service on Dundas Street West, City Council approval of this report is required. Pomerleau, is constructing a 10-storey affordable housing building. The site is located on the south side of Dundas Street West, east of Ossington Avenue. Transportation Services is requesting authorization to close the south sidewalk and a portion of the eastbound curb lane on Dundas Street West, between a point 44 metres east of Ossington Avenue and a point 39 metres further east, for a period of 24 months, from September 1, 2026 to September 1, 2028, in order to facilitate construction staging operations. During the closure period, pedestrians will be accommodated within a temporary covered and protected walkway located in the closed portion of the eastbound curb lane.
