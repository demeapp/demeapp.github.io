Accessible Loading Zone Mavety Street south of Dundas Street West

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council decided whether to add an accessible parking spot on Mavety Street south of Dundas Street West for a resident with mobility needs, replacing one metered spot.

The official record

What the item proposed

A request for an accessible parking space to accommodate a request on Dundas Street West was reviewed by Transportation Services. Transportation Services seek approval to install a designated on-street accessible parking space on neighbouring Mavety Street instead of Dundas Street West to avoid the parking restrictions on Dundas Street West. The installation of the proposed on-street accessible parking space will result in the loss of one pay-and-display parking space on Mavety Street. Transportation Services have determined that this change will have minimal impact on local parking demands. This installation will improve accessibility for neighbouring residents with mobility needs by providing a designated accessible parking space. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Designated an on-street accessible parking space to be in effect at all times on the east side of Mavety Street, between a point 30 metres south of Dundas Street West and a point 5.5 metres further south. 2. Rescinded the existing maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect at all times, on both sides of Mavety Street, between Annette Street (west intersection) and Dundas Street West.
