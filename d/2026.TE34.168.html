Davenport Village Traffic Safety Plan: Additional Improvements

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add parking rules on Brandon Avenue in Davenport Village to clear blocked sightlines at intersections.

The official record

What the item proposed

In June 2025, Toronto and East York Community Council adopted the Davenport Village Traffic Safety Plan, which approved new traffic safety measures designed to address speeding, heavy cut-through traffic, and other road safety concerns. As implementation of this plan moves forward, residents have raised additional safety concerns regarding unsafe parking and sightline obstructions at intersections in the area. Transportation Services staff reviewed the feasibility of introducing additional parking restrictions to address community concerns and improve safety. A review determined that there are currently no signed parking regulations on either side of Brandon Avenue, west of Lansdowne Avenue. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited standing at all times, on the south side of Brandon Avenue, between Foundry Avenue and Lansdowne Avenue. 2. Rescinded the existing standing prohibition in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Brandon Avenue, between a point 73 metres east of Primrose Avenue and a point 23 metres further east. 3. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, car-share vehicles excepted, on the west side of Brandon Avenue, between a point 35 metres east of Lansdowne Avenue and a point 22 metres further east, to be in effect on the south side of Brandon Avenue. 4.
