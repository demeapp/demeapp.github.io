Improving Pedestrian Safety at Turnberry Avenue and Alentejo Street

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add an all-way stop at Turnberry Avenue and Alentejo Street and ban parking near the corner to make it safer around the nearby school and parks.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The intersection of Turnberry Avenue and Alentejo Street is located within a residential neighbourhood home to General Mercer Junior Public School, Turnberry North Park, Turnberry South Park, S.A.D.R.A. Park, and surrounding residential communities. In 2023, traffic calming measures were installed on Turnberry Avenue, west of Old Weston Road, in response to concerns regarding speeding and dangerous driving. I have heard from area residents that, while the traffic calming measures have helped, the intersection of Turnberry Avenue and Alentejo Street remains dangerously unsafe. To improve safety, I recommend implementing an all-way stop at this intersection and parking restrictions to clear sightlines for drivers. These measures would directly address safety concerns and support safer conditions for all road users accessing the surrounding parks and school.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Turnberry Avenue and Alentejo Street. 2. Rescinded the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, on the north side of Turnberry Avenue, between Union Street and Old Weston Road. 3. Prohibited parking to be in effect at all times, on the north side of Turnberry Avenue, between Old Weston Road and Algarve Crescent. 4. Prohibited standing to be in effect at all times, on the north side of Turnberry Avenue, between Algarve Crescent and a point 80 metres west. 5. Prohibited parking to be in effect at all times, on the north side of Turnberry Avenue, between Benny Stark Street and a point 80 metres west of Algarve Crescent.
