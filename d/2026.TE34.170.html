Improving Permit Parking on Concord Avenue

Adopted with changesJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to clean up confusing parking rules on Concord Avenue between College and Dewson streets, with a $3.25 parking charge.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Concord Avenue, between College Street and Dewson Street, is a local street that serves both local residents and the nearby Dewson Street Junior Public School. Residents contacted my office to raise concerns about the lack of available parking during weekday afternoons and evenings, particularly for permit holders returning home from work. I joined local residents and Transportation Services staff for a site meeting to better understand these concerns and discuss possible solutions. During the meeting, the existing street parking regulations were shown to be inconsistent and confusing about where and when residents can park. Following an additional review, Transportation Services identified parking amendments to resolve these issues, including aligning the school bus loading and short-term parking restrictions with school operating hours. I recommend authorizing the parking amendments…
