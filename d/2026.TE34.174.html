Installation of Traffic Calming Measures on Concord Avenue

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add speed humps on Concord Avenue between Bloor Street West and College Street after residents reported speeding and near-collisions.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Concord Avenue, between Bloor Street West and College Street, is located between two major arterial roads and experiences significant volumes of both pedestrian and vehicular traffic. This block serves as a key route for people travelling to nearby destinations while also attracting drivers seeking to bypass potential congestion at surrounding intersections. Residents have consistently raised concerns about excessive vehicle speeds along this stretch of Concord Avenue and the resulting impacts on neighbourhood safety. Many commnuity members have shared experiences of near-collisions and expressed concerns for the safety of all road users, particularly children travelling to and from Dewson Street Junior Public School. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (12 speed humps) on Concord Avenue, between Bloor Street West and College Street, generally as shown in Attachments 2 and 3 to the letter (July 2, 2026) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo.
