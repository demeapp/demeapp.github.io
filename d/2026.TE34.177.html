Toronto City Council · Decision

Installation of Traffic Calming Measures on Gilbert Avenue

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add speed humps on Gilbert Avenue between Kitchener Avenue and Eglinton Avenue West, after residents reported near accidents.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I have heard from residents on Gilbert Avenue, between Kitchener Avenue and Eglinton Avenue West, who have shared concerns about speeding vehicles travelling through this community. Gilbert Avenue extends south from Eglinton Avenue West and often attracts drivers seeking to bypass traffic on busy roads. Residents have shared their experiences with near accidents as they and their children walk down this street. In a survey my office hand-delivered to homes, residents strongly supported installing speed humps as a traffic calming measure. I also heard directly from residents at their doors, and many confirmed the urgent need for traffic safety measures on this section of Gilbert Avenue. Traffic calming measures are already in place on other sections of Gilbert Avenue, and installing speed humps on the remaining section will complete this network.
