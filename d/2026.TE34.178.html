Toronto City Council · Decision

Installation of Traffic Calming Measures on Greenlaw Avenue

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add speed humps on Greenlaw Avenue near St. Clair Avenue West, after residents reported speeding and aggressive driving.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Greenlaw Avenue extends south from a busy section of St. Clair Avenue West and is an important connection to schools, small businesses, and parks in the area. I have heard from residents who shared that drivers too often speed along Greenlaw Avenue to avoid travelling through nearby major intersections. This increased vehicle volume has resulted in worsening traffic safety issues for residents, including drivers who speed through stop signs and engage in aggressive driving. As many young children live and walk through this neighbourhood, there is an urgent need for traffic safety improvements to protect them and other residents. Through a neighbourhood survey hand-delivered to homes by my office, residents voted in strong support of installing speed humps to reduce speeding and improve traffic safety on Greenlaw Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (six speed humps) on Greenlaw Avenue, between Ashburnham Road and St. Clair Avenue West, generally as shown in Attachments 2 and 3 to the letter (June 26, 2026) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo.
