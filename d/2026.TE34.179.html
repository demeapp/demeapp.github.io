Installation of Traffic Calming Measures on Kirknewton Road

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add speed humps on Kirknewton Road south of Eglinton Avenue West, after residents reported excessive speeds.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents on Kirknewton Road have contacted my office to raise concerns regarding excessive vehicle speeds and dangerous driving on their street. Kirknewton Road is a residential street, running south from Eglinton Avenue West, where people walk and cycle to nearby schools, businesses, and parks. Yet speeding drivers continue to impact the safety and livability of the neighbourhood. In response to these concerns, my office conducted a neighbourhood survey that was hand-delivered to homes. The survey confirmed strong neighbourhood support for installing speed humps as a traffic calming measure to reduce vehicle speeds. Through conversations with residents while door-knocking on Kirknewton Road, I heard directly about ongoing concerns regarding excessive vehicle speeds and further support for traffic calming measures that would make the street safer for all residents. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (seven speed humps) on Kirknewton Road, between Dynevor Road and Eglinton Avenue West, generally as shown in Attachments 2 and 3 to the letter (June 26, 2026) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo.
