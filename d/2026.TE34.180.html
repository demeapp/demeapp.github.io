Toronto City Council · Decision

Installation of Traffic Calming Measures on Margueretta Street

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add speed humps on Margueretta Street between College Street and Dundas Street West, after residents reported daily speeding.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Margueretta Street, between College Street and Dundas Street West, is located between two busy major arterials. This block attracts both heavy pedestrian activity heading towards nearby locations and heavy cut-through traffic attempting to avoid nearby intersections. I have heard from many residents that speeding vehicles are a daily challenge, impacting the safety of all road users. Many residents have shared their experiences of near-accidents and highlighted the urgent need for traffic safety measures. In response to this issue, my office conducted a neighbourhood survey to hear feedback about ways to improve safety. Residents strongly supported installing speed humps as a traffic calming measure. The installation of speed humps will help to reduce speeding, redirect traffic, and improve safety for residents and their families.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (three speed humps) on Margueretta Street, between Dundas Street West and College Street, generally as shown in Attachment 2 to the letter (July 2, 2026) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo.
