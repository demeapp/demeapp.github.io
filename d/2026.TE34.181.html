Toronto City Council · Decision

Installation of Traffic Calming Measures on Muir Avenue

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add speed humps on Muir Avenue west of Dufferin Street, after residents reported speeding and dangerous driving.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Muir Avenue is an important local route located south of Dufferin Mall and runs west from Dufferin Street. I have heard from residents on Muir Avenue who have shared concerns about speeding vehicles and dangerous driving that are impacting safety. Residents have shared their experiences with near accidents and have requested urgent traffic safety improvements. In a neighbourhood survey my office hand-delivered to homes, residents voted in support of traffic calming measures in response to these issues. Traffic calming measures are already in place on nearby streets, and this additional installation on Muir Avenue will support existing improvements. These measures will help reduce speeding, reduce cut-through traffic, and improve safety for all who are driving, cycling, or walking.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (four speed humps) on Muir Avenue, between Brock Avenue and Dufferin Street, generally as shown in Attachment 2 to the letter (June 26, 2026) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo. 2. Rescinded the parking prohibition in effect from the 16th day to the last day of each month, from April 1 to November 30, inclusive on the north side of Muir Avenue, between Harvie Avenue and Dufferin Street. 3. Prohibited parking to be in effect from the 16th day to the last day of each month, from April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the north side of Muir Avenue, between Brock Avenue and Dufferin Street.
