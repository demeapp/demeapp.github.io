Toronto City Council · Decision

Installation of Traffic Calming Measures on Pelham Avenue

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add speed humps to close the gap on Pelham Avenue near Charles-Sauriol Elementary School, after residents voted unanimously in favour.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In 2018, Toronto and East York Community Council approved speed humps on Pelham Avenue to extend only from Uxbridge Avenue to Laughton Avenue. For many residents on Pelham Avenue, this decision created a gap that left a large section of the street without he appropriate traffic calming measures. This remaining block of Pelham Avenue is used frequently by young students heading to Charles-Sauriol Elementary School or by families dropping by Pelham Avenue Playground. I have heard directly from residents that drivers are speeding towards the existing speed humps, highlighting the need for urgent measures. In response to this issue, my office conducted a neighbourhood survey, and residents voted unanimously in support of speed humps. These measures will help to reduce speeding and improve safety for residents travelling throughout the neighbourhood.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (three speed humps) on Pelham Avenue, between Osler Street and Uxbridge Avenue, generally as shown in Attachment 2 to the letter (July 2, 2026) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo.
