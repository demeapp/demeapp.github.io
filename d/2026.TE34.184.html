Parking Amendments on Wade Avenue

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add a no-standing zone on the west side of Wade Avenue south of Paton Road, so cars stop blocking a business's parking entrance.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I have heard from a business owner on Wade Avenue regarding vehicles routinely parking in front of the business's paid boulevard parking area. These incidents occur too frequently, creating operational challenges and delays for employees, deliveries, and guests. To assess these concerns, Transportation Services conducted a site visit in April 2026. While the City of Toronto's statutory parking regulations already prohibit parking directly in front of a legal boulevard without signage, Transportation Services determined that additional measures are warranted, given ongoing enforcement challenges. As a result, staff recommend implementing a nso-standing regulation on the west side of Wade Avenue, south of Paton Road, adjacent to the entrance and exit of the business's boulevard parking area.
