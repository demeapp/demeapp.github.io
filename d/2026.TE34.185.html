Re-opening and Amending 2025.TE25.42 - Accessible Parking Spaces

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to scrap an accessible parking spot on Chudleigh Road that is too short for the applicant's car, and move the Morrison Avenue spot to a better location.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At the September 18, 2025 meeting of Toronto and East York Community Council, accessible parking spaces were approved on Chudleigh Road and Morrison Avenue. The accessible parking space on Chudleigh Street is not sufficient in length for the original applicant’s vehicle and, therefore, is no longer needed. The location of the accessible parking space on Morrison Avenue will be relocated to better meet the needs of the applicant. As a year has not passed since the adoption of this item, a motion is required to re-open and rescind the installation of the above accessible parking spaces and approve the installation of the relocated accessible parking space on Morrison Avenue.
