Through Restriction at Bloor Street West and Pauline Avenue/Collegiate Road

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to block through-traffic where the new Collegiate Road meets Bloor Street West at Pauline Avenue, to keep cut-through drivers out of the neighbourhood.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

As part of the development at 1141 Bloor Street West, 980 Dufferin Street, and 90 Croatia Street, a new public roadway, Collegiate Road, will be constructed to connect Croatia Street with Bloor Street West at Pauline Avenue. To accommodate this new connection, the existing pedestrian crossover at Bloor Street West will be replaced with a traffic control signal. These changes will create a new route between Bloor Street West and Croatia Street that is expected to be used by hundreds of vehicles travelling to and from Dufferin Mall. I have heard from residents on Pauline Avenue who are concerned that the opening of Collegiate Road and the installation of traffic control signals will increase traffic infiltration into the surrounding residential neighbourhood. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council prohibit northbound straight through movement at all times, bicycles excepted, at the intersection of Bloor Street West and Pauline Avenue/Collegiate Road.
