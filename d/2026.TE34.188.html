Advancing Parking and Traffic Amendments, Speed Reduction and Allowing for Future Speed Humps on Bruyeres Mews

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to approve parking and traffic changes on Bruyeres Mews in Fort York, including lower speeds and a path to future speed humps.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

What the item proposed

My office has been working with local residents, the Fort York Neighbourhood Association, and City staff around road safety improvements on Bruyeres Mews. Transportation Services staff have already begun working with my office to install crosswalk lines and a yellow divider line at the intersection of Ianuzzi Street and Bruyeres Mews. Resident concerns have focused around vehicles parking in a no standing area on the north side near Bathurst Street, causing congestion and driver frustration, including excessive honking and shouting, as vehicles have limited space to pass. This poses visibility concerns for pedestrians and cyclists. Parking adjustments will improve sightlines at crosswalks and mitigate congestion while still providing some street parking for nearby businesses. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Prohibited stopping to be in effect at all times, on the north side of Bruyeres Mews, between Ianuzzi Street and a point 15 metres east. 2. Amended the stopping prohibition in effect at all times, on the north side of Bruyeres Mews, between Bathurst Street and a point 15 metres west, to be in effect between Bathurst Street and a point 30.5 metres west. 3. Amended the existing maximum 15-minute parking regulation in effect at all times, on the north side of Bruyeres Mews, between Iannuzzi Street and a point 15 metres west of Bathurst Street, to be in effect between a point 15 metres east of Iannuzzi Street and a point 30.5 metres west of Bathurst Street. 4.
