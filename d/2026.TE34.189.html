Advancing Parking and Traffic Amendments, Speed Reduction and Allowing for Future Speed Humps on Iannuzzi Street

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to approve parking changes and pre-approve two speed humps for Iannuzzi Street, so the humps can go in quickly once the city takes over the road.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office has been working with local residents, the Fort York Neighbourhood Association, and City staff to prioritize road safety on Iannuzzi Street. These concerns include speeding, as well as vehicles parking on the east side of Iannuzzi Street creating sightline concerns for vehicles exiting from a nearby condo complex. Engineering and Construction Services have determined that Iannuzzi Street has not yet been assumed and/or classified by the City. As a result, physical changes to the road in the form of traffic calming measures (i.e., speed humps), cannot be installed on these road segments at this time. However, bylawing two speed humps on Iannuzzi at this time will greatly expedite their installation by the City once the road is assumed in future. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the standing prohibition in effect at all times, on the east side of Iannuzzi Street, between Fort York Boulevard and a point 52 metres south, to be in effect, between Fort York Boulevard and a point 63 metres south. 2. Authorized parking for a maximum period of 15 minutes, on the east side of Iannuzzi Street, between a point 63 metres south of Fort York Boulevard and a point 15 metres north of Bruyeres Mews. 3. Amended the existing parking prohibition in effect at all times, on the west side of Iannuzzi Street, between Fleet Street and Bruyeres Mews, to be in effect between Fleet Street and Fort York Boulevard. 4. Amended the existing speed limit of kilometres per hour on Iannuzzi Street, between Fleet Street and Bruyeres Mews to be kilometres per hour on Iannuzzi Street, between Fleet Street and Fort York Boulevard 5.
