Dundas Street West - Parking Amendments

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council decided whether to allow curbside parking in the lay-by on Dundas Street West between Bathurst Street and Denison Avenue, to help the Scadding Court Community Centre and nearby shops.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

On behalf of the Scadding Court Community Centre and nearby businesses, I have asked staff to consider amending the parking regulations on Dundas Street West, between Bathurst Street and Denison Avenue to remove the existing peak period parking and stopping restrictions and allow curbside parking on Dundas Street West in the one lay-by area on the south side. These lay-bys were created as part of civil works completed in 2018 and 2019, however, no parking changes were made at the time until preparations for the FIFA World Cup began. These changes have made it difficult for Scadding Court Community Centre and 707 Market businesses to access deliveries and short term parking.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 910, Parking Machines and Meters, and Chapter 950, Traffic and Parking, generally as outlined in Attachment 1 to the letter (July 6, 2026) from Deputy Mayor Ausma Malik.
