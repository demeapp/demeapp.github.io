Ordnance Street, Between Strachan Avenue and the South End of Ordnance Street - Traffic Calming

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add three speed humps on Ordnance Street between Strachan Avenue and its south end, after most surveyed residents supported them.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office has been working with City staff and local residents around traffic calming and safety on Ordnance Street. Following a request from a nearby resident, we solicited feedback from residents in local affected condo buildings on Ordnance Street through property management and received over 100 responses to an online survey hosted by my office. A majority of respondents to the survey were in favour of speed humps on their street. I’m looking forward to advancing these improvements to improve safety at a key intersection in our downtown, and to continue working with residents on improvements in their neighbourhood.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (three speed humps) on Ordnance Street, between Strachan Avenue and the south end of Ordnance Street, generally as shown in Attachment 2 to the letter (July 6, 2026) from Deputy Mayor Ausma Malik.
