Traffic Calming - Denison Avenue, between Queen Street West and Wolseley Street

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add one speed hump on Denison Avenue between Queen Street West and Wolseley Street, after the local business association reported speeding.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Queen Street West BIA contacted my office to report concerns about motorists speeding as they exit the neighbourhood onto Queen Street West. In response, my office worked with City staff to assess the feasibility of implementing traffic calming measures, including speed humps, on Denison Avenue between Queen Street West and Wolseley Street. This area experiences significant pedestrian and cyclist activity due to nearby destinations such as St. Stanislaus Kostka Church, TPA Municipal Carpark Lot #106, Randy Padmore Park, local businesses along Queen Street West, and surrounding residential neighbourhoods.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (one speed hump) on Denison Avenue, between Queen Street West and Wolseley Street, generally as shown in Attachment 2 to the letter (June 19, 2026) from Deputy Mayor Ausma Malik.
