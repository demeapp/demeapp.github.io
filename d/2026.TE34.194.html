Traffic Calming - Scadding Avenue, between Princess Street and Hahn Place, and Speed Limit Reduction on Scadding Avenue, between Lower Sherbourne Street and Hahn Place

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add traffic calming on Scadding Avenue and lower its speed limit between Lower Sherbourne Street and Hahn Place.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office has been working with residents and the St Lawrence Neighbourhood Association around road safety in their neighbourhood. We have heard from local residents that this neighbourhood and the surrounding area experiences a high volume of pedestrian traffic associated with Parliament Square Park, Princess Street Park, David Crombie Park (Playground and Basketball Court). This neighborhood also has a school, daycare, community centre and old age building. Motorists are seen speeding on the subject street, and it is used as a cut through to bypass The Esplanade, between Lower Sherbourne Street and Berkeley Street/ Hahn Place. In a residential area like this one, these are concerns I take seriously. What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming ( four speed humps) on Scadding Avenue, between Princess Street and Hahn Place, generally as shown in Attachment 2 to the letter (June 19, 2026) from Deputy Mayor Ausma Malik. 2. Amended the speed limit of 30 kilometres per hour on Scadding Avenue, between Lower Sherbourne Street and Hahn Place, to be in effect between Hahn Place and the west end of Scadding Avenue.
