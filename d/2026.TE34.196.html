Toronto City Council · Decision

Annex Intersections Safety Study

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council asked staff to study two wide, awkward intersections in the Annex (St. George at Bernard, Kendal at Walmer Road) and report back in early 2027 on how to make them safer for walkers.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

There are two wide, non-standard, offset intersections in the Annex that create chronic dangers for pedestrians: St. George Street at Bernard Avenue, and Kendal Avenue at Walmer Road. Both are heavily used, including by many children on the way to school. The Annex Residents Association has requested a study of opportunities to improve pedestrian safety at these intersections, for example, with pedestrian bump outs or similar mechanisms to right size traffic lane width and to encourage drivers to stop at the intersections.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services, to report back to the first meeting of the Toronto and East York Community Council in 2027 with recommendations on pedestrian safety improvements at the intersections of St. George Street at Bernard Avenue and Kendal Avenue at Walmer Road
