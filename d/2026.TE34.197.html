Chin Radio Building - Loading Zone

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to change parking rules in front of the CHIN Radio building at 622 College Street to add passenger pick-up and drop-off space.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The CHIN Radio building is located at 622 College Street, east of Grace Street. It provides media, medical and technology offices along with private event workplace terraces. CHIN Radio, in consultation with the Ward Councillor, expressed concern regarding parking fronting the building and the lack of passenger pick-up/drop-off opportunities in this area. Loading zone signage of unknown provenance was reported to have been present in front of the building until recently. The current parking prohibition signed in front of this building does not meet the building’s requirements. Transportation Services detected inconsistences in the traffic by-law related to the current signed parking regulations along the subject section of College Street. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council designate a passenger loading zone to be in effect at all times, except 3:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the north side of College Street, between a point 53 metres east of Grace Street and a point 13 metres further east. 2. City Council prohibit parking at all times on the north side of College Street, between a point 30.5 metres east of Grace Street and a point 22.5 metres further east. 3. City Council rescind the existing maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday to Friday; 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Saturday, on the north side of College Street, between Manning Avenue and a point 30.5 metres east of Grace Street.
