Toronto City Council · Decision

Mount Pleasant Sidewalk Study

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council asked staff to report back in early 2027 on adding a sidewalk on the west side of Mount Pleasant Road between Crescent Road and Roxborough Drive.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

There is no sidewalk on the west side of Mount Pleasant Road between Crescent Road and Roxborough Drive, which creates a dangerous situation for pedestrians wishing to travel north along Mount Pleasant Road from Roxborough Street East to access the park, Yellow Creek or the signalized crossing to the east side of Mount Pleasant Road.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Directed the General Manager, Transportation Services to report back to the Toronto and East York Community Council in the first quarter of 2027 with recommendations on improving pedestrian safety by installing a sidewalk on the west side of Mount Pleasant Road between Crescent Road and Roxborough Drive.
