The official record

What the item proposed

666-660 Spadina Avenue was constructed, in error, with its commercial ground floor and front doors several inches above the sidewalk, without an internal ramp to allow barrier free access from the interior to the sidewalk. This did not comply with its approved site plan or with provincial accessibility requirements. The developer requested permission to correct its error by encroaching onto the sidewalk, in an area of the city where we already lack adequate sidewalk space for a growing number of pedestrians. The Toronto and East York Community Council rejected this encroachment request on March 13, 2026. As a result, the commercial premises remained vacant and unusable, creating an undesirable dead zone.
