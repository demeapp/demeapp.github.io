Yonge Streetscape Study

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council asked staff to design a streetscape vision for Yonge Street between Bloor Street and Summerhill, where new towers are rising but sidewalks and storefronts are struggling.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Because of its dense transit connections, Yonge Street is undergoing extreme intensification between Bloor Street and Summerhill, much of it with little parking. These developments assume that most new residents will shop in the neighbourhood by foot. However, the combination of rundown sidewalks, endless construction, and retail displacement/ empty storefronts have make Yonge Street much less attractive and pleasant for pedestrians than it was a few years ago. This area urgently needs a clear vision for an inspiring and attractive Yonge streetscape that can be progressively realized, comparable to the streetscape design prepared for Elm Street, 2025.TE27.30 .

What was decided

The Toronto East York Community Council : 1. Directed the Director, Urban Design, in consultation with the Chief Planner and Executive Director, City Planning and the General Manager, Transportation Services, to prepare a streetscape vision for Yonge Street between Bloor Street and Summerhill Avenue and report back to the Toronto East York Community Council in the second quarter of 2027.
