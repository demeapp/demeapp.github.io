Toronto City Council · Decision

Vesta Drive Traffic Calming

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add three speed humps on Vesta Drive between Eglinton Avenue West and Old Forest Hill Road, after residents petitioned for them.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am kindly asking for your support of my motion to traffic calming on Vesta Drive, between Eglinton Avenue West and Old Forest Hill Road. Residents of Vesta Drive submitted a petition to my office requesting the installation of speed humps to help reduce speeding, discourage cut-through traffic and improve the safety of their street. Neighbours have reported that high volume of traffic and excessive vehicle speeds create a hazardous environment for residents, especially students walking to and from Forest Hill Collegiate. With the passage of this motion, the community will receive the traffic calming measures they have been organizing for.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (three speed humps) on Vesta Drive, between Eglinton Avenue West and Old Forest Hill Road, generally as shown on Attachment 2 to the letter (June 29, 2026) from Councillor Josh Matlow.
