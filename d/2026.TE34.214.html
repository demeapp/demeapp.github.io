Leader Lane - Traffic and Parking Amendments

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to change parking rules on Leader Lane between King Street East and Colborne Street after the hotel and condo residents raised concerns.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The Omni King Edward Hotel (37 King Street East) and King Edward Private Residences (22 Leader Lane) are located adjacent to and have entrances onto Leader Lane, between King Street East and Colborne Street. Leader Lane is a public lane located next to these properties. It is signed to operate as one-way southbound and provides entrances to the hotel and residence on the west side. The following parking regulations are currently in effect on this section of Leader Lane: West side - A Commercial Loading Zone that operates at all times - The corner parking prohibitions at King Street East and at Colborne Street are signed with a standing prohibition at all times East Side - Stopping is prohibited at all times The Omni King Edward Hotel and King Edward Residences expressed concerns regarding parking on this section of Leader Lane and the lack of short-term delivery opportunities next to…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Amended the existing standing prohibition in effect at all times on the west side of Leader Lane, between King Street East and a point 30.5 metres south, to be in effect between King Street East and a point 15 metres south. 2. Amended the existing commercial loading zone in effect at all times on the west side of Leader Lane, between a point 30.5 metres south of King Street East and Colborne Street, to be in effect between a point 15 metres south of King Street East and a point 15 metres south. 3. Designated a Delivery Vehicle Parking Zone to be in effect at all times for a maximum period of 20 minutes, on the west side of Leader Lane, between a point 30 metres south of King Street East and a point 24 metres south. 4.
