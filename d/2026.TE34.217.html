The official record

What the item proposed

Residents who live on Creek Place in Ward 19 have faced long-standing challenges related to road servicing and access to their properties. My office has been working with these residents to implement a sustainable solution to these challenges. Creek Place is currently classified as a laneway, but it demonstrates several unique characteristics. While four properties located at the northern end of the street have rear access from Creek Place while fronting onto Dawes Road, an additional nine properties at the southern end rely on Creek Place as their primary means of access. At its entrance from Donora Drive, the right-of-way is approximately four to six metres wide, which is typical of a laneway, and runs behind the four properties fronting on Dawes Road. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the General Manager, Transportation Services to undertake the work required to reclassify Creek Place from a public laneway to a local road, and report back to the Toronto and East York Community Council for any approvals required, including any associated operational, legal or implementation considerations as appropriate, and, pending completion of the reclassification, provide winter maintenance services on Creek Place consistent with the service levels provided to local roads.
