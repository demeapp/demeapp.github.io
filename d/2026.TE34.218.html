Northline Road - Parking Amendments

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to fix mismatched parking signs on Northline Road and set a standard three-hour limit to support municipal election operations.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

City staff have advised our office that, following a request from Election Services to review the parking regulations on Northline Road to accommodate operational needs for the upcoming municipal election, they identified a discrepancy between the existing parking by-law and the posted parking restrictions. To address the discrepancy and support Election Services' operational needs, staff are recommending amendments to the parking regulations and signage. Once implemented, the standard three-hour maximum parking regulation will apply to this section of Northline Road.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Northline Road, between Cranfield Road and O’Connor Drive.
