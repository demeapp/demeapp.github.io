Fir Avenue and Fernwood Park Avenue - All-way Compulsory Stop Controls

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add an all-way stop at Fir Avenue and Fernwood Park Avenue, where residents say drivers and walkers can't tell who has the right of way.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents have indicated that there have been issues between drivers and pedestrians in identifying right of way at this intersection and have requested an all way stop control in order to rectify this problem. Therefore, I request the installation of all-way stop control at the intersection of Fir Avenue and Fernwood Park Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized an all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Fir Avenue and Fernwood Park Avenue.
