Toronto City Council · Decision

Aldridge Avenue - Traffic Calming

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add three speed humps on Aldridge Avenue between Danforth Avenue and Murdock Avenue, after residents petitioned for them.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents have reached out to my office with concerns about pedestrian safety on Aldridge Avenue and on surrounding neighbourhood streets. Our office has received a petition from residents in support of the installation of speed humps on Aldridge Avenue. I am requesting that speed humps be installed on Aldridge Avenue, between Danforth Avenue and Murdock Avenue.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (three speed humps) on Aldridge Avenue, between Danforth Avenue and Murdock Avenue, generally as shown in Attachment 1 to the letter (July 7, 2026) from Councillor Brad Bradford.
