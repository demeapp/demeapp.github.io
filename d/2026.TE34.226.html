Toronto City Council · Decision

The community council decided whether to add speed bumps to the east end of Ben Kerr Lane, which already has bumps on its western section.

Official title: Ben Kerr Lane - Speed Bumps

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents have reached out to me about speeding in Ben Kerr Lane. This is a busy area, which services residents, businesses and a religious establishment. The western section of the lane already has speed bumps, while the eastern section is without them. For the sake of public safety, I am supportive of the installation of speed bumps in this laneway.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of speed bumps on Ben Kerr Lane, between Euston Avenue and the east end of Ben Kerr Lane, as shown in Attachment 2 to the letter (July 7, 2026) from Councillor Paula Fletcher.
