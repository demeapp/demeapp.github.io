Wood Street and Grenville Street - Commercial Loading Zones

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add temporary commercial loading zones on Wood Street and Grenville Street during TTC track work on College and Carlton streets.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services has requested temporary commercial loading zones on Wood Street and Grenville Street to accommodate local business during the westbound closure for TTC track replacement on College Street and Carlton Street from Bay Street to Church Street. The recommendations outlined below to Toronto and East York Community Council are being requested for approval in lieu of a staff report.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing no stopping regulation in effect at all times on the south side of Wood Street between Yonge Street and Church Street. 2. Designated a commercial loading zone to be in effect at all times, on the south side of Wood Street, between a point 146 metres east of Yonge Street and a point 40 metres further east. 3. Prohibited stopping at all times on the south side of Wood Street, between Yonge Street and 146 metres east of Yonge Street and 186 metres further east of Yonge Street to Church Street. 4. Enacted no stopping at all times on the south side of Grenville Street, between Yonge Street and a point 55.5 metres west. 5. Designated a commercial loading zone to be in effect at all times, on the south side of Grenville Street, between a point nine metres west of Yonge Street and a point 25 metres further west. 6.
