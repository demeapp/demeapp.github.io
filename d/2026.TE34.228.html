Permanently Pedestrianizing Scott Street between Front Street East and Scott Lane

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council asked staff to study permanently closing Scott Street between Front Street East and Scott Lane to cars, to create event space for the St. Lawrence Centre for the Arts and Meridian Hall.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In discussion with TO Live, there is significant interest in pedestrianizing a portion of Scott Street, between Front Street East and Scott Lane (also known as “Reg Taylor Way”) to permit expanded outdoor space for programming and events that support the St. Lawrence Centre for the Arts and Meridian Hall. This motion is to request that staff study the potential for this section of Scott Street to be pedestrianized, with a report back in the new term.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services to consult with the President and Chief Executive Officer, TO Live, the Executive Director, Corporate Real Estate Management, Old Town Toronto Business Improvement Area, the St. Lawrence Neighbourhood Association, residential buildings and businesses adjacent to Scott Lane, and other City divisions and stakeholders as identified about the permanent closure of Scott Street, between Front Street East and Scott Lane, and to report back to the Toronto and East York Community Council in the first quarter of 2027 with recommendations regarding closing this section of Scott Street permanently.
