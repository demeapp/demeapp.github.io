Toronto City Council · Decision

Rushton Road Traffic Calming

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add two more speed humps on Rushton Road between Maplewood Avenue and Humewood Gardens, after neighbours reported reckless driving.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Dear Chair and Members of Toronto and East York Community Council, I am kindly asking for your support of my motion to authorize the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) along Rushton Road, between Maplewood Avenue and Humewood Gardens. I have heard from many neighbours along Rushton Road that additional speed humps are required along the block to respond to increased speeds and reckless driving. Residents of Rushton Road have previously demonstrated their strong support for traffic calming along their street. With the passage of this motion, the community will receive the traffic calming measures they have been organizing for.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (two additional speed humps) on Rushton Road, between Maplewood Avenue and Humewood Gardens, generally as shown in Attachment 2 to the letter (July 7, 2026) from Councillor Josh Matlow.
