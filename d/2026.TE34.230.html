Kendal Avenue and Bridgman Avenue and Macpherson Avenue - All-way Stop Control

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add an all-way stop at Kendal, Bridgman and Macpherson avenues, where walkers cross to the Dupont subway station and George Brown College without a marked crosswalk.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am kindly asking for your support of my motion to authorize an all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Kendal Avenue and Bridgman Ave and Macpherson Avenue. Continuous concerns have been raised by residents regarding the pedestrian safety at the intersection of Kendal Avenue and Bridgman Avenue and Macpherson Avenue, where there are a higher volume of pedestrians crossing east-west at this intersection travelling to/from the Dupont subway station and the George Brown College – Casa Loma Campus without a designated crosswalk across Kendal Avenue. The installation of an all-way stop at this intersection will enable the implementation of this desired east-west crosswalk connection and improve the overall safety at this intersection.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized an all-way compulsory stop control at the intersection of Kendal Avenue and Bridgman Avenue and Macpherson Avenue.
