Toronto City Council · Decision

Edmund Avenue Traffic Calming

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add three speed humps on Edmund Avenue between Avenue Road and Poplar Plains Road, after neighbours petitioned for them.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am kindly asking for your support of my motion to authorize the installation of traffic calming (speed humps) along Edmund Avenue, between Avenue Road and Poplar Plains Road. Neighbours along Edmund Avenue have submitted a petition to my office demonstrating their support for traffic calming along their street. I have heard concern from the community regarding increased volumes and speed along Edmund Avenue. The street is frequently used as a shortcut to Avenue Road in the morning and evenings, and cars frequently speed to reach the traffic light at the end of the block. With the passage of this motion, the community will receive the traffic calming measures they have been organizing for.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (three speed humps) on Edmund Avenue, between Avenue Road to Poplar Plains Road, generally as shown in Attachment 2 to the letter (July 7, 2026) from Councillor Josh Matlow.
