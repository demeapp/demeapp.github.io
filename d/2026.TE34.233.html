Toronto City Council · Decision

Davisville Avenue Accessible Loading Zone

Adopted with changesJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council decided whether to add an accessible loading zone in front of the new community centre on the Davisville school site, for Wheel-Trans vehicles and permit holders.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

I am kindly asking for your support of my motion to designate an accessible loading zone in front of the new aquatic and muti-use community centre on the Davisville Junior Public School and Spectrum Alternative School site. The accessible loading zone will allow curbside access for accessible vehicles equipped with a side or rear loading ramp (e.g., Wheel-Trans) and permit holders during pick-up/drop-off periods.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council amend the existing stopping prohibition in effect at all times, on the north side of Davisville Avenue, between Yonge Street and a point 231 metres east, to be in effect between Yonge Street and a point 74.6 metres east. 2. City Council prohibit stopping at all times, on the north side of Davisville Avenue, between a point 86.6 metres east of Yonge Street and a point 144.4 metres further east. 3. City Council designate a commercial loading zone to be in effect at all times, on the north side of Davisville Avenue, between a point 74.6 metres east of Yonge Street and a point 12 metres further east.
