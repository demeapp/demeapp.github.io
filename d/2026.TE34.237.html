Wellington Place Neighbourhood Traffic Study

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council asked staff to study how traffic projects are working together in the Wellington Place neighbourhood and report back in early 2027.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office has heard from residents and the Wellington Place Neighbourhood Association about the need for a comprehensive analysis of how the many transit and transportation projects in the area are working together to achieve their intended objectives, including improved safety, effective congestion management, and fast, reliable transit service in a clear and transparent manner.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Requested the General Manager, Transportation Services to conduct a transportation analysis of the Wellington Place neighbourhood and the recent traffic pattern changes on Portland Street, including impacts on Front Street, Bathurst Street and Spadina Avenue, and report back in the first quarter of 2027.
