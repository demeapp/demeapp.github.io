Queen Street West - Accessible Loading Zone

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add an accessible loading zone on Queen Street West east of Walnut Avenue for a Wheel-Trans user's household, removing two metered spots.

The official record

What the item proposed

My office has been working with City staff in Transportation Services who received a request from an applicant to install an accessible loading zone on Queen Street West, east of Walnut Avenue, to support Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) Wheel-Trans services for a household with limited mobility. The applicant provided documentation confirming their registration with Wheel-Trans. Transportation Services supports the installation of the accessible loading zone on Queen Street West. The installation of the proposed accessible loading zone will result in the loss of two pay-and-display parking spaces on Queen Street West during the operating hours of the accessible loading zone. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council designate an accessible loading zone to be in effect from 11:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the south side of Queen Street West, between a point 33 metres west of Walnut Avenue and a point 11 metres further west.
