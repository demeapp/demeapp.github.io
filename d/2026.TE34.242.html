Safer Streets for Davenport: Lightbourn-Chandos Traffic Safety Plan

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to approve a traffic safety plan for the Lightbourn-Chandos neighbourhood in Davenport, after residents raised speeding and cut-through concerns.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The neighbourhood bounded by Davenport Road to the north, Geary Avenue to the south, Dufferin Street to the east, and Primrose Avenue to the west is a vibrant residential area with a network of parkettes, local shops, and other popular community spaces. The residents who call this neighbourhood home include seniors walking to nearby services, families with young children travelling to schools and playgrounds, and transit users walking over to a bus stop. I hosted a park pop-up event in Chandos Park last summer to hear directly from residents about their priorities and the issues affecting the area. Residents consistently raised traffic safety concerns on local streets as a major problem. These concerns included speeding vehicles, constant cut-through traffic, unsafe intersections, and a lack of safe pedestrian crossings to travel between parks.
