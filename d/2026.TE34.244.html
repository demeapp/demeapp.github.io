Oakwood Village Streets Plan: Additional Improvements for Davenport

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to approve more street improvements under the Oakwood Village Streets Plan, after further resident feedback.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

The City of Toronto’s Oakwood Village Streets Plan is an important step towards improving road safety and mobility in this community. Residents have expressed many concerns about traffic safety on many streets in the Oakwood Village area, and the need for improvements. In response to these concerns, I advocated for the development of a comprehensive streets plan and participated alongside many residents in public consultation meetings to discuss potential solutions. The City’s final plan introduced several important improvements that address concerns regarding speeding, unsafe intersections, and traffic infiltration on local streets. These investments will make our neighbourhood safer and more welcoming for residents, pedestrians, cyclists, and all road users. While these improvements are a significant step forward, feedback from residents has made it clear that there is still more work…

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (two speed humps) on Glenholme Avenue between Conway Avenue and Millerson Avenue, generally as shown in Attachment 3 to the letter (July 2, 2026) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo. 2. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (four speed humps) on Glenhurst Avenue between Appleton Avenue and Winona Drive, generally shown in Attachment 4 to the letter (July 2, 2026) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo.
