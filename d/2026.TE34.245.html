Toronto City Council · Decision

Installation of Traffic Calming Measures on Northumberland Street

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add speed humps on Northumberland Street between Ossington and Westmoreland avenues, after residents reported speeding.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Northumberland Street, stretching between Ossington Avenue and Westmoreland Avenue, is a high-traffic route connecting residents to many small businesses, schools, and Ossington Station. Despite its importance as a heavily travelled route, Northumberland Street remains one of the few streets in the area without traffic calming measures. I have heard from residents who shared concerns about speeding along Northumberland Street, highlighting a significant safety issue for pedestrians, cyclists, and drivers. In response to these issues, my office conducted a neighbourhood survey that confirmed strong community support for installing speed humps. Implementing these traffic calming measures will help reduce vehicle speeds and improve road safety for all who use Northumberland Street.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized the installation of traffic calming (three speed humps) on Northumberland Street, between Westmoreland Avenue and Concord Avenue, generally as shown in Attachment 2 to the letter (July 2, 2026) from Councillor Alejandra Bravo.
