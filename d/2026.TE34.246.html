Improving Permit Parking on Old Weston Road

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to add permit parking on the east side of Old Weston Road between Davenport Road and St. Clair Avenue West, creating about 25 spaces.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Old Weston Road serves local residents, businesses, and visitors as an important arterial corridor. However, parking regulations on certain sections of the street do not adequately reflect current neighbourhood needs and have led to ongoing concerns from residents. Residents on the east side of Old Weston Road, between Davenport Road and St. Clair Avenue West, do not have access to permit parking and contacted my office to request improvements. Many households have limited access to garage parking and rely on curbside parking. Introducing permit parking on this section of the street would help address these concerns, align with surrounding neighbourhood parking regulations, and provide approximately 25 on-street permit parking spaces for local residents. City Council rescind the existing stopping prohibition in effect from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, except public holidays, on the east side of Old Weston Road, between St. Clair Avenue West and a point 142 metres north. 3.
