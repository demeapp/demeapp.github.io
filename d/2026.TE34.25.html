What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of a Rental Housing Demolition application which proposes to demolish 210 rental dwelling units located at 420-444 Yonge Street and 1-23 College Street. The new development on the site is proposed to include 216 replacement rental units located in the first phase of development within a 65-storey building. The proposal includes a Tenant Assistance Plan that addresses the right of existing tenants to return to replacement rental units at similar rents and provides financial compensation to mitigate hardship. The proposed development on the site at 420-444 Yonge Street and 1-23 College Street is the subject of a related Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application (25 191175 STE 11 OZ).
