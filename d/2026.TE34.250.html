Toronto City Council · Decision

Improving Permit Parking on Rusholme Road

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to overhaul confusing permit parking rules on Rusholme Road, extending permit hours and removing alternate-side parking.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Transportation Services has reviewed concerns from local residents on Rusholme Road regarding the lack of parking availability, challenges with overnight permit parking, and confusion around existing parking regulations. The current regulations do not fully reflect local parking needs, especially when residents return from work, and improvements are needed. A neighbourhood survey delivered to residents by my office confirmed that parking is a challenge and that there is support for the proposed improvements. These changes include extending permit parking hours, reducing visitor parking hours, and removing alternate side parking requirements. This plan will improve parking availability for residents and clear inconsistent parking regulations while maintaining on-street spaces for visitors.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. each Thursday, April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the odd (east) side of Rusholme Road, from Dundas Street West to St Annes Road. 2. Rescinded the existing permit parking regulation in effect from 12:01 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily, except each Thursday, April 1 to November 30, inclusive, on the even (west) side Rusholme Road, from Dundas Street West to St Annes Road. 3.
