Toronto City Council · Decision

Kippendavie Avenue Parking Amendments

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to limit parking on the west side of Kippendavie Avenue between Queen Street and Kewbeach Avenue to one hour, with permit holders exempt.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

In response to concerns raised by residents regarding limited parking availability on Kippendavie Avenue, my office has received a petition from community members requesting changes to the current parking regulations. Specifically, residents are asking that parking on the west side of Kippendavie Avenue, between Queen Street and Kewbeach Avenue, be limited to a maximum of one hour. On-street permit parking holders would remain exempt from the one-hour limit.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized parking for a maximum period of one hour, on the west side of Kippendavie Avenue, at all times, between a point 131.1 metres south of Queen Street East and Kewbeach Avenue.
