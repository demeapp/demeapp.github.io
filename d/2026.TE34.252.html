Ontario Place Construction Management Plan for Pedestrian and Cycling Safety and Traffic Management

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council considered requiring a construction management plan for the Ontario Place redevelopment, after the province skipped the city's usual approval process.

The official record

What the item proposed

Due to the Province’s choice to cut short the City’s planning approval process for Ontario Place redevelopment, the usual oversight by the City on development implementation has not been followed. This includes the submission of a Construction Management Plan to the City which is typical of all development approvals. Any construction at Ontario Place will require access through the Lake Shore Blvd. W. corridor, an area that a City staff review have confirmed experiences heavy traffic, and is close to Exhibition Place which hosts signature events such as the CNE and Toronto Caribbean Festival. It will also require access across the busy Martin Goodman Trail, a major cycling and pedestrian multi-use trail that connects our city, which a City staff report has identified for safety improvements. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council request the Ministry of Tourism, Culture and Gaming to provide a Construction Management Plan for the Ontario Place redevelopment project, including traffic management and pedestrian and cycling safety measures. 2. City Council request the Ministry of Tourism, Culture and Gaming convene a Construction Liaison Committee for Ontario Place to provide a forum for information sharing and feedback on the work and access taking place around the site.
