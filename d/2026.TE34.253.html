Re-opening and Amending Item TE29.41 - Accessible Parking Spaces - February 2026 (Delegated)

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to replace an accessible parking spot on Gladstone Avenue with an accessible loading zone, after the applicant's health needs changed.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

At the February 19, 2026, meeting of Toronto and East York Community Council, accessible parking spaces were approved at several locations, including a section of Gladstone Avenue. The original applicant of the accessible space on Gladstone Avenue has contacted my office to share that they instead need an accessible loading zone due to changing health needs. Transportation Services staff confirmed that the resident meets the requirements for an accessible loading zone at this location. The recommendations below will amend our previous decision and approve the installation of an accessible loading zone for this resident.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Re-opened Item TE29.41 titled "Accessible Parking Spaces - February 2026 (Delegated)”. 2. Amended its previous decision on Item TE29.41 by: a. deleting the following entry in Attachment 1 to the report (February 19, 2026) from the Director, Enforcement and Street Management, Transportation Services, only as it pertains to Gladstone Avenue: Table 1: On-Street Accessible Parking Spaces to be Installed Gladstone Avenue, west side, between a point 48 metres north of Stonehouse Crescent and a point 5.5 metres further north b. enacting the following: designate an accessible loading zone to be in effect from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Gladstone Avenue, between a point 42.5 metres north of Stonehouse Crescent and a point 11 metres further north.
