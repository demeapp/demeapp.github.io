Toronto City Council · Decision

Victor Avenue - Parking Amendments

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The community council decided whether to extend permit parking on Victor Avenue between Broadview Avenue and Howland Road to weekday afternoons, after 63 percent of residents backed the change.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

Residents on Victor Avenue have reached out to my office regarding concerns of non-permit parking on their street. This is a residential street close to a very busy park and businesses. Residents submitted a petition to my office and in a subsequent confidential ballot, there was 63 percent support for this change.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Authorized additional period of permit parking only on Victor Avenue between Broadview Avenue and Howland Road between 4:00 p.m. and 7:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, in addition to the existing permit parking restriction from 12:01 a.m. to 10:00 a.m.
