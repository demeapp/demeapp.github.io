Indian Grove - Accessible Loading Zone

AdoptedJul 8, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item. The community council decided whether to add an accessible loading zone on the west side of Indian Grove south of Glenlake Avenue for Wheel-Trans users.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This staff report is about a matter that Community Council has delegated authority from City Council to make a final decision. Transportation Services is requesting approval for the installation of a designated on-street accessible loading zone on the west side of Indian Grove, south of Glenlake Avenue. The accessible loading zone will operate from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, and from 11:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Saturday and Sunday. The establishment of this accessible loading zone will provide unimpeded curbside access for the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) Wheel-Trans users and permit holders during pick-up/drop-off scenarios in the subject area. During the investigation, Transportation Services discovered redundant parking by-laws. Recommendations 2 and 3 will rectify these inconsistencies.

What was decided

The Toronto and East York Community Council: 1. Designated an accessible loading zone to be in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, and from 11:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Saturday and Sunday, on the west side of Indian Grove between a point 137 metres south of Glenlake Avenue and a point 11 metres further south. 2. Rescinded the existing maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Indian Grove, between a point opposite Glen Gordon Road and a point 82 metres north of Bloor Street West (west intersection). 3. Rescinded the existing maximum one-hour parking regulation in effect from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, on the west side of Indian Grove, between a point opposite Glen Gordon Road and Glenlake Avenue.
