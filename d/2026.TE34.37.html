What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of a Rental Housing Demolition application which proposes to demolish 32 rental dwelling units located at 30-32 and 38-40 Huntley Street and 120-124 Isabella Street. The 32 rental units are proposed to be replaced as part of the new development on the site. The proposal includes a Tenant Assistance Plan that addresses the right of existing tenants to return to replacement rental units at similar rents and provides financial compensation to mitigate hardship. The proposed development on the site at 30-34 and 38-40 Huntley Street, 112-124 Isabella Street, and a Portion of 1 Mount Pleasant Road is the subject of a related Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application (25 265180 STE 13 OZ).
