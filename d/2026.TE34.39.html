The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of an application to amend the Zoning By-law to permit a 69-storey (220.5 metres, plus 8-metre mechanical penthouse) residential building containing 818 dwelling units at 90-94 Isabella Street, with permissions for small-scale non-residential uses in 94 Isabella Street. On December 13, 2023, City Council approved a previous application to amend the Zoning By-law for the properties at 90-94 Isabella Street to permit a 69-storey residential building. This application reconfigures the previous approval by reducing the size of the tower floor plate, and reorienting the tower from an east-west direction to a north-south direction. The reorientation of the tower form and redistribution of the massing allows for the removal of the previously approved tower massing over 94 Isabella Street and facilitates the full retention of that heritage building.
