City Council decided whether to allow the demolition of 38 rental homes on Isabella Street, replaced by 50 rental units, with $20,000.00 in tenant compensation.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of a Rental Housing Demolition application which proposes to demolish and replace 38 rental dwelling units located at 164-166 Isabella Street. The proposal includes a Tenant Assistance Plan that addresses the right of existing tenants to return to replacement rental units at similar rents and provides financial compensation to mitigate hardship. The proposed development on the site at 164-168 Isabella Street is the subject of a related Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application (25 210128 STE 13 OZ). The proposed development would permit a 70-storey residential building with 599 dwelling units, including a total of 50 replacement rental units. The 50 replacement rental units consist of 12 studio units to replace the existing 25 rental dwelling rooms at 168 Isabella Street and the 38 rental dwelling units proposed for demolition and replacement…
