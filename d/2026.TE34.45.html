What the item proposed

This report reviews and recommends approval of an application to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law, with a holding provision, to permit a 36-storey (122.5 metres including mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building, containing 409 dwelling units (including 65 replacement rental units) and 852 square metres of non-residential gross floor area at 64-66 Wellesley Street East and 552-570 Church Street. The application to amend the Official Plan is required to amend Site and Area Specific Policy 382 to permit a tall building in the Wellesley Wood Character Area. A related Rental Housing Demolition application to permit the demolition and replacement of 65 rental dwelling units on site will be considered by City Council in conjunction with this report.
