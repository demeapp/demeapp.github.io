What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of a revised Rental Housing Demolition application which proposes to demolish 65 rental dwelling units located at 64-66 Wellesley Street East and 552-570 Church Street. The demolition of the 65 rental dwelling units was previously approved by City Council at its meeting on July 19 and 20, 2023 (21 235906 STE 13 RH) in conjunction with Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application (21 235897 STE 13 OZ) proposing a 31-storey mixed-use building with 405 dwelling units, including 65 replacement rental units. Since then the applicant has submitted a new Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment application (26 119147 STE 13 OZ) to construct a 36-storey mixed-use building with 409 dwelling units, including the 65 replacement rental units.
