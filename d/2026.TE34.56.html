The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of the application to amend the Zoning By-law, with a holding provision, to permit a 49-storey (162.7 metres, including mechanical penthouse) mixed-use building. The proposal contains 545 dwelling units including seven rental replacement units, approximately 350 square metres of non-residential gross floor area on the ground floor, and a new 143 square metre on-site parkland dedication at 329-337 Queen Street East 135R-141R and 139-141 Berkeley Street. A Rental Housing Demolition Report will be considered by City Council in conjunction with this report. The proposal includes the demolition of seven rental housing units, all of which will be replaced in the new development of the site.
