What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of the application to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law to permit the intensification of a previously approved four block Master Plan. The application proposes 14 mixed-use buildings, including ten (10) towers ranging in height from 28-storeys to 53-storeys, with a proposed total gross floor area ("GFA") of 325,000 square metres (9.31 FSI for the net site area). Of this total, approximately 308,750 square metres will be residential GFA, and 16,250 square metres will be designated for non-residential uses. Full site buildout is planned with a total of 4,969 new dwelling units, community benefits including affordable housing and a childcare centre in the first phase; and an enhanced parks and open spaces network.
