City Council decided how to respond at the Ontario Land Tribunal to an 18-storey proposal at 262 to 266 St. George Street with 121 homes.

The official record

What the item proposed

On January 9, 2025, an application to amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law was submitted and deemed complete on June 16, 2025, satisfying the City's minimum application requirements. A revised application reflective of an increase in height and density was submitted to the City on December 19, 2025. The revised application is for an Official Plan Amendment which proposes to redesignate the lands from Neighbourhoods to Apartment Neighbourhoods, and a Zoning By-law Amendment which proposes an 18-storey (61 metres excluding the mechanical penthouse) residential building, containing 121 residential dwelling units, of which 14 are replacement rental units. The application proposes the demolition of 40 dwelling rooms and three rental dwelling units. What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council direct the City Solicitor and appropriate City staff to attend the Ontario Land Tribunal in opposition to the current application regarding the Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment Application appeal for the lands municipally known as 262, 264 and 266 St. George Street and to continue discussions with the applicant in an attempt to resolve outstanding issues. 2. City Council authorize the City Solicitor and City staff to take any necessary steps to implement City Council's decision, including requesting any conditions of approval that would be in the City's interest, in the event the Ontario Land Tribunal allows the appeal, in whole or in part.
