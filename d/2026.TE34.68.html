468-470 Queen Street West- Demolition of a Designated Heritage Property under Part V, Section 42 of the Ontario Heritage Act- Approval

AdoptedJul 29, 2026 · Toronto City Council

City Council decided whether to allow the demolition of a non-heritage building at 468 to 470 Queen Street West to make way for a new public park.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends that City Council approve the demolition of the building at 468-470 Queen Street West. This is on a non-contributing property located within the Queen Street West Heritage Conservation District. Demolition of the existing building is proposed to facilitate a new public park at the corner of Queen Street West and Augusta Avenue. Heritage Planning staff support this application which complies with the provisions of the Queen Street West Heritage Conservation District Plan.

What was decided

City Council on July 29 and 30, 2026, adopted the following: 1. City Council approve the demolition of the building at 468-470 Queen Street West, which is a non-contributing property in the Queen Street West Heritage Conservation District, in accordance with subsections 42(1) 4 of the Ontario Heritage Act.
