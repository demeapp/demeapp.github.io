The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends approval of the application to amend the Zoning By-law to permit a 25 storey mixed-use building at 1730 Bloor Street West. The proposed building includes 200 residential units, retail at grade, and an office space that is located on the ground floor and on a portion of the second floor. The total gross floor area of the proposed building is 14,569 square metres of which 454 square metres is for non-residential uses. The proposed development includes a 0.4 metre right-of-way widening along the Bloor Street West frontage. The proposed Zoning By-law Amendment application is consistent with the Provincial Planning Statement (2024) and conforms to the City's Official Plan.
