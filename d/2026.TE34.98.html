The official record

What the item proposed

Fort York National Historic Site (FYNHS) is a nationally significant cultural heritage asset and one of Toronto's most important archaeological sites, serving as a key destination for public programming, learning, and community use. Today, the site is owned and operated by the City of Toronto, one of 10 historic sites promoted under the moniker of Toronto History Museums. As the surrounding area continues to intensify and evolve, the site faces increasing pressure to balance public programming and learning activations, community needs, heritage conservation, archaeological protection, public access and engagement and revenue-generating activities. This report describes plans for an Integrated Operations and Heritage Stewardship Study for Fort York.
