What the item proposed

The Audit & Risk Management Committee (ARMC) Terms of Reference establishes the ARMC as having oversight responsibility for compliance with laws and regulations. As such, it is the responsibility of the TTC CEO, General Counsel and ARMC to maintain the public’s trust by demonstrating transparency and accountability over legal compliance to reduce the risk of liability to the organization, its employees, officers, and directors. To assist the ARMC, CEO, and General Counsel in fulfilling their compliance oversight responsibilities, the Audit, Risk and Compliance Department (ARC) developed an Executive Compliance Certificate (ECC) in collaboration with the Legal Department. The ECC is conducted annually and is intended to provide a mechanism for demonstrating Executive accountability for compliance with statutes and by-laws through formal compliance assertion reporting.
