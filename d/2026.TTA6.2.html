What the item proposed

The TTC is required to prepare audited annual financial statements under Public Sector Accounting Standards and to forward them to City Council for information through the City’s Audit Committee. Effective January 1, 2025, the TTC adopted Canadian public sector accounting guideline amendments to PSG-2 Leased Tangible Capital Assets. The amendments update references within the standard, rename Appendix A as the glossary, and add paragraphs PSG‑2.4A to PSG‑2.4C, which establish minimum requirements for lessee accounting for operating leases of tangible capital assets. As at December 31, 2025, the TTC determined that the adoption of these amendments did not have an impact on the amounts presented in the financial statements.
