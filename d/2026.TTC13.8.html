The official record

What the item proposed

This report provides an update on the acceleration plan to target the start of revenue service by September 30, 2026, on the SRT Busway. This report is a follow up to the July 17, 2025 Board report, SRT Busway Update, which provided an interim update on the measures taken to support the acceleration goal of advancing the revenue service date in response to a motion adopted by the Board at its meeting of May 14, 2025, “That the Board direct staff to work with the City Manager, the General Manager, Transportation Services, and the Executive Director, Transit Expansion Division to expedite all required land acquisitions and permits to accelerate construction of the SRT busway and report back in July on the acceleration plan.”
