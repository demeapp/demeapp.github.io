What the item proposed

The TTC has completed a review and update of its Records Retention Schedule to ensure continued alignment with legislative requirements, operational needs, and records management best practices. This update supports the TTC’s commitment to responsible information governance, risk mitigation, and compliance with the City of Toronto Act, 2006. Key changes include revisions to retention periods, consolidation of record series, introduction of new record categories, and administrative improvements to enhance clarity and usability for staff. These amendments support improved consistency in recordkeeping, reduce risk associated with unmanaged or outdated information, and provide clearer guidance to staff on the retention and disposition of TTC records.
