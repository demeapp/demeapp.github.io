The official record

What the item proposed

The Canadian Union of Public Employees, Local 2 (CUPE, Local 2) bargaining unit is comprised of approximately 700 employees. CUPE, Local 2 represents electrical skilled trades. This report will provide the TTC Board with details regarding the outcome of contract negotiations between the TTC and CUPE, Local 2 in arriving at a tentative agreement for a renewal collective bargaining agreement between these parties. The tentative agreement was reached on May 18, 2026.

What was decided

The TTC Board: 1. Adopted the recommendations as set out in the Confidential Attachment; and 2. Authorized that the information set out in the Confidential Attachment be released upon the ratification of the tentative agreement by both the TTC and the Canadian Union of Public Employees, Local 2.
