Toronto City Council · Decision

Delegation of Authority during the Board Recess

AdoptedJul 22, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The TTC Board decided whether to give the interim CFO and CEO delegated spending authority of $6 million and $1 million during the board recess after the July 22, 2026 meeting.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report recommends the TTC Board provide additional delegated authorities to the Interim Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) to ensure continuity of services during a municipal election year, and critical and time-sensitive TTC operating and capital activities continue to move forward after the last TTC Board meeting of the current 2022-2026 term on July 22, 2026. The recommended delegated authorities, if approved, would come into effect following the July 22, 2026 Board meeting with the authority under Recommendations 1 and 2 remaining in effect until the first scheduled TTC Board meeting of the new term. Any use of the delegated authorities during the Board recess period, beyond the Interim CFO’s or the CEO’s otherwise regular authority, will be reported by staff at the first TTC Board meeting of the new term.
