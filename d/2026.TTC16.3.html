The official record

What the item proposed

The 2026 Accessibility Plan Status Report (Status Report) describes the TTC’s progress toward achieving the objectives of the 2024-2028 TTC 5-Year Accessibility Plan (5YAP), and it fulfills provincially legislated requirements in the Integrated Accessibility Standards Regulation (IASR) under the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) to prepare an annual status report on the progress of measures taken to implement the 5YAP, and steps taken to comply with the IASR. The Status Report also includes updates on the Wheel-Trans Transformation Program and Easier Access Program for a comprehensive update on accessibility initiatives. The 5YAP identified 46 specific initiatives to improve the accessibility of TTC services and facilities for customers by the end of 2028. What was decided

The TTC Board: 1. Received this report for information. 2. Directed Staff to review acceleration options for the Islington Station Easier Access project to recover time from unforeseen underground site conditions in regards to Toronto Hydro and utility works. Options to include: incentivization with the Contractor to mitigate schedule delays, including reviewing additional staging options to complete Right of Way (ROW) work under Islington Avenue with Toronto Hydro, to provide an accessible path to a new Islington Station entrance as soon as possible (Q2 2027 or earlier).
