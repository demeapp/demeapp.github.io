Toronto City Council · Decision

Honouring Howard Moscoe

AdoptedJul 22, 2026 · Toronto City Council

The TTC Board decided whether to name the Yorkdale Station atrium the Howard Moscoe Atrium to honour the former TTC Chair, who died May 23, 2026. New signs for the naming would cost about $6,000.

How the vote went

Adopt Item — Carried, 8-0

Vote taken at: Toronto Transit Commission

The official record

What the item proposed

At its June 3, 2026 meeting, the TTC Board directed staff to report to the July 22, 2026 Board meeting with recommendations on options to commemorate former TTC Chair Howard Moscoe and his contributions to the Toronto Transit Commission, including consideration of a commemorative naming, and further directed that staff consult with the family of Howard Moscoe and relevant local stakeholders in developing the recommendations. Mr. Moscoe, a former Chair, Vice-Chair, and Commissioner of the TTC, passed away on May 23, 2026 at the age of 86. Following a review of commemoration options, TTC staff recommend a commemorative naming: the naming of the atrium at Yorkdale Station to the Howard Moscoe Atrium – the space that will once again house Michael Hayden’s Arc en Ciel light installation. What was decided

The TTC Board: 1. Approved the naming of the atrium at Yorkdale Station as the Howard Moscoe Atrium, in recognition of his service as the first Chair of the Toronto Transit Commission of the amalgamated City of Toronto and his decades of advocacy for public transit in Toronto, including his sustained efforts to return the Arc en Ciel light installation by artist Michael Hayden to Line 1’s Yorkdale Station, in principle and subject to: a. Consultation with the family of Howard Moscoe and receipt of the family’s written consent, in accordance with the TTC’s Commemorative and Honourary Recognition Policy; and b. Consultation with the artist, Michael Hayden, regarding the association of his work with the commemoration, and confirmation that the naming and any related signage are consistent with the artist’s moral rights and with the terms of the applicable restoration agreement. 2.…
