What the item proposed

The Workplace Harassment and Discrimination Policy (formerly titled Respect and Dignity Policy) was reviewed in accordance with the Occupational Health and Safety Act (OHSA), which mandates an annual review of workplace harassment-related policies. The updates also align with TTC’s Corporate Plan , best practices, the Ontario Human Rights Commission guidelines, and recommendations from the City of Toronto Auditor General’s Audit of the Toronto Transit Commission’s Non-Union Workforce Planning and Management report. In their report, the Auditor General recommended that the TTC update current policies and procedures to ensure clear roles and responsibilities for each investigative unit and other involved personnel including management, Employee Relations, and Human Resources, and communicate this information to employees.
