The official record

What the item proposed

The TTC Chair’s office has been in receipt of a health and safety related complaint, reported on April 10, 2026, where the complainant(s) expressed initial concerns about the health and safety conditions at a particular worksite, and subsequently raised further concerns regarding the timing or visibility of corrective actions following identification of an issue. This has raised questions about how health and safety concerns are escalated, tracked, and resolved once reported. I am requesting that staff present to the Committee about the particular incident, and in general with respect to the physical and procedural controls that exist to deal with workplace health and safety issues to satisfy the Committee that: there are clear, effective processes in place to ensure health and safety concerns raised by employees are addressed in a timely manner, and to understand where escalation,…

What was decided

The People, Culture and Governance Committee received the staff presentation on Health and Safety Management and information provided in-camera.
