What the item proposed

This report recommends an increased commitment to Holman Exhibits Limited for RFP-28 Design-Build of Retail Store in the Community Conservation Centre Project (Front Entrance and Welcome Area Project) as a result of scope changes.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Approved an increase in commitment to Holman Exhibits Limited (Holman) of $113,298.22 from $457,000.00 to $570,298.22 exclusive of applicable taxes.
