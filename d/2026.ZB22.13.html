Toronto City Council · Decision

Potential Development Opportunity

Adopted with changesJun 22, 2026 · Toronto City Council

This is a routine item. The Toronto Zoo board decided whether to explore a development opportunity on part of Parking Lot 1 with the City and a community partner.

No recorded vote. Made without a name-by-name vote, so no member's position is attached to it in the public record. Only about 9 in every 100 Council decisions has one.

The official record

What the item proposed

This report seeks authorization from the Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo to collaborate with the City of Toronto and a community partner for the purposes of pursuing a potential development opportunity at the Toronto Zoo.

What was decided

The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo: 1. Approved the confidential instructions to staff in Confidential Attachment 1, as amended in closed session, to the report (June 4, 2026) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Zoo. 2. Directed Zoo staff to report back to the Board in the first quarter of 2027 on the status of discussions prior to the execution of any agreement(s) related to the development opportunity described in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (June 4, 2026) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Zoo. 3. Directed that the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to the report (June 4, 2026) from the Chief Executive Officer, Toronto Zoo remain confidential in its entirety, as it contains information about an ongoing negotiation that affects the Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo.
