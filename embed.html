Embed

Put your ward’s record on your site

A small, free card for community sites, tenants’ associations, libraries, and blogs. Readers pick a ward — or you pre-pick yours — and see that ward’s member, their attendance and votes cast, and the latest City Council decisions tagged to the ward. Every decision links out to its full record on Deme.

The card is just the public record, in plain language. Attendance means: present and voting, out of recorded votes where a vote was theirs to cast. Deme publishes no grades, no rankings, and no tally of visitors’ positions — and neither does the card.

Copy the snippet

Paste this where your site accepts HTML. If you picked a ward above, the snippet already carries it; leave the ward off and readers choose their own.

To pre-select a ward, add ?ward= and the ward number to the address — for example https://demeapp.github.io/embed-card.html?ward=19 for Ward 19, Beaches–East York.

Sizing

The card is built for a narrow column: it works from about 320px to 480px wide. Set the iframe to width="100%" with a max-width of 480px and a height around 520px, as the snippet does. Shorter wards and longer decision titles vary the content a little — if your layout clips the credit line at the bottom, raise the height to 560px. The card loads its data from Deme, so it stays current on its own; there is nothing to update on your site.

Terms

Free to use on any site — just keep the “On Deme” credit line on the card as it appears.
