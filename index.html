Why Deme exists

Most decisions leave no names behind

Most Council decisions come with no recorded vote — no public list of who voted yes and who voted no. Deme keeps the whole record readable, and flags every decision where the names are on it, so you can see exactly how your representative voted. How Deme works →

The gap Deme fills

Built to complete the record, not just cover it

Existing tools do part of the job. Deme picks up where they stop: a plain-language record matched to your ward.

  • GovTrackUnited States

    What it does well: tracks US Congress bills and member voting records.

    What's missing: US federal only — nothing for Canadian municipal voters.

  • openparliament.caCanada (federal)

    What it does well: excellent, searchable Hansard and House of Commons votes.

    What's missing: stops at the federal level — no municipal or ward-level view.

Deme is built for Canada's cities: matched to your ward · your positions save to your account — only yours to see, never tallied · a permanent public record. Start searching the record →

Our vision

A world where no vote happens in the dark — and every representative answers for their record.

This is the record behind that vision: every decision, every recorded vote, and the promises on the record — public, permanent and plain. Every representative shown fairly and without favour. Every resident trusted to judge the record for themselves.
