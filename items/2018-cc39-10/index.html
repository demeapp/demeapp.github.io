Actions Taken to Date Arising from Council's Decision Respecting the Auditor General's Investigation into a Certain Land Transaction

2018.CC39.10 · Apr 24, 2018 · City Council

In plain language

This report provides information on actions taken to date in response to Council's consideration at its July 2017 meeting of the Auditor General's report on her observations of a land acquisition at Finch Avenue West and Arrow Road by the Toronto Parking Authority. This report also transmits a report from the Interim Chair of the Toronto Parking Authority (TPA) Board with respect to certain actions the TPA has taken in response to Council's directions. The Interim City Manager recommends that City Council direct that the confidential information contained in Confidential Attachment 1 to Appendix 1 of this report remain confidential in its entirety, as it relates to personal matters about identifiable individuals, including municipal or local board employees.
