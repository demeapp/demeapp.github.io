Ontario's Action Plan for Seniors Includes a Focus on Long-Term Care

2018.CD28.1 · May 22, 2018 · City Council

In plain language

Community Development and Recreation Committee requested a staff report if ever the Province announces an expansion of long-term care beds. Increasing the capacity of long-term care beds is included in the Province's Aging with Confidence: Ontario's Action Plan for Seniors. The General Manager, Long-Term Care Homes and Services recommends that City Council direct the General Manager, Long-Term Care Homes and Services to enter into discussions with provincial officials to improve capital and operating funding and to strengthen the City's role in service planning for long-term care services, and report back.
