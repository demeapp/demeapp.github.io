Proposed Amendments to Chapter 918 - Parking on Residential Front Yards and Boulevards for Ward 11

2018.EY32.87 · Jul 23, 2018 · City Council

In plain language

There are many locations in Ward 11 where residential 'front yard parking pads' exist. My office has received numerous calls from homeowners stating that their front yard parking pads were pre-existing and that they have no mechanism of legalizing them. City Council is asked to amend City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 918, Parking on Residential Front Yards and Boulevards, by inserting a new § 918-7.11 as follows: § 918-7.11 Ward 11, Pilot Project a.
