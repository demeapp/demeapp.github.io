TransformTO - Critical Steps for Net Zero by 2040

2021.IE26.16 · Dec 15, 2021 · City Council

In plain language

The climate crisis grows more urgent every year. The window to make significant and lasting change is disappearing. Action must happen immediately and it must be at the necessary scale required to respond to this crisis. City Council is asked to endorse the targets and actions outlined in Attachment B entitled TransformTO Net Zero Strategy (December 2, 2021) from the Interim Director, Environment and Energy.
