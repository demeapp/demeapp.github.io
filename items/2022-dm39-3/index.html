Extending Repayment Due Date for Eligible Properties from the 2020 Property Tax Deferral Program - by Councillor Kristyn Wong-Tam, seconded by Josh Matlow

2022.DM39.3 · Feb 2, 2022 · City Council

In plain language

This member motion from Councillors Wong-Tam and Matlow asks Council to amend the tax by-law to extend the repayment deadline for the 2020 COVID-19 property tax deferral program from November 30, 2020 to June 30, 2021, for eligible properties that could not meet the original date.
