Update on Bill 23, More Homes Built Faster Act, 2022

2023.CC1.2 · Nov 23, 2022 · City Council

In plain language

This is an information item: On October 25, 2022 the Honourable Steve Clark, Minister of Municipal Affairs and Housing, introduced Bill 23, More Homes Built Faster Act, 2022 in the Ontario Legislature. Bill 23 proposes extensive changes to the policy-led planning and development system under which municipalities in Ontario work. City Council is asked to receive the report for information.
