Renaming the Stadium at Centennial Park - by Councillor Paul Ainslie, seconded by Councillor Shelley Carroll

2023.MM13.11 · Dec 13, 2023 · City Council

In plain language

Councillor Paul Ainslie, seconded by Councillor Shelley Carroll, recommends City Council rename the stadium at Centennial Park as the 'Rob Ford Stadium', notwithstanding the city's naming policy. Rob Ford served on Council for nearly 15 years, including four years as Mayor, and the city has historically recognized former mayors with commemorative renamings such as Mel Lastman Square and June Rowlands Park.
