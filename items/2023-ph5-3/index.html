Our Plan Toronto: Recommendations on Seventy Employment Area Conversion Requests and Chapter 7 Site and Area Specific Policy Review - Final Report

2023.PH5.3 · Jul 19, 2023 · City Council

In plain language

The Chief Planner recommends City Council adopt Official Plan Amendment 653, providing staff recommendations on seventy employment area conversion requests and the Chapter 7 site and area specific policy review as part of the Municipal Comprehensive Review of the Official Plan. Toronto's employment areas are home to over 21,600 establishments employing over 390,000 people, and businesses there create about 27 percent of the City's estimated 2021 GDP. City Council is also asked to authorize seeking the Minister of Municipal Affairs and Housing's approval.
