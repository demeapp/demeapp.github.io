Alcohol in Parks Pilot: Evaluation Findings

2024.EC11.4 · Apr 17, 2024 · City Council

In plain language

This report presents the evaluation findings of the Alcohol in Parks Pilot Program, which ran from August 2 to October 9, 2023 in 27 parks. The pilot found high satisfaction among park visitors, with 92 percent of surveyed visitors satisfied, few issues, and minimal operational impacts. Staff recommend that City Council make personal alcohol consumption permanent in the 27 pilot parks, with health and safety restrictions, by amending the Parks chapter of the municipal code.
