2444 Eglinton Avenue East - Zoning Amendment - Decision Report - Approval

2024.PH13.3 · Jun 26, 2024 · City Council

In plain language

Staff recommend approving a three-tower mixed-use development at 2444 Eglinton Avenue East: a 31-storey condominium with 307 units, plus two co-op buildings of 19 and 41 storeys with 612 co-op units (306 market co-op and 306 affordable co-op). The towers sit on podiums containing above-grade parking and ground-floor non-residential uses. City Council is asked to approve the Zoning By-law amendment.
