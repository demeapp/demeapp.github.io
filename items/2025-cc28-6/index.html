Review of Councillor Remuneration

2025.CC28.6 · Mar 26, 2025 · City Council

In plain language

This is a procedural item: council is asked to receive it for information. The report advises that the City Manager and Chief People Officer will submit a report on a review of councillor remuneration before the March 26-28, 2025 council meeting, when any changes to councillor pay would be considered. No decision is proposed at this stage.
