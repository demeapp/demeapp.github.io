Commencing Public Consultations for Communities Impacted by New Shelter Locations - by Councillor James Pasternak, seconded by Councillor Parthi Kandavel

2025.DM31.1 · Jun 25, 2025 · City Council

In plain language

Communities near six planned new shelter locations could get individual public consultations. The member motion asks council to direct shelter staff to hold separate consultations for each of the six new shelter sites unveiled in December 2024, working with the local councillor on dates and format, instead of the current generalized consultation approach. The sites are being developed under the city's 10-year Homelessness Services Capital Infrastructure Strategy. Council adopted the motion with an amendment that consultations happen if requested by the local councillor, and added a direction for staff to undertake a 'reset' of the community engagement process for the proposed shelter at 1220 Wilson Avenue, including liaising with the local councillor and addressing multilingual requirements before advancing construction at the site.
