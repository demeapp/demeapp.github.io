Reimagining Weston Lions Arena through a Nominal Lease with MLSE LaunchPad

2025.EX27.6 · Nov 12, 2025 · City Council

In plain language

Weston Lions Arena could get a new life under a nominal lease. Council is asked to approve a nominal, very low-cost 20-year lease, with two possible 10-year extensions, giving MLSE LaunchPad use of the city-owned Weston Lions Arena to expand free, high-quality youth recreation programs in the area. The arena has been city-owned and managed by the Weston Lions Club under a nominal lease since 1970. The report cites a $25 million figure in connection with the revitalization.
