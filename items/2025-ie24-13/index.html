Optimizing the Automated Speed Enforcement Program to Stop Speeding

2025.IE24.13 · Oct 8, 2025 · City Council

In plain language

This motion from the Mayor asks Council to adjust the automated speed enforcement camera program, including a seven-day grace period before a second ticket from the same camera, and to install additional and larger warning signs near cameras by October 31, 2025. The motion cites a study by SickKids and Toronto Metropolitan University researchers finding that speed cameras reduced speeding by 45 per cent in Toronto, and the July 2025 death of 15-year-old Christina Huang near Finch Avenue East and McCowan Road.
