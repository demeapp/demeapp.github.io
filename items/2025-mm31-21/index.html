Addressing Gaps in the City’s Heat Relief Strategy - by Mayor Olivia Chow, seconded by Councillor Alejandra Bravo

2025.MM31.21 · Jun 25, 2025 · City Council

In plain language

The City's Heat Relief Strategy would get a formal review plus immediate upgrades ahead of the next heat warning. Council would direct staff to review the strategy and pursue changes — such as longer pool hours, surge shelter spaces and cooling measures — after a summer that saw the humidex hit 46, health rules close some pools, and Project Water unable to deliver water bottles to people experiencing homelessness for the first time in 25 years. Ahead of the June 22 heat event, the Mayor's office deployed three water trucks running 24/7 and 14,000 bottles of water through outreach workers, with Red Cross volunteers checking on seniors and low-income residents. People without air conditioning, seniors and those experiencing homelessness are most at risk in extreme heat. Council would also direct up to $50,000 from the corporate extreme weather reserve to Project FoodChain so frontline agencies can distribute at least 500,000 bottles of water in 2025.
