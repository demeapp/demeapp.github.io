Budget Implementation Including Property Tax Rates, User Fees and Related Matters

2026.CC38.1 · Feb 10, 2026 · City Council

In plain language

Approving the 2026 property tax rates and related measures needed to put the 2026 budget into effect: an average budgetary tax rate increase of 0.57 percent — 0.70 percent for residential and industrial properties, 0.35 percent for multi-residential and commercial — plus a City Building Fund levy increase of 1.50 percent (residential/industrial) and 0.75 percent (multi-residential/commercial) for transit and housing capital investment, goes to City Council for approval.
