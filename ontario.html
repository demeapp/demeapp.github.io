Members of Provincial Parliament

Facts only — no grades, no rankings. Recorded-vote participation is the share of recorded divisions in a member's time in the Assembly where the member cast a vote — not a count of sittings attended. Ontario publishes no pairing record: a member who is not listed in a division did not vote in it, for any reason.

Earlier members (2022–2026)

Members who cast recorded votes in this archive and have since left the Assembly, in the same record.

    Coverage. This layer is the archive as gathered on October 20, 2026. Recorded votes: 315 divisions, August 10, 2022 – June 2, 2026 (the last sitting captured); the Assembly publishes searchable name-by-name votes only from the 43rd Parliament (2022) on, so no record here reaches further back. Disclosure statements: the complete series, 2005–2025. Expenses: payments from April 2024 to December 2025, plus annual totals back to 2015–16 — some early annual figures rest on scanned documents, and ministers' expenses are published in a separate provincial system and are not included. Lobbying: Ontario publishes lobbyist registrations — statements of intent to lobby — not records of contacts or meetings; each current member's page shows the registration picture for their office, labelled as such.
