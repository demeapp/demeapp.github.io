Privacy

Deme privacy policy — plain language

What Deme is

A non-partisan record of Toronto City Council. Most of Deme works without any account and without sending us anything.

What stays on your device

Your picks (Support/Oppose) are stored only in your browser. They are never sent to us — with or without an account. Deme keeps no tally of visitors' positions, and never will.

What an account holds

If you create a Deme account, we hold: your name, your email address, your ward, a second watched ward if you set one, the topics you follow, and whether you asked for Council alerts (and when you asked; the source of your consent is recorded alongside the time). Your password is held only by our sign-in provider — Deme never sees or stores it. Account data is held by Supabase (Canada (Central)) and is protected so only you can read or change your own record.

Council alerts

If you sign up for alerts without an account, your email is held in the sign-up form so digests can reach you. Digests are sent through our email provider, only after you confirm, one per Council agenda, reviewed by a person before sending. Every digest carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing works immediately.

What we never do

We don't sell or rent your information. We don't share it with campaigns, parties, or advertisers. We don't track you across other websites. The site sets no tracking cookies; it uses your browser's local storage only for the things described above. We count visits with a cookieless counter that does not identify you or track you across sites.

Deleting your account

In My Record, “Delete my account and data” removes your account and everything held on our server for you. Before you delete, you can download a copy of what your account holds — “Download my data (JSON)” is on the same page. Your picks and Your Deme on the device you're using are yours and stay until you erase them there (the erase button is on the same page). If you signed up for alerts through the form and want that email removed, write to us and we will remove it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a deletion request: ourvote.app@gmail.com.
