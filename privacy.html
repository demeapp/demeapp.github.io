Privacy

Deme privacy policy — plain language

The short version

You can read the whole record with no account. Nothing is sent to us.

If you make an account, we keep your name, your email, your ward, the topics you follow, your picks, and the items you watch. Only you can see them. Your password is kept by our sign-in service. Deme never sees it.

Deme never adds up visitors' picks. We do not sell or rent your information. We do not share it with campaigns, parties, or ads.

You can download your data or delete your account at any time in My Record. The full policy is below. It is the one that counts.

What Deme is

A non-partisan record of your elected representatives — Toronto City Council, the Ontario Legislature and the House of Commons. Most of Deme works without any account and without sending us anything.

Your picks — and the sign-in rule

Reading the record never needs an account. To weigh in — Support or Oppose on an upcoming item — you sign in, so Deme can save your pick to your account and notify you when your voting card is ready. A saved pick is just the item's reference, your position, and when you took or changed it. Picks are protected so only you can read or change your own, and Deme uses them only to build your voting card and your notifications. Deme keeps no tally of visitors' positions, publishes no totals or percentages of visitors' positions, and never will. Picks you took while signed out stay in your browser only, until you choose to add them to your account.

What an account holds

If you create a Deme account, we hold: your name, your email address, your ward, a second watched ward if you set one, the topics you follow, your simpler-text preference, and whether you asked for Council alerts (and when you asked; the source of your consent is recorded alongside the time), and the picks you save while signed in (each one: the item's reference, your position, and when you took or changed it), and the items you watch (each one: the item's reference, when you started watching it, and — once Council decides it — the outcome that was emailed to you). Your password is held only by our sign-in provider — Deme never sees or stores it. Account data is held by Supabase (Canada (Central)) and is protected so only you can read or change your own record. Sign-in is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a person — not a bot — is signing in.

Who processes your information

Three services process information for Deme, each only for the job named here. Supabase holds account data — your profile, your saved picks, and the items you watch — in Canada (Central). Cloudflare Turnstile runs the bot check when you sign in or create an account. Mailjet delivers Deme's email, including account notifications and, once they launch, Council alerts. No other service receives your account information from us.

Council alerts

Council alerts are part of a Deme account: you opt in when you create your account, or turn alerts on later in your account settings. Alerts have not launched yet — no digest email has been sent. When alerts launch, digests will be sent to your account email, held on your account as described above, through our email provider, Mailjet, only after you confirm, one per Council agenda, reviewed by a person before sending. Every digest will carry an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing will work immediately. You can also turn alerts off at any time in your account settings.

What we never do

We don't sell or rent your information. We don't share it with campaigns, parties, or advertisers. We don't track you across other websites. The site sets no tracking cookies; it uses your browser's local storage only for the things described above. We count visits with GoatCounter, a cookieless counter that receives no account information.

Deleting your account

In My Record, “Delete my account and data” removes your account and everything held on our server for you — including every pick saved to your account and every item you watch. Before you delete, you can download a copy of what your account holds, picks included, from the account panel on the same page; “Download my data (JSON)” there exports this device's own copy. The copies of your picks and your profile on the device you're using are yours and stay until you erase them there (the erase button is on the same page). If you signed up for alerts through the form and want that email removed, write to us and we will remove it.
