Deme for reporters

Deme is a non-partisan, public record of your elected representatives — the House of Commons, the Ontario Legislature and Toronto City Council — the same decisions and votes each body publishes, in plain language, searchable by topic, by place, and by the person who cast the vote. This page is the data room: coverage, sources, method, and limits. The archive below is Toronto's, where the decision-level record lives; the Ontario and Canada directories carry their own coverage statements.

2018–2026 recorded votes in the item archive — every recorded, name-by-name vote in that span
94 members appear in the voting record, across five council terms
  • 4,856 House of Commons divisions — 2004–2026, each with its members' ballots name-by-name; MPs' ethics-registry documents and the federal lobbying-registry record sit on their profiles.
  • 505 Ontario divisions — July 2018 to June 2, 2026, each with its members' ballots name-by-name; MPPs' disclosure statements from the Integrity Commissioner sit on their profiles.
  • 33,795 Toronto decisions — each with its reference, title, date, deciding body, outcome, subject terms, the wards it touches, and a plain-language brief.
  • Recorded votes — every name-by-name vote in the item archive, 2018–2026, motion by motion; members’ totals reach back across five terms, to 2006, as aggregates — individual vote rows are stored from 2018 onward only.
  • Every member’s record — recorded-vote participation, votes cast, Yes and No counts, absences, and declared interests, in the same format for every member.
  • Campaign promises, with sources — on the pages of 25 of the 26 current members, the promises they campaigned on, each shown with its campaign source.
  • The lobbying-registry record — on each member’s page, a summary drawn from the City’s lobbying registry: who lobbied, on what, and when.

Deme publishes facts only — no grades, no rankings, and no tally of visitors’ positions. Readers, including reporters, draw their own conclusions.

Provenance

The record is built from the City Clerk’s published council record — the agenda items, decisions, and voting records the City of Toronto itself posts — together with City Open Data. Deme adds plain-language briefs and matching (grouping decisions by topic and ward, and lining each member’s votes up in one place). The record itself is unchanged: no outside facts about any member or decision, and every decision page links to the official record, so any figure here can be checked against the source in one tap. Where a brief and the official record differ, the official record is right.

Methodology note

How to cite and check this record

How decisions and votes are captured

Decisions are captured from the City Clerk’s published council record: one entry per agenda item decided, with the item’s reference, title, date, body, and outcome as published. Votes are captured from the same record’s recorded votes — the name-by-name results Council publishes for an item’s motions. Each brief is prepared from the official record; it summarizes and never replaces it, and the official wording is linked from every decision page.

What “recorded vote” means

A recorded vote is a vote where the public record attaches each member’s position — Yes, No, absent, or a declared interest — to the item by name. Only about 9 in every 100 decisions in the archive carries one. For the rest, the public record attaches no member’s position, and Deme says so rather than guessing.

What recorded-vote participation means

Recorded-vote participation, everywhere on Deme, means: present and voting, out of recorded votes where a vote was theirs to cast — the share of recorded votes where the member was present and voting. It is not a count of meetings attended; Toronto publishes no register of who attended. Stepping back from a vote for a declared personal interest is a duty, not an absence — Deme never counts declared interests against a member’s participation. Deme’s interest counts come from recorded votes where a member declared an interest and did not vote; they are not the City’s official declarations register, and the nature of each interest is not in this data.

Known limits

  • Decisions without a recorded vote carry no names: no member’s position can be reported for them from the public record, and Deme does not infer one.
  • Pre-2018 service is counted in members’ totals — totals reach back across five terms, as aggregates, with no individual vote rows before 2018 stored — but the item-by-item decision archive starts in 2018.
  • About half of decisions are tagged to a specific ward. The rest are citywide decisions, or carry no ward in the record.
  • Briefs are summaries. For publication, quote the official record linked from the decision page, not the brief.

What’s downloadable

Everything this site loads is published as open data — no account, no key. In brief:

  • Decision cards by year. The full search index — all 33,795 decisions in one file — is the bulk dataset, sent by email on request.
  • Decision details for every decision with a recorded vote, including motion-by-motion, name-by-name votes.
  • The members file: all 94 members’ terms, votes cast, declared interests, and recorded-vote participation.
  • One vote-row file per member, from 2018 onward.
  • The latest and upcoming City Council items, story threads, and subject terms.

The full list, with a link to every file, is on the open data page.
