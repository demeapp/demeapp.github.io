Deme for reporters

Deme is a non-partisan, public record of Toronto City Council — the same decisions and votes the City publishes, reorganized into plain language and made searchable by topic, by ward, and by the person who cast the vote. This page is the data room: what the record covers, where it comes from, how it is built, and where its limits are.

What the record covers

33,796 decisions in the archive, 2018–2026, from City Council and every council body
2018–2026 recorded votes in the item archive — every recorded, name-by-name vote in that span
94 members appear in the voting record, across five council terms
  • 33,796 decisions — each with its reference, title, date, deciding body, outcome, subject terms, the wards it touches, and a plain-language brief.
  • Recorded votes — every name-by-name vote in the item archive, 2018–2026, motion by motion; members’ totals reach back across five terms, to 2006.
  • Every member’s record — attendance, votes cast, Yes and No counts, absences, and declared interests, in the same format for every member. Attendance means: present and voting, out of all recorded votes in their record.
  • Campaign promises, with sources — on each current member’s page, the promises they campaigned on, each shown with its campaign source.
  • The lobbying-registry record — on each member’s page, a summary drawn from the City’s lobbying registry: who lobbied, on what, and when.

Deme publishes facts only — no grades, no rankings, and no tally of visitors’ positions. Readers, including reporters, draw their own conclusions.

Provenance

The record is built from the City Clerk’s published council record — the agenda items, decisions, and voting records the City of Toronto itself posts — together with City Open Data. Deme adds plain-language briefs and matching (grouping decisions by topic and ward, and lining each member’s votes up in one place). The record itself is unchanged: Deme adds no outside facts about any member or decision, and every decision page links to the official record, so any figure here can be checked against the source in one tap. Where a brief and the official record ever differ, the official record is right.

Methodology note

How to cite and check this record

How decisions and votes are captured

Decisions are captured from the City Clerk’s published council record: one entry per agenda item decided, carrying the item’s reference, title, date, body, and outcome as published. Votes are captured from the same record’s recorded votes — the name-by-name results Council publishes for an item’s motions. Each decision’s plain-language brief is prepared from the official record; it summarizes and never replaces it, and the official wording is linked from every decision page.

What “recorded vote” means

A recorded vote is a vote where the public record attaches each member’s position — Yes, No, absent, or a declared interest — to the item by name. Only about 9 in every 100 decisions in the archive carries one. For the rest, the public record attaches no member’s position, and Deme says so rather than guessing.

What attendance means

Attendance, everywhere on Deme, means: present and voting, out of all recorded votes in their record. It is a plain statistic over recorded votes only. When a member declares a personal interest in an item, stepping back from that vote is a duty, not an absence — Deme never counts declared interests against a member’s attendance.

Known limits

  • Decisions without a recorded vote carry no names: no member’s position can be reported for them from the public record, and Deme does not infer one.
  • Pre-2018 service is counted in members’ totals — the voting record reaches back across five terms — but the item-by-item decision archive starts in 2018, so item-level detail before 2018 is not in the archive.
  • About half of decisions are tagged to a specific ward. The rest are citywide decisions, or carry no ward in the record.
  • Briefs are summaries. For publication, quote the official record linked from the decision page, not the brief.

What’s downloadable

Everything this site loads is published as open data — no account, no key. The open data page lists each file, what it holds, and how it can be reused. In brief:

  • Decision cards by year, and the full search index — all 33,796 decisions.
  • Decision details for every decision with a recorded vote, including motion-by-motion, name-by-name votes.
  • The members file: all 94 members’ terms, votes cast, declared interests, and attendance.
  • One vote-row file per member, from 2018 onward.
  • The latest and upcoming City Council items, story threads, and subject terms.

The full list, with a link to every file, is on the open data page.
